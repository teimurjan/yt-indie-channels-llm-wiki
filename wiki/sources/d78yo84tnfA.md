## Summary

Tim Gabe gives a 6-minute beginner tutorial on the Figma pen tool. He shows where to find the tool and how styles apply to a shape or to one point. He then draws straight and curved shapes. He ends with shortcuts for editing paths. The tool is useful for icons, shapes and illustrations. The video is a how-to, not a business story, so it has no business metrics.

## Key facts

- Shortcut: press P for the pen tool. Once you start a path, the bend tool appears. [0:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=20s)
- Fill, stroke width, stroke position (center, inside, outside) and stroke style (dashed or solid) apply to the whole shape. [0:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=20s)
- The join setting (for example bevel or round) and corner radius can apply to one selected point. [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=82s)

## Tactics

- **Straight lines:** hold Shift while you draw. The path snaps to straight angles. To remove rounding, set the corner radius to 0. [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=147s)
- **Curves, method 1:** draw the path, then pick the bend tool and click points to bend them. Click and drag to shape the curve by hand. [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=184s)
- **Curves, method 2:** with the pen tool, hold the mouse button down as you place each point and drag to curve it. [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=184s)
- **Delete a point without opening the path:** Backspace alone opens the path. Shift+Backspace keeps it closed. [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=241s)
- **Select several points:** use the move tool, or hold Shift and click points one by one. [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=241s)
- **Break handle mirroring:** handles mirror by default. Hold Alt or Option to move one handle alone. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=302s)
- **Exit path editing fast:** press Escape twice to return to the move tool. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=302s)
- **Paint bucket:** in edit mode, the paint bucket adds or removes fills on closed areas of the shape. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=302s), [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=362s)
- **Masking:** the pen tool also works for masks. Tim covers this in a separate video. [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d78yo84tnfA&t=362s)
