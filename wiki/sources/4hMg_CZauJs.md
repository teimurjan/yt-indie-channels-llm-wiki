# I Built an App with Cursor and Made $100K on Launch Day

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Alex Finn, a former MongoDB consultant who built Creator Buddy alone with AI coding tools. Creator Buddy pulls a user's posts from X and uses an AI model to coach them on better content. A viral thread on the open-sourced X algorithm grew Alex's audience to hundreds of thousands of followers. He then quit his job and built the app over about 7 months with public feedback. The launch reached $100K ARR in 15 minutes because the audience was already waiting.

## Key facts

- Alex found Cursor in August 2024 and had a working prototype on day one. He launched on January 24, 2025. [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)
- Launch: $100K ARR in 15 minutes and $200K in 2 hours. It reached $300K ARR in 2 weeks (2025-01). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=0s), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s)
- Almost 500 active paid subscribers (2025-06). Revenue is about $25K/month (2025-06). [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s)
- He started posting on Twitter in December 2021. His thread on the X algorithm, open-sourced in March 2023, went viral and got a retweet from Elon Musk. [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=107s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=155s)
- 150 beta testers from December 2024. Alex met each one to walk through the app. [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)
- Stack: he moved from Cursor to Windsurf in October 2024. ChatGPT o3 acts as product manager. Next.js on Vercel ($20/month), Supabase ($20/month), Resend for email. [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s)
- Costs: about $250/month for AI APIs (Claude, ChatGPT and others) and $5,000/month for the X API. Total is about $5,300/month, so margin is about 80% (2025-06). [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=668s)

## Tactics

- **Break each feature into micro steps.** Ask the AI for one small block at a time: an input box, then a button, then the model call. Small requests give fewer bugs. [4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=244s), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=291s)
- **Use ChatGPT to plan.** Paste a feature into ChatGPT, ask for micro steps, and feed the steps to Cursor. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=291s)
- **Solve your own problem.** Alex tracked his tweets in a spreadsheet each night, then automated that. In the worst case, the app makes your own life easier. [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=367s)
- **Run a long public beta.** Watch what testers click and where they get stuck. He beta tested until the day before launch. [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)
- **Announce a launch date.** He announced on January 12 for January 24, then hosted X Spaces all launch day. Hundreds of people were ready to buy at minute one. [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=473s)
- **Build the audience first.** He posted for 3 years before he had a product, about 5 minutes a day. Fast, strong opinions about the platform drew a community. [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=556s)
- **Treat distribution as the moat.** Anyone can now copy the software. A clone with 10 followers loses to the founder with an audience. [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)
- **Figure it out with AI.** Use AI before you hire a landing page or thumbnail consultant. [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)
- **Act instead of overthinking.** Build or post something, even in 5 to 10 minutes a night after a day job. Each attempt shows what works. [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=744s)

## Quotes

- "The smaller you can break down your problems, your challenges, your requests, the better your results will be." [4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=244s)
- "Knowledge and product is no longer the moat. The moat is your distribution." [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)
