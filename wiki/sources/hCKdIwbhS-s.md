# How To Design a $5k Website in 5 Minutes

## Summary

Tim Gabe takes a challenge: turn a free Framer SaaS template into a site that looks like a $5K client project, in 5 minutes and on about $0. He invents a premium chocolate brand, builds a palette on Coolors, removes sections, swaps the fonts for free Google Fonts and adds a paper texture. He makes product mockups in Midjourney and cuts them out in Canva. He finishes in 5 minutes 22 seconds. Midjourney's monthly fee was the only cost. The point: a template can become a unique site with brand, color, type and assets. A real client still needs real copy, assets and branding.

## Key facts

- Price benchmark: Tim cites a Sortlist study. Websites like this can cost up to $10K from a freelancer and $20K to $28K from an agency (2024-07). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=0s)
- The base is a free SaaS template made by Framer. Copy and publish gives a responsive, animated site in about 20 seconds. [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=64s)
- The brand and palette took 1 minute 20 seconds. [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=140s)
- The final time was 5 minutes 22 seconds. Tim jokes that profit "lands at $4,990" after the Midjourney fee. [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=382s)
- Tim sells a Framer "Zero to Hero" course with 23+ hours of material (2024-07). [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=260s)

## Tactics

- **Start from the brand, not the palette.** Tim first opened Coolors, then stopped. He needed to know the brand first. A chocolate brand led to a brown base color. [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=64s), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=126s)
- **Lock one color, then generate.** In Coolors, press space until a good brown appears. Lock it. Then press space again for the rest of the palette. [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=140s)
- **Remove what you do not need.** Delete template sections that do not fit the brand, and make corners more square. [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=140s), [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=201s)
- **Add texture and type.** Use a free paper pattern PNG as the background. Change every text style to free Google Fonts. [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=204s)
- **Generate assets with AI tools.** Ask a chat assistant for copy. Make product mockups in Midjourney. Remove backgrounds and add shadows in Canva on its free trial. [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=260s), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=320s)
- **Know the limit.** Tim says a real client needs unique copy, assets and maybe branding. The result is still close to a $5K project. [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=382s)
