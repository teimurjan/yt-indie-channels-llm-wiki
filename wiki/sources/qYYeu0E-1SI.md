## Summary

Evan Yadegari, a ninth grader and the younger brother of Cal AI founder Zach Yadegari, grew Locked to over $14K/month (2026-06). Locked is a gamified self-improvement app: users check off tasks, earn XP and compete. He tested Reddit, X, influencers and paid ads, and influencers won. His team sent over 500 manual DMs a day and used four deal structures. The minimum view clause deal worked best. He then launched 10X, a "Shopify for apps" builder that hit $30K ARR in 30 days.

## Tactics

- **Gamify a Gen Z pain.** A friend struggled to focus. Evan applied video game mechanics to self-improvement. [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=254s), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=300s)
- **Design in Figma, build with Claude.** He and his then co-founder scoped onboarding and main screens in Figma, then built the prototype with Claude. [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=319s)
- **Pick niches with a natural format fit.** Self-improvement, gym and "day in the life" creators. Day-in-the-life videos work best, because creators check off tasks in the app on camera. [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=411s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=450s)
- **Judge engagement, not followers.** Look for 5K to millions of views per video and real comment threads. Many emojis are a bad sign. [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=604s), [18:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1135s)
- **DM at scale.** Instagram limits an account to about 100 DMs a day. So they ran many accounts, each with 1,000+ bought followers and verification. [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=743s), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=764s)
- **DM template.** Open with "paid promo?", then one line about the partnership. Always push for a call, not DM negotiation. [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=780s)
- **Four deal structures.** Standard ($500 for 1 video, $1,200 for 4). CPM ($1 to $2 per 1,000 views, with a cap). Minimum view clause ($1,000 per post for 500K views). Bonus ($250, plus $250 at 250K views). Keep CPM under RPM. [13:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=819s)
- **Minimum view clause details.** Set it at a $1 to $2 CPM. The creator posts until the views add up, or takes a pro-rata payout. [15:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=951s), [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=968s)
- **Brief rules.** Show the app within the first 15 seconds. Seed a "what's this app?" comment that the creator pins and answers. [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1018s)
- **Attribute by spikes.** Instagram allows no direct links, so he matches download spikes to posts. He renews winners with bigger deals. [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1070s), [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1096s)
- **Ideas from new AI models and social trends.** For a fast $10K/month, solve a problem with a large market. Trends such as the "imposter" party game also work. [25:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1515s), [26:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1562s)
