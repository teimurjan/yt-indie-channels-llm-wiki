# Our simple app made $100K in 90 days

## Summary

Nikita (in Serbia) and Evgeniy (in Poland) built Natural Write, an AI "humanizer" that rewrites AI-generated text so it passes AI detectors. Students use it most, and marketers use it too. They picked the idea because TikTok already had many viral humanizer videos, and they built the product in two weeks. Paid influencers gave views but few sales. So they filmed themselves, copied proven viral formats and posted daily, and made $100K in three months. They share a 7-step TikTok playbook and the videos that worked.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $41K in month 1, $32K in month 2 and $27K in month 3, about $100K in total (2025-09) [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=10s), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s).
- Funnel: about 9M TikTok views across their accounts, about 300K site visits, 250K signups (about 80% visit-to-signup) and $100K revenue (2025-09) [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=544s).
- They built the product in two weeks [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s).
- Team: two marketers plus a third member, Alexander, who does the tech [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s).
- Stack and costs: Next.js, Postgres, Coolify and Resend. The server is $35/month, Resend $20/month, a VPN $5/month for five devices and US phone numbers $5/month each (2025-09) [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s).
- Top video: a Jim Carrey comedy format ("you have an essay to submit in 5 minutes") with 4M views. Another copy got 1.5M views, while the original it copied had about 150K [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=520s).

## Tactics

- **Growth-first idea selection.** Pick a product category that already goes viral on TikTok, because it is easier to promote [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s).
- **Paid influencers failed.** They got views and almost no conversions. Self-filmed daily posts made a sale from the first video [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s).
- **The 7-step TikTok playbook** [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=328s):
  1. Niche research before you code. Use TikTok search and Creator Search Insights to find competitors with viral videos.
  2. Account setup. If you live outside the target market, use a VPN, an iPhone, a local phone number, a new email and a new Apple ID. Register the first account on the phone number.
  3. Warm-up. Spend 15 to 30 minutes a day for 3 to 5 days on niche content: like, comment, follow and search.
  4. Recreate. Make high-quality 1:1 copies of viral videos in the niche, and add captions in the TikTok editor.
  5. Post one video a day at the audience's peak time, usually the second half of the day.
  6. Innovate with your own formats only after videos go viral.
  7. Scale with more accounts and hired creators.
- **Show the problem, then the fix.** Both top videos show a "100% AI" text turning into "100% human" text [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s).
- **Do it yourself first.** Learn what works before you delegate [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=627s).
