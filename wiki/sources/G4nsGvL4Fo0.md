# I Make $15K/Month With 2 AI Apps

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Fernando, a product designer from Argentina who lives in Sweden. Fernando runs two freemium SaaS tools by himself: Resume Maker Online and AI Carousels. He started AI Carousels with a 10-day build-in-public challenge after his employer fired him. His method is to enter a market that is already validated and serve a narrow slice of it with a simpler tool. SEO through about 30 free tools is his main channel. He works 4 hours a day.

## Key facts

- The two products together make about $15K MRR (2024-12). [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s)
- Both products use a freemium model. A subscription unlocks the AI and pro features. [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s)
- He ran an agency for over a decade, then went back to a 9-to-5 job. The company fired him after about 1.5 years, one day before his vacation. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=0s), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s)
- AI Carousels got 5 paying users from its Product Hunt launch, while the product was still buggy. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)
- Resume Maker Online won Product of the Week on Product Hunt. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)
- AI Carousels launched at $9.99 in beta. Now it costs $4.95 (2024-12). [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s)
- Resume Maker Online started as a one-time purchase. He relaunched it as a subscription in 2024. Churn is high, but revenue is more predictable. [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s)
- Costs are about $1,000/month, so margins are above 90% (2024-12). He has no employees or co-founders, only a freelancer for content work. [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=654s)
- Tools: Notion to organize both products and Outseta, a no-code tool for billing, CRM and subscriptions. [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=654s)

## Tactics

- **Niche down inside a big market.** Canva has to serve everyone. A solo founder can serve one narrow task better. He only needs 0.1% of the market. [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=150s)
- **Remove friction that rivals keep.** Other resume builders made users sign up before they saw a result. His tool shows the resume after one click. [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=218s)
- **Sell time, not design.** He gets users to "good enough" fast. [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=218s)
- **Ship a bare MVP under a deadline.** A 10-day public challenge forced urgency. A failure after 10 days costs little. After 6 to 12 months, founders get attached and ignore bad feedback. [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=385s)
- **Payment is the real validation.** Praise is weak proof. Five people paid for a buggy product, so he doubled down. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)
- **Free tools for SEO.** He built about 30 free tools that extend the main product. Each one brings search traffic without deep SEO skill. He also posts build-in-public updates on Twitter and LinkedIn and runs an affiliate program. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)
- **Price as affordable, not cheap.** He lowered the AI Carousels price as the product matured. [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s)
- **Market one thing, retain with a bundle.** He sells AI Carousels as "the best AI carousel generator". Inside, users find more tools for their online presence, so fewer cancel. [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=547s)
- **Use small size in support.** He replies fast and often fixes a bug within an hour. His emails say "I", not "we", and the about page says only one person runs the product. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=589s)
- **Ignore feature races.** He follows user feedback, not competitor features. [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=716s)
- **Limit work hours.** He works 4 hours of deep work each morning, also on weekends. The limit makes him pick the important tasks, and the business grew faster. [12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=741s)
