# I Built a $30k/mo App That Helps You Quit Smoking

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Asher Hurstman and Thatcher Kloff, co-founders of Clear 30. The app is a science-based program to quit or cut down on weed. Their third co-founder is the clinical psychologist Dr. Fred Munch. Organic channels were an uphill battle, so they moved to paid ads on Meta and TikTok. Revenue went from $10K to $30K a month in about two months. They credit a rebuilt onboarding that follows a "value equation", plus one paywall change that moved conversion from 20% to 30%.

## Key facts

- Subscriptions went from $10K to $30K in one month (2025-08). [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=147s)
- They tried influencers, Reddit, organic content and SEO before paid ads started the flywheel. [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=178s)
- Paywall conversion by age: 18 to 20 year olds convert at 0.19, and 26 to 30 year olds at almost double that (2025-08). [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=380s)
- Conversion by quit reason: "saving money" 23%, "legal obligations" 29%. The average user spends over $55 a week on weed. The program costs $30 a year (2025-08). [7:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=441s)
- About 85% to 90% of users pass the intro screen (2025-08). [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=578s)
- App Store reviews grew from about 50 to almost 1,000 after they added a rating prompt in onboarding (2025-08). [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s)
- Paywall conversion went from about 20% to 30% (2025-08). Only the first paywall screen changed. [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s), [25:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1559s)
- The app has a grant from the National Institute of Health. A partnership with the University of Michigan is pending. [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=899s), [30:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1846s)

## Tactics

- **Hyper-specific call-outs in ads.** For example: "hang out in your basement after school and hit your pen until 3 a.m." Comedy and detail grab attention. Some of the best ads star Asher's mother in a calm, mature tone for older users. [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=307s), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=380s)
- **Ask "where did you hear about us?"** They use it next to an MMP and Amplitude dashboards for ROAS after the Apple fee, by channel. [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=488s), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=505s)
- **Track from the first screen.** At first they collected no data until phone sign-in. Early drop-off was invisible. [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=578s)
- **Value equation.** Dream outcome times perceived likelihood, divided by time delay and effort. Each screen serves one of these terms. [11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=679s)
- **Good friction.** Users spend 20 to 30 seconds on the goals and triggers questions with under 5% drop-off. Each answer gets a reply with social proof, such as "81% of users improve mental clarity". [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=730s), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=782s)
- **Show the pain, then the plan.** A comparison screen with cited sources ("you smoke 94% more"), then a 30-day outcome that includes "$220 more". [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=825s), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=918s)
- **Say the price early.** A screen taken from Ahead tells users the program costs money and anchors it to the cost of the habit. It raised conversion by about 3%. [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1038s), [18:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1100s)
- **Fewer "this is paid" complaints.** They aim to cut these comments on social media by 50%. They collect phone numbers and send human SMS copy through Twilio. [19:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1147s), [20:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1202s)
- **Rating prompt before reviews.** This came from a teardown of Cal AI. It runs after free personalized feedback, so most ratings are five stars. [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1225s), [21:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1317s)
- **Trial paywall.** "No payment due now", a Blinkist-style trial timeline and a reminder 2 days before the trial ends. Most users pick the annual plan with a 3-day trial. [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1405s), [23:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1439s)
- **The 20% to 30% change.** They replaced a feature carousel with a day-by-day plan for the first 7 days. The plan shows early wins, for example "CB-1 receptors bounce back within 48 hours". [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s)
- **Quiz ads.** They copied a "take the quiz" ad from Reframe. It did not work for them, which they blame on their onboarding at the time. [26:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1618s), [27:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1649s)
