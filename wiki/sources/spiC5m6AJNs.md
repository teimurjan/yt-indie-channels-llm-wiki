## Summary

Jacky Chou runs a portfolio of about 13 small internet businesses that make about $250K/month (2026-03). He started the SEO agency Indexsy in 2015 and built content sites that did well. In late 2023, Google's helpful content update and AI Overviews wiped out the content site income. The agency kept him afloat, and he used his local SEO skills to build the SaaS Local Rank. His daily YouTube videos get only 1K to 2K views each but drive most of his sales. His advice is to diversify revenue.

## Tactics

- **Treat YouTube as insurance.** He had posted for 2 to 3 years before the Google crash. That audience saved the business. His stream-of-consciousness style works at low view counts. [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)
- **Seven-step YouTube playbook:** [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s), [7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=433s), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=495s)
  1. Own a narrow niche, for example local SEO, AI SEO or SEO for plumbers.
  2. Post daily for six months: one long-form and one short-form video a day. Thirty bad videos teach more than two perfect ones.
  3. Solve problems your audience already has. A video like "How do I rank on ChatGPT?" works well. It can just explore the question without a final answer. The call to action is a link to your product.
  4. Hook in the first 5 seconds. He dropped his creatine-scoop intro because viewers left.
  5. Ride trends and search. Be first to cover a new AI model and you rank, even as a small channel.
  6. Build a simple funnel. Each video links to a newsletter lead magnet with show notes and documents. He pitches later by email, not early in the video.
  7. Make it a routine.
- **Diversify around one customer.** Pat Walls notes the projects all serve the same SEO customer in different ways. [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=630s)
