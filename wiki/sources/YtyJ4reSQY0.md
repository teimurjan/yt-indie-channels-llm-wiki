## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Mark Vassilevskiy, a 19-year-old designer from Kazakhstan. Mark runs Skale, a design agency for AI startups that makes websites, launch videos and billboards. He opened it in November 2024 and grew it to about $80K/month in 10 months (2025-10). His main client channel is free redesigns of famous brands, posted on X. He shares a five-step playbook to grow an X audience from zero, and his view that volume beats luck.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $80K/month on average, with a target of $100K the next month (2025-10). Revenue doubled about every two months: $10K, $20K, $40K, $80K. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)
- Team: 20 people (2025-10). [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=54s)
- 6 to 7 new clients a month, some one-off and some on retainer. Average retainer: about $20K/month (2025-10). [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)
- Clients include Bolt.new, Krea AI, Framer and Contra. Skale aims for work that gets noticed and goes viral, not landing page conversion tweaks. [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)
- Timeline: he moved from front-end development to design in Figma more than 5 years ago. He opened his X account in January 2024 and had 5K to 6K followers a few months later. He opened the agency in November 2024. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)
- X reach: 25K followers and more than 25M views this year (2025-10). [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)
- Tools: Figma, Blender, After Effects and Cinema 4D for 3D motion, Slack, Notion, and the top X Verified Organizations plan for brand awareness. [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=457s)

## Tactics

- **Turn rejection into free public work.** Every design job he applied for in summer 2024 rejected him. He then redesigned the hero section of a well-known brand's landing page and posted it. The post got about 110K views overnight and 10 to 12 cold bookings, and he closed most of them. [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)
- **Show the free work to the brand.** He showed a Bolt redesign to the Bolt team at a Bolt event in San Francisco. They reached out a week later and became a client. [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=209s)
- **Pick brands that people talk about now.** A redesign of the acquisition.com homepage got a reply from Alex Hormozi. It brought 15 to 16 cold bookings overnight, his best post. [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=209s), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=360s)
- **Step 1, comment a lot.** He left about 50 comments a day on other designers' posts. [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=248s)
- **Step 2, run giveaways.** Give away knowledge, a product or a service in exchange for a follow, comment and repost. Three giveaways took him from about 500 to 5K followers in a couple of months. [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=248s), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=299s)
- **Step 3, study viral posts.** He uses the TweetHunter extension to find the most viral posts in his niche and copy their formats. [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=299s)
- **Step 4, test freely at 3K to 5K followers.** At that size a post can blow up, but a flop does not hurt your reputation. [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=299s)
- **Step 5, make great work for trending brands.** Spend real time on top-quality work for the brand of the month. [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=360s)
- **Start with free tools.** Figma is free. Some of his friends make $30K to $40K MRR with no website. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=490s)
- **Volume negates luck.** Ask how many DMs or posts make the chance of zero leads close to zero, then do that many. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=512s)
