## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Deven Bhooshan, an indie hacker from India and co-founder of SuperGrow. SuperGrow is an AI tool that helps professionals write LinkedIn posts in their own voice. Deven had almost no audience and 2 to 3 customers. A lifetime deal (LTD) on RocketHub then brought in $65K in 3 days. He used the LTD buyers for feedback and as ambassadors, then moved to subscriptions and reached about $19K/month (2025-09). He defends LTDs against the usual objections and explains how he limited the downside.

## Key facts

- LTD launch: $65K in 3 days (2025-09). RocketHub took a 40% cut. [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=372s)
- Now: more than $19K/month, $230K/year, 800+ recurring customers, and 15,000+ landing page visits a month (2025-09). [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=56s), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s)
- LTD tiers: $79 (basic), $199 (pro), $299 (agency). Buyers got 4 to 5 years of value. [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=288s)
- 250+ LTD buyers. Only about 10% stay active. About 20 to 25 used it every week. [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=466s)
- Audience at start: 600 followers on X and about 500 on LinkedIn. [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=232s)
- The later Product Hunt launch won Product of the Week and brought 50 customers. [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s)
- Costs: about $3K/month on marketing and affiliates, $1K+ on design and product freelancers. Profit margin: 60 to 70% (2025-09). [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=825s), [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=840s)
- Stack: Rails, React, Tailwind, Postgres, Supabase for auth and assets, Railway for hosting, Framer for the landing page, Intercom, Mixpanel, Stripe and Microsoft Clarity. [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=801s), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=812s)

## Tactics

- **Pick a validated market and be 1% better.** Deven used Taplio, ContentIn and AuthoredUp every day. Taplio had many features, but its core content creation was weak. GPT-3 was able to copy a user's writing style, so he built a weekend MVP. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=100s), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=150s), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=210s)
- **Let the LTD platform do the marketing.** RocketHub co-founder Charlie found Deven through his posts on LinkedIn. RocketHub set the prices, emailed a list of hundreds of thousands of LTD buyers, ran Instagram and Facebook ads, and made screenshots and videos. SuperGrow only built the product and did support. [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=232s), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=325s)
- **Cap the deal.** He ran the LTD for 3 days and capped it at 300 users. This keeps support load and lost revenue small. [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s)
- **Paid users beat free users for feedback.** Free users ghost you. LTD buyers paid, so they tell you what is broken, often bluntly. [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=581s), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=466s)
- **LTD beats a cheap annual plan.** Buyers feel special and feel urgency. He doubts an annual plan can earn $65K in 3 days. [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=606s)
- **Obsess over LTD buyers, then productize.** The team spent 1 to 2 hours a day in Intercom. They did tasks by hand for users and turned those workflows into features. Clarity and Mixpanel showed which features people used. [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=680s)
- **Turn LTD buyers into launch ambassadors.** 10 to 15 happy buyers posted about SuperGrow on LinkedIn, X, Product Hunt and WhatsApp groups on launch day. [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s)
- **Upsell LTD buyers later.** He upsold many of them, which helped build the subscription business. [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s)
- **Know the downsides.** A high platform cut, angry feature requests, and cheap customers that can hurt how people see the product. [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=412s)

## Quotes

- "LTD exposes bad products really faster because these LTD customers are going to give very brutal feedback to you." [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=562s)
- "Free users don't actually validate your product." [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=581s)
- "It's okay to build a better version of an existing product that's getting traction." [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=842s)
