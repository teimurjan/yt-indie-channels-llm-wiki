## Summary

Max Hirsch is a University of Michigan graduate who did finance internships and had never shipped an app. He saw non-coders build apps, bought a Claude Code plan and built Maxed AI, an all-in-one lifting and health coach. He saw a gap: running has Strava and Nike Run Club, but lifters spread across several apps. A mentor told him to use a hard paywall. He filmed himself every day, ran his best organic videos as paid ads and tweeted his journey. Six months after he started, the app made $14K to $15K per month.

## Key facts

- Revenue: nearly $14K in the last 28 days, about $14K to $15K per month (2026-10) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s).
- Pricing: a single $24.99 yearly fee charged upfront, about $2 a month. MRR looks low for this reason [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s).
- 1,000 active subscribers, 164 App Store ratings, 20,000 iOS downloads. New customers: over 20,000 in 28 days and over 40,000 all time (2026-10) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s).
- His earlier idea, a food review app called Dish Rate, stalled. He did not know how to build it [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=238s).
- Stack: Claude Code (started on a $15 plan), Supabase at $20/month, Vercel, Expo and RevenueCat [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=238s), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=344s).
- Paid ads: about $250 a day (2026-10). He was starting a Sideshift campaign to find five more daily creators [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s).
- About a third of revenue comes from people who follow him on X. He started to tweet 3 months before the interview [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s).
- Features: an AI coach wired into every screen, plus sleep data and custom programs. Users track lifts, nutrition and supplements. A leaderboard and a social feed add competition [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=161s), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=566s).

## Tactics

- **Hard paywall from the start.** He first planned a free app with ads. A mentor he met on Instagram told him to hard paywall it [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s).
- **Charge yearly, upfront.** He does not want revenue that depends on people who forget to cancel [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s).
- **Film yourself before you hire UGC.** He posted 5 to 10 videos a day and checked hold rate and skip rate. The winners became paid ads. He raised the budget only after he saw product-market fit [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s).
- **Skip UGC creators early.** Until you know which angles and features drive downloads, creators burn money [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s).
- **Prefer Meta over TikTok for paid ads.** In his view, Meta buyers show higher intent and higher willingness to pay [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s).
- **Try clipping.** It brings organic and international downloads, and the wave is still early [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s).
- **Build in public, not in stealth.** Share what works, what fails and your weak points [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s).
- **A crowded market is fine.** Pat and Gus note at least three lifting apps on the channel. Personal brand is the part rivals cannot copy [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=680s).
