# I make $32K/month from 3 simple, boring websites

## Summary

Rashid Khasanov runs three bootstrapped database products: AngelMatch, InvestorHunt and JournalistHunt. Together they make about $32K MRR. The idea came from his own fintech startup, where he had to build an investor list by hand to raise money. He validated with a 40,000-investor list on Product Hunt, then grew through SEO, free tools and some Meta ads. Pat and Gus close on the lesson: solve a painful B2B problem.

## Key facts

- Portfolio: about $32K MRR (2026-08). Peak AngelMatch MRR was $43K. [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=66s), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s)
- AngelMatch: a database of about 125,000 angels and VCs. $29K MRR, 360 active subscribers, 33% trial conversion, 800 to 1,000 clicks a day. Plans start at $59/month and go to a few thousand dollars (2026-08). [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)
- InvestorHunt: $2,800 MRR from SEO only. Tiers at $57, $97 and $297 (2026-08). [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)
- JournalistHunt: a database of 200,000 journalists. $260 MRR on $49 to $99/month tiers (2026-08). [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=188s)
- For his earlier fintech app, he raised $100,000 from family and friends and maxed out credit cards. [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)
- The Product Hunt launch with 40,000 investors made about $4,000 in its first month. [5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=353s)
- Programmatic SEO brings about half of revenue (2026-08). [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)
- Stack: Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Ahrefs, DigitalOcean, Nylas for in-app email outreach, Cloudflare, Klaviyo and Google Workspace. Tools cost about $62/month in total. [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)

## Tactics

- **Productize a painful step you did by hand.** He built an investor list by hand for his own raise. It showed him two things: cold email works, and list-building wastes time. [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)
- **Go from 60 clicks a day to SEO.** He hired six content writers, published blogs and built free tools. After 6 to 8 months, SEO plus Meta ads took MRR from $3K to $20K. [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s)
- **Three-step database playbook.** Pick a database that solves a specific problem. Collect the data by hand or with automation. Launch, look for payment signals, then lean on programmatic SEO. [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)
- **Pick a niche and an audience you belong to.** He targeted early-stage founders because he was one. [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=463s)
- **Ideas for 2026:** an influencer database sorted by location, audience size and niche behind a paywall, or a newsletter sponsorship database. [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=503s)
- **Sell to businesses.** Pat argues directories rarely work as B2C. A founder who cannot find investors cannot make payroll, so they pay. [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)
