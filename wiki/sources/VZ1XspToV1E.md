# I Built a $1M SaaS Using YouTube

## Summary

Vasco and his co-founder Afonso grew Arvow, an AI SEO writer for agencies and business owners, to about $70K MRR in two years. The growth was fully organic, and most users came from YouTube. Vasco started with rank-and-rent SEO, then sold SEO services on Fiverr and Upwork. Next he built and sold an SEO marketplace called Vetted, and then he built Arvow. His playbook has three steps. First, make long-form "money-making asset" videos. Second, multiply himself with owned channels run by hired creators. Third, boost videos that sold with YouTube in-feed ads.

## Key facts

- Arvow: about $70K MRR (2025-10). The video description says $79K/month, and the title calls it a $1M business [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=58s), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=104s).
- Pricing: from about $99/month up to almost $700/month. It has about 600 paying clients (2025-10) [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=71s).
- Background: he began in 2015 or 2016 after an Alex Becker video on rank-and-rent SEO. He sold his SEO marketplace Vetted the year before this video [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=104s).
- A rough "how to make a Wikipedia page" video from 2 years earlier still gets about 300 views a day and 1.2M impressions. 65% of its traffic is from YouTube search and 20% from Google search (2025-10) [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=486s).
- Output: he recorded 20 to 30 minute videos every day for almost a year [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=634s).
- Stack: Loom Premium (HD), Miro as a whiteboard, Camtasia for editing and a Shure SM7B mic [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=608s).

## Tactics

- **Why YouTube.** Trust makes people buy. Long videos of your face and voice build that trust. He thinks YouTube gives the best results for the effort [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=159s).
- **Step 1: three content buckets.** The first bucket is evergreen videos, for example "best AI SEO writer". The second is news videos that spike for 2 to 3 days, for example a Google update. The third is MrBeast-style viral videos applied to software [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=216s).
- **Step 2: multiply yourself.** He owns several niche channels ("SEO tips", "SEO news") and puts a creator on each. He finds Upwork freelancers who have a video on their profile, because they are comfortable on camera, and he avoids established creators. He pays per video and per performance, and expands into Portuguese and Spanish [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=216s), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=277s).
- **Step 3: in-feed ads on proven videos.** Sales follow views, and views stall. So he pays for YouTube in-feed ads on videos that already sold. The ads show on the home page and in the sidebar [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s).
- **Attribution.** He uses a UTM per video and a coupon per video that viewers claim through the support chat. The onboarding survey shows the creators' faces so users can pick who referred them [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=381s).
- **CTA placement.** He moved the call to action from the end to the start and middle, which raised signups "by a lot". He uses specific, interactive CTAs that ask the viewer to do one action in the product [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=381s).
- **Beginner plan.** Post one video every day for 45 days. Polish is not needed: he recorded whiteboard videos in his basement [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=548s).
