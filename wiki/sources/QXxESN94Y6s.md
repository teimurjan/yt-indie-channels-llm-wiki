## Summary

Vadim Strizheus is 19 and had no coding experience. He built Vugola, an AI video editing agent that turns long videos into short clips. About 5 months after he started marketing, it made $11.2K MRR. He built it by prompting Lovable for 4 months, then moved to Claude and Codex. Twitter posts that rode AI trends got him to $2K to $3K MRR. A faceless-style Instagram UGC account then took him past $10K. He copies proven competitor formats and posts 3 to 6 times a day in about 45 minutes.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $11.2K MRR over the last 4 weeks. Tens of thousands of signups and about 400 paying users (2026-10) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=91s).
- Customers: clippers, content creators, agencies and long-form brands [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=91s).
- Timeline: idea in October, 4 months of building, marketing from March [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=312s), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s).
- Background: worked at a marketing startup from age 17. He quit to build with no coding skills, to force himself to commit [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=179s).
- Twitter alone took the app to $2K to $3K MRR [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s).
- New Instagram UGC account: 10.8K followers in a few months. Top videos got 132K, 21K and 18K views (2026-10) [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=478s).
- Only paid tool for growth: ManyChat at $15/month [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=554s).
- Stack: Codex, GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, PostHog, DataFast and AWS for video rendering [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=331s).

## Tactics

- **Enter a proven market.** He saw podcast, gaming and stream clips all over his feed. A small share of the clipping market is enough [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=208s).
- **Graduate from Lovable.** He asked Claude how to leave Lovable. It pointed him to GitHub and Supabase, which do the same job for less money [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=259s).
- **Ride viral AI topics on X.** He built a personal brand on Twitter. He tied Vugola to trending tools such as an OpenClaw plugin, because anything about OpenClaw went viral [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s).
- **The 45-minute Instagram routine** [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=418s), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=478s):
  1. Open a new Instagram account and connect ManyChat.
  2. Scroll for a full day to find competitor UGC campaigns, creators and viral formats.
  3. Copy the format exactly: text, audio, transitions, hook length, demo length and CTA. Film your own face and swap in your product.
  4. Post 3 to 6 times a day.
  5. Change only the hook and the sound. Keep the demo and CTA the same.
  6. Repeat a winning format until it saturates, then copy the next trend.
- **Time over money.** For B2C, posting and editing are free. Batch the content in 30 to 45 minutes a day [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=554s).
- **Simplify everything.** He says a solo founder can cut effort by at least 50%. A simpler product also sells better [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=657s).
- Host Gus says the same routine got his own apps thousands of views [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=705s).
