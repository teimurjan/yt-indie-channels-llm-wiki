# This 20yr old girl is a marketing genius…

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Eunice Lie, a 20-year-old college student. She became the marketing lead (called CMO) of the AI social network startup Series. She went viral on X by posting her Hinge chats with tech workers who asked about her startup. She also ran a "race to the Hamptons" intern campaign and made launch videos. She explains contrarian content formats, borrowing clout through collaborations, and how founders can hire and manage a content person. At the time of the video she had moved to Sonder, a startup from her Carnegie Mellon friends.

## Key facts

- Her content got over 3M impressions across LinkedIn and X. She made 3 viral launch videos for Series. Her New York Tech Week events got over 3,000 RSVPs. Her personal Instagram reels passed 2M views (2025-09). [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=34s)
- Her first tweet got about 20 likes on an old account from 2016. [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s)
- An earlier cinematic launch video went viral among VCs and helped that startup raise $3M. [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=378s)
- She turned down startup offers at 2x to 3x her Sonder pay. [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=540s)
- A Cupidly launch video that featured her got about 1.5M views. Its founder had 2,000 to 3,000 followers before it. [14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=886s), [14:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=851s)
- A calm, horizontal 90-second Instagram reel got only 35K views but brought 200 to 250 followers. [15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=932s)
- Series Hamptons campaign: about 2,500 applications from one LinkedIn post and job. 150 people joined the race for 12 show spots. Over 100 interns posted about it on LinkedIn in 2 days. The winner invited 120 people in one day. [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1104s), [20:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1202s), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1225s)

## Tactics

- **Skip the common trend.** "Build in public" tweets went unnoticed because everyone posted them. Real Hinge chats about her startup stood out. [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s)
- **Hook, story, soft CTA.** She dropped hints about her startup on Hinge and posted the replies. On one date, she walked the man through Series onboarding. [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=169s)
- **Day-by-day challenge formats.** People follow along because they want to know the result. [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=270s)
- **Founders need a personal brand.** The brand stays with you, and companies come and go. A big creator cannot always build someone else's brand. [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=309s)
- **Pitch a creator with a plan.** Name the target audience and the format. "Make me a viral TV show" is a weak pitch. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=418s)
- **Commit fully to a persona.** Take one true trait and play it up to the maximum. Half-commitment looks fake. [12:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=721s)
- **Borrow clout with a real fit.** Cupidly, a dating product, put the "Hinge girl" in its launch video. Outrage in the comments raised engagement. Both sides must fit for a collab to work. [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=782s), [15:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=919s)
- **Do the opposite format.** Everyone posts loud vertical video. Keep the hook, but use calm, less-edited, horizontal video. [16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=992s)
- **Guerrilla gifts and social proof.** Startups gift donuts and doormats that recipients post. Once one or two interns posted, the rest felt safe to post. [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1050s), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1104s)
- **LinkedIn is underpriced.** She says LinkedIn followers are the highest-value followers. [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1160s)
- **Turn a cap into a contest.** The show had 12 spots, so she turned 2,500 applicants into a race with invite challenges. [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1225s)
- **Hire for storytelling, manage with trust.** Judge the skill to make a boring topic engaging, not the follower count. Set qualitative goals (brand identity, key message) first. Add numeric KPIs only after you have data. [21:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1272s), [21:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1313s)

## Quotes

- "My strategy for marketing or just producing content is to just try do the opposite of what people are doing right now." [16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=992s)
- "As much as I hate it sometimes, but outrage and absurdity causes virality." [13:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=827s)
- "Your personal brand stays with you forever, but the company will come and go." [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=309s)
