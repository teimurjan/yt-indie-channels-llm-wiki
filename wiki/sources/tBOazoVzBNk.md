## Summary

Tim Gabe recreates the hero section of Hannah's award-winning portfolio site in Framer in under five minutes. The hero is mostly image elements that float and loop: a butterfly, a head, a snail and mushrooms. He collects the images from her live site, rebuilds the layout, and animates it with Framer's Loop effect. The heading uses an appear animation with a mask. A working file is on his site.

## Key facts

- Elements: grainy background, yellow frame with a border, logo, flower, head, mushrooms, circle, snail and a butterfly. [0:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=41s)
- Butterfly wings: Loop effect with mirror on, 3D rotation on the Y axis, and the right wing set to +40. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=100s)
- Butterfly flight: a second mirrored loop moves it across and off screen. The duration goes from 1 s to 10 s. [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=162s)
- Head: a loop rotation of -10 degrees over about 2 s, with mirror on so it rocks back and forth. [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=188s)

## Tactics

- **Deconstruct the original.** Watch the site closely, then take each image element from the live page before you rebuild it. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=0s)
- **Mirror loops for natural motion.** Mirror makes an element play forward and back, so wing flaps and head turns look smooth. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=100s), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=188s)
- **Copy and paste effects.** Copy one wing's effects to the other and change only the angle. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=100s)
- **Slow down travel loops.** A 1-second flight is too fast. Long movement needs a long duration. [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=162s)
- **Masked text reveal.** Put the heading in a box that clips overflow. Then add an appear effect that slides in from the bottom with a short delay. [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=229s)
