# How I Built It: $30K/Month Micro-SaaS

## Summary

Gus of Starter Story interviews Charlie Clark, a former Squarespace engineer who built Liinks, a link-in-bio and simple website tool. He built it in 2 weeks in 2019 as a nicer-looking Linktree alternative, while his first startup, a print-on-demand marketplace called Visual, struggled. He went back to his job during COVID and grew Liinks on the side. He quit for good in August 2024 once MRR matched his salary. Google Search Ads drive most growth, and the pricing is low on purpose.

## Key facts

- About $31,500 MRR (2026-10). [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=69s), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=219s)
- A price rise from $4 to $5/month in late June (2026) added nearly $5K MRR. [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)
- The first premium plan was about $3/month and reached $100 MRR within a couple of months. [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=424s)
- A Pro plan starts at $12/month with a custom domain and 5 pages. Some customers pay over $100/month for many pages (2026-10). [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)
- Timeline: quit Squarespace in 2019 for Visual, built Liinks near the end of that year, returned to Squarespace during COVID, quit again in summer 2024. [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=318s), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=597s)
- Spend (2026-10): about $100/day on Google Ads and $100 to $200/month on a coding agent. [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=788s)
- Stack: a DigitalOcean VPS, AWS for image assets, Resend for transactional and marketing email, Tinybird (on ClickHouse) for analytics, OpenAI API for AI features. [12:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=770s), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=788s)

## Tactics

- **Enter a crowded market with a better look.** Linktree had millions of users, but its pages looked plain. He built a nicer, more flexible alternative with multiple pages, richer blocks and more customization. [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=23s), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=100s), [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=351s)
- **Set up pages for people by hand.** He built pages for friends and contacts and sent them over. Early friends stay on a free grandfathered plan. [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=351s)
- **Growth phases.** Warm and cold outreach, then a Product Hunt launch, then a paid tier, then Instagram ads. After an Instagram algorithm change in 2021 or 2022, he moved to Google Search Ads. Search intent made it "money in, money out" for about 4 years. [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=424s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=485s)
- **Undercut the leader.** Anchor the paid plan below Linktree. He prefers many customers who pay a little. A Pro tier for multi-page users balances it out. [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)
- **Small price rises compound.** In a high-volume, low-price product, $1 more per month added nearly $5K MRR. [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)
- **Onboard from Instagram.** New users can import their Instagram profile. The app scrapes the picture and name, builds a banner, and offers templates and themes. [13:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=817s)
- **Add small tools as features.** Forms, QR codes, short links and a mock-up builder from a separate project. [14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=869s)
- **Quit at salary parity.** He tells his employer about side projects up front. He waited for a salary match and a family need for flexibility, rather than "burn the boats". [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=597s), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=685s)

## Quotes

- "I would much rather have lots of customers paying like a small amount than fewer customers paying more." [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)
- "That like $1 increase actually translated to nearly 5,000 in additional MRR." [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)
- "Pick a space that's validated, iterate until you feel like you got something, and then lock in." [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=913s)
