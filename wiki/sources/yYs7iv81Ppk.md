## Summary

Tim Gabe argues that engagement loops are only "layer one" of retention. The apps that win in 2026 add a second layer that he calls the "intelligence trap". Each session trains a model of the user that the user cannot export. He builds on Nir Eyal's "stored value" idea from Hooked (2014). He shows three cases: Midjourney (creative taste), Oura (body data) and Ramp (company spend rules). Each case ends with three steps to apply.

## Key facts

- Tim is a product designer with over 10 years of work, including for Spotify [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=0s).
- Midjourney: $500M revenue in 2025, $0 VC funding, about 150 employees, or over $3M revenue per employee. It started as a Discord bot and grew by word of mouth (2026-03) [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=177s).
- Midjourney V7 turns personalization on by default. Over 70% of active users adopted it within 3 months [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=222s).
- The IKEA effect study by Norton, Mochon and Ariely found that people value things they helped make 63% more [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s).
- Oura: millions of rings sold, on track for $1B revenue, and a $97M US Department of Defense contract (2026-03) [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=354s).
- Oura retention is in the high 80s (percent) behind a $5.99/month subscription (2026-03) [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s).
- Ramp: over 50,000 companies, including Shopify, Figma and Notion. Its AI agents made millions of spend decisions in one month (2026-03) [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=540s).
- Capital One bought Brex in 2026-01 for less than half its peak valuation [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=602s).

## Tactics

- **Map the investment loop.** Take each core action. Does it make the product better for this user, or only better in general? [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s).
- **Design cumulative personalization.** Find your version of Midjourney's image-pair ranking and make it frictionless [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=222s), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s).
- **Measure the compounding.** Compare results for a 6-month user and a 10-day user. No gap means no lock-in [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s).
- **Learn from data, do not just store it.** Under the EU Data Act, Oura users can export CSVs. They cannot export the model trained on them [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s).
- **Make the investment visible.** Oura shows how many nights it tracked, such as 1,095 [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=478s).
- **Ask the exit question.** What must a user rebuild at a competitor? [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=478s)
- **Learn from repeated decisions.** Ramp learns spend rules. A project tool can learn workflows, and a CRM can learn sales playbooks [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=540s), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=602s).
- **Count integrations.** Each connected system raises the cost of a switch. Ramp plugs into accounting, HR and Slack [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=602s), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=660s).
- **Automate the deposit.** Users add value with no conscious effort, as each Ramp transaction trains the AI [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=660s).
