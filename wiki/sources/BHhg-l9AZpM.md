# I Make $1.7M/Year In The Most Boring Niche Imaginable

## Summary

Bohdan "Bo" Drozdov co-founded Savvy Nomad. It helps US citizens who live abroad change their state domicile so they stop paying state taxes. It runs as a subscription with three plans and makes about $140K MRR. Bo argues that unsexy categories such as tax and compliance have real demand, high willingness to pay and few capable founders. He shares three related ideas and a playbook: find painful, overpaid problems, then productize one workflow.

## Key facts

- $1.7M ARR, $140K MRR, over 1,400 customers, six employees (2026-05). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=0s), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=60s), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s)
- About $10M in state taxes saved for customers (2026-05). [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=60s)
- Adds 120 to 150 new customers a month. Charges up front, so net volume runs above MRR (2026-05). [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s)
- Three plans: basic domicile, a mid tier (most popular) and domicile premium. [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s)
- Origin: Bo rebuilt his life after the war in Ukraine and worked on a previous digital-nomad tax startup that he shut down. His co-founder met California surfers abroad who still paid California state tax and built the first version. Bo joined as the marketing and business co-founder. [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=216s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=810s)
- Most customers finish the online part in under an hour. [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=713s)
- Stack: Bubble (whole product), Framer (marketing site), Ghost (content), ChatGPT and Claude (operations), Ahrefs (SEO), Customer.io (email), Power BI plus BigQuery (dashboards), Stripe (billing), SavvyCal (calls). [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s)

## Tactics

- **Four reasons it works.** Low competition for real demand. People pay to make the problem go away. The value is concrete: show the savings next to the price. The market fit the team's strengths. [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=272s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=317s)
- **Show the savings in onboarding.** Qualifying questions lead to an estimate of how much the user can save, then a plan recommendation. [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=713s)
- **Three ideas from Bo.** (1) Productize immigration workflows: agencies charge $8K to $15K, while a guided product with a lawyer review can charge $1K to $2K. (2) Productize entry to tax-friendly places (Dubai, Costa Rica, Malaysia) and exit from sticky ones (Canada, UK, Spain). (3) International banking, asset holding and estate planning. It is one customer with several revenue events. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=387s), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=449s)
- **Playbook.** Start where people are stressed and already overpay. Check whether they DIY from blog posts and Reddit or pay a professional thousands. Weigh competition against opportunity, not only market size. Pick one painful workflow and productize it, with a recurring layer on top. Savvy Nomad did only the state domicile change. [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=545s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=596s), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=656s)
- **Optimize for non-technical operators.** A no-code stack lets non-engineers run the business. [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s)
