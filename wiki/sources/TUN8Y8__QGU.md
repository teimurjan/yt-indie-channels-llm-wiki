# I was stuck at $100 for 2 years until I made one change. Now my app makes $10K/month

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Piotr, a Polish tech lead who built Visualizee on nights and weekends. Visualizee is an AI rendering tool that turns sketches and models into realistic renders and videos for architects and interior designers. For two years it made only about $100 to $150 a month. The first version used a complex node-based interface like ComfyUI. Six months before the interview he replaced it with a ChatGPT-style chat and an AI prompt assistant, and moved from one-time payments to subscriptions. Revenue then grew to about $10K/month.

## Key facts

- Revenue went from about $150/month to close to $10K/month in about 6 months after the pivot (2026-09). [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=21s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=63s)
- MRR is $8.2K. With one-time payments, total revenue is close to $10K/month (2026-09). [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s)
- Pricing: a 7-day free trial, then $35/month or $80/month by render volume (2026-09). [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s)
- About 300 active subscribers. Over 300 landing page visitors a day, 5 to 10 new trials a day, and 25% of trials convert to paid (2026-09). [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s)
- He has over 20 years as a developer and still works full time as a tech lead at a Polish software house. He launched almost 3 years before the interview. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=63s), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=205s)
- The first version took about 5 months of after-hours work. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=249s)
- Stack: Cursor and Claude Code to build, fal.ai for GPUs (the transcript says "4 AI"), Ahrefs, MailerLite, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, Next.js, PostHog, Google Analytics and Sentry. [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=557s)

## Tactics

- **Pick a niche, not a generic AI toy.** He saw AI image generation early and aimed it at architects and interior designers. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=224s)
- **Replace complexity with chat.** Users said the node system was too hard. The new chat assistant, "Vizi", asks follow-up questions and writes the prompt. Users only upload an image and pick options such as materials and lighting. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=249s), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=374s)
- **Pivot the presentation, not the audience.** The audience and core idea stayed the same. Only the interface changed. [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=352s)
- **Watch the activation data.** Before the pivot there were few sign-ups. After it, people started to render. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=450s)
- **Switch to subscriptions.** One-time payments gave no repeat revenue. Monthly plans worked. Charge from day one. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=450s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s)
- **Remove friction before blaming marketing.** For 2 years he blamed marketing. The real problem was the path from visitor to first success. [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s)
- **Use competitors as a signal to keep going.** Competitors had many customers, so he looked for the reason his product did not work. [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=521s)
- **Pat's takeaway.** Making the product simpler, close to a ChatGPT wrapper, created the growth. Passion for the problem kept him in the game long enough to find the pivot. [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=290s), [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=730s)
