## Summary

Rob Hallam is a laid-off software engineer who started posting on X in early 2023. His first five products made $0. A viral post about those failures brought clients for a dev agency. Later he partnered with Tibo, a former Tweet Hunter co-founder, to build SuperX, a tool that finds viral posts and helps users write their own. He grew SuperX almost entirely through his own X account, and it reached about $13K MRR four months after launch. The video gives his seven-step playbook to build an audience in public.

## Key facts

- SuperX MRR went from $1K to over $13K in the four months after launch, with 25% month-over-month growth (2025-12) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s).
- Price: $29/month. About 450 active subscribers (2025-12) [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=126s).
- Trial rate about 30%, about 60 active trials, churn close to 30%. Payments recently moved between Stripe and Lemon Squeezy [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=155s).
- His X account gets about 100,000 impressions a day. X brings 95% of traffic and most users. He grew to over 34,000 followers in just over a year (2025-12) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=155s).
- His failure post got about 150,000 views and led to his first $3,000 agency client [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=288s).
- A January 2025 post about filming in public passed 1 million views [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=394s).
- The July 2025 launch post got 32,000 views and 26 sign-ups. A selfie post after it got 46,000 views and added $1,000 MRR in 24 hours [7:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=447s), [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=507s).
- Monthly stack costs (2025-12): X API $2,000 to $3,000, OpenAI and Claude APIs about $1,000, AWS $200, Zilliz Cloud vectors $200, Claude Code $200, Screen Studio $10, Framer $10. He also uses Next.js, Node.js, Tailwind, SQLite, Stripe and PostHog [17:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=1043s).

## Tactics

Rob's seven steps to build an audience from zero [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s):

1. **Set the profile first.** Use a friendly face photo and keep it. Write a one-sentence bio. Pin your goal or pitch, for example "left my job to build my dream SaaS to $10K a month" [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s).
2. **Pick one goal and document it daily.** This gives consistency, a storyline and trust [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s).
3. **Run the content loop.** Post something entertaining or vulnerable to get attention. Follow with education or a pitch. Then post the result, such as a sales screenshot, with what it means to you. Repeat [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=709s), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=753s).
4. **Systemize with data.** Study top posts of big accounts in your niche. Copy the concept, not the words. He built a cursor for tweets and an algorithm simulator after he saw them go viral, then posted demos [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=796s).
5. **Double down and kill.** Track each post. For him, video, feature releases and honest moments won. He removed the viral algorithm simulator because few people used it [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=840s).
6. **Build real connections.** Do not spam AI replies. Reply with real value to the same people every day [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=840s), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=895s).
7. **Use what the platform pushes.** X pushes video now. Video gave him 10x the reach of text [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=895s).

Other lessons from five failed products: distribution matters, charge from day one, and build painkillers, not vitamins [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=225s). Personal posts can beat product posts. His selfie drove more sign-ups than the launch demo [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=507s).
