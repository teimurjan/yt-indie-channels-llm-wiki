# I Built A $30K/Month App: Here's My Exact Process

## Summary

Benjamin "Benji" Chen is a 20-year-old NYU student who built more than 45 apps in about a year. He then focused on one: Snag, an iOS app that shows free items near the user. He built it with his team at 10X Studio, and it reached $30K MRR about 4 months after launch. His process starts from the marketing hook, then a Figma design, then Claude Code, and an app takes 4 to 5 hours without the backend. Growth comes from UGC creators he vets by interview. He then turns videos that pass 50K views into Meta ads and scales them with a ROAS test.

## Key facts

- Snag: about $30K MRR, over 100K authenticated users, 9,000 conversions and over $80K total proceeds, as shown in Superwall (2026-05) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s).
- Plans are weekly, monthly, yearly and one-time. It has about 3.3K App Store ratings (2026-05) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s).
- Earlier apps: Pillar, a self-improvement app, got 11,000 users at almost zero CAC. Then came Height GPT and 43 more products [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=152s).
- Background: he sold a media company for six figures, then worked in Congress, at investment firms (quant research) and in media [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=152s).
- Build time: 4 to 5 hours for a good app without the backend [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=226s).
- UGC economics: about 10% of interviewed creators are good, so 9 to 10 per 100. Every 100K views makes about $1K to $2K in subscription profit. One soft-sell video got 240K views (2026-05) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=506s).
- One ad took about $3,000 in total spend before its ROAS turned negative [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=539s).
- Stack: Cursor and Claude Code Max for code, Figma for design and Supabase for the backend. GoDaddy hosts domains, and Loops sends win-back emails to churned users. Superwall runs paywall A/B tests, and Mixpanel tracks onboarding [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=300s).

## Tactics

- **Start from the marketing.** Work out the value proposition first. Then ask what the app must look like to hook a user in 3 seconds. Then wireframe in Figma and give the designs to Claude Code [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=226s).
- **Ideas.** Look on YouTube and Twitter. Or find top earners on Sensor Tower and build the same app 10% better. Height GPT had about 20 copycats, and some made hundreds of thousands with better UI, marketing and funnels [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=345s).
- **UGC at scale.** Reach out to many creators and interview each one. Pay a monthly retainer plus CPM. Videos over 50K views become Meta ad tests [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s).
- **Meta scaling.** A test campaign runs at about $50/day. With ROAS above 1 or a high CTR, raise the budget to $100, $200, then $300. Returns fall as spend rises and creatives fatigue, so keep making new creatives: "more tickets to the lottery" [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=539s).
- **4-step playbook for 2026** [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=593s), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=638s), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=673s), [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=703s):
  1. Pick a scalable idea with clear value. Snag charges a few dollars a month for access to items worth hundreds.
  2. Build a single-use-case API wrapper with Claude Code, and include account creation so App Review approves it.
  3. With no budget, film the videos yourself and hire an editor on a package deal.
  4. Improve the product to raise LTV and lower CAC.
- **Network with builders.** He credits Blake Anderson for much of what he learned [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=732s).
