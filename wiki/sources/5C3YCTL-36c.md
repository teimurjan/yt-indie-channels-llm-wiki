## Summary

Oliver Brocato started Tabs Chocolate, a "sex chocolate" made from natural libido supplements, in his college apartment. He expects about $11M in revenue in 2023 with no outside money and no employees. A friend sent him a viral TikTok review of a similar product. That seller had no website or social pages. The video proved that the concept can go viral. Growth comes from a network of creators and reposters who post hundreds of native-looking short videos a day. A July 2025 description update says he exited Tabs and now builds Bustem, brand protection software.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $11M expected this year (2023-06), all reinvested, no outside investment. [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)
- Startup cost: $30,000, nearly his whole bank account. He thinks an ecommerce MVP can start with about $10K. [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=564s)
- Idea to launch took one year. The first inventory sold out in three weeks, and the first month did over $280K at about 50% net margin. [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=485s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=547s)
- Year one: out of stock for more months than in stock, yet still about $4M in sales. A boat shipment of 60,000 to 70,000 boxes got delayed and sales fell close to zero. [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=254s)
- Content machine: over 60 creators make videos, and thousands of accounts post them (2023-06). [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=642s)
- Supply chain: boxes made in China. Chocolate and anything touching it made in the US, from four or five supplement manufacturers. [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=181s)

## Tactics

- **Copy a validated viral concept.** The original TikTok review had 8M views and 2M likes, but the seller had no brand presence. He built the polished brand version. [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=91s)
- **Pick a product that triggers emotion.** A novel product creates "emotional arousal". Viewers watch to the end and share, which drives virality. [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=136s)
- **Native creator content at volume.** Videos follow whatever sound or trend is popular, so they do not look like ads. Validated concepts get pushed harder. [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=701s)
- **Paid meme pages and light bottom-funnel.** He pays meme pages with tens of millions of followers to post his best creatives. He also runs a little Google Ads, plus email and SMS. [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=727s)
- **Find manufacturers by brute force.** He searched "chocolate contract manufacturer" and went through 20 to 25 Google pages. He put each one in a spreadsheet, then called and emailed them. Small manufacturers are more willing to work with a new brand. [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=616s)
- **Design packaging yourself first.** He drafted box designs in Canva and Figma. A designer cleaned them up. 3D renders then went to the manufacturer for the prototype. [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=181s)
- **Brand over dropshipping.** A brand creates repeat customers for new SKUs and equity for an exit. Dropshipping has no edge over Amazon or 150 other sellers. [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=366s), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=427s)
