## Summary

Lino Leighton is 18 and not technical. About 5 months after launch, his first app AbMaxx was on pace for $60K/month. AbMaxx gives users a custom abs plan and an "ab scan". At the gym, his friends kept comparing abs. That gave him the idea. Paid UGC with big creators lost money at first. A fitness influencer then joined as an equity partner. Paid ads on that creator's content scaled the app, first on Meta and then on TikTok, where the same creatives earned about twice the ROAS.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $55K this month, on pace for $60K. 16,000 downloads in the past 28 days. Just over 3,000 paying users (2026-10) [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=105s), [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=10s).
- First month after launch: just over $7K revenue [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s).
- Build: 2 months in Lovable from February. He threw that version away and rebuilt V2 in Rork in 1 month [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s).
- Early growth from big UGC deals and his own faceless TikTok slideshows reached $3K to $4K/month at only 5% to 10% margins [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s).
- Meta ads ran at 2x to 2.5x ROAS. The same creatives on TikTok ads got 4x to 5x ROAS. The target user is 15 to 30, and TikTok skews younger (2026-10) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).
- Stack: Supabase (auth), Rork Max (development) and Sensor Tower (research). He also uses an e-sign tool for creator contracts, PostHog and RevenueCat [5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=329s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=96s).
- He dropped out of college after one semester [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=860s).

## Tactics

- **Niche down a timeless desire.** "Abs for summer" is an old TV ad promise. A niche abs app beats a general workout tracker [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=208s), [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=956s).
- **Trade equity for distribution.** He gave equity to one large fitness creator. That creator makes the content and the team runs paid ads behind it [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).
- **Cut what loses money.** At $10K/month he dropped every UGC and influencer deal and doubled down on Meta ads [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).
- **Port creatives across networks.** He moved Meta winners to TikTok ads unchanged [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).
- **His five-step playbook** [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=525s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=583s), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=627s), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=687s):
  1. Join the app founder community on X. Search Sensor Tower for niches where many apps make over $10K/month.
  2. Post before you build. Run a faceless TikTok with 3 to 4 slideshows a day. Make one slide name your app, with the CTA "comment app and I will send it". Many comments after 7 days mean demand.
  3. Ship in 7 days or less. Improve the product after launch.
  4. Pay micro-creators with 500 to 1,000 followers about $100 for 10 videos. Test hooks, formats and CTAs to find a winning style.
  5. Scale UGC through SideShift, Tribe or direct sourcing. A Fiverr VA cold-DMs hundreds of creators with 5K to 50K followers who post often, show their face and have active comments. With 10 to 15 creators posting, run paid ads behind the best content.
- **Product last.** Rebuild the product or hire a developer only after marketing is stable at $5K to $10K/month [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=687s).
- **Avoid his mistake.** Do not pay big influencers thousands per video before you have a proven format [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s).
