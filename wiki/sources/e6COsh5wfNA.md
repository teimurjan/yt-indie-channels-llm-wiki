# They Built A $2M Business To Fund Their Dreams

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Henry Belcaster and Dylan Jardon, college roommates in Chicago. They run Clipt, an animation and video editing agency that matches top animators with companies. The agency makes about $2M/year and funds Smart Nonsense, their animated YouTube Shorts brand. They won big clients with "blitz" and "squatter" marketing: free, unasked-for videos for famous creators. They chose not to be profitable and put agency cash into their own content, in a Mr Beast style.

## Key facts

- Clipt does about $2M/year in top-line revenue (2023-10). The intro says $1.8M/year, built in 2 years. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=0s), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s)
- Smart Nonsense posts one highly animated 60-second Short per day. It reached 1 billion combined views and 1M YouTube subscribers in about a year (2023-10). [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=125s), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)
- Their Smart Nonsense newsletter got 10K subscribers in the first month and 20K three weeks later (2023-10). Each Short is also turned into an illustrated email. [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=149s)
- Origin: they met in 2015, started a podcast in 2020, and had 14 views after 3 months on unemployment money. [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)
- They carried about $40K of credit card debt for months while early clients paid late. [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=297s)
- The team has about 50 animators and editors, hired mostly in the Philippines. About 10 work only on the founders' own content (2023-10). [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=87s), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)
- The content team costs $25K to $30K/month, while the Shorts earn about $5K/month (2023-10). The content runs at a loss. [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)
- Clients include My First Million, the All-In Podcast, Ali Abdaal, Will Smith and Naval. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=338s)

## Tactics

- **Blitz strategy.** Sam Parr and Shaan Puri asked on Twitter for help with video. Henry and Dylan offered to do it free and showed up the next day. For All-In they sent one video, then a second and a third. After the first, Jason Calacanis hired them. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=284s)
- **Squatter marketing.** Act as though a big creator hired you and make videos for them for a year unpaid. They share the work, their network assumes you work with them, and new clients follow. [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=338s)
- **Story beats abstraction.** Early book-quote content failed. A good story in 60 seconds holds people for about 55 seconds, because viewers want to close the loop. Animation in a "South Park for nerds" style adds a visual journey. [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=428s)
- **Outsource editing at once.** A US editor at $5K to $6K was too expensive. They hired in the Philippines. This let the founders post daily without burnout. [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)
- **Hire people better than you.** Accept only a portfolio that gives a "jaw on the floor" reaction. A hesitant gut means no. [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=491s)
- **Pair a shipper with a perfectionist.** Henry ships at 80%. Dylan sweats details. Each corrects the other. [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s)
- **Stay above the clouds.** They outsourced agency operations. Henry spends about 1 hour a day on the agency, and the rest goes to research and content. [11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=690s), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=726s)
- **Scratch your own itch.** They hired editors for their own podcast, then sold the same editors to other podcasts. [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=779s)
- **Keep the core simple.** Matching one animator with one client for 2 years matters more than pricing, churn models or extra managers. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=810s)
