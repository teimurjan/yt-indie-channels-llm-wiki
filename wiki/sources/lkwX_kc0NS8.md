# I made 4,000 app paywalls and learned this

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Jonathan Parra, the former lead paywall designer at Superwall. He designed about 4,000 to 4,500 paywalls. His main finding: no paywall works everywhere, because the same design can win in one app and fail in another. Design and price packaging are the biggest levers, more than price tests. Simple wins: fewer plans, a plain "Continue" button, "No commitment, cancel anytime" and a single-page bullet list as the baseline. He also explains exit drawers, one-time offers, and why AI apps with tuned prompts can charge more.

## Key facts

- Jonathan joined Superwall pre-revenue. Paywalls first ran on Webflow, at about 12% of Webflow's egress costs, then in Svelte, then in the current editor. [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=225s)
- He designed 2,000 paywalls in 2 years (2024-09) and about 4,000 to 4,500 by 2026-02. [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=292s), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=311s)
- A cleaner product group with "Continue" and "No commitment, cancel anytime" beat the control by 10%. [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=344s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=538s)
- A simple "try for 50% off" paywall with a big product image beat a comparison-table paywall by 111%. [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1564s)
- AI wrapper apps with text inference often charge $30 to $40/year. $29.99/year is a common market price (2026-02). [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s), [20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1235s)
- On average, about 50% of trial users cancel after they subscribe. [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=703s)
- Average paywall conversion is about 8%. Superwall work often lifts it to 15% to 20% after a few iterations (2026-02). [54:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3254s), [55:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3316s)

## Tactics

- **Cut copy.** Users do not read. Drop repeated plan names like "AI assistant weekly" and say "Weekly". Remove small "limited time" badges, and put urgency in the heading only on one-time offer paywalls. [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=456s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=583s)
- **Add "No commitment, cancel anytime".** He adds it to almost every paywall. A chevron arrow on the button also bumps conversion. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=477s), [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=896s)
- **Reduce plan choice.** Show 2 plans, such as annual and weekly, and hide the rest behind "View all plans". Order plans by length and preselect annual, which has the highest LTV. [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=641s), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=862s)
- **Use an exit drawer, then a one-time offer.** On close, show "Not ready to commit for a year?" with the same annual price in weekly terms. For example, $39/year is 76 cents/week. On a second "no", offer 25% to 33% off. Save bigger discounts for Black Friday so the brand stays premium. [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s), [18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1115s), [19:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1186s)
- **Read the plan mix.** Many monthly or weekly buyers suggest the onboarding did not sell the product well enough. [16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1006s)
- **Charge for the prompt.** Users pay for niche apps with tuned prompts and end-to-end flows. Apps that target an insecurity, like looksmaxxing or dating replies, can charge more. One chat app bills $20 to $22/month. [22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1332s), [23:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1417s)
- **Start from a bullet list baseline.** Use a heading, USP subtitle, bullets, social proof and a large CTA, often 65 points tall. Test images, video and extra pages against it. Test design and packaging before price, because price tests need cohort management. [32:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1962s), [28:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1681s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2171s)
- **Use a hero's journey for transformation apps.** The Clear 30 style paywall shows progress steps. Blinkist's trial timeline still performs after years. [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=896s), [40:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2417s)
- **Show the app in a video.** He records the app on his phone and drops it into Rotato. The style comes from Mojo. [43:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2638s), [45:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2737s)
- **Try a native SwiftUI look.** A plain Apple-style paywall beat custom designs twice. Also test friends-and-family plans and dark, premium contrast for a top tier. [46:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2800s), [49:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2991s), [52:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3133s)
