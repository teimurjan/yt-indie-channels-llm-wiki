# How To Grow Your App 23x Faster Than The Competition

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Canyon of Sideshift, a UGC creator marketplace. Canyon sees creator programs that reach 7 to 10 figures of views a month. He explains how to staff a UGC team: a generalist manager first, a strategist and coach while you look for content-market fit, then recruiters to scale. He also covers how to judge creators, how to write briefs and why Instagram now beats TikTok. His main growth angle is a niche that looks too small, such as knitting, ballet or wrestling, followed by adjacent markets and cheaper international creators. He ends with a demo of Sideshift V3, which runs organic winners as paid ads.

## Key facts

- Manager pilot: a one-month retainer of $2K to $4K/month, or about $150 per creator, plus about 5% of all creator bonuses (2026-09) [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=270s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=317s).
- Example creator deal: a $1,000 retainer plus a $1 CPM [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=317s).
- Instagram drives about 10 to 20% more views than TikTok in aggregate, a shift seen over the 3 to 6 months before 2026-09. TikTok shadowbans "monetization niche" apps (side hustle, crypto, settlements) [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1171s), [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1232s).
- Narrow niche apps can usually reach about $10K/month. Adjacent markets can take them to $100K or $1M/month [23:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1420s), [26:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1615s).
- A video costs about $25 in the US and about $15 in LATAM for similar quality (2026-09) [32:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1975s).
- Sideshift onboarded about 30 agencies in summer 2026 [46:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2772s).
- Paid-ad signal: a video with 5 to 10x the creator's normal views, 8 to 10%+ engagement and comments that ask about the product [50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3015s).

## Tactics

- **Team order.** A program changes once it has more than 10 creators. First hire a generalist manager, ideally an ex-viral creator, who reviews videos and checks in daily. Pay the manager a share of creator bonuses so the incentives match [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=223s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=317s).
- **Two phases.** In discovery, a strategist and coach finds formats and hooks and reviews every video. In scale-up, recruiters matter most. Look for creators with only 1 or 2 campaigns behind them. They have not yet seen high retainers, so they come cheap and stay loyal [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=477s), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=593s).
- **Judge creators by content quality, not past views.** Look for a "face card", storytelling to camera and fast replies in onboarding. More than 5 active campaigns is a red flag. Zero campaigns is a mild flag unless the creator is new [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=666s), [13:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=822s).
- **Briefs.** Use reference videos, hook ideas, CTA slots and themes, not word-for-word scripts. Review videos before they go live. Reviewers record Loom breakdowns of good and bad videos [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=917s), [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1012s).
- **Cross-post to Facebook Reels.** It needs little extra effort, and most creators accept a bonus with little or no raise in base pay [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1171s).
- **Own a tiny niche, then expand.** Fewer competitors means winning formats last longer. Then grow into adjacent groups, for example from ballet to all dance [22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1324s), [23:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1420s).
- **Go international.** Use LATAM as a cheap place to test formats. Europe and East Asia have strong purchasing power and less UGC. Start with 3 to 5 creators per market, one market at a time, with a manager who speaks the language. Promote top creators to managers [33:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2035s), [35:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2151s).
- **Organic to paid.** Run organic winners as Spark or partnership ads. Pay creators a share of ad spend or revenue, which lowers the risk compared with retainers or CPM. A video that flopped organically can still win as an ad [37:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2257s), [51:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3075s).
- **AI.** Use agents for recruiting, rate negotiation and creator ops, such as cutting creators under 300 views a post. Creative taste stays human [41:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2474s), [44:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2663s).
