## Summary

Frank Smith is a sports short-form creator with over 4M followers. He is not technical. He built GeoSports, a daily sports geography web game, with Claude in about 8 hours over one weekend. Players read a clue and tap the place on a map, five questions a day. Three months later, the Geo Games family (GeoSports, GeoHistory, GeoFooty) makes about $40K/month (2026-09). Most revenue comes from programmatic ads, and 98% of traffic comes from word of mouth.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $40K/month, three months after launch (2026-09). [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=47s)
- About 700K monthly active users (2026-09). [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)
- Programmatic ads bring $30K to $35K/month, about $1,200/day. About 1,800 Pro subscribers pay through Stripe (2026-09). [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)
- Organic share rate: 53%. Of 100K daily players, about 53K share their score. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)
- Over 1M unique players in under one month, with no email campaign. [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)
- Launch: a reply to his own NBA tweet went viral. It brought about 40K players, then 150K players the next day. The game was 5 days old. [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=636s)
- His own posts drive about 2K clicks a day, about 2% of daily traffic. He has made one video about the game. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=357s)
- Stack: Claude Max 20x and Vercel Pro for hosting and the domain. MapLibre draws the globe. Upstash Redis holds leaderboards and stats. Google Sheets holds the questions. Stripe runs Pro plans. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=496s)
- Earlier game: Five Card Draw had about 110K players. [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=247s)

## Tactics

- **Remix a game you love.** His brother showed him MapTap, a map game with no trivia. Frank added a sports trivia twist. [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=413s)
- **Spec first, then build with Claude.** He gave Claude the game details and had it research MapTap and write a spec. When he got stuck, he sent screenshots and asked questions. [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=456s), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=585s)
- **Build for web first.** People played inside X without leaving the app. Near-zero friction helped it go viral. [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)
- **Make the product shareable.** The score share is the growth engine. Most players come from group chats. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=357s), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)
- **No tutorial needed.** The landing page is simple on purpose. Players learn by tapping. [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=146s)
- **Guest hosts.** He DMs sports media people and athletes who liked game posts. They write the five daily questions. He announces the host and they usually retweet it, which reaches a new audience. [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=714s)
- **Daily habit for retention.** The game relies on players who return every day. [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=714s)
- **Spin-offs.** The same mechanic runs for other niches, such as history and football. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)
- **Block 8 hours.** Set aside one uninterrupted block to start. When stuck, ask the LLM. [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=585s)
- **Stay in the game.** He made YouTube videos from 2007 but only broke out in 2020. His games took off in 2026. [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=766s)
