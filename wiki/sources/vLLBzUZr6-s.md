# My Apps Make $4.5M/Year With $0 Marketing

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Erikas, a Lithuanian designer in Paris who builds Shopify apps with a developer co-founder. Their Kaching apps help merchants raise average order value, and they reach $4.5M ARR at about 90% profit. They spend almost nothing on ads. Growth comes from Shopify App Store ranking, which depends on five-star reviews. Most reviews come from a technical 24/7 support team that earns bonuses per review.

## Key facts

- Five apps make $4.5M ARR at about 90% profit margin (2025-04) ([1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=62s)).
- Kaching Bundles, the first app, is at about $400K MRR (2025-04). Later apps: post-purchase upsell, pop-up upsell and cart drawer upsell. All sit in the Shopify discount category ([1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).
- They launched in August 2022. Merchants made over $400M in extra revenue with the apps (2025-04) ([1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).
- Erikas started at a web design agency for $2 per hour. He began freelancing around 2015 or 2016 ([3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=198s)).
- The Shopify Partners page said the top 25% of app developers make almost $300K per year. That made him enter the ecosystem ([3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=198s), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=261s)).
- About 95% of reviews come from customer support ([11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s)).
- Profit was about 90% from day one ([14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=853s)).

## Tactics

- **Gap between indie and VC apps.** Indie Shopify apps had poor, technical UX. VC-backed apps had good UX but shipped slowly. A designer-led team can beat both ([2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=136s)).
- **Three idea filters.** The app is easy to build in a couple of months. It is useful to any Shopify store. It has low competition ([4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=280s)).
- **Validate with a design mockup.** He posted a screenshot of the unbuilt app in dropshipper Discords and e-commerce Facebook groups. It got over 100 likes and people asked where to download it ([5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=341s)).
- **Design from competitors.** He goes through competitor apps, notes likes and dislikes, then designs an app that is clearly better ([8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=518s)).
- **Recruit a co-founder like a sale.** He posted in a local developer Facebook group. He pitched his Shopify experience, his design skill and his existing Shopify clients ([9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=580s)).
- **Manual first installs.** A new app sits at the bottom of the listings. He asked clients, friends and communities to install the free app. Installs become reviews, and reviews become rank and revenue ([9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=580s), [10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=640s)).
- **Review engine.** The app asks for a review in the product. A monthly email shows the revenue the app made for the merchant and asks for a review. Support agents can fix merchant code ([11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s)).
- **Gamified support.** Agents get a bonus per review, a monthly leaderboard and raffles. A "persuader of the month" award goes to the best ask-to-review conversion rate ([12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=733s)).
- **Launch playbook.** Launch free. Monetize only after reviews and keyword rank arrive. Grandfather early users as free and charge only new users. Offer a free trial and full refunds, because a one-star review costs more ([13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=812s)).
- **Usage tiers.** Price rises with usage ([13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s)).
- **Lean tools.** Slack, Intercom for support, features.vote for feature requests, Linear for tasks, Mantle for Shopify metrics and affiliates ([14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=853s)).
- **Two-person start.** A designer and a developer are enough. Do support yourself until tickets grow ([16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=974s)).

## Quotes

- "I think around 95% of our reviews for our apps come from customer support." ([11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s))
- "We offer full refunds, no questions asked, because I think the risk of getting a one-star review is just too big." ([13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=812s))
