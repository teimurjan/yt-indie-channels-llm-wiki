# The Stupid Simple Content Strategy That's Making Apps Go Viral

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Casper, a finance and side-hustle creator with about 9M followers. He is the 50/50 distribution co-founder of Payout, a class-action settlement app that made $217K in 5 months (2026-03). This is the creator's side of the Connor Burd episode. Casper treats paid brand deals as free experiments: when a sponsored format wins, he reuses its hook for his own app. He shares the pitch that would make him say yes, his two winning formats, conversion numbers, and how a coordinated push took the app onto the App Store charts.

## Key facts

- Payout made $217K in 5 months, and Casper calls it a 100K/month app (2026-03). [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=209s)
- The idea came from a competitor that offered him only $500 for a sponsored video. [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=350s)
- Funnel on one video: about 13,000 commenters asked for the link, about 50% clicked (about 6,500), and download-to-paid is about 6.7% (2026-03). [14:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=897s), [15:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=912s)
- A reused Gelato hook ("Watch me make up to $900 in 30 seconds") made about $5K+ in subscriptions. One Temu skit video made about $10K. [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=779s), [16:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=986s)
- About 70% of views come from non-followers. His average is about 1.4M views per post across platforms (2026-03). [24:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1459s), [30:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1808s)
- Five creators posted on one day and made $9,700 in revenue. The app ranked about #70 to #80 in Finance. [46:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2771s), [47:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2833s)
- A 17M-view video had only 2.9M views in its first week. It still gets 30 to 50 views an hour 500+ days later. [43:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2629s)
- For his third app he gave the developer only 15%. He could instead pay $10K to $15K for a build. [50:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=3037s)

## Tactics

- **Mine a creator's sponsored posts for ideas.** High like-to-view ratios show organic winners: 50K likes on 2M views is organic, but 2K likes on 2M views was boosted. [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=568s)
- **The pitch:** "We built this app for you, based on an ad that worked for you. Here's 50%. Just make these videos." Better still, let the creator try one video and keep 100% of its revenue. [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=681s), [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1716s)
- **Clone the hook, swap the details.** Keep the structure. Change the number, the time and the background to avoid ad fatigue. [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=953s)
- **The skit format:** "Hey [Company], is it true I can make $1,000 from your lawsuit?" It turns into a how-to after about 30 to 40% of the video. [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1042s), [21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1279s)
- **Never say the app's name.** "Go to this app" sounds like a friend's tip, not an ad. [20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1229s)
- **Sell the bag, not the news.** Videos about why a company was sued got views but no sign-ups. [22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1338s)
- **Retention craft.** Script and idea come first. Then use moderate hand movement, emoji pop-ups, and one path of logic a 12-year-old could follow. [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1069s), [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1092s)
- **Platform-specific calls to action.** Use ManyChat "comment CLAIM" on Instagram. On TikTok and Shorts, cut the video shorter and pin a soft comment naming the app. [18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1129s), [19:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1193s)
- **Reuse organic winners as Meta ads.** One boosted organic video stayed profitable for a few weeks. Outside creators charge $1K to $1.5K per UGC video with limited usage rights. [25:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1504s), [26:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1573s)
- **ROI apps sell easily.** Pay about $40/year, recover $200+ in claims. [33:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1981s)
- **Coordinate pushes.** Have friend creators post on one day, at rates up to 70% off. Top-20 category rank brings organic App Store traffic. A $1,500 post can still lose money. [47:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2851s), [48:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2880s)
