# Copy This Growth Strategy, It'll Blow Up Your App

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Phoenix Do, who builds consumer apps in the manifesting and self-help niche. His app Copia turns a viral ChatGPT "day in my dream life" prompt into an app with affirmations. Growth came from organic TikTok and Instagram "hook and demo" videos, then a UGC program with about 40 creators. A longer onboarding with social proof and questions raised the install-to-paywall rate from about 50% to 85-90%. Phoenix argues that hard paywalls hide weak products and that lasting apps need a user "investment" loop.

## Key facts

- Copia: about $25K MRR and 100K downloads (2025-10) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=0s).
- Co-founder Chloe's hook-and-demo video on Instagram reached about 10M views. That proved the concept [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s).
- A 6.6M-view video without the demo got an estimated 20-30% fewer downloads than it could have [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=376s).
- "Crash out" button slideshow: 13M views, 10K-15K downloads from two TikTok slides. Phoenix estimates a hook-and-demo version would have got 30K-40K [19:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1184s), [19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1192s).
- Funnel before the fix: 50-55% install to paywall, 15-20% paywall to trial. After the fix: 85-90% reach the paywall, which doubled conversion (2025-10) [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=696s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s).
- Creator targets: 50K-250K TikTok followers, at about $1 CPM to start [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=616s).
- The core audience is women aged 18-25. He used SideShift to hire 10-20 good UGC creators fast [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s).
- The Astronomer CEO "fake daughter" account got about 200K followers and about 50M views in a week. A Copia placement got 5M views but only a couple thousand downloads [24:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1447s), [25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1507s).

## Tactics

- **Turn a viral prompt into an app.** Women posted emotional reactions to a ChatGPT "day in my dream life" prompt, with 10-20M views. Copia wraps that magic moment in an app [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=132s).
- **Put the magic moment after a commitment.** Viral prompt content alone did not convert, because people already have ChatGPT. Users get their dream-life story inside the app after they enter their goals [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=226s).
- **Hook and demo, Gymshark-style.** Show the product's magic moment, but do not give away so much that it feels like an ad. Let the comments ask "what's Copia?" [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=98s), [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=398s).
- **Repeatable deadline format.** "If you're seeing this before May 7th, repeat these affirmations for 7 days." It copies Sarah Pearl, a manifesting creator who sold about 100K books this way. Turn an info product into a subscription app [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=424s).
- **Start with mid-size creators.** Million-follower creators protect their brand and an early product is not ready for them. Smaller creators lack brand deals and give you funnel data [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=556s).
- **Build trust in onboarding.** Add social proof ("proven methods"), an onboarding video and progressive tap questions instead of free text. Use Duolingo-style screens with about five words each [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s).
- **Strict guidelines for UGC creators.** Keep the demo the same and A/B test only the first 10 seconds [19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1192s).
- **Steal from e-commerce.** Affiliate e-com marketers compete only on ROAS. Dominate one channel to a 2-3x ROAS before you expand [20:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1253s), [22:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1334s).
- **Pick a brandable name for ASO.** "AI Journal" cannot be found after a viral video [23:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1386s).

> Phoenix thinks a hard paywall signals a weak product, and he praises Strava's trial-led model. He cites Cal AI as an app with trigger, action and variable reward but no investment [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=855s), [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=903s).
