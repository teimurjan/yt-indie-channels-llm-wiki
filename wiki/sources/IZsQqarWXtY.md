# I Built a $1M AI App [No Code]

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews David Bressler, who built Formula Bot (first called Excel Formula Bot) on Bubble with no code. He built it in about 6 weeks of paternity leave in July 2022 while he still had a senior full-time job. Two Reddit posts made it go viral. The OpenAI bill then almost killed it, until donations, sponsorships and finally a paywall kept it alive. Copycats and Microsoft's own AI pushed him from a single formula generator to a broader data-analysis product.

## Key facts

- About 750K users and about 5,000 paying subscribers (2024-02). The product is valued at over $1M. [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s)
- Recurring revenue is stated as "$226,000 a monthly recurring" in the auto-captions. The caption is garbled, so treat the exact figure with care (2024-02). [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=130s)
- Three products: AI inside Excel and Google Sheets, formula generators, and a Data Analyzer where users upload data and ask questions in plain language. [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s)
- He started in July 2022, with about 6 weeks before his youngest child was born. He has worked full time since 2011. [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)
- The app was 100% Bubble until a few months before the video. It is now about 5 to 10% no-code. [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=315s)
- He spent about $5,000 on OpenAI API costs in days after going viral (2022). [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)
- The price went from $2.99 to $6.99 to $9 for unlimited use. About 5 to 10% of churn is because of ChatGPT (2024-02). [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=823s)
- Within weeks of launch, about 10 copycat "Excel formula" sites appeared. [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=700s)

## Tactics

- **Scratch your own itch.** Junior analysts kept asking him for Excel help, so he built a tool for it. He asked OpenAI for a name and bought the domain on GoDaddy. [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)
- **Learn no-code from YouTube.** He read the Bubble docs, then used free YouTube tutorials for specific problems. [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=254s)
- **Launch on niche subreddits.** A plain post titled "AI Excel formula generator" with a link became the top post of the week on r/excel. A comment then sent him to r/InternetIsBeautiful, where a post got about 10,000 upvotes and made the app go viral for months. [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=385s)
- **Survive the API bill.** He added a Stripe donation link and sold an ad to the host of an Excel esports event shown on ESPN. Google Ads made only a little. A few months later he launched a paywall with logins. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)
- **Trade free help for interviews.** About once a week a user emailed an Excel file. He did the work for free, but only on a call where they told him what they liked and wanted. This turned the product into a data-analytics platform. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=589s)
- **Build a moat against the platform.** Microsoft offered to build his add-on for free, then announced its OpenAI partnership. His early edge over ChatGPT was convenience inside Excel and language settings. His plan is to build what neither ChatGPT nor Microsoft can copy. [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=760s), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=885s)

## Quotes

- "I blew through like $5,000 in OpenAI API costs in days. I had a decision to make: it's either shut it down or keep going." [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)
- "They would ask me what's your broader vision and I would just say more generators... I never got called back." [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=589s)
- "My vision moving forward is to build something that cannot be replicated both in ChatGPT as well as Microsoft." [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=885s)
