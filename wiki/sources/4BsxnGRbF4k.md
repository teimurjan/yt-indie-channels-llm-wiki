## Summary

Adrian Horning is a solo Node.js developer from Austin. A Twitter follower sent him an Acquire.com listing for a social media scraping API. He did not buy it. He rebuilt the same concept as Scrape Creators, which makes about $20K/month (2025-10). He grew it through his Twitter presence and free-credit offers, not SEO. He shares a nine-step playbook to find proven ideas on Acquire.com and copy the concept.

## Key facts

- About $20K/month revenue, credit-based with no subscription (2025-10). [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=80s)
- 600 paying customers, about 20M API requests a month (2025-10). About 12 customers pay most of the revenue. [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=80s), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)
- The listed business made $30K MRR after 3 years, with fewer than 100 customers, all from SEO. [3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=190s)
- Background: App Academy bootcamp, 3 years as an engineer in Utah. He quit with $30K in savings and tried freelancing, a course and other products first. [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=109s)
- First version took a couple of weeks: scrapers on a Node.js server on Render, docs in Notion, a basic website. First customer came a few weeks later. [3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=230s)
- Pricing: $10 for 5,000 credits, $50 for 25,000, $500 for 500,000 (2025-10). [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)
- Scrapes public data from Instagram, YouTube, Twitter and ad libraries (Facebook, LinkedIn). Customers include link-in-bio tools and short-form analytics tools. [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)
- Stack: Node.js, the impit HTTP package from Apify, proxies from Evomi, Webshare and Massive, Render and AWS Lambda, Cursor ($20/month), Supabase, Astro plus React. [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=620s)
- About 80% margin. Monthly costs: about $1,500 proxies, $500 for a night-shift developer in the Philippines who watches for outages, about $400 servers (2025-10). [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=666s)

## Tactics

- **Use listings as validation.** An Acquire.com listing shows real revenue. Adrian already had 3 years of scraping skills, so he knew that he was able to build it. [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=150s), [3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=190s)
- **Acquire.com copy playbook:** [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=376s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=437s), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=490s)
  1. Open the Acquire.com marketplace.
  2. Filter by SaaS.
  3. Filter by asking price of at least $300K, or by ARR, to find pre-validated ideas.
  4. Pick a market you know.
  5. Find the real website. Google phrases from the listing, or search "<competitor> alternative".
  6. Reverse engineer how they get customers. Read the site, and find the founder on Twitter, LinkedIn, podcasts and YouTube. This is the most important step.
  7. Build it.
  8. Copy the concept, not the exact words.
  9. Do something every day to promote or improve it.
- **Comment on launches.** He replies to launch posts about social media scraping with an offer of 10K free credits. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)
- **Accidental marketing.** His first customer was a CTO who commented on a post where Adrian had scraped that company's site. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)
- **Win on reliability and access.** Social media scrapers break often. He keeps his working, tells customers about outages, and is easy to reach. [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)
