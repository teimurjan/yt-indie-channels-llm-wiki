## Summary

Steve Hoyek and his co-founder built Journable, an AI calorie counter, about 18 months before the video. They bootstrapped it from under $1K to over $100K/month in revenue (2026-03). Most revenue comes from Android, not iOS. Ads on iOS cost about 4 times more, while iOS users convert only about 20% better. Google App campaigns, mainly Play Store ads, drive growth at about break-even. Steve shares a step-by-step Google Ads playbook for Android.

## Tactics

- **Pick Android by data.** They used the RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025 report. iOS users cost about 4 times more to reach. They convert only about 20% better. [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=235s), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=296s)
- **Test both, then double down.** iOS Meta campaigns were too expensive, so they ran Android in the US. Android converted as well as iOS, and Android ads spilled some installs over to iOS. [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=350s)
- **Play Store ads are the core.** Google App campaigns run across Search, Display, YouTube, Discover and the Play Store. The Play Store placement performs best, for keywords like "calorie counter". [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=458s)
- **Google Ads playbook for Android:** [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=558s), [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=618s), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=678s), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=739s), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=800s)
  1. Set up attribution. Send installs, paywall views, trial starts and purchase values to Google Ads.
  2. Launch install campaigns to feed the algorithm. Supply 5 headlines, 5 descriptions, and up to 20 images and 20 videos. Stock media is fine at first.
  3. Optimize assets over time. Start at $10 to $15/day. Take big creative swings, not font or color tweaks. Improve the Play Store listing, because App campaigns use its assets.
  4. Move to target CPA campaigns. Budget 10 times your target cost per event per day. A $15 trial needs $150/day, because Google needs about 10 target events a day.
  5. Scale budgets as conversion rate rises. Test the paywall, pricing and business model.
- **Find Android gaps.** Look for apps that succeed on iOS with no Android equivalent. Also look for niche problem overlaps that no incumbent solves. [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=913s)
- **Keep it simple.** Users type a meal or snap a photo, and AI returns calories and macros. Simplicity is the main message in the marketing. [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=166s), [16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=1005s)
- **Regret: no content.** Steve wishes he had documented the journey on social media from the start. [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=1148s)
