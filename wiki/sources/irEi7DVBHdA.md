# Meet the Kid Who Made $1M with ChatGPT

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Joe Popelas, a 20-year-old computer science student in Toronto. Joe runs an ebook brand. He writes the books with GPT-3 and ChatGPT and sells them through Pinterest ads to middle-aged moms. He got the idea from a publishing company that made about $2M a year without AI. He built the brand self-funded, at night, while in school and working an $11/hour job. He then sold 40% of it to an investor.

## Key facts

- Before the business, Joe stocked shelves for $11/hour. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=0s)
- He worked on GPT-3 beta use cases, which led to paid AI advice for small businesses in 2022. [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=48s)
- In July 2022 a publishing company showed him its numbers: about $2M revenue. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=108s)
- Timeline: he made 10 to 20 books in August 2022 and 40 by the end of September. His first profitable day was October 3 ($20). His first $1K profit day was December 28. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=108s), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=171s)
- Self-funded from minimum-wage pay. He says a restart now costs under $500. [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=171s)
- $390K total profit and a $930K valuation (2023-08). Profit margin is 70% to 80% (2023-08). [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=362s)
- Costs: about $200/month in extension subscriptions, about $3 of GPT-3 usage per book, a proofreader on retainer, and ads (2023-08). [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=362s)
- The first offer on a marketplace listing was $930K. He is now selling 40% of the brand to an investor (2023-08). [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=545s)
- He dropped 2 courses and worked 5 to 6 hours each night on the brand. [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=171s)

## Tactics

- **Pick an ambitious niche.** Choose a niche with traffic and people who want a clear outcome. Then show the product as the path to that outcome. [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=220s)
- **Study competitor books.** His first book was poor. He bought and read competitor books to learn the structure. [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=220s)
- **Generate from an outline.** Ask ChatGPT for an outline (for example 5 chapters with 5 topics each). Then generate each topic one at a time. Do one careful proofreading pass. [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=281s), [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=362s)
- **Pinterest ads in two stages.** Start with consideration campaigns to test which niche responds. Then install the Pinterest tag and move to conversion campaigns. Tune every metric there. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=423s)
- **Always use a landing page.** It gives room for a value stack and chapter previews, and it sets the brand image. A bounce rate above 70% to 80% means the page looks unprofessional. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=423s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=484s)
- **Ignore saturation for now.** He advertises only on Pinterest. Google and Facebook are still open, and loyal customers protect him. [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=484s)
- **Test the exit market.** He listed the brand on marketplaces only to see offers. Build a brand, sell part of it, then build another. [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=545s)
- **Side income from teaching.** Afternoons go to teaching others to build book brands. [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=605s)
