## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Canyon, co-founder of Sideshift, a marketplace where college students take UGC jobs. Sideshift grew from about 10K to over 150K job seekers in 3 to 4 months with high-volume organic TikTok, on about $10K of spend (2025-07). Canyon walks through the creator brief, account warm-up, pay structure and how long to test. Two case studies follow. Photogenic, an AI photo app, got 35M views from one creator. App studio Vert Ventures turns organic winners into paid ads.

## Key facts

- Sideshift: about 10K job seekers in late February 2025, over 150K active job seekers 3 to 4 months later. Total spend about $10K (2025-07). [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=40s)
- Growth broke the product. With one engineer, 50K new creators joined in a week or two, and some businesses got 3,000 applicants per job. They paused funnels for a while. [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=101s)
- Last campaign: about 16M to 20M views from about 300 posts in 30 days. Five creators posted about twice a day each. A winning format took 2 to 3 weeks to find. [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=176s), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=210s)
- A "rate out of 10" slideshow got 84K views on an account with about 1,900 followers. [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=359s), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=375s)
- Common creator pay: $15 to $20 per video plus a base retainer, with generous performance bonuses (2025-07). [14:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=847s)
- Photogenic: about 600 applicants for one creator role. 46 posts drew about 35M views. Weekly downloads grew about 10x. App Store page conversion rose from 5 to 10% to over 50% (2025-07). [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1292s), [22:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1353s), [23:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1414s)
- Vert Ventures: its astrology app Astra and Bible study app Haven moved in and out of their category top five for weeks (2025-07). [26:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1616s)
- Sideshift visited 25 college campuses in person to onboard its first 10K to 20K users. [40:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=2445s)

## Tactics

- **Ride macro and TikTok trends.** Content about rejection from jobs despite a master's degree resonated with the job market mood. Plug the product subtly, often only on the last slide. [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=311s), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=448s)
- **Engineer the comments.** Creators reply to comments. Questions like "where do you find jobs?" are the cue to plug the app. Engagement pushes videos further. [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=508s)
- **Keep the brief short:** [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=603s), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=663s), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=726s), [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=786s)
  - The company and its value in your own words, with Loom walkthroughs.
  - A clear target persona. Products aimed at everyone make bland content.
  - Links to past viral videos and to viral formats from adjacent players.
  - A few hook ideas at most, and simple rules. A 50-page brief goes unread.
- **Warm up like a human.** Spend 3 to 5 days acting as the target user and engaging in the niche. Start posting on a Monday. [16:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=998s), [17:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1060s)
- **Hire on proof, then let go.** Photogenic picked a creator with past viral posts and her own ideas. Her ideas beat the founder's, so he let her run it. Videos of the AI failing, such as a third leg, drove engagement. [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1292s), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1472s)
- **Spark organic winners into paid.** Vert Ventures tests many creators and formats, then reuses winners as ads. [26:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1616s)
- **Test an app in two weeks.** Hire a few creators and post 30 to 50 videos. Check retention and payment, not only downloads. For paid tests, book creators week to week for 5 to 10 formats. [31:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1894s), [33:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1987s)
- **Use a research tool for formats.** SpyTok, co-founded by Joseph, finds viral TikToks that promote apps in a niche. [15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=924s), [44:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=2641s)
- **Treat creators as a career path.** Content creators can grow into strategists and growth roles. [41:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=2505s)
