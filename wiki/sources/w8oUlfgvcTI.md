# My two apps made $30M

## Summary

Will Cannon built two bootstrapped software companies by copying markets that already had product-market fit. UpLead is a B2B contact-data tool, and Signaturely is a simple DocuSign alternative. His framework has four steps: pick a proven market, find a twist in competitors' bad reviews, acquire customers profitably, then execute. UpLead reached its first $1M ARR with one short cold email. Signaturely grew on SEO with free tools and contract templates.

## Key facts

- UpLead: over 4,000 customers, over $30M in total sales, and over 100K website visits per month (2025-10). [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)
- Signaturely: over 1.6M users, over $1.5M in total sales, and over 800K visits per month (2025-10). [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)
- No outside funding for either company. [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=59s)
- Background: he started a mortgage company at 19 with his future wife. The 2008 crash wiped it out. He then sold lead lists as an agency for about 10 years, from about 2007 to 2017. [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=84s)
- Vendor data was only 50% to 60% accurate, and scraped data was 40% to 50% accurate. His team verified phone numbers through a call center in the Philippines. That pain became UpLead. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s)
- Signaturely started in 2020. At that time, document signing moved online. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s)
- He failed at 15 to 20+ businesses before these two. [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=294s), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=620s)

## Tactics

- **Step 1: pick a market with product-market fit.** DocuSign was public with a lot of revenue but a complex product. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)
- **Step 2: find a twist in 1-star and 2-star reviews.** A lower price alone is not a twist. Customers who dislike the leader are the easiest to win. [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)
- **Step 3: acquire customers profitably.** You need one skill: cold email, cold calling, SEO or ads. He learned his from books and YouTube. [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)
- **Step 4: execute and keep going.** Expect the playbook to fail the first time. [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=294s)
- **The $1M cold email.** UpLead got its first $1M ARR from cold email alone. [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s)
  - Subject: "Hi {first name}", so it looks like it came from a person.
  - Neutral opener: "I was checking out your LinkedIn profile and thought this could be of value to you."
  - Familiar value prop: "like ZoomInfo but with real-time email verification, and we're really affordable."
  - Easy ask: "Are you open to trying it out at no cost?"
- **SEO with free tools and templates.** Signaturely built a free tool to type or draw a signature and download it. The tool then pushes users to sign documents. It also ranked many contract templates (marketing, construction, landlord). This brought its first 1,000+ customers. [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=570s)
- **Ideas he likes for 2025.** A simple CRM. Payroll software for small businesses that copies the FreshBooks SEO playbook of templates and tools. A website analytics tool that tracks mentions in LLMs. [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=334s)
