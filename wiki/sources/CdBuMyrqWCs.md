# The EXACT System to Go From 0-$10M+ With Your App

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Kieran, a founding team member who runs marketing at StudyFetch, an AI study platform for students. Kieran's first TikToks, filmed in his own college lectures, drove tens of thousands of signups overnight. StudyFetch bootstrapped for 18 months on organic video, then raised an $11.5M Series A. Its creator program now mixes hundreds of UGC creators, paid influencer posts, and influencers who start new zero-follower StudyFetch accounts. Kieran explains creator vetting, pay, TikTok vs Reels conversion, and how to design features that look good on camera.

## Key facts

- Over 7M users in about 2.5 years, seven-figure MRR, eight-figure ARR, and over 2B views (2026-05). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=0s), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=182s)
- Launched September 15, 2023, after the founders pivoted from an essay writing tool. The team started at 7. [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=210s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=465s)
- Bootstrapped for about 18 months, then raised an $11.5M Series A from Owl Ventures and the College Board, skipping seed. [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=23s), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=498s)
- Kieran's first TikTok got about 3M views overnight. A December 2023 lecture-hall video got 75M views and hundreds of thousands of signups. [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=254s), [33:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2031s)
- New-account influencer deals run five figures a month, about $10K/month for a daily video from a creator with about 750K followers. That is 10x to 20x a UGC creator, who costs a couple hundred to a couple thousand dollars a month (2026-05). [13:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=809s), [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1069s), [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1085s)
- UGC pay: base rates of $50 to $2,500 per video plus a bonus of $100 per 100K views (2026-05). [41:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2480s), [41:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2519s)
- Creators: hundreds now, almost 1,000 at the peak. [42:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2578s)

## Tactics

- **Zero-follower accounts with real influencers.** An influencer opens a separate account with "StudyFetch" in the name. Her fans follow her there and trust her study advice. Kieran says this hedges against viewers tiring of obvious UGC ads. [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=542s), [15:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=949s), [16:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=982s)
- **Mix three creator types.** UGC creators, influencers on new accounts, and one-off sponsored posts, weighted by monthly goals. Attribution is hard, so comment sections are the signal. [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=646s), [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=689s)
- **Vet on comments, not views.** A creator with 10K views and an active comment section beats one with big views and no following. Low monthly retainers can pair with high bonuses. [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=853s), [15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=909s), [17:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1041s)
- **Prefer TikTok for conversion.** TikTok's audience has higher intent. Reels can go viral abroad and convert near zero. For US creators the target is over 80% US viewers. Non-US creators post on TikTok rather than Reels. [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s), [20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1227s), [22:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1341s)
- **Recruit TikTok Shop creators.** They are paid on affiliate, so they know how to sell. Tell them to sell the outcome: better grades. [23:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1413s), [25:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1508s)
- **Build features for the camera.** Live lecture notes stream onto the screen while hands stay off the keyboard. That is the wow moment. [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s)
- **Match the CTA to the creator.** Use a clear URL overlay in the last 4 to 5 seconds for demo videos. Known influencers weave the product into lifestyle vlogs with no hard CTA. Do not bolt the product onto the last 5 seconds of an unrelated video. [27:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1658s), [28:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1715s), [31:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1886s)
- **Target receptive viewers, not raw virality.** Focus on med students and type-A learners who do not mind an ad. [5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=329s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=396s)
- **Team structure.** Contractors failed on incentives. Full-time roles now: an outbound scouting team (DMs, email, LinkedIn), creative managers who work with creators daily, and a head of the creator program. [37:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2254s), [38:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2296s), [40:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2426s)
- **First marketing hire.** Hire a growth generalist who is operational and creative. TikTok Shop experience is a plus. [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2837s), [48:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2909s)
- **After the Series A.** Money went to scaling creators and to brand work such as cafe pop-ups. [36:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2166s), [36:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2219s)
