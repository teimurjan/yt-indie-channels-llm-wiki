# How Top 1% Apps Earn Your Second Visit

## Summary

Tim Gabe walks through his studio's redesign of Undertone, an app that records therapy sessions and makes a recap. Onboarding completion was above average, even with a 10+ minute flow. But about half of the users who finished it never recorded a session. High intent, not good design, drove the onboarding number. Two problems hurt retention: a mic test that ended in a dead end, and a week with nothing to do between sessions. PostHog data showed one voluntary return. About 10 minutes after a session, users opened the app to read the recap. The team rebuilt the brand first. Then they moved the mic test into onboarding, used the Dynamic Island while a session processes, and turned the recap into an "unboxing" moment.

## Key facts

- Onboarding took 10+ minutes and still beat the industry average. About half of the users who finished it never recorded a therapy session (2026-10). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=0s), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=56s)
- Test recordings averaged about 20 seconds. A popup then said the recording was too short, and the app asked for 10 minutes. A founder guesses this dead end lost about 10% of users. [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=177s)
- Users opened the recap a median of 10 minutes after a session ended. The fastest was 6 minutes and the slowest was 44. A recap takes 3 to 5 minutes to read. [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=344s), [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=362s), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=373s)
- Some users forgot to stop recording, which made 4- to 6-hour recordings. These broke transcription, and a few sessions were lost. [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=706s)
- Brand rules: pistachio and sage on off-white, with a charcoal that is never fully black. The style sits between "clinical and minimal". [7:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=447s), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=499s)
- Undertone was raising money at the time of the video (2026-10). [18:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=1133s)

## Tactics

- **Do not trust a high onboarding rate.** Users with urgent intent and few other options finish long flows anyway. The number measures need, not what happens after. [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=95s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=124s)
- **Find where users already come back on their own.** Often the trigger sits outside the app, such as a therapy calendar. Look in analytics for the one voluntary return moment. [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=276s), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=344s)
- **Fix the brand and design system first.** Every later fix has to feel like the same product. They set type, color, icons, components and button states first. [7:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=447s), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=518s)
- **Move friction into the flow where users are motivated.** The mic test moved into onboarding. A demo recap replaced the "too short" wall. [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=562s), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=627s)
- **Stay present outside the app.** A Dynamic Island Live Activity shows each step: transcribing, anonymizing, summarizing. This calms users and also stops runaway recordings. [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=665s), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=736s)
- **Design the peak and the end (peak-end rule).** The founders admired Apple's packaging, which led to seven steps for the recap. 1) Hold the thread in the Dynamic Island. 2) Send a "recap ready" notification. 3) The recap arrives wrapped, with a small bounce. 4) The user peels the tape off. 5) Confetti and a haptic. 6) Frame it as the first entry in a mental health record. 7) Put the prompt to book the next session inside that peak. [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s), [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=942s)
- **Fit the emotion to the context.** The brief: nothing clinical and nothing cute, because this is therapy. [16:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=986s)
- **Craft is the moat.** Clean UI is table stakes. Personality is what sets Undertone apart from ChatGPT or Granola transcription. [17:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=1021s), [17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=1047s)
