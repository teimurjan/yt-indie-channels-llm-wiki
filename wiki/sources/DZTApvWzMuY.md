# He Makes $125,000/year As A Niche YouTuber

## Summary

Shervin (channel: Shervin Shares) quit a six-figure software engineering job, and later a product manager job, to make tech, gadget and fitness videos full time. He made about $300 in his first year on YouTube. By 2023-04 the title puts his income at $125K/year, and 80% of it comes from affiliate links in search-based product reviews. Pat Walls visits the three-creator apartment in New York City that Shervin shares to cut costs. Shervin explains his upload cadence, his viral Apple Watch Ultra video, how he hires editors and how he finds ideas.

## Key facts

- Income: about $125K/year (2023-04, from the video title). The split is 80% affiliate marketing and 20% YouTube AdSense. Two companies make up about 50% of affiliate income. He had no sponsorships yet (2023-04) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s).
- First year on YouTube: about $10/month in AdSense, then about $300 for the year after YouTube added the 1,000-subscriber threshold [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=380s).
- 1,000 subscribers took him about 2 to 3 years [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=344s).
- When he quit, YouTube made $2K to $3K/month and he had $5K to $10K in the bank plus invested assets. He moved back in with his parents to cut costs [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=154s), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=213s).
- NYC rent: $4,500/month for three creators; his share is $1,600 (2023-04) [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=592s).
- Top video: "I replaced my iPhone with an Apple Watch Ultra", about 1.56M views (2023-04) [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=671s).
- First editor came from Upwork at $7 to $10/hour [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=771s).

## Tactics

- **Search-based product reviews with affiliate links.** A fitness-band review with a referral link earned him 10 years of free membership. The company then invited him to an affiliate program that paid $50 per referral instead of a free month [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=414s), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=475s).
- **Hold off on sponsors to protect watch time.** He feared that a sponsor read can cut views [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s).
- **Cadence.** Once a week, twice a week or twice a month are the three cadences he sees work [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=322s).
- **Viral formula = timing + topic + past data.** He re-ran a format that had worked before. He shipped it the day after the Apple Watch Ultra launch, with rough editing [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=718s).
- **Frame the idea by search demand.** Search YouTube for the topic and its neighbours. "Testosterone supplements" gets few views, but "gaining muscle" on the same content does well [14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=843s).
- **Hire through the network first.** He asks creator friends and people at events, then uses Upwork and offshore editors [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=771s).
- **Build the habit before you quit.** He tells people with jobs to give one day a week, such as Sunday, to content [13:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=804s).
- **Live among creators.** He found creator events on Meetup, and the invitations snowballed from there [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=899s).
