## Summary

Josef Buettgen and Timo Nikolai met at a coworking space. Josef was a freelance software engineer, and Timo lives in Bangkok. Both had spent months on products with no customers. For Setter AI, an AI appointment setter that follows up with and qualifies leads, they sold before they built. A fake landing page with an AI-generated demo ranked on Google and earned a $500 Stripe payment before any code existed. They grew to $10K MRR (2025-09) through SEO, free tools, comparison posts and long-form YouTube.

## Key facts

- About $10K revenue last month, with higher months before. August is slow in B2B (2025-09). [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)
- Pricing starts at about $50/month. Some customers pay over $500, and the largest pays up to $5,000/month (2025-09). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s)
- Target markets: solar, coaches and financial services, anywhere a sales rep can be replaced. [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s)
- About 2,000 business signups but only 38 paying customers, by design, because high payers churn less (2025-09). [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)
- First $500 payment came in February 2024, before the product existed. A company with $1B in revenue booked a call. [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)
- Applied to YC five times, with one interview. YouTube channel has 600 subscribers. They have acquisition offers (2025-09). [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)
- Margins: 90%+ without ads, about 80% with ads and freelancers. OpenAI API about $250/month, Upwork freelancers up to $500/month (2025-09). [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=692s)
- Stack: Ahrefs, AnswerThePublic, Zapier, Beehiiv, Calendly, Webflow, TypeScript, SvelteKit, Postgres on Render and Supabase (about $150/month), Puppeteer, OpenAI, Brandfetch, Plausible ($29/month), Netlify, Cursor. [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=633s), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=680s)

## Tactics

- **Fake landing page on a warm domain.** One H1 keyword, a fake AI caller demo made with ElevenLabs, and a "book demo" calendar link. He put it on an old domain that Google had already indexed. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=100s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=163s)
- **Pick a keyword with volume and no difficulty.** "Cold call automation" came from Ahrefs. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=163s)
- **Force indexing.** Request indexing in Google Search Console so the page is live in search within 24 hours. [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=207s)
- **Validation means a card swipe.** Traffic and booked calls do not count. A payment does. [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)
- **Five-step validation playbook:** [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=288s), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=350s)
  1. Have a job at some point. B2B pain points come from inside businesses, not Reddit.
  2. Set up a landing page around buyer search keywords.
  3. Add a Calendly link. Calls build trust, and you try to close on the call.
  4. Use the deposit framework. Take a 100% refundable one-time deposit, such as $500, for full access at launch and a spot in the first cohort. Go high ticket, not $20.
  5. Let SEO incubate. It takes time but brings buyers who search for a solution.
- **Double down on the validation channel.** The real site ranked on page one within a week and is now number one worldwide for its keyword. [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)
- **Three SEO plays.** Free tools such as a sales script generator, "HubSpot alternatives" comparison articles, and long-form YouTube. Most closed customers watched at least one of their videos before a call. [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)
- **Ideas they suggest:** AI glue and workflow automation for non-technical people, and a Lovable-style builder for native mobile apps. [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=461s)
