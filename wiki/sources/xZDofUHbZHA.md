## Summary

Pat Walls tells how he started Starter Story. At nearly 30 he had a corporate job, student loan and credit card debt, and weekends lost to drinking. On a hungover flight he decided to chase his dream for 365 days. He wrote a one-card manifesto, dropped a first idea he had no passion for, and picked an idea that fit his skills: a site of founder case studies. He worked from a Starbucks at 6 a.m. before his job. After one year the business made $3,500/month (2024-10), and he quit his job.

## Key facts

- Starting point: nearly 30, many years into a corporate career, in debt. [0:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=12s), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=109s)

## Tactics

- **Write a manifesto.** Inspired by *Think and Grow Rich*, he wrote his goals and plan on a note card. Stop wasteful spending, work nights and weekends, start a business. He nailed it above his bed and read it every day. [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=109s), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=170s)
- **Give yourself permission to fail.** His fear of failure came from a need for control. Starting in a tiny way broke the paralysis. [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=204s)
- **Pick the idea only you can build.** The idea must fit your skills, your passions and a way to make money. Starter Story shows how regular people found ideas, launched and got customers. [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=265s)
- **Change your environment.** A new environment changes people. His new place was the Starbucks down the street at 6 a.m. [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=367s)
- **Cut the old habits.** He mostly stopped drinking, stopped weekend spending, and stopped 12-hour Sundays of football. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=427s)
- **Find one distribution channel.** Reddit gave him the audience. A newsletter then gave him sponsor revenue. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=427s)
- **Expect a cost.** He grew apart from old friends and family, moved from a city he loved and missed a friend's wedding. They later welcomed him back. [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=519s), [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=580s)
