## Summary

Cedric Roberge is a college student who built Pep AI, a tracker for peptide and GLP-1 users. It logs doses, injection sites, vials, nutrition, weight and workouts. Rival GLP apps ran only paid ads. Cedric did the opposite: he signed almost every peptide creator on TikTok and Instagram, tracked each one with AppsFlyer links and paid retainers plus affiliate cuts. Pep AI made $100K in its first 70 days. Only then did he move winning creator videos into Meta ads. Joseph Choi hosts this Superwall podcast episode.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $30K in month one, $50K in month two, on pace for $80K in month three (2026-06) [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=14s).
- Marketing spend: about $10K to $15K in month one and about $20K to $25K in month two. Month three: $15K to $20K spent, with $70K made so far [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=990s).
- He built from late January and launched at the end of February (2026) [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=237s).
- About 100 creators post for Pep AI (2026-06) [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=486s).
- Meta cost per download is about $2.50, against a $5 rule of thumb. The best static ad, made in ChatGPT, costs under $2. Spend is about $400/day on Meta plus about $150/day on Apple Search Ads (2026-06) [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=399s), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=436s), [47:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2833s).

- About 60% of users are over 25. The best paying group is 25 to 40 [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1716s), [48:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2926s).

An angel investor, a dropshipper from his fraternity, funded marketing only [34:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2099s), [38:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2339s), [43:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2606s).

## Tactics

- **Do what rivals skip.** GLP apps only ran ads. Cedric copied the creator playbooks of Cal AI and Quitter for an untouched niche [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=331s).
- **Creator deal structure.** Trial month: 4 videos, 4 stories and a link in bio. A creator with about 5,000 views per video gets about $200. Good results lead to a 3-month deal. Big creators get up to $4,000/month, and one large signing cost over $20,000 [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=913s), [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1038s).
- **Affiliate cut.** Most creators get a one-time 20% commission on subscriptions. So they post more than asked [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1297s).
- **Track every creator.** Creator codes and AppsFlyer OneLinks show clicks, downloads and subscriptions per creator. Weak creators get a lower rate [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=540s), [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=742s).
- **Rent the link in bio.** Peptide creators send traffic to their paid communities from every video. One creator kept the link up for 2 weeks without a post and still drove many downloads [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=836s).
- **Slideshows beat talking heads.** Tip carousels that end with "track it in the app" perform best. Joke videos from one creator reach hundreds of thousands of views [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=588s), [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=650s).
- **Outreach at volume.** The team sends over 100 DMs a day and over 1,000 a week. The first line reads "Paid promo / affiliate deal?". Then the creator moves to WhatsApp and a call. Check comments to spot bought views [41:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2516s), [44:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2640s), [44:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2673s).
- **Loose briefs.** Creators get a Google Doc of top videos and full creative freedom. They send the first video for review [20:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1204s), [36:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2161s).
- **Age-gated onboarding.** Users under 25 get a short flow. Older users get a long flow with a nutrition plan that sells the need to track [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1716s).
- **Ship and prune weekly.** Two or three updates ship each week. Users asked for cycles and a syringe calculator, which drove traffic. Lawyers checked the calculator first. Unused body scan and bloodwork features were cut [31:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1897s), [32:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1944s).
- **Use Meta, not TikTok, for paid.** TikTok rejects peptide Spark ads. Meta accepts them [50:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=3034s).
