## Summary

Kyle Fowler built Cardstock, a sports card scanner app, and later Scanomon for Pokémon cards. Together they make about $41K MRR. Lifetime revenue is about $700K, with no VC money. A Reddit post validated Cardstock. Keyword work in App Store optimization (ASO) then held about $10K/month for 2 years while he was in college. After he lost the top keyword rank in 2024-05, he hired his friend Jacob from Wendy's to make TikToks. They found a repeatable "scan the card, reveal the value" format. They now scale TikTok slideshows through Noise, a creator platform, at about $1 CPM.

## Tactics

- **Validate on Reddit, then launch there.** He posted the idea in r/baseballcards and got 10 to 15 replies. The launch post linked back to the first post, so it did not read like an ad [0:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=292s), [0:06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=365s).
- **Cheap ASO research.** Apple's free $100 Search Ads credit got him into ASO. He brainstormed keywords and looked up rank and popularity with free Sensor Tower trials. He tracks keywords in Astro and puts the highest-value ones in the title and subtitle [0:12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=771s), [0:17:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1048s), [0:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1241s).
- **Brand page views do not equal downloads.** Silent pack-opening videos got views but no installs, because they did not show the app [0:23:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1404s).
- **The winning format.** Show a desirable card for 3 to 5 seconds with a hook such as "what is this MJ card worth?". Then scan it with the phone in hand and reveal the value. The goal is "what's the app?" comments. They film at card shows [0:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1466s), [0:25:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1544s).
- **Control every variable.** Pick formats where you control the hook, first image and story. Then you can test and repeat a series, not chase one viral hit. Kyle wrote down his TikTok rules for Jacob [0:27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1670s), [0:29:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1752s).
- **Brand inside the product.** A hidden setting shows the app name inside the scanner for marketing videos [0:39:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2361s).
- **Scale slideshows with Noise.** Upload hook images and captions. Noise builds templates, hands them to creators, tracks attribution and pays out. Volume wins: most posts get hundreds of views, but thousands of posts add up [0:41:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2462s), [0:45:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2745s).
- **Learn it before you hire it.** Do the channel yourself first, so you can tell a good agency from a bad one. This matters for bootstrapped founders [0:33:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1980s).
- **Every channel works.** Pick the one you can execute. To learn a new channel, ask your network first, then communities such as Consumer Club, then search tools [0:52:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=3153s), [0:54:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=3284s).
