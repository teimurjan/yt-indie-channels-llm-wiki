# Why Leaderboards Kill App Retention (How To Fix It)

## Summary

Tim Gabe argues that most founders reach for features, notifications and streaks to fix retention. The apps that keep users for years make them feel they are in a race instead. He calls this the "invisible scoreboard". A leaderboard is only the surface, and social comparison is the engine underneath. Shipped without that psychology, a leaderboard is "a glorified list". He uses Strava and Peloton to show three ways to build one.

## Key facts

- Strava has over 100 million athletes. Its core loop is segments: public leaderboards on specific stretches of road or trail. [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)
- Strava lets you rank against your neighborhood, your friends, or the dozen riders who rode the same hill, not only the world. [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)
- Peloton kept people paying $44/month for years (2026-06). Tim credits instructors who call out riders' names live in front of thousands. [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=197s)

## Tactics

- **Put the comparison at completion.** Show the signal that someone is ahead at the end of a workout, a transaction or a listen, whatever completion means in your app. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=77s)
- **Choose who sits on the other side.** Chasing a world record holder feels very different from chasing your neighbor. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=77s)
- **Shrink the cohort until winning feels possible.** A global board feels unwinnable, so people stop trying. A local board feels winnable, so they keep showing up. Cohort size is the lever. [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)
- **Recognize effort like a human.** The leaderboard creates the race, and the callout makes people push. Most apps cannot do live shout-outs, but they can design a recognition moment that feels social and emotional, not mechanical. [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=197s), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=254s)
- **The three rules, in order:** put the comparison at completion, localize the cohort, and acknowledge progress and effort in a human way. [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=268s)

## Quotes

- "A leaderboard is the surface. Social comparison is the psychological engine underneath." [0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=5s)
- "Without the psychology baked in, you don't have a retention tool. You have a glorified list." [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=68s)
- "The rule of thumb is to shrink it until winning feels possible." [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)
