# How I Built It: $15K/month Mobile App

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Sebastian Röhl, a 32-year-old developer in Germany who built HabitKit, a simple habit tracker for iOS and Android. He quit his job with 12 months of runway, had few downloads after 6 months, and went back to his old job. He kept building on the side, launched HabitKit in 2022, and quit again at the start of 2024. Early growth came from building in public. Later growth came from app store optimization. It put him in the top 5 for "habit tracker" in several countries.

## Key facts

- About $15K MRR at the end of 2024, up from $3K MRR at the end of 2023. [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=65s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=268s)
- About 300K downloads across the App Store and Google Play, and over 2,000 reviews and ratings (2025-06). [1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=110s)
- Idea in summer 2022. MVP built in about 2 months with Flutter. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=198s)
- MVP scope: a tile grid chart of habits, basic tracking, local-only data, no sign-in. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s)
- Apple Search Ads budget: about $100/month (2025-06). [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s)
- Costs: RevenueCat takes 1% of revenue. Other costs total about $200 to $300/month (2025-06). No employees. [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=636s)
- Tools: Flutter, Cursor, Astro for keyword research, Appfigures for analytics, RevenueCat for subscriptions. [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=562s)

## Tactics

- **Build in public.** He posted progress and wins and failures on X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads. Positive replies to early screenshots told him to ship fast. It led to podcasts, features and developer contacts. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s)
- **Put the main keyword first in the app name.** He targets "habit tracker" and starts the app name with it. ASO covers keywords, name, subtitle and screenshots. [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)
- **Ask for a review at the first success moment.** The native review dialog shows right after a user checks off their first habit. Most people give 5 stars. [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)
- **Small Search Ads spend as a ranking hedge.** He runs $100/month in Apple Search Ads in the hope that Apple favors advertisers. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s)
- **Privacy as a feature.** No account and local storage keep the app simple. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s)
- **Find ideas by building.** Start anything. Ideas come once you are building. Validate by solving your own problem, so you are always the first customer. [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=520s)
- **Save for a sabbatical.** After 8 hours of coding at work he had no energy for his own apps. A 12-month runway with a deadline made the start possible. A job was still there as a fallback. [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=713s)
