## Summary

Tim Gabe argues that strong landing pages rely on four simple tricks. Pick a good font pair and tune it. Cut the copy short. Vary placement, size and spacing to make different sections. Then add beautiful assets and match the colors to them. He builds four section types in Figma from the same rules and shows the method works with other fonts and images.

## Key facts

- Font source: Fontshare.com, which has a "Pairs" tab with free font pairings. [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=23s)
- Example hero: center aligned, "mega" heading at 160 px, 40 px spacing between heading, body and call to action. [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=149s), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=212s)
- Example second section: left aligned, heading at 120 px, 32 px spacing, asset on the right. He calls the numbers arbitrary. [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=212s)

## Tactics

- **Typography first.** Without good type, the rest of the page fails. Download a free pair, then tune line height (space between rows) and letter spacing. [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=23s), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=85s)
- **Short copy.** Replace long, boring text with a short, sweet message. People read it, and it takes less space. [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=112s)
- **Placement, size, spacing.** Make new sections from the same parts. Hero: centered, mega size, large spacing. Then left aligned with an asset on the right. Then right aligned with a carousel on the left. Then a four-item grid with smaller headings. Each step down reduces size and spacing. [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=149s), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=212s), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=272s)
- **Assets and color matching.** Use a strong background image with a radial gradient so the text stays readable. Take the primary color for buttons from the image. With an existing design system, do it the other way: pick assets that match the system colors. [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=305s)
- **Reuse one image.** Add a layer blur to the same image for an "aurora" background. Lower its opacity, change its shape, or recolor the asset for variety. [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=366s)
- **The method transfers.** A new font pair and a new asset give a different but equally polished page. [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=428s)
