# I Built a $1M SaaS 100% with No Code (Bubble)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Jacob Seeger and his co-founder Alex about Faceless Video. The SaaS runs faceless social media channels on autopilot: the user gives a topic, and the app writes, makes and posts a video every day. Jacob built it on Bubble with no code and still runs it there at more than $1M ARR. Alex found the idea in his own TikTok feed. A $200 Twitter thread ad, influencers and word of mouth drove growth. Bubble sponsors the video and is also the build tool.

## Key facts

- Faceless Video does more than $1M ARR, 100% bootstrapped (2025-06). It reached $1M in about 10 months. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=0s), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=60s)
- More than 1.1M users signed up (2025-06). The app makes thousands of videos a day. Some videos passed 1M views. [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=80s)
- Faceless Video was Jacob's seventh SaaS idea, after six that failed. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=0s)
- Jacob started as a musician. He took his own music to almost 1M streams on a small budget. He then ran Domino, a music marketing agency, before he moved to SaaS. [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=114s)
- The first ad was a Twitter thread with about $200 of spend. It got hundreds of thousands of views. [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s)
- Word of mouth is always in the top 5 attribution sources. [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s)

## Tactics

- **Let the feed show you ideas.** Alex saw viral faceless videos on TikTok. Daily editing was hard to sustain, and the videos were simple enough to automate. [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=192s), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=238s)
- **Make the tool a product.** Alex wanted an internal tool. Jacob turned it into SaaS. [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=192s)
- **Prove the technical core first.** For the first month, the MVP only turned text into a faceless video with Bubble and APIs. User accounts, UX, payments and pricing came later. [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s)
- **Stay on no code until it breaks.** Bubble handles security, scale, privacy rules and backend work. Bubble support fixed the one scale issue they hit. [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=328s)
- **Marketplaces suit no code.** Jacob sees them as the easiest no-code build because one tool manages both the front end and the backend. [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=437s)
- **B2C needs viral potential.** Low prices make heavy ad spend hard. They use SEO, ads, influencers and organic content, but the key is the message. [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s)
- **Use ads to start the story.** Influencers who saw the ads promoted the product without any outreach. That brought the biggest growth. [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s)
- **Ride an emerging trend first.** Show how the product differs and how it taps an existing trend in a new way. [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s)
- **Plan for the best case.** Ask: can the app and the pricing survive 100,000 signups in one day? [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=700s)
- **Fail fast and stay bootstrapped.** Six failed ideas came first. Bootstrapping forces a lean build. [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=700s)
- **Learn by building.** Skip generic tutorials and pick a project you care about. Reuse old skills: Alex's film background shaped the story in the ads. [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=700s), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=761s)
