# I Speedran an AI App From 0 to $100K Exit in 26 Days

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Caleb Dean, who sold his ranked running app Runafy at about 5x ARR after only 26 days of revenue. Caleb validated the idea first. He copied the Instagram Reels format of Liftoff, a ranked gym app. Under 50 videos sent traffic to a ChatGPT-built page with a $5 Stripe link. He then posted 9 Reels a day with an internal video generator. He used App Store pre-orders to put 3,000 users on the leaderboard at launch. A buyer building an app studio found him through his tweets, and Caleb kept 30% equity.

## Key facts

- Runafy had 26 to 27 days of revenue plus 20 to 30 pending 7-day trials. The parties estimated about $3K MRR and valued it at about 4.5x to 5x ARR, a six-figure sale (2026-03). [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=16s), [46:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2797s)
- Liftoff made $200K to $300K/month during validation. Joseph later shows about 300K downloads and $700K/month (2026-03). [22:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1326s), [23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1412s)
- Validation: under 50 copied videos in 2 weeks gave 2,000 waitlist sign-ups and 90+ payments of $5. One video got about 2 million views. [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1472s), [25:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1533s)
- Instagram videos averaged 5K to 10K views, and about 1 in 10 passed 500K. The account got about 5 million views in the first month. [29:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1779s), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=102s)
- About 90% of traffic was organic: mostly Instagram, some TikTok. [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=479s)
- Pre-orders gave 3,000 downloads in about 2 weeks. The leaderboard filled to its 1,000+ limit within an hour of launch. [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=536s)
- At the DM stage: $2K to $3K revenue, 2K to 3K downloads, 50 to 100 downloads a day and 7 sessions per device. [45:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2741s), [45:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2754s)
- Deal terms: Caleb kept 30% equity and got a 6-month earnout plus a cash bonus. Due diligence took a couple of days. [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2837s), [48:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2938s)
- His first app, Yuri Brave for shy bladder, was built on FlutterFlow. It made $5K to $10K, mostly from ASO, with a 30%+ conversion rate. [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=701s), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=761s)
- He got a CMO job at Blake Anderson's 10X with a Notion pitch that listed 50 influencers with CPMs. He was hired within 48 hours. [14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=880s)

## Tactics

- **Copy a proven format into an adjacent niche.** Liftoff ranks gym lifts. Runafy ranks runs, and GPS makes ranks hard to cheat. [22:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1346s)
- **Charge before you build.** A one-line landing page and a $5 "early adopter" Stripe link gave lifetime access. Paying fans became bug testers on WhatsApp and TestFlight. [25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1558s), [34:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2090s), [36:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2177s)
- **Post 9 Reels a day on Instagram.** Instagram did not punish volume. On TikTok, views fell to about 5 after volume went up. [29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1796s), [30:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1803s)
- **Automate variations.** The CTO built a tool that makes 10,000 variations of a format in about a minute. Track-and-field distances did best. [28:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1732s), [30:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1824s)
- **Use App Store pre-orders to seed a social app.** Ship a bare 2-tab v1 to get approval, then list it for pre-order. On release, the app installs itself and Apple emails users. [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=479s), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=536s)
- **Watch the pre-order date.** He forgot to push the date back once. A buggy build went live to 1,000 users. [39:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2359s)
- **Design the whole app first.** He built every Figma screen in 3 days, so the developer had no guesswork. [31:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1878s)
- **Sell the outcome in onboarding.** Conversion rose after the pitch moved from "fun ranks" to "become a better runner". [33:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2027s)
- **Let followers decide the roadmap.** Instagram polls split evenly between Garmin, Apple Watch and Strava, so the team built all three. [37:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2252s)
- **Launch email with a discount.** A 50% referral code drove most launch-day revenue. 32% of emails hit spam and about 20% were opened. [27:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1627s), [41:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2493s)
- **Build in public.** Bullish tweets from a 200-follower account drew the acquirer. [44:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2668s)
- **Idea filter.** Enter a niche where 1 to 3 apps make at least $100K/month, without cloning one. Discount VC-funded apps such as Reframe, which raised $24M. [54:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3246s), [55:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3309s)
