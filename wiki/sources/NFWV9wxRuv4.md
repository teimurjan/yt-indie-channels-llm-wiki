## Summary

Rob Hallam is a solo founder who makes $22K/month and runs his business alone while he travels (2026-08). His product is SuperX, and he documents the journey on X. The video is about how he works, not about growth tactics. He wakes late, does chores and the gym first, then works one long block at night. His main rule is "productivity follows happiness".

## Key facts

- $22K/month, run alone while he travels (2026-08). The description says $20K/month. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=0s)
- He started as a backpacker who built startups. For about a year, he made no money. [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s)
- SuperX work moved slowly until he stayed in Colombia for 3 months straight from July 2025. [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s)
- For about the last 6 months, he sets up a base for 2 to 3 months, then moves (2026-08). [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s)
- He lived in more than 13 countries in the last few years. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=465s)
- He sleeps 9 hours and spends 9.5 hours in bed. [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=409s)

## Tactics

- **Base for 2 to 3 months.** Moving every few days made deep work almost impossible. A longer base gives routine and still lets him travel. [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s)
- **Night owl schedule.** He wakes at 11:00 to 12:00, gets sun, answers a few support emails, and goes to the gym around 14:00 to 15:00. [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=62s) [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=212s)
- **Front-load chores, then one long block.** He finishes food, gym and chores first. At night he works a solid block of up to 8 hours with no messages. [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=212s)
- **Rolling task list.** Every month or two he keeps a list: the top three or four features, then improvements, then bugs. When a list gets too long, he copies only the important items to a new note. [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=264s) [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=283s)
- **Let customers set priority.** A critical bug brings five emails. A one-off request that nobody repeats was not important. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=283s)
- **Boring work moves the business.** He wants to work on visual, flashy things. In his view, 99% of the time the boring work matters most. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=283s)
- **Surfing analogy.** Spend time "in the water" to learn which waves will break. Then pick big waves and ride them. This explains why people who win once tend to win again. [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=359s)
- **Protect sleep.** He turns devices off an hour before bed. Motivation, creativity and the gym all depend on sleep. [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=409s) [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=455s)
- **Work in a state of play.** He shipped the most in the places where he was happiest. He keeps a Rubik's Cube between AI prompts. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=465s)

## Quotes
