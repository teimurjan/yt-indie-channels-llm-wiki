# I Quit My Job at Meta to Build a $30K/Month SaaS

## Summary

Pat Walls meets Matt Robinson at a Los Angeles golf course. Matt is a senior software engineer on Meta's AI team. He built Live Tourney, a web app that golf courses use to run tournaments, on nights and weekends over about 4 years. It makes about $30K/month, with $350K ARR and about 150 courses (2026-07). Matt has two young kids, and doing both jobs was no longer sustainable. Three weeks after the visit, he quits Meta to go full-time.

## Key facts

- About 150 golf courses use Live Tourney. ARR is about $350K, with a run rate near $800K based on the start of the year (2026-07). [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=87s)
- Revenue is about $30K/month. Pat also says $30K to $40K (2026-07). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=0s), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=62s)
- Matt built the first version for his local course's event about 4 years ago. [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=174s)
- Stack: a Next.js web app with Google Firebase as the back end. [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=205s)
- Players scan a QR code on the scorecard, enter scores by group, and see a live leaderboard. [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=214s)
- Matt spent 6 years at Meta and saw six or more rounds of layoffs. He was not laid off in the last round. [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=403s), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=728s)
- Pat quit his own job with $12K in the bank. [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=461s)

## Tactics

- **Find the gap a known tool leaves.** His course used no scoring tool. The pro said Golf Genius was too complex and too expensive. Matt built a simpler one. [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=174s)
- **Build in your own hobby.** Matt plays in the tournaments he serves. Pat contrasts this with founders who build for dentists without knowing dentistry, then give up after 3 months. [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=539s), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=554s)
- **Block fixed hours next to a job.** Matt worked on the app from 6:00 to 9:00 each morning. [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=523s)
- **Keep the salary until the business is ready.** Building on the side let him grow at a steady pace with no income risk. The business needed him full-time, so he left. [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=344s), [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=705s)
- **Count job risk too.** Repeated layoffs made the salary feel less safe than it looks. [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=403s)
- **Go public after you leave.** At Meta, Matt did not post about the app. His next step is to build in public on LinkedIn and social media. [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=799s)
