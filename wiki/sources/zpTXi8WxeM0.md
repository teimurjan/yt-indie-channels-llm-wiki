# This 23 Yr Old Genius Makes $1M/Year with No-Code Apps. Here's How

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Connor Burd, a 23-year-old indie developer with 4 or 5 earning apps and over $1M/year in total (2025-12). His main app, Payout, finds class action settlements. It makes about $45K/month almost entirely from influencer posts. He does not pay the influencers per post. He gives them equity and builds each app for their audience. He explains why email beats DMs for outreach, how he generates ideas, and when to switch from equity deals to paid deals.

## Key facts

- Payout makes about $45K/month from influencers alone. He has 4 or 5 apps that earn, most kept private because of partners (2025-12). [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=197s), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s)
- His partner Casper has driven about 35,000+ clicks to the site, not counting organic installs. [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s)
- One Casper video got 288K views on TikTok and 206K on YouTube. About 9,400 comments (about 4,000 people) asked for the link. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=496s), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=614s)
- Instagram beats TikTok for these posts. YouTube is about the same as TikTok. [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=527s)
- Android brings at most 15% of revenue, so he prefers Swift and iOS only. Payout itself is React Native and took about 2 weeks to build. [26:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1561s), [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1608s)
- Paid rates: about $1,500 per video for creators with 500K to 1M followers. Others pay $4K to $5K for 2 to 3M followers (2025-12). [28:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1702s)
- A friend's app, Ride Pal, launched with an Instagram influencer and got over 10,000 downloads on day one. [17:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1071s)
- His first app, a map app for pickup games, made nothing. His second app reached $10K to $20K/month. His posture app made about $4K to $5K in a few months. [40:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2422s), [41:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2498s)

## Tactics

- **Build for the creator's audience.** He made Figma mock-ups of about 5 app ideas and asked Casper which one his audience would want. [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=677s)
- **Equity sells to creators.** Brand-deal income is unstable, "eat what you kill". An equity stake gives creators something they own. [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=737s)
- **Email, do not DM.** Big creators turn notifications off. Self-managed Gmail addresses answer best. The email behind YouTube's "view email address" CAPTCHA gets fewer bot pitches. [14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=863s), [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1012s)
- **ManyChat plus custom product pages.** "Comment CLAIM" sends a DM with the link. Each link uses an App Store custom product page for attribution. ManyChat and organic installs came out about even. [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=307s)
- **Cross-post everywhere,** including Facebook video, because Instagram can auto-post to it. [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=393s)
- **Ideation.** Ask creators which apps pitch them brand deals. Scan App Store top charts and TikTok trends, then remix apps that already work. [22:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1333s)
- **Pay once you have capital.** With cash flow he will pay creators instead of giving equity, and plans an app studio. [27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1670s)
- **Judge conversion by comments.** Look for a deep audience that asks about the creator's life, not just "wow". Hard integrations get fewer views, and equity makes that less of a worry. [29:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1763s), [31:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1872s)
- **Niche: middle, then slightly narrower.** He targets apps at $30K to a few hundred thousand dollars per month. [35:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2110s)
- **Avoid sunk cost.** Give 60 to 70% of a neglected app to a creator, or sell it, rather than let it sit. [43:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2626s)
