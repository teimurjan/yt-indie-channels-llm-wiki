# 9 Things That Make Me $1.8M/Year

## Summary

Pat Walls, founder of Starter Story, walks through the tools and habits behind a business that makes over $1M a year. The business items are Loom, Reddit, YouTube and Klaviyo email marketing. The habit items are deep work, tea instead of coffee, tennis, a 16-inch MacBook Pro and a nicotine habit he wants to quit. The main lessons: sell what you already know, get first customers on Reddit, use YouTube for distribution, and set up email flows.

## Key facts

- A 5-minute Loom video that walked Pieter Levels through Starter Story's Klaviyo flows became a product. Pat sold it with a Stripe payment link and made about $20,000 in pre-orders in one day (2024-10). The transcript also says "$220,000" once, which looks like a caption error. [0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=31s)
- Pat built Starter Story next to a full-time job. After 365 days of early-morning deep work it made money and he quit. [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=187s), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=248s)
- One Reddit post with about 400 upvotes brought about 1,000 email signups and the first customers. [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=373s)
- The channel passed 100,000 YouTube subscribers (2024-10). [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=544s)
- Email list: 250,000 to 300,000 people. Klaviyo costs about $4,000 a month and sends millions of emails a month. Email generates about $80K to $120K a month (2024-10). [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=667s)
- Klaviyo was Starter Story's first sponsor, with a $12,000 check for the newsletter. [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=667s)

## Tactics

- **Replace emails with Loom.** Use video for feedback, delegation and training. Good internal explainers can become paid products. [0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=31s)
- **Do deep work around a day job.** Wake early and go to a coffee shop. Block sites and turn off the phone. Do the hardest tasks first: coding, writing, delegating. [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=187s)
- **Get first customers on Reddit without spam.** Sort target subreddits by top posts and study posts that quietly show off a startup. Copy that format. Keep trying after the first failure. [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=373s), [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=434s)
- **Treat YouTube as distribution.** Pat calls it the most important part of Starter Story's business because viewers build a relationship with you. [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=544s)
- **Set up email flows.** A 5-email welcome sequence that tells a story, and an abandoned-checkout sequence that reminds buyers and can offer a discount. [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=727s)
- **Pick a hobby that feels like play.** Tennis gives him one to two hours a day away from metrics. [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=462s)
