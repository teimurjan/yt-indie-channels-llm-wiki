# How I Built It: $30K/Month Micro SaaS (Subscribr Breakdown)

## Summary

Gil Hildebrand built Subscribr, an AI scriptwriting tool for YouTube creators. He came from VC-backed startups (co-founder of Squidoo, then a crypto accounting company) and chose to bootstrap this time. He knew little about YouTube, so he built an audience on X and an email list first. He pre-sold 50 lifetime licenses for $20,000 before writing the product. Subscribr was profitable from day one and makes about $30K/month.

## Key facts

- About $30K/month in subscription revenue, over $700K in sales in the past year, and over 4,000 customers, 18 months after launch (2025-12). [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=75s)
- Plans cost $49 to $300/month (2025-12). [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s)
- Programmatic SEO brings over 30,000 views a month from Google (2025-12). Word of mouth and social media bring the rest. [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=75s)
- Background: coding from age 15, college dropout, freelancer. After Hurricane Katrina he moved to New York and co-founded Squidoo, which grew past $10M revenue and was acquired. He later raised millions for a crypto accounting startup and sold it. [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=113s)
- The pre-sale of 50 lifetime licenses sold out in 2 to 3 days for $20,000. [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)
- The email list passed 1,000 people before the pre-sale. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s)
- Stack: Claude Code writes about 90% of the code. The app is Laravel (PHP) on DigitalOcean. [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=796s)
- Monthly costs: about $3,500 AI compute, $2,000 ads, $1,500 for scraping, hosting and email (2025-12). [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=796s)

## Tactics

- **Find the bottleneck in your own work.** He ran faceless YouTube channels and found that good scripts were the hardest part. ChatGPT alone was not good enough. [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=180s)
- **Enter a new niche on X.** He opened a new account with zero followers and followed the YouTube crowd. He made free tools and content and used viral giveaways to grow an email list. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s)
- **Talk to each subscriber.** He emailed the list weekly and messaged people one by one about the idea. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=599s)
- **Money is the only validation.** The question "how much will you pay?" gives false comfort. Sell a painkiller, not a vitamin. [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=443s), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=505s)
- **Tiered pre-sale pricing.** The first 10 licenses were very cheap, and the price rose for every next 10. This created FOMO. [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)
- **A full refund until delivery.** He promised delivery in 60 days plus 2 weeks of trial, with a refund at any time. This removed buyer risk and gave him a hard deadline. [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s), [12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=763s)
- **Four-step pre-sale playbook.** [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=538s), [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=649s), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=709s)
  1. Build an audience with free, useful content and raw build-in-public posts.
  2. Email the list at least once a week and use replies as feedback.
  3. Do the math. He needed $20,000 for 3 months, which meant 50 buyers at his price, which set the list size from expected conversion.
  4. Run a 7-day daily email sequence on benefits, then give a date only. On launch day, queue several emails and end-of-sale reminders, and post on every channel.
- **Make an offer people cannot refuse.** He cites Alex Hormozi's "$100M Offers". The first 10 licenses were maybe not even profitable. [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=709s)
- **Profit over growth.** Avoid expensive agencies and side directions that eat profit. [14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=850s)
