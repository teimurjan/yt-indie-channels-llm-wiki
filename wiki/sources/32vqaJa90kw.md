## Summary

Nicole built four consumer apps in two years with her co-founder Aaron. She handles design and distribution, and he codes. Glam Up, a glow-up and makeup app for women, got 1M users in 6 months and peaked at $150K MRR. Sprout (formerly PrepearAI), a swipe-to-apply job app for college students, reached about $250K MRR in 8 months. Two newer apps in stealth make about $200K MRR combined (2026-05). All of them grow through a managed UGC creator system that drives 400 to 500M views a month.

## Key facts

- Glam Up: 1M users in the first 6 months, about 2M total downloads, peak $150K MRR (2026-05). [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=115s)
- Sprout: about $250K MRR in 8 months (recorded October 2025), now steady at around $200K MRR (2026-05). [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=115s)
- Two stealth apps built in the past 3 months: about $200K MRR (2026-05). [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s)
- Views: about 100M/month in late 2025, now 400 to 500M/month across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts. One 7-day period hit 400M views. [0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=36s), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)
- Glam Up at peak: 40 to 50M monthly TikTok views and about 200M on Instagram, from faceless UGC. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)
- At the scaling peak they managed about 200 active creators at once. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)
- Model: hard paywall on every app, with weekly or monthly subscriptions. [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=175s)
- Stack: React Native, RevenueCat, Superwall for paywalls, PostHog for analytics, Sideshift for creator recruiting, Attio as CRM. [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=773s)

## Tactics

- **Copy a proven male app for women.** Glam Up is a female version of Umax, a looksmaxxing app from the same accelerator. Sprout moved to college students for higher spending power. [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s)
- **Design the app for virality.** Treat welcome pages and onboarding as part of distribution. The app needs to look good on social media. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=357s)
- **Find the viral channel first.** Test Reddit, faceless TikTok and Instagram content (slideshows, varied CTAs), and face UGC, real or AI. Give each 2 to 3 weeks and try many variations inside it. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=465s)
- **Start small with creators.** Be your own creator, or train 2 to 10 creators until one goes viral. Then build the system. [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=514s)
- **UGC system, step 1: source.** Match creators to the format (talkers for talking heads, expressive faces for reactions). Inbound: application forms in UGC group chats, Instagram, Reddit, Sizzle. Outbound: VAs who message micro-influencers. [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=542s)
- **Step 2: interview and train.** A vibe-check call, then a Typeform plus a video course with a content bank and a quiz per module. Over 50% of Sprout creators who finish the course go viral within 2 weeks. [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=578s)
- **Step 3: manage.** Use influencers or top creators as managers, Discord chats, and biweekly or monthly feedback calls. [10:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=626s)
- **Step 4: systematize.** Add a referral program for creators and growth dashboards. [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=655s)
