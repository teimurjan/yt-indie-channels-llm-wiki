# These 5 mistakes will RUIN your UI/UX portfolio

## Summary

Tim Gabe lists five portfolio mistakes that stop designers from getting interviews. He draws on his work as a freelancer and as a product designer at Spotify, where he saw designers rejected in hiring. His core point: a portfolio is a sales pitch and a story, and the work is the main character. The portfolio site must be well designed but simple, and it must actually go live.

## Key facts

- Tim was a product designer at Spotify and took part in hiring designers there (2022-12). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=0s), [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=144s)
- At Spotify, a portfolio site with weak UI or odd UX was enough to reject a designer. [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=206s)
- He calls a badly designed portfolio site "probably the biggest turn off" for a recruiter. [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=144s)

## Tactics

- **Do not explain the full process.** Interviews cover the detail. A long case study bores the recruiter early. Write each project as a short story or sales pitch that shows your thinking fast. [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=27s)
- **Show the problems, not a perfect result.** A flawless story makes recruiters skeptical and hides your problem solving. Put the obstacles and your fixes up front. [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=69s)
- **Keep the site simple.** Good-enough UI and UX is enough. The work is the main character, not the site. [1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=110s)
- **Publish it.** Designers who tweak gradients and animations often never launch. Treat the portfolio as a work in progress. [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=144s)
- **Still design it well.** Simple does not mean sloppy. Make the site functional so the work stands out. [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=144s), [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=206s)

## Quotes

- "No one's going to be impressed with a perfect outcome." [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=69s)
