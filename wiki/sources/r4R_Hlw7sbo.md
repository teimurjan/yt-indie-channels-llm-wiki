# I Built a $20K/Month App in 83 Days

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Brian Shin, who built Once with his girlfriend and co-founder. Once is a disposable camera app for events such as weddings and parties. It reached $20K monthly revenue 83 days after its December 2025 launch. Brian's key idea is the "commitment metric". Before writing app code, he required 10 real events with fixed dates to commit to use the product. He got them from his personal network and cold messages on Instagram.

## Key facts

- Launched December 2025. Reached $20K monthly revenue in 83 days (2026-05) ([0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=56s)).
- Revenue this month is about $22K. Weekly active users are 10,000 to 12,000 (2026-05) ([1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)).
- Over 300 events in February and about 700 booked for March ([2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=141s)).
- Price depends on guest count: $2 for a 10-person party, $50 for a 150-guest wedding ([1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)).
- Brian earlier co-founded a VC-backed, sales-driven B2B startup with 50 people. Once is fully bootstrapped ([3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=202s)).
- Cold outreach: about 250 to 300 prospects, about 15 replies, about 12 events booked in one month ([7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=480s)).

## Tactics

- **Idea from their own habit.** The couple carried disposable cameras across 7 or 8 countries. The limits (no instant preview, few shots) make the experience feel real. Once copies that digitally ([4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=266s)).
- **Throwaway web prototype.** He built a web app in 1 to 2 weeks for a friend's Halloween party, with printed invite codes. It broke often but proved that guests love a shared album ([4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=266s), [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=314s)).
- **No AI for design.** They threw out the prototype and designed the app by hand in Figma. Brian thinks consumer apps are a craft that needs taste. He uses AI for code and finance ([5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=349s)).
- **Stack.** Figma, Claude Code, Conductor to run parallel Claude Code agents on separate worktrees, Supabase, Vercel for the web parts ([6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=395s)).
- **Five-step validation playbook** ([8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=510s), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=571s), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s)):
  1. Define a commitment metric with a deadline and a target number. It need not be payment. It must be a signal of real usage.
  2. Exhaust your personal network. Open X, LinkedIn and Instagram and go through every contact. Apply the Mom Test.
  3. Build a quick mockup in 2 to 3 days with Figma or AI tools.
  4. Go where your users are, such as Reddit, TikTok or YouTube. That channel becomes your marketing channel later.
  5. Set the go threshold. For Once, it was 10 events with real dates before any app code.
- **Network sweep.** Four friends said yes: a Halloween party, a birthday, a wedding and a networking event ([7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s)).
- **Hashtag prospecting.** He searched Instagram hashtags such as #wedding and #birthdayparty. He sent a 2 to 3 sentence cold pitch to each prospect ([7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=480s)).
- **Push outreach hard.** Brian's test of effort: get banned on a platform at least twice ([10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s)).
- **Usage-based pricing.** Hosts pay by guest count and shots per person ([1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=685s)).
- **Pat and Gus's take.** AI makes building easy, so founders skip the hard part of talking to people. The commitment metric forces that step ([14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=855s), [15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=906s)).

## Quotes

- "We set a number and didn't start building until we hit it." ([0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=0s))
- "If you haven't been banned in these platforms at least two times, you haven't tried enough." ([10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s))
