# Hey! I'm Tim, and I'm here to teach you some design.

## Summary

This is the first video on Tim Gabe's channel, about two minutes long. Tim introduces himself as a product designer with seven years of experience in UX, UI and product design. He worked at agencies and product companies, most recently Spotify. He starts the channel because he likes design, content creation and filmmaking. He plans design theory and tutorials, mostly about UX, UI and product design.

## Key facts

- Seven years of product, UX and UI design experience (2022-05). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=0s)
- Work history: agencies and product companies. His last product company was Spotify. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=0s)
- Planned content: design theory, tutorials and other topics where he can add value. Other creative topics are possible too. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=63s)

## Tactics

- **Combine skills into a channel.** Tim joins design experience with an interest in content and film. He calls this "the perfect kind of storm" for a YouTube channel. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=0s)
- **Ask for the subscribe anyway.** He dislikes the cliché of asking viewers to subscribe and turn on the bell. He still asks, because viewers forget. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=63s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=124s)
