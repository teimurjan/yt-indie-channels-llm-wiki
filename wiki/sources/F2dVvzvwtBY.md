## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Nick Lawton, co-founder of SideShift, a marketplace where apps run UGC creator campaigns. Nick lays out a full path from a first $2K to $4K month to a paid ads machine. First, research and remix formats. Pay creators a base plus a view bonus, and look for content market fit. Then move 90% of creators onto the winner. Last, put ad spend behind videos with a 5% engagement rate. He says AI-generated UGC gets vanity views but does not convert. He also says big spenders put 80% of budget into performance ads, not organic.

## Key facts

- The top SideShift brands spend from $250K to over $700K per month on the platform (2026-08). Cursor is a customer. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=0s), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=182s), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=339s)
- Hot verticals: peptide and GLP-1 trackers, movies and songs, and enterprise B2B brands (2026-08). [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=182s), [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=262s)
- Typical start: $2K to $4K in the first month. [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=262s)
- Creator pay: $600 to $800 base for 30 to 60 videos, plus a $1 to $2 CPM view bonus, capped or uncapped. [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=472s)
- Content market fit: 5 or 6 videos in the first campaign that clear 10K views. Nick calls a video viral at 100K. [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=571s)
- Budget split at large spenders: about 20% creative and organic, about 80% performance. [26:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1568s)
- Paid trigger: about 5% engagement rate. Start with about $100/day in total across winners and judge it over 14 days. Spend ranges from $300/day to $100K/day. [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s), [30:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1815s), [31:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1884s)
- A SideShift warm-up video about job rejection got about 700K likes. [17:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1037s), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1104s)

## Tactics

- **Research before launch.** Search TikTok and Instagram for competitors. Remix a winning format by 10 to 20% so it is not a copy. [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=402s), [11:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=719s)
- **Mine everyday problem videos.** For new formats, take organic videos about the problem your product solves and add a CTA. Examples: "my face before peptides" and job market rants. [15:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=919s), [17:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1037s)
- **No budget? Post yourself.** A small team can create accounts and post UGC daily. [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=448s)
- **Judge by comments, not views.** AI UGC gets views but comments say "is this AI?". Real creators get "what is the app?". A program with 10M monthly views that converts at 0.0001% burns money. [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s), [14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=861s)
- **Warm up accounts.** Post controversial, CTA-free videos first so the algorithm sees a real person and learns the niche. Then add a subtle CTA. [18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1129s)
- **Manage creators in one group chat.** Share wins, watch-time breakdowns and leaderboards with the whole cohort. [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=571s), [10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=632s), [24:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1485s)
- **90/10 split.** After a winner, recruit more creators with a 5 to 10 minute call. Put 90% on the winner and keep 10% (one or two creators) testing new formats against saturation. [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1242s), [21:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1302s)
- **Organic finds, paid scales.** Paid ads give the attribution that organic lacks. Organic only shows correlation spikes against revenue. [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s)
- **Spark vs Meta.** TikTok Spark ads push organic posts at cheap CPMs but have no install button. Meta ads have a CTA and attribution, so they scale. [33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2000s), [34:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2054s)
- **Pay creators on ad sales.** Move from retainer plus CPM to retainer plus a percentage of tracked ad revenue, as TikTok Shop does. [34:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2093s)
- **AI agents for operations, not content.** SideShift's agent takes a budget, a website and a creator count, then handles briefs, creator selection and spend. A human campaign manager still helps. [38:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2317s), [42:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2540s)
