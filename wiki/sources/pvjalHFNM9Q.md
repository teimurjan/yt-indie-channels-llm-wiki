# How I Used Reddit to Build a $34K/Month SaaS

## Summary

Roman Czerny co-founded Gojiberry AI, a tool that finds high-intent leads on LinkedIn and runs AI-personalized outreach. It went from $0 to about $30K MRR in roughly 4 to 6 months. Reddit brought the first 100 customers through story posts with proof that got over 11M impressions. Roman shares an 8-step Reddit playbook: account warm-up, early upvotes from a group, no direct product links, and replies to every comment. Pat Walls adds that Reddit is for the first 10 to 100 customers and for honest feedback, not for scale.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $30K MRR, with a dashboard showing about $24K, 4 months after $0 (2026-01). The title says $34K/month. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=94s), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=133s)
- Pricing: three plans. Most users are on the $99/month plan with unlimited high-intent leads. [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=133s)
- Reddit results: over 11M impressions, over 40K website visitors and about the first 100 customers (2026-01). [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=237s)
- Background: mechanical engineer, then affiliate marketing and freelancing. In 2023 he launched Coco AI, grew it to $50K MRR with outreach and sold it to a US company. [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=192s)
- Post "I paid 5 influencers on LinkedIn to promote my SaaS. Here's what $1,250 got me": 160K views, 543 upvotes, about 2,000 visitors and 10 to 15 clients. [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=276s)
- Post about failing the Y Combinator interview: over 179K views and about 15 clients. [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=320s)
- Marketing tool costs per month: Instantly about $800 (high-volume cold email), Outrank $99, Gojiberry $99, Calendly $20, Framer $49, ChatGPT plus Nano Banana about $40, Sales Navigator $100. [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=767s), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=800s)

## Tactics

- **Tell a story and show proof.** Proof makes the post believable and stops arguments. If you have no wins yet, write about your process or a failure. [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=276s), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=638s)
- **Draft by voice with AI.** Roman is not a native English speaker. He tells the story to ChatGPT by voice and asks it to translate and correct. [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=409s)
- **Do not put the SaaS in the post.** Create curiosity instead. The product link sits in the profile bio. [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=409s)
- **Use one account per browser.** Do not pair a new email with a new Reddit account, or the account gets banned. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=477s)
- **Hide your profile feed.** Then readers cannot see that every post is about the same product. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=477s)
- **Warm up for 7 to 14 days.** Only comment and upvote to build karma. Then post, and only later market. Skip subreddits that ban promotion. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=477s), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=526s)
- **Get 10 upvotes in the first 10 minutes.** A group of 15 marketers upvotes and comments on each other's posts. [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=526s)
- **Change the angle each post.** Link to a YouTube video or an X post instead. [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=526s)
- **Reply to every comment.** Engagement lifts the ranking. Block people who only insult you. [9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=587s), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=638s)
- **Reddit also helps sales.** Leads on demo calls say "you are the Reddit guy", which helps close them. [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=237s)
- **Pat's view.** Pat got Starter Story's first 1,000 users on Reddit, in spite of a petition to ban it. His 5th to 7th post got 400 upvotes. Reddit gets the first 10 to 100 customers, not the first 1,000. [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=668s), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=885s), [15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=941s)
