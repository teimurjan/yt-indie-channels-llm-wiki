# I Built a $30K/Month Business From a Remote Island (How I Work)

## Summary

Milly Tamati founded Generalist World, a brand for people who do many different things and do not fit into one box at work. It is a newsletter, a members community, a job board, a summer school and an invented holiday, International Generalist Day. She runs it from a 200-year-old cottage on a Scottish island of 191 people with a very small team. The video is a day-in-the-life. She designs her days and her business around calm, not scale, and keeps it small on purpose with no investors.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $30K/month as a solo founder with a very small team (2026-09). [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s)
- Nearly $600K total revenue over 4 years (2026-09). She also calls it "a couple of hundred grand a year". [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)
- Products: newsletter, members community, job board, summer school and International Generalist Day. [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s)
- Reach: hundreds of thousands of people see, read or watch her content each month (2026-09). [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=289s)
- She moved to the island about 5.5 years ago. The island has 191 people and no restaurants or food delivery. [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=458s)
- Her largest partnership is with Beehiiv. They signed a one-year deal. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)
- No investors. The small team are also her friends. [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)

## Tactics

- **Make calm the main KPI.** She wants the business to last decades, not to peak fast. [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s)
- **Slow mornings first.** She takes a walk, meditates and then creates content before Slack or email. She believes calm leads to better decisions on calls. [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=83s), [2:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=137s)
- **Split the day by energy.** Creative work goes in the morning. Then comes a long lunch walk of about 40 minutes. Meetings, podcasts and partnerships go in the afternoon. She stops at about 3 p.m. [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=248s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=458s)
- **Fall in love with a big problem, not an idea.** Tens of millions of generalists feel undervalued. That problem never runs out, so the work never ends. [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=347s)
- **Choose the life first.** Thousands of support-heavy customers do not fit her walks in the forest. Be honest about the life you want. [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)
- **Pitch partners with proof.** She did not wait for Beehiiv to say yes. She sent a body of work and its results. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)
- **Be more weird.** The internet looks bland. Her success grew as she got more honest and more weird. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)
