# Masking in 4 Minutes (Figma Tutorial)

## Summary

Tim Gabe shows the two ways to mask in Figma: image fills and regular masks. Image fills are best for one image inside one shape or text layer. Regular masks clip two or more layers to a shape. He ends with a "stylized PNG mask". A cut-out PNG becomes the mask for a background, and a blended copy of the PNG goes on top.

## Key facts

- Figma has two masking options: image fills and "Use as mask". [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=14s)
- An image fill works on shapes and on text. [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=14s), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=74s)
- "Use as mask" is the most-used option. It is in the top toolbar. [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=90s)
- The mask layer must be at the bottom of the group. [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=90s)

## Tactics

- **Image fill.** Click the rectangle tool menu and choose "Place image". Or set a shape's fill from Solid to Image. Use Crop in the fill panel to move the image inside the shape. [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=14s)
- **Image in text.** Choose "Place image", pick the image and click the text layer. Use Crop to move the image. [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=74s)
- **Regular mask.** Put the masking shape at the bottom, select all layers and click "Use as mask". The masked group has no fill. To get a background color, add a "background" layer above the mask and below the content. [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=90s), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=150s)
- **Stylized PNG mask.** Use a transparent PNG of a person as the mask for a background image. Paste a copy of the PNG on top. Try blend modes such as Screen, Hard light and Soft light on that copy. [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=177s)

## Quotes

- "Image fills are good for cases where you just need to mask a single image." [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=14s)
- "Play around with it because you can achieve amazing things." [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KamNFblohHc&t=177s)
