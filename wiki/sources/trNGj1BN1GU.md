## Summary

Marcos Ruiz runs The Birdhouse, a Twitter (X) ghostwriting agency for coaches, consultants and education companies. He went from $0 to $65K/month in one year. Pat Walls interviewed him in Miami, at about $70K/month with 60% margins. He got his first clients on Upwork. He then grew with 100 to 200 cold DMs a day, warm DMs and inbound leads from his own Twitter content. His key move was to sell monetization, not followers: if the client makes more than they pay, they stay.

## Tactics

- **Stack case studies.** He used his first Upwork job as social proof in DMs. A client message about "seven free trial subscribers" became a testimonial that landed the next client [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=268s).
- **Cold, warm and hot DMs.** Cold: 100 to 200 DMs a day with a profile-based compliment, the offer and a low-pressure close, tracked in Notion. Warm: DM people who follow and engage. Hot: inbound leads. Pair this with 2 to 3 tweets a day, including a thread [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=410s).
- **Pick a niche that monetizes.** Coaches, consultants and education companies can turn views into sales fast [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s).
- **Sell revenue, not followers.** Growth is a by-product. The offer is "done for you" monetization on Twitter, which keeps clients long term [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=593s).
- **Close with honesty.** He used "The Closer" framework, but says sales is mostly conviction and plain talk about how you will help [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s).
- **Hire with a test.** He tweeted that he needed a writer. He gave 4 to 5 candidates the same thread prompt. He picked the best writer who also fit the culture [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=524s).
- **Be your own case study.** Grow your own account first. Prospects with more followers than you will ignore you [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=634s).
- **Implement, do not tactic-hunt.** Do the same outbound and content daily for 6 to 12 months to build a referral and inbound flywheel [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s).
- **Theme days.** Mondays hold 12 to 15 meetings. Tuesdays stay blank for editing and deep work [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=634s).
