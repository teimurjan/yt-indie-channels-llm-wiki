# How To Build A $100M Podcast Empire

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Danny Miranda in Austin. Danny started the Danny Miranda Podcast in his parents' basement in September 2020 and grew it to guests like Gary Vaynerchuk and Andy Frisella. He explains how a podcast makes money and how to start with almost no gear. He also shows how he researches guests and lands big names through Twitter. His growth engine is a heavy daily clip schedule across YouTube, Instagram and Twitter, plus a strict cadence of three episodes a week.

## Key facts

- Danny launched in September 2020 with three episodes a week. The show reached 30K to 50K downloads/month (2023-06). [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=64s)
- He had done 359 episodes in two and a half years without a missed week (2023-06). [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=747s)
- Gary Vaynerchuk came on as episode 39. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=387s)
- Podcast business models he lists: advertising (most common for big shows), funnels to courses or newsletters, consulting, and conferences built from past guests. Danny earns from consulting: he helps new shows book strong guests from day one. [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=186s)
- Market reference points: Joe Rogan's $100M Spotify deal, Alex Cooper's reported $20M/year from Spotify, and a podcast company sale he cites at $300M. [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=242s)
- Starter kit: a Shure MV7 microphone, a Zoom connection, and Anchor (free) for hosting. He still used Anchor in 2023. [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=319s)
- Clip schedule (2023-06): at least 1 full YouTube video over one minute, 2 YouTube Shorts, 3 Instagram Reels and 5 Twitter clips every day. An editor handles it now. [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=634s)

## Tactics

- **Send more messages.** New podcasters underestimate how many people say yes. Send 100 DMs and stop rejecting yourself first. [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=282s)
- **Start with friends.** Record the first 10 episodes with people you are comfortable with and excited to talk to. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=387s)
- **Research deeply.** Spend 5 to 20 hours per guest on their past podcasts, Google, Twitter and Instagram. Aim for questions that give the guest a new insight about themselves. [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=342s)
- **Hack attention on the platform the guest uses most.** Twitter is the most untapped channel because everyone checks mentions. Danny tweeted that Gary Vaynerchuk was a dream guest and shared a 2009 blog post about him. Gary followed him, followers pushed Gary in the replies, and Gary came on. [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=457s), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=517s)
- **A big guest is not marketing.** Cut episodes into short clips that tap a bigger conversation, such as entrepreneurship. Strangers then care, although they do not know the host. [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=583s)
- **Care more, or niche down.** Care more than others about clips and the guest experience. Or own a niche or taboo topic, as Call Her Daddy did. [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=683s)
- **Reverse-engineer consistency.** He copied Joe Rogan's three episodes a week. He treats each episode like a gym rep for communication skill. [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=747s)
- **Build presence.** Meditation, morning walks and fitness help him listen well in interviews. [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=805s)

## Quotes

- "There are more people who will come on your podcast than you probably imagine. Just send 100 messages." [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=282s)
- "You need to hack their attention. How do you hack their attention? You use the platforms that they spend the most time on and you give them value." [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=387s)
- "Tapping into bigger conversations is really how you grow the podcast." [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=583s)
