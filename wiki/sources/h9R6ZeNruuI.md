## Summary

Adam Lyttle ran a web design agency that made about $300K profit per year. Wix and Squarespace then killed demand for $10,000 websites, and he was left with $200K of credit card and car loan debt. He mowed lawns for money, and a routing problem from that work led to his first app. That app failed, so he set a challenge to ship one app every month. App sales paid his debts for two years. Later a portfolio of small apps, plus a new paywall and onboarding flow, took him to $50K to $60K per month and out of debt.

## Key facts

- The agency peaked at about $300K profit per year. He charged $10,000 per website [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=157s), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=219s).
- Debt: $200K, unsecured, after the agency collapsed and his cars were taken [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=341s).
- He found mowing jobs on Airtasker at $20 to $30 per lawn [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=377s).
- The first app, for lawn mowing businesses, took 6 months to build and did not sell well [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=466s).
- One early app from the one-app-a-month challenge made about $11K/month. A buyer paid two times annual revenue: $224K [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=650s).
- For about two years he built and sold apps to extend his runway [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=712s).
- After ChatGPT came out he aimed for 10 apps in one month and shipped 9. Each app made $2 to $500 per month, but together they paid the bills [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=797s).
- He has built over 50 apps. He started a YouTube channel in July (2024) to test and share results [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s).
- A new paywall and a new onboarding flow took revenue from $10K/month to $50K/month. It then held at $50K to $60K/month, and he paid off all his debt (2025-02) [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s).

## Tactics

- **Find ideas in daily life.** The lawn app came from mowers who planned routes on paper. He looks for a need, or for apps that do not do the job well [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=437s), [15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=924s).
- **Ship one app per month.** He learns by building. Fast launches show which ideas work [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=466s).
- **Sell apps for runway.** He sold apps at about 2x annual revenue. Each sale bought time to launch the next hit [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=712s).
- **Move from flipping to a portfolio.** Many small apps give steady monthly revenue. He no longer has to sell an app to stay afloat [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=797s).
- **Test monetization in public.** He ran paywall and onboarding tests on his apps and shared them on YouTube. That one change gave a 5x revenue jump [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s).
- **Build from passion, not only for money.** He says a project you do not care about feels as bad as debt [15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=924s).
