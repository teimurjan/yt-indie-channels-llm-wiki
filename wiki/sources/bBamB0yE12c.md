# Can You Copy 6 Websites in 66 Seconds?

## Summary

Tim Gabe tests Framer's new "HTML to Framer" extension. He tries to copy 6 live Webflow template sites into Framer in 66 seconds: a blog, a portfolio, a SaaS landing page and 3 ecommerce pages. He misses the time limit and finishes in about 2 minutes. The copies have some misaligned, missing or duplicated elements, but they are editable and can be published in one click. He warns viewers to copy only work they have permission to use.

## Key facts

- The 6 targets are free Webflow templates: a blog, a portfolio, a SaaS landing page and 3 ecommerce pages. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=0s)
- 4 pages copied in about 57 seconds. All 6 took about 2 minutes. [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=125s)
- The workflow per page: open the extension on the live site, press Shift+Escape to target the layout, "copy layout", then paste the layers into Framer. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=63s)
- For some ecommerce pages, a full-page copy failed. He had to copy sections one by one and missed the top nav at first. [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=125s)
- Tim was building a beginner Framer course with a waitlist at the time (2023-09). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=0s)

## Tactics

- **Import, then fix.** Use the extension to get a working layout fast. Framer is a visual editor, so fix the odd alignment by hand after the paste. [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=189s)
- **Copy by section as a fallback.** On complex ecommerce layouts the full-page copy failed, so he copied section by section. [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=125s)
- **Respect ownership.** Use the feature on your own sites or free templates. Do not take other people's work without permission. [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=189s)
- **Use a challenge format for a tool demo.** A timed challenge with a ticking clock turns a feature demo into a watchable video. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=63s)
