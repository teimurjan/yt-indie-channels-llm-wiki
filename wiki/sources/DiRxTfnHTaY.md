# How We Built This $20K/Month AI App

## Summary

Pat Walls meets Christian and Braylin, two college sprinters in Los Angeles. They built 3AK, an all-in-one track and field app, with no coding experience. They used the AI app builder Rork and checked its work with Claude and Claude Code. The app launched to an audience of hundreds of thousands of track followers that they built with their own videos. It makes over $20K/month. Their advice: build in a niche where you already have influence and interest.

## Key facts

- 3AK launched on December 16. It made about $70K in its first 6 months, with about 40,000 users and a 4.7 App Store rating (2026-06). [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=74s), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=82s)
- The app makes over $20K/month (2026-06). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=0s)
- They built the app in about 2 weeks. It made $10K in the first month. [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)
- The founders are 19 and 20. One runs track at Texas Southern and the other at Texas State. Christian dropped out to work on apps. [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=97s), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=524s)
- Before the app, they lost about $3,000 day trading. [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)
- Tools: Rork, Claude, Claude Cowork, Claude Code, RevenueCat, an Apple developer account and Supabase. They say an app can reach the App Store for less than $500. [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=172s), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=204s)
- Pricing: $10/month or $30/year (2026-06). [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)
- Features: workout plans, workout and form tracking, AI video analysis of sprint form, meal analysis and a community. [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=334s)
- They also launched a football app. During that launch they worked 50 to 60 hours over about four days with little sleep. [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=559s)

## Tactics

- **Prompt the builder in plain words.** They tell Rork what UI they want, like a chat with ChatGPT. When they get stuck, they ask Claude to fix and check the code. [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=172s), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=188s)
- **Pick a niche with no dedicated apps.** Many people run track, but no app served track and field. [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=299s)
- **Build the audience first.** Their track videos had hundreds of thousands of followers with no paid marketing. [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=312s)
- **Seed the brand before launch.** They had a 3AK Shopify store and put "3AK method" in videos and bios. When the app dropped, the audience already knew the name. [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=318s)
- **Price for a young audience.** Users are teens who spend $10 to $20 at a time on things like V-Bucks and Robux. They will not pay $60 to $70 for a program. So the yearly plan is only $30. [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)
- **Make a demo moment.** The AI analyzer scores a sprint start. Pat scored 56, then 68 after one tip. That kind of instant result is easy to show on video. [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=388s), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=472s)
- **Work in shifts with a co-founder.** One studies while the other builds. They say a student founder needs a partner. [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=568s)
- **Monetize what you already do.** Build in a niche you love, because you will stay in it longer than someone who only wants money. [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=702s)
- **Solve tomorrow's problem today.** Find a problem you have now that will recur, and build for it. [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=722s)
