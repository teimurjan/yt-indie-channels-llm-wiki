# I Built A $30K/Month in 35 Days

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Cedric Roberge, a University of Oregon student who built Pep AI, a peptide dose tracker and research app. A roommate mentioned peptides, then TikTok showed him the trend, and he found no successful app in the niche. He built it in about 2 weeks with Replit and Claude, fought through Apple's medical-advice rejections, and launched with a 300-person waitlist. Niche influencer posts drove the early revenue. It reached $51K total revenue in about 7 weeks.

## Key facts

- RevenueCat dashboard (2026-05): $33K revenue in the last 28 days, almost 2,000 active subscriptions, $11K MRR, $51K total revenue. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)
- Pricing (2026-05): $45/year or $10/month, with a 3-day free trial. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)
- Build: about 2 weeks with Replit, Claude as a "senior developer", RevenueCat, Resend and Firebase. [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=205s)
- App Review took about a week. Apple rejected it many times for features that looked like medical advice. Expedited review cut review time from about two days to about two hours. [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=205s), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=264s)
- His first app, a University of Oregon student marketplace, got 800 users and no revenue. [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=142s)
- Pre-launch waitlist of about 300 people from influencer posts and Reddit. [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s)
- One influencer's Instagram story made about $1,000. His first feed post got 50K views and about $4,000 that day, and over $10,000 in total (2026-05). [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s)
- Pep AI ranked number 3 for "peptide tracker" in the App Store behind ads (2026-05). [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=818s)

## Tactics

- **Stack signals.** A friend mentioned it, TikTok showed it, and he got curious. Pat calls these three signals important. [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=398s)
- **Five-step trend playbook.** [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=506s), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=551s), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=687s)
  1. Watch TikTok and Instagram daily for what creators are obsessed with.
  2. Look for existing apps. A few weak competitors prove demand.
  3. Read every competitor review. Feed likes, dislikes and wishes to Claude or ChatGPT to shape the product.
  4. Get niche influencers posting before launch.
  5. Do not build without a marketing plan.
- **Use the search wave.** Users search the trend term in the App Store, so organic installs come free. When copycats named "Peptide AI" went viral on X, his app was the top result. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=418s), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=471s)
- **Reach trusted creators first.** He trusted peptide creators himself, so he guessed their followers will download an app they endorse. [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s)
- **Work around App Review.** Study how similar apps phrase health features. Use expedited review for urgent fixes. [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=205s), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=264s)
- **Core features.** A dose calculator, dose log with injection-site rotation, Duolingo-style quizzes, a PubMed-sourced research library, meal scanning, weight tracking, Apple Health and progress photos. [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=736s), [13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=798s)
- **Rebrand from reviews.** Pat cites another founder who fed reviews, Stripe cancellation reasons and support tickets to Claude, rebranded, and grew about 350%. [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=572s)
- **Build on a rising trend.** Pat argues even a weak app makes money on a growing trend. [16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=996s)
