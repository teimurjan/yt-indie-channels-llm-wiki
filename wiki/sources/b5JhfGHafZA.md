## Summary

Pat Walls visits the three co-founders of Castmagic in Miami: Ramon Berrios, Blaine Bolus and Justin. Castmagic uses AI to turn podcasts, videos and other media into written content. Blaine co-hosts DTC Pod. He found the idea in its show notes: an assistant did not know enough context to write them. They built the first version in two weeks for an AppSumo launch and grew to over $120K MRR in about 10 months (2024-06). They stay bootstrapped and cap marketing spend at 20% of revenue.

## Key facts

- Over $120K MRR about 10 months after start, growing over 20% month over month (2024-06). [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s) [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)
- AppSumo lifetime deal: about $350K in revenue and about 10,000 users. AppSumo offered the slot two weeks before launch. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s)
- Net churn in single digits (2024-06). [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)
- Marketing spend is always under 20% of revenue. [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)
- Stack: Elixir with Phoenix LiveView for front end and back end in one language. The landing page uses Webflow templates. [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=370s)
- Pricing: monthly subscription with a usage cap. They track LLM and transcription cost to protect margin. [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=408s)
- Team: three remote founders in Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Austin. One person runs marketing with contractors. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s) [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=691s)
- One co-founder's past startups raised over $80M (through Series C) and a $2.5M seed from CRV (Omni Panel). Ramon tried to raise for Trend.io for almost 4 years. [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=629s)

## Tactics

- **Solve your own workflow problem.** AI apps that sit inside an existing workflow and remove friction win. Treat the product as a painkiller, not a vitamin. [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=96s) [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=120s)
- **Borrow distribution early.** Either build a personal brand that pulls people inbound, or find a distribution partner. The AppSumo lifetime deal gave brand, a user base and feedback, at the cost of recurring revenue from those users. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s)
- **Creator program at volume.** Scrape Instagram and other platforms for creators in adjacent niches by hashtag. Reach out at volume, then use the replies to negotiate CPM-based rates. Keep the ones who perform. Some creators get paid, others join as affiliates. [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=264s)
- **Systems for a one-person marketing team.** Study how similar companies got distribution. Ramon replaced creator ideation calls with a Notion doc of content concepts. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s)
- **Ship imperfect.** Accept an imperfect first build. Use templates for the site so engineering time goes to the product. [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=370s)
- **Moat vs ChatGPT.** Output quality and full workflow coverage beat a model provider, which solves one small pain point. [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=439s) [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=489s)
- **Co-founder fit.** Pick partners for alignment and complementary skills, not convenience. [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=582s)
- **Remote cadence.** Async work, 30-minute standups on Monday and Friday, and a week together every quarter. [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=691s) [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=746s)
