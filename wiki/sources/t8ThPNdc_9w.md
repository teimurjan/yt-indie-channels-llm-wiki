## Summary

Tim Gabe compares Webflow and Framer in four areas: pricing, Figma export, CMS and overall UI. He calls pricing a tie. Framer wins on Figma export and UI, because it feels like Figma and gives designers more freedom. Webflow wins on CMS, which he calls its strongest advantage. As a designer he picks Framer, and says he built a custom website in Framer in 30 minutes.

## Key facts

- Pricing (2023-09): Framer's Mini plan costs $5. Webflow's Basic plan costs $14. Both are for simple landing pages and portfolios. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s)
- For mid-size projects such as blogs, Webflow's CMS plan is cheaper than Framer's Pro plan. For big projects, Framer's Pro plan is a bit cheaper. Verdict: tie. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s)
- Figma export: Framer is a copy and paste of the design. Webflow needs extra steps, and you must build the Figma file the way Webflow works. Verdict: Framer. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)
- CMS: Framer can do full e-commerce, but complex setups need creative use of its CMS. Verdict: Webflow. [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)
- UI: Framer has Figma-like hotkeys and familiar feature places. Webflow's classes and drag-and-drop builder suit developers. Verdict: Framer, for designers. [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)

## Tactics

- **Pick by role.** Designers who want design freedom and a two-step export go with Framer. Teams who need a strong CMS or think like developers go with Webflow. [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)
- **Structure Figma files for Webflow first.** Webflow has less design freedom than Figma or Framer. So set up the structure in Figma before you export. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)
- **Match the plan to the site size.** Framer is cheaper for small and large sites. Webflow is cheaper for mid-size CMS sites such as blogs. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s)
