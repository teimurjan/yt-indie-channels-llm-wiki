# 6 Stunning Websites For UI/UX Inspiration

## Summary

Tim Gabe reviews six websites for design ideas, on desktop and then on mobile. The six are Glaze, Sketch, a YouTube page about creators, Miro's "Ways We Work" page and Jitter. The last is a minimal product site he calls "Stritz" or "Strides". The captions do not make the name clear. He praises smooth scroll, off-grid type, interactive product demos and short value statements. He also flags weak spots: long loaders, small body text, a mobile overflow bug and heavy pages on old phones. He contrasts brand-first sites with sites built to convert.

## Key facts

- Glaze: a mouse-driven moving background, off-grid type, horizontal story scroll and damped smooth scroll. Weak spots: a long loading animation and small body text on mobile, a possible accessibility issue. [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=15s), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=139s)
- Sketch: visitors can drag real interface parts on the landing page, like a small prototype of the tool. On mobile, the container overflows and scrolls sideways. [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=322s)
- YouTube: creator cards (for example MrBeast) with video backgrounds, hover glow, a carousel and zoom transitions into story "worlds". Touch triggers the hover effect on mobile. [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=392s), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=454s)
- Miro: a blob loading animation, parallax cursors and pie charts that reveal on scroll. Type rescales well on mobile. [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=562s), [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=623s)
- Jitter: a minimal site built for conversion, with the value proposition "Motion design made simple". [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=697s), [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=759s)
- Strides: a black background with type and product shots in the style of Apple product pages, plus a bottom navigation. [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=942s)

## Tactics

- **Let visitors use the product on the landing page.** Sketch shows what the tool does through live interaction, not through effects. [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s)
- **Use small micro-interactions.** Small hover effects on tabs make a page a pleasure to scroll. [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=260s)
- **Break the grid with type.** Off-grid headings and logos add energy, as on Glaze and Miro. [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=15s), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=562s)
- **State the value, then show it.** Jitter puts a clear one-line value proposition next to a GIF of the product. It then repeats big, bold calls to action. [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=759s), [13:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=820s)
- **Adapt media for mobile.** Jitter crops the GIF to its left side on mobile so it stays large. A logo row turns into a ticker. [14:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=881s)
- **Choose brand or conversion on purpose.** Experience-first sites like Strides look great but can convert less. Decide which goal matters more. [16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=1005s)
- **Test on mobile and on slow devices.** Heavy animated sites can fail on older phones and slow internet. [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=139s), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=516s)
