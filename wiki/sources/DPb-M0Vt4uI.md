# I Make $50K Per Month Working 5 Hours A Week

## Summary

Timo, 23, a former software engineer at a German car company, built Chart Detector AI with his brother. The app analyzes a photo of a stock or crypto chart with AI. Almost all growth comes from TikTok paid ads, so he works about 20 hours a month while he travels. He shares his full TikTok ads playbook: onboarding that sells the outcome, a hard paywall, AppsFlyer tracking, Smart+ campaigns optimized for subscriptions, and a steady pipeline of new videos. His profit margin is about 25%.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $56K/month; 13K to 14K downloads/month (2026-07) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s).
- First 13 months: zero to over 90K downloads and over $260K total revenue. He quit his job after 6 months (2026-07) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=62s).
- Reach: close to 40M total impressions, 7.8M views in the last 30 days (2026-07) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s).
- Pricing: hard paywall, no free trial. $12.99/week or $59.99 per 6 months (2026-07) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s).
- April P&L: $43.7K revenue, about $20K ad spend, about 15% Apple fees, about $11.5K profit, a margin of about 25% (2026-07) [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=358s).
- TikTok ad spend: about $18K/month (2026-07) [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=666s).
- Best ad: a plain demo of someone using the app. Close to 5M views and over $15K revenue [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=331s).
- Stack: React Native, Expo, Supabase, Cursor, an AI model API, RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, CapCut, TikTok Ads [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=666s).
- Origin: a Telegram bot that analyzed charts after ChatGPT added image upload, later turned into a mobile app [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=216s).

## Tactics

- **Know your ROAS math.** Pay less per download than an average user earns. Example: $1.50 per download and $3 per user is a 2x ROAS. At that point, spend as much as you can [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=263s).
- **Step 1: prepare the app for ads.** The onboarding sells the outcome, not features or benefits. Add a lot of social proof. Study other apps' flows with Screens Design. Use a hard paywall with no trial, because ads need cash back fast [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s).
- **Step 2: tracking.** Create a TikTok Business Manager account and use an MMP such as AppsFlyer, so TikTok learns who paid. Hire an Upwork freelancer for the setup, because a misconfiguration costs a lot [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=454s).
- **Step 3: campaign setup.** Start with at least 6 videos. Use Smart+ campaigns, which handle audience and budget. Optimize for subscription events, not installs. Spend at least $50/day. Target broad and pick only the country. Countries outside the US often give a better ROI [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=485s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=533s).
- **Leave the learning phase alone.** It takes about 7 days. Do not change settings [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=578s).
- **Step 4: weekly monitoring.** If ROAS is stable and profitable, raise the budget, but by no more than 20% every 3 days, or the campaign goes back into learning. If it is unprofitable, add new videos. Keep a constant pipeline of creatives to beat creative fatigue [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=605s).
- **Ride new API features.** ChatGPT image upload created the idea. Pat Walls compares it to other apps that caught a wave after an API release [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=233s).
