# I Studied 10,000 Paywall Screens (THIS Makes People Pay)

## Summary

Tim Gabe reviews 10,000 paywall screens and the 2026 RevenueCat and Adapty reports, which cover about $3B in subscription revenue. He names six patterns that separate top apps from average ones. Weekly plans are the new default. The first session decides almost all purchases. Plan structure and trials beat price tests. Honest trial terms raise conversion. Higher prices can convert better. Above all, top apps run many more paywall experiments. Cal AI, Lose It, Yazio, Blinkist and Freecash are the examples.

## Key facts

- Top apps make 18.7x more revenue than regular ones, and their paywalls make 636% more LTV than average (2026-05) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=0s).
- Weekly plans grew from 43% to 55.6% of subscription app revenue in two years. Monthly fell from 21.1% to 11.7% (2026-05). Weekly converts 1.7 to 7.4x better than annual at every price tier [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=83s).
- 82% of subscription starts happen on install day according to RevenueCat, and 89.4% according to Adapty. 55% of 3-day trial cancellations happen on day 0 [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s).
- Cal AI: $35M revenue in year 1 with a 28-screen onboarding before the price. Lose It: $3.3M/month at 350K installs. Yazio: about $3M/month at 700K installs (2026-05) [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=256s), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s).
- Adapty LTV uplift by test type: localization +62.3% and trial structure +59.6%. Plan-count tests give 63% more conversion uplift than any price test [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s).
- A weekly plan without a trial has $7.40 LTV over 12 months. With a trial it has $54.50 [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s).
- Blinkist added an explicit line that tells users when they will be charged: conversion rose 23%, complaints fell 55% and churn did not change [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=462s).
- RevenueCat 2026 download-to-paid medians: 2.8% for high-priced apps and 2% for mid-priced apps. The top quartile of high-priced apps reaches 6.1% [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=556s).
- Apps that run 50+ paywall experiments a year make 18.7x the revenue of apps that run one. Top performers average about 15 a year [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=638s).

## Tactics

- **Lead with weekly.** Many users who refuse $38/year accept $7/week. "Ask for a coffee." By month 4 the annual plan is an easy upsell [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=130s).
- **Win the first session.** Show the paywall after the user has felt value. Cal AI used a 3-screen trial explainer with the headline "No payment due now" and a promised reminder before the charge. Lose It put a trial timeline on the paywall [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=256s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=317s).
- **Test structure before price.** Test localization, trial setup and the number of plans before you test $9.99 against $12.99. Yazio tuned price last [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s).
- **Be explicit.** State the charge date, what the user gets and how to cancel. Surprised users cancel and leave bad reviews [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=507s).
- **Do not race to the cheapest price.** Higher prices attract users with higher intent. He does not say to raise prices tomorrow [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=607s).
- **Raise testing speed.** One home-screen widget redesign for Freecash lifted day-7 retention by several points [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=638s).
