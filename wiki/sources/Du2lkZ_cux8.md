# The Secret Behind Weirdly Addictive Apps

## Summary

Tim Gabe argues that features are easy to copy now, so the lasting edge is emotional design: how a product makes people feel. He uses three case studies. Duolingo uses character animation to build habits. Phantom uses playful motion to make crypto feel less scary. Revolut uses rich, tactile visuals to sell a premium product. Each case ends with concrete rules for your own app.

## Key facts

- In 2022 Duolingo added a full character animation system with facial reactions, lip sync and idle animations. [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s)
- After that rollout, Duolingo daily active users grew from 14.2M to over 34M within 2 years. Paid subscribers also more than doubled. [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s)
- Tim notes that new languages, a better curriculum and marketing also drove that growth. Duolingo's team and CEO Luis von Ahn name the animations as one major reason for engagement. [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=185s)
- Phantom refreshed its brand in mid-2023. It animated its ghost mascot and added playful motion to wallet creation. [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=335s)
- Phantom became the number 2 app in the US App Store utilities category, above WhatsApp and Instagram (2025-05). [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=390s)
- Phantom CEO Brandon Millman calls it "a design-led company". Its goal is to onboard the next billion people to web3. [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=410s)
- Revolut's design became more polished as it moved up-market. [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=545s)

## Tactics

- **Compete on feel, not features.** APIs, no-code tools and AI models make features cheap. Users remember how the product left them feeling. [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=25s), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=86s)
- **Give instant emotional feedback.** Apps with repeat actions, such as check-ins, journals or habit logs, gain from a small bounce, glow or sparkle after each action. [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s)
- **Celebrate small wins.** Success states do not need to be big, but they must feel intentional. [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s)
- **Let a mascot show emotion.** Nods, smiles and animated reactions encourage users, because emotions spread. [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s)
- **Animate progress.** Show streaks, levels or completed journals with motion to give a sense of momentum. [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=306s)
- **In high-stakes fields, polish builds trust.** For finance, crypto, health or insurance, treat motion and transitions as core features. Every micro-interaction is a trust signal. [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=471s), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=477s)
- **Keep it approachable and built for normal people.** Friendly visuals make heavy topics lighter. Do not assume users know the rules. Smooth feedback raises confidence. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=477s)
- **Make premium feel tactile.** Revolut's onboarding uses rich visuals and smooth transitions. Charts glow under your finger. Cards flip, rotate and catch light in 3D. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=589s)
- **Final checklist.** Polish the onboarding and welcome moments. Add subtle delight such as fades, hover effects and gestures. Make charts, feedback and cards feel dynamic. [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=652s)
