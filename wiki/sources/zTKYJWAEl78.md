# I Make $5M/Year With 3 Businesses

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Tim Stoddart in Denver. Tim got sober, started a blog on Seth Godin's advice, and found SEO by accident. The blog became Sober Nation, and rehab centers asked him how he built the audience. He turned that into Stuzzi, an SEO lead generation agency. Agency cash now funds a holding company: Recovery Local (sober.com, detox.com and other domains), Copyblogger, and stakes in five or six other companies. He expects $5.2M to $5.5M in total for 2024 (2024-04).

## Key facts

- Stuzzi Internet Marketing: about $2.5M expected for 2024 (2024-04). The holding company in total: $5.2M to $5.5M (2024-04). [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)
- Recovery Local owns sober.com, yourfirststep.com, detox.com and a medication-assisted treatment site. [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)
- Copyblogger teaches internet writing, newsletters, SEO and LinkedIn writing. Tim read it daily as a beginner and later bought it. [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=194s)
- The first agency client paid $2,000/month. [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)
- One keyword started the agency: "drug rehab marketing". Then he wrote for "drug rehab SEO" and "drug rehab lead generation". [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)
- Other holdings: Moving Local and Stem Cell Authority are lead gen sites. Koala is an AI SEO writing tool. He was a founding investor in SparkLoop. Your Boulder is a local newsletter. Digital Commerce is a second SEO agency. Hey Creator is a creator studio. [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=485s), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=545s)
- Tool stack: Slack, Loom, Todoist (holding company), ClickUp (agency), HubSpot CRM, Google Drive. [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=608s)
- He hired early staff from halfway houses. The team has grown together over 10 years. [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=670s)

## Tactics

- **One keyword at a time.** Do not try to build a giant media brand. Find what people search on trends.google.com. Then write the article that best solves that problem. [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)
- **Turn traffic into leads.** Treatment centers wanted his audience, so the blog became a lead generation media outlet. [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)
- **Start with an agency.** It needs no capital and is profitable from the first client. The downside: every 2 to 4 new clients need another hire. [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)
- **Close deals in three steps.** Ask questions until the prospect states the pain, and write it down. Raise price and commitment at the start, to avoid "let me think about it". Then ask "Are you ready to get started?", mute the phone and wait. [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=419s)
- **Reinvest agency cash into cash-flowing assets.** The goal is a portfolio that does the work for him. [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)
- **Three investment filters.** He must like the founder. The business must make cash. It must be evergreen, like healthcare. [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=732s)
- **Delegate with Loom and Todoist.** Each morning he records Loom videos and assigns tasks. Afternoons are for deals: follow-ups, calls, proposals, contracts. [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=608s), [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=771s)
- **Get out of the way.** He credits The E-Myth Revisited: document processes, teach, then step back. [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=670s)
