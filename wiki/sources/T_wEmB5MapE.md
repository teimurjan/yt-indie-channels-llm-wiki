# How I Built A $1M Business From This Starbucks

## Summary

Pat Walls tells how he started Starter Story in 2017 as a side project while he worked a six-figure 9-to-5 job in New York. For months he walked past a Starbucks every day and did not start. After he read *Deep Work* by Cal Newport, he made a contract with himself to do 2 focused hours a day in that Starbucks. The blog of founder case studies grew a newsletter, went viral on Reddit, and earned its first money from sponsorships and affiliates. A large sponsorship from the CEO of Klaviyo gave him the push to quit his job. Five years later Starter Story makes over $1M a year.

## Key facts

- Starter Story makes over $1M a year (2024-01). Pat started it about 6 years earlier, in summer 2017, at age 26. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=0s)
- Time budget: 2 hours a day of deep work, after work and later from 6:00 AM before work. [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=180s)
- Month 1: he picks the name and buys starterstory.com for $12/year. He launches with a few stories, and a few hundred people visit. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)
- Month 2: dozens of stories, a newsletter, first 100 subscribers. Month 4: a story goes viral on Reddit and thousands of people join the newsletter. Month 6: first money from a newsletter sponsorship and affiliate revenue. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)
- The site has over 4,000 case studies and business breakdowns (2024-01). [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=307s)
- The CEO of Klaviyo emailed, then offered to sponsor the newsletter. The auto captions give the amount as both $12,000 and $112,000. The closing line says "$12,000". [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=373s), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=496s)
- After that payment he sat on the fence for months, then resigned. He has not had a regular job since. [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=443s), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=496s)

## Tactics

- **Turn your own questions into the product.** Pat did not find how small online businesses got started. So he researched successful founders and published what he found. [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=60s)
- **Make a written contract for a fixed focus block.** He wrote down and visualized the next day: walk in, order a coffee, set a 2-hour timer, focus. A fixed place and timer broke months of procrastination. [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=180s)
- **Build audience before revenue.** He published stories for months, added a newsletter, and treated a viral Reddit post as validation before he tried to monetize. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)
- **Copy the model of businesses a few steps ahead.** Most similar businesses made their money from sponsorships. So he sold newsletter sponsorships and added affiliate links. [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=307s)
- **Share progress in public.** The Klaviyo CEO liked both the stories and Pat sharing his own progress, and that led to the sponsorship deal. [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=373s)
