## Summary

Dan Kwon was a social media creator before he marketed apps. Joseph Choi of Consumer Club interviews him on the Superwall channel. Dan grew Conch AI, an AI writing tool that bypassed AI detection, to about $2.2M in revenue from about 220M organic views, then sold it. He also ran TikTok Shop marketing for brands like Final Boss Sour. Later he launched Arise, a Solo Leveling fitness app, and Shepherd, a Duolingo-style Bible app. His method: find a "momentum wave" and an existing "cult", then post scrappy, native-looking short videos.

## Key facts

- Conch AI hit $1M ARR in about 30 days and did $500K in one month. The school threatened to sue, and the team almost got expelled [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s).
- Conch AI lifetime: about 220M views and about $2.2M revenue, mostly organic. Paid ads were tested and dropped for low margins. Sold in early 2025 after talks with 3 or 4 buyers [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=625s), [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=647s).
- Some rivals posed as buyers only to extract information [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=647s).
- His TikTok Shop work ran about 8 months and was backed by Science Inc. Final Boss Sour ranked first in food and beverage on TikTok Shop [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=683s), [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=744s).
- Arise reached about $50K/month with 70% to 80% margins. Influencers and meme pages took $1 to $2 CPM, against about $6 to $7 RPM (2025-10) [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s).
- Shepherd: a two-week build plus a two-week growth sprint. About 7 videos got 20M views in two weeks, over 100,000 downloads and $75K MRR (2025-10) [21:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1319s), [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1355s).
- Shepherd reached about #10 in its App Store chart for a week. Its Discord has about 10,000 members [25:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1525s), [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s).

## Tactics

- **Go to an extreme.** The best UGC picks one extreme. It is so cringe that it reads like a friend's ad. Or it is so real that it looks like a Snap story. Low quality footage shot in the Snap app reads as real [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=327s), [6:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=406s).
- **Watch the "alpha ladder".** Culture starts on Snapchat, then moves to Instagram and TikTok, then marketers copy it [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=374s).
- **Use us-versus-them positioning.** Conch videos framed busywork and school bureaucracy as the enemy. Side with whichever crowd has the louder emotion [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=496s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=549s).
- **TikTok Shop pillars.** Use the "small business" angle with founder-shot content, and run clearance or flash sales. A "we made too much candy" video was copied by every creator and hit each time [14:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=846s), [15:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=908s).
- **Ride a momentum wave into an existing cult.** Arise launched near the Solo Leveling season two finale. Its carousels spoke to fans who wanted "the system" in real life. Rival apps earned from ads and lacked long onboarding and social features [17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1058s), [19:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1156s).
- **"Frankenstein" content.** Stitch viral clips on your subject, such as podcast clips of a Christian influencer, with your own hooks and a demo [24:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1458s).
- **Build on news.** Shepherd launched while Duolingo faced backlash for AI content: "God first lamb over the AI first owl." Later, users pushed back on an AI chat feature. The team made it a custom devotional feature and removed "AI" from the copy [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s), [28:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1727s).
- **Founder-led content first.** In a cold start, the founder makes the most content [16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1002s).
- **Three engines.** Post your product, stitch related viral videos, and turn influencers into full-time UGC accounts. Do not touch paid until you have at least five winning formats [31:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1900s).
- **Fix K-factor after the spike.** Shepherd paused creator work to build invite loops for prayer and accountability buddies [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1355s).
