## Summary

Ryan Thorp ran a portfolio of 30 to 35 acquired consumer apps with over 50M downloads in total, starting from the journaling app Reflectly. His team built an in-house "university" that trained about 250 regular creators. They ran a three-level TikTok ad scaling system and spent about 75% of the yearly ad budget in January. Their habit app Done became the number 1 free app in the US App Store on January 2 and 3, two years in a row. Ryan sold most of the portfolio and now builds Flo, an AI analytics and agent platform for mobile apps (2026-01).

## Key facts

- Reflectly launched in 2017 and peaked at about $3M to $4M ARR, but ad spend was about the same as revenue. [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=286s)
- Around 2020 they bought the habit app Done with 5 or 6 bundled apps. The purchase paid back in 12 to 18 months. They then acquired 30 to 35 apps from 2020 to 2025. [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=419s)
- One creator's video reached 66.9M views. Copies by 20 to 40 other creators failed, but her next videos also hit. [20:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1253s)
- Team: Ryan, one media buyer and one head of content. [18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1080s)
- Creator funnel: just over 2,000 creators on the books, about 10% regular, and 1% to 2% viral stars. About 250 worked with them regularly. [34:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2049s), [38:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2313s)
- Creator pay: flat $30 to $40 per video at the low tier, up to $80 to $100 for reliable creators. Bonuses: a flat amount at 50K to 100K views, and $200 to $1,000 for an ad that takes off. [40:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2444s), [41:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2464s)
- B2B wellness contracts for The Mindfulness App can be six figures over 3 to 5 years, with 6 to 12 months to close. [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2574s), [43:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2631s)
- They sold 80% to 90% of the apps to a buyer from gaming. [49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2952s), [49:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2982s)

## Tactics

- **Buy, do not only advertise.** An undermonetized app with better paywalls paid back faster and more reliably than ad spend, and the asset stayed theirs. Most of the year, buying apps beat buying ads. [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s), [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1216s)
- **Early UGC arbitrage.** From 2018 to 2020, local micro-influencers read scripts for Facebook ads. The raw, unpolished videos beat the polished ads of big brands. [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=518s)
- **Push a winner four ways.** Post organically on brand accounts. Boost videos that break out of 0 to 300 views into 5K to 10K. Then Spark Ad them on the creator's account. Lots of comments signal a hook. [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=693s), [12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=733s), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=812s)
- **Three-level TikTok scaling:** [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=971s), [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1031s)
  1. Level 1: each video in its own ad set at $10 to $20. Kill a failing video after $5 to $10.
  2. Level 2: winners move to ad sets of 4 or 5 videos at $100 to $500.
  3. Level 3: the top 1 or 2 become evergreen ads that take 90% of spend. Keep lower levels running while they work.
- **Replicate after a few hundred dollars.** A video that hits target metrics at a few hundred dollars a day becomes a brief for all creators. [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=788s)
- **Strict creator briefs.** Four types: lifestyle, trend, educational, testimonial. Specify trending sound, hook type, 10 to 15 seconds, about 25 to 27 cuts, and POV shots of the app. They paid someone to list trending songs each week. [22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1342s), [30:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1818s), [32:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1923s)
- **Creators, not influencers.** Best follower range: 2K to 15K or 20K. Filter with a paid test task and on-time delivery. Recruit by DM and email with a creator landing page that shows testimonials. [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2080s), [35:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2115s), [37:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2256s)
- **Familiar voices as hooks.** A background clip of Emma Chamberlain drove 50M to 60M views until a cease-and-desist letter arrived. They then tested AI celebrity voices with care. [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=812s), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=871s)
- **January seasonality for health and productivity apps.** Acquisition cost is about half in early January. [19:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1166s), [24:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1442s), [25:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1528s)
  - December 25 is dead. Test from December 26 to 28, then raise spend from December 28 to 30.
  - The peak is the first Sunday of the year. In 2026 that is January 3 or 4, because New Year's Day is a Thursday. [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1564s)
  - Each week, Sunday to Tuesday converts best. Each month, days 1 to 3 convert best. [27:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1640s)
- **B2B as the next revenue line.** Ads hit a ceiling at some point. Then wellness apps sell to companies. They prove ROI with data, such as stress tests before and after. [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2574s), [44:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2684s)
