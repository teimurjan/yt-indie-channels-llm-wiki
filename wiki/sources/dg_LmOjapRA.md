# i'm back, and EVERYTHING is changing

## Summary

Tim Gabe returns to YouTube after about a year away, past 100K subscribers. He stopped because making his Framer course was stressful and not fun. In 2023 he started a Web3 design agency instead, which peaked at $40K/month. For 2025 he moves the channel away from Figma and Framer tutorials. New videos are research-led and cover creativity, AI, the future of apps and design, and how to stay irreplaceable. He also launches a newsletter as a personal journal that feeds future videos.

## Key facts

- The channel passed 100K subscribers (2025-01). [0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=35s)
- He was off YouTube for about a year. His one upload in that time was a Framer course video recorded in 2023. [0:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=6s)
- His design agency, started in 2023 in Web3, peaked at $40K/month (2025-01). [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=68s), [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=123s)
- The first newsletter issue is titled "My plan to survive in 2025 as a creative". [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=420s)

## Tactics

- **Drop the work you do not enjoy.** Building a software course was "taxing" and "stressful". So he left the course business. He does not rule out more courses later. [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=68s), [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=123s)
- **Go back to client work in your niche.** He returned to hands-on design through an agency in Web3, where he has a background. [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=68s), [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=123s)
- **Zoom out from tool tutorials.** New content is an "open journal": he researches topics and reports his findings. Formats stay open, from talking head and occasional tutorials to podcasts and vlogs. [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=173s), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=313s)
- **Cover AI's effect on jobs.** He expects AI to change design, marketing, bookkeeping and most digital work, and to need fewer people. He plans to build with AI himself and share how he stays irreplaceable. [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=252s)
- **Newsletter first, video later.** The newsletter gets research and deep dives early, plus exclusive pieces. It covers AI agents for workflows, the future of apps and his agency. Some issues become videos. [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=313s), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=360s)
