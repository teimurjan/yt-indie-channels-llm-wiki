# The NEW Short Form Strategy Dominating For App Growth

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Yiannis, a machine learning scientist who now runs organic TikTok for consumer apps. Yiannis does not hire creators. He runs "reposters": warmed-up accounts that post content his team makes centrally. He finds formats by studying outlier TikToks in a niche and splits each video into format (the structure) and content (the words and subject). His key lesson: going viral is not the same as converting. A few words of text that create intent decide whether views turn into downloads. He uses SpyTok to match viral posts to App Store rank jumps, and he validates a format before he builds the app.

## Key facts

- Last year: about 300M views and, he estimates, 3M to 5M downloads (2026-05). Clients rarely share hybrid results, so attribution is unclear. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=0s), [56:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3415s)
- He opens 50 to 100 new accounts per week (2026-05). [52:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3120s)
- Monthly views: 10M to 15M in the intro, "10 to 50 million" later in the episode. This year so far: about 50M views, mostly tests (2026-05). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=0s), [56:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3415s)
- He started by posting 50 to 100 times by hand on his own French account. [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=171s)
- His first reposter fleet was for a dating app. A script ran fake conversations on a Tinder messaging clone and screen-recorded them. [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=252s), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=367s)
- One comment-bait post asked for "unhinged" side hustles to save $10K. It got 10M views, 1M likes and 5.4K comments. [11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=690s)
- Joseph says each reposter video costs about $20 to make. [18:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1082s)
- SpyTok's outlier score is a post's views divided by the account's median views. One example was 200x or more. [49:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2975s)

## Tactics

- **Keep the creative in-house.** UGC teams hand the creative to 7 to 10 creators. Yiannis makes the content and has reposters only warm up accounts and post. New accounts are easy to spin up and perform about the same. [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=315s), [22:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1361s)
- **Brief ambassadors 80/20.** With creators, 80% of the post follows a strict structure. The other 20% is their own spin. [24:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1483s)
- **Learn the platform language.** Each niche has visual rules. Finance news uses red backgrounds and screenshot overlays. Savings goals use a face hook. [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=532s)
- **Separate format from content.** A hook-plus-demo structure transfers to any market. A finance-news format or a well-known face does not. [29:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1742s), [31:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1905s)
- **Pick a "gem".** A gem is a format that goes viral on 5 to 20 posts across accounts. It must be easy to mass-produce with AI or people. Skits and street interviews, like Cheater Buster's, have too many variables. [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1633s), [33:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1997s)
- **Use comment bait to find the audience.** A viral bait post targets the right people. Later posts from that account then get a ripple of views and can sell the solution. [12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=751s), [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=826s)
- **Build intent in the words.** A scratch-map video with "post-breakup activity" got millions of views and no downloads. The same video with "procrastinating for my exams" moved the app up the App Store in several countries. [37:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2221s), [37:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2277s)
- **Curate sounds and length.** About 20% to 30% of good sounds are years old. Lengths: 7 to 9 seconds for quick hooks. 9 to 15 seconds for an app that needs explanation. About 30 seconds for a full skit. Health and finance need longer content. [40:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2459s), [43:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2580s)
- **Watch rank movement outside the US.** Organic hits show up first in other English-speaking App Stores. A rank jump in many countries at once points to organic, not paid. [47:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2859s)
- **Validate the format before the app.** Find angles that already convert in your category, such as claim finders in finance. Go viral with one account a few times, then build. [53:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3182s), [54:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3243s)
- **Post yourself first.** A founder or core team member must do the research and posting before managing others. [54:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3295s)
