## Summary

Ayush is the marketing co-founder of Elephas, a Mac AI assistant. It indexes local files, PDFs and Apple Notes into "super brains" that users can chat with. A two-person team grew it to about $150K/year in 3 years in two phases. First, Reddit posts took it from $0 to $3K MRR in 6 months. Then SEO took it to about $12K/month. He gives a repeatable playbook for each phase. Elephas sells monthly, annual and lifetime plans, because Mac users expect a one-time payment.

## Tactics

- **Reddit post format.** Lead with a short video demo inside a problem-and-solution story: "My friend had this problem, so I built this. Here is how it works. Try it free for 30 days. I want feedback." Reddit users dislike being sold to but respect clear reasons. One such post shaped the super brain feature, which became the main product ([6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=399s), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=459s)).
- **Reddit playbook** ([8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=499s), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=544s)):
  1. List at least 15 subreddits where the ideal customer spends time. Use the open-source Map of Reddit. Subreddits with over 5,000 members are fine. Small ones have simpler rules and more forgiving mods.
  2. Pick one feature or pain point. Make a short demo and add a UTM-tagged link with a 30-day free trial.
  3. Post in one subreddit per day, never all on the same day. Adjust the copy each time.
- **Expect bans.** Keep backup accounts and develop a thick skin ([10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=611s)).
- **Measure Reddit by feedback.** A normal post gets 30-50 upvotes and a viral one 300-400. The real value is the honest comments ([10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=639s)).
- **Find SEO by accident, then do it on purpose.** A support article on creating OpenAI API keys ranked #1 for 6-8 months. They then wrote listicles such as "18 best ChatGPT Mac apps", which ranked #1 and converted. AI answer engines now cite these articles and send ready-to-buy traffic ([11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=691s), [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=762s)).
- **SEO playbook.** Know your position first (ChatGPT apps for Mac). In Ahrefs, filter for keyword difficulty under 20 and volume over 500. Buy one month and use the 500 credits to list 30-50 topics. Write 2-3 posts a week and grow to 6-7. Use AI for research and outlines, and add new information of your own ([13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=826s), [14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=869s), [15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=931s)).
