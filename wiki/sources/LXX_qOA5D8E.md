# I Studied 500+ Gamified Apps (Here's What Actually Works)

## Summary

Tim Gabe reviews research and case studies on gamification in mobile apps. He sorts them into patterns. Points, badges and leaderboards (PBL) are the most common mechanics and also the best-documented failures. The patterns that work are local, winnable competition (Strava), variable rewards (Gameblazers), completion drive (Apple Watch rings) and competence feedback (Peloton). Too many mechanics cut engagement, and streaks turn into obligation over time. His agency, Zipsnap, designed Gameblazers and works with Freecash.

## Key facts

- LinkedIn retired its Community Top Voice gold badges in 2024. Badge chasers posted quantity over quality. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=77s)
- Foursquare dropped mayorships and badges in 2014. Google News also dropped badges. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=77s), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=126s)
- Strava: 180M registered users, about 1 hour of activity for every 2 minutes in the app. Clubs grew 59% in 2024, and users gave 14 billion kudos in 2025. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=205s)
- A 2025 Frontiers in Psychology study found an S-curve: engagement rises with more features up to a point, then falls. In a Habitica study, 100% of participants had counterproductive effects. [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=266s), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=320s)
- A 2023 study of almost 2,500 adolescents linked Snapchat streaks to FOMO and problematic phone use. Nevada sued Snapchat in 2024. The EU Digital Fairness Act targets addictive streaks, with a proposal expected in late 2026. [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=362s)
- Apple Watch rings produced a 49.5% behavior change across 160,000 people. Regular ring closers are 48% less likely to sleep poorly. [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=556s)
- Peloton members who use social and output features work out 15% more often. Freecash paid out over €300M (2026-05). [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=509s), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=646s)

## Tactics

- **Build the game, not the scoreboard.** PBL alone does not motivate behavior. [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=126s)
- **Make competition small and winnable.** Strava segments are thousands of local leaderboards, sorted by age and gender. Kudos raise future run frequency. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=205s)
- **Stop before the peak of the S-curve.** Do not stack streaks, points, badges, challenges and leaderboards together. [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=320s)
- **Give streaks agency.** Duolingo lets users pick a goal level and buy a streak freeze. A streak with no pause or exit is the version that regulators watch. [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=410s)
- **Use variable reward size.** Gameblazers pack opening runs anticipation, then a card-by-card reveal, then celebration with glow and haptics. One reward becomes five. [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=458s), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=509s)
- **Use completion drive.** A 90% ring is an open loop that the brain wants to close (the Gestalt principle of closure). [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=556s)
- **Engineer mastery.** A 2024 Springer Nature meta-analysis found gamification does little for competence. Peloton personal records, Chess.com Elo and Garmin readiness scores show real progress. [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=646s), [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=705s)

## Quotes

- Quoting Yu-kai Chou: "PBL is the scoreboard of a game, not the game itself." [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=126s)
- "Streaks run on fear of losing what you built. Variable rewards run on the pull towards what's next." [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=509s)
- "Build mechanics that signal you got better at the actual thing, not mechanics that signal you opened the app a lot." [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=705s)
