# The Secret Trick For Amazing Animations in Figma

## Summary

Tim Gabe explains spring animations in Figma prototypes and compares them with Bézier curves. Bézier curves are easy to use but can feel generic and lifeless. Springs follow real-world physics and feel alive, but their three settings are harder to grasp. He defines stiffness, mass and damping. He then gives three presets: aggressive, smooth and fun.

## Key facts

- Find springs under Prototype, then Smart Animate, then "Custom spring" at the bottom of the easing list ([1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=117s)).
- Spring settings: stiffness, damping and mass ([1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=117s)).
- Custom Bézier lets you set only how an object starts and ends its motion. One handle shapes the start and one shapes the end ([0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=22s)).
- The video links a Figma file with the examples.

## Tactics

- **Bézier pros and cons.** Handles are easy to understand. Animations are hard to perfect, can stop oddly, and often feel lifeless ([1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=84s)).
- **Spring pros and cons.** Springs look smoother and more alive. They are harder to learn ([2:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=160s)).
- **Stiffness** sets the number of bounces. Drag the graph on the horizontal axis. Drag right for less stiffness and left for more ([2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=178s)).
- **Mass** sets the speed and the bounce height. High mass is like a container dropped from a mountain. Low mass is like a feather. More mass makes the animation longer ([3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=221s), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=338s)).
- **Damping** sets how springy it is. More damping means less spring ([3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=221s), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=284s)).
- **Three presets** ([4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=293s), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bdR3p6PvYQQ&t=338s)):
  - Aggressive: fairly stiff, little damping, low mass, short timing. It enters fast and ends fast.
  - Smooth: lower stiffness, more damping, more mass.
  - Fun: high stiffness, some damping, high mass. Very bouncy, a "clown feeling".
