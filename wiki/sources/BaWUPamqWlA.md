# How I Used Reddit to Hit $17K MRR (With ZERO Audience)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Diego, the solo founder of App Alchemy. App Alchemy is an AI tool that makes mobile app designs, "like Cursor but for designing mobile apps". Diego had fewer than 100 followers on X and no other audience. He reached about $17K/month in 4 months with Reddit posts only and $0 spent on marketing. He shares a five-step Reddit playbook, his stack, and his costs.

## Key facts

- About $17K/month revenue, 4 months after launch (2025-07). [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=55s)
- Over 1 million views on the product from Reddit, with $0 spent on marketing (2025-07). [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=55s)
- More than 1K paying customers, about 20K signups from Reddit, and about 20K visits per month (2025-07). [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=248s)
- At his first message to Pat, Diego had about 70 followers on X. At recording, he had fewer than 100 (2025-07). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=0s), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=266s)
- The idea came from his own problem. An Upwork designer charged hundreds of dollars and charged extra for each small change. [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=90s)
- He built the MVP in about 2 weeks. His first $1K/month came only from Reddit. [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=116s)
- Stack: Cursor, Next.js, React and Firebase for auth and database. [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=613s)
- Costs: Cursor $20/month, Vercel hosting about $40/month, AI API backends about $2.5K/month. Margins are about 70% (2025-07). [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=633s)

## Tactics

- **Build with popular tools.** AI writes better code for the most-used libraries. UI component libraries such as Chakra UI or Ant Design make prototypes faster and nicer. [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=143s)
- **Why Reddit.** Reach is free, and subreddits give precise targets for almost any niche. [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=266s)
- **Step 1: warm up your account.** Use Reddit daily. You learn which posts do well, and Reddit filters out posts from new accounts. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=326s)
- **Step 2: list many subreddits.** Volume matters. Open the Reddit Ads campaign builder and search communities in the targeting section. You do not pay for ads. A search for "no code" returns many related communities. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=326s), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=372s)
- **Step 3: write a value-first post.** Start with a catchy headline that does not announce the product. Give value first. His best post was a case study of a successful mobile app that he found on Twitter. Put the product link at the end. A direct link gets more traffic than a name only. [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=372s), [7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=433s)
- **Plug a complementary product too.** Strict subreddits ban self-promotion. Diego mentions Cursor next to his design tool, so the post looks less like an ad. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=496s)
- **Avoid the launch-post pattern.** A post with the product in the headline, a feature list and a Discord link got little engagement. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=496s)
- **Step 4: cross-post at volume.** Post the same piece to many subreddits. 10 posts at about 10K views each give about 100K views, even without a front-page hit. [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=557s)
- **Step 5: repeat and vary.** Test videos, case studies and other formats. Post 2 to 3 times per week. More than that risks a ban. [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=557s)
- **Pick a growing market.** Vibe coding tools grow. Tools for newspaper owners shrink. Spend early time on marketing, not on features no one asked for. Use speed as a solo founder. [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=656s)
