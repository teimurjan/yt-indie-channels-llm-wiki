## Summary

Andre Haykal Jr. built ListKit, a B2B lead-list SaaS for cold email. It reached $1M ARR 87 days after launch and was near $200K MRR in June 2024. He first sold the same lead lists as a productized service, fulfilled by hand behind a SaaS-like front end. That service gave him cash flow and market research. Its profits then went to developers who failed to ship, and he nearly quit. A coaching student, Oliver, joined as co-founder and shipped the app in six months. Growth came from cold email with a free-leads offer, then ads, affiliates, content and his existing agency and coaching audience.

## Key facts

- ListKit revenue: $1M ARR within 87 days of launch, about to pass $200K MRR (2024-06). The intro calls it a $2.4M company. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)
- Customers: 1,000 paying in the first 6 months, over 1,500 after about a year (2024-06). [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s)
- Pricing: from $97/month, up to a few hundred for enterprise. Credit-based pricing. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=462s)
- Costs: team over $100K/month. Total cost with ads and acquisition is $150K to $175K/month (2024-06). [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)
- Team: 5 co-founders (CEO, CTO, head of sales, two marketing/ads leads), about 40 people in total, 20 in support and sales. [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s), [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=653s)
- Prior businesses: a lead generation agency that reached $100K/month, then Client Ascension, a coaching program built with Daniel Fazio. [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=178s)
- The productized service made $105K to $110K/month. All of it went into product development that never shipped. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=357s)

## Tactics

- **Productized service before SaaS.** Customers order 100 to 10,000 leads through a form. The team fulfills the order by hand within 24 hours. This needs no development budget, proves demand, and builds cash flow. [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=227s), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=289s)
- **Launch to a warm list.** He told the full story on Twitter, including the failure, and asked existing customers what they wanted. The launch was a few emails, live calls and tweets. [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=415s)
- **Fix one flaw of a proven competitor.** Apollo leads were not verified, so ListKit copied the model and sold verified leads. [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=702s)
- **Price from cost, then competitors.** He starts from cost, checks competitor prices, and asks their customers what they like and dislike about them. [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=462s)
- **Cold email with a lead magnet.** Cold email works for high-ticket offers. For a low-ticket tool he offered 50 free leads to anyone who replied. Competitors now copy this. [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=498s)
- **Ads with a high-ticket setup offer.** He sells a done-for-you cold email setup next to the tool. Above $300 to $400/day in spend, creatives fatigue, so he tests about 20 a week: 10 statics and 10 videos. [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=561s)
- **No free trial.** A money-back guarantee replaces it. Trials attract "freebie seekers". He says trials fit tools that monetize users through ads or data. [10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=619s)
- **Offshore team.** Developers are in Ukraine and support staff in Lebanon. They cost $1,000 to $2,000/month, against $5,000 to $8,000/month for a US hire. [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=653s)
- **Onboarding call for every customer.** He asks why each customer signed up and whether they get that result. He uses the answers to cut churn. [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=872s)
- **Complementary co-founders.** Marketing, sales, technology and a team leader. Check that values and vision align. [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=750s), [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=811s)
