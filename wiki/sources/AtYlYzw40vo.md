# This App Makes $2M/Year With This Viral TikTok Strategy

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Jay, CEO of Bloom, a Gen Z "learn to invest" app that mixes Robinhood-style investing with Duolingo-style lessons. TikTok paid ads stopped paying back in late 2023, so Bloom moved to organic TikTok in 2024. It runs AI avatar accounts, micro-creators on retainer plus CPM bonuses, and a comment team. Viral organic videos then become paid ads. Jay also covers Bloom's onboarding, the free-stock hook, and a $10K starter plan.

## Key facts

- Bloom does "millions of dollars in ARR" and has over 1M active users (2025-07). The title says $2M/year. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=0s)
- Bloom scaled TikTok paid ads in 2023. CAC got worse from mid-2023 and fell off by the end of 2023. [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)
- AI content: an agency plus an in-house team run 5 to 10 grown accounts at about 2 posts per account per day. Winners get reposted to about 20 accounts. Bloom later scaled to 20 accounts with HeyGen. [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s), [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2085s)
- Creator pay: a $1,000/month flat rate, or a $500 retainer for 60 videos with a higher CPM. CPM bonuses run $2 to $4 per 1,000 views, with a cap of $1,000 per video at about 1M+ views (2025-07). [7:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=470s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=583s)
- Bonuses start at 10K views for new accounts and at 100K for creators with 5K to 10K followers. [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=583s)
- The signup offer is "up to $75" in stock. Users actually get about $2 to $4 of free stock and earn the rest through games and loot boxes. Bloom tested $40, $60 and $75 (2025-07). [22:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1331s), [23:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1392s)
- Content formats last about 3 to 6 months. The billionaire-nanny story ran for about 6 months. [27:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1648s)

## Tactics

- **A broad hook, a deep product.** "How billionaires raise their kids" videos lead into "they start investing early". Retention holds because the app teaches long-term investing. [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=159s), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=213s)
- **Indirect CTA plus a comment team.** Videos tell a story and show the screen. Then a comment team names the app, because "download Bloom" reads as an ad. [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=355s)
- **Pick creators by craft, not reach.** Lauren from Pebble finds small creators with good angles, light and trends. Creators open fresh accounts, warm them up, and post 2 videos a day across 1 to 2 accounts. [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=355s), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=522s)
- **Three-phase creator pay.** Pure CPM made new creators quit early. A small retainer keeps them going. Bonuses at 10K to 50K views reward effort. Over 100K views, bonuses reward conversion. [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=650s), [11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=711s)
- **Organic winners become ads.** After a viral video fades, Bloom runs it as a paid ad aimed at the right age and country. This flattens the peaks and valleys of organic. Add a last 15 seconds with the app and a soft CTA ("download now to flex on your friends"). [14:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=876s), [17:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1078s)
- **Native-looking ads.** A Reddit-story "underrated iPhone apps" video reads as organic, with a small disclaimer at the end. [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=937s), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1018s)
- **Onboarding order: urgency, features, trust.** Start with a compound-growth chart: a start at 18 gives $1M at 65, and a start at 30 gives about $340K. Then animate features with changing colors. End with press logos, SIPC, YC and reviews. [19:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1145s), [20:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1207s), [21:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1267s)
- **Split dense screens into story taps.** One wordy screen became about 5 tap-through screens like Instagram stories. Removing the "get started" button also helped. Signups from download to personal info went up. [24:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1488s), [26:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1596s)
- **Borrow a movement's angle.** Bible, therapy and self-care narratives are rising. Quittr markets to Christians without being a Christian app. Bloom plans to test a Proverbs 13:11 angle. [30:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1848s), [32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1927s)
- **A $10K starter plan.** Make AI video (Veo 2 or Veo 3) on in-house accounts. Recruit 2 to 3 creators through Sideshift or Handshake, then grow to 5. Use SpyTok to find formats. Spend it over the first 2 to 3 months. [37:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2225s), [41:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2512s)
- **AI B-roll for lifestyle.** Use AI views of houses and cars with overlay text such as "if you invested in Netflix five years ago". [39:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2386s)
- **Give it 90 days.** He calls 90 days the minimum test period for organic video. [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2574s)
