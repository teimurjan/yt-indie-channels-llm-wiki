# He Went From Struggling to Make $1 From His App to Turning Down $1M

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews solo founder Jack Friks about four products. Curiosity Quench, a hobby app against doomscrolling, took 8 months to make money and then peaked at $15K in one month. He got there by copying one viral TikTok slideshow format 400 times. His social scheduler Post Bridge got a $1M offer from Sahil of Gumroad, which he turned down. He built the food diary Doof to $1K in 30 days. His couples app Lovely got 100K downloads in 10 weeks, from Twitter virality and then from a TikTok format that shows the app in use. That format converts about 20x better than slideshows. His method is a daily 15-minute scroll to find formats that already work in the niche.

## Key facts

- Curiosity Quench: 1,000 users in the first 2 months. Then 8 months of 50 to 60 downloads a day before real revenue. It peaked at $15K in one month (September 2024) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=196s), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=529s), [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=579s).
- Paywall effect: $500 to $1K/month for about 5 months with a confusing credit "shop". After a Superwall template paywall with a free trial, revenue grew to $2K to $4K/month [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=946s), [16:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1007s).
- Post Bridge: Sahil of Gumroad offered $1M on Twitter (around April 2024). It made about $12K/month then and grew 30 to 40% a month. It made about $20K MRR at recording (2025-12) [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=718s), [13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=781s), [13:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=824s).
- Doof: $1K in revenue in its first 30 days. One meme slideshow got 5M views [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s), [20:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1256s).
- Lovely: Russian news coverage brought 10K downloads in one day. A user tweet showed the love-note widget used as a grocery list and got 16M views. His quote tweet of it brought 40K downloads overnight [27:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1627s), [28:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1689s).
- Lovely totals: 102K users, about $10K proceeds and about $4.2K MRR, against a goal of $5K MRR in 10 weeks (2025-12) [31:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1875s).
- Twitter and the news added about $2K to $3K MRR. He switched from a hard paywall to freemium after bad reviews [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1730s).

## Tactics

- **Scroll to find formats.** He sets a 15-minute timer, scrolls his niche and competitors, and saves candidates. Later he spends 30 to 60 minutes making one video a day [35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=2142s).
- **Repeat a winner.** He found a 4-picture "most attractive hobbies" post with 1M views and swapped in his app's widgets. He made it about 400 times and pinned a download CTA in the comments. Angry commenters pushed the pinned comment up [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=304s), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=386s).
- **Views against conversion.** Meme slideshows are "silver": millions of views but few downloads. An 8 to 10 second clip of the app in use is "gold" and converts about 20x better. He copied a competitor's whiteboard format, built the feature in one day and posted the next day [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s), [30:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1813s).
- **Do not make it look like an ad.** Organic videos must not read as ads. So the app goes in the caption or in a few slides [20:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1256s).
- **Story-driven launch tweet.** He announced a public $0 to $5K MRR goal and quote-tweeted it about 6 times with updates [26:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1571s).
- **Use paywall standards.** Templates, a free trial and testing of headers, copy and gated features made the app look legitimate [17:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1068s), [19:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1141s).
- **Couples apps monetize well.** The partner pays, and shared use drives word of mouth [42:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=2571s).
- **Scale volume, not ads.** He spent about $1K to hire people who run 20 to 40 accounts that post the winning format [46:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=2793s).
