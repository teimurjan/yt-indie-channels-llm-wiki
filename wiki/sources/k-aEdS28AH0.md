# How I Work: $40K/Month Solo App Builder

## Summary

Jack Friks, a bootstrapped solo founder, describes how he works. He makes $40K/month. He works in sprints: 1 to 2 hours a day in calm weeks and 8 to 10 hours a day around a launch. Every morning starts with customer support, because fast replies kept users and grew his products. He builds each product for his own need. Then the market judges the idea and its framing. He stops at dinner and protects sleep.

## Key facts

- He makes $40K/month as a solopreneur (2026-08). Some weeks he works 4 hours in total, other weeks 40. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=0s)
- His first mobile app seemed stuck at $3K/month. About 2.5 years before the video, he broke down in tears on a walk. Two months later the app hit a peak month of $15K. [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=218s)
- He had headaches and anxiety for about 2 years. The anxiety eased once MRR passed a few thousand a month and a year of savings built up. [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=218s)
- He built Post Bridge, a social scheduling tool, to grow his own app. It became 10x to 100x bigger than the app, so he dropped the app. [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=284s)
- He later built another mobile app as his own case study for Post Bridge. [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=284s)
- Daily shape: wake at 7 or 8, workout, coffee, customer support, one project, stop at about 5 p.m., bed at about 11 p.m. [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=420s), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=481s)

## Tactics

- **Support first, every day.** It is his least favorite task, so he does it first. Fast replies and fast fixes made paying users happier and kept them around. [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s)
- **One fixed workspace.** Cafes do not work for him. A clean desk and chair in the same place help him focus. [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=82s)
- **Work in sprints, not fixed days.** Launch weeks get 10-hour days. Quiet months get 1 to 2 hours a day. [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=107s)
- **One project per day.** Besides support, he only works on one thing a day. He picks what excites him most. [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=166s)
- **Build for your own need.** Each product solved a problem he had. Copying a hit in someone else's niche kills the fun, and that shows in the results. [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=284s), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=333s)
- **Let the market judge the idea.** He puts out a landing page or a TikTok. If 1,000 people visit and nobody buys, the idea or its framing is weak. [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=333s)
- **Ask how to frame the value.** A good product saves time, saves money or meets another basic desire. He also must want to use it himself. [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=393s)
- **Set a stop time.** Two years of laptop time during dinner hurt his relationship. Now dinner is the hard stop. [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=420s)
- **Sleep 7 to 8 hours.** Most of all before a heavy workday. [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=481s)
- **Keep a folder of hope.** He saved inspiring images in one folder for hard days. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=531s)
