# Zero to $40K/Month With One Marketing Channel (No Social Media)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Miquel Palet ("Mickey"), a Spanish founder who built Late, a social media API for developers. Late wraps the official Facebook, Instagram, Twitter and LinkedIn APIs into one API with no limits. It reached $40K MRR in about 7 months with Google as its only channel. SEO on bottom-of-funnel keywords brings about $8K MRR, and Google Search Ads on the same keywords bring about $15K MRR. Mickey gives a five-step Google playbook. Pat and producer Gus then argue that informational SEO is dying, but intent-based search still works.

## Tactics

- **Capture intent, do not create it.** Social media is noisy. People who search "social media scheduling API" have about 10x the purchase intent. SEO and search ads have predictable unit economics. [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s)
- **One page, two jobs.** Write useful content for each keyword. Pages that rank get free traffic. Run ads on pages that do not rank. [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=381s)
- **Organic recipe.** Use Ahrefs to find bottom-of-funnel keywords with 300 to 800 monthly searches and low competition. Write in-depth guides, such as "how to schedule Instagram posts automatically." Keep the site fast and clean, and buy or earn backlinks. Track with Ahrefs and Google Search Console. [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=411s)
- **Paid recipe.** Bid on the same commercial keywords. Give each ad group its own landing page, such as an Instagram API page. Use automated bidding with a target CPA. Write ad copy on value, such as "30 days of social media in 1 hour." A/B test headlines, descriptions and pages all the time. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s)
- **Five-step playbook for a new SaaS:**
  1. List core high-intent keywords in a sheet with volume and difficulty. Aim to rank in 3 to 6 months. [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=607s)
  2. Publish 10 to 15 high-quality pieces in week one: blogs, landing pages or free tools. Add big sign-up CTAs and internal links. [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=668s)
  3. Launch Google Ads groups on the same keywords, with 3 to 5 copy variants each. [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=668s)
  4. Pause weak keywords and double down on high-ROI ones. Raise the budget each month while ROAS holds. [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=728s)
  5. Track CAC, ROAS, revenue and traffic per channel. Keep CAC at 20% to 40% of LTV and under 30% of first-year revenue. [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=789s)
- **One channel only.** Pat and Mickey agree: go all in on the first channel that works. [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=344s), [15:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=935s)
- **Docs are the product.** For a developer API, the team spends the most time on clear API docs. [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=882s)
- **SEO is not dead.** Pat says informational queries move to ChatGPT. Intent queries like "social media scheduler" still convert on Google. [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=1070s)
