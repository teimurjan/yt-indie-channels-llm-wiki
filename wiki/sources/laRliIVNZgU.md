# I Quit YouTube, Built a $1M App Without My Audience

## Summary

Sebastian Ghiorghiu grew a YouTube channel to nearly 1M subscribers with flashy money content. He felt out of line with that persona, stopped posting, and built a SaaS app, Atlas (helloatlas.io), without promoting it to his fans. He bought out his non-technical co-founder at about $13K to $14K MRR and grew to $250K/month in recurring revenue. Growth came from YouTube creator videos, short-form clips and paid ads. He compares an audience business with a product business.

## Key facts

- Background: dropshipping and ecommerce, then YouTube, then SaaS. [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)
- His viral "Lamborghini" clip got about 100M impressions on Twitter alone, plus a Vox piece and 2 Fox interviews. [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=212s)
- Team of 10+ people with "multi six figures a month" in recurring revenue (2025-09). [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)
- He bought out his co-founder on January 6, 2025, at about $13K to $14K MRR. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)
- He reached $250K/month in recurring revenue in June 2025, about 9 months from zero. [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s), [11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=688s)
- YouTube videos, short-form content and paid ads bring 60% to 65% of traffic (2025-09). Affiliates, ChatGPT answers, SEO and App Store search bring the rest. [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=756s)

> Conflict: the video description says the app "makes $250K/year". In the transcript Sebastian says $250K a month in recurring revenue. [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s)

## Tactics

- **Build in the wild.** He expected that an app sold only to his fans fails against the competition. Influencer SaaS products "come and go". He wanted users to adopt and share it on their own. [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=284s)
- **Hire for quality.** Neither founder knew how to code, and many early developer hires were weak. Each good hire lifted the whole team. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)
- **Write the target down.** On the buyout day he wrote a revenue target and the steps to reach $250K/month. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)
- **Audience still helps behind the scenes.** He did not promote the app on his channel, but his name made outreach and hiring easier. [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=456s)
- **Grow in stages.** First, share with a few people and Discord communities and listen to users, which brought about $10K/month. Next, pay YouTube creators to make videos. Then copy what works in short-form until videos go viral. Last, add paid ads. [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)
- **Content flywheel.** Cut each creator video into shorts and into ads. People then see the brand everywhere. [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)
- **Show up in ChatGPT answers.** He says the right content gets the app listed in ChatGPT results. [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=756s)
- **Choose the long game.** At $10K/month from a business and $10K to $20K/month from an audience, pick the business. Do not chase sponsors and info products. [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=639s)
