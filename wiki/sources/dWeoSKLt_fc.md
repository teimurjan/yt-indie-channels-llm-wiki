## Summary

Evan Yadegari is 14. He built Locked, a gamified habit and focus app, and grew it to $14K/month about 4 months after launch. He used one channel only: paid posts by short-form influencers on TikTok and Instagram. He buys views at a CPM below his RPM, so each deal makes money. The app took about 6 weeks from idea to App Store, built with Figma, Xcode and Claude Code.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $14K/month about 4 months after launch (2026-04) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=0s).
- Paywall: $40/year with a 3-day free trial, or $7/week. A transaction-abandon paywall offers $20/year to people who close the first paywall [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s).
- Product: choose a character in onboarding, then earn XP, levels and badges and climb leaderboards. Each task gives 100 XP [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=557s).
- Prior apps: Problem Pal peaked at about $2K/month and was sold. Clear AI took a few weeks to build and went nowhere [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=159s).
- Before apps: Roblox games at age 9, then drop shipping, reselling, clipping and vending machines. All failed. His older brother Zach pushed him into apps [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=159s).
- Build: 2 weeks of design in Figma, then Xcode with YouTube tutorials and Claude Code. About 1.5 months from idea to live [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=216s).
- Unit economics: RPM of about $2 to $3 per 1,000 views. Target creator CPM of $1 to $1.50 (2026-04) [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=245s).
- Best video: Jeremiah Jones, posted around 2026-02-06. Daily revenue went from about $300 to $600 to $1,000. About 1M views across platforms, 1,800 downloads and about $3K revenue. Cost: about $800 with a 600K minimum view clause (2026-02) [5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=330s).
- Stack: Figma, Claude Code ($200/month plan), Superwall for paywall A/B tests (1% of revenue), Supabase (free tier) [10:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=601s).

## Tactics

- **Know RPM before you buy.** Close creators only at a CPM below your revenue per 1,000 views [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=245s).
- **Pick creators by audience.** He checks for high-quality comments, a topic that matches the app, and a natural fit. The app must show in the first 15 seconds [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=283s).
- **Outreach script.** Search a niche term on Instagram or TikTok. Open creator accounts and send the same DM each time, starting with "paid promo?". Then get them on a call [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=439s).
- **Four deal types** [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=486s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=511s):
  1. Flat rate, for example $500 per reel or $1,200 for four reels. Use only with creators who get views every time.
  2. CPM, $1 or $2 per 1,000 views, always with a cap such as $500.
  3. Minimum view clause (MVC), for example $500 for 500K views. This is his default. A creator who misses the MVC posts more videos until they hit it.
  4. Bonus, for example $500 per video plus $500 more at 1M views.
- **Launch the deal.** Send video rules and a contract with a 1-week deadline. Keep working with creators who drive revenue [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=511s).
- **Second-chance paywall.** A discounted yearly offer catches users who dismiss the main paywall [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s).
- **Gamify a crowded category.** Habit trackers are common. XP, leaderboards and characters make Locked different [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=207s). Pat Walls notes that habit trackers also win through App Store optimization [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=684s).
