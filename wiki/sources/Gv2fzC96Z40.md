# The Underdog: From His Parent's Basement to $25M

## Summary

Pat Walls tells the story of David Park, co-founder of Jenni AI, an AI writing assistant. After about nine failed startups with his co-founder Henry, David found the idea in a GPT-2 output. He cold-called agencies from his parents' house and got stuck at $2K/month until he changed how he talked to users. A $100K check from Jason Calacanis, a viral Twitter thread and TikTok content then took Jenni past $3M ARR. A thyroid cancer diagnosis almost made him sell, but he chose to keep going.

## Key facts

- David started his first company, a clothing brand, at 16. He was 27 at filming, with almost a decade of failures (2024-01). [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=59s), [11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=682s)
- He dropped out of college. He and Henry ran 9 or 10 failed startups together before Jenni. [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=123s)
- Henry coded and David sold. The first target was agencies that write content. [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)
- After GPT-3 came out in 2020, Jenni plateaued at about $2K/month in revenue. [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)
- A scout for Jason Calacanis heard David on a small podcast. That led to a $100K check. The founders moved to Malaysia to cut burn by about a quarter. [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)
- A viral Twitter thread by Zain Kahn included Jenni. MRR went from about $2K to $10K in one month. [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=435s)
- A second wave from TikTok and Instagram Reels took MRR to $50K to $80K, close to $1M/year. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=496s)
- Growth stalled while David was out for cancer surgery. He shopped Jenni to buyers for "a quick few million." [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=578s), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=642s)
- Six to seven months later, Jenni passed $3M ARR. David puts its worth at $10M to $30M (2024-01). The title cites a $25M valuation. [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=743s)

## Tactics

- **Cold-call to the first dollar.** David called agencies 8 hours a day for weeks. About 99% hung up or said no. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=0s), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)
- **Ask users the hard questions.** Do not ask why they like your product. Ask what they dislike, what they love about rivals, and how Jenni fits their workflow. Ask them to share a screen and write an article with you. This showed that users wanted a friendly AI-assisted writing journey. [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)
- **Say yes to small podcasts.** A podcast with few listeners reached the scout who brought the first check. [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)
- **Stretch the runway.** They moved to a cheaper country right after the raise. [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)
- **Turn luck into a channel.** Virality comes and goes. After the Twitter spike, David hired a college friend to run TikTok and Instagram Reels. [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=435s)
- **Explore, then deploy capital.** Up to $1M they tested many paths. From $1M to $3M they put money into the ones that worked. [11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=682s)
- **Scale a TikTok format.** Start an account and test many creative formats. Turn the winner into a series, then multiply accounts. Pay creators a base monthly salary plus bonuses for views or conversions. [11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=682s)
