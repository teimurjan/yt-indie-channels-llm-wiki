## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Yev, head of growth at Sizzle AI, an edtech learning app, and a former data scientist. Yev also runs over 10 faceless YouTube Shorts channels with billions of views. His core idea is "tipping points": find the funnel step with the biggest drop-off and fix it for 2x to 3x gains. He uses paid ads to amplify the top organic videos, then scales winners with headline, country and in-app variations. For a new app he suggests spreading a small budget across as many creatives as possible to find outliers fast.

## Key facts

- Fixing a funnel step with over 50% drop-off gave 2x to 3x growth moments. Feature work gave only about 10% gains. [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=156s)
- One winning TikTok had 79K likes. It came from 20 to 30 organic videos, and it drove hundreds of purchases as a paid ad (2025-06). [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=520s), [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=661s), [19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1167s)
- Cost per purchase targets: $50, $100 or $200, set by the goal of each ad (2025-06). TikTok works better for US targeting and Facebook for global targeting. [16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=964s)
- Churn is 3 to 7 months depending on audience. Each purchase is worth about $30 to $70 LTV, higher on iOS (2025-06). [18:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1120s)
- Install-to-purchase conversion about 1% on test traffic. In-app tests cut cost per purchase from about $500 to $300 within days. [17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1024s), [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1085s)
- Attribution gaps: real purchases are often about double what ads report. At least 10% arrive after day 1. Another 10% to 20% come from other attribution methods, and 10% to 20% from spillover, such as App Store search. [22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1336s), [23:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1386s)
- One faceless YouTube channel did 26M views in 28 days. Yev runs over 10 such channels (2025-06). [30:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1844s), [31:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1904s)

## Tactics

- **Fix the biggest drop-off first.** Map every funnel step from content to install, onboarding and paywall. Point the whole team at the worst step. [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=210s)
- **Amplify organic with paid.** Put a few dollars behind every organic video. Low-view videos can still convert, and paid spend reveals which ones do. [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=328s), [12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=731s)
- **Creative is the 10x lever.** Targeting and small tweaks give about 10%. The right creative gives about 10x. [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=785s)
- **Viral is not the same as converting.** The most viral videos often convert worse. Middle-virality videos in the top 25% can convert better. [13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=834s), [14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=884s)
- **Ad structure.** A shocking facial-expression hook, then a product demo that calls out the audience, then a pattern-interrupt ending. The paid ad adds the link, so the video needs no call to action. [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=540s), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=600s)
- **Creator sources.** Brand-creator marketplaces such as JoinBrands and Sideshift, plus their own Discord of creators. [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=429s)
- **Split ad budget by job.** One part runs at break-even for profit. Another part buys cheap data, with tens of thousands of installs, to speed up onboarding and paywall tests. [17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1024s), [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1085s)
- **Scale a winner in layers.** Test one variable at a time: headlines first, then countries and devices, then in-app flows per country. Move through each layer within a week. [25:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1500s), [26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1563s), [27:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1625s)
- **Track assisted conversions.** Some screens do not convert directly but help later purchases. Use this as a tiebreaker in neutral tests, for example on social proof. [28:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1731s)
- **Measure lift.** Turn ads off and on to estimate true incremental sales. [22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1336s)
- **$5K launch budget plan:** [32:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1939s), [36:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2168s)
  1. Make as many creative variations as you can, up to 100.
  2. TikTok: 20 creatives at the $20 minimum each, and turn them off after about $5. Facebook: 30 to 40 creatives at lower amounts.
  3. Judge on clicks within hours. Pick the outlier that is 2x to 10x better than the rest.
  4. Track that winner down to purchase and make variations.
- **Rent an audience.** Buying or partnering with an existing channel for a few hundred to $3K can skip the build-up. [35:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2108s)
