# How I Built It: $40K/Month iPhone App

## Summary

Steven Cravotta builds viral mobile apps without writing code or doing design. His quit-vaping app Puff Count makes about $40K/month in recurring revenue (2024-09). He outsources design to 99designs and development to Upwork, and he markets with organic TikTok. A hard paywall with a free trial and a long onboarding flow changed the business overnight.

## Key facts

- Puff Count: $43K in sales in the last 30 days and just over $112K in the last 90 days (2024-09). [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s)
- His apps have over 12M downloads in total (2024-09). His earlier word games made money from ads. [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s)
- Puff Count went live 4 years before the video. It made decent money only in the last 6 to 7 months (2024-09). It made no money in its first 4 to 6 months. [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=813s), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=245s)
- A simple MVP from an Upwork developer costs under $5K. A ThemeForest template can bring the cost under $1K (2024-09). [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=344s)
- After the switch to a hard paywall with a free trial, conversion went up to 20% to 25% (2024-09). [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=547s)
- He A/B tested prices from $4 up to $12 and kept the price with the highest LTV. [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=617s)
- One TikTok with 8.3M views drove tens of thousands of downloads. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=430s)

## Tactics

- **Scratch your own itch.** Solve a problem you have, so you are the ideal user. Transformation problems (weight loss, quitting vaping) make marketing easy. [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=152s)
- **Validate with data.** He used Sensor Tower for quit-smoking and quit-drinking apps, Google Trends for vaping, and TikTok for viral vape videos. [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=245s)
- **Design from sketches.** Brain-dump features and competitors in Google Docs. Sketch on paper. Upload the sketches to 99designs, where 50 to 70 designers submit concepts. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=291s)
- **Hire developers per project.** He hires Eastern European developers on Upwork. He pays on completion, with no bugs, not per hour. A 15-minute call checks that the developer is excited and brings ideas. [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=344s)
- **Copy viral formats on TikTok.** Save the top videos for your niche in a spreadsheet and study the hook. Puff Count copied a "take apart a vape" video that had 20M+ views. Make it entertainment first, with a 2-second call to action at the end. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=430s)
- **Organic winners become paid ads.** A video that does well organically is a good creative. Put it on Meta or TikTok ads and let the algorithm find buyers. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=496s)
- **Influencers are hard.** Most want a lot of cash. Look for cheap "diamond in the rough" creators. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=496s)
- **Hard paywall after onboarding.** Tool apps do not suit ads. The user must start a free trial before they can use any feature. [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=547s)
- **Long onboarding.** Ask many questions so the user thinks about their own problem before the paywall. The data shows that this lifts conversion. [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=648s)
- **Stack.** He hires on Upwork and gets UI from 99designs. Superwall runs paywall tests and RevenueCat gives LTV data. AppsFlyer is the MMP for TikTok and Facebook ads. He stays on the free plans of Mixpanel or Amplitude. Keep CAC below LTV. [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=687s)
- **Ship a simple MVP.** Do not spend 2 months on a feature before launch. Get user feedback first. [15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=909s)
