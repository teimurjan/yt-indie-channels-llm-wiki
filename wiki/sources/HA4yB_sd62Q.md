# The Weird Design Playbook of 6 App Outliers

## Summary

Tim Gabe went through more than 200 top-charting App Store apps. He says most of them copy the leader of their category. He picks four outliers that broke a category design assumption, an approach he calls "first principles design". Tiimo uses visual time blocks for ADHD users. Cal AI makes the camera the home page. MacroFactor removes shame from macro tracking. Ladder puts each team on the same workout every day. Each section ends with a takeaway and a plug for Tim's agency, Zipsnap.

## Key facts

- Tiimo is from Copenhagen and won iPhone App of the Year 2025. It first targeted neurodivergent users. [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=67s), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=115s)
- The Cal AI team sold Cal AI to MyFitnessPal. A few months later, their second app, in stealth, made over $300K MRR (2026-06). [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=250s)
- MyFitnessPal logging takes 4 to 5 steps per meal: add food, search, scroll, select, confirm. [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=250s)
- PictureThis launched in 2017 with camera-first plant identification. [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=313s)
- MacroFactor comes from Stronger by Science, is bootstrapped, costs $72 a year and has 500,000 users (2026-06). [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s)
- Ladder was an App of the Year 2025 finalist. About 5,000 strangers per team do the same workout each day. CEO Greg Stewart cited 500% growth on the Sub Club podcast. The funnel was TikTok to a quiz (2026-06). [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=585s), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)

## Tactics

- **Niche depth.** Do not reskin the mass-market interface for a niche. Tiimo dropped text rows for colored, illustrated blocks sized by duration, so users see their schedule instead of reading it. Notion cannot follow, because its audience is too broad. [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=115s), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=176s)
- **Rebuild primitives around new tech.** Cal AI made the camera the first action. AI writes the entry, and the user corrects it only by exception. Do not bolt new tech onto old UI. [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=313s), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=375s)
- **Question what the category assumes about users.** Macro trackers assumed shame drives compliance: red numbers, broken streaks, guilt notifications. MacroFactor expects missed targets. It uses a trend weight averaged over about 3 weeks to back-calculate metabolism. Honest logs give better data than MyFitnessPal's gamed database. [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=504s), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=565s)
- **Turn solo moments into shared rituals.** Ladder's cohort calendar makes a skipped workout visible to the team. The quiz funnel converted users, but the cohort kept them. [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)

## Quotes

- "Founders who add a feature ship a feature. Founders who reset the primitives ship a category-defining product." [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=375s)
- Quoting Greg Nuckols: "What if nutrition apps didn't try to guilt people into losing weight?" [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s)
- "You own the niche by going deep on the interface, not by doing more of what the leaders do." [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=176s)
