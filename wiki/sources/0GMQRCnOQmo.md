# I Redesigned a Neobank App (Here's Why People Started Funding It)

## Summary

Tim Gabe presents his studio Zipzap's redesign of a crypto neobank card. The captions spell it "Soulcard", "Soul Card" and "Salt Card", and the wiki uses the slug `solcard`. After the relaunch, users put 79% more money on their cards and successful deposits rose 71% (2026-10). He organizes the work around three worries a money app must answer. Is this company real? What if something goes wrong? Am I still in control? Each worry is tied to a psychology finding.

## Key facts

- After the redesign, money loaded onto cards rose 79% and successful card deposits rose 71% (2026-10). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=0s), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)
- The client had used other agencies before and did not feel confident going live. The old site felt dated and rushed. [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=48s)
- Zipzap designed 15 status screens, including processing, on hold and declined. [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)
- Most payments go through Apple Pay or Google Pay, so users open the app mainly to check their money. [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=280s)
- Tim says Microsoft later shipped a Copilot mark very like their two-stacked-cards "S" logo. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=108s)

## Tactics

- **Look real, not fun.** First-time visitors ask if the company will still exist in a year. The design is the evidence they use. [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=48s)
- **Answer objections in the hero.** The hero animation ends on plain promises: card issued in seconds, no annual fees, refund anytime. The site shows the card in hotels, taxis and groceries so it reads as a card for normal life, not only crypto. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=108s)
- **Make the card the thread (endowment effect).** The card appears on the site, as the first step after sign-up (pick your card), and in the center of the iOS app. People value what feels like theirs, as in the coffee mug study. [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=170s)
- **Show fees before payment.** StubHub's hidden fees raised purchases 14%. A fee found at the end feels like a trick, though, and kills the second deposit. [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=219s)
- **Replace "maybe" with clear warnings.** People stress more over uncertainty than over a sure thing. A plain warning next to the address says the wrong network can lose funds. [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=280s)
- **Status screens are the product.** An explained wait feels shorter. Use "on hold while we complete routine checks" with a support button, and crypto progress like "2 of 8". [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)
- **Put control one tap away.** In a 1972 noise study, just having a stop button reduced stress. So freeze card, lock online payments, spending limits and show PIN are each one tap away, with freeze on the web dashboard. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=396s), [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=439s)
- **Privacy as safety.** 2025 set a record for "wrench attacks", so users can hide or blur the balance, and flipping the phone face down hides it too. [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=439s)
- **One calm home for rewards.** A crypto-back history (for example 85 cents on an Uber) instead of rewards everywhere, which feels scammy. [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=439s)
- **Human support.** WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram or email works like the stop button: users rarely use it but feel in control. [8:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=500s)

## Quotes

- "Trust isn't won on the happy path. It's won on the screens you hope nobody sees." [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)
- "Give people something that feels like theirs before you ask them for anything." [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=170s)
- "Nobody makes a second deposit with an app that tricked them." [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=280s)
