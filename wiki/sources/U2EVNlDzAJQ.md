## Summary

Tim Gabe tests Framer AI, which makes a website from a text prompt. He sets a 5-minute timer to build a startup landing page for a made-up company called "Deemable". He prompts, picks a theme and fonts, adds images and assets, and adds appear animations. Then he publishes the site live. His verdict: Framer AI gives a strong base for a landing page in under 2 minutes, and you then customize it by hand.

## Key facts

- Framer AI makes a site from one prompt. The right sidebar then offers color schemes and font pairs, and you can theme each section [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=0s).
- The base page takes less than 2 minutes to generate (2023-06) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=0s).
- "Retry" makes a new layout and also changes the fonts and the color palette [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=84s), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=108s).
- The finished page went live with the publish button inside the 5-minute window [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=311s), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=366s).

## Tactics

- **Give the prompt context.** He starts with "a modern landing page for a startup called Deemable" and adds more detail. More context gives better output [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=54s).
- **Pick a theme, then apply it to all sections.** He chose a dark scheme and set it on every section. He chose fonts by hand rather than shuffle, with a more techy body font [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=127s).
- **Swap in real visuals.** He pasted images from his library, set the hero image to absolute position and swapped card images for icon assets. Low resolution images look less clean [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=175s), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=208s).
- **Add delight with appear effects.** Text slides in from the bottom with a tuned spring. He copied the effect to other items and staggered pricing cards with 0.25 s and 0.5 s delays. The hero image scales in [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=267s), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=311s).
- **Use AI for the base, not the finish.** The AI output is a start point. Layout, alignment, assets and motion still need manual work [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=0s).
