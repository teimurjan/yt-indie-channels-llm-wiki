## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Louis David, a Canadian developer who built about 10 apps that failed. With a co-founder, he then built GlowUp, an AI app that lets women try makeup looks on a photo of themselves. This time they made TikTok content first, based on comments under makeup videos, and built the app only after the content went viral. GlowUp reached 100K users in about 3 days and $800K in revenue in its first year (2025-12). Louis shares his step-by-step TikTok playbook and one viral video that made $12.5K in a day.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $800K total in about one year, now about $15K/month (2025-12). [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=72s), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s)
- Users: 0 to 100K in about 3 days. About 2M total users and about 70,000 paying customers (2025-12). [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=72s), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s)
- Rank: #1 in Lifestyle and #4 in top free apps on the App Store. [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s)
- Pricing: $9.99/week or $39.99/year for unlimited AI makeup looks. Subscriptions are the only revenue. [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s)
- One video: close to 48M views, 2M likes, 60K downloads and about $12.5K in sales in one day. [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=488s)
- At peak: about 7 TikTok accounts posting 8 to 12 times a day. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)
- Costs: about $1K/month backend, about $500/month AI, $0 marketing for self-made posts. Profit margin: about 90% (2025-12). [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=644s)
- Stack: FlutterFlow for the MVP, Firebase and Google Cloud Functions for the backend, Superwall for payments, Mixpanel for events. [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=615s)
- Background: 2 years of software engineering school, then dropped out. He built a portfolio of apps and worked for startups. [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=142s)

## Tactics

- **Content first, app second.** His earlier apps failed because he marketed only after he built. For GlowUp he posted first and built a fast MVP once the content went viral. [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s)
- **Mine TikTok comments for ideas.** Read the comments under big niche videos to see what people ask for. Pick a niche by hashtag size, number of videos and views. Makeup was one of the biggest. [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s)
- **Step 1, warm up a US account.** Use a new iPhone, a US SIM card and an always-on US VPN. Watch, like and comment in your niche 15 to 30 minutes a day for about 3 days. [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s)
- **Step 2, post once a day, then scale.** Copy a trend that works and put your app on it. After traction, post 4 to 6 times a day. Choose a format that is easy to repeat and can be faceless. [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=333s)
- **Step 3, add an anchor link late.** The "download now" anchor link drove most downloads. Add it only after a video passes a few thousand views. Below about 1,000 views it kills reach. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)
- **Step 4, add accounts.** Run 2 to 3 accounts per phone. Buy another phone for more. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)
- **Referral codes that feed the comments.** Invite three friends and you get a free makeup look. Users came back to the video to post their codes, which pushed engagement. The anchor link went on at about 500K views. [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=488s)
- **Long, personal onboarding.** Questions about sensitive skin, wrinkles, preferred looks and skin tone make results feel personal. A rating prompt comes before the result, and most users leave 5 stars. [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s)
- **Pay for UGC only with budget.** UGC and influencers work but cost a lot. With no money, post faceless videos or film yourself. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)
