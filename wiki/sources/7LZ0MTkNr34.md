# I Make $1M/Year With One Website

## Summary

Alex Goldberg runs FinvsFin, a product review site that compares health and wellness products, mostly telehealth brands. He started it in 2018 as a side project while he had a full-time job, and it now makes over $1M a year from affiliate commissions. The breakthrough was a new niche with no content yet (telehealth) and direct partnerships with brands instead of only affiliate networks. He grew it into a portfolio of about six sites, added paid media, and is now working on a sale.

## Key facts

- Revenue: over $1M/year, "seven-figure" business (2024-03) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=0s), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s).
- Model: the site sends shoppers to brands and earns a commission on each referred sale [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s).
- Started in 2018 as a side hustle with a business partner. The first idea was to compare fintech companies, which gave the name "fin vs fin" [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=120s).
- Alex wrote the first 60 articles himself and learned WordPress to build the site [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s).
- It took about 6 months to get significant organic traffic [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s).
- He kept his job until his income from the site replaced his $150K salary, about one year in. He went full-time after about 2 years and bought out his partner (2024-03) [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=242s), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=454s).
- Portfolio: about six affiliate sites, each in a narrower niche (2024-03) [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s).
- Team: outsourced writers, a virtual assistant for publishing, a full-time editor, a designer and a web developer, spread across the world (2024-03) [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=689s).
- Next step: selling the business (2024-03) [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=750s).

## Tactics

- **Take a true 9-to-5 job.** He moved from early-stage startups to a larger company so he could close the laptop at 5 pm and build on the side [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=120s).
- **Pick a new, uncrowded niche.** Telehealth companies were raising $100M Series A rounds, but almost nobody had written reviews of them. A new site cannot win crowded queries, but it can win queries that nobody has written for. His criteria: the category is new, has high margins, and has momentum [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=120s), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=337s).
- **Target low-competition long-tail keywords.** Learn high-intent query patterns ("best X for Y") and plug in brands. Pick the brands that will still exist in 3 years, then write the best page online about each one: deeper or more concise than the top result, whichever matches the intent [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=398s).
- **Partner with brands directly.** After about 6 months of traffic, he pitched brands: "I'm pretty sure we're driving you sales; there could be more if we work together." Brands then name the price they pay per customer [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s).
- **Reuse partnerships across a portfolio.** One partner can go on five sites, which gives economies of scale [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s).
- **Expect rankings to erode.** Big publishers (Forbes, Healthline) compete in every niche. Keep publishing fresh content inside your topical authority [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=577s).
- **Diversify traffic with paid media funded by partners.** He pitched partners a test spend of "a few thousand" dollars for paid campaigns. If it works, it scales far beyond SEO [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=577s).
- **Hire in order:** writing first, then a VA for publishing, then a full-time editor, then design and dev. This freed him to focus on partnerships, which have the most revenue leverage [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=689s).
