# this 14 yr old makes $100k/mo PROFIT building apps with ai

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Rafael Kramer on the Superwall podcast. Rafael started at 13 with no-code mobile games about viral memes and promoted them with his own TikTok dev logs. At 14, a game about the DaBaby "car head" meme made more than $100K in one month from ads alone. He later ran a company that built games with influencers, then moved to consumer apps in 2025. He has 50+ apps, about 100M social views and three exits. His next app is a "big swing" in mental health and wellness.

## Key facts

- The DaBaby game got about 1.5M to 2M downloads in about a month and made more than $100K in that month (2020 to 2021). It reached about #2 overall on the App Store. [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=378s), [7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=438s)
- Only 9 TikToks promoted it, at 100K to 1M views each. They drove the first 300K to 400K downloads. Sharing and the charts drove the rest. [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=739s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=758s)
- He built games in Buildbox, a no-code builder, and hired Unity developers once money came in. [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=507s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=548s)
- Monetization: Google AdMob only, with an ad every second death and a 30-second rewarded ad to revive. Revenue was about 10 cents per user. Good modern games earn 80 cents to $1 per user. [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=562s), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=591s)
- At 15, he started an influencer game company with up to 10 staff. He sold it at 17 for at least six figures. Its Skibidi Toilet game got 2M to 3M downloads in about 2 weeks from one YouTube Short with 50M+ views. [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=274s)
- Debloat AI got about 40,000 downloads. TikTok ads cost a few cents per download for a few days, then about 30% profit on spend for weeks. He sold it. Copycats now make $20K to $30K/month (2026-02). [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s), [15:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=947s)
- Amanda AI, an AI relationship coach that read text screenshots, sold as a small exit after TikTok restricted its ads. [26:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1618s), [27:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1647s)

## Tactics

- **Find a meme early and make it playable.** A meme is 2D: you laugh and scroll. A game lets people be the meme, and they share it. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=465s)
- **Post dev logs and A/B test hooks.** He tested hooks and frames in early videos. Trending subjects got pushed out by the algorithm. Viewers sent feature ideas in the comments. [10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=659s), [11:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=719s)
- **Use comment sections as demand signals.** Under debloat transformation videos, about 100 people asked "how?". [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1061s)
- **Run cheap faceless slideshow pages.** He used about 8 accounts and paid on a CPM basis. A friend who ran the same play for a height app sent him the creators. [18:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1095s), [18:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1116s)
- **Write scripts yourself, outsource editing.** Hire one editor for the whole video. Recruit through Twitter posts and Discord servers, not Upwork, and ask for a free sample. [19:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1194s), [20:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1250s), [21:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1288s)
- **Scan before the paywall.** Debloat AI ran the face scan first, which created sunk cost. It showed a slightly exaggerated bloat score, then the paywall. [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1498s)
- **Look outside your age group.** Founders build for themselves. Niches like plant care for older users stay under-served. [25:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1548s), [26:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1595s)
- **Look credible in the DM.** A strong Instagram profile, mutual followers and a blue check raise influencer reply rates. [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=312s), [33:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1992s)
- **Pick influencers by audience fit, not size.** A 5M-follower creator with 2M views a video still flopped. Look for an inside joke or a story universe. Read comments for real community, not flame emojis. [33:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1992s), [34:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2069s), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2172s)
- **Give equity and ownership.** Games used 50/50 splits with influencers. Weekly calls and character updates made the influencer feel it was their game. [39:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2389s), [41:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2510s)
- **Use AI influencers.** Hyper-realistic AI before-and-after accounts pass as real. He partnered with one team on a looksmaxxing app. [29:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1759s)
