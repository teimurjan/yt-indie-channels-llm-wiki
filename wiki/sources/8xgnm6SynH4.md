## Summary

Pat Walls visits three young founders in Austin, Texas, who earn from Roblox. Cole is one of the top 50 Roblox developers, and his game Hide or Die makes about $45K/month (2025-04). His twin brother Ian made Bake the Baby, now Bathe the Baby, over a college winter break and dropped out on its income. Their friend Jake runs Vector 3, an agency that makes trailers and TikTok campaigns for brands that enter Roblox. Their lessons: test a game fast, make it clickable, social and replayable, and win agency clients with LinkedIn content.

## Key facts

- Roblox: about 400M monthly active users. One in two US kids under 16 plays daily. Kids spend twice as much time on Roblox as on TikTok (2025-04). [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=312s)
- Dress to Impress opened Roblox to older girls: over 1M concurrent players and over 1B plays. MrBeast made his own Roblox game. [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=373s)
- Developers earn Robux from in-game purchases and cash them out. Brand-sponsored games are a newer revenue stream. [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=245s)
- Cole: about 1M daily active users across his games, mostly US and Canadian kids. He started playing in 2013 and moved to Austin at 18 instead of college (2025-04). [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=93s)
- Hide or Die: launched about 6 months earlier, about $45K/month. With revenue shares from helping other games, Cole expects about $70K this month, split 50/50 with his programmer partner. 1M Robux pays out about $3.5K (2025-04). ## Tactics

- **Test first, then build.** After a 3-year, $300K game failed, Cole and his programmer made Hide or Die in 2 weeks. [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=720s)
- **Clickable, social, replayable.** A dramatic title gets the click. Friends play together. Levels and locked content bring players back. Expect a few attempts before a hit. [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=753s), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=797s)
- **Monetize with shops.** Ian sells skins, coins and abilities, such as a meteor or invisibility, through daily shops. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)
- **Agency inbound from LinkedIn.** About 95% of Vector 3 clients come from LinkedIn posts of education and past work. Jake does no cold outreach. [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)
- **Get paid for the lead.** Paid consulting calls often turn into execution work. [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)
- **Systemize delivery.** A brand trailer follows a 45-step template. Each step has an SOP and a recorded video. The team works remotely over Discord. [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)
- **Build skills before a business.** Try many interests, get a bit better than average, freelance, then scale what you like. [17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1049s)
- **Work or rest, nothing between.** They work 4 to 5 hours with phones in another room, then stop at sunset. [18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1090s)
- **Schedule friendship.** Their friendship began as a weekly Google Calendar call. Keep friends separate from deals. [19:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1151s)
