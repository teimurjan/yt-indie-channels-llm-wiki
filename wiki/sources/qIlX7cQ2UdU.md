# I Make $15K/Month From One Website

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Maddox, a college student who built DuckMath, an "unblocked games" website paid by Google AdSense. The site sat at about $1K/month for years. Then a surprise $240 day pushed him to market it hard with high-volume TikTok posts. In 4 months it went from 5K to 150K daily active users and to about $15K/month. He then sold it for $120K (2026-03). Pat and Gus close with the idea of the "ego business" versus the business you are meant to build.

## Key facts

- DuckMath grew from about 5K to 150K daily active users, and from about $1K to $15K/month, in 4 months (2026-03). It launched 4 years earlier. [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=49s)
- He sold it a few weeks before the video for $120K, after a $100K lowball and a counter-offer, with escrow (2026-03). [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=70s), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=519s)
- About 1.5 million users per month, at about 1 cent of AdSense revenue each. A normal day was $600 to $700 (2026-03). [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=70s), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=129s)
- His main account had 4.6 million views. The top video got 2.1M views on TikTok and 3.2M on Instagram, about 5M in total. He guesses it earned about $1,000. [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=129s), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=388s)
- Snapchat steadily gave 20,000+ views per video. [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s)
- Stack: Cloudflare for hosting (domains about $200), Supabase at $20/month for auth, leaderboards and coins, PostHog with $50K in startup credits, AdSense, Repurpose.io, and a Discord with about 7,000 members. [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=646s), [11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=708s)

## Tactics

- **Copy a creator, then out-post them.** A younger kid on TikTok promoted his own unblocked-games site. Maddox built his own in a week or two and posted 5 days a week. [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=159s)
- **Follow the money.** A passive $240 day showed him the site was worth real effort, so he dropped his B2B ideas. [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=204s)
- **Volume.** He ran 3 TikTok accounts with 3 posts a day, 5 days a week, and cross-posted to Reels, Shorts and Snapchat with Repurpose.io. He copied the best competitor videos and worked twice as hard as they did. [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s)
- **Iterate daily.** He checked engagement on yesterday's videos before he made 3 new ones. [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=129s)
- **Lo-fi hook, clear link.** His top video took 30 minutes: phone footage of his laptop, a visual hook, then the exact link. It was part 30 of one series with new hooks each time. [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=388s)
- **Delegate content.** A person he mentored made videos in his format. He paid his girlfriend about $200 a week for videos. [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=371s), [11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=708s)
- **A/B test for ad revenue.** Test for time on site and pages per visit, because more pages mean more ad views. [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=252s)
- **Find a buyer.** He contacted a bigger company that had bought a similar game site. [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=519s)
- **2026 playbook.** Pick a niche you can market to. Build an MVP in 2 to 3 days with AI. Study 10 to 20 top competitor videos for hook and call to action. Post daily on TikTok. [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=574s)
