# The Underdog: From $10/hr to $1.5M/Year

## Summary

Starter Story tells the story of Marc Lou, a French developer who went from a $10/hour waiter job to about $1.5M/year (2024-08) as a solopreneur. He spent about 5 years failing: a "Tinder for sports" app, a VC-funded AI startup in South Korea, and selling gloves. A small escape-room marketing tool died when COVID hit. After a breakdown and a $9K/month software job that ended in a layoff, he switched to small bets, shipped in public, and launched ShipFast, a reusable code boilerplate that made about $40K in its first month.

## Key facts

- He worked as a part-time waiter for about $10/hour while he spent a year on a "Tinder for sports lovers" app. He threw away the code. [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=184s)
- A friend's VC-funded AI startup in South Korea (2017) took 6 months to build and got zero customers. [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=246s)
- His escape-room customer tool grew to about $1K/month, then $4K MRR, then fell to $0 within 24 hours when COVID closed physical businesses. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=430s), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=491s)
- At 28 he took a software engineering job at $9K/month, after about 5 years at about $1K/month. He was later laid off. [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=615s), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s)
- He shipped 6 projects in 7 months, including Mood to Movie and Habits Garden (about 10,000 users), and was still at about $1K/month. [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s)
- Painkiller apps with paywalls and launch skits moved revenue to about $4K/month by mid-2023. [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s)
- ShipFast made $500 in its first 2 hours and $3K to $4K the next day. It did about $40K in month one and held near $50K/month, with $65K in a Black Friday month. [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s), [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=981s)
- After free tools and a YouTube episode, ShipFast hit about $85K, then $135K and $135K per month, then slowed to about $80K/month (2024-08). He says he failed about 30 startups. [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s), [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1102s)

## Tactics

- **Sell before you build.** A marketer told him to sell first. He sent cold emails, and an Australian escape-room owner agreed to pay after a 42-minute call. [6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=369s)
- **Make small bets.** He set rules: never spend months on a product, never raise money, never hire. Each failure is a lesson unless you quit. [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s)
- **Build in public as insurance.** If an app fails, the following he built stays for the next launch. [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s)
- **Painkillers over vitamins.** He moved from fun apps like a habit tracker to apps that solve a known pain and have a paywall. [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s)
- **Launch with comedy skits.** He edited himself onto a Joe Rogan podcast or into a film with Leonardo DiCaprio to get attention on launch day. [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s)
- **Productize your own boilerplate.** After 10 to 15 products he noticed he repeated the same setup (landing page, payments, emails, accounts). He built it as a code base in under a week and sold it to other developers. [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s)
- **Free tools as ads.** A free logo generator carries a banner for ShipFast. [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s)
- **Pick the medium you are comfortable with.** Text goes on Twitter, video on TikTok, and images on Instagram. [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1102s)
