## Summary

Joseph, a self-taught coder in Lakeland, Florida, built StealthGPT. The web app writes AI text that AI detectors do not flag, or rewrites text from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. A TikTok video about the GPTZero detector gave him the idea in January 2023. He bought the domain for $150 in February and had paying customers in March. In 15 months it grew to $190K MRR (2024-07), mostly through Google Ads. Before this, a failed crypto firm left him with a settlement with the CFTC.

## Key facts

- $190K MRR after about 15 months. Profit is $10K to $30K/month after expenses (2024-07). [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)
- Marketing spend is about $100K/month and rising (2024-07). [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)
- Team of 6 to 7, with 2 more hires open. Salaries cost $50K to $60K/month. Database, hosting and AI stack cost about $5K/month (2024-07). [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=136s)
- 400K lifetime signups, 15K to 20K lifetime subscribers, about 8,500 active subscribers (2024-07). [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=136s)
- Domain cost $75/year with a two-year minimum, so $150. First site used Next.js and React. [6:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=407s)
- Launch pricing: 10 free tries, then three tiers from $4.99 to about $15. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=465s)
- Early Google Ads cost $10 to $20 per conversion. [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)
- Prior failure: J Squared Invest, a crypto onboarding firm started at 23, collapsed in 2018. The CFTC case took 2 to 3 years, ended in a $400K settlement and cost $50K in legal fees. [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=217s), [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=278s)
- Stack: Next.js, React, Vercel, Supabase, Discord, Buffer, Zendesk, Google Workspace, GitHub. [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=652s)

## Tactics

- **Ride a new behavior.** He expected AI content to flood the web and people to push back. He reverse engineered GPTZero for a few days until every output passed as human. [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=345s)
- **List on AI directories.** He listed on Futurepedia. A TikTok creator who tracks that site covered StealthGPT and brought the first two customers. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=465s)
- **Educate the market with UGC.** The product category was new, so early TikTok and Instagram content explained what it does. [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)
- **Pour profit into Google Ads.** After summer 2023, low cost per conversion let them reinvest revenue into Google Ads, "gasoline on a fire". [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)
- **Solve broad problems in one click.** Use feedback for quality and ease of use. Refuse niche features that cost hundreds of hours for one user. [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=579s), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=639s)
- **Hire from Twitter.** He hired help 2 months in, once traction was clear. All developer hires came from Twitter posts, with no pedigree needed. [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=701s)
- **Daily routine.** Check that all systems are green, then check ads for today and yesterday. Then ideate and sync with the team. [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=795s)
- **Marketing first.** Without distribution, the product does not matter. [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=856s)
