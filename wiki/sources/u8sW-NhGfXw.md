# I Built a $1M App in 5 Hours

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Dawson Botsford in his Sprinter van in Boulder, Colorado. Dawson left Uber, travelled for a year, and then built Earni.fi (spoken as "nfy" in the captions) in about 5 hours at a month-long hackathon. Earni.fi shows Ethereum users the airdrops they did not claim. One tweet brought 10,000 signups in 48 hours. He then put every airdrop result behind a paywall, reached over $1M ARR, and sold the company to Bankless.

## Key facts

- Earni.fi grew to over 250,000 free users and 5,000 paid users, at over $1M ARR (2024-04). [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=55s)
- The intro claims the paywall scaled it to over $100K/month (2024-04). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=0s)
- The average user found $750 in unclaimed airdrops over the life of the site (2024-04). [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=90s)
- He built the first version in 4 to 5 hours, after 2 to 3 weeks of thinking about the idea. [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=300s)
- Launch: 10,000 organic signups in 48 hours from one tweet. [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)
- Stack: TypeScript full stack, Node.js, React and Next.js, edited in VS Code. [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=609s)
- David Hoffman of the Bankless podcast sent a Twitter DM. After 2 years of solo growth, Bankless bought the company and Dawson became CTO. [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=652s)
- Background: software engineer at Uber in 2016, before the IPO. [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=191s)

## Tactics

- **Solve your own pain.** He was an Ethereum user and knew the problem. He waits until the idea is clear, then builds fast with conviction. [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=300s)
- **Hackathons as a launch pad.** A weekend build can have real users by Sunday. [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=131s)
- **A demo video in the launch tweet.** The video searched an address with many airdrops. It teased the money people can find, and a retweet felt like helping friends. [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)
- **One clear call to action.** A signup button sits in the header and as a large button above the fold. Drop shadows and borders pull the eye to it. [7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=421s)
- **The "anti-email" strategy.** Each email told a user about a matched airdrop and its dollar amount. He sent no other email. Open rates were very high. Then he paywalled every airdrop result. [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=455s)
- **Public call-out campaigns.** "25 Days of Christmas": each day for 25 days he tagged a person on Twitter with a screenshot of their unclaimed dollars. [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=455s), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=516s)
- **Show up in person.** Crypto conferences brought more signups. [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=516s)
- **Win on quality and trust.** Competitors sent too many low-value alerts. Earni.fi alerts meant real money, which matters in a space full of phishing. Being early helped too. [9:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=543s)
- **Avoid the solo echo chamber.** Use co-working, communities and conferences to test ideas. [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=579s)
- **Speed is a growth feature.** Next.js made the site load fast, which he says helped growth. [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=609s)
