## Summary

Nic Weber co-founded Noise, a platform where up to 50,000 people post an app's TikTok content and get paid per view. It began as an internal growth hack for his earlier app, Playbite. Founders on Noise got over 2.5B views in its first year (2026-01). Nic treats virality as a science. Volume ("shots on goal") finds the details that turn 1,000 views into 10M. He runs two prongs at once. High-effort UGC with a soft call to action aims to go viral. Thousands of cheap slideshows with a hard call to action act like retargeting ads.

## Key facts

- Over 2.5B views from founders on Noise in about one year. One video had 22M views in 4 days (2026-01). [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=212s)
- At least 100,000 posts a day go out, most at 500 to 2,000 views each. [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=212s), [4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=253s)
- Brands set the CPM they pay creators, usually from their target cost per install. Noise gets about $0.30 installs on its own app. One viral format ran at $0.08 installs for 3 days (2026-01). [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=647s), [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=865s)
- Noise's own content hit #5 and #7 in its App Store category (2026-01). [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=803s), [18:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1083s)
- Noise's own dashboard: 54M views in 30 days across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube (2026-01). The slideshows gave only 2M of those. [23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1425s), [24:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1478s)
- Start: July 2024. One slideshow did 20,000 views in a week. Then 30 to 35 engaged Playbite users posted Canva slideshows for pay. [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=527s), [21:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1274s)
- The usual way: pay a creator $200 to $500/month for 20 slideshows. [29:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1792s)

## Tactics

- **Eggs theory.** Food slideshows with eggs in the hook image got far more views. Eggs read instantly as healthy, cheap and achievable. Small, relatable visual details decide reach. [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=317s), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=378s)
- **Copy proven formats.** Find what already went viral and slot your app into it. The Pingo format had people cry while they tried to pronounce a word, and the AI roasted them. It reached 20M views overnight. Popular subreddits like r/RoastMe suggest formats. [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=681s), [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=742s), [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=803s)
- **Tune the first line.** The opener "everybody needs a side hustle" flopped. The same video, opened with an order not to get a second job, went viral. Nobody likes to be told what they need. [15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=927s)
- **Anchor on small money.** "$20 a night" feels achievable. "$500 a month" then maps to a car payment. Big claims like $7,000 a week read as a scam. [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1000s)
- **The visual matters.** Dishwashing outperformed vacuuming and pouring coffee in the same caption format. [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=865s)
- **Super-implicit posts plus comment seeding.** Some posts never name the app except in the description. Users then comment that Noise paid for the thing in the video. One seeded comment got about 1,686 likes. [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1102s), [19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1165s)
- **Two prongs at once.** Premium UGC hunts for million-view hits with a soft call to action. Basic slideshows with an explicit call to action reach the same viewers, like retargeting. [25:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1538s), [27:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1658s)
- **Randomize every variant.** Captions, hook images, descriptions and hashtags are shuffled so each post is unique. Users paste text in TikTok instead of baking it into the image, which sends a more native signal. [23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1425s), [24:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1478s)
- **Campaigns like Meta.** Campaigns hold playbooks (like ad sets) that hold posts. Pause a playbook after 1 to 3 weeks without a hit. 446 posts with 200K total views was a fail. A Christmas money playbook hit 9M. [29:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1792s), [30:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1842s)
- **Simple wins.** Hand-edited captions beat AI output. The posts that took the least time often went viral. [27:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1673s)
- **Low-view posts still convert.** TikTok sends even 500 views to the right 500 people. [31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1887s)
- **Daily views budget.** Each niche has a fixed daily attention budget. Flooding it spreads the views across more posts instead of making more big hits. [32:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1941s)
