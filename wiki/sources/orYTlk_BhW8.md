# I vibe coded a $12K/month mobile app (Stoppr Breakdown)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews David Attias, a former quant trader from France with no coding background. He heard that the teen founders of Quitter made $200K/month after 3 months, so he cloned Quitter's app screen for screen with Cursor. He moved it to a new niche: Stoppr helps Gen Z women stop sugar cravings, and it is marketed through French influencers. It made $5K in the first month and about $12K/month 5 months later (2025-10). He shares a five-step playbook for cloning apps.

## Key facts

- Stoppr makes about $12K/month, 5 months after launch (2025-10). The first month made $5K. [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=58s)
- 60,000 downloads and about 900 paying customers in 5 months (2025-10). [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s)
- Users are women aged about 13 to 25. Plans are weekly, monthly, and yearly with a trial. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=85s)
- He worked 8 years as a quant trader before he built apps. [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=142s)
- The full copy of Quitter took 2.5 weeks. Apple approval of the first version took 1 week. [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=314s)
- Costs: Cursor at $20/month, Mixpanel at about $100/month, and TikTok Spark Ads at about $100/month. Profit margin is 35% after 5 months (2025-10). [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=483s)

## Tactics

- **Copy a proven app, change the niche.** He copied Quitter's onboarding exactly because it was so good. Then he "changed black by pink" and replaced the original content with sugar. [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=237s), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=488s)
- **Localize an English app.** No one in France offered this kind of app, so he worked mainly with French influencers. [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s)
- **Validate with trends and content.** The "stop sugar" keyword had risen for 5 years on Google Trends. Many women influencers on TikTok and Instagram already talked about quitting sugar. [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=278s)
- **Clone from screenshots.** He took a screenshot of every screen and the flow between them, then asked Cursor for the same screen with the same visuals, colors and images. [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=314s)
- **Five-step cloning playbook:** [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=348s)
  1. Find a very successful app that targets a niche or a region. Confirm its MRR on Sensor Tower.
  2. Check that the niche is worth it on TikTok and Google Trends.
  3. Get every screenshot in one click from Mobbin or screens.design, then import them into Figma with a plugin. About 80% come in near perfect.
  4. Add Firebase only if you need a back end. It handles Google, email and Apple sign-in.
  5. Connect the Figma MCP to Cursor. He thinks this cuts the MVP from 1 month to 2 weeks. [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=408s)
- **Find influencers who already promote apps.** Top Yappers scrapes TikTok, Instagram and YouTube for creators who have worked with other apps. He then emails them through an outreach tool. [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s)
- **Boost winners with Spark Ads.** He recently started Meta ads and TikTok Spark Ads to add views to reels that already work. [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s)
- **Pat's caveat:** copy the app, but not the niche, so you do not take the original's customers. [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=488s)
