# I Turned This Spreadsheet Into a $30K/Month Micro-SaaS

## Summary

Jack ran a high-margin e-commerce brand that sold music production software. Then he consulted for agencies and Shopify brands. He built profit spreadsheets for clients: P&L, LTV, ROAS calculators and scenario planners. He did not know how to code. He uploaded the spreadsheet as a CSV to Cursor and said "build it". Then he fixed the calculations over 3.5 to 4 months. The result, Profit AI, is a Shopify app for profit analytics and AI marketing automation. About 6 months after launch it is at $30K MRR for the app plus just under $40K MRR for services, mostly from word of mouth.

## Key facts

- Profit AI launched in December 2025. Shopify approved it on Christmas Day. The first weeks had only free beta users. [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)
- It has made $147,000 in total and is at 30K MRR for the app and just under 40K MRR for services (2026-06). [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)
- 122 users, mostly free, and 38 or 39 paying customers (2026-06). Churn equals new installs: 47 installs against 43 uninstalls. [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)
- The paid plan was $5,000/month and got only a couple of existing clients. It dropped to $800/month. The free plan includes a 30-day trial of the paid plan. [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)
- Features: profit by ad channel after all costs, ad-spend forecasting for large stores, and a P&L from all costs. A "Claude wrapper" with skills and connectors launches ads, sends email and builds landing pages from Slack. [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s)
- His first brand launched in 2016. A $1,000 Facebook ads test "just worked". [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=215s)
- Stack (2026-06): Claude Code Max at about $200/month, Codex in the terminal (free after a discount), Instantly for outreach, Framer for the site and Google Sheets. [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=437s)
- Pat says another Shopify plugin founder, Eric Kos, grew from about $200K/month to over $1M/month in a year (2026-06). [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=463s)

## Tactics

- **Productize the spreadsheet you already sell.** His client spreadsheets made complex finance simple, with color and large numbers. Pat compares them to the scenario planners that consultancies such as Deloitte charge tens of thousands of dollars for. [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=335s)
- **CSV to AI coding tool.** Export the sheet as CSV and upload it to the AI coding tool. Ask it to build the app. Then correct each wrong calculation. Pat rebuilt his own net-worth spreadsheet in Claude Code in a day. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=390s), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=413s)
- **Find boring, repetitive client tasks.** Pat's frame: turn repeated work into a simple product. [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=278s)
- **If only insiders buy, lower the price.** At $5,000/month only existing clients paid. At $800 the app sells. [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s)
- **Free plan plus full trial.** He wants everyone to see the whole app, so free users get 30 days of the paid plan. [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)
- **Pipe data through a Slack bot.** Agency clients ask the Claude-based Slack bot to send data into their own spreadsheets. [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=505s)
- **E-commerce reporting is underrated.** Pat cites a Klaviyo reporting tool at $1M/year (2026-06) and calls e-commerce tooling a less crowded space than AI creator tools. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=531s)
- **Mine your own Drive.** Pat suggests letting Claude Code read your last 20 spreadsheets and build an app from one. [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=705s)
