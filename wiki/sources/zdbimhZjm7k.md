## Summary

Julian Alvarez co-founded Jungle, an AI study app. It turns PDFs, slides and videos into quizzes and flashcards, with a Duolingo-style game layer. It makes $80K to $100K/month. He found the idea in a viral tweet and DMed every person who engaged, until Twitter banned his account. One micro-influencer video took MRR from $2K to almost $15K in one to two weeks. Repeat influencer deals then lost money, and Jungle moved to a UGC program of about 30 creators. Julian warns that this kind of UGC is a growth hack that saturation will kill. He credits retention, a fast "time to magic" and word of mouth for long-term growth.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $80K to $100K/month (2025-11) [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=188s).
- Origin: 4 to 5 failed pivots and 3 months of runway. A comment on a viral "AI flashcards" tweet brought over 200 downloads and about 200 DMs [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=198s).
- First 100 users came from DMs to everyone who engaged with that tweet [4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=253s).
- Breakout: creator Agogo, a medical student, had about 3 videos over 1M views. One video took MRR from $2K to almost $15K in 1 to 2 weeks and earned an estimated $20K [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=670s).
- Word of mouth: 30% to 40% of new users, measured with a "how did you hear about us" step (2025-11) [13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=837s), [18:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1118s).
- The growing tree raised core engagement (questions answered) by 70% [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s).
- UGC: about 30 creators post 10 to 12 videos a week each, close to 400 videos a week. CPMs are about $2 (2025-11) [21:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1308s), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1368s). The intro cites about 40 creators and 150M total views [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=0s).
- Team: Julian is technical. Co-founder David is the designer and product thinker [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s).

## Tactics

- **Two weeks of distribution only.** A mentor told them to launch and not touch the product for two weeks. If nothing happens, pivot [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s).
- **Get 30 to 100 new users a week.** Without that floor, experiments have no signal [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=358s).
- **DMs for feedback, not growth.** One-on-one DMs do not scale, but they open a direct feedback channel [6:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=379s).
- **AI tool directories.** Listings such as Future Tools helped early. Julian thinks they are saturated now [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=269s), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s).
- **Micro-influencers.** Target 5K to 100K followers for better ROI. Use multi-video deals to lower cost per video. Brief creators with pain points and viral hooks, but leave room for their own style [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=484s), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=529s).
- **Use minimum view clauses.** Influencer spend outran revenue. Julian regrets that they did not use these clauses [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=789s).
- **Cut time to magic.** Users upload a document straight from the landing page. A 7-question mini review hides all settings and shows core value first [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=885s).
- **Paywall placement.** Show the paywall at the point that the most users reach. Jungle splits onboarding into three phases: sign-up after the first generation, exam and reminder setup, then a home-screen phase with character choice, attribution and weekly goal [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1056s), [18:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1118s).
- **Visible game elements spread the app.** Students sit close together in libraries and see the growing tree on other screens [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s).
- **Freemium over hard paywall.** Jungle lets users keep studying for free and converts them later. Bootstrapped founders can do this, but it is harder before they are cash-flow positive [20:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1217s), [21:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1277s).
