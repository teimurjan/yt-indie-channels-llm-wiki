# I Spent 24 Hours With iOS Millionaires

## Summary

Pat Walls spends a day in New York with Blake Anderson and Zach Yadegari, two of the four co-founders of Cal AI. Cal AI is an AI calorie-tracking app that they sold for "multiple millions" in 2026. They explain how four friends split roles and handled big decisions through trust. They argue that the iOS opportunity is still large in 2026, but copycat apps have a low ceiling. Both founders already work on new projects.

## Key facts

- Cal AI revenue: $30K in month 1, over $100K in month 2, and $1M/month within 8 months. It was on track for about $50M in the year of the sale (2026-08). [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=147s), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=151s)
- Team: Zach Yadegari (CEO, 19), Blake Anderson (co-founder, investor and advisor, 25), Jake Castillo (CMO/COO, 31) and Henry (CTO, 18) (2026-08). [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=86s), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=196s)
- Zach started coding at 7 and shipped his first app at 12. His first company reached 5M users in 2 years and made $60K a year. He sold it at 16. [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=112s)
- Blake started building apps at Tulane. His first apps mostly failed before he moved to AI apps. [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=86s)
- At the end of 2024, they got low eight-figure acquisition offers and turned them down. [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=349s)
- They see the App Store as a $100B market that grows over 10% a year. They expect it to pass $200B in about 6 years. Cal AI at $50M/year is about 1/2000 of it (2026-08). [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=460s)
- New projects: 10X App Builder ("Shopify for apps") from 10X Studio, and Flow, a productivity and health brand that its founder documents on YouTube. [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=702s), [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=727s)

## Tactics

- **One domain per founder, full trust.** Each co-founder owned one area. They had no real fights because each one focused on his own area. [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=220s)
- **Recruit from your network.** Zach and Henry met at a coding camp at age 10. Zach cold-DMed Blake. They hired Jake as COO after they saw his work at Study Buddy. [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=298s)
- **Decide big things with open communication.** During the early buyout offers, they called each other constantly and reached a shared "no". [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=349s)
- **Copycats cap out.** Blake thinks a copy with strong distribution can reach $10K or even $100K a month. New ideas have higher potential. He says he never answers a DM about a copycat app. [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=518s)
- **Solve your own problem.** You know the design you want and the pain points to market to, because you market to yourself. [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=556s)
- **Pat's verdict.** The opportunity is still open, but you need a distinct idea or a different spin on features, approach or distribution. [12:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=770s)
