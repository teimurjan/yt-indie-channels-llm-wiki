# Meet the Guy Who Solved Growing Apps: Hunter Isaacson

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Hunter Isaacson, who built NGL, Wink and the crypto app Bags. NGL is an anonymous messaging app built on Instagram's story link feature. It sat unused for 6 months, then one TikTok made it take over the world. Hunter explains his formula: take an existing behavior, move it onto the biggest distribution graph, and keep one simple funnel with a north star metric. He walks through his Figma files of past apps, many of them failures. He closes with his plan for a growth budget at $10K MRR.

## Key facts

- NGL has 150 million monthly active users, about 3 years after launch (2025-07). [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=79s)
- Before NGL, Hunter's apps had over 70 million downloads. [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=455s)
- Instagram opened story links to all users at the end of October 2021. The team started NGL on a Halloween Zoom call and built it in about a month. [2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=128s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=155s)
- For 6 months nobody used it. They rebranded, chose the name NGL and the domain ngl.link, and tested influencers at about $100 per post. [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=155s)
- A paid influencer activated one school in a lower-income country, so they built Android. One TikTok in 2022-05 got about 1 million views and brought the first 1,000 users in a day. [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=205s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=268s)
- NGL makes multi-eight figures a year from weekly subscriptions priced $1 to $7 by country GDP per capita (2025-07). A small share of users pay. [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=370s)
- His first app took 2.5 years with a development agency and died in TestFlight. Zoom University, a double-dating app, had a 40,000-person waitlist with a 7:1 female-to-male ratio. It reached the top 10 in the social category. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=810s), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=871s), [16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=968s)
- Wink, a swipe app to add people on Snapchat, did 70 million downloads. A PvP mini-game app reached close to an hour of time spent per user. [16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=975s), [17:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1062s)
- PopStream, a paid meet-and-greet video app, failed because a top influencer post cost about $20K. [20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1235s)
- Bags charges a 1% fee on trades and passes most of it to the inviter. Its token launchpad pays creators a 1% royalty on every trade: $2M volume gives $20K. [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1367s), [23:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1395s)

## Tactics

- **Know group psychology.** Predict what a person feels on each screen and how that drives the action you ask for. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=94s)
- **Edgy hooks.** The viral TikTok showed a shocking anonymous message, with the chance to find out who sent it. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=249s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=268s)
- **Tight viral loop.** Churned users return because friends keep the NGL link in their bio, so redownloads stay high. [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=316s)
- **Sell the result.** "Get your own messages" converts better than "download this app". Onboarding takes 15 to 20 seconds. [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=615s), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=639s)
- **One funnel, one north star.** For NGL: users who post the link and get at least two replies. [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=639s)
- **Old behavior, new distribution.** Anonymous Q&A existed with Formspring, YOLO and Yik Yak. NGL won by riding the Instagram graph and by not adding feeds and tabs. [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=712s)
- **Comfort drives opt-in.** On Zoom University, women joined with a best friend, and all four people had to match. [15:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=923s)
- **Avoid games as a small team.** Paid ads dominate, and retention has to reach an hour or more. [18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1128s)
- **Long tutorials work after the sale.** Share-menu apps such as Faves and Safari extensions need 4 to 6 setup steps. Sell the value first, then measure drop-off at each step. [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1484s), [26:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1609s)
- **Add invites to every product.** A shared link works as an endorsement. [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1633s)
- **At $10K MRR with $20K/month.** Split the budget into many small UGC and ad tests, about a dozen creatives. Track the cohort from each. Pair it with invites to lower the real CPI. [27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1670s), [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1730s)
