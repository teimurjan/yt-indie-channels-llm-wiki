# I Make $60K/Month with 4 Apps

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Jure Sotošek, a 26-year-old developer who left Microsoft. Jure runs four businesses that together make $60K/month. Wrumer is a car device that plays fake engine sounds. Parakeet AI is a real-time AI job interview assistant. Optibase is an A/B testing tool for Webflow. The fourth is the Parakeet AI Apply Agent. His main lesson: marketing is as hard as building, and organic short-form video is the best bet for a new business. He ships an MVP that can make its first sale in about two weeks.

## Key facts

- The four businesses make about $60K/month in total (2025-04). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)
- Wrumer plugs into a car's OBD2 port. Launched about 2 years before the video. Last year it sold 6,000 units for about $300K in revenue (2025-04). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)
- Wrumer content got about 200M organic short-form views in one year. That gave about 600K page visits, over 6,000 orders and about $300K (2025-04). [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s)
- Parakeet AI launched about 6 months before the video. It made over $35K in the last 30 days (2025-04). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)
- Optibase launched about a year before and makes about $6.5K MRR (2025-04). Sales come from the Webflow app store, Google and word of mouth. [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)
- Parakeet AI Apply Agent applies to jobs for the user. It made about $1,000 in its first two weeks (2025-04). [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)
- An early business made about $700 in month one, $4,000 in month two and about $12,000 in month three. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=0s)
- Jure built apps from age 13 or 14 but made no money until about 3 years before the video. He worked at Microsoft for about 1.5 years. [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=203s)
- Parakeet AI stack: Next.js, React, Speechmatics for transcription and ChatGPT for answers. Transcription costs about $1/hour, or $2K to $3K/month. The AI costs $1K to $2K/month (2025-04). [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=836s)

## Tactics

- **Get ideas by doing.** The Wrumer idea came while he built Shopify stores and bought a used car. A diagnostics device read live RPM, so he used it to make engine sounds. [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=277s)
- **Pick products that sell in 10 seconds.** A cheap, funny, slightly controversial product suits TikTok. Products that need long consideration do not sell well there. [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=333s)
- **Validate against competitors.** Someone has almost always built your idea before. Ask two questions: can you make a better product, or can you market it better? [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=355s)
- **Bet on organic short-form video.** He thinks most other channels are saturated. One phone can produce a TikTok with a million views. [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=355s)
- **Viable means it can make a sale.** He turned a proof of concept into Parakeet AI, with a landing page, in about two weeks. Speed doubles your chances against slower rivals. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=427s)
- **One channel for two months.** List about 10 channels, pick the one with the best odds, and give it full attention for two months. Facebook ads and Google ads did not work for Wrumer. [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=0s)
- **Mix and match video parts.** His co-founder writes 3 hooks, 5 main parts and 3 endings, then films the combinations. When a concept works, they remake it many times. [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=631s)
- **Find a creator co-founder by cold email.** He emailed hundreds of TikTok creators. Urban said yes, and his second video went viral. They split revenue. [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=671s)
- **Skip small A/B tests early.** Wrumer uses a simple landing page. With low volume, only make big changes that you know will help. [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=712s)
- **Fit pricing to usage.** Parakeet AI uses one-time credits, not a subscription. A banner says "no subscription, one-time payment" because rivals charge monthly. [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=756s)
