# The Hidden App Growth Killer (How To Avoid It)

## Summary

Tim Gabe argues that weak onboarding, not missing features or bad marketing, is the main reason apps lose new users. He gives five onboarding tricks from well-known apps, each tied to a psychology principle. Deliver value first, keep steps few, let users learn by doing, personalize early, and show progress. For long flows, he warns against showing a step count.

## Key facts

- On average, apps lose 77% of users within 3 days (cited 2025-07). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=0s)
- When users feel the core value within the first 60 seconds, retention goes up significantly. [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=50s)
- He bases the list on about 10 years of design work for fast-moving tech companies. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=0s)

## Tactics

- **Value first (Eureka effect).** Breathwrk puts the user into a real breathing session during onboarding, with no setup. Like a grocery store sample booth, one taste sells the product. [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=50s), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=113s)
- **Do less (Zeigarnik effect).** If the next step feels doable, people finish multi-step tasks. IKEA instructions work this way. One app the transcript calls "Stomper's" shows only three screens after sign-in, with no input fields. [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=134s), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=186s)
- **Learn by doing (trial-and-error learning).** A Sudoku app gives a first puzzle with guided hints instead of rules. If the key flow is simple, let users try it during onboarding. [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=254s)
- **Personalize early (familiarity principle).** Speechify asks for voice tone, highlight style and listening speed at the start, like adjusting the seat in a new car. [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=314s), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=374s)
- **Show progress (peak-end rule, goal-gradient effect).** Marathon, a social TV app, shows a simple progress bar at the top of onboarding. "Step 2 of 5" keeps people going. [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=374s), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=430s)
- **Hide long step counts.** Users who see 12 steps before any value leave. Typeform does not show steps by default in long templates. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=430s), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=491s)

## Quotes

- "If you've built something worthwhile, one taste of that soup is going to be enough." [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=113s)
- "Multi-step tasks are only finishable if they feel finishable." [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=134s)
- "The faster your product feels like theirs, the longer they'll stick around." [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=374s)
