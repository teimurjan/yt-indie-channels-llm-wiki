## Summary

Dickie Bush left a hedge fund trading job at BlackRock and built a portfolio of four writing businesses that is on pace for $10M in 2024. He started a newsletter in January 2020 and did a 30-day writing challenge. A viral tweet led to a $5,000 ghostwriting job. He then turned the challenge into Ship 30 for 30, a paid cohort writing course, and ran it before and after his day job. The side business doubled his salary, and then he quit.

## Key facts

- Portfolio pace: $10M in 2024 (2024-07). [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)
- Ship 30 for 30, a community writing course for beginners: about $3M/year (2024-07). [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)
- Premium Ghostwriting Academy, an evergreen group coaching program for freelance ghostwriters: about $5M/year (2024-07). [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)
- Write With AI, a paid newsletter on training AI writing tools: on pace for $500K (2024-07). Typeshare is a SaaS with templates, analytics and hosting for online writers. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s)
- His BlackRock job paid about $180K at age 22. [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s)
- On day 27 of his challenge, Naval and others retweeted a post. It reached 4,000 to 5,000 likes. His newsletter grew from 250 to 1,000 subscribers. [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s)
- First ghostwriting client: he let the client name the price. He hoped for $250 and got $5,000. [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=437s)
- First Ship 30 cohort: $50, with a full refund for people who finished all 30 days. After 50+ customer interviews he relaunched at $99 and made $30K in December. [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s)
- Ship 30 made $1M in 2021 at about 90% margin. [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s)

## Tactics

- **Do not quit first.** He started a newsletter inside BlackRock, then moved it to the public internet. He says that quitting first makes you act from scarcity. [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=200s)
- **Publish daily and expect nothing.** The viral post came on day 27, after zero engagement the day before. [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s)
- **Productize the input, not the output.** Ghostwriting capped out at $5K to $10K/month as a side hustle. The 30-day challenge that produced the attention was scalable, so he sold that. [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s)
- **Charge, then interview.** A cheap first version with a refund promise, then 50+ one-on-one interviews to decide what the $99 version must deliver. [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s)
- **Fixed side-hustle routine.** Wake at 4:30 a.m., write for 2 hours, work the day job, build in the evening. He partnered with Nicolas Cole to turn the Slack channel into a full course. [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s)
- **Ghostwrite before you build an audience.** Build the skill, borrow someone else's audience, and earn $5K to $10K/month. Then grow your own audience from a position of enough money. [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=677s), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=737s)
- **Write with an empathetic lens.** The reader cares only about what the writing does for them. Solve one problem fast. He recommends Cole's book "The Art and Business of Online Writing". [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=616s), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=677s)
- **Keep tools minimal.** Apple Notes for ideas, Typeshare for publishing, search his own Twitter for old work. [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=762s)
- **Monk mode sprints.** Three or four times a year he runs a fully scheduled sprint toward one outcome. Between them he rests in "maintenance mode". [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=823s)
- **Learn just in time.** Read only the chapter you need for the current project, for example subject lines for a newsletter. [14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=884s), [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=946s)
