# I make $25K/month from one simple website

## Summary

Gus of Starter Story interviews Roman, a non-technical founder (X: @rlakhnyuk). Roman runs Career Hound, a job board that scrapes jobs straight from company websites. The product is plain, so the video argues that marketing wins it. Roman researches his niche for hours to find "timeless" content formats. He then pays older UGC creators up to $20 a video to repeat those formats. He sells with a hard paywall and has run the same playbook for 2 years.

## Key facts

- Career Hound made about $25K to $28K last month, with $12K MRR (2026-09). Yearly plans make total revenue higher than MRR. [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)
- Pricing is a hard paywall with no free plan: $7/week, $20/month or $50/year. [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)
- More than 8,000 users, 779 active subscriptions, about $50 LTV and 31% churn (2026-09). Churn is high because users stop after they find a job. [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)
- 11.7M social views in the last 30 days (2026-09). [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)
- Launched about 2 years ago. Roman and his co-founder first aimed to make $1,000 each. [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=77s), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)
- Roman studied psychology and sold alarm systems door to door for 5 summers. He also managed sales teams, which helps him manage creators. [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=177s)
- Stack: Short URL for view tracking, PostHog for experiments, WhatsApp for creators, Microsoft Clarity, Hetzner servers, Postmark email, Google Workspace and Cloudflare. [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=739s)

## Tactics

- **Productize a friend's process.** A friend found his own job with a scraper of company career pages. They turned that process into the product. [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)
- **Win on emotion, not features.** Roman says his edge is content that spikes emotion, built on deep niche research. [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=310s)
- **Prefer timeless content to timely content.** Trends die in a week and force a constant chase. Timeless topics keep working. [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=364s)
- **Do "content thrifting".** Scroll Instagram, Reddit and TikTok for hours, in video and text. Save whatever grabs your attention. Repeated patterns show the timeless topics. [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=364s), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=426s)
- **Repeat one converting list format.** His timeless format lists companies that hire remotely and worldwide, then sends viewers to the site. He runs it again and again. [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=459s)
- **Match the creator to the niche.** For job advice, older creators with gray hair signal wisdom and credibility. [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s)
- **Recruit by DM and keep pay low.** He DMs people who already post for fun. In about 90% of cases, pay no more than $20 a video. [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s)
- **Make the brief stupid simple.** Creators get a sheet of formats and do zero research. People in their 50s with no content history can follow it. [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=607s)
- **Find ideas through creator spend.** On SideShift, see which companies hire creators, because they have money. Sensor Tower shows apps with downloads. Copy a product you like and use its content as inspiration. [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=657s)
- **Run daily marketing experiments.** Each failed test removes a wrong option, like each door in door-to-door sales. [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=703s), [11:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=710s)

## Quotes

- "The key to escape the hamster wheel in UGC and content marketing is to discover formats that are converting and repeatable." [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=459s)
- "You get closer and closer to your first dollar with every marketing experiment you do." [11:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=710s)
- "It just takes consistent work over couple years." [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=775s)
