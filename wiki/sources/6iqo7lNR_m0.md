# I Built This $5M/Year Business From A Van

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Jeremy, known online as "Van Man", at his warehouse in San Diego. After failed startups, Jeremy sold his belongings and lived in a $2,000 Craigslist van. He posted on Twitter about animal-based health and his own baking-soda tooth powder, and his followers asked to buy it. Vanman now sells handmade natural hygiene products, led by a tallow honey balm and a tooth powder. Growth comes from divisive Instagram posts, meme marketing and email.

## Key facts

- Vanman started in 2021 at about $5,000/month. It later had its first $1M month and did more than $5M in the last year (2024-03). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=0s), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=71s)
- The first Shopify launch sold out in about 20 minutes, about 100 tooth powders. Jeremy expected to sell 5 to 15. [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s)
- The team makes every product by hand in one San Diego building. They ship 500 to 600 orders a day on good days (2024-03). [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s)
- Best sellers: the tallow honey balm, then the mint bone toothpaste. The line also has deodorant, mouthwash and hair oil, with soap next. [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s), [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s)
- Most products cost under $20. [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s)
- Almost 40% of buyers come back to buy again (2024-03). [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)
- Long-term plan: own cows and bison, sell milk and meat, and offer premium meat at Walmart prices. [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)

## Tactics

- **Document the product in public.** Jeremy tweeted each tooth powder experiment, such as adding eggshell powder. Followers asked to try it before it existed. [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s)
- **Sell to a community that shares your beliefs.** His first buyers already lived in the "animal-based" frame, so the product needed no persuasion. [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s)
- **Post divisive content.** Controversial Instagram posts get comments, which push the posts to more people. [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s)
- **Turn current events into memes.** He puts his message on a fresh, famous photo. One example: Mel Gibson and Mark Wahlberg at a UFC fight, posted the next day. A post takes 5 minutes to make but days of browsing to find. [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=359s)
- **Use email.** Email worked better than he expected. [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s)
- **Expand the product line to raise order value.** Five or six products under $20 give bigger orders. Bigger orders lower shipping and processing cost per order. Free cash flow started at that point. [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)
- **Fix every customer problem at once.** Strong service drives the repeat rate. [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)
- **Stay in the comments.** For hours after each post, Jeremy answers comments and DMs himself. He does not delegate this, because it gives him the pulse of the audience. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s)
- **Start on Etsy.** Browse Etsy for product ideas, add your own twist, and sell back on Etsy. Test ads on many products to find one winning ad. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s)
