# How To Solve The App Onboarding Paradox

## Summary

Tim Gabe looks at a paradox: some top apps have very short onboarding and others have very long onboarding. Granola, the AI notes app, gets users to value in two screens. Cal AI uses a quiz with more than 20 steps, and Noom uses 113 screens. His rule: keep onboarding as short as possible and show real value fast. Long onboarding is right only if it buys something the user needs to stick. That can be a personal first experience that needs the data, or a filter that keeps only likely buyers.

## Key facts

- Apps lose about 77% of their users within 3 days on average, as he states it [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=0s).
- Granola raised $125M in March 2026 at a $1.5B valuation, 6x its value 12 months earlier (2026-03). Its onboarding has two screens: sign in with Google, then grant microphone access [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=85s).
- Cal AI made $35M in revenue in its first year, with an onboarding quiz of more than 20 steps (2026-06) [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=143s).
- Noom, a weight-loss app "worth billions", runs users through 113 onboarding screens [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=143s).

## Tactics

- **Do not onboard like a government form.** Sign-up screens, permission requests and walkthroughs that are not linked to value lose users. If the product does not depend on personalization, onboarding must do work for the user, not for you [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=55s).
- **Put value in the next natural action.** After two screens, the next Granola step is your next meeting. The app records, transcribes and summarizes it. Competitors in AI productivity add feature tours and goal wizards [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=85s).
- **Long onboarding job 1: earn personalization.** Without the quiz data, Cal AI's home screen feels like every other calorie tracker. The user gives up speed and gets a first experience built for them [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=188s).
- **Long onboarding job 2: filter for buyers.** Users who leave a 20-step quiz were less likely to convert anyway. Those who finish show deep pain and willingness to pay. This works best in deep-pain niches such as calorie tracking [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=188s).
- **The rule.** By default, keep onboarding short or show tangible value fast. If it buys personalization or buyer qualification, make it long [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=239s).
