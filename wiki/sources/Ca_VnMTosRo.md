## Summary

Matt vibe coded a citation-checker startup for students and grew it with his own TikToks. Jenni AI, an AI essay-writing tool, acqui-hired him after a casual Instagram DM. Three months later he runs Jenni's UGC program: 150 creators, 75M views, under $2 CPM (2025-11). He picks creators for "camera charisma", not follower counts, and trains them from scratch. He also explains "engagement bait", which split opinion on one clip and drove 30M views on X.

## Key facts

- Jenni AI: about $775K MRR and 5 to 6M users (2025-11). The title says $750K/month. [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=26s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=163s)
- UGC program in its first 3 months: 0 to 75M views at under $2 CPM, 0 to 150 creators (2025-11). Target was 80 creators in the first month, and he hit it. [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=297s), [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=304s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=810s)
- Work pace: 7 days a week, at least 16 hours a day. [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=321s)
- The best creator came from beauty with no UGC history. She needed 25 videos to pass 10K views, then made over $5K in one month from bonuses. [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=691s)
- Matt's first real TikTok (December 2024) got about 245K views. Before it he posted only 5 memes. [19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1164s)
- Creator Ruby's second video got 30M views on X after a screenshot sparked a debate. Her top TikTok had 1.5M views. [28:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1690s), [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2163s)
- The acquisition began with a DM from Jenni that tried to hire his "creator". It took about 5 months from February to close. [53:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3197s)

## Tactics

- **Pick for camera charisma.** Matt ignores stats on tools like Mod Dash. His checklist: emotive delivery, an aesthetic first frame (light, framing, text size), and an extroverted "yapper" who tells stories. [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=409s), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=475s)
- **Hire one exceptional skill, teach the rest.** Low-follower creators from other niches are cheaper and often have one strong trait. Expect weeks of coaching before results. [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=691s)
- **Casual cold DMs with specific proof.** Write in lowercase for college creators. Use concrete social proof, not vague claims. Reply rates were lower than with influencers, but call close rates were high. [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=871s), [15:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=950s)
- **Founder content first.** Without traction you have no proof for creators. He started with a talking-head video built on a known format: a comically long URL that names the pain. [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1067s), [21:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1283s)
- **Build "viral sense".** Scroll 30 minutes a day in your niche and 30 minutes outside it. Outlier tools like SpyTok help, but you also need to see what fails. [22:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1352s), [23:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1428s)
- **Iterate, do not clone.** A frame-by-frame copy of their creators, outfits included, flopped. Ask which one thing you can do better. [25:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1520s)
- **Engagement bait over rage bait.** A take that splits viewers in half gets both attackers and defenders in the comments. [31:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1867s), [32:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1921s)
- **Plug the product hard enough.** A viral video that does not name Jenni is useless. A clear CTA ("use a humanizer for the grammar and Jenni for the fake citations") likely converts better at fewer views. [33:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2020s), [38:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2289s)
- **Acqui-hire young founders for growth.** They know conversion, not only creativity. TikTok Shop creators have the same business sense. [36:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2217s), [39:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2384s)
- **Build product backwards from a format.** Jenni is testing a study tool designed from a TikTok format seen in another niche. [47:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2868s), [48:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2886s)
