# I built a $100K/month AI app

## Summary

Dustin Stout built Magai, a paid chat app that gives access to many AI models (text, image and video) in one subscription. He is not a developer. He took an online course and built the MVP in 8 weeks, mostly with Bubble. Magai made $3K in its first month and grew to almost $100K/month in about 2.5 years. Growth came from a 10-year personal brand with a 100K-subscriber email list, a recurring affiliate program and friends in the marketing industry. He did no paid ads.

## Key facts

- Revenue: almost $100K/month and over $1M total (2025-10). [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=55s)
- Timeline: MVP in 8 weeks. First customers on launch day. $3K in month 1, $4K in month 2, $10K in month 3 and $30K/month after 1 year. [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)
- Background: he moved to California to act, then started a social media and web design agency. In 2014 he co-founded a WordPress plugin company. After he left it, two more products failed. [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=79s)
- His personal blog had over 100K email subscribers after 10 years. [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)
- Pricing: no free plan. Solo is $20/month. Team is $40/month for up to 5 users with 3x the usage. Each extra seat is $20/month. Users can buy more usage. [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)
- Stack: Bubble front end, Stripe, ConvertKit, OpenRouter for LLMs, fal.ai for image, video and audio models, some direct APIs (top OpenAI tier), Google Analytics and Ahrefs. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=490s)
- Models in the app include GPT-5, Claude, DALL-E, Flux, GPT Image and Ideogram. Users can switch models inside one conversation. [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=412s), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=473s)

## Tactics

- **Fix gaps in the leader's product.** Early ChatGPT had no chat search and no folders. Dustin built those quality-of-life features. [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s)
- **Sell against subscription fatigue.** People do not want 5 or 6 AI subscriptions. One subscription for all top models solves that. [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=193s)
- **Build it yourself with no-code.** He had no money for developers. He built the MVP on Bubble plus a little code he understood. [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)
- **Launch to an existing audience.** His blog and email list gave him a built-in audience. He did no ads and no real marketing apart from building in public. [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)
- **Pay affiliates recurring commission.** The affiliate program was in the product from day one. A referral that pays 12 times pays the affiliate 12 times. [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=327s)
- **Use relationships.** Friends who are marketers helped spread the product. [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=327s)
- **Use aggregator APIs.** OpenRouter gives one API format for many LLMs. fal.ai does the same for media models. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=490s)
