## Summary

Tim Gabe takes one screen from a real client project at his agency, a voting rewards screen, and redesigns it at four levels: beginner, junior, mid-level and senior. He grades each level on five areas: copy, visuals, color, font sizes, and spacing and structure. For each area he names the typical mistake and a quick fix. Examples: the 60-30-10 color rule, four font sizes and two weights at most, and the 8-point grid. He ends with a mistake that even seniors make: they design static screens instead of a full experience.

## Tactics

- **Copy: clear and short.** Beginners use four words where one works, and the button label does not match the action. Seniors do not repeat words that a heading already says, which cuts cognitive load. [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=62s), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=677s)
- **Fix copy with ChatGPT.** Paste a screenshot of your UI, add project context, and ask for copy that is clear but short. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=302s)
- **Visuals: simple beats flashy.** Beginners overuse gradients and blur. Juniors add heavy skeuomorphic shadows. Mid-levels overwork visuals but make them work. Seniors add emphasis only where it matters and reuse one visual pattern, a pulsing circle, to link parts of the UI. [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=62s), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=357s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=484s), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=726s)
- **Color: 60-30-10.** 60% neutral (white or light gray), 30% complementary (black), 10% brand color. Too much strong red makes everything shout. Too little makes the UI dull. Use shades and tints of one color, as in Erik Kennedy's HSB guide. [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=122s), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=170s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=547s)
- **Save red for meaning.** The senior removes the red error-style button and keeps red for the graph, the rewards icon and the commit indicator. [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=726s)
- **Fonts: cut sizes and weights by at least two.** Go from 6 sizes and 4 weights to 4 and 2. Use a monospace style for big numbers. Reuse the heading size for decimals so the number does not collide as it grows. [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=181s), [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=785s)
- **Spacing: the 8-point grid.** Make every value divisible by 8 or 4: 24 instead of 25, 12 instead of 11. Group related elements. [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=240s), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=615s)
- **Design the experience, not the frame.** AI will flood stores with apps. So treat UI like a movie and join screens into a flow that is a pleasure to use. Phantom Wallet, Airbnb and Duolingo do this. It also raises a designer's price. [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=830s), [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=896s)
