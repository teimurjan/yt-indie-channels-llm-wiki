# The $1.8M Solopreneur Playbook

## Summary

Pat Walls breaks down how Brett Williams built DesignJoy, a one-person "unlimited design" subscription service, into a business of about $1.8M to $2M a year. Brett copied a subscription design agency he found online, but sold directly from a landing page with no sales calls and no team. The video names five steps. Underprice to get the first client fast. Set strict boundaries. Move up to $5,000 clients. Pick a high-demand, low-touch service. Add digital products that scale without his time.

## Key facts

- Brett makes over $2M a year with zero employees, zero contractors and about 6 working hours a day (2024-01). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=0s)
- He was a 9-to-5 graphic designer doing landing pages, logos and brand work before he started. [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s)
- He built the DesignJoy landing page in 12 hours for $29 and got the first paying client within 24 hours. [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s), [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=114s)
- Launch price: $449/month for unlimited requests. Later steps: $1,000, $3,000, $5,000, and some clients at about $8,000/month (2024-01). [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=127s), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=187s)
- 16 clients at $5,000/month gives about $1M/year (2024-01). [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=187s)
- Before boundaries he worked 16-hour days and was close to burnout. [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s)
- Alternatives for clients: a $200K/year in-house designer, or an agency at $30K to $40K per project. Brett turns work around in 48 hours. [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=342s), [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=403s)
- Scribbles (2019) took 6 hours to make, sold for $4.99 and had over 25,000 downloads. [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=568s)
- Info products, including the course Productize Yourself, are 29% of his income (2024-01). [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=629s)

## Tactics

- **Underprice to start.** A too-good price got the first customer in a day. The early low-paid work gave him thousands of reps, and he raised prices as demand grew. [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=127s), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=187s)
- **No sales calls.** Clients buy directly on the website. [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s)
- **Strict boundaries.** All requests go asynchronously through a Trello board. A client can have one active request at a time and cannot change it. There are no calls or meetings. Clients write fuller requests and send fewer of them. [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=298s)
- **Target $5,000 clients, not $500 clients.** Cheap clients nag, ask for refunds and churn. Rich businesses see a $5K invoice as cheap next to hiring or an agency. Cancel-anytime terms work like a Netflix subscription. [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=342s), [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=403s)
- **Pick high demand, low touch.** Websites are high impact for startups, so clients pay well. A landing page takes a couple of hours and does not need much upkeep. Walls thinks productized bookkeeping or video editing is less profitable. [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=451s), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=512s)
- **Build once, sell forever.** Turn the knowledge into templates and a course for other designers, so income grows without more hours. [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=568s), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=629s)
