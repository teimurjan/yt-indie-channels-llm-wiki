## Summary

Pat Walls visits Cardinal Mason in Miami. Mason was a 21-year-old college dropout who packed boxes for $10/hour, taught himself copywriting, and made $2.3M in under 3 years (2023-08). He grew freelance copywriting to $48K/month at his peak, moved into an email marketing agency, and then built Copy MBA, an education program. The video covers how he found his first client with cold email, how he got fast at writing, and why retainers beat one-off gigs.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $2.3M total by age 21 (2023-08). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=0s)
- Freelance peak: $48K/month (2023-08). He says it was not sustainable. [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)
- The email marketing agency did "multiple six figures a year" (2023-08). The education program did $1M in 96 days (2023-08). [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)
- First gig: a music blog at 1.5 cents/word, or $15 per 1,000 words. [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)
- First real client: $2,500/month for emails, ads and blogs. [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)
- Market size: 35M small businesses in the US and about 45K to 50K freelance copywriters. Four to six clients can pay six figures a year. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=163s), [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=211s)
- Freelancer income tops out at about $30K to $40K/month. Past that you must hire and build an agency or another business. [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=522s)
- Minimum setup cost: about $40 for a domain and a Carrd website. Tools: Mailtrack (email open tracking) and Hunter.io (find email addresses). [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=365s)

## Tactics

- **Find leads from Instagram ads.** He scrolled Instagram and noted every company that ran ads. Then he found the founder's email and sent a cold email. A company that pays for ads has a budget for copy. [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)
- **Sell retainers, not one-off gigs.** Beginners write 100 product descriptions for $1,000 and then chase the next client. Mason sells agency-style retainers of $2K to $4K/month. One email a week for $800/month is "probably fair". [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=630s)
- **Get fast by volume and immersion.** He wrote 8 hours a day, read and edited copy, and listened to marketing podcasts. Tight deadlines force speed. Adding a client does not add hours one for one. [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=412s)
- **Network with a memorable gift.** He sent Chase, an email marketing influencer, a custom onesie for his newborn that read "email marketing is ~~dead~~ dad". Chase then gave him one of his first gigs. Mason still ghostwrites the "Alex in my Inbox" series for Chase. [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=461s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=583s)
- **Use freelance clients as a business school.** Working with agencies, info-product sellers and e-commerce brands showed him which model to pick next. He chose an agency. [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=522s)
- **AI as a tool, not a replacement.** Owners hire a copywriter to think for them. AI needs prompts from someone who understands copy. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=163s)
- **Content as distribution for the program.** His day is mostly TikTok videos, livestreams and student calls. [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=691s)
