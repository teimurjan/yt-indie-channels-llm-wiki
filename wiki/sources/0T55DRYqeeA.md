## Summary

CJ Zafir built CodeGuide (codeguide.dev), a tool that writes detailed project documentation for AI coding tools such as Cursor, so the models hallucinate less. He found the idea while running an MVP agency and validated it with a viral Twitter demo and a Bolt landing page. The landing page got 1,800 waitlist signups in two weeks before any product existed. A two-person team built the app in 14 days. It reached $42K MRR about 90 days after launch, with no paid marketing. Pat Walls hosts.

## Key facts

- Launch: 2024-12-24. 100 users on day one ([8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=498s)).
- Revenue: $17K/month after 49 days, then $42K MRR after about 3 months, with over 4,800 users. The projection was about $90K for the next month (2025-05) ([0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=0s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=96s)). The intro also says over $500K ARR ([0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=51s)).
- Validation: one tweet got about 400 retweets and 300 signups. The waitlist reached 1,800 emails in two weeks ([5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=352s), [7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=438s)).
- Origin: his "12 startups in 12 months" challenge. He built 11 products and 10 failed ([3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=229s)).
- Team: 2 people. CJ does the front end and marketing. A friend does the back end and support ([7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=438s)).
- Pricing: no free trial, because the AI models are expensive. $29/month, with a plan to raise it to $39-49 within 6 months. The yearly plan has a 40% discount and partner perks with Cursor, Windsurf, Bolt and Lovable (2025-05) ([12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=761s)).
- Costs: about $3,500/month in total. The largest item is the OpenAI API at $2,800. Others: Claude API $300+, Vercel $48, Supabase $25, Hetzner $25, Cursor $20, Claude $20, Windsurf $15 and ConvertKit $45. Gemini Flash is free. Clerk handles auth (2025-05) ([13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=807s), [14:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=868s)).
- Stack: Next.js, Supabase (Postgres), Vercel, Clerk and ConvertKit ([13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=807s)).

## Tactics

- **Solve your own repeated problem.** He spent 9-10 hours per client project prompting ChatGPT for docs. He chained the prompts in make.com and saved 7 of those hours ([5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=306s)).
- **Validate before you build.** Post a screen-recorded demo on X, then build a landing page in Bolt in 20-30 minutes and collect waitlist emails. Next, DM each commenter to ask how they handle the problem today ([5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=352s)).
- **Ride an existing audience.** CodeGuide connects to the users of AI coding tools. It does not need to build its own audience from zero ([2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=132s)).
- **Content-audience fit.** Post for 14 days to warm up an audience. Pick their main pain, build for that one problem, then give it to the people who first responded ([10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=624s)).
- **"Tutorial marketing".** Write 4 threads and 3 long-form posts per week. The hook names the problem, the body shows the full solution, and the product is one part of that solution. Content that people bookmark helps the algorithm and builds trust. Assets are made in Canva and Figma ([10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=624s), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=676s)).
- **Simple funnel.** Daily content sends traffic to a waitlist page. The emails become the first 100 customers ([12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=729s)).
