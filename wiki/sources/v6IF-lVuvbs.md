## Summary

Justin Welsh runs a one-person knowledge business that made $1.7M in 2022 and expects about $2.5M in 2023. He sells two online courses (LinkedIn for business and building a content system), newsletter sponsorships, and a monthly paid copywriting email. He burned out after ten years in tech sales. A panic attack in December 2018 ended that career. He then built an audience on LinkedIn and Twitter. His funnel is simple: short social posts for discovery, a long-form newsletter for trust, then a low-pressure offer.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $1.7M last year, about $2.5M forecast for this year (2023-04). No employees. [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=116s)
- Three revenue lines: two courses, sponsorships in a newsletter near 100,000 subscribers, and a monthly subscription email on social copywriting (2023-04). [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=59s)
- Business costs: about $620/month (2023-04). Kajabi is the biggest line at $399/month. Other tools include Testimonial.to, Taplio and Hypefury. He has never run a paid ad. [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=307s)
- Background: VP of Sales at 33 at a company with no revenue that grew to about $50M recurring in 4.5 years, after 5 years at Zocdoc. [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=199s)
- He works about five hours a day: 3 to 4 hours of focused creative work after noon, plus 45 to 60 minutes of morning engagement. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=0s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s)

## Tactics

- **Post daily on two platforms.** He posts on LinkedIn and Twitter two to three times a day. At scale it becomes a numbers game: 1% to 2% of a million followers buying a product at a few hundred dollars. [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=125s), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s)
- **Discovery, trust, offer.** Social posts reach new people. The weekly newsletter and website guides show "how your brain works" and build trust and authority. The offer comes last and is not aggressive. [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=426s)
- **Fixed publishing slot.** Content goes out at 8:15 a.m. Eastern on both platforms every day. He then engages with replies for 45 to 60 minutes. [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s)
- **Themed weekdays.** Monday is the newsletter, Tuesday to Thursday is content, and Friday is strategic work such as new products and meeting people. [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=584s)
- **Ideas from proven topics.** He reads aggregator newsletters and checks the most popular videos on relevant YouTube channels, then puts his own spin on them. He captures ideas on his phone. [7:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=474s), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=521s)
- **Systems and tools.** He uses schedulers for publishing, Notion for ideas and Trello for projects, so he always knows what to do each day. [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=185s)
- **Hypothesis-driven creation.** He tests an idea. If it works, he does it 2x to 5x more. If not, he drops it. Russell Brunson's books gave him the basics of persuasion and storytelling. [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=621s)
