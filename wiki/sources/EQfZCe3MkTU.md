# This Stupidly Simple App Makes $100K/Month

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Michael, a 20-year-old NYU finance student (X: @michaelque22). He built Go Tall, a height prediction app for teenagers, after rejections from his dream jobs. He found the idea in an Acquire.com listing, matched it to his own teenage searches, and confirmed demand in TikTok comments. He grew it with one TikTok format, then UGC creators, then paid ads, to about $100K/month. The episode ends with Pat and producer Gus on "cringe mountain": success needs you to drop your ego.

## Key facts

- Go Tall makes about $100K/month on average (2026-07). It launched in summer 2025. [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s)
- Pricing: $35/year or $5/week, plus a freemium tier with some features. [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=102s)
- About 700,000 App Store downloads and a few hundred thousand on Android, about 1M in total (2026-07). [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=102s)
- A similar app was for sale on Acquire.com for about $20,000. Michael built his own instead. [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)
- The first sale came 2 days after launch, on few views. [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)
- Revenue path: about $3K/month from his own content and a few UGC creators. Then $10K/month after paywall work, and about $30K/month in month 5. Paid ads took it past $100K/month. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)
- Stack: Expo for iOS and Android, Superwall for paywalls, Adapty for advance payouts, and Singular as the MMP at 3 cents per install. [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=624s)
- The paywall show rate is about 91%, despite a long onboarding. [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=681s)

## Tactics

- **Mine marketplaces for proven ideas.** Browse Acquire.com for apps with proven demand, then build your own version. [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=352s)
- **Read the comments.** Under height videos, teens post their age, height and parents' heights without being asked. His first viral format replied to those comments with a prediction. [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=341s)
- **Count the DMs.** He got 10 to 20 DMs a day that asked for a prediction. That showed real pain. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=396s)
- **Do not fear competition.** Calorie trackers still make $30K/month next to Cal AI. Copy an idea and add your own tweaks. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=396s)
- **Test the idea against Maslow's needs.** Height hits esteem and love and belonging, 2 of the 5 levels. More levels is better. [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=456s)
- **Find one format, then scale it with people.** He posted 10 TikToks a day for a month to find the format. He then proved it on 2 more accounts, including his girlfriend's. After that, he DMed about 50 creators a day to copy it cheaply. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)
- **Use one channel for clean attribution.** With one format and one channel, he knew how many downloads 100K views gave. That set the price he paid creators. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)
- **Put ads behind proven organic content.** Paid ads came only after $30K/month. [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=602s)
- **Let users make the onboarding long.** Commenters added detail to get a better prediction, so the quiz grew long. Questions link to research and adjust a CDC baseline. [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=660s), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=681s)
- **Gate the result, retain with habits.** The final prediction is paid. Stretches, meal scans and sleep logs keep users, and the prediction updates as they log. [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=726s)
