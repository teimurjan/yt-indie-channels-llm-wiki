## Summary

Tim Gabe explains the 8-point (and 4-point) grid in a Figma tutorial. The rule: use sizes and spacing that divide by 8 or 4. This removes random values, keeps designs consistent and makes handoff to developers easier. He shows where to apply the grid (padding, margins, buttons, inputs, icons) and where not to (fluid container widths and heights). He also covers the rare case where breaking the rule looks better.

## Key facts

- The scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 and up. [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=25s)
- Typical values in his example: buttons at 40, 48 or 64. Padding at 24 or 32. [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=25s) [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=88s)
- Example button and input: 40px high with 16px side padding. Vertical padding is 10px, because the 40px height is the fixed "master". [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=215s)
- Card example: 32px padding. Width is 350.5px and height is 195px, and neither divides by 4 or 8. [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=215s) [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=336s)
- A common page container width is 1140px. [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=276s)

## Tactics

- **Consistency with no effort.** A fixed scale stops values like 52, 47 and 31 from creeping in. [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=25s)
- **Shared language with developers.** An odd value such as 51px is easy to spot as a mistake. Fewer choices mean fewer errors. [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=88s)
- **4 vs 8 depends on precision.** Use 4 for tight, related parts, like a link and its icon. A gap of 8 there makes them look separate. Use bigger steps, like 64, between sections. [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=149s)
- **Apply it to spacing and components.** Padding, margins, button and input heights, and icon sizes (for example 96px) follow the grid. [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=215s) [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=276s)
- **Do not force it on fluid sizes.** Card widths follow the column system and shrink with the page. A fixed grid width breaks the column layout. Container heights come from padding plus content, so they can be any number. [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=276s) [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=336s)
- **Break the rule last.** Small text next to an icon can look best at 6px. First try to change the icon size or another value, and keep the system. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=400s)
