# How I Built the #1 App on The App Store (Twice)

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Roger Chen, who built two consumer social apps that hit #1 on the App Store. Lobby is a drop-in video hangout app. It reached 500K daily active users with no paid marketing, after a group-picture feature raised friend density. Bro is an AI companion that floats over your texts and lock screen. It hit #1 in the US off TikTok, after Roger tested clickable mockups with paid ads before he finished the app. His frame: use ads early for feedback, not for scale. Use AI to create context for human talk, not to replace it.

## Key facts

- Lobby reached 500K DAU and #1 on the App Store in five countries with no paid marketing. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=0s)
- Lobby did not grow for about a year. In 2022 it went viral in Israel, a market of about 7M people, and hit #1. [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)
- Day-90 retention in Israel was 25% to 30%, about twice that of other markets. [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)
- The key metric was the share of users with 5 friends on day one. It was about 60% in Israel and about 20% elsewhere. [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s)
- About 3 months after the group-picture feature, Lobby hit #1 in the UK, Italy and Germany. It then spread to Egypt and Saudi Arabia. [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s), [23:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1436s)
- Bro's brand-account mockup video got 7.9M views. Some creator videos got 2M to 3M views, and one got 2.3M. [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=335s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2171s)
- Bro peaked at about 50 TikTok creators, which fell to 10 to 20 as many stopped posting. [34:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2052s)
- In 2023 Roger tried Super Movie, an AI interactive story app, but lacked passion for it. [38:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2312s)

## Tactics

- **Prototype before code.** Co-founder Leo built tappable fake apps in ProtoPie. Roger carried them all day to feel the flows. Many designs look good in Figma but fail in the hand. [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=348s), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=449s)
- **Ads for validation, not scale.** Before launch, buy TikTok ads on mockup videos. It shows at least one working format and whether people want the product. UGC programs take 2 to 3 months, and a few thousand dollars in ads is cheap feedback. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=531s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=596s)
- **Find a concrete wedge.** "Always with you" is too vague. Bro started with texting, then dating apps. [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=212s)
- **Hook and demo with a human.** Open with a real person in a scenario, then demo. A raw app screen gets swiped. The best video hid the demo inside the story: an autocorrect "I love you" to a crush, with Bro floating over iMessage. [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=271s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2171s)
- **Source creators by hashtag.** Search TikTok tags like #relationship, find similar creators and email them. Getting the content to work took far longer than outreach. [34:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2052s)
- **Network density over content.** Content marketing brings strangers with no friends on the app. Lobby needed friends to invite friends through the product. [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s), [22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1338s)
- **Group-picture viral loop.** During a call, a button starts a 3-2-1 countdown, the phone vibrates and everyone gets a watermarked boomerang. Users share it to Snapchat and Instagram stories. Friends who missed it join next time. The button lights up every 5 to 10 minutes and prompts again at exit. [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s), [25:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1530s)
- **Create temporal density.** A one-minute "roll call" call pops up at a random minute each night. Like BeReal, it makes everyone show up at once, and the short length keeps pressure low. [26:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1597s), [28:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1710s)
- **Founder-market fit.** Pick what you love. Roger loves social products. The AI Dungeon founder loves world building. [38:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2312s)

## Quotes

- "Instead of using AI to create content, we want to use AI to create context." [30:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1846s)
