# This Insanely Simple App Makes $50K/Month

## Summary

Jake Glacer, 24, is a former Big Four accountant and self-taught coder. He built Early (erly.co), an iPhone alarm app that turns off only after you do push-ups on camera. He shipped an accountability MVP in 19 days, added the alarm when Apple's AlarmKit API arrived with iOS 26, and posted his first influencer video 7 days after launch. He grew from zero to $50K/month in 4 months with paid influencer videos that fit into creators' day-in-the-life content. His main lesson: building is easy, so put almost all effort into views.

- Revenue: a little over $50K in the last 28 days; launched at the end of 2025 (2026-07) [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=11s), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=98s).

## Tactics

- **Painful, simple, and inherently viral.** If a problem crosses your mind daily, it is painful and people will pay. A simple app shows its value fast in a video. If the idea is not viral, add a novel twist (push-ups to turn off the alarm) [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=458s).
- **Pivot for marketability.** The first MVP was only an accountability log. It was not viral enough for influencers, so he took a day to rethink and added the alarm [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s).
- **Watch for new platform APIs.** AlarmKit in iOS 26 let apps use the native alarm system. Older alarm apps used unreliable sound workarounds. New APIs (widgets, Screen Time, camera) open a short window for a wave of new apps [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s), [16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=1006s).
- **Validate with views, not features.** One influencer video that produced purchases was his validation signal [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=368s).
- **Pick creators whose content already fits.** He DMed many TikTok and Instagram day-in-the-life and lifestyle creators. The app fits their existing videos, so the post does not feel like an ad and costs less [9:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=552s).
- **Winning format:** "POV: day in the life of a college student". The alarm goes off, push-ups, the app shows a green check. Viewers comment "what's the app?" and he pins the answer on Instagram [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=611s).
- **Pay for guaranteed views and expect a power law.** Most videos do okay. The money comes from rare 1M to 5M view videos [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=674s).
- **Measure spikes, not creators.** Track views per video and match spikes in views with spikes in conversions. Treat it as a black box: put in $5K, get out $10K. You will lose money on some creators and not know which ones [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=732s).
