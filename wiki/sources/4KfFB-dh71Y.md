## Summary

Ben Leavitt runs FollowBuddy, a subscription app that shows who unfollowed you on Instagram. It uses Instagram's own data export, so it does not break Instagram's terms. He gets every customer from organic search on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, with $0 spent on ads. His playbook: map buyer questions on an awareness ladder, find how people phrase them, and make plain search-ranked videos for each one. He says the method needs almost no creativity.

## Tactics

- **Search traffic is high intent.** People who search already want a solution. In low-competition niches even a weak video can rank #1. [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=252s) [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=337s)
- **Step 1: awareness ladder.** List the questions buyers ask at each stage: aware of the need, aware of some solutions, aware of specific solutions, aware of benefits, ready to buy. ChatGPT helps here. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=390s)
- **Find the exact phrasing.** Check search volume with Keywords Everywhere or vidIQ. Start with 10 to 20 keywords, ranked by buyer intent and volume. Make the video first where nothing relevant ranks. Where something ranks, make it shorter and better. [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=444s)
- **Step 2: record for the viewer.** Repeat the search phrase at the start, so the viewer knows they are in the right place. Be honest and show cons, which he says raised conversion. End with a link to the next video up the ladder. [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=506s) [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=551s)
- **Step 3: package and optimize.** Front-load the keyword in the title, but write for a human. Use the first two description lines for keyword-rich copy, not only the app link. Add tags for every phrasing of the target. [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=506s) [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=612s)
- **Step 4: stack bricks.** Remake variations of target keyword videos until they rank. Unlike Google, YouTube lets one brand hold several top results, across several channels. [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=672s) [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=717s)
- **Hire the best builder you can.** Non-technical founders: give skin in the game: a salary, job security or a revenue split. A weak first partner cost him about a year and a lot of money. [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=862s)
- **Pat's take.** YouTube search is underrated and low on competition, because most people prefer AI-written blog SEO. [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=963s)
