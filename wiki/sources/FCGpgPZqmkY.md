# How I built my first SaaS without an audience ($25K/month)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Hassam, a non-technical founder who ran two Amazon private-label brands beside a corporate job. After 10 to 12 failed AI side projects, he built Launch Fast, an AI Amazon product research tool, with Cursor in 48 hours. He had no audience, so he pitched it to Legacy X, an Amazon coaching company he once bought a course from. Legacy X took equity and launched it to its students. The tool reached about $21.8K MRR in 90 days.

## Key facts

- Growth: about $10K MRR at day 30, $17K to $18K at day 60, about $21.8K at day 90 (2026-01). [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s)
- Pricing: $50/month for Legacy X coaching students. The public price reads "$1.99 per month" in the auto captions, likely $199 (2026-01). [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s)
- The official Chrome extension has 330 active users, all paying (2026-01). [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s)
- Manual product research used to take him 20 to 30 hours per product in Google Sheets. [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=200s)
- Gus estimates the partner took about 50% equity and Hassam nets about $10K/month (2026-01). [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=805s)
- Stack: Cursor, Vercel, Supabase (database, auth, storage, MCP), Resend, Apify, TypeScript and Next.js. Cursor Max at $200/month is the main cost. [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=672s), [12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=733s)

## Tactics

- **Trade equity for distribution.** Find a company that already has your customers. He offered to build Legacy X a better tool in 48 hours, with no strings attached. They called the next day and told him to quit his job. [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=251s), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=284s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=342s)
- **48-hour build plan.** Hours 1 to 4: map the partner's SOPs and data. Hours 5 to 12: core features in Cursor. Hours 13 to 20: fix bugs. Hours 21 to 30: polish UI and branding. Hours 31 to 40: edge cases. Then a final polish and a recorded demo. [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=284s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=342s)
- **Build in your domain.** His AI video and job-application projects failed because he did not know the users. Amazon selling he knew from A to Z. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=450s)
- **Six-step playbook.** [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=529s), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=590s)
  1. List 3 to 5 domains where you have deep knowledge.
  2. Find niches with paid SaaS tools already, using Sensor Tower or Google Search Console. You do not need a unique idea.
  3. Mine pain points on Reddit, Facebook groups, X and review sites.
  4. Build the one feature with the highest ROI and the easiest build.
  5. List 5 distribution partners (coaching companies, communities, influencer groups) and pitch 3 this week with a demo.
  6. Ship at least one improvement a day for 30 days.
- **Ship to 10 people.** Build something this weekend, get feedback from at least 10 people, then iterate. [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=753s)
- **Do it for the first app.** Pat says founders who are weak at marketing can use this shortcut for a first product. They can keep full ownership of later ones. [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=842s)
