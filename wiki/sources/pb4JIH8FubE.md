# Full App Growth Guide 2026 - How to Make Viral Content to Get Users & Make Your App Retentive

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews An, founder of Flame, a couples app built around a daily question that partners answer together. An raised day-30 retention from 0.8% to 50% with BJ Fogg's habit model, "carrot and stick" notifications and widgets. But he neglected distribution, ran low on money and could not raise a larger round. He then learned TikTok, built an "iPhone farm" of 20 to 30 accounts, and used AI playbooks to post about 200 pieces of content a day. The result was 50M views and 150K users, all organic. His VSC framework (viral, scalable, convertible) decides which formats to scale.

## Key facts

- Day-30 retention: 0.8% (Sparks, the date-planning version), then 4.2% (Flame AI, a ChatGPT wrapper), then about 30%, then 50% after the daily-question pivot. He compares this with about 30% for X, 35% for Instagram and 40% for TikTok. The work took about 8 months [0:00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=13s), [0:10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=644s), [0:13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=799s).
- Reach: 50M views and 150K users, 100% organic (2026-02) [0:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=123s).
- Timeline: launched as Sparks in October 2022 (TechCrunch Startup Battlefield, 800% growth in a month). A trademark cease-and-desist forced the rename to Flame in November 2022. Flame AI launched in December 2022 [0:07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=472s), [0:08:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=501s).
- Raised a small round in 2023 and was almost out of money by the end of 2023. Flame was free until then. He talked to top VCs and a16z Speedrun but got no term sheet without distribution [0:14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=845s), [0:21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1279s).
- Background: moved from India to China in 2013 and opened the first 24-hour gym in Beijing (7 locations, 15M RMB revenue). Then worked at a Berlin scale-up from Series B to C [0:04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=273s).
- Farm: 20 to 30 TikTok accounts for Flame. His team has about 10 numbered iPhones (mostly iPhone 8) with 3 to 4 accounts each (2026-02) [0:24:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1490s), [0:28:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1715s).
- Conversion (view to download) by format: memes and slideshows 0.05% to 0.2%; reaction plus app clip 0.3% to 0.5%; direct app demo 0.5% to 1% [0:33:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2001s).

## Tactics

- **Pick the feature people repeat daily.** Users came back for the daily question, not the AI coach. Flame put it on the home screen [0:10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=650s).
- **Carrot and stick.** A morning "carrot" notification reminds users to answer. A later "stick" warns that the streak and the partner are at risk. Tie the stakes to the partner's feelings, and hide the partner's answer until you answer [0:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=711s), [0:15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=924s), [0:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=974s).
- **Add home and lock-screen widgets.** Couplejoy did this first with a distance widget [0:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=974s).
- **Measure conversion by cohort.** His early "15% conversion" mixed renewals with new users [0:20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1213s).
- **Learn the platform first.** He scrolled TikTok 4 hours a day for 2 months [0:23:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1385s).
- **Farm setup.** Factory-reset each phone, use a new Apple ID and a proxy per phone. One shared Apple ID linked all accounts and cost 120 team hours [0:26:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1591s).
- **The 300-view rule.** Under 300 views means an account problem. 300 to 1K means a content problem. 1K to 5K means keep the format and vary the copy until it passes 100K [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s), [0:29:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1790s).
- **Optimize watch time, likes and shares.** Total watch time beats the 3-second hook. App videos need likes; question slideshows need shares and saves [0:30:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1819s), [0:31:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1881s).
- **AI playbooks.** A playbook of about 10 pages has product context, winning scripts and a master prompt, plus Nano Banana Pro image prompts. Track every post by hand in a daily sheet, and feed winners back into the prompt [0:34:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2070s), [0:38:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2317s), [0:41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2501s).
- **VSC framework.** Viral: over 100K views on accounts with under 5K followers. Scalable: you can recreate it daily with AI tools such as Sora and Nano Banana (Veo got flagged). Convertible: comments ask "what's the app?" [0:47:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2821s), [0:48:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2883s).
- **Build the product for TikTok.** Eye-catching loaders and animations, like the Cal AI scan [0:51:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=3065s).
- **Small ask, give first.** He offered retention lessons in Consumer Club and got TikTok lessons back [0:56:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=3397s).
