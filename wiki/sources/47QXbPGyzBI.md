## Summary

Florin Pop is a developer and content creator. Over 6 years he made over $500K from a portfolio of small projects, not from one big hit. His income came from a course, a SaaS that he sold, YouTube, freelancing, an ebook, consulting and small products. He builds one project at a time, makes it run without him, then moves on. He advises that all projects serve one niche, so that they cross-sell. His five-step loop is: build small, ship in public, get feedback, monetize early, then double down or move on.

## Tactics

- **Treat projects like an index fund.** Most projects fail because of timing, distribution or the market. One bet is risky and leads to burnout. Many small bets spread the risk [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=363s).
- **One project at a time.** Grow each project until it runs on its own. Then remove yourself from daily work and start the next one [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=399s).
- **Stay in one niche.** A common mistake is a fitness app plus B2B SaaS for creators. Florin made content, then products, then a learning platform, all for developers [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=448s). Pat Walls adds that one customer lets you cross-sell: a course buyer also signs up for the SaaS [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=540s).
- **The five-step playbook** [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=573s), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=633s):
  1. Build something small and useful in days or weeks. A tool for yourself works best.
  2. Ship it in public on Twitter, YouTube, Reddit and TikTok. Most projects fail because nobody hears of them.
  3. Get feedback. Watch usage, questions, return visits and complaints.
  4. Add a paid tier early. Payment is the real validation.
  5. After a few weeks or months, double down on traction. Move on from a flat project with no guilt.
- **Automate the winner.** Simplify and automate a working project. This frees time for the next idea and makes the project easier to sell. His course still earns passive revenue [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=633s).
- **Content feeds products.** Content gets attention. Attention makes products easier to sell [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s).
