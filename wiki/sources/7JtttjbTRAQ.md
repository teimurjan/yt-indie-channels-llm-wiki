# Copy This Facebook Ads Strategy (It'll Blow Up Your App)

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Alex, co-founder of Boost App Social, an iOS app for Instagram growth with hashtags and editing tools. The app reached $100K MRR almost only on Meta ads and sold to a private-equity-style buyer for seven figures. Alex wasted about $800K of $1.5M to $1.8M in ad spend to learn one rule: only 1 in 85 creatives works, so you need volume. He built a pipeline of about 300 UGC creators with 1,000 Gmail accounts and Telegram. His three ad lessons are captions, green screen and wild hooks. He now runs Screens Design, a library of app onboarding flows and paywalls.

## Key facts

- Boost App Social made about $100K MRR and sold for seven figures (2026-04). [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=26s), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=240s)
- Total Meta spend was $1.5M to $1.8M over about 2.5 years, maybe over $2M in the app's life. About $800K was wasted. One duplicated ad at 11:30 p.m. burned $8K. [20:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1254s)
- Daily spend grew from $100 to $500, $1K and then $10K a day. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=377s)
- Only 1 video in 85 got the spend and worked. About 97% of creatives failed. [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=38s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=746s)
- Meta banned the accounts 6 to 8 times, some permanently. They won every account back. [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=514s)
- The product started in 2018 or 2019 as a web hashtag tool with a vector database. It had no traction. They moved to iOS after they saw Hashtag Expert succeed. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=249s)
- Extra features raised the price above about $40/year, toward $100/year. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=377s)
- The sale closed in January after 4 to 5 months of talks. The broker was Charlie from UpFlip. The contract ran about 100 pages, and payment went through a Singapore escrow. [32:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1924s), [33:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2033s)
- The sale included the warm ad account, the Meta rep, about 300 perpetual-rights creator contracts and the dashboards. An NDA bars them from the same niche for one year. [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=118s), [39:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2345s)
- Screens Design holds over 2,200 apps with full onboarding flows and paywalls, and grows only through organic channels (2026-04). [41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2476s)

## Tactics

- **Mass creator outreach.** 1,000 Gmail accounts in a shared inbox each send 10 emails a day by script. Creators list emails in their Instagram bios. Replies run at 10% to 20%. [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=796s), [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=838s)
- **Move talks to Telegram.** VAs move interested creators to a Telegram group for chat, contracts and large video files. A $100 upfront payment proves the deal is real. [15:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=925s), [16:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1016s)
- **Buy perpetual rights.** With a one-year license, a creator can demand $100K for a winning ad. Perpetual rights also made the creative library part of the sale. [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=673s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=746s)
- **Brief, then let creators adapt.** Creators get a one-page brief with hooks that work. Alex rejects low-effort hooks. [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1055s), [18:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1094s)
- **Lesson 1: captions.** About 80% of users watch Instagram without sound. [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s)
- **Lesson 2: green screen.** A person talks in front of a large app demo. Filming the phone in a hand with a second camera also works. [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s), [27:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1668s)
- **Lesson 3: wild hooks.** The first 5 seconds matter most, in both copy and image. A specific pain works, such as "How to avoid getting stuck on 1,000 views?" [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1367s), [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1564s)
- **Remix with restraint.** They turn about 5 creator videos into about 15 cuts with faster edits. Do not spin one video 2,000 ways. [29:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1753s)
- **Use AI for extreme hooks.** Ask an LLM for scripts at the edge of emotion, then generate clips with Veo 3 and edit in CapCut. Study the Meta Ad Library for ideas. [30:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1806s), [30:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1842s)
- **Sell from strength.** Platform risk from bans or doubled CPMs made the exit a way to "take chips off the table". [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2145s)
- **Build business apps, not clones.** Business users pay $200 to $300/year because they earn it back. Copycat apps lose to older apps with ASO history, such as Adam Lyttle's portfolio of about 100 apps. [37:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2233s), [39:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2345s)
