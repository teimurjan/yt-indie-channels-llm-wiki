# I flipped 4 apps and made $500,000

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Lotts Ezeike, a London founder who builds consumer apps to $10K to $20K MRR and then sells them for cash. Since 2022 he has sold four apps, most of them for a Christian audience, for a total of over $500K. At the start, he decides if an app is "legacy" or "cash flow". Cash-flow apps get built for a clean sale. His playbook: spot App Store trends and add a niche. Keep the app single-player, and make it shareable or sticky. Grow it with rage-bait UGC and Meta ads. Sell it while the last three months go up.

## Key facts

- Four apps sold since 2022 for total sales of over $500K. The apps had over 1M users in total (2025-11). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s)
- Bible Buddy was an AI therapist for Christians. Magic Music was a wrapper on Suno. Toxic Traits read personality traits from chat screenshots. Pray Screen blocks apps until you pray. [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s)
- Before apps he worked in banking, then ran a VC-backed startup for about 3.5 years. It failed. [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=130s)
- Bible Buddy first ran on WhatsApp through a third party. WhatsApp fees pushed costs to about $15K/month, so he hired an Upwork engineer to move to iOS and Android. A buyer from the My First Million Facebook community bought it. [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=161s)
- His goal: exit in 6 to 12 months for about $250K, not a 10-year VC path. [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=229s)
- Pray Screen had 60% day-30 retention. Its US cost per install on Meta was $0.30 to $0.50 (2025-11). [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s)
- Valuations he sees: 2x to 4x EBITDA (2025-11). [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=673s)
- Costs for a $10K/month app: about $1,000/month in tools. 10 UGC creators cost $5K to $10K/month for 10 videos a day. Target margin is at least 70% (2025-11). [14:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=868s)
- He has an email list of 1.2M Christians (2025-11). [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=900s)

## Tactics

- **Spot trends in the charts.** Check who is number one in each App Store category. Look for apps in the top 20 that do the same thing for other niches. Use Sensor Tower to check revenue of $100K to $500K MRR. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)
- **Add a niche with an edge.** He takes trends to the Christian market, because faith gets new technology last. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)
- **Single-player first.** The user gets value alone. Social features double the marketing work. [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=489s)
- **Share an asset, not the app.** Magic Music songs opened in a web view with a big "Create your own song" button. People made songs that roasted friends, and the friends then downloaded the app. [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=489s), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s)
- **Keep the stack plug-and-play.** A non-technical buyer must be able to run it. Messy operations and long answers kill deals. [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=300s), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s)
- **UGC first, then Meta.** Run several UGC accounts with rage-bait content that drives rewatches and comment fights. Once a hook works, put it on Meta ads. [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s)
- **Sell on an uptick.** Grow to $10K to $20K MRR with a rising last three months. List on MicroAcquire and post on Twitter. [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s)
- **Sell to the third-highest bidder.** The top bid takes longest and falls through most. He tells the third bidder that two higher bids exist. If that bidder closes in 3 to 4 days, he picks them. [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=300s), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=361s)
- **Reuse the audience.** Meta lookalikes from his Christian email list give low CPIs on each new app. He cross-sells new apps inside old ones. [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=900s)
- **Tools.** React Native for builds, a TikTok view tracker, appstoretracker.com for rank and metadata changes (ASO), and Creator Check for UGC creator payouts. [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=813s)
- **Ideas he named.** A meal tracker for a partner. A Korean glass-skin tracker. "Chill Guy AI", which reminds you of dates and gifts for a partner. [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=701s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=758s)
