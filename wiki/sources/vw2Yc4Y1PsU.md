# I make $10K/month from 28 apps

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Max, an iOS engineer with a full-time job and two kids. Max worked for years on one pet app with no success. A video by Adam Lyttle changed his approach. He now ships small one-feature apps fast, leaves them, and lets App Store data pick the winners. In 8 months he shipped 28 apps and grew from $200 to $10K per month. He finds ideas through App Store keyword research in Astro.

## Key facts

- 28 apps in 8 months, built outside his day job (2025-12) ([1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- Revenue grew from $200 to $10K per month (2025-12) ([0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=30s)).
- Over 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 to 5,000 daily users across all apps (2025-12) ([1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- Four apps make about $1,500 per month each. The rest make very little, an 80/20 split (2025-12) ([1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- His record is one app built in 2 hours, from idea to App Store review. Some apps take a week ([7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=451s)).
- Monthly costs: Cursor $20, OpenAI about $200, Gemini $50, Firebase $5 to $10, Astro about $10. Fastlane, Mixpanel and FoxData cost $0 (2025-12) ([11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=699s)).

## Tactics

- **Start from a keyword.** Users search the App Store for a problem. Max picks a category in Astro and searches related keywords ([3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)).
- **Keyword thresholds.** Popularity of at least 20. Difficulty around 60 to 70 or lower. In the tree identifier demo, "wood identification" had good popularity and low difficulty ([3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=337s)).
- **Revenue floor.** Top competitors must make at least $100 to $200 per month. He checks this with Sensor Tower or similar tools. Below that, the market has no money ([3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=249s)).
- **Keyword clusters.** One app opens related keywords for the same users. For students: physics AI, chemistry AI, math AI ([3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)).
- **Six-step process** ([9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=553s), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=614s)):
  1. Find a strong keyword in a market where top apps make real revenue.
  2. Study 2 to 3 competitors. Pick the single core feature tied to the keyword.
  3. Plan with AI. Ask Cursor or Claude to list all screens, features and flows.
  4. Build a lean MVP with only that feature.
  5. Ship it and move on. The App Store gives a new app a boost that fades. Data shows which apps sink and which float.
  6. Return to apps with organic traction or retention. Polish them, fix bugs and add paid ads.
- **Reuse everything.** He drags in ready-made settings, onboarding and paywall screens. Some apps reuse 90% of an older app's code. Screenshots and icons come from Figma templates. ChatGPT writes the App Store description to match the keyword ([6:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=389s)).
- **Stack.** Flutter, Fastlane, Cursor, Firebase for backend, auth, database and hosting, OpenAI and Gemini for image recognition, Mixpanel, Astro and FoxData ([11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=660s)).

## Quotes

- "Once the app is live, just let it go and move on to the next project." ([0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=38s))
