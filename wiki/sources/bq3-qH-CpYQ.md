# I Made $1.5M From An App You've Never Heard Of

## Summary

Jordan Rejaud is a solo, bootstrapped founder who built Parakeet Chat, an AI chatbot and messaging service for people in US prisons. Inmates use it through the internal prison email system, and their families on the outside pay a monthly subscription. He got the idea when a freelance client went to prison and told him that prison services are overpriced and poor. The product grew only by word of mouth plus a referral credit, with no ads and no content. It made over $300K in 2025 and $1.5M in lifetime revenue.

## Key facts

- Lifetime revenue: $1.5M; 2025 revenue: a little over $300K (2026-04). Revenue is public on TrustMRR [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s).
- Price: $15 or $20/month depending on the plan, with a yearly discount (2026-04) [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=590s).
- Build time: 1 month for the prototype, 1 more month for payments. Profitable from the second month [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=127s).
- 200 paying users within the first month [0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=24s), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=331s).
- About 30,000 people have tried it, which he says is 20% of the US federal prison population. About 100,000 family connections and 9 million messages sent (2026-04) [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s).
- Main use: case law and legal research. Also family messages, sports stats and many entrepreneurship questions [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=665s).
- Stack: TypeScript, React, Postgres, Redis (queues), Auth0, Prisma, Zod, Docker. He has not opened a code editor in 3 to 6 months because AI writes his code now [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=198s).
- Background: master's degree, worked on a moon rover, Fortune 100 researcher, then self-taught coder and freelancer in San Francisco. About 10 years of software experience before this project. His first business (robotics work for self-driving car and drone companies) also passed $1M [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=54s), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=89s).

## Tactics

- **Build for a customer nobody builds for.** Prison is a closed market with old, overpriced tools. He learned about it through letters with a former client in prison [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=271s).
- **Work inside the platform's limits.** There is no mobile app. Users email a fixed address through the prison email system, and a bot replies with answers from ChatGPT and other services [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=524s).
- **Split user and buyer.** The incarcerated person uses it; the family pays for it as a monthly SaaS [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=590s).
- **Validate with the MVP itself.** A landing page and email list cannot reach a closed ecosystem, so he built the MVP and gave it to his contacts inside [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=331s).
- **Accept that validation can kill the idea.** The method matters less than the willingness to validate and let the idea die [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=386s).
- **Word of mouth plus a referral credit.** Users showed it to other inmates. A customer who recruits a paying customer gets about a month of free credits [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=456s).
- **Treat growth as experiments.** Try a strategy, expect it to be bad, use the data, and iterate [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=456s).
- **Speed over stack.** The language does not matter; speed does [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=198s).
