# He's Launching A New $1M Business Every Month

## Summary

Hunter Hammonds runs Assembly, a studio that builds creator-led B2B brands. The plan is 12 businesses in 12 months, each one aiming for a $1M run rate in its first 30 days. Every business so far is a productized service, co-owned with a well-known creator who brings distribution. Hunter treats these services as a cash-flow engine that will fund products and SaaS later.

## Key facts

- Assembly launched its first company about 4 months before the video. All four public companies were above $1M ARR (2023-09). Three were at "multiple seven figures" a year (2023-09). [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s)
- Portfolio: Hey Friends is a done-for-you YouTube agency with Ali Abdaal. Off Menu is a design agency. Viral Cuts is a short-form video agency with Sam Parr and Codie Sanchez. Keyframe is an animation studio. More businesses were in stealth. [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=49s)
- Target: about $1M/month across the group by end of 2023 (2023-09). [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=49s)
- Hunter predicted that Off Menu will pass a $5M run rate by end of October 2023. [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s)
- Hey Friends "Creator package": from $10K/month for 6 high-quality YouTube videos per month (2023-09). [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s)
- Off Menu has fairly high churn, but average customer value is $60K to $75K over the customer's lifetime (2023-09). [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=425s)
- Team grew from 3 to over 50 people in 4 months (2023-09). [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)
- Backstory: he started his first agency at 19. Next, he built a startup to 3.2M active users, but it never made money. Then he built Everest, an agency with eight figures of revenue and clients like Nike and Samsung. He burned out and took a 6-month sabbatical. [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=170s)
- Keyframe launched 3 weeks after he and Sahil Bloom agreed on the idea. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=291s)
- He works 10 to 14 hours a day, 6 days a week. [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=613s)

## Tactics

- **Pick crowded markets.** Enter with a strong brand, case studies, a premium concierge experience and a trusted creator for launch distribution. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s)
- **Product-audience fit.** Match the service to what the creator already teaches. Ali Abdaal teaches YouTube, so he fronts a YouTube agency. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s)
- **Creators are equity partners, not affiliates.** They shape the insights and the service. [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)
- **Fixed scope, no "unlimited".** Tell customers exactly what volume they get for the price. [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=170s)
- **Simple premium price.** With distribution, a clear $10K/month price with good contribution margins is easy to sell. [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=425s)
- **Move fast, then clean up.** Break things to scale, then stop and fix them. Hire people who are comfortable with ambiguity. [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)
- **Hiring by role.** Senior designers come from the network. Video editors at volume come from a few careful hires plus generous referral bonuses. [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=613s)
- **"DAM" week.** Monday is decisions, Tuesday is actions, Wednesday is messages, Thursday is calls, Friday is writing and deep work. [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=613s)
- **Productized-service checklist.** Have a clear value proposition, over-deliver to earn referrals, and build a business, not "freelancing in disguise". [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=735s)
- **Give before you ask.** He built his creator network over years by never asking for anything. [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)
