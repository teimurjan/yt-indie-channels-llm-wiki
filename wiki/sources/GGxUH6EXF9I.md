# The Underdog: From Dead Broke to $30,000,000

## Summary

KC Holiday was a waiter and bartender in Beverly Hills. He and his restaurant manager, Ted, both got married and both kept losing or removing their metal rings at the gym. They found no silicone wedding ring on the market and started Qalo with their combined savings. The first batch of rings was unsellable, so they hand-trimmed about 50,000 rings with eyebrow scissors. A free ring sent to NFL quarterback Andy Dalton appeared on HBO's Hard Knocks and tripled sales. Qalo grew to $30M a year with about 100 employees, and has sold over $100M of silicone rings in total (2024-03).

## Key facts

- KC's first goal was to replace his $2,000/month restaurant income (2024-03). [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=131s)
- Deal between the founders: KC gave time and energy, Ted (who had a child and more savings) gave money. It was a handshake deal. [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=131s)
- They crashed a manufacturer's meeting at an LA hotel with metal rings bought at Marshalls and asked for silicone copies. [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=251s)
- Both founders emptied their savings for the first inventory batch. KC and his wife moved in with his mother. The batch cost "tens of thousands of dollars" and arrived with uneven edges. [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=267s), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=328s)
- They hand-trimmed about 50,000 rings with eyebrow scissors. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=390s)
- They agonized over a $99 Shopify theme. The store launched to zero visitors. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=450s)
- Hard Knocks spent about 10 minutes of an episode on Andy Dalton's rubber ring. Sales went up 3x to 4x the next day. [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)
- KC then quit the restaurant. In 6 to 8 months, Qalo grew to 13 people in a 200 sq ft office. It hired 2 to 3 people a month. [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s)
- Peak: $30M/year, close to 100 employees, 20% profit margin (2024-03). Lifetime sales of over $100M (2024-03). [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=0s)

## Tactics

- **Start from your own problem.** People own gear for each activity, like a watch for the gym. But no ring for activity existed. [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=71s)
- **Fix the product with what you have.** They had no cash for a second batch. So they made the bad batch sellable by hand and did not shut down. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=390s)
- **A website is not distribution.** Launching the Shopify store brought no traffic. They had to find people on their own. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=450s)
- **Seed product through personal connections.** KC sent a Facebook message to Andy Dalton's wife, a college friend. He offered a ring as a wedding gift. The payoff came months later on national TV, with no script. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=512s), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)
- **Use the attention spike.** Press moments fade in about 2 days. What you do with the attention before the next moment matters most. [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)
- **Move fast as first mover.** They expected competitors with more money to arrive. So they hired quickly and built systems for growth. [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s)
- **Ship imperfect, then talk to customers.** State the problem clearly, get even a poor product to customers, and ask them how to improve it. [12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=769s)
