## Summary

Alejandro and Mario built Pushscroll, an app that blocks social media until the user does push-ups, squats or a plank. Alejandro validated the idea before any code existed. He posted a TikTok with borrowed footage, and it got 80K views and about 500 comments that asked him to build it. They met through YC Co-Founder Matching. They shipped a three-screen MVP in about 2 weeks. The app reached over $30K/month within 4 months, all from organic short-form video, and they now scale with ads. They share a six-step "content-first" playbook.

## Key facts

- Revenue: over $30K/month, 4 months after launch, all from organic content at first (2025-12) ([1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=84s)).
- Users: about 300,000 downloads on iOS and Android, and about 4,000 paying users (2025-12) ([1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=105s)).
- Pricing: a hard paywall at about $30/year ([1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=105s)).
- Validation video: 80K views and about 500 comments ([0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s), [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=32s)).
- Launch: the MVP was free for anyone who downloaded in the first week. That brought 20-30K downloads ([3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=220s)).
- One later video got 6M views and added about $10K MRR ([9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=571s)).
- Mechanic: 1 push-up earns 1 minute of screen time. It also has difficulty levels, a Duolingo-style journey tab and workouts ([10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=625s)).
- Stack: Compose Multiplatform, Supabase (database and auth), Superwall (paywall tests), Amplitude, Sentry and Claude Code ([10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=651s)).
- Background: Mario was a software engineer whose earlier app reached about 5M downloads. Alejandro studied computer science and had failed startups because he ignored distribution ([2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=147s), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=164s)).

## Tactics

- **Make the video before the app.** The hook: "What if you could stop your doom scrolling addiction by doing 20 push-ups?" A five-year-old must understand it. It has a curiosity gap and it is new. Show the value, not the features: an AI push-up detection clip from YouTube, the phone against a wall, then scrolling. End by asking viewers whether to build it. ([4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s)).
- **A bad video tells you nothing.** With a weak video, you cannot tell which part failed: the idea or the video ([4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s)).
- **Six-step content-first playbook** ([7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=467s), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=497s), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=540s), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=571s)):
  1. Warm up a new TikTok account. Watch, comment on, save and share niche videos, and follow creators. This also helps you avoid a shadowban.
  2. Pick ideas with one strong visual element. The idea must fit in three words and meet a basic human desire.
  3. Post daily test videos until one idea takes off.
  4. Start a waitlist or a Discord community to hold the early audience.
  5. Ship an MVP so simple that it is embarrassing. Theirs had 3 screens and a half-broken push-up detector. Give it free to the first users and show everyone else a hard paywall. Post organic content yourself until $5-10K MRR, so you learn which formats sell.
  6. Scale with UGC creators, influencers and paid ads. Fix broken steps in the funnel before you tune what already works.
