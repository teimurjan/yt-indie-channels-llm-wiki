# How My App Hit $60K/Month in 2 Months

## Summary

Lara Acosta co-founded Kleo (spelled "Cleo" in the captions). Kleo is an AI ghostwriter for founders who post on LinkedIn. It launched in October 2025 and hit $30K MRR in 4 days and about $60K MRR in under 2 months. Kleo did not sell to the public. It launched only to a waitlist that the founders built with educational LinkedIn posts. More than 10 nurture emails, a LinkedIn Live webinar, a 500-spot limit and a lifetime 50% discount drove the conversion. Lara's background is in agencies and info products. She brought those launch tactics to SaaS.

## Key facts

- $30K MRR in 4 days. About $60K/month in under 2 months after an October 2025 launch (2026-01). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)
- The Polar dashboard shows $62K MRR and 932 active subscriptions, over 1,000 in total (2026-01). [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)
- There were two revenue spikes: the first launch to the waitlist and a relaunch. The product is still not for sale to the public. Visitors join a waitlist. [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=124s), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=228s)
- Lara started posting on LinkedIn about 3 years earlier and built two businesses from it before Kleo. [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s)
- More than 10 emails went to the waitlist in the 4 weeks before the launch. [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=451s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=511s)
- Offer: 500 spots only and 50% off for life. [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=856s)
- Build stack: Claude and Claude Code, Next.js and TypeScript, Vercel and Clerk for auth.

- Host math: a 1% conversion of a 10,000-person waitlist gives 100 paying customers. [17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=1046s)

## Tactics

- **The playbook has three parts: content, waitlist, webinars.** Build demand for a product that nobody has seen yet, then launch. [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=228s)
- **"Edu-selling" posts with no CTA.** Posts teach LinkedIn tips or name founder problems. They mention the tool only at the end. Posts came from both founders and a brand account, The LinkedIn Creator. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=291s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=342s)
- **Nurture the list before you sell.** The first email, "the problem with AI content", answered the main objection. ChatGPT and Claude are free, so why pay for Kleo? The answer: the problem is differentiation, not AI. The emails had no CTA until launch day. On launch day the CTA was in the first line. [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=451s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=511s)
- **Email converts better than viral posts.** With email you do not compete with an algorithm. [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=572s)
- **The 4-3-2-1 framework.** Post 4 times a week. Use 3 pillars: education, storytelling and sales content. Write for 2 audiences: the ideal client persona and the ideal follower persona. Make 1 lead magnet, such as a Google Doc or a Loom, in exchange for an email. [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=651s), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=712s)
- **Webinar format.** Run a LinkedIn Live or a Zoom call: 20 minutes of teaching, 20 minutes of demo, then the pitch and the link. [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=744s)
- **White-glove onboarding calls.** Walk early users through the product, record the calls, and look for patterns and bugs. Users churn because they do not understand the software. [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=807s)
- **Scarcity beat curiosity and FOMO** as the first push to buy. [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=856s)
- **Do things that do not scale.** Co-founder Rob gave his personal phone number to every user. [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=905s)
