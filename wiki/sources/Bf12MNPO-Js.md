# She Makes $400K/Mo From AI Apps While in College

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Nicole, a college senior who built four consumer apps in two years. Glam Up, a glow-up and makeup app for women, got 1M users in 6 months. Sprout, a "Tinder for jobs", reached $250K MRR in 8 months. She explains the slideshow formula, a referral gate that filled TikTok comments with her app name, and price rises that raised conversion. She also covers onboarding that pitches the app and a creator course that gets new accounts viral in 2 weeks. A newer stealth app is near $200K MRR after 3 months.

## Key facts

- Glam Up: 1M users in the first 6 months, peak about $150K MRR, about 2M downloads and over $600K total revenue (2026-05). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=0s), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=525s), [20:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1244s)
- Glam Up reached number 3 on the App Store for a few days, above Tinder and Hinge. [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=525s)
- She and her co-founder Aaron picked the idea in a 10-minute call. It was "Umax for women", based on makeup, not facial scores. The first MVP took 3 weeks to a month. [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=264s), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=378s)
- A copycat app with a similar name went viral but did not rank. Its traffic searched for Glam Up and brought $100K in a week. [5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=329s)
- Sprout hit $250K MRR by October after 8 months, then held at about $200K MRR (2026-05). Views were 100M a month under Nicole. Later, the team hit 400M views in one week. [30:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1831s), [31:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1860s)
- Sprout has a team of 10 and charges up to $80/month (2026-05). [51:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3080s), [51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3085s)
- The newest app launched in January and is near $200K MRR (2026-05). [52:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3146s), [53:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3188s)

## Tactics

- **Lock one viral format.** Glam Up posts show a plain face, then full makeup, then the app. They removed makeup from Pinterest photos with another app for contrast. Every post used the same caption, hashtags and about 10 audios. The first slideshow got about 10M views. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=589s), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=651s), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=747s)
- **Find the format yourself first.** Nicole and Aaron each warmed up 3 accounts. After a format went viral, they scaled to 30 or 40 creators. [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s)
- **Refer three friends or pay.** Scan results are blurred. Users pay or invite 3 friends. The share text says "download Glam Up", so comments rank the app name in TikTok search. Sharers still converted well. [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1096s), [19:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1140s), [21:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1266s)
- **Fake the scan, run the API after payment.** This saves API cost. [26:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1596s)
- **Raise prices.** Glam Up went from $3 to $9 a week, and conversion rose. On the new app, a 20% rise lifted conversion and revenue per download, so they test another 20%. [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1368s), [24:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1480s)
- **Discount on exit, upsell on usage.** Weekly plans cost $8.99 to $12.99. Closing the paywall shows a 50% discount. Weekly scan limits trigger an upgrade paywall. [24:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1496s), [25:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1545s)
- **Pitch the app inside onboarding.** Questions prime the need. "Fun fact" pages after a question compare the app with rivals. A rating step comes before notifications and the referral code. [17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1047s), [38:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2293s), [27:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1671s)
- **Test the hard paywall.** At first, users explored the app before the paywall. A hard paywall right after onboarding lifted conversion by 50%. Sprout skips annual plans and takes payment through Stripe. [40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2401s), [44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2647s)
- **Run creators like a studio.** Creators pass a 15-minute interview, then a course with quizzes. Over 50% go viral within 2 weeks. The content bank has line-by-line scripts with emotions and shots. One 6.7-second spreadsheet video brought hundreds of millions of views. [31:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1902s), [33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2000s), [35:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2147s), [45:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2731s)
- **Recruit and pay creators.** Recruit from influencer Discords and Handshake. Pay $10 to $30 per post plus a falling CPM bonus, such as $40 at 10K views and $800 at 1M. [47:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2870s), [48:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2931s)
- **Learn from apps, not X.** About 90% of her learning came from downloading apps and going through their onboarding. [53:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3202s)
