## Summary

Leandro Zubrezki is a developer in Buenos Aires, Argentina. He quit 8 years of freelancing during COVID to build his own products. He built Sync2Sheets, a Google Sheets add-on that syncs Notion databases to Sheets, in 2 weeks. It does one thing and makes about $9K/month (2025-06). Growth came from the Google Workspace Marketplace, SEO blog posts and Reddit replies. Removing the free plan was the biggest revenue jump.

## Key facts

- About $9K/month, 4 years old (2025-06). [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=45s)
- Over 70K users, over 450 paying customers, about 5K visitors a month. Lifetime revenue about $120K, mostly from the last 1 to 2 years (2025-06). [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=69s)
- Built in 2 weeks with Google Apps Script and VS Code. No AI, only Stack Overflow and Google. [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=269s)
- Margin about 90%. The main cost is cloud compute for real-time sync (2025-06). [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=722s)
- Removing the free plan took revenue from about $5K to $8K/month in a few months. [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s)
- Stack: TypeScript, VS Code and some Cursor, Google Cloud and Firebase, SendGrid for email, Mixpanel for analytics. Paddle handles payments because Stripe is not available in Argentina. A chat tool runs on the landing page for support. [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=676s)

## Tactics

- **Spot a new API gap.** Notion released its official API. Leandro already knew the Sheets API from client work. [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)
- **Validate on Reddit.** He searched the Notion subreddit for "sheets", "Google Sheets", "Excel" and "CSV". Many people wanted to export Notion data to Sheets. [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)
- **Mine Zapier for ideas.** Look at popular app pairs, such as QuickBooks and Sheets. Multi-step zaps for creates, updates and deletes are messy. One tool that does it all solves a real pain. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=163s)
- **Mine Upwork for ideas.** See what people pay freelancers to build, then productize it. [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=310s)
- **Validation steps.** Check competitors and their pricing. Find an angle that differentiates you, or drop the idea. Get 5 users to try it while you build. [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=347s)
- **MVP scope.** First, only move the data. Then add details that match Notion: conditional formatting, dropdown validation, and support for moved or hidden columns. [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=413s)
- **Launch.** Publish to the Google Workspace Marketplace, then post in Notion Facebook groups and on Reddit, and DM users. Marketplace search brings steady installs. [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=542s)
- **SEO blog posts** about Notion and Google Sheets are now a main traffic channel. [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)
- **F5Bot keyword alerts.** If a Reddit post mentions one of his keywords, the free tool emails him. He replies with value and says he is the founder. [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)
- **Launch sites give traffic, not customers.** He launched on Product Hunt 18 months after launch and reached the Hacker News front page. Both gave traffic spikes and backlinks, but few paying users. [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=651s)
- **Charge from day one.** Free products get usage. When you start to charge, that usage disappears. [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=836s)
- **Work in spikes.** Batch tasks into a sprint, then rest. Skip tasks that do not move the needle. [13:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=794s)
