## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Alejandro and Mario, the European founders of Pushscroll. The app blocks social media until you do push-ups, which on-device pose detection counts. Alejandro validated the idea with a viral TikTok before any product existed. He then found Mario on YC co-founder matching. They shipped an MVP in 2 weeks. Paid influencers lost money, so they moved to one repeated organic format plus a ManyChat "comment APP" automation. The app made $30K profit in the month before the interview (2025-10).

## Key facts

- $30K profit last month, all from organic marketing (2025-10). [0:01:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=107s)
- Launch video: about 175K views and about 20K downloads, about 10% view-to-download. The host says the usual rate is 1% or less. [0:14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=843s) [0:14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=861s)
- Launch offer: free lifetime premium for one week on Android and 48 hours on iOS. Android got 8K downloads in 3 days. iOS took 16 days and 7 Apple rejections. [0:22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1332s) [0:23:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1393s)
- Discord community: 3K to 4K members (2025-10). [0:16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1015s)
- Influencer test: DM sent about 2,000 times. Deal: 4 videos at about $2 CPM on 100K average views, so $800 flat, paid 50% up front. 5 to 6 influencers in total. One video got 38K views and only 200 to 500 downloads. Net loss about $2K. ## Tactics

- **Pre-validate with a fake product video.** Pitch the concept on TikTok as a real app and ask viewers to vote on it. Wait for a viral video, then build. Visual hooks such as a pose skeleton overlay help. [0:02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=137s) [0:06:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=399s) [0:08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=481s)
- **Scarcity launch.** A time-limited free lifetime deal pushed shares. They kept the iOS window short because iOS users pay more. [0:15:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=912s) [0:22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1332s)
- **Discord as a cheap research lab.** Users vote on designs, the logo and features. A request seen 20 to 50 times gets built. Members also send UGC. [0:15:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=925s) [0:19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1174s) [0:20:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1252s)
- **One winning organic format.** They copied a competitor's over-the-shoulder hook: "my bro is doing push-ups at 2 a.m. to scroll Reels", read by an AI voice, then a direct product demo. A bizarre setting drives curiosity. A blunt ad converted better than a cinematic one. [0:30:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1815s) [0:32:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1966s) [0:36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2172s)
- **Skip rate is the key metric.** Spend 80% of effort on the hook. Their best video had a 19% skip rate. At 30%, change the format. Aim for about 18%. Keep 3 to 4 formats to avoid fatigue. [0:34:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2076s) [1:03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3792s) [1:03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3832s)
- **ManyChat comment trigger.** "Comment APP" sends a DM with a direct link. The real gain is the comment count, which the algorithm reads as engagement. [0:37:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2250s) [0:39:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2374s)
- **Onboarding built on Hormozi's value equation.** Modeled on Quittr: ask for goals (dream outcome) and daily phone time (pain). List the methods the user already tried, explain with cited research why they fail, and add social proof between screens. Build trust before the permission screens. Use micro-commitments. Let users try push-up detection before the paywall. Conversion rose from 7% to 16%. [0:42:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2543s) [0:44:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2667s) [0:46:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2761s) [0:49:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2970s) [0:51:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3097s)
- **Paywall tests with ARPU as north star.** A 7-day trial beat 3 days. Weekly beat monthly by a small margin. A UGC-style video paywall won. An abandoned-transaction offer of about 80% off targets price-sensitive users. Tests run in separate cohorts: US, under 18 and 18+. [0:52:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3166s) [0:53:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3218s) [0:55:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3329s)
- **Reach the US through local creators.** VPN, SIM and remote-phone tricks got them shadowbanned. Pay US creators about $1,000/month instead of $400, and warm accounts for 3 to 4 days before promotion. [0:58:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3539s) [0:59:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3593s)
- **Pay for expert feedback.** Paid calls about onboarding with Chris (ex-Quittr) and "Brock Anderson" (likely Blake Anderson of Cal AI) gave a very high return. [0:47:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2862s)
