## Summary

Vikash Prajapat is a former Photoshop freelancer from India. He built BulkMockup, a Photoshop plugin that makes print-on-demand mockups in bulk. It makes about $12K/month (2026-02). The idea came from an Upwork job: he made 1,800 mockups in 30 minutes with a script, and the client paid $300 for the script. Growth comes from a "content flywheel". He turns customer pain into low-view, long-tail YouTube tutorials that rank on YouTube and Google and convert every day.

## Key facts

- About $12K to $13K/month on Gumroad for the past year (2026-02). [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=103s)
- Saves 30 to 60 minutes of manual mockup work, down to about 2 minutes. 50 mockups in about 1 minute in the demo. [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=103s), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=696s)
- Origin: an Upwork client expected 3 to 4 days for 1,800 mockups. Vikash did it in about 30 minutes and sold the script for $300. [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=136s)
- Sold as a one-time lifetime deal for a couple of years. Then he hired an Upwork developer to polish it into a monthly product. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=224s)
- Video economics: one video with 370 views in 4 months brought 3 customers at $15/month, $45 MRR. Another with 12,000 views in 6 months brought $213. [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s)
- 22% of the channel's views come from Google search (2026-02). [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)
- Over 1,500 recorded support videos and calls in 3 years. Over 100 five-star reviews, mostly about support. [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=410s)
- Tools (2026-02): a Loom alternative from AppSumo ($19 lifetime) and BoldDesk for tickets ($15/month). Senja shows reviews. Zoom costs $20 and Adobe $40/month. Notion ($12/month) holds the content system, and Cal.com is free. [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=773s)

## Tactics

- **Mine freelance platforms.** Upwork and Fiverr jobs show repeated work that people pay to automate. [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=193s)
- **Content flywheel.** Find customer pain, make content about it, attract customers, and learn new pain from them. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=283s)
- **Step 1, harvest pain from four touch points:** [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=410s), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s)
  1. Read communities where customers hang out. List repeated pains. Never sell there.
  2. Onboarding email: offer each new customer a custom tutorial. Requests reveal long-tail problems.
  3. Support as education: ask for the file, record a custom video, or solve it on a call. Record everything.
  4. YouTube comments: find niche videos with few views but many comments. List the objections and answer them in a better video.
- **Step 2, create.** Skip the intro and studio setup. Record the solution and speak to the problem directly. [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)
- **Step 3, distribute through search.** Pick an SEO angle. Put the keyword in the title, the description and the first 30 seconds of the transcript. YouTube videos rank on Google without the backlinks a blog post needs. [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=643s)
- **Ignore views.** Each video is an asset that brings customers every month. [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s)
- Pat Walls adds a test for ideas: does the tool make money, save money or save time? [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=746s)
