# How to Use AI to Find a $1M Idea [Reddit, Claude]

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews marketer Steph France about his "gold mining framework". The framework goes from no idea to a business idea and a landing page in about 45 minutes, with six tools and five prompts. It has five steps: pick a market, check demand with search data, collect Reddit threads, process them with Claude prompts, and generate a landing page in Lovable. The worked example finds a co-parenting niche and produces "Transition Garden", a tool for child custody handoffs. Steph then advises a quiz waitlist to check demand again before you build.

## Key facts

- The framework uses 6 tools, 5 prompts and 45 minutes, with no code and no copy written by hand (2025-06). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=0s)
- Steph's sample co-parenting landing page took about an hour. He knew nothing about the niche before he started. [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=58s)
- The three core markets are health, wealth and relationships, because people already pay in them. [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=129s)
- "Swedish massage near me" has about 20,000 searches, and its Google Trends line goes up and down (2025-06). [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=368s)
- "Co-parenting" has about 40,000 searches and a steady, growing trend, with related queries for apps, counseling and classes (2025-06). [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=429s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=480s)
- One example Reddit thread had 60 comments from 5 months earlier in r/coparenting. [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=671s)
- The prompts were free at starterstory.com/goldmining (2025-06). [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1355s)

## Tactics

- **Expand a market with a prompt.** A "market idea expander" prompt breaks a sub-niche into deeper niches. For example, stress management becomes massage therapy or guided meditation. Pick one where you have interest, knowledge or a network. [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=129s), [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=246s)
- **Paste prompts as markdown.** Markdown keeps the prompt's structure for the LLM. Steph uses Claude for its human-like copy. For a 1M-token context, he uses Gemini. [3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=190s)
- **Check demand with search data.** Use Google with the Keywords Everywhere Chrome extension for volume and related queries. Then use Google Trends, and prefer a "topic" over a search term because it groups related terms in all languages. Look for steady human needs, not AI hype that dies in 3 months. [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=315s), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=368s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=480s)
- **Mine Reddit with an advanced Google query.** The query filters for threads where people describe a problem. Read the results and keep only relevant subreddits. Paste each thread into one doc, split by "---". Twenty threads is fine. [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=547s), [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=723s)
- **Run three prompts in a row.** A pain point extractor groups pain points with real user quotes. A market gap generator applies segmentation and differentiation frameworks to suggest ideas. A landing page prompt creator writes a Lovable prompt from Lovable best practices. [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=777s), [15:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=902s), [17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1074s)
- **Use the customer's own words.** The landing page reuses the pain points and wording from Reddit, so it speaks in the customer's language. [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=105s)
- **Test demand again with a quiz waitlist.** A popup says the app is in development and asks a few questions, then offers launch updates. Set a threshold, for example 100 people, then build fast and launch to the list. [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1232s), [21:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1293s)
