## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Brett Bauman and Zack Hargett, two former Loom employees who built Coconote. Coconote is an AI note-taker and study assistant for college students. They bootstrapped it to $6.7M ARR and sold it to Quizlet about 18 months after launch. They grew with a small team of part-time creators, and they cared more about views that convert than about raw views. They also share three monetization wins: a longer onboarding, login after the paywall, and a trial extension in the cancel flow.

## Key facts

- Revenue ramp: $100K ARR in 45 days, $1M ARR in 4 months, about $2M ARR in 5 months, $5M ARR after a year, then $6.7M ARR (2026-04). No VC money. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=0s), [32:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1967s), [33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2030s)
- 2M+ registered users, over 50% EBITDA, and a small team. Quizlet acquired Coconote about 18 months after launch. [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=139s)
- Team: 4 engineers. The content team peaked at 10 to 12 part-time contractor creators. One coach covers 12 creators. [26:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1581s), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s), [18:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1139s)
- The "PDF to brain rot" feature got 100M to 200M views. One video alone got 40M. It drove 3,000 trial starts in a few days. At full conversion that was worth $360K, but it made only about $25K. [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=251s), [5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s)
- A problem-solution video ("My mom just changed my college life forever") got 18M views, 99% organic, within about 2 months. [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=514s), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=563s)
- Onboarding went from about 5 to about 13 screens. Initial trial conversion rose 16%. [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s)
- A 7-day trial extension in the cancel flow saves 27% of users who start to cancel. [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1906s), [32:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1967s)
- Chris Parker at Creative Partners was their investment banker for the Quizlet deal. [36:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2187s)

## Tactics

- **Target intent, not views.** A 10M-view video that reaches students with a real problem beats a 40M-view novelty video. Novelty content drew a younger audience with less money. [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=453s), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=660s)
- **Shock hook plus demo works early.** It works while the product category is still new, as in spring 2024. Later the content needs more craft. [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=607s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=616s)
- **Creator-only screens.** Engineers built eye-catching screens, such as a big rainbow audio wave with the app name, that only creators use on camera. [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=660s), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=706s)
- **Navy SEALs, not the Navy.** Keep a small team of part-time creators with freedom. Hire marketers, not influencers. The content lead must have views that earn the team's respect. Allison did hundreds of millions of views. [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s), [14:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=846s), [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=905s)
- **Scout undiscovered talent anywhere.** Sources: Handshake, Instagram and TikTok DMs, email and referrals. Zack recruited his online Spanish tutor. Nine months later she was a top creator for Latin America. [14:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=846s), [16:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=967s)
- **Creator skill pyramid.** Base: wants to win (shows up on time, camera on, active in Slack). Middle: can study viral content and guess why it worked. Top: creates original trends. [17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1029s), [17:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1079s)
- **Creators sit in Slack with engineers.** They give product feedback and ask for hidden screens. Zack interviews every creator and decides in 15 to 30 minutes. [18:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1139s), [20:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1200s)
- **Echo user words everywhere.** The first App Store screenshot says "great for ADHD", which matches the content and what users said in reviews. [25:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1526s), [26:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1581s)
- **Make one big onboarding jump.** Small one-screen tests gave small wins. Doubling the length with personalization questions gave the 16% lift. [28:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1714s), [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s)
- **Login after the paywall.** Let people pay before they create an account. [30:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1801s)
- **Paywall after the value.** Users record a full lecture, an hour or more, before they see the paywall. [31:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1862s)
- **Handle M&A inbound with care.** Most strategic calls waste time. Ask for a price range early. Use an investment bank so founders keep building. [34:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2074s), [35:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2136s), [37:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2247s)
- **Without a moat, ship wow moments.** AI lowers barriers, so copycats show up within a week. Pick a repeat use case tied to identity, like Ladder for gym workouts or Runna for runners. [40:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2444s), [45:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2756s), [47:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2848s)
- **Track creators with Viral.app.** Zack tried five or six tools before it. [44:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2650s)
