# How a Chinese Unicorn App “Out-Addicts” Netflix

## Summary

Tim Gabe is a designer with more than 10 years of work for Spotify and many startups. He breaks down ReelShort. ReelShort is a short-drama app that now holds users longer per day than the big streaming services. It shoots whole series in 4 days in a repurposed COVID hotel in China. Its product design copies mobile games such as Candy Crush, with coin wallets, ad-gated episodes and cliffhanger paywalls. Tim names three patterns that other products can use: the graduated gate, the anti-calculator, and the zero-decision player.

## Key facts

- ReelShort holds users longer per day than the biggest streaming services (2026-05). It shoots a full series in 4 days. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=0s)
- Free tiers: episodes 1 to 5 are free with no sign-up. Episodes 6 to 8 need a 30-second ad each, for about 60 seconds of content. Episode 9 and later need coins. [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=88s), [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=148s)
- Episodes cost 42 to 66 coins each. A typical cliffhanger unlock is 52 coins. [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=148s), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=257s)
- Coin bundles: 500 coins for $4.99 and 1,100 coins for $9.99 (2026-05). [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=257s)
- One show costs about $40 over about 80 unlocks. That is about 3 times a monthly Netflix subscription. [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=318s), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=380s)
- Netflix opens with at least 6 decision points before you watch. ReelShort plays content as soon as it opens. [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=476s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=538s)

## Tactics

- **Graduated gate.** Do not jump from free to paid in one step. Ask for attention first, then effort, then money. The ads are annoying on purpose, so that paying feels like relief. The coin paywall comes at the emotional peak of a cliffhanger. [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=88s), [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=148s), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=192s)
- **Anti-calculator (a dark pattern).** Variable episode prices, odd coin-to-dollar ratios and no spend total stop users from adding up the cost. Prices also rise deeper into a show, and sunk cost keeps users paying. [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=257s), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=318s)
- **The ethical version.** Credits and tokens (OpenAI tokens, Vercel compute units, AWS hours) feel lighter than dollars. That is normal. Show users a running total of what they spend. [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=380s), [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=442s)
- **Zero-decision player.** The player has no episode list, no progress bar, no "Are you still watching?" prompt and no rotation. A swipe up opens the next episode. 60-second vertical episodes make these controls unneeded. YouTube autoplay, Spotify continuous play and TikTok swipe use the same idea. [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=538s)
- **Map every decision point in the core flow.** Each tap, extra screen or confirm prompt is an exit ramp. Remove the ones that do not protect the user from a real mistake. [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=599s)
