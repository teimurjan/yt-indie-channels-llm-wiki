## Summary

Domenico Gagliardi ("Dom") is a non-technical founder. In January 2022, while he still had a full-time analyst job, he started a challenge: launch, validate and sell a micro-SaaS every 30 days. He sold six small AI tools for about $85K in total. Then he grew Softgen, an early AI coding app, from $20K to $500K ARR in 3 months. He sold it for close to 3x ARR, which was over $1M. He describes a five-step build-and-sell framework. He now works on Kortix, which helps companies replace manual work with AI agents.

## Tactics

- **Five-step framework** ([4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=261s), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=321s)):
  1. Set the exit goal (3 months, 6 months or more). The goal sets the roadmap and the speed.
  2. Validate with a one-page site on yep.so. Share it on X, collect emails and build in public. Aim for 15% conversion.
  3. Build one key feature. Send it to the waitlist, get feedback, fix and relaunch. Repeat the loop.
  4. Grow organically with build-in-public posts.
  5. Package the business as an asset that someone else can run. Buyers look at ARR, gross margin, ARPU, LTV, CAC and churn.
- **Ride trends from X.** Every project started from a trend or the mood on X. Timing made the difference between winners and losers ([6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=400s)).
- **Build around a 9-to-5.** He planned his calendar and used the Pomodoro technique (25-minute focus blocks). After Softgen looked strong, he quit ([7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=430s)).
- **Reframe setbacks.** In 2024 a new job offer was withdrawn after he signed. He used that moment to go full-time on Softgen ([9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=565s)).
