## Summary

Jack Friks quit McDonald's and dropped out of college to build online. He taught himself to code with ChatGPT and built two products from his mom's basement that make $10K/month together (2025-03). Curiosity Quench is a mobile app that gives people hobby action plans so they scroll less. Post Bridge is a social media scheduling tool he built because he spent 30 minutes a day cross-posting. He grows both with one hour a day of organic short-form video and no ad spend.

## Key facts

- Curiosity Quench: $3K MRR, over $60K in the last year, over 100K downloads. About $30/year with a 7-day free trial. It took 4 months to build while he learned to code (2025-03). [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s)
- Post Bridge: $7K MRR after 4.5 months and about $40K in the first 5 months. Plans start at $9/month (2025-03). [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=139s)
- Over 300 million views from about 1,000 videos. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=0s)
- One 2x2-image meme template, posted about 300 times on two accounts, drove 60K to 70K signups. [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=530s)
- Before coding he tried a book, t-shirts and a crypto YouTube channel over 3 years. [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=156s)
- Costs: Twitter API $200/month, about $400/month in total, so margins are over 95% (2025-03). [16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=966s)
- Stack: Next.js, React Native with Expo, ShipFast boilerplate by Marc Lou, Supabase, Apple Notes, Trello, CapCut. [14:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=883s)

## Tactics

- **Solve your own painful problem.** His test: is this a problem he pays money to solve? If not, he does not build it. [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=225s), [5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=301s)
- **Validate with TikTok comments.** A 60-second talking-head video drew 15 comments like "I need this". He kept posting until hundreds asked for it. [5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=301s)
- **Undercut on price.** Rival schedulers cost about 10 times what he wanted to pay. He priced Post Bridge at a level he is happy to pay himself. [4:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=287s), [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=856s)
- **One hour a day of organic marketing:** [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=530s), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=590s)
  1. Open TikTok and Instagram accounts. Warm them up for 2 days, 15 minutes a day each. Save viral videos, comment and follow in your niche. Do not post yet.
  2. Learn CapCut and remake proven formats.
  3. Put the call to action in the caption and pinned comment, or in the last 3 seconds or less.
  4. Make videos about 6 seconds long to push watch time over 100%.
  5. Add a deliberate mistake, such as mixing up astrology and astronomy, to drive comments.
  6. Double down on a winning format and repost new versions until it stops working.
- **Post daily, not in batches.** Same-day posting lets him test a new idea every day. [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=639s)
- **Spread posts across accounts.** He runs three Instagram accounts and spaces posts 3 to 4 hours apart. He never reposts the same file, because platforms detect duplicate metadata. [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=639s)
- **Build in public by doing.** Share tangible work, not quotes. Most people watch from the sidelines and focus on themselves. [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=699s), [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=759s)
- **Mobile monetization.** Onboarding explains the problem and the fix, then a skippable paywall offers a premium trial. [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=795s)
- **Web monetization.** A 7-day card-required free trial with no free plan worked better than pushing yearly plans. A 40% yearly discount also lifted revenue. [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=795s)
- **Focus blocks.** He works in timed blocks of 1 to 2 hours on one task and stops at dinner. [15:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=944s), [16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=966s)
