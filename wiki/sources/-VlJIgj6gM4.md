## Summary

An Nayal built Flame, a daily ritual app for couples, in New York with his partner, now his wife. Flame gives couples one question a day and an AI relationship coach. It went from $3K to $10K MRR in under 8 months on organic TikTok alone (2026-06). His VSC framework (Viral, Scalable, Convertible) checks on TikTok whether an idea or format can grow before you build or scale it. He runs 15 to 20 TikTok accounts across phones and tracks every post in a spreadsheet.

## Key facts

- 250,000 downloads, 50M organic views, $3K to $10K MRR in under 8 months (2026-06). [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=70s)
- Content tracker shows about 58M cumulative views over the last year. The app was in the middle of a sale at the time of the video (2026-06). [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)
- Target: the 80% of couples who say their relationship needs work but will not pay $200 for therapy. Subscription model. [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)
- Background: he scaled a fitness chain in China to $50M ARR across 7 locations. Then he helped a German scale-up go from a $40M Series B to a $200M Series C. [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=169s)
- At one point they posted 150 pieces a day to test a single hook. [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s)
- UGC from Eastern Europe and Russia cost about $1 per 60-second video, via a Russian Fiverr-like site. [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s)
- A slideshow with 120,000 views gave only 8 or 9 trials. Comment seeding lifted conversion to about 1%. [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=452s), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=492s)
- Stack: Claude Code, Claude Design, RevenueCat, Mixpanel free tier, TikTok native analytics. Each phone holds 2 to 5 TikTok accounts. [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=715s)

## Tactics

- **V for viral.** Look for a format or pain point with over 100,000 views on several similar posts from accounts under 5,000 followers. Small accounts prove that the content went viral, not the account's reach. [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s)
- **S for scalable.** Pick a format you can make 100 times without burnout. Use AI video (Kling 3.0 and similar) or cheap UGC creators. [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=385s)
- **C for convertible.** Read the comments. "What is the app?" means the format converts. "lol" or arguments mean it does not. [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s), [7:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=432s)
- **Slideshows that plug the product.** A strong text overlay, such as a long-distance relationship worry, then the app on the next slide. Nano Banana makes the images. [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=452s), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=492s)
- **Comment seeding.** They post and reply to comments from the same account and from their other accounts to steer viewers to the app. [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=492s)
- **The "ugly" tracker.** One sheet logs each account, its phone, warm-up status (phase 1 or 2, paused, blocked), channels and format. The team logs views and likes daily with notes. A second sheet tracks each hook. Winning hooks get small wording and placement changes the next day. [9:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=543s)
- **Small details matter.** In reaction videos, text on the woman's neckline, not her face, performed best. [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=604s)
- **Product hooks for retention.** You cannot see your partner's answer until you answer. Other features: AI coach modes, a gifting genie, a shared bucket list and a private memory timeline. [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=227s), [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=661s)
