# How to Make Viral Content 100% Using AI in 2025

## Summary

Joseph Choi of Consumer Club interviews Zuhair Lani, founder of Double Speed, an a16z Speedrun startup. Double Speed generates AI slideshow and hook-and-demo content and posts it to TikTok from a phone farm of real devices. Zuhair moved from sneaker and retail botting to hype marketing for his dad's restaurant. He then made $100K in one month on TikTok Shop with AI-made content and ran an AI content agency at $40K/month while in college. His rule: AI does 95% of the work and a human checks the last 5%. Owning both creation and posting gives a feedback loop he calls "attention intelligence".

## Key facts

- One Double Speed client got 4.7M views from 15 accounts in under 4 weeks (2025-10). [19:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1149s)
- His agency reached $40K/month while he was in college. He raised $1M from the a16z Speedrun program (2025-10). [0:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=33s), [27:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1651s)
- The first month on TikTok Shop made $100K in sales with AI content only. Over New Year's, his 3PL failed on 1,000 orders, and TikTok banned the shop. [26:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1602s), [26:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1612s)
- The first poster video used ElevenLabs and Midjourney. It had about 5K views and 3 sales on day one, then reached 280K views. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=0s), [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=724s)
- Hiring college creators for 30 videos in 30 days costs $500 to $1,000/month each, with weak accountability. [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=144s)
- Android emulators such as BlueStacks worked in 2023 but fail now because of TikTok device fingerprinting. [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s)
- The restaurant waitlist reached 6,000 people and the restaurant sold out for 2 months. [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=487s)
- At one point he ran content for 15 brands with AI while in school, and later 25 face-based slideshow accounts. [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s), [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=903s)

## Tactics

- **Phone farms.** Real phones run software that swipes, reposts and comments like a human. Warm-up searches the account's niche terms. Screenshots go to an LLM, which decides to engage or keep swiping. [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s), [26:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1583s)
- **Own creation and posting.** Content tools without posting have no performance data. With both, the system learns which prompt, model and format won. [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=304s), [22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1377s)
- **Tactic arbitrage.** He took Supreme-style drops to a restaurant. Reservations dropped every Wednesday at 4 p.m. in a local Facebook group. He announced "sold out" with 20 of 100 seats booked. [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=487s), [10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=635s)
- **Test before you scale supply.** After 3 poster sales, he paid a print shop a $100 deposit to print on demand. [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=785s)
- **Subtle slideshow CTAs.** A "cool stuff for your room" slideshow puts the product on one slide. A car account showed 8 Porsche 911 photos with the poster last. Prove the format by hand before you automate it. [13:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=832s), [14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=854s)
- **Pain-then-solutions slideshow.** Slide one names the pain. The next slides give high-effort fixes, such as weighing every meal. The app appears as the easy fix. [16:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=962s), [16:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=980s)
- **AI face plus image bank.** Slide one uses a synthetic person from a trained LoRA (Flux, Higgsfield). Other slides come from a bank of about 50 images per slot, which supports about 300 slideshows. [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1018s), [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1055s)
- **Clone a winning TikTok, then vary it.** Paste a TikTok link to make a template. Change hooks, images and text so the copy has a chance to win. Find formats by searching your niche for hits from the last 1 to 2 months. [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1211s), [24:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1477s)
- **Skip creators for hook-and-demo.** Auto-match stock hooks with app screen recordings and add copy. [22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1377s)
- **Vary accounts, but not too much.** Give each account its own niche terms, such as self-care or motivation, so you test something real. [25:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1508s), [25:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1556s)
- **Formats decay.** One strategy works for a few months, a year at most. Mass creator accounts have run for 2 to 3 years, and big brands such as Duolingo now use them. [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s)

## Quotes
