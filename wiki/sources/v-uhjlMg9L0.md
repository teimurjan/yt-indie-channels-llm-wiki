# I sold my company

## Summary

Pat Walls documents the day he sold Starter Story. He flies to New York, visits his old apartment, the Starbucks where he did morning deep work, and the office where he quit his job. He talks through why he sold after he had said online that he never planned to sell: the business had become his identity and a cage. HubSpot, a public company, acquired Starter Story. He does not share the price but calls it life-changing.

## Key facts

- About 10 years before the sale, Pat moved to New York for a six-figure developer job at a small startup. [0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=37s), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=86s)
- He put in two hours of deep work at a Starbucks each morning before work, for about a year. Stripe revenue went from zero to a few hundred dollars. [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=138s), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=192s)
- He quit with about $12,000 in savings. [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s)
- His first hire was his sister. [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s)
- HubSpot acquired Starter Story (2026-02). The team, mission and format stay the same, with more resources for interviews, shows, production and tools. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s)
- The sale price is private. Pat calls it "life-changing" (2026-02). [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=573s)

## Tactics

- **Build before work, every day.** Two hours each morning on writing, coding, outreach and founder interviews. Consistency mattered more than any single result. [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=138s)
- **Ship daily and let results compound.** Growth "never exploded overnight." Content, interviews and code accumulated until people noticed. [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s)
- **Pick a buyer for alignment, not only price.** Pat chose HubSpot because it wanted to scale Starter Story without changing it. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s)
- **Ask the hard question before you sell.** Is it right on paper, or are you tired? He decided the company had grown beyond him, and that he was the one who held it back. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=357s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=396s)
- **Watch for founder identity lock-in.** The business he built for freedom took over every minute of his life. [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=295s)

## Quotes
