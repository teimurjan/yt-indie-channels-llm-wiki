# The Secrets of Consumer Club: Insights from Joseph Choi

## Summary

Taylor Fox of Superwall interviews Joseph Choi, founder of Consumer Club. Consumer Club is a Discord community where app founders share distribution tactics, mostly for organic TikTok. Joseph's thesis: distribution can beat product, and tactics flow from affiliate to e-commerce to consumer apps to B2B. He walks through TikTok formats, comment-section conversion, a tool that finds outlier videos, and the start of AI avatar content. He advises founders to learn a new channel themselves rather than hire an agency.

## Key facts

- Consumer Club members range from solo indie devs with millions in ARR to venture-backed founders (2025-04). [0:02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=171s)
- Tabs Chocolate went from $0 to $10M in 1 year with organic TikTok only. Teenagers posted on new zero-follower accounts. Its founder, Oliver Brocato, then started Study Buddy to use the same playbook in software. [0:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=357s)
- Joseph says Cal AI makes about $2M/month (2025-04). The host guesses "$25M a year". [0:42:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2546s), [0:48:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2903s)
- A New York founder dinner had a $400K MRR study app (2025-04). It also had a dating app with 300K downloads from organic TikTok and $0 spent on ads (2025-04). [0:35:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2120s)
- Joseph advises Alex Lieberman of Morning Brew on a new app, built with a co-founder from the club. [1:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4020s)
- Joseph's first video on a new TikTok account got 50K views with no retention edits. [0:58:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=3532s)
- Format timeline from his tweet: influencers (2021), mass UGC (2022), slideshows (2023), HeyGen avatars over slides (2024), Flux avatars (2025). All of them still work. [0:49:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2973s)

## Tactics

- **TikTok is market research.** Comments show unfiltered pain points. [0:04:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=254s), [0:20:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1255s)
- **App demo formats.** Use a talking head over a green-screen app image, or film the phone with another phone. Slideshow "story time" carousels are cheap to make. One shower-head carousel got about 200K likes. Accounts that post daily get 5K to 10K views per video, with some hits at 1M to 2M. [0:12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=728s)
- **Soft calls to action.** People rarely click bio links. Viewers close TikTok and search the App Store by name. Cal AI only flashes the app on screen. [0:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=966s)
- **Convert in the comments.** Focus Tree flashes its screen for a few seconds in faceless "study-tok" videos. Viewers ask in the comments, and the creator names the app. [0:17:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1027s), [0:18:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1082s)
- **Video replies work like retargeting.** A video reply to "what app is this?" reaches people who engaged with the first video. That reply can carry the hard sell. [0:20:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1205s)
- **Build the viral moment into the product.** Cal AI keeps users with tracking, but the photo scan is the viral feature. [0:22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1363s)
- **Find outliers.** His tool ranks videos by views compared with the account's median. Search adjacent niches too. One format, a 7-second loop of long text over B-roll, gets about 5x watch time. [0:24:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1496s), [0:26:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1595s)
- **Pay creators to copy, not to create.** Give a second-by-second brief of a proven video. This avoids $1K to $2K per video for an influencer's creative work. [0:29:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1766s)
- **Scale posting across accounts.** Three posts a day per account avoids shadowbans. One founder runs 5 accounts per phone, which gives 15 posts a day per creator. He gave creators in the Philippines iPhones with US SIM cards. [0:36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2171s)
- **Seed a tribe.** NGL followed high-school students from private accounts with the school name and logo. Many students followed back before launch. [0:39:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2391s)
- **Niche down on new models.** Each new AI model enables many wrappers. A vegan Cal AI at 1/10 of the revenue still makes about $200K/month. [0:42:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2546s)
- **Start with the channel.** Know what the content looks like before you build, to de-risk the product. [0:45:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2753s)
- **Be careful with agencies.** Experts in a new channel usually build their own apps. Agencies in this space rarely last more than about 6 months. [1:18:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4703s), [1:19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4764s)
- **Web-to-app funnels.** Affiliate and e-commerce people send users through a web onboarding and payment, then to the iOS app. Superwall was building this product. [1:23:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=5003s)
- **Start test.** Pick 3 content formats from niche videos that already work, and copy them. Getting to 5K views means there is something there. [1:29:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=5381s)
