# I Make $845,601 Writing This Online...

## Summary

Pat Walls of Starter Story visits Alex Garcia in Austin, Texas. Alex runs Marketing Examined and four sister newsletters, all paid for by ad sponsors. He learned the trade at The Hustle and then grew his own list with daily Twitter threads. He gives a blueprint for the first 1,000 subscribers, a simple tool stack, and a way to find sponsors. His main thesis: a newsletter is the top of the funnel for any other business you want to build.

## Key facts

- Five newsletters: Marketing Examined, Content Examined, Social Examined, Landing Page Examined and DTC Examined. Together they have 150K to 160K subscribers (2023-07). [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)
- Revenue is about $65K to $70K/month, all from ad sponsors (2023-07). A services line that he is turning into a product makes $15K to $20K more (2023-07). [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)
- He worked at The Hustle for about one year before he left to build his own newsletter. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=94s)
- He cites Morning Brew: it sold part of the company for $75M cash and does over $100M in revenue. He guesses The Hustle made $20M to $30M from one newsletter plus a paid product. [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=133s)
- He wrote 50 marketing threads in 50 days on Twitter. His followers went from 500 to 40,000, and the newsletter went from about 2,000 to 8,000 or 9,000 subscribers. [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)
- Pricing rule of thumb: with 10,000 subscribers you can sell an ad slot for more than $1,000. [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)
- Core tools: Zapier, Notion, Slack and an email service provider (ESP). [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=453s)
- He pays one writer per niche newsletter. [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=482s)

## Tactics

- **Pick a niche two ways.** Write about what you already read and research in your free time. Or, to build and sell, pick a topic that is exploding. [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=176s)
- **"Hand-to-hand combat" first.** A tactic from Noah Kagan: do things that do not scale. DM people on LinkedIn, Twitter and Instagram, ask friends to share, and run small giveaways that add 10 or 15 subscribers each. [4:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=273s)
- **Then "buffet marketing".** Try many channels, find the ones with 10x potential, then focus on the one or two that drive growth. [4:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=273s)
- **Find topics nobody covers.** Use a tool that lists the most-shared articles on a topic. Use Twitter advanced search filters to find hidden marketing campaigns. [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=365s)
- **Monetize with sponsors first.** Treat the list like an ad platform with reach and return on ad spend. A second option is a paid tier, as in Lenny's Newsletter at about $8.99 to $10.99. Alex adds services as a test bed for a product. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=396s)
- **Find writers deep in Google.** Search a narrow topic and read pages 2, 3 and 4 of the results. When you find a good writer, do everything to keep them. [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=482s)
- **Three growth channels.** First, Twitter threads push readers to the newsletter. Second, paid ads on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit reuse the CTAs that won on Twitter. Third, cross-promotion networks such as SparkLoop, Beehiiv and ConvertKit take a set budget, for example $10K/month. [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=508s)
- **Get sponsors inbound and outbound.** Make it easy to buy an ad slot on the site. For outbound, list the advertisers in other newsletters in your niche and pitch them, because they already pay for newsletter ads. [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)
- **Become a better marketer.** Learn from someone ahead of you. Build small products and try to get each to $1,000 in sales. Trust intuition, not only data. [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=609s)
