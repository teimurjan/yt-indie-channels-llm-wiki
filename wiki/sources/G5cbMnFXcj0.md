## Summary

Charlie Chang, 31, runs six YouTube channels from Southern California that make $200K to $250K a month, plus Paired, an overseas staffing company. He tried many side hustles, then started making finance videos in 2018. In 2020 he earned $750 in AdSense in one day, and the channel took off. Affiliate marketing is his biggest revenue source. He now has a 26-person media team built mostly from overseas talent, and he wants to grow Paired toward an acquisition.

## Key facts

- YouTube revenue: $200K to $250K in a typical month, close to $300K in the best month (2024-02). Over $2.4M a year. [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)
- Channels: Charlie Chang with about 1.1M subscribers, a second channel with about 50,000, plus Charlie Chang Show and newer channels with about 600 subscribers (2024-02). [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)
- Revenue mix: affiliate 50% to 55%, brand sponsorships 20% to 25%, AdSense 20% to 25% (2024-02). [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)
- Paired: low six figures, started in 2023 (2024-02). It charges a one-time placement fee. A full-time hire costs about $600 to $1,000/month. Recruiters screen 300 to 500 people to present one or two. [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)
- Media team: 26 people at about $20K to $25K/month (2024-02): Charlie, a COO, 2 to 3 project managers, editors, thumbnail, graphics and web people. [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)
- Breakout in 2020: 500 to 1,000 new subscribers a day and $10K to $20K/month in AdSense, all done solo. [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=240s)

## Tactics

- **Make videos people search for.** Much of his channel is evergreen search content, such as "how to invest in stocks", not only viral attempts. [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)
- **Title formula.** Use a relatable age, a big number and a dollar amount, for example "10 sources of income". [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)
- **Ride trends with your niche.** He shifted to crypto, stocks and side hustles as each topic boomed. His Coachella VIP video combined a rare experience, a trending event and strong storytelling. [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=349s), [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)
- **Explain in plain terms.** Most viewers are not technical, so he breaks information down. An introvert can learn this in one to two years. [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=290s)
- **Repurpose long-form into shorts.** He uses Submagic, Descript and Opus to cut short clips from long videos. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=430s), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=477s)
- **Production stack.** ClickUp holds the content plan. Editors post drafts to Frame.io for review. 1of10 and Creator Hooks Pro give title and thumbnail ideas. The team uses Slack, and Hubstaff for time tracking. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=430s)
- **Hire overseas.** He finds people via Upwork, Fiverr and OnlineJobs, and now mostly via Paired. [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)
- **Use the audience to build equity.** He started Paired to own equity in a traditional business and grow it with his channels. [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)
- **Start with a service.** He recommends learning a high-value skill and starting an agency or service business, which needs little capital. [9:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=586s)
