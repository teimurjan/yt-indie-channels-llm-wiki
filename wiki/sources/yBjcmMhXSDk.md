## Summary

Mau Baron is a finance graduate who taught himself to code with AI. He built Prayer Lock, an iOS app that blocks your phone until you pray. He coded it in 3 days with Cursor and ChatGPT, and it reached $21K/month 6 months after launch (2026-01). He copied the idea and the distribution of a competitor, PrayScreen. Growth came from UGC "girl reaction" videos posted in volume, a longer onboarding, and TikTok Spark Ads on organic winners.

## Key facts

- Monthly sales: $10K in October, $15K in November, $21K in December 2025. Growth over 50% a month. He expects about $40K in January (2026-01). [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=685s)
- About 58K downloads, 6.7K ratings at 4.9 stars, review rate over 12% (2026-01). [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=71s), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s)
- Pricing: $49.99/year or $9.99/week. Conversion rate 43%. Proceeds per paying user $23.60. Total proceeds $41K (2026-01). [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s)
- Reached number 22 in the Reference category in November 2025. [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s)
- Built in June 2025: 3 days to code, over a week for App Store approval. No login and no database. All data stays on the device. [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=236s), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=294s)
- Ad spend: about $360/day. In December he spent about $9K and made about $20K. Cost per trial is $1.88 (2026-01). [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=899s)
- Stack and monthly cost (2026-01): [14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=844s)
  - Build: Cursor $20, ChatGPT with Codex CLI $20, a design tool $25.
  - Analytics and paywalls: PostHog about $10, Superwall $0.20 per conversion.
  - Ads: Singular MMP, $0.05 per conversion after 15K free conversions.
  - Backend and AI: AWS about $2, Gemini 2.5 Flash about $9.

## Tactics

- **Copy and improve.** After 3 months of apps nobody wanted, he found PrayScreen. He studied its design, features and distribution and decided he was able to do better. [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=170s)
- **No mockups.** He put the best onboarding screens of competitors in a Canva file and picked the best ones. Build in blocks. Do not try to one-shot an app. [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=294s)
- **Volume on organic.** He posted about 40 times a day across 12 accounts. His girlfriend recorded reaction videos. The first hook that worked came after 40 posts. 10K views a day gave 200 to 300 downloads a day. [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=426s), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=488s)
- **Longer onboarding.** He first optimized ads for downloads and failed. He grew onboarding from 5 to 15 minutes, and organic conversion rose more than 3 times. [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s)
- **TikTok over Meta.** Meta at $20/day gave about $10 per trial. A broad TikTok campaign, US only, with his best UGC videos gave $4 per trial. He put about $4K of savings into it. [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s)
- **Spark Ads on a creator.** A 15-year-old UGC creator, Matthew, got about 10K organic views per video. Spark Ads on his videos cut cost to $3 per trial, so Mau spent another $6K. [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s)
- **2026 playbook:** [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=700s), [12:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=748s)
  1. Find niches with apps over $50K/month in Sensor Tower.
  2. Pick an app you like and improve it a lot.
  3. Code it in less than 2 weeks.
  4. Then stop changing the app. Copy working formats and post at least 20 times a day.
  5. Iterate on onboarding until download-to-trial conversion is at least 10%.
  6. Put TikTok Spark Ads on organic videos with more than 10K views.
