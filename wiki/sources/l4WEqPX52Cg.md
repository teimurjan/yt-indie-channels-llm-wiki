## Summary

Joseph Lee founded Supademo, an AI tool that makes interactive, clickable product demos. In about 2.5 years it reached over 150K users and over $3M ARR, about $250K MRR (2026-03). He shares a three-step playbook for the first 100 SaaS customers. First, capture existing demand with SEO across the funnel. Second, do things that do not scale, such as making free demos for founders on Reddit. Third, be present in every channel where users are.

## Key facts

- Over $3M ARR, just over $250K MRR, over 150K users, about 2.5 years after launch (2026-03). [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=97s)
- Named G2's number 5 fastest-growing product in 2025. [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s)
- Joseph scaled two products to over $3M ARR. Before Supademo he ran a venture-funded B2B seafood marketplace. [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=59s), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=162s)
- Product-led growth: most revenue comes from the Starter and Premium self-serve tiers. [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=97s)
- Validation: he talked to more than 100 B2B SaaS founders. They hated making demos and hated hearing their own voice. [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=229s)
- Free tools bring about 20% of traffic and convert 15% to 20% of visitors into sign-ups (2026-03). [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=354s)
- Traffic mix (2026-03): 30% to 40% from SEO and LLMs, about 30% from word of mouth, watermarks and referrals, about 20% from building in public on LinkedIn. [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)
- Stack: Claude Code, Cursor and Codex, Linear, AWS, Postmark, Intercom, Ahrefs, Clay, Zapier. They rotate through 50+ tools a month. [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=759s)

## Tactics

- **Capture existing demand first.** Write SEO content for the top, middle and bottom of the funnel. [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s)
  - Bottom: comparison pages against every competitor you can find, to piggyback on their traffic. Ship an imperfect version 1.0 fast to get ranked and cited in LLMs. Then optimize the pages that LLMs cite.
  - Middle: dozens of free tools in adjacent spaces, such as screenshots and SOPs. Parts of the product are ungated, with no sign-up or trial. [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=354s)
  - Top: programmatic how-to pages with an embedded interactive demo, such as "how to export Figma to PDF" or "how to merge cells in Excel". [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=452s)
- **Do things that do not scale.** On Reddit and Indie Hackers, Joseph asked founders to post their product URL. He made a free demo for each one and replied with it in public. Other readers saw the demos and signed up too. [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=483s)
- **Distribution density.** There is no single magic channel in 2026. Combine SEO and LLM content, communities, direct outreach, product updates, and building in public on LinkedIn and Indie Hackers. [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)
- **Built-in virality.** Watermarks on shared demos create a viral loop. [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)
- **Launch early.** The common mistake is chasing perfection. Speed and urgency are the only edge a founder has over an incumbent. [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=618s)
