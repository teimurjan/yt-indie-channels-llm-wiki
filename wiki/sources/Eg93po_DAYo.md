# She Built This $340K/Month App in 60 Days

## Summary

Sarah Pearl ("Hot High Priestess") built Stella, an AI manifestation app. Stella makes custom visualizations in the voice of your future self and gives daily custom affirmations. It passed $300K MRR within 2 months of launch (2026-07). Sarah is a creator with millions of followers. She vibe-coded the MVP with Claude and Google AI Studio. Her thesis: distribution beats product, women own distribution, and AI coding tools remove the technical barrier.

## Key facts

- Stella: over $300K MRR in 2 months, and about $350K to $360K MRR at recording (2026-07). The title says $340K/month. [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s)
- 12,000 paying customers and over 200,000 downloads (2026-07). [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s)
- Audience: 1.2M Instagram followers and 2.8M TikTok followers, with over 1B combined TikTok views (2026-07). [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=150s)
- A new TikTok account she started after launch grew to over 40,000 followers in a couple of months. Another spam-post account has over 700,000 (2026-07). [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=169s)
- She also runs a TikTok Shop brand that makes "seven figures a month" (2026-07). [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=220s)
- MVP in about 2 days. Full app in 2 to 3 months. Tools: Claude, Claude Code and Google AI Studio. [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=399s)
- The Starter Story audience is 93% to 94% male (2026-07). [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=429s)

## Tactics

- **Virality is a skill, not an audience.** She claims she can grow any new account to 50,000 followers in 3 to 4 months. [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=169s)
- **Build for women.** Many apps ignore female problems because men designed them. She found no app that made custom visualizations or meditations. [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=220s), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=315s)
- **Speed first.** Ship an MVP fast and test it. [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=399s)
- **The viral recipe.** Pick a niche and find its top content. Study the psychology, then recreate winning videos "to infinity". She reuses the same viral formats year after year. [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=559s)
- **Insert the product into a proven video.** Work backward from a viral video. Ask which product fits it so well that viewers leave TikTok to download it. [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=559s)
- **Skit plus demo.** She reads affirmations. A man asks "How can I remember all these?" She answers with Stella's custom lock-screen affirmations. [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=633s)
- **Make savable content.** "Repeat these affirmations for 7 days" makes people save the video. Saves raise engagement. [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=656s)
- **ManyChat for Instagram.** "Comment DOWNLOAD" triggers a DM with the app link. The DM also asks for an App Store review. [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=656s)
- **Show the use case.** Videos that mention Stella but do not demo it convert worse. One demo video got only 300K views but converted very well. Balance reach videos with demo videos. [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=717s)
- **Use hate.** Her most controversial videos perform best, so she treats criticism as an algorithm boost. [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=489s)
- **Content first, product second.** Read the comments for "I wish this existed" and build that. Pat Walls gives the same advice: build content before you pick an app. [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=777s), [14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=850s)
- **Invent, do not copy.** She says Stella worked because nothing like it existed. [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=830s)
