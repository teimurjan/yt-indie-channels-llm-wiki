# 5 Tools I Wish I Found Sooner (For Designers)

## Summary

Tim Gabe is a designer and design-agency owner. He lists five tools that he uses every day: Contra, Lovable, Mobbin, Kortex and Serif. Some of the tools sponsor the video, but he says that he contacted them himself after he picked the list. His main point is that designers can raise their rates with AI full-stack builders like Lovable. He also likes Mobbin, which replaces hours of manual screenshot research.

## Key facts

- **Contra** is a professional network for freelance creative work. His agency hires top Rive animators through it. Rive is a Contra partner tool and a high-value skill (2025-04). [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=43s)
- **Lovable** makes full-stack coding easy, as Framer did for pixel-perfect design. Some agencies built on Lovable make over $200K per month from internal tools for companies (2025-04). [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=99s)
- He built an internal tool for his brother's company on his phone in about 3 hours. [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=99s)
- **Mobbin** has a library of 1,000+ apps. You can filter by app, user flow or UI element, and you can text-search inside screens (2025-04). [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=174s)
- **Kortex**, by Dan Koe, is a second-brain note tool. Its AI assistant "Kai" answers from your linked notes, PDFs, articles and videos. [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=272s)
- **Serif** is an AI email assistant. It needs about 20 minutes to learn your voice and sort your inbox. Then it drafts replies in your style. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=390s)

## Tactics

- **Offer full-stack builds as a designer.** Designers who add Lovable development to design work can stand out and charge more. [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=99s)
- **Learn a rising tool early.** Rive animation is a skill that is quickly gaining value. Contra lists it as an official partner tool. [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=43s)
- **Start every UI project in Mobbin.** Do not make dummy accounts and take screenshots. Filter by flow, save screens to collections, add comments, or copy screens into Figma with one click. [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=174s), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=235s)
- **Link research in one place.** Capture ideas on your phone, then connect them to user interviews, articles and videos. The AI can then answer from all of them. [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=272s), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=333s)
- **Hand off email.** Let an AI draft replies so creative time stays creative. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=390s)

## Quotes

- "Lovable does to full stack coding what Framer did to pixel perfect design. For the first time, it's easy." [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=99s)
- "If you're doing UI design, there is no better tool to use at the start of a project than Mobbin." [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=174s)
