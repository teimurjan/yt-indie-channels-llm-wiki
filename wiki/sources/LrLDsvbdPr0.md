# I built this app for my dad. Now it makes $18K/month

## Summary

Kourosh built Voicy, a speech-to-text dictation app that runs on OpenAI's Whisper API. He made it for his father, who typed slowly. It grew as a side project next to his 9-to-5 job. Dictation is a crowded market with well-funded rivals like Wispr Flow. Customer calls showed that seniors and people with disabilities loved the product as it was, so he built for them. Growth came from the Chrome Web Store, Reddit, SEO and a YouTube series called "AI for Seniors."

## Key facts

- Revenue: $16K to $20K a month, with $11K MRR. The rest is lifetime deals (2026-10). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=0s), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s)
- Price: $8.49/month or about $260 lifetime. A 20% disability discount is available by email, and 20% of users use it (2026-10). [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=186s)
- About 2,500 DAU, 10,000 MAU and 1,400 active subscriptions (2026-10). [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s)
- Build timeline: Chrome extension MVP in 2 weeks, Windows Electron app in about 3 months, Mac in 1 month, Linux in 2 weeks. iOS came about 6 months before the video. He says Claude Opus built the Android app in one shot. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=357s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=410s)
- His first app, Meet Waves, a community analytics tool, reached $5K MRR. [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=209s)
- SEO assets: about 200 blog posts, 30 landing pages and a site in 4 to 5 languages (2026-10). [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)
- The YouTube channel went from 1,000 to 12,000 subscribers in about a month (2026-10). [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=566s)
- Stack: Framer site (about $200/month), Cursor and Codex, PostHog, Loops, Tella, Ionos VPS (about $40/month), and his own Romeo SEO. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=450s)

## Tactics

- **Launch as a Chrome extension.** The Chrome Web Store sends free traffic. Five friends' reviews brought the first page views and about 100 users. [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)
- **Repost one proven Reddit angle.** He posted the same content in five or six subreddits until he got blocked. This got him to 200 to 300 users. [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)
- **Build for Windows.** Most of the business world uses Windows, not Mac. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=357s)
- **Find the painkiller segment in user calls.** 60% to 70% of users were fast-typing professionals who wanted 10% more speed. 20% to 30% were seniors or disabled users who needed it to work and were happy with the MVP. [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=602s)
- **Win by being seen first.** One article that ranks above a rival gets the click, even with a weaker product. Seniors compare Voicy to 15-year-old dictation tools, not Wispr Flow. [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=684s)
- **Let the ICP pick you.** Post everywhere early and chase the easiest market, not the biggest. Romeo SEO found local trades want it more than indie hackers. [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=684s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=746s)
- **Do 5x the volume.** Write 10 posts, not one. Quantity brings learning faster than perfection. [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=796s)
