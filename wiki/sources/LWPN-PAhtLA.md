# My website makes $35K/month (built in 3 hours)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Pontus Abrahamsson, who co-founded the bootstrapped startup Midday with his friend Victor. On a 3-hour flight to France, Pontus saw that no site collected Cursor rules in one place. When he landed, he and Victor built Cursor Directory in about 3 hours. It went viral on X and Hacker News and now makes about $34K to $35K/month (2025-11) with a 99.8% gross margin and about 3 hours of upkeep per month. He argues that directories still work if they catch a new wave, and that design is the main moat.

## Key facts

- Cursor Directory makes about $34K to $35K/month with a 99.8% gross margin (2025-11). [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=21s), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=159s)
- 49,000 registered users and 2.2 million unique visitors since launch (2025-11). [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=159s)
- Running costs are just under $500/month, about $525/month in total. Maintenance is about 3 hours per month because the rules are open source and merged on GitHub. [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=159s), [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=715s)
- One launch post on X got about 1 million impressions. It also reached the Hacker News front page. [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=343s)
- Revenue comes from paid job listings and paid featured MCP listings. [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=616s), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=665s)
- Stack: Next.js and TypeScript, shadcn components, Resend for email, OpenPanel for analytics, Vercel for hosting, Polar as merchant of record, Notion and GitHub. [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=715s)

## Tactics

- **Spot the gap while you learn.** He watched Cursor videos on the flight and saw the same pattern around rules. Rules only existed in scattered gists and forums. [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=201s)
- **Ship in hours.** Victor designed in Figma while Pontus started a Next.js project with a hard-coded JSON file of rules. It was on Vercel in about 30 minutes, and he bought the exact-match domain. [3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=230s)
- **Act on side ideas.** They usually hold off on the fast ideas they find once or twice a week. This time they did not. [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=294s)
- **Launch where your audience is.** They built in public on X with fully open-source code, posted to Hacker News, and YouTubers then covered it. [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=343s)
- **Design is the moat.** AI-generated websites all look the same. Find sites you love, copy their patterns and tone, and reuse your own design system. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=418s)
- **Directories work on a new wave.** They solve a timeless discovery problem. If you catch a new wave and own it, they are very strong. [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=544s)
- **Extend with each new wave.** An MCP section and a rules generator from an uploaded package.json brought a second burst of adoption. [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=665s)
- **Use the stack you know.** Pick tools with no servers and no friction. [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=715s)
