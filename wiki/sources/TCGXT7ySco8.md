# How I Built It: $12K/Month Micro SaaS

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Dmytro Krasun, a former backend developer who quit his job two years earlier and built ScreenshotOne, an API that automates website screenshots. He narrowed his ideas to API products because building APIs was his strongest skill. He treats competitors as proof of demand and sees 10 paying strangers as real validation. Twitter and Google are his main channels, with Zapier, Make and YouTube tutorials as less obvious sources. He cut churn by emailing every cancelled customer one yes/no question.

## Key facts

- ScreenshotOne has 280 customers and about $12,000 MRR (2024-11). Plans start around $17/month, with extra usage billed on top. [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=98s)
- He also built an email validation API but found it boring. [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=134s)
- The first build took 5 months. Today he aims to launch in 1 month or less. [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=256s)
- First dollar: one customer paid $7/month after a month of promotion on Twitter, Reddit and Indie Hackers. [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=508s)
- Monthly churn fell from about 11% to close to 7%. His target is under 5% (2024-11). [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)
- Profit margin is 40% to 60%. He renders about 2M screenshots a month, pays $3K to $4K for servers, and total costs are about $4,500/month (2024-11). [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=641s)
- Stack: TypeScript and Puppeteer run headless browsers. Go handles rate limits and API keys. Cloudflare stores screenshots. Google Search Console and Keyword Planner cover SEO. PostHog tracks funnels and attribution. Crisp gives live chat on his phone. [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=577s)

## Tactics

- **Filter ideas by your super skill.** He kept only API ideas because building APIs was his strongest skill. [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=134s)
- **Competitors are a good sign.** They prove people pay. Check that the market is big enough for your revenue goal, for example $10K MRR. [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=185s)
- **Validate with strangers' money.** Friends are nice, and some pay for a product that does not exist. 10 paying customers from outside your network is the real test. [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=222s)
- **Launch small.** Build one feature without auth or payments, let friends test it, launch on Twitter, and reach out directly to buyers. Add payments and notifications later. [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=256s)
- **Use integration marketplaces.** Listing on Zapier and Make brings users who automate their business. Product Hunt helped awareness and SEO. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=357s)
- **YouTube tutorials for SEO.** A third party's cold-email tutorial with his product brought paying users. Google shows videos for some keywords, so a tutorial can rank high. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=357s)
- **Churn interviews by email.** The cancel popup gave weak answers. He studied each churned company, guessed the reason and asked one yes/no question. He got close to 100% replies. [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=424s)
- **Fix churn in the copy.** Some users expected no-code, so he stated clearly that the product is for developers. [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)
- **Raise prices and cut the free plan.** Margins at $5 to $7 were near zero. Higher prices give a stronger signal of real users. [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)
- **Focus on your first 10 customers, not competitors.** Ship features and marketing for them, and stay kind. [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=668s)

## Quotes

- "You need to be persistent enough to try anything to just get to this first dollar." [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=508s)
- "When you raise prices it really helps. It's a much stronger signal to get people who really want to use your product." [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)
- "Don't outsource your decision to anybody. You need to own it." [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=788s)
