# How This SaaS Hit $69K/Month In Just 2 Months

## Summary

Gaurav, from Australia, built Fastlane with co-founder Joe. It is an AI tool that makes short-form content for solo founders who build apps and SaaS. It reached $69K MRR about two months after its public launch. His stated secret is about 2,000 customer calls at roughly 20 a week. The calls ran in three phases: discovery, usability tests and customer success. They stored the calls in Notion and built a Claude-powered "customer intelligence" dashboard. The calls led to a narrower product and a Tinder-style swipe UI.

## Key facts

- $69K MRR from software, over 1,000 paid users, over $1M ARR across the business (2026-08). Grew from about $16K ARR of paid beta users. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s)
- About 2,000 customer calls at about 20 a week since August of the prior year. [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=449s)
- Gaurav started building apps after the Starter Story video on Puff Count. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=154s)
- Timeline: a horizontal tool, Cast AI (SEO, LLM SEO, Reddit, short-form), built July to September. Pivoted to short-form only in October. Beta to a 2,000-person waitlist in December. Public launch on March 23. [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=214s)
- Stack: Convex for backend and database, Vercel for the front end, Railway for the content microservice, Clerk for auth. Resend sends email and Axiom keeps logs. OpenAI and Claude models write copy, and fal makes images and video. They code with Claude Code on the 20x Max plan. [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=283s)

## Tactics

- **Pay for calls with access.** A rainbow-bordered button in the UI says "book a call with the team for 7 days of extra access". It links to Calendly. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=400s)
- **Phase 1, discovery.** Reach your network and DM people on Twitter. When necessary, pay them for the call. Use The Mom Test. Ask how they solve the problem now and how they solved it the last time. Ask what it costs and what doing nothing costs. Never ask whether they like the idea. [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=623s)
- **Phase 2, usability tests.** Find users on Reddit threads and with lead magnets. Invite each waitlist signup to a call. Watch them share their screen and say as little as possible. [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=683s)
- **Phase 3, customer success.** Use analytics to find power users, then interview them. This showed that views, conversions and app installs from short-form content were the core value. [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=729s)
- **Customer intelligence dashboard.** An AI note taker records the calls into Notion, and Claude indexes them. Segment by signup reason: "curious" users retained worse than "I need marketing now". A "customer love score" drives the roadmap. The non-technical co-founder built it with Claude Code and MCPs. [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=481s), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=542s)
- **Swipe UI.** A trending niche post (one slideshow had nearly 3M views) sits next to a generated version of it. Swipe right to schedule to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. AI writes the caption, and the post is live in about 5 minutes. [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=780s)
