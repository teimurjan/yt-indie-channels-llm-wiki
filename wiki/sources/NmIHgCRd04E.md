# He Built a Simple $120k/Yr App, Then Sold It For Multiple 6 Figures!

## Summary

The Superwall podcast interviews Lots, a founder who builds faith and viral consumer apps and sells them fast. His fourth exit is PrayScreen, a screen-time app that makes you pray before you unlock other apps. He copied the one sec model for Christians. PrayScreen reached $120K ARR in about 6 months and sold within a week of his tweet. Lots explains his rage-bait TikTok hooks, cheap Meta installs from organic winners, an ad-supported free tier, and his rules for quick exits.

## Key facts

- PrayScreen launched in December 2024. It reached about $120K ARR in 5 to 6 months and sold for multiple six figures (2025-09) ([2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s)).
- The deal closed in about one week. The buyer had tried to buy Bible Buddy 2 years earlier ([2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s), [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1212s)).
- Day-30 retention was 60%, with users praying four times a day ([5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=328s)).
- Meta ads in the US: best day $0.39 per install, average $0.70 to $0.80 (2025-09) ([5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=355s)).
- One video drove about 70% of spend and installs, out of 20 to 30 videos with the same angle ([6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=387s), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=418s)).
- Install-to-paid conversion was only about 2.5%. Ads made about $0.05 per user per day, with payback in 14 to 15 days ([8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=501s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=548s)).
- He says one sec made about $100K per month and Opal about $5M ARR (2025-09) ([4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=262s), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=272s)).
- Bible Buddy, an earlier app, had 100,000 monthly active users ([12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=776s)).
- Sha's Car Spotter, "Pokemon for cars", hit $8K MRR in about 4 months with one TikTok and one Instagram account (2025-09) ([9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=577s), [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=618s)).
- Exit multiples he sees: 2x to 6x ARR. Over 90% of deals fall through ([20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1242s), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1297s)).

## Tactics

- **Copy a trend for an underserved niche.** one sec makes you breathe for 3 seconds before Instagram opens. Lots built the same thing for Christians. Within 2 to 3 months, about 70 "Bible focus" clones appeared ([4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=272s), [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=744s)).
- **Comment-farming hooks.** An agency ran 10 to 12 TikTok accounts. The hook used shock, the "opposite of religion", so people argued in the comments. Christian songs added positive comments ([2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=170s), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=448s)).
- **One emotion per video.** Pick the single emotion the viewer must feel ([3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=223s)).
- **Splash screen in the content.** Make the launch animation beautiful and show it for about a second. Then show the core action and the shareable result screen, not the onboarding ([3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=223s), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=473s)).
- **Organic winners become ads.** TikTok picks the audience for organic posts. A post that wins with a general audience gets low CPIs once you target the right audience ([6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=387s)).
- **Ads for non-payers.** With 2.5% conversion, you lose 97.5% of users. Strong retention made a free tier with four ads per day pay back. He also tells founders with one paywall to add a second paywall ([8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=501s), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=551s)).
- **Follower targeting on X.** For Bible Buddy, he targeted followers of the 20 to 25 most famous pastors. X still offers follower lookalikes ([13:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=820s), [14:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=875s)).
- **Ship fast on new AI launches.** Suno launched AI music, and he built Magic Music on it in 4 days ([14:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=892s)).
- **Rage bait.** Toxic Traits posts show an ambiguous chat labeled a red flag, so viewers argue. The app has 330 content pieces ready and is for sale ([15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=942s), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1026s)).
- **Idea: a Bible survival game.** Whiteout Survival makes about $53M per month, he says. Bible stories have no IP owner ([10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=649s), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=651s)).
- **Leave growth levers for the buyer.** He never translates his apps. A buyer then sees untried levers such as new languages and markets ([17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1050s), [21:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1318s)).
- **Exit rules.** Decide early: cash flow or life legacy. Prefer the third-highest offer that closes in 4 to 5 days over the top price. Show at least 3 months of steady revenue. Offer 1 to 2 months of transition help ([20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1242s), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1368s), [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1405s)).
- **Get a first win.** A $5K to $10K MRR app can sell. Sales build skills and a network of buyers and funders ([18:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1109s), [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1212s)).
