## Summary

Ryan Chen was paralyzed in a snowboard jump accident at 19. With his college best friend Kent Yoshimura, he started NeuroGum: focus and energy supplements in gum and mints instead of pills. They funded R&D with savings and Ryan's cashed-out 401(k), then raised $30K on Indiegogo. They ran it as a side project from Kent's apartment for about two years before going full-time. They turned down Shark Tank offers, but a guest shark, Daniel Lubetsky, later helped them survive a trademark lawsuit. NeuroGum does almost $10M/month (2025-02).

## Key facts

- Revenue: almost $10M/month (2025-02), which the video frames as a $100M company. [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=852s)
- Year 1: a little over $100K. Year 2: $750K. [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s)
- Indiegogo: hit the $30K goal in 3 to 4 days, helped by a Reddit community that Kent was part of. Time Magazine coverage and a Dr. Oz appearance followed. [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)
- Both kept day jobs at first: Ryan as a financial analyst at Hulu, Kent as a music producer at Sony Music. [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s)
- About one year of R&D to find a manufacturer. The first 4,000 lb of gum sat in Kent's downtown LA apartment. [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=346s), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s)
- No salary in years 3 and 4. A good salary only from about year 7. [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s), [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)
- Shark Tank (2019): two offers below their floor. They walked away with no deal. [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s)
- A trademark lawsuit nearly bankrupted them. Daniel Lubetsky advised them for 18 months and offered to back their legal fees. [11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=708s)
- Ryan says NeuroGum is a top 10 TikTok Shop brand. Employee satisfaction: 96% (2025-02). [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=852s)

## Tactics

- **New format for a known need.** They mixed nootropic pills in a dorm room. On a scuba trip they saw that pills are hard to share with strangers. So they put the ingredients in gum. [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=208s), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)
- **Cheap early validation.** Ryan gave samples to coworkers at Hulu. A manager stayed awake until 3 a.m. That was the signal that the product worked. [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=346s)
- **Crowdfund the first batch.** Indiegogo paid for inventory and brought press. [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)
- **Go full-time on repeat-buyer data.** User feedback and the share of revenue from repeat buyers showed real adoption. Then they quit. [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)
- **Use TV for exposure, not only money.** Shark Tank gave a "step change" in growth without a deal. [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s)
- **Earned media compounds.** Unpaid mentions from Joe Rogan, Steve Aoki, Ashley Graham and others, plus repurposed clips, keep going viral on TikTok. [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)
- **Pick a co-founder you trust.** Inventory, freight and border delays were a grind. Ryan says you go into battle with your best friend. [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)
