# I Make $250K/Month From 20 Apps

## Summary

John Rush spent 10 years in VC-backed startups. In 2022 he moved to bootstrapping: he bought the website builder Unicorn Platform for almost $1M, and its founder mentored him for a year. In 2025-05 he runs about 26 products for founders, with about $3M ARR combined. Seven products earn most of the revenue, and the free tools send traffic to the paid ones. He explains his validation blueprint (waitlist, then a 90%-off pre-sale, then manual delivery), his 50/50 "co-maker" partnerships and his cross-promotion between tools.

## Key facts

- Portfolio: 26 startups, about 1M B2B users and about $3M ARR, or roughly $250K/month (2025-05) [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=58s).
- He paid almost $1M for Unicorn Platform in 2022. The seller mentored him for a year as part of the deal [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s).
- Unicorn Platform has 600,000 users. SEO Bot reached $100K MRR in less than 12 months (2025-05). Listing Bot submits products to directories [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s).
- Margins: SEO Bot makes $100K/month and pays $30K/month for AI APIs, which is about a 70% margin. Non-AI tools have about a 90% margin, and human support is the main cost (2025-05) [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s).
- Before he built in public, 90% of his products failed. After, only 10% failed [15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=906s).
- Stack: JavaScript and Tailwind, his own AI code generator, Grok for research, Discord for project management and Apple Notes for writing. Inside the products he uses the Claude, Gemini and OpenAI APIs and lets users choose the model [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=714s).

## Tactics

- **Validation blueprint.** He starts from his own pain at work and looks for existing solutions. If none fit, he posts about the pain to see if others share it. Next he opens a waitlist and aims for 100 signups. Then he emails them a 90%-off pre-sale, and at 5 sales he delivers the solution by hand. He changes the manual service until users are happy, and only then does he build [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=215s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=275s).
- **Co-maker model.** He pitches a validated idea, with pre-sales done, to makers who failed at something similar before. Ownership is 50/50. He does operations, legal and accounting, and the co-maker does code and support. They build for 2 to 3 months, then launch on Twitter [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=317s).
- **Marketing order.** He starts with SEO. Next he posts the same content every day on X, LinkedIn, Substack and Facebook, at least 30 tweets a month. Then he lists the products on directories, which works best for "clickbaity" AI products. Last comes cross-promotion [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s).
- **Integrate, do not just link.** In SEO Bot, a "boost my domain rating" button opens Listing Bot, so users move across his tools [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=576s).
- **AI everywhere.** He uses AI for code, logos, images, research, SEO, emails, copy and ads. An internal AI project manager, Nova, coordinates his co-makers [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=623s).
- **Keep human support.** People chat with humans and build a relationship. With an AI agent, they ask a question and leave [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s).
- **Founder-idea fit.** Solve the problems you see in your own job [15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=959s).
