## Summary

Marc Lou makes $77K/month as a solo founder (2026-04). He shipped about 35 startups. He lives the same day 365 days a year. First the gym with his wife, then 4 to 6 hours of offline deep work. Email and Twitter come late in the day, and bed is at 9 p.m. About 5% of his launches work. So his strategy is to "keep rolling the dice" with many small launches, not to nurse one pet project. His AI setup is deliberately plain: one code editor with one chat on the side.

## Key facts

- Income: $77,000/month as a solo founder (2026-04). [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=1s)
- About 35 startups shipped. About 30 make almost nothing, a hit rate of about 5%. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=163s)

## Tactics

- **Protect a deep work block.** No phone, email, social media or support tickets at the start of the day. A bug report read early pulls him away from new work. [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=72s), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=100s)
- **Play the numbers.** With a 5% hit rate, many launches beat one. Each launch also teaches him something and grows his audience. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=163s), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=558s)
- **Build what you want to exist.** The posture app solved his own slouching. TrustMRR came from curiosity. [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=222s), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=267s)
- **Launch on many channels at once.** He announced the posture app with a tweet and shared it on LinkedIn, Threads and Reddit. Before launch he tested the license system and the checkout emails. [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=222s)
- **Keep a ship list.** Feedback from running apps fills a to-do list. After each cycle he picks the item with the most user impact, or the one that makes him happiest. [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=317s)
- **Ship with a buy button.** The only proof of an idea is a launch with a way to pay. [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s)
- **Count shipped things, not agents.** He rejects multi-agent productivity setups. The one KPI is still the number of things you ship. [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s)
- **Kill the fear once.** The fear of showing work is like a first gym visit. One launch removes about 80% of it. [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=364s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=377s)
