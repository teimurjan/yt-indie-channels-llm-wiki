# He Made $10M with 3 iPhone Apps

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Blake Anderson. Blake taught himself to code with ChatGPT and built or co-founded three AI iPhone apps: Rizz GPT, Umax and Cal AI. Together the apps made over $10M. Blake gives two reasons that apps pay well now. LLMs solve new problems at low cost, and TikTok and Instagram push views to new accounts. He finds ideas in niches where people already spend money, builds with React Native, and markets with influencers and in-house UGC. He left Rizz GPT and now builds Apex, a self-improvement brand.

## Key facts

- Blake learned to code with ChatGPT in mid-2023 and launched Rizz GPT in July 2023 ([4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s)).
- Rizz GPT makes about $2.5M per year (2024-12). Umax made almost $5M in the past year (2024-12). Cal AI launched about 6 months earlier and makes over $1M per month (2024-12) ([1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=69s)).
- Rizz GPT launch: he paid two small creators $50 each. The posts got 5 to 10M views. Downloads: 45,000 on day one, 200,000 in the week, 500,000 in the month ([4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s)).
- Rizz GPT started at $80K MRR and peaked a little over $250K MRR. It then settled at $150K to $200K MRR (2024-12) ([5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=338s)).
- Umax makes a little over $100K profit per month. Cal AI makes a few hundred thousand dollars profit per month (2024-12) ([14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=878s)).
- Apple takes 15% up to the first $1M, which is closer to 20% in practice. Above $1M it takes 30%, closer to 33% in practice ([13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- AI costs are under 3% of revenue. Image generation and advanced voice cost more ([13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- Profit margin by channel: in-house UGC 50 to 80%, influencer marketing 25 to 70%, paid ads 0 to 30% ([13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- After college his goal was to make $50,000 in 12 months ([3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=202s)).

## Tactics

- **Ideas from monetized niches.** People spend money on skin care, hair care and gua sha. A niche with high spend on physical products converts, and software can serve it. That is how Umax started ([7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=459s)).
- **Open ideas.** One idea is an all-in-one career AI platform that combines many small tools. Another is a voice AI language tutor. Duolingo makes about $33M per month, so 1% of that market is a $4M per year app ([6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=398s)).
- **Validation by immersion.** Make a new social account and watch only the niche's content. Think like the target user, then build what you want ([8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=533s)).
- **Stack.** Figma with reference apps for design. React Native with Expo, Cursor as the IDE, and Node.js. CapCut for content. Mercury for banking ([9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=578s), [15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=932s)).
- **Upwork hiring.** Hire 5 to 10 people for 1 to 2 hours of work. Keep the best one ([15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=932s)).
- **Marketing.** Use influencer marketing and internal UGC. Internal UGC means slideshows, faceless video or talking-head posts on app-branded accounts. Iterate fast and double down on what is profitable ([10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=640s)).
- **Persistent creator outreach.** He found a Discord link in a creator's bio. He sent a message every 10 minutes until the creator replied. He even messaged a creator's mom ([11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=698s)).
- **Low-price subscriptions.** Low prices get more users, more word of mouth and better sentiment ([12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)).
- **Paywall tests.** He uses Superwall to test weekly, monthly and yearly offers and paywall placements. This can lift revenue per download 1.5 to 2x ([12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)).
