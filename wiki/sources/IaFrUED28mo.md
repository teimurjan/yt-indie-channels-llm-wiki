## Summary

Tim Gabe asks whether UI designers will lose their jobs to AI, at the time of DALL-E 2 and Midjourney (2022-11). He names three AI advantages: it scales with compute, it is very fast, and it learns all the time. Pixel pushing, such as landing pages that look alike, is the easiest work for AI to replace. UI kits and templates had already made that work a commodity. His answer is to think as a designer: solve real problems for real people in a real context. AI cannot handle that yet.

## Key facts

- AI advantage 1: it scales with compute and Moore's law, before quantum computing. [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=70s)
- AI advantage 2: speed. In an MKBHD challenge, designer Tim beat DALL-E more often than not. But he took 3 days, and DALL-E took about 10 seconds per image. [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=93s)
- AI advantage 3: it learns day and night. Once it beats humans at a task, there is no coming back. [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=138s)
- Landing pages share a structure, so AI can scrape website galleries and reproduce them at scale. [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=138s), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=198s)

## Tactics

- **Do not compete on pixel pushing.** Visual work alone is the perfect target for AI. UI kits and templates already sell it cheaply. [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=138s), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=198s)
- **Think as a designer.** Ask who the user is, what their day looks like and what motivates them. Ask about the use case: are they in a car, are they in a hurry? Keep some focus on visuals too. [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=247s)
- **Own the context and tradeoffs.** His example prompt asks for a fintech transaction flow for several scenarios. It must build trust and speed for a new user. That was beyond AI at the time. Layered tradeoffs like this are the designer's moat. [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=282s)
