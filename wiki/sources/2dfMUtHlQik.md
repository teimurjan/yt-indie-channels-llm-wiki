# How I Built It: $400K/Month Mobile App (Gravl)

## Summary

Julian is a developer from Argentina who lives in Australia. He co-founded Gravl, an AI app that builds gym workouts. Before Gravl, he and his partners ran an influencer marketing startup for mobile games that became an agency. That work taught them user acquisition and showed how much fitness apps earn. Julian liked Fitbod's UX but found its workouts bad, so Gravl pairs a similar UX with a better workout engine. A Reddit post got the first few thousand users. Paid Meta ads in Spanish for South America at under $50/day then grew it to over $440K/month.

## Key facts

- Revenue: over $440K last month, over 70,000 subscribers, about 2 years after launch (2025-11). [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=71s)
- The MVP took about 2 to 3 months. It started as a workout tracker and then became a workout generator. [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=241s)
- A Reddit post on how he built the app (then called "Games AI") got a few hundred upvotes in hours and over 300K impressions. It brought the first few thousand users. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=291s)
- The first paid ad got a subscription in the first 10 minutes. Early ad spend was under $50/day in South America. [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=424s)
- Ad spend on Meta and TikTok, plus some Google and Apple Search Ads, is about a third of revenue (2025-11). [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s)
- Team: 3 people in year one (Julian on development, Matias and Aaron on growth and ads). Now 13 to 14 people with part-timers and contractors. Salaries are $50K to $80K/month (2025-11). [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s), [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=803s)
- Other costs: Apple takes 15%, a 1% revenue fee, an MMP at about $1K/month and infrastructure plus AI at about $1K/month (2025-11). [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=803s)
- Stack: React Native and Expo, a .NET back end, Next.js and React for admin dashboards, and Cursor with tight control over which files it edits. [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=732s)

## Tactics

- **Clone a good UX and fix the core.** Fitbod gave workouts on the spot, but some were weird or unsafe. Gravl kept that UX and built a proper workout engine. The engine uses equipment, frequency, consistency, gender, weight and age. [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=173s), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=241s)
- **Launch on Reddit with a build story.** Developers liked the technical post. Many also found the gym intimidating, so they used the app and sent bugs and feature requests. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=291s)
- **Validate payment before ads.** Prove that the product works and that people pay. [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s)
- **Translate and buy cheap traffic.** The founders speak Spanish. They translated the app and ran ads in South America, which costs less than the US. [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=424s), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s)
- **Make ad creative cheap and in volume.** Pay creators as little as $50 per piece. Use AI video tools and CapCut. Volume is the hard part. Most winning ads are UGC. [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=534s)
- **Copy from the Meta Ads Library.** Competitors' ads are public. Copy what they spend the most on. [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=534s)
- **Long onboarding, then a hard paywall.** The quiz asks goals, experience, one-rep maxes, split, focus muscles and gym equipment. A "generating your plan" screen comes next, then a plan summary. A hard paywall comes before the user sees the product, and many pay. Show the AI in onboarding, because AI sells. [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=576s), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=638s)
- **Human support 24/7.** An in-app chat answered by real people is something users value and helped growth. [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=700s)
- **Know when to quit.** The founders kept the earlier agency business for about 1 extra year. It was a mistake. [13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=837s)
