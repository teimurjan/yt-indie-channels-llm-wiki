# This app makes $2m/year using this ridiculously easy strategy

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Dan, founder of Massive, a "Tinder for jobs" web and mobile app. Massive matches users to jobs, and AI fills out the applications. The idea came from a pivot after a $3M VC-backed product failed. A LinkedIn post tested it and got about 8M impressions. Massive now makes about $200K/month (about $2M ARR) with a hard paywall and growth from paid ads, UGC and influencers. Dan's main lesson is audience targeting in organic video: views are easy, but views from people who pay are hard. He also calls out fake-testimonial UGC as illegal and tells founders to think bigger than indie hacking.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $200K/month, about $2M ARR (2025-08). [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)
- Over 10M applications submitted in about 2 years. Team: Dan, one full-time engineer and a few VAs and ops staff (2025-08). [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=78s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=168s)
- The earlier product raised $3M from VCs and failed. The test LinkedIn post came from an account with about 500 connections. It got about 8M impressions and 5,000 comments. [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)
- The waitlist reached 40K to 50K sign-ups in 2 months. Only about 0.5% of it even signed up after launch. [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=268s)
- From April to December, manual work (Hacker News, scraped emails, personal messages) took them to about $20K MRR. They were weeks from running out of money. [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=328s)
- Massive gets about 3M to 4M views a week, with 3 to 5 viral videos a day (2025-08). [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=437s), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=489s)
- One 11M-view video brought only about $20K MRR. It converted only between about 3M and 3.5M views. [17:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1025s), [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1070s)
- Pricing changes doubled revenue. A specific way of running ads added 50%. [30:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1803s)
- Dan says Headway makes $200M to $300M a year at 30% margins and 2x yearly growth, bootstrapped on paid ads. [23:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1420s)

## Tactics

- **Test with a post.** Dan disliked the idea, so he posted it on LinkedIn. The response proved demand. [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)
- **Use a clear analogy.** "Tinder for jobs" lands at once. "Job search on autopilot" does not. [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=181s)
- **Skip waitlists and charge.** Dan calls waitlists useless. Get people to pay, even for a small version. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s)
- **Validate with influencers.** For a new app, Dan spends $1K to $2K max. He picks a few influencers with steady views and gives them a tight value prop. UGC takes too long to iterate. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s)
- **Call out the audience in the first 3 seconds.** "Stop using LinkedIn to apply to jobs" reaches white-collar workers and converts. The same video with Indeed reaches blue-collar workers, gets hate and does not convert. College students do not pay. [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=489s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=549s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=596s)
- **Learn conversion signals early.** With few viral videos, you can see which one moved revenue in Stripe. Build that skill before you post many videos a day. [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=489s)
- **Creative beats checkout polish.** Yoga Body's specific ads ("running 10+ miles with tight hips") sold Dan a $100 course. Paying took 15 minutes and did not work on mobile. [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=642s), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=687s)
- **80/20 for organic.** About 80% of videos copy formats that already went viral, and 20% are new. One creator iterated on a "discovery" format for weeks: 382 views on one version, 1.7M on a nearly identical one. [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=801s), [15:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=950s)
- **Use paid ads for data.** UGC needs a lot of management and gives weak conversion signals. Paid ads optimize for conversions and iterate faster. [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1178s), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1225s)
- **Do not fake testimonials.** Dan says a competitor copied Massive's onboarding and ran fake "I landed a job" videos that convert in the comments. He calls it illegal. [21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1279s), [22:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1347s)
- **Re-target the same product.** In note-taking apps, one app aimed at older users makes about 3x more money with a fraction of the downloads. Masterschool tripled revenue in a quarter with an AI scholarship funnel. [27:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1642s), [28:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1694s)
- **Execute instead of researching.** About 80% of experiments fail. Most founders never post a single video. [30:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1803s), [31:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1863s)
