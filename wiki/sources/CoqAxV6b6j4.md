# I Make $100K/Year From 2 AI Side Projects

## Summary

Ramsri Goutham is an app builder in India. He runs two AI SaaS apps next to a full-time job: Supermeme.ai, an AI meme generator, and Questgen.ai, an AI quiz generator. Together they make $8,500/month and run with about 2 hours of work a week (2025-06). He got his users by posting daily on LinkedIn and Twitter before and during the build, and spent $0 on marketing. He keeps his job by choice: no VC money, no team, and time for family.

## Key facts

- Combined revenue: $8,500/month, a little over $100K ARR (2025-06). [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=51s), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s)
- Supermeme: about $130K lifetime revenue, 1.5M+ users, about 400 paying customers (2025-06). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s)
- Questgen: about $100K lifetime revenue, 150K+ users, about 180 to 200 paying customers (2025-06). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s)
- Time spent: about 2 hours a week, on Saturday afternoons (2025-06). [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=51s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=155s)
- Timeline: online courses 2015 to 2018. Experiments 2018 to 2020. A Udemy course in 2021 and 2022 that made $10K. The first Questgen version, built with no-code tools, in 2022 and 2023. [3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=202s), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=252s)
- The first Questgen had two text boxes and no design. He then spent 6 months on a full-stack rebuild. [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=252s)
- Supermeme has three co-founders: Ramsri (AI), a full-stack developer and a marketer. [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=252s)
- Stack: Next.js, Supabase, Typedream for no-code landing pages, plus Windsurf and Cursor. [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=540s)
- Profit margin is 70% to 75%. The main cost is OpenAI and Anthropic APIs. There are no employees (2025-06). [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=577s)

## Tactics

- **Idea framework.** Find manual tasks that people pay for today. Then ask which ones current or soon-to-come generative AI can improve or replace. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s)
- **Bet on where AI is going.** In 2020, AI was not yet able to make good quizzes from text. He saw that publishers make many quizzes. He bet that the models were about to catch up. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s)
- **Build distribution before the product.** He wrote blogs on quiz generation, made YouTube videos and posted daily on LinkedIn. Both apps were built in public. [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=451s)
- **Posting finds co-founders too.** His Supermeme co-founders found him through his posts. [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)
- **Launch on Product Hunt with your audience.** Early fans and a viral meme feature made Supermeme number one on launch day. [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)
- **Learn one hour a day.** SaaS needs payments, auth, databases, marketing and SEO. He learned them over years with 1 to 2 hours a day. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=108s)
- **Stitch tools together.** Prototype with Lovable or Bolt, or with Make, n8n or Zapier. Do not spend months on R&D. [11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=664s), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=726s)
