## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Samuel Rondot, a former optician who taught himself to code from a YouTube course. Samuel runs three SaaS products that make about $35K/month together (2025-07). His rule: build only what already exists and already makes money, then make it a little better. He finds ideas through revenue screenshots on X, checks traffic sources in Ahrefs, and copies products whose traffic comes from paid ads. His growth stack is ads first, then SEO, faceless video channels and an affiliate program.

## Key facts

- Combined revenue: about $35K/month across three apps (2025-07). [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=58s)
- useArtemis, a LinkedIn scraping and email finding tool: about $15K/month and 10,000 customers (2025-07). Running cost: about $4K/month, because it runs server instances for automation. [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s), [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=637s)
- StoryShort, an AI faceless video generator for TikTok and YouTube: about $20K/month and about 4,000 customers (2025-07). Running cost: about $5K/month. [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s), [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=637s)
- Capacity, a new AI coding tool: about $900/month and 50 users (2025-07). [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)
- useArtemis was his first real app and broke his own rule. It competes with Apollo and lemlist and is hard to maintain. [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)
- He learned to code from one 15-hour YouTube course. He applied each lesson at once to a tool he wanted to rebuild for Instagram. [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=72s)
- Stack: Next.js and Node.js, Vercel for hosting, Stripe for payments, Ahrefs for SEO research, and Outrank to write and post blog articles. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=589s)

## Tactics

- **Four filters for an idea.** He wants to use it himself. It already works. The founder does not spend thousands on marketing, so demand is real. It is simple to maintain. [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=173s)
- **Find proof on X.** MRR or Stripe screenshots in solopreneur and build-in-public circles are his best traction signal. [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=239s)
- **Check traffic sources in Ahrefs.** Ads plus SEO means strong demand and an easy copy. SEO only is harder because it takes time. [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=239s)
- **Prefer competitors that live on paid ads.** StoryShort copied a tool whose traffic came 100% from Facebook ads. He copied that traffic source in about a week. [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)
- **Ads first, then SEO.** He starts with Google or Meta ads to test the market fast. After traction he adds SEO, because it compounds and adds value at sale time. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=695s)
- **Faceless channels on autopilot.** StoryShort has a feature that posts daily UGC-style videos about your product to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s)
- **Affiliates for fixed-cost growth.** Affiliates make YouTube videos and articles about StoryShort. He pays a known cost per customer and gets reach. [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=558s)
- **Ship an MVP in two weeks.** Skip password reset and settings pages. Launch, run ads, then finish the product after customers arrive. [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=668s), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=695s)
- **Learn with AI on a real project.** Take a screenshot of a product you like. Ask ChatGPT how to build its landing page. Ask again at each roadblock. [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=118s)
