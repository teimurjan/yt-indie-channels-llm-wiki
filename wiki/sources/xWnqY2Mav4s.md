# I Built Two Apps That Make $120K/Month

## Summary

Kyle Fowler is a self-taught iOS developer who builds apps around his own hobby, collecting cards. CardStock scans sports cards with the phone camera and shows their market value. Scanemon is the same app for Pokemon cards. He built CardStock by hand in 2019 and generated the Scanemon MVP in one day with the Cursor agent in 2025. His main growth channel now is TikTok slideshows, posted on his own accounts and through paid creators.

## Key facts

- Combined revenue: $120K/month, about $1.5M/year, with a team of five (2026-06) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s).
- CardStock: about 15,000 active subscriptions, up to about $75K MRR, about 13,000 downloads/month (2026-06) [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=119s).
- Scanemon: launched about a year earlier. About 3,400 active subscriptions, $17K MRR, about 16,000 new customers/month, which is more than CardStock gets (2026-06) [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=119s), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=162s).
- The two MRR figures add up to about $92K. The video does not explain the gap to $120K/month.
- CardStock started 6 years earlier as "three guys in a basement making a few hundred bucks a month". Kyle taught two friends to code and split revenue three ways for 2 to 3 years [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=175s).
- CardStock timeline: building started in winter 2019. It took about 6 months from idea to launch, with a 3-month gap. He announced it in a Reddit post and got downloads at once [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=325s).
- CardStock stack: Swift, SwiftUI, Core Data, RevenueCat for subscriptions, and an AWS EC2 server for the card database [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=292s).
- Current tools: Codex (through ChatGPT), Cursor, Claude, Notion, Slack, Framer (website), Supabase, Railway, CodeRabbit, Wispr Flow, Granola [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=368s).

## Tactics

- **Solve your own hobby problem.** He wanted to catalog his cards, found no app for it on the App Store, and built one. If it solves your problem, others probably have it too [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=400s), [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=453s).
- **Clone your own winner for a next niche.** Scanemon reuses the CardStock concept for Pokemon cards. Kyle had Cursor's agent generate the MVP in one day [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=325s).
- **His 4-step idea process:** keep a notes file of slow, repetitive tasks in your life; find the most minimal solution; build it (AI makes this easy); monetize with Superwall or RevenueCat. AI can set up the paywall and even drive the browser [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=488s), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=526s).
- **TikTok slideshows as the main channel.** Find a format, then balance what gets views with what converts. Attribution is easy only if it is your only channel [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=566s).
- **Pay creators per view.** He uses a platform that sends a slideshow template to creators and pays them by views. With more time than money, start with your own accounts or a few new accounts to validate the format [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=566s), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=591s).
- **Show recent sales in the app.** The scan result shows the matched card, its market value and recent sales, which collectors care about most [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=621s).
