# I Quit My $250K Job To Become an Author

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Paul Millerd in Austin. Paul quit a $250K consulting job in New York in 2017 with no plan, started writing every day, and self-published *The Pathless Path*. He turned down Penguin Random House to keep creative control and higher royalties. The book now earns more than half of his income, next to a course, workshops and some coaching. He markets the book through podcasts and by gifting copies.

## Key facts

- Paul quit his job in 2017 at age 32. His employer made him repay a $24,000 signing bonus and relocation costs. He had $50K to $60K in savings. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=0s), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=149s), [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=174s)
- Year one of the book: about 10,000 copies and about $50,000 in royalties, roughly $5 per book (2023-12). [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)
- Year two, first five months: about 15,000 copies and about $75,000 in royalties (2023-12). [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)
- Total top-line revenue grew from $100K to $249K in one year, and he was on track to beat that (2023-12). [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)
- Traditional publishers pay about 10% of list price on hardcovers, about $2 a book. Self-published, he makes about $7 per paperback and $8 per hardcover. [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=240s)
- Amazon royalties per unit: $8.24 hardcover, $7.45 paperback, $3.98 audiobook, $4.28 Kindle. Sales mix: 50% ebook, 29% print, 21% audio (2023-12). [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=551s)
- Costs: editing and coaching $3,900, cover $742, IngramSpark fee $250, audiobook $1,280. Royalties to date, almost all profit: $125K to $130K (2023-12). [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=577s)

## Tactics

- **Write daily before work.** A year before quitting, he wrote on Quora every day for 100 days. His blog cadence then went from every 3 months to weekly. [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=149s)
- **Self-publish to keep control and margin.** Write intensely for a month or two, step back to fix structure, repeat. Then upload a PDF to Amazon and ship books in about 3 days. You can list yourself as the publisher. [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=276s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=480s)
- **Spend on a developmental editor.** It is the biggest cost. Get the cover on 99designs, format with Reedsy or Amazon's tools, and hire a proofreader at the end. [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=480s)
- **Market through what you enjoy.** He goes on podcasts and gifts books, which costs $4.50 including US shipping. [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=480s), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=540s)
- **Fund the writing with other income.** Freelancing first, then a course (Strategy U), then a workshop business with a fixed training at a fixed price. Coaching stays minimal. [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=604s)
- **Take a 1 to 3 month sabbatical** to reconnect with hobbies and let the right work emerge. Quit or delegate what does not light you up. [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=420s)
- **Get reps to beat fear.** Early on nobody reads, so nobody judges. Ideas come from wandering, reading and podcasts. [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=654s)
