# Copy This UGC Strategy, It'll Make Your App Go Viral

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Jay, the solo founder of NomadTable, an app that helps solo travelers meet people nearby through activities. Jay ran the content himself first, got good at going viral, and only then hired creators. He now runs about 60 to 70 creators on TikTok and Instagram. He pays them $1 to $2 CPM with no retainer. He still posts daily himself. His key signal is the share of viewers who watch past 3 seconds. He explains why he avoids paid ads for a social app and how he solved the cold start with a waitlist.

## Key facts

- Over 1M downloads and about $65K/month in revenue, as a bootstrapped solo founder (2026-02). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=0s), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=364s)
- Plans are weekly, monthly and annual. The annual plan is $69.99 (2026-02). [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=430s)
- 30M to 44M organic views a month across about 60 to 70 creators, all managed by Jay in-house (2026-02). [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=508s), [9:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=555s)
- About 10% to 15% of new users come from word of mouth. Almost all the rest come from UGC (2026-02). [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=181s)
- His own content brought the first 50K to 100K downloads. He was the top creator by views for the past 2 to 3 months. [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=464s), [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=610s)
- The waitlist had about 12K to 15K people. About 6K installed on day one. Barcelona had about 20 users at launch. [47:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2836s), [47:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2863s)
- The top creator payout was about $8,000 in one month for about 8M views, mostly from one video. [43:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2599s), [43:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2612s)
- He worked 3 years in big tech. He got the idea on a 3-week solo trip and quit about a week after he came back. [35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2107s), [50:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3004s)

## Tactics

- **Founder tests, creators scale.** Jay tests every hook himself and gives creators only the formats that hit. All the flops happen on his own accounts. [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s), [53:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3239s)
- **The golden metric.** In a good format, 75% to 80% of viewers watch past 3 seconds, and average watch time is 6 to 10 seconds. Such a format gets at least 4K to 5K views per TikTok and goes viral in time. Under about 50 views, drop it. [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1624s), [27:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1667s), [28:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1695s), [29:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1751s)
- **Avoid the view trap.** A clickbait hook ("this girl in my hostel showed me this") got 300K views but only 174 likes and 8 comments. The video did not deliver on the hook. [17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1051s), [18:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1107s)
- **Hook, then demo.** His first viral video used a hook from another niche: "I have done X for N years, and now I find out about this". A creator version got 74K likes and 30K bookmarks. [13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=783s), [14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=845s), [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1340s)
- **Specific details sell.** A line about "the most unsocial hostel in Thailand" makes the video feel real. [15:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=908s)
- **Volume beats luck.** He posts once a day and gets about one viral video a month. With 60+ creators, the baseline views alone drive downloads, and viral hits keep getting views for months, once for a full year. [31:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1882s), [32:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1943s)
- **Hands-on creator management.** He uses a large WhatsApp group plus one-on-one feedback. He gives creators some freedom so the work stays fun. [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=558s), [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=672s)
- **Performance pay after proof.** Start with retainers, or better, prove virality on your own account first. Then offer $1 to $2 CPM. The highest offer he saw was $3. He pays monthly, does not cap payouts, and counts views on old posts for 3 months. [38:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2328s), [39:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2390s), [40:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2435s), [44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2687s)
- **No paid ads for social apps.** Low ARPU means even cheap installs do not pay back. Paid ads only help to solve the cold start. [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2172s)
- **Monetize lightly.** He says in-app ads can push revenue past $100K/month. He does not add them, to protect quality. [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=388s)
- **Cold start for a moving audience.** City-by-city launches do not work for travelers. He built a waitlist instead. Two users in one place are enough for the app to work. He recommends "The Cold Start Problem" by Andrew Chen. [45:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2748s), [46:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2802s), [47:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2836s)
- **Founder-led content first.** Early talking-head videos about his story and milestones built fans who wanted him to win. [48:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2930s)
- He learned about multi-account UGC from Joseph Choi's interview with Greg Isenberg and began to hire creators about 2 months later. [51:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3097s), [52:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3177s)
