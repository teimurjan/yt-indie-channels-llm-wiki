## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Thomas Frank, who sells two premium Notion templates: Ultimate Brain and Creator's Companion. Thomas burned out on his main productivity channel (about 3M subscribers) and started a small niche channel, Thomas Frank Explains, that only teaches Notion. That niche channel feeds an email list, and the list and long template walkthrough videos sell the templates. He has sold about $2.5M of templates to about 30,000 customers (2024-04).

## Key facts

- About $2.5M lifetime template sales to about 30,000 customers (2024-04). [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)
- Main channel: close to 3M subscribers and about 180M lifetime views. Thomas Frank Explains: about 230K subscribers (2024-04). [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)
- Email list: 60K to 70K on the core Notion list, 200K total (2024-04). [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)
- Creator's Companion made about $3K/month in its first months and grew to $15K/month within 6 months of launch (2021). [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)
- Ultimate Brain launch: about $90K in 30 days from one email to a waitlist of about 3,200 people. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=0s) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)
- After a 45-minute template walkthrough video, revenue rose to about $100K/month, later $100K to $120K/month (2024-04). [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=377s)
- Pricing: Creator's Companion launched at $99 and $149 for the higher edition. Full bundle is $229. The transcript gives Ultimate Brain alone as "$19" (likely $119) (2024-04). A price increase cut volume a little but raised total revenue. [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s)
- Stack: Notion, Circle (paid support community with logins), Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record, handles tax), ConvertKit (email), Pipedream (automation). [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)
- Running cost for a copycat: camera gear plus a $15 to $30/month Framer site. Total: under a few hundred dollars a month. [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=603s)

## Tactics

- **Niche channel over big channel.** The niche channel fills demand that exists: people already search for Notion help. He models this on Zapier's content strategy: cast a slightly wider net around workflow tools, then show the subset what the product does. [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=678s)
- **Four content types.** Build guides (each with a free template), new-feature videos, a free fundamentals course, and listicles. Each video pitches a paid template, a free template, or a resource page behind an email opt-in. [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)
- **Email funnel.** The first autoresponder delivers the free templates, then offers Ultimate Brain with a subscriber discount code. [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)
- **Product = your own tool, polished.** Creator's Companion is the system his team used from 2018 to 2021 to make videos. He added extras like a repurposing dashboard. [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=408s)
- **Long demo video as an ad.** A 45-minute walkthrough shows so much that it stays educational, and it drove the jump to $100K/month. [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)
- **Founder-led support.** He did support 8 hours a day for at least a month to learn pain points. Templates are easy to pirate, so the paid value is gated support in Circle. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=377s) [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)
- **Replicate on any growing niche.** The model works for any software with "groundswell". Monetize with consulting, courses, templates or SaaS. [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=739s)
- **"Fan first" networking.** Become a fan of people you admire. Share and comment on their work. Over time, this turns into friendships. [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=842s)
