# Become A Self Taught UI/UX Designer in 2023

## Summary

Tim Gabe gives a five-step path to a UI/UX design job without design school. He went to design school himself, but says 90% of his early knowledge came from work at a small local agency. The steps, in order: learn to think like a problem solver, learn basic design principles, recreate existing designs, build a portfolio, and land an internship with senior designers. He claims a committed learner can get a design job within a year (2022-11).

## Key facts

- Tim's path: a physical product idea in his early 20s, a startup incubator, design school, then a local agency started by people from the incubator. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=0s), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=61s)
- About 90% of his early design knowledge came from agency work, not school. [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=122s)
- He started at a small local agency. A few years later he was freelancing for Spotify, his dream company. [11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=694s)
- Principle example: keep a paragraph to at most 50 to 75 characters wide. [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=310s)

## Tactics

- **Think like a problem solver.** The key skill is problem solving, not pixel pushing. Uber's 2018 e-scooter case study shows how the designer first learned what riders need. E-bike and e-scooter riders have different problems. [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=196s), [4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=258s)
- **Question every app you use.** Ask why a button sits where it does, or why a feature is behind a menu. [4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=258s)
- **Understand principles, do not memorize them.** Tim memorized for exams and forgot within a week. [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=310s)
- **Copy designs pixel for pixel.** Rebuild sites you like and note font sizes, spacing and size contrast. Patterns appear that you can reuse. Learn the tool, Figma for Tim, as problems come up. [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=366s), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=428s)
- **Redesign something with clear flaws.** A local restaurant website is easier than Netflix. It can even become a first paid gig. [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=468s)
- **Pick UI or UX for the portfolio.** A UX portfolio shows the thought process. A UI portfolio shows the best visuals. [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=531s)
- **Finish one project.** A half-done project is the same as no project. Publish one piece, then add more. [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=593s)
- **Get an internship with senior designers.** Feedback drives fast growth, and you get real projects for the portfolio. Proof of effort and creativity can beat a weak portfolio. [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=633s), [11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=694s)
