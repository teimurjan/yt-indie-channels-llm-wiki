# How This App Makes $35K/Month

## Summary

Flo (Florian Vates) is a German iOS developer and former mobile UX lead. He built MonAI, a minimal expense tracker with voice entry and Apple Pay automation. For 1.5 years alone, he reached only $300 MRR. Then a large Colombian tech and lifestyle creator found his socials in the app and became a profit-share partner. With three story-driven videos per month, MonAI grew to over $35K MRR in about a year, a 10,000% increase. Flo shares a creator outreach playbook and wishes he had started Meta ads sooner.

## Key facts

- MonAI made about $300 MRR after 1.5 years of solo work. It reached over $35K MRR about a year after the partnership began (2026-04). [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=63s), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)
- It is a subscription app with a hard paywall and a 7-day trial. A/B tests showed that this beat freemium. [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)
- The first creator video went out at the end of 2024. MRR grew 10x within a week and reached almost $8K within a month. [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=271s)
- Video by video: $300 to $7.8K MRR, then a jump of more than $5K to $13K. A WWDC invite story got 1.7 million views and added almost $5K MRR. A feature story pushed MRR over $35K. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=418s), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=479s)
- The deal is now a share of profit plus a fixed monthly retainer. A share of revenue did not scale well. [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=281s)
- His previous app took 2 years to build, and nobody cared. He built MonAI v1 in 1 to 2 months. [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=177s)
- Monthly tool costs (2026-04): Claude Code Max $100, RevenueCat about $400, Appwrite $25, and OpenAI plus Anthropic about $200. Helm lifetime cost about $175. He uses Xcode and the free Figma plan. [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=930s)

## Tactics

- **One creator, not twenty.** Three high-quality, story-driven videos per month. They seldom get millions of views, but they convert well. The creator owns the ideas. [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=333s)
- **Give the creator skin in the game.** A profit share makes the creator think about growth, not only deliver a video. [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=333s), [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=1148s)
- **Step 1: find an aligned partner.** Look for charisma, a creator who replies to comments, and an audience that cares about the creator's life, not only specs. [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=567s)
- **Step 2: warm up.** Follow and comment for weeks before outreach. If time is short, be honest about it. [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=567s), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=627s)
- **Step 3: be specific.** Name a specific video, moment or joke. "Let's collaborate" alone fails. [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=627s)
- **Step 4: show the fit.** His creator's audience values aesthetics and tech, and MonAI is minimal with AI automation. [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=627s), [11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=688s)
- **Step 5: signal pay early.** Offer a long-term profit share. Use Sensor Tower data to show that niche apps make $50K to $100K/month. [11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=688s)
- **Keep outreach short.** A personal video that names the creator stands out. [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=749s)
- **Put your socials in the app.** That is how the creator found Flo. Inbound creators also give you better terms. [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=749s)
- **Look outside the US.** His partner reaches the Colombian market. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=810s)
- **Reuse creator videos as Meta ads.** Early tests looked strong. Flo regrets the delay. [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=990s)
- **Crowded categories are fine.** Budget tracking has no clear winner. Distribution matters more than novelty. [19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=1174s), [20:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=1214s)
