## Summary

Josh heads M&A at Blue Throne. The firm buys consumer apps for seven and eight figures and scales them. Before that he spent 3 years buying game studios for Plarium, maker of Raid: Shadow Legends. He gives Joseph Choi six rules to sell an app for nine figures. Own a narrow category. Build for retained "locals". Earn organic installs. Charge premium prices anchored on ChatGPT's $20/month. Build the story for the right buyer type. Sell before you need to. Waking Up, Flo, Runna and MyFitnessPal serve as examples.

## Key facts

- Two paths to a $100M+ exit. A fast-growing app with 30% to 50% EBITDA margin can get up to 6x run-rate revenue, so about $17M ARR is enough. A slower app is valued on the last 12 months and needs about $30M to $33M ARR (2026-07). [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=373s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=511s)
- Waking Up, Sam Harris's meditation app, charges $20/month or $150/year. It gets about 25K downloads a month by Sensor Tower but makes over $1M/month (2026-07). [26:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1600s)
- Purpose, Mark Manson's AI mentor app: about 20K downloads and $200K revenue a month (2026-07). [28:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1689s)
- Pricing math for $30M ARR: at $4.99/month you need about 500K payers. At $20/month you need about 125K. [25:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1538s)
- Before ChatGPT, consumers paid at most about $60/year for an app. ChatGPT reset the anchor to $20/month. [24:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1487s)
- Year-one resubscribe benchmarks: 50% amazing, 40% good, 30% okay, 20% bad. Dating apps run lower. [17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s)
- Organic install share: 50% great, 35% to 50% good, under 20% means a UA treadmill. [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1216s)
- About 80% more apps were submitted to the App Store this year than last, so organic ranking is harder (2026-07). [23:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1423s)
- Flo had 70M+ monthly active users and raised $200M+ from General Atlantic at a $1B+ valuation. [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=621s)
- MyFitnessPal sold to Under Armour for $475M in 2015. Francisco Partners paid over $100M less in 2020 after growth slowed. [43:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2636s)
- Blue Throne buys apps that make about $400K to $15M a year. It has 70 staff in R&D, QA, growth, monetization, finance and HR (2026-07). [41:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2509s), [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2799s)

## Tactics

- **Rule 1: own a narrow category.** Define it narrowly enough to be number one, but keep it believable. Flo narrowed health to period tracking. Runna narrowed fitness to Couch to 5K. Size markets in Sensor Tower from the top 30 apps. [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=560s), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=700s), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=800s)
- **Rule 2: build for locals, not tourists.** Multiples are present-value math on LTV. Plot the retention curve of the top 20% of users by engagement and look for where it flattens. Resubscribe rate beats DAU as a signal. [14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=854s), [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=900s), [19:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1156s)
- **Rule 3: earn organic.** Josh counts TikTok UGC as paid because you pay for every video. Buyers want the machine that repeats virality, not one viral video. [21:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1287s), [22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1357s)
- **Rule 4: charge premium prices.** Below $10/month, A/B test higher prices on 10% to 20% of traffic and find the ceiling. Weekly plans suit short-need apps such as dating, but they rarely make a sticky acquisition target. [31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1861s), [33:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2010s)
- **Creator-led apps convert, but sell hard.** The distribution sits in one person, so a buyer needs that person to stay on. [28:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1721s), [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1798s)
- **Rule 5: build the narrative for one buyer type.** Aggregators such as Blue Throne look for growth they can 10x. Strategics pay the most because the app fits their business, as Runna's beginner funnel fit Strava. Private equity wants cash flow and keeps the founder 3 to 5 years. Acquihires are rare in consumer apps. Build relationships 6 to 12 months ahead and study the buyer's last five deals. [35:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2118s), [36:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2215s), [38:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2295s)
- **Distribution is an asset too.** MyFitnessPal bought Cal AI for its funnel, not its tech. [39:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2375s)
- **Rule 6: sell before you need to.** Your alternative to a sale sets your price. Let the buyer pay for the coming peak. Twelve months of growth reads as a machine. Three months can be three viral videos. [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2591s), [44:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2691s), [45:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2757s)
- **Early levers at six figures.** Crack organic early. Run many paywall and pricing tests. Flo ran over 100 experiments a month. Push one paid channel to its ROAS ceiling before you add another. [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2902s), [49:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2946s)
