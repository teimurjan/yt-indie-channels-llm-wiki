## Summary

Shaan Arora is co-founder and CEO of Alia, a bootstrapped SaaS that sells email capture pop-ups to e-commerce brands. Alia began as a college club project and was sold as a customer education and loyalty tool. It had two customers, and buyers did not know where it fit in their stack. After his co-founder read April Dunford's book "Obviously Awesome", the team saw that their best customers used Alia as a pop-up tool. They repositioned around pop-ups only and grew from $0 to $4M ARR in about a year.

## Key facts

- Alia went from $0 to $4M ARR in about one year after the repositioning (2025-08) [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=26s).
- Before the change, it took about 6 months to grow from 2 to about 20 customers [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=14s).
- The first two customers were a co-founder's cousin and a classmate [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=143s).
- About 1,500 brands use Alia, among them Nike Strength and Toms (2025-08) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=95s).
- A typical Alia pop-up offers 15% off in exchange for an email [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=95s).
- Team: 12 people, 3 co-founders (Shaan, Cory and Bill), bootstrapped (2025-08) [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=582s).

## Tactics

- **Ask how your best customers see you.** The best customers paid for a pop-up tool, not an education tool. If they pay for that, others like them will too [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=217s).
- **Reposition in four places** [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=270s), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=316s):
  1. Website copy. The tagline reads "the next generation of pop-ups", and the menu says "pop-up features", not "product features".
  2. Content. Shaan pinned a post that says "Pop-ups equals Alia. Alia equals pop-ups." The tight link creates inbound demand. People who ask in Slack groups or on X for the best pop-up tool get Alia as the answer.
  3. Sales calls. He opens each call with "I'm Shaan, we do pop-ups."
  4. Internal language. Sales, support and management all use the same words.
- **Test the new position in steps.** Sales calls came first. They got shorter and closed more often. Content came next and beat the old content. The website and internal language changed last [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=483s).
- **Repositioning framework.** Read the book. Do its positioning exercises with your co-founders. Test the position on live sales calls and watch the close rate. If it fails, go back to the exercises [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=515s).
- **Set the next small goal.** With two customers, the only goal was a third, then a fourth. Learn how you got the first five, then repeat it [7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=421s).
- **Use speed against incumbents.** Pop-up rivals are 15 to 20 years old and move slowly [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=608s).
