# I Built an App That Makes $2K Per Day

## Summary

Pat Walls and Gus interview Rasty Turek, a former Google engineer and repeat founder. His first iOS app, Vault Air, stores encrypted files and photos on the device, with no servers and no shared keys. He built it in about 3 weeks with coding agents. It made $66 in its first month and $41K in one month about 6 months later. Growth comes from SEO, with no TikTok and no paid ads. A Claude agent writes one guide per day from Google keyword data.

## Key facts

- Built in about 3 weeks, his first iOS app. First month revenue: $66. Now about $2K to $3K per day (2026-10). [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=74s)
- $41K in a single month, about 6 months after a March start (2026-10). [0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=7s), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=95s)
- About 60% of purchases are lifetime, and lifetime brings more than 80% of revenue. Downloads passed 60,000 (2026-10). [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s)
- Prices: $1.99/week, $19.99/year, $39.99 lifetime. Day-1 download-to-paid is 5.47%, and it grows by day 35 (2026-10). [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)
- Daily revenue is about $2,000 and rises slowly. MRR looks low because most buyers choose lifetime (2026-10). [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=221s), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)
- Web traffic is the main source, and it converts to the app at about 10% (2026-10). [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=452s)
- Background: grew up in a Slovak village of 700 people. Worked on Gmail at Google. Sold one company to Amazon. Sold Pex, a VC-backed copyright service, the year before. [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=295s)
- Stack: Swift with UIKit and iCloud features, Codex, Claude, local models, and the free tier of PostHog. [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=599s)

## Tactics

- **Pick a proven, simple category.** The app is "Dropbox, but encrypted". He built it for his own crypto wallet keys and ID photos. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=342s)
- **Expect slow conversion for utility apps.** Cal AI must convert on day 1. Vault Air has its lowest conversion on day 1 and its highest near day 60. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s)
- **Win on the web, not in the App Store.** Competitors are 15 or more years old, and Apple ranks them first. Rasty targets the same keywords on the web with a feature site and how-to guides, for example "how do I store my photos on iPhone". [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=392s)
- **Run a daily SEO agent.** His open-source tool gum puts CLI and MCP access on top of Google services. Each morning, Claude reads Keyword Planner and search data. It finds keywords with impressions but no clicks, for example 16,000 views at rank 50. Then it writes and publishes a guide. [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=479s)
- **Publish one good guide per day.** Google indexes a new guide in about 3 to 4 days. Internal links from pages with traffic point to the new guide. He avoids mass AI content. [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=479s), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=539s)
- **Place CTAs where readers stop.** The agents put app links at the points where readers usually end an article. [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=392s), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=452s)
- **Make privacy a feature.** An FBI agent asked for help with a vault. The design makes it impossible to tell what a vault holds. [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=670s)
- **A different view.** Gus still thinks SEO is mostly dead. He says it works here because the niche is narrow, and LLM answers and word of mouth add untracked traffic. [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=842s)
