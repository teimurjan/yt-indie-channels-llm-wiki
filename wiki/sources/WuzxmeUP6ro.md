# How I Built It: $37K/Month Notion App

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Julien Nahum, a 28-year-old former software engineer who built NotionForms, a form builder for Notion users. He built the MVP in 6 days after Notion released its public API, copying Airtable's forms feature. He grew it by posting in Notion communities on Reddit and Facebook, then by a viral loop: every shared form carries a link back to the product. He monetized by tagging beta features "Pro" and then flipping a paywall on. He argues for building on an existing platform and shipping fast with a stack you already know.

## Key facts

- Close to 100K registered users and close to $37K MRR (2024-11), over $400K/year. [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=70s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=0s)
- He built the first MVP in 6 days, while commuting 3 hours a day at a 9-to-5 job. [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=0s)
- First sale: a yearly plan for $90 on launch night of the paid tier. Then 2, then 3 sales, then none for 3 days. [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s)
- Product Hunt launch brought traffic and a backlink but, by his account, not a single sale. [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=463s)
- Stack: Laravel (PHP) backend, Nuxt (Vue) frontend, PostgreSQL, AWS, Stripe billing, Amplitude, Crisp, Sentry, Featurebase. He uses the same stack he started with about 10 years ago. [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=811s), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=358s)
- Monthly costs (2024-11): team of 3 full-time plus 1 part-time at $5K total (excluding him), $1K servers and SaaS tools, about $3K Google Ads, about $2K freelancers. [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=811s), [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=872s)
- Cursor roughly doubles his speed (2024-11). [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=773s)

## Tactics

- **Build on a platform.** Notion already had a big subreddit and many Facebook groups. Solve a sub-problem and borrow its community. Accept the platform risk. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=135s)
- **Copy a feature from an adjacent product.** Forms existed in Airtable but not in Notion. Timing with the API release helped. [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s)
- **Find ideas** by solving your own problem, asking non-technical friends what they do manually for hours, and watching Twitter for new tech. [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=170s)
- **Validate by building.** Ship an MVP in about a week and share it widely. Reuse the previous codebase and UI so you only write the core logic. [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=239s)
- **Post in niche communities.** Notion subreddits and Facebook groups tolerated the links because the product was new and free. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=400s)
- **Viral loop.** Form respondents see the product branding and a link back. Find a way for users to share a piece of your product, like a badge or widget. [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=511s)
- **Do support yourself first.** He handled all support for a year. Now he has one support agent and an AI chatbot trained on docs and past chats, plus in-app bug, feedback and feature buttons. He builds only what users ask for. [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=579s)
- **Free-to-paid switch.** At about 1,000 users he tagged new features "Pro, free during beta". Then he turned on billing. Early users got a lifetime discount code. [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s)
- **Price below incumbents, then raise.** He started cheaper than all form builders, raised prices with no visible conversion drop, and later added higher tiers. [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=706s)
- **Prefer subscriptions.** Selling $50/month is easier than selling $500 once. [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=743s)

## Quotes

- "It's fine to start small as soon as you get some users and some feedback you're able to iterate much faster." [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s)
- "Since the beginning I've only built what people ask me to build." [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=579s)
- "Selling something for $50 per month is much easier than selling one thing for $500 one time." [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=743s)
