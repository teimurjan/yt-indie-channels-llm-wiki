## Summary

Tim Gabe walks through the six-stage process his agency Zipsnap uses to take an app from napkin sketch to production-ready design. The stages: onboarding, discovery, UX, UI, emotional integration, and delivery. Each stage reduces risk, so engineering moves fast after handoff and users feel something. He says the formula helped clients raise tens of millions of dollars and reach tens of thousands of users. His main point: most AI-era apps stop at usability, and emotional design is how a product stands out. The video promotes free design strategy calls.

## Tactics

- **Client OS from day one.** Each client gets a Notion portal with timelines, tasks, decisions, files and meeting notes. They also get comms channels, a welcome checklist and a discovery doc. He learned this from John D. Saunders. [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=147s), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=204s)
- **Balanced discovery.** At least one workshop defines outcomes, constraints and success metrics. They collect taste references and download and test competitor apps. Research covers key flows only, so it takes weeks, not months. [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=233s), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=294s)
- **Wire flows for key flows.** Map user journeys for the whole app. Onboarding gets deep attention, settings less. Use low-fidelity layouts to get buy-in early. Settle states and edge cases before visuals. [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=325s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=387s)
- **Pin emotional moments early.** During UX, mark the screens where a feeling matters. Prototype key interactions to test them. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=387s)
- **Broad UI exploration, then strict QA.** Start with mood boards and concept screens, then move to high fidelity. Misaligned radii, too many shades and unclear emphasis add up and hurt both quality and developers. Check every screen against a QA list, then turn the work into a component library. [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=468s), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=529s)
- **Emotional integration tied to goals.** Animations are light and often interactive. A crypto client got colour meaning on its portfolio. A trading client got a striking first impression for its launch. Freecash got diamond tiers. Nila Health got a logo tied to its core experience. A Web3 fintech got trust-building moments in onboarding. [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=600s), [11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=662s), [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=724s)
- **Clean handoff.** Cover remaining edge cases, states and accessibility. Run a final QA pass and package source files, specs and tidy components. [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=724s), [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=786s)
