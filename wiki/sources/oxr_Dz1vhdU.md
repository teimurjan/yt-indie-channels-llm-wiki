# $16M AI CEOs: How to Make $1.3M/Month With AI Apps

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Patrick Stuart-Constant and Pablo of Social, a profitable app studio with 22 apps at $16M annualized revenue. They often buy apps that are in decline after a viral peak. Then they grow them with high-volume AI ads and constant A/B tests on monetization. A key test went against industry advice: removing the onboarding paywall on Celebs made more money. They tag the reason behind every test so AI agents can learn from it. They also show WiseSam, their consumer OpenClaw agent.

## Key facts

- Social: $16M annualized revenue, profitable, three years old, with only a $1M pre-seed raised (2026-07). [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)
- It once hit $11M annualized revenue with 11 people, which is $1M per employee. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=0s)
- Output per month: about 4,000 AI video ads, hundreds of playable ads, thousands of static ads and about 100 A/B tests (2026-07). [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)
- Portfolio: 22 apps and over 40 million downloads (2026-07). [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=16s), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=514s)
- Font Maker, which turns handwriting into a keyboard font, has about 7M lifetime downloads and makes about $200K/month. LitStick, an iMessage sticker app, makes just over $300K/month. Celebs is a bit bigger. Autopaste is close to Font Maker (2026-07). [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=514s), [8:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=535s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=548s), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=554s)

## Tactics

- **Buy apps after the viral peak.** Sellers of declining apps often do not know how to grow them. Celebs looked like a short-term hit, but the curiosity it serves is universal. [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=709s)
- **Screen acquisitions top-down and bottom-up.** Top-down: market size, willingness to pay and growth. Bottom-up: run standard ads for apps they do not own yet and compare against their benchmark. [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=404s)
- **Run the LTV/CAC loop.** Strong acquisition brings users. Dozens of tests per app raise revenue per user, which funds more acquisition. [13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=808s)
- **Remove the onboarding paywall if later placements convert well.** Celebs converts in about 10 places. Without the first paywall, users reached the reveal faster and converted later at the result, share and reopen moments. [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s), [17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1059s)
- **Retest old losers.** A test that failed twice worked on the third try because the rest of the app had changed. [16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=977s), [16:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=995s)
- **Tag each test with its hypothesis.** Log the hypotheses, results and learnings. Agents and new hires can then apply a principle like "remove friction" to other apps. [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1138s)
- **Use "happy friction" in long, personal funnels.** The ad warms the user. A long survey then reflects their answers back and builds intrigue. Direct web visitors get a shorter path. [25:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1537s), [26:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1587s)
- **Guide first use of an agent.** Preset buttons send starter prompts. The team aims to find about 10 use cases that impress every user fast. [33:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2012s), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2172s)
- **Humans make ideas, AI makes ads.** Explore new concepts across the "infinite search space" before you make variants of a winner. Look at other industries for ideas, not only the market leader. [41:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2460s), [47:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2878s), [49:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2992s)
