# My App Makes $20K/Month: How I Found A Simple Idea And Turned It Into A Profitable Business

## Summary

Ethan is 19. He left a computer engineering degree after AI tools built in a day what took him a week. He is a former national wrestling champion, so he built CutCoach, an iOS app that gives combat sport athletes a weight-cut plan. It launched in September 2025 and made over $20K in its best month (2026-04). Growth came from small organic posts with high intent, then micro-influencers found on TikTok and Reels, then those influencer videos run as paid ads. Pat Walls argues that a painful problem in a small, passionate niche can beat a broad app.

## Key facts

- $0 to over $60K total revenue in 6 months, with about 38K to 39K downloads (2026-04). Best month: over $20K. [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=71s), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=101s)
- In wrestling season, athletes must make weight, so revenue peaks. [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=101s)
- His first app took 6 months, in summer 2024. In early 2025 he built several apps and marketed none, which he calls a mistake. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=154s)
- He built the MVP in about one month (June 2025) with Cursor and ChatGPT. [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)
- Version 1 had coaches give plans to athletes. His own club did not use it. In July and August he changed it so the app gives the plan to the athlete. [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)
- Stack: Cursor, ChatGPT, Supabase, Vercel, the OpenAI API, RevenueCat, Mixpanel, Superwall and cron jobs. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=477s)
- Early organic posts got 200 to 500 views each but brought 10 to 15 downloads a day (2026-04). [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=508s)
- The app logs meals with AI nutrition extraction, tracks daily weight and suggests meals that fit the limits. [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=654s)

## Tactics

- **Solve a problem inside your hobby.** You know the activity, so you know the pain. He also asked ChatGPT for app ideas within a chosen niche. [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s)
- **Copy the layout of a proven app.** Wireframe in Figma, then take elements from a popular app in the same niche. Pat notes that apps like Duolingo test their onboarding for years. [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s), [7:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=432s)
- **Front end first.** Tell Cursor to match the Figma design, then build the back end. [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=457s)
- **Beta test with a real group.** He gave the MVP to his wrestling club. Their non-use showed the coach-first idea had too much friction. [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)
- **Small views can convert.** A before-and-after UFC weight cut video with a call to action reached a narrow, high-intent audience. Pat estimates a 5% to 10% view-to-install rate. [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=508s), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=562s)
- **DM micro-creators.** He found creators by scrolling his For You page and Reels. Most said yes. He started with creators at 1K to 10K views per video, then moved to 20K+. [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=585s)
- **Run influencer videos as paid ads.** This added revenue and scaled more efficiently. [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=585s)
- **Pick niches where failure is costly.** Athletes pay about $30 because missing weight costs far more. [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=780s)
