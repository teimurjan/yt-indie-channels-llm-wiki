# I speedran a new app from 0 to $2m/year in 6 months (here's how)

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Daniel Heintzman. Daniel co-founded Sway AI, a dating-profile analyzer, and took it to $2M ARR in 6 months. He is now Head of Product at Cal AI, which MyFitnessPal acquired. Sway built distribution before code: an Instagram account about Hinge prompts sold $15 manual profile reviews. TikTok slideshows took it to $30K to $40K a month, and paid ads scaled it from there. Daniel also explains Cal AI's retention work, its "three screens an influencer can show" design rule, and his framework for new app ideas.

## Key facts

- Sway AI: $0 to $2M ARR in 6 months, launched summer 2024 with co-founders Luis (marketing, operations) and Charles (iOS engineer). Daniel did design and product. [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s), [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=882s)
- Before the app, followers paid about $15 for a manual profile review over DM. [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=340s)
- Slideshows got 10K to 50K views each. One Christmas Day post reached about 4M views and made $5K to $6K in one day (2024-12). [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=459s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=578s)
- Organic plus one influencer got Sway to $30K to $40K a month before paid ads. [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=645s)
- About 11% of revenue came from a credits upsell. AI photo packs sold at $14.99 (1 pack) and $49.99 (10 packs). [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1131s), [19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1167s)
- When Daniel joined, Cal AI made a couple million a month. It has a 3-day trial. The MyFitnessPal deal closed around December, and the apps stay separate (2026-05). [25:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1529s), [26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1563s), [49:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2949s)
- Joseph cites an AI therapy app at about $800K a month on iOS plus $200K on Android, from Sensor Tower (2026-05). [39:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2373s)

## Tactics

- **Mine Reddit for the idea.** People post their Hinge profiles on Reddit for feedback. Sway made that private. [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=207s)
- **Batch slideshows.** Copy the most viral posts of a successful niche account. Design backgrounds in Figma, then fill slides from a spreadsheet with Canva. One or two hours makes a month of posts. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=390s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=437s)
- **Warm up unbranded.** Post aesthetic, unbranded carousels until videos get traction. Then add branding and a CTA on every slide. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=490s)
- **One influencer as the ad engine.** Ads ran from the creator's own page. The limit was creative volume. Dating creators sell their own coaching, so few promote apps. Cal AI does not compete with fitness coaches, so it avoids this. [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=665s), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=757s), [13:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=822s)
- **Onboarding as a sales funnel.** First, short videos explain the problem and the app. Then easy multiple-choice questions, then the dream result. The aha moment comes after the paywall to keep the path simple. [15:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=938s), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1297s), [23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1412s)
- **Default to annual and sell to whales.** Annual collects cash up front to fund growth. Weekly plans grew slower. Consumables have no spending cap. [19:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1177s), [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1211s)
- **Retention at Cal AI.** Redesigned logging, camera and food detail screens. Groups and leaderboards (from Strava and Ladder), streaks (from Duolingo), and shareable progress photos and milestone badges. [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1621s), [27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1662s), [29:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1767s)
- **Three demo screens.** Influencer videos show the camera, the loading screen and the food detail page, so those must be clear at a glance. [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2163s)
- **Idea framework.** Easy to understand in a video, novel, and in a big proven market. Plug into a large viral content niche with many cheap influencers. [37:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2230s), [40:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2453s), [43:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2586s)
- **Influencer equity.** Ship many vibe-coded apps and give big creators equity, through a talent agency. [44:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2669s)
- **Test with influencers.** They give guaranteed views, so they test one variable. UGC tests virality and payment at the same time. [47:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2826s), [48:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2909s)
