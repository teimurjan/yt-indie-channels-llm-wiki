# This App Makes $25,000/Month

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Lukas Hermann, a software developer in Germany who built Stagetimer, a remote-controlled countdown timer for live events and video productions. He found the idea by watching a friend walk to another room to start a timer on an old Flash app. He validated it with one free post in a small video-production subreddit. He shipped the MVP in 3 days and earned his first dollar 224 days later. He now runs it with his wife. Growth comes from Google search on niche integration keywords and from a branded free tier that spreads through event freelancers.

## Key facts

- About $25K/month revenue, 20K users, 4,400 paying or past paying customers, and 86K unique visitors a month (2025-06). [0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=46s)
- First commit in November 2020. MVP shipped in 3 days, built in 1 hour each evening. First dollar after 224 days. [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=246s), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=176s)
- He quit his job in 2022 at about $3K/month. He reached $10K/month in September 2023. [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=246s), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=307s)
- Traffic (2025-06): about 50% from Google and about a third from referrals and word of mouth. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)
- Monthly costs (2025-06): $280 servers, $250 tools, $1,400 paid ads. Margin 80% to 90%. [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=591s)
- Stack: JavaScript, Vue.js, Node.js, Airtable as CRM with automations, Postmark for email. He pastes code into Claude rather than using Cursor or Copilot. [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=549s)
- Customers include election TV broadcasts and horse races. [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=660s)

## Tactics

- **Find the niche subreddit.** He searched for where video production people hang out. He posted a link with no price and asked for feedback. Users replied with feature lists. One post per subreddit, no spam. The post had 58 upvotes. [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=79s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=0s)
- **Use the stack you know.** New tech means learning its limits first. Known tools let him ship in 3 days. [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s)
- **Split roles in a family business.** His wife, a former teacher, learned marketing. She runs Google Ads, sales emails and support. He handles product, finance and development. [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=189s)
- **Integration pages for long-tail search.** Write docs and YouTube videos for searches like "countdown timer Stream Deck companion". Low views, but searchers have a concrete problem and buy. Watch what people click and double down. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=461s)
- **Brand every shared link.** Inspired by Dropbox referrals, every shared timer shows the logo with the name "stagetimer.io". People see it at events and try it. [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)
- **Free tier for freelancers.** Freelancers bring the free version to client events, hit a limit, and buy. [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)
- **Pick a niche too small for big companies.** It is perfect for a solo founder. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s)
- **Find ideas by observing work.** Watch people outside tech do jobs in awkward ways. Many tools still have 1999 interfaces. [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=695s), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=739s)

- "I believe there's so many $1 million niches with little apps that you can build. The only hard thing is to find them." [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=739s)
