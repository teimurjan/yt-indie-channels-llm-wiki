# I Copied a $100M SaaS, Undercut Their Prices and Hit $10K/Month

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Abhishek, the solo founder of Youform, a cheaper Typeform alternative. Abhishek calls his method "finding the gap". He picks a popular tool with many users, finds what its users complain about, and builds a small version that fixes those pains. Users of his earlier chatbot builder, Botflow, used it as a conversational form after Typeform raised prices. That pointed him to the gap. Youform launched in 2023 and makes about $11K MRR on a generous freemium plan.

## Key facts

- Youform launched in 2023 and grew to about $11K MRR in 2 years (2025-08) ([0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=56s)).
- Botflow, his earlier no-code chatbot builder, had about 200 users. Most of them used it as a conversational form ([1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s)).
- About 35,000 registered users and about 500 paying customers (2025-08) ([7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s)).
- Over 90% of features are free. Free to paid conversion is 1.5 to 2% (2025-08) ([7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s)).
- About 35,000 unique visitors per month and over 4M form submissions (2025-08) ([7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s)).
- He built the MVP in 2 weeks ([6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s)).
- Monthly expenses are under $1,200 (2025-08) ([7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s)).

## Tactics

- **Do not invent.** An indie founder cannot build the next Uber. Pick a market with big players, because they prove demand. Then find the gap ([2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=163s)).
- **The four-step gap method** ([3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s)):
  1. Pick a popular tool. Search Twitter and Reddit for "X alternative".
  2. List the pains it does not solve. Price is a valid pain only for a tool that is too expensive.
  3. Reach those users with a basic landing page. The message is the key.
  4. Build a one-click migration tool. On Youform, you paste a Typeform URL and get a Youform form in seconds.
- **Check more than price.** Typeform's price rise was not enough on its own. He searched Twitter, Reddit and Typeform's forum for missing features ([1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s)).
- **Ship a tiny MVP.** Version one had basic fields (name, email, star rating) and CSV export. It had no integrations, no logo and no real landing page. His wife made the current landing page after 200 users ([6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s)).
- **Use AI against abuse.** A free plan attracts bad actors. He uses OpenAI for fraud detection ([7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s)).
- **Stack.** Laravel on AWS, Cloudflare, Stripe, OpenAI, Slite for help docs, Simple Analytics, Mailgun, and a feedback tool ([7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s)).
- **Open gaps he names.** A simple Calendly alternative, because Calendly moves up market. A Forest habit app alternative, because its recent reviews are mostly one star ([4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=256s)).
- **Avoid.** Do not build social media or marketplaces as an indie founder ([4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=256s)).
- **Lessons.** Messaging must speak to the ideal customer. After that, product quality and support keep users and drive referrals. Do not neglect marketing ([8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=521s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=538s)).

## Quotes

- "In my opinion, the best approach to build a SaaS is you should not invent things." ([2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=163s))
- "It is fine if the pain point is pricing, but only if pricing is too high." ([3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s))
- "Don't build things because you can." ([8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=538s))
