# How I grew my mobile app to $17K per month

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews George Lampropoulos, an 18-year-old college freshman who cannot code. He built Wrestle AI, an AI wrestling coach that analyzes match videos, with the vibe-coding tool Rork, ChatGPT and a $250 Fiverr developer. It went from idea to the App Store in about a month and makes about $17K/month. Growth came almost only from influencers, starting with his co-founder Kaden, a big wrestling influencer. He shares a six-step build process and a detailed influencer outreach and deal playbook.

## Key facts

- Revenue is about $17K/month, about $8K of it MRR from monthly plans and the rest from yearly plans (2025-12). [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=96s)
- Pricing: $9.99/month or $59.99/year. The free trial sits on the yearly plan, so many users buy yearly. [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=96s)
- Over 2M social impressions and about 17,000 downloads (2025-12). [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=96s)
- At 15 he lost his savings on a self-improvement app. Three dev agencies took 1.5 years to ship it, and the 10K-follower waitlist lost interest. [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=150s)
- Build: one week of prompting Rork, one week for a $250 Fiverr developer to add payments and auth, two weeks on Apple review rejections. [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=220s), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=280s)
- Launch: about 3,000 to 4,000 pre-orders. The AI API went down 30 minutes before launch and came back at 5 a.m. The app reached #19 on the App Store and made over $1,000 on day one. [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=1061s)
- A second app, Green, made with a 1M-follower influencer, got about 100 downloads from 1.8M impressions because it was not novel. [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=361s), [7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=421s)
- Costs: Rork $25/month, Supabase about $30/month, OpenAI inference $40 to $60/month, ChatGPT $20/month (2025-12). [16:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=1004s)
- He takes no money out until $100K MRR. [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=614s)

## Tactics

- **Idea pillars: uniqueness, helpfulness and a "gotcha moment".** The gotcha moment shows the value in about 5 seconds and stops the scroll in a short video. [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=361s)
- **Design for the buyer.** Answer "who is it for?" and "what UI fits them?", then give the answers to Rork. Plan how users share the app organically. [7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=421s), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=466s)
- **Use cheap public APIs**, for example a nutrition database for a calorie tracker. [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=466s)
- **Copy onboarding that converts.** Study Opal and Cal AI. His formula: educate, personalize with questions, create FOMO, then let the user do the gotcha moment but hide the result behind the paywall. A long onboarding adds sunk cost, so more users start the trial. [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=559s)
- **Hire for what you cannot do.** His best return was a developer from Pakistan. Test a hire on a small task first. Past $5K/month, invest in product quality with designers. [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=559s), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=614s)
- **Launch on a partner's audience.** A pre-order with his co-founder's audience put the app at #18 on launch day. When that audience was saturated in month two, he spent about $500 on more influencers. [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=685s)
- **Influencer outreach.** DM 100 people a day. Start the DM with "Paid promo?". Early on, buy followers for your personal Instagram and pay for verification, then switch to the business account. [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=756s)
- **Close on the phone.** Move to a call fast, and do not take a "what's your offer?" hard close in DMs. [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=756s), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=812s)
- **Deal terms.** Pay 20% to 50% up front for 4 to 5 videos, with a view guarantee at a $2 to $5 CPM. Example: $225 for videos with a 100K-view guarantee, $50 to $100 up front. [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=812s)
- **VA timing.** Use an overseas VA for mass DMs at the start and at large scale. In between, he DMs from the business account for 20 minutes a day. [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=812s), [14:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=874s)
- **Build an ecosystem.** Wrestle AI also has a calorie tracker with photo scan and practice mode with live coaching. It adds training plans, a match calendar and a weight journal. [15:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=923s)
