## Summary

Tim Gabe, a product designer, breaks down eight A/B test results from subscription apps. He groups them into two levers: the free trial and paywall flow, and the upgrade moment. Each case ties one small design or copy change to a named bias. The biases include framing, the decoy effect, anchoring, loss aversion and the certainty effect. He reports each result as a percent lift.

## Key facts

All results are as Tim reports them in the video (2025-07).

- Blinkist: a visual day-by-day timeline of the 7-day trial gave +23% trial signups [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s).
- Headspace: renaming "trial" to "30-day guest pass" gave +7% new signups [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=106s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=168s).
- Moonly: a free trial on the annual plan only gave +39% conversion and +47% revenue per 100 installs [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s).
- Slopes: a one-tap trial start gave +25% trial starts [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=259s).
- Headspace: a 7-day trial on monthly and a 14-day trial on annual gave a double-digit lift in conversions [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s).
- Mobbin: "Pro" labels on locked content across the product gave +35% free-to-paid conversion [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=375s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=437s).
- Busuu: free users get one language and must upgrade for a second. This gave +83% conversion among engaged users [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=472s).
- Uber: one estimated price in place of a price range gave a double-digit lift in rides per user [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=525s).

## Tactics

- **Show the trial as an itinerary.** People fear the unknown more than commitment. Show what happens each day of the trial [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s).
- **Reframe the copy.** Pick words for the emotion you want. "Guest pass" feels like an invitation, while "trial" feels like an obligation [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=168s).
- **Give the best plan an exclusive perk.** Put the trial on annual only, or make the annual trial longer. That plan then looks like the obvious choice [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s).
- **Cut steps to start a trial.** Each extra step before the trial costs users and revenue [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=259s).
- **Show what users miss.** Label paid content inside the free product. Loss aversion drives the upgrade [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=437s).
- **Lock where intent peaks.** Do not punish beginners. Put the lock at the point where engaged users want more [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=472s).
- **Be specific on price.** Uncertainty kills purchase decisions. One clear number beats a range [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=525s).

## Quotes

- "People [do not] fear commitment as much as they fear the unknown." [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s)
- "Highlight what's missing, not just what your users will gain." [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=437s)
