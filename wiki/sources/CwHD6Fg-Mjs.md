## Summary

Connor Burd is a 23-year-old founder with no computer science background. His six or so mobile apps have made over $1M in subscription revenue. He built his latest app, Payout, in under 2 weeks with AI. Payout finds class action settlements that a user can claim. It reached $20K/month in about 50 days and won the "Build and Grow" prize in the RevenueCat Shipaton hackathon, which had over 55,000 entrants. His playbook: copy the best onboarding flows, write the data structures before coding, use creator content, and then scale with Facebook ads.

## Key facts

- Portfolio: about 6 apps and over $1M in total revenue (2025-10) ([0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=58s), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
- Payout: launched in early August 2025. $20K/month, over 12,000 downloads and over 1,300 new subscriptions (2025-10) ([1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
- Hackathon: the RevenueCat Shipaton had over 55,000 entrants. The Build and Grow award ($65K total prize pool) goes to the app with the most growth in 2 months ([1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
- First app: Hotspot Events, a social app. It took 6-8 months to build and failed. He then moved to utilities, which one person can scale more easily ([1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=119s)).
- Paywall: weekly and yearly plans. The design pushes users to the yearly plan, which has the highest LTV ([10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=610s)).
- Stack: Figma (UI, App Store screenshots, icon), Mixpanel, Claude Code, Next.js and TypeScript, Vercel, GitHub, Expo and RevenueCat (analytics and price tests) ([11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=714s)).

## Tactics

- **Collect onboarding examples.** Download about 20 apps in the niche, plus other well-designed apps. Screenshot every onboarding screen, put them in one row in Figma, pick the best and restyle them ([2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s)).
- **Spend as much time on onboarding as on features.** With a paywall, about 90% of users only ever see the onboarding. Make it emotional and show the main benefit. Make it feel personal, and add charts that make it feel scientific ([2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=239s)).
- **Write the data structures before vibe coding.** Write a text file with JSON examples and a note for each field. Then Claude or Cursor does not have to guess. Build the core feature first, and give the AI screenshots so it writes whole screens ([3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=239s), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=299s)).
- **Validate cheaply.** A modified copy of an existing app is already validated. For a new idea, look for people who ask about the problem in TikTok and Instagram comments. He skips the waitlist and just builds ([7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=434s)).
- **Creators first, then paid ads.** One niche creator drove thousands of downloads. Repeat the formats that work with more creators. Those videos become a library of tested ads for Facebook. Ads must be entertaining, which lowers cost, and must still sell the product ([8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=492s), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=554s)).
- **Target core desires.** Health, money and looks. Payout sells "make money", which is easy to pay for ([11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=683s)).
