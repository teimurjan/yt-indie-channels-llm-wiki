## Summary

Jay was a software engineer at Amazon. On a trip he got the idea for NomadTable, a meetup app for solo travelers, expats and nomads. He quit to build it as a solo founder. About 15 months after launch it makes $140K MRR with 1.8M users (2026-09). The video is a day in his life. It covers his routine and his habit of working from new cities. His main advice: only build what you care about.

## Key facts

- $140K MRR, 1.8M users, about 100K meetups through the app, about 1 year and 3 months after launch (2026-09). [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)
- Bootstrapped and run as a solo founder (2026-09). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=0s)
- He started the idea while still a software engineer at Amazon. [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)
- His WhatsApp is busy each morning with creators, which points to a creator-driven growth channel. The video does not give details. [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=56s)
- He spent 4 months in Bangkok, a hotspot for the app. [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s)

## Tactics

- **Frame the risk.** He already had a big tech job and knew he was able to get one again. Regret over "what if" felt riskier than failure. [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)
- **Use your own app on the ground.** In Bangkok he went to meetups from his own app and talked to users. This gave him many feature ideas and showed him new sub-segments of his audience. [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s)
- **Stay close to the original problem.** Many products drift from what made them good as they mature. He puts himself back in his users' shoes. [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s)
- **Routine.** He checks his phone first for breakages and creator messages. He leaves for a cafe or WeWork by 9 to 11 a.m. He works 2 focused hours first, then takes breaks. After 3 to 4 hours he changes location. [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=56s), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=199s)
- **Evenings.** He works out (running, padel, tennis, lifting), sees friends, then does light work on the couch before bed. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=357s)
- **Travel as input.** New places and awkward situations give him energy and ideas. For him this is worth more than a perfect desk setup. [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=317s)
- **Passion as an edge.** Someone who enjoys the work every day is hard to compete with. [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=405s)
- **Start now, even with a job.** Software is fast to build, so you can test ideas next to your work. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=465s)
