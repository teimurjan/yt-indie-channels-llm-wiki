## Summary

George is a non-technical former wrestler. He runs three AI sports apps: Wrestle AI, Brawler (boxing) and AbMax (fitness, written "AdMax" in the description). He built the first app, Fight AI, with Rork about a year earlier to get feedback on his own training. Users kept asking for wrestling, so he partnered 50/50 with wrestling creator Kaden Henshaw. Wrestle AI passed 100,000 downloads on about 10 creators alone. Brawler and AbMax grow mainly through paid ads, where raw screen recordings and stitch-style videos beat polished creatives. Joseph Choi hosts.

## Key facts

- Revenue: almost $41K MRR and $84K total revenue in the last 28 days, across three apps (2026-10). It was about $25K/month two months earlier. [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s), [30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1827s)
- Fight AI: an MMA video-analysis app. Posts by George and friends made about $1,000 at pure margin. [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=216s), [4:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=255s)
- Wrestle AI launch: a pre-order hyped by Kaden made $8K in the first week. The app reached #19 in Sports, briefly above the NFL app. [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=503s)
- Wrestle AI paywall: $59/year or $9.99/month, split about 50/50. The 12-month LTV of a monthly subscriber is about $40 (2026-10). Users are 90% high schoolers. [22:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1363s), [23:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1393s), [23:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1436s)
- Word of mouth: in January, a quarter to a half of Wrestle AI revenue came from teammates seeing the app at matches. The season runs September to March. [15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=957s), [16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1005s)
- Brawler: a raw screen-recording ad ran at a $10 CPA against a $40 break-even, about 3x to 4x ROAS (2026-10). Revenue is 75% ads and 25% influencers. About 2,500 active subscriptions. [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=86s), [26:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1609s), [42:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2578s)
- AbMax: one stitch ad has had over $20K in spend and returned close to $60K over 3 months. TikTok CPA is about $15 per purchase. On Meta, a $24 CPA is close to 2x ROAS (2026-10). [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=788s), [15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=927s)
- Brawler paid a creator with about 1M followers $4,500 for three videos. He expected 25% to 50% back and got 75%. [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1633s)

## Tactics

- **Find partners through mutual follows.** His Instagram DM VA was his first expense. The VA reached Kaden through someone who already knew him. [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=327s), [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=365s)
- **Set structure with creator partners.** Use minimum posts and vesting. George had none and got lucky. [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=397s)
- **Influencers first in an empty niche.** No other wrestling app existed, so creators had no sponsorship fatigue. He kept about 10 creators and cut the weak ones. In fitness, Cal AI already wore out subtle integrations. [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)
- **Ads for scale.** Expect to burn about $2K in the first 2 weeks while the pixel learns. Scaling spend too fast raised CAC. Mass-producing creatives is the open problem. [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s), [30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1827s)
- **Clarity and raw creatives.** An ad must clearly show what the app does. Raw screen recordings, talking heads and stitch or reaction videos work. Polished visuals got near-zero revenue. Put the CTA in the first 15 seconds. [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=544s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=648s), [14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=849s)
- **Ranking-style paid ads.** A "best wrestling apps" list that ends with your app fails organically but works as a paid ad. [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=699s)
- **Virality lowers paid CAC.** The Rork Max launch got 10M views on X, and Meta CAC dropped by about 75% for a month. AbMax runs clipping at about a $0.30 CPM to build awareness. [33:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2027s), [34:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2089s)
- **Onboarding pattern.** Show the main feature, then social proof and an attribution question. Ask profile and pain-point questions, build a program, show a sample breakdown, then the paywall. A calorie tracker for making weight raised retention. [19:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1189s), [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1582s)
- **Retention.** An in-app TikTok-style feed favors new posts. Partner creators must also post there, which solves the cold start. Daily notifications copy Duolingo's style. [37:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2238s), [40:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2415s), [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s)
- **Founder calls.** On the third app open, Brawler offers a call with a founder. Calls showed that users had never found features built over weeks. [42:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2556s), [43:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2601s)
