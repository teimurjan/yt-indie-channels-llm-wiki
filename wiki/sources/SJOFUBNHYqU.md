# I make $120K/year from 3 mobile apps

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Presh, founder of The Wellness Company, a small studio of health and wellness apps. In about 10 months he launched Go Polar, SunSeek and Posture AI and grew them to about $120K ARR. Each app solves a problem he has himself, and all of them serve one overlapping audience, so each new app markets to existing users. He worked 7 years for an angel investor before he started. His five-step playbook: pick a passion, find a problem in it, build an MVP fast, plan distribution from day zero, and put in the reps.

## Key facts

- Revenue went from $0 to $120K ARR in about 10 months. Monthly revenue is $10.4K, all subscriptions (2025-12). [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)
- Over 20,000 downloads, mostly organic App Store search. Over 1,500 paying subscribers (2025-12). [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)
- Free-to-paid conversion is about 8%, which he links to the niche focus. Day-30 retention of paid users is 61% (2025-12). [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)
- Go Polar tracks cold plunge and sauna sessions with Apple Watch or iPhone. SunSeek tracks morning light and vitamin D, with a lux meter. Posture AI scans posture for desk workers. Tempo, not yet launched, combines wearable data and blood tests into a health-span score with an AI chat. [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s), [9:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=594s), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=655s)
- He dropped out of college after year one, cold-emailed investors, and worked 7 years for an angel investor. There he saw thousands of founder pitches. [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)
- Tools cost about $300/month. Apple takes 15% under $1M, so margins are 80% to 85% (2025-12). [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=736s)
- Stack: Figma, Claude Code and Xcode, Supabase, iMessage for support, RevenueCat for paywalls, Sentry, PostHog, Linear, ChatGPT, CapCut, Framer for landing pages, Lovable for MVP tests. [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=697s)

## Tactics

- **Build for your own problem.** No good app tracked cold plunges, so he built one. If you feel the pain, you build a better product and care more about the details. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=291s), [5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=330s)
- **One audience, many apps.** Users of one app also fit the others. New launches are not cold marketing, because the list already exists. [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)
- **Step 1 and 2: passion, then problem.** When you do the activity yourself, problems come naturally. [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=453s), [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s)
- **Step 3: ship an MVP fast** with Lovable, Replit or Bolt. Do not overthink the solution. [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s)
- **Step 4: distribution on day zero.** Find a creator in the niche, or be that creator, because you know why you built it. [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s)
- **Step 5: put in the reps.** He runs the company as a product studio because the first app rarely wins. SunSeek was better on day one than Go Polar because of earlier reps. [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=574s)
- **Pat's takeaway.** Serving the same customer is the secret to a multi-app portfolio, not random apps in different categories. [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=786s)
