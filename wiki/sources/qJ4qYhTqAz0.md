# How Top Apps Scientifically Design Paywalls That Convert

## Summary

Tim Gabe argues for "structure before price". When a paywall already sells, the offer's structure can move revenue more than the price. Structure means which plans show, which plan comes first, how the trial works, and how often you test. He gives three case studies: Mojo, Built With Science and Pregnancy+. In all three, revenue went up at the same price. He also shows his agency's redesign of the Undertone paywall.

## Key facts

- **Mojo** (video and story editor) passed $1M/month in 2024. In January 2026, it hid AI credit packs on its first paywall and changed nothing else. New subscription revenue rose 14%, and the result was clear within a week (2026-01). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=0s), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)
- **Built With Science** (fitness) spent about $100K/month on Meta ads. It showed annual first, moved monthly one tap away, and laid out the trial day by day. The share of annual picks went from about 60% to 75%-80% at the same price (2026-10). [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)
- **Pregnancy+** has about 80M downloads. It went from 1 paywall test every 3 months to 3+ tests a month after tests became fast to ship (2026-10). [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)
- In a Pregnancy+ test, a free trial with nothing to pay up front made 140% more revenue than a discounted intro price. The team had expected the discount to win. [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)

## Tactics

- **Remove escape hatches.** Credit packs beside subscriptions turned one choice into a comparison (choice overload). Users who wanted to subscribe bought small packs instead. Superwall calls such an option an "escape hatch". [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)
- **Use the default effect.** The plan shown first feels like the normal choice, and many people pick it. Lead with annual. [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)
- **Show the trial day by day.** Built With Science added this in the same change that put annual first. [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)
- **Prefer a free trial over a discount.** Through the endowment effect, users who try the full product feel it is theirs. They convert more often and stay longer. [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)
- **Do not blur the user's own content.** Undertone's old paywall half-blurred the user's own therapy recaps, which feels like a loss. The redesign removes the blur and asks one question: "How many sessions a month do you need?" [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)
- **Make testing fast.** Faster test shipping let Pregnancy+ run more than 10x as many tests. [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)
- **This week's homework.** Run one paywall test with the same price and change only the structure. For example, hide one option or change which plan comes first. [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)

## Quotes

- "Once your app sells, how you build the offer can move revenue more than the price number does." [0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=5s)
- "Whatever plan you show first tends to feel like the normal choice, and lots of people just go with it." [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)
- "None of the wins in this video came from cutting the price." [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)
