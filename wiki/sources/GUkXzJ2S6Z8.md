# How to Build a $2M/Month App (Copy Me)

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Marcus Burke, a Meta ads consultant for subscription apps. Marcus has run Meta ads for 14 years. He scaled an app to $2M per month, and one ad concept passed $1M in spend. He explains why D2C talking-head UGC fails for apps, and why app ads must prepare viewers for the App Store. He walks through a Reddit plus Claude copy research pipeline and AI ad production with Higgsfield and Weavy. He also covers account structure, placement analysis and Meta value rules that push spend toward older, higher-converting users.

## Key facts

- Marcus ran Meta ads for 14 years. He scaled an app to $2M per month. His best ad passed $1M in spend (2026-06) ([0:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=0s)).
- Career: InnoGames (mobile games, 2012 to 2019), Blinkist from 2019 for about 1.5 years, then StudySmarter and Tandem. He went freelance in 2022 ([0:02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=140s), [0:05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=323s)).
- At Blinkist, his team was among the first to scale ads that sent users to a blog instead of the App Store ([0:05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=323s)).
- The Moonate green-noise ad concept had about 100 versions of one cut and about 400 iterations in total. Lifetime spend was well above $1M ([0:09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=548s), [0:10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=603s), [0:10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=634s)).
- His freelancers make 99% of client ads with AI (2026-06) ([0:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1300s)).
- Most users aged 18 to 24 cannot pay about $70 per year for an annual plan ([0:40:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2452s)).
- Trial to paid comes too late for Meta. A trial starts on day 0, but conversion happens about 7 days later ([0:45:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2733s)).

## Tactics

- **Fix the funnel first.** A purchase event plus "register, then paywall" is no longer enough. Marcus also works on onboarding, paywalls and activation ([0:06:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=380s), [0:06:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=416s)).
- **Copy with a reason.** Know why an ad works for its product before you adapt it ([0:07:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=427s)).
- **Do not hide that it is an app.** Say "app" in the copy or show a demo. Otherwise the App Store jump hurts install rate ([0:08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=491s)).
- **Use the audience's words.** Moonate users masked tinnitus with noise, so the team made tinnitus versions. Hyper-specific copy such as "rewire toxic attachments" draws engagement from the right people. Meta then finds them with broad targeting ([0:09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=548s), [0:12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=733s), [0:13:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=793s)).
- **Soft CTA for apps with long onboarding.** The onboarding makes the sale, so the ad sells emotion. Simple apps such as the Me+ habit tracker need little explanation ([0:14:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=873s), [0:15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=932s)).
- **Web vs app funnels.** Web funnels reach people far from the solution with advertorials, such as Nibble by Headway. App ads must sell more up front ([0:16:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1014s), [0:19:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1163s)).
- **Statics.** App promotion campaigns deliver statics poorly. Start with video, but still test statics such as text walls, which reach Facebook feed ([0:19:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1163s), [0:20:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1258s)).
- **Five-step ad process.** Copy research, visual research, copywriting, generative AI, editing ([0:25:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1524s)):
  - Copy research: feed 10 to 20 high-engagement Reddit threads to Claude. Ask for sub-audiences, pains, desires and vivid emotional language. App reviews and competitor reviews also work ([0:24:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1465s), [0:25:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1524s)).
  - Visual research: train your Reels feed on the niche for 5 minutes. Note the common visual language, not only viral posts ([0:29:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1773s)).
  - Copywriting: make one context file per sub-audience plus a product file. Ask Claude for three distinct hooks, or feed it a competitor text wall to adapt ([0:32:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1956s)).
  - Generation: use Higgsfield as a model aggregator. Make the scene with an image model first, such as GPT Image 2 or Nano Banana. Then animate it with Seedance 2 or Veo 3. This saves credits ([0:33:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2019s), [0:34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2080s), [0:35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2142s)).
  - Scale: build a node workflow in Weavy for a proven format and generate many variants ([0:35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2142s), [0:36:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2202s)).
- **Why AI.** It is not cheaper yet. It makes creative variables controllable and testable ([0:22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1345s)).
- **Audience equation.** Message + media + content type, times funnel, equals audience. Video skews younger on Reels. Raw text walls and statics skew older on Facebook ([0:38:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2291s), [0:39:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2352s)).
- **Check placement and age mix.** Ask age in onboarding to map trial to paid by age. Young trials are cheap but convert worst ([0:41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2501s), [0:43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2622s)).
- **Value rules.** Keep broad targeting, but tell Meta that users 45+ are worth 30 to 40% more. Also make ads for older users ([0:43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2622s), [0:44:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2682s)).
- **Pull out outlier ads.** Meta spends about 80% on one ad in an ad set. Move on-target ads with unusual placement mixes into their own ad sets ([0:47:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2849s)).
- **Ad set buckets with own budgets.** Group by media type, format (text walls, memes, UGC) and message. Set budgets and cost targets per ad set. Memes must buy traffic at about half price ([0:50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3015s), [0:52:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3176s)).
- **Small budgets.** Put all ads in one ad set and learn weekly. Split only near break-even ([0:54:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3269s)).
- **Ride identity trends.** ADHD, anxious attachment and overthinking are wellness buzzwords. Weekly user interviews also feed ad copy ([0:56:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3391s), [0:57:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3451s)).
