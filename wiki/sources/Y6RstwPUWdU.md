# Beginners Guide to Buying & Selling Apps 2026

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Steve Young of App Masters, who started in apps in 2010. Steve buys apps that already have organic App Store distribution but weak monetization. He funds them with loans. Then he adds a hard paywall, raises prices and widens keyword coverage. He explains his ASO keyword system: find the keyword first, then build the app. He also covers the numbers that make Meta ads work and paywalls that change by traffic source.

## Key facts

- One app he bought in 2024 has made over $1M lifetime and about $500K last year, mostly from App Store search (2026-07). [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=517s)
- Another health and fitness app made about $6M last year, about $4M of it from organic App Store search (2026-07). [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=517s)
- He says one or two channels can reach $5M ARR. The big three are ASO, Apple Ads and Meta. [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=360s)
- Keyword rule: two keywords with search volume of about 30 each, ranked number one, bring about 100 to 150 downloads a day. [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s), [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1131s)
- A 2022 purchase charged a $2 to $3 one-time fee. A hard paywall at $60/year took it from $1K to $2K a month up to $8K to $10K a month on the same downloads. [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s), [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1131s)
- On one app, the hard paywall raised trial start rate from 6% to 13%. Trial-to-paid did not change, so revenue doubled. [19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1174s)
- A client app named after a found keyword hit number one in its first week and made $1,600 in 21 days with no trial. It is now close to $20K MRR (2026-07). [15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=929s), [24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1466s)
- Migraine Buddy makes about $50K to $70K a month on about 30K iOS downloads, per Sensor Tower (2026-07). [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s), [12:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=778s)
- Loan math: buy a $10K/month-profit app for $350K with a 10-year loan at about $5K/month. He nets about $5K/month from day one. [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2591s)
- He bought Indie App Santa in 2024 and earned the price back in 3 to 4 months. An $18,000 purchase flopped. [41:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2469s), [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s)

## Tactics

- **Choose a channel that fits the founder.** Founders with no edge in creatives can start with ASO and Apple Ads. Both only need keywords. [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=282s), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=360s)
- **Hunt painkiller niches.** Start from categories with high revenue per install. Niche down from "meditation" to "green noise" or "migraine tracker." [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=653s), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s)
- **Judge difficulty by the results page, not tool scores.** Check how many top apps use the keyword in the title. Check the review counts of the apps at rank two and three. Few titles and about 100 reviews mean you can win. [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s), [12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=769s)
- **Find outlier apps.** Look for apps that rank high with few reviews. Put them in ASOMobile to see every keyword they index for. [23:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1404s)
- **Title and subtitle are most of ASO.** Copy the App Store autofill phrases into the subtitle and keyword field. [21:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1269s), [21:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1308s)
- **Stack keyword variants on a winner.** After an acquisition he found 24 variants of the main keyword with volume of 24+. Add related terms to the title, as in "Migraine Buddy: Track Headache." [46:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2783s), [47:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2849s)
- **Use a hard paywall to test product fit.** Aim for 5% to 10% trial starts. If trial-to-paid is below the 30% to 40% norm, fix the product, not the channel. Expect some one-star reviews. [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1716s), [29:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1775s), [20:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1234s)
- **Run Meta only above a floor.** Aim for about $3 revenue per install, or a $10 to $15 cost per trial. One app at $1 RPI failed on Meta. [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=990s), [33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2030s)
- **Send early pixel events to Meta.** Registration, content view as paywall view, and initiate checkout. Still optimize for purchase. Put all creatives in one ad set. [34:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2090s), [38:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2287s)
- **Vary the paywall by source.** Show a soft paywall to search and referral users to limit bad reviews, and a hard paywall to Meta traffic. Onboarding answers can set the price. [38:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2310s), [39:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2388s)
- **Buy apps with organic distribution.** Paid-only apps are "a money eating machine." Look for weak monetization or untapped channels, such as Meta for older users. [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s), [44:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2652s)

## Quotes
