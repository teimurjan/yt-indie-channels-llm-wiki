## Summary

Tim Gabe opens a four-part Figma series on landing page design with the first three of nine "secrets". Secret one: research look and feel and page structure on curated inspiration sites, then collect it on an "inspo" page in Figma. Secret two: "Frankenstein wireframing", which joins the best sections from other sites into one page skeleton. Secret three: do not underestimate assets, typography and copy, because they make up most of a layout. A cloneable Figma file comes with the video.

## Tactics

- **Steal ideas, not work.** He cites "good artists copy, great artists steal". Copy ideas without shame, but never lift a design as is [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=78s).
- **Split research in two.** "Look and feel" sets tone, shape, imagery and colors. "Structure" sets which sections to use [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=140s), [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=201s).
- **Get responsive design for free.** Sections taken from live sites are already tested on mobile. Save the source links so you can check their responsive layouts later [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=201s), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=388s).
- **Organize in Figma.** Hoard tabs, screenshot what you like and put it all on one "inspo" page. Add comments, then filter favorites. Keep at least two color palettes, one style for imagery and favorite sections. Iterate on a separate "explorations" page [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=201s), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=264s), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=327s).
- **Frankenstein wireframe.** Treat sections from your structural inspiration as Lego bricks. Stack them in an auto layout to get the page skeleton [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=327s).
- **Spend real time on assets.** Search Figma Community for "shape", "pattern" or "mock-up" and like your favorites to find them later. A mock-up can break a flat illustration style [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=570s).
- **Play safe with fonts.** Beginners do best with popular, tested fonts. Match the fonts in your inspiration as closely as you can [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=692s).
- **Fit copy to layout.** A layout can look great with two words and weak with four. Match the copy length of the inspiration design [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=753s).
