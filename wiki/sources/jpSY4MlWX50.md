# I Built an App with Cursor, Made $30K, and Quit My Job.

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Paulius Masalskas, who built Creator Hunter, a SaaS database that matches startups with influencers. He cannot code well, so he built it on his daily commute with Perplexity, Bolt and Cursor, and designed it with Framer templates. It made $30K in about 7 months (2025-05) and he quit his job on 24 December 2024. Growth came almost entirely from build-in-public posts on X that joined a bigger debate.

## Key facts

- Creator Hunter has over 1,000 users and over 350 paid users, with $30K in total revenue about 7 months after launch (2025-05). [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=59s)
- Launch night brought about 20 to 30 sales. [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=187s)
- Margins are about 90%. Vercel and Supabase are on free tiers. The main cost is a scraping API (2025-05). [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=657s)
- His best post got close to 500K impressions on X. [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=554s)
- Background: door-to-door sales at 18, then web design, copywriting and sales funnels. He studied game development but "completely sucked at code". [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=85s)
- Full time on Creator Hunter since 24 December, when he quit his job. [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=715s)

## Tactics

- **Plan with Perplexity.** Tell it you are a complete beginner and get a deep-research, step-by-step build plan with current docs. [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=223s)
- **Bolt for the MVP, Cursor for production.** Prompt in Bolt one step at a time and paste errors back. Then download the project into Cursor to connect Supabase and add Clerk auth. Bolt gets you about 80% of the way. [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=223s), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=286s)
- **Design from a free Framer template.** Pick a clean template, a good font and a button style, and ship fast. [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=440s)
- **Spend 80% of design time above the fold.** The hero needs the full message in seconds: a headline on time, money or pain saved, a subheading, and a big call to action. [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=475s)
- **UI prompts.** Ask Cursor for "clean, functional, modern" with free shadcn components. Sketch flows like onboarding in Figma and paste them in. [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=510s)
- **Join the bigger conversation.** Build-in-public posts rarely go viral alone. His top post used a simple visual demo and raw reaction, and it tapped the debate over whether AI coding can build a full SaaS. [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=554s)
- **Ship often, borrow formats.** He adapted a post format Marc Lou used for the influencer conversation. Copycats who clone exactly do not take off, so keep an edge that AI tools cannot copy. [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=607s)
- **Use domain knowledge.** People already know the problems in their field and can "prompt their way" to a product. [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=685s)

## Quotes
