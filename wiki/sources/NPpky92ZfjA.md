## Summary

Zac Cherin and his partner Brendan built Hiro Analytics, a reporting and automation platform for email and SMS marketing agencies. They launched in October 2024 and passed $1M ARR in about 19 months. The core idea is to sell to agencies instead of brands. One agency brings 10 to 30 brands and many seats. Agencies are easy to find because platforms such as Klaviyo and Shopify publish ranked partner directories. Pat Walls and Gus argue that every industry has an agency layer like this.

## Key facts

- Hiro went from $0 to over $1M ARR in about 19 months after an October 2024 launch (2026-06). [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=58s)
- Stripe showed about $96.2K MRR, growing by close to $10K MRR each month, with very low churn (2026-06). [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=90s)
- Pricing: subscription with a 30-day free trial for every agency. The fee scales with client count: about $500/month for 10 clients, $600/month for 20. Agencies with about 200 clients pay a couple thousand a month. [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=149s)
- Seat pricing: about $10/month per seat. An agency with 10 strategists and 20 clients who each want a seat adds about $200/month. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s)
- Background: the founders ran an email marketing agency for about 6 years with about 30 clients, all matching Hiro's ideal customer. [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=187s)
- Stack: early on, Snowflake and AWS with high bills, plus Retool for the app layer. For the last 6 to 8 months it is all built with Claude Code. [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=267s)
- The path took about 10 years: a data visualization startup that Amazon bought, work at Amazon, a failed newsletter, the agency, then Hiro. [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=571s)

## Tactics

- **Sell to the agency layer.** One sale brings many brands and many seats. All agencies use the same platforms and have the same problem, so 99% of features help every user. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)
- **Use partner directories as a lead list.** The Shopify and Klaviyo partner directories rank agencies by size and influence. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)
- **Pitch on two pains.** Agency owners want more clients without more staff, and they want time back. Manual reports for 20 clients are the real pain, not the charts. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=490s), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=698s)
- **Defend against the platform.** Hiro also connects Postscript, Attentive and Omnisend. Klaviyo is unlikely to show data from competitors, and a $1M business unit does not matter to a public company. [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=445s)
- **Find your own agency layer.** Look at your current industry. Example: restaurants have agencies that run DoorDash and Uber Eats ads and must report results. Ask ChatGPT or Claude where else the model applies. [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=490s), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=657s)
- **Recommendation as a channel.** Pat compares it to Gusto and QuickBooks, which his bookkeeper chose for him. [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)
