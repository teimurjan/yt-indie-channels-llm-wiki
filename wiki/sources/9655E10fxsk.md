# He went from 0 to $150k by building an AI fashion app

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Reid Moncada, founder of Fitted, an AI closet app that digitizes your clothes, suggests outfits and supports resale. Fitted began 8 years earlier as a college laundry service in 18 cities. Reid found his co-founder Max through a viral TikTok of an outfit generator Max built in class. He paid Max $20 a video, then gave him equity and a revenue share. Max's account drove about 95% of 500M+ views and 600K downloads. Reid says distribution is solved, but the product has no product-market fit yet. His next steps: drop the hard paywall and add a crypto token.

## Key facts

- Over 500M lifetime views, 600K downloads, and about $150K in subscription revenue (2025-11). [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=572s), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=600s)
- Downloads cost 1 to 15 cents each by putting ad spend behind organic videos that already went viral (2025-11). [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)
- About 95% of distribution came from Max, cross-posted to the main account and Instagram Reels. [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=574s)
- A week in a Palm Springs apartment, funded by $10K from a friend, produced a 2M-view video on day one and a 100K-person waitlist. [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=214s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s)
- One format hit 136M views. A repeat hit 60M. They get a viral video about once a month. [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=859s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s)
- The web app did not convert. Reid paid $1,400 for a 15-minute call with Nikita Bier, who told him to go mobile. The mobile app took 6 months. [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=336s)
- Over 2M clothing items uploaded (2025-11). FitCoin trades close to $250K in volume a day (2025-11). [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s), [18:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1127s)
- Partnerships: Paramount (official virtual closet for Clueless), a Poshmark one-click resale integration, and TaskRabbit taskers who catalog closets. [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1340s)

## Tactics

- **Crawl, walk, run with a creator.** Find a creator with 1K to 50K followers who can go viral but is not expensive yet. Pay $20 a video on a sub-account ("Fitted by Max"). Once it works, add vested equity and a revenue share, and raise it as effort grows. [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=38s), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1624s), [27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1662s), [28:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1712s)
- **Copy the Breathwrk playbook.** Breathwrk grew a TikTok account from zero to about 3M followers with a creator who had equity. [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)
- **Embed the creator in the product.** Max reads comments and DMs, joins design meetings, and shapes the roadmap. He is the voice of the customer. [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=669s), [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=730s)
- **Show the product, do not sell it.** The breakout video was a plain "how to use the Fitted closet". Hook fast, then invite viewers to follow the build. [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=408s), [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=805s)
- **Test features on TikTok first.** "Stalking my boyfriend's closet" hit as a web-only feature, so it went into the mobile roadmap. Write taglines that make people stop scrolling. [15:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=911s), [16:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=983s)
- **Boost winners with ads.** Keep finding new formats, cross-post them everywhere, and put ad money behind the hits. [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s)
- **Remove the hard paywall to win share.** He says the hard paywall limits growth and churn is high. The next updates make the app free with paywalled features. He reduces closet-building friction with receipt scraping, selfie add and quick add. [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s), [23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1433s)
- **Hold celebrities until the product is ready.** He can reach celebrities but waits until retention is solid. [24:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1493s)
- **Hide the token behind a familiar paywall.** Like Dupe's token and Duolingo's gems, FitCoin aims to join consumers, brands and speculators in one flywheel. [20:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1233s), [21:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1293s)

## Quotes
