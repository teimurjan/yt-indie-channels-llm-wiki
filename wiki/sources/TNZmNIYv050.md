# Faceless: From Immigrant to $1M/Year

## Summary

Nick Buzz is an anonymous designer on X who says he makes $1.2M a year. He grew up in India, moved alone to Canada at 17, freelanced through university, and then worked at Meta. At the end of 2022 he quit with about 2 months of savings and a baby on the way. He grew an anonymous X audience by "roasting" landing pages and sold small design kits. Then he co-founded Baked, a monthly design subscription agency, with a designer named Alex. Baked grew from its first revenue in July to $160K MRR within months.

## Key facts

- Nick says he makes $1.2M/year as an anonymous designer (2024-09). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=0s)
- He entered about 200 logo design contests over 6 months before his first win, which paid 10,000 rupees (about $200). [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=209s), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=256s)
- His university tuition in Canada was $16K, about 3x what a local student pays. [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=452s)
- In 2016 a developer found him on Dribbble. The first client paid $3,200 for 2 days of work and stayed for 3.5 years. Freelancing paid $80K to $90K/year. [8:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=494s)
- His Meta (Facebook) internship paid $8,000 USD. [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=576s)
- He left Meta at the end of 2022 with about 2 months of savings. [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=614s)
- His first product was a kit of about 50 landing pages for $9. Landing page roasts then went from $50 to $300 each and peaked at $6K to $7K in one month. [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=777s)
- At peak he ran about 12 projects at once and worked 16 to 20 hours a day. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=810s)
- Baked's first client paid $4,317/month and came from a "tiny UI" post. [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=877s)
- Baked MRR: first money at the end of July, then $24K, $48K, $94K, $110K, $120K and $160K, then it flattened to $100K to $120K (2024). [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)
- Team: Nick and Alex alone until December, then one full-time hire plus a few part-time freelancers. [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)

## Tactics

- **Stay anonymous.** Nick wanted no attention on his identity and did not want family to question why he left Meta. Anonymity also suited him as an introvert. [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=677s)
- **Roast other people's work in public.** He quote-tweeted founders' landing page posts with "roasted" and showed his improvements. This showed skill without anyone asking. [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)
- **Validate with a cheap product.** A $9 kit of 50 landing pages proved that people pay. After that, he raised prices on roasts. [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)
- **Focus after burnout.** Running 12 projects split his focus. He narrowed to one business, Baked. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=810s)
- **Go back to content.** At first, leads did not pay $2K to $6K a month. The team went back to posting roasts. Then inbound leads came. [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=855s)
- **Market all the time, with a strong tone.** Nick says controversy works. [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)
- **Be authentic with a faceless account.** Talk to people, tell your story, build a community, and do not only post about money. [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=1012s)
