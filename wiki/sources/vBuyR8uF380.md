## Summary

Tim Gabe shows his full Figma workflow for a desktop landing page, part 3 of his "9 Secrets" series. He builds each section from a "Frankenstein" wireframe and reference designs: nav, hero, cards, a curved-text section, a CTA and a footer. The example product is a payment app he calls "Moneypile". Two secrets come up in this part: secret 7 is minimalism, and secret 8 is to break a pattern on purpose to create interest. He iterates in grayscale first, then duplicates frames to test colour schemes.

## Key facts

- Spacing follows an 8-point grid, for example 64 and 72 px gaps. Layouts use a 12-column grid. [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=215s), [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=821s)
- Plugins and assets: Vector Logos for brand logos, Feather Icons, the Unsplash plugin for photos, and "Text to Path" for arced text. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=154s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=275s), [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=636s), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=698s)
- Timeline of the video: nav at 0:25, hero at 1:34, colours at 5:40, cards at 6:38, curved text at 10:36, CTA at 14:45, footer at 16:32. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=0s)

## Tactics

- **Trace the reference.** Drop the reference design into the frame, lock it and lower its opacity. Then match its text styles and copy length on top. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=94s), [16:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=993s)
- **Equal-width auto layouts in the nav.** Give the logo group and the button group the same fixed width, so the links stay centred. [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=26s)
- **Name button sizes.** Reuse the nav buttons in the hero as "button medium" and "button large" to keep buttons consistent. [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=154s)
- **Use stroke-based icons.** Stroke icons stay consistent across the page. He swapped a filled icon for one from Feather Icons. [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=215s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=275s)
- **Opacity colours while exploring.** Use one dark colour at an opacity for buttons and cards, so a background change does not need many edits. Switch to real hex colours at 100% in the final file. [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=275s), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=1306s)
- **Duplicate frames to test palettes.** Try inverted, beige, blue, white and green versions side by side. He fixed dark text for accessibility and made the CTA black for contrast. [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=341s), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=1367s)
- **Minimalism (secret 7).** Use minimal illustrations, few colours, one font and a small set of font sizes. [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=341s)
- **Reuse auto-layout cards.** Duplicate a finished card or section rather than rebuild its padding. Mask parts of one illustration to get matching card art. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=400s), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=523s)
- **Break patterns on purpose (secret 8).** The arced text breaks both the horizontal text pattern and the 12-column container, which adds interest. [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=821s)
- **Group copy, offset the button.** Treat heading and text as one unit and place the CTA a bit lower, so the section feels designed. [14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=886s), [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=946s)
