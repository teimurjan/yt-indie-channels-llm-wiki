## Summary

Morrie Schonfeld co-founded Pingo, an AI voice companion for speaking practice in about 20 languages, with his college roommate Michael. Pingo launched in late December 2024 from a Northwestern dorm. Fourteen months later it makes $450K to $500K/month and got 350M to 400M views in 30 days (2026-04). About 100 creators post videos that always show the app. The top format is Pingo roasting a user until they cry. Morrie also covers creator pay, a Russia virality trap, paywall tests and Y Combinator.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $450K to $500K/month, 14 months after launch (2026-04). [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=135s), [37:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2226s)
- Views: 350M to 400M in the last 30 days (2026-04). [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=196s)
- Users: about 3M (2026-04). [33:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1988s)
- The top roast video got 52M views. [0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=44s)
- The two founders made all videos themselves until $20K MRR, for about 3 months. [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1105s)
- Team: two people until mid-October 2025, then four with one content hire and one engineer. [40:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2404s)
- Creators: about 100 after cutting 30 to 50. The top 20% to 30% drive most views. [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1484s), [40:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2451s)
- Russia: about 200K Russian users in February brought $0. App Store and Google Play block payments there. [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=98s), [28:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1684s)
- Annual plan: $99.99/year. [46:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2813s)
- Trial test in the US: 3-day and 7-day trials convert within 0.3 to 0.7 points. ARPU differs by about 20 cents. The 3-day trial has a 5% to 7% higher cancel rate. Internationally, the 7-day trial converts about 2 points higher. [52:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3164s)
- After YC, they booked about 100 investor meetings and closed a round in about 3 days. [55:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3301s)

## Tactics

- **Build virality into the product.** Michael only wanted to build an app that goes viral. Talking to an AI was the hook. [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=380s)
- **Show the product in every video.** Viral videos without Pingo do not convert. His rule: virality that converts shows the product and does not just tell. [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=90s), [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1000s)
- **Let creators find formats.** The roast format came from the custom scenario feature. Creators prompt Pingo to be mean. Users now ask for a "mean Pingo" toggle. [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=737s), [13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=798s)
- **Format history.** Beginner learning hooks came first. Cultural hooks followed, such as the TikTok ban and RedNote, K-dramas and Squid Game audio. Humor came last. Slideshows worked briefly, then burned out and converted far worse. [19:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1168s), [20:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1256s), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1306s)
- **Recruit language learners.** VAs DM micro creators on TikTok and Instagram, then move to email for a CRM. Targets: under 10K or 10K to 30K followers, with at least one breakout video. Search "learning German" first, then college majors, then native speakers. [25:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1528s), [31:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1865s), [32:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1926s)
- **Pay base plus non-stacking bonuses.** Creators get a base per video plus a tier bonus at 50K, 100K, 200K, 500K and 1M views. Only the highest tier pays. Noise creators run on a capped CPM model for fast tests and scaling. [34:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2040s), [35:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2103s)
- **Run creators like a VC portfolio.** Cut the bottom, keep the top, coach the middle. Coaching fixes lighting, camera angle, audio and framing, and adds an activity like knitting. [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1484s), [41:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2497s)
- **Watch which country goes viral.** Native speakers find the jokes funnier, so videos spread among them, not learners. Track viewer country and payability. [28:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1696s)
- **Paywall: show the trial timeline.** A page with "reminded 2 days before trial ends" won the tests. The annual plan has the trial and shows a weekly price. The monthly plan has no trial and sits behind "view all plans". [45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2716s), [46:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2764s), [49:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2987s)
- **Layer trial offers.** Users who abandon a 3-day trial and then get a 7-day offer convert higher. Next test: a 10-day or 14-day offer after a 7-day abandon. [50:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3048s)
- **Stripe web checkout in the US.** They shift US billing to Stripe for better margins, so paywalls split US and international. [47:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2866s)
