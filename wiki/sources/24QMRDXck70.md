# Our No-Code Apps Made $50M Using THIS Paywall

## Summary

Joseph Choi (Consumer Club) interviews Vahe Baghdasaryan of Tangent Growth on the Superwall podcast. Vahe says he has run over 10,000 paywall experiments and helped apps add over $50M in revenue, including Cal AI, Quitter, Claim and Rizz. His main point: many apps that look healthy leave money on the table. They copy a friend's paywall, never improve it, and miss key paywall placements. He walks through real paywalls: multi-step free-trial paywalls, trial toggles, spin-the-wheel discounts, price-hidden paywalls and transaction abandonment offers. He also gives benchmarks and a simple way to set prices.

## Key facts

- Benchmarks (2025-11): a trial start rate above 15% is good. Trial-to-paid above 30% is good. Take an app with no trial and mostly US users. Install-to-paid of 10%+ is very good. 4% to 10% is OK. Under 4% is a problem. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=291s), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=337s)
- An entertainment app with 40K to 50K organic installs/month went from 1.1% to close to 4% install-to-paid from April to September. It got no new users and no paid spend. [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=397s), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=415s)
- Some apps added $100K in revenue in 3 months from paywall experiments alone. [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=364s)
- A well-made multi-step paywall gives a 20% to 40% uplift over a single-screen paywall. [14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=884s)
- Claim had a 34% trial start rate and a 50% to 55% trial-to-paid rate. After that, they removed the trial and went paid-only, because it made more money. [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=953s), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=999s)
- A spin-the-wheel discount beat a plain discount screen by a double-digit percentage. [21:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1318s)
- Rizz trial toggle: $9.99/week with a 7-day trial, or $6.99/week with "skip free trial". [22:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1328s), [23:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1389s)
- About 10% of users pick the no-trial option on a transaction abandonment paywall with a toggle. [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2085s)
- Superwall's free tool at paywallexperiments.com uses 422 profitable paywall experiments to suggest tests from a screenshot. [2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=128s)

## Tactics

- **Repeat "free" many times.** Claim's 3-screen paywall says "free" 5 to 7 times. It calls the annual plan "Try it free", not "yearly". The button says "Try for $0". A reminder before the trial ends removes trial anxiety. [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=645s), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=737s)
- **Do not show the trial length on the plan card.** "3-day trial" makes people think about cancelling. Show a timeline instead. [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s)
- **Paid-only needs strong numbers.** You need high user volume, a strong trial start rate, a strong trial-to-paid rate and a pressing problem. [16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1017s), [17:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1079s)
- **Spin-the-wheel discounts.** The user "wins" the discount, so it feels earned. Copy tactics from e-commerce, such as Temu and Shein, but adapt them to your category. [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1105s), [20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1213s)
- **Trial toggle with or without a discount.** Test both. High-intent apps such as Cal AI keep one price. Lower-intent or younger audiences, such as Rizz, need a cheaper no-trial price. [25:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1512s), [26:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1614s)
- **Hide prices until the user taps.** Quitter's 3 screens show the app, then features, then App Store reviews. The prices appear only after "Start my journey today". The price sheet shows "60% off" and "limited spots". [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1624s), [29:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1759s)
- **Odd prices feel authentic.** $3.33/month ($39.96/year) feels like $3. It also gives about $4/year more per user to spend on ads. [31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1866s), [32:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1959s)
- **Transaction abandonment offer.** Discount only the annual plan. Discounts on weekly or monthly plans did not work in dozens of tests. Example: 80% off with a trial, 90% off without one. Add an undiscounted monthly or weekly plan as an anchor. [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s), [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2085s)
- **Set the price from competitors, ROAS and cost.** Pick a price that keeps ads ROAS-positive, or that pays back in about a month. For AI apps, add perceived value on top of the cost per user. [37:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2240s), [38:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2286s)
- **Cover every placement first, then test.** Show a paywall on session start (once a day) and after X actions. Show one when free credits run low. Show an offer after a user cancels a trial or a subscription. [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=510s), [41:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2467s), [41:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2515s)
