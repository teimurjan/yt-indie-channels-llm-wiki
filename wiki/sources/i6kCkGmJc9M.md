## Summary

Pat Walls visits Karl Hughes in Chicago. Karl runs Draft.dev, a productized service that writes technical content for companies that sell to software developers. He started it in 2020 with no website. He had only a page of writing samples on his personal site. He grew it past $2.5M in revenue in about two to three years. He positions it as the premium provider and sells quarterly packages. He then bought a second business through entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA).

## Key facts

- Draft.dev revenue: over $2.5M (2023-10), reached in about 3 years. The intro says 2 years. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)
- Team: 6 to 7 full-time staff and hundreds of contractors in 54 countries (2023-10). [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=335s)
- Product: packages of 12, 24 or 48 pieces of content (blog posts, tutorials). Practicing engineers write, editors clean up, and full-time engineers do tech reviews. Delivery is weekly or twice a week. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)
- Clients moved from early-stage startups to larger companies such as Redpanda, Cloudflare and Dropbox. [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=125s)
- Within 3 months of freelancing in 2020, he had too much work for one person and began to hire. [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=216s)
- The team shrank from its peak because tech was in a downturn. VC funding was about a third of the prior year (2023-10). [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=335s)
- Acquisition channels split in thirds. One third is referrals and word of mouth. One third is organic search plus social. One third is cold outreach and other channels. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=396s)
- He tried at least 10 side projects before Draft.dev worked. [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=575s)
- For the acquisition, he and a friend talked to 50 to 100 founders, short-listed 3 or 4 and bought one with debt. His 10-year goal is $100M in total revenue. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=0s), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=655s)

## Tactics

- **Start with no website.** He made a single "Karl's writing" page with samples. He sent it to his network and asked people to pay him to write for them. Calls with developer relations people showed the demand. [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=156s), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=216s)
- **Keep a network spreadsheet.** About 50 people he wants to stay in touch with, and a weekly reminder to email or meet a few. The first 5 to 10 customers came from this list. [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=312s)
- **Pick a niche at the overlap** of what you like and what customers pay for. Either side alone fails. [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s)
- **Position as premium.** No discounts, no one-off trials, quarterly commitments. Costs are high because engineers write the articles. [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=427s)
- **Validate part-time.** He kept his job until the business was viable. COVID moved conference budgets into content, and a narrow niche built trust faster than a general content agency. [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=575s), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=634s)
- **Buy instead of build.** ETA lets you skip years of work, at the cost of debt. [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=655s), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=716s)
- **For a first productized service,** choose work you can do yourself at the start. Interview 10 to 12 customers who were unhappy with existing agencies and redesign the service around their complaints. [12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=734s), [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=795s)
