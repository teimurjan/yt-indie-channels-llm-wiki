# How I Built It: $23K/month micro-saas

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Andy Cloke, a solo founder in London. Andy built Data Fetcher, an Airtable extension that pulls data from any API into Airtable on a schedule. He copied the idea from API Connector, a successful Google Sheets add-on, and brought it to Airtable's new marketplace. Data Fetcher makes $23K MRR from 600 paying customers at an 85% margin. Andy shares a six-step framework to find ideas on growing platforms and names platforms to build on now.

## Key facts

- $23K MRR from 600 paying customers, run solo (2025-08) ([0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s)).
- His first paid product was a TikTok influencer directory built on scraped data. It reached a few thousand dollars MRR and he sold it ([1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=63s)).
- At the time, API Connector for Google Sheets had about 100,000 users ([2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s)).
- Growth: first customer after a few days, $1K MRR after a few months, $3K MRR after a year. No-code integrations then took it to $10K MRR. It reached $20K MRR after 3 years ([6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- Costs: hosting about $2,500 per month, SaaS tools about $1,000, co-working $150. Margin is 85% (2025-08) ([8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=520s)).
- Each time growth slowed, he started a side business. He lost about 6 months this way ([9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=548s)).

## Tactics

- **Port a proven add-on to a new platform.** API Connector worked on Google Sheets. Airtable grew fast and had just launched a marketplace. He checked Airtable's forums for the same pain ([2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s)).
- **Why platforms work.** The marketplace sends a steady stream of qualified leads. Users trust approved apps. The market is big enough to change a solo founder's life but too small for VC-funded rivals ([3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s)).
- **Platform risk.** The platform can make your tool redundant overnight. Data Fetcher sits between Airtable scripting and no-code imports, so a native clone seems unlikely ([3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s)).
- **Six-step idea framework** ([4:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=285s), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=346s)):
  1. Find a growing platform. He uses Exploding Topics.
  2. Find a pain in its forums, on Reddit and on Twitter.
  3. Borrow a proven add-on and its UX from an older platform. Make it feel native.
  4. Check for a public API, a marketplace or an extension SDK.
  5. Do napkin math: users, how common the problem is, and price points from the older platform.
  6. Judge whether the platform will build it. Read the roadmap and support forums.
- **Platforms to build on now.** Notion, because its API is new and automation and reporting gaps remain. Figma, with export to Webflow, Framer or other site tools. Do not build for ChatGPT or Claude, because competition is too high ([6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=374s)).
- **Use-case content marketing.** He wrote blog posts and made YouTube videos for the most common integrations ([6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- **No-code integrations.** Ready-made integrations let less technical users succeed. This drove growth to $10K MRR ([6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- **Stack.** TypeScript, React and Airtable's extension SDK. Backend in Node, Postgres and GraphQL. Next.js, Tailwind and shadcn for the site. Heroku plus Hetzner for workers. Help Scout, Fastmail, Plausible, MailerLite and ChartMogul ([7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=472s)).
- **Claude as a focus coach.** He asks Claude to act as a business coach and talk him out of new side projects ([9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=548s)).
- **User tests.** He went almost a year without talking to users. One afternoon of user tests found UX issues, and the fixes raised revenue and usage almost overnight ([9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=596s)).

## Quotes

- "The biggest lesson has been the power of focus over chasing shiny objects." ([9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=548s))
- "Speak to the people that are using what you've built." ([9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=596s))
