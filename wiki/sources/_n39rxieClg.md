## Summary

Tim Gabe gives himself 20 minutes to redesign and animate the Midjourney landing page in Figma. He thinks the real page puts too much focus on the team and too little on the output. His version leads with a grid of generated images, a single primary CTA ("Join the beta") and three short sections. He then fakes scroll animation with Smart Animate and drag interactions between frames.

## Key facts

- Time limit: 20 minutes. The video title says he finished in 19 [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=0s).
- Critique: the original page does not show what the tool can produce. It puts weight on the people behind the project [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=27s).
- The original nav had about five actions: get started, a gallery link, join the beta, sign in and one more. He kept only "Sign in" and "Join the beta" [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=194s).
- Structure: three sections. The hero says "Midjourney is the AI tool of our generation". The prompt section says "Enter a prompt, watch the magic happen" above a "/imagine" input. The footer CTA says "Ready to be blown away? Join the beta today" [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=368s), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=428s), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=602s).

## Tactics

- **Lead with the output.** For a generative tool, the hero shows a dynamic grid of results [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=27s), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=72s).
- **Bulk image fill.** In Figma, use "Place image and video" to drop many images into rectangles in one pass [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=72s).
- **Blurred-image glow.** Duplicate a vivid image and add a heavy layer blur behind the grid. The background then takes the image colors. Fade edge images to 50% to 70% [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=133s), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=663s).
- **One primary action.** Make "Join the beta" the only filled button, in the brand color. A drop shadow with 24 blur gives it a glow [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=194s), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=256s).
- **Spacing system.** Use auto layout with "space between" for the nav and 24 px vertical padding. Size and space on an 8-point grid. Give sections room, such as 64 px gaps [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=318s), [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=541s), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=602s).
- **Name layers.** Clear names make the animation step easier [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=541s).
- **Scroll prototype.** Build one frame per scroll state. Load in with "after delay 1000 ms" and a slow 600 ms spring Smart Animate. Move between frames on drag, and offset elements a little so motion feels less uniform. Short travel distances make the motion less jagged [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=852s), [17:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=1035s), [19:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=1155s).
