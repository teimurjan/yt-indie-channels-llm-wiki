## Summary

David Park is the founder and CEO of Jenni AI, a set of AI research and writing tools for students and researchers. Jenni AI grew to $10M/year in 2 years, mostly with low-budget TikTok and Instagram videos (2025-03). In this video he shares his short-form video playbook in three steps: gather ingredients, explore, then exploit winners. It covers influencer outreach, deal terms, UGC creator pay, posting rules, viral series and a multi-account strategy.

## Key facts

- Jenni AI: over $10M/year, profitable, over 5M users (2025-03). Most growth came in the last 2 years. [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)
- Growth has three pillars: distribution, conversion and retention. This video covers only distribution. [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)
- Expect more than 50% of influencers to not reply. [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=614s)
- Bundle example: 3 videos for $1,200 instead of $500 for one. [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=732s)
- Cross-posting the same video to TikTok costs about 30% more. [13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=781s)
- UGC creator pay: about $2,000/month base plus a bonus, which ends near $3,000/month. Creators with an audience: $3,000 to $5,000/month plus a bonus (2025-03). [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=960s)
- The "POV: you have a paper due" series ran twice a week for about 6 months. It got hundreds of millions of views, tens of thousands of paid users, and probably over $500K (2025-03). [21:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1276s)
- Meme page reposts cost $50 to $100. [22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1345s)
- At one point Jenni ran 5 accounts on 3 platforms, so 15 videos a day. An account with 48 followers sometimes beat the main account with 55K followers. [24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1446s)

## Tactics

- **Research the audience first.** Ask users for their Instagram handles and see who they follow. Make a fresh Instagram or TikTok account that follows those creators. The algorithm then suggests similar creators who still get served. [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=171s), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=231s)
- **Judge creators by ceiling, not floor.** Look for at least one or two videos with 1M to 10M views. Check whether their sponsored posts flop. [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=288s)
- **Test variables.** Vary the person on camera, the hook, the account, the editing pace and the lighting. If an account is stuck at 100 to 300 views, start a new account with a new SIM card. [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=425s)
- **Outreach by email and DM.** DMs get a much higher reply rate. Follow up within a few days. Show real fandom of a specific video. Do not mention headcount, funding or press. [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=492s), [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=553s)
- **Align incentives.** Avoid paying everything up front. Pay half up front at most. Tie the rest to conversions with a coupon code or UTM link, or add view and click bonuses. [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=670s)
- **Negotiate by unbundling.** Ask for a price with exclusivity, usage rights, stories and a link in bio. Then remove items one by one to find the price of only what you need. Then ask for a 30% to 40% bulk discount. [13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=781s)
- **Post on every platform.** Repost to Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Remove watermarks and rewrite the hook text for each platform. Post at least one video a day and track hooks in a spreadsheet. [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1018s), [18:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1081s)
- **Turn a winner into a series.** Repost the same video with small changes several times a week. [20:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1215s)
- **Multiply accounts.** Make country and language accounts (USA, Germany, Australia, Mandarin). Hire charismatic UGC creators to remake the winning video on small accounts. This reaches different pockets of TikTok. [22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1345s), [24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1446s)
- **Prefer niche creators.** Large creators have agents who extract more money. Let the creator draft the script in their own voice, then tweak it. [25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1507s), [26:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1569s)
