# I Rebuilt a $1B App and Now Make $14K/Month

## Summary

Denis Yurchak is a trained diplomat who taught himself to code. In March 2025, Microsoft announced the end of Skype, and Pieter Levels tweeted for someone to build an alternative. Denis built Yadaphone, a browser dialer for cheap international calls, in one weekend. He launched it on Reddit and got his first sale minutes later. Seven months later it makes $14K/month as a pay-as-you-go service with a B2B plan. His playbook: pick markets with slow "dinosaur" competitors, ship fast, and take over the SEO a departing giant leaves behind.

## Key facts

- Yadaphone makes $14K/month and has 10,000 registered users, 7 months after launch (2025-10). [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=58s)
- Revenue: $4,000 in month one, $10,800 by month three and $13,300 in month six (2025-10). [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=80s)
- 4,500 users became paying customers. It has 20 enterprise clients. Users made 27,000 calls in September 2025. [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=80s)
- A 10-minute international call can cost $50 to $100 with a normal provider. Yadaphone uses VoIP to call mobile or landline numbers for cents. [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s)
- Pricing is pay as you go with credits. Competitors charge subscriptions of about $30/month or per-seat fees. Businesses buy a shared credit balance for all members. [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s)
- Denis learned to code in 2 months from books and courses. He had about 60 Twitter followers at launch. [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=198s), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=391s)
- He got 150 users in the first week. [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=452s)
- Stack: Next.js, Cursor, Vercel, Ahrefs and Stripe. Twilio handles calls and costs about 35% of monthly revenue (2025-10). [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=320s), [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=723s)
- His first enterprise customer still pays about $1,000/month (2025-10). [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=705s)

## Tactics

- **Ride a shutdown.** The Skype news made posts about alternatives go viral. Pieter Levels's tweet showed the demand. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=249s), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=471s)
- **Launch on Reddit with screenshots.** His first post showed the clean web dialer and a few sentences. Travel subreddits banned him fast, but sales came before the ban. Entrepreneur subreddits allow self-promotion and links. [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=391s), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=452s)
- **Tell a story.** His Reddit story: a lone engineer stands up to replace a giant company. [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s)
- **Pick markets with dinosaur competitors.** Telecom players are 20 to 30 years old and slow. A better design and UX wins in a validated market. [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=520s)
- **Ship one core feature well.** For Yadaphone, that was an outbound call. [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=520s)
- **Take the departing giant's SEO.** He found articles that rank for "Skype alternative" and messaged the authors on X, LinkedIn or email. Some added Yadaphone. Some replaced Skype with it. [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s)
- **Text every paying customer.** For 6 months he asked each one for feedback. This showed his best segments and caught bad experiences before bad reviews. [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=644s)
- **Add B2B early.** Enterprise clients pay more and churn less. He coded the enterprise plan overnight after a prospect asked for one. [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=644s)
- **Automate support to stay solo.** He built his own support system and admin panel with Cursor. [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=723s)
- **Do SEO from day one.** Competitors outranked him because his sitemap lacked the "www" prefix, so Google ignored his pages. [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=767s)
