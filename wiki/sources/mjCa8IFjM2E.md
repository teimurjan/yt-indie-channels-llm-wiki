# How I Built It: $10K/Month AI Image Generator

## Summary

Pauline built AI Crea, an AI home-staging tool. A user uploads a photo of a room, picks a style, and gets a redesigned room in seconds. She built it on nights and weekends while she worked at IBM. She quit 2 months before the interview. Her playbook is a fast MVP, early sales to real estate agents, and marketing where the customers already are.

## Key facts

- AI Crea has more than 8,000 users and makes about 8,000 per month (2024-10). The description says $100K/year. [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s)
- Pricing: a free trial, then a one-time pack with a set number of photos or a monthly plan with extra features (2024-10). [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s)
- She learned to code in engineering school and worked in data science at IBM. She left IBM in June 2024. [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=134s)
- The first MVP took about a month. It had simple features and bad image quality. A year of work followed to improve it. [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=243s)
- Running costs: about 3,000 to 4,000 EUR per month for servers, some ads, freelancers and events (2024-10). [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=541s)
- Stack: the team talks on Discord, and Crisp runs support chat. Buffer schedules tweets, Beehiiv sends her newsletter, and Brevo automates email. Supabase holds the database and auth. The app uses Next.js, and the AI models use Python. [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=541s)

## Tactics

- **Interview friends about their problems.** Ask how often the problem happens. Then ask four questions: can I do better, can I build it, can I monetize it, can I find clients? [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=162s)
- **Validate with payment.** She showed the MVP to real estate agents from her network and from online searches. Some paid right away. [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=204s)
- **Ship early.** You cannot know what clients want until you have clients. [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=243s)
- **Go where customers are.** Search Facebook, LinkedIn and Reddit for the customer type. For AI Crea, Facebook groups and in-person events work best, because real estate is a human business. For Next.js Directory, her developer audience on Twitter works best. [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=348s)
- **Launch for backlinks.** She launches on Product Hunt mostly for the SEO backlink, because its visibility is lower than before. She also launches on Twitter and her newsletter. [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=404s)
- **Price for positioning and support.** A low price brings a low-cost image and different clients. A higher price brings fewer clients but less support work. [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=437s)
- **Offer subscription and one-time payment.** Some people hate subscriptions. Make cancellation easy to earn trust. [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s)
- **Cut churn three ways.** She added a yearly plan, added a free trial, and improved output quality so the images look like photos. [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s)
- **Opportunity.** She thinks the biggest AI opportunity is to add AI into existing products and services, for example email replies. [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=618s)
