# I Built 3 SaaS Apps to $200K MRR: Here's My Exact Playbook

## Summary

Mike is an Australian founder and former agency owner. He runs a bootstrapped portfolio of B2B SaaS apps that make just over $200K MRR together. Each app copies a proven idea, starts with four equal co-founders and follows the same 10-step playbook. Lifetime deals (private, then AppSumo) fund each company. That cash pays for early SEO content. Then reviews and Reddit answers carry the move to MRR. The goal is $1M MRR in 5 years with a small team and no outside capital.

## Key facts

- Revenue: just over $200K MRR across the portfolio (2025-11). Mike says three businesses make that money and two more are on the way. The intro says five apps. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=0s), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s)
- Apps:
  - Curator.io: a social media aggregator for websites and events.
  - "thrill.co" in the captions, likely Frill: a feedback, roadmap and announcement tool.
  - Juno: digital signage for cafes, gyms, schools and shops.
  - Fluke: no-code product tours for SaaS.
  - Smile: group e-cards for B2B, launching soon.
  - Sources: [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=81s), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=106s)
- Goal: $1M MRR in 5 years with the smallest team possible and no raised capital (2025-11). [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s)
- Mike was a Flash developer, ran a digital ad agency and sold it. [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=138s)

## Tactics

The 10-step playbook. [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s)

1. **Pick an idea that exists.** New ideas need validation. Look for competitors that do well in spite of bad UX. [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)
2. **Define a good-enough MVP** from what competitors' customers want most, and ship fast.
3. **Offer a lifetime deal** for a single payment of about $59 to $100.
4. **Never give accounts away free.** People who pay use the product and give feedback. [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s)
5. **Sell a private LTD** in Reddit, private Facebook groups, X and LTD groups.
6. **Write content early** with the LTD money: landing pages, blog posts, competitor and "alternative to" pages. Google and ChatGPT index them over time.
7. **Launch on AppSumo**, either the marketplace (lower fee) or the Select program with their sales team. [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s)
8. **Run one last private LTD** at a higher price to your list, then close the LTD forever.
9. **Collect reviews** from LTD customers on Trustpilot and G2. This helps domain ranking. [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s)
10. **Answer questions on Reddit** where people ask about competitors. Reddit now does the job that Quora did. Live on LTD cash until MRR covers costs.

Other rules:

- **Four co-founders reduce founder fallout.** Teams are tech-heavy: front end, back end, designer and one generalist. "Good design sells." [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)
- **Optimize for salaries, not exits.** Spend little on ads and staff so profit goes to founders. [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s)
- **Avoid AI-dependent businesses.** Reliance on an API you do not control is platform risk. A documentation tool is a candidate idea, because AI needs good docs to recommend a product. [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=621s), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=655s)
- **Move the website to Framer** so marketing no longer needs the tech team. [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=674s)
