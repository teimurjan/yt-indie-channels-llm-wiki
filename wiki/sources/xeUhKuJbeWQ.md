# I built 4 SaaS apps to $100K MRR. Here's my exact playbook

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Tibo, a French founder who built and sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio. He now runs a portfolio of five SaaS products that make about $700K/month together. His main claim: builders add features, but they skip the hard part, which is to talk to users every day. He gives a 12-step playbook. Steps 1 to 8 find stickiness with a few real users. Steps 9 to 12 find and scale one or two acquisition channels. He runs a portfolio because one AI release or platform change can kill a single product.

## Key facts

- Revid (the transcript says "Rabbit.ai") turns video or text into engaging videos. It makes about $400K/month and grows about 10% month over month (2025-12). [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)
- Outrank started as a blog post generator and is becoming an all-in-one SEO tool. It just passed $200K/month (2025-12). [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)
- SuperX, a tool to grow on X, makes about $13K/month. PostSyncer, which posts to 10 platforms, makes about $1.5K/month (2025-12). [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)
- He bought Feather, a Notion-to-blog tool, for $250K. It makes about $10K/month (2025-12). [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)
- The portfolio makes about $700K/month from 50K paying customers. Total revenue grew about 20% a month for over 6 months (2025-12). [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)
- He estimates a 90% failure rate for new products. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=377s)
- Outrank grew from $0 to $20K/month with building in public only. Ads, a dedicated SEO hire and an affiliate program took it from $20K to $200K. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=810s)
- Elon Musk's takeover of X almost killed Tweet Hunter. At that time it made $200K/month. [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=981s)

## Tactics

- **Step 1: MVP in days or weeks.** Take shortcuts: no-code tools such as Bubble, or boilerplates. With a 90% failure rate, a year per try means 9 years to a win. [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=377s), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=423s)
- **Step 2: reach 5 to 10 relevant people** through tweets, subreddits or email. Feedback from people outside the target audience is worthless. [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=423s)
- **Step 3 and 4: build relationships and talk daily.** Learn their workflow and core pain, and find out why they come back. Until a product reaches $10K/month, its support link opens his Twitter DMs. A fix in 5 to 10 minutes can win a customer for life. [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=542s)
- **Step 5: learn the user's ultimate goal.** Helping with the end goal gives 10x to 100x more value. [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=542s)
- **Step 6: fix users' problems, not yours.** He uses his own products. Shipping a requested feature within an hour or two turns the user into an advocate. [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=542s), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=593s)
- **Step 7 and 8: iterate until they cannot live without it.** Do not go broad on acquisition with low retention, or 99% of new users leave. Complaints are a good sign of commitment. [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=593s), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=655s)
- **Step 9: go broad.** Product Hunt, social posts and building in public are free. He uses them until $10K/month. [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=701s)
- **Step 10: become a media company.** Pick social, SEO or cold email, and build a content pipeline with industry takes, testimonials and case studies. [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=762s)
- **Step 11: scalable channels.** SEO, ads and affiliates. An ad that works at $1K/month often scales to $10K or $100K. [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=762s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=810s)
- **Step 12: scale what works, kill what does not.** Growth usually comes from one or two channels. Go all in on them. [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=871s)
- **Portfolio for resilience.** He has a family. A new OpenAI feature or a platform change can kill one product, but not the company. [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=930s), [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=981s)
