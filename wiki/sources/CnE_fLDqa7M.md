# He Turned $500 Into $10M

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Karthik Shanadi in New York. Karthik and his co-founder Luke started a custom apparel business for fraternities and sororities in college with $500. It grew into four apparel brands: Greek House, College Thread, Threadly and Athlete's Thread. Karthik ran it on the side of full-time jobs at Amazon and Salesforce for about two and a half years. The newest brand, Athlete's Thread, sells licensed merchandise co-branded with colleges and student athletes after the NCAA name, image and likeness (NIL) ruling.

## Key facts

- Four brands, built with no funding. The video frames the total as $10M (2023-04). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s)
- Brand split: Greek House serves fraternities and sororities. College Thread serves college departments and organizations. Threadly makes custom merch for companies, fundraisers and non-profits. Athlete's Thread sells licensed athlete merch on Shopify. [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s)
- They hold about 300 college licenses (2023-04). [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=61s)
- Started in 2013. Year one did $50K, year two $100K, both part time while in school. [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s)
- Schedule at Amazon: night shift Wednesday to Sunday, then work on Greek House by day. At Salesforce: 8 AM to 5:30 PM, then the business until midnight. Six days a week for 2 to 2.5 years. [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=261s)
- Went full time in 2016. Four months in, a licensing audit for unreported royalties produced a $40K bill. Sales were just under $1M at the time. They negotiated a lump sum plus payments over 12 to 14 months. [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s)
- No in-house manufacturing. US print partners fulfil orders through a vendor portal that Karthik's team built. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s)

## Tactics

- **Sell in person through your network.** In college they texted friends in Greek houses, found who bought the T-shirts and pitched at student organization meetings. [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=396s)
- **Give suppliers software.** The vendor portal makes their account take about one tenth of the time to service. Partners then prioritize them. [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s)
- **Use licensing as a moat.** Brands need college licenses plus rights from each athlete. Karthik says 99.99% of companies will not get through that. [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=683s)
- **Brand through service.** They reply in under 4 hours and ship in 2 to 3 business days. They add neck labels, custom packaging and sports content made by a young team. [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=732s)
- **Channel mix.** (1) Outbound one-to-one email, then outbound sales. (2) An ambassador or affiliate program that gives free product in exchange for content and promotion. (3) Paid search on Google for high-intent queries such as a player's T-shirt. (4) Free marketplaces: Etsy and Google Shopping. [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s), [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s)
- **Three skills.** Clear communication, including async video for a remote team. The ability to teach yourself. Good co-founders, advisors and hires. [15:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=935s)
