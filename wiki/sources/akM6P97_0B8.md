# The Multipreneur: He Makes $10M/Year With 6 Businesses

## Summary

Pat Walls visits Greg Isenberg in Miami. Greg runs Late Checkout, a holding company with six businesses: an innovation agency, an AI community, a design agency, two AI-assisted marketing businesses and a community for "multipreneurs". He started every business with $0 and builds each one with his "ACP funnel": audience, then community, then product. After WeWork ran out of cash, he chose profitable, community-led businesses over venture-backed ones. He hires operators so he can start the next business once one makes $100K profit.

## Key facts

- Late Checkout makes eight figures of revenue and mid seven figures of profit (2024-06). Pat says over $10M/year revenue and over $5M/year profit. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=0s), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s)
- The six companies are LCA (innovation agency), You Probably Need a Robot (AI community) and a design agency. Boring Holdings owns Boring Marketing (AI-assisted SEO) and Boring Ads (AI-assisted ads). The sixth is a multipreneur community. [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s)
- Greg sold Islands, "a Discord for college students", to WeWork. He became head of product at WeWork, which was worth $47B before it ran out of cash. [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s)
- LCA charges clients such as Nike, Dropbox, Shopify and TikTok about $1.5M/year on average, against the typical agency fee of $1,500/month. Year one: $1.5M revenue from 1.5 clients. Year two: $5M+ (2024-06). [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s)
- At the start of LCA, his Twitter audience was about 10,000 followers. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)
- Dispatch, a design subscription, did seven figures in year one with $450K profit. [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s)
- Boring Marketing's AI SEO tool made $350K profit in year one and was forecast at $2M to $3M profit in year two (2024-06). [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=509s)
- Nice Break, built product-first, was shut down. [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s)

## Tactics

- **ACP funnel.** Build an audience on social media first. Move part of it into a community where you own emails and SMS and learn pain points. Then build the product they want most. Greg met his first LCA clients at "orange wine disco parties" he promoted to his audience. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s)
- **Price on outcome, not hours.** A new product line can make 9 to 10 figures for the client, so $1.5M/year looks cheap. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)
- **Spin up fast once the audience exists.** The Dispatch Twitter account hit a few thousand followers in weeks. The team made a landing page, brand and team in 72 hours. [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s)
- **Multipreneur model.** Several businesses at $25K/month each insure you against a Google or Instagram change hurting one. Start as a solopreneur. Once a business makes at least $100K profit, hire an operator and start or buy the next one. [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=130s), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=645s)
- **Have a lane.** His lane: community-based products beat non-community products. [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=645s)
- **Hire from your community and by cold DM.** He recruits the most active members ("nerds in residence"). He cold DMs interesting people, even employed ones. [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=529s)
- **Find ideas on Reddit and YouTube.** RedditList ranks subreddits by growth. GummySearch lists a subreddit's biggest problems. VidIQ sorts YouTube comments by top questions. [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=569s)
- **Stack.** Framer for landing pages, ConvertKit for email, Slack, Notion, Figma and Loom. [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=569s), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=629s)
