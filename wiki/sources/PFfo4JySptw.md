# 0 to $300k/mo in 45 days with my ai app (just copy me)

## Summary

Dillion Verma and his partner built Halo AI, an AI photo editor on the Gemini API, after 18 months of failed products. It was his first consumer app. One creator TikTok returned about 6x on cost, so he took a $100K personal loan to pay more creators. Halo AI reached $300K MRR in 45 days. Growth came from mass UGC: 85 creators, one fixed video format, and about 300 posts a day across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook. Views hit 600M in 60 days and 1.2B in 120 days. Dillion treats UGC as math: revenue equals views times conversion.

## Key facts

- $300K MRR in 45 days (2026-03). The app went live in mid to late October 2025 after 2 to 3 weeks of building. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=0s), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=203s)
- Reach: 0 to 600M views in 60 days and 1.2B views in 120 days. There are 85 creators now (2026-03), up from about 10, then 25 during the first scale-up. [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=27s), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=300s)
- A given format converts at a steady 0.3% to 0.5% of viewers. [6:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=383s)
- Hiring: posts in UGC Reddit and Facebook groups brought 30 to 50 applicants a day, about 600 in total. A 5-day paid trial at $20/video for 5 videos costs $100. [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=695s), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=743s)
- Hit rate: about 5% of videos pass 100K views and about 1% pass 1M. The team makes about 300 posts a day (2026-03). [24:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1471s), [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1502s)
- Pay: $20 base per video plus milestone bonuses of $60, $200, $500 and $800 at 20K, 100K, 500K and 1M views. That is under $1 CPM in effect. [35:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2156s), [36:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2204s)
- In a choice between CPM and milestone pay, 29 of 30 creators chose milestones. They chose it even though CPM paid slightly more. [33:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1990s)
- Paywall: 3-page free-trial paywall at $8.99/week. Weekly is the main plan. A skippable onboarding paywall plus a gated paywall at first action both convert at 16.5%. [40:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2440s), [41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2501s)
- Money: early UGC ROAS was 5:1 to 6:1. He funded growth with a $100K personal loan at break-even for 4 to 5 weeks. Margin is now about 50% (2026-03). The goal is $1M/month by June 2026 with paid ads. [50:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3039s), [51:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3062s), [52:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3123s)

## Tactics

- **Model views as a product.** Total views = creators x videos per creator x posts per video (platforms) x views per post. Raise each factor. [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=591s), [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=652s)
- **Screen creators for three things.** Past UGC work and a US base or US VPN are required. The trial then checks consistency, proactive communication and a feel for hook, build-up and payoff. Native English speakers and Gen Z creators do better. [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=805s), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=852s), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1050s)
- **One format only.** The hook is an absurd AI image, such as "Pranking my dad I cooked his koi fish". Next, a big Halo AI loading screen acts as a soft call to action. Last, a text-message story ends with a twist. A good format is viral, repeatable and converts. [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1212s), [22:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1335s), [23:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1384s)
- **No posting cap.** Some creators post 20+ videos a day per platform. YouTube and Instagram accept unlimited posts. On TikTok, ramp up from 3 to 5 to 10 a day. [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1092s), [18:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1135s)
- **Copy every hit across all accounts.** An automation sends each viral video to all creators, and all of them remake it the same day. [25:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1551s)
- **An AI coach in Discord.** An OpenClaw agent knows every video with 1M+ views (Gemini transcripts) and trending TikTok keywords from Scrape Creators. Creators ask it for scripts. [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1502s), [27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1663s), [28:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1737s)
- **Build a creator community.** Discord channels for wins, ideas and feedback keep creators motivated and share what they learn. [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1798s), [31:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1869s)
- **Pay one milestone per video.** Only one of the 4 posts needs to hit a milestone, so cross-platform views are free. Milestone pay is also easier to run than CPM. [34:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2052s), [38:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2294s)
- **Soft paywall, then a gate at the first action.** Users can skip the first paywall and browse templates. They submit an edit prompt and sign in. Then the paywall comes back. [42:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2559s), [43:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2619s)
- **Next ideas:** apply each new model type (text, voice, image, video) to existing demand. Focus on health, wealth and relationships for retention. [53:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3238s), [55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3300s)
- **Cut bad fits fast.** He kept weak creators 2 to 3 weeks too long. [55:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3353s)
