## Summary

Matt Giovanisci runs Swim University, a pool and hot tub care education site that made $1M in 2023. While he was marketing director at a pool company, he built the site at night. His boss found out and fired him. The business runs on one loop: a weekly long-form video becomes short-form clips, blog posts for Google and emails. Most revenue comes from courses and a printed book. It is a three-person family business, and Matt optimizes for lifestyle, not maximum revenue.

## Key facts

- Swim University hit $1M in revenue in 2023 (2024-05). The intro says over $100K/month. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=0s), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=65s)
- Team: Matt, his wife Steph (scripts, short-form writing, long-form editing) and his brother (short-form edits, customer service). All three earn the same pay. [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=65s), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=568s)
- Audience: YouTube channel with about 225K subscribers and a newsletter with 100K pool owners, mailed 2 to 3 times a week (2024-05). [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s)
- Revenue streams: YouTube AdSense, affiliate links (mostly Amazon), three pool courses, one hot tub course, and a physical book. Courses and books bring in most of the money. [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)
- Year one after the firing: $20K from the site plus web design work. Year two: $40K. [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=139s), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=200s)
- First product: a PDF. It did not sell at $50, $40 or $30. It sold some at $29 and a lot at $24. [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)
- Roasty Coffee reused the Swim University playbook, reached about 100K uniques/month in 2 years and was sold. [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=642s)
- Tools: Asana, Google Drive, WordPress, Semrush, Rank Math, Lasso for affiliate links, ChatGPT as a sounding board, and Klaviyo for email. [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=522s)

## Tactics

- **One script, many formats.** One long-form video a week, then three short clips a week on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Twitter and LinkedIn. Scripts become SEO blog posts. Visitors join the email list. Paid ads supplement this. [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)
- **Content quality beats production.** His top video every year is an unscripted PowerPoint on pool care from start to finish. [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)
- **Monetization ladder.** AdSense first for traffic, then affiliate only (the ads looked irrelevant), then his own products. [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)
- **Find price by stepping down** until people buy. Then bundle ebook and video course, and later print the book. [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)
- **Boring niche, outcome focus.** Nobody wants to master pool care. Take them from "disgusting" to "clear and safe" fast, and keep content short and punchy. [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=434s)
- **Pick a niche you like a little.** Walk around your house and list objects you care about. When content gets hard, some interest keeps you going. [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=476s), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=505s)
- **Lifestyle over money.** He rejects growth that needs a big team to manage. [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=797s)
