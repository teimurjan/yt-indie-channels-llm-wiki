# How I Grew My App to $13K/month (Even Though I Hate Marketing)

## Summary

Bhanu is a solo developer from India. He built SiteGPT, an AI chatbot that answers questions about a business's website content. He grew it to $13K MRR with $0 in paid marketing. Most traffic comes from Google, and about 90% of that comes from almost 50 free tools, a tactic he calls engineering as marketing. He shares a seven-step Ahrefs workflow to choose which free tools to build.

## Key facts

- SiteGPT: $13K MRR and about $500K total revenue since its March 2023 launch (2026-01). [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s)
- His earlier SaaS, Feather, reached $5K to $6K MRR. He sold it for $250,000 to focus on SiteGPT. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s)
- SiteGPT went from idea to launch in two weeks and hit about $10K MRR in its first month (2023). [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s)
- Over 1 million site visitors since launch. 60% to 70% of traffic is from Google, and about 90% of Google traffic lands on free tools (2026-01). [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=107s)
- Funnel per month: about 50,000 visitors, 200 leads, 60 trials, and 25% to 40% of trials convert (2026-01). [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s)
- About 130 business customers at roughly $100 average revenue each. LTV is $1,700 to $1,800 (2026-01). [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=204s)
- Pricing is a monthly fee based on chatbots, content size and expected messages. [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s)
- Stack: Ahrefs, SiteGPT for support, Cal.com, PostHog, Bento for email, Feather for blog posts, Featurebase, Mintlify, Claude Code and ChartMogul. [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=716s)

## Tactics

- **Build for an existing audience first.** He made the chatbot for his 100 Feather customers, then split it into its own product. [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=229s)
- **Make free tools that relate to the main product.** Examples: PDF to Markdown, a chatbot name generator. Users of each tool are possible SiteGPT customers. [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s)
- **Clone tools with AI.** He tells Cursor to copy an existing tool's pattern for a new keyword. A new tool takes under 5 minutes. [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s)
- **Seven-step keyword workflow:**
  1. Open Ahrefs Keywords Explorer and search with an empty query to get all keywords. [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=465s)
  2. Filter by include terms, for example "AI" plus "generator". [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=525s)
  3. Keep keyword difficulty under 10. "AI image generator" is KD 87. [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=525s)
  4. Keep monthly volume at 1,000 or more. [9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=587s)
  5. List the keywords with volume and KD in Notion. [9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=587s)
  6. Write a CTA under each tool that leads to the main product. [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=648s)
  7. Score each tool on volume, KD, build effort and relevance, then build the best first. [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=648s)
- **Launch the core feature fast.** Let user feedback set the direction. [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=755s)
