# I speedran an ai app from 0 to $1m/yr in 10 months

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Koby Conrad, founder of Sunflower. Sunflower helps people quit addictions to alcohol, cannabis, hard drugs or gambling. It went from about $0 to $1M annualized run rate in about 10 months and passed 10K paying subscribers. Koby's main growth move was to hire the top sobriety influencer, Eric ("Mr. Impulsive"), as VP of Sobriety with salary and equity, instead of paying per post. The app combines a sobriety timer, an AI sponsor named Sam, a community and content. Paywall changes, above all trial reminders, raised revenue sharply.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $1M annualized run rate in about 10 months, with over 10K paying subscribers (2026-03). [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=170s)
- Eric gets 50M to 100M views a month and drives 50K to 100K link clicks a month to Sunflower (2026-03). He has about 3.5M followers. [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=212s), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=279s), [54:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3243s)
- Koby has spent $10M to $20M on influencer marketing over 10 years. [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=279s)
- The AI sponsor Sam handled over 4M messages in the last year. Users track over 109 years of sobriety every day (2026-03). [52:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3131s), [53:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3181s)
- Funding: about $6.5M total from YC, a16z Speedrun, Flybridge (seed lead) and angels. Koby first put in $100K of his own money and raised about $350K. [38:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2309s), [40:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2406s)

## Tactics

- **Hire the top influencer as an employee.** Offer salary, equity and a mission instead of a per-post fee. It is buying in bulk. It is also defensible, because rival apps cannot easily take him. He learns the roadmap and gets better at driving clicks. Function Health did the same with Mark Hyman. [5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=353s), [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=453s)
- **Bootstrapped version: partner 50/50.** Make the creator the hero. You build an app for their audience, and they promote it. [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=654s)
- **Put the influencer in Slack.** Eric sees "this saved my life" feedback, which keeps him motivated. [17:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1021s)
- **Be the biggest sponsor.** At conferences, ask what $200K gets you instead of buying a small booth. Partners then care about you. [31:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1877s)
- **Skip pure brand marketing.** Every impression is brand. Spend on what is directly profitable or builds an audience. [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2163s), [37:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2223s)
- **Make the simple core feature great.** The most important feature is the sobriety timer, not the AI. [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1055s)
- **The AI companion is not the product alone.** On its own, engagement and retention are bad. Sam grew from a ChatGPT wrapper into an agent with memory, follow-ups, safety escalation to clinicians and biometrics. [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=933s), [23:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1389s)
- **Build where model labs lack outcome data.** Sunflower tracks relapse and sobriety, so it can optimize for outcomes. [26:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1567s)
- **Paywall sequence.** Monthly only, then add annual, then make annual the default, then a trial toggle, then weekly, monthly and annual options. Users are price-sensitive, so lowering the price made more money. Trial reminders cut trial anxiety. [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s), [44:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2675s), [45:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2735s)
- **Pitch the future.** The deck that raised money showed revenue growth, proof of outcomes and pilot LOIs, not usage. [41:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2499s), [42:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2559s)
- **Avoid account farms.** Koby sees 100-account TikTok farms as arbitrage. A $100M business needs real audiences, ads, community or retail. [29:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1774s)
- **Plan for life after subscriptions.** Koby predicts cheap software turns apps into lead magnets for services, physical goods and ads. [49:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2962s), [51:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3087s)
