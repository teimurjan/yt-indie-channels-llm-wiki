## Summary

Brett runs DesignJoy, a one-person design studio that sells unlimited design requests as a monthly subscription. He makes about $1.3M/year (2023-05) with no employees or contractors. He built the first version in one day on Webflow, launched it on Product Hunt, and ran it beside a full-time job for 3.5 years. He quit only at $80K/month, and revenue doubled the month after. Today most leads come from his tweets.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $1.3M/year (2023-05). Plans cost about $5,000/month. [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=54s)
- About 20 clients at once, served by Brett alone. Zero employees, zero contractors (2023-05). [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=85s)
- He kept his day job for 3.5 years and quit at $80K/month. Revenue doubled the month after he went full-time. [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=179s), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=611s)
- Build time: 6 to 7 hours on one Saturday. A one-page Webflow site plus a Trello board structure. [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=126s)
- Running costs: $176/month (2023-05). Figma, Webflow hosting, Adobe, and Shutterstock ($100/month, the biggest cost). Trello and Airtable on free plans. [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=292s)
- Marketing spend: zero. [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)
- Work hours: about 11:30 to 17:00, after mornings with his kids. [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=476s)

## Tactics

- **Productize one skill.** Sell fixed packages with unlimited requests instead of hourly rates or quoted projects. No proposals. The effective hourly rate varies by client, from $5,000/hour for one request to $300/hour for many. [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s)
- **Async delivery.** Each client gets a Trello board on signup and adds requests to a queue. Brett works one request per client every couple of days, 30 to 60 minutes each. No calls and no Slack after onboarding. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=85s)
- **Copy and improve an existing model.** He saw an agency that sold graphic design this way. He applied the model to web, landing page and product design, at a higher quality. [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=126s)
- **Niche to what you are fast at.** Bundle only the work you do fastest (for him: branding, product design, landing pages). Price it so it is attractive and worth your time. Then put up a website. [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=250s)
- **Launch, then build in public.** Product Hunt brought the first clients. Then he joined communities and built in public. Twitter became the main lead source about a year before the video. [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)
- **Ship the minimum.** He launched DesignJoy in a day and a course as audio files recorded in his car. He tests whether an idea "bites" before he invests more. [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=359s)
- **Learn design by doing.** He is self-taught. He copied good design, browsed Dribbble and landing page galleries, and did fake rebrands and redesigns. [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=435s)
