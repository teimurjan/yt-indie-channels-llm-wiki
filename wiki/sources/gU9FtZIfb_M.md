## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Ian Myers, founder and CEO of Oceans. Oceans places full-time offshore staff, mostly in Sri Lanka, inside client teams as executive assistants and operators. Ian left finance and a venture-backed app company because he wanted a business that served his life, not a board. Oceans grew from $650K in its first year to about $15M in revenue, mostly through referrals and with no paid marketing. He explains the model, how he sold to friends first, how he hires, and why he keeps his tool stack small.

## Key facts

- First contract in February 2022, after the idea came in late 2021. Year 1 revenue: $650K (2022). Last year: about $15M revenue (2025-03). The title says $10M/year. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)
- Oceans made millions of dollars in profit and raised no money. Ian owned 100% at the start. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)
- Price: about $3,000 to $4,000/month per full-time headcount (2025-03). An EA costs less, a financial controller costs more. Oceans pays wages and benefits and keeps the margin. [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=155s)
- Staff work 40 hours a week, long term, and join the client's Slack, calendar and all-hands. Most live in Sri Lanka, some in South Africa. [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)
- Oceans hires 30 to 40 people a month (2025-03). [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)
- Google has about 180,000 staff, with 120,000 offshore. Covid made remote work normal for small firms, so offshoring opened up to doctors, accountants, law firms and agencies. [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=306s)
- Tools: Google Workspace and Google Sheets for most work, Stripe for payments, Notion for reference docs, tldv for meeting recordings, HubSpot as CRM. [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=761s)

## Tactics

- **Improve a market that already exists.** People already wanted offshoring. Ian set out to build a better product in it. [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=366s)
- **Niche down in a commodity space.** Ian gets 10 sales emails a day from other offshoring firms. He suggests a vertical such as video editing, illustration or CAD for architects. He says a niche firm can reach $1M to $2M revenue. [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=366s), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=428s)
- **Test with three hires and three friends.** He paid a 10-year EA to build a training course. Then he trained a first cohort of three people in Sri Lanka. He sold to friends at a discount and made no money on the first three customers. [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)
- **Sell first to people you know.** His test: can he call someone tomorrow and get paid within days? He does not start with Facebook ads to strangers. [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=565s)
- **Build for referrals.** Great service comes first. Then make customers look like heroes to their friends. Business owners talk about hiring all the time, so the category has built-in word of mouth. [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=618s), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=678s)
- **Over-deliver for the first 20 to 50 clients.** Fast email replies, comped months after mistakes, and the founder on the phone. [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=678s), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=739s)
- **Write processes down before you grow past 5 to 8 people.** The jump from a small team to an organization broke everything. Getting knowledge out of people's heads was the hardest part. [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)
- **Hire from unusual backgrounds.** His first US hire ran children's summer camps. She took sales calls and is now chief of staff. Oceans hires smart operators, not former EAs, and trains them. [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=871s)
