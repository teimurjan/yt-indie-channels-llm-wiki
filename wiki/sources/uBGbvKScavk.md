## Summary

Kevin Espiritu founded Epic Gardening, which he calls the world's most followed gardening brand. He started gardening with his brother around 2013 as a hobby and registered a blog for it. He wrote blog articles 12 hours a day until the blog paid an accountant-level salary. He then saw that his audience kept asking about a metal raised garden bed, so he sold it directly instead of taking brand deals. Product revenue turned a media business into a brand that makes tens of millions a year (2024-03).

## Key facts

- Bootstrapped to $7.3M/year, now "tens of millions a year" in revenue (2024-03). The video description says over $30M/year. [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=1s)
- Headquarters is his homestead in suburban San Diego: about a third of an acre, a 1,000 ft² house, nine hens, 25 fruit trees and hundreds of plants. The team films content, tests products and grows seed varieties there. [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=61s)
- Blog growth: about $400/month at first, $2K to $3K/month after 2 to 3 months of full-time writing, and $4K to $5K/month later in 2016. [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s)
- Revenue in the first 6 months full-time: about $70,400 by the auto caption. He says it left him with almost no money.
- Revenue by year: $75K in 2017, $225K in 2018, $540K in 2019, $2.8M in 2020, $7.3M in 2021. That is roughly 3x or more each year. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s)
- 2019 split: about $250K media revenue and $250K product revenue. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)
- First product drop: a metal corrugated raised bed, sold through a basic Shopify store and one Instagram story. It sold out in 2 weeks. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)

## Tactics

- **Write a lot, early.** He spent about 12 hours a day writing researched gardening articles. He also commented in forums and dropped links to get traffic. [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s)
- **Be your own sponsor.** Creators earn from brand deals. Kevin decided to be the brand that sponsors his own content. [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s)
- **Let the audience pick the product.** Viewers kept asking about the raised bed in his garden. He sold that product instead of inventing a new one. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)
- **Distribute before you develop.** He had no product development background. He imported a product that already existed but was not sold in the US. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)
- **The business sits under the content.** Free education built the audience. The product line became the real business. [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)
- **Block time for the craft.** He schedules gardening time with no agenda and no phone, so the job does not consume the hobby. [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=461s)
- **Act right after you learn.** Do not wait for a full business plan, business cards and a website. Consume a little, then put it into action. [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=534s)
