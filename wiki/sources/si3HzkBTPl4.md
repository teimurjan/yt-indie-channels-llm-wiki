## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Peter, a co-founder of Stronger, a gamified workout tracker from Northern Ireland. Three engineers bootstrapped it, and it makes about $600K ARR from about 1.2M users. Almost all growth is organic TikTok and Instagram content that the founders make in-house. One 6-second "fade-in" Reels format alone brought an estimated 150M to 300M views. The biggest monetization win was a switch to a hard paywall, which a bug revealed by accident. Peter also covers brand ambassadors, why in-house beats agencies, and which moats survive vibe coding.

## Key facts

- Stronger: about 1.2M users and about $600K ARR (2025-08). The US is the biggest market. The UK has the highest penetration. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s)
- Age: 2.5 to 3 years. Bootstrapped with government grants from Northern Ireland. It began as a hardware idea. Team: 3 people. [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=86s), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=311s), [17:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1023s)
- The fade-in format: about 6 seconds, a fade from black to one image. Average watch time was over double the video length. Estimated 150M to 300M views in total, with some videos at 40M. One month reached 100M views (2025-08). [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=412s), [8:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=502s)
- First viral TikTok: a Canva-made video, posted from a break at work, gained about 15K followers in the first week before the app was ready. The TikTok page has about 4.9M likes (2025-08). [31:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1867s), [32:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1927s), [35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2142s)
- Peak chart rank: number 3 in Health and Fitness. [33:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2018s)
- A hard paywall (trial required to reach the free tier) gave about a 25% boost overnight. [24:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1452s), [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1498s)
- Second app: a gamified focus app described as "Forest meets FarmVille", an isometric tile game. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s), [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2902s)

## Tactics

- **Make the product the marketing asset.** The strength score heat map compares your muscles with people of the same body weight. They design features to look good as a screenshot, for example lifetime-volume badges such as "you lifted a Boeing 747". [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=269s), [26:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1606s)
- **Scale a winner horizontally.** A competitor posted their format from about 10 accounts and grew faster. Now they make micro edits with AI and Figma (hook words, light background, legend) so each copy is a new video. [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=530s), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=644s)
- **Educate or entertain.** Entertainment ("gym bro humor") needs platform flair. Education is a pure A/B test on hook, saves and shares, and it fits older, higher-value users. [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=760s), [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=953s)
- **Train the feed like your customer.** He swipes past content that a 20-year-old gym bro will not like. [14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=869s)
- **Stitch replies.** After the first viral video, the co-founder stitched comments such as "I benched 220 lb" and showed the object equivalent on his phone. Each reply became new content. [34:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2083s), [35:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2153s)
- **Look a little rough.** Visible share and download buttons make the post feel like real app content, not an ad. [39:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2353s)
- **Borrow formats across niches.** Study why a format worked in another vertical, then reskin it. Treat each video as a funnel: hook, body, download. [39:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2398s), [41:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2506s)
- **Test macro paywall changes first.** How often the paywall shows and how hard it is matter more than copy. Micro tests need months of volume to be significant. [22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1357s), [23:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1418s)
- **Accept the K-factor trade-off.** A paid app gets fewer friend shares, but the hard paywall still won. [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1498s), [25:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1547s)
- **Gamify lightly.** Heavy ranks appeal to ages 15 to 23. The money is in 30 to 40 year olds. [28:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1709s), [29:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1771s)
- **Few, strong ambassadors.** Runna, Represent and Ladder sell a lifestyle through 3 or 4 niche ambassadors. Quality beats spray and pray. [17:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1023s), [19:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1196s)
- **Keep growth in-house.** Agencies add about a 20% margin and weaker incentives. Use them only to scale a format you already found. [43:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2614s), [44:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2691s)
- **Moats after vibe coding.** Hidden internal content tooling, technical difficulty (game engines) and network effects. Copycats can also grow the category leader, as with Cal AI in App Store search. [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2902s), [50:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=3026s), [51:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=3108s)
