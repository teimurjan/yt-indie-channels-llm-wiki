# The Multipreneur: He's Building A $10M Portfolio of Income Streams

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Alex Lieberman, co-founder of Morning Brew, about his plan to build a personal holding company. Alex wants a dozen or more small bootstrapped businesses in 5 to 10 years, where he is co-founder and chairman but never CEO. The first one is Storyarb, a ghostwriting agency for B2B executives on Twitter and LinkedIn, which he validated with a single tweet. He explains how he measures product-market fit and how he hires and pays CEOs. He also tests ideas step by step instead of taking a leap of faith.

## Key facts

- Morning Brew is a 200-person company with more than $75M/year in revenue (2023-11). It sold to Axel Springer in October 2020. Alex stepped down as CEO about 6 months later. [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=86s), [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=263s)
- Morning Brew had 30,000 subscribers and $0 in revenue at the time Alex quit his Wall Street job. [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=201s)
- Storyarb has 12 clients at $7,000/month: $84K/month top line, about $1M annualized (2023-11). [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)
- Team: 2 full-time staff (a CEO and a content strategist) plus 3 freelance ghostwriters (2023-11). [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)
- Goal: $10M/year with about 110 clients. Margin is 30 to 35%, so $3M to $3.5M/year in cash flow. [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s)
- Delivery: one client interview per month, then 12 pieces of content per week per client. [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s)
- Target owner time per business after product-market fit: 5 to 10 hours/week. [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)
- CEO pay: $100K to $200K salary plus 10% to 50% equity. After product-market fit, all profits go to equity holders every quarter. [12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=738s)

## Tactics

- **Validate with a tweet before building.** Alex tweeted at executives willing to pay $5K to $10K/year for audience building and got 25 DMs. He then matched clients with 2 ghostwriters in a Slack channel. [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)
- **Hire the CEO early.** After 2 to 3 months of proof, he advertised for a CEO. He did not want to run the business himself. [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)
- **Two product-market fit metrics.** At least 50% of clients must be willing to recommend Storyarb with their reputation on the line. The target for average retention is 10 months, against the 3 to 5 months typical for ghostwriting agencies. [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=609s)
- **Hire for "unteachables".** An obsessive brain, critical thinking, aligned values (integrity, communication) and work ethic. He prefers humility and self-awareness over raw talent. [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=676s)
- **Be a magnet for problems.** Do not brainstorm on a couch. Collect people's problems and look for new solutions. His free idea: a workflow automation agency that uses Notion, Airtable, Zapier, Make, AI and offshore talent. [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=775s)
- **Go slow to go fast.** Business one must have product-market fit before business two starts. Four successful businesses beat 12 weak ones. [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=836s)
- **The holdco owner is a product.** An owner who adds too little value cannot claim enough equity and cannot attract talent. [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=896s)
- **Build around your edge.** Example: Greg Isenberg is known for community, so he launched a paid community for community builders. [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=958s)
- **Step pebble to pebble.** Test in order: tweet, thread, newsletter, video. Interest at each step unlocks the next step. [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=1000s), [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=1061s)
