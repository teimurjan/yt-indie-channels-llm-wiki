# The First App I Ever Built Makes $25K/Month

## Summary

Kyan is a 21-year-old computer science student at San Diego State University. His first app, ToneAdapt, tells guitarists how to set up their own guitar, amp and pedals to sound like a given song. He vibe coded the web app in about a week over Thanksgiving break and later shipped a native iOS app. In under 5 months it reached over 100K users and about $25K in 4 weeks of revenue. Growth came from face-on short-form videos, posted 3 times a day across platforms. He had learned that skill doing UGC for brands.

## Key facts

- Revenue: over $25K in the past 4 weeks: about $11.5K on the web (Stripe) and about $14K on mobile (2026-06). [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=108s), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=146s)
- Over 100K users in under 5 months (2026-06). [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=69s)
- Mobile has 397 active subscribers in its first month. The web made $45K and got 1,400 new subscribers in 3 months (2026-06). [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=146s)
- Pricing: $10/week or about $60/year. [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=108s)
- The database has over 1,500 guitars and 2,000 amps. A result takes under 30 seconds. [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=747s)
- V1 took about 1 week. Every line was vibe coded. He says it takes a couple of hours today. [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=328s)
- Web stack: Supabase, Vercel (hosting and analytics), Mailgun, Stripe, the OpenAI API, the Tavily web search API and Claude Code. [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=328s)
- iOS stack: native Swift, Vercel API routes, Supabase, RevenueCat for subscriptions and Superwall for paywalls and A/B tests. [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=375s)
- Pat says ToneAdapt ranks #2 after sponsored results for "guitar tone" in the App Store (2026-06). [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=698s)

## Tactics

- **Solve your own tiny problem.** Kyan used ChatGPT for guitar tones and found it inaccurate. Guitarists on Reddit and in Instagram comments had the same problem. [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=226s), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=571s)
- **Pick a hobby you already practice.** You are your own customer, so you need no market research. [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=516s)
- **Ship V1 on the web first.** Do not polish or pick a logo. Charge from day one. Build mobile later from the feedback. [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=611s)
- **Post 3 times a day for at least a month.** Repost on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. Show the product solving a problem in real time. [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=416s), [10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=632s)
- **Put your face on the brand.** Users feel they join the journey. The first video is the only embarrassing one. [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=416s), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=472s)
- **Scale the winner.** Remake a converting video 50 times. Pay UGC creators to copy it 60 times a month. Put Meta and TikTok ad spend behind it. After it dies, find the next winner. [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=670s)
- **Content is the moat.** Pat argues that AI coding floods every niche with apps, so content decides who wins. [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=1012s)

## Quotes
