# The Lazy Method: How I Build $10K/Month Apps

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Will Baker, a UK designer who runs two iOS apps that make over $10K/month together. Glowly is a looksmaxing face-scan app. Overlow is a fitness app with a body scan. Will's "lazy" method skips UGC armies and influencers. He clones proven app categories with better design and grows them with a few AI-made TikTok ads. He builds apps to sell to operators who can scale them. Pat and producer Gus note that he skips idea validation by picking categories that already work.

## Key facts

- Glowly makes about $10K/month and Overlow about $800/month passive (2026-08). [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s)
- Will tried dropshipping and selling likes at 16, then NFT projects after university. Freelance web design funded his startup tests. [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=126s), [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=131s)
- A TikTok subscription campaign costs about $16 per subscription. LTV per paying user is about $30 to $35 (2026-08). [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s)
- One batch of winning ads can carry an app for 4 to 5 months. [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s)
- Onboarding has 12 to 15 steps before the paywall. [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=405s)
- Stack: RevenueCat for subscriptions, Claude CLI to build, and AppsFlyer as the mobile measurement partner. [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=499s)

## Tactics

- **Pick paid ads over UGC.** UGC and influencers meant managing people and payments, with unclear attribution. Paid ads give clear numbers for little ongoing work. [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s)
- **Build to sell.** Validate, grow to sellable revenue, then sell to a buyer who can scale with UGC. Repeat. [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=220s)
- **Mine your feed for ideas.** Instagram and TikTok show what people want, for both the app and the content. Designers improve an app that already works. They do not invent from scratch. [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=341s)
- **One-screenshot viral feature.** Will builds only apps with a viral feature that explains itself in one screenshot and sells a transformation. [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=380s)
- **Personalize before the paywall.** A 12 to 15 step onboarding tells the story and ends in a personal insight. Users feel the app already works for them, so conversion rises. [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=405s)
- **AI makes creative cheap.** Make 10 to 20 variants of one format with AI and test them. Treat it like a lottery and repeat. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s)
- **Native TikTok ads.** Copy native formats such as memes and talking to camera. Do not show the app in the first 3 to 4 seconds. Weave the core viral feature into the story. [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s)
- **Scale slowly.** Raise spend on a winner by 20% every 3 days until it caps out. [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s)
- **Build a personal brand.** Post wins and lessons on Twitter. Will sees a personal brand as a moat that AI cannot copy. [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=520s)
- **Ads do not validate ideas.** Pat says ads test creative, not demand. Will chose proven categories such as fitness and looksmaxing, so demand was already known. [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=669s), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=714s)

## Quotes

- "Lazy maxing to me is just finding the easiest possible way to get what I want done." [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=178s)
- "I will not work on an app unless it has a clear viral feature that explains itself in essentially one screenshot." [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=380s)
- "I just scale the spend by 20% every 3 days until eventually it caps out." [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s)
