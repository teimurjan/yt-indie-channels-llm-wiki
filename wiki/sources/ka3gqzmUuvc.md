## Summary

Matt Espinoza runs Clover Labs, an organic distribution company in Canada that reached $8M a year within six months. It drives about 250M views a month for clients such as OpusClip, Superwall and Typing.com. His main claim: about 90% of a winning campaign is cloned content and 10% is new experiments. Clover's tool Echos clones competitor slideshows and videos, splits videos into hook and demo, and posts variants across many accounts in 20 countries. He also explains how Clover ranks apps in AI search through Reddit and blog citations. Joseph Choi hosts.

## Key facts

- Clover Labs: $0 to $8M a year in six months, about 250M views a month (2026-09). The company is a year and three weeks old. [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s), [39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s)
- A clone gets a median of about 20% of the original's views. Five to ten clones match the original. Some do 2x to 6x better. [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s)
- A cloned trucker slideshow (original: 110K views) got 34K views in about 10 days on one account. [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=211s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=268s)
- The Natural camera app campaign reached about 100K views and about 200 signups in 5 days (2026-09). It cloned posts from competitor Dazz Cam. [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=405s)
- Clover scrapes a database of about 4M TikTok and Instagram accounts. A clone takes about 1 to 1.5 minutes per image, and a full set takes 6 to 7 minutes. [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=545s), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=606s)
- Each new country adds about a 20% view lift. Example: 1M views/month plus 200K. A Rhyme video with 37.4M views was redone in German, which was Rhyme's best market outside North America. [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=815s)
- AI search: about 150M views across clients from Reddit and blog content (2026-09). Clover controls about 4,000 subreddits. [29:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1746s), [37:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2227s)
- Creator pay: base plus performance is best. Some campaigns pay $3 per post plus a $3.50 CPM. [28:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1687s)

## Tactics

- **Clone with small changes.** Change the hook, CTA, avatar, color or background, so the platform does not flag the post as a duplicate. Choose a subtle, moderate or dramatic variance. For slideshows, the account's look matters most. [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=346s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=458s)
- **Clone competitor formats.** Find a competitor's top posts, swap in your own product or logo, and make several variants. [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=545s)
- **Hook and stitch.** AI splits a video at the cut between the 2-second hook and the demo. Keep a proven hook and attach your own demo. [16:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1009s), [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1069s)
- **Evergreen campaigns.** Track your UGC creators. A post that passes a view threshold, for example 1M, gets new hooks and demos and goes to other accounts. Take the top 10 of 60 posts, make 3 variants of each, and you have 30 new posts. [18:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1119s), [20:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1221s)
- **Expand by country.** Clover creates accounts in bulk on physical phones in North America and rents phones elsewhere. Best markets: Spain, Germany and Australia, then Italy and Brazil. Adding Canada to US content is an easy 20% lift. [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=687s), [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=933s), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=958s)
- **Dub and lip-sync.** AI translates the speech, matches the dialect, swaps the text overlay and syncs mouth movement. He rates the output 98% of the way there. [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=815s), [24:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1492s)
- **Pay creators by stage.** Pay a high CPM while you search for formats. Pay mostly a base fee for proven clone content. [28:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1687s)
- **Rank in AI search.** Test about 2,000 prompts. Take Google volume from Ahrefs or Search Console and assume about 30% of it goes to LLMs. Then find which URLs ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews cite. [29:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1746s), [31:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1868s)
- **Post seeding and comment seeding.** Write a post that outranks the cited thread, or comment in the cited thread with your own top list. LLMs now often cite one comment, not the post. Reddit, Quora and Pinterest citations rose in the last 3 weeks. [32:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1930s), [33:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1998s), [35:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2121s)
- **Four distribution channels.** Search, short video, agentic paid ads that use organic learnings, and outreach for B2B. [39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s)
