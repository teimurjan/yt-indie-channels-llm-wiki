## Summary

Umberto Mezzadra built Floga, a mobile app for yoga teachers and practitioners. He pre-launched it on 2025-05-05 with a lifetime deal (LTD) and made over $120K in 24 hours from 500 to 600 early buyers. The app was not finished. He sold it with a five-week email warm-up, a feature video and three price tiers. He then moved to monthly, quarterly and yearly subscriptions. His launch skills came from a yoga card deck brand that raised over $200K on Kickstarter.

## Key facts

- Launch: 2025-05-05 at 2:00 p.m. $117K by the end of that day and over $120K in 24 hours (2025-05) [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=83s), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=116s).
- About 500 to 600 lifetime buyers (2025-05) [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s).
- Now: about 4,000 active users (paid and free) and $9K to $10K/month (2026-03) [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=116s).
- Plans: monthly, quarterly (web only) and yearly [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=116s).
- LTD tiers: about $109 (limited features), about $199 (more features), $349 (the full roadmap) [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=838s).
- Pre-launch email sequence ran about 5 weeks [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=516s).
- Background: economics degree, a failed seed-funded startup in 2012, then ski instructor and fashion photographer. From 2016 he worked as an ad and growth strategist. In 2020 he co-founded Play Pause Be, whose first Kickstarter raised over $200K in its first month [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=166s).
- He is not a developer. He hired one a few months after he sketched the app [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=223s).
- Stack: Flutter, Firebase (about $25/month), RevenueCat, Vimeo for video, OneSignal for push [18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1134s).

## Tactics

- **Monetize early.** Define the minimum feature set that makes people buy the LTD. Build the product and the revenue engine at the same time [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=298s).
- **Demo honestly, no refunds.** A YouTube video showed every current feature and the roadmap. Buyers who wanted a trial had to wait for subscriptions [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s), [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=838s).
- **Curiosity emails.** Email 1 told a story and revealed nothing. Email 2 showed the physical product next to a hidden new item. A later email revealed the app "behind the red curtain" and linked the demo video. He also reused the emails as blog posts [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=516s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=578s).
- **Hide the price until launch day.** People then judge the features and vision, not the price [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=578s).
- **Scarcity.** Limit the number of spots and keep the window to 5 to 7 days [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=899s).
- **Decoy tiers.** The two lower tiers anchor the top tier and catch skeptical buyers. Do not price low out of fear [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=777s), [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=838s).
- **Launch playbook.** Validate with 5 to 10 target users first, with The Mom Test method. Define the "minimum launchable product". Prepare a buffer of emails, graphics, videos and landing pages before any lead generation [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=716s), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=777s).
- **Use LTD buyers as a feedback group.** Buyers who paid up front report bugs and send detailed feedback. Floga put them in a Telegram group, and many later features came from them [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s).
- **Why an LTD beats a trial early on.** Early on you do not know LTV or churn [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s).
- **LTD buyers pay more.** People who hate subscriptions pay 3 to 5 times a yearly plan for lifetime access. It is capital from customers, with no equity given away [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s).
- **Price the LTD on LTV.** Pat Walls prices it near 24 months of subscription revenue [17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1046s).
