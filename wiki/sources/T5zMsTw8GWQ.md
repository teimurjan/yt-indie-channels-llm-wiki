## Summary

John McEvoy, a self-taught graphic designer from Scotland, built Momego, a live bus and train tracking app for over 160 cities. He runs it alone. It has about 5.2M downloads and makes about $30K MRR (2025-10). Free, hyper-local App Store Optimization (ASO) drives almost all growth. Banner ads paid about $8K/month until the pandemic killed ad revenue. He then moved to subscriptions, and paywall A/B tests raised conversion from 0.5% to 8%.

## Key facts

- About $30K MRR, almost all from subscriptions (2025-10). [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=77s)
- About 5.2M downloads since 2017, about 400K monthly active users, about 75K ratings (2025-10). [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=52s), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=77s)
- Build history: designs in Illustrator, first versions in Xamarin (C#/.NET), then a rebuild in native Swift and Flutter in 2 to 3 months. [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=172s), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=235s)
- Revenue path: banner ads at about $8K/month, lost in the pandemic. A subscription update shipped in August 2020. In 2021 he ran about 10 paywall experiments over 2 to 3 months. Conversion went from 0.5% to 8%. Revenue rose from about $8K to over $30K/month. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=573s)
- Paywall: yearly plan with a 7-day free trial, a weekly plan, and a lifetime plan. [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=592s)
- Stack: Laravel PHP backend, Adobe Creative Suite, and Lottie animations from After Effects. Appfigures for ASO research, RevenueCat for subscriptions, Mixpanel for event analytics. Cloudflare costs $90/month and ChatGPT $20/month. [11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=711s)
- Biggest costs: about 20 dedicated servers at about $2,500/month, and third-party APIs such as maps at about $1,000/month (2025-10). [12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=763s)

## Tactics

- **Solve your own problem.** He worked for a bus company in Edinburgh and wanted to see his bus on a map, like an Uber. [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=133s)
- **ASO step 1: local keywords.** Put city-specific terms in the title and metadata. Use each city's own words, for example "MTA subway" in New York and "CTA L train" in Chicago. Make location-specific screenshots for each localization. [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=292s)
- **Use extra localizations.** Keywords in the Mexican Spanish localization also index in the US App Store, with the same weight as US English. [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=292s), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)
- **ASO step 2: find more keywords.** Test a few keywords at a time with Apple Search Ads, or read the App Store autocomplete list. Specific long-tail phrases have low competition, so a top-5 rank is easy. [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)
- **ASO step 3: ask for ratings at a "golden moment".** For Momego, the moment is the first view of a live bus that moves on the map. A steady flow of ratings pushes the app to number one. [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=414s)
- **Short onboarding for impatient users.** People install at a bus stop. So the app asks for location first, shows a few value screens with social proof, then the paywall. [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=592s)
- **Reverse trial on close.** A user who closes the paywall gets 7 days of Pro free, with no payment details. John calls this "a real unlock". [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=646s)
- **Event analytics over vanity metrics.** Monthly active users did not move the needle. Event-based analytics and paywall A/B tests did. [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=789s)
