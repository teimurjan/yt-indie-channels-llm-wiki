# I built a $14K/month SaaS using Discord

## Summary

Sam Adeniyi is a university student with no coding background. He vibe coded Algrow, a SaaS that helps creators find and copy viral short-form formats and then generate videos with AI. He validated and launched it inside Discord servers: he sat in voice chats sharing his screen while he used the tool, and people asked what it was. He got his first 400 users on Discord and reached about $14K in 30 days with over 10,000 users about six months after launch. He gives a five-step playbook for getting customers from niche communities.

## Key facts

- Pricing: $25, $45 and $80 per month plans (2026-01). [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=101s)
- Last four weeks: over £10K, about $13K to $14K, and 480 new customers (2026-01). [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=157s)
- Over 10,000 users. The first 400 came from Discord (2026-01). [0:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=18s), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=69s)
- Built first for faceless YouTubers. Influencers and drop shippers now use it too. [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=157s)
- Background: about $10K from affiliate marketing with faceless texting-story videos, then YouTube automation. His friend Mason gave him the idea. [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=195s)
- He built the first MVP in about a week by pasting ChatGPT code into Notepad, then VS Code, then Cursor. The first user got an application error on Heroku but still used the core feature. [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=243s), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=292s)
- Monthly costs (2026-01): Cursor $200, Nano Banana image generation about $100, Sora 2 video about $200, Heroku hosting about $100, MailerLite about $80, Gemini compute $300 to $500. [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=728s)

## Tactics

- **Find demand with Disboard.** Search disboard.org by keyword to find niche servers. In the "moneymind" server, people kept asking how to find a niche. That was the demand signal. [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=331s)
- **Silent screen share.** He joined voice chats muted and used his tool on screen for about a week. People asked about it. The server owner made a YouTube video about the tool without being asked or paid. He then posted a waitlist link. [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=331s), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=376s)
- **Help, do not pitch.** Most servers ban self-promotion. He built rapport by using the tool to find channels for people who asked. Word of mouth followed. [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=504s)
- **Five-step playbook.** (1) Find where your ideal customer lives, for example Blender and Cinema 4D servers for a 3D-asset tool. (2) Listen first: paste days of chat history into ChatGPT and ask for recurring pain points. (3) DM people a short sped-up Loom of the tool and get 5 or 6 strong reactions across servers. (4) Build with users through a waitlist or your own Discord server. (5) Give early users free access so they demo it to friends who pay. [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=558s), [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=612s), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=673s)
- **Discord over email for young users.** Sam thinks Discord is 10x better than email for building a relationship with users. [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=673s)
- **Prompt for scale.** Tell Cursor to plan for 100,000 users so the architecture holds later. [12:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=778s)
