## Summary

Mauro Botanes, 22, built Symmetry, a gym tracking app for the Spanish-speaking market in Spain and Mexico. It reached about $160K/month within a year of launch. Growth comes from a large UGC operation that pays creators about half of revenue. The video focuses on how he decides what to build: quarterly OKRs, event analytics, user interviews and an A/B test for every change. Pat Walls and his new co-host Gus close with the point that most tests do not move the needle.

## Key facts

- Revenue: about $160K/month in RevenueCat over the last 30 days (2026-09). [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=106s), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=126s)
- About 3M downloads across iOS and Android (2026-09). [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=106s)
- Launched June 6 of the prior year. The company started 2 years before the video. The founders were fitness YouTubers before. [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=185s), [14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=878s)
- Over the summer after launch they had only about 30K downloads. The spike came in January. [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=185s)
- UGC in the last year: nearly 80,000 videos, more than 500 accounts, nearly 8B views (2026-09). One video reached 17M views. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s)
- Creators get nearly half of revenue. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=281s)
- One commitment screen at the end of onboarding raised retention by 3%. Two redesigns of the home workout screen moved nothing. [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)
- Stack: Claude with MCP connections to PostHog, Superwall and RevenueCat, Wispr Flow for dictation, Superwall for paywall tests, PostHog for other A/B tests, Notion and Slack. [13:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=784s)

## Tactics

- **Find the channel that works.** The YouTube community and influencer marketing both failed. UGC worked, so they doubled down on it. [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s)
- **Track formats and sub-formats.** A top format shows an iPhone lock screen: a breakup text from a girlfriend, then a Symmetry notification that says "go to the gym". [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s)
- **Build in three phases.** What we wanted (terrible), what users asked for (mediocre), then a scientific method. [5:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=332s)
- **Test high-traffic screens.** A theme toggle touches about 2% of daily users. A paywall test touches most users. In Symmetry, the exercise search screen drives activation and retention. [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=361s)
- **Set ambitious quarterly OKRs.** For a new app, ambitious targets are easy to hit. They run a 90-day "warm-up" and review metrics weekly or daily. [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=453s)
- **Pick the metric that leads revenue.** For a gym app, activation means a logged workout, and retention follows it. For a calorie app, one photo is activation. [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=453s), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=514s)
- **Track downloads, retention and ARPU.** ARPU by country sets how much you can pay creators per download. [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=539s)
- **Instrument every onboarding screen.** Funnels show where users drop off. Interviews, Discord and Reddit show why. [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=593s), [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=604s)
- **Experiment loop ("Evelyn").** Start from a problem in user interviews. Write hypotheses and guardrail metrics. Run control against variants for at least 2 weeks. Drop areas where tests are inconclusive. [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=663s), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)
- **Social features for retention.** Ranks per muscle group and exercise, a Strava-like feed, streaks shared with friends, and levels. [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=821s)
