# My App Makes $41K/Month

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Joe Rozek, a former Shutterfly engineering manager. Joe built Waitly, a waitlist and reservations app for restaurants and other businesses with waiting customers. The idea came at brunch in 2017. A Yelp waitlist link forced him to download an app and sign up. Waitly grew slowly for years and now makes over $40K/month as a solo business. It competes with Yelp and OpenTable on price, simplicity and fast support. Apple Search Ads is the main paid channel.

## Key facts

- Waitly makes over $40K/month (2025-09). [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=59s)
- Yearly revenue: year 1 $0, year 2 $14,500, year 3 about $116K, year 4 $186K, year 5 $307K, year 6 $354K, year 7 $445K. The current year is on pace for over $500K (2025-09). [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=242s)
- Timeline: he had a prototype at 6 months. He launched on the App Store at about 1.5 years and got 10 downloads in week one. In-app purchases came at about 2 years. [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s)
- Over 15,000 businesses tried Waitly. It has 700 paying customers in the US and Canada and over 59 million parties waitlisted (2025-09). [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=531s)
- Apple Search Ads CAC is about $130. LTV is $750 to $1,000 (2025-09). [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)
- Waitly charges a flat $100/month for all features. Yelp and OpenTable charge hundreds per month plus cover fees per reservation (2025-09). [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)
- During COVID, retail chains put Waitly in over 700 locations. That year the business grew 10x. [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)
- Stack: Google Firebase for hosting, auth and database, Node.js back end, Swift iOS app and a React web app. [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=554s)
- Monthly costs (2025-09): text messages about $2,500, Firebase about $700, Apple Search Ads about $500 and Claude Code about $200. Small tools: Freshdesk $40, SurveyMonkey $35, Hive $25, ChatGPT $20 and Calendly $12. RevenueCat takes 1% and Stripe takes 3% of revenue. [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=578s), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=621s)

## Tactics

- **Fix a friction you feel.** Waitly sends a text with a link. The guest sees the wait time with no app download and no account. [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=326s)
- **Buy keywords while LTV beats CAC.** With LTV at 6x to 8x CAC, every extra dollar on Apple Search Ads returns profit. [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)
- **Nurture trials.** Trial users get tip emails and quick-start videos. They can book Zoom onboarding and staff training. Near the end of the trial, a promotion pushes the first purchase. [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)
- **Beat giants on three things.** Waitly wins on a flat low price, on simplicity and on support. A person answers every support call at once. [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)
- **A narrow product travels across industries.** Because Waitly only does waitlists and reservations, retail stores used it during COVID capacity limits. [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)
- **Idea framework in four steps.** Improve a solved problem and find an underserved niche, such as small restaurants. Build an MVP with AI in a couple of weeks. Get feedback from early customers. Expect to adapt. [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=436s)
- **Ideas he likes now.** A pickleball league management app, an AI support chatbot trained on a product, and a social network for friends and family. [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=477s)
