# My Side Project Makes $15K/Month (Quit My 9-5)

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Ben Bozzay, a software developer who built Tech Lockdown. Tech Lockdown helps adults block websites and apps through a filtered VPN connection. Ben built an audience with free guides, YouTube videos and Reddit posts for about a year before he charged money. When his employer laid him off in 2023, the product made about $3,000/month. He went full-time, and it grew 5x to over $15K MRR. His core advice: solve customer acquisition before you quit your job.

## Key facts

- Tech Lockdown has 1,300 customers and over $15K MRR (2025-07). Its mailing list has about 20,000 contacts (2025-07). [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s)
- Over 2 million people found the site in 2 years through Google, referrals and social channels (2025-07). [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s)
- Pricing: a 14-day free trial, then $15/month, or about $10/month on an annual plan (2025-07). [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=104s)
- The product uses a content policy. Users schedule blocks and block millions of sites by category or keyword. Devices connect through a VPN. [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=85s)
- At the 2023 layoff, revenue was about $3,000/month. That did not cover his costs. Revenue then grew 5x. [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=249s)
- The idea started in 2020. Remote work made him spend too much time online. He monetized about a year after his first YouTube video and Reddit post. [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=203s)
- Earlier failures: a lead generation platform for freelance developers, a landing page builder, and a web agency that ran for about 2.5 years. [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=147s)
- Stack: Supabase for database and auth, SvelteKit, Vercel at $20/month, Mailgun and Elastic Email at about $150/month, Google Gemini, an Ahrefs Lite plan and Plausible Analytics. [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=703s)
- He says a 70% profit margin is easy to reach with all optional tools added. [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=758s)

## Tactics

- **Build the audience before the product.** The audience must care about the topic. A general social following is not enough. Then you have a list of qualified buyers on launch day. [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=484s)
- **Skip branding early.** He had no logo until he had a few hundred customers. His failed projects stalled on colors, logos and names. [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=292s), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=147s)
- **Split the day by energy.** He coded from 5:30 to about 8:30 in the morning before his job. Evenings went to marketing, which needs less focus. [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=343s)
- **Pick one channel that energizes you.** For Ben it was content marketing. For others it can be face-to-face sales. [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=343s)
- **Write deep, free, shareable guides.** One step-by-step guide shows how to turn an iPhone into a "dumb phone". It got hundreds of thousands of reads. [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=588s)
- **Put the value in the Reddit post itself.** Reddit punishes promotion. He puts the best content in the post and adds a short link to the guide or video. More engagement pushes the post to the front page. [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=588s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=648s)
- **Show your face.** Rivals are faceless apps that look corporate. He writes "I", not "we". [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=671s)
- **Push annual plans, keep monthly.** He wants annual buyers, but many users quit after a month, so he offers both. [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=104s)
- **Keep CAC low.** Many side projects sell "a dollar for 90 cents" and fail at scale. A sustainable business needs low customer acquisition cost. [12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=774s)
- **Ship the simplest complete version.** Avoid ideas that take 2 years to build with no smaller release. [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=484s), [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=811s)

## Quotes

- "The customer acquisition problem is a thing you have to solve before you leave your job." [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=396s)
- "The hard part is making it into a viable business." [12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=774s)
- "Getting bogged down in the details leads to burnout and not actually shipping the product." [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=811s)
