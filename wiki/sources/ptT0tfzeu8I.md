## Summary

Pat Walls tells how Starter Story got its first traction. He spent months polishing the site while working a six-figure job in New York, launched, and got zero users. He then posted to Reddit. Moderators removed his link posts, so he put the full content inside self-posts with a small link at the end. Those posts hit the front page again and again. He turned the traffic into an email list of tens of thousands of subscribers. Then Redditors petitioned to ban his posts, and he quit his job.

## Key facts

- Starting point: a 9-to-5 job with a six-figure salary in New York City. [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=55s)
- First launch: months of nights and weekends on a "perfect" site, then zero users. [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=55s), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=116s)
- First Reddit link post: 10, 20, then 100 users, then removed by moderators. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=135s), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=208s)
- The first native self-post got hundreds of upvotes, dozens of comments, and thousands of site visitors. [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=302s)
- Repeated posts reached the front page. Hundreds of thousands visited the site and millions read the content. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)
- End state: Redditors started a "petition to ban starterstory.com posts". By then the email list had tens of thousands of subscribers, and he quit his job. [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=420s), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=445s)

## Tactics

- **Go where customers already are.** He studied how successful companies got traction. They used free channels (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit) and made content for their customers there. [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=135s)
- **Make the post native, not spam.** The subreddit allowed only self-posts. He put the whole case study inside the Reddit post and added a small link at the end for readers who wanted more. [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=208s)
- **Repeat what works, fast.** He expected moderators and copycats to close the gap, so he posted again and again while it worked. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)
- **Convert rented attention into owned attention.** He optimized a signup box to collect emails, so he no longer depended on Reddit. [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=445s)
- **Distribution before polish.** The lesson he draws from thousands of founder interviews: getting in front of customers is the "secret sauce", not a perfect product. [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=445s)
