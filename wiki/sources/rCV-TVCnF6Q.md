## Summary

Eyal and Yahli Hazan are college students who built Prop GPT, a sports betting analytics app with a machine learning model that scores picks. Influencer marketing brought steady downloads and many trials, but few trial users paid. Revenue stalled at $1K to $2K MRR. They stopped all marketing for 4 months and rebuilt the app to give users ready answers instead of tools. After the relaunch they hit $30K MRR in 10 weeks and peaked at $40K MRR.

## Key facts

- The first version took about 5 months to build. It launched in the middle of the NFL season (2024) [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=120s).
- Early influencer spend of a few thousand dollars gave about 20 downloads a day and 5 to 10 trials a day [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=185s).
- Before the rebuild: 45% install to trial, but only 13% trial to paid [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s).
- The new version launched on April 15 (2025) at $1,700 MRR, about $15 a day [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s).
- After the relaunch, trial-to-paid rose above 50%. About 2.5 months later they peaked at $40K MRR and 2,000 downloads in one day [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s).
- About $30K/month, over 40,000 downloads, over 3,000 paying users, 48% install-to-trial, about $3.30 revenue per download (2025-11) [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=103s).
- One influencer video, about their 70th, got 600,000 views. It took ARR from about $8K to $38K in 3 days [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=436s).
- Stack: React Native, TypeScript, Python for the ML model and data fetch, a Neon database, RevenueCat and Superwall for revenue and paywalls [8:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=493s).
- Costs per month (2025-11): Superwall $0.20 per conversion, RevenueCat about 1%, sports data APIs $100, Neon $10, LLM $20, marketing about $10,000. Margin is about 50% [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=522s).

## Tactics

- **Read the funnel gap.** High install-to-trial with low trial-to-paid means people want the promise, but the product does not deliver it. On Super Bowl day many users started trials, and almost none stayed [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=337s).
- **Give answers, not tools.** Users did not want to enter their own bets and check them. They wanted the right picks handed to them, so the rebuild showed pre-analyzed bets [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s).
- **Pause marketing to fix the product.** They spent 4 months on engineering and design only. Then they went all in on marketing for the NBA playoffs [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s).
- **Four-step app playbook** [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=436s):
  1. Know exactly who you build for and their pain.
  2. Listen to your conversion data.
  3. Track onboarding drop-off and feature clicks in PostHog. The most-clicked feature is your best value claim, so use it in your marketing.
  4. Scale with influencer marketing. Followers trust the creator, and one viral video can change the business.
- **Match launches to the sports calendar.** Both launches lined up with big seasons: the NFL season and the NBA playoffs [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=120s).
