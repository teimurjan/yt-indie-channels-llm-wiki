## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Dawson Gibbs, founder of the UGC agency Evo Marketing. Dawson posted for six years with little success, then grew a fitness page and became an early face of Cal AI. His core framework splits products into two kinds. Products that need trust (travel, buying a business) need credible creators. Impulse products (shopping, a Gen Z Bible app) need relatable creators. He walks through campaigns for Jetpack, SMB Market, Dupe and Bible BFF. He covers attribution domains, creator pages, Instagram Stories giveaways, green screen formats and a repost rule for early winners.

- Dawson posted daily for 5 to 6 years: about 1,500 to 2,000 videos and only 2,000 followers. Then he grew to hundreds of thousands of followers in about 4 months. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=0s), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=207s)

## Tactics

- **Credible vs. relatable.** A flight is a high-trust purchase that people make later. It needs an expert hook, for example "I have been a flight attendant for 3 years." Impulse buys, such as a $40 saving or a Bible app, need "I am just like you" messaging. [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=313s), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=375s), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=558s)
- **Vanity domains for attribution.** flightcheatcode.com redirects to Jetpack, so Evo can separate its organic traffic from paid ads and other agencies. The domain also says the value better than a brand name nobody knows. Evo did the same for SMB Market with a buy-a-business domain. [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=706s), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=736s), [14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=878s)
- **Do not demo a boring app.** Jetpack's UI is dull on video, so the creator sells the outcome. Viral products like Cal AI or Bible BFF show the product in use. [13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=833s), [24:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1440s)
- **Match creator age to buyer.** For SMB Market, Evo dropped 20-year-old creators and get-rich hooks. It switched to 35 to 40-year-old creators. They used green screens of the founder to borrow his credibility, which brought higher intent. [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1056s), [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1138s)
- **Platform by age.** Use Facebook and Instagram for 30 to 40-year-olds. Use TikTok and Snapchat for 18 to 25-year-olds. [22:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1363s)
- **Hands always busy.** Every video is approved before posting. Creators must move: fix a ponytail, eat snacks, squeeze a toy. More faces keep viewers past 5 seconds. [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=637s), [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1608s), [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1730s)
- **Build creator pages for high-trust apps.** Use CTAs such as "share with three friends" or "comment a word for a DM" with ManyChat. Then run Instagram Stories giveaways, such as $1,000 with the brand. A 200K-follower page gets about 10K story views. [31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1861s), [32:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1961s)
- **Green screen reaction format.** It is low effort but still a talking video. Creators react to a viral clip with an extreme spoken hook. Talking to the camera converts better than text-only or face-reaction formats. [35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2107s), [38:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2326s)
- **Spell the URL slowly.** Dupe shows "dupe.com" for about 5 seconds as the call to action. [41:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2490s), [41:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2509s)
- **Repost early winners.** A video at 10K to 20K views with 3%+ engagement gets reposted. Or the creator shoots it again with a new text hook. If repeats hold 10K+ views for 4 to 5 days, one usually hits 500K to 1M. [43:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2596s)
- **Brands repost via collabs.** Videos with about 20K views get collab-posted to the brand page for extra views. [36:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2164s)
- **Moms and older creators convert best.** Family influencers convert family products at very high rates. [46:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2774s)
- **Two-phase campaigns.** In weeks 1 to 2, chase views with broad creators, hooks and long text formats. In weeks 3 to 4, move the winning scripts into talking-head formats with a credible source. [48:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2923s), [49:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2984s)
