# I Studied 1,000,000 UGC Ads, This Is What Will Grow Your App

## Summary

Joseph Choi interviews Drew Levin, co-founder of SideShift, a marketplace that supplies UGC creators and campaign managers to consumer apps. Drew explains how the best programs run: about 1 manager for every 10 creators, hands-on founders at the start, and a steady supply of new creators. Good videos then get boosted as TikTok Spark ads or Meta partnership ads. He gives concrete rules for engagement rate, Spark budgets and kill points, CPM targets by month, and creator pay structures. SideShift trains its own creators with a 6-hour, Duolingo-style course.

## Key facts

- SideShift has 850,000 creators, about 150,000 monthly active, and about 100 vetted campaign managers (2026-06). [49:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2959s)
- About 5,000 new brands create SideShift accounts each month (2026-06). [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=520s)
- One long-running program: over 10,000 posts, 500M views and 14M engagements, which took the app to #1 on the App Store. [29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1740s)
- Suno reached #1 in the App Store music category with heavy SideShift use and about 8 creator managers. [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=614s), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=642s)
- A SideShift talking-head video about the job market got 9.6M views at a 16% engagement rate. After Nick cracked the format, 6 or 7 of 15 creator videos got 150K to 300K views each. [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1067s), [19:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1155s)
- Top programs spend over $100K/month and reach sub-$1 CPMs, usually $0.80 to $0.90 (2026-06). Expect $5 to $8 in month one and under $3 by month three. [46:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2777s), [47:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2876s)
- Trained creators earn $10K to $15K a month after 1 to 2 months (2026-06). Some brands do not pay their creators. In those cases SideShift pays, close to six figures a month in total. [25:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1545s), [28:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1695s)
- GoWish reached #1 on the App Store almost entirely through Spark ads run by Seen Marketing. [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s)

## Tactics

- **Build virality into the product.** Apps where users earn money or get something, such as Snag and Settlemate, do well on organic. AI products can lean into "people hate AI" comments, as Suno does. Health, scanning and GLP-1 apps ride the health trend. [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=278s), [6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=369s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=410s)
- **New format: 10-minute stories.** One creator grows one account to 10K followers and reuses it for several brands. Average watch time is 16%, and the CTA comes at minute nine. [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=278s), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=333s)
- **Managers and coaches.** Managers handle output, CTAs, recruiting and firing. Coaches give feedback on delivery. Keep 1 manager per 10 creators, with a range of 5 to 15. [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=569s), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=680s)
- **Founders start hands-on.** Review videos before posting in the first 3 days, and hold weekly calls. Step back once CPMs stay under $4. Top founders then only do payouts and monthly check-ins. [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=716s), [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=896s)
- **Make creator onboarding frictionless.** Use a DM, a 5-minute call, then a Notion page to join and submit handles. Find 2 to 3 reliable "rock" creators as the backbone. [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=792s), [13:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=835s)
- **Engagement rate predicts virality.** It is (likes + comments + saves + shares) / views. Above 7.5% to 8% is good, and above 12% often goes viral. [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1104s), [18:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1132s)
- **Spark rules.** Rank by comment sentiment ("what app is this?"), then engagement rate, then views. Start at $20 to test, or $50 to $100 a day for a week. If engagement falls below 5%, kill it. Let organic peak for a day or two first. Spark videos that do 3x to 5x the account average, because this also lifts the account out of shadowbans. [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s), [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2145s), [36:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2214s)
- **Do not pay view bonuses on paid views.** Separate Spark views from organic views. [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s)
- **Meta partnership ads.** Use targeting on Meta and reach on TikTok. Partnership ads on small UGC accounts convert better than Spark. [37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2251s), [38:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2331s)
- **Pay structures.** Cross-post to 4 platforms and pay only for the best one. Top programs pay $800/month for 60 videos with non-stacked view bonuses. New creators get $200 to $300 for the same package. Cap bonuses by views, not time, so later views are free. [39:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2391s), [43:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2611s), [44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2647s)
- **Do not cap a breakout creator.** One creator got 180M views in 2 weeks but was capped at $4K instead of about $75K. The brand lost him. [44:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2698s)
