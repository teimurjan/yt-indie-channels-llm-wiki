# I Built a $10K/Month SaaS Using Other People's Customers

## Summary

Ivan, a founder from Macedonia, runs a software agency that got most of its clients from Upwork. He built Lancer, an AI agent that finds, qualifies and bids on Upwork jobs for freelancers and agencies. Lancer reached $10K MRR in its third or fourth month with zero paid ads. Most growth came from two Upwork coaches. Ivan calls them "connectors": people who already have the trust of his ideal customers. He pays them lifetime affiliate commissions.

## Key facts

- Revenue: $10K/month by month 3 or 4 after launch (2026-03). [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=0s), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=78s)
- Pricing: pay-as-you-go is $79 for 30 proposals plus $2 per extra. Light is $300 for 250 proposals plus $1.50 per extra. Unlimited was a $500/month launch offer. [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=78s), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=140s)
- Ivan's agency, MB Masters, did over seven figures over 5 years and grew to almost 20 full-time staff. Upwork was its main channel. [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=153s)
- Upwork has about 200K job posts a month. A human needs about 10 minutes to qualify a job and write a proposal. [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=153s)
- Beta: agency-owner friends closed three five-figure clients within 2 weeks. [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=206s)
- Upwork coaches get 5 to 20 new paying clients a month. Each client pays them $600 to over $1,000. [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=463s)
- Commission: a coach who signs and onboards a client gets 30% for life. A plain referral gets 20%. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s)
- Stack: TypeScript with Next.js and Node.js, written in Cursor with Opus 4.5. OpenRouter serves the LLMs. Hosting is Hetzner and GCP (Firestore early on). Proxy providers connect to Upwork accounts. Elasticsearch stores and queries jobs. Tolt ("Told" in the captions) tracks affiliates. [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=266s), [12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=741s), [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=801s)

## Tactics

- **Build an internal tool first.** Ivan hacked the first version over a weekend for his agency. Then he built the MVP in 3 months on the agency's stack. [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=266s)
- **Automate work on a big platform.** A platform with a large user base has public, easy-to-find customers. LLMs handle job qualification and proposal writing. [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=206s), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=325s)
- **Sell to the layer above the customer.** One connector deal is a single high-ticket call. It replaces hundreds of calls for a $200/month product. [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s)
- **Get connectors by intro or by paying.** A beta user introduced the first coach, and a demo closed him. For the second coach, Ivan sent LinkedIn outreach and offered $1,000 for a call. [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s)

The five-step connector playbook: [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=571s)

1. **Define the ICP** from paying users. Look for low-friction onboarding and low churn. For Lancer this is high-volume agencies more than freelancers.
2. **Find connectors** who have a wide network of that ICP and high trust. [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=631s)
3. **Send a very personal pitch**, for example a long Loom video that references their work.
4. **Agree on terms.** The standard is a 20% to 30% lifetime commission. For a big network, an up-front payment can be part of the deal. [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=692s)
5. **Track and pay monthly** with affiliate software.
