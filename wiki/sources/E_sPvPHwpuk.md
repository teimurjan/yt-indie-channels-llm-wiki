# How I Built a $1M Business in 117 Days

## Summary

Pat Walls interviews Yasser Elsaid, who built Chatbase as a solo founder with no funding. Chatbase lets a business build a ChatGPT-style chatbot on its own data. He launched it in his final year of university to 16 Twitter followers, and the launch tweet went viral. Chatbase reached $1M ARR in 117 days and $6.8M ARR about 2.5 years later. He explains the tweet, his build-in-public method, product-led growth, and his stack.

## Key facts

- He launched Chatbase about 2.5 years before the interview, to 16 followers on Twitter. It was about his third tweet ever. [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=43s), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s)
- He did no validation. Two or three sales a day led him to drop out of university. [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)
- $0 to $1M ARR in 117 days. [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=322s)
- $6.8M ARR, 10,000 paying customers at $40 to $500/month, and about 600,000 registered users (2025-07). He says "7 million" in the intro. [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=431s), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=43s)
- Growth is product-led only, with no sales team (2025-07). [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=431s)
- One sponsored post on a large LinkedIn page brought about $4,000 in one day (2025-07). [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=393s)
- Stack: Vercel hosting and AI SDK, Supabase database, models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Google Gemini and Groq, Stripe billing, Dub for affiliates, Mintlify for docs. [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=570s)

## Tactics

- **Build what demos show but nobody sells.** People demoed "ChatGPT for your data", but no one had a product. He shipped one before others did. [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)
- **Design the launch tweet.** Three things made it work: a familiar ChatGPT-style streaming interface, a demo that reaches the aha moment in about 20 seconds, and a mention of the tools used. [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s)
- **Tag new tools for a viral loop.** LangChain and Pinecone had few real users to show. They reposted his demo, which spread it further. [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=193s)
- **Build in public every day.** Expect 2 to 3 months of practice with few views. Post your face and videos on X, tell a personal story, and share real opinions. [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=210s), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=259s)
- **Frame each feature as a new launch.** Write each release for people who do not know you or the product. [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s)
- **Free bots for traffic.** He made free chatbots for books in book subreddits and for influencers such as Paul Graham and Naval. He lost money on them. The aim was visits and domain authority, which led to paid signups. [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=393s)
- **Sponsored posts on days without a launch.** On a day with no launch to tweet, pay a large page to post. [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=393s)
- **Product-led growth.** Put a Stripe button on a good landing page and do the marketing off-site. Ship one core feature (upload a PDF and chat with it) with a clean UI and no bugs. [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=456s)
- **Build ahead of the model.** Pick a product that today's models cannot quite do. Bet that the next model can. He had Chatbase ready before the ChatGPT API. On the API launch day, he tweeted it as new. [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=499s), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=560s)
- **Learn agents from vendor docs.** Use the OpenAI cookbooks and Agents SDK, and the Vercel AI SDK examples. [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=499s)
