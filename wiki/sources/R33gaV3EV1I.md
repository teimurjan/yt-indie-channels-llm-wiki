# I Made the Biggest Movie Ever Into a Website

## Summary

Tim Gabe builds a website for the movie Avatar in Framer. His test: can zoom effects, video carousels and scroll animations make a site feel like a blockbuster? He sketches the hero first, then builds three story sections: Joy, Agony and Salvation. Each section uses layered images, particle effects, scroll-linked motion and fade-in text. The video also promotes his upcoming Framer course through a waitlist link.

## Key facts

- The site has four parts: a hero with a cinematic zoom, then the "Joy", "Agony" and "Salvation" sections. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=0s), [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=144s)
- The whole build is in Framer, including its built-in slider component for the final 3D carousel of movie clips. [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=305s)
- The call to action is a waitlist for his Framer course (2023-10). [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=144s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=377s)

## Tactics

- **Sketch before you build.** He starts on a black canvas with a plan. The hero shows a character on a cliff under a vivid sky. On load, the view zooms in and bold text rises from the bottom. Floating dust, a scroll hint and a small teaser line finish it. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=0s)
- **Fake depth with two layers.** Cut the character out of the background. Then zoom both images at different rates for a cinematic zoom. [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=67s)
- **Put the headline between layers.** He adds a blend mode and shadow for a glassy look. The text sits behind the character and in front of the background, so it seems to sit on the horizon. [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=67s)
- **Stagger the entrance.** The logo, scroll icon and teaser text enter from each edge of the screen. The big heading slides up from the bottom. [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=129s)
- **Blur for contrast.** In the Joy section, a dark blurred block sits behind the text on the right. A banshee flies across the screen as the user scrolls. The button gets a shine effect. [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=206s)
- **Glow from duplicates.** For Agony, he duplicates each image and blurs the copy behind it. The images spin and move at different scroll speeds. He recolors the dust as fire particles and centers the content for variety. [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=242s)
- **Reuse one effect in new ways.** For Salvation, he changes the particle size, shape, count and color, blurs them, and puts them behind the slider. This gives an aurora glow. [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=305s)
- **Add 3D scroll effects to a carousel.** Movie clips in the slider fall into place as the user scrolls. [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=305s)
- **Match the button to the brand.** The last call to action uses an "Avatar blue" shiny button. [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=367s)
