# How Postiz Went From $17K to $143K/Month in 4 Months

## Summary

Nevo returns to Starter Story 5 months after his first interview. Postiz, his social media scheduler for 30+ platforms, pivoted to an "agentic" scheduler that AI agents such as Claude, OpenClaw and ChatGPT drive. Oliver Henry wrote viral X articles on how he runs TikTok for his app Snugly with OpenClaw and Postiz. That brought about 700 trials a day. Nevo then wrote his own X articles and paid influencers to repost them. MRR grew from about $17K to $143K in 4 months, and churn halved.

## Key facts

- Postiz took 1.5 years to reach $21K MRR. Then it grew to $143K MRR in 4 months, about $1K MRR per day (2026-07). [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=82s)
- MRR posted on X: $41K (2026-02), $76K (2026-03), $80K (2026-04), $109K (2026-05) and $140K (late 2026-06). [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=26s)
- 4,271 paying subscribers (2026-07). Monthly churn fell from 26% to 28% before the pivot to 13.7% after it. [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=148s)
- Oliver Henry's first X article got 7.2 million views and brought about 700 trials a day. A second got about 1.5 million views. [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=199s)
- Nevo had 200 X followers. His own OpenClaw article still got about 500,000 views, and another got 500,000 more. [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=328s)
- A paid quote repost from an influencer costs $60 to $350 (2026-07). [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=549s)
- With the new revenue he hired two people, one for support and one for development. At $17K MRR, hiring was not affordable. [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=549s)

## Tactics

- **Ride the hype with your existing product.** OpenClaw was launched by a developer named Peter. Nevo shipped the tools to make Postiz agent-ready within days. [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=199s)
- **X is now an interest platform.** X shows a post to the niche that wants its topic, even from an account with few followers. X also pushes long articles because people read them longer than posts. [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=328s)
- **Buy reposts from accounts that already get views.** Check "view activity" on viral posts by other companies. Influencers in that list offer paid reposts. Each of his articles gets about 20 paid reposters, tagged as paid promotion. [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=390s)
- **Make the API agent-ready.** Turn your public API into a markdown file for agents. List it in every place you can, such as Claude and ChatGPT marketplaces. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=430s)
- **Build before the trend arrives.** A late builder loses the gain to someone else. [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=430s)
- **Agents reduce churn.** Users churn because they stop the daily manual work. An agent posts every day without a person in the loop. [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=491s), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=512s)
- **Stop features and make it reliable.** Nevo froze new features until all 30 channels worked. Pat adds that agents learn which tools break. [9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=587s)
- **Next unlock: agent payments.** Today a person still has to finish a Stripe checkout. Nevo hopes agents can buy on their own soon. [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=647s)
- **Pat's caveat.** The agentic pivot was only the vehicle. Marketing and a product that already solved a problem drove the growth. [13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=781s)
