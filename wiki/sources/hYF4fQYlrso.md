# I Make $16K/Month Even in a Tiny Niche

## Summary

Nic is a Ukrainian developer. He built BlogToPin, a SaaS that scans a website and creates and schedules Pinterest pins with AI. He found the problem through his own cocktail blog, where Pinterest beat every other traffic channel. The lifetime-deal launch made $0 at first, so he moved to subscriptions. He grew to $16K MRR in 2 years through word of mouth, affiliates, SEO and LLM recommendations. He shares a seven-step micro-SaaS playbook: copy a paid workflow, research competitor reviews, build fast, and treat the first customers like employers.

## Key facts

- BlogToPin makes a bit over $16K MRR with more than 400 active subscribers (2026-02). Monthly churn is about 10%. [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=164s)
- The starter plan costs about $39/month. Agency and enterprise plans cost more. [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s)
- Pinterest has over 500 million active users. One pin takes 5 to 10 minutes to design by hand, and growth needs 5 to 10 pins per day. [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s)
- Nic started coding at 15. After the 2022 war in Ukraine, he left the corporate path, found indie hacker communities and built several AI apps that made $0. [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=194s)
- He got the idea in December 2023 and built at night next to a 9-to-5 job with GitHub Copilot. A scrappy version took 7 days, and the real product took about 2 months. [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s)
- He had 1,000 Twitter followers at launch. The first paying customer came after 10 days. [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s)
- Stack: Vercel, Hetzner for scrapers, Oxylabs for proxies, Gemini for text, Fal for images and Sequenzy for emails that reduce churn. [14:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=867s)

## Tactics

- **Solve a pain you felt.** He paid someone to make pins for his blog, then saw a way to automate the job. [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=248s), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=540s)
- **Switch away from a lifetime deal.** Moving to a normal subscription and improving the product brought the first real customers within a month. [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s)
- **Asymmetric marketing.** Early grind on articles, influencer outreach, Reddit posts and backlinks now brings customers "on autopilot" from SEO and ChatGPT. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=511s)
- **Care drives word of mouth.** If a user hits a problem, he watches the screen recordings. He builds requested features in hours or days. [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s)
- **Playbook, steps 1 and 2.** Pick something people already do and pay for. Read competitor reviews on G2, AppSumo and Capterra, and watch every YouTube video about the tools. Check how little the incumbents use AI. [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=584s)
- **Step 3.** Build an MVP that is faster, cheaper and better for one specific person, with Claude Code, in 1 to 2 weeks. [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=644s)
- **Step 4.** Talk to your ICP on Reddit, X, cold outreach, YouTube, TikTok, Reels and Facebook groups. Directory submissions or a Product Hunt launch will not replace this. [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=644s), [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=704s)
- **Steps 5 to 7.** Treat the first customer as your employer and ship their suggestions the same day. Repeat for a few dozen users. Then improve churn, onboarding and emails by 1% a day. [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=704s)
- **The math.** One new paying customer a day with 10% monthly churn gives about 200 customers in a year. At $50 each, that is $10K MRR. [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=704s), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=765s)
- **Show value in onboarding.** A new user adds a website. In about a minute the app shows sample pins from that site, then asks for settings and generates a month of pins. [13:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=790s)
- **Judge mature markets by growth and churn.** Pat argues an old, proven market with an AI twist grows slowly but lasts. [16:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=972s)
