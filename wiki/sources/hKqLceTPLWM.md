## Summary

Steven Cravotta returns to Starter Story to explain the marketing playbook behind PuffCount, his quit-vaping app. He grew it to $44K MRR and sold it to a larger mobile app studio in Europe. The playbook has three phases: research viral TikToks in the niche, post high volume organically, then scale the proven videos with paid ads. He also demos Posted, his new marketplace where creators submit videos to a brief and the brand pays only for the ones it approves.

## Key facts

- PuffCount reached $44K MRR before the exit to a European app studio (2025-09). [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=78s)
- Organic TikTok: 120,000 followers, over 50M views and hundreds of thousands of installs for $0 (2025-09). [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=512s)
- Best organic video: 5M views. A different video got 11.6M views and about 1M likes but drove almost no installs. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=512s) [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=775s)
- Paid ads: $82K total spend took MRR from $3K to $44K. CAC was $20 to $24 and LTV was $55 to $70+, so $1 in returned about $2. [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s) [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)
- Cost per click from day one: 3 to 35 cents, because the ads reused proven organic videos. [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)
- Posted creator prices seen in the demo: $40 to $110 a video. A 552K-follower creator charged $110 and got 30K views. A 768-follower creator got 53K views (2025-09). [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=917s)

## Tactics

- **Pick the idea for marketability.** A painful problem, many sufferers and a topic that goes viral make a strong product. Vaping was all three for him. [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=115s) [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=168s)
- **TikTok research.** Search niche keywords and filter by most liked of all time. Before his first post, he scrolled for 7 days and logged each viral video in a spreadsheet: URL, hook, storyline and call to action. Note how it is shot, not only what is said. [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=211s) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=277s) [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=435s)
- **Copy formats, not ideas.** He dunked a vape in a glass of water because other viral videos did it first. [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=257s)
- **Hook, problem, solution.** Hook in the first 3 seconds. A sound hook helps. End with a 2-second product shot. A fast call to action makes people rewatch, which feeds engagement. The video only needs to send people to the App Store. [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=512s) [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=573s)
- **Volume beats perfection.** Post every day. Ignore post times. When a video pops, remake that concept. The first viral hit lifts the floor out of "200 view jail". Cross-post to Reels, Shorts and X. [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=663s) [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=724s)
- **Track installs, not views.** Pin the two highest-converting videos to the profile. His best converters were public interviews, product demos, trend hijacks and problem-solution stories. [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=775s) [13:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=835s)
- **Paid ads, step by step.** Set up an MMP such as Adjust or AppsFlyer. Do not start with a purchase campaign. Optimize for views, then clicks, installs, trials and finally subscriptions as data builds. [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s) [18:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1088s)
- **One platform first.** TikTok traffic was cheaper but lower quality. Meta was pricier but higher quality. Most of his spend went to TikTok because his content was made for it. [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1148s)
- **LTV is the north star.** Track cost per install, trial conversion, subscription rate, retention and LTV with Superwall or RevenueCat. Aim for LTV at 3x CAC. [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1270s)
