# He built 100 apps and made $0. Can he fix it in 30 days?

## Summary

This is episode one of the Starter Story Challenge. Gaurabh Mathure is a product designer. He built products for Nike and Samsung. He built 72 apps in 60 days and made $0. Pat Walls says the problem is an addiction to building, shiny object syndrome and fear of rejection. He sets a $1,000 goal with two rules: no new building, two customer conversations a day, and one post a day. The first 30 days brought $100. After a switch to vlog-style video on LinkedIn and a narrower audience, he reached about $6,500 by day 60.

## Key facts

- 72 apps built in 60 days, $0 revenue before the challenge (2026-08). [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=65s)
- Two focus products: Attention Design, an inspiration library for YouTube creators, and VueMagnet, a QR code and lead-magnet platform for video creators. [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=100s)
- Days 1 to 30: he posted on X, LinkedIn, Reddit and YouTube. One sale of Attention Design at $99. [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s)
- About 250 link engagements on LinkedIn, mostly from professionals in high-spending cities. [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=499s)
- After 60 days: about $6,500 in revenue (2026-08). Attention Design Pro has 5 customers at $99. Most revenue came from a VueMagnet agency plan that agencies use to manage several creators. [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=629s)
- Next goal: hold about $6K per month for 6 months, then grow. [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=788s)

## Tactics

- **Stop building.** Pat banned new features and projects for 30 days. One 15-minute customer call is worth more than 70 apps. [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)
- **Use a personal brand as the channel.** Post once a day and document the journey. Pat calls it the highest-ROI channel for him. [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)
- **Spray, then focus.** Posting on every platform gave little, but it showed LinkedIn as the signal. [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s)
- **Pattern break.** Vlog-style video on LinkedIn stood out among launch videos and expert interviews. Video posts got the most impressions. [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s)
- **Re-target the customer.** He moved from YouTubers to experts and professionals who want a personal brand on LinkedIn. The tagline changed to "where experts become creators". [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s), [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=729s)
- **Do things that do not scale.** Cold outreach with free mock-ups and personalized demos. [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=550s)
- **Four lessons.** Live the problem you solve. Start where you already have a network and credibility. Meet customers at their stage. Video is the language of the internet. [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=669s), [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=729s)
