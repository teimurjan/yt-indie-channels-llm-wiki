## Summary

Nico Jeannen quit a bank job at 19, tried a tie brand and dropshipping, then locked himself in a hotel room for 2 months to learn to code. In about a year he built 17 apps. Most failed, but he sold two of them, MakeLogo.ai and TalkNotes, for a combined $265K (2025-03). Most indie makers rely on organic short-form video. Nico grew his apps with Facebook ads instead. He validates ideas with no-code hacks first and sells apps through Acquire.com.

## Key facts

- 17 apps built in a year, 2 sold for $265K total (2025-03). [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)
- MakeLogo.ai: AI logo generator, one-time payment for packs of 5, 10 or 15 logos plus customization. $26K in sales, then sold for $65K cash, about $85K total. Built from November, sale closed in March, about 6 months. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s)
- TalkNotes: turns voice notes into formatted text. $77K in sales, sold for $200K in July 2024. About 10,000 users. Pricing moved from a one-year pass to a subscription of $60/year or $12/month. [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s)
- Peak: $0 to $7K MRR with Facebook ads. [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=0s)
- TalkNotes ad spend: $20 to $100/day. [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=616s)
- TalkNotes hit about $1.5K MRR organically before he started ads. [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s)
- Stack: Nuxt with NextUI components (about 3x faster coding). Cursor with the Nuxt and NextUI docs embedded (about 10x faster). v0 for UI, Ahrefs for SEO. He also built his own feedback widget and his own A/B testing tool. [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=812s), [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=872s)

## Tactics

- **Four idea filters:** the value of the problem to others, market scale, his own skill to build it, and a profitable distribution channel. Most winners came from his own needs. [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=266s)
- **Hard deadline first.** A fixed date forces only essential features. Then he writes the feature list and collects design ideas from Dribbble, Google Images and v0. He uses Figma only for complex UIs. Next come a reusable SaaS boilerplate (auth, payments), a landing page, and SEO and mobile polish. [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=316s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=377s)
- **Fake the backend.** For MakeLogo.ai he used a Typeform to collect the brief and the payment, with no backend. First sales proved demand before he built the real interface. [7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=422s)
- **Validate organically, then pay.** He started ads only after organic traction. He began with basic static ads, then built landing pages and ads for specific audiences, which is where growth "blew up". [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s)
- **UGC-style ads.** His best TalkNotes ad was a paid person reading a script in his office. It looked organic, led with the problem, and named the app only near the end before the call to action. [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=654s), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=714s)
- **Selling on Acquire.com.** Write the listing like a landing page. Get several buyers bidding. MakeLogo.ai bids went from about $50K to $65K paid over a year. Then one buyer offered $65K cash, with a close in 2 weeks. Acquire.com does not handle the wire. [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=752s)
- **Sprint and rest.** In a grind phase he wakes at 5:30 to 6:00 a.m., gyms, and works until 7 to 8 p.m. Then he rests and picks the next project. [14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=886s)
