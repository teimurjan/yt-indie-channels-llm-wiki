## Summary

Scott Millard, a former freelance video editor, runs a productized service that sells short animated video ads to funded B2B SaaS companies. The video does not name the company. He grew from about $40-50K/year as a freelancer to about $50K/month in roughly one year. Three moves drove the growth: a narrower niche, a switch from single videos to packages, and cold outreach next to daily organic posting on Twitter and LinkedIn. He runs it with a small international team of contractors and a closer.

## Key facts

- Offer: short animated "scroll stopper" videos that B2B SaaS companies run as paid ads. Clients are mostly VC-backed companies at seed or Series A, because they can pay ([0:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=28s), [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=48s)).
- Revenue: about $50K/month on average, with a range of $35-55K/month (2023-03) ([4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=282s)). Before this he earned at most $40-50K/year pre-tax ([3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=199s)).
- Monthly revenue path: $7K, $5K, $9K, $12K, $23K, $33K. It stalled at about $12K until he added packages ([13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=825s)).
- Pricing: the first video sold for $500. Average order value is about $3K, and new packages sell for $6-12K (2023-03) ([11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=677s)).
- Margins: about 65% gross and 40-50% net. The tech stack (cold email software, list tool, Slack, Stripe fees) costs about $1,200/month (2023-03) ([9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=581s)).
- Team: about 4 people. No full-time employees. He has full-time contractors, fractional roles and a sales closer, all international ([8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=505s)).
- Cold email reply rate for software buyers: about 4% (2023-03) ([14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=877s)).
- Background: he left college after 2 months, at age 19 (2019). He then joined a vacation-rental startup in Colorado. He has made videos since 2016 ([2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=157s), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=243s)).

## Tactics

- **Productize the skill.** Sell a fixed package with set deliverables instead of hours. Put a down-sell and an upsell around a main package (for example $3K/month). Then hire people to deliver it ([6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=397s), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=445s)).
- **Niche down in steps.** He went from "videos" to "short videos under 60 seconds", then "for online brands", then "for B2B SaaS". Turning more people away made it clear who the product was for, and it let him raise prices ([10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=648s), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=677s)).
- **Sell the outcome, not the video.** The package has a set of videos, reformats for each ad platform, and retargeting ad setup. The single video is now the down-sell ([12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=768s)).
- **Two-sentence cold email.** "We noticed your landing page has no video above the fold. That's something we do. Have you looked into it?" Long paragraphs do not get read ([14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=877s)).
- **Simple funnel.** Top: organic posts, DMs (LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Twitter), cold email and ads. Middle: the prospect checks his profile and books a call. Bottom: a sales call with his closer ([15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=941s)).
- **Do sales first, then hand it off.** He took about 150 sales calls, wrote down the structure, and gave it to a closer ([16:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=988s)).
- **Hire from the audience.** All of his current contractors reached out to him after seeing his content ([9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=557s)).
- **Post daily.** Spend an hour each day and write educational content for the buyer, for example how brands get more customers with video ([5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=336s)).
