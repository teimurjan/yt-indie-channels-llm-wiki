# Figma vs Framer in 100 Seconds

## Summary

Tim Gabe compares Figma and Framer and says which one to pick. Figma is a general design tool. Framer is a no-code web design tool that publishes live websites. His analogy: Figma draws the blueprint, and Framer builds the building. The choice depends on what you make. He also promotes his upcoming Framer course.

## Key facts

- Figma lets you design anything, but an app or website still goes to a developer or another tool to be built. [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=16s)
- Framer designs and publishes in one place, with no help from others. Its limit is that it only makes things for the web. [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=40s)
- The two tools have similar interfaces (2023-10). [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=56s)

## Tactics

- **Pick by output.** For a designer who only makes websites, Framer is enough as the only tool. [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=56s)
- **Keep Figma for non-web work.** Graphics, apps and dashboards need Figma in the workflow. [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=56s)
- **Learn one, then the other.** The interfaces are close, so skill in one transfers. [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=56s)
