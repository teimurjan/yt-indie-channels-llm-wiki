Stella is an AI manifestation app that makes custom visualizations in the voice of your future self and gives daily custom affirmations.

## What it is

- An AI app for manifestation: custom visualizations, meditations and daily affirmations, including lock-screen affirmations [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=315s), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=633s).
- The founder found no app that made custom visualizations or meditations. She says many apps ignore female problems because men designed them [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=220s).

## Founders

- [Sarah Pearl](../people/sarah-pearl.md) ("Hot High Priestess"), a creator with 1.2M Instagram and 2.8M TikTok followers. She also runs a TikTok Shop brand that she says makes "seven figures a month" (2026-07) [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=220s).
- She [vibe-coded](../topics/vibe-coding.md) the MVP in about 2 days with Claude and Google AI Studio. The full app took 2 to 3 months [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=399s).

## Business model

- A paid subscription app with 12,000 paying customers (2026-07) [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s).

## Metrics

- Over $300K MRR within 2 months of launch, and about $350K to $360K MRR at recording (2026-07). The title says $340K/month [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s).
- Over 200,000 downloads (2026-07) [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s).

## Growth channels

- **The founder's audience.** Over 1B combined TikTok views. A new TikTok account started after launch passed 40,000 followers in a couple of months [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=150s), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=169s).
- **Recreate proven videos and insert the product.** Work backward from a viral video and ask which product fits it [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=559s).
- **Skit plus demo.** She reads affirmations, a man asks how to remember them, and she shows Stella's lock-screen affirmations [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=633s).
- **Savable content and ManyChat.** "Repeat these affirmations for 7 days" drives saves. "Comment DOWNLOAD" sends a DM with the link and a review request [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=656s).
- **Demo videos convert.** Videos that mention Stella without a demo convert worse. One demo with 300K views converted very well [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=717s).
- **Controversy.** Her most controversial videos perform best [source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=489s).
