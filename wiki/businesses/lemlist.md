lemlist is a bootstrapped cold email tool that won a crowded market on personalization.

## What it is

- A sales email tool focused on human, personalized emails, tied to meetings booked as a direct ROI ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=542s)). See [Cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- Other founders treat it as an incumbent: [useArtemis](useartemis.md) competes with Apollo and lemlist and is hard to maintain ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)).

## Founders

- [Guillaume Moubeche](../people/guillaume-moubeche.md) started it with $1,000 in the bank after a failed startup. Two co-founders left at the plateau ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=482s), [15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=932s)).

## Business model

- SaaS subscription. Early buyers got campaigns written by Guillaume in return for appearing as success stories ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=643s)).

## Metrics

- First month: $600, then 40% month-over-month growth ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=706s)).
- Activation rose from 15% to 35% after a redesign; growth fell to 0% that month, then jumped about 60% ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=768s)).
- $30M ARR, $10M EBITDA, about 100 staff, customers in over 100 countries, valued above $150M (2024-12) ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1092s)).

## Growth channels

- **Live demos and outbound** closed the first 100 customers ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=643s)).
- **Founder-as-user loop.** Use the product, help customers, publish the best campaigns to a community, then improve the product ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=706s)). See [Community](../topics/community.md).
- **Magnet persona.** Users who never churn and attract others were sales reps, so it became "the sales tool for sales teams" ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1031s)). See [Positioning](../topics/positioning.md).

## Timeline

- MVP in two weeks ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=643s)).
- ARR: $250K after year one, $1M in year two, about $8M in year three, $10M at three and a half years ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=836s)).
- Plateau near $10M ARR; a year and a half to rebuild the architecture ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [16:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=993s)).
- 2024-12: $30M ARR ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1092s)).
