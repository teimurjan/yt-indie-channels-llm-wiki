Late Checkout is Greg Isenberg's holding company of six community-led businesses.

## What it is

- A holding company. The six businesses are LCA (innovation agency), You Probably Need a Robot (AI community), a design agency, Boring Marketing (AI-assisted SEO), Boring Ads (AI-assisted ads) and a community for "multipreneurs". ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s))

## Founders

- [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md). He sold Islands to WeWork and became head of product there. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s))

## Business model

- Each business starts with $0 and follows the "ACP funnel": audience, then community, then product. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s))
- Once a business makes at least $100K profit, Greg hires an operator and starts or buys the next one. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=645s))

## Metrics

- Eight figures of revenue and mid seven figures of profit (2024-06). Pat Walls says over $10M/year revenue and over $5M/year profit. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=0s), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s))
- [LCA](lca.md) charges clients such as Nike and Shopify about $1.5M/year on average. $1.5M revenue in year one, $5M+ in year two (2024-06). ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s))
- Dispatch, a design subscription, did seven figures in year one with $450K profit. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s))
- Boring Marketing made $350K profit in year one, forecast at $2M to $3M profit in year two (2024-06). ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=509s))

## Growth channels

- **Greg's audience.** About 10,000 Twitter followers at the start of LCA. He met the first clients at parties he promoted to that audience. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s))
- **Fast spin-ups.** The Dispatch account hit a few thousand followers in weeks. The landing page, brand and team took 72 hours. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s))
- **Hiring from the community.** The most active members become "nerds in residence". ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=529s))

## Timeline

- After WeWork ran out of cash, Greg chose profitable, community-led businesses over venture-backed ones. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s))
- Nice Break, built product-first, was shut down. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s))
- 2024-06: six businesses, eight figures of revenue. ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s))
