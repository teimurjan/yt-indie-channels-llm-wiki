Webflow is a visual website builder with a CMS and hosting. In the wiki it shows up as a site tool for founders, as an app platform, and as [Framer](framer.md)'s main rival.

## What it is

- A no-code website builder that needs an understanding of HTML structure and CSS (2023-05) ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=43s)).
- Its classes and drag-and-drop builder suit developers ([source](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)).
- It has an app store. [Optibase](optibase.md), an A/B testing tool for Webflow, gets sales from it ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)).

## Business model

- Hosting plans. Basic costs $14 against Framer's $5 Mini plan (2023-09). For mid-size CMS sites like blogs, Webflow's CMS plan is cheaper than Framer's Pro plan. For big projects Framer is a bit cheaper ([source](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s)).

## Webflow versus Framer

- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) says designers move to Framer because of the learning curve, who the tool is for, the community and shipping speed. Webflow can do anything, but that adds complexity (2023-05) ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=100s)).
- Webflow has a helpful forum, but Tim calls Framer's Discord the more passionate community ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=149s)).
- Figma export to Webflow needs extra steps, and the Figma file must follow Webflow's structure. Framer wins on export and UI for designers. Webflow wins on CMS: "probably its strongest competitive advantage over Framer" (2023-09) ([source](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)).
- Framer's "HTML to Framer" extension copied 6 free Webflow templates in about 2 minutes, with some broken elements ([source](../sources/bBamB0yE12c.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=0s)).

## How founders use it

- [DesignJoy](designjoy.md): [Brett Williams](../people/brett-williams.md) built the first version in 6 to 7 hours on one Saturday, a one-page Webflow site plus a Trello board. Running costs were $176/month including Webflow hosting (2023-05) ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=126s), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=292s)).
- [Setter AI](setter-ai.md) lists Webflow in its stack (2025-09) ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=633s)).
- Superwall's first paywalls ran on Webflow, at about 12% of Webflow's egress costs, before it moved to Svelte and then its own editor ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=225s)).
- [Optibase](optibase.md) makes about $6.5K MRR from the Webflow app store, Google and word of mouth (2025-04) ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)).
