Cat on Chair is an iOS Pomodoro timer in which a cat sits on your chair while you focus and leaves a gift when the session ends.

## What it is

- A focus timer with a game layer. After a good session the cat leaves a gift. After a failed one it leaves trash, a nod to the dead-mouse meme ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=505s)).
- Customization inspired by Animal Crossing: cats with backstories, furniture, backgrounds and a gift shop ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=433s)).

## Founders

- [Ryan Yao](../people/ryan-yao.md), a former product designer who left a $200K job. He wrote the code with AI tools ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md)).
- His girlfriend Stephanie draws the art. Her style gives the app a personality that rivals lack ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=553s)).

## Business model

- Free app with pro features. Lifetime $29.99, annual $19.99, and a family share for up to six people. Most sales are lifetime (2026-04) ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=129s)).
- Costs: ChatGPT Pro for code, RevenueCat at $200 to $300/month, an ASO tool at $10/month (2026-04) ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=382s)).

## Metrics

- Over $18K in the 30 days from April 13, and close to $80K in sales over 8 to 9 months (2026-04) ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=84s)).
- 127K total downloads, about 24K first-time downloads in the last 30 days (2026-04) ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=84s)).
- Peak rank: #3 free productivity app and #11 of all free apps in Taiwan ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=129s)).

## Growth channels

- [Organic social](../topics/organic-social.md) on RedNote, where Stephanie already shared her art. Beta users came from there ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=340s)).
- Instagram "app collection" posts by [influencers](../topics/influencer-marketing.md). One passed 2M views. A Taiwanese user's Threads post then doubled downloads and revenue overnight ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=300s)).
