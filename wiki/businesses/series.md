Series is an AI social network startup. The only source on it is an interview with its former marketing lead, [Eunice Lie](../people/eunice-lie.md) ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=34s)).

## What it is

- An AI social network with an onboarding flow. Eunice once walked a Hinge date through it ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=169s)).

## Founders

- The source does not name the founders. Eunice Lie, a 20-year-old college student, became its marketing lead, called CMO. She later moved to Sonder, a startup from her Carnegie Mellon friends ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=34s)).

## Business model

- The source does not describe how Series makes money.

## Metrics

- Eunice made 3 viral launch videos for Series. Her New York Tech Week events got over 3,000 RSVPs (2025-09) ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=34s)).
- Hamptons intern campaign: about 2,500 applications from one LinkedIn post. 150 people joined a race for 12 show spots. Over 100 interns posted about it on LinkedIn in 2 days. The winner invited 120 people in one day ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1104s), [20:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1202s), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1225s)).

## Growth channels

- **Founder-style content on X.** Eunice posted real [Hinge](hinge.md) chats with tech workers who asked about her startup. Generic "build in public" tweets had gone unnoticed ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s)).
- **A race to the Hamptons.** The show had 12 spots, so she turned 2,500 applicants into a race with invite challenges ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1225s)).
- **Social proof on LinkedIn.** Once one or two interns posted, the rest felt safe to post ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1104s)). Eunice says LinkedIn followers are the highest-value followers ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1160s)).
- **Launch videos.** She made three viral launch videos ([source](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=34s)).
