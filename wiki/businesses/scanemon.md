Scanemon is a Pokémon card scanner app that shows a card's market value. Kyle Fowler cloned it from his sports card app, Cardstock.

## What it is

- The phone camera scans a Pokémon card and shows the matched card, its market value and recent sales ([xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=621s)).
- One source spells it "Scanomon" ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md)).

## Founders

- [Kyle Fowler](../people/kyle-fowler.md), a self-taught iOS developer who builds around his card-collecting hobby. His team is five people (2026-06) ([xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s)).
- Jacob, a friend Kyle hired from Wendy's, makes TikToks for both apps ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1670s)).

## Business model

- Subscription app, like [Cardstock](cardstock.md), monetized with Superwall and RevenueCat ([xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=526s)).

## Metrics

- About $20K monthly revenue and about $9K MRR (2025-11) ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:40:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2457s)).
- About 3,400 active subscriptions, $17K MRR and about 16,000 new customers a month, more than Cardstock gets (2026-06) ([xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=119s), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=162s)).
- Both apps together: about $41K MRR (2025-11), then $120K/month revenue (2026-06) ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=0s), [xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok slideshows through [Noise](noise.md).** Noise turns hook images and captions into templates, gives them to creators, tracks attribution and pays out. About 7,600 posts in a month for $2,600, about $1 CPM (2025-11) ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:42:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2558s), [0:45:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2745s)).
- **Scan-and-reveal format.** Show a desirable card with a hook, scan it in hand and reveal the value. The goal is "what's the app?" comments ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1466s)).
- **Per-view creator pay**, after validating a format on your own accounts ([xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=566s)).
