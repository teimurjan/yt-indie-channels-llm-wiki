# Legacy X

Legacy X is an Amazon seller coaching company that took equity in [Launch Fast](launchfast.md) and launched it to its students.

## What it is

- A coaching company that teaches people to sell on Amazon. [Hassam](../people/hassam.md) once bought a course from it ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md)).

## Founders

- The source does not name its founders.

## Business model

- Coaching and courses for Amazon sellers. Through Launch Fast it also earns from software: its students pay $50/month for the tool (2026-01) ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s)).
- Gus estimates that Legacy X took about 50% of Launch Fast's equity (2026-01) ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=805s)).

## Metrics

- Launch Fast, launched to Legacy X students: about $10K MRR at day 30, $17K to $18K at day 60 and about $21.8K at day 90 (2026-01) ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s)).

## Growth channels

- **A distribution partner for software.** Hassam had no audience. He offered to build Legacy X a better product research tool in 48 hours, with no strings attached. Legacy X called the next day and told him to quit his job ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=251s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=342s)).
- Pat Walls calls this equity-for-distribution deal a shortcut for founders who are weak at marketing ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=842s)).

## Timeline

- Hassam buys a Legacy X course while he runs Amazon private-label brands ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md)).
- He pitches a 48-hour build. Legacy X takes equity and launches Launch Fast to its students ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=284s)).
- 90 days later: about $21.8K MRR (2026-01).
