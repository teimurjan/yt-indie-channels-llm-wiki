Avenue is an embeddable booking and inquiry widget for trades businesses in Australia, such as electricians and mechanics.

## What it is

- It shows a business's services visually, so customers can buy or inquire without a call ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=130s)).
- It plugs into Google reservations on the Google Business Profile. The same widget sits on the client's site, so all inquiries land in one inbox ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=600s)).

## Founders

- [Tom Holliday](../people/tom-holliday.md) and his brother. Tom had no coding background ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md)).
- Tom's 10-year-old marketing agency still had 70+ hosting clients. That income paid the first hires ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=350s)).

## Business model

- Subscription software for service businesses, fully bootstrapped ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=69s)).
- Twilio SMS is the biggest tool cost, at 4,000 a month (2026-09) ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=559s)).

## Metrics

- A$85K/month, about US$55K/month (2026-09) ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=69s)).
- Stripe: A$597 in August 2025, A$63,000 in August 2026 ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=155s)).

## Growth channels

- **Door-to-door demos.** iPads showed the widget in the owner's own branding. Printed brochures explained it ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=391s)).
- **Small clients first.** Seeing Avenue in many small shops made bigger clients trust it ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=391s)).
- **The niche chose them.** They pitched every business. Trades responded best ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=457s)).
- **Referrals from rejections.** Owners with no use for it sent them to friends ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=517s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
