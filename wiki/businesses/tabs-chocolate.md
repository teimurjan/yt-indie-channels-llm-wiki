Tabs Chocolate is a bootstrapped e-commerce brand that sells a "sex chocolate" made with natural libido supplements. It grew on organic TikTok, and app founders later copied its multi-account content playbook.

## What it is

- One product: a chocolate with natural libido supplements, started in a college apartment ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).
- The boxes are made in China. The chocolate is made in the US by four or five supplement manufacturers ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=181s)).

## Founders

- [Oliver Brocato](../people/oliver-brocato.md) founded it with no outside money and no employees (2023-06) ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).
- He later started Study Buddy to use the same playbook in software ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=357s)). A July 2025 description update says he exited Tabs and now builds Bustem, brand protection software ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).

## Business model

- Direct-to-consumer sales of a branded product. Oliver chose a brand over dropshipping, because a brand brings repeat buyers for new SKUs and equity for an exit ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=366s), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=427s)).
- Startup cost was $30,000, nearly his whole bank account ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=564s)).

## Metrics

- First month: over $280K in sales at about 50% net margin. The first inventory sold out in three weeks ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=485s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=547s)).
- Year one: about $4M in sales, though it was out of stock for more months than in stock ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=254s)).
- About $11M expected revenue in 2023 (2023-06) ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).
- Over 60 creators make videos, and thousands of accounts post them (2023-06) ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=642s)).
- [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) says it went from $0 to $10M in one year on organic TikTok only (2025-04) ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=357s)).
> Conflict: [5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md) gives about $4M in year one and $11M expected in 2023. [_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) says $0 to $10M in one year.

## Growth channels

- **A validated viral concept.** A friend sent Oliver a TikTok review of a similar product with 8M views and 2M likes. That seller had no website or social pages, so he built the brand version ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=91s)).
- **An emotional product.** Novelty creates "emotional arousal", so viewers watch to the end and share ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=136s)).
- **Native content at volume.** Videos follow the current sound or trend, so they do not look like ads ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=701s)). Teenagers posted on new zero-follower accounts ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=357s)).
- **Paid meme pages.** He pays meme pages with tens of millions of followers to post his best creatives. A little Google Ads, email and SMS cover the bottom of the funnel ([source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=727s)).

He reads its videos as an early hook-and-demo: a shocked face, a text overlay, then the reveal ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [28:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1694s)).
- [Dan](../people/dan.md) of [Massive](../businesses/massive.md) first heard of UGC that took off through Oliver's work at Tabs ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=268s)).
