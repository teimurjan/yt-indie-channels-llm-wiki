This page collects what the sources say about Supademo, an AI tool that makes interactive product demos.

## What it is

Supademo makes interactive, clickable product demos with AI ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s)).

## Founders

- [Joseph Lee](../people/joseph-lee.md) founded it. He scaled two products to over $3M ARR ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=59s)).
- Before Supademo he ran a venture-funded B2B seafood marketplace ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=162s)).

## Business model

- It is product-led. Most revenue comes from the Starter and Premium self-serve tiers ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=97s)).
- Joseph validated it with more than 100 B2B SaaS founders. They hated to make demos and to hear their own voice ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=229s)).

## Metrics

- Over $3M ARR, just over $250K MRR and over 150K users, about 2.5 years after launch (2026-03) ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s)).
- G2 named it the number 5 fastest-growing product in 2025 ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s)).
- Free tools bring about 20% of traffic and convert 15% to 20% of visitors into sign-ups (2026-03) ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=354s)).

## Growth channels

Traffic mix (2026-03): 30% to 40% from SEO and LLMs, about 30% from word of mouth, watermarks and referrals, and about 20% from LinkedIn ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)).

- **Comparison pages**. Write one against every competitor to piggyback on their traffic. Ship a rough version fast so LLMs cite it ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s)). See [Competitor alternatives](../topics/competitor-alternatives.md).
- **Free tools** in adjacent spaces, with no sign-up ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=354s)).
- **Programmatic how-to pages** with an embedded demo, such as "how to export Figma to PDF" ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=452s)).
- **Free demos on Reddit**. Joseph made a demo for each founder who posted a URL and replied in public ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=483s)).
- **Watermarks** on shared demos create a viral loop ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)).
