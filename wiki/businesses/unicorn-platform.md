Unicorn Platform is a website builder that John Rush bought in 2022 and uses as the base of his portfolio of founder tools.

## What it is

- A website builder with 600,000 users (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).
- Part of a portfolio of about 26 products, including [SEO Bot](../businesses/seo-bot.md) and [Listing Bot](../businesses/listing-bot.md) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=58s)).

## Founders

- Owner: [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md). He paid almost $1M in 2022, and the seller mentored him for a year as part of the deal ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s)). See [acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).

## Business model

- SaaS. Non-AI tools in the portfolio run at about 90% margin; human support is the main cost (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s)).

## Metrics

- 600,000 users (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).
- Portfolio: about 1M B2B users and about $3M ARR, roughly $250K/month (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=58s)).

## Growth channels

- [SEO](../topics/seo.md) first, then daily posts on X, LinkedIn, Substack and Facebook, then directories, then cross-promotion between tools ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s)).
- Products link into each other, for example a "boost my domain rating" button in SEO Bot that opens Listing Bot ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=576s)).

- 2025-05: 600,000 users ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).
