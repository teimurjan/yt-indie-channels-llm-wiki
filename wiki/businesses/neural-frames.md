Neural Frames is a bootstrapped AI music video generator for hobby, indie and AI-music creators.

## What it is

- AI video for musicians. The tagline moved from "text to video for everyone" to "the platform to create music videos" ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=395s)).

## Founders

- [Nico Jeannen](../people/nico-jeannen.md), a former physics PhD and musician, started it at the end of 2022 as his first full-stack project and built it alone for a year ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=119s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions for creators. GPUs (RunPod) and text-to-video APIs (fal) cost about $45K/month, almost half of revenue; little profit remains (2025-07) ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=599s)). See [Unit economics](../topics/unit-economics.md).

## Metrics

- About $100K revenue last month, team of 5 (2025-07) ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=52s)).
- About 1,500 customers, about 100,000 monthly visitors, 1.5M videos made (2025-07) ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=527s)).

## Growth channels

- **Hacker News launch** a week after going live: top 6 on a Sunday, 350 people on the site in 30 minutes, backlinks and first revenue ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=448s)).
- **[SEO](../topics/seo.md)** with landing pages and free tools ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=448s)).
- **Indie brand.** "No VC money, just a tiny company" in the footer, the founder's photo, and self-recorded tutorials ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=510s)).

## Timeline

- 2025-07: about $100K/month, 5 people ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=52s)).
