Capacity is a new AI coding tool by Samuel Rondot, the third app in his SaaS portfolio.

## What it is

- An AI coding tool ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)). See [vibe coding](../topics/vibe-coding.md).

## Founders

- [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md), a former optician who taught himself to code. He also runs [useArtemis](useartemis.md) and [StoryShort](storyshort.md) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=58s)).

## Business model

- [SaaS](../topics/saas.md) subscription ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).

## Metrics

- About $900/month and 50 users (2025-07) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
- Samuel's three apps make about $35K/month together (2025-07) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=58s)).

## Growth channels

- The source gives no Capacity-specific channel. Samuel's general method is ads first, then SEO, faceless video channels and affiliates ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=558s)).

## Timeline

- 2025-07: newly launched, at about $900/month ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
