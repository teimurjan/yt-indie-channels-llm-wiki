Netflix is a video streaming company. In the wiki it appears as a design case study, mostly in [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md)'s breakdowns.

## What it is

- A subscription streaming service. It started in 1997 as a DVD-by-mail company and launched streaming in 2007 with a basic grid UI ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=60s)).

## Founders

- Reed Hastings, co-founder. Tim Gabe quotes him: the product has to be so good people want to talk about it ([source](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=25s)).

## Business model

- Monthly subscription. A ReelShort show costs about $40 over about 80 coin unlocks, about 3 times a monthly Netflix subscription (2026-05) ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=318s)).

## Metrics

- Tim calls Netflix a $400 billion company (2026-01) ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=0s)).
- Skip Intro had 136 million presses a day by 2022 and saved users 195 years of time. Before it, 15% of viewers fast-forwarded intros by hand ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=133s), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=193s)).
- Each homepage is unique for 230 million users. About 80% of viewing comes from recommendations ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=321s)).
- ReelShort now holds users longer per day than the biggest streaming services (2026-05) ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=0s)).

## Design lessons

- **Micro-friction removal.** Streaming caused choice paralysis: users browsed about 30 minutes and gave up. Skip Intro removed a repeated annoyance, and users who skip intros binge longer ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=60s), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=193s)).
- **Algorithmic intimacy.** The 2015 personalization engine tailors each homepage ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=321s)).
- **Viral UX.** "Are you still watching?", the "ta-dum" sound, a "97% match" score and "Because you watched" rows are shareable moments ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=498s)).
- **Contrast with ReelShort.** Netflix opens with at least 6 decision points before you watch. [ReelShort](reelshort.md) plays content as soon as it opens ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=476s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=538s)).
- **Carousel pattern.** Tim teaches [Framer](framer.md) components by rebuilding a Netflix-style movie carousel with hover overlay cards ([source](../sources/13OKVxg6ecI.md), [0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=13OKVxg6ecI&t=7s), [7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=13OKVxg6ecI&t=422s)).

## Timeline

- 1997: DVD-by-mail company. 2007: streaming launch ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=60s)).
- 2015: AI personalization engine ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=321s)).
- 2022: Skip Intro at 136 million presses a day ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=193s)).
