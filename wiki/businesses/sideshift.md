SideShift is a marketplace where apps hire UGC creators, often college students, and run creator campaigns.

## What it is

- A marketplace where college students take UGC jobs ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=40s)).
- It supplies creators and campaign managers to consumer apps ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=569s)).
- An AI agent takes a budget, a website and a creator count. It then handles briefs, creator selection and spend, with help from a human campaign manager ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [38:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2317s)).
- SideShift V3 runs organic winners as paid ads. It launched the day after the 2026-09 recording ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [43:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2601s)).
- It trains its own creators with a 6-hour, Duolingo-style course ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1484s)).

## Business model

- Brands pay for creator campaigns on the platform. Top brands spend from $250K to over $700K a month there (2026-08). Cursor is a customer ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=182s), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=339s)).
- Manager pilot: a $2K to $4K one-month retainer, or about $150 per creator, plus about 5% of creator bonuses (2026-09) ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=270s)).
- Some brands do not pay their creators. SideShift then pays them, close to six figures a month in total (2026-06) ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [28:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1695s)).

## Metrics

- About 10K job seekers in late February 2025. Over 150K active job seekers 3 to 4 months later, on about $10K of spend (2025-07) ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=40s)).
- 850,000 creators, about 150,000 monthly active, and about 100 vetted campaign managers (2026-06) ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [49:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2959s)).
- About 5,000 new brands sign up each month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=520s)).
- About 30 agencies onboarded in summer 2026 ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [46:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2772s)).
- Customer results: Suno reached #1 in App Store music with about 8 creator managers. One long program made over 10,000 posts and 500M views ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=614s), [29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1740s)).
- Photogenic got about 35M views from 46 posts by one creator. Its App Store page conversion rose from 5-10% to over 50% (2025-07) ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1292s), [23:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1414s)).

## Growth channels

- High-volume organic TikTok. One campaign got about 16M to 20M views from about 300 posts in 30 days ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=176s)).
- Job market content. A talking-head video got 9.6M views at a 16% engagement rate ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1067s)). A job rejection video got about 700K likes ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [17:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1037s)).
- In-person visits to 25 college campuses brought the first 10K to 20K users ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [40:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=2445s)).

## How founders use it

- [Phoenix Do](../people/phoenix-do.md) hired 10 to 20 good UGC creators fast through SideShift ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s)).
- A $10K starter plan: recruit 2 to 3 creators through SideShift or Handshake, then grow to 5 ([source](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [37:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2225s)).
- Other creator sources sit beside it, such as JoinBrands, Tribe and an in-house Discord ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=429s), [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=627s)).
- [Max Hirsch](../people/max-hirsch.md) started a campaign to find five more daily creators (2026-10) ([source](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s)).
- [Nicole](../people/nicole.md) uses it for creator recruiting ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=773s)). [Connor Burd](../people/connor-burd.md) was setting up a campaign (2025-12) ([source](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s)).
- [Roman Lakhnyuk](../people/roman-lakhnyuk.md) uses it to find ideas: companies that hire creators have money ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=657s)).

## Playbook the founders teach

- **Pay.** Rates differ by date. $15 to $20 per video plus a base and bonuses (2025-07) ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [14:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=847s)). Top programs pay $800/month for 60 videos (2026-06) ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [39:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2391s)). $600 to $800 base for 30 to 60 videos plus a $1 to $2 CPM (2026-08) ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=472s)).
- **Staff.** 1 manager per 10 creators ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=680s)). Hire a generalist manager first, then recruiters to scale ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=223s)). See [hiring](../topics/hiring.md).
- **Split.** After a winner, put 90% of creators on it and 10% on new formats ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1242s)).
- **Organic to paid.** Boost winners as TikTok Spark or Meta partnership ads ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s), [37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2251s)).
- **Engagement threshold for paid.**
  - Nick Lawton boosts at about 5% engagement ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s)).
  - Drew Levin calls 7.5% to 8% good and kills a Spark below 5% ([source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1104s), [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s)).
  - Canyon looks for 8% to 10%+ ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3015s)).
> Conflict: [F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) puts the paid trigger at about 5% engagement. [qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) and [SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md) want 7.5% to 10% or more.
