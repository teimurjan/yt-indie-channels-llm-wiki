Copyblogger is a long-running publication that teaches internet writing, newsletters, SEO and LinkedIn writing. [Tim Stoddart](../people/tim-stoddart.md) read it daily as a beginner and later bought it.

## What it is

A media and education brand about writing for the internet: newsletters, SEO and LinkedIn writing. [zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=194s)

## Founders and owner

The source does not name the founders. Its owner is Tim Stoddart's holding company, which he funds with cash from his SEO agency [Stuzzi](stuzzi.md). [zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)

## Business model

The source does not describe Copyblogger's revenue model. It sits in a portfolio of cash-flowing assets next to [Recovery Local](recovery-local.md) and lead generation sites. [zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)

## Metrics

No Copyblogger-only numbers. The holding company expects $5.2M to $5.5M in total for 2024, and Stuzzi about $2.5M of that (2024-04). [zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)

## Why Tim bought it

- **Reinvest agency cash into cash-flowing assets.** The goal is a portfolio that does the work for him. [zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)
- **Three filters for any deal.** He must like the founder, the business must make cash, and it must be evergreen. [zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=732s)
