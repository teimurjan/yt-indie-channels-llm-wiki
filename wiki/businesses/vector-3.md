Vector 3 is an agency that makes trailers and TikTok campaigns for brands that launch on Roblox.

## What it is

- It made Roblox launch content for Nicki Minaj and SpongeBob: about 450 videos and over 100M views (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=195s)). See [Roblox](roblox.md).

## Founders

- [Jake](../people/jake.md), a friend of Roblox developers [Cole](../people/cole.md) and [Ian](../people/ian.md) in Austin, Texas ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=195s)).

## Business model

- Retainers plus project work. Paid consulting calls often turn into execution work ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)).
- A brand trailer follows a 45-step template with an SOP and a recorded video per step. The team is remote on Discord ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)).

## Metrics

- $36,150 so far in the month, $20K to $25K of it retainers. Costs $10K to $15K/month, including two full-timers at about $10K total (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)).

## Growth channels

- About 95% of clients come from LinkedIn posts of education and past work. No cold outreach ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)). See [Personal brand](../topics/personal-brand.md) and [Agencies](../topics/agencies.md).
