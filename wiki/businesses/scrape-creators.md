Scrape Creators is a credit-based API that scrapes public data from social media platforms and ad libraries.

## What it is

- It scrapes Instagram, YouTube, Twitter and ad libraries (Facebook, LinkedIn). Customers include link-in-bio tools and short-form analytics tools ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)).
- [Halo AI](../businesses/halo-ai.md) uses it to feed trending TikTok keywords to an AI script coach for its creators ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1663s)).

## Founders

- [Adrian Horning](../people/adrian-horning.md), solo. He rebuilt the concept of an Acquire.com listing instead of buying it. The listed business made $30K MRR after 3 years, all from SEO ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=190s)).

## Business model

- Credits, no subscription: $10 for 5,000, $50 for 25,000, $500 for 500,000 (2025-10) ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)).
- About 80% margin. Monthly costs: about $1,500 proxies, $500 for a night-shift developer in the Philippines, about $400 servers (2025-10) ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=666s)).

## Metrics

- About $20K/month, 600 paying customers, about 20M API requests a month. About 12 customers pay most of the revenue (2025-10) ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=80s), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)).

## Growth channels

- Adrian's Twitter presence and replies to scraping-related launch posts with 10K free credits. Not SEO ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)).
- The first customer was a CTO who commented on a post where Adrian scraped that company's site ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)).

## Timeline
