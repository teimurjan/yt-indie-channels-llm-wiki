Harpagia is a free-to-play, text-based idle RPG for iOS and Android, aimed at fans of incremental games like RuneScape.

## What it is

- A text-based idle RPG built by a solo developer with AI coding tools [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s).

## Founders

- [Kenneth Law](../people/kenneth-law.md) ("Ken"), a former engineering manager at Meta with 12 years in big tech. He built it after his first app, the card game Big Two Wild [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=221s).

## Business model

- Free to play with optional in-app purchases from $2 to $10. Players buy at $4.99 to $9.99; above $9.99, conversion drops [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s).
- Costs: about $500/month for assets, freelance art and tools. Ad spend: $0 [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s).

## Metrics

- Past year: about $13K/month on average and over $150K total; over $3 revenue per active user (2026-09) [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s).
- 3,500 to 4,000 MAU, 500 to 600 DAU, over 50,000 downloads. About 6% of players pay, against a 2% to 5% norm for games (2026-09) [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s).
- Peak: about $18K/month after more Reddit posts [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [8:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=537s).

## Growth channels

- Reddit. The first post, six months after launch, drew a harsh thread whose debate brought thousands of players. Revenue went from about $1K to $10K/month almost overnight. He now posts only major updates every few months, within subreddit self-promotion rules [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=394s), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=478s).

## Timeline

- Early 2024: Ken builds Big Two Wild in about six weeks [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=221s).
- Launch: first $10 sale within days, about $150 in month one, about $1,500 in month two [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=370s), [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=394s).
- Six months after launch: the Reddit post lifts revenue to $10K/month, his quit number, and he leaves Meta that month [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=394s).
- 2026-09: about $13K/month average over the past year [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s).
