The Pathless Path is a self-published book by Paul Millerd that earns more than half of his income.

## What it is

- A book about leaving the default career path. Paul turned down Penguin Random House to keep creative control and higher royalties [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=0s).

## Founders

- [Paul Millerd](../people/paul-millerd.md). He quit a $250K consulting job in New York in 2017 at 32 and had to repay a $24,000 signing bonus and relocation costs [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=149s), [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=174s).

## Business model

- Self-published through Amazon KDP and IngramSpark. Paul makes about $7 per paperback and $8 per hardcover. A traditional publisher pays about $2 per hardcover [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=240s).
- Amazon royalties per unit: $8.24 hardcover, $7.45 paperback, $3.98 audiobook, $4.28 Kindle. Sales mix: 50% ebook, 29% print, 21% audio (2023-12) [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=551s).
- Costs: editing and coaching $3,900, cover $742, IngramSpark $250 and audiobook $1,280 [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=577s).
- The book sits next to a course, workshops and some coaching [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=604s).

## Metrics

- Year one: about 10,000 copies and about $50,000 in royalties (2023-12) [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s).
- Year two, first five months: about 15,000 copies and about $75,000 (2023-12) [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s).
- Royalties to date, almost all profit: $125K to $130K (2023-12) [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=577s).
- Paul's total revenue grew from $100K to $249K in one year (2023-12) [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s).

## Growth channels

- Podcasts and gifted books. A gift costs $4.50 with US shipping [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=480s), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=540s).
- Years of daily writing: 100 days on Quora, then a weekly blog [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s).

## Timeline

- 2017: Paul quits his job [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=0s).
- Writing cycles: a month or two of intense writing, then a step back to fix structure, repeated. Upload a PDF and books ship in about 3 days [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=276s).
- 2023-12: about 25,000 copies sold in about 17 months [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s).
