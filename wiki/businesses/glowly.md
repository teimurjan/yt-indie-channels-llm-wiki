Glowly is a looksmaxing face-scan iOS app that designer Will Baker grows with AI-made TikTok ads.

## What it is

- A face-scan app in the looksmaxing category. Its viral feature explains itself in one screenshot and sells a transformation ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=380s)).

## Founders

- [Will Baker](../people/will-baker.md), a UK designer who also runs [Overlow](overlow.md) ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s)).

## Business model

- Subscription via RevenueCat, with AppsFlyer for attribution ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=499s)).
- Onboarding of 12 to 15 steps ends in a personal insight before the paywall ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=405s)).
- Built to sell to an operator who can scale it with UGC ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=220s)).

## Metrics

- About $10K/month (2026-08) ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s)).
- About $16 per subscription on TikTok; LTV about $30 to $35 per paying user (2026-08) ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s)).

## Growth channels

- **[TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md), not UGC armies.** 10 to 20 AI-made variants per format. Native formats, no app in the first 3 to 4 seconds ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s), [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s)).
- Spend on a winner rises 20% every 3 days until it caps out. One batch of winners can carry the app for 4 to 5 months ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s)).

## Timeline
