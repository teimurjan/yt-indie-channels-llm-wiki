AudioPen is an app that turns a voice note into clean text in a chosen style. Its founder built the MVP in about 12 hours.

## What it is

- A one-feature voice-to-text writing tool. Prime users get about 15 minutes per recording ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=563s)).

## Founders

- [Louis Pereira](../people/louis-pereira.md), who works full-time in his family business in India and builds apps part-time ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [0:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=20s)).

## Business model

- $99/year or $159 for 2 years, as non-recurring plans that users choose to renew, plus a free tier (2025-12) ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=120s)).
- No-code stack: Bubble (about $130/month), Xano (about $260/month), Loops (about $800/month), Plausible ($19/month), Draftbit (about $300/year) (2025-12) ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=690s)).

## Metrics

- About $15K/month, about 200,000 users and over 5,000 paying customers (2025-12) ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=104s), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=120s)).

## Growth channels

- **Build in public.** Louis had posted on Twitter for months to years, so he had credibility and hype at launch ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=426s)).
- **Half Day Build.** His own Twitter hackathon community cheered the launch ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s)).
- **Indie voice.** Users like being part of his story ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=526s)).

## Timeline across sources

- 2015: first try at no-code. 2021: back with Bubble, 15 to 20 failed tools before AudioPen ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=171s)).
- 2022: Half Day Build starts (idea to $1 between noon and midnight on a Sunday). AudioPen was one of 4 or 5 tools he built that week. He designed it in Figma first, opened a waitlist about 10 hours in, and testers paid via Stripe before the 12 hours ended ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=206s), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s)).
- 2025-12: about $15K/month as a focused one-feature product. He resists adjacent markets ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=628s)).
