Savewise is a tool that helps people stack credit card points, airline miles and cashback on online purchases.

## What it is

- A deal-stacking tool for points and cashback users ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=4s)).

## Founders

- [Avneesh](../people/avneesh.md), formerly at Microsoft, Instagram, Foursquare and Dropbox ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=94s)).

## Business model

- About 97% of revenue comes from a [lifetime plan](../topics/lifetime-deals.md); users asked for it on launch day (2025-08). Avneesh: "consumers hate subscriptions" ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=678s)).

## Metrics

- About $25K/month after 15 months, $0 spent on marketing (2025-08) ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=4s), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=64s)).
- Tens of thousands of users, about 1,500 paying (2025-08) ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=678s)).

## Growth channels

- **Not launch sites.** Product Hunt, Hacker News and Indie Hackers traffic bounced at 95% to 96%; 300 to 400 influencer emails got one reply ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=146s)).
- **[Reddit](../topics/reddit-marketing.md) and Facebook groups where users already gather**: lurk, help first, use F5Bot alerts ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=222s), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=367s)).
- **Free data asset.** A sheet of Rakuten and Amex offers in a Facebook group sent over 1,500 visitors ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=531s)).
- **Weekly Q&A threads, then a mod-approved post** after about 3.5 months; it took him from thousands to tens of thousands of users ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=599s), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=4s)).

## Timeline

- Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News and Indie Hackers, which failed ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=146s)).
- 2025-08: about $25K/month after 15 months ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=4s)).
