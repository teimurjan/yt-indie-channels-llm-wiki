Blue Throne is a firm that buys consumer apps for seven and eight figures and scales them.

## What it is

- An app aggregator. It buys apps that make about $400K to $15M a year (2026-07) ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [41:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2509s)).
- As an aggregator, it looks for growth it can 10x ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [35:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2118s)).

## Founders

- [Josh](../people/josh.md) heads M&A. Before that he spent 3 years buying game studios for Plarium, maker of Raid: Shadow Legends ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Buy apps, then grow them with in-house teams ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2799s)). See [acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).

## Metrics

- 70 staff in R&D, QA, growth, monetization, finance and HR (2026-07) ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2799s)).

## What Blue Throne looks for

- **A narrow category the app owns,** as Flo owns period tracking ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=560s)).
- **Retained users.** Year-one resubscribe of 50% is amazing, 40% good, 30% okay and 20% bad ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s)).
- **Organic installs.** A 50% organic share is great. Under 20% means a UA treadmill. Josh counts TikTok UGC as paid ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1216s), [21:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1287s)).
- **Premium prices.** For $30M ARR you need about 500K payers at $4.99/month, or about 125K at $20/month ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [25:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1538s)). See [pricing](../topics/pricing.md).
- **Valuation.** A fast-growing app with 30% to 50% EBITDA margin can get up to 6x run-rate revenue. A slower one is valued on the last 12 months (2026-07) ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=373s)).
