Pingo is an AI voice companion app for speaking practice in about 20 languages, known for viral videos where the AI roasts learners.

## What it is

- Users talk out loud with an AI voice to practice a language ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=380s)).
- A custom scenario feature lets users set the AI's role. Creators prompt Pingo to be mean, and users now ask for a "mean Pingo" toggle ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=737s), [13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=798s)).

## Founders

- [Morrie Schonfeld](../people/morrie-schonfeld.md) and his college roommate Michael. They launched it in late December 2024 from a Northwestern dorm ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=135s)).
- The team was two people until mid-October 2025. Then it grew to four, with one content hire and one engineer ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [40:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2404s)).
- Pingo went through Y Combinator. After YC, the founders booked about 100 investor meetings and closed a round in about 3 days ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [55:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3301s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions with a free trial. The annual plan costs $99.99/year, includes the trial and shows a weekly price. The monthly plan has no trial and sits behind "view all plans" ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [46:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2813s), [49:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2987s)).
- The winning paywall shows a trial timeline with "reminded 2 days before trial ends" ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2716s)).
- In the US, 3-day and 7-day trials convert within 0.3 to 0.7 points of each other. The 3-day trial has a 5% to 7% higher cancel rate. Abroad, the 7-day trial converts about 2 points higher ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [52:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3164s)).
- Users who abandon a 3-day trial and then get a 7-day offer convert higher ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [50:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3048s)).
- US billing is moving to Stripe web checkout for better margins ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [47:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2866s)).

## Metrics

- About $450K to $500K/month, 14 months after launch (2026-04) ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=135s)).
- 350M to 400M views in 30 days and about 3M users (2026-04) ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=196s), [33:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1988s)).
- $500K/month and over 1B views with one format (2026-09) ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=617s), [11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=679s)).
- About 200K Russian users in February brought $0, because app stores block payments there ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=98s), [28:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1684s)).

## Growth channels

- **The roast format.** A creator tries to pronounce a word, fails, and the AI roasts them until they cry. The top roast video got 52M views ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=44s)). [Nic Weber](../people/nic-weber.md) says it reached 20M views overnight ([source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=681s)).
- **Product as the main character.** The roast videos went viral. They also sold the product ([source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=92s)). Viral videos without Pingo in them do not convert ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=90s)).
- **Referential concepting.** [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) cites Pingo as an example of moving a whole concept from another niche into yours ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=617s)).
- **Creator program.** The founders made every video themselves until $20K MRR, for about 3 months. Then about 100 creators posted, after 30 to 50 were cut. The top 20% to 30% drive most views ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1105s), [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1484s)).
- **Creator pay.** A base per video plus a tier bonus at 50K, 100K, 200K, 500K and 1M views. Only the highest tier pays. Creators on [Noise](../businesses/noise.md) run on a capped CPM ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [34:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2040s)).
- **Recruiting.** VAs DM micro creators with under 30K followers and at least one breakout video. They search "learning German" first, then college majors, then native speakers ([source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [25:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1528s), [32:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1926s)).

## Timeline
