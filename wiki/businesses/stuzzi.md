Stuzzi is an SEO lead generation agency for addiction treatment centers and other clients.

## What it is

- Stuzzi Internet Marketing grew out of Tim Stoddart's blog Sober Nation, after rehab centers asked how he built the audience [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s).

## Founders

- [Tim Stoddart](../people/tim-stoddart.md). He hired early staff from halfway houses, and the team has grown together over 10 years [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=670s).

## Business model

- Agency retainers. The first client paid $2,000/month. It needs no capital and is profitable from the first client, but every 2 to 4 new clients need another hire [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s).
- Agency cash funds a holding company: [Recovery Local](recovery-local.md), [Copyblogger](copyblogger.md) and stakes in other companies [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s).

## Metrics

- About $2.5M expected for 2024; the holding company $5.2M to $5.5M in total (2024-04) [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s).

## Growth channels

- SEO, one keyword at a time: "drug rehab marketing" started the agency, then "drug rehab SEO" and "drug rehab lead generation" [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s).
- A three-step close: get the prospect to state the pain, raise price and commitment early, then ask "Are you ready to get started?" and wait [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=419s).

## Timeline

- Sober Nation blog, started on Seth Godin's advice, becomes a lead gen outlet [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s).
- First agency client at $2,000/month [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s).
- 2024-04: about $2.5M expected for the year; cash reinvested into Copyblogger and other assets [source](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s).
