# Sheets & Giggles

Sheets & Giggles is a direct-to-consumer bedding brand founded by [Colin McIntosh](../people/colin-mcintosh.md).

## What it is

- A bedding brand that sells sheets and, since late 2022, a mattress. The catalog grew from 5 colors and 5 sizes to 17 colors, 7 sizes and hundreds of SKUs ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=132s)).
- The brand voice is playful and polarizing: "Be something for someone." One rule: never iron the sheets ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=549s)).

## Founders

- [Colin McIntosh](../people/colin-mcintosh.md), a former recruiter. He was fired twice and laid off once before he started it ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md)). He picked the business model first and the product second ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=213s)).
- Colin later started [Sheets Resume](sheets-resume.md) as a side project ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md)).

## Business model

- Ecommerce. About 80% to 90% of sales come from the Shopify site and the rest from Amazon (2024-05) ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=72s)).
- Upsells through Rebuy (in-cart) and AfterSell (post-purchase). Reviews through Okendo ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=644s)).
- About 40 people touch the business daily, with agencies for Amazon ads, performance marketing and affiliates (2024-05) ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=728s)).

## Metrics

- Pre-launch list: 11,000 emails at a 46% capture rate, for a $100 set offered at $69 ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=360s)).
- Crowdfunding launch: 450 customers and $45K on day one at about $100 AOV, $284K over the campaign ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s)).
- First $1M month: about $1.2M in November 2020 ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=72s)).
- About 100 mattresses shipped a day (2024-05) ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=132s)).
- A SiteGPT chatbot at about $50/month cut support tickets by about 30% (2024-05) ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=644s)).

## Growth channels

- **Waitlist plus survey.** Sign-ups locked in a launch price. Colin then surveyed the list on colors and price, so people felt they helped build the product ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=300s)).
- **Crowdfunding.** Plan for 30% of the goal on day one. Lists convert at about 3%, so a $100K goal needs about 10,000 emails ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s)).
- **Podcast and YouTube sponsorships.** The surprise winner. Each sponsorship gets a landing page in that audience's voice, and those pages convert far better than the rest of the site ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=488s)).
- **Paid ads.** Facebook, Instagram, Google and Amazon ads also work ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=488s)).
- **Personal contact.** For years Colin answered every comment and support email himself ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=460s)).
