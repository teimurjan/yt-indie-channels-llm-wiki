ScreenshotOne is an API that automates website screenshots for developers.

## What it is

- An API that renders screenshots with headless browsers. It is built in TypeScript and Puppeteer, with Go for rate limits and API keys and Cloudflare for storage ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=577s)).
- It is for developers. Some users expected no-code, so the copy now says so clearly ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)).

## Founders

- [Dmytro Krasun](../people/dmytro-krasun.md), a former backend developer. He kept only API ideas because building APIs was his strongest skill ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=134s)).

## Business model

- Plans start around $17/month, with extra usage billed on top (2024-11) ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=98s)).
- He raised prices and cut the free plan, because margins at $5 to $7 were near zero ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)).

## Metrics

- 280 customers and about $12,000 MRR (2024-11) ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=98s)).
- Monthly churn fell from about 11% to close to 7%, with a target under 5% (2024-11) ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)).
- About 2M screenshots a month, $3K to $4K for servers, about $4,500/month total costs and a 40% to 60% margin (2024-11) ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=641s)).

## Growth channels

- **Twitter and Google** are the main channels ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md)).
- **Integration marketplaces.** Listings on Zapier and Make bring users who automate their business. Product Hunt helped awareness and SEO ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=357s)).
- **YouTube tutorials.** A third party's cold-email tutorial that used the product brought paying users ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=357s)).
- **Churn interviews by email.** He studied each churned company, guessed the reason, and asked one yes/no question. Close to 100% replied ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=424s)).
