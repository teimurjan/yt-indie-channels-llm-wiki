Overlow is an iOS fitness app with a body scan, run by designer Will Baker alongside his face-scan app Glowly.

## What it is

- A fitness app built around a body scan [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s).
- Will only builds apps with a viral feature that explains itself in one screenshot and sells a transformation [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=380s).

## Founders

- [Will Baker](../people/will-baker.md), a UK designer who also runs [Glowly](../businesses/glowly.md). He clones proven categories with better design [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=341s).

## Business model

- Subscriptions behind a [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) after a 12 to 15 step [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md) that ends in a personal insight [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=405s).
- Will builds apps to sell to operators who can scale them with UGC [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=220s).

## Metrics

- About $800/month, passive (2026-08) [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s).

## Growth channels

- **AI-made [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md).** Will picks paid ads over UGC for clear numbers and little ongoing work. He makes 10 to 20 AI variants of one format and raises spend on a winner by 20% every 3 days [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s), [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s).
- Across his apps, a TikTok subscription campaign costs about $16 per subscription against $30 to $35 LTV (2026-08) [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s).
