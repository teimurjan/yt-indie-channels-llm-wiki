Robinhood is a stock and crypto trading app. In these sources it is a design case study, for its trade confetti and its community bet.

## What it is

- A trading app. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls trading lonely and anxious, which drives churn ([source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=592s)).

## Founders

The sources do not name the founders. [Daniel Heintzman](../people/daniel-heintzman.md) worked there as a designer on the Robinhood Crypto trade flow ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [31:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1909s)).

## Metrics and events

- 2016: added full-screen confetti after a trade. March 2021: removed it ([source](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=331s)).
- 2024-01: paid a $7.5M fine to Massachusetts regulators. The consent order bans celebratory imagery tied to trading frequency ([source](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=331s)).
- Robinhood Crypto went from idea to launch within about a month and went viral ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [30:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1850s)).
- 2025: announced Robinhood Social at Hood Summit. Users share live, verified trades and follow traders. The rollout is small and invite-only (2026-04) ([source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=532s), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=592s)).

## Lessons the sources draw

- **Celebration changes behavior.** Regulators banned the confetti, which shows how strong reward ceremonies are ([source](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=331s)).
- **Community as a moat against agents.** An AI agent can trade for you, so a pure trading tool has almost no defense. Robinhood Social focuses on learning and discussion, not copy trading ([source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=592s)).
- **Ship fast.** Daniel says a whole company that rallies behind one project can move as fast as a startup ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [30:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1850s)).
