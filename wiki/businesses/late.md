Late is a social media API for developers that wraps the official platform APIs into one, grown only through Google.

## What it is

- One API over the official Facebook, Instagram, Twitter and LinkedIn APIs, with no limits [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=0s).
- For a developer API, the team spends the most time on clear docs [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=882s).

## Founders

- [Miquel Palet](../people/miquel-palet.md) ("Mickey"), from Spain. He spent 5 years on startups before, including a VC-backed edtech company with over 100 employees [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=176s).

## Business model

- Priced per connected account, in three plans: Build, Accelerate and Unlimited [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=96s).
- Profitable from day one [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=96s).

## Metrics

- $40K MRR about 7 months after launch (2026-03) [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=96s).
- 50K total signups, over 700 paying users and churn under 10% (2026-03) [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=156s).
- Organic SEO brings over $8K MRR (2026-03) [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=472s).
- Google Search Ads: about $8K/month spend, $2.50 revenue per $1, about $15K MRR and a $120 target CPA (2026-03) [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=548s).

> Conflict: [E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) gives two figures in one video. At [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=72s) Mickey says $30K MRR in 7 months. At [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=96s) he says $40K MRR.

## Growth channels

- **Google only, no social media.** Searchers for "social media scheduling API" have about 10x the purchase intent of social traffic [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s).
- **One page, two jobs.** Pages that rank get free traffic. Ads run on pages that do not rank [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=381s).
- **Organic:** bottom-of-funnel keywords with 300 to 800 monthly searches and low competition, in-depth guides and backlinks [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=411s).
- **Paid:** the same keywords, one landing page per ad group, automated bidding with a target CPA, and constant A/B tests [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s).
- **Unit economics rule:** keep CAC at 20% to 40% of LTV and under 30% of first-year revenue [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=789s).
