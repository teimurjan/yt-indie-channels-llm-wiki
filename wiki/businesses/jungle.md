Jungle is an AI study app that turns PDFs, slides and videos into quizzes and flashcards, with a game layer.

## What it is

- Users upload study material and get quizzes and flashcards. A Duolingo-style game layer sits on top ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=188s)).
- A growing tree raised core engagement (questions answered) by 70% ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s)). See [gamification](../topics/gamification.md).

## Founders

- [Julian Alvarez](../people/julian-alvarez.md) is the technical co-founder. His co-founder David does design and product ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s)).

## Business model

- Freemium. Users keep studying for free and convert later ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [20:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1217s)).
- The paywall sits at the point that the most users reach. Onboarding runs in three phases ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1056s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).

## Metrics

- About $80K to $100K/month (2025-11) ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=188s)).
- Word of mouth brings 30% to 40% of new users (2025-11) ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=837s)).
- About 30 UGC creators post close to 400 videos a week at about $2 CPM (2025-11) ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [21:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1308s)).

## Growth channels

- **A viral tweet.** A comment on an "AI flashcards" tweet brought over 200 downloads. The first 100 users came from DMs to everyone who engaged ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=198s)).
- **AI tool directories** such as Future Tools helped early ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=269s)).
- **Micro-influencers.** One video took MRR from $2K to almost $15K in 1 to 2 weeks ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=670s)).
- **Use minimum view clauses.** Later deals lost money without them ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=789s)).
- **UGC program.** Julian calls it a temporary growth hack ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1405s)). See [UGC](../topics/ugc.md).
- **Visible game elements.** Students in libraries see the tree on other screens ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s)).

## Timeline

- Before Jungle: 4 to 5 failed pivots and 3 months of runway ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=198s)).
- Launch: two weeks of distribution only, by a mentor's rule ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s)).
- Breakout: a medical student creator took MRR from $2K to almost $15K ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=670s)).
- 2025-11: about $80K to $100K/month ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=188s)).
