Floga is a mobile app for yoga teachers and practitioners that pre-launched with a lifetime deal and made over $120K in 24 hours.

## What it is

- A yoga app built in Flutter on Firebase, RevenueCat, Vimeo and OneSignal ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1134s)).

## Founders

- [Umberto Mezzadra](../people/umberto-mezzadra.md), not a developer. He hired one a few months after he sketched the app ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=223s)).
- He learned launches with Play Pause Be, a yoga card deck brand whose first Kickstarter raised over $200K in its first month ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=166s)).

## Business model

- Pre-launch [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) in three tiers: about $109, about $199 and $349 for the full roadmap ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=838s)).
- Then subscriptions: monthly, quarterly (web only) and yearly ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=116s)).

## Metrics

- $117K by the end of launch day and over $120K in 24 hours, from about 500 to 600 lifetime buyers (2025-05) ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=83s), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s)).
- About 4,000 active users and $9K to $10K/month (2026-03) ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=116s)).

## Growth channels

- **Five-week curiosity email sequence.** Story first, then a hidden new item, then the app "behind the red curtain" with a demo video. The emails doubled as blog posts ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=516s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=578s)).
- **Hidden price until launch day**, limited spots and a 5 to 7 day window ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=638s), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=899s)).
- **Honest demo, no refunds.** A video showed every current feature and the roadmap ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s)).
- **LTD buyers as a feedback group** in Telegram. Many later features came from them ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s)).
