Postiz is an open-source social media scheduler. In 2026 it became an "agentic" scheduler that AI agents drive.

## What it is

- It schedules posts to 25 platforms (2026-01), later 30+ (2026-07). [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md)
- The paid cloud product is the same code as the open-source project. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md)
- After the pivot, agents such as Claude, OpenClaw and ChatGPT drive it through its public API. [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=430s)

## Founder

[Nevo](../people/nevo.md) built it alone. Before Postiz, he spent 2 years at an open-source notification startup that grew from 0 to 32,000 GitHub stars. Then he did open-source consulting for about a year. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=246s)

## Business model

- Subscription tiers: Standard, Team, Pro and Ultimate. They differ by channel count and features. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=145s)
- Self-hosting acts as a free tier. Developers rarely pay, but they bring word of mouth, bug reports, blog posts and directory links. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=454s)
- Margin is about 80%. AI image and text generation is the biggest cost (2026-01). [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [19:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=1155s)

## Metrics

- $17K MRR from 472 subscribers, and 21% trial-to-paid conversion on 3,830 trials since August 2025 (2026-01). [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=145s)
- Over 25K GitHub stars and 5M downloads (2026-01). [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=14s)
- MRR posted on X: $41K (2026-02), $76K (2026-03), $80K (2026-04), $109K (2026-05), $140K (late 2026-06). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=26s)
- $143K MRR and 4,271 paying subscribers (2026-07). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=82s), [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=148s)
- Monthly churn: 19% (2026-01). [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=145s). After the pivot it fell to 13.7% (2026-07). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=148s)

> Conflict: [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) gives monthly churn of 19% (2026-01). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) says churn was 26% to 28% before the pivot.

## Growth channels

- **Open-source launch in one week.** Treat the README as the landing page. Write articles on dev.to and Medium. Post a Show HN that links to GitHub, and post in r/selfhosted. Push all other traffic to GitHub. The goal is GitHub trending. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=635s), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=818s), [15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=934s)
- **Front page of Hacker News.** It brings about 10,000 views. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=818s)
- **SEO.** Outrank writes one SEO article per day. Dub.co handles short links and affiliates. [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) [19:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=1155s)
- **Other people's viral articles.** Oliver Henry's X article on running TikTok with OpenClaw and Postiz got 7.2M views and brought about 700 trials a day. [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=199s)
- **Own X articles plus paid reposts.** Nevo had 200 followers, but his article still got about 500,000 views. Each article gets about 20 paid reposters at $60 to $350 each (2026-07). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=328s), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=390s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=549s)
- **Agent marketplaces.** Turn the API into a markdown file for agents and list it in Claude and ChatGPT marketplaces. [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=430s)
