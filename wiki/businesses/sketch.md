Sketch is a UI design tool. The sources mention it as a peer of Figma and as an example of a good landing page.

## What it is

- A design tool in the same class as Figma. A designer who knows Sketch or Figma feels at home in Framer after a couple of hours (2023-05) ([NT8w5ZS13ao](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=43s)).

## Website

- Visitors can drag real interface parts on the landing page, like a small prototype of the tool ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls it "such a smart way to show your product on the landing page" ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s)).
- Small hover effects on tabs make the page a pleasure to scroll ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=260s)).
- Weak spot: on mobile, the container overflows and scrolls sideways (2023-03) ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=322s)).
