Duolingo is a language-learning app. No source features it directly. Founders in this wiki use it as the main reference for streaks, mascots and emotional design.

## What it is

Duolingo teaches languages through short, game-like lessons with a mascot owl, streaks and gems. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s), [9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md) [20:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1233s)

## Founders

Luis von Ahn is the CEO. He names the character animations as one major reason for engagement. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=185s)

## Business model

- Freemium with paid subscriptions. Gems act as an in-app currency. [9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md) [20:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1233s)
- Koby Conrad of [Sunflower](sunflower.md) says Duolingo spent "like six or seven years" before it started to charge. He copied the free-first approach. [zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s)

## Metrics

- In 2022, Duolingo added a full character animation system with facial reactions, lip sync and idle animations. Daily active users then grew from 14.2M to over 34M within 2 years, and paid subscribers more than doubled (reported 2025-05). New languages, a better curriculum and marketing also drove the growth. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=185s)
- The streak freeze comes from a University of Pennsylvania and UCLA study on "slack" in goals. A rise from 1 to 2 freezes per user lifted daily active learners by 0.38% (reported 2026-09). [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=264s), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=326s)

## Growth channels and product tactics

- **Emotional design.** Duolingo celebrates progress, makes users feel guilty for missed days and builds emotional investment. [AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md) [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=504s). Its screens join into one flow that is a pleasure to use. [86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=830s)
- **Interactive animation with Rive.** Duolingo uses Rive for its interactive animations. [wmTkiF23GRQ](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=600s)
- **Streaks with agency.** Users pick a goal level and can buy a streak freeze. A streak with no pause or exit is the version that regulators watch. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=410s)
- **Mass creator accounts.** Big brands such as Duolingo now run mass creator accounts on short-form video (2025-10). [kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s)
- **Years of onboarding tests.** Pat Walls notes that apps like Duolingo test their onboarding for years, so new apps copy their layouts. [PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md) [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s)

## How other apps copy it

- [Cal AI](cal-ai.md) took streaks from Duolingo for retention. [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1621s)
- [Brawler](brawler.md) copies the style of Duolingo's daily notifications. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s)
- [Jungle](jungle.md) puts a Duolingo-style game layer on AI quizzes and flashcards. Students see the growing tree on each other's screens in libraries. [zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md) [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s)
- [Shepherd](shepherd.md) is a Duolingo-style Bible app. It launched while Duolingo faced backlash over AI content, with the line "God first lamb over the AI first owl". [2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s)
- Freecash got a diamond streak system from [Zipsnap](zipsnap.md). Tim Gabe says a plain Duolingo streak is "decoration" next to a full progression system. [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=451s), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=815s)
