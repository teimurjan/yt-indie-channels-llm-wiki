Symmetry is a gym tracking app for the Spanish-speaking market in Spain and Mexico.

## What it is

- A workout logging app with social features: ranks per muscle group and exercise, a Strava-like feed, shared streaks and levels [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=821s).

## Founders

- [Mauro Botanes](../people/mauro-botanes.md), 22. The founders were fitness YouTubers before [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=0s), [14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=878s).

## Business model

- Subscription app with Superwall paywall tests and RevenueCat. Creators get nearly half of revenue [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s), [13:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=784s).
- ARPU by country sets how much they can pay creators per download [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=539s).

## Metrics

- About $160K/month over the last 30 days and about 3M downloads (2026-09) [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=106s).
- UGC in the last year: nearly 80,000 videos, over 500 accounts and nearly 8B views. One video reached 17M views (2026-09) [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s).
- A commitment screen at the end of onboarding raised retention 3%. Two home screen redesigns moved nothing [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s).

## Growth channels

- **UGC at scale.** A YouTube community and influencer marketing both failed. UGC worked [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s).
- **Track formats.** A top format shows a breakup text on a lock screen, then a Symmetry notification that says "go to the gym" [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s).
- **Test high-traffic screens** such as the paywall and exercise search, not a theme toggle that touches 2% of users [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=361s).
- **Experiment loop.** Start from an interview problem, write hypotheses and guardrails, and run tests at least 2 weeks [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=663s).
