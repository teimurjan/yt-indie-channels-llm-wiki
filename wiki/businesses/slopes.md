## Business model

- Subscriptions with a free trial ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=259s)).

## Metrics

- A one-tap trial start gave +25% trial starts, as [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) reports it (2025-07) ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=259s)).

## Growth channels

- **Fewer steps to a trial.** Tim's lesson: each extra step before the trial costs users and revenue ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=259s)).
