DesignJoy is Brett Williams's one-person design studio that sells unlimited design requests as a monthly subscription.

## What it is

- A [productized service](../topics/productized-services.md): fixed monthly packages with unlimited requests, no hourly rates and no proposals ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s)).
- It covers the work Brett does fastest: branding, product design and landing pages ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=250s)).
- Delivery is async. Each client gets a Trello board and can have one active request at a time. There are no calls or meetings ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=85s), [source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s)).

## Founders

- [Brett Williams](../people/brett-williams.md), a self-taught designer. He was a 9-to-5 graphic designer before DesignJoy ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s)).
- He copied an agency that sold graphic design this way and applied it to web and product design ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=126s)).

## Business model

- Price path: $449/month at launch, then $1,000, $3,000 and $5,000. Some clients pay about $8,000/month (2024-01) ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=127s), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=187s)).
- Clients buy directly on the website, with no sales calls ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s)).
- Clients compare it with a $200K/year in-house designer or a $30K to $40K agency project ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=342s)).
- Running costs: $176/month, with Shutterstock the biggest at $100. Marketing spend is zero (2023-05) ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=292s), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)).
- Info products such as the course Productize Yourself are 29% of Brett's income (2024-01) ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=629s)). See [info products](../topics/info-products.md).

## Metrics

- About $1.3M/year, about 20 clients at once, plans at about $5,000/month (2023-05) ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=54s), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=85s)).
- About $1.8M to over $2M a year, with zero employees and about 6 working hours a day (2024-01) ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- Product Hunt brought the first clients. Then community posts and [build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)).
- Twitter became the main lead source about a year before 2023-05 ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)). See [personal brand](../topics/personal-brand.md).

## Timeline

- Launch: a one-page Webflow site plus a Trello board.
> Conflict: [dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md) at [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=126s) says he built it in 6 to 7 hours on one Saturday. [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) at [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s) says 12 hours for $29.
- The first paying client came within 24 hours ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=114s)).
- He ran it beside a full-time job for 3.5 years and quit at $80K/month. Revenue doubled the month after ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=179s), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=611s)).
- Before strict boundaries he worked 16-hour days and was close to burnout ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s)).
- 2023-05: about $1.3M/year. 2024-01: about $1.8M to $2M/year.
