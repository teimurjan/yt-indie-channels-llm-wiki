Posted is Steven Cravotta's creator marketplace: creators submit videos to a brief, and the brand pays only for the ones it approves.

## What it is

- A UGC marketplace for app marketers. A brand posts a brief, creators submit videos, and the brand pays only for approved ones ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md)).

## Founders

- [Steven Cravotta](../people/steven-cravotta.md), who built it after selling [Puff Count](puff-count.md) ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=78s)).

## Metrics

- Creator prices in the demo: $40 to $110 a video. A 552K-follower creator charged $110 and got 30K views. A 768-follower creator got 53K views (2025-09) ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=917s)).
- The demo shows that follower count does not predict views.
