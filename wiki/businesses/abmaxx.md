AbMaxx is a consumer fitness app that gives users a custom abs plan and an "ab scan".

## What it is

- A niche abs app built on the old "abs for summer" promise. The target user is 15 to 30 [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=208s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).

## Founders

- [Lino Leighton](../people/lino-leighton.md), 18 and not technical, built it as his first app. A large fitness influencer joined as an equity partner [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=10s), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s).
- [George](../people/george.md) lists "AbMax" as one of his three apps, next to [Wrestle AI](wrestle-ai.md) and [Brawler](brawler.md) [source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=788s).

> Conflict: [RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) presents AbMaxx as Lino Leighton's app. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) presents AbMax as one of George's apps. Neither source names the other founder.

## Business model

- Subscription app. Stack: Lovable for V1, then Rork for V2, Supabase, PostHog and RevenueCat [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s), [5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=329s).

## Metrics

- First month after launch: just over $7K revenue [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s).
- About $55K in the current month, on pace for $60K. 16,000 downloads in 28 days. Just over 3,000 paying users (2026-10) [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=105s).
- One stitch ad has had over $20K in spend and returned close to $60K over 3 months. TikTok CPA is about $15. On Meta, a $24 CPA is close to 2x ROAS (2026-10) [source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=788s), [15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=927s).

## Growth channels

- Early big UGC deals and faceless TikTok slideshows reached $3K to $4K/month at 5% to 10% margins [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s).
- Paid ads behind the equity creator's content: Meta at 2x to 2.5x ROAS, the same creatives on TikTok at 4x to 5x ROAS (2026-10) [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).
- Stitch and reaction ads, plus clipping at about a $0.30 CPM for awareness [source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=788s), [34:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2089s).

## Timeline

- February (2026): V1 built in Lovable over 2 months, then rebuilt in Rork in 1 month [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s).
- At $10K/month: dropped all UGC and influencer deals and went all in on Meta ads [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).
- 2026-10: on pace for $60K/month, about 5 months after launch [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=105s).
