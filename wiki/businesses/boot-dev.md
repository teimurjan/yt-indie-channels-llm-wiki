# Boot.dev

Boot.dev is a gamified, interactive platform for learning back-end development.

## What it is

- About 30 courses for back-end learners, a market that front-end-heavy rivals ignore (2025-04) ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=96s), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=127s)).
- Stack: Go, Postgres, Google Cloud, Vue, Nuxt, PostHog, Stripe ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=724s)).

## Founders

- [Lane Wagner](../people/lane-wagner.md). He started it as a side project while managing a Go team, after Go job posts got only 5 to 7 applicants ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [4:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=255s)).

## Business model

- All content is free. Members pay for interactivity, which works as a long free trial ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=666s)).
- $330K angel funding from a former CFO and his partner ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=226s)).

## Metrics

- Almost $1M/month, about $30K/day (2025-04) ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=0s)).
- 25,332 active paying members (2025-04) ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=96s)).
- 2024: $5.7M revenue, $300K COGS, $600K to $700K salaries and contractors, almost $2M marketing, about $2.5M profit ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=772s)).

## Growth channels

- $0 to $2K/month: Lane's blog ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=481s)).
- $10K to $30K/month: free 8-hour courses on freeCodeCamp's YouTube channel ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=481s)).
- Past $30K/month: paid YouTube integrations, mostly on gaming channels, not coding channels. They find creators by asking users who they watch ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=541s), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=575s)).
