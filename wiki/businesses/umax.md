Umax is an AI looksmaxxing app for men that scores facial structure and gives feedback on appearance.

## What it is

- A looksmaxxing app for guys. It gives scores for facial structure, such as jawlines ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=431s), [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s)).
- It rates the user's photos and gives feedback ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1047s)).

## Founders

- [Blake Anderson](../people/blake-anderson.md) came up with the idea, designed and built the app, and led most of the marketing. It was his second app after [Rizz GPT](rizz-gpt.md) ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=69s)).
- Umax's founder was in the H0 accelerator cohort with the co-founder of [Glam Up](glam-up.md) ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=431s)).

## Business model

- A subscription AI app sold to a niche that already spends money. People spent on skin care, hair care and gua sha, but no software solved the problem ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=459s)).

## Metrics

- Almost $5M in revenue in the past year (2024-12) ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=69s)).
- A little over $100K profit per month, after influencer marketing, Apple's cut and servers. Blake says it used to make more (2024-12) ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=878s)).

## Growth channels

- Blake markets his apps with influencers and in-house [UGC](../topics/ugc.md) ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=69s)). See [influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md).

## Influence on other apps

- [Glam Up](glam-up.md) started as "Umax for women", based on makeup instead of facial scores ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=264s), [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s)).
- [Sway AI](sway-ai.md) mixed the Umax rating idea with the dating niche of Rizz GPT ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
