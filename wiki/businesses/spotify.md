Spotify is a music and podcast streaming app. In these sources it appears mostly as a design case study and as the former employer of designer [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md).

## What it is

- A streaming app with a recommendation engine. The engine uses listening history, skips, time of day, device and listen length ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=61s)).
- It also owns podcast businesses. Tim rebuilds the website hero of Anchor, "a Spotify podcast company", in a Figma tutorial ([blDSmD5I61s](../sources/blDSmD5I61s.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blDSmD5I61s&t=0s)).

## Metrics

- Valuation rose from $8B to over $25B at the 2018 listing, two years apart. Tim credits personalization and a smooth UI ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=641s)).
- 600M+ users (2026-01) ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [13:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=831s)).
- Discover Weekly: 100B tracks streamed and 56M+ new artist discoveries each week (2026-01) ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=154s)).
- Wrapped: tens of millions of social shares each year (2026-01) ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=546s)). It reached 200M users in 24 hours "last year" (2026-04) ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [7:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=474s)).

## Growth channels

- **Trojan horse.** Discover Weekly hides complex tech inside a normal playlist. Early Pandora made users train its system with ratings, which felt like homework ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=117s), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=279s)).
- **Predictable delight.** A fresh playlist every Monday is a moment users expect, so it builds a habit ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=154s)).
- **Vanity mirror (Wrapped).** People share Wrapped to say something about themselves. Spotify is only the vehicle ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=488s)). The same "flex" pattern shows in Strava run maps and the Wordle grid ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=639s)).
- **A ceremony, not a report.** Wrapped gates the data, reveals it once a year and wraps it in sequential slides. Most copies just build data summaries ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [7:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=474s)).
- **Comfort trap.** Every playlist and recommendation behaves the same way, so leaving means relearning habits ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=704s)).
- Retention comes before virality. Sharing works only after users form an emotional attachment ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=606s)).

## Timeline

- July 2015: Discover Weekly launches with 30 new songs every Monday. Apple Music, YouTube Music and Amazon Music later copy the pattern ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=154s), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=279s)).
- 2018: public listing at over $25B ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=641s)).
- 2022-05: Tim Gabe starts his YouTube channel after Spotify, his last product company ([N0vlBuMggKc](../sources/N0vlBuMggKc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=0s)).
