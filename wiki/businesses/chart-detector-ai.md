Chart Detector AI is a mobile app that analyzes a photo of a stock or crypto chart with AI.

## What it is

- Users photograph a chart and get an AI analysis [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=0s).
- It began as a Telegram bot that analyzed charts after ChatGPT added image upload [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=216s).

## Founders

- [Timo Nikolai](../people/timo-nikolai.md), 23, a former software engineer at a German car company, and his brother [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=0s).

## Business model

- Hard paywall, no free trial: $12.99/week or $59.99 per 6 months (2026-07) [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s).
- Stack: React Native, Expo, Supabase, Cursor, an AI model API, RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, CapCut and TikTok Ads [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=666s).

## Metrics

- $56K/month and 13K to 14K downloads/month (2026-07) [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s).
- First 13 months: over 90K downloads and over $260K total revenue [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=62s).
- April P&L: $43.7K revenue, about $20K ad spend, about 15% Apple fees and about $11.5K profit, a margin of about 25% (2026-07) [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=358s).
- Close to 40M total impressions and 7.8M views in the last 30 days (2026-07) [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s).
- Timo works about 20 hours a month (2026-07) [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=0s).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok paid ads**, about $18K/month (2026-07) [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=666s). The best ad is a plain demo of the app in use: close to 5M views and over $15K revenue [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=331s).
- **Playbook** [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=485s), [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=605s):
  1. Onboarding sells the outcome, with a lot of social proof and a hard paywall.
  2. Track with an MMP such as AppsFlyer so TikTok learns who paid.
  3. Start with at least 6 videos in Smart+ campaigns, optimized for subscriptions, at $50/day or more, broad targeting.
  4. Leave the 7-day learning phase alone. Raise budget by at most 20% every 3 days. Keep new creatives coming.
- **ROAS math.** A $1.50 download that earns $3 is 2x ROAS. At that point, spend as much as you can [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=263s).
