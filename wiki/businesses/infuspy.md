Infuspy was a bootstrapped Instagram and Snapchat influencer finder for ecommerce sellers, now shut down.

## What it is

- A tool that finds Instagram and Snapchat influencers for ecommerce stores ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s)).

## Founders

- [Loïc Berthelot](../people/loic-berthelot.md), a French founder who later built [Minea](minea.md) and [Drop Magic](drop-magic.md) ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s)).

## Business model

- SaaS for ecommerce sellers, 100% [bootstrapped](../topics/bootstrapping.md) ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=64s)).

## Metrics

- $20K MRR in 2 months ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s)).

## Growth channels

- The source gives no Infuspy-specific channel. Loïc's later products grow through YouTube creators, which bring over 60% of new customers (2025-11) ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)).

## Timeline

- One of three products, out of more than 10 launches, that worked for Loïc. It is now shut down (2025-11) ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s)).
