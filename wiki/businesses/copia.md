# Copia

Copia is a manifesting and self-help app that turns a viral ChatGPT "day in my dream life" prompt into an app with affirmations.

## What it is

- Women posted emotional reactions to a ChatGPT "day in my dream life" prompt, with 10M to 20M views. Copia wraps that magic moment in an app ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=132s)).
- Users get their dream-life story only after they enter their goals. The prompt alone did not convert, because people already have ChatGPT ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=226s)).
- The core audience is women aged 18 to 25 ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s)).

## Founders

- [Phoenix Do](../people/phoenix-do.md), who builds apps in the manifesting niche, and co-founder Chloe. Chloe's first hook-and-demo video reached about 10M views on Instagram ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s)).

## Business model

- A subscription app with a trial. Phoenix thinks a hard paywall signals a weak product ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=855s)).

## Metrics

- About $25K MRR and 100K downloads (2025-10) ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=0s)).
- Before the onboarding fix: 50% to 55% of installs reached the paywall, and 15% to 20% of those started a trial. After the fix: 85% to 90% reached the paywall, which doubled conversion (2025-10) ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=696s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s)).

## Growth channels

- **Hook and demo.** Show the magic moment, but not so much that it feels like an ad. A 6.6M-view video without the demo got an estimated 20% to 30% fewer downloads ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=376s)).
- **Deadline affirmation format.** "If you're seeing this before May 7th, repeat these affirmations for 7 days." It copies [Sarah Pearl](../people/sarah-pearl.md) ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s)).
- **UGC creators.** About 40 creators, with 50K to 250K followers at about $1 CPM to start. Mid-size creators give funnel data ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=556s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=616s)). [SideShift](sideshift.md) found 10 to 20 good creators fast ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s)).
- **Strict creator guidelines.** Keep the demo the same and A/B test only the first 10 seconds ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1192s)).
- **Viral spikes do not always convert.** A placement on a viral account got 5M views but only a couple thousand downloads ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [25:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1507s)).

## Timeline

- First hook-and-demo video reaches about 10M views and proves the concept ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s)).
- A longer onboarding with social proof and tap questions doubles conversion ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s)).
- 2025-10: about $25K MRR and 100K downloads.
