This page collects what the sources say about Magai, a paid chat app that gives access to many AI models in one subscription.

## What it is

Magai is a chat app for text, image and video AI models. Users can switch models inside one conversation ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=412s)). Models include GPT-5, Claude, DALL-E, Flux, GPT Image and Ideogram ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=473s)).

## Founders

- [Dustin Stout](../people/dustin-stout.md) built it. He is not a developer ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)).
- He moved to California to act, then started an agency. In 2014 he co-founded a WordPress plugin company. Two later products failed ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=79s)).

## Business model

- There is no free plan. Solo costs $20/month. Team costs $40/month for up to 5 users, and each extra seat costs $20/month ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)).
- It sells against subscription fatigue: one plan instead of 5 or 6 AI subscriptions ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s)).
- It started by fixing gaps in early ChatGPT, such as chat search and folders ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s)).
- The front end is Bubble. OpenRouter serves the LLMs, and fal.ai serves media models ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=490s)).

## Metrics

- Almost $100K/month and over $1M in total revenue (2025-10) ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=55s)).
- $3K in month 1, $4K in month 2, $10K in month 3 and $30K/month after 1 year ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)).

## Growth channels

- **A personal brand**. Dustin's blog had over 100K email subscribers after 10 years. He did no paid ads ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)).
- **Recurring affiliates**. The affiliate program was in the product from day one. A referral that pays 12 times pays the affiliate 12 times ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=327s)).
- **Friends in marketing** helped spread the product ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=327s)).
- **Build in public**. "People want to see the journey" ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)).
