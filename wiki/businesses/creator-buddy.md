# Creator Buddy

Creator Buddy is an AI app that pulls a user's posts from X and coaches them on better content. [Alex Finn](../people/alex-finn.md) built it alone with AI coding tools.

## What it is

- It reads a user's X posts and uses an AI model to give content advice ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md)).
- It automates Alex's own habit: he tracked his tweets in a spreadsheet each night ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=367s)).

## Founders

- [Alex Finn](../people/alex-finn.md), a former MongoDB consultant and solo founder ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md)).

## Business model

- Paid subscription ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s)).
- Costs: about $250/month for AI APIs and $5,000/month for the X API. Total about $5,300/month, so margin is about 80% (2025-06) ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=668s)).
- Stack: Windsurf (after Cursor), ChatGPT o3 as product manager, Next.js on Vercel, Supabase and Resend ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s)).

## Metrics

- Launch: $100K ARR in 15 minutes, $200K in 2 hours, $300K ARR in 2 weeks (2025-01) ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=0s), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s)).
- Almost 500 active paid subscribers and about $25K/month (2025-06) ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s)).

## Growth channels

- **Audience first.** Alex posted on X for 3 years before he had a product, about 5 minutes a day. A thread on the open-sourced X algorithm went viral and got a retweet from Elon Musk ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=155s), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=556s)).
- **Long public beta.** 150 beta testers from December 2024. Alex met each one to walk through the app ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)).
- **Announced launch date.** He announced on January 12 for January 24 and hosted X Spaces all launch day ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=473s)).
- **Distribution as the moat.** Anyone can copy the software, but a clone with 10 followers loses ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)).
