HabitKit is a simple habit tracker for iOS and Android, built by one developer in Germany. It grows mainly through app store optimization.

## What it is

The MVP had a tile grid chart of habits and basic tracking. Data stays on the device, and there is no sign-in. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s)

## Founder

[Sebastian Röhl](../people/sebastian-rohl.md), a 32-year-old developer in Germany. He quit his job with 12 months of runway, had few downloads after 6 months, and went back to his old job. He kept building on the side and quit again at the start of 2024. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=713s)

## Business model

Subscriptions through RevenueCat, which takes 1% of revenue. Other costs total about $200 to $300/month, with no employees (2025-06). [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=636s)

## Metrics

- About $3K MRR at the end of 2023 and about $15K MRR at the end of 2024. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=65s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=268s)
- About 300K downloads on the App Store and Google Play, and over 2,000 ratings (2025-06). [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=110s)

## Growth channels

- **Build in public.** He posted progress, wins and failures on X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads. Good replies to early screenshots told him to ship fast. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s)
- **App store optimization.** The app name starts with the main keyword, "habit tracker". This put it in the top 5 for that keyword in several countries. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)
- **Review prompt at the first success.** The native review dialog shows right after a user checks off their first habit. Most people give 5 stars. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)
- **A small Apple Search Ads hedge.** About $100/month, in the hope that Apple favors advertisers (2025-06). [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s)

## Stack

Flutter, Cursor, Astro for keyword research, Appfigures for analytics and RevenueCat. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=562s)
