Kleo is an AI ghostwriter for founders who post on LinkedIn. It launched only to a waitlist built with LinkedIn content and email.

## What it is

- AI that writes LinkedIn posts for founders. The captions spell it "Cleo" ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=0s)).

## Founders

- [Lara Acosta](../people/lara-acosta.md), whose background is agencies and info products. She started posting on LinkedIn about 3 years earlier ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s)).
- Co-founder Rob gave his personal phone number to every user ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=905s)).

## Business model

- Subscription SaaS, sold only to a waitlist. The launch offer was 500 spots and 50% off for life ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=228s), [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=856s)).
- Stack: Claude Code, Next.js, Vercel, Clerk, Loops, Calendly and Polar ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=937s)).

## Metrics

- $30K MRR in 4 days. About $60K/month in under 2 months (2026-01) ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)).
- $62K MRR and 932 active subscriptions on Polar (2026-01) ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)).

## Growth channels

- **Edu-selling posts with no CTA** from both founders and a brand account ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=291s)).
- **Waitlist nurture.** More than 10 emails in the 4 weeks before launch. The first answered "why pay when ChatGPT is free?". No CTA until launch day ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=451s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=511s)). See [email marketing](../topics/email-marketing.md).
- **Webinars.** 20 minutes of teaching, 20 minutes of demo, then the pitch ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=744s)).
- **Scarcity** beat curiosity and FOMO as the first push to buy ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=856s)).
