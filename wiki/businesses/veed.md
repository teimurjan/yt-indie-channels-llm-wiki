VEED is a browser-based video editor from London. It grew from $0 to $40M in revenue through programmatic SEO and YouTube videos.

## What it is

The first version did basic video editing in the browser: upload, add text and trim. Giphy's GIF editor inspired it. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=205s)

## Founders

[Sabba Keynejad](../people/sabba-keynejad.md) and his co-founder Tim founded it in 2018. They met online through a global hackathon a year after university. This Tim is not [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md). [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=0s), [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=145s)

## Business model

Freemium SaaS. For its first two years, VEED charged nothing. Y Combinator asked why they did not charge users. They added payments over one weekend and got 20 paid users. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=519s), [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=581s)

## Metrics

- 0 to 30,000 monthly users in about 9 months, all free. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=346s)
- Revenue went from $0 to $1M in the first year. It hit $2M 4 months later, $3M 2 months later and $6M 6 months after that. Later it reached $10M, $20M, $30M and $40M (2025-05). [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)
- The video title values the company at $200M (2025-05). [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md)

## Growth channels

- **Programmatic SEO by brute force.** Sabba found about 500 search terms such as "trim video" and "crop video". He built a landing page for each one. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)
- **One YouTube video per landing page.** [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)
- **One goal metric.** After the YC rejection, Sabba's only job was to beat the traffic and revenue of the day or week before. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=647s)

## Timeline

- 2018: founded in London with about 1 year of runway. They asked Crisp to cut its chat tool from $100/month to $20/month, and Crisp agreed. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=241s), [4:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=285s)
- About 2020: after 2 years with no revenue, both founders took jobs. Half of each salary paid for 2 engineers. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=346s)
- YC rejected them after the interview. After the weekend of 20 paid users, YC rejected them again. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=581s), [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=631s)
- Then: the SEO push and growth to $40M (2025-05). [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)
