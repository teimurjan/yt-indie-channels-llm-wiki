Jenni AI is an AI research and writing tool for students and researchers, grown mostly with short-form video.

## What it is

- AI tools that help students and researchers write papers, essays and citations ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)).
- The idea came from a GPT-2 output. The first target was content agencies ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)).

## Founders

- [David Park](../people/david-park.md) (CEO, sales) and his co-founder Henry (code). They ran 9 or 10 failed startups together before Jenni ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=123s), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)).
- [Matt Gittleson](../people/matt-gittleson.md) joined through an acqui-hire of his citation-checker startup. He runs the UGC program ([source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [53:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3197s)).

## Business model

- Subscription software for students, profitable (2025-03) ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)).
- Funding: one $100K check from Jason Calacanis, after a scout heard David on a small podcast ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)).

## Metrics

- About $2K/month, stuck after GPT-3 came out in 2020 ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)).
- $2K to $10K MRR in one month after a viral Twitter thread. Then $50K to $80K MRR from TikTok and Reels ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=435s), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=496s)).
- Over $3M ARR. David puts its worth at $10M to $30M (2024-01) ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=743s)).
- Over $10M/year, profitable, over 5M users (2025-03) ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)).
- About $775K MRR and 5 to 6M users (2025-11). The video title says $750K/month ([source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=26s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=163s)).
> Conflict: [KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md) gives over $10M/year (2025-03). [Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) gives about $775K MRR, about $9.3M/year (2025-11).

## Growth channels

- **Cold calls.** David called agencies 8 hours a day for weeks. About 99% said no ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=0s)).
- **Short-form video.** Three steps: gather ingredients, explore, exploit winners ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)). The "POV: you have a paper due" series ran twice a week for about 6 months. It got hundreds of millions of views and probably over $500K (2025-03) ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [21:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1276s)).
- **Many accounts.** At one point 5 accounts on 3 platforms posted 15 videos a day. A 48-follower account sometimes beat the 55K-follower main account ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1446s)).
- **Paid creators.** UGC creators get about $2,000/month base plus a bonus, near $3,000/month in total. Creators with an audience get $3,000 to $5,000/month plus a bonus (2025-03) ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=960s)).
- **UGC program.** 0 to 150 creators and 75M views at under $2 CPM in 3 months (2025-11). Creators are picked for "camera charisma", not followers ([source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=297s), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=409s)).
- **Engagement bait.** A creator's clip split opinion and got 30M views on X ([source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [28:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1690s)).

- After the $100K check, the founders moved to Malaysia to cut burn ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)).
- David's thyroid cancer surgery stalled growth. He shopped Jenni for "a quick few million", then kept it ([source](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=578s), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=642s)).

- 2025-03: Over $10M/year ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)).

- 2025-11: About $775K MRR ([source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=26s)).
