Starter Story is Pat Walls' media business of founder case studies and interviews, sold to HubSpot in 2026.

## What it is

- A site and YouTube channel that show how regular people found ideas, launched and got customers ([xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=265s)).
- Over 4,000 case studies and business breakdowns (2024-01) ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=307s)).
- The channel runs founder interviews. Examples:
  - [App Alchemy](app-alchemy.md), about $17K/month from Reddit alone (2025-07) ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=55s)).
  - [Charlie Chang Media](charlie-chang-media.md), six YouTube channels at $200K to $250K a month (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
  - [Rabbit Holes AI](rabbit-holes-ai.md), about $80K in 6 months after a pricing change (2025-10) ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=47s)).
  - [TaskMagic](taskmagic.md), about $3M a year before its sale (2026-04) ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=134s)).
  - [Neural Frames](neural-frames.md), about $100K a month (2025-07) ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=52s)).
  - [Vault Air](vault-air.md), about $2K to $3K a day (2026-10) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=74s)).
- It also gives away free tools, such as the "gold mining" idea prompts at starterstory.com/goldmining (2025-06) ([L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1355s)).

## Business model

- Newsletter sponsorships and affiliate links first. Pat copied the model of similar businesses a few steps ahead ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=307s)).
- Paid products. A 5-minute Loom of Starter Story's Klaviyo flows became a product with about $20,000 in pre-orders in one day (2024-10) ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=31s)).
- Email. The list sends millions of emails a month and makes about $80K to $120K a month (2024-10) ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=667s)).

## Growth channels

- **Reddit.** Moderators removed his link posts. He put full case studies in self-posts with a small link at the end ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=208s)).
  - Repeated posts hit the front page. Hundreds of thousands visited the site ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)).
  - One post with about 400 upvotes brought about 1,000 email signups ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=373s)).
  - Redditors started a petition to ban starterstory.com posts ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=420s)).
  - Pat's view: Reddit gets the first 10 to 100 customers, not scale ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=941s)).
- **Email.** He turned rented Reddit attention into an owned list ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=445s)).
- **YouTube.** Pat calls it the most important part of the business. Viewers build a relationship with you ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=544s)).
- **Build in public.** The Klaviyo CEO liked Pat's shared progress, which led to a sponsorship ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=373s)).

## Timeline

- 2017: Pat starts it as a side project. He works 2 hours a day in a Starbucks, later from 6 a.m. before work ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=180s)).
- Month 1: he buys starterstory.com for $12/year and launches with a few stories ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)).
- The first "perfect" launch gets zero users. Reddit then brings the first traffic ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=116s)).
- A Reddit story goes viral. First money comes from a newsletter sponsor and affiliates ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)).
- Klaviyo becomes the first big sponsor. Pat resigns a few months later ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=443s)).
- Day 365: $3,500/month. He quits with about $12,000 in savings ([xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=488s), [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s)).
- 2024-01: over $1M a year ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=0s)).
- 2026-02: HubSpot acquires Starter Story. The price is private. Pat calls it "life-changing" ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s)).
- Why he sold: the business became his identity and a cage. HubSpot wanted to scale it without change ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=295s)).
