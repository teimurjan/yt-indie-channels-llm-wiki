This page collects what the sources say about Natural Write, an AI "humanizer" that rewrites AI text so it passes AI detectors.

## What it is

Natural Write rewrites AI-generated text so that it passes AI detectors. Students use it most, and marketers use it too ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s)).

## Founders

- [Nikita Logvinenko](../people/nikita-logvinenko.md) lives in Serbia, and [Evgeniy Mikholap](../people/evgeniy-mikholap.md) lives in Poland. Both are marketers ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=10s)).
- A third member, Alexander, does the tech ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s)).

## Business model

- It is a web app. The stack is Next.js, Postgres, Coolify and Resend ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s)).
- The server costs $35/month and Resend $20/month (2025-09) ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s)).

## Metrics

- $41K in month 1, $32K in month 2 and $27K in month 3, about $100K in total (2025-09) ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)).
- Funnel: about 9M TikTok views, about 300K site visits and 250K signups (2025-09) ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=544s)).
- The top video, a Jim Carrey comedy format, got 4M views ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s)).
- Another copy got 1.5M views, while the original it copied had about 150K ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=520s)).

## Growth channels

- **Pick a category that already goes viral**. TikTok had many viral humanizer videos before they built ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s)).
- **Paid influencers failed**. They got views and almost no sales. Self-filmed daily posts made a sale from the first video ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)).
- **A 7-step TikTok playbook**. Research the niche, set up a local account, warm it up, copy viral videos 1:1, post daily, innovate after hits, then scale ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s)).
- Outside the US they use a VPN, an iPhone and a local phone number for each account ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=328s)).
- **Show the problem, then the fix**. Both top videos turn "100% AI" text into "100% human" text ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s)).
