Done is a habit-tracking app in [Ryan Thorp](../people/ryan-thorp.md)'s acquired app portfolio. It reached #1 free app in the US App Store with heavy January ad spend.

## What it is

- A habit app. Ryan's team bought it around 2020, bundled with 5 or 6 other apps ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s)).

## Founders

- Owner and operator: [Ryan Thorp](../people/ryan-thorp.md), who started with [Reflectly](reflectly.md) ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s)). The source does not name Done's original founder.

## Business model

- A subscription app. Ryan's team improved paywalls on undermonetized apps like Done ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s)).

## Metrics

- The purchase paid back in 12 to 18 months ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s)).
- Top free app in the US App Store on January 2 and 3, two years in a row, ahead of TikTok and Chrome ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [23:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1393s), [23:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1409s)).

## Growth channels

- **January ad push.** The team spent about 75% of its yearly ad budget in January, when acquisition costs about half. The peak is the first Sunday of the year ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [19:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1166s), [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1564s)).
- **Creator videos as TikTok ads**, scaled in three levels from $10 test ad sets to evergreen ads ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=971s)). See [ad creative](../topics/ad-creative.md).

## Timeline

- About 2020: Acquired ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s)).
- Two Januaries: #1 free app in the US on January 2 and 3 ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [23:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1409s)).
- By 2026-01: Ryan sold 80% to 90% of the portfolio to a buyer from gaming ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2952s)).
