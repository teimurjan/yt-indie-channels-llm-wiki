Flame is a couples app that gives partners one question a day to answer together, plus an AI relationship coach.

## What it is

- A daily ritual app for couples. You cannot see your partner's answer until you answer. Other features: AI coach modes, a gifting genie, a shared bucket list and a private memory timeline ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=227s), [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=661s)).
- Target: the 80% of couples who say their relationship needs work but will not pay $200 for therapy ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)).

## Founders

- [An Nayal](../people/an-nayal.md) launched it in New York and built it with his then girlfriend ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=169s)).
- An opened the first 24-hour gym in Beijing (7 locations) and worked at a Berlin scale-up from Series B to Series C ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=273s), [source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=169s)).

## Business model

- Subscription ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)). Flame was free until the end of 2023 ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=845s)).
- Stack: Claude Code, Claude Design, RevenueCat, Mixpanel free tier and TikTok analytics ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=715s)).

## Metrics

- Day-30 retention: 0.8% (Sparks), 4.2% (Flame AI), about 30%, then 50% after the daily-question pivot, over about 8 months ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=13s), [0:10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=644s)).
- 50M views and 150K users, all organic (2026-02) ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=123s)).
- 250,000 downloads, 50M organic views, and $3K to $10K MRR in under 8 months (2026-06) ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=70s)). About 58M views over the last year. The app was being sold (2026-06) ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)).
- View-to-download conversion by format: memes and slideshows 0.05% to 0.2%, reaction plus app clip 0.3% to 0.5%, direct app demo 0.5% to 1% (2026-02) ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:33:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2001s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok farm.** 20 to 30 accounts on about 10 numbered iPhones with 3 to 4 accounts each (2026-02) ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:24:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1490s)). 15 to 20 accounts with 2 to 5 per phone (2026-06) ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=715s)).
- **Volume.** About 200 pieces of content a day from AI playbooks ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:34:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2070s)). At one point 150 pieces a day to test one hook ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s)).
- **VSC framework.** Viral (over 100K views on accounts under 5K followers), Scalable (you can make it 100 times), Convertible (comments ask "what's the app?") ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s), [source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:47:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2821s)).
- **The 300-view rule.** Under 300 views is an account problem, 300 to 1K a content problem ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s)).
- **Cheap UGC and comment seeding.** UGC from Eastern Europe at about $1 per 60-second video. A 120K-view slideshow gave 8 or 9 trials until comment seeding lifted conversion to about 1% ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=492s)).

> Conflict: farm size. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) gives 20 to 30 accounts (2026-02). [-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md) gives 15 to 20 accounts (2026-06).

## Retention work

- Users came back for the daily question, not the AI coach, so the question moved to the home screen ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=650s)).
- "Carrot and stick" notifications: a morning reminder, then a warning that the streak and the partner are at risk. Home and lock-screen widgets ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=711s), [0:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=974s)).
