Savvy Nomad helps US citizens who live abroad change their state domicile so they stop paying state taxes.

## What it is

- A guided online service to change state domicile. Most customers finish the online part in under an hour [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=713s).

## Founders

- [Bohdan Drozdov](../people/bohdan-drozdov.md) ("Bo"), the marketing and business co-founder. He rebuilt his life after the war in Ukraine and shut down an earlier digital-nomad tax startup [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=216s).
- His co-founder met California surfers abroad who still paid California state tax, and built the first version [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=810s).

## Business model

- Subscription with three plans: basic domicile, a mid tier (the most popular) and domicile premium. It charges up front, so net volume runs above MRR [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s).
- Stack: Bubble for the whole product, Framer, Ghost, ChatGPT and Claude, Ahrefs, Customer.io, Power BI with BigQuery, Stripe and SavvyCal. No-code lets non-engineers run it [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s).

## Metrics

- $1.7M ARR, $140K MRR, over 1,400 customers and six employees (2026-05) [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=0s), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s).
- 120 to 150 new customers a month (2026-05) [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s).
- About $10M in state taxes saved for customers (2026-05) [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=60s).

## Growth channels

- **Show the savings in onboarding.** Qualifying questions lead to an estimate of savings, then a plan recommendation [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=713s).
- **A boring niche.** Tax and compliance have real demand, high willingness to pay and few capable founders. The value is concrete, because the savings sit next to the price [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=272s).
- **Productize one workflow.** Savvy Nomad does only the state domicile change, with a recurring layer on top [source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md) [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=545s).
