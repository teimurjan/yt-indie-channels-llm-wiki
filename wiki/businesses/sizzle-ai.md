# Sizzle AI

Sizzle AI is an edtech learning app that grows by turning organic short-form videos into paid ads.

## What it is

- An AI learning app for students. [Yev](../people/yev.md), a former data scientist, is its head of growth ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md)).

## Founders

- The source does not name the founders. It features Yev, head of growth ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md)).

## Business model

- Paid subscriptions. Each purchase is worth about $30 to $70 LTV, higher on iOS. Churn comes after 3 to 7 months, depending on the audience (2025-06) ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [18:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1120s)).

## Metrics

- One winning TikTok had 79K likes. It came from 20 to 30 organic videos and drove hundreds of purchases as a paid ad (2025-06) ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=520s), [19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1167s)).
- Install-to-purchase about 1% on test traffic. In-app tests cut cost per purchase from about $500 to $300 within days ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1024s)).
- Cost per purchase targets: $50, $100 or $200, set by each ad's goal (2025-06) ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=964s)).
- Real purchases are often about double what ads report ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1336s)).

## Growth channels

- **Organic creators, then paid amplification.** Put a few dollars behind every organic video. Low-view videos can still convert ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=328s), [12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=731s)).
- **Creators from marketplaces.** JoinBrands, [SideShift](sideshift.md) and their own creator Discord ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=429s)).
- **TikTok for the US, Facebook for global** targeting ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=964s)).
- **Fix the biggest funnel drop-off.** A step with over 50% drop-off gave 2x to 3x growth, while feature work gave about 10% ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=156s)).
- **Ad structure.** A shocking facial-expression hook, a demo that calls out the audience, then a pattern-interrupt ending ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=540s)).

## Timeline

- The source gives no launch date. As of 2025-06, the growth loop is: organic videos, paid amplification of winners, then layered tests on headlines, countries and in-app flows ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [25:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1500s)).
