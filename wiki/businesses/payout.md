Payout is an iOS app that finds class action settlements. Creator posts drive almost all of its growth.

## What it is

- The app lists class action settlements that a user can claim ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
- It sells "make money", a core desire ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=683s)). Users pay about $40/year and can recover $200+ in claims ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [33:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1981s)).
- Built in under 2 weeks with AI, in React Native. Stack: Figma, Mixpanel, Claude Code, Next.js, Vercel, Expo and RevenueCat ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=714s), [zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1608s)).

## Founders

- [Connor Burd](../people/connor-burd.md), a 23-year-old indie developer with no computer science degree ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=58s)).
- [Casper](../people/casper.md) has 9M followers. He is the 50/50 distribution partner ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=209s)).
- The idea came from a competitor that offered Casper only $500 for a sponsored video ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=350s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with weekly and yearly plans. The paywall pushes users to the yearly plan, which has the highest LTV ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=610s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).
- The creator gets equity, not a per-post fee ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=737s)).

## Metrics

- $20K/month, over 12,000 downloads and over 1,300 new subscriptions about 50 days after launch (2025-10) ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
- About $45K/month from influencers alone. Casper had driven 35,000+ clicks (2025-12) ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=197s), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s)).
- $217K in 5 months. Casper calls it a $100K/month app (2026-03) ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=209s)).
- Funnel on one video: about 13,000 commenters asked for the link, about 50% clicked, and download-to-paid was about 6.7% (2026-03) ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [14:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=897s)).

## Growth channels

- **Creator posts with ManyChat.** "Comment CLAIM" sends a DM with the link. Custom product pages track each link ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=307s)). Instagram beats TikTok for these posts ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=527s)). See [influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md).
- **Reused hooks.** A reused Gelato hook made about $5K+ in subscriptions. A Temu skit video made about $10K ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=779s), [16:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=986s)).
- **Coordinated pushes.** Five creators posted on one day, made $9,700 and pushed the app to about #70 to #80 in Finance ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [46:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2771s)).
- **Creators, then paid ads.** Winning creator videos become a library of tested Facebook ads ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=492s)). See [Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md).
