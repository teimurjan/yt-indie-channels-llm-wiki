Framer is a no-code web design tool that lets designers build and publish live websites in one place. For how-to tutorials, see the [Framer topic page](../topics/framer.md).

## What it is

- Framer designs and publishes websites with no developer. Its limit is that it only makes things for the web. Figma draws the blueprint, and Framer builds the building ([source](../sources/XPim-a5R6yw.md), [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=16s), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=40s)).
- Pages are live URLs. Text styles have built-in breakpoints. It has a CMS, scroll effects, real CSS sizing units and one-click publishing ([source](../sources/XLWXztyA80Q.md), [13:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=809s), [15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=934s)).
- A Figma plugin copies frames into Framer. Framer stacks, Figma auto layout and CSS flexbox follow the same model ([source](../sources/AJBaBScKdwI.md), [0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJBaBScKdwI&t=24s), [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJBaBScKdwI&t=439s)).
- The Insert panel holds ready-made sections, embeds and components such as carousel, ticker and slideshow ([source](../sources/XLWXztyA80Q.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=140s), [source](../sources/nS_9yAABLwc.md)).
- Site settings cover SEO, social previews, password protection, custom code, domains, redirects and built-in analytics ([source](../sources/XLWXztyA80Q.md), [13:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=809s)).

## Business model

- Framer sells site plans. In 2023-09 the Mini plan cost $5 against Webflow's Basic at $14. For mid-size blogs, Webflow's CMS plan was cheaper than Framer's Pro plan. For big projects, Framer's Pro plan was a bit cheaper ([source](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s)).
- More than one CMS collection needed a Pro plan (2023-09) ([source](../sources/ADrNwS19i5I.md), [3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADrNwS19i5I&t=190s)).
- Framer Sites was free at the time of a 2022-09 tutorial ([source](../sources/0SzJOMS0RPA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SzJOMS0RPA&t=0s)).
- Framer makes free templates. One SaaS template copies and publishes as a responsive, animated site in about 20 seconds (2024-07) ([source](../sources/hCKdIwbhS-s.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=64s)).
- One founder pays about $200/month for his Framer site (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=450s)).

## Metrics

The sources give no revenue, user or valuation figures for Framer. One data point on pace: Framer shipped seven big updates in the month before 2023-05 ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=220s)).

## Growth channels

- **Win designers from Webflow.** Webflow needs HTML and CSS knowledge. In 2023-05, a designer who knew Figma felt at home in Framer after a couple of hours ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=43s)). Framer wins on Figma export and UI, while Webflow wins on CMS ([source](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s)).
- **Hands-on migrations.** The Framer team helped Dribbble migrate to Framer at no cost (2023-05) ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=193s)).
- **Community.** The Framer Discord gives several fast answers to a question ([source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=149s)).
- **Creator education.** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) made dozens of Framer tutorials and sold a "Framer Zero to Hero" course of 23+ hours (2024-07). He says Framer did not pay him ([source](../sources/hCKdIwbhS-s.md), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=260s), [source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md)). His channel passed 100K subscribers by 2025-01 ([source](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md), [0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=35s)).
- **Agency partners.** Framer is a client of the design agency Skale (2025-10) ([source](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)).
- **Default for indie marketing sites.** Many founders use Framer for the landing page and run the product elsewhere. Examples are [Savvy Nomad](../businesses/savvy-nomad.md), [SuperGrow](../businesses/supergrow.md), [Voicy](../businesses/voicy.md) and [Creator Hunter](../businesses/creator-hunter.md) ([source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s), [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=801s), [source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=450s), [source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=440s)). [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md) uses Framer for [Late Checkout](../businesses/late-checkout.md) landing pages ([source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=569s)). [Kyle Fowler](../people/kyle-fowler.md) and [Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md) also run their sites on it ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=368s), [source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=437s)).

## Timeline

- **2022-09:** Framer Sites is free and imports Figma designs onto its canvas. Its design tools are less flexible than Figma's ([source](../sources/0SzJOMS0RPA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SzJOMS0RPA&t=0s)).
- **2022-12:** Templates such as "Starter" and "Boost" ship with desktop, tablet and phone frames ([source](../sources/XLWXztyA80Q.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=62s)).
- **2023-05:** Tim Gabe calls Framer the fastest and most flexible web design tool on the market. Designers move over from Webflow ([source](../sources/PYskozIWh-E.md), [source](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md)).
- **2023-06:** Framer AI makes a base landing page from one prompt in under 2 minutes ([source](../sources/U2EVNlDzAJQ.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=0s)).
- **2023-09:** An "HTML to Framer" extension copies live sites into Framer. Full-page copies fail on complex e-commerce layouts ([source](../sources/bBamB0yE12c.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=0s), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=125s)). The CMS supports typed fields and detail pages ([source](../sources/ADrNwS19i5I.md), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADrNwS19i5I&t=296s)).
- **2023-11:** Spline 3D scenes embed in Framer as sticky layers ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=905s)).
- **2024-07:** A free 2-hour mini course teaches the box model, stacks, style guides and breakpoints ([source](../sources/wgMgje60Ovw.md), [0:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wgMgje60Ovw&t=0s)).
- **2025-04:** Tim Gabe says Lovable does for full-stack code what Framer did for pixel-perfect design ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=99s)).
