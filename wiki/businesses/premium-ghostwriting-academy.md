Premium Ghostwriting Academy is an evergreen group coaching program for freelance ghostwriters, part of Dickie Bush's writing business portfolio.

## What it is

- An evergreen group coaching program that teaches people to work as freelance ghostwriters ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- Sister businesses: [Ship 30 for 30](ship-30-for-30.md), [Write With AI](write-with-ai.md) and [Typeshare](typeshare.md) ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s)).

## Founders

- [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md), who left a hedge fund trading job at BlackRock. It paid about $180K at age 22 ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s)).
- Dickie partners with Nicolas Cole across the writing businesses ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s)).

## Business model

- Paid group coaching, sold all year in place of cohorts ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- The pitch rests on Dickie's own path. Build the skill, borrow someone else's audience as a ghostwriter, and earn $5K to $10K/month ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=677s)).

## Metrics

- About $5M/year (2024-07) ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- The full portfolio is on pace for $10M in 2024 (2024-07) ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).

## Growth channels

- **The founder's audience.** Dickie published daily. A post on day 27 of his challenge got retweets from Naval and others ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s)).
- **The Ship 30 for 30 community** is the beginner entry point to the portfolio ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).

## Timeline

- January 2020: Dickie starts a newsletter and a 30-day writing challenge ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s)).
- A viral tweet leads to his first ghostwriting job. He lets the client name the price and gets $5,000 ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s)).
- Ghostwriting caps out at $5K to $10K/month as a side hustle. He productizes the challenge in place of the output ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s)).
- 2024-07: the academy makes about $5M/year ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
