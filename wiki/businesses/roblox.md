Roblox is a game platform where independent developers build games and earn from in-game purchases.

## What it is

- About 400M monthly active users. One in two US kids under 16 plays daily. Kids spend twice as much time on Roblox as on TikTok (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=312s)).
- Dress to Impress opened Roblox to older girls: over 1M concurrent players and over 1B plays ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=373s)).

## Business model for developers

- Developers earn Robux from in-game purchases and cash them out. 1M Robux pays out about $3.5K (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=245s), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=676s)).
- Brand-sponsored games are a newer revenue stream ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=245s)).

## Metrics from developers

- [Cole](../people/cole.md): about 1M daily active users across his games. [Hide or Die](hide-or-die.md) makes about $45K/month, split 50/50 with his programmer (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=93s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s)).
- [Ian](../people/ian.md): [Bathe the Baby](bathe-the-baby.md) has about 150K daily active users and makes about $25K/month (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).
- [Jake](../people/jake.md): [Vector 3](vector-3.md), an agency for brands that enter Roblox, made $36,150 so far in the month (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)).

## Growth channels

- Big Roblox YouTubers launched Ian's game with about 100M views ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=149s)).
- Games that win are clickable, social and replayable: a dramatic title, friends playing together, and levels and locked content ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=753s)).
- Test fast. After a 3-year, $300K game failed, Cole made Hide or Die in 2 weeks ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=720s)).
