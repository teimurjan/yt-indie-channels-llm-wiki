Welltory is a health app that reads Apple Watch data and shows people what drains their energy and raises their stress.

## What it is

- The app turns wearable data into stress, energy and sleep insights. It is about 10 years old (2026-08) [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s).
- Free tier: energy, stress, sleep score, activity and blood pressure. Paid tier: trends, a day timeline and activity advice [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [41:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2475s).

## Founders

- The sources do not name the founders. [Assia Poloni](../people/assia-poloni.md) is Chief Product Officer [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s).

## Business model

- Subscription. $99.99/year, shown as $8.83/month, sometimes with a monthly option. It is a "hardish" paywall with no trial, but users can close it (2026-08) [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [39:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2363s), [40:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2413s).
- The paywall has no feature list. It repeats the pain point from onboarding [source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md) [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=367s).

## Metrics

- About $18M ARR and about 17M to 18M downloads after 10 years (2026-08) [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=280s).
- Over 90% of users connect health data on the first CTA screen (2026-08) [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [27:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1640s).
- Over 60% of conversions happen after day one, over the next couple of months (2026-08) [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [40:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2427s).
- About 70% of women say they feel tired or drained. Men tend to say they feel fine, even with worse data [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [28:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1727s).

## Growth channels

- **Meta ads with a scary inside view.** A long-term promise does poorly on cold Meta traffic. Ads say the app shows what happens inside your body, for example "this Apple Watch app catches high cortisol" [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=386s), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=609s).
- **Onboarding as a six-part pitch:** human problem, promise, key ("sedentary stress"), mechanism, proof and FOMO [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1074s).
- **CTA on screen one.** "Your Apple Watch knows what makes you tired and stressed" leads to "sync my health data". Every intro screen tested before it did worse [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1608s).
- **The user's own data as proof**, for example 192 minutes of stress in one day [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [21:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1296s).
- **Close with loss.** "Stress is building right now" beat an energy-bank progress chart [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [35:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2122s).
- **Ask for notifications early.** It breaks best practice but worked best for Welltory [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [28:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1689s).

## Timeline

- About 2016: launch, by the "10 years old" figure in 2026-08 [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s).
- For years, marketing and product worked in silos. The team now builds to the promise that ads prove: make hidden body processes visible [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=531s).
- 2026-08: about $18M ARR [source](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s).
- 2026-10: Joseph Choi cites its onboarding as a pain-point example [source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md) [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=367s).
