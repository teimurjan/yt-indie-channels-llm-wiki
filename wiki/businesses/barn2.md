Barn2 is a remote team that sells a portfolio of 19 WordPress and WooCommerce plugins plus one Shopify app.

## What it is

- 20 products built over 10 years. Nineteen are WordPress and WooCommerce plugins, and one is a new Shopify app ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=0s)).
- Examples: WooCommerce Protected Categories (the first), Posts Table Pro, Document Library Pro, product tables and product options ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=112s), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=220s)).

## Founders

- [Katie Keith](../people/katie-keith.md) runs it with a remote team ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Plans for 1, 5 or 20 sites, sold as an annual subscription or a lifetime deal. Lifetime deals are about 15% of revenue (2026-02) ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=217s)).
- Affiliate income and development services add to plugin sales ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).

## Metrics

- 2025 plugin revenue just over $1.7M, and about $1.8M in total (2026-02) ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
- About $150K/month on average. November runs higher and December lower ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
- $9.8M in lifetime plugin sales, premium plugins on over 90,000 sites and 17,000 active subscriptions (2026-02) ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).

## Growth channels

- **Idea forums.** The first idea had many votes on the WooCommerce Ideas Forum and no plugin yet ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=220s)).
- **SEO tutorials.** Most customers come from organic search. Katie writes a helpful post for each use case. The first plugin ranked at the top of Google at once and sold within days ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=317s)).
- **Cross-selling** ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=461s), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=503s)):
  - Each product has one companion plugin to upsell, plus two-plugin bundles.
  - Three days after a purchase, an email offers 50% off the next plugin.
  - On Black Friday, prospects and existing customers get separate campaigns.
  - Banners on plugin settings pages promote related plugins.
- **Customer requests.** Some feature requests fit better as a new plugin ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=281s)).

## Timeline

- **About 2016:** The first plugin, WooCommerce Protected Categories, launches from a forum idea ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=220s)).
- **Next years:** Posts Table Pro grows out of a client project. More plugins follow from customer requests ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=220s), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=281s)).
- **2025:** Plugin revenue just over $1.7M ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
- **2026-02:** 20 products, including a new Shopify app ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=0s)).

Note: the "about 2016" date comes from "20 products over 10 years" in a 2026-02 video. The source does not state the year.

Stack: ClickUp, GitHub, Zapier, WordPress and Easy Digital Downloads for sales and licensing ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=713s)).
