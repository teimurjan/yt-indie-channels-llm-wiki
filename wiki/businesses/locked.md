Locked is a gamified habit and focus app built by 14-year-old Evan Yadegari.

## What it is

- Users pick a character in onboarding. They check off tasks, earn XP, levels and badges, and climb leaderboards. Each task gives 100 XP ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s)).
- Habit trackers are a crowded category. Game mechanics make Locked different ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=207s)). See [gamification](../topics/gamification.md).
- The idea came from a friend who struggled to focus ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=254s)).

## Founders

- [Evan Yadegari](../people/evan-yadegari.md), a ninth grader and the younger brother of [Zach Yadegari](../people/zach-yadegari.md) of [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=147s)).
- He built it with a then co-founder. They designed in Figma for 2 weeks, then built with Xcode and Claude Code ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=216s)).

## Business model

- $40/year with a 3-day free trial, or $7/week (2026-04) ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s)).
- A second paywall offers $20/year to users who close the first one ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).
- $30/year or $7/week (2026-06) ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=553s)).

> Conflict: [dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md) gives the annual price as $40/year (2026-04). [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) gives $30/year (2026-06).

## Metrics

- $14K/month about 4 months after launch (2026-04) ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=0s)).
- Over $14K/month. Download-to-subscriber conversion is a bit over 10% (2026-06) ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=567s)).
- RPM of about $2 to $3 per 1,000 views (2026-04) ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=245s)). A later video says $3 to $4 (2026-06) ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=578s)).

## Growth channels

- **Paid short-form influencers.** Evan tested Reddit, X, influencers and ads. Influencers won ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=367s)). See [influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md).
- **CPM below RPM.** He pays creators $1 to $1.50 CPM, below his revenue per 1,000 views ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=245s)).
- **Minimum view clause.** His default deal, for example $500 for 500K views. A creator who misses it posts again ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=486s)).
- **DMs at scale.** The team contacted 5,000 to 10,000 influencers, over 500 DMs a day at peak. Reply rates were 3 to 4% on Instagram and about 2% on TikTok ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=687s)).
- **Day-in-the-life creators.** They tick off tasks in the app on camera ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=450s)).

> Conflict: the intro clip of [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) says Reddit was the best channel. In the same interview, and in [dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), Evan says influencers were best.
