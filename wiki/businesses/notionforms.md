NotionForms is a form builder that saves responses into Notion databases.

## What it is

- A form builder for Notion users, modeled on Airtable's forms feature and launched right after Notion released its public API [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s).

## Founders

- [Julien Nahum](../people/julien-nahum.md), a 28-year-old former software engineer. He built the MVP in 6 days while working a 9-to-5 [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s).

## Business model

- Subscription SaaS. It started cheaper than all form builders, raised prices with no visible conversion drop, and added higher tiers later [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=706s).
- Free-to-paid switch at about 1,000 users: new features were tagged "Pro, free during beta", then billing went on. Early users got a lifetime discount code [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s).

## Metrics

- Close to 100K registered users and close to $37K MRR (2024-11) [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=70s).
- First sale: a $90 yearly plan on the night the paid tier launched [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s).
- Monthly costs (2024-11): $5K team (3 full-time, 1 part-time, excluding Julien), $1K servers and tools, about $3K Google Ads, about $2K freelancers [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=811s), [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=872s).

## Growth channels

- Notion subreddits and Facebook groups, which tolerated links because the product was new and free [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=400s).
- Viral loop: every shared form shows the product branding and a link back [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=511s).
- Product Hunt gave traffic and a backlink but, by his account, no sales [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=463s).
- Google Ads at about $3K/month (2024-11) [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=811s).

## Timeline

- Notion API release: MVP built in 6 days [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s).
- About 1,000 users: paid tier switched on [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s).
- First year: Julien handles all support himself. Later, one support agent plus an AI chatbot [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=579s).
- 2024-11: about $37K MRR [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=70s).
