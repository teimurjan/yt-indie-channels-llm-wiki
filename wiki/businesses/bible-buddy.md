# Bible Buddy

Bible Buddy is an AI therapist app for Christians that [Lotts Ezeike](../people/lotts-ezeike.md) built and sold.

## What it is

- An AI therapist for a Christian audience ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s)).
- It is one of four apps Lotts sold since 2022 for a total of over $500K (2025-11) ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s)).

## Founders

- [Lotts Ezeike](../people/lotts-ezeike.md), a London founder who builds consumer apps to sell them ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s)).

## Business model

- Consumer app built as a "cash flow" asset for a clean sale, not a long-term company ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=229s)).

## Metrics

- 100,000 monthly active users (2025-09) ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=776s)).
- On WhatsApp, third-party fees pushed costs to about $15K/month (2025-11) ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=161s)).

## Growth channels

- **X follower targeting.** Lotts targeted followers of the 20 to 25 most famous pastors on X ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [13:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=820s)).

## Timeline

- The app first ran on WhatsApp through a third party ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=161s)).
- An Upwork engineer moved it to iOS and Android to cut WhatsApp costs ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=161s)).
- A buyer from the My First Million Facebook community bought it ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=161s)).
- About 2 years before the PrayScreen sale, the later [PrayScreen](prayscreen.md) buyer had tried to buy Bible Buddy ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1212s)).
