Carrot Weather is a paid iPhone weather app with a sarcastic character. A solo developer built it and beat Apple's free weather app.

## What it is

- A weather app that uses the same forecast data as Apple Weather. Its headline is a character with custom poses, scenery and dialogue that reacts to the weather. On a heatwave it sweats and calls the user a "meatbag" ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)).

## Founders

- Brian Mueller built it alone in 2013 and ran it solo for over a decade ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s)).

## Business model

- Paid, against a free default app ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s)).

## Metrics

- Won the 2021 Apple Design Award for Interaction ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)).

## Why it works

- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls it the "human pattern": the way you deliver the data is the product. Users know how the weather feels before they read a number ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)).
- A brand voice compounds. Competitors cannot copy a real personality in a weekend ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)).
- Personality sits in the core screen, not in empty states or loading screens ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=91s)).
