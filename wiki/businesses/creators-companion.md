Creator's Companion is a premium Notion template for YouTube creators, sold by [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md).

## What it is

- It is the system Thomas Frank's team used from 2018 to 2021 to make videos, polished and extended with extras like a repurposing dashboard ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=408s)).
- It sells beside [Ultimate Brain](ultimate-brain.md), his larger Notion template ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).

## Founders

- [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md), who also runs a productivity channel of close to 3M subscribers and [Thomas Frank Explains](thomas-frank-explains.md) (2024-04) ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).

## Business model

- One-time purchase. It launched at $99, with a $149 higher edition. The full bundle is $229 (2024-04). A price increase cut volume a little but raised total revenue ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s)).
- Templates are easy to pirate, so the paid value is gated support in a Circle community ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)).

## Metrics

- About $3K/month in its first months, then $15K/month within 6 months of launch (2021) ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- Across both templates: about $2.5M lifetime sales to about 30,000 customers (2024-04) ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).

## Growth channels

- The niche channel Thomas Frank Explains teaches only Notion. Each video pitches a paid template, a free template or an email opt-in ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)).
- An email list of 60K to 70K on the Notion list, 200K total (2024-04). The first autoresponder delivers free templates, then offers a discount ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)).
- Stack: Notion, Circle, Lemon Squeezy, ConvertKit and Pipedream ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)).

## Timeline

- 2021: launch; $15K/month within 6 months ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- 2024-04: both templates have sold about $2.5M in total ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).
