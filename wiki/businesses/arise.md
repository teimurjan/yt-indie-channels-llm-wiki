# Arise

Arise is a fitness app themed on the anime Solo Leveling, built to give fans "the system" in real life.

## What it is

- A consumer fitness app with long onboarding and social features. Rival apps earned from ads and lacked both ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [19:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1156s)).

## Founders

- [Dan Kwon](../people/dan-kwon.md), a former social media creator who earlier grew and sold [Conch AI](conch-ai.md) ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md)).

## Business model

- Subscription consumer app with 70% to 80% margins (2025-10) ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s)).

## Metrics

- About $50K/month (2025-10) ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s)).
- Influencers and meme pages cost $1 to $2 CPM, against about $6 to $7 RPM (2025-10) ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s)).

## Growth channels

- **Momentum wave into an existing cult.** It launched near the Solo Leveling season two finale. Carousels spoke to fans who wanted "the system" ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1058s)).
- **Influencers and meme pages** at low CPM ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s)).
