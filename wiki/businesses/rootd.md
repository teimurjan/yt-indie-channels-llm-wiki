Rootd is an iPhone app for panic attack and anxiety relief.

## What it is

- An app with an SOS panic button, a breathing tool, lessons on anxiety, a journal, over 100 visualizations and sleep sounds, and simple games [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=697s).
- Users feel better from a panic attack in under 2 minutes. It rates 4.8 out of 5 (2025-08) [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=643s).

## Founders

- [Ania Wysocka](../people/ania-wysocka.md), who had a panic attack in her fourth year of university and has no technical background. She paid a student developer from her savings, because an agency was too expensive [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=208s), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=259s).

## Business model

- A consumer app sold on the App Store, plus partnerships with therapy groups and B2B clients [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=571s).

## Metrics

- Over 4M downloads and over $1M revenue since the 2019 launch (2025-08) [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=59s).
- About 10K downloads in year 1, 100K in year 2 and 1M in year 3 [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=318s).
- One release a week [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=571s).

## Growth channels

- **Helpful comments.** Ania spent hours writing useful comments on anxiety posts, with content from the app and a link, not "download my app" [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=430s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=533s).
- **Press from cold pitches.** A personal story pitched to mental health writers led to Cosmopolitan, Women's Health and Time [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=491s).
- **[App Store optimization](../topics/app-store-optimization.md).** The page promises what the product delivers. Users then use the same keywords in reviews, which strengthens ranking [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=491s).

## Timeline

- Idea: competitor reviews showed no app explained panic attacks or helped in the moment [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=127s).
- 2019: launch of a rough MVP. The first few hundred users asked her to keep going despite bugs [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=259s).
- Several years at 4 days a job and 3 days on Rootd. About 2.5 to 3 years in, she went full time [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=343s), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=366s).
- 2025-08: over 4M downloads and $1M revenue [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=59s).
