CardStock is an iPhone app that scans sports cards with the camera and shows their market value. Its founder later built a copy for Pokemon cards, [Scanemon](scanemon.md).

## What it is

- The scan result shows the matched card, its market value and recent sales, which collectors care about most ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=621s)).
- Stack: Swift, SwiftUI, Core Data, RevenueCat for subscriptions, and an AWS EC2 server for the card database ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=292s)).

## Founders

- [Kyle Fowler](../people/kyle-fowler.md), a self-taught iOS developer and card collector. He wanted to catalog his own cards and found no app for it ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=400s)).
- He taught two friends to code and split revenue three ways for 2 to 3 years: "three guys in a basement making a few hundred bucks a month" ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=175s)).
- His friend Jacob, hired from Wendy's, makes the TikToks ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1466s)).

## Business model

- Launch: a $4.99 paid download ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:08:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=521s)).
- The move to subscriptions in 2021 was the biggest revenue jump. Price: $5/month, $50/year or $99 lifetime ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=685s), [0:19:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1180s)).
- Early paywall: 5 free scans, plus a paywall after onboarding with a close button and no trial. Most subscribers bought there before they used the product ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:30:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1813s)).
- After the TikTok wins around Christmas, a hard paywall took CardStock to about $30K/month ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:34:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2042s)).

## Metrics

- Launch day: 12 paid downloads, about $60 ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=556s)).
- ASO years: about $9K to $10K MRR with no marketing ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=942s)).
- CardStock plus Scanemon: about $41K MRR and about $700K lifetime revenue, no VC money (2025-11) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1165s)).
- CardStock alone: about 15,000 active subscriptions, up to about $75K MRR, about 13,000 downloads a month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=119s)).
- Both apps: $120K/month, about $1.5M/year, with a team of five (2026-06) ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s)). The two MRR figures in that video add up to about $92K, and it does not explain the gap.

## Growth channels

- **Reddit.** Kyle posted the idea in r/baseballcards and got 10 to 15 replies. The launch post linked back to the first post, so it did not read like an ad ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=292s), [0:06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=365s)).
- **App Store optimization.** CardStock was #1 for "baseball card scanner" for 2 to 3 years. The title and subtitle carry the top keywords ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=838s)). Kyle found keywords with Apple's free $100 Search Ads credit and free Sensor Tower trials ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=771s)).
- **TikTok "scan and reveal".** Show a desirable card for 3 to 5 seconds with a hook such as "what is this MJ card worth?". Then scan it and reveal the value. The goal is "what's the app?" comments ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1466s)). Silent pack-opening videos got views but no installs ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:23:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1404s)).
- **A hidden marketing setting** shows the app name inside the scanner in videos ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:39:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2361s)).
- **Paid slideshows.** Noise sends slideshow templates to creators and pays them by views: about 7,600 posts in a month for $2,600, about $1 CPM (2025-11) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:42:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2558s), [source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=566s)).

## Timeline

- Winter 2019: building starts. About 6 months from idea to launch, announced on Reddit ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=325s)).
- 2021: switch to subscriptions ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=685s)).
- 2024-05: CardStock loses the #1 keyword rank, so Kyle hires Jacob for TikTok ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=971s)).
- 2025: Kyle has Cursor's agent build the Scanemon MVP in one day ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=325s)).
- 2025-11: about $41K MRR across both apps ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1165s)).
- 2026-06: $120K/month across both apps ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s)).
