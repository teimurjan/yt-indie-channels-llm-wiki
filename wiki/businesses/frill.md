# Frill

Frill is a B2B SaaS tool for customer feedback, roadmaps and product announcements, part of [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md)'s app portfolio.

## What it is

- A feedback, roadmap and announcement tool. The captions call it "thrill.co"; it is likely Frill ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=81s)).
- Sister apps: [Curator.io](curator-io.md), [Juno](juno.md), [Fluke](fluke.md) ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=106s)).

## Founders

- [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md), a former Flash developer and agency owner. Each portfolio company has four co-founders at 25% each ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)).

## Business model

- Funded by lifetime deals, first private, then AppSumo, then MRR ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s)). See [Lifetime deals](../topics/lifetime-deals.md).

## Metrics

- About $30K from a private lifetime deal ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s)).
- The whole portfolio: just over $200K MRR (2025-11) ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s)).

## Growth channels

- **Private LTD** in Reddit, private Facebook groups, X and LTD groups ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s)).
- **Early SEO content** and "alternative to" pages paid for by LTD money ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s)).
- **Reviews and Reddit answers** on Trustpilot, G2 and competitor threads ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s)).

## Timeline

- Private LTD raised about $30K. The target is to close all LTDs with about $100K in the bank ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s)).
