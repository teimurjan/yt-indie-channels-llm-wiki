Momego is a live bus and train tracking app for over 160 cities, run by one person.

## What it is

- Live transit tracking for over 160 cities. Users often install it at a bus stop, so onboarding asks for location first, shows a few value screens with social proof, then the paywall ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=592s)).

## Founders

- [John McEvoy](../people/john-mcevoy.md), a self-taught graphic designer from Scotland. He worked for a bus company in Edinburgh and wanted to see his bus on a map ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=133s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions: a yearly plan with a 7-day free trial, a weekly plan and a lifetime plan ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=592s)).
- Reverse trial: a user who closes the paywall gets 7 days of Pro free with no payment details ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=646s)).
- Biggest costs: about 20 dedicated servers at about $2,500/month and third-party APIs at about $1,000/month (2025-10) ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=763s)).

## Metrics

- About $30K MRR, almost all from subscriptions (2025-10) ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=77s)).
- About 5.2M downloads since 2017, about 400K monthly active users and about 75K ratings (2025-10) ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=52s)).
- About 10 paywall tests in 2021 raised conversion from 0.5% to 8% and revenue from about $8K to over $30K/month ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s)).

## Growth channels

- **Hyper-local [ASO](../topics/app-store-optimization.md).** City-specific words in the title and metadata ("MTA subway", "CTA L train") and location-specific screenshots per localization ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=292s)).
- **Extra localizations.** Mexican Spanish keywords also index in the US App Store ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)).
- **Keyword discovery** with [Apple Search Ads](../topics/apple-search-ads.md) tests and App Store autocomplete ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)).
- **Ratings at the golden moment**: the first view of a live bus that moves on the map ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=414s)).

## Timeline

- 2017: launch. Designs in Illustrator, first versions in Xamarin, later rebuilt in native Swift and Flutter in 2 to 3 months ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=172s)).
- Banner ads paid about $8K/month until the pandemic killed ad revenue ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s)).
- 2020-08: subscription update ships ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=573s)).
- 2021: about 10 paywall experiments over 2 to 3 months ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s)).
- 2025-10: about $30K MRR ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=77s)).
