Cal AI is an AI calorie-tracking iPhone app: the user takes a photo of food and gets calories and macros. MyFitnessPal acquired it.

## What it is

- The camera is the home page. AI writes the food entry, and the user corrects it only by exception ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=313s)).
- The photo scan is the viral feature. Tracking is what keeps users ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1363s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a 3-day trial (2026-05) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [26:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1563s)).
- Onboarding is a quiz of more than 20 steps ([source](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=143s)), or 28 screens before the price ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s)). The quiz data personalizes the home screen ([source](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=188s)).
- A 3-screen trial explainer says "No payment due now" and promises a reminder before the charge ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=256s)).
- A high-intent app, so it keeps one price on its trial toggle ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [25:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1512s)). It must convert on day 1 ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s)).
- The rating prompt runs after free personalized feedback, so most ratings are five stars ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1225s)).

- Over $1M per month about 6 months after launch, with a few hundred thousand dollars profit per month (2024-12) ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=69s), [14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=878s)).

- $35M revenue in its first year (2026-06) ([source](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=143s)).

## Growth channels

- **Influencers at scale.** The program grew from 20 to 30 creators to 200+ posting at once, about four videos a month each. About 25 assistants sent about 2,000 cold emails a day. They contacted about 220,000 creators and signed about 2,000 to 3,000 (2026-08) ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=191s), [34:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2075s)).
- **The golden moment.** Creators get no script. They fit the food scan into a normal video and show the app in the first 15 seconds ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=373s), [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1340s)).
- **Minimum view clause.** A paid view floor a little below the creator's claim, for example 700K to 800K for a 1M claim ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=575s)).
- **What converted.** Day-in-the-life videos from big creators and mom influencers, some profitable at a $10 CPM. Recipe, cooking and mukbang creators did not convert ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [17:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1048s), [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1171s), [20:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1247s)).
- **Soft calls to action.** Videos only flash the app; viewers search the App Store by name ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=966s)).
- **Early creator pages.** [Dawson Gibbs](../people/dawson-gibbs.md) drove about 200M views in the first ~5 months and referred about 100 creators ([source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=241s)).
- **Saturation.** Later, viewers already knew Cal AI, so each video converted better and the team paid more per deal ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=739s)). Other founders say Cal AI wore out subtle fitness integrations ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).

## Retention work

- Redesigned logging, camera and food detail screens. Groups and leaderboards from Strava and Ladder, streaks from Duolingo, and shareable progress photos and badges ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1621s), [29:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1767s)).
- [Phoenix Do](../people/phoenix-do.md) says Cal AI has trigger, action and variable reward but no investment step ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=903s)).

## Influence on other apps

- [Cal Buddy](cal-buddy.md) copied it for Israel and reached over $81K/month (2026-08) ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=104s)).
- Founders copy its onboarding ([source](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s)) and its creator playbook ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=331s)). Other calorie trackers still make $30K/month beside it ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=396s)).
- Copycats can grow the category leader in App Store search ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [51:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=3108s)). Blake thinks copies cap out at $10K to $100K a month ([source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=518s)).
- Zach's brother [Evan Yadegari](../people/evan-yadegari.md) built [Locked](locked.md), a gamified self-improvement app, to over $14K/month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=152s)).
