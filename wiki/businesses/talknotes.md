TalkNotes is an app that turns voice notes into formatted text. Its founder sold it for $200K in July 2024.

## What it is

- It turns voice notes into formatted text ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).

## Founders

- [Nico Jeannen](../people/nico-jeannen.md). He built 17 apps in about a year and sold two of them, TalkNotes and [MakeLogo AI](makelogo-ai.md) ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).

## Business model

- Pricing moved from a one-year pass to a subscription of $60/year or $12/month ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s)).

## Metrics

- About $1.5K MRR from organic growth before ads ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s)).
- $77K in total sales and about 10,000 users at the sale ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).
- Nico's peak with Facebook ads: $0 to $7K MRR ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- **Facebook ads after organic traction.** Ad spend was $20 to $100/day. He started with basic static ads, then built landing pages and ads for specific audiences ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=616s)).
- **UGC-style ad.** The best ad was a paid person reading a script in Nico's office. It led with the problem and named the app only near the end ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=654s), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=714s)).

## Timeline

- Built during Nico's 17-apps year ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md)).
- Organic growth to about $1.5K MRR, then Facebook ads ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s)).
- July 2024: sold for $200K. Nico sells his apps through Acquire.com ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s), [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=752s)).
