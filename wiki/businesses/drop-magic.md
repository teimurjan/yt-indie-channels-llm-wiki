# Drop Magic

Drop Magic is an AI store builder for beginner ecommerce sellers, co-founded with a YouTube creator.

## What it is

- An AI tool that builds an online store for beginners. Each free user gets 5 AI images ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=712s)).

## Founders

- [Loic Berthelot](../people/loic-berthelot.md), a French founder who also built Infuspy and [Minea](minea.md), plus a YouTube creator as co-founder for equity ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s)).

## Business model

- SaaS subscription, 100% bootstrapped ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=64s)).
- Costs: about $100/month for servers and about $1.5K/month for LLM calls. Target margin 70% to 80% (2025-11) ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=712s)).
- Stack: Supabase, Next.js, Vercel, Sentry, Trigger.dev, Cursor and Claude Code, Nano Banana, Gemini, OpenAI and Loops ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=671s)).

## Metrics

- Over $45K MRR in its first 4 months (2025-11) ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s)).
- The creator co-founder made $10K MRR certain at launch ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s)).

## Growth channels

- **Creator as co-founder.** It removes the distribution risk. The creator is a power user who brings audience feedback. The team tests scripts, funnels, pricing and angles on the creator's channel at no cost. Those unit economics then set what to pay other creators ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=597s)).
- **YouTube creators at scale.** Over 500 creators across Minea and Drop Magic. YouTube brings over 60% of new customers (2025-11) ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=233s)).
- **Why YouTube over TikTok.** Long videos demo the app, and old videos still convert 3 years later. TikTok builds awareness but does not convert ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)).
- **Four-step creator system.** Find creators that competitors use. Qualify on views over 10% of followers and 100+ comments. Send a 7-email sequence. Pay a fixed fee plus commission, then buy 3 to 4 video packages after a break-even test ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=485s)).

## Timeline

- Loic's earlier products: Infuspy ($20K MRR in 2 months, later shut down) and Minea ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s)).
- Drop Magic launches with a creator co-founder at $10K MRR.
- 2025-11: over $45K MRR after 4 months.
