Intercom is a customer support software company. Founders in the wiki use it for support chat, and its AI agent [Fin](fin.md) is a case study in outcome-first design.

## What it is

- A support chat and help desk tool. Indie SaaS founders use it to talk to users ([source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=680s), [source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=853s)).

## Founders and leaders

- The sources name chief product officer Paul Adams, not the founders ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=247s)).

## Business model

- Software subscription. [Fin](fin.md) charges per resolved ticket, so the price follows the outcome ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=303s)).
- [Neural Frames](neural-frames.md) paid about $100/month for Intercom (2025-07) ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=599s)).

## Metrics

- Fin became Intercom's fastest-growing product ever and the company's main focus (2025-12) ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=333s)).

## How founders use it

- [SuperGrow](supergrow.md)'s team spent 1 to 2 hours a day in Intercom with lifetime-deal buyers. They did tasks by hand, then turned those workflows into features ([source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=680s)).
- [Kaching Bundles](kaching-bundles.md) runs support on Intercom. Its support team drives about 95% of its app store reviews ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=853s)).

## Timeline

- Within a week of the ChatGPT launch, Intercom paused its roadmap and built Fin ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=247s)).
- 2025-12: [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) cites Fin, with [Dia](dia.md) from [The Browser Company](the-browser-company.md), as products that replace interfaces with outcomes ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=0s)).
