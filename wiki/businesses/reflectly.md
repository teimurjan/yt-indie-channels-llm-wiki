Reflectly is a journaling app that became the base of Ryan Thorp's portfolio of 30 to 35 acquired consumer apps.

## What it is

- A journaling app launched in 2017 [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s).
- The portfolio built on it passed 50M downloads and included the habit app [Done](../businesses/done.md) and The Mindfulness App [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s).

## Founders

- [Ryan Thorp](../people/ryan-thorp.md) ran it with one media buyer and one head of content. He now builds [Flo](../businesses/flo.md), an AI analytics and agent platform for mobile apps (2026-01) [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1080s).

## Business model

- Consumer subscriptions, grown with paid ads and later with app acquisitions [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s).
- **Buy apps, not only ads.** An undermonetized app with better paywalls paid back faster than ad spend, and the asset stayed theirs [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1216s). See [acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).
- B2B wellness contracts for The Mindfulness App can reach six figures over 3 to 5 years, with 6 to 12 months to close [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2574s).

## Metrics

- Reflectly peaked at about $3M to $4M ARR, but ad spend was about the same as revenue [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=286s).
- The Done purchase paid back in 12 to 18 months [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s).
- Done was the number 1 free app in the US on January 2 and 3, two years in a row [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [24:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1442s).

## Growth channels

- **Early [UGC](../topics/ugc.md) arbitrage.** From 2018 to 2020, local micro-influencers read scripts for Facebook ads. Raw videos beat polished big-brand ads [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=518s).
- **A creator "university".** Just over 2,000 creators on the books, about 250 regular, and 1% to 2% viral stars. Pay was $30 to $100 a video plus bonuses [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [34:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2049s), [40:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2444s).
- **Three-level [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md).** Single-video tests at $10 to $20, then 4 to 5 video ad sets at $100 to $500, then 1 or 2 evergreen ads with 90% of spend [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=971s).
- **January seasonality.** About 75% of the yearly ad budget went into January, when acquisition cost is about half. The peak is the first Sunday of the year [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [19:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1166s), [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1564s).

## Timeline

- 2017: Reflectly launches [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s).
- 2018 to 2020: UGC ads on Facebook [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=518s).
- About 2020: they buy Done with 5 or 6 bundled apps. 2020 to 2025: 30 to 35 apps acquired [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=419s).
- By 2026-01: 80% to 90% of the apps sold to a buyer from gaming [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2952s).
