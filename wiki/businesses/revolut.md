This page collects what the sources say about Revolut, the finance app, as an example of emotional design.

## What it is

Revolut is a finance app. In these sources [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as one of three cases of emotional design, next to [Duolingo](duolingo.md) and [Phantom](phantom.md) ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=25s)).

## Founders

The sources do not cover the founders.

## Business model

The sources say only that Revolut sells a premium product. Its design became more polished as it moved up-market ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=545s)).

## Metrics

The sources give no Revolut metrics.

## Design lessons

- **Make premium feel tactile**. The onboarding uses rich visuals and smooth transitions. Charts glow under your finger. Cards flip, rotate and catch light in 3D ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=589s)).
- **Polish builds trust in high-stakes fields**. In finance, every micro-interaction is a trust signal ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=477s)).
- **Tim's checklist**. Polish the onboarding and welcome moments. Make charts, feedback and cards feel dynamic ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=652s)).
- Tim ends his talk on Apple's design principles with Revolut as an example of emotional design ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=722s)).
