Starcrossed is a soulmate prediction app. It predicts the date you meet your soulmate and draws their face.

## What it is

- It predicts the month and year you will meet your soulmate and draws their face ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=121s)).
- Its FAQ says each soulmate trait comes from a few preset options that fit you. The result is assembled, not discovered ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=182s)).

## Founders

- A solo founder who built it off her own TikTok. The source does not name her ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=121s)).

## Business model

- Onboarding asks for birthday, exact birth time and birth city. A "generating" loader follows, and a [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) hides the reveal ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=121s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls this "the mirror". The Barnum effect makes generic text feel personal. The effort of a long quiz makes the result feel earned ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=243s)). See [behavioral design](../topics/behavioral-design.md) and [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md).
