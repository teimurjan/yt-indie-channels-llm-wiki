AngelMatch is a paid database of about 125,000 angel investors and VCs for founders who raise money ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).

## What it is

- A subscription database with in-app email outreach through Nylas ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)).
- Sister products: [InvestorHunt](investorhunt.md) and [JournalistHunt](journalisthunt.md), a database of 200,000 journalists ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).
- Stack: Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Ahrefs, DigitalOcean, Nylas, Cloudflare, Klaviyo and Google Workspace. Tools cost about $62 a month in total ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)).

## Founders

- [Rashid Khasanov](../people/rashid-khasanov.md). For his earlier fintech app, he raised $100,000 from family and friends and built an investor list by hand ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).

## Business model

- Plans start at $59 a month and go to a few thousand dollars (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).
- Pat Walls argues directories rarely work as B2C. A founder who cannot find investors cannot make payroll, so they pay ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)).

## Metrics

- $29K MRR, 360 active subscribers, 33% trial conversion and 800 to 1,000 clicks a day (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)).
- Peak MRR was $43K ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s)).
- The three products make about $32K MRR together (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=66s)).

## Growth channels

- **Product Hunt.** A launch with a list of 40,000 investors made about $4,000 in its first month ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=353s)).
- **SEO and free tools.** He hired six content writers and built blogs and free tools. With Meta ads, this took MRR from $3K to $20K in 6 to 8 months ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s)).
- **Programmatic SEO** brings about half of revenue (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
