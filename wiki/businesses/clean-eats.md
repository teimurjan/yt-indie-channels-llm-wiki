Clean Eats is a food barcode scanner app that Kelechi built and sold within about 4 months.

## What it is

- Users scan food barcodes. Most users are women aged 18 to 28 [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=674s).

## Founders

- [Kelechi](../people/kelechi.md), a 22-year-old founder from Nigeria who moved to the US with $100. His first big app was [Social Wizard](social-wizard.md) [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=0s).

## Business model

- Consumer app. The source gives no Clean Eats price or model.
- Kelechi's apps run at over 90% margin after Apple's cut, with about 90% of spend on marketing (2025-10) [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=765s).

## Metrics

- About $10K revenue in 2 weeks [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s).
- Clean Eats and Social Wizard made $1.5M in total revenue in 12 months, with over 700K downloads (2025-10) [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=0s).

## Growth channels

- **Sell the outcome.** "This food hurts your skin" sells better than "healthy alternatives". Both of his apps sell confidence [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=674s).
- **Adjacent creators.** A fitness app can use cooking creators. They reach the same audience [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=596s).
- **Test on TikTok.** Start with 3 videos a day across 2 accounts, then 10, then 20 a day [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=533s).

## Timeline

- Launch: about $10K in the first 2 weeks [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s).
- About 4 months later: sold to a UK company [source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s).
