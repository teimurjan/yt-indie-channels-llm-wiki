ShipFast is a Next.js code boilerplate that [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md) sells to developers who want to launch a product fast.

## What it is

- A reusable code base with the setup Marc repeated in every product: landing page, payments, emails and accounts. He built it in under a week after 10 to 15 products ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s)).
- Other founders build on it. [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) used it for [Post Bridge](post-bridge.md) ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [14:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=883s)).

## Founders

- [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md), a French solo developer and former $10/hour waiter. He says he failed about 30 startups ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=184s), [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1102s)).

## Business model

- A product for developers, sold as a [productized](../topics/productized-services.md) code asset. No funding and no hires, by Marc's own rules ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s)).

## Metrics

- $500 in the first 2 hours, $3K to $4K the next day, about $40K in month one ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s)).
- Near $50K/month, with $65K in a Black Friday month ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=981s)).
- About $85K, then $135K and $135K per month, then about $80K/month (2024-08) ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s)).

## Growth channels

- **Build in public.** Marc's following from earlier launches carried over to ShipFast ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s)).
- **Launch skits.** He edited himself into a Joe Rogan podcast or a film with Leonardo DiCaprio ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s)).
- **Free tools as ads.** A free logo generator carries a ShipFast banner ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s)).
- **YouTube.** A YouTube episode helped push revenue to $135K/month ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s)).

## Timeline

- Launch: about $40K in month one ([source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s)).
