Vanman is a San Diego brand that sells handmade natural hygiene products, led by a tallow honey balm and a tooth powder.

## Founders

- [Jeremy](../people/jeremy.md), known online as "Van Man". After failed startups he sold his belongings and lived in a $2,000 Craigslist van ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Direct-to-consumer ecommerce on Shopify. Most products cost under $20. The line has balm, toothpaste, deodorant, mouthwash and hair oil, with soap next ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s), [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s)).
- The team makes every product by hand in one building and ships 500 to 600 orders a day on good days (2024-03) ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s)).
- More products raised order value and lowered shipping cost per order. Free cash flow started there ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)).

## Metrics

- About $5,000/month at the start in 2021. A first $1M month later, and more than $5M in the last year (2024-03) ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=71s)).
- Almost 40% of buyers buy again (2024-03) ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)).

## Growth channels

- Twitter posts about animal-based health and tooth powder experiments. Followers asked to buy before the product existed ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s)).
- Divisive Instagram posts and memes on fresh news photos ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=359s)).
- Email worked better than Jeremy expected ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s)).
- Jeremy answers comments and DMs himself for hours after each post ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s)).

## Timeline

- 2021: the first Shopify launch sold out about 100 tooth powders in about 20 minutes. Jeremy expected 5 to 15 ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s)).
- 2024-03: more than $5M in the last year ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=71s)).
- Plan: own cows and bison and sell premium meat at Walmart prices ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)).
