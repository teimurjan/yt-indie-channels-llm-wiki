Screens Design is a library of mobile app onboarding flows and paywalls, run by [Alex](../people/alex.md), the co-founder who sold [Boost App Social](boost-app-social.md).

## What it is

- A library of over 2,200 apps with full onboarding flows and paywalls (2026-04) ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2476s)).
- Other founders use it to study competitor flows. [Timo](../people/timo-nikolai.md) tells app makers to study other apps' onboarding with Screens Design before they run ads ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s)).

## Founders

- [Alex](../people/alex.md), co-founder of Boost App Social. Boost App Social reached about $100K MRR almost only on Meta ads and sold for seven figures (2026-04) ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=26s), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=240s)).

- It grows only through organic channels (2026-04) ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2476s)).
