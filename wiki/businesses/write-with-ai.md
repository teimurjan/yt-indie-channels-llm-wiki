This page collects what the sources say about Write With AI, a paid newsletter in Dickie Bush's writing portfolio.

## What it is

Write With AI is a paid newsletter that teaches readers to train AI writing tools ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).

## Founders

- It belongs to [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md)'s portfolio of four writing businesses. He left a hedge fund trading job at BlackRock to build it ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s)).
- Dickie partnered with Nicolas Cole on the earlier course [Ship 30 for 30](ship-30-for-30.md) ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s)).

## Business model

- Readers pay a subscription for the newsletter ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- The source does not give the price.

## Metrics

- On pace for $500K (2024-07) ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- The whole portfolio was on pace for $10M in 2024 ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).

## Growth channels

- The source does not give channels for Write With AI alone.
- The portfolio grew from Dickie's public writing. A day-27 post that Naval retweeted took his newsletter from 250 to 1,000 subscribers ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s)).
