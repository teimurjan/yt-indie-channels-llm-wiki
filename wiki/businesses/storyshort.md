StoryShort is an AI faceless video generator for TikTok and YouTube, one of three SaaS products run by Samuel Rondot.

## What it is

- An AI tool that makes faceless videos. One feature posts daily UGC-style videos about the user's product to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s)).

## Founders

- [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md), a former optician who learned to code from one 15-hour YouTube course. He also runs [useArtemis](useartemis.md) and [Capacity](capacity.md) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=72s), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).

## Business model

- SaaS subscription. Running cost about $5K/month (2025-07) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=637s)).
- Stack: Next.js, Node.js, Vercel, Stripe. Ahrefs for research and [Outrank](outrank.md) for blog posts ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=589s)).

## Metrics

- About $20K/month and about 4,000 customers (2025-07) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
- Samuel's three apps make about $35K/month together (2025-07) ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=58s)).

## Growth channels

- **Copy a competitor's paid channel.** StoryShort copied a tool whose traffic came 100% from Facebook ads. Samuel copied that traffic source in about a week ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)).
- **Ads first, then SEO**, because SEO compounds and adds value at sale time ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=695s)).
- **Faceless channels** run by its own feature ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s)).
- **Affiliates** who make YouTube videos and articles, at a known cost per customer ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=558s)).

## Timeline across sources

- Built to Samuel's rule: "never build something that doesn't already exist and isn't already successful." He ships an MVP in about two weeks ([source](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=173s), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=668s)).
- 2025-07: about $20K/month.
