This page collects what the sources say about Coconote, an AI note-taker for college students that Quizlet bought.

## What it is

Coconote is an AI note-taker and study assistant for college students. Users record a full lecture, and the app turns it into notes and study material ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [31:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1862s)).

## Founders

[Brett Bauman](../people/brett-bauman.md) and [Zack Hargett](../people/zack-hargett.md) built it. Both worked at Loom before ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=0s)).

## Business model

- It is a subscription app with a free trial and no VC money ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=0s)).
- Users record a lecture of an hour or more before they see the paywall ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [31:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1862s)).
- Users pay before they create an account ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [30:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1801s)).
- The team was 4 engineers plus 10 to 12 part-time creators at the peak ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [26:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1581s)), ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s)).

## Metrics

- $100K ARR in 45 days, $1M ARR in 4 months, and $5M ARR after a year ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [32:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1967s)).
- $6.7M ARR at the sale, with 2M+ registered users and over 50% EBITDA (2026-04) ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=139s)).
- Joseph Choi also cites $6.7M a year (2026-09) ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=437s)).
- A longer onboarding, from about 5 to about 13 screens, raised trial conversion by 16% ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s)).
- A 7-day trial extension in the cancel flow saves 27% of users who start to cancel ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1906s)).

## Growth channels

- **A small creator team**. The founders wanted "the Navy SEALs, not the Navy": a few part-time creators with freedom ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s)). See [Creator programs](../topics/creator-programs.md).
- **Views that convert**. The "PDF to brain rot" feature got 100M to 200M views but made only about $25K ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=251s)). A problem-solution video got 18M views, 99% organic ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=514s)).
- **Screens for the camera**. Engineers built eye-catching screens that only creators use on video ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=660s)).
- **Scouting**. Creators come from Handshake, DMs, email and referrals. Zack recruited his online Spanish tutor, who became a top creator for Latin America ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [16:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=967s)).
- **User words in the App Store**. The first screenshot says "great for ADHD", which matches the content and the reviews ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [25:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1526s)).

## Timeline

- Spring 2024: Shock-hook plus demo videos work while the category is new ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=607s)).
- Day 45: $100K ARR. Month 4: $1M ARR. Month 5: about $2M ARR ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2030s)).
- Year 1: $5M ARR ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [32:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1967s)).
