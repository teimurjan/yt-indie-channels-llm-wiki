This page collects what the sources say about Sober Nation, a recovery blog that grew into an SEO lead generation business.

## What it is

Sober Nation is a recovery blog. Treatment centers wanted its audience, so it became a lead generation media outlet ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).

## Founders

- [Tim Stoddart](../people/tim-stoddart.md) started it after he got sober. Seth Godin's advice led him to blog, and he found SEO by accident ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).

## Business model

- The blog sends leads to treatment centers ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- Rehab centers asked Tim how he built the audience. That question became his SEO agency, [Stuzzi](stuzzi.md) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- The first agency client paid $2,000/month ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).

## Metrics

The source gives no metrics for Sober Nation alone. Tim's whole holding company expected $5.2M to $5.5M in 2024 (2024-04) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).

## Growth channels

- **SEO, one keyword at a time**. Find what people search, then write the article that best solves that problem ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)).
- Tim says that "just being yourself" is terrible SEO advice ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)).

## Timeline

- Tim gets sober and starts a blog on Seth Godin's advice ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- The blog becomes Sober Nation and turns into a lead generation outlet ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- Rehab centers ask for help, and Stuzzi starts with the keyword "drug rehab marketing" ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- Agency cash later funds [Recovery Local](recovery-local.md) and the purchase of [Copyblogger](copyblogger.md) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)), ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=194s)).
