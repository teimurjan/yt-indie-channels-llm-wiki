Launch Fast is an AI product research tool for Amazon sellers, launched through a coaching company partner.

## What it is

- AI product research for Amazon private-label sellers, with a Chrome extension. Manual research used to take its founder 20 to 30 hours per product [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=200s).

## Founders

- [Hassam](../people/hassam.md), a non-technical founder who ran two Amazon brands beside a corporate job. He built it with Cursor in 48 hours after 10 to 12 failed AI side projects [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=0s).
- [Legacy X](legacy-x.md), an Amazon coaching company, took equity. Gus estimates about 50% (2026-01) [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=805s).

## Business model

- Subscription: $50/month for Legacy X students. The public price reads "$1.99 per month" in the captions, likely $199 (2026-01) [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s).
- Stack: Cursor, Vercel, Supabase, Resend, Apify, TypeScript and Next.js. Cursor Max at $200/month is the main cost [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=672s).

## Metrics

- About $10K MRR at day 30, $17K to $18K at day 60 and about $21.8K at day 90 (2026-01) [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s).
- The Chrome extension has 330 active users, all paying (2026-01) [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s).
- Hassam nets about $10K/month, by Gus's estimate (2026-01) [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=805s).

## Growth channels

- **Equity for distribution.** Hassam had no audience. He offered Legacy X, whose course he once bought, a better tool in 48 hours with no strings. They called the next day and told him to quit his job [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=251s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=342s).
- Legacy X launched it to its coaching students [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=0s).
