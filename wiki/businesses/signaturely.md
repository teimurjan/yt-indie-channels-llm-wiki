# Signaturely

Signaturely is a simple e-signature tool, built as a DocuSign alternative by [Will Cannon](../people/will-cannon.md).

## What it is

- A document-signing app with a simpler product than DocuSign. DocuSign was public with a lot of revenue but a complex product ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)).

## Founders

- [Will Cannon](../people/will-cannon.md), who also founded [UpLead](uplead.md). He failed at 15 to 20+ businesses before these two ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=294s)).

## Business model

- Software subscription, bootstrapped with no outside funding ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=59s)).

## Metrics

- Over 1.6M users, over $1.5M in total sales and over 800K visits per month (2025-10) ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)).

## Growth channels

- **SEO with a free tool.** A free tool lets people type or draw a signature and download it. The tool then pushes them to sign documents ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=570s)).
- **SEO with contract templates.** Many templates rank in search, such as marketing, construction and landlord contracts. Tools and templates brought the first 1,000+ customers ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=570s)).
- **Win the leader's unhappy customers.** Will's method: find a twist in the leader's 1-star and 2-star reviews ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)).

## Timeline

- 2020: launched as document signing moved online ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s)).
- 2025-10: 1.6M+ users and $1.5M+ in total sales ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)).
