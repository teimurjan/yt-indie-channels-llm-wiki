Vault Air is an iOS app that stores encrypted files and photos on the device, with no servers and no shared keys. Its founder calls it "Dropbox, but encrypted" ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s)).

## What it is

- He built it for his own crypto wallet keys and ID photos ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=342s)).
- The design makes it impossible to tell what a vault holds ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=670s)).
- Stack: Swift with UIKit and iCloud features, Codex, Claude, local models and the free tier of PostHog ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=599s)).

## Founders

- [Rasty Turek](../people/rasty-turek.md), a former Google engineer who worked on Gmail. He sold one company to Amazon and sold Pex, a VC-backed copyright service, the year before. Vault Air is his first iOS app ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=295s)).

## Business model

- $1.99/week, $19.99/year or $39.99 lifetime (2026-10) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)).
- About 60% of purchases are lifetime, and lifetime brings more than 80% of revenue. So MRR looks low ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)).
- Day-1 download-to-paid is 5.47%, and it grows by day 35. Conversion is lowest on day 1 and highest near day 60, the opposite of [Cal AI](cal-ai.md) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)).

## Metrics

- First month: $66 (2026) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=74s)).
- $41K in a single month, about 6 months after a March start (2026-10) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=7s)).
- About $2K to $3K per day, and downloads passed 60,000 (2026-10) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=74s), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s)).

## Growth channels

- **SEO, not the App Store.** Competitors are 15 or more years old, and Apple ranks them first. Rasty targets the same keywords on the web with how-to guides ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=392s)). No TikTok and no paid ads ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=7s)).
- **A daily SEO agent.** Each morning, Claude reads Keyword Planner and search data. It finds keywords with impressions but no clicks, then writes and publishes one guide ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=479s)).
- **CTAs where readers stop.** App links sit where readers usually end an article. Web traffic converts to the app at about 10% (2026-10) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=452s)).
- Gus still thinks SEO is mostly dead. He says it works here because the niche is narrow ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=842s)).
