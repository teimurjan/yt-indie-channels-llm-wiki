Creator Hunter is a SaaS database that matches startups with influencers. A founder who cannot code well built it with AI tools.

## What it is

- An influencer database for startups ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=59s)).

## Founders

- [Paulius Masalskas](../people/paulius-masalskas.md). He did door-to-door sales at 18, then web design, copywriting and funnels. He studied game development but "completely sucked at code" ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=85s)).

## Business model

- Paid SaaS with about 90% margin. Vercel and Supabase are on free tiers. The main cost is a scraping API (2025-05) ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=657s)).
- Built on his commute with Perplexity for planning, Bolt for the MVP and Cursor for production, plus Supabase and Clerk ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=223s), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=286s)).

## Metrics

- Over 1,000 users, over 350 paid users and $30K total revenue about 7 months after launch (2025-05) ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=59s)).
- Launch night brought about 20 to 30 sales ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=187s)).

## Growth channels

- **Build in public on X**, joined to a bigger debate: can AI coding build a full SaaS? His best post got close to 500K impressions ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=554s)).
- He adapted a post format from [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md) ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=607s)).
- **Landing page from a free Framer template**, with 80% of design time above the fold ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=440s), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=475s)).

## Timeline across sources
