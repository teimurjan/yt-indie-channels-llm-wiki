Fitted is an AI closet app. It digitizes your clothes, suggests outfits and supports resale ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=572s)).

## What it is

- Over 2M clothing items uploaded (2025-11) ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s)).
- Partnerships: Paramount (the official virtual closet for Clueless), a Poshmark one-click resale integration, and TaskRabbit taskers who catalog closets ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1340s)).
- FitCoin, its crypto token, trades close to $250K in volume a day (2025-11) ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [18:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1127s)).

## Founders

- [Reid Moncada](../people/reid-moncada.md). Fitted began 8 years earlier as his college laundry service in 18 cities ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=38s)).
- [Max](../people/max.md), co-founder and creator. Reid found him through a viral TikTok of an outfit generator Max built in class. He first paid Max $20 a video, then gave him vested equity and a revenue share ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1624s), [27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1662s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions behind a hard paywall (2025-11). Reid says the paywall limits growth and churn is high. The next updates make the app free with paywalled features ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s), [23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1433s)).
- FitCoin aims to join consumers, brands and speculators in one flywheel, like Dupe's token and Duolingo's gems ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [20:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1233s)).

## Metrics

- Over 500M lifetime views, 600K downloads and about $150K in subscription revenue (2025-11) ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=572s), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=600s)).
- Ad spend goes behind organic videos that already went viral. Downloads then cost 1 to 15 cents each (2025-11) ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)).
- One format hit 136M views, and a repeat hit 60M. They get a viral video about once a month ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=859s)).
- Reid says distribution is solved, but the product has no product-market fit yet ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1433s)).

## Growth channels

- **One creator account.** About 95% of distribution came from Max, cross-posted to the main account and Instagram Reels ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=574s)). It copies Breathwrk, which grew a TikTok account to about 3M followers with an equity creator ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)).
- **Show the product, do not sell it.** The breakout video was a plain "how to use the Fitted closet" ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=408s)).
- **Test features on TikTok.** "Stalking my boyfriend's closet" hit as a web-only feature, so it went into the mobile roadmap ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [15:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=911s)).
- **Boost winners with ads** ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s)).

## Timeline

- A friend funded a week in a Palm Springs apartment with $10K. It produced a 2M-view video on day one and a 100K-person waitlist ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=214s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s)).
- The web app did not convert. Reid paid $1,400 for a 15-minute call with Nikita Bier, who told him to go mobile. The mobile app took 6 months ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=336s)).
- 2025-11: $150K lifetime revenue. Plans to drop the hard paywall and grow FitCoin ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=600s), [23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1433s)).
