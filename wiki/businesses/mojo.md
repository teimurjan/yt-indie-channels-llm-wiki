Mojo is a video and story editor app. Sources cite it for a paywall test and for its app-preview video style.

## What it is

- A mobile video and story editor that sells subscriptions and AI credit packs ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=0s)).

## Founders

- No source names the founders.

## Business model

- Subscriptions, plus AI credit packs that it later removed from the first paywall ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)).

## Metrics

- Passed $1M/month in 2024 ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=0s)).
- Hiding AI credit packs on the first paywall raised new subscription revenue by 14%, with nothing else changed (2026-01). The result was clear within a week ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)).

## Lessons from Mojo

- **Remove escape hatches.** Credit packs beside the subscription turned one choice into a comparison. Users who would have subscribed bought small packs instead ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md) and [pricing](../topics/pricing.md).
- **App-preview video style.** [Jonathan Parra](../people/jonathan-parra.md) records the app on his phone, drops it into Rotato, and takes the style from Mojo ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [43:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2638s), [45:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2737s)).

## Timeline

- 2024: Passed $1M/month ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=0s)).
- 2026-01: Removed credit packs from the first paywall; +14% new subscription revenue ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)).
