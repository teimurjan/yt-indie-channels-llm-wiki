Photogenic is an AI photo app that grew through one hired UGC creator.

## What it is

- An AI photo app. Videos of the AI failing, such as a person with a third leg, drove engagement ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1472s)).

## Founders

The source does not name the founder.

The source does not describe it.

## Metrics

- About 600 applicants for one creator role. 46 posts drew about 35M views. Weekly downloads grew about 10x. App Store page conversion rose from 5% to 10% to over 50% (2025-07) ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1292s), [22:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1353s), [23:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1414s)).

## Growth channels

- **One UGC creator hired on proof.** The founder picked a creator with past viral posts and her own ideas. Her ideas beat his, so he let her run the content ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1292s), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1472s)).
- The hire went through [Sideshift](sideshift.md), the UGC job marketplace whose co-founder [Canyon](../people/canyon.md) tells the story to [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [21:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1292s)).
