Moonly is a subscription app that Tim Gabe uses as a free-trial A/B test case.

## What it is

- A subscription app with monthly and annual plans. The source gives no more detail on the product [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s).

## Founders

- The source does not name them.

## Business model

- Subscriptions, with the free trial on the annual plan only [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s).

## Metrics

- A free trial on the annual plan only gave +39% conversion and +47% revenue per 100 installs, as reported by [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) (2025-07) [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s).

## Growth channels

- Paywall design: give the best plan an exclusive perk so it looks like the obvious choice [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s).
