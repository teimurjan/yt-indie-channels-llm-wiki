Waitly is a solo-run waitlist and reservations app for restaurants and other businesses with waiting customers.

## What it is

- The guest gets a text with a link and sees the wait time with no app download and no account ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=326s)).
- It competes with Yelp and OpenTable on price, simplicity and support ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)).

## Founders

- [Joe Rozek](../people/joe-rozek.md), a former Shutterfly engineering manager. The idea came at brunch in 2017 ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s)).

## Business model

- Flat $100/month for all features, against hundreds per month plus cover fees at Yelp and OpenTable (2025-09) ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)).
- Trials get tip emails, quick-start videos, optional Zoom onboarding and an end-of-trial promotion ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)).
- Biggest costs: text messages about $2,500/month, Firebase about $700, Apple Search Ads about $500 (2025-09) ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=578s)).

## Metrics

- Over $40K/month (2025-09) ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=59s)).
- Over 15,000 businesses tried it; 700 paying customers in the US and Canada; over 59M parties waitlisted (2025-09) ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=531s)).
- Apple Search Ads CAC about $130; LTV $750 to $1,000 (2025-09) ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)).

## Growth channels

- **[Apple Search Ads](../topics/apple-search-ads.md).** With LTV at 6x to 8x CAC, each extra dollar returns profit ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)).
- **Fast human support.** Someone answers every support call at once ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)).
- **Cross-industry use.** Retail chains put it in over 700 locations during COVID; that year grew 10x ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s)).

## Timeline

- Prototype at 6 months; App Store launch at about 1.5 years with 10 downloads in week one; in-app purchases at about 2 years ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s)).
- Yearly revenue: $0, $14,500, ~$116K, $186K, $307K, $354K, $445K; on pace for over $500K (2025-09) ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=242s)).
