# Copy MBA

Copy MBA is a copywriting education program built by [Cardinal Mason](../people/cardinal-mason.md) after his freelance and agency work.

## What it is

- An education program that teaches people to become freelance copywriters for small businesses ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)).
- Its pitch: the US has 35M small businesses and only about 45K to 50K freelance copywriters. Four to six clients can pay six figures a year ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=163s), [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=211s)).

## Founders

- [Cardinal Mason](../people/cardinal-mason.md), a college dropout who packed boxes for $10/hour and taught himself copywriting. He made $2.3M in under 3 years, by age 21 (2023-08) ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Info product. Mason's day is mostly TikTok videos, livestreams and student calls ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=691s)).
- It teaches retainers over one-off gigs: $2K to $4K/month, and one email a week for about $800/month ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=630s)).

## Metrics

- $1M in 96 days (2023-08) ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)).
- Before Copy MBA: freelance peak of $48K/month, then an email agency at "multiple six figures a year" (2023-08) ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)).

## Growth channels

- **Short-form content and livestreams** on TikTok ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=691s)).
- **The founder's own proof.** The method it teaches is the one he used: find companies that run Instagram ads, find the founder's email with Hunter.io, and send a cold email ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s), [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=365s)).

## Timeline

- First gig: a music blog at 1.5 cents a word. First real client: $2,500/month for emails, ads and blogs ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)).
- Freelance peak: $48K/month, which he says was not sustainable. A freelancer tops out at about $30K to $40K/month ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=522s)).
- Email marketing agency.
- Copy MBA: $1M in 96 days (2023-08).
