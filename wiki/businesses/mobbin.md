Mobbin is a searchable library of screenshots and flows from real apps. Designers and app builders use it as reference material.

## What it is

- The library has 1,000+ apps (2025-04). You can filter by app, user flow or UI element and search the text inside screens. [qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md) [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=174s)
- You can save screens to collections, add comments, or copy screens into Figma with one click. [qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md) [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=235s)
- It has an MCP server. Tim Gabe says a founder can plug it into an AI agent and get a decent design in a day (2026-10). The auto captions spell it "Marvin's MCP". [8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=62s)

## Business model

Freemium with a paid Pro tier. "Pro" labels on locked content across the product raised free-to-paid conversion by 35% (reported 2025-07). [puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=375s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=437s)

## How founders use it

- **Start every UI project here.** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) says to use Mobbin instead of making dummy accounts and taking screenshots. [qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md) [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=174s)
- **Clone a proven app.** [David Attias](../people/david-attias.md) grabs every screenshot of a successful app from Mobbin or screens.design and imports it into Figma. About 80% of screens come in near perfect. [orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=348s). He used this to build [Stoppr](stoppr.md).
