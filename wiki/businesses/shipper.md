Shipper is an AI app builder that makes web apps, mobile apps, Chrome extensions and bots for people with no technical skills.

## What it is

- An AI app builder in the same market as [Lovable](lovable.md) and Base44. It promises to turn an idea into a live business with zero skills. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=485s), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=529s))

## Founders

- [David](../people/david.md) and his brother Daniel, non-technical founders who learned marketing instead of code. Their first success, Legit Check, a luxury goods authentication service, funded later projects. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=177s))
- The team at the start was the two brothers and one developer. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=300s))

## Business model

- Credit-based subscriptions with no free plan. The cheapest plan is $25/month. Builder credits cover building, and cloud credits cover back-ends. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=53s), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=99s))
- 90% of revenue is subscriptions and 10% is one-off credit top-ups (2026-05). ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=99s))

## Metrics

- $25.6K MRR, about $307K ARR, about 690 paid users (2026-05). ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=99s))
- Gross volume about $71K for the month, because of credit top-ups (2026-05). The title says $50K/month. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=99s))

## Growth channels

No paid ads. The channels came in this order: ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s))

1. A Product Hunt launch in week 1 gave the first $50 MRR.
2. Reddit posts with about 400 upvotes took it from $50 to $1K MRR.
3. SEO on high-intent keywords such as "<competitor> alternatives" and "how much does <competitor> cost".
4. Building in public on X went parabolic at about day 50, with about $20K MRR in 1 to 2 weeks. The product link goes in the second tweet of a thread, a tip from [Rob Hallam](../people/rob-hallam.md).

- **Mining competitor complaints.** They read rivals' Trustpilot pages, public roadmaps and Discord servers. Requests to turn websites into mobile apps led them to add mobile apps. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=485s))

## Timeline

- Month 3: a working product. Month 4: level with competitors. Months 5 and 6: ahead on features. The first version broke a lot, but they shipped it anyway. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=300s))
- 2026-05: $25.6K MRR. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=99s))
