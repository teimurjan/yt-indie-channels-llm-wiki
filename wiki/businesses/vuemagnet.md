VueMagnet is a QR code and lead-magnet platform for video creators, built by product designer Gaurabh Mathure.

## What it is

- A QR code and lead-magnet platform for video creators [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=100s).
- An agency plan lets agencies manage several creators [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=629s).

## Founders

- [Gaurabh Mathure](../people/gaurabh-mathure.md), a product designer who built products for Nike and Samsung. He built 72 apps in 60 days and made $0 before the Starter Story Challenge. He also runs [Attention Design](../businesses/attention-design.md) [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=65s), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=100s).

## Business model

- Paid plans, with an agency tier that brought most revenue (2026-08) [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=629s).

## Metrics

- Gaurabh's two products made about $6,500 in the 60-day challenge. Most of it came from VueMagnet's agency plan (2026-08) [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=629s).
- Next goal: hold about $6K a month for 6 months [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=788s).

## Growth channels

- **[Personal brand](../topics/personal-brand.md) on LinkedIn.** Posting on every platform showed LinkedIn as the signal. Vlog-style video broke the pattern and got the most impressions [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s).
- **A narrower customer.** He moved from YouTubers to experts who want a personal brand on LinkedIn: "where experts become creators" [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=729s).
- **Unscalable outreach.** Cold messages with free mock-ups and personalized demos [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=550s).

## Timeline

- Days 1 to 30 of the challenge: posts on X, LinkedIn, Reddit and YouTube brought one $99 sale, of Attention Design [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s).
- Days 31 to 60: LinkedIn video and the new audience. About $6,500 by day 60 (2026-08) [source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s).
