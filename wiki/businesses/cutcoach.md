# CutCoach

CutCoach is an iOS app that gives combat sport athletes a plan to cut weight before a weigh-in.

## What it is

- The app logs meals with AI nutrition extraction, tracks daily weight and suggests meals that fit the limits ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=654s)).
- Version 1 had coaches give plans to athletes. His own wrestling club did not use it. In July and August 2025 he changed it, so the app gives the plan to the athlete ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)).

## Founders

- [Ethan](../people/ethan.md), 19, a former national wrestling champion. He left a computer engineering degree after AI tools built in a day what took him a week ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md)).

## Business model

- Subscription, about $30. Athletes pay because missing weight costs far more ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=780s)).
- In wrestling season, athletes must make weight, so revenue peaks ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=101s)).
- Stack: Cursor, ChatGPT, Supabase, Vercel, the OpenAI API, RevenueCat, Mixpanel and Superwall ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=477s)).

## Metrics

- $0 to over $60K total revenue in 6 months, with about 38K to 39K downloads. Best month: over $20K (2026-04) ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=71s), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=101s)).
- Early organic posts got 200 to 500 views each but brought 10 to 15 downloads a day ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=508s)).

## Growth channels

- **Small, high-intent organic posts.** A before-and-after UFC weight cut video with a call to action. Pat Walls estimates a 5% to 10% view-to-install rate ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=508s), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=562s)).
- **Micro-creators by DM.** Ethan found them on his For You page and Reels. Most said yes. He started at 1K to 10K views per video, then moved to 20K+ ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=585s)).
- **Influencer videos as paid ads** ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=585s)).

## Timeline

- Summer 2024: Ethan's first app takes 6 months. Early 2025: he builds several apps and markets none ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=154s)).
- 2025-06: CutCoach MVP in about one month with Cursor and ChatGPT ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)).
- 2025-07 to 2025-08: pivot from coach-first to athlete-first.
- 2025-09: launch.
- 2026-04: over $60K total revenue, best month over $20K.
