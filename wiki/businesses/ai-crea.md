# AI Crea

AI Crea is an AI home-staging tool: a user uploads a room photo, picks a style, and gets a redesigned room in seconds.

## What it is

- AI image generation for real estate agents ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md)).
- Stack: Next.js app, Python AI models, Supabase, Crisp, Brevo ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=541s)).

## Founders

- [Pauline](../people/pauline.md), an ex-IBM data scientist. She built it on nights and weekends and left IBM in June 2024 ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s)).

## Business model

- Free trial, then a one-time photo pack or a monthly plan with extra features (2024-10) ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s)).
- She offers both subscription and one-time payment because some people hate subscriptions ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s)).

## Metrics

- More than 8,000 users and about 8,000 per month in revenue (2024-10). The video description says $100K/year ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s)).
- Running costs about 3,000 to 4,000 EUR/month (2024-10) ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=541s)).

## Growth channels

- **Direct sales.** She showed the MVP to real estate agents, and some paid right away ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=204s)).
- **Facebook groups and in-person events**, because real estate is a human business ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=348s)).
- **Product Hunt for the backlink**, plus Twitter and her newsletter ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=404s)).

## Timeline

- First MVP in about a month, with bad image quality. A year of work followed ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=243s)).
- She cut churn with a yearly plan, a free trial and better output quality ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s)).
- June 2024: Pauline goes full-time ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=134s)).
