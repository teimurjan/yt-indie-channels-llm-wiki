Go Polar is an iOS app that tracks cold plunge and sauna sessions. It is the first app of Presh's studio, The Wellness Company.

## What it is

- It tracks cold plunge and sauna sessions with an Apple Watch or iPhone ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s)).
- No good app tracked cold plunges, so Presh built one for himself ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=291s)).

## Founders

- [Presh](../people/presh.md), who worked 7 years for an angel investor before he built apps ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions, with RevenueCat for paywalls ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=697s)).
- It shares one audience with [SunSeek](../businesses/sunseek.md) and [Posture AI](../businesses/posture-ai.md). Each new app markets to existing users ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)). See [app portfolios](../topics/app-portfolios.md).

## Metrics

- The source gives numbers for the whole studio only.
- Three apps: $10.4K/month, $120K ARR, over 20,000 downloads and over 1,500 subscribers (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).
- About 8% free-to-paid and 61% day-30 retention of paid users (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).

## Growth channels

- Mostly organic App Store search ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)). See [App Store optimization](../topics/app-store-optimization.md).
- Cross-promotion to the shared audience of the studio's other apps ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)).

## Timeline

- First app of the studio. Later apps were better on day one because of the reps from Go Polar ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=574s)).
- 2025-12: the three apps reached $120K ARR in about 10 months ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s)).
