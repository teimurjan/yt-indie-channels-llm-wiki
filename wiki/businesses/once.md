Once is a disposable camera app for events such as weddings and parties: guests get a few shots each and see the shared album later.

## What it is

- A digital copy of a disposable camera. The limits (no instant preview, few shots) make the experience feel real ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=266s)).

## Founders

- [Brian Shin](../people/brian-shin.md) and his girlfriend, who is his co-founder. Brian earlier co-founded a VC-backed, 50-person B2B startup. Once is bootstrapped ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=202s)).

## Business model

- Usage-based pricing by guest count and shots per person: $2 for a 10-person party, $50 for a 150-guest wedding ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=685s)).

## Metrics

- $20K monthly revenue 83 days after launch. About $22K this month. 10,000 to 12,000 weekly active users (2026-05) ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=56s), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)).
- Over 300 events in February and about 700 booked for March (2026) ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=141s)).

## Growth channels

- **Commitment metric.** Before app code, Brian required 10 real events with fixed dates ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=510s), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s)). See [idea validation](../topics/idea-validation.md).
- **Personal network.** Four friends said yes: a Halloween party, a birthday, a wedding and a networking event ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s)).
- **Instagram hashtag prospecting.** About 250 to 300 cold pitches gave about 15 replies and about 12 events in one month ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=480s)).

## Timeline

- A throwaway web prototype, built in 1 to 2 weeks for a friend's Halloween party, proved that guests love a shared album ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=266s), [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=314s)).
- The app was then designed by hand in Figma and built with Claude Code, Conductor, Supabase and Vercel ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=349s), [6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=395s)).
- December 2025: Launch ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=56s)).
- 2026-05: About $22K/month ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)).
