SolCard is a crypto neobank card whose website and app were redesigned by [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md)'s studio [Zipsnap](zipsnap.md). The captions spell the name several ways.

## What it is

- A crypto-funded payment card. Most payments go through Apple Pay or Google Pay, so users open the app mainly to check their money ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=280s)).
- The client had used other agencies before. The old site felt dated and rushed ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=48s)).

## Metrics

- After the redesign, money loaded onto cards rose 79% and successful deposits rose 71% (2026-10) ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=0s), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)).

## Redesign choices

- The hero ends on plain promises: card issued in seconds, no annual fees, refund anytime. It shows the card in hotels, taxis and groceries ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=108s)).
- The card is the thread across site, sign-up and app, to use the endowment effect ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=170s)).
- Fees show before payment. Clear warnings replace vague ones ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=219s), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=280s)).
- 15 status screens explain waits, such as processing, on hold and declined ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)).
- Freeze card, payment locks, limits and show PIN are each one tap away. Users can hide the balance ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=396s), [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=439s)).
- Human support through WhatsApp, WeChat, Telegram or email ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [8:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=500s)).
