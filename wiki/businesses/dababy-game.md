The DaBaby game is a meme mobile game about the DaBaby "car head" meme. Rafael Kramer built it at 14, and it made more than $100K in one month from ads.

## What it is

- A no-code mobile game built in Buildbox that let players "be the meme" ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=465s), [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=507s)).

## Founders

- [Rafael Kramer](../people/rafael-kramer.md), who started making meme games at 13 and later hired Unity developers ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=548s)).

## Business model

- Google AdMob only: an ad every second death and a 30-second rewarded ad to revive. About 10 cents per user. Good modern games earn 80 cents to $1 ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=562s), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=591s)).

## Metrics

- About 1.5M to 2M downloads in about a month and more than $100K that month (2020 to 2021). About #2 overall on the App Store ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=378s), [7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=438s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok dev logs.** Only 9 TikToks at 100K to 1M views each drove the first 300K to 400K downloads. Sharing and the charts drove the rest ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=739s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=758s)).
- Rafael A/B tested hooks and frames, and viewers sent feature ideas in comments ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=659s)).

## Timeline across sources

- 2020 to 2021: launch and the $100K month.
- Afterwards Rafael ran an influencer game company and built [Escape the Toilet](escape-the-toilet.md) and [Debloat AI](debloat-ai.md) ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s), [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s)).
