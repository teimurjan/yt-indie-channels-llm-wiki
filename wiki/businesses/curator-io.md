Curator.io is a social media aggregator for websites and events, and one of the apps in Mike Hill's bootstrapped B2B SaaS portfolio.

## What it is

- A social media aggregator that shows social posts on websites and at events. ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=81s))

## Founders

- Part of [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md)'s portfolio. Mike is an Australian former Flash developer who ran and sold a digital ad agency. ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=138s))
- Each portfolio company has four co-founders at 25% each. ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s))

## Business model

- B2B SaaS. Portfolio companies grow to about $10K MRR to cover costs. Above that, profits go to the founders. ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s))

## Metrics

- The source gives no Curator.io figures. The portfolio makes just over $200K MRR (2025-11). ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s))

## Growth channels

- The portfolio playbook: copy a proven idea, sell lifetime deals (private, then AppSumo), spend that cash on SEO and "alternative to" pages, collect reviews on Trustpilot and G2, and answer competitor questions on Reddit. ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s))
