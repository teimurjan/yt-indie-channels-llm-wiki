ToneAdapt is a web and iOS app for guitarists. It tells them how to set their own guitar, amp and pedals to sound like a given song ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=69s)).

## What it is

- The database has over 1,500 guitars and 2,000 amps. A result takes under 30 seconds ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=747s)).
- Web stack: Supabase, Vercel, Mailgun, Stripe, the OpenAI API, the Tavily web search API and Claude Code ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=328s)).
- iOS stack: native Swift, Vercel API routes, Supabase, RevenueCat and Superwall ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=375s)).

## Founders

- [Kyan](../people/kyan.md), a 21-year-old computer science student at San Diego State University. It is his first app ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=69s)).
- He used ChatGPT for guitar tones and found it inaccurate. Guitarists on Reddit and in Instagram comments had the same problem ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=226s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions at $10/week or about $60/year ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=108s)).
- It charged from day one ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=611s)).

## Metrics

- Over 100K users in under 5 months (2026-06) ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=69s)).
- Over $25K in the past 4 weeks: about $11.5K on the web and about $14K on mobile (2026-06) ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=108s), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=146s)).
- Mobile: 397 active subscribers in its first month. Web: $45K and 1,400 new subscribers in 3 months (2026-06) ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=146s)).
- Pat Walls says it ranks #2 after sponsored results for "guitar tone" in the App Store (2026-06) ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=698s)).

## Growth channels

- **Face-on short-form video.** Kyan posts 3 times a day on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook, and shows the product solving a problem in real time. He learned the skill doing UGC for brands ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=416s)).
- **Scale the winner.** Remake a converting video 50 times. Pay UGC creators to copy it 60 times a month. Put Meta and TikTok ad spend behind it ([source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=670s)).
