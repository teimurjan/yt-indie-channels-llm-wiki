Social Wizard is an AI texting and social-skills app for young men, built by [Kelechi](../people/kelechi.md).

## Founders

- [Kelechi](../people/kelechi.md), a 22-year-old founder from Nigeria who moved to the US with $100. He got the idea from a dating problem and an OpenAI script that wrote texts ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=0s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=168s)).

## Business model

- Subscription: about $10/week, $20/month or $80/year. Users are mostly young men aged 16 to 24 ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=674s)).
- Margins over 90% after Apple's cut. Infrastructure $1K to $2K/month (2025-10) ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=765s)).
- Stack: React Native, NestJS on Render or Heroku, Firebase and Mixpanel ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=737s)).

## Metrics

- Peaked at about $60K/month. Over 600K downloads (2025-10) ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s)).
- With his second app [Clean Eats](clean-eats.md): $1.5M total revenue in 12 months and over 700K downloads (2025-10) ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=21s)).
- He says he spent $0 on marketing in the last year and it still makes money (2025-10) ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=184s)).

## Growth channels

- Paid collabs with micro streamers. The format: a streamer puts a girl's Instagram story into the app and reads the AI reply. Nobody pitches the app ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=312s)).
- One collab cost about $120, got 2M views and brought in tens of thousands of dollars ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=358s)).
- Kelechi found the format himself on TikTok, then gave it to streamers with 100K to 200K views. Expect about 100 creators to get one that works ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=472s)).
- Streamers posted Fortnite and reaction clips, adjacent to his audience ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=596s)).

## Timeline

- Early 2024: launch. First sale of about $10 in late January ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=184s)).
- After 4 weeks of tests: a viral format. MRR $3.5K 2 weeks later, over $25K after 1 month ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=184s)).
- Cash collected: about $250K by month 6, about $500K by end of 2024 ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=184s)).
