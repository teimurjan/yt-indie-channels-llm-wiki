Parakeet AI is a real-time AI job interview assistant built by Jure Sotošek.

## What it is

- An assistant that listens to a job interview and suggests answers in real time. A spin-off, Parakeet AI Apply Agent, applies to jobs for the user. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s))

## Founders

- [Jure Sotošek](../people/jure-sotosek.md), a 26-year-old developer who left Microsoft. He runs four businesses. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=203s))

## Business model

- One-time credits, not a subscription. A banner says "no subscription, one-time payment" because rivals charge monthly. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=756s))
- Stack: Next.js, React, Speechmatics for transcription and ChatGPT for answers. Transcription costs $2K to $3K/month and AI $1K to $2K/month (2025-04). ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=836s))

## Metrics

- Over $35K in the last 30 days, about 6 months after launch (2025-04). ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s))
- Apply Agent: about $1,000 in its first two weeks (2025-04). ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s))

## Growth channels

- Jure bets on organic short-form video, because he thinks most other channels are saturated. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=355s))
- His team writes 3 hooks, 5 main parts and 3 endings, then films the combinations. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=631s))

## Timeline

- About 2024-10: launched. Jure turned a proof of concept into the product, with a landing page, in about two weeks. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=427s))
- 2025-04: over $35K in 30 days, and the Apply Agent launched. ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s))
