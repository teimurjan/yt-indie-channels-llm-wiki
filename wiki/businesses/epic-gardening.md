Epic Gardening is a gardening media brand that grew into a product company selling gardening goods.

## What it is

- Kevin Espiritu calls it the world's most followed gardening brand. Its headquarters is his San Diego homestead, where the team films, tests products and grows seeds [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=61s).

## Founders

- [Kevin Espiritu](../people/kevin-espiritu.md). He started gardening with his brother around 2013 and registered a blog for the hobby [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=1s).

## Business model

- Free education (blog, video, social) builds the audience. Products sold on Shopify are the real business. Kevin chose to be the brand that sponsors his own content instead of taking brand deals [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s).

## Metrics

- Blog: about $400/month at first, $2K to $3K/month after 2 to 3 months full time, $4K to $5K/month later in 2016 [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s).
- Revenue by year: $75K (2017), $225K (2018), $540K (2019), $2.8M (2020), $7.3M (2021) [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s).
- 2019 split: about $250K media and $250K product [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s).
- "Tens of millions a year" (2024-03); the video description says over $30M/year [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=1s).

## Growth channels

- SEO content: about 12 hours a day writing researched articles, plus forum comments with links [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s).
- Audience-led products: viewers kept asking about a metal raised bed in his garden, so he imported it. One Instagram story and a basic Shopify store sold it out in 2 weeks [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s).

## Timeline

- ~2013: hobby gardening and a blog [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=1s).
- 2016: full-time blogging at $4K to $5K/month [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s).
- 2019: first product drop, the raised bed; product revenue matches media revenue [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s).
- 2021: $7.3M revenue [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s).
