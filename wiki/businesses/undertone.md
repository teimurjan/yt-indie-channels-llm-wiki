Undertone is an app that records therapy sessions and turns them into a recap. Tim Gabe's studio, ZipSnap, redesigned it.

## What it is

- The user records a therapy session, and the app makes a recap. A recap takes 3 to 5 minutes to read ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=0s), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=373s)).
- Processing steps: transcribing, anonymizing and summarizing ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=665s)).
- Brand: pistachio and sage on off-white, with a charcoal that is never fully black. The style sits between "clinical and minimal" ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [7:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=447s)).

## Founders

- The sources do not name the founders. They admired Apple's packaging, which shaped the recap design ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s)).
- Design partner: [ZipSnap](zipsnap.md), led by [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Session-limited plans. The old [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) half-blurred the user's own recaps, which feels like a loss. The redesign removes the blur and asks one question: "How many sessions a month do you need?" ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)).
- The old app had two different "used all your sessions" screens with no count or date. The redesign uses one pattern: a "2/2 sessions used" badge and the exact refresh date ([source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=311s)).

## Metrics

- Onboarding took 10+ minutes and still beat the industry average. About half of the users who finished it never recorded a session (2026-10) ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=56s)).
- Test recordings averaged about 20 seconds and hit a "too short" dead end. A founder guesses it lost about 10% of users ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=177s)).
- Users opened the recap a median of 10 minutes after a session. The range was 6 to 44 minutes ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=344s)).
- Some users forgot to stop recording, which made 4- to 6-hour files that broke transcription ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=706s)).

## Growth channels

- The sources cover [retention](../topics/retention.md) and design, not acquisition.
- Redesign moves ([source](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=562s), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=665s), [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=942s)):
  - The mic test moved into [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md). A demo recap replaced the "too short" wall.
  - A Dynamic Island Live Activity shows each processing step and stops runaway recordings.
  - The recap arrives as an "unboxing" with tape, confetti and a haptic. The prompt to book the next session sits inside that peak.
- Users wanted "anything instead of nothing" after a short mic test ([source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md), [2:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=160s)).
