SunSeek is an iOS app that tracks morning light and vitamin D, part of The Wellness Company app studio.

## What it is

- It tracks morning light and vitamin D and has a lux meter ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s)).
- One of three live apps in [The Wellness Company](../businesses/the-wellness-company.md), with [Go Polar](../businesses/go-polar.md) and [Posture AI](../businesses/posture-ai.md) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s)).

## Founders

- [Presh](../people/presh.md), who worked 7 years for an angel investor before he started ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)).

## Business model

- [Subscriptions](../topics/subscriptions.md) through RevenueCat. Tools for the studio cost about $300/month, and margins are 80% to 85% (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=736s)).

## Metrics

- Not given for SunSeek alone. The three apps together: $120K ARR, $10.4K/month, over 1,500 paying subscribers, about 8% free-to-paid (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).

## Growth channels

- Mostly organic App Store search ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).
- Cross-promotion to the same health audience as the other studio apps ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)). See [app portfolios](../topics/app-portfolios.md).

## Timeline

- SunSeek came after Go Polar and was better on day one because of the earlier reps ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=574s)).
- 2025-12: the studio reaches $120K ARR about 10 months after the first launch ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s)).
