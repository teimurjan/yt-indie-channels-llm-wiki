Cal Buddy is an AI calorie tracker in Hebrew for the Israeli market, a local copy of [Cal AI](cal-ai.md).

## What it is

- Photo in, calories and macros out, like Cal AI. The changes are language and how Israelis eat and talk about food ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=342s)).

## Founders

- [Tomer](../people/tomer.md), a marketer with no developer background. He heard [Zach Yadegari](../people/zach-yadegari.md)'s Cal AI podcast in March 2025 and copied the idea for Israel ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=143s), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=249s)).

## Business model

- A subscription app. Stack and monthly cost (2026-08): Claude $200, PostHog $20, RevenueCat, Supabase $35 and Expo $100 ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=603s)).

## Metrics

- About $1K in the first month, June 2025 ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=104s)).
- About $20K/month after 4 months of paid ads alone (2025) ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=411s)).
- Over $81K in the month before the interview (2026-08) ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=104s)).

## Growth channels

- Paid ads. Israeli CPMs are much lower than US CPMs, so ads were profitable without much testing. Creatives remix viral content from similar US apps and Israeli fitness creators ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=411s)).
- Revenue-share deals with two big Israeli fitness influencers took it from $20K to $80K/month. Contracts set content pieces and filming days each month ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=464s), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=499s)).
- Waiting until $20K/month gave Tomer the leverage to avoid a 50/50 split. Partners also mention the app on TV and in interviews ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=556s), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=574s)).
