Feather is a SaaS tool that turns Notion pages into a blog.

## What it is

- A Notion-to-blog tool [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s). [SiteGPT](sitegpt.md) still uses it for its blog posts [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=716s).

## Founders and owners

- [Bhanu](../people/bhanu.md), a solo developer from India, built it and sold it for $250,000 to focus on SiteGPT [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s).
- [Tibo](../people/tibo.md) bought it for $250K and runs it in his SaaS portfolio [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s).

## Business model

- Subscription SaaS for people who write in Notion.

## Metrics

- Under Bhanu: $5K to $6K MRR, with about 100 customers [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=229s).
- Under Tibo: about $10K/month (2025-12) [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s).
- Sale price: $250K [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s), [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s).

## Growth channels

- Its customer base became the seed audience for SiteGPT: Bhanu built the chatbot for his 100 Feather customers first, then split it out [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=229s).

## Timeline

- Before 2023-03: Bhanu runs Feather and builds a chatbot for its users, which becomes SiteGPT [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=229s).
- Later: Bhanu sells Feather for $250K; Tibo is the buyer [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s), [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s).
- 2025-12: about $10K/month in Tibo's portfolio [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s).
