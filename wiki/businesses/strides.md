# Strides

Strides is a minimal product website that [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) reviews as a UI/UX example; the captions also spell it "Stritz", so the name is unclear.

## What it is

- A product site with a black background, type and product shots in the style of [Apple](apple.md) product pages, plus a bottom navigation ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=942s)).
