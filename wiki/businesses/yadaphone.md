Yadaphone is a browser dialer for cheap international calls, built as a Skype alternative by [Denis Yurchak](../people/denis-yurchak.md).

## What it is

- It uses VoIP to call mobile or landline numbers for cents. A 10-minute international call can cost $50 to $100 with a normal provider ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s)).

## Founders

- [Denis Yurchak](../people/denis-yurchak.md), a trained diplomat who learned to code in 2 months. He had about 60 Twitter followers at launch ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=198s), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=391s)).

## Business model

- Pay as you go with credits. Competitors charge about $30/month or per seat. Businesses buy a shared credit balance ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s)).
- Twilio handles calls and costs about 35% of monthly revenue (2025-10) ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=723s)).

## Metrics

- $14K/month and 10,000 registered users (2025-10) ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=58s)).
- 4,500 paying customers, 20 enterprise clients, 27,000 calls in September 2025 ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=80s)).
- The first enterprise customer still pays about $1,000/month (2025-10) ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=705s)).

## Growth channels

- Reddit launch with screenshots of the dialer and a lone-engineer-versus-giant story. Travel subreddits banned him, but sales came first ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=452s), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s)).
- SEO from the departing giant: he asked authors of "Skype alternative" articles to add Yadaphone ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s)).
- A missing "www" in the sitemap let competitors outrank him for a while ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=767s)).

## Timeline

- March 2025: Microsoft announces the end of Skype. Pieter Levels tweets for an alternative. Denis builds Yadaphone in a weekend and gets 150 users in week one ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=249s), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=452s)).
- Month 1: $4,000. Month 3: $10,800. Month 6: $13,300 ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=80s)).
- He coded the enterprise plan overnight after a prospect asked ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=644s)).
