Superpower ChatGPT is a browser extension that adds features to the ChatGPT interface, built and run alone by Saeed Ezzati.

## What it is

- A Chrome extension on top of ChatGPT. The first version had two features in plain JavaScript, HTML and CSS. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s))

## Founders

- [Saeed Ezzati](../people/saeed-ezzati.md), a software engineer with almost a decade at startups. He is the only person on it. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=76s))

## Business model

- Free for the first 9 months. A newsletter for its users earned sponsor money first. Paid features came after 9 to 10 months, and every existing feature stayed free. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=583s))

## Metrics

- $20K to $30K MRR, about 260K to 270K downloads and about 150K weekly active users (2024-08). ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=76s))

## Growth channels

- No paid ads. Reddit posts, word of mouth and third-party "top 10 ChatGPT extensions" lists. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=413s))
- He builds what people ask for in subreddits, Discord, Slack and Facebook groups, then tells them the feature exists. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=228s))

## Timeline

- Late 2022: built in 2 to 3 days right after ChatGPT launched. The first Reddit post reached the top of its subreddit that day. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s))
- 3 to 4 months in: newsletter started. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s))
- 9 to 10 months in: paid features. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=583s))
- 2024-08: $20K to $30K MRR. Platform risk remains: OpenAI can kill it any day. ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=676s))
