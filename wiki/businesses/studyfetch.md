StudyFetch is an AI study platform for students that grew on organic short-form video and a large creator program.

## What it is

- An AI study tool. Its camera-friendly feature streams live lecture notes onto the screen while the student's hands stay off the keyboard ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s)).

## Founders

- The founders pivoted from an essay writing tool. The team started at 7 people ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=210s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=465s)).
- [Kieran](../people/kieran.md), a founding team member, runs marketing ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Paid AI study software for students. The sources give no price.
- Bootstrapped for about 18 months, then an $11.5M Series A from Owl Ventures and the College Board, with no seed round ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=23s), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=498s)).

## Metrics

- Over 7M users in about 2.5 years, seven-figure MRR, eight-figure ARR and over 2B views (2026-05) ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=0s), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=182s)).
- About $10M ARR and 2B views (2026-09) ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=0s)).
- Creators: hundreds now, almost 1,000 at the peak (2026-05) ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [42:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2578s)).

## Growth channels

- **Founder-filmed TikToks.** Kieran's first TikTok got about 3M views overnight. A December 2023 lecture-hall video got 75M views and hundreds of thousands of signups ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=254s), [33:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2031s)).
- **Three creator types.** UGC creators, influencers who open new zero-follower StudyFetch accounts, and one-off sponsored posts ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=646s)).
- **Creator pay (2026-05).** UGC base rates of $50 to $2,500 per video plus $100 per 100K views. New-account influencer deals run about $10K/month for a daily video from a creator with about 750K followers ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [13:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=809s), [41:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2480s)).
- **TikTok over Reels.** TikTok viewers have higher intent. Reels can go viral abroad and convert near zero ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s)).
- **TikTok Shop creators** sell the outcome: better grades ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [23:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1413s)).
- **Team.** Contractors failed on incentives. Full-time scouts, creative managers and a head of the creator program replaced them ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [37:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2254s)). [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) lists the same three roles as non-negotiable ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=695s)).
- After the Series A: more creators and brand work such as cafe pop-ups ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [36:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2166s)).

> Conflict: revenue. [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) says seven-figure MRR and eight-figure ARR (2026-05). [0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md) says about $10M ARR (2026-09). Seven-figure MRR implies $12M+ ARR.
