UpLead is a bootstrapped B2B contact-data tool that sells verified emails and phone numbers for sales teams.

## What it is

- A B2B contact database with real-time email verification. It competes with ZoomInfo [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s).

## Founders

- [Will Cannon](../people/will-cannon.md). He sold lead lists as an agency for about 10 years, from about 2007 to 2017. He also built [Signaturely](signaturely.md) [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=84s).

## Business model

- B2B software, no outside funding [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=59s).
- The pitch: "like ZoomInfo but with real-time email verification", and affordable [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s).

## Metrics

- Over 4,000 customers, over $30M in total sales and over 100K website visits per month (2025-10) [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s).
- First $1M ARR came from cold email alone [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s).

## Growth channels

- **One short cold email** [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s):
  - Subject "Hi {first name}", so it looks personal.
  - Opener: "I was checking out your LinkedIn profile and thought this could be of value to you."
  - Value prop that names a known rival: "like ZoomInfo but with real-time email verification".
  - Easy ask: "Are you open to trying it out at no cost?"

## Origin

- Vendor data was only 50% to 60% accurate, and scraped data 40% to 50%. Will's agency verified phone numbers through a call center in the Philippines. That pain became UpLead [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s).
- He used his four-step method: pick a proven market, find a twist in 1-star and 2-star reviews, acquire customers profitably, then execute [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s).

## Timeline

- After the agency: UpLead launches and reaches $1M ARR on cold email [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s).
- 2025-10: over $30M in total sales [source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s).
