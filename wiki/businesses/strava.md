Strava is a social fitness tracking app for runners and cyclists, often cited as the model for social retention and local competition.

## What it is

- An activity tracker whose home feed shows what friends did. Tracking feeds the social layer, which is the real product [source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=187s).
- Its core loop is segments: public leaderboards on specific stretches of road or trail. You can rank against your neighborhood, your friends or the riders on the same hill [source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s).

## Founders

- The sources do not name them. Strava hired gaming leaders: a chief product officer from Fortnite growth at Epic Games (2024-04), and a CTO who led FarmVille at Zynga for over a decade [source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=249s), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=299s).

## Business model

- Freemium subscription. A strategic buyer: Runna's beginner funnel fit Strava's business, which is why strategics pay the most [source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) [35:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2118s).

## Metrics

- About $500M ARR and a $2.2B valuation (2025). Runkeeper and other trackers had equal or better GPS but lost [source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=187s).
- 180M registered users and about 1 hour of activity for every 2 minutes in the app. Clubs grew 59% in 2024; users gave 14 billion kudos in 2025 (cited 2026-05) [source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=205s).
- Over 180M registered athletes; it deleted 3.9M activities in 2025 and early 2026 because users gamed segment leaderboards, for example with e-bike rides (2026-04) [source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=593s).
- Over 100 million athletes (2026-06) [source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s).

> Conflict: [BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md) says over 100 million athletes (2026-06). [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) and [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) say 180M registered users (2026-04/05). The lower figure may count active athletes.

## Growth channels and retention

- **Small, winnable competition.** Segments are thousands of local leaderboards sorted by age and gender. Kudos raise future run frequency [source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=205s).
- **Shrink the cohort until winning feels possible.** Tim Gabe uses Strava as his main example [source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s).
- **Identity moat.** Users whose running club and segment records live in Strava cannot be poached by a smarter AI agent [source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=249s).
- **Shareable flex.** Run maps work like Spotify Wrapped: a brag that doubles as distribution [source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=639s).

## Copied by other apps

- Cal AI copied groups and leaderboards from Strava and Ladder [source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) [27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1662s).
- Symmetry added a Strava-like feed with ranks per muscle group [source](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=821s).
- Runafy built Strava, Garmin and Apple Watch integrations after an even Instagram poll [source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md) [37:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2252s).
