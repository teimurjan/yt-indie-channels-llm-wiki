Evo Marketing is a UGC agency that runs short-form video campaigns for apps and consumer brands.

## What it is

- An agency that posts hundreds of thousands of creator videos a month for clients such as Jetpack, SMB Market, Dupe and Bible BFF (2026-10) [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=637s).

## Founders

- [Dawson Gibbs](../people/dawson-gibbs.md). He posted daily for 5 to 6 years with only 2,000 followers, then grew a fitness page to hundreds of thousands in about 4 months [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=0s), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=207s).
- He drove about 200M views for [Cal AI](cal-ai.md) in its first 5 months and referred about 100 creators to it for a fee each [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=241s).

## Business model

- Service agency. Every video is approved before it posts [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=637s).
- Vanity domains, such as flightcheatcode.com for Jetpack, separate Evo's traffic from paid ads and other agencies [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=706s).

## Metrics

- Founded about two years before the video. It has driven billions of views (2026-10) [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=170s).
- SMB Market: about 40M views and about $1.2M for the client, but churn hit about 45% from get-rich-quick viewers [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1000s).
- A Dupe creator video got 6.9M views and 170K likes [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [44:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2656s).

## Growth channels and methods

- **Credible or relatable.** High-trust buys need an expert hook. Impulse buys need "I am just like you" [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=313s).
- **Match creator age to buyer.** SMB Market moved to 35 to 40-year-old creators and founder green screens, which brought higher intent [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1056s).
- **Repost early winners.** A video at 10K to 20K views with 3%+ engagement gets reposted or reshot with a new hook [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [43:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2596s).
- **Two-phase campaigns.** Weeks 1 to 2 chase views. Weeks 3 to 4 move winning scripts into credible talking-head formats [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [48:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2923s).
