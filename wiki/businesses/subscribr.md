Subscribr is an AI scriptwriting tool for YouTube creators that pre-sold $20,000 of lifetime licenses before the product existed.

## What it is

- An AI tool that writes YouTube scripts. ChatGPT alone was not good enough for scripts ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=180s)).

## Founders

- [Gil Hildebrand](../people/gil-hildebrand.md). He co-founded Squidoo, which passed $10M revenue and was acquired. He later raised millions for a crypto accounting startup and sold it. This time he chose to bootstrap ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=113s)).
- He ran faceless YouTube channels and found that good scripts were the hardest part ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=180s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions from $49 to $300/month (2025-12) ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s)).
- It was profitable from day one ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=21s)).
- Monthly costs: about $3,500 AI compute, $2,000 ads and $1,500 for scraping, hosting and email (2025-12) ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=796s)).

## Metrics

- About $30K/month in subscription revenue, 18 months after launch (2025-12). Over $700K in sales in the past year, and over 4,000 customers ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=75s)).
- The pre-sale of 50 lifetime licenses sold out in 2 to 3 days for $20,000 ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).

## Growth channels

- **An audience before a product.** Gil opened a new X account with zero followers. He made free tools and content and ran viral giveaways. The email list passed 1,000 people before the pre-sale ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s)).
- **Tiered pre-sale.** The first 10 licenses were very cheap, and the price rose for each next 10 ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- **Full refund until delivery.** He promised delivery in 60 days plus 2 weeks of trial, with a refund at any time ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- **Programmatic SEO.** Over 30,000 views a month from Google (2025-12). Word of mouth and social media bring the rest ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=75s)).

## Timeline

- **About mid-2024 (18 months before 2025-12):** Pre-sale of 50 lifetime licenses, then launch ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- **2025-12:** About $30K/month, 18 months after launch ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s)).

Stack: Claude Code writes about 90% of the code. The app is Laravel on DigitalOcean ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=796s)).
