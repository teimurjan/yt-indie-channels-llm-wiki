Indexsy is an SEO agency that Jacky Chou started in 2015. It is the anchor of his portfolio of about 13 small businesses.

## What it is

- An SEO agency. It kept Jacky afloat when Google updates wiped out his content site income in late 2023 ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=0s), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=187s)).

## Founders

- [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md) ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Agency services for SEO clients. The same SEO customer also buys from his other products, such as [Local Rank](local-rank.md) ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=630s)).

## Metrics

- $85K/month and $500K in the last 12 months through Stripe alone. Bank transfers and PayPal are extra (2026-03) ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
- The whole portfolio makes about $250K/month (2026-03) ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=54s)).

## Growth channels

- **Daily YouTube.** Jacky has posted daily for 2.5 years. Videos get 1K to 2K views but drive most sales. He calls YouTube his "insurance policy" ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=337s)).
- **Newsletter funnel.** Each video links to a lead magnet. He pitches later by email ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=495s)).

## Timeline across sources

- 2015: Indexsy starts. Late 2023: Google's helpful content update and AI Overviews wipe out the content sites. 2026-03: $85K/month ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=0s), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
