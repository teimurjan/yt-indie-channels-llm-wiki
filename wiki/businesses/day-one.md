Day One is a long-lived indie journaling app that beats Apple's free journal apps on craft.

## What it is

- A journaling app. Its text is the same as in Apple Notes or Apple Journal. Day One adds weather, a location pin, embedded photos, a timeline of your years and strong search ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=437s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as his "craft pattern" example: enrich what surrounds commodity data ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=437s)).

## Founders

- Paul Mayne and the small team Bloom Built ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s)).

## Business model

- Subscription (2026-06) ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s)). See [subscriptions](../topics/subscriptions.md).

## Metrics

- A 4.8 rating from over 116,000 reviews, with monthly updates (2026-06) ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s)).

## Growth channels

- The source covers product craft, not acquisition. Tim compares it to a Moleskine: the details around the text make the difference ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=482s)). See [UX design](../topics/ux-design.md).

## Timeline

- 2014: wins the Apple Design Award for Mac ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s)).
- 2026-06: still shipping monthly updates ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=437s)).
