Resume Maker Online is a freemium resume builder run by one person.

## What it is

- A resume builder that shows the resume after one click, without a signup first [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=218s).

## Founders

- [Fernando](../people/fernando.md), a product designer from Argentina who lives in Sweden. He also runs [AI Carousels](ai-carousels.md) [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s).

## Business model

- Freemium; a subscription unlocks AI and pro features. It started as a one-time purchase and relaunched as a subscription in 2024. Churn is high, but revenue is more predictable [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s).

## Metrics

- With AI Carousels: about $15K MRR together (2024-12) [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s).
- Both products cost about $1,000/month, so margins are above 90% (2024-12) [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=654s).

## Growth channels

- Product Hunt: Product of the Week [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s).
- SEO through about 30 free tools across his products, plus build-in-public posts and an affiliate program [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s).
- Niche positioning against Canva: serve one narrow task better; "I only need 0.1% of the market" [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=150s).

## Timeline

- Launch as a one-time purchase; Product of the Week on Product Hunt [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s).
- 2024: relaunched as a subscription [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s).
