Parakeet Chat is an AI chatbot and messaging service for people in US prisons. Inmates use it through the prison email system, and their families pay for it.

## What it is

There is no mobile app. Inmates email a fixed address through the internal prison email system, and a bot replies with answers from ChatGPT and other services. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=524s). The main use is case law and legal research. Users also send family messages and ask about sports stats and business. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=665s)

## Founder

[Jordan Rejaud](../people/jordan-rejaud.md), a solo and bootstrapped founder. He worked on a moon rover and as a Fortune 100 researcher, then became a self-taught coder and freelancer. His first business, robotics work for self-driving car and drone companies, also passed $1M. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=54s), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=89s)

The idea came from a freelance client in prison. The client told him that prison services are overpriced and poor. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=271s)

## Business model

The user and the buyer differ. The incarcerated person uses it, and the family pays a monthly subscription of $15 or $20, with a yearly discount (2026-04). [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=590s)

## Metrics

- $1.5M lifetime revenue and a little over $300K in 2025 (2026-04). The revenue is public on TrustMRR. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s)
- About 30,000 people tried it, which Jordan says is 20% of the US federal prison population. About 100,000 family connections and 9 million messages (2026-04). [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s)
- 200 paying users within the first month. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=24s), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=331s)

## Growth channels

- **Word of mouth only.** No ads and no content. Users showed it to other inmates. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=456s)
- **Referral credit.** A customer who recruits a paying customer gets about a month of free credits. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=456s)
- **MVP as validation.** A landing page cannot reach a closed market, so Jordan gave the MVP to his contacts inside. [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=331s)

## Stack

TypeScript, React, Postgres, Redis, Auth0, Prisma, Zod and Docker. Jordan says AI writes his code and he has not opened a code editor in 3 to 6 months (2026-04). [bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=198s)
