Post Bridge is a social media scheduling tool built by solo founder Jack Friks to cross-post his own app videos.

## What it is

- A tool that schedules and cross-posts short-form video to many social accounts. Jack built it because he spent 30 minutes a day cross-posting ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md)).

## Founders

- [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md), a self-taught bootstrapped solo founder who learned to code with ChatGPT. He also built [Curiosity Quench](curiosity-quench.md), [Doof](doof.md) and [Lovely](lovely.md) ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions from $9/month. Rival schedulers cost about 10 times what he wanted to pay, so he priced it at a level he would pay himself (2025-03) ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=139s), [4:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=287s)).
- A 7-day card-required free trial with no free plan worked better than pushing yearly plans. A 40% yearly discount also lifted revenue ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=795s)).
- Costs about $400/month, mostly the $200 Twitter API, so margins are over 95% (2025-03) ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=966s)).
- Stack: Next.js, ShipFast boilerplate by [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md), Supabase ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [14:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=883s)).

## Metrics

- $7K MRR after 4.5 months and about $40K in the first 5 months (2025-03) ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s)).
- About $12K/month and growing 30 to 40% a month when Sahil of Gumroad offered $1M on Twitter. Jack turned it down ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=718s), [13:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=781s)).
- About $20K MRR (2025-12) ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [13:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=824s)).
- Jack makes $40K/month across his products. Post Bridge grew 10x to 100x bigger than his first app (2026-08) ([k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=0s), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=284s)).

> Conflict: [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) dates the $1M offer to around April 2024, but [qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) says Post Bridge was only 4.5 months old at $7K MRR in 2025-03. The offer date in the first source is likely wrong by a year.

## Growth channels

- **Validated with TikTok comments.** A 60-second talking-head video drew 15 "I need this" comments. He kept posting until hundreds asked ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=301s)).
- **Dogfooding as marketing.** He uses Post Bridge to grow his own apps, and built a new mobile app as a case study for it ([k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=284s)).
- **[Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md)** by sharing real work on Twitter ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=699s)).
- **Fast support every morning** kept users and grew the product ([k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s)).
