The Wellness Company is Presh's small studio of health and wellness iPhone apps that all serve one audience.

## What it is

- Apps: [Go Polar](go-polar.md) tracks cold plunge and sauna sessions. [SunSeek](sunseek.md) tracks morning light and vitamin D. [Posture AI](posture-ai.md) scans posture for desk workers. [Tempo](tempo.md), not yet launched, turns wearable data and blood tests into a health-span score ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=655s)).

## Founders

- [Presh](../people/presh.md). He dropped out of college and worked 7 years for an angel investor, where he saw thousands of pitches ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)).

## Business model

- All revenue is [subscriptions](../topics/subscriptions.md), with RevenueCat paywalls ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s), [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=697s)).
- Tools cost about $300/month. Apple takes 15% under $1M, so margins are 80% to 85% (2025-12) ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=736s)).

## Metrics

- $0 to $120K ARR in about 10 months, and $10.4K/month (2025-12) ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s)).
- Over 20,000 downloads, over 1,500 paying subscribers, about 8% free-to-paid and 61% day-30 retention of paid users (2025-12) ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).

## Growth channels

- Mostly organic App Store search ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).
- Cross-promotion: users of one app fit the others, so a new launch is not cold marketing ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)). See [app portfolios](../topics/app-portfolios.md).
- Plan distribution on day zero: find a niche creator, or be that creator ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s)).

## Timeline

- 2025: Presh starts the studio with Go Polar, built for his own cold plunge habit. About 10 months later it makes $120K ARR ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=291s)).
- SunSeek launched better than Go Polar on day one because of earlier reps ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=574s)).
- 2025-12: three live apps at $120K ARR. Tempo is in progress ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s)).
