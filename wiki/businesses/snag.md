Snag is an iOS app that shows free items near the user. It charges a few dollars a month for access to items worth hundreds.

## What it is

Snag lists free items that people give away nearby. Its value proposition is simple: pay a small fee and get things worth far more. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=593s)

## Founders

[Benjamin Chen](../people/benjamin-chen.md), a 20-year-old NYU student, built it with his team at 10X Studio. He built more than 45 apps in about a year before he focused on Snag. Earlier he sold a media company for six figures. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=152s)

## Business model

Subscriptions with weekly, monthly and yearly plans, plus a one-time purchase. Superwall runs the paywall A/B tests. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=300s)

## Metrics (2026-05)

- About $30K MRR, about 4 months after launch. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s)
- Over 100K authenticated users, 9,000 conversions and over $80K total proceeds. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s)
- About 3.3K App Store ratings. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s)
- Every 100K UGC views make about $1K to $2K in subscription profit. One soft-sell video got 240K views. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=506s)

## Growth channels

- **UGC creators, vetted by interview.** About 10% of interviewed creators are good. Each gets a monthly retainer plus CPM. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s)
- **Meta ads from winning UGC.** Videos over 50K views become Meta ad tests at about $50/day. A test with ROAS above 1 or a high CTR scales to $100, $200, then $300 a day. One ad took about $3,000 in spend before its ROAS turned negative. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=539s)
- **A product that is viral by nature.** [Drew Levin](../people/drew-levin.md) of [SideShift](sideshift.md) names Snag as an example. Apps where users earn money or get something for free do best on organic video. [qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=278s)
- **Win-back emails.** Loops emails churned users. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=300s)

## How it was built

Benji starts from the marketing hook, then designs in Figma, then hands the designs to Claude Code. A good app takes 4 to 5 hours without the backend. The stack is Cursor, Claude Code Max, Figma, Supabase, Loops, Superwall and Mixpanel. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=226s), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=300s)
