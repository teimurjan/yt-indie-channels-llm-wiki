Debloat AI is an app that scans a user's face and shows a "bloat score". Rafael Kramer built it and sold it.

## What it is

- A face scan comes first, which creates sunk cost. Then it shows a slightly exaggerated bloat score, then the paywall ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1498s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).

## Founders

- [Rafael Kramer](../people/rafael-kramer.md), who started building no-code games at 13 ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md)).

## Business model

- Subscription app with a paywall after the scan ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1498s)).

## Metrics

- About 40,000 downloads ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s)).
- Copycats now make $20K to $30K/month (2026-02) ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=947s)).

## Growth channels

- **Demand from comments.** Under debloat transformation videos, about 100 people asked "how?" ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1061s)).
- **TikTok ads.** A few cents per download for a few days, then about 30% profit on spend for weeks ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s)). See [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md).
- **Faceless slideshow pages.** About 8 accounts paid on CPM ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [18:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1095s)). See [TikTok farming](../topics/tiktok-farming.md).

## Timeline

- Built and grown with TikTok ads and slideshow pages, then sold ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s)).
- 2026-02: copycats make $20K to $30K/month ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=947s)).
