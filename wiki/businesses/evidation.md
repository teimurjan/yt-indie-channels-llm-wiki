This page collects what the sources say about Evidation, a health tracking app that runs on the watch you already own.

## What it is

Evidation is software-only health tracking. It runs on the watch a user already owns, so there is no ring or band to buy ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).

## Founders

- The source quotes co-founder Aditya Agarwal: "A $500 ring or band is out of reach for a lot of people" ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).

## Business model

- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses Evidation as an example of a "hidden assumption" flip. Tracking apps assume users buy a device plus a membership. Evidation drops the device ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).
- Device makers cannot follow. To follow, they must tell customers to skip what they sell ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).

## Metrics

- Evidation said it grew more than 8x in a year (2025-10) ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).
- It raised a Series A from General Catalyst (2025-10) ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).

## Growth channels

The source does not cover Evidation's growth channels.

## The lesson

- Find the assumption that every big app in a category makes about the user. Circle the one their business depends on ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=0s)).
- The best assumption to bet against is the one the leader makes money from ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).
- The same video gives two other cases: [Timeleft](timeleft.md) against swiping, and [Speak](speak.md) against tap-through language games ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s)).
