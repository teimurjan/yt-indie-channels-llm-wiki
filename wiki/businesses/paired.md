Paired is an overseas staffing company that YouTuber Charlie Chang started to place remote hires with clients.

## What it is

- Paired recruits overseas talent and places full-time hires. Recruiters screen 300 to 500 people to present one or two ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)).

## Founders

- [Charlie Chang](../people/charlie-chang.md), who runs six YouTube channels through [Charlie Chang Media](charlie-chang-media.md) ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
- He started Paired to own equity in a traditional business and grow it with his channels ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)).

## Business model

- A one-time placement fee per hire. A full-time hire costs the client about $600 to $1,000/month (2024-02) ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)).

## Metrics

- Low six figures in revenue (2024-02) ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).

## Growth channels

- Charlie's YouTube audience ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)).
- Charlie's own media team uses it. He now finds most of his 26-person team through Paired instead of Upwork, Fiverr or OnlineJobs ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)).

## Timeline

- **2023:** Paired starts ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
- **2024-02:** Low six figures in revenue. Charlie wants to grow it toward an acquisition ([source](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)).
