Alia is a bootstrapped SaaS that sells email-capture pop-ups to e-commerce brands.

## What it is

- A typical Alia pop-up offers 15% off in exchange for an email ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=95s)).
- It began as a college club project and was first sold as a customer education and loyalty tool. Buyers did not know where it fit in their stack ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md)).

## Founders

- Three co-founders: [Shaan Arora](../people/shaan-arora.md) (CEO), Cory and Bill ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=582s)).

## Business model

- SaaS subscription for e-commerce brands. Bootstrapped, with a team of 12 (2025-08) ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=582s)).

## Metrics

- About 6 months to grow from 2 to about 20 customers before the repositioning ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=14s)).
- $0 to $4M ARR in about one year after the repositioning (2025-08) ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=26s)).
- About 1,500 brands, among them Nike Strength and Toms (2025-08) ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=95s)).

## Growth channels

- **Repositioning.** The best customers paid for a pop-up tool, not an education tool. The team repositioned around pop-ups only, after a co-founder read April Dunford's "Obviously Awesome" ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=217s)).
- **One message in four places.** The website tagline reads "the next generation of pop-ups". Shaan pinned a post: "Pop-ups equals Alia. Alia equals pop-ups." Sales calls open with "we do pop-ups". Internal language uses the same words ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=270s), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=316s)).
- **Inbound from word of mouth.** People who ask in Slack groups or on X for the best pop-up tool get Alia as the answer ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=270s)).
- **Speed against incumbents.** Pop-up rivals are 15 to 20 years old and move slowly ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=608s)).

## Timeline

- Start: a college club project. The first two customers were a co-founder's cousin and a classmate ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=143s)).
- About 6 months later: about 20 customers ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=14s)).
- Repositioning, tested in steps: sales calls first (shorter and more closes), then content, then the website and internal language ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=483s)).
- 2025-08: $4M ARR about one year after the change ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=26s)).
