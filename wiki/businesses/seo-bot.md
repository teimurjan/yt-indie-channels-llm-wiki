SEO Bot is an AI SEO tool in John Rush's portfolio of about 26 products for founders.

## What it is

- An AI SEO product. Its "boost my domain rating" button opens [Listing Bot](listing-bot.md), a sister tool that submits products to directories [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=576s).

## Founders

- [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md). He builds most products with 50/50 "co-makers": he does operations, legal and accounting, and the co-maker does code and support [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=317s).

## Business model

- Subscription SaaS on the Claude, Gemini and OpenAI APIs; users choose the model [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=714s).

## Metrics

- $100K MRR in less than 12 months (2025-05) [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s).
- About $30K/month on AI APIs, so about a 70% margin, against about 90% for his non-AI tools (2025-05) [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s).

## Growth channels

- John's order: SEO first, then the same post daily on X, LinkedIn, Substack and Facebook, then directories, then cross-promotion [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s).
- Cross-promotion: "Every user that tries one of my product ends up trying at least one more product." [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=538s)
- Human support over AI support to build relationships [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s).

## Timeline

- 2022: John buys [Unicorn Platform](unicorn-platform.md) for almost $1M and starts his bootstrapped portfolio [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s).
- 2025-05: SEO Bot at $100K MRR, under 12 months after launch [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s).
