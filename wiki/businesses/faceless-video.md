Faceless Video is a SaaS that runs faceless social media channels on autopilot: the user gives a topic, and the app writes, makes and posts a video every day.

## What it is

- An AI video tool that writes, makes and posts a faceless video each day [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=0s).
- Built and still run on Bubble with [no code](../topics/no-code.md) [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s).

## Founders

- [Jacob Seeger](../people/jacob-seeger.md), a former musician who ran Domino, a music marketing agency. Faceless Video was his seventh SaaS idea after six failures [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=0s), [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=114s).
- [Alex](../people/alex.md), who found the idea in his TikTok feed and brings a film background to the ads [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=192s), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=761s).

## Business model

- B2C subscriptions. Low prices make heavy ad spend hard, so the product needs viral potential [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s).

## Metrics

- More than $1M ARR, 100% bootstrapped, reached in about 10 months (2025-06) [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=60s).
- More than 1.1M signups and thousands of videos a day. Some videos passed 1M views (2025-06) [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=80s).

## Growth channels

- **A $200 Twitter thread ad** that got hundreds of thousands of views [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s).
- **Influencers who saw the ads** promoted it with no outreach, which brought the biggest growth [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s).
- **[Word of mouth](../topics/word-of-mouth.md)** is always in the top 5 attribution sources. SEO and organic content also run [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s).

## Timeline

- Month 1: an MVP that only turned text into a faceless video. Accounts, UX and payments came later [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s).
- About month 10: $1M ARR [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=60s).
- 2025-06: more than $1M ARR and 1.1M signups [source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=80s).
