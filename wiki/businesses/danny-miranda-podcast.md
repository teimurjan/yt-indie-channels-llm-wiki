The Danny Miranda Podcast is an interview show that Danny Miranda started in his parents' basement and grew to guests such as Gary Vaynerchuk.

## What it is

- A three-episodes-a-week interview podcast. Gary Vaynerchuk came on as episode 39 ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=387s)).
- Starter kit: a Shure MV7 microphone, Zoom, and Anchor (free) for hosting ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=319s)).

## Founders

- [Danny Miranda](../people/danny-miranda.md), visited by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) in Austin ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md)).

## Business model

- Danny earns from consulting: he helps new shows book strong guests from day one. Other podcast models he lists: ads, funnels to courses or newsletters, and conferences built from past guests ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=186s)).

## Metrics

- 30K to 50K downloads/month (2023-06) ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=64s)).
- 359 episodes in two and a half years without a missed week (2023-06) ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=747s)).

## Growth channels

- Daily clips: at least 1 YouTube video over one minute, 2 Shorts, 3 Instagram Reels and 5 Twitter clips. An editor makes them (2023-06) ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=634s)). See [short-form video](../topics/short-form-video.md).
- Guest outreach on Twitter. A public "dream guest" tweet about Gary Vaynerchuk led to the booking ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=457s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
