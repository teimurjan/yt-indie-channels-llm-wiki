Timeleft is an app that seats you at dinner with five strangers, with no profiles, swiping or planning.

## What it is

- Users take a personality quiz, join a group, and learn the restaurant just before dinner. Its homepage line is "No bios, no swiping, no planning." [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s).

## Founders

- The source does not name them.

## Business model

- Paid dinners matched by the app; the source does not give prices.

## Metrics

- About 2 years in, on track for $16M a year, as cited by [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) (2026-10) [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=0s).
- About 120,000 people a month brought together (2025-08) [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s).

## Growth channels and moat

- Tim Gabe's "hidden assumption" case: meeting apps assume you want to look people up first. Timeleft removed choice. Bumble, whose main app depends on swiping, had to launch a separate group app in 2026-06 to try the opposite [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s).

## Timeline

- 2025-08: about 120,000 people a month [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s).
- 2026-06 to 2026-08: Bumble responds with a group-meetup app and plans to move away from the swipe [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s).
