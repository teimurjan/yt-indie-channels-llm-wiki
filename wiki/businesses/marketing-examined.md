Marketing Examined is a marketing newsletter by Alex Garcia. It leads a group of five sponsor-funded newsletters.

## What it is

- Five newsletters: Marketing, Content, Social, Landing Page and DTC Examined ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)).
- Alex pays one writer for each niche newsletter ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=482s)).

## Founders

- [Alex Garcia](../people/alex-garcia.md). He worked at The Hustle for about a year first ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=94s)).

## Business model

- Ad sponsors pay for all newsletter revenue. A services line adds $15K to $20K/month (2023-07) ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)).
- Rule of thumb: 10,000 subscribers can sell an ad slot for over $1,000 ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)).

## Metrics

- 150K to 160K subscribers and $65K to $70K/month (2023-07) ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)).

## Growth channels

- **Twitter threads.** 50 threads in 50 days took followers from 500 to 40,000. Subscribers went from about 2,000 to 8,000 or 9,000 ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)). See [content marketing](../topics/content-marketing.md).
- **Paid ads** on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit reuse winning Twitter CTAs ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=508s)).
- **Cross-promotion networks** such as SparkLoop, Beehiiv and ConvertKit ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=508s)).
- **Hand-to-hand combat first.** DMs, friends and small giveaways ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [4:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=273s)).

## Timeline

- About a year at The Hustle, then his own newsletter ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=94s)).
- 50 threads in 50 days grew the list about 4x ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)).
- 2023-07: five newsletters, $65K to $70K/month ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)).
