Prop GPT is a sports betting analytics app that uses a machine learning model to score picks.

## What it is

- The first version gave users tools to enter and check their own bets. The rebuild hands users pre-analyzed picks instead ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- Stack: React Native, TypeScript, Python for the ML model and data fetch, a Neon database, RevenueCat and Superwall ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [8:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=493s)).

## Founders

- College students [Eyal Hazan](../people/eyal-hazan.md) and [Yahli Hazan](../people/yahli-hazan.md) ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [0:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=18s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a [free trial](../topics/free-trials.md) and a Superwall paywall ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [8:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=493s)).
- Monthly costs (2025-11): Superwall $0.20 per conversion, RevenueCat about 1%, sports data APIs $100, Neon $10, LLM $20, marketing about $10,000. Margin is about 50% ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=522s)).

## Metrics

- Before the rebuild: 45% install-to-trial, only 13% trial-to-paid, and $1K to $2K MRR ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s)).
- Relaunch on 2025-04-15 at $1,700 MRR. Trial-to-paid then rose above 50%. It peaked at $40K MRR and 2,000 downloads in one day about 2.5 months later ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- About $30K/month, over 40,000 downloads, over 3,000 paying users, 48% install-to-trial and about $3.30 revenue per download (2025-11) ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=103s)).

## Growth channels

- [Influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md). Early spend of a few thousand dollars gave about 20 downloads and 5 to 10 trials a day ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=185s)).
- About their 70th influencer video got 600,000 views and took ARR from about $8K to $38K in 3 days ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=436s)).
- PostHog shows the most-clicked feature, which becomes the main claim in marketing ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s)).

## Timeline

- 2024: the first version took about 5 months and launched in the middle of the NFL season ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=120s)).
- Early 2025: many trials on Super Bowl day, almost none stayed. The founders stopped all marketing for 4 months to rebuild ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=337s), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- 2025-04: relaunch timed to the NBA playoffs, then $30K MRR within 10 weeks ([source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
