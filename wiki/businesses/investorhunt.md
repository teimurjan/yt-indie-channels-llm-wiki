InvestorHunt is an investor database for founders, one of three database products run by Rashid Khasanov.

## What it is

- A paid database of investors that helps founders build fundraising lists. It sits beside [AngelMatch](angelmatch.md) (about 125,000 angels and VCs) and [JournalistHunt](journalisthunt.md) (200,000 journalists) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).

## Founders

- [Rashid Khasanov](../people/rashid-khasanov.md). For his earlier fintech startup he raised $100,000 from family and friends and built an investor list by hand. That showed him cold email works and list-building wastes time ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).

## Business model

- Subscription tiers at $57, $97 and $297 (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)).
- B2B: Pat Walls argues directories rarely work as B2C, but a founder who cannot find investors cannot make payroll, so they pay ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)).

## Metrics

- $2,800 MRR from SEO only (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)).
- The portfolio makes about $32K MRR. AngelMatch alone is $29K MRR (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=66s), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).
- Tools for the portfolio cost about $62/month (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)).

## Growth channels

- **[SEO](../topics/seo.md) only.** Programmatic SEO brings about half of portfolio revenue (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- **Database playbook:** pick a database that solves one problem, collect the data by hand or with automation, launch, look for payment signals, then lean on programmatic SEO ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
