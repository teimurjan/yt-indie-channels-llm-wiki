Uneed is a launch platform for indie products, built as a Product Hunt alternative.

## What it is

- Makers list products for a launch. Uneed began as a directory of front-end tools ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=274s)).

## Founders

- [Thomas Sanlis](../people/thomas-sanlis.md). He launched over 30 projects that failed before Uneed worked ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=81s)).

## Business model

- Makers pay to skip the waiting line or to advertise ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)). See [platform marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
- Polar is the merchant of record ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=556s)).

## Metrics

- $8K to $10K/month, with $10K in the month before the video (2025-08) ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)).
- About 40,000 users, 2,000 paying customers and 30,000 unique visitors a month (2025-08) ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)).
- About 10,000 clicks sent to listed products in a month, his most important number (2025-08) ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)).

## Growth channels

- **A market moment.** Indie hackers said Product Hunt only featured big products. He pivoted then ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=315s)). See [launch strategy](../topics/launch-strategy.md).
- **His own Twitter following** and knowledge of indie hackers ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=426s)). See [build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md).
- **Steady posting.** Revenue swings with his social posts ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)).

## Timeline

- Tool directory phase: peaked at about $200/month ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=274s)).
- Pivot to a launch platform. Revenue dropped at first, then grew ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=315s)).
- 2025-08: about $10K/month ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)).
