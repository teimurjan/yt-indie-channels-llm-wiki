Life Reset is a habit tracker app wrapped in a game, with characters, quests and boss fights.

## What it is

- Your character fights a monster every two days. The result depends on the tasks you did in those two days ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=413s)).
- A visible countdown shows the next fight. Each fight plays as a short cinematic scene ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=413s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as an example of his "tick tock formula": the hero feature runs on a clock, so users come back without a prompt ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=457s)).
