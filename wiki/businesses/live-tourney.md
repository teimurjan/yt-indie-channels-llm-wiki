Live Tourney is a web app that golf courses use to run tournaments with live scoring and leaderboards.

## What it is

- Players scan a QR code on the scorecard, enter scores by group and see a live leaderboard [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=214s).
- A simpler, cheaper option than Golf Genius, which his course's pro found too complex and too expensive [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=174s).
- Built with Next.js and Google Firebase [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=205s).

## Founders

- [Matt Robinson](../people/matt-robinson.md), a senior software engineer on Meta's AI team for 6 years. He plays in the tournaments he serves [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=403s), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=539s).

## Business model

- [B2B SaaS](../topics/b2b-saas.md) sold to golf courses [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=87s).

## Metrics

- About 150 golf courses and about $350K ARR, with a run rate near $800K based on the start of the year (2026-07) [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=87s).
- About $30K/month. Pat also says $30K to $40K (2026-07) [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=0s), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=62s).
