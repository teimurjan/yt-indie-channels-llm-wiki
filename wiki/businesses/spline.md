Spline is a browser-based 3D design tool. Tim Gabe calls it "basically like Figma or Framer but for 3D".

## What it is

- A 3D design and animation tool whose scenes embed in websites ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=194s), [20:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=1200s)).

## How it is used

[Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) builds a scroll-driven 3D site with [Figma](figma.md), [Framer](framer.md) and Spline (2023-11):

- Model a cube from a Figma SVG, extruded by 300. Color #0000FF, matcap and bloom ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=258s), [6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=395s)).
- Use states (base, multiply with a cloner count of 8, grow) driven by scroll over 1,500 px ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=420s), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=482s)).
- Give the camera its own states for cinematic moves ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=572s)).
- One group per animation: a "levitate" loop inside a "slide up" group ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [12:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=721s)).
- In export settings, turn off the logo, page scroll, orbit, pan and zoom ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=1165s)).
- Embed the code in Framer as a sticky 100vh layer inside a 300vh scroll container, with overflow visible ([source](../sources/4Byq1fjaUSY.md), [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=905s), [15:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Byq1fjaUSY&t=952s)).
