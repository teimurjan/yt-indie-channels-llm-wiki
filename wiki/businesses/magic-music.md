Magic Music is an AI song app built on Suno by [Lotts Ezeike](../people/lotts-ezeike.md), one of the four apps he built and sold.

## What it is

- A wrapper on Suno's AI music model. Users make songs, often to roast friends ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=489s)).

## Founders

- [Lotts Ezeike](../people/lotts-ezeike.md), a London founder who builds consumer apps to $10K to $20K MRR and sells them. His other apps are [Bible Buddy](bible-buddy.md), [Toxic Traits](toxic-traits.md) and [PrayScreen](prayscreen.md) ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s)).

## Business model

- A "cash flow" app, built for a clean sale rather than as a long-term legacy business ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=229s)).

## Metrics

- The source does not give Magic Music's own revenue. His four sold apps made over $500K in total sales and had over 1M users (2025-11) ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s)).

## Growth channels

- Share an asset, not the app. Songs opened in a web view with a big "Create your own song" button. Friends who got roasted downloaded the app ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=489s), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s)).

## Timeline

- Suno launched AI music, and Lotts built Magic Music on it in 4 days ([source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [14:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=892s)).
- Sold, as one of his four exits since 2022 ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s)).
