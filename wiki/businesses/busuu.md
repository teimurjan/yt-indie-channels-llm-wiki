This page collects what the sources say about Busuu, a language-learning subscription app.

## What it is

Busuu is a language-learning app with a free tier and a paid upgrade. It appears in one source, as an A/B test example from [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=472s)).

## Founders

The source does not cover the founders.

## Business model

- Free users get one language. They must upgrade to learn a second one ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=472s)).

## Metrics

- The one-language limit gave +83% conversion among engaged users, as Tim reports it (2025-07) ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=472s)).

## Lesson

- **Lock where intent peaks**. Do not punish beginners. Put the lock at the point where engaged users want more ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=472s)).
- The same video covers other upgrade-moment tests. [Mobbin](mobbin.md) put "Pro" labels on locked content and got +35% free-to-paid conversion ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=375s)).
