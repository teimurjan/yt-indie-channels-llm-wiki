BlogToPin is a SaaS that scans a website, then creates and schedules Pinterest pins with AI.

## What it is

- It automates pin design. One pin takes 5 to 10 minutes by hand, and growth on Pinterest needs 5 to 10 pins a day [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s).
- In onboarding, a new user adds a website and sees sample pins in about a minute, then generates a month of pins [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [13:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=790s).

## Founders

- [Nic](../people/nic-weber.md), a Ukrainian developer. Pinterest beat every other traffic channel for his cocktail blog, and he paid someone to make pins before he automated it [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=248s).

## Business model

- [Subscriptions](../topics/subscriptions.md). The starter plan is about $39/month, with agency and enterprise plans above it [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s).
- A [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) launch made $0. A normal subscription and a better product brought the first real customers within a month [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s).

## Metrics

- A bit over $16K MRR from more than 400 active subscribers. Monthly churn is about 10% (2026-02) [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=164s).

## Growth channels

- **Early grind that compounds.** Articles, influencer outreach, Reddit posts and backlinks now bring customers from [SEO](../topics/seo.md) and ChatGPT "on autopilot" [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s).
- **Word of mouth and affiliates.** He watches screen recordings when a user hits a problem and builds requests in hours or days [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s).

## Timeline

- 2023-12: idea. He built at night next to a 9-to-5 job. A scrappy version took 7 days and the real product about 2 months [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s).
- Launch with 1,000 Twitter followers. First paying customer after 10 days [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s).
- 2026-02: about $16K MRR, about 2 years in [source](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=116s).
