Visualizee is an AI rendering tool that turns sketches and models into realistic renders and videos for architects and interior designers.

## Founders

- [Piotr](../people/piotr.md), a Polish developer with over 20 years of experience. He still works full time as a tech lead and built Visualizee on nights and weekends (2026-09) ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=63s), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=205s)).

## Business model

- A 7-day free trial, then $35/month or $80/month by render volume, plus some one-time payments (2026-09) ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s)).
- Stack: Cursor and Claude Code to build, fal.ai for GPUs, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, Next.js, MailerLite, PostHog and Sentry ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=557s)).

## Metrics

- $8.2K MRR, close to $10K/month with one-time payments (2026-09) ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s)).
- About 300 active subscribers, over 300 landing page visitors a day, 5 to 10 new trials a day and 25% trial-to-paid (2026-09) ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s)).

## Growth channels

- The growth came from product changes, not marketing. For 2 years Piotr blamed marketing, but the real problem was the path from visitor to first render ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s)).

## Timeline

- About 3 years before 2026-09: launch, after about 5 months of after-hours work. The first version used a complex node interface like ComfyUI ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=249s)).
- For two years: about $100 to $150/month on one-time payments ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=21s)).
- About 6 months before 2026-09: he replaced the node system with a chat assistant, "Vizi", that writes the prompt, and moved to subscriptions. The audience stayed the same ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=352s), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=374s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=450s)).
- 2026-09: close to $10K/month ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=63s)).
