Toxic Traits is a consumer app that reads personality traits from chat screenshots. Lotts Ezeike built it and later sold it.

## What it is

- The app reads personality traits from chat screenshots [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s).

## Founders

- [Lotts Ezeike](../people/lotts-ezeike.md), a London founder who builds consumer apps to $10K to $20K MRR and then sells them [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=0s).

## Business model

- A "cash flow" app built for a clean sale, not a long-term "legacy" app. His goal for such apps is an exit in 6 to 12 months for about $250K [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=229s).
- The sources give no price or revenue for Toxic Traits itself.

## Metrics

- Toxic Traits is one of four apps that Lotts sold since 2022, for over $500K in total. The four apps had over 1M users in total (2025-11) [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s).

## Growth channels

- **Rage bait on TikTok.** Posts show an ambiguous chat labeled a red flag, so viewers argue in the comments [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=942s).
- A bank of 330 content pieces came ready for the buyer (2025-09) [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1026s).

## Timeline
