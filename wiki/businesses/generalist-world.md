Generalist World is a newsletter and community brand for people who do many different things at work, run from a small Scottish island.

## What it is

- It serves "generalists", people who do not fit into one box at work. The products are a newsletter, a members community, a job board, a summer school and an invented holiday, International Generalist Day ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s)).

## Founders

- [Milly Tamati](../people/milly-tamati.md). She runs it from a 200-year-old cottage on a Scottish island of 191 people. She moved there about 5.5 years before 2026-09 ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=458s)).
- A very small team that are also her friends. No investors ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)).

## Business model

- Revenue comes from the community, the job board, the summer school and partnerships ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s)).
- Its largest partnership is a one-year deal with Beehiiv ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).
- The business stays small on purpose. Thousands of support-heavy customers do not fit the life Milly wants ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)).

## Metrics

- $30K/month (2026-09) ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s)).
- Nearly $600K total revenue over 4 years (2026-09). She also calls it "a couple of hundred grand a year" ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)).
- Hundreds of thousands of people see, read or watch its content each month (2026-09) ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=289s)).

> Conflict: [ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md) gives $30K/month, which is about $360K a year. In the same video Milly says "a couple of hundred grand a year", and nearly $600K over 4 years. The monthly figure is likely recent, and the totals cover earlier, smaller years.

## Growth channels

- **Content every morning.** Milly creates content before Slack or email ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=83s)).
- **Partner pitches with proof.** She sent Beehiiv a body of work and its results, instead of waiting for a yes ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).
- **Be more weird.** Her results grew as she got more honest and more weird ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).
