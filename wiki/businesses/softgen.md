Softgen is an early AI coding app that Domenico Gagliardi grew fast and then sold.

## What it is

- An early AI coding app ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=60s)).

## Founders

- [Domenico Gagliardi](../people/domenico-gagliardi.md) ("Dom"), a non-technical founder. He studied economics, ran a micromobility business from 2019 and then worked as an analyst ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=87s)).

## Business model

- The source does not give Softgen's pricing. Dom built it to sell. His rule: package the business as an asset that someone else can run ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=321s)).
- Buyers look at ARR, gross margin, ARPU, LTV, CAC and churn ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=321s)).

## Metrics

- Grew from $20K to $500K ARR in 3 months (2025-10) ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=60s)).
- Sold for close to 3x ARR, over $1M (2025-10) ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=241s)).

## Growth channels

- **Trends on X.** Every one of Dom's projects started from a trend or the mood on X. He says timing made the difference ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=400s)).
- **Build in public.** His framework grows each product with organic build-in-public posts ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=261s)).
- **Waitlist loop.** A one-page site collects emails. He aims for 15% conversion. Then he ships one key feature to the waitlist, fixes it and relaunches ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=261s)).

## Timeline

- January 2022: Dom starts a challenge to launch and sell a micro-SaaS every 30 days. Six small AI tools sell for about $85K in total ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s)).
- 2024: a new job offer is withdrawn after he signs. He goes full time on Softgen ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=565s)).
- Softgen grows from $20K to $500K ARR in 3 months and sells for over $1M ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=241s)).
- 2025-10: Dom works on Kortix, which helps companies replace manual work with AI agents ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=60s)).
