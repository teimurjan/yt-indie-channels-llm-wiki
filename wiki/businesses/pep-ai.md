Pep AI is an iPhone tracker for peptide and GLP-1 users, built by a college student and grown with niche creators.

## What it is

- It logs doses, injection sites, vials, nutrition, weight and workouts ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=14s)).
- Features: a dose calculator, dose log with injection-site rotation, Duolingo-style quizzes, a PubMed-sourced research library, meal scanning, weight tracking, Apple Health and progress photos ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=736s), [13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=798s)).

## Founders

- [Cedric Roberge](../people/cedric-roberge.md), a University of Oregon student. His first app, a student marketplace, got 800 users and no revenue ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=142s)).
- Team (2026-06): 2 developers paid in equity, 2 marketing managers, 4 outreach staff, a CFO and a product lead. An angel investor funds marketing only ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [34:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2099s), [43:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2606s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a 3-day free trial.
  - $45/year or $10/month (2026-05) ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)).
  - $40/year or $10/month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [23:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1381s)).
> Conflict: [vbEKEWtnndU](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md) gives $45/year. [mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md) gives $40/year.
- About 60% of users are over 25. The best paying group is 25 to 40 ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1716s), [48:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2926s)).

## Metrics

- $33K in the last 28 days, almost 2,000 active subscriptions, $11K MRR, $51K total revenue in about 7 weeks (2026-05) ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)).
- $30K in month one, $50K in month two, on pace for $80K in month three. $100K in the first 70 days (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=14s)).
- Marketing spend: $10K to $15K in month one, $20K to $25K in month two (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=990s)).
- Meta cost per download about $2.50. About $400/day on Meta plus $150/day on Apple Search Ads (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=399s), [47:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2833s)).
- About #250 in Health & Fitness (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [50:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=3034s)).

## Growth channels

- **Niche creators before launch.** A 300-person waitlist came from influencer posts and Reddit. One creator's first feed post got 50K views and about $4,000 that day (2026-05) ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s)).
- **Sign almost every creator in the niche.** About 100 creators post (2026-06). A trial month is 4 videos, 4 stories and a link in bio. A creator with about 5,000 views per video gets about $200. Most also get a one-time 20% commission ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=486s), [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=913s), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1297s)).
- **Track each creator** with creator codes and AppsFlyer OneLinks ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=540s)).
- **Outreach at volume.** Over 100 DMs a day, opening with "Paid promo / affiliate deal?" ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [41:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2516s)).
- **App Store search.** Users search the trend term. Pep AI ranked #3 for "peptide tracker" behind ads (2026-05) ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=418s), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=818s)).
- **Meta, not TikTok, for paid.** TikTok rejects peptide Spark ads ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [50:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=3034s)).
