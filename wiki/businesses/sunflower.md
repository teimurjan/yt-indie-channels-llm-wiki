Sunflower is an app that helps people quit alcohol, cannabis, hard drugs or gambling.

## What it is

- A sobriety timer, an AI sponsor named Sam, a community and content ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1055s)).
- The timer is the most important feature, not the AI ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1055s)).
- Sam grew from a ChatGPT wrapper into an agent with memory, follow-ups and escalation to clinicians ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [23:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1389s)).

## Founders

- [Koby Conrad](../people/koby-conrad.md). He led growth at Rupa Health from $5M to $75M in 3.5 years ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [37:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2223s)).
- He learned to code at a bootcamp in 2020 and 2021 to build V1 ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [21:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1268s)).

## Business model

- About $9/month or $35/year ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [43:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2607s)).
- Paywall steps: monthly only, then annual, then annual by default, then a trial toggle ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s)).
- Users are price-sensitive, so a lower price made more money ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [44:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2675s)).
- Trial reminders raised revenue 48% and trial conversions 46% ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [46:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2797s)). See [free trials](../topics/free-trials.md).
- Funding: about $6.5M total from YC, a16z Speedrun, Flybridge and angels ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [38:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2309s)).

## Metrics

- About $1M annualized run rate in about 10 months, over 10K paying subscribers (2026-03) ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=170s)).
- Sam handled over 4M messages in the last year (2026-03) ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [52:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=3131s)).
