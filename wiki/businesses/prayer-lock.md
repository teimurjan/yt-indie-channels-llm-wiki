Prayer Lock is an iOS app that blocks your phone until you pray.

## What it is

- A screen-blocking prayer app. It has no login and no database; all data stays on the device [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=294s).
- It copies and improves on a competitor, [PrayScreen](prayscreen.md) [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=170s).

## Founders

- [Mau Baron](../people/mau-baron.md), a finance graduate who taught himself to code with AI. He coded the app in 3 days with Cursor and ChatGPT [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=236s).

## Business model

- Subscriptions: $49.99/year or $9.99/week. Conversion rate 43%, proceeds per paying user $23.60 (2026-01) [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s).
- Running costs are tiny: Cursor $20, ChatGPT $20, PostHog about $10, AWS about $2, Gemini 2.5 Flash about $9, plus per-conversion fees for Superwall and Singular (2026-01) [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=844s).

## Metrics

- Monthly sales: $10K (2025-10), $15K (2025-11), $21K (2025-12). He expected about $40K for 2026-01 [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=685s).
- About 58K downloads and 6.7K ratings at 4.9 stars (2026-01) [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=71s).
- Ad spend about $360/day; December 2025 spend about $9K for about $20K revenue; $1.88 per trial (2026-01) [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=899s).

## Growth channels

- Organic UGC volume: about 40 posts a day across 12 accounts, mostly "girl reaction" videos recorded by his girlfriend. 10K views a day gave 200 to 300 downloads a day [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=426s), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=488s).
- Longer onboarding: 5 to 15 minutes raised organic conversion more than 3x [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s).
- TikTok ads beat Meta: $4 per trial on TikTok against about $10 on Meta. Spark Ads on a 15-year-old creator's videos cut it to $3 [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s).
