MacroFactor is a macro and calorie tracking app built to remove shame from tracking.

## What it is

- A nutrition tracker that expects users to miss targets. It uses a trend weight averaged over about 3 weeks to back-calculate the user's metabolism ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=504s)).
- Other trackers assume shame drives compliance: red numbers, broken streaks and guilt notifications. MacroFactor drops them, so users log honestly and the data gets better than MyFitnessPal's gamed database ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=565s)).

## Founders

- It comes from Stronger by Science. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) quotes Greg Nuckols: "What if nutrition apps didn't try to guilt people into losing weight?" ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s)).

## Business model

- Bootstrapped subscription at $72 a year (2026-06) ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s)).

## Metrics

- 500,000 users (2026-06) ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s)).

## Growth channels

- Creator-led: it grew from the Stronger by Science audience. Josh of [Blue Throne](blue-throne.md) says creator-led apps convert well but are hard to sell, since a buyer needs the creator to stay ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [28:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1721s), [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1798s)).
