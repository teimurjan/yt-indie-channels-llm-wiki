Liinks is a link-in-bio and simple website tool, built as a nicer-looking Linktree alternative.

## What it is

- A link-in-bio tool with multiple pages, richer blocks and more customization than Linktree [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=100s), [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=351s).
- New users can import their Instagram profile. The app builds a banner from the picture and name, then offers templates [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [13:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=817s).
- Small tools ship as features: forms, QR codes, short links and a mock-up builder [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=869s).

## Founders

- [Charlie Clark](../people/charlie-clark.md), a former Squarespace engineer. He built it in 2 weeks in 2019 while his first startup, Visual, struggled [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=318s).

## Business model

- Low prices on purpose, anchored below Linktree. "I would much rather have lots of customers paying like a small amount than fewer customers paying more" [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s).
- The base plan is $5/month. Pro starts at $12/month with a custom domain and 5 pages. Some customers pay over $100/month for many pages (2026-10) [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s).

## Metrics

- About $31,500 MRR (2026-10) [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=69s).
- A [price](../topics/pricing.md) rise from $4 to $5/month in late June 2026 added nearly $5K MRR [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s).
- Spend: about $100/day on Google Ads and $100 to $200/month on a coding agent (2026-10) [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=788s).

## Growth channels

- **Hand-built pages for friends,** who stay on a free grandfathered plan [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=351s).
- **Outreach, Product Hunt, then Instagram ads** [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=424s).
- **[Google Ads](../topics/google-ads.md) on search intent.** After an Instagram algorithm change in 2021 or 2022, search ads became "money in, money out" for about 4 years [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=485s).

## Timeline

- 2019: Charlie quits Squarespace for Visual and builds Liinks near year end [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=318s).
- First premium plan at about $3/month reaches $100 MRR in a couple of months [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=424s).
- 2020: back to Squarespace during COVID, Liinks grows on the side [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=318s).
- 2024-08: he quits once MRR matches his salary [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=597s).
- 2026-06: price goes from $4 to $5. 2026-10: about $31.5K MRR [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s).
