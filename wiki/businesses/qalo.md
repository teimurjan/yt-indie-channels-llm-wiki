Qalo is a silicone wedding ring brand for people who are active.

## What it is

- Silicone rings for the gym and sports. No ring for activity existed, while people own gear for each activity ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=71s)).

## Founders

- [KC Holiday](../people/kc-holiday.md), a waiter and bartender in Beverly Hills, and Ted, his restaurant manager ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md)).
- Handshake deal: KC gave time and energy. Ted gave money ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=131s)).

## Business model

- Direct-to-consumer sales through a Shopify store ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=450s)). See [ecommerce](../topics/ecommerce.md).

## Metrics

- Peak: $30M/year, close to 100 employees, 20% profit margin (2024-03) ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s)).
- Over $100M in lifetime sales (2024-03) ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- **A website is not distribution.** The store launched to zero visitors ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=450s)).
- **Seeding through friends.** KC offered a ring to NFL quarterback Andy Dalton through his wife, a college friend ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=512s)).
- **TV moment.** HBO's Hard Knocks spent about 10 minutes on the ring. Sales went up 3x to 4x the next day ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)).
- Press moments fade in about 2 days, so use the spike fast ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)).

## Timeline

- The founders emptied their savings for the first batch. KC and his wife moved in with his mother ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=267s)).
- The batch had uneven edges. They hand-trimmed about 50,000 rings with eyebrow scissors ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=390s)).
- After Hard Knocks: KC quit the restaurant. In 6 to 8 months the team grew to 13 ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s)).
- 2024-03: over $100M lifetime sales ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=0s)).
