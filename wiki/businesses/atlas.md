Atlas (helloatlas.io) is a SaaS app that former YouTuber [Sebastian Ghiorghiu](../people/sebastian-ghiorghiu.md) built without promoting it to his audience ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)). The source does not describe the product in detail.

## What it is

- A subscription software product with a team of 10+ people (2025-09) ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)).

## Founders

- Sebastian Ghiorghiu, who grew a YouTube channel to nearly 1M subscribers with money content, then stopped posting ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)).
- He bought out his non-technical co-founder on January 6, 2025, at about $13K to $14K MRR ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)).

## Business model

- Recurring revenue from subscriptions ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s)).

## Metrics

- $250K a month in recurring revenue in June 2025, about 9 months from zero ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s), [11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=688s)).
- "Multi six figures a month" in recurring revenue (2025-09) ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)).
> Conflict: the [laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md) description says $250K a year, while the transcript says $250K a month. Both come from the same source.

## Growth channels

- YouTube videos, short-form content and paid ads bring 60% to 65% of traffic. Affiliates, ChatGPT answers, SEO and App Store search bring the rest (2025-09) ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=756s)).
- **Growth in stages.** First a few people and Discord communities, about $10K/month. Then paid YouTube creator videos. Then short-form until videos went viral. Last, paid ads ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)).
- **A content flywheel.** Each creator video is cut into shorts and ads, so people see the brand everywhere ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)).
- **Not to his own fans.** Sebastian expected an app sold only to his fans to fail against the competition. His name still helped with outreach and hiring ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=284s), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=456s)).

## Timeline

- 2025-01-06: co-founder buyout at about $13K to $14K MRR. Sebastian writes down a $250K/month target and the steps ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)).
- 2025-06: $250K/month in recurring revenue ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s)).
