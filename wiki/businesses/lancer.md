Lancer is an AI agent that finds, qualifies and bids on Upwork jobs for freelancers and agencies.

## What it is

- Upwork has about 200K job posts a month, and a human needs about 10 minutes to qualify a job and write a proposal. Lancer uses LLMs to do both [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=153s), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=325s).
- "Every platform which has a huge user base is something that you can automate and build something on top of" [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=266s).

## Founders

- [Ivan Nedlekovski](../people/ivan-nedlekovski.md), a founder from Macedonia. His software agency, MB Masters, did over seven figures over 5 years with almost 20 staff, mostly from Upwork clients [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=153s).

## Business model

- Usage-based plans: $79 for 30 proposals plus $2 per extra, or $300 for 250 proposals plus $1.50 per extra. Unlimited was a $500/month launch offer [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=78s), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=140s).
- The best customers are high-volume agencies more than freelancers [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=571s).

## Metrics

- $10K/month by month 3 or 4 after launch, with zero paid ads (2026-03) [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=78s).
- In beta, agency-owner friends closed three five-figure clients within 2 weeks [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=206s).

## Growth channels

- **"Connectors".** Two Upwork coaches drove most growth. Each coach gets 5 to 20 new paying clients a month [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=463s). One connector deal replaces hundreds of sales calls [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s).
- **Lifetime [affiliate](../topics/affiliate-marketing.md) commissions.** 30% for a coach who signs and onboards a client, 20% for a plain referral. Tolt tracks them [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s), [12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=741s).
- **Getting connectors.** A beta user introduced the first coach. For the second, Ivan offered $1,000 for a call over LinkedIn [source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s).
