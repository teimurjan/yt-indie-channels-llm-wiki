Glaze is a website that Tim Gabe reviews as a reference for UI and UX design.

## What it is

- The source covers only the Glaze website, not its product ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=15s)).
- The site has a mouse-driven moving background, off-grid type, horizontal story scroll and damped smooth scroll ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=15s)).

## Design notes

- **Strengths.** Off-grid headings and logos add energy ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=15s)).
- **Weak spots.** A long loading animation and small body text on mobile. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) flags a possible accessibility issue ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=139s)).
- **Lesson.** Heavy animated sites can fail on older phones and slow internet. Test on mobile and slow devices ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=139s)).
