Youform is a cheaper Typeform alternative with a generous free plan, run by a solo founder.

## What it is

- An online form builder. Over 90% of features are free [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s).
- A one-click migration tool turns a pasted Typeform URL into a Youform form in seconds [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s).

## Founders

- [Abhishek](../people/abhishek.md), solo. His earlier no-code chatbot builder, Botflow, had about 200 users, and most used it as a conversational form [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s).

## Business model

- Freemium SaaS. Free to paid conversion is 1.5% to 2% (2025-08) [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s).
- Monthly expenses under $1,200 (2025-08). OpenAI handles fraud detection on the free plan [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s).
- Stack: Laravel on AWS, Cloudflare, Stripe, OpenAI, Slite, Simple Analytics and Mailgun [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s).

## Metrics

- About $11K MRR, 2 years after its 2023 launch (2025-08) [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=56s).
- About 35,000 registered users and about 500 paying customers (2025-08) [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s).
- About 35,000 unique visitors per month and over 4M form submissions (2025-08) [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s).

## Growth channels

- **"Finding the gap."** Pick a popular tool, list the pains it does not solve, reach its users with a basic landing page, and offer one-click migration [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s).
- **Search for "X alternative"** on Twitter, Reddit and Typeform's forum. Price alone was not enough, so he also looked for missing features [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s).
- **Product and support drive referrals** once the message speaks to the ideal customer [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=521s).

## Timeline

- Typeform raises prices, and Botflow users show the gap [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s).
- 2023: launch after a 2-week MVP with basic fields and CSV export, no integrations and no logo [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s).
- After 200 users: his wife designs the current landing page [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s).
- 2025-08: about $11K MRR [source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=56s).
