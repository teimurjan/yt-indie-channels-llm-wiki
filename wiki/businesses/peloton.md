Peloton is a connected fitness company that sells an exercise bike and a class subscription. Tim Gabe uses it as a retention example.

## What it is

- A bike plus live and recorded classes with star instructors such as Cody Rigsby and Ally Love ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=655s)).

## Founders

- The sources do not name the founders.

## Business model

- Hardware plus a [subscription](../topics/subscriptions.md). The bike costs $1,700 (2026-04) ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=501s)). The membership is $44/month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=197s)).

## Metrics

- 90% annual subscriber retention (2026-04) ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=501s)).
- Members who use social and output features work out 15% more often (2026-05) ([source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=509s)).

## Retention mechanics

- **Live callouts.** Instructors call out riders' names in front of thousands. Tim credits this for people paying for years ([source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=197s)).
- **Parasocial bonds.** AI can copy features but not human connection with instructors ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=655s)).
- **Totals that never cap.** Classes, miles and output keep growing, so there is no finish line ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=561s)).
- **Competence feedback.** Personal records show real progress ([source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=646s)).
- See [gamification](../topics/gamification.md), [retention](../topics/retention.md) and [behavioral design](../topics/behavioral-design.md).
