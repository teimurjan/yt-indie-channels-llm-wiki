Outrank is an SaaS tool that started as a blog post generator and is becoming an all-in-one SEO tool.

## What it is

- It writes and posts blog articles. Founder Tibo is turning it into an all-in-one [SEO](../topics/seo.md) tool ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)).
- [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md) uses it to write and post blog articles for his own SaaS products ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=589s)).

## Founders

- [Tibo](../people/tibo.md), a French founder who built and sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md)).

## Business model

- SaaS subscription. One of five products in Tibo's [portfolio](../topics/app-portfolios.md), with [Revid AI](../businesses/revid-ai.md), [SuperX](../businesses/superx.md), [PostSyncer](../businesses/postsyncer.md) and [Feather](../businesses/feather.md) ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)).

## Metrics

- Just passed $200K/month (2025-12) ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)).

## Growth channels

- $0 to $20K/month with [building in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) only ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=810s)).
- $20K to $200K/month with ads, a dedicated SEO hire and an [affiliate program](../topics/affiliate-marketing.md) ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=810s)).
