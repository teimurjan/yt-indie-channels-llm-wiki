Whoop is a wearable health tracker with a subscription. Sources cite it as an app that builds personal identity metrics that AI agents cannot copy.

## What it is

- Whoop Age estimates physiological age from 9 metrics, such as sleep consistency, VO2 max, resting heart rate, lean body mass and steps ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=357s)).
- Pace of Aging updates weekly on a scale from -1 to 3, based on the last 30 days ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=419s)).

## Business model

- Hardware plus membership. The single weekly identity number drives behavior: [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) changes his sleep and steps for his score and competes with his wife on it ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=419s), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=482s)).

## Metrics

- Raised over $800M in total at a valuation above $3B. Over 50% of members still use it daily 18 months after purchase (2026-04) ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=482s)).

## Why sources cite it

- Tim Gabe's "agent paradox": agents replace the reason users open an app, so the emotional layer becomes the product. Whoop is his example of a personal identity metric, beside [Strava](strava.md) (social identity) and [Robinhood](robinhood.md) (community) ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=67s)).
