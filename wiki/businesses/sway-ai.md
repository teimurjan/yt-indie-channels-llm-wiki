Sway AI is a dating-profile analyzer app. It went from $0 to $2M ARR in 6 months.

## What it is

- An app that analyzes a user's dating profile and gives feedback. The idea mixes [Rizz GPT](rizz-gpt.md) and [Umax](umax.md): it sits in the dating niche and rates your profile and looks ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
- The idea came from Reddit. People post their [Hinge](hinge.md) profiles there for public feedback. Sway made that feedback private ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=207s)).

## Founders

- [Daniel Heintzman](../people/daniel-heintzman.md) did design and product. Luis ran marketing and operations. Charles was the iOS engineer ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=882s)).
- Daniel later became Head of Product at [Cal AI](cal-ai.md). He worked at Robinhood, Snap and Meta before ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [25:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1529s), [30:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1850s)).

## Business model

- Subscription that defaults to annual. Annual collects cash up front to fund growth. Weekly plans grew slower ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [19:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1177s)).
- A credits upsell for AI photo packs: $14.99 for 1 pack, $49.99 for 10. It made about 11% of revenue. Consumables have no spending cap, so "whales" can spend a lot ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1131s), [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1211s)).
- Onboarding works as a sales funnel: short videos on the problem, easy multiple-choice questions, then the dream result. The aha moment comes after the paywall ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [15:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=938s), [23:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1412s)).

## Metrics

- $0 to $2M ARR in 6 months after a summer 2024 launch (2026-05) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
- Organic content plus one influencer got it to $30K to $40K a month before paid ads ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=645s)).
- One Christmas Day slideshow got about 4M views and made $5K to $6K in a day (2024-12) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=578s)).

## Growth channels

- **Distribution before code.** An Instagram account about Hinge prompts sold manual profile reviews for about $15 over DM ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=340s)).
- **TikTok slideshows.** The team copied the most viral posts of a niche account. Backgrounds came from Figma and slides from a spreadsheet in Canva. One or two hours made a month of posts. Slideshows got 10K to 50K views each ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=390s), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=459s)).
- **Warm up unbranded.** Aesthetic posts with no brand came first. Branding and a CTA on every slide came after videos got traction ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=490s)).
- **One influencer as the ad engine.** Paid ads ran from the creator's own page. The limit was creative volume. Few dating creators promote apps, because they sell their own coaching ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=665s), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=757s)).

## Timeline across sources

- Before Sway, Daniel and a co-founder tried an AI financial advisor. Personal finance was hard to monetize, so they moved to dating, one of the highest-monetizing niches ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
- Summer 2024: launch. 2024-12: the 4M-view Christmas post.
- About 6 months after launch: $2M ARR.
