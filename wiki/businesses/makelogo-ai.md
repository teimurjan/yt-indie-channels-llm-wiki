MakeLogo.ai is an AI logo generator that Nico Jeannen built and sold through Acquire.com.

## What it is

- An AI logo generator. Users buy one-time packs of 5, 10 or 15 logos, plus customization ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).

## Founders

- [Nico Jeannen](../people/nico-jeannen.md). He built 17 apps in about a year and sold two of them, MakeLogo.ai and [TalkNotes](talknotes.md), for $265K in total (2025-03) ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).

## Business model

- One-time payments per logo pack, not a subscription ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).

## Metrics

- $26K in sales before the exit ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s)).
- It sold for $65K cash, so the total return was about $85K (2025-03) ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s)).

## Growth channels

- **Fake backend first.** Nico used a Typeform to collect the brief and the payment, with no backend. First sales proved demand before he built the real interface ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=422s)).
- **Facebook ads.** Nico grows his apps with Facebook ads rather than organic short-form video. He starts ads only after organic traction ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=0s), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s)).

## Sale

- He listed it on Acquire.com and wrote the listing like a landing page. Bids rose from about $50K to $65K paid over a year. Then one buyer offered $65K in cash, with a close in 2 weeks ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=752s)).
- Acquire.com does not handle the wire transfer ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=752s)).

## Timeline

- **November (about 2024):** Nico starts building ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s)).
- **March (about 2025):** The sale closes, about 6 months after the start ([source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s)).
