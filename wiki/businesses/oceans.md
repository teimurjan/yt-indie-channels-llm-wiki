Oceans is a bootstrapped offshore staffing company that places full-time executive assistants and operators, mostly in Sri Lanka, inside client teams.

## What it is

- Staff work 40 hours a week, long term, and join the client's Slack, calendar and all-hands. Most live in Sri Lanka, some in South Africa ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).

## Founders

- [Ian Myers](../people/ian-myers.md), founder and CEO. He left finance and a venture-backed app company because he wanted a business that served his life, not a board. He owned 100% at the start ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)).

## Business model

- About $3,000 to $4,000/month per full-time headcount. Oceans pays wages and benefits and keeps the margin (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=155s)).
- No outside funding. Millions of dollars in profit (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- Small tool stack: Google Workspace and Sheets, Stripe, Notion, tldv and HubSpot ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=761s)).

## Metrics

- Year 1 revenue: $650K (2022). Last year: about $15M (2025-03). The video title says $10M/year ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- Hires 30 to 40 people a month (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).

## Growth channels

- **Referrals, no paid marketing.** Great service makes customers look like heroes to their friends. Business owners talk about hiring all the time ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=618s)).
- **Friends first.** The first three customers were friends at a discount, with no profit ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)).
- **Over-deliver for the first 20 to 50 clients**: fast replies, comped months after mistakes, the founder on the phone ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=678s)).

- 2022-02: first contract. 2022 revenue $650K ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- Growth past 5 to 8 people broke processes. Writing them down was the hardest part ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).
- 2025-03: about $15M revenue in the prior year ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
