Forest is a long-lived focus app. It grows a virtual tree during a focus session. A switch to another app kills the tree.

## What it is

- The session screen shows a tree, a timer and a tag. A switch to Instagram kills the tree. The timer is secondary ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=257s)).
- Users earn coins. These coins led to over 2 million real trees planted (2026-06) ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=317s)).

## Founders

The sources do not name the founders.

## Business model

The sources do not describe pricing. Users earn coins in the app. The coins fund real trees ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=317s)).

## Metrics

- Over 2 million real trees planted through user coins (2026-06) ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=317s)).

## Why it works

- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses Forest as the example of his "behavior pattern": show a stake on screen, not a metric. The absence of the behavior costs the user something ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=257s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=317s)).
- "The dying tree did the discipline work for them." ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=317s))

## Weak spots

- [Abhishek](../people/abhishek.md) names a Forest alternative as an open gap. He says its recent reviews are mostly one star (2025-08) ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=256s)).

> Conflict: [MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md) (2026-06) holds Forest up as a top 1% indie app that beats giants. [_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) (2025-08) says its recent reviews are mostly one star and that the niche is open to a competitor.
