BulkMockup is a Photoshop plugin that makes print-on-demand mockups in bulk. It grows through long-tail YouTube tutorials.

## What it is

The plugin cuts 30 to 60 minutes of manual mockup work to about 2 minutes. The demo makes 50 mockups in about 1 minute. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=103s), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=696s)

## Founder

[Vikash Prajapat](../people/vikash-prajapat.md), a former Photoshop freelancer from India. An Upwork client expected 3 to 4 days for 1,800 mockups. Vikash did the job in about 30 minutes with a script and sold the script for $300. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=136s)

## Business model

It sold as a one-time lifetime deal for a couple of years. Then an Upwork developer polished it into a monthly product, sold on Gumroad. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=224s)

## Metrics

- About $12K to $13K/month on Gumroad for the past year (2026-02). [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=103s)
- 22% of the YouTube channel's views come from Google search (2026-02). [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)
- Over 1,500 recorded support videos and calls in 3 years, and over 100 five-star reviews, mostly about support. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=410s), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s)

## Growth channels

- **Content flywheel.** Find customer pain, make content about it, attract customers, and learn new pain from them. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=283s)
- **Four sources of pain.** Customer communities (never sell there), a custom-tutorial offer in the onboarding email, recorded support, and comments on niche YouTube videos. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=410s), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s)
- **Search-first YouTube.** Put the keyword in the title, the description and the first 30 seconds. YouTube videos rank on Google without the backlinks a blog post needs. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=643s)
- **Ignore views.** One video with 370 views in 4 months brought 3 customers at $15/month. Another with 12,000 views in 6 months brought $213. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s)

## Tools (2026-02)

A Loom alternative from AppSumo ($19 lifetime), BoldDesk ($15/month), Senja, Zoom ($20), Adobe ($40/month), Notion ($12/month) and Cal.com. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=773s)
