ReelShort is a Chinese short-drama app with 60-second vertical episodes.

## What it is

- It holds users longer per day than the biggest streaming services (2026-05). It shoots a full series in 4 days, in a repurposed COVID hotel in China ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=0s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls it "Candy Crush with a plot" because its design copies mobile games ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=46s)).

## Business model

- A graduated gate. Episodes 1 to 5 are free with no sign-up. Episodes 6 to 8 each need a 30-second ad. Episode 9 and later need coins ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=88s), [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=148s)).
- Episodes cost 42 to 66 coins. A typical cliffhanger unlock is 52 coins. Bundles: 500 coins for $4.99 and 1,100 coins for $9.99 (2026-05) ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=257s)).
- One show costs about $40 over about 80 unlocks, about 3 times a monthly [Netflix](netflix.md) plan ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=318s)).
- Tim calls the pricing an "anti-calculator" dark pattern. Variable prices, odd coin ratios and no spend total stop users from adding up the cost ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=257s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md) and [pricing](../topics/pricing.md).

## Metrics

- Engagement per day above the big streaming services (2026-05) ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- The source covers product design, not acquisition. Retention comes from a "zero-decision player". It has no episode list and no progress bar. A swipe up opens the next episode ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=538s)). Netflix shows at least 6 decision points before you watch ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=476s)).
