Threadly makes custom merch for companies, fundraisers and non-profits. It is one of four apparel brands from the team behind [Greek House](greek-house.md).

## What it is

Custom merch for companies, fundraisers and non-profits. Its sister brands are Greek House (fraternities and sororities), [College Thread](college-thread.md) (college departments and organizations) and [Athlete's Thread](athletes-thread.md) (licensed athlete merch on Shopify). [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s)

## Founders

[Karthik Shanadi](../people/karthik-shanadi.md) and his co-founder started Greek House in 2013, part time while in school. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s)

## Business model

No in-house manufacturing. US print partners fulfil orders through a vendor portal that Karthik's team built. The portal makes their account take about a tenth of the time to service, so partners prioritize them. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s)

## Growth channels

The group uses four channels. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s), [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s)

1. Outbound one-to-one email, then outbound sales.
2. An ambassador or affiliate program that gives free product for content and promotion.
3. Paid Google search for high-intent queries.
4. Free marketplaces: Etsy and Google Shopping.

Service is part of the brand: replies in under 4 hours, shipping in 2 to 3 business days, neck labels and custom packaging. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=732s)
