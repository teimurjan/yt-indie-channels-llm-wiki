Built With Science is a fitness app. In this source it is a paywall case study.

## What it is

- A fitness app that spent about $100K/month on Meta ads (2026-10) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)).

## Founders

The source does not name the founders.

## Business model

- Subscription with annual and monthly plans and a free trial ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).

## Metrics

- After a paywall change, the share of users who picked annual went from about 60% to 75%-80% at the same price (2026-10) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).

## The paywall change

- Show annual first and move monthly one tap away, so annual becomes the default choice.
- Lay out the trial day by day.
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as proof of "structure before price": revenue rose with no price change ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).

## Growth channels

- Meta ads, about $100K/month (2026-10) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)).
