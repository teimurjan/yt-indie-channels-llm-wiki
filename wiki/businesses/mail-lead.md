Mail Lead is a simple cold email tool. Jeremy Redman built it to send paid users to TaskMagic.

## What it is

- A simple, specific cold email tool. Specific products rank faster in search than general ones ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)).
- It serves TaskMagic's users: business owners, agencies and freelancers who need sales ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)).

## Founders

- [Jeremy Redman](../people/jeremy-redman.md), founder of [TaskMagic](taskmagic.md), with his CTO ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=172s)).

## Business model

- A "tentpole" side product. It ranks on its own and funnels paid users to TaskMagic ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=215s)).
- An "automation" button inside Mail Lead opens TaskMagic. Users who hit a limit start to pay for TaskMagic ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=511s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=534s)).
- Mail Lead users need leads, so the team built LeadQuest next. Each product cross-sells the others ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=548s)).

## Metrics

- "Almost seven figures" in revenue (2026-04) ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=606s)).

## Growth channels

- [SEO](../topics/seo.md) from a narrow, specific product, plus cross-sells inside the TaskMagic ecosystem ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)).
