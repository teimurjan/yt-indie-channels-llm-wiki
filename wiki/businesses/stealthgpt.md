This page collects what the sources say about StealthGPT, a web app that writes AI text that AI detectors do not flag.

## What it is

StealthGPT writes text that AI detectors do not flag. It also rewrites text from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=345s)).

## Founders

- [Joseph](../people/joseph.md) is a self-taught coder in Lakeland, Florida ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)).
- His earlier crypto firm, J Squared Invest, collapsed in 2018. The CFTC case ended in a $400K settlement and cost $50K in legal fees ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=217s)), ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=278s)).

## Business model

- At launch, users got 10 free tries, then three tiers from $4.99 to about $15 ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=465s)).
- The team is 6 to 7 people. Salaries cost $50K to $60K/month, and the tech stack costs about $5K/month (2024-07) ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=136s)).
- The stack is Next.js, React, Vercel and Supabase ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=652s)).

## Metrics

- $190K MRR after about 15 months. Profit is $10K to $30K/month after expenses (2024-07) ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)).
- Marketing spend is about $100K/month and rising (2024-07) ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)).
- 400K lifetime signups and about 8,500 active subscribers (2024-07) ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=136s)).

## Growth channels

- **AI directories**. A TikTok creator who tracks Futurepedia covered it and brought the first two customers ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=465s)).
- **UGC to explain a new category**. Early TikTok and Instagram content explained what the product does ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)).
- **Google Ads**. Early ads cost $10 to $20 per conversion. After summer 2023, the team put revenue back into Google Ads ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)). See [Google ads](../topics/google-ads.md).
