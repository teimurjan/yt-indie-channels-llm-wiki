Swim University is a pool and hot tub care education site run by Matt Giovanisci and his family.

## What it is

- Videos, blog posts, courses and a printed book on pool and hot tub care ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s)).
- The promise: take pools from "disgusting" to "clear and safe" fast ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=434s)).

## Founders

- [Matt Giovanisci](../people/matt-giovanisci.md). He built it at night while marketing director at a pool company. His boss found out and fired him ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=139s)).
- Team: Matt, his wife Steph and his brother. All three earn the same pay ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=568s)).

## Business model

- AdSense, Amazon affiliate links, four courses and a physical book. Courses and books make most of the money ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)). See [info products](../topics/info-products.md).
- The first PDF did not sell at $50, $40 or $30. It sold a lot at $24 ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)).

## Metrics

- $1M revenue in 2023 ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=65s)).
- About 225K YouTube subscribers and 100K newsletter subscribers (2024-05) ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s)).

## Growth channels

- **One script, many formats.** One long video a week becomes three short clips, SEO blog posts and emails ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)). See [content marketing](../topics/content-marketing.md).
- **Quality over production.** His top video each year is an unscripted PowerPoint ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)).
- **SEO tools:** Semrush and Rank Math on WordPress ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=522s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).

## Timeline

- Year one after the firing: $20K. Year two: $40K ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=200s)).
- 2023: $1M revenue ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=65s)).
- Matt reused the playbook on Roasty Coffee, which reached about 100K uniques/month in 2 years and sold ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=642s)).
