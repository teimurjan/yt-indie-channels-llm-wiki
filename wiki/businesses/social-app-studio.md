Social is a profitable app studio that buys and grows consumer apps with high-volume AI ads and constant A/B tests.

## What it is

- A studio of 22 apps with over 40 million downloads, including [Celebs](celebs.md), Font Maker, LitStick and Autopaste. It also built [WiseSam](wisesam.md), a consumer OpenClaw agent (2026-07) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=16s), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=514s).

## Founders

- [Patrick Stuart-Constant](../people/patrick-stuart-constant.md) and [Pablo](../people/pablo.md) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s).

## Business model

- Subscription apps, often weekly plans; Celebs runs about $6.99 to $14.99/week. WiseSam costs $1 for a promo week, then $10/week through Stripe web checkout [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=866s), [27:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1675s).
- Acquisitions: it buys apps after their viral peak, when sellers do not know how to grow them [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s).

## Metrics

- $16M annualized revenue, profitable, three years old, only a $1M pre-seed raised (2026-07) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s).
- Earlier: $11M annualized with 11 people [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=0s).
- Font Maker about $200K/month, LitStick just over $300K/month, Celebs a bit bigger (2026-07) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=514s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=548s).
- Revenue per user up about 3x in 18 months; portfolio LTV:CAC about 3x (2026-07) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=808s).
- Per month: about 4,000 AI video ads, hundreds of playable ads, thousands of statics, about 100 A/B tests (2026-07) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s).

## Growth channels

- Paid ads at volume; a six-person ad team, with the creative team tripled in 6 months [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [42:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2571s).
- Monetization tests: removing the onboarding paywall on Celebs made more money, because later paywall placements convert well [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s).
- Each test is tagged with its hypothesis so AI agents and new hires can reuse the learning [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1138s).
