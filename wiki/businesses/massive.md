Massive is a "Tinder for jobs" web and mobile app that matches users to jobs and fills out applications with AI.

## What it is

- Job matching plus AI-written applications. Over 10M applications submitted in about 2 years (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=78s)).
- "Tinder for jobs" lands at once. "Job search on autopilot" does not ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=181s)).

## Founders

- [Dan](../people/dan.md). His earlier product raised $3M from VCs and failed. Team: Dan, one full-time engineer and a few VAs and ops staff (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=168s)).

## Business model

- Hard paywall subscription. Pricing changes doubled revenue, and a specific way of running ads added 50% ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [30:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1803s)).

## Metrics

- About $200K/month, about $2M ARR (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)).
- 3M to 4M views a week, with 3 to 5 viral videos a day (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=437s)).
- One 11M-view video brought only about $20K MRR ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [17:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1025s)).

## Growth channels

- **Validation post.** A LinkedIn post from an account with about 500 connections got about 8M impressions and 5,000 comments ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)).
- **Manual hustle.** Hacker News, scraped emails and personal messages took it to about $20K MRR from April to December ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=328s)).
- **UGC and influencers with audience targeting.** "Stop using LinkedIn to apply to jobs" reaches white-collar workers who pay. The Indeed version reaches blue-collar workers and does not convert ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=489s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=549s)).
- **80/20 formats.** 80% copied viral formats, 20% new. One format got 382 views on one version and 1.7M on a nearly identical one ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=801s), [15:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=950s)).
- **Paid ads** for faster conversion data than UGC ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1178s)).

## Timeline across sources

- After the failed $3M product, the LinkedIn test. The waitlist reached 40K to 50K in 2 months, but only about 0.5% signed up after launch, so Dan calls waitlists useless ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=268s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s)).
- April to December of the first year: about $20K MRR, weeks from running out of money.
- 2025-08: about $2M ARR.
