Pregnancy+ is a pregnancy tracking app that Tim Gabe uses as a paywall testing case study.

## What it is

- A pregnancy app with about 80M downloads (2026-10) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).

## Founders

- The source does not name the founders.

## Business model

- Subscription with a [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) and a [free trial](../topics/free-trials.md) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)).
- In one test, a free trial with nothing to pay up front made 140% more revenue than a discounted intro price. The team had expected the discount to win ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)).

## Metrics

- About 80M downloads (2026-10) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).
- Tests became fast to ship. The pace went from 1 paywall test every 3 months to 3+ tests a month (2026-10) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)). See [A/B testing](../topics/ab-testing.md).

## Growth channels

- The source covers monetization, not acquisition.
