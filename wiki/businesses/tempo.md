Tempo is an unlaunched health app from Presh's studio, The Wellness Company.

## What it is

- Tempo combines wearable data and blood tests into a health-span score. It adds an AI chat ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [9:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=594s)).
- It was not yet launched at the time of the video (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=655s)).

## Founders

- [Presh](../people/presh.md), founder of [The Wellness Company](the-wellness-company.md). He worked 7 years for an angel investor and saw thousands of founder pitches ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)).

## Business model

- The source gives no pricing for Tempo. The studio's other apps earn only from subscriptions ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s)).

## Metrics

- Tempo has no metrics yet. The studio's three live apps make about $120K ARR, or $10.4K a month (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).

## Growth channels

- **One audience, many apps.** Users of [Go Polar](go-polar.md), [SunSeek](sunseek.md) and [Posture AI](posture-ai.md) also fit the next app. A new launch is not cold marketing ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)).
- The studio's apps grow mostly through organic App Store search ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).

## Timeline

- 2025-12: in development as the fourth app of the studio ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=655s)).
