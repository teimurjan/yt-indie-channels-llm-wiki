Profit AI is a Shopify app for profit analytics and AI marketing automation, built from a consultant's spreadsheets.

## What it is

- It shows profit by ad channel after all costs, forecasts ad spend for large stores, and builds a P&L. A Claude-based Slack bot with skills and connectors launches ads, sends email and builds landing pages ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=505s)).

## Founders

- [Jack](../people/jack.md) ran an e-commerce brand that sold music production software from 2016, then consulted for agencies and Shopify brands. He could not code ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=215s)).
- He exported his client spreadsheets as CSV, uploaded them to Cursor and said "build it". Fixing the calculations took 3.5 to 4 months ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=390s)).

## Business model

- Free plan with a 30-day trial of the paid plan. The paid plan started at $5,000/month and only a couple of existing clients bought it. It dropped to $800/month (2026-06) ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)).
- Services revenue sits beside the app ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)).

## Metrics

- $147,000 total revenue. $30K MRR for the app and just under $40K MRR for services (2026-06) ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)).
- 122 users, 38 or 39 paying. 47 installs against 43 uninstalls, so churn equals new installs (2026-06) ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)).
- Stack: Claude Code Max at about $200/month, Codex, Instantly, Framer and Google Sheets (2026-06) ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=437s)).

## Growth channels

- Mostly [word of mouth](../topics/word-of-mouth.md) from his consulting network ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md)).
- Instantly for outreach ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=437s)).
