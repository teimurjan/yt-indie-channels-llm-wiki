# Optibase

Optibase is an A/B testing tool for [Webflow](webflow.md) sites.

## What it is

- A Webflow app for A/B tests. It is one of four businesses run by [Jure Sotošek](../people/jure-sotosek.md), next to [Wrumer](wrumer.md) and [Parakeet AI](parakeet-ai.md) ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)).

## Founders

- [Jure Sotošek](../people/jure-sotosek.md), a 26-year-old developer who left Microsoft after about 1.5 years ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=203s)).

## Business model

- B2B SaaS subscription ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md)).

## Metrics

- About $6.5K MRR, about a year after launch (2025-04) ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)).
- All four of Jure's businesses: about $60K/month (2025-04) ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)).

## Growth channels

- The Webflow app store, Google and word of mouth ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)). See [Platform marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
