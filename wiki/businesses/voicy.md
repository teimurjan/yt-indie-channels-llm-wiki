Voicy is a speech-to-text dictation app on OpenAI's Whisper API that found its market in seniors and people with disabilities.

## What it is

- Dictation for Chrome, Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS and Android ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=357s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=410s)).

## Founders

- [Kourosh](../people/kourosh.md) built it for his father, who typed slowly, as a side project next to his 9-to-5 job. His first app, Meet Waves, reached $5K MRR ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=0s), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=209s)).

## Business model

- $8.49/month or about $260 lifetime. A 20% disability discount by email, used by 20% of users (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=186s)).

## Metrics

- $16K to $20K a month: $11K MRR plus lifetime deals (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s)).
- About 2,500 DAU, 10,000 MAU and 1,400 active subscriptions (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s)).
- YouTube went from 1,000 to 12,000 subscribers in about a month (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=566s)).

## Growth channels

- **Chrome Web Store.** Five friends' reviews brought the first views and about 100 users ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)).
- **Reddit.** The same post in five or six subreddits until he got blocked: 200 to 300 users ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)).
- **SEO.** About 200 blog posts, 30 landing pages and 4 to 5 languages. Ranking above a rival wins the click, and seniors compare Voicy to old dictation tools, not Wispr Flow ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=684s)).
- **YouTube** series "AI for Seniors" ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=566s)).

## Timeline across sources

- Chrome extension MVP in 2 weeks, Windows in about 3 months, Mac in 1 month, Linux in 2 weeks, iOS about 6 months before the video, Android in one shot with Claude Opus ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=357s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=410s)).
- User calls: 60% to 70% were fast-typing professionals wanting 10% more speed. 20% to 30% were seniors or disabled users who needed it. He built for the second group ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=602s)).
- 2026-10: $16K to $20K a month.
