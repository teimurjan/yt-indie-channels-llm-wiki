Career Hound is a job board that scrapes jobs straight from company websites and grows through cheap, repeatable UGC videos.

## What it is

- A job board that scrapes listings from company career pages ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)).
- A friend found his own job with such a scraper. The founders turned that process into the product ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)).

## Founders

- [Roman Lakhnyuk](../people/roman-lakhnyuk.md), a non-technical founder, and a co-founder. At first they each aimed to make $1,000 ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)).
- Roman studied psychology and sold alarm systems door to door for 5 summers. Managing sales teams helps him manage creators ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=177s)).

## Business model

- A hard paywall with no free plan: $7/week, $20/month or $50/year (2026-09) ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).

## Metrics

- About $25K to $28K last month, with $12K MRR. Yearly plans make total revenue higher than MRR (2026-09) ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).
- More than 8,000 users, 779 active subscriptions, about $50 LTV and 31% churn (2026-09). Churn is high because users stop once they find a job ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).
- 11.7M social views in the last 30 days (2026-09) ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).

## Growth channels

- **Content thrifting.** Roman scrolls Instagram, Reddit and TikTok for hours and saves what grabs his attention. Repeated patterns show the timeless topics ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=364s)).
- **Timeless over timely.** Trends die in a week. Timeless topics keep working ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=364s)).
- **One converting list format.** Videos list companies that hire remotely and worldwide, then send viewers to the site ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=459s)).
- **Older creators, low pay.** For job advice, creators with gray hair signal wisdom. Roman DMs people who already post for fun. In about 90% of cases he pays no more than $20 a video ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s)).
- **A stupid-simple brief.** Creators get a sheet of formats and do zero research ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=607s)).
- **Daily experiments.** Each failed test removes a wrong option ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=703s)).
