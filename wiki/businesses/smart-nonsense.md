This page collects what the sources say about Smart Nonsense, an animated YouTube Shorts brand that an agency funds.

## What it is

Smart Nonsense posts one highly animated 60-second Short per day ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=125s)). It also has a newsletter that turns each Short into an illustrated email ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=149s)).

## Founders

- [Henry Belcaster](../people/henry-belcaster.md) and [Dylan Jardon](../people/dylan-jardon.md) are college roommates in Chicago. They met in 2015 ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
- Henry ships at 80%, and Dylan sweats the details. Each corrects the other ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s)).

## Business model

- Their agency, [Clipt](clipt.md), pays for the content. Clipt makes about $2M/year (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s)).
- The content team costs $25K to $30K/month, and the Shorts earn about $5K/month. The content runs at a loss on purpose (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)).
- About 10 of the agency's 50 animators and editors work only on the founders' own content (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)).

## Metrics

- 1 billion combined views and 1M YouTube subscribers in about a year (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
- The newsletter got 10K subscribers in its first month and 20K three weeks later (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=149s)).

## Growth channels

- **Story beats abstraction**. Early book-quote content failed. A good story holds people for about 55 of 60 seconds ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=428s)).
- **Daily posting with outsourced editing**. Editors in the Philippines let the founders post every day without burnout ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)).
- **The Mr Beast model**. They chose not to be profitable and put agency cash into their own content ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)).

## Timeline

- 2015: Henry and Dylan meet ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
- 2020: They start a podcast. It has 14 views after 3 months ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
- They hire editors for the podcast, then sell those editors to other podcasts. That becomes Clipt ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=779s)).
- 2023-10: Smart Nonsense has 1B views and 1M subscribers ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
