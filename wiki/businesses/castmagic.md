Castmagic is a bootstrapped AI tool that turns podcasts, videos and other media into written content.

## What it is

- AI turns audio and video into show notes, posts and other text ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s)).
- The idea came from DTC Pod's show notes: an assistant did not know enough context to write them ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=96s)).
- The founders say output quality and full workflow coverage beat ChatGPT, which "has a feature, not a product" ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=489s)).

## Founders

- Three remote co-founders: [Ramon Berrios](../people/ramon-berrios.md), [Blaine Bolus](../people/blaine-bolus.md) (DTC Pod co-host) and [Justin](../people/justin.md), in Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Austin ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s)).
- One co-founder's past startups raised over $80M. Ramon tried to raise for Trend.io for almost 4 years ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=629s)).
- They work async, hold 30-minute standups on Monday and Friday, and meet for a week each quarter ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=691s)).

## Business model

- Monthly subscription with a usage cap. The team tracks LLM and transcription cost to protect margin ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=408s)).
- Marketing spend stays under 20% of revenue ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).
- Stack: Elixir with Phoenix LiveView; Webflow templates for the site ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=370s)).

## Metrics

- Over $120K MRR about 10 months after start, growing over 20% month over month (2024-06) ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).
- Net churn in single digits (2024-06) ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).
- AppSumo lifetime deal: about $350K revenue and about 10,000 users ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)).

## Growth channels

- **AppSumo [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md).** It gave brand, users and feedback, at the cost of recurring revenue from those users ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s)).
- **Creator program at volume.** Scrape creators in adjacent niches by hashtag, reach out at volume, negotiate CPM rates, keep the performers. Some get paid, others join as affiliates ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=264s)). See [Influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md).
- One person runs marketing with contractors and a Notion doc of content concepts ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s)).

## Timeline

- 2024-06: over $120K MRR, still [bootstrapped](../topics/bootstrapping.md) ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).
