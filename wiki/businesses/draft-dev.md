Draft.dev is a productized service that writes technical content for companies that sell to software developers.

## What it is

- Packages of 12, 24 or 48 pieces of content. Practicing engineers write, editors clean up and full-time engineers review. Delivery is weekly or twice a week ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)).
- Clients moved from early-stage startups to companies such as Redpanda, Cloudflare and Dropbox ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=125s)).

## Founders

- [Karl Hughes](../people/karl-hughes.md), in Chicago ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Premium positioning: no discounts, no one-off trials, quarterly commitments ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=427s)).

## Metrics

- Over $2.5M revenue, reached in about 3 years (2023-10) ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)).
- 6 to 7 full-time staff and hundreds of contractors in 54 countries (2023-10) ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=335s)).

> Conflict: the intro says $2.5M in 2 years. Karl says about 3 years ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)). Both claims come from [i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md).

## Growth channels

- One third referrals and word of mouth, one third organic search and social, one third cold outreach and other channels ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=396s)).
- The first 5 to 10 customers came from a network spreadsheet of about 50 people ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=312s)).

## Timeline across sources

- 2020: started with no website, only a "Karl's writing" sample page. Within 3 months of freelancing he had too much work and began to hire ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=156s), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=216s)).
- COVID moved conference budgets into content ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=634s)).
- 2023-10: team smaller than its peak because VC funding was about a third of the prior year. Karl bought a second business with debt ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=335s), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=655s)).
