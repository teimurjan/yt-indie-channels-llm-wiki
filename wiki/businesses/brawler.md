Brawler is an AI boxing app, one of three AI sports apps run by [George](../people/george.md). It grows mainly through paid ads with raw creatives.

## What it is

An AI boxing training app. Its sister apps are [Wrestle AI](wrestle-ai.md) and [AbMax](abmaxx.md). George built the first app, [Fight AI](fight-ai.md), with Rork. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=216s)

## Founders

George, a non-technical former wrestler. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s)

## Business model

Subscription app. Revenue is 75% from ads and 25% from influencers (2026-10). [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [26:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1609s)

## Metrics (2026-10)

- About 2,500 active subscriptions. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [42:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2578s)
- A raw screen-recording ad ran at a $10 CPA against a $40 break-even, about 3x to 4x ROAS. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=86s)
- George's three apps together make almost $41K MRR and $84K in the last 28 days. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s), [30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1827s)

## Growth channels

- **Raw paid creatives.** Raw screen recordings, talking heads and stitch videos work. Polished visuals got near-zero revenue. Put the CTA in the first 15 seconds. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=544s), [14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=849s)
- **Ranking-style ads.** A "best apps" list that ends with your app fails organically but works as a paid ad. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=699s)
- **Large creator deals.** Brawler paid a creator with about 1M followers $4,500 for three videos. George expected 25% to 50% back and got 75%. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1633s)
- **Pixel learning.** Expect to burn about $2K in the first 2 weeks while the pixel learns. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)

## Retention

- Daily notifications copy the style of [Duolingo](duolingo.md). [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s)
- On the third app open, Brawler offers a call with a founder. The calls showed that users had never found features built over weeks. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [42:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2556s), [43:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2601s)
