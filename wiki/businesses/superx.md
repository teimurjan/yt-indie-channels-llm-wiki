SuperX is a SaaS tool for growing on X: it finds viral posts and helps users write their own.

## What it is

- It finds viral posts and helps users write posts in the same style ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md)).
- Rob shipped features that went viral as demos, such as a cursor for tweets and an algorithm simulator. He removed the simulator because few people used it ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=796s), [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=840s)).

## Founders

- [Rob Hallam](../people/rob-hallam.md), a laid-off software engineer, built it with [Tibo](../people/tibo.md), a former Tweet Hunter co-founder ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md)).
- SuperX is one of the five products in Tibo's portfolio. The portfolio makes about $700K/month (2025-12) ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)).
- Rob runs the business alone while he travels (2026-08) ([NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Subscription at $29/month, with a trial. The trial rate is about 30% (2025-12) ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=126s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=155s)).
- Largest costs (2025-12): X API $2,000 to $3,000/month and OpenAI plus Claude APIs about $1,000/month ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [17:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=1043s)).

## Growth channels

- **Rob's X account.** X brings 95% of traffic. The account gets about 100,000 impressions a day and passed 34,000 followers in just over a year (2025-12) ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=155s)).
- **A content loop.** Post something entertaining or vulnerable, then education or a pitch, then the result with what it means to you ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=709s)).
- **Video.** X pushes video, and video gave Rob 10x the reach of text ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=895s)).
- **Link in the second tweet.** Other founders use Rob's tip to put the product link in the second tweet of a thread ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s)).
