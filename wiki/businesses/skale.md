Skale is a design agency for AI startups that makes websites, launch videos and billboards.

## What it is

- A design agency for AI startups. It aims for work that gets noticed and goes viral, not landing page conversion tweaks ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)).
- Clients include Bolt.new, Krea AI, [Framer](framer.md) and [Contra](contra.md) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)).
- Tools: Figma, Blender, After Effects and Cinema 4D for 3D motion, plus Slack and Notion ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=457s)).

## Founders

- [Mark Vassilevskiy](../people/mark-vassilevskiy.md), a 19-year-old designer from Kazakhstan (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=54s)).

## Business model

- One-off projects and retainers. The average retainer is about $20K/month (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)).

## Metrics

- About $80K/month on average, with a target of $100K the next month (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)).
- Revenue doubled about every two months: $10K, $20K, $40K, $80K ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)).
- 20 people on the team (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=54s)).
- 6 to 7 new clients a month (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)).

## Growth channels

- **Free redesigns of famous brands, posted on X.** The first one got about 110K views overnight and 10 to 12 cold bookings ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)).
- **Show the work to the brand.** He showed a Bolt redesign to the Bolt team at a Bolt event. They became a client a week later ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=209s)).
- **Trending brands.** An acquisition.com redesign got a reply from Alex Hormozi and 15 to 16 cold bookings overnight ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=360s)).
- **X audience.** 25K followers and more than 25M views in the year (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)).

## Timeline

- Summer 2024: every design job rejects him. He starts posting free redesigns ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)).
- November 2024: he opens Skale ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)).
