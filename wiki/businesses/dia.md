Dia is an AI browser from [The Browser Company](the-browser-company.md). [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as a case of "outcome-first" design.

## What it is

- A browser that aims to deliver browsing outcomes without making you browse: you ask and it answers ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=164s)).

## Founders

- Built by The Browser Company, which sunset its Arc browser despite backlash to build Dia ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=98s)). The source names no founders.

## Business model

- The source gives no pricing or revenue for Dia.

## Lessons

- **Do not pad AI onto a legacy UI.** Tim compares teams that bolt AI onto old interfaces to BlackBerry in the iPhone era ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=36s), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=98s)).
- **Ask what the outcome is and how many steps you can skip** ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=377s)).
- Intercom's [Fin](fin.md) is the paired example: it prices per resolved ticket ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=303s)).
