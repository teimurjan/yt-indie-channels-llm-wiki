Pckgr is a browser-based B2B tool that deploys apps to Microsoft Intune in one click. An IT admin built it to fix his own problem.

## What it is

- Packaging one application for Intune sometimes took an hour. Pckgr does it in one click from the browser ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s)).
- The app has no fancy design. It does one useful job ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=548s)).

## Founders

- [Thomas Mahony](../people/thomas-mahony.md), an IT admin who used Intune every day. He saw the same complaint on forums, and existing tools were too technical or too expensive ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s)).
- He built it before Cursor and Claude Code existed, so he wrote most of it by hand ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions. The first price was $25/month, set after a free beta ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s)).
- It targets smaller businesses, while competitors chase companies with millions of devices ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s)).
- Buyers ask about SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=277s)).

## Metrics

- About $60K/month, 100% bootstrapped, with a small team (2026-03). A chart shows a jump from $447K to $910K, which looks like year-over-year revenue ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=96s)).
- The video description says 1,000+ customers ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=277s)).
- Pat Walls guesses churn is very low because IT teams use it inside companies. This is not a stated number ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=664s)).

## Growth channels

- **Free launch on Reddit.** The free MVP got fast traction, feedback and some harsh comments ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s)).
- **Niche experts.** Microsoft MVPs made demo videos for a narrow audience. One YouTube video keeps bringing customers years later ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=254s)).

## Playbook

Thomas's five steps for niche SaaS ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=396s)):

1. Build where you already have credibility.
2. Look for pain points, not ideas.
3. Avoid the mass market.
4. Charge early, even at a low price.
5. Optimize for freedom, not scale at all costs.
