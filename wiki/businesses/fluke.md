Fluke is a no-code product tour tool for SaaS, part of Mike Hill's bootstrapped app portfolio.

## What it is

- No-code product tours for SaaS products [source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=81s).

## Founders

- [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md), an Australian former agency owner. Each portfolio company starts with four co-founders at 25% each [source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=138s), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s).

## Business model

- B2B SaaS with no outside capital. Each company grows to about $10K MRR to cover costs. Above that, profits go to the founders [source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s).
- The portfolio playbook funds each app with lifetime deals: private deals first, then AppSumo, then one last private deal before the LTD closes [source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s).

## Metrics

- The source gives no figures for Fluke alone. The portfolio makes just over $200K MRR (2025-11). Mike says three businesses make that money and two more are on the way. The source does not say which group Fluke is in [source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s).

## Growth channels

- The portfolio playbook: LTD cash pays for early SEO content and "alternative to" pages. Then reviews on Trustpilot and G2 and answers on Reddit carry the move to MRR [source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s).
