Gravl is an AI gym workout app that pairs Fitbod-style UX with a better workout engine.

## What it is

- The engine builds workouts from equipment, frequency, consistency, gender, weight and age. The MVP started as a tracker and became a generator in 2 to 3 months ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=173s), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=241s)).

## Founders

- [Julian](../people/julian.md) (development), with Matias and Aaron (growth and ads). They earlier ran an influencer startup for mobile games that became an agency ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s)).

## Business model

- Subscription. A long quiz, a "generating your plan" screen and a plan summary come before a [hard paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=576s), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=638s)).
- Ad spend is about a third of revenue. Salaries $50K to $80K/month for 13 to 14 people; Apple takes 15% (2025-11) ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s), [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=803s)).
- Human in-app support 24/7 ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=700s)).

## Metrics

- Over $440K last month and over 70,000 subscribers, about 2 years after launch (2025-11) ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=71s)).

## Growth channels

- **Reddit build story.** A post got a few hundred upvotes, over 300K impressions and the first few thousand users ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=291s)).
- **[Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md) in Spanish for South America** at under $50/day; the first ad got a subscription in 10 minutes ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=424s)).
- **Cheap creative in volume.** Creators at about $50 per piece, AI video tools and CapCut. Most winners are UGC. Copy what competitors spend most on in the Meta Ads Library ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=534s)).
- Later also TikTok, Google and Apple Search Ads ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s)).
