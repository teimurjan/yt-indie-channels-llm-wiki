GeoSports is a daily sports geography web game that Frank Smith built with Claude in about 8 hours.

## What it is

- Players read a clue and tap the place on a map. There are five questions a day ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=47s)).
- It belongs to the Geo Games family with GeoHistory and GeoFooty ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- Stack: Claude Max 20x, Vercel Pro, MapLibre for the globe, Upstash Redis for leaderboards, Google Sheets for questions and Stripe for Pro plans ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=496s)).

## Founders

- [Frank Smith](../people/frank-smith.md), a non-technical sports short-form creator with over 4M followers ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=47s)).

## Business model

- Programmatic ads bring $30K to $35K/month, about $1,200/day (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- About 1,800 Pro subscribers pay through Stripe (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).

## Metrics

- About $40K/month across the Geo Games family, three months after launch (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=47s)).
- About 700K monthly active users (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- Share rate 53%: of 100K daily players, about 53K share their score (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- Over 1M unique players in under one month, with no email campaign ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)).
- 98% of traffic comes from word of mouth. Frank's own posts drive about 2% ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=357s)).

## Growth channels

- **Score sharing.** Most players come from group chats ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=357s)).
- **Web first.** People played inside X without leaving the app. Near-zero friction helped it spread ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)).
- **Guest hosts.** Sports media people and athletes write the five daily questions. They usually retweet the announcement, which reaches a new audience ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=714s)).
- **Daily habit.** The game relies on players who return every day ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=714s)).

## Timeline

- Idea: his brother showed him MapTap, a map game with no trivia. Frank added a sports trivia twist ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=413s)).
- Build: about 8 hours over one weekend. Claude researched MapTap and wrote a spec first ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=456s)).
- Day 5: a reply to his own NBA tweet went viral. It brought about 40K players, then 150K the next day ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=636s)).
- 2026-09: about $40K/month and 700K MAU ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
