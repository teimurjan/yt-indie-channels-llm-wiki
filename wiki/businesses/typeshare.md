Typeshare is a SaaS tool for online writers, with templates, analytics and hosting. It is one of four writing businesses in [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md)'s portfolio.

## What it is

Writers use Typeshare's templates to draft posts, its analytics to track them, and its hosting to publish. [OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s)

## Founders

The source places Typeshare in Dickie Bush's portfolio. Dickie built the portfolio with his partner Nicolas Cole. [OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s)

## Business model

SaaS. The source gives no price.

## Metrics

The source gives no Typeshare-only numbers. The whole portfolio was on pace for $10M in 2024 (2024-07). [Ship 30 for 30](ship-30-for-30.md) made about $3M/year and [Premium Ghostwriting Academy](premium-ghostwriting-academy.md) about $5M/year. [Write With AI](write-with-ai.md) was on pace for $500K. [OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)

## Growth channels

- **Its own founder as user.** Dickie keeps his tools minimal: Apple Notes for ideas and Typeshare for publishing. [OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=762s)
- **A writing audience.** The sister products, Ship 30 for 30 and the ghostwriting academy, teach the same online writers that Typeshare serves. [OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)
