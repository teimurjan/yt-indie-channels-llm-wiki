MonAI is a minimal iPhone expense tracker with voice entry and Apple Pay automation, grown by one creator partner.

## What it is

- An expense tracker with a minimal design and AI automation ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=627s)).

## Founders

- [Florian Vates](../people/florian-vates.md) (Flo), a German iOS developer and former mobile UX lead. His previous app took 2 years to build and nobody cared. He built MonAI v1 in 1 to 2 months ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=177s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a hard paywall and a 7-day trial. A/B tests showed it beat freemium ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)).
- The creator partner gets a share of profit plus a fixed monthly retainer. A share of revenue did not scale well ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=281s)).
- Monthly tool costs (2026-04): Claude Code Max $100, RevenueCat about $400, Appwrite $25, OpenAI and Anthropic about $200 ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=930s)).

## Metrics

- About $300 MRR after 1.5 years alone ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=63s)).
- Video by video: $300 to $7.8K MRR, then $13K. A WWDC invite story got 1.7M views and added almost $5K MRR ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=418s), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=479s)).
- Over $35K MRR about a year after the partnership began (2026-04) ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)).

## Growth channels

- **One creator, not twenty.** A large Colombian tech and lifestyle creator found Flo's socials in the app. Three story-driven videos a month convert well ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=333s), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=749s)).
- **Markets outside the US.** The partner reaches Colombia ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=810s)).
- **Meta ads from creator videos.** Early tests looked strong. Flo regrets the delay ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=990s)).

## Timeline

- 1.5 years solo: about $300 MRR ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=63s)).
- End of 2024: First creator video. MRR grew 10x within a week and reached almost $8K within a month ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=271s)).
- 2026-04: Over $35K MRR ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)).
