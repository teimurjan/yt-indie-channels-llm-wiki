Celebs is a celebrity lookalike app owned by the app studio Social.

## What it is

- A celebrity lookalike app. It is six years old and bigger than ever (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s)).

## Founders

- Owned by [Social App Studio](social-app-studio.md), run by [Patrick Stuart-Constant](../people/patrick-stuart-constant.md) and [Pablo](../people/pablo.md). The source does not name the original builders ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).
- Social often buys apps in decline after a viral peak. Celebs looked like a short-term hit, but the curiosity it serves is universal ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=709s)).

## Business model

- Weekly plans run about $6.99 to $14.99 (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=866s)).
- It converts in about 10 places in the app ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s)).

## Metrics

- Celebs is a bit bigger than LitStick, which makes just over $300K/month (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=548s)).
- Social as a whole: 22 apps and $16M annualized revenue (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).

## Growth channels

- **High-volume AI ads.** Social makes about 4,000 AI video ads a month across its apps (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).
- **No onboarding paywall.** Removing it was a big win. Users reached the reveal faster and converted later at the result, share and reopen moments ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1059s)).

> Conflict: [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) says this result goes against industry advice. Other sources, such as [CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), say most users only ever see the onboarding and its paywall.

## Timeline

- About 2020: Celebs launches, six years before the video ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s)).
- After its viral peak, Social buys it ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=709s)).
- 2026-07: bigger than ever ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s)).
