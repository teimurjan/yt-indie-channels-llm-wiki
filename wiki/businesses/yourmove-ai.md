YourMove AI is an AI dating assistant that writes replies, writes and reviews profiles, and makes AI photos.

## What it is

- It writes dating-app replies, writes and reviews profiles, and makes AI photos ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=189s)).
- It started in 2022 as a party joke on OpenAI's Davinci model, about a year before the RizzGPT wave ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=239s)).

## Founders

- [Dmitri](../people/dmitri.md) built it as a side project while he was a data science manager at Opendoor ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md)).

## Business model

- A soft paywall on the core product and a hard paywall on AI photos, because photos cost more compute ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=392s)).
- A web funnel sends each blog post and Google ad to the best-fit product ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [15:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=951s)).
- At one point 10 to 12 Upwork contractors worked on it, most a few hours a week. A support person cost $5/hour ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [43:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2594s)).

## Metrics

- Peak about $30K/month at about 80% margin. At the point Dmitri stopped work on it, it made $28K/month (2026-01) ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=189s), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=420s)).
- SEO drove nearly half of traffic ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1105s)).

## Growth channels

- **PR into SEO.** Coverage in Fox, CNBC, Wired, the Washington Post and the Wall Street Journal brought few users. It gave the site high domain authority with no paid backlinks ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=510s), [13:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=839s)).
- **Low-competition keywords.** Pickup lines and "good morning texts", with in-article pop-ups that promote the app ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [14:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=858s)).
- **SEO decline.** AI answers replaced many informational blog clicks, so traffic fell ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [15:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=908s)).
- **Reddit over TV.** One Fox Business spot got fewer views than one good Reddit post ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=765s)).

## Timeline

- 2022: started as a party joke ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md)).
- 6 months free, then about a year to $1K/month, a year to $10K/month, and a year more to $28K/month. About 2.5 years in total ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=34s), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=448s)).
- Dmitri quit Opendoor at $17K to $18K/month, with layoff severance from the November layoff ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [51:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3065s), [53:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3183s)).
- About 4 to 5 months before the interview, he sold the company. He did not share the price ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=420s)).
