JournalistHunt is a paid database of about 200,000 journalists, one of three database products run by Rashid Khasanov.

## What it is

- A database of 200,000 journalists for press outreach ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).
- It sits beside [AngelMatch](angelmatch.md), a database of about 125,000 angels and VCs, and [InvestorHunt](investorhunt.md) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).

## Founders

- [Rashid Khasanov](../people/rashid-khasanov.md). He had to build an investor list by hand to raise money for his own fintech startup. That showed him that cold email works and that list-building wastes time ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).

## Business model

- Subscription tiers from $49 to $99/month (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=188s)).

## Metrics

- $260 MRR (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=188s)).
- The three-product portfolio makes about $32K MRR. AngelMatch brings $29K of that (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=66s), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)).
- Tool costs for the stack are about $62/month in total ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)).

## Growth channels

- The portfolio grows through programmatic SEO, which brings about half of revenue, plus free tools and some Meta ads (2026-08) ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- Rashid's database playbook has three steps. Pick a database that solves one problem. Collect the data by hand or with automation. Launch, look for payment signals, then lean on programmatic SEO ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- Pat Walls argues that directories rarely work as B2C. Businesses pay because the problem blocks them ([source](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)).
