Elephas is an AI assistant for Mac. It indexes local files, PDFs and Apple Notes into "super brains" that users can chat with.

## What it is

A native Mac app, with an iOS app too. Users build "super brains" from their own files and chat with them. A Reddit post shaped the super brain feature, which became the main product. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=399s), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=459s)

## Founders

Two people. [Ayush](../people/ayush.md) does marketing, and his partner codes. Ayush joined as a consultant after 11 years in a corporate job. The two have over 30 failed projects between them. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=175s), [18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=1134s)

## Business model

- Monthly, annual and lifetime plans, because Mac users expect a one-time payment. Prices rise gradually. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=97s)
- Sales channels: its own website with Gumroad licenses (about $110K this year), the Setapp bundle, the Mac App Store (about $12K) and the iOS App Store (2026-01). [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=140s)

## Growth channels

- **Reddit, phase one.** Posts took it from $0 to $3K MRR in 6 months. Each post leads with a short video demo inside a problem-and-solution story and offers a 30-day free trial. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=228s), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=399s)
- **Reddit playbook.** List at least 15 subreddits with Map of Reddit. Pick one pain point per post. Post in one subreddit per day and change the copy each time. Expect bans and keep backup accounts. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=499s), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=611s)
- **SEO, phase two.** A support article on OpenAI API keys ranked #1 for 6 to 8 months by accident. Listicles such as "18 best ChatGPT Mac apps" then ranked #1 and converted. AI answer engines now cite them. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=691s), [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=762s)
- **SEO playbook.** In Ahrefs, filter for keyword difficulty under 20 and volume over 500. List 30 to 50 topics, then write 2 to 3 posts a week and grow to 6 to 7. [R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md) [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=826s), [14:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=869s)
