# Early

Early (erly.co) is an iPhone alarm app that turns off only after you do push-ups on camera.

## What it is

- An alarm app built on Apple's AlarmKit API from iOS 26. The first MVP was only an accountability log ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s)).
- Stack: Firebase, Superwall, OpenAI API, Mixpanel, Claude Code ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [13:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=791s)).

## Founders

- [Jake Glacer](../people/jake-glacer.md), 24, a former Big Four accountant and self-taught coder. Solo founder with some help (2026-07) ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [13:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=787s)).

## Business model

- Free trial, then $29.99/year or $9.99/month (2026-07) ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=98s)).

## Metrics

- A little over $50K in the last 28 days (2026-07) ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=11s)).
- About 50,000 downloads in the last 30 days, over 200,000 in 2026 (2026-07) ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=98s)).

## Growth channels

- **Paid influencers.** He DMs day-in-the-life and lifestyle creators whose content already fits the app. Deals are 2 to 4 videos a month with a view guarantee at about $2 to $3 CPM (2026-07) ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [9:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=552s), [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=674s)).
- **Winning format.** "POV: day in the life of a college student": the alarm, push-ups, a green check. Viewers ask "what's the app?" ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=611s)).
- **Measure spikes.** Match spikes in views with spikes in conversions, and treat creator spend as a black box ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=732s)).
