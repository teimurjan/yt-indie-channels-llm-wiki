Bank Statement Converter is a website that turns PDF bank statements into spreadsheets. One person runs it.

## What it is

- It does one thing: PDF bank statements in, spreadsheets out ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=62s)).
- Stack: Kotlin core engine, Next.js on Netlify, AWS EC2, Brevo and Stripe ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=154s)).

## Founders

- [Angus Cheng](../people/angus-cheng.md), a developer in Hong Kong. He built it for his own runway planning ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=62s)).
- A friend helped with the MVP and left after a few weeks ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=95s)).

## Business model

- Paid plans via Stripe. No employees and no outside money ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md)).
- About $39K of $40K monthly revenue is profit (2025-07) ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [8:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=500s)).

## Metrics

- MRR: $6K (2022), $14K (2023), $27K (2024), $40K (2025-07) ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=214s)).
- 75,000 users, about 1,000 paying customers, about 40,000 visitors a month (2025-07) ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=231s)).

## Growth channels

- **Organic demand.** With ads off, it still got 2 to 3 signups a day. He then fixed every complaint ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=292s)).
- **Failed channels.** Google ads cost about $1,000 per $300 in sales. Cold email brought 1 sale in 3 months ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=247s)). See [Google Ads](../topics/google-ads.md).
- **No social media.** His users are not on Twitter or his blog ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s)).

## Timeline

- 2021: Angus quit his job and launched an MVP in about a week ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=95s)).
- About 6 months in: he cut Google ads ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=247s)).
- 2 to 3 years in: revenue covered his rent ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s)).
- 2025-07: $40K MRR ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=214s)).
