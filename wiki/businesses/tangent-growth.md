Tangent Growth is a paywall optimization firm for subscription apps run by Vahe Baghdasaryan.

## What it is

- It designs and tests paywalls for consumer apps. Vahe says he has run over 10,000 paywall experiments and helped apps add over $50M in revenue, including [Cal AI](cal-ai.md), Quittr, Claim and Rizz (2025-11) ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md)).

## Founders

- [Vahe Baghdasaryan](../people/vahe-baghdasaryan.md), interviewed by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) of [Consumer Club](consumer-club.md) on the Superwall podcast ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md)).

## Metrics and benchmarks

- Benchmarks (2025-11): trial start rate above 15% is good. Trial-to-paid above 30% is good. For a no-trial, mostly US app, install-to-paid of 10%+ is very good and under 4% is a problem ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=291s), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=337s)).
- An entertainment app with 40K to 50K organic installs/month went from 1.1% to close to 4% install-to-paid from April to September, with no new traffic ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=397s)).
- Some apps added $100K in 3 months from paywall tests alone ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=364s)).
- A good multi-step paywall lifts 20% to 40% over a single screen ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=884s)).

## Playbook

- **Cover every placement first:** session start (once a day), after X actions, when credits run low, and after a trial or subscription cancel ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=510s), [41:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2467s)).
- **Say "free" 5 to 7 times** and show a timeline instead of "3-day trial" ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=737s), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s)).
- **Spin-the-wheel discounts** beat a plain discount by a double-digit percentage ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [21:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1318s)).
- **Trial toggles:** high-intent apps keep one price. Lower-intent audiences need a cheaper no-trial price ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [25:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1512s)).
- **Discount only the annual plan** on transaction abandonment. About 10% pick the no-trial option ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s), [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2085s)).
- **Odd prices** such as $3.33/month feel authentic and add about $4/year per user for ads ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1866s)).
- **Price from competitors, ROAS and cost**, so ads pay back in about a month ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [37:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2240s)).

## Client examples

- Claim: 34% trial start and 50% to 55% trial-to-paid, then went paid-only because it made more money ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=953s)).
- Rizz: $9.99/week with a 7-day trial, or $6.99/week to skip the trial ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [22:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1328s)).
