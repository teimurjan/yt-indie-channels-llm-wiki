Recovery Local is Tim Stoddart's group of addiction recovery websites, such as sober.com and detox.com, inside his holding company.

## What it is

- It owns sober.com, yourfirststep.com, detox.com and a medication-assisted treatment site ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).
- It sits in a holding company with [Copyblogger](copyblogger.md), [Stuzzi](stuzzi.md) and stakes in five or six other companies ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)).

## Founders

- [Tim Stoddart](../people/tim-stoddart.md). He got sober, started a blog on Seth Godin's advice and found SEO by accident. The blog became [Sober Nation](sober-nation.md) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).

## Business model

- Lead generation. Treatment centers wanted his audience, so his recovery content became a lead generation outlet ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- Cash from the Stuzzi agency funds the holding company. The goal is a portfolio of cash-flowing assets ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)).

## Metrics

- The source gives no separate figure for Recovery Local. The whole holding company expects $5.2M to $5.5M for 2024 (2024-04) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).

## Growth channels

- **SEO, one keyword at a time.** Find what people search on Google Trends. Write the article that best solves that problem ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)).
- **Exact-match domains** in the recovery niche, such as sober.com and detox.com ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).

## Timeline

- Tim starts a recovery blog, which grows into Sober Nation ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).
- Rehab centers ask how he built the audience. He starts the Stuzzi agency. The first client pays $2,000/month ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- Agency cash funds Recovery Local and the other holdings ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)).
- 2024-04: the holding company expects $5.2M to $5.5M for the year ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).
