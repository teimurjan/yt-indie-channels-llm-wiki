Conch AI is an AI writing tool that bypassed AI detection, grew on organic short-form video, and was sold in early 2025.

## What it is

- An AI writing tool for students that bypassed AI detection. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s))

## Founders

- [Dan Kwon](../people/dan-kwon.md) grew it. He was a social media creator before he marketed apps. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s))

## Business model

- A paid AI tool. Paid ads were tested and dropped because margins were low. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=625s))

## Metrics

- $1M ARR in about 30 days and $500K in one month. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s))
- Lifetime: about 220M views and about $2.2M revenue, mostly organic (2025-10). ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=625s))

## Growth channels

- **Organic short-form video.** Scrappy, native-looking videos. Low-quality footage shot in the Snap app reads as real. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=327s))
- **Us-versus-them positioning.** Videos framed busywork and school bureaucracy as the enemy. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=496s))

## Timeline

- Launch: $1M ARR in about 30 days. The school threatened to sue, and the team almost got expelled. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s))
- Early 2025: sold after talks with 3 or 4 buyers. Some rivals posed as buyers only to extract information. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=647s))
