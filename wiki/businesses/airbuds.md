Airbuds is a widget app that shows what your friends listen to and sends a weekly music recap.

## What it is

- A home-screen widget with friends' listening. A weekly Sunday recap shows your top artists ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as his "flex" example: the app turns something users want to brag about into the growth action ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s)).
