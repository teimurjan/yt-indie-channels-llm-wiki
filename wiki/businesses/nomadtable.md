NomadTable is a bootstrapped meetup app where solo travelers, expats and nomads create activities and meet people nearby.

## What it is

- Users create activities and meet other travelers nearby ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=0s)).
- Two users in one place are enough for a meetup, so the app works without city-by-city density ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [46:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2802s)).

## Founders

- [Jay](../people/jay.md), a former Amazon software engineer, runs it alone. He got the idea on a 3-week solo trip and quit about a week after he came back ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2107s), [0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with weekly, monthly and annual plans. The annual plan is $69.99 (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=430s)).
- No in-app ads. Jay says ads can push revenue past $100K/month, but they hurt quality ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=388s)).

## Metrics

- Over 1M downloads and about $65K/month (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=364s)). A 2026-03 recap repeats these numbers ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md)).
- $140K MRR, 1.8M users and about 100K meetups, about 15 months after launch (2026-09) ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)).
- 10% to 15% of new users come from word of mouth. Almost all the rest come from UGC (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=181s)).

## Growth channels

- **Founder content first.** Jay's own videos brought the first 50K to 100K downloads ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=464s)).
- **UGC creators on CPM.** About 60 to 70 creators get 30M to 44M views a month. They earn $1 to $2 CPM with no cap and no retainer (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=508s), [40:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2435s)). See [UGC](../topics/ugc.md).
- **3-second retention.** Good formats hold 75% to 80% of viewers past 3 seconds ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1624s)).
- **No paid ads.** ARPU is too low for a social app to pay back installs ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2172s)).
- **Waitlist for the cold start.** About 12K to 15K people joined. About 6K installed on day one ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [47:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2836s)).

## Timeline

- Jay worked 3 years in big tech, then quit after a solo trip ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2107s)).
- Launch with a 12K to 15K waitlist. Barcelona had about 20 users ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [47:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2863s)).
- April: first hired creators, about 2 months after he saw multi-account UGC on Joseph Choi's show ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [52:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3177s)).
- 2026-02: over 1M downloads, about $65K/month ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=364s)).
- 2026-09: $140K MRR, 1.8M users. Jay spent 4 months in Bangkok, a hotspot for the app ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s), [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s)).
