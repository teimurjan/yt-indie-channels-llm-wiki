CodeGuide (codeguide.dev) is a SaaS tool that writes detailed project documentation for AI coding tools such as Cursor, so the models hallucinate less.

## What it is

- It chains prompts to produce project docs. [CJ Zafir](../people/cj-zafir.md) spent 9 to 10 hours per client project on these docs and automated 7 of those hours ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=306s)).
- It rides an existing audience: users of AI coding tools ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=132s)).

## Founders

- CJ Zafir does front end and marketing. A friend does back end and support ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=438s)).
- The idea came from his MVP agency and his "12 startups in 12 months" challenge, where 10 of 11 products failed ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=229s)).

## Business model

- $29/month, no free trial because AI models are expensive. Plan to raise to $39-49. Yearly plan at 40% off with partner perks (2025-05) ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=761s)).
- Costs about $3,500/month, mostly the OpenAI API at $2,800 (2025-05) ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=807s)).

## Metrics

- 100 users on launch day ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=498s)).
- $17K/month after 49 days. $42K MRR after about 3 months with over 4,800 users. About $90K projected for the next month (2025-05) ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=96s)).
- The intro also says over $500K ARR (2025-05) ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=51s)).
