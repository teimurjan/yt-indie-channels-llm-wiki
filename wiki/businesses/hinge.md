Hinge is a dating app. Sources cite it for its timed Standouts feature and as a platform that other founders build content and products around.

## What it is

- A dating app where users like profiles and prompts. Sources do not give its revenue or founders.

## Product mechanics

- **Standouts.** Added in December 2020: a daily batch of chosen profiles on a 24-hour clock. Users send a rose to show interest ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=242s)).
- **Loss aversion.** A missed Standouts window is lost for good, so users open Hinge from fear of missing out, not only from excitement. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls this the "tick tock formula": the hero feature changes on its own clock, like [FarmVille](farmville.md) crops and [Life Reset](life-reset.md) boss fights ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=242s), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=303s)).

## Hinge as a growth surface for others

- **Content.** [Eunice Lie](../people/eunice-lie.md) went viral on X by posting her Hinge chats with tech workers who asked about her startup, [Series](series.md). On one date she walked the man through Series onboarding ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=169s)).
- **Ideas.** People post their Hinge profiles on Reddit for feedback. [Sway AI](sway-ai.md) made that private, starting with an Instagram account about Hinge prompts that sold $15 manual profile reviews. Sway reached $2M ARR in 6 months ([ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=207s), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=340s), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
