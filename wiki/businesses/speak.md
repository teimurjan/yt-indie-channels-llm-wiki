Speak is a language app that builds its lessons around speaking out loud, in place of quick tap games.

## What it is

- Lessons center on speaking out loud. Speak asks users to talk and picks results over fun ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).

## Founders

- The source quotes Speak CEO Connor Zwick: "We will pick efficacy 100% of the time." ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s))

## Business model

- The source does not describe pricing. It reports annualized revenue only ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).

## Metrics

- Over $100M in annualized revenue, as Speak announced it (2025-11) ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).

## Why it works

- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses Speak as a case of the "hidden assumption". Language apps assume users want a quick tap game. Speak flips how much effort users give ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).
- Game-style apps can add speaking. They cannot put it first without asking more of their own users ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=121s)).
- Tim's rule: bet against the assumption the leader makes its money from ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).
