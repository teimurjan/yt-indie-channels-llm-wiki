Listing Bot is a tool in John Rush's product portfolio that submits products to directories.

## What it is

- It submits products to directories ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).
- [SEO Bot](seo-bot.md) has a "boost my domain rating" button that opens Listing Bot ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=576s)).

## Founders

- [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md). He spent 10 years in VC-backed startups. In 2022 he bought [Unicorn Platform](unicorn-platform.md) for almost $1M ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s)).
- John often builds with a 50/50 "co-maker". He does operations, legal and accounting. The co-maker does code and support ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=317s)).

## Business model

- The source gives no Listing Bot pricing. Non-AI tools in the portfolio have about a 90% margin (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s)).

## Metrics

- No separate figure. The portfolio has 26 startups, about 1M B2B users and about $3M ARR (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=58s)).

## Growth channels

- **Cross-promotion inside the portfolio.** Users who try one of John's products try at least one more ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=538s)).
- **Directories.** John lists his products on directories. He says this works best for "clickbaity" AI products ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s)).

## Timeline

- 2022: John buys Unicorn Platform and moves to bootstrapping ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s)).
- 2025-05: Listing Bot is one of 26 products ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).
