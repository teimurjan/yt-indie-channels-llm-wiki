Zipsnap is the app and product design agency that Tim Gabe runs. His videos use its client work as case studies.

## What it is

- A design studio for apps and websites. Captions spell the name "Zipzap" and "ZipSnap". The wiki uses the slug `zipsnap` [source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=0s), [source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=0s), [source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=0s).
- Clients include fintech and crypto apps, Freecash, Undertone, Nila Health and Postdocs.ai [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=600s), [source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s), [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=223s).

## Business model

- Client design projects. Tim describes the market price bands: mid-level designers take projects of about $3K to $6K, and senior deals run from $20K to over $100K because clients trust them to move business metrics (2025-04) [source](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=484s), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=615s).
- A six-stage process: onboarding, discovery, UX, UI, emotional integration and delivery [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=113s).
  - Each client gets a Notion portal with timelines, tasks, decisions, files and meeting notes [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=147s).
  - Discovery covers key flows only, so it takes weeks, not months [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=233s).
  - UI ends in a strict QA list and a component library. Handoff packages source files, specs and components [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=529s), [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=724s).
- Tools: Notion, Google Drive, Figma and Rive [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=147s). The agency hires Rive animators through Contra (2025-04) [source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md) [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=43s).

## Metrics

- Clients raised tens of millions of dollars and reached tens of thousands of users, by Tim's claim (2026-04) [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=0s).
- Freecash, a client, was #2 on the App Store (2026-04). Zipsnap designed its diamond streak tiers: 7 days unlock the first diamond and 42 days unlock another [source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=662s), [source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=451s).
- [SolCard](solcard.md) (crypto card) redesign: money loaded onto cards rose 79% and successful deposits rose 71% (2026-10). The team designed 15 status screens [source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=0s), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s).
- The same crypto card client grew new accounts 60% after a redesign with a clear declined-payment state (2026-10) [source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=512s).

## Client cases

- **Undertone.** The old paywall half-blurred the user's own therapy recaps. The redesign removed the blur and asks one question about sessions per month [source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s). Two different "used all sessions" screens became one pattern with a "2/2 sessions used" badge and a refresh date [source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=311s).
- **Postdocs.ai.** A plan step before generation made the 20-page surprise smaller [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=223s).
- **Crypto trading and AI transcription clients.** Secondary data sits in expandable tooltips. Voice-linked audio waves make the tech feel alive [source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md) [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=324s), [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=612s).
- **Voting rewards screen.** Tim redesigns one client screen at four skill levels [source](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=62s).
