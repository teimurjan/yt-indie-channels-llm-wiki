Clear 30 is a science-based app program that helps people quit or cut down on weed.

## What it is

- A 30-day program to quit or cut down on weed ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=0s)).
- It has a grant from the National Institute of Health. A partnership with the University of Michigan was pending (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=899s), [30:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1846s)).

## Founders

- [Asher Hurstman](../people/asher-hurstman.md), [Thatcher Kloff](../people/thatcher-kloff.md) and clinical psychologist Dr. Fred Munch ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=0s)).

## Business model

- Subscription. The program costs $30 a year, against over $55 a week that the average user spends on weed (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [7:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=441s)).
- Most users pick the annual plan with a 3-day [free trial](../topics/free-trials.md). The [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) says "No payment due now" and promises a reminder 2 days before the trial ends ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1405s), [23:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1439s)).
- Superwall's former lead paywall designer cites the Clear 30 paywall as a "hero's journey" style that shows progress steps ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=896s)).

## Metrics

- Subscriptions went from $10K to $30K a month in about one month (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=147s)).
- Paywall conversion went from about 20% to 30% after one change to the first paywall screen (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s)).
- Users aged 26 to 30 convert at almost double the rate of users aged 18 to 20 (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=380s)).
- By quit reason, "saving money" converts at 23% and "legal obligations" at 29% (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [7:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=441s)).
- App Store reviews grew from about 50 to almost 1,000 after a rating prompt in onboarding (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s)).

## Growth channels

- Influencers, Reddit, organic content and SEO came first. Paid ads on Meta and TikTok started the flywheel ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=178s)). See [Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md) and [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md).
- Ads use hyper-specific call-outs and comedy. Some of the best star Asher's mother, for older users ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=307s)).
- A "take the quiz" ad copied from Reframe did not work ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [26:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1618s)).

## Onboarding

- Built on a "value equation": dream outcome times likelihood, divided by time delay and effort ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=679s)). See [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md).
- A screen that says the program costs money raised conversion by about 3% ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1038s)).
- The paywall change that lifted conversion replaced a feature carousel with a day-by-day plan for the first 7 days ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s)).
