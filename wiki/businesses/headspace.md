Headspace is a meditation subscription app. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as an example of mood-setting design and trial tests.

## What it is

- A meditation app whose color, animation and sound set a calm mood before a session starts. Tim says the company invested millions in this ([source](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=302s)).

## Founders

- No source names the founders.

## Business model

- Monthly and annual subscriptions with free trials ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s)).

## Metrics

Test results as Tim Gabe reports them (2025-07):

- Renaming "trial" to "30-day guest pass" gave +7% new signups ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=106s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=168s)).
- A 7-day trial on monthly and a 14-day trial on annual gave a double-digit lift in conversions ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s)).

## Design lessons

- **Emotional anchoring.** Emotion starts at onboarding. The app's tone tells users how to feel. Color, type and motion are psychology, not decoration ([source](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=302s), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=360s)).
- **Framing.** "Guest pass" feels like an invitation; "trial" feels like an obligation ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=168s)).
- **Give the best plan a perk.** A longer trial on annual makes annual the obvious choice ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s)). See [free trials](../topics/free-trials.md) and [behavioral design](../topics/behavioral-design.md).
