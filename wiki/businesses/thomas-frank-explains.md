Thomas Frank Explains is a niche YouTube channel that teaches only Notion and sells Thomas Frank's Notion templates.

## What it is

- A second channel next to Thomas Frank's main productivity channel. He started it after he burned out on the main channel [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s).
- It fills demand that already exists, because people search for Notion help. He models it on Zapier's content strategy [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=678s).

## Founders

- [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md) [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s).

## Business model

- The channel feeds an email list. The list and long template walkthroughs sell [Ultimate Brain](ultimate-brain.md) and [Creator's Companion](creators-companion.md) [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s).
- Four content types: build guides with a free template, new-feature videos, a free fundamentals course and listicles. Each video pitches a paid template, a free template or an opt-in resource page [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s).

## Metrics

- About 230K subscribers, against close to 3M on the main channel (2024-04) [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s).
- Core Notion email list of 60K to 70K, out of 200K total (2024-04) [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s).
- Templates: about $2.5M lifetime sales to about 30,000 customers (2024-04) [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s).
- After a 45-minute template walkthrough, revenue rose to about $100K/month, later $100K to $120K/month (2024-04) [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s).

## Growth channels

- YouTube search for Notion topics [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=678s).
- Email: the first autoresponder delivers free templates, then offers Ultimate Brain with a subscriber discount code [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s).

## Timeline

- 2021: Creator's Companion launches and grows from about $3K to $15K/month within 6 months [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s).
- Ultimate Brain launch: about $90K in 30 days from one email to a waitlist of about 3,200 [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=0s).
- 2024-04: about 230K subscribers [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s).
