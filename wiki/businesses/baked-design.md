Baked Design is a monthly design subscription agency co-founded by Nick Buzz and Alex.

## What it is

- A design agency that clients pay monthly, a form of [productized service](../topics/productized-services.md) ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=0s)).

## Founders

- [Nick Buzz](../people/nick-buzz.md), an anonymous designer on X and ex-Meta employee, and a designer named [Alex](../people/alex.md) ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).

## Business model

- Monthly design subscription. The first client paid $4,317/month ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=877s)).
- Team: Nick and Alex alone until December, then one full-time hire plus a few part-time freelancers (2024) ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).

## Metrics

- MRR went from first money at the end of July to $24K, $48K, $94K, $110K, $120K and $160K. It then flattened to $100K to $120K (2024) ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).

## Growth channels

- Nick's anonymous X account and its [personal brand](../topics/personal-brand.md). The first client came from a "tiny UI" post ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=877s)).
- Public landing page "roasts". At first, leads did not pay $2K to $6K a month. The team went back to roasts, and inbound leads came ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=855s)).
- Nick markets all the time, with a strong tone. He says controversy works ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).

## Timeline

- End of 2022: Nick leaves Meta with about 2 months of savings ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=614s)).
- He runs about 12 projects at once, burns out, and narrows to Baked ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=810s)).
- 2024: first revenue at the end of July, a peak of $160K MRR within months ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).
