Flo is the name of two different products in the sources: Flo Health, a period-tracking app, and Flo, an AI analytics and agent platform for mobile apps built by [Ryan Thorp](../people/ryan-thorp.md).

## What it is

- **Flo (period tracker).** A health app that narrowed the broad health category to period tracking ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=560s)).
- **Flo (app analytics).** It pulls data from App Store Connect, Google Play, RevenueCat and ad managers into one place, and the user asks it questions. Flo 2.0 adds AI "employees" for a mobile app, such as ASO, Apple Search Ads, monitoring and forecasting agents ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2799s)).

## Founders

- Flo (period tracker): the sources do not name the founders.
- Flo (app analytics): [Ryan Thorp](../people/ryan-thorp.md). He started from the journaling app [Reflectly](reflectly.md) and ran a portfolio of 30 to 35 acquired apps. He sold most of it and now builds Flo (2026-01) ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md)). He built it because his day was spent pulling data from many platforms into spreadsheets ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2799s)).

## Metrics

- Flo (period tracker): 70M+ monthly active users. It raised $200M+ from General Atlantic at a $1B+ valuation ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=621s)). The video does not date these figures.
- Flo (app analytics): no metrics in the sources.

## Growth channels

- Flo (period tracker): [Josh](../people/josh.md) of Blue Throne uses it as the example for owning a narrow category. Define the category narrowly enough to be number one, but keep it believable ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=560s), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=700s)).
- Flo (period tracker) ran over 100 experiments a month. Josh cites this to tell six-figure apps to run many paywall and pricing tests ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2902s)).
- Flo (app analytics): Ryan's pitch is that AI agents will run growth tasks in the background, the way Google Analytics connects to a website ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [47:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2879s)).

## Timeline

- Flo (app analytics): Ryan's team acquired 30 to 35 apps from 2020 to 2025. They sold 80% to 90% of them to a buyer from gaming, and then Ryan moved to Flo ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=419s), [49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2952s)).
