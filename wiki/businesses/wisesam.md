This page collects what the sources say about WiseSam, a consumer AI agent app from the app studio Social.

## What it is

WiseSam is a consumer AI agent built on OpenClaw ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [27:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1675s)).

## Founders

- It comes from [Social](social-app-studio.md), the app studio of [Patrick Stuart-Constant](../people/patrick-stuart-constant.md) and [Pablo](../people/pablo.md) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).
- Social runs 22 apps at $16M annualized revenue (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).

## Business model

- WiseSam costs $1 for a promo week, then $10/week. Checkout is on the web through Stripe (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [27:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1675s)).

## Metrics

The source gives no WiseSam metrics.

## Growth channels and design

- **A long, personal funnel**. The ad warms the user. A long survey reflects their answers back and builds intrigue. The team calls this "happy friction" ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [25:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1537s)).
- Direct web visitors get a shorter path ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [26:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1587s)).
- **Guided first use**. Preset buttons send starter prompts ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [33:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2012s)).
- The team aims to find about 10 use cases that impress every user fast ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2172s)).
- **AI ads at volume**. Social makes about 4,000 AI video ads a month across its apps (2026-07) ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).
