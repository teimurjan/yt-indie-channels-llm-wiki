Local Rank is a local SEO SaaS that Jacky Chou built after Google updates wiped out his content site income.

## What it is

- A SaaS for local SEO. Jacky built it on the local SEO skills of his agency, [Indexsy](indexsy.md) ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=0s)).

## Founders

- [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md), who runs a portfolio of about 13 small internet businesses ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=54s)).

## Business model

- B2B SaaS that serves the same SEO customer as Jacky's other projects ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=630s)).

## Metrics

- $20K MRR at launch ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=151s)).
- $454K since it started "last March", about $50K/month (2026-03) ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).

## Growth channels

- [YouTube](../topics/youtube.md). 80% of the $20K MRR launch came from one video with 1.8K views. The launch tweet on X got about 30K views but brought only 20% of revenue ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)).
- Each video links to a newsletter lead magnet, and Jacky pitches later by email ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=495s)).

## Timeline

- Late 2023: Google's helpful content update and AI Overviews cut Jacky's content site income ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=187s)).
- 2025-03 (approx.): launch at $20K MRR ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
- 2026-03: about $50K/month ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
