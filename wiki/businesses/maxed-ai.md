This page collects what the sources say about Maxed AI, an all-in-one lifting and health coach app.

## What it is

Maxed AI is an iOS app for people who lift. Users track lifts, nutrition and supplements, and an AI coach is wired into every screen ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=161s)). It also has sleep data, custom programs, a leaderboard and a social feed ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=566s)).

## Founders

- [Max Hirsch](../people/max-hirsch.md) is a University of Michigan graduate who did finance internships. He had never shipped an app ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=238s)).
- He saw a gap. Running has Strava and Nike Run Club, but lifters spread across several apps ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=161s)).

## Business model

- A single $24.99 yearly fee, charged upfront. That is about $2 a month, so MRR looks low ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s)).
- A hard paywall. A mentor told him to use one instead of a free app with ads ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s)).
- The stack is Claude Code, Supabase at $20/month, Vercel, Expo and RevenueCat ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=344s)).

## Metrics

- Nearly $14K in the last 28 days, about $14K to $15K per month (2026-10) ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s)).
- 1,000 active subscribers, 164 ratings and 20,000 iOS downloads (2026-10) ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s)).
- Paid ads run at about $250 a day (2026-10) ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s)).

## Growth channels

- **Founder videos**. Max posted 5 to 10 videos a day and checked hold rate and skip rate ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s)).
- **Meta ads behind winners**. The best organic videos became paid ads. He prefers Meta to TikTok because its buyers show higher intent ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s)). See [Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md).
- **X**. About a third of revenue comes from people who follow him on X (2026-10) ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s)).
- **Creators next**. He was starting a [SideShift](sideshift.md) campaign to find five more daily creators ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s)).
- **Clipping**. He says it brings organic and international downloads ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s)).
