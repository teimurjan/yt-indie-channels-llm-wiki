Bloom is a Gen Z "learn to invest" app that mixes Robinhood-style investing with Duolingo-style lessons.

## What it is

- Investing plus game-style lessons. The app teaches long-term investing ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=213s)).
- Signup offer: "up to $75" in stock. Users get about $2 to $4 free and earn the rest through games and loot boxes ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [22:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1331s)). See [gamification](../topics/gamification.md).

## Founders

- [Jay Bloom](../people/jay-bloom.md) is the CEO ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=0s)).

## Business model

- The source does not detail pricing. Bloom tested $40, $60 and $75 stock offers ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [23:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1392s)).

## Metrics

- "Millions of dollars in ARR" and over 1M active users. The title says $2M/year (2025-07) ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok paid ads, then organic.** CAC worsened from mid-2023, so Bloom moved to organic TikTok in 2024 ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)). See [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md).
- **AI avatar accounts.** 5 to 10 accounts post about 2 times a day each. Winners get reposted to about 20 accounts ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s)). See [TikTok farming](../topics/tiktok-farming.md).
- **Micro-creators.** A small retainer plus CPM bonuses of $2 to $4, capped at $1,000 per video (2025-07) ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=583s)). See [UGC](../topics/ugc.md).
- **Comment team.** Videos never say "download Bloom". A comment team names the app ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=355s)).
- **Organic winners become ads.** Faded viral videos run as paid ads with a soft CTA at the end ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [14:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=876s)).
- **Broad hooks.** "How billionaires raise their kids" leads into early investing. Formats last about 3 to 6 months ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [27:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1648s)).

## Onboarding

- Order: urgency, then features, then trust. It opens with a compound-growth chart and ends with press logos and reviews ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [19:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1145s)).
- One wordy screen became about 5 tap-through story screens. Signups rose ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [24:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1488s)). See [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md).

## Timeline

- 2023: scaled TikTok paid ads. CAC fell off by year end ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)).
- 2024: moved to organic TikTok ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)).
- 2025-07: over 1M active users. Scaled to 20 AI accounts with HeyGen ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2085s)).
