Fin is Intercom's AI customer support agent, priced per resolved ticket.

## What it is

- An AI agent that resolves support tickets. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as a case of "outcome-first" design ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=247s)).

## Founders

- Built inside [Intercom](intercom.md). Intercom paused its roadmap within a week of the ChatGPT launch to build it, per chief product officer Paul Adams ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=247s)).

## Business model

- Charges per resolved ticket, so price follows the outcome ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=303s)). See [Pricing](../topics/pricing.md).

## Metrics

- Intercom's fastest-growing product ever and the company's main focus (2025-12) ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=333s)).

## Growth channels

- The source gives no channel detail. It frames Fin, with [Dia](dia.md) from [The Browser Company](the-browser-company.md), as replacing interfaces with outcomes ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=0s)).

## Timeline

- Late 2022: Intercom pauses its roadmap after ChatGPT launches ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=247s)).
- 2025-12: Intercom's main focus ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=333s)).
