Consumer Club is a Discord community where app founders share distribution tactics, mostly for organic TikTok ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=171s)). Its founder also hosts the Superwall podcast, and most sources on this page are his interviews.

## What it is

- A community for consumer app founders. Members range from solo indie developers with millions in ARR to venture-backed founders (2025-04) ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=171s)).
- Founders use it to learn a new channel. [Kyle Fowler](../people/kyle-fowler.md) asks his network first, then communities such as Consumer Club, then search tools ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:52:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=3153s)).
- Members trade skills. [An Nayal](../people/an-nayal.md) of [Flame](../businesses/flame.md) offered retention lessons in the club and got TikTok lessons back ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:56:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=3397s)).
- It also runs in person. At a New York founder dinner, one study app made $400K MRR. A dating app there had 300K downloads from organic TikTok alone (2025-04) ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:35:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2120s)).

## Founders

- [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) founded it. He hosts the Superwall podcast ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=508s)).
- He advises founders directly. He advised Alex Lieberman of Morning Brew on a new app, built with a co-founder from the club ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [1:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4020s)).

## Growth channels

- **The Superwall podcast.** Joseph interviews app founders on Superwall's YouTube channel. The episodes below are the main public face of the club.
- **Organic TikTok.** His first video on a new TikTok account got 50K views with no retention edits ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:58:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=3532s)).

## What the club teaches

- **Distribution can beat product.** Tactics flow from affiliate marketing to e-commerce, then to consumer apps, then to B2B ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=300s)).
- **Formats change, but old ones still work.** Influencers (2021), mass UGC (2022), slideshows (2023), HeyGen avatars over slides (2024), Flux avatars (2025) ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:49:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2973s)).
- **Organic beats paid on margin, but it has a ceiling.** Mass UGC runs at about 50% margin and paid ads at 10 to 30%. Organic caps at about $1M to $3M a year per app, so winners become paid ads ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=63s), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=192s)).
- **Three layers of creative.** Copy formats, then change one variable at a time, then move a whole concept from another niche ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=508s), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=617s)).
- **Three roles.** A head of social growth, a creator coach and a talent scout ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=695s)).
- **Learn the channel yourself.** Agencies in this space rarely last more than about 6 months ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [1:18:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4703s)). At $0/month, do it yourself. At $50K to $100K/month, consider an agency ([source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=852s)).
- **Start test.** Copy 3 formats that already work in the niche. 5K views means there is something there ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [1:29:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=5381s)).
