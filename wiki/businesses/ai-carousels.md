AI Carousels is a freemium SaaS tool that generates social media carousels with AI, run by one founder.

## What it is

- Marketed as "the best AI carousel generator". Inside, users find more tools for their online presence ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=547s)).

## Founders

- [Fernando](../people/fernando.md), a product designer from Argentina who lives in Sweden. He also runs [Resume Maker Online](resume-maker-online.md) ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=0s)).
- He started it with a 10-day build-in-public challenge after his employer fired him ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s)).

## Business model

- Freemium. A subscription unlocks the AI and pro features ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s)).
- It launched at $9.99 in beta. Fernando lowered the price to $4.95 (2024-12) ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s)).
- The bundle of extra tools keeps users from canceling ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=547s)).

## Metrics

- About $15K MRR together with Resume Maker Online (2024-12) ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s)).
- Costs about $1,000/month for both products, so margins are above 90% (2024-12) ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=654s)).
- 5 paying users from its Product Hunt launch, while the product was still buggy ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).

## Growth channels

- **Niche inside a big market.** Canva serves everyone. A solo tool can serve one narrow task better ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=150s)).
- **Free tools for SEO.** About 30 free tools bring search traffic. He adds build-in-public posts and an affiliate program ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)).
- **Product Hunt** for the first paying users ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).
- **Solo support as a feature.** He replies fast and writes "I", not "we" ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=589s)).

## Timeline

- Fired from a 9-to-5 job after about 1.5 years. He starts AI Carousels in a 10-day public challenge ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s)).
- Product Hunt launch: 5 paying users, so he doubles down ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).
- 2024-12: price at $4.95. Both products make about $15K MRR ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s)).
