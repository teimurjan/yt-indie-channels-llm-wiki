Tiimo is a visual planner app from Copenhagen that first targeted neurodivergent users, such as people with ADHD.

## What it is

- A schedule shown as colored, illustrated blocks sized by duration. Users see their day instead of reading it ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=115s)).
- It won iPhone App of the Year 2025 ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=67s)).

## Design lesson

- **Niche depth.** Do not reskin the mass-market interface for a niche. Tiimo dropped text rows for visual time blocks ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=115s)).
- Notion cannot copy this, because its audience is too broad ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=176s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) calls this "first principles design". He picked Tiimo as one of four outliers from more than 200 top apps ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md)).
