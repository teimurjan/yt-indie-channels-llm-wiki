Shervin Shares is a niche YouTube channel about tech, gadgets and fitness that earns mostly from affiliate links in product reviews.

## What it is

- A YouTube channel of search-based product reviews in tech, gadgets and fitness ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=414s)).

## Founders

- [Shervin](../people/shervin.md). He quit a six-figure software engineering job, and later a product manager job, to make videos full time ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=154s)).
- His first editor came from Upwork at $7 to $10/hour ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=771s)).

## Business model

- 80% affiliate marketing and 20% YouTube AdSense. Two companies make up about 50% of affiliate income. No sponsorships yet (2023-04) ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
- He held off on sponsors, because a sponsor read can cut views ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
- A fitness-band review with a referral link first earned him 10 years of free membership. The company then moved him to an affiliate program that paid $50 per referral ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=414s), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=475s)).

## Metrics

- About $125K/year, from the video title (2023-04) ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
- First year: about $10/month in AdSense, then about $300 for the year ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=380s)).
- 1,000 subscribers took about 2 to 3 years ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=344s)).
- When he quit, YouTube made $2K to $3K/month ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=154s)).
- Top video: "I replaced my iPhone with an Apple Watch Ultra", about 1.56M views (2023-04) ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=671s)).

## Growth channels

- **YouTube search.** Frame each idea by search demand. "Testosterone supplements" gets few views, but "gaining muscle" with the same content does well ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=843s)).
- **Timing plus past data.** He re-ran a format that had worked before. He shipped it the day after the Apple Watch Ultra launch ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=718s)).
- **Steady cadence.** Once a week, twice a week or twice a month are the cadences he sees work ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=322s)).
- **Creator community.** He shares a New York apartment with two other creators, at $1,600/month for his share (2023-04). He found creator events on Meetup ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=592s), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=899s)).

## Timeline

- **Early years:** About $300 in the first year, then 2 to 3 years to reach 1,000 subscribers ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=344s), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=380s)).
- **Quit:** At $2K to $3K/month from YouTube, he quit and moved back in with his parents ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=213s)).
- **Before 2023-04:** The Apple Watch Ultra video goes viral, the day after the watch launch ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=718s)).
- **2023-04:** About $125K/year ([source](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
