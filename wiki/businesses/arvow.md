# Arvow

Arvow is an AI SEO writer for agencies and business owners.

## What it is

- AI SEO content software ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md)).

## Founders

- [Vasco](../people/vasco.md) and his co-founder Afonso. Vasco started with rank-and-rent SEO in 2015 or 2016, sold SEO services on Fiverr and Upwork, then built and sold the SEO marketplace Vetted ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=104s)).

## Business model

- Subscription from about $99/month to almost $700/month (2025-10) ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=71s)).

## Metrics

- About $70K MRR after two years; the description says $79K/month (2025-10) ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=58s)).
- About 600 paying clients (2025-10) ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=71s)).

## Growth channels

- **YouTube, fully organic at first.** Three buckets: evergreen, news and MrBeast-style viral videos ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=216s)). See [YouTube](../topics/youtube.md).
- **Owned niche channels** run by hired Upwork creators, paid per video and per performance ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=277s)).
- **In-feed ads** on videos that already sold ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s)).
- **Attribution** with a UTM and a coupon per video ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s)).

## Timeline

- Vasco recorded 20 to 30 minute videos every day for almost a year ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=634s)).
- 2025-10: about $70K MRR, two years in ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=58s)).
