PostSyncer is a social media tool that posts to 10 platforms and is one of the SaaS products in Tibo's portfolio.

## What it is

- A tool that posts content to 10 platforms. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s))

## Founders

- Part of the portfolio of [Tibo](../people/tibo.md), who built and sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s))

## Business model

- SaaS subscription. The source gives no prices.

## Metrics

- About $1.5K/month (2025-12). ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s))
- It is the smallest product in a portfolio that makes about $700K/month from 50K paying customers (2025-12). ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s))

## Growth channels

- The source does not describe PostSyncer's own channels. Tibo's portfolio playbook uses free channels (Product Hunt, social posts, building in public) until $10K/month, then SEO, ads and affiliates. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=701s), [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=762s))

## Timeline

- 2025-12: about $1.5K/month. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s))
