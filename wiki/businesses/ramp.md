Ramp is a corporate spend management company that Tim Gabe uses as an example of retention through a learned model of the customer.

## What it is

- A spend platform that learns a company's spend rules. Its AI agents made millions of spend decisions in one month (2026-03). ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=540s))

## Founders

- The source does not name the founders.

## Business model

- B2B software for company spend. It plugs into accounting, HR and Slack. ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=602s))

## Metrics

- Over 50,000 companies, including Shopify, Figma and Notion (2026-03). ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=540s))
- Its rival Brex sold to Capital One in 2026-01 for less than half its peak valuation. ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=602s))

## Growth channels

- **The "intelligence trap".** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) argues that each transaction trains Ramp's AI, so users add value with no conscious effort. A user who leaves must rebuild that learned model. ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=660s))
- **Integrations raise switching cost.** Each connected system makes a move harder. ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=602s))
