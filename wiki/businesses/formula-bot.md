Formula Bot is a no-code AI tool, first called Excel Formula Bot, that writes spreadsheet formulas and analyzes data in plain language.

## What it is

- Three products: AI inside Excel and Google Sheets, formula generators, and a Data Analyzer where users upload data and ask questions ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s)).
- It was 100% Bubble until a few months before 2024-02. It is now about 5% to 10% no-code ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=315s)).

## Founders

- [David Bressler](../people/david-bressler.md). He built it in about 6 weeks of paternity leave in July 2022, while he still had a senior full-time job ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)).
- Junior analysts kept asking him for Excel help, so he built a tool for it ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions. The price went from $2.99 to $6.99 to $9 for unlimited use (2024-02) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=823s)).
- Before the paywall, he kept it alive with a Stripe donation link and an ad sold to an Excel esports event. Google Ads made only a little ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).

## Metrics

- About 750K users and about 5,000 paying subscribers. Valued at over $1M (2024-02) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s)).
- The captions give recurring revenue as "$226,000 a monthly recurring". The caption is garbled, so treat it with care (2024-02) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=130s)).
- About $5,000 in OpenAI API costs in the days after it went viral (2022) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).
- About 5% to 10% of churn is because of ChatGPT (2024-02) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=823s)).

## Growth channels

- **Niche subreddits.** A plain post on r/excel became the top post of the week. A post on r/InternetIsBeautiful then got about 10,000 upvotes and made the app go viral for months ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=385s)).
- **Free help for interviews.** About once a week a user emailed an Excel file. David did the work for free, but only on a call where the user told him what they wanted. This turned the product into a data-analytics platform ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=589s)).

## Competition

- About 10 copycat "Excel formula" sites appeared within weeks of launch ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=700s)).
- Microsoft offered to build his add-on for free, then announced its OpenAI partnership. His plan is to build what neither ChatGPT nor Microsoft can copy ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=760s), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=885s)).
- Another founder, Domenico Gagliardi, built and sold his own Excel formula generator for about $25K after 3 months ([source](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s)).

## Timeline

- **2022-07:** David builds the first version on Bubble ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)).
- **2022:** Reddit posts make it go viral. The OpenAI bill almost kills it ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=385s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).
- **A few months later:** A paywall with logins launches ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).
- **2024-02:** About 750K users and 5,000 paying subscribers ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s)).
