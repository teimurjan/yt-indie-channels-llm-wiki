Hide or Die is a Roblox game by Cole, one of the top 50 Roblox developers.

## What it is

- A Roblox game that earns from in-game purchases. Developers earn Robux and cash them out. 1M Robux pays out about $3.5K (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=245s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).

## Founders

- [Cole](../people/cole.md) and a programmer partner, who split revenue 50/50. Cole started playing Roblox in 2013 and moved to Austin at 18 instead of college [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=93s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).

## Business model

- In-game purchases on Roblox. Cole spends about $5K/month on modelers and graphics contractors and runs no ads (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).

## Metrics

- About $45K/month, about 6 months after launch (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).
- With revenue shares from other games he helps, Cole expected about $70K that month (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=676s).
- Cole's games together reach about 1M daily active users, mostly US and Canadian kids (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=93s).

## Growth channels

- **Clickable, social, replayable.** A dramatic title gets the click, friends play together, and levels and locked content bring players back [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=753s).

## Timeline

- Before: Cole lost about $500K over 3 years on two failed projects, including a 3-year, $300K game [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=676s), [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=720s).
- About late 2024: built in 2 weeks as a fast test [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=720s).
- 2025-04: about $45K/month [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).
