Boost App Social is an iOS app for Instagram growth that reached $100K MRR almost only on Meta ads, then sold for seven figures.

## What it is

- An iOS app with hashtag and editing tools for Instagram growth ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=26s)).
- It started in 2018 or 2019 as a web hashtag tool with a vector database. It had no traction, so the team moved to iOS after they saw Hashtag Expert succeed ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=249s)).

## Founders

- Co-founder [Alex](../people/alex.md). He now runs [Screens Design](screens-design.md), a library of app onboarding flows and paywalls ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2476s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions. Extra features raised the price from about $40/year toward $100/year ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=377s)).

## Metrics

- About $100K MRR at its peak (2026-04) ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=26s)).
- Daily ad spend grew from $100 to $500, $1K and then $10K ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=377s)).
- Total Meta spend was $1.5M to $1.8M over about 2.5 years, maybe over $2M in all. About $800K was wasted ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [20:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1254s)).
- Only 1 video in 85 got spend and worked. About 97% of creatives failed ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=38s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=746s)).

## Growth channels

- **Meta ads with UGC creators.** A pipeline of about 300 creators. 1,000 Gmail accounts each sent 10 emails a day by script, and replies ran at 10% to 20% ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=796s)).
- **Telegram for deals.** VAs moved creators to Telegram for contracts and files. A $100 upfront payment proved the deal was real ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [15:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=925s)).
- **Perpetual rights.** With a one-year license, a creator can demand $100K for a winning ad. Perpetual rights also made the creative library part of the sale ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=673s)).
- **Three ad lessons.** Add captions, because about 80% watch Instagram without sound. Use a green screen in front of an app demo. Write wild hooks for the first 5 seconds ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1367s)).

## Timeline

- **2018 or 2019:** Starts as a web hashtag tool with no traction ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=249s)).
- **Next years:** Moves to iOS and scales Meta ads to $10K a day ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=377s)).
- **During the run:** Meta bans the ad accounts 6 to 8 times, and the team wins every one back ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=514s)).
- **January (year not stated):** The sale closes after 4 to 5 months of talks, through broker Charlie of UpFlip and a Singapore escrow. The contract ran about 100 pages ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [32:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1924s), [33:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2033s)).

## Sale

- A private-equity-style buyer paid seven figures (2026-04) ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=240s)).
- The sale included the warm ad account, the Meta rep, about 300 perpetual-rights creator contracts and the dashboards. An NDA bars the sellers from the niche for one year ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=118s), [39:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2345s)).
- Platform risk from bans or doubled CPMs made the exit a way to "take chips off the table" ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2145s)).
