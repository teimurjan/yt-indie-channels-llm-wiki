Serif is an AI email assistant that sorts the inbox and drafts replies in the user's own writing style.

## What it is

- The user connects an email account. Serif takes about 20 minutes to learn the user's voice and sort the inbox. Then it drafts replies in that style ([qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=390s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) says it even copies his lowercase writing style ([qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=390s)).

## Growth channels

- **Creator sponsorship.** Serif is one of five tools in a Tim Gabe video for designers. Some of the tools sponsor the video. Tim says he contacted them himself after he picked the list (2025-04) ([qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md)).
- **Pitch to creatives.** Tim's angle: email is work that creatives dislike, so an AI that drafts replies keeps creative time creative ([qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=390s)).
