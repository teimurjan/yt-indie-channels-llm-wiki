This page collects what the sources say about Apple, mostly as a design reference for founders and designers.

## What it is

Apple makes the iPhone, Mac and iOS, and it runs the App Store. In these sources Apple is not a case study of a business. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses its product design, its web pages and its packaging as examples to copy.

## Founders

The sources do not cover the founders in detail. Tim cites Steve Jobs's 2001 iPod launch, where Jobs sold "1,000 songs in your pocket" and not 1 GB of storage ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=189s)).

## Business model

- Apple takes a cut of App Store sales. It takes 15% up to the first $1M, which is closer to 20% in practice. Above $1M it takes 30%, closer to 33% in practice ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- Its free default apps set the bar that indie apps must beat. [Carrot Weather](../businesses/carrot-weather.md) charged money against the free Apple Weather app and ran solo for over a decade ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s)).
- [Day One](../businesses/day-one.md) competes with Apple Notes and Apple Journal. It adds weather, location, photos and a timeline around the same text ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=437s)).

## Metrics

- iOS 17 reached 77% adoption within 6 months. Android 14 reached 13% after 1 year (as cited 2025-12) ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=511s)).
- Apple Design Awards in the sources: Day One for Mac in 2014, and Carrot Weather for Interaction in 2021 ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)), ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s)).

## What designers copy from Apple

### Software design principles

Tim names four principles: physical feedback, math-based polish, simplicity and consistency ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=47s)).

- **Physical feedback**. The rubber-band scroll makes software feel like an object with weight ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=244s)).
- **Cues, not tutorials**. Over about 10 years, Apple moved users from a home button to a swipe with no tutorial ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=203s)).
- **Math-based polish**. Squircle corners are smoother than standard rounded corners. Figma now offers them as a setting ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=306s)).
- **Simplicity**. Keep key actions within about three taps, as with the camera or Apple Pay ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=569s)).
- **Consistency**. Apple was the first major tech company to formalize human interface guidelines, starting with the Apple II ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=511s)).

### Packaging and the "gift" moment

- Apple has a room built only to perfect how a box opens. The lid falls over 2 to 4 seconds through vacuum resistance ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=181s)).
- Tim uses this for his "gift or receipt" idea: anticipation, reveal, then celebration ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=0s)).
- The [Undertone](../businesses/undertone.md) founders admired Apple's packaging. That led to a seven-step "unboxing" for the session recap ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s)).

### Product pages and animations

- Many animations on Apple product pages rely on sticky scroll ([DRvge30wQFM](../sources/DRvge30wQFM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRvge30wQFM&t=0s)).
- Tim rebuilds the iMac 24 keyboard reveal in Figma in four frames ([NfqdWTfNYKI](../sources/NfqdWTfNYKI.md), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NfqdWTfNYKI&t=663s)).
- He rebuilds the Dynamic Island animation with three triggers: drag, delay and click ([dL-U6bkm0a0](../sources/dL-U6bkm0a0.md), [0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL-U6bkm0a0&t=36s)).
- Undertone uses a Dynamic Island Live Activity to show each processing step after a session ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=665s)).
- The Strides site copies the style of Apple product pages: a black background with type and product shots ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=942s)).
- Apple and Google use different breakpoints, even from page to page. Tim says no breakpoint is universally correct ([tJCv91c6Krk](../sources/tJCv91c6Krk.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tJCv91c6Krk&t=124s)).

## Timeline

- 1973: Xerox PARC invents drag and drop. Apple later makes it mainstream with the Macintosh ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=159s)).
- 2001: Steve Jobs launches the iPod with an outcome message ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=189s)).
- 2013: Brian Mueller launches Carrot Weather against Apple's free weather app ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s)).
- 2014: Day One wins the Apple Design Award for Mac ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s)).
- 2021: Carrot Weather wins the Apple Design Award for Interaction ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)).
- 2022-09: Tim rebuilds the new Dynamic Island in Figma ([dL-U6bkm0a0](../sources/dL-U6bkm0a0.md), [0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL-U6bkm0a0&t=36s)).
