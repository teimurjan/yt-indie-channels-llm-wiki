Contra is a professional network for freelance creative work. In these sources it appears as a hiring tool, an agency client and a source of AI design research.

## What it is

- A network where companies hire freelance creatives. Rive animation is one of its official partner tools (2025-04) ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=43s)).

## Founders

The sources do not name the founders.

## Business model

The sources do not describe it.

## How the sources use it

- **Hiring.** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md)'s agency [ZipSnap](zipsnap.md) hires top Rive animators through Contra (2025-04). He calls Rive a skill that is quickly gaining value ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=43s)).
- **Agency client.** [Skale](skale.md), the agency of [Mark Vassilevskiy](../people/mark-vassilevskiy.md), lists Contra as a client beside Bolt.new, Krea AI and [Framer](framer.md) (2025-10) ([source](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)).
- **AI design research.** A Contra study found that models with the best taste won on vague briefs. With a specific creative direction, a model that followed directions well beat them (2026-09) ([source](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=162s)).

The sources give none.

## Growth channels

- Sponsorship of design creators. Tim Gabe's tools video features Contra, though he says he picked the list before contacting sponsors ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=43s)).
