Glam Up is a glow-up and makeup app for women, built by [Nicole](../people/nicole.md) and her co-founder Aaron as "Umax for women".

## What it is

- A female version of [Umax](umax.md), a looksmaxxing app from the same accelerator. It is based on makeup, not facial scores ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s), [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=264s)).

## Founders

- [Nicole](../people/nicole.md) handles design and distribution. Aaron codes. They picked the idea in a 10-minute call. They also built [Sprout](sprout.md) ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=264s), [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s)).

## Business model

- Hard paywall with weekly or monthly subscriptions ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=175s)).
- The price rose from $3 to $9 a week, and conversion rose ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1368s)).
- Scan results are blurred. Users pay or invite 3 friends. The scan is faked until payment, then the API runs, to save cost ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1096s), [26:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1596s)).
- Stack: React Native, RevenueCat, Superwall, PostHog, [SideShift](sideshift.md) and Attio ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=773s)).

## Metrics

- 1M users in the first 6 months, about 2M downloads, peak about $150K MRR (2026-05) ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=115s)).
- Over $600K total revenue (2026-05) ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [20:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1244s)).
- Reached number 3 on the App Store for a few days, above Tinder and Hinge ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=525s)).
- At peak: 40 to 50M monthly TikTok views and about 200M on Instagram from faceless UGC ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)).

## Growth channels

- One locked slideshow format: plain face, full makeup, then the app. Same caption, hashtags and about 10 audios. The first slideshow got about 10M views ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=589s), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=747s)).
- The referral share text says "download Glam Up", so comments rank the app name in TikTok search ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [19:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1140s)).
- Nicole and Aaron warmed up 3 accounts each, then scaled to 30 or 40 creators ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s)).
- A copycat with a similar name went viral but did not rank. Its traffic searched for Glam Up and brought $100K in a week ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [5:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=329s)).

## Timeline

- The first MVP took 3 weeks to a month ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=378s)).
- First 6 months: 1M users ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s)).
- 2026-05: reported peak of about $150K MRR ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=115s)).
