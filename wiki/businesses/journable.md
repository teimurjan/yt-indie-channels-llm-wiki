Journable is an AI calorie counter app that earns most of its revenue on Android.

## What it is

- Users type a meal or snap a photo, and AI returns calories and macros. Simplicity is the main marketing message ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=166s)).

## Founders

- [Steve Hoyek](../people/steve-hoyek.md) and a co-founder. Steve went from 125 kg to 80 kg by tracking calories ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=166s)).

## Business model

- [Subscriptions](../topics/subscriptions.md), monthly and annual plans only (2026-03) ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=85s)).
- [Bootstrapped](../topics/bootstrapping.md). Tools include Claude Code $100, AppFollow $180 and Webflow $18 per month (2026-03) ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [18:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=1085s)).

## Metrics

- Last 28 days: $125K revenue, about $80K MRR, just under $1M ARR (2026-03) ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=85s)).
- Over 30K active subscribers, about 40K total subscribers, nearly 1M Android downloads (2026-03) ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=85s)).
- About 80% of users and revenue come from Android ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=146s)).

> Conflict: the video description says 92% of revenue comes from Android; in the interview Steve says about 80% ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md)).

- Google Ads all-time: just under $500K spent, about the same in attributed revenue (2026-03) ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=417s)).

## Growth channels

- [Google Ads](../topics/google-ads.md) App campaigns at about break-even. The Play Store placement works best, for keywords like "calorie counter" ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=458s)).
- Android over iOS by data: iOS ads cost about 4 times more and iOS users convert only about 20% better ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=235s)).
- Product Hunt front page gave a couple hundred installs and two paid subscribers ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=350s)).

## Timeline

- About 2024-09: launch, about 18 months before the video ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=0s)).
- Early: iOS Meta campaigns prove too expensive. Android campaigns in the US convert as well as iOS ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=350s)).
- 2026-03: from under $1K to over $100K/month in revenue ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=85s)).
