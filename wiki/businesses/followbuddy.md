FollowBuddy is a subscription app that shows who unfollowed you on Instagram, grown only through organic search videos.

## What it is

- It reads Instagram's own data export, so it does not break Instagram's terms ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).

## Founders

- [Ben Leavitt](../people/ben-leavitt.md). He ran a marketing agency for 7 years. His own "compare lists" video got over 1M views and sent traffic to someone else's tool. He waited about 4 years for build costs to drop, then built FollowBuddy about 2 years before 2026-03 ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=119s), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=213s)).
- One full-time developer builds it. A weak first technical partner cost Ben about a year and a lot of money ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=833s), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=862s)).

## Business model

- Weekly, monthly and yearly plans after a free trial. The yearly plan at $50/year gets the most push (2026-03) ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).

## Metrics

- The video title says $17K/month. Ben says $20K/month (2026-03) ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=2s), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).
- 82,000 users, 3,128 paying users and $0 spent on ads (2026-03) ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).
- RevenueCat showed 484 active trials and 2,752 active subscriptions (2026-03) ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).

> Conflict: the title of [4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md) says $17K/month, while Ben says $20K/month in the same video.

## Growth channels

- **YouTube search.** In 28 days the branded channel got 40,000 views, mostly from search (2026-03) ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=96s)). One 20-minute video, "best Instagram unfollower tracker apps", has 25,000 views after 7 months and ranks #1 for several keywords ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=291s)).
- **Awareness ladder.** List the questions buyers ask at each stage of awareness. Check search volume with Keywords Everywhere or vidIQ, and start with 10 to 20 keywords ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=390s), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=444s)).
- **Honest videos.** Repeat the search phrase at the start and show cons. Ben says honesty raised conversion ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=506s)).
- **Stack bricks.** YouTube lets one brand hold several top results across several channels ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=672s)).
- Organic search on TikTok and Instagram brings the rest ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).

## Timeline

- **About 2020 (about 4 years before the build):** Ben's "compare lists" video passes 1M views ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=161s)).
- **About 2024 ("about 2 years ago" in 2026-03):** FollowBuddy launches ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=213s)).
- **2026-03:** About $20K/month with $0 ad spend ([source](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).
