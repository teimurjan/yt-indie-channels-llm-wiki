Stronger is a bootstrapped, gamified workout tracker from Northern Ireland that grows almost entirely on organic TikTok and Instagram.

## What it is

- A workout tracker with a strength-score heat map that compares your muscles with people of the same body weight, and badges such as "you lifted a Boeing 747" ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=269s), [26:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1606s)).
- Features are designed to look good as screenshots, so the product is the marketing asset ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [26:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1606s)).

## Founders

- Three engineers, including [Peter](../people/peter.md), whom [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) interviewed. It began as a hardware idea and was funded with Northern Ireland government grants ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=86s), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=311s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a [hard paywall](../topics/paywalls.md): a trial is required to reach the free tier. A bug revealed it, and it gave about a 25% boost overnight ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [24:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1452s), [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1498s)).
- The hard paywall cuts friend shares, but it still won ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [25:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1547s)).
- Gamification stays light: heavy ranks appeal to ages 15 to 23, but the money is in 30 to 40 year olds ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [28:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1709s)).

## Metrics

- About 1.2M users and about $600K ARR, 2.5 to 3 years old; the US is the biggest market (2025-08) ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s)).
- Peak rank: number 3 in Health and Fitness ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [33:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2018s)).
- TikTok page about 4.9M likes (2025-08) ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2142s)).

## Growth channels

- **The 6-second fade-in Reels format**: an estimated 150M to 300M views, some videos at 40M, one month at 100M (2025-08) ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=412s), [8:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=502s)).
- **Horizontal scaling.** Micro edits with AI and Figma make each copy a new video ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=644s)).
- **Stitch replies** to comments such as "I benched 220 lb" ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [34:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2083s)).
- **A few strong ambassadors**, after Runna, Represent and Ladder ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [17:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1023s)).
- **In-house, not agencies.** Agencies add about 20% margin and weaker incentives ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [43:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2614s)). See [Organic social](../topics/organic-social.md).

## Timeline

- A Canva-made TikTok gained about 15K followers in a week before the app was ready ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [31:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1867s)).
- 2025-08: about $600K ARR; a second app, a "Forest meets FarmVille" focus game, in progress ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s), [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2902s)).
