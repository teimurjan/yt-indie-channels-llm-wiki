Greek House is a custom apparel business for fraternities and sororities and the first of four related apparel brands.

## What it is

- Custom apparel for fraternities and sororities ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s)).
- Sister brands: [College Thread](college-thread.md) for college groups, [Threadly](threadly.md) for company merch and [Athlete's Thread](athletes-thread.md) for licensed athlete merch ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s)).

## Founders

- [Karthik Shanadi](../people/karthik-shanadi.md) and his co-founder Luke. They started in college with $500 ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s)).

## Business model

- No in-house manufacturing. US print partners fulfil orders through a vendor portal that Karthik's team built ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s)).
- The portal cuts service time to about one tenth, so partners put their orders first ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s)).
- About 300 college licenses act as a moat (2023-04) ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=61s)).

## Metrics

- Year one $50K, year two $100K, both part time in school ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s)).
- Sales just under $1M in 2016 ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s)).
- The video frames the four brands at $10M in total, built with no funding (2023-04) ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- **In person through the network.** They texted friends in Greek houses and pitched at student group meetings ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=396s)).
- **Outbound email, then outbound sales** ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)).
- **Ambassadors.** Free product in exchange for content and promotion ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)).
- **Paid search and free marketplaces.** Google ads for high-intent queries, plus Etsy and Google Shopping ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s)).
- **Service as brand.** Replies in under 4 hours and shipping in 2 to 3 business days ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=732s)).

## Timeline

- 2013: started in college with $500 ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s)).
- About 2.5 years: Karthik runs it next to jobs at Amazon and Salesforce, six days a week ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=261s)).
- 2016: full time. Four months later a licensing audit brings a $40K bill. They pay it over 12 to 14 months ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s)).
- After the NCAA name, image and likeness ruling, they launch Athlete's Thread ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=683s)).
