Oura makes a smart ring that tracks sleep and body data, sold with a monthly subscription. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses it as a case of retention through a personal model that users cannot export.

## What it is

A ring plus an app that learns from the wearer's body data night after night. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=354s)

## Business model

Hardware plus a $5.99/month subscription (2026-03). [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s)

## Metrics (2026-03)

- Millions of rings sold, on track for $1B in revenue. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=354s)
- A $97M contract with the US Department of Defense. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=354s)
- Subscription retention in the high 80s, in percent. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s)

## Growth and retention tactics

- **Learn from data, do not only store it.** Under the EU Data Act, users can export CSVs. They cannot export the model trained on them. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s)
- **Make the investment visible.** The app shows how many nights it tracked, such as 1,095. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=478s)
- **Ask the exit question.** What must a user rebuild at a competitor? For Oura, years of body history. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=478s)

Tim calls this the "intelligence trap": each session trains a model of the user, which builds on Nir Eyal's "stored value" idea. [Midjourney](midjourney.md) and [Ramp](ramp.md) are the other two cases. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=0s)
