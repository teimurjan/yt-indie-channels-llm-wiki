# Storyarb

Storyarb is a ghostwriting agency for B2B executives on Twitter and LinkedIn, and the first business in [Alex Lieberman](../people/alex-lieberman.md)'s personal holding company.

## What it is

- One client interview per month, then 12 pieces of content per week per client ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s)).

## Founders

- [Alex Lieberman](../people/alex-lieberman.md), co-founder of Morning Brew, as co-founder and chairman, not CEO. He hired a CEO after 2 to 3 months of proof ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).

## Business model

- Retainer at $7,000/month per client (2023-11) ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
- Margin 30 to 35% ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s)).
- CEO pay: $100K to $200K salary plus 10% to 50% equity. After product-market fit, profits go to equity holders each quarter ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=738s)).

## Metrics

- 12 clients, $84K/month top line, about $1M annualized (2023-11) ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
- Team: a CEO, a content strategist and 3 freelance ghostwriters (2023-11) ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
- Goal: $10M/year with about 110 clients ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s)).
- PMF tests: at least 50% of clients would recommend it, and average retention of 10 months against 3 to 5 typical ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=609s)).

## Growth channels

- **One tweet.** Alex tweeted at executives willing to pay $5K to $10K/year for audience building and got 25 DMs ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)).

## Timeline

- Validation tweet, then 2 ghostwriters matched with clients in a Slack channel ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)).
- After 2 to 3 months: CEO hire ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
- 2023-11: about $1M annualized ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
