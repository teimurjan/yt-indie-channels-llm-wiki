TaskMagic is a browser automation tool for jobs that Zapier cannot reach through APIs. Its founder sold it on Acquire.com.

## What it is

- Browser automation for business owners, agencies and freelancers ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)).

## Founders

- [Jeremy Redman](../people/jeremy-redman.md), who started non-technical, plus one CTO. The two ran it alone ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=172s)).

## Business model

- Lifetime deals plus usage-based pricing, because users did not want constant subscriptions. It won AppSumo awards and a Product Hunt Golden Kitty ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=228s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=548s)).

## Metrics

- Over 60,000 users, about 8,000 paying customers, over $400K in some months and about $3M a year (2026-04) ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=134s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=163s)).
- Made the Inc. 5000 list ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=228s)).
- Sold for "mid upper seven figures". Over 100 buyers messaged after the listing, and the sale took 6 to 7 months (2026-04) ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=181s), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=696s)).

## Growth channels

- **Tentpole strategy.** Cheap, specific side products rank in search on their own and funnel paid users to the core product ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=215s)).
- [Mail Lead](mail-lead.md), a simple cold email tool, made "almost seven figures". An "automation" button inside it opens TaskMagic ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=511s)).
- LeadQuest, a lead tool for Mail Lead users, completes the ecosystem ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=548s)).
- AppSumo lifetime deals ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=228s)).

## Timeline across sources

- V1 was a slow no-code app builder. It made money in 2019, which paid for the first hire. A 2020 rebuild reached seven figures. In 2021 it moved to automation at a few hundred thousand in revenue, then grew to about $3M in 3 years ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=260s)).
- During the sale, an investor buyout drained cash. Jeremy put $50K on a credit card and took on $200K of personal debt ([source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=696s), [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=744s)).
- 2026-04: sold on Acquire.com.
