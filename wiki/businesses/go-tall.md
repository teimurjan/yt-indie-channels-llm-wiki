Go Tall is a height prediction app for teenagers on iOS and Android ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s)).

## What it is

- A long onboarding quiz asks for details that adjust a CDC baseline, with each question linked to research ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=660s)).
- The final prediction is paid. Stretches, meal scans and sleep logs keep users, and the prediction updates as they log ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=726s)).
- Stack: Expo, Superwall, Adapty for advance payouts, and Singular as the MMP at 3 cents per install ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=624s)).

## Founders

- [Michael Que](../people/michael-que.md), a 20-year-old NYU finance student. He built it after rejections from his dream jobs ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s)).
- A similar app was for sale on Acquire.com for about $20,000. He built his own instead ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)).

## Business model

- $35/year or $5/week, plus a freemium tier with some features ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=102s)).
- The paywall show rate is about 91%, despite the long onboarding ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=681s)).

## Metrics

- About $100K a month on average (2026-07) ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s)).
- About 700,000 App Store downloads and a few hundred thousand on Android, about 1M in total (2026-07) ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=102s)).

## Growth channels

- **Comment replies.** Teens post their age, height and parents' heights under height videos. His first viral format replied to those comments with a prediction ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=341s)).
- **One format, then people.** He posted 10 TikToks a day for a month to find the format and proved it on 2 more accounts. Then he DMed about 50 creators a day to copy it cheaply ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)).
- **Clean attribution.** With one format and one channel, he knew how many downloads 100K views gave. That set the price he paid creators ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)).
- **Paid ads last.** Ads came only after $30K a month ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)).

## Timeline

- Summer 2025: launch. The first sale came 2 days later ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)).
- About $3K a month from his own content and a few UGC creators, then $10K a month after paywall work ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)).
- Month 5: about $30K a month ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)).
- 2026-07: about $100K a month after paid ads ([source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)).
