Questgen (Questgen.ai) is an AI quiz generator built by [Ramsri Goutham](../people/ramsri-goutham.md) as a side project.

## Founders

- [Ramsri Goutham](../people/ramsri-goutham.md), an app builder in India who keeps a full-time job. He also runs [Supermeme](supermeme.md) ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=51s)).

## Business model

- AI SaaS. Margin is 70% to 75%. The main cost is OpenAI and Anthropic APIs. No employees (2025-06) ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=577s)).
- Stack: Next.js, Supabase, Typedream for landing pages, plus Windsurf and Cursor ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=540s)).

## Metrics

- About $100K lifetime revenue, 150K+ users and about 180 to 200 paying customers (2025-06) ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s)).
- With Supermeme: $8,500/month on about 2 hours of work a week (2025-06) ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=51s)).

## Growth channels

- Distribution before product: blogs on quiz generation, YouTube videos and daily LinkedIn posts. $0 on marketing ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=451s)).

## Timeline

- 2020: Ramsri saw that publishers make many quizzes and bet that models would soon write good ones ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s)).
- 2022 to 2023: first version with no-code tools, two text boxes and no design. Then a 6-month full-stack rebuild ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=252s)).
