# Bathe the Baby

Bathe the Baby (first called Bake the Baby) is a Roblox game made by [Ian](../people/ian.md) over a college winter break.

## What it is

- A game on [Roblox](roblox.md), a platform with about 400M monthly active users (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=312s)).
- Ian made it over a college winter break, then dropped out on its income ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md)).

## Founders

- [Ian](../people/ian.md), twin brother of [Cole](../people/cole.md), who makes [Hide or Die](hide-or-die.md). Revenue is split 50/50 with a partner (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).

## Business model

- In-game purchases in Robux, which developers cash out. 1M Robux pays out about $3.5K ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=245s), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=676s)).
- Daily shops sell skins, coins and abilities, such as a meteor or invisibility ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).
- Brand deals add about $5K a month (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).

## Metrics

- About 150K daily active users, about $1.50 revenue per paying user and about $25K/month, split 50/50 (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).
- About 100M views at release from big Roblox YouTubers ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=149s)).

## Growth channels

- **Roblox YouTubers.** Big creators covered the game at release ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=149s)).
- **Clickable, social, replayable.** A dramatic title gets the click, friends play together, and levels and locked content bring players back ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=753s)).

## Timeline

- College winter break: Ian builds Bake the Baby.
- It is renamed Bathe the Baby, and Ian drops out of college on its income.
- 2025-04: about 150K DAU and about $25K/month ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).
