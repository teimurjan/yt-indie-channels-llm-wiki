App Masters is Steve Young's company. It buys apps that have organic App Store traffic but weak monetization, and it does ASO work.

## What it is

- Steve buys apps with App Store search distribution, then adds a hard paywall, raises prices and widens keywords ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s)).
- It also helps client apps find keywords before they build ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=929s)).

## Founders

- [Steve Young](../people/steve-young.md), who started in apps in 2010 ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md)).

## Business model

- Buy apps with loans. A $10K/month-profit app bought for $350K on a 10-year loan nets about $5K/month from day one ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2591s)). See [acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).
- Paid-only apps are "a money eating machine", so he buys organic ones ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s)).

## Metrics

- An app bought in 2024: over $1M lifetime and about $500K last year (2026-07) ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=517s)).
- A health app made about $6M last year, about $4M from App Store search (2026-07) ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=517s)).
- A 2022 buy went from a $2 to $3 one-time fee to $60/year. Revenue rose from $1K-2K to $8K-10K a month on the same downloads ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1131s)).
- Indie App Santa (bought 2024) paid back in 3 to 4 months. An $18,000 buy flopped ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [41:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2469s)).

## Growth channels

- **ASO.** Two number-one keywords with volume of about 30 bring 100 to 150 downloads a day ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s)). See [App Store optimization](../topics/app-store-optimization.md).
- **Apple Ads and Meta.** Run Meta only at about $3 revenue per install ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=990s)). See [Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md).
- **Paywall by source.** Soft paywall for search traffic, hard paywall for Meta traffic ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [38:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2310s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).
