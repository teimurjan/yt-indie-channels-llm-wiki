# Assembly

Assembly is [Hunter Hammonds](../people/hunter-hammonds.md)'s studio that builds creator-led B2B productized services, with a plan of 12 businesses in 12 months.

## What it is

- Each business is a productized service co-owned with a well-known creator who brings distribution ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md)).
- Portfolio (2023-09): Hey Friends, a done-for-you YouTube agency with Ali Abdaal; Off Menu, a design agency; Viral Cuts, a short-form video agency with [Sam Parr](../people/sam-parr.md) and Codie Sanchez; Keyframe, an animation studio ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=49s)).

## Founders

- [Hunter Hammonds](../people/hunter-hammonds.md). He earlier built Everest, an agency with eight figures of revenue and clients like Nike and Samsung ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=170s)).

## Business model

- Fixed-scope, premium-priced services. Hey Friends starts at $10K/month for 6 YouTube videos (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s)).
- Creators are equity partners, not affiliates ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)).
- Services are phase one. Cash flow will fund products and SaaS later ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=674s)).

## Metrics

- All four public companies above $1M ARR; three at "multiple seven figures" a year (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s)).
- Target: about $1M/month across the group by end of 2023 (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=49s)).
- Off Menu customer lifetime value $60K to $75K, with fairly high churn (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=425s)).
- Team grew from 3 to over 50 in 4 months (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)).

## Growth channels

- **Creator distribution.** Match the service to what the creator teaches, then launch to their audience ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s)).
- **Referrals.** Over-deliver to earn referrals ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=735s)).
