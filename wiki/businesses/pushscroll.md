Pushscroll is a mobile app that blocks social media until the user does push-ups, squats or a plank.

## What it is

- On-device pose detection with Google MediaPipe counts reps. They chose it over an LLM for latency ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=762s)).
- 1 push-up earns 1 minute of screen time. It has difficulty levels, a Duolingo-style journey tab and workouts ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=625s)).

## Founders

- [Alejandro](../people/alejandro.md) studied computer science and had failed startups because he ignored distribution. [Mario](../people/mario.md) is a software engineer whose earlier app reached about 5M downloads. They met through YC Co-Founder Matching ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=147s), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=164s)).

## Business model

- Hard [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) at about $30/year (2025-12) ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=105s)).
- Paywall tests with ARPU as north star: a 7-day trial beat 3 days, weekly beat monthly slightly, a UGC-style video paywall won, and an abandoned-transaction offer gives about 80% off ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [52:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3166s)).
- [Onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md) modeled on Quittr and Hormozi's value equation lifted conversion from 7% to 16% ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [42:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2543s), [51:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3097s)).

## Metrics

- $30K profit in the month before the interview, all organic (2025-10) ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=107s)).
- Over $30K/month 4 months after launch, about 300,000 downloads and about 4,000 paying users (2025-12) ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=84s), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=105s)).
- About 70% of users are 18 or younger, mostly European. US conversion is almost double Europe's (2025-10) ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [50:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3036s)).
- Android has about 3x the users and earns about as much as iOS or more (2025-10) ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [57:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3475s)).

> Conflict: the validation video had 80K views and about 500 comments in one source ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s)); the other cites a launch video at about 175K views and about 20K downloads ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=843s)). These may be two different videos.

## Growth channels

- [Organic](../topics/organic-social.md) [short-form video](../topics/short-form-video.md): one over-the-shoulder format ("my bro is doing push-ups at 2 a.m. to scroll Reels"). Skip rate is the key metric; their best was 19% ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [30:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1815s), [34:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2076s)). One video got 6M views and added about $10K MRR ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=571s)).
- ManyChat "comment APP" automation: almost 90,000 DMs, about 33% click-through (2025-10) ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [39:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2367s)).
- A Discord [community](../topics/community.md) of 3K to 4K members votes on features (2025-10) ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1015s)).
- Paid influencers lost about $2K net ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [29:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1744s)). They now scale with ads ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=84s)).
