Juno is a digital signage SaaS for cafes, gyms, schools and shops, part of [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md)'s bootstrapped app portfolio.

## What it is

- Juno sits in a portfolio with [Curator.io](curator-io.md), [Frill](frill.md), [Fluke](fluke.md) and Smile ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=81s), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=106s)).

## Founders

- [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md) and co-founders. Each portfolio company has four co-founders at 25% each: front end, back end, designer and one generalist ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)).

## Business model

- Each company grows to about $10K MRR to cover costs. Above that, profits go to the founders ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s)).

## Metrics

- The portfolio makes just over $200K MRR (2025-11). The source does not give Juno's own revenue ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s)).

## Growth channels

- The portfolio playbook: copy a proven idea, sell private lifetime deals, fund SEO content and "alternative to" pages, launch on AppSumo, collect reviews and answer Reddit questions about competitors ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s)).
