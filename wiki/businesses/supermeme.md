# Supermeme

Supermeme (Supermeme.ai) is an AI meme generator.

## What it is

- An AI SaaS app built with Next.js and Supabase. Sister app: [Questgen](questgen.md), an AI quiz generator ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=540s)).

## Founders

- Three co-founders: [Ramsri Goutham](../people/ramsri-goutham.md) (AI), a full-stack developer and a marketer. The co-founders found Ramsri through his posts ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=252s), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)).

## Business model

- Subscription SaaS. Margin 70% to 75%; the main cost is OpenAI and Anthropic APIs (2025-06) ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=577s)).

## Metrics

- About $130K lifetime revenue, 1.5M+ users, about 400 paying customers (2025-06) ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s)).
- With Questgen: $8,500/month on about 2 hours of work a week (2025-06) ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=51s)).

## Growth channels

- **Build in public.** Daily LinkedIn and Twitter posts before and during the build. $0 on marketing ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=451s)).
- **Product Hunt.** Early fans and a viral meme feature made it number one on launch day ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)).
