SiteGPT is a solo-built AI chatbot that answers questions about a business's website content.

## What it is

- A chatbot trained on a company's site content, used for support ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s)).

## Founders

- [Bhanu](../people/bhanu.md), a solo developer from India. He first built the chatbot for the 100 customers of his earlier SaaS [Feather](feather.md), then split it out. He sold Feather for $250K to focus on SiteGPT ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=229s), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s)).

## Business model

- Monthly fee based on number of chatbots, content size and expected messages ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s)).
- About 130 business customers at about $100 average revenue; LTV $1,700 to $1,800 (2026-01) ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=204s)).

## Metrics

- About $10K MRR in its first month (2023) ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s)).
- $13K MRR and about $500K total revenue since its March 2023 launch, $0 on paid marketing (2026-01) ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s)).
- Monthly funnel: about 50,000 visitors, 200 leads, 60 trials, 25% to 40% trial conversion (2026-01) ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s)).
- Customer result: [Sheets & Giggles](sheets-and-giggles.md) pays about $50/month and cut support tickets by about 30% (2024-05). Its bot offers code "AI" for 10% off after a good chat ([source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=644s), [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=705s)).

## Growth channels

- **Free tools for [SEO](../topics/seo.md).** 60% to 70% of traffic comes from Google, and about 90% of that lands on almost 50 free tools (2026-01) ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=107s)).
- Tools relate to the product (PDF to Markdown, chatbot name generator). Cursor clones a tool for a new keyword in under 5 minutes ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s)).
- **Ahrefs workflow.** Keyword difficulty under 10, volume of 1,000+, a CTA under each tool, then score by volume, KD, effort and relevance ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=525s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=648s)).

## Timeline

- 2024-05: in use at Sheets & Giggles ([source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=644s)).
- 2026-01: $13K MRR ([source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s)).
