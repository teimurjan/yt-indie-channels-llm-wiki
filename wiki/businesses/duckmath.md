DuckMath is an "unblocked games" website paid by Google AdSense that Maddox grew with TikTok and sold for $120K.

## What it is

- A games website for students that runs on ad revenue. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=49s))

## Founders

- [Maddox](../people/maddox.md), a college student. He built it in a week or two after he saw a younger creator promote a similar site. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=159s))

## Business model

- Google AdSense, at about 1 cent per user. A/B tests aim at time on site and pages per visit, because more pages mean more ad views. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=70s), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=252s))

## Metrics

- About 5K to 150K daily active users and about $1K to $15K/month in 4 months (2026-03). ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=49s))
- About 1.5 million users per month. A normal day was $600 to $700 (2026-03). ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=70s), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=129s))
- A Discord with about 7,000 members. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=708s))

## Growth channels

- **High-volume short-form video.** 3 TikTok accounts, 3 posts a day, 5 days a week, cross-posted to Reels, Shorts and Snapchat with Repurpose.io. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s))
- **Lo-fi hooks.** The top video took 30 minutes and got about 5M views across TikTok and Instagram. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=388s))
- Snapchat steadily gave 20,000+ views per video. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s))

## Timeline

- About 2022: launched. It sat at about $1K/month for years. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=49s))
- A passive $240 day pushed Maddox to market it hard. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=204s))
- Early 2026: sold for $120K after a $100K lowball, through escrow, to a bigger company that had bought a similar site. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=519s))
