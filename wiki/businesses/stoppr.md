Stoppr is an iOS app that helps Gen Z women stop sugar cravings, built as a screen-for-screen clone of Quittr.

## What it is

- A habit-quitting app for women aged about 13 to 25. It copies Quittr's onboarding and swaps the content for sugar [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=85s), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=488s).

## Founders

- [David Attias](../people/david-attias.md), a former quant trader from France with 8 years in trading and no coding background. He built it with Cursor [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=142s).

## Business model

- Weekly, monthly and yearly plans with a trial [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=85s).
- Costs: Cursor $20/month, Mixpanel about $100/month, TikTok Spark Ads about $100/month. 35% profit margin (2025-10) [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=483s).

## Metrics

- $5K in month one; about $12K/month 5 months after launch (2025-10) [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=58s).
- 60,000 downloads and about 900 paying customers in 5 months (2025-10) [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s).

## Growth channels

- French influencers: no one in France offered this kind of app [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s).
- Top Yappers finds creators who already promoted other apps; then email outreach [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s).
- Meta ads and TikTok Spark Ads on reels that already work [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s).

## Timeline

- Idea: David hears Quittr's teen founders made $200K/month after 3 months [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=142s).
- Build: 2.5 weeks to copy, 1 week for Apple approval [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=314s).
- 2025-10: about $12K/month [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=58s).
