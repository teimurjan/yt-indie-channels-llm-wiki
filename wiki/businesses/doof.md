Doof is a food diary app by solo founder [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s)). It is one of four products he discusses in one source.

## What it is

- A food diary app for iPhone ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s)).

## Founders

- Jack Friks, who also built [Curiosity Quench](curiosity-quench.md), [Post Bridge](post-bridge.md) and [Lovely](lovely.md) ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=196s)).

## Business model

- The source does not give Doof's price. Jack's other apps use free trials and Superwall template paywalls ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [17:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1068s)).

## Metrics

- $1K in revenue in its first 30 days ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s)).
- One meme slideshow got 5M views ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [20:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1256s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok slideshows.** Organic videos must not read as ads, so the app goes in the caption or in a few slides ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [20:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1256s)).
- **Views versus conversion.** Jack calls meme slideshows "silver": millions of views but few downloads. A short clip of the app in use is "gold" and converts about 20x better ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s)).

## Timeline

- First 30 days: $1K in revenue (2025-12) ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s)).
