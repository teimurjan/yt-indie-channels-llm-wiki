FarmVille is a Zynga farming game that launched on Facebook in 2009. Tim Gabe uses it as the main example of a product that runs on a clock.

## What it is

- Players plant crops that ripen on a timer. A 4-hour crop was ready in 4 hours and stayed safe for about 8 hours. After that it rotted ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=64s), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=112s)).

## Metrics

- At its peak it had tens of millions of monthly players ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s)).

## Growth channels

- **The clock.** A crop that rots is an open loop the mind cannot drop. This is the Zeigarnik effect ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=112s)). See [behavioral design](../topics/behavioral-design.md).
- **Facebook feed reminders.** Zynga filled feeds with friend requests and neighbor help requests ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s)). See [viral loops](../topics/viral-loops.md).

## Timeline

- 2009: launch on Facebook ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=64s)).
- Peak: tens of millions of monthly players ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s)).
- Decline: Facebook limited how often games post to feeds. The reminder engine broke ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=235s)).

## Lesson

- Put the hero feature on a timer so it changes without the user ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=457s)).
- Do not depend on one platform's feed ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=235s)).
