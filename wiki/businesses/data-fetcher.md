Data Fetcher is a solo-run Airtable extension that pulls data from any API into Airtable on a schedule.

## What it is

- An Airtable marketplace extension for API imports. [Andy Cloke](../people/andy-cloke.md) copied the idea from API Connector, a Google Sheets add-on with about 100,000 users ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s)).
- It sits between Airtable scripting and no-code imports, so a native clone seems unlikely ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s)).

## Founders

- [Andy Cloke](../people/andy-cloke.md), a solo founder in London ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s)).

## Business model

- B2B subscription. Costs: hosting about $2,500/month, SaaS tools about $1,000, co-working $150; 85% margin (2025-08) ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=520s)).
- Stack: TypeScript, React, Airtable's extension SDK, Node, Postgres, GraphQL, Heroku and Hetzner ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=472s)).

## Metrics

- $23K MRR from 600 paying customers (2025-08) ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s)).

## Growth channels

- **[Platform marketplace](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).** Airtable's marketplace sends qualified leads, and users trust approved apps ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s)).
- **Use-case content.** Blog posts and YouTube videos for the most common integrations ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- **No-code integrations** let less technical users succeed and drove growth to $10K MRR ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- **User tests.** One afternoon of tests after a year without user contact found UX issues; fixes raised revenue and usage almost overnight ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=596s)).

## Timeline

- First customer after a few days, $1K MRR after a few months, $3K MRR after a year, $10K MRR after no-code integrations, $20K MRR after 3 years ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- 2025-08: $23K MRR ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s)).
- Side businesses during slowdowns cost about 6 months ([source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=548s)).
