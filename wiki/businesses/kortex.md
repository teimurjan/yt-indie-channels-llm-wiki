Kortex is a second-brain note-taking tool by Dan Koe, with an AI assistant that answers from your own notes.

## What it is

- Kortex links notes, PDFs, articles and videos in one place. Its AI assistant "Kai" answers questions from that linked material ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=272s)).
- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) lists it as one of five tools he uses every day (2025-04). Some of the tools in that video were sponsors, but he says he picked the list first ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=0s)).

## Founders

- Dan Koe built it ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=272s)).

## Business model and metrics

The source gives no price, revenue or user numbers.

## Growth channels

- Creator recommendations. Tim Gabe presents it to designers in a tools roundup ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=272s)).

## How it is used

- Capture ideas on the phone. Then connect them to user interviews, articles and videos, so the AI can answer from all of them ([source](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=272s), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=333s)).
