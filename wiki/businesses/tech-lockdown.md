Tech Lockdown helps adults block websites and apps through a filtered VPN connection.

## What it is

- A content policy that users schedule. It blocks millions of sites by category or keyword. Devices connect through a VPN [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=85s).

## Founders

- [Ben Bozzay](../people/ben-bozzay.md), a software developer. Earlier failures: a lead platform for freelance developers, a landing page builder and a web agency that ran about 2.5 years [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=147s).

## Business model

- 14-day free trial, then $15/month, or about $10/month on an annual plan (2025-07). He pushes annual but keeps monthly, because many users quit after a month [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=104s).
- Stack: Supabase, SvelteKit, Vercel ($20/month), Mailgun and Elastic Email (about $150/month), Google Gemini, Ahrefs Lite and Plausible. A 70% margin is easy with all tools added [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=703s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=758s).

## Metrics

- 1,300 customers, over $15K MRR and a mailing list of about 20,000 (2025-07) [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s).
- Over 2 million people found the site in 2 years (2025-07) [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s).

## Growth channels

- **Audience before product.** Free guides, YouTube videos and Reddit posts for about a year before he charged [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=203s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=484s).
- **Deep, free guides.** A guide to turn an iPhone into a "dumb phone" got hundreds of thousands of reads [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=588s).
- **Value inside the Reddit post**, with a short link to the guide [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=648s).
- **Show your face.** Rivals are faceless apps. He writes "I", not "we" [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=671s).

## Timeline

- 2020: the idea, after remote work made him spend too much time online [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=203s).
- About a year later: first paid version. No logo until a few hundred customers [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=292s).
- 2023: laid off at about $3,000/month, which did not cover his costs. He went full-time [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=249s).
- 2025-07: over $15K MRR, 5x since the layoff [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s).
