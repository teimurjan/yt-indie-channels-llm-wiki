Escape the Toilet was a mobile game tied to the Skibidi Toilet meme, launched by Rafael Kramer's influencer game company.

## What it is

- A mobile game for iOS and Android. It is no longer live, and the reason is not public ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=250s)).

## Founders

- [Rafael Kramer](../people/rafael-kramer.md) started the company behind it at 15. It helped influencers launch their own games and had up to 10 staff ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s)).
- The Skibidi Toilet creator promoted it. He had about 5M subscribers at the time ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=274s)).

## Business model

- Rafael's influencer games used 50/50 splits with the influencer ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [39:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2389s)).

## Metrics

- 2M to 3M downloads across iOS and Android in about 2 weeks ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=250s)).

## Growth channels

- [Influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md). One YouTube Short from the Skibidi Toilet creator got more than 50M views. Word of mouth did the rest ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=274s)).

## Timeline

- Launched while Rafael ran the company, between ages 15 and 17 ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s)).
- Rafael sold the company at 17 for at least six figures ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=346s)).
