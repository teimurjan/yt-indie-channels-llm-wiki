HubSpot is a public software company that sells CRM and marketing tools. In this wiki it appears as the buyer of [Starter Story](starter-story.md) and as a CRM that founders use.

## What it is

- A public company. [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md)
- A CRM. [Oceans](oceans.md) uses HubSpot as its CRM next to Google Workspace, Stripe, Notion and tldv (2025-03). [gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md) [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=761s)

## Acquisition of Starter Story

- HubSpot acquired Starter Story (2026-02). The team, mission and format stay the same. HubSpot adds resources for interviews, shows, production and tools. [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s)
- The price is private. [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) calls it "life-changing". [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=573s)
- Pat chose HubSpot for alignment, not only price, because it wanted to scale Starter Story without changing it. [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s)
