Curiosity Quench is a mobile app that gives people hobby action plans so they scroll less.

## What it is

- A hobby app against doomscrolling. It gives action plans for new hobbies [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s), [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=196s).
- It took 4 months to build while the founder learned to code with ChatGPT [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s).

## Founders

- [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md), a solo founder who quit McDonald's and dropped out of college. He also built [Post Bridge](../businesses/post-bridge.md), Doof and Lovely [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=156s), [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s).

## Business model

- A subscription at about $30/year with a 7-day [free trial](../topics/free-trials.md) (2025-03) [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s).
- On mobile, onboarding explains the problem and the fix, then a skippable paywall offers a premium trial. On the web, a 7-day card-required trial with no free plan beat pushing yearly plans [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=795s).
- A confusing credit "shop" earned $500 to $1K/month for about 5 months. A Superwall template [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) with a free trial raised it to $2K to $4K/month [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=946s), [16:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1007s).

## Metrics

- 1,000 users in the first 2 months, then 8 months of 50 to 60 downloads a day before real revenue [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=196s).
- Peak: $15K in one month (2024-09) [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=579s).
- $3K MRR, over $60K in the last year and over 100K downloads (2025-03) [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s).

## Growth channels

- **One repeated slideshow format.** Jack found a 4-picture "most attractive hobbies" post with 1M views and swapped in the app's widgets. He pinned a download CTA in the comments, and angry commenters pushed it up [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=304s), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=386s).
- **One hour a day of [organic social](../topics/organic-social.md), no ad spend.** Warm up accounts, remake proven formats in CapCut, keep videos about 6 seconds, and add a deliberate mistake to drive comments [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=530s), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=590s).
- **Several accounts.** Three Instagram accounts with posts 3 to 4 hours apart, and never the same file twice [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=639s).

> Conflict: [qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) says the meme template was posted about 300 times on two accounts and drove 60K to 70K signups ([8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=530s)). [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) says about 400 times ([5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=304s)).

## Timeline

- About 2023 to 2024: 4-month build, then 1,000 users in 2 months and 8 slow months [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=196s).
- 2024-09: peak month at $15K [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=579s).
- 2025-03: $3K MRR. With Post Bridge, Jack's products make $10K/month together [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s).
