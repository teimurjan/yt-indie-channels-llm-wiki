This page collects what the sources say about Midjourney, the AI image generator.

## What it is

Midjourney makes images from text prompts. It started as a Discord bot ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=177s)). In most of these sources it is a tool that designers and marketers use, not a case study.

## Founders

The sources do not name the founders. They describe the company as about 150 employees with no VC funding (2026-03) ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=177s)).

## Business model

- Midjourney charges a monthly fee. In one challenge, the fee was Tim Gabe's only cost to restyle a website (2024-07) ([hCKdIwbhS-s](../sources/hCKdIwbhS-s.md), [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=382s)).
- Personalization is the lock-in. Each image-pair ranking trains a model of the user's taste that the user cannot take elsewhere ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=222s)).
- Tim calls this the "intelligence trap". It builds on Nir Eyal's "stored value" idea ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s)). See [Retention](../topics/retention.md) and [Personalization](../topics/personalization.md).

## Metrics

- Revenue was $500M in 2025, with $0 VC funding and about 150 employees. That is over $3M revenue per employee (as cited 2026-03) ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=177s)).
- V7 turns personalization on by default. Over 70% of active users adopted it within 3 months ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=222s)).

## Growth channels

- Word of mouth from a Discord bot ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=177s)).
- Tim thinks its landing page did not show what the tool makes. The old page put weight on the team ([_n39rxieClg](../sources/_n39rxieClg.md), [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=27s)).
- His redesign leads with a grid of generated images and one primary button, "Join the beta" ([_n39rxieClg](../sources/_n39rxieClg.md), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=194s)). See [Landing pages](../topics/landing-pages.md).

## How others use it

- **Website visuals**. Tim lists Midjourney as a free source of visuals for a site ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=624s)). His 30-second flow asks for a white background, then removes it and upscales in Canva ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=712s)).
- **Product mockups**. He made chocolate product shots in Midjourney for a template site and cut them out in Canva ([hCKdIwbhS-s](../sources/hCKdIwbhS-s.md), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=260s)).
- **Hero animations**. One Midjourney wallpaper became a layered Framer hero after a split in Photoshop ([m2NXQjKPUrg](../sources/m2NXQjKPUrg.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2NXQjKPUrg&t=0s)).
- **Ad creative**. As the only designer at a crypto protocol, Tim used Midjourney so much that marketing ran ads without design help ([bOm9GDv96Tk](../sources/bOm9GDv96Tk.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOm9GDv96Tk&t=303s)).
- **Social content**. [Zuhair Lani](../people/zuhair-lani.md)'s first AI poster video used ElevenLabs and Midjourney. It got 3 sales on day one and later 280K views ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=0s)), ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [12:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=724s)).

## Effect on designers

- In 2022-11, Tim said pixel pushing is the easiest work for tools like DALL-E 2 and Midjourney to replace ([IaFrUED28mo](../sources/IaFrUED28mo.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=138s)).
- In 2025-06, he said AI tools get most design tasks about 80% done in minutes. The last 20% still needs human insight ([bOm9GDv96Tk](../sources/bOm9GDv96Tk.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOm9GDv96Tk&t=45s)).
