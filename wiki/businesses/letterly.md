# Letterly

Letterly is a paid mobile app that turns speech into well-written text, built by [Anton](../people/anton.md).

## What it is

- A voice-to-text app for notes, messages, emails, social posts and meeting notes. The target user finds speaking easier than typing ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=533s)).
- It competes with free tools such as ChatGPT and wins on simplicity. One widget tap starts a recording, and one tap stops it and gives the text ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=320s)).
- The idea came from early prototypes that combined ChatGPT with OpenAI transcription. None of them felt finished enough to use ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=146s)).

## Founders

- [Anton](../people/anton.md). He spent 15 years on six or seven startups that failed before Letterly ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md)).

## Business model

- Paid app with a free trial ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=113s)).
- Monthly costs: about $30K in salaries for a team of 10, about $5K in AI costs and about $200K on ads (2025-11) ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=676s)).
- Stack: started in React Native, moved to Swift on iOS. Python backend ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=655s)).

## Metrics

- $250K/month, 2 years after launch (2025-11) ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=90s)).
- 30K monthly active users, 20K paid subscribers and 150,000 downloads in 2 years (2025-11) ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=113s)).

## Growth channels

- **Paid ads**, at about $200K a month (2025-11) ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=676s)).
- **UX as the growth multiplier.** Good UX helps onboarding, retention, conversion and referrals ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=320s)).
- **One-tap actions.** Users pin favorite rewrite styles under the note. A one-click copy button became one of the most used features ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=533s)).

## Timeline

- Prototypes on ChatGPT plus OpenAI transcription ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=146s)).
- Launch, then a move from React Native to Swift ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=655s)).
- 2025-11: $250K/month and 20K paid subscribers, 2 years after launch.
