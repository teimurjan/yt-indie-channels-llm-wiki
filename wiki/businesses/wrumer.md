Wrumer is a car device that plugs into the OBD2 port and plays fake engine sounds. It sells almost entirely through organic short-form video.

## What it is

A device for the car's OBD2 port. A diagnostics device reads live RPM, and Wrumer turns that into engine sounds. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=277s)

## Founders

[Jure Sotošek](../people/jure-sotosek.md), a 26-year-old developer who left Microsoft. He got the idea while he built Shopify stores and bought a used car. His creator co-founder Urban came from a cold email to hundreds of TikTok creators. Urban's second video went viral, and they split revenue. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=277s), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=671s)

## Business model

A one-off physical product sold from a simple landing page. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=712s)

## Metrics

- 6,000 units and about $300K in revenue last year (2025-04). [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)
- About 200M organic short-form views in one year gave about 600K page visits and over 6,000 orders (2025-04). [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s)
- Jure's four businesses together make about $60K/month (2025-04). [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)

## Growth channels

- **Organic TikTok.** A cheap, funny, slightly controversial product sells in 10 seconds. Products that need long thought do not sell well there. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=333s)
- **Mix and match video parts.** Urban writes 3 hooks, 5 main parts and 3 endings, then films the combinations. A concept that works gets remade many times. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=631s)
- **Failed channels.** Facebook ads and Google ads did not work for Wrumer. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s)
- **No small A/B tests.** With low volume, only make big changes that you know will help. [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=712s)
