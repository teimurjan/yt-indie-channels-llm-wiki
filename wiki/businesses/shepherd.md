Shepherd is a Duolingo-style Bible app launched by [Dan Kwon](../people/dan-kwon.md).

## What it is

- A gamified Bible learning app pitched against [Duolingo](duolingo.md): "God first lamb over the AI first owl" ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s)).

## Founders

- [Dan Kwon](../people/dan-kwon.md), a former social media creator who grew [Conch AI](conch-ai.md) and [Arise](arise.md) ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [21:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1319s)).

## Metrics

- About 7 videos got 20M views in two weeks, over 100,000 downloads and $75K MRR (2025-10) ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [21:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1319s), [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1355s)).
- About #10 in its App Store chart for a week. Its Discord has about 10,000 members (2025-10) ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [25:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1525s), [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s)).

## Growth channels

- Launched on news: Duolingo faced backlash for AI content at the time ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s)).
- "Frankenstein" content: viral podcast clips of a Christian influencer stitched with Shepherd hooks and a demo ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [24:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1458s)).
- After the spike, the team paused creator work to build invite loops for prayer and accountability buddies ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1355s)).

## Timeline

- A two-week build plus a two-week growth sprint ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [21:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1319s)).
- Users pushed back on an AI chat feature. The team made it a custom devotional feature and removed "AI" from the copy ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [28:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1727s)).
