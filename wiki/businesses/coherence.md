Coherence is a breathwork app that helps users regulate the nervous system.

## What it is

- A simple app with six breathing techniques, a timer, inhale and exhale sounds and a streak counter [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=565s).

## Founders

- Brothers [Jack Sweeney](../people/jack-sweeney.md) and [Nick Sweeney](../people/nick-sweeney.md). Nick turned to breathwork after a skiing injury and coaches others. Jack ran a YouTube channel of about 20,000 subscribers and built no-code apps [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=112s).

## Business model

- $40/year with a 3-day free trial, or $10/month with no trial (2025-11) [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=611s).
- Monthly costs $5K to $6K, mostly editors, influencers and a thread ghostwriter; about 50% margin (2025-11) [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=662s).

## Metrics

- About $85K revenue and over 15,000 downloads 8 months after launch (2025-11) [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=72s).
- About $11K/month on average, best month $22K, just over 2,000 users (2025-11) [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=92s).

## Growth channels

- Content first: ideas tested on X, winners moved to Instagram and TikTok, then to YouTube [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=286s).
- A polarizing "what they don't want you to know" video reached about 8M views across platforms [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=519s).
- Batch filming of 30 to 60 videos at a time, Spark Ads and Meta ads on winners [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=417s).

## Timeline

- Before the app: a viral "vortex breath" post gives them the app idea [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s).
- 2025-02: MVP built with Cursor and launched [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=315s).
- 2025-11: about $85K total revenue [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=72s).
