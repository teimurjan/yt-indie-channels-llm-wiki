Posture AI is an iOS app that scans the posture of desk workers, made by The Wellness Company studio.

## What it is

- A posture scanner for desk workers. It is one of three live apps from [The Wellness Company](the-wellness-company.md), next to [Go Polar](go-polar.md) and [SunSeek](sunseek.md) [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s).

## Founders

- [Presh](../people/presh.md), who worked 7 years for an angel investor before he started building apps [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s).

## Business model

- Subscriptions with RevenueCat paywalls. The studio's tools cost about $300/month, and margins are 80% to 85% under Apple's 15% small-business fee (2025-12) [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=697s), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=736s).

## Metrics

- The source gives only studio-wide numbers: $120K ARR ($10.4K/month), over 20,000 downloads, over 1,500 subscribers, about 8% free-to-paid and 61% day-30 paid retention across the three apps (2025-12) [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s).

## Growth channels

- Mostly organic App Store search [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s).
- Cross-promotion: all the studio's apps serve one overlapping health audience, so each launch markets to existing users [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s).

## Timeline

- 2025: launched as one of three apps in about 10 months [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s).
