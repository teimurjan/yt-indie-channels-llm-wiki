Papermark is an open-source alternative to DocSend for secure document sharing, analytics and data rooms.

## What it is

- [Open-source](../topics/open-source.md) document sharing with view analytics and data rooms. It targets DocSend, which Marc calls a slow, enterprise-focused incumbent about 10 years old ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=126s)).

## Founders

- [Marc Seitz](../people/marc-seitz.md) and [Iuliia Shnai](../people/iuliia-shnai.md), a husband-and-wife team. Iuliia could not code at the start ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=779s)).

## Business model

- Open core: free self-hosting, a paid hosted version, and paid enterprise licenses for self-hosters ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=267s)).
- Costs: about 80% freelancers and founder salaries, 15% marketing experiments, 5% tools ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=706s)).

## Metrics

- $20K MRR after year one. $75K MRR, close to $900K ARR, by the middle of year two (2025-08) ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s)).
- About 30K users, about 1,000 paying customers, 60 contributors, 7K GitHub stars (2025-08) ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s)).
- North star: 800K views on shared documents (2025-08) ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s)).

## Growth channels

- [Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) on Twitter and LinkedIn, plus Hacktoberfest ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=546s)).
- Positioning as a [competitor alternative](../topics/competitor-alternatives.md) that out-ships the incumbent ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=735s)).
