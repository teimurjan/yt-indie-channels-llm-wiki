PrayScreen is a screen-time app for Christians that makes you pray before it unlocks your other apps.

## What it is

- It copies the one sec model, which makes you breathe for 3 seconds before Instagram opens, for a Christian audience [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=272s).
- Within 2 to 3 months about 70 "Bible focus" clones appeared [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=744s). Mau Baron studied PrayScreen's design, features and distribution before he built [Prayer Lock](../businesses/prayer-lock.md) [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=170s).

## Founders

- [Lots](../people/lotts-ezeike.md) built it. It was his fourth app exit [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s). His other apps include [Bible Buddy](../businesses/bible-buddy.md), [Magic Music](../businesses/magic-music.md) and [Toxic Traits](../businesses/toxic-traits.md) [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s).

## Business model

- Subscriptions, but only about 2.5% of installs paid. A free tier shows about four ads a day, which made about $0.05 per user per day, with payback in 14 to 15 days [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=501s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=548s).
- Strong retention made the ad tier work [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=551s).

## Metrics

- About $120K ARR within 5 to 6 months of a December 2024 launch [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s).
- Day-30 retention of 60%, with users praying four times a day [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=328s), [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s).
- US Meta cost per install: best day $0.39, average $0.70 to $0.80 (2025-09) [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=355s). In a later video: $0.30 to $0.50 (2025-11) [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s).

> Conflict: [NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) gives a $0.70 to $0.80 average US CPI. [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) gives $0.30 to $0.50.

## Growth channels

- **[Organic TikTok](../topics/organic-social.md) through an agency.** It ran 10 to 12 accounts with shock hooks, the "opposite of religion", so people argued in the comments [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=170s).
- **Organic winners as [Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md).** One video drove about 70% of spend and installs, out of 20 to 30 videos with the same angle [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=387s).
- **[UGC](../topics/ugc.md) first, then Meta.** Several UGC accounts with rage-bait content drive comment fights. A working hook then goes on Meta ads [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s).
- **Show the splash screen and result screen** in content, not the onboarding [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=223s).

## Timeline

- 2024-12: launch [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s).
- About mid-2025: about $120K ARR. Lots tweeted it was for sale, and the deal closed in about a week for multiple six figures. The buyer had tried to buy Bible Buddy 2 years earlier [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s), [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1212s).
- 2025-11: Lots counts it among four apps sold since 2022 for over $500K in total [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s).
- 2026-01: Prayer Lock, a rival built after studying PrayScreen, made $21K in December 2025 [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s).
