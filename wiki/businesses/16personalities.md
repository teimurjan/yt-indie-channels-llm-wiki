16Personalities is a personality test site that gives each user a four-letter type. Tim Gabe uses it as the example of identity as retention.

## What it is

- A test that assigns a type such as INFJ. People have taken it over a billion times ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=339s)).
- Users put their type in dating bios and use it at work ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=339s)).

## Growth channels

- **The label.** Users get a label they are proud to claim and show in public ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=401s)). See [behavioral design](../topics/behavioral-design.md).
- The label must be specific enough to feel earned and flattering enough to want. People rarely churn from their own identity ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=462s)). See [retention](../topics/retention.md).
- The Barnum effect makes generic text feel personal ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md)).
