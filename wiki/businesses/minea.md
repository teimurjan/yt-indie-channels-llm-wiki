Minea is a bootstrapped product research tool for dropshippers, built by Loïc Berthelot.

## What it is

- Product research for ecommerce dropshippers [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s).

## Founders

- [Loïc Berthelot](../people/loic-berthelot.md), a French founder. He spent his first five years on a SaaS nobody used, then launched more than 10. Three worked: Infuspy, Minea and [Drop Magic](drop-magic.md) [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=0s).

## Business model

- SaaS, 100% bootstrapped. Minea ran at about 80% margin (2025-11) [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=64s), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=712s).

## Metrics

- Peaked at "750K MRR" within 2 years, as Loïc states it [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s).
- Minea and Drop Magic work with more than 500 YouTube creators. YouTube brings more than 60% of new customers (2025-11) [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=233s), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s).

> Conflict: the video title says "$35K/month apps", and Loïc says each of the three apps hit at least $35K MRR early. For Minea he states a "750K MRR" peak. Both are from [-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=64s), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s). The peak figure is unverified.

## Growth channels

- **YouTube creators**, paid in cash or affiliate commission. YouTube has the best ROI and a predictable view floor, and old videos still convert 3 years later [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s).
- **Creator system** [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=485s):
  1. Find creators by keyword and by which ones competitors use.
  2. Qualify: views over 10% of followers and at least 100 comments per video.
  3. Send a 7-email sequence, plus Instagram and Twitter messages.
  4. Pay a fixed fee plus commission. Test one video, then buy a 3 to 4 video package.
