# Kaching Bundles

Kaching Bundles is a Shopify app that helps merchants raise average order value with bundle discounts, and the first of a family of Kaching apps.

## What it is

- A Shopify discount-category app. Later apps cover post-purchase upsell, pop-up upsell and cart drawer upsell ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).

## Founders

- [Erikas](../people/erikas.md), a Lithuanian designer in Paris, and a developer co-founder he recruited in a local developer Facebook group ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=580s)).

## Business model

- Launch free, then charge with usage tiers. Early users stay free; only new users pay. Free trial and full refunds ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=812s)).

## Metrics

- Kaching Bundles: about $400K MRR (2025-04) ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).
- All five apps: $4.5M ARR at about 90% profit margin (2025-04) ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=62s)).
- Merchants made over $400M in extra revenue with the apps (2025-04) ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).
- About 95% of reviews come from customer support ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s)).

## Growth channels

- **Shopify App Store rank.** Almost no ads. Rank depends on five-star reviews ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md)). See [Platform marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
- **Manual first installs** from clients, friends and communities ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=580s)).
- **Review engine.** In-product asks, a monthly revenue email that asks for a review, and support agents with bonuses per review ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s), [12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=733s)).
