3AK is an all-in-one track and field app built by two college sprinters with no coding experience, using the AI app builder Rork.

## What it is

- Workout plans, workout and form tracking, AI video analysis of sprint form, meal analysis and a community ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=334s)).
- The AI analyzer scores a sprint start. Pat Walls scored 56, then 68 after one tip ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=388s), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=472s)).

## Founders

- [Christian](../people/christian.md) and [Braylin](../people/braylin.md), aged 19 and 20, who run track at Texas Southern and Texas State. Christian dropped out to work on apps ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=97s), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=524s)).

## Business model

- Subscription: $10/month or $30/year (2026-06). Users are teens who spend $10 to $20 at a time, so the yearly plan is cheap ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)). See [pricing](../topics/pricing.md).
- Tools: Rork, Claude, Claude Code, RevenueCat and Supabase. They say an app can reach the App Store for under $500 ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=172s), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=204s)).

## Metrics

- $10K in the first month ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)).
- About $70K in the first 6 months, about 40,000 users, 4.7 rating (2026-06) ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=74s)).
- Over $20K/month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=0s)).

## Growth channels

- **Own audience first.** Their track videos had hundreds of thousands of followers, with no paid marketing ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=312s)).
- **Seeded brand.** A 3AK Shopify store and "3AK method" in videos and bios meant the audience knew the name at launch ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=318s)).
- **Empty niche.** No app served track and field ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=299s)).
