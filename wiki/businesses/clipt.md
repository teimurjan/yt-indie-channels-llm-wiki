Clipt is an animation and video editing agency that matches top animators with companies and funds its founders' own content brand.

## What it is

- An agency that matches animators and editors with companies ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s)).
- Clients include My First Million, the All-In Podcast, Ali Abdaal, Will Smith and Naval ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s)).

## Founders

- [Henry Belcaster](../people/henry-belcaster.md) and [Dylan Jardon](../people/dylan-jardon.md), college roommates in Chicago. They met in 2015 ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
- Henry ships at 80%. Dylan sweats details. Each corrects the other ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s)).

## Business model

- Service revenue from client work. The agency cash funds [Smart Nonsense](smart-nonsense.md), their animated YouTube Shorts brand ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=125s)).
- The content team costs $25K to $30K/month. The Shorts earn about $5K/month, so the content runs at a loss (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)).
- The core job: match one animator with one client for 2 years ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=810s)).

## Metrics

- About $2M/year in top-line revenue (2023-10). The intro says $1.8M/year, built in 2 years ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=0s)).
- About 50 animators and editors, mostly in the Philippines. About 10 work only on the founders' content (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)).
- Smart Nonsense: 1 billion combined views and 1M YouTube subscribers in about a year (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).

> Conflict: [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) gives both about $2M/year and $1.8M/year for the same period.

## Growth channels

- **Blitz.** Sam Parr and Shaan Puri asked on Twitter for video help. The founders offered it free and showed up the next day. For All-In they sent three unasked-for videos before Jason Calacanis hired them ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s)).
- **Squatter marketing.** Make videos for a big creator for a year unpaid. Their network assumes you work with them, and new clients follow ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=338s)).
- **Own content.** One animated 60-second Short a day on Smart Nonsense, plus a newsletter ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=149s)).

## Timeline

- 2020: they start a podcast and have 14 views after 3 months ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)).
- They hire editors for their own podcast, then sell the same editors to other podcasts ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=779s)).
- Early on they carry about $40K of credit card debt while clients pay late ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=297s)).
- 2023-10: about $2M/year ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s)).
