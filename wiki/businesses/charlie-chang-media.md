Charlie Chang Media is Charlie Chang's group of six personal finance and business YouTube channels.

## What it is

- Six channels, led by Charlie Chang with about 1.1M subscribers and a second channel with about 50,000 (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).

## Founders

- [Charlie Chang](../people/charlie-chang.md), 31, in Southern California. He tried many side hustles, then started finance videos in 2018 ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md)).
- He also runs [Paired](paired.md), an overseas staffing company started in 2023 ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).

## Business model

- Revenue mix: affiliate 50% to 55%, brand sponsorships 20% to 25%, AdSense 20% to 25% (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)).
- Team of 26 at about $20K to $25K/month, mostly overseas: a COO, 2 to 3 project managers, editors, thumbnail, graphics and web people (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)).

## Metrics

- $200K to $250K in a typical month, close to $300K in the best month, over $2.4M a year (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
- 2020 breakout: 500 to 1,000 new subscribers a day and $10K to $20K/month in AdSense, solo ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=240s)).

## Growth channels

- **Search content.** Evergreen videos such as "how to invest in stocks", not only viral attempts ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)).
- **Title formula**: a relatable age, a big number and a dollar amount ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)).
- **Trend riding** into crypto, stocks and side hustles as each boomed ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=349s)).
- **Shorts from long-form** with Submagic, Descript and Opus ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=477s)).
- Production runs on ClickUp, Frame.io, 1of10, Creator Hooks Pro, Slack and Hubstaff ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=430s)).

## Timeline

- 2018: first finance videos ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md)).
- 2020: $750 in AdSense in one day. The channel takes off ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=240s)).
- 2023: Paired starts, to build equity in a traditional business ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)).
- 2024-02: over $2.4M/year across six channels ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
