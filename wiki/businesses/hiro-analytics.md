# Hiro Analytics

Hiro Analytics is a reporting and automation platform for email and SMS marketing agencies.

## What it is

- Reporting for agencies that run Klaviyo, Postscript, Attentive and Omnisend for many brands ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=445s)).
- Built first on Snowflake, AWS and Retool. For the last 6 to 8 months it is all built with Claude Code (2026-06) ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=267s)).

## Founders

- [Zac Cherin](../people/zac-cherin.md) and his partner Brendan. They ran an email marketing agency for about 6 years with about 30 clients, all matching Hiro's ideal customer ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=187s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a 30-day free trial. The fee scales with client count: about $500/month for 10 clients, $600/month for 20, a couple thousand for about 200 (2026-06) ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=149s)).
- Seats at about $10/month each ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s)).
- Selling to agencies, not brands: one sale brings 10 to 30 brands and many seats ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s)).

## Metrics

- Over $1M ARR about 19 months after launch (2026-06) ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=58s)).
- About $96.2K MRR, growing close to $10K MRR each month, with very low churn (2026-06) ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=90s)).

## Growth channels

- **Partner directories as lead lists.** Shopify and Klaviyo rank their partner agencies by size and influence ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)). See [Platform marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
- **Pitch on two pains:** more clients without more staff, and time back from manual reports ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=490s)).
