Fight AI is an MMA video-analysis app and the first app George built before his wrestling and boxing apps.

## What it is

- An AI app that analyzes MMA training video and gives feedback. ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=216s))

## Founders

- [George](../people/george.md), a non-technical former wrestler. He built it with Rork to get feedback on his own training. ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=216s))

## Business model

- A paid AI app. The source gives no price for Fight AI itself.

## Metrics

- Posts by George and his friends made about $1,000 at pure margin. ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [4:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=255s))

## Growth channels

- Organic posts by George and friends. ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [4:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=255s))
- Users kept asking for wrestling. That demand led to [Wrestle AI](wrestle-ai.md), a 50/50 partnership with creator [Kaden Henshaw](../people/kaden-henshaw.md). ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=327s))

## Timeline

- About 2025-10: built with Rork. ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=216s))
- 2026-10: George's three later apps, Wrestle AI, [Brawler](brawler.md) and [AbMax](abmaxx.md), make almost $41K MRR. ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s))
