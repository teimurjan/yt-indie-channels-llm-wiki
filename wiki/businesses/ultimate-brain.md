# Ultimate Brain

Ultimate Brain is a premium Notion productivity template that [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md) sells.

## What it is

- A paid Notion template. Thomas also sells [Creator's Companion](creators-companion.md), and a bundle of both ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md)).

## Founders

- [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md), a productivity YouTuber with close to 3M subscribers (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).

## Business model

- One-time digital product. The full bundle is $229. The transcript gives Ultimate Brain alone as "$19", likely $119 (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s)).
- Templates are easy to pirate, so the paid value is gated support in a Circle community ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)).
- Stack: Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record, ConvertKit, Pipedream ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)).

## Metrics

- Launch: about $90K in 30 days from one email to a waitlist of about 3,200 people ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- After a 45-minute walkthrough video, template revenue rose to about $100K/month, later $100K to $120K/month (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=377s)).
- All templates: about $2.5M lifetime sales to about 30,000 customers (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).
- A price increase cut volume a little but raised total revenue ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s)).

## Growth channels

- **Niche YouTube channel.** [Thomas Frank Explains](thomas-frank-explains.md) only teaches Notion, with about 230K subscribers (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=678s)).
- **Email funnel.** Free templates go out first, then an Ultimate Brain offer with a subscriber discount code. Core Notion list 60K to 70K (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)).
- **Long demo video as an ad** ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).

## Timeline

- Launch by email to a waitlist, then a 45-minute walkthrough video lifts sales ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- Thomas did support 8 hours a day for at least a month after launch to learn pain points ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=377s)).
