# NeuroGum

NeuroGum is a brand that sells focus and energy supplements as gum and mints instead of pills.

## What it is

- Nootropic ingredients in gum and mints. The founders saw on a scuba trip that pills are hard to share with strangers ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=208s), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)).

## Founders

- [Ryan Chen](../people/ryan-chen.md) and his college best friend [Kent Yoshimura](../people/kent-yoshimura.md). Ryan was paralyzed in a snowboard accident at 19 ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md)).
- Both kept day jobs at first: Ryan as a financial analyst at Hulu, Kent as a music producer at Sony Music ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)).

## Business model

- Consumer packaged goods sold direct and through retail channels. Ryan calls it a top 10 TikTok Shop brand (2025-02) ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)).
- Bootstrapped with savings and Ryan's cashed-out 401(k), then a $30K Indiegogo campaign ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)).

## Metrics

- Year 1: a little over $100K. Year 2: $750K ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s)).
- Almost $10M/month (2025-02) ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=852s)).
- Employee satisfaction 96% (2025-02) ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=852s)).

## Growth channels

- **Crowdfunding.** Indiegogo hit the $30K goal in 3 to 4 days with help from a Reddit community. Time Magazine and Dr. Oz followed ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)).
- **TV.** Shark Tank gave a step change in growth with no deal ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s)).
- **Earned media and TikTok.** Unpaid mentions from Joe Rogan, Steve Aoki and others, plus repurposed clips, keep going viral ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)).
- **In-house affiliates.** [Dan](../people/dan.md) of [Massive](massive.md) says NeuroGum has almost in-house affiliates on TikTok Shop. Because the product is for everyone, it mostly cares about views, and paid ads may work less well for it (2025-08) ([r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1178s), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1225s)).

## Timeline

- About one year of R&D to find a manufacturer. The first 4,000 lb of gum sat in Kent's LA apartment ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=346s)).
- Ran as a side project for about two years. They went full-time when repeat-buyer revenue showed real adoption ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)).
- 2019: Shark Tank. Two offers below their floor, no deal ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s)).
- A trademark lawsuit nearly bankrupted them. Daniel Lubetzky advised them for 18 months ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=708s)).
- No salary in years 3 and 4. A good salary only from about year 7 ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)).
