Revid is an AI tool that turns video or text into engaging short videos. It is the largest product in [Tibo](../people/tibo.md)'s SaaS portfolio.

## What it is

Revid turns a video or a text into an engaging video. The transcript spells it "Rabbit.ai". [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)

## Founders

Tibo, a French founder who built and sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio. He runs Revid inside a portfolio with [Outrank](outrank.md), [SuperX](superx.md), [PostSyncer](postsyncer.md) and [Feather](feather.md). [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)

## Business model

SaaS subscription. The portfolio has 50K paying customers in total (2025-12). [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)

## Metrics

- About $400K/month, with about 10% growth month over month (2025-12). [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)
- The whole portfolio makes about $700K/month and grew about 20% a month for over 6 months (2025-12). [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s)

## Growth channels

The source gives Tibo's general playbook, not Revid-specific channels:

- Free channels until $10K/month: Product Hunt, social posts and building in public. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=701s)
- Then become a media company and add scalable channels: SEO, ads and affiliates. An ad that works at $1K/month often scales to $10K or $100K. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=762s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=810s)
- Until a product reaches $10K/month, its support link opens Tibo's Twitter DMs. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s)

## Why a portfolio

Tibo has a family. One new OpenAI feature or a platform change can kill one product but not the company. Elon Musk's takeover of X almost killed Tweet Hunter at $200K/month. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=930s), [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=981s)
