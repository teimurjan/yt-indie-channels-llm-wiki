SuperGrow is an AI tool that helps professionals write LinkedIn posts in their own voice.

## What it is

- [B2B SaaS](../topics/b2b-saas.md) for LinkedIn content. It competes with Taplio, ContentIn and AuthoredUp by doing core content creation better ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=100s), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=150s)).

## Founders

- [Deven Bhooshan](../people/deven-bhooshan.md), an indie hacker from India, and a co-founder. Deven built a weekend MVP once GPT-3 could copy a user's writing style ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=210s)).

## Business model

- Started with a [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) on RocketHub: $79, $199 and $299 tiers. RocketHub took 40% ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=288s), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=372s)).
- Now subscriptions. Costs: about $3K/month on marketing and affiliates, $1K+ on freelancers. 60 to 70% profit margin (2025-09) ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=825s)).

## Metrics

- LTD: $65K in 3 days, 250+ buyers, about 10% still active ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=466s)).
- More than $19K/month, $230K/year, 800+ recurring customers, 15,000+ landing page visits a month (2025-09) ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=56s)).

## Growth channels

- RocketHub did the LTD marketing: an email list of hundreds of thousands of buyers, ads and creatives ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=325s)).
- Product Hunt: Product of the Week and 50 customers. 10 to 15 LTD buyers posted about it on launch day ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s)). See [launch strategy](../topics/launch-strategy.md).
- Upsells of LTD buyers to subscriptions ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s)).

## Timeline

- Start: 600 followers on X, about 500 on LinkedIn, 2 to 3 customers ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=232s)).
- RocketHub LTD for 3 days, capped at 300 users: $65K ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s)).
- Product Hunt launch, then subscriptions. 2025-09: about $19K/month ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s)).
