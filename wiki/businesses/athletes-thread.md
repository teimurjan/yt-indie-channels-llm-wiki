Athlete's Thread is a Shopify brand that sells licensed merchandise co-branded with colleges and student athletes.

## What it is

- The newest of four apparel brands from the same founders, launched after the NCAA name, image and likeness (NIL) ruling. The others are [Greek House](greek-house.md), [College Thread](college-thread.md) and [Threadly](threadly.md) [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s).

## Founders

- [Karthik Shanadi](../people/karthik-shanadi.md) and his co-founder Luke. They started the group with $500 in college in 2013 [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s).

## Business model

- Licensed apparel. It needs college licenses plus rights from each athlete; Karthik says 99.99% of companies will not get through that [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=683s).
- No in-house manufacturing. US print partners fulfil orders through a vendor portal the team built [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s).

## Metrics

- About 300 college licenses (2023-04) [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=61s).
- The video frames the four-brand group at $10M, built with no funding (2023-04) [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s).

## Growth channels

- Outbound one-to-one email, then outbound sales [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s).
- Ambassador program: free product for content and promotion [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s).
- Google paid search for high-intent queries such as a player's T-shirt, plus free marketplaces: Etsy and Google Shopping [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s).
- Service as brand: replies in under 4 hours, ships in 2 to 3 business days, custom labels and packaging [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=732s).

## Timeline

- 2013: the group starts as Greek House; $50K in year one, $100K in year two [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s).
- 2016: founders go full time; a $40K licensing audit bill hits at just under $1M in sales [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s).
- After the NIL ruling: Athlete's Thread launches [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s).
