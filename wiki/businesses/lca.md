# LCA

LCA is the innovation agency inside [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md)'s holding company [Late Checkout](late-checkout.md).

## What it is

- An agency that builds new product lines for large brands such as Nike, Dropbox, Shopify and TikTok ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).

## Founders

- [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md), a former WeWork head of product ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s)).

## Business model

- Price on outcome, not hours: about $1.5M/year per client on average, against a typical agency fee of $1,500/month (2024-06) ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).

## Metrics

- Year one: $1.5M revenue from 1.5 clients. Year two: $5M+ (2024-06) ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s)).

## Growth channels

- **Audience, then community, then product.** Greg had about 10,000 Twitter followers at the start. He met first clients at "orange wine disco parties" he promoted to that audience ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s)).

## Timeline

- Started with $0 and about 10,000 Twitter followers ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- Year two: $5M+ revenue (2024-06) ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s)).
