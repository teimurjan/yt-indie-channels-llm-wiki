ListKit is a B2B SaaS that sells verified lead lists for cold email. It reached $1M ARR 87 days after launch.

## What it is

- Verified B2B leads, built as a copy of Apollo with one fix: Apollo's leads were not verified ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=702s)).

## Founders

- [Andre Haykal Jr.](../people/andre-haykal-jr.md), who earlier ran a lead generation agency to $100K/month and the coaching program Client Ascension with Daniel Fazio ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=178s)).
- Oliver, a coaching student, joined as co-founder and shipped the app in six months. There are 5 co-founders in total: CEO, CTO, head of sales and two marketing and ads leads (2024-06) ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)).

## Business model

- Credit-based plans from $97/month up to a few hundred for enterprise ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=462s)).
- No free trial. A money-back guarantee instead, because trials attract "freebie seekers" ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=619s)).
- A done-for-you cold email setup sells next to the tool ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=561s)).

## Metrics

- $1M ARR within 87 days of launch, about to pass $200K MRR (2024-06). The intro calls it a $2.4M company ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)).
- 1,000 paying customers in the first 6 months, over 1,500 after about a year (2024-06) ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s)).
- Team over $100K/month. Total cost with ads $150K to $175K/month. About 40 people, 20 in support and sales (2024-06) ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s), [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=653s)).
- Offshore hires in Ukraine and Lebanon cost $1,000 to $2,000/month, against $5,000 to $8,000 for a US hire ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=653s)).

## Growth channels

- **Warm launch.** A few emails, live calls and tweets that told the full story, failure included ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=415s)).
- **Cold email with 50 free leads** to anyone who replied ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=498s)).
- **Ads.** Above $300 to $400/day, creatives fatigue, so the team tests about 20 a week ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=561s)).
- Affiliates, content and the existing agency and coaching audience ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=415s)).

## Timeline across sources

- First a productized service: customers ordered 100 to 10,000 leads through a form, and the team filled orders by hand within 24 hours. It made $105K to $110K/month, all of which went into developers who never shipped ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=227s), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=357s)).
- Oliver shipped the SaaS in six months. 87 days later: $1M ARR.
- 2024-06: near $200K MRR.
