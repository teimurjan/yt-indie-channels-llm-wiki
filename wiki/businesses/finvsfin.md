FinvsFin is a product review site that compares health and wellness products, mostly telehealth brands, and earns affiliate commissions.

## What it is

- A review site that sends shoppers to brands and earns a commission on each referred sale ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s)).
- The first idea was to compare fintech companies. That gave the name "fin vs fin" ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=120s)).

## Founders

- [Alex Goldberg](../people/alex-goldberg.md) started it in 2018 as a side hustle with a business partner. He later bought out the partner ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=454s)).

## Business model

- Affiliate commissions. After about 6 months of traffic, Alex pitched brands directly. Brands then name the price they pay per customer ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- One partner can go on five sites in the portfolio. That gives economies of scale ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)).

## Metrics

- Over $1M/year in revenue (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=0s)).
- About six affiliate sites, each in a narrower niche (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)).
- Team: outsourced writers, a VA for publishing, a full-time editor, a designer and a web developer (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=689s)).

## Growth channels

- **SEO in a new niche.** Telehealth companies raised $100M Series A rounds, but almost nobody reviewed them. His criteria: new, high margin, with momentum ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=337s)).
- **Long-tail keywords.** Learn high-intent patterns such as "best X for Y" and plug in brands. Write the best page online for each ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=398s)).
- **Paid media funded by partners.** He pitched partners a test spend of "a few thousand" dollars ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=577s)).
- **Risk.** Big publishers such as Forbes and Healthline erode rankings in every niche ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)).

## Timeline

- 2018: started as a side project. Alex wrote the first 60 articles and learned WordPress ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- About 6 months in: significant organic traffic ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- About 1 year in: site income replaced his $150K salary. He went full time after about 2 years ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=242s)).
- 2024-03: over $1M/year. He works on a sale of the business ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=750s)).
