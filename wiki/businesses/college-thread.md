This page collects what the sources say about College Thread, one of four custom apparel brands run by [Karthik Shanadi](../people/karthik-shanadi.md).

## What it is

College Thread makes custom apparel for college departments and organizations. It is one of four brands in one group, next to [Greek House](greek-house.md), [Threadly](threadly.md) and [Athlete's Thread](athletes-thread.md) ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s)).

## Founders

- Karthik Shanadi and his co-founder Luke started the group in college with $500 ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s)).
- Karthik ran it next to full-time jobs at Amazon and Salesforce for 2 to 2.5 years ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=261s)).

## Business model

- The group has no in-house manufacturing. US print partners fulfil orders through a vendor portal that Karthik's team built ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s)).
- It holds about 300 college licenses (2023-04) ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=61s)).

## Metrics

The source gives metrics for the whole group, not for College Thread alone.

- The video frames the group at $10M, built with no funding (2023-04) ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s)).
- Year one did $50K, and year two did $100K ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s)).

## Growth channels

- **In-person sales through a network**. In college they pitched at student organization meetings ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=396s)).
- **Outbound email**, then outbound sales ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)).
- **An ambassador program** that trades free product for content ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)).
- **Paid search and free marketplaces** such as Etsy and Google Shopping ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s)).
- **Service as brand**. They reply in under 4 hours and ship in 2 to 3 business days ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=732s)).
