# Earni.fi

Earni.fi is a web app that shows Ethereum users the airdrops they did not claim. [Dawson Botsford](../people/dawson-botsford.md) built it and sold it to Bankless.

## What it is

- A crypto tool. You enter an address, and it lists unclaimed airdrops. The average user found $750 in unclaimed airdrops over the life of the site (2024-04) ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=90s)).

## Founders

- [Dawson Botsford](../people/dawson-botsford.md), a software engineer at [Uber](uber.md) from 2016, before the IPO. He left, travelled for a year, then built Earni.fi at a month-long hackathon ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=191s)).

## Business model

- Freemium with a paywall. Every airdrop result went behind the paywall after the email strategy built trust ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=455s)).
- Stack: TypeScript full stack with Node.js, React and Next.js ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=609s)).

## Metrics

- Over $1M ARR from 5,000 paid users, plus 250,000+ free users (2024-04) ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=55s)). The video intro claims over $100K/month, which does not match $1M ARR ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=0s)).
- Launch: 10,000 organic signups in 48 hours from one tweet ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)).

## Growth channels

- **A demo video in the launch tweet.** It searched an address with many airdrops. A retweet felt like helping friends ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)).
- **"Anti-email" strategy.** Each email named a matched airdrop and its dollar amount, and nothing else. Open rates were very high ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=455s)).
- **Public call-outs.** "25 Days of Christmas": each day he tagged a person on Twitter with a screenshot of their unclaimed dollars ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=516s)).
- **Crypto conferences** brought more signups ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=516s)).
- **Trust over volume.** Competitors sent many low-value alerts. Earni.fi alerts meant real money, in a space full of phishing ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [9:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=543s)).

## Timeline

- Hackathon: first version in 4 to 5 hours, after 2 to 3 weeks of thinking ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=300s)).
- Launch tweet: 10,000 signups in 48 hours.
- Paywall added. Growth to over $1M ARR.
- After 2 years of solo growth: David Hoffman of Bankless sends a Twitter DM. Bankless buys the company and Dawson becomes CTO ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=652s)).
