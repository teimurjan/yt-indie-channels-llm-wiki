The Birdhouse is a Twitter (X) ghostwriting agency for coaches, consultants and education companies.

## What it is

- A done-for-you Twitter service: tweets, threads, long-form posts, articles, replies, Spaces setup and clips cut from client videos and podcasts [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s).
- It targets niches that turn views into sales fast: coaches, consultants and education companies [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s).

## Founders

- [Marcos Ruiz](../people/marcos-ruiz.md). He started with about $2,000 and spent it all on a $2,000/month course [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=136s).

## Business model

- Monthly retainers. Early clients paid $1,000/month, then $3,000/month [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=196s).
- The offer is revenue, not followers: "If I just make somebody more money than they pay me then I [will] have a client forever" [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=593s).

## Metrics

- $0 to $65K/month in one year (2024-06) [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=0s).
- About $70K/month at about 60% margin. Target $100K/month (2024-06) [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s).
- Team of 5 to 6: two senior writers, one junior writer, an ops manager, a sales rep and Marcos (2024-06) [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s).

## Growth channels

- **Upwork for the first clients,** then those jobs as case studies [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=268s).
- **[Cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md) by DM.** 100 to 200 cold DMs a day with a profile-based compliment, the offer and a low-pressure close, tracked in Notion. Warm DMs go to people who engage [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s).
- **Own Twitter content.** 2 to 3 tweets a day, including a thread. About 10K followers after eight months [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=410s), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=328s).

## Timeline

- About 2023: first two $1,000/month clients, then a $3,000/month coach. One thread likely made that client $20K to $30K [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=196s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=268s).
- 2024-06: about $70K/month, team of 5 to 6 [source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s).
