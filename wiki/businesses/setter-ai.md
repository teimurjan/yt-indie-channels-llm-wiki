Setter AI is an AI appointment setter that follows up with leads and qualifies them for sales teams ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s)).

## What it is

- It targets markets where a sales rep can be replaced: solar, coaches and financial services ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s)).
- Stack includes TypeScript, SvelteKit, Postgres on Render and Supabase, Puppeteer, OpenAI, Webflow and Netlify ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=633s)).

## Founders

- [Josef Buettgen](../people/josef-buettgen.md), a freelance software engineer, and [Timo Nikolai](../people/timo-nikolai.md), who lives in Bangkok. They met at a coworking space. Both had spent months on products with no customers ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=15s)).

## Business model

- B2B subscription from about $50/month. Some customers pay over $500, and the largest pays up to $5,000/month (2025-09) ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s)).
- About 2,000 business signups but only 38 paying customers, by design, because high payers churn less (2025-09) ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).
- Margins: 90%+ without ads, about 80% with ads and freelancers (2025-09) ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=692s)).

## Metrics

- First $500 payment in February 2024, before the product existed ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
- About $10K in revenue last month, with higher months before. August is slow in B2B (2025-09) ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).
- They applied to YC five times and had one interview. The YouTube channel has 600 subscribers. They have acquisition offers (2025-09) ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).

## Growth channels

- **A fake landing page before code.** The page had one H1 keyword, a fake AI caller demo made with ElevenLabs, and a "book demo" link. It sat on an old domain that Google already indexed ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=100s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=163s)).
- **SEO.** "Cold call automation" came from Ahrefs, with volume and no difficulty ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=163s)). The real site ranked on page one within a week ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)).
- **Three SEO plays.** Free tools such as a sales script generator, "HubSpot alternatives" comparison articles, and long-form YouTube. Most closed customers watched at least one video before a call ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)).

## Timeline

- 2024-02: a $500 Stripe payment from the fake page. A company with $1B in revenue booked a call ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
- 2025-09: about $10K MRR and 38 paying customers ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).

## Lessons

- **Validation means a card swipe.** Traffic and booked calls do not count ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=350s)).
- **Take a refundable deposit.** For example $500 for full access at launch and a spot in the first cohort. Go high ticket, not $20 ([source](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=288s)).
