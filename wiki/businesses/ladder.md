Ladder is a strength-training app where each team does the same workout every day. No source in the wiki features Ladder. Founders cite it as a model for retention and brand.

## What it is

- It uses workout videos in onboarding and in the app ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [36:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2203s)).
- About 5,000 strangers per team do the same workout each day. A cohort calendar makes a skipped workout visible to the team ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=585s), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)).
- Coconote's founders give Ladder as an example of a repeat use case tied to identity: gym workouts ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [47:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2848s)).

## Founders

- CEO Greg Stewart ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)).

## Business model

- A subscription fitness app sold through a quiz funnel ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)).

## Metrics

- App of the Year 2025 finalist. Greg Stewart cited 500% growth on the Sub Club podcast (2026-06) ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=585s), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok to a quiz.** The quiz funnel converted users, but the cohort kept them (2026-06) ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s)).
- **A few niche ambassadors.** [Stronger](stronger.md)'s Peter says Ladder, Runna and Represent sell a lifestyle through 3 or 4 strong ambassadors instead of spray-and-pray influencer deals ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [17:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1023s), [19:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1196s)).

## Influence on other apps

- [Cal AI](cal-ai.md) copied groups and leaderboards from Strava and Ladder for retention ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1621s)).
- George, who runs Wrestle AI and [Brawler](brawler.md), put in-app instructional videos and a content feed into his apps "solely because of Ladder". He thinks content in the app drives Ladder's retention ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [35:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2147s), [37:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2238s)).
