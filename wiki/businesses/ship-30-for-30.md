Ship 30 for 30 is a paid cohort writing course for beginners, built around a 30-day writing challenge.

## What it is

- A community writing course where students publish every day for 30 days [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s).

## Founders

- [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md), a former BlackRock trader. He partnered with Nicolas Cole to turn the Slack channel into a full course [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s).

## Business model

- Cohort course. It is one of four writing businesses in Dickie's portfolio, with [Premium Ghostwriting Academy](premium-ghostwriting-academy.md), [Write With AI](write-with-ai.md) and [Typeshare](typeshare.md) [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s).

## Metrics

- First cohort: $50, with a full refund for anyone who finished all 30 days. Relaunched at $99 after 50+ interviews and made $30K in December [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s).
- $1M in 2021 at about 90% margin [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s).
- About $3M/year; the portfolio is on pace for $10M in 2024 (2024-07) [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s).

## Growth channels

- Dickie's own daily writing on Twitter. A day-27 post retweeted by Naval grew his newsletter from 250 to 1,000 subscribers [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s).
- Productize the input: he sold the challenge that produced the attention, not the ghostwriting it led to [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s).
