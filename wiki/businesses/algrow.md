# Algrow

Algrow is a SaaS that helps creators find and copy viral short-form video formats and then generate videos with AI.

## What it is

- Built first for faceless YouTubers. Influencers and drop shippers now use it too ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=157s)).

## Founders

- [Sam Adeniyi](../people/sam-adeniyi.md), a university student with no coding background who vibe coded it. His friend Mason gave him the idea ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=195s)).

## Business model

- Subscription: $25, $45 and $80 per month (2026-01) ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=101s)).
- Monthly costs include Cursor $200, Sora 2 about $200 and Gemini compute $300 to $500 (2026-01) ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=728s)).

## Metrics

- Last four weeks: about $13K to $14K and 480 new customers (2026-01) ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=157s)).
- Over 10,000 users; the first 400 came from Discord (2026-01) ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [0:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=18s)).

## Growth channels

- **Discord servers.** He found niche servers on Disboard and sat muted in voice chats sharing his screen while using the tool. People asked about it, and a server owner made an unpaid YouTube video about it ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=331s)).
- **Help, do not pitch.** He used the tool to find channels for people who asked. Word of mouth followed ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=504s)).

## Timeline

- MVP in about a week, from ChatGPT code pasted into Notepad, then VS Code, then Cursor ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=243s)).
- About six months after launch: over 10,000 users and about $14K in 30 days (2026-01) ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md)).
