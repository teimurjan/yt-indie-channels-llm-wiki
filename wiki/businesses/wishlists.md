# Wishlists

Wishlists is a simple mobile app to store wishes and share them with family.

## What it is

- A wish list app built with Flutter, Firebase and RevenueCat ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=628s)).

## Founders

- [Chris](../people/chris.md), an app developer. Solo, no funding, no paid marketing ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=49s)).

## Business model

- A paywall for premium features, such as custom wish list images. Most money comes from affiliate links: imported Amazon product links become his affiliate links ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=661s)).

## Metrics

- About $150K/year; about $6K/month in the low season, about 5x in the Christmas season (2025-09) ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [9:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=598s)).
- 1.1M registered users, about 4K paying customers, 110K monthly active users in the low season (2025-09) ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [9:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=598s)).
- About 99% margin (2025-09) ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=628s)).

## Growth channels

- **App Store reviews.** He asked everyone he knew for reviews at the start. The in-app review prompt shows after a success moment, such as adding a wish ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=498s)).
- **Close the loop on support.** He emails users when their request ships, and asks for a review after a positive reply ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=559s)).
