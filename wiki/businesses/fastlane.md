Fastlane is an AI tool that makes short-form content for solo founders who build apps and SaaS ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s)).

## What it is

- A Tinder-style swipe UI shows a trending niche post next to a generated version of it. Swipe right to schedule it to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. AI writes the caption, and the post is live in about 5 minutes ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=780s)).
- Stack: Convex, Vercel, Railway, Clerk, Resend and Axiom. OpenAI and Claude write copy, and fal makes images and video. The team codes with Claude Code on the 20x Max plan ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=283s)).

## Founders

- [Gaurav](../people/gaurav.md), from Australia, and his non-technical co-founder Joe ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=542s)).
- Gaurav started building apps after the Starter Story video on [Puff Count](puff-count.md) ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=154s)).

## Business model

- Paid software subscriptions, with over 1,000 paid users (2026-08) ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s)).

## Metrics

- $69K MRR from software about two months after the public launch. Over $1M ARR across the business. It grew from about $16K ARR of paid beta users (2026-08) ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s)).
- About 2,000 customer calls at about 20 a week ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=449s)).

## Growth channels

- **Customer calls as the engine.** A rainbow-bordered button offers 7 days of extra access for a call with the team ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=400s)).
- **Three phases of calls.** First, discovery calls with The Mom Test. Next, usability tests with waitlist signups. Last, interviews with power users ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=623s), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=683s), [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=729s)).
- **A customer intelligence dashboard.** An AI note taker puts calls into Notion, and Claude indexes them. Users who signed up "curious" retained worse than those who said "I need marketing now". A "customer love score" drives the roadmap ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=481s)).
- **Reddit and lead magnets** found usability testers ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=683s)).
