Phantom is a crypto wallet app that [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses as a case study in emotional, motion-led design.

## What it is

- A web3 wallet. Its goal is to onboard the next billion people to web3. ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=410s))
- Tim Gabe names it, with Airbnb and [Duolingo](duolingo.md), as a product that designs a full experience, not static screens. ([86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md), [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=830s))

## Founders

- CEO Brandon Millman calls Phantom "a design-led company". ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=410s))

## Business model

- The sources do not cover how Phantom makes money.

## Metrics

- Number 2 app in the US App Store utilities category, above WhatsApp and Instagram (2025-05). ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=390s))

## Growth channels

- **Design as the edge.** Playful motion makes crypto feel less scary. In high-stakes fields such as finance and crypto, every micro-interaction is a trust signal. ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=471s), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=477s))
- **Approachable for normal people.** Friendly visuals make a heavy topic lighter, and the app does not assume users know the rules. ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=477s))
- Tim Gabe groups it with Duolingo and [Revolut](revolut.md) as apps that use emotional design in the way Apple does. ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=783s))

## Timeline

- Mid-2023: brand refresh. Phantom animated its ghost mascot and added playful motion to wallet creation. ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=335s))
- 2025-05: number 2 in US utilities. ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=390s))
