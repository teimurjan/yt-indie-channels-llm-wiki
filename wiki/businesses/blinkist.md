Blinkist is a subscription app whose paywall trial timeline other app founders copy. The sources only mention it, so this page covers its growth and paywall lessons.

## What it is

- A subscription app with a 7-day free trial [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s). No source in the wiki features Blinkist or its founders.

## Founders

- The sources do not name the founders.
- [Marcus Burke](../people/marcus-burke.md) worked there on growth from 2019 for about 1.5 years [source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md) [0:02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=140s).

## Business model

- Subscriptions behind a free trial. The paywall shows the trial as a day-by-day timeline [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s).

## Metrics

- A visual day-by-day timeline of the 7-day trial raised trial signups 23% [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s).
- A paywall line that states the charge date raised conversion 23%. Complaints fell 55% and churn did not change [source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=462s).

> Conflict: [puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) credits the 23% lift to the visual trial timeline. [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) credits a 23% lift to the explicit charge-date line. Both are from Tim Gabe. It is not clear if they describe one test or two.

## Growth channels

- Paid ads to web content. Marcus Burke's team was among the first to scale ads that sent users to a blog instead of the App Store [source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md) [0:05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=323s).

## Influence on other apps

- Founders copy the "Blinkist-style trial timeline". Quit-smoking app founders pair it with "No payment due now" and a reminder 2 days before the trial ends [source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md) [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1405s).
- Jonathan Parra, after 4,000 paywalls, says the Blinkist trial timeline still performs after years (2026-02) [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=896s).
