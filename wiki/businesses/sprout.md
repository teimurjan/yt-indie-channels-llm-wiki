Sprout is a swipe-to-apply job app for college students, a "Tinder for jobs", built by Nicole and Aaron.

## What it is

- Users swipe to apply for jobs. It was first called PrepearAI. It targets college students for their higher spending power [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=0s), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s).

## Founders

- [Nicole](../people/nicole.md) handles design and distribution. Her co-founder Aaron codes [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=0s).
- They also built [Glam Up](glam-up.md) and two apps in stealth [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=0s).

## Business model

- Subscription, up to $80/month (2026-05). No annual plans. Payment goes through Stripe [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [51:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3085s), [44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2647s).
- A hard paywall right after onboarding lifted conversion by 50%. Before that, users explored the app first [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2401s).

## Metrics

- About $250K MRR 8 months after launch, recorded in October 2025 [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s).
- Steady at about $200K MRR (2026-05) [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=115s), [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [30:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1831s).
- Team of 10 (2026-05) [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [51:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3080s).

## Growth channels

- **Managed UGC creators.** Views were 100M a month under Nicole. Later the team hit 400M views in one week [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [31:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1860s).
- **Train creators.** A vibe-check call, then a Typeform and a video course with a content bank and a quiz per module. Over 50% of Sprout creators who finish the course go viral within 2 weeks [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=578s).
- **Pay per post plus a bonus.** $10 to $30 per post plus a falling CPM bonus, for example $40 at 10K views and $800 at 1M. Creators come from influencer Discords and Handshake [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [47:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2870s), [48:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2931s).
- **Systematize.** A referral program for creators and growth dashboards [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=655s).
