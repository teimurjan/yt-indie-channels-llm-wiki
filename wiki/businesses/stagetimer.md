# Stagetimer

Stagetimer is a remote-controlled countdown timer for live events and video productions.

## What it is

- One person clicks start and other people see the timer. Customers include election TV broadcasts and horse races ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=660s)).
- Stack: JavaScript, Vue.js, Node.js, Airtable as CRM, Postmark ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=549s)).

## Founders

- [Lukas Hermann](../people/lukas-hermann.md), a developer in Germany. His wife, a former teacher, runs Google Ads, sales emails and support ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=189s)).

## Business model

- Freemium SaaS. Freelancers bring the free tier to client events, hit a limit and buy ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)).

## Metrics

- About $25K/month, 20K users, 4,400 paying or past paying customers, 86K unique visitors a month (2025-06) ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=46s)).
- Costs: $280 servers, $250 tools, $1,400 paid ads a month. Margin 80% to 90% (2025-06) ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=591s)).
- Traffic: about 50% from Google, about a third from referrals and word of mouth (2025-06) ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)).

## Growth channels

- **Niche subreddit.** One free post with no price in a video-production subreddit got 58 upvotes and feature lists ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=79s)).
- **Integration search.** Docs and YouTube videos for searches like "countdown timer Stream Deck companion" ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s)).
- **Branded shared links.** Every shared timer shows "stagetimer.io", like Dropbox referrals ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)).
