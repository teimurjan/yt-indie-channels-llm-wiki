useArtemis is a LinkedIn scraping and email-finding tool built by [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md).

## What it is

- A tool that scrapes LinkedIn and finds emails. It competes with Apollo and lemlist ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
- It runs server instances for automation, so it costs more to run than a simple app ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=637s)).

## Founders

- [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md), a former optician who taught himself to code from one 15-hour YouTube course ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=72s)).
- It was his first real app. It broke his own rule to build only simple tools, because it competes with big players and is hard to maintain ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)).

## Business model

- SaaS on Stripe, built with Next.js and Node.js on Vercel ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=589s)). The source gives no prices.

## Metrics

- About $15K/month and 10,000 customers (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
- Running cost about $4K/month (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=637s)).
- It is one of Samuel's three apps, which make about $35K/month together (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=58s)).

## Growth channels

- Samuel's general playbook: ads first to test the market, then SEO, because SEO compounds and adds value at sale time ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=695s)).
- He uses Ahrefs for SEO research and Outrank to write and post blog articles ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=589s)).
