# Double Speed

Double Speed is an a16z Speedrun startup that makes AI slideshow and hook-and-demo videos. It posts them to TikTok from a phone farm of real devices.

## What it is

- AI makes the content and real phones post it. Software on the phones swipes, reposts and comments like a human. Warm-up searches the account's niche terms, and an LLM reads screenshots to decide whether to engage ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s), [26:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1583s)).
- It owns both creation and posting. Zuhair calls the feedback loop "attention intelligence": the system learns which prompt, model and format won ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=304s), [22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1377s)).
- Users can paste a TikTok link to make a template, then vary hooks, images and text ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1211s)).

## Founders

- [Zuhair Lani](../people/zuhair-lani.md). He moved from sneaker botting to hype marketing for his dad's restaurant, then to TikTok Shop and an AI content agency ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md)).

## Business model

- A content and distribution service for brands and apps. The alternative it replaces: college creators at $500 to $1,000/month each for 30 videos, with weak accountability ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=144s)).
- Rule: AI does 95% of the work and a human checks the last 5% ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md)).

## Metrics

- Raised $1M from a16z Speedrun (2025-10) ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [27:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1651s)).
- One client got 4.7M views from 15 accounts in under 4 weeks (2025-10) ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [19:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1149s)).
- Before Double Speed, Zuhair's AI content agency reached $40K/month while he was in college ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [0:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=33s)).

## Growth channels

- The product is itself a growth channel. Formats it uses:
  - **Pain-then-solutions slideshows.** Slide one names the pain, the next slides give hard fixes, and the app appears as the easy fix ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [16:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=962s)).
  - **AI face plus image bank.** A synthetic person from a trained LoRA on slide one. A bank of about 50 images per slot supports about 300 slideshows ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1018s)).
  - **Hook-and-demo without creators.** Stock hooks matched with app screen recordings ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1377s)).
- Formats decay. One strategy works for a few months, a year at most ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s)).

## Timeline

- 2023: Android emulators such as BlueStacks still worked for posting. TikTok device fingerprinting later broke them, so Double Speed uses real phones ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s)).
- First month on TikTok Shop: $100K in sales with AI content only. Then a 3PL failure on 1,000 orders got the shop banned ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [26:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1602s)).
- AI content agency at $40K/month, content for 15 brands while in school ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s)).
- 2025-10: Double Speed, with $1M from a16z Speedrun.
