GlowUp is an AI app that lets women try makeup looks on a photo of themselves. Its founders made viral TikTok content first and built the app after.

## What it is

Users upload a photo and try unlimited AI makeup looks. A long onboarding asks about sensitive skin, wrinkles, preferred looks and skin tone, so results feel personal. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s)

## Founders

[Louis David](../people/louis-david.md), a Canadian developer, and a co-founder. Louis studied software engineering for 2 years, dropped out and built about 10 apps that failed. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=142s)

## Business model

Subscriptions only: $9.99/week or $39.99/year. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s)

## Metrics (2025-12)

- $800K in revenue in about one year. It now makes about $15K/month. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=72s), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s)
- 0 to 100K users in about 3 days. About 2M users and about 70,000 paying customers in total. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=72s), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s)
- #1 in Lifestyle and #4 in top free apps on the App Store. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s)
- About 90% profit margin. The backend costs about $1K/month and AI about $500/month. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=644s)

## Growth channels

- **Content first.** Louis read the comments under big makeup videos to see what people asked for. He posted first and built a fast MVP once the content went viral. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s)
- **Warmed-up US TikTok accounts.** A new iPhone, a US SIM card and a US VPN. Engage in the niche for 15 to 30 minutes a day for about 3 days. Then post once a day, and 4 to 6 times a day after traction. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s)
- **Late anchor links.** The "download now" anchor link drove most downloads. Below about 1,000 views it kills reach, so add it later. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)
- **Many accounts.** At peak, about 7 accounts posted 8 to 12 times a day. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)
- **Referral codes in the comments.** Invite three friends to get a free look. Users posted their codes under the video, which raised engagement. One video got close to 48M views, 60K downloads and about $12.5K in sales in one day. [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=488s)
