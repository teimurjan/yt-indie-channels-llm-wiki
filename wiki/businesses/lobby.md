Lobby is a drop-in video hangout app where friends join calls together. It reached 500K daily active users and #1 on the App Store in five countries with no paid marketing ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=0s)).

## What it is

- A consumer social app built around live group video ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=0s)).
- Its viral feature is the group picture. During a call, a button starts a 3-2-1 countdown and everyone gets a watermarked boomerang to share ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s)).

## Founders

## Metrics

- 500K DAU and #1 in five countries ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=0s)).
- Day-90 retention in Israel was 25% to 30%, about twice other markets ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)).
- The key metric was the share of users with 5 friends on day one: about 60% in Israel and about 20% elsewhere ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s)).

## Growth channels

- **Network density, not content.** Content marketing brings strangers with no friends on the app. Lobby needed friends to invite friends through the product ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s), [22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1338s)).
- **The group-picture loop.** Users share the boomerang to Snapchat and Instagram stories, and friends who missed it join next time. The button lights up every 5 to 10 minutes and prompts again at exit ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s), [25:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1530s)).
- **Temporal density.** A one-minute "roll call" pops up at a random minute each night, like BeReal, so everyone shows up at once ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [26:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1597s)).

## Timeline

- About a year with no growth ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)).
- 2022: viral in Israel, a market of about 7M people, and #1 there ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)).
- About 3 months after the group-picture feature: #1 in the UK, Italy and Germany, then spread to Egypt and Saudi Arabia ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s), [23:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1436s)).
