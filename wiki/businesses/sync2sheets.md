Sync2Sheets is a Google Sheets add-on that syncs Notion databases to Sheets. It does one thing.

## What it is

- A Google Workspace add-on that moves Notion database data into Sheets, with conditional formatting, dropdown validation and support for moved or hidden columns ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=413s)).

## Founders

- [Leandro Zubrezki](../people/leandro-zubrezki.md), a developer in Buenos Aires who left 8 years of freelancing during COVID ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=45s)).

## Business model

- Paid plans only. There is no free plan. Payments run through Paddle because Stripe is not available in Argentina ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=676s), [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s)).
- About 90% margin. The main cost is cloud compute for real-time sync (2025-06) ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=722s)).

## Metrics

- About $9K/month at 4 years old (2025-06) ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=45s)).
- Over 70K users, over 450 paying customers, about 5K visitors a month, and about $120K lifetime revenue (2025-06) ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=69s)).
- Removing the free plan took revenue from about $5K to $8K/month in a few months ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s)).

## Growth channels

- **Google Workspace Marketplace** search brings steady installs ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=542s)).
- **SEO blog posts** about Notion and Google Sheets ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)).
- **Reddit replies** via F5Bot keyword alerts ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)).
- Product Hunt and a Hacker News front page gave traffic and backlinks but few paying users ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=651s)).

## Timeline across sources

- Notion released its official API. Leandro validated demand by searching the Notion subreddit for "sheets", "Excel" and "CSV" ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)).
- Built in 2 weeks with Google Apps Script ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=269s)).
- Product Hunt launch 18 months after the first launch ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=651s)).
- 2025-06: about $9K/month.
