Wrestle AI is an iOS app that acts as an AI wrestling coach and analyzes match videos.

## What it is

- An AI wrestling coach that analyzes match videos ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s)).
- Other features: a photo calorie tracker, practice mode with live coaching, training plans, a match calendar and a weight journal ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [15:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=923s)).
- The calorie tracker for making weight raised retention ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1582s)).

## Founders

- [George Lampropoulos](../people/george-lampropoulos.md), a college freshman, 18, who cannot code (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s)).
- The later video calls him [George](../people/george.md), a non-technical former wrestler ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s)).
- [Kaden Henshaw](../people/kaden-henshaw.md), a wrestling creator and 50/50 partner. George built Fight AI first, and users kept asking for wrestling ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=327s)).

## Business model

- Subscriptions at $9.99/month or $59.99/year. The free trial sits on the yearly plan, so many users buy yearly (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=96s)).
- Later: $59/year or $9.99/month, split about 50/50. The 12-month LTV of a monthly subscriber is about $40. Users are 90% high schoolers (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [22:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1363s), [23:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1436s)).
- Costs: Rork $25/month, Supabase about $30/month, OpenAI inference $40 to $60/month, ChatGPT $20/month (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [16:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=1004s)).

## Metrics

- About $17K/month, about $8K of it from monthly plans (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s)).
- Over 2M social impressions and about 17,000 downloads (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=96s)).
- Over 100,000 downloads on about 10 creators alone (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).
- George's three apps together: almost $41K MRR (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s)).

## Growth channels

- **Partner audience at launch.** About 3,000 to 4,000 pre-orders. The app made over $1,000 on day one ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=1061s)). The pre-order made $8K in the first week ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=503s)).
- **Influencers.** DM 100 people a day and open with "Paid promo?". Pay 20% to 50% up front for 4 to 5 videos. Add a view guarantee at a $2 to $5 CPM ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=756s), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=812s)).
- **Empty niche.** No other wrestling app existed, so creators had no sponsorship fatigue ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).
- **Word of mouth at matches.** In January, teammates at matches drove a quarter to a half of revenue ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [15:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=957s)).

## Timeline

- Build: one week of prompting Rork, one week for a $250 Fiverr developer to add payments and auth, two weeks of Apple rejections ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=220s)).
- Launch: the AI API went down 30 minutes before launch. The app still reached a top-20 rank ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=1061s)).
- Month two: the partner audience was saturated, so he spent about $500 on more influencers ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=685s)).
- 2025-12: about $17K/month. 2026-10: over 100,000 downloads.

> Conflict: [8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md) gives the launch rank as #19 on the App Store and also as #18. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) says #19 in Sports.
