Noise is a creator platform where many people post an app's short-form content and get paid per view.

## What it is

- Up to 50,000 people post an app's TikTok content and get paid per view. It began as an internal growth hack for Playbite, an earlier app [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=0s).
- Founders upload hook images and captions. Noise builds templates, gives them to creators, tracks attribution and pays out [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:41:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2462s).

## Founders

- [Nic Weber](../people/nic-weber.md) co-founded it [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=0s).

## Business model

- Brands set the CPM they pay creators, usually from their target cost per install [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=647s).
- Noise keeps half of the CPM. Kyle Fowler paid about $2,600 for about 7,600 posts in a month, about $1 CPM, and Noise earned about $1,300 of it (2025-11) [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:42:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2558s), [0:51:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=3107s).
- Creators run on a capped CPM model, which suits fast tests and scaling [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [34:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2040s).
- The usual alternative is to pay one creator $200 to $500/month for 20 slideshows [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [29:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1792s).

## Metrics

- Over 2.5B views for founders on Noise in about one year. One video had 22M views in 4 days (2026-01) [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=212s).
- At least 100,000 posts a day, most at 500 to 2,000 views each (2026-01) [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=212s).
- Noise's own app: installs at about $0.30, one format at $0.08 for 3 days, and #5 and #7 in its App Store category (2026-01) [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=865s), [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=803s).
- Noise's own dashboard: 54M views in 30 days, of which slideshows gave only 2M (2026-01) [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1425s).

## Growth channels

- Its own platform. Super-implicit posts name the app only in the description, and seeded comments say Noise paid for the thing in the video. One seeded comment got about 1,686 likes [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1102s).
- Two prongs: premium UGC with a soft call to action hunts for hits, and cheap slideshows with a hard call to action work like retargeting [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [25:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1538s).

## Customers

- [Cardstock](cardstock.md) and [Scanemon](scanemon.md) scale TikTok slideshows through Noise (2025-11) [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:41:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2462s).
- [Pingo](pingo.md) uses Noise creators next to its own creator deals (2026-04) [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [34:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2040s). A Pingo format reached 20M views overnight [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=681s).

## Timeline

- July 2024: start. One slideshow did 20,000 views in a week. Then 30 to 35 Playbite users posted Canva slideshows for pay [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=527s).
- 2025-11: Cardstock runs about 7,600 posts a month on Noise [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:42:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2558s).
- 2026-01: over 2.5B views in its first year [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=212s).
