Bags is a crypto trading app with a token launchpad, built by Hunter Isaacson, the maker of NGL.

## What it is

- A crypto app for trading, plus a launchpad where creators launch tokens ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1367s), [23:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1395s)).

## Founders

- [Hunter Isaacson](../people/hunter-isaacson.md). He also built [NGL](ngl.md), which had 150 million monthly active users (2025-07), and Wink ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=79s)).

## Business model

- Bags charges a 1% fee on trades and passes most of it to the user who invited the trader ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1367s)).
- The launchpad pays token creators a 1% royalty on every trade. For example, $2M of volume pays the creator $20K ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [23:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1395s)).

## Metrics

The source gives no revenue, volume or user numbers for Bags.

## Growth channels

- **Invites built into the fee.** The inviter earns from each trade, so users have a reason to recruit others ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1367s)).
- **Creator royalties.** Creators who launch tokens earn from trading volume, so they promote their tokens ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [23:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1395s)).
- Hunter's general rule: add invites to every product, because a shared link works as an endorsement ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1633s)).
