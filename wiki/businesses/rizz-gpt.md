# Rizz GPT

Rizz GPT is an AI dating app for iPhone, the first hit app of [Blake Anderson](../people/blake-anderson.md).

## What it is

- An AI app in the dating category. Blake launched it in July 2023, after he taught himself to code with ChatGPT ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s)).
- It started a wave of AI dating apps. [YourMove AI](yourmove-ai.md) began about a year before "the RizzGPT wave" ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=239s)).

## Founders

- [Blake Anderson](../people/blake-anderson.md). He later left Rizz GPT and co-founded [Umax](umax.md) and [Cal AI](cal-ai.md) ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md)).

## Business model

- An iPhone subscription app. Blake keeps subscription prices low, because low prices bring more users, more word of mouth and better sentiment ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)).
- He tests weekly, monthly and yearly offers and paywall placements in Superwall. This can lift revenue per download 1.5x to 2x ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)).
- AI costs are under 3% of revenue across his apps ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).

## Metrics

- Launch: 45,000 downloads on day one, 200,000 in the first week and 500,000 in the first month (2023-07) ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s)).
- Started at $80K MRR, peaked a little over $250K MRR, then settled at $150K to $200K MRR (2024-12) ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=338s)).
- About $2.5M per year (2024-12) ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=69s)).

## Growth channels

- **Two $50 creators.** Blake paid two small creators $50 each. Their posts got 5 to 10M views ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s)).
- **Persistent creator outreach.** He found a Discord link in a creator's bio. He sent a message every 10 minutes until the creator replied ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=698s)).
- **Influencers plus in-house UGC.** Slideshows, faceless video and talking heads on app-branded accounts ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=640s)).
- **Category limit.** [Daniel Heintzman](../people/daniel-heintzman.md) says few dating creators promote apps, because they sell their own coaching ([ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=757s)).

## Timeline
