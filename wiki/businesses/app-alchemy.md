App Alchemy is an AI tool that makes mobile app designs, "like Cursor but for designing mobile apps".

## What it is

- AI-generated mobile app designs [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=0s).
- The idea came from the founder's own problem. An Upwork designer charged hundreds of dollars and extra for each small change [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=90s).

## Founders

- [Diego](../people/diego.md), solo. He had about 70 followers on X when he first messaged Pat, and fewer than 100 at recording (2025-07) [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=0s), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=266s).

## Business model

- Paid SaaS. Costs: Cursor $20/month, Vercel about $40/month and AI APIs about $2.5K/month, for about a 70% margin (2025-07) [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=633s).
- Stack: Cursor, Next.js, React and Firebase [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=613s).

## Metrics

- About $17K/month, 4 months after launch (2025-07) [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=55s).
- Over 1M product views from Reddit and $0 spent on marketing (2025-07) [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=55s).
- Over 1K paying customers, about 20K signups from Reddit and about 20K visits per month (2025-07) [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=248s).

## Growth channels

- **Reddit only** [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=326s), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=557s):
  1. Warm up the account with daily use.
  2. List many subreddits with the Reddit Ads targeting search, without buying ads.
  3. Write a value-first post with a catchy headline. His best post was a case study of a successful app. Put the link at the end.
  4. Cross-post at volume: 10 posts at about 10K views each give about 100K views.
  5. Repeat 2 to 3 times a week and vary the format.
- **Plug a complementary product.** Mentioning Cursor next to App Alchemy makes the post look less like an ad [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=496s).
