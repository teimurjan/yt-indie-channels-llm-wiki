Puff Count is a quit-vaping iPhone app by Steven Cravotta. It grew on organic TikTok to about $44K MRR and was sold to a European app studio.

## What it is

- An app that helps people quit vaping. Its founder calls vaping a painful problem with many sufferers and a topic that goes viral ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=115s)).

## Founders

- [Steven Cravotta](../people/steven-cravotta.md). He does not code or design: design comes from 99designs, development from Eastern European Upwork developers paid per finished project ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=291s), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=344s)).
- His apps have over 12M downloads in total. His earlier word games made money from ads (2024-09) ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s)).

## Business model

- Hard paywall with a free trial after a long onboarding quiz. The switch "changed my business overnight" and lifted conversion to 20% to 25% (2024-09) ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=547s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=648s)).
- Prices tested from $4 to $12. He kept the one with the highest LTV ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=617s)).
- Stack: Superwall, RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, free Mixpanel or Amplitude ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=687s)).

## Metrics

- $43K in the last 30 days and just over $112K in the last 90 days (2024-09) ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s)).
- $44K MRR before the exit (2025-09) ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=78s)).
- Organic TikTok: 120,000 followers, over 50M views and hundreds of thousands of installs for $0 (2025-09) ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=512s)).
- Paid ads: $82K total spend took MRR from $3K to $44K. CAC $20 to $24, LTV $55 to $70+, so $1 returned about $2. Cost per click 3 to 35 cents from day one ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)).

## Growth channels

- **TikTok research.** He scrolled for 7 days before his first post and logged each viral video's hook, storyline and CTA in a spreadsheet ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=211s), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=435s)).
- **Copied formats.** A "take apart a vape" format with 20M+ views. A vape dunked in water. One video with 8.3M views drove tens of thousands of downloads ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=430s), [hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=257s)).
- **Volume, and track installs not views.** A video with 11.6M views and about 1M likes drove almost no installs ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=663s), [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=775s)).
- **Organic winners as paid ads**, mostly on TikTok, optimizing from views up to subscriptions as data builds ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [18:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1088s), [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1148s)).
- **Inspired others.** [Gaurav](../people/gaurav.md) started building apps after the Starter Story video on Puff Count, and later built [Fastlane](fastlane.md) ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=154s)).

## Timeline

- About 2020: launch. No money in the first 4 to 6 months ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=245s), [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=813s)).
- Early 2024: hard paywall. Decent money only in the 6 to 7 months before 2024-09 ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=813s)).
- 2024-09: about $40K/month ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s)).
- By 2025-09: $44K MRR and sold to a larger European app studio. Steven moves on to [Posted](posted.md) ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=78s)).
