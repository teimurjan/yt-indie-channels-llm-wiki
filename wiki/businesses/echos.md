Echos is Clover Labs' tool that clones competitor slideshows and videos and posts variants across many accounts.

## What it is

- It clones slideshows and videos and splits videos into hook and demo ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [16:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1009s)).
- It posts variants across many accounts in 20 countries ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s)).
- It scrapes a database of about 4M TikTok and Instagram accounts ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=545s)).
- A clone takes about 1 to 1.5 minutes per image. A full set takes 6 to 7 minutes ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=606s)).

## Founders

- Built by [Clover Labs](clover-labs.md), which [Matt Espinoza](../people/matt-espinoza.md) runs ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s)).

## Business model

- The source does not give Echos pricing. Clover uses it for client campaigns ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s)).
- Clover Labs went from $0 to $8M a year in six months (2026-09) ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s)).

## Metrics

- A clone gets a median of about 20% of the original's views. Five to ten clones match the original ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s)).
- A cloned trucker slideshow got 34K views in about 10 days on one account. The original had 110K ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=268s)).
- The Natural camera app got about 100K views and about 200 signups in 5 days from clones of Dazz Cam posts (2026-09) ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=405s)).
- Each new country adds about a 20% view lift ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s)).
- Clover drives about 250M views a month for clients (2026-09) ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s)).

## How it is used

- **Clone with small changes.** Change the hook, CTA, avatar, color or background, so the platform does not flag a duplicate ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=346s)).
- **Hook and stitch.** AI splits a video between the 2-second hook and the demo. Keep the proven hook and attach your own demo ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1069s)).
- **Evergreen campaigns.** Take the top 10 of 60 posts and make 3 variants of each ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [20:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1221s)).
- **Dub and lip-sync.** AI translates the speech and syncs the mouth. Matt rates it 98% of the way there ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [24:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1492s)).
