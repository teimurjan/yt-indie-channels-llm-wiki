Clover Labs is a Canadian organic distribution company that clones winning short-form content across many accounts and countries for app clients.

## What it is

- An agency plus tooling. Its tool [Echos](echos.md) clones competitor slideshows and videos, splits videos into hook and demo, and posts variants across accounts in 20 countries [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=32s), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=545s).
- Clients include OpusClip, Superwall and Typing.com [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s).

## Founders

- [Matt Espinoza](../people/matt-espinoza.md) [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s).

## Business model

- Organic distribution service for apps: short video, AI search (Reddit and blog citations), agentic paid ads and B2B outreach [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s).
- Creator pay: base plus performance, for example $3 per post plus a $3.50 CPM [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [28:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1687s).

## Metrics

- $0 to $8M a year in six months; about 250M views a month; the company is a year and three weeks old (2026-09) [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s), [39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s).
- A clone gets a median of about 20% of the original's views; 5 to 10 clones match the original [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s).
- Each new country adds about a 20% view lift [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s).
- About 150M views across clients from Reddit and blog content for AI search; it controls about 4,000 subreddits (2026-09) [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [29:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1746s), [37:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2227s).

## Growth channels and methods

- 90% cloned content, 10% new experiments [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=32s).
- Evergreen campaigns: posts over a view threshold get new hooks and demos and go to other accounts [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [18:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1119s).
- AI dubbing and lip-sync to expand by country; best markets Spain, Germany and Australia [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=815s), [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=933s).
- Post and comment seeding on Reddit threads that LLMs cite [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [32:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1930s).
