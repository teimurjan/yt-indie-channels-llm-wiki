Halo AI is an AI photo editor built on the Gemini API that grew to $300K MRR in 45 days through mass UGC.

## What it is

- An AI photo editor on the Gemini API. Users browse templates and submit edit prompts ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [42:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2559s)).

## Founders

- [Dillion Verma](../people/dillion-verma.md) and his partner. It was Dillion's first consumer app, after 18 months of failed products ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md)).

## Business model

- A 3-page free-trial paywall at $8.99/week. Weekly is the main plan ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [40:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2440s)).
- A skippable onboarding paywall and a gated paywall at the first edit both convert at 16.5% ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2501s)).

## Metrics

- $300K MRR in 45 days (2026-03) ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=0s)).
- 0 to 600M views in 60 days and 1.2B views in 120 days ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=27s)).
- A format converts at a steady 0.3% to 0.5% of viewers ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [6:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=383s)).
- About 300 posts a day. About 5% of videos pass 100K views and about 1% pass 1M (2026-03) ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [24:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1471s)).
- Early UGC ROAS was 5:1 to 6:1. Margin is about 50% (2026-03) ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [50:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3039s), [52:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3123s)).

## Growth channels

- **Mass UGC with one format.** 85 creators post across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook. The hook is an absurd AI image. A big Halo AI loading screen follows as a soft CTA. A text-message story with a twist ends it ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1212s), [22:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1335s)).
- **Views as math.** Total views = creators x videos per creator x platforms x views per post ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=591s)).
- **Creator hiring.** Posts in UGC Reddit and Facebook groups brought about 600 applicants. A 5-day paid trial costs $100 per creator ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=695s)).
- **Creator pay.** $20 base per video plus bonuses of $60, $200, $500 and $800 at 20K, 100K, 500K and 1M views. That is under $1 CPM in effect ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [35:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2156s)).
- **Copy every hit.** An automation sends each viral video to all creators, who remake it the same day ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [25:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1551s)).
- **AI coach in Discord.** An OpenClaw agent knows every 1M+ view video and trending keywords, and creators ask it for scripts ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1663s)).

## Timeline

- Mid to late October 2025: live after 2 to 3 weeks of building ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=203s)).
- One creator TikTok returned about 6x on cost. Dillion took a $100K personal loan to pay more creators and ran at break-even for 4 to 5 weeks ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [51:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3062s)).
- 45 days in: $300K MRR. Creators grew from about 10 to 25 to 85 (2026-03) ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=300s)).
- Goal: $1M/month by June 2026 with paid ads ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [52:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3123s)).
