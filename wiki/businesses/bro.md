Bro is an AI companion app that floats over your texts and lock screen. It hit #1 on the US App Store off TikTok.

## What it is

- An AI friend that sits over iMessage and other apps. Its wedge started with texting, then dating apps, because "always with you" was too vague ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=212s)).
- The founder's frame: use AI to create context for human talk, not content ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [30:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1846s)).

## Founders

- [Roger Chen](../people/roger-chen.md), who also built [Lobby](lobby.md), with co-founder Leo, who built the ProtoPie prototypes ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=348s)).

## Metrics

- A brand-account mockup video got 7.9M views. Creator videos got 2M to 3M views ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=335s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2171s)).
- About 50 TikTok creators at peak, falling to 10 to 20 as many stopped posting (2025-12) ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [34:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2052s)).

## Growth channels

- **Ads on mockups before launch.** TikTok ads on clickable mockup videos tested demand and found a working format. A few thousand dollars in ads is cheaper feedback than a 2 to 3 month UGC program ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=531s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=596s)).
- **Story first, demo inside.** The best video showed an autocorrect "I love you" to a crush, with Bro floating over iMessage ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2171s)).
- **Creators found by hashtag** such as #relationship, then emailed ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [34:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2052s)).
