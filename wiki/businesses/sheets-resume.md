This page collects what the sources say about Sheets Resume, an AI resume builder.

## What it is

Sheets Resume is an AI resume builder trained on its founder's own resume rules. A review that took him about 3 hours now runs hundreds of times a day ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=124s)).

## Founders

- [Colin McIntosh](../people/colin-mcintosh.md) is a former recruiter and the founder of [Sheets & Giggles](sheets-and-giggles.md). Sheets Resume is his side project ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=0s)).
- Nate, his former recruiting partner and a software engineer at Eventbrite, built it in about 4 months. A third partner came from Reddit, and equity is split three ways ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=343s)), ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=482s)).
- Colin spends at most 5 to 10 hours a week on it ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=482s)).

## Business model

- $99 for lifetime access or $29 for one week. There is no monthly plan (2025-01) ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=68s)).
- Colin calls monthly plans for job seekers predatory ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=551s)).
- The stack is OpenAI and Gemini, a PHP Laravel API, AWS, Gmail, Stripe and Mercury ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=707s)).

## Metrics

- About $20K in sales in its second month (2025-01). Pat Walls frames it as about $250K a year ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=374s)).
- Colin estimates about 90% net margin ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=767s)).
- The first $1,000 day came within one week of launch ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=343s)).

## Growth channels

- **A Reddit guide**. Colin's 2018 resume guide became the top Google result for "resume advice Reddit". It drew about 20,000 questions over 6 years ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=222s)).
- **A free launch inside that audience**. He edited the tool into his Reddit post. Thousands of free resumes drove word of mouth before pricing ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=551s)).
- **Channels that run without him**. An affiliate agency, basic Google Ads and Reddit posts that rank on Google ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=624s)).
- In those Reddit posts, keep the product link low so readers trust the content ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=687s)).

## Timeline

- 2018-02: Colin posts the Reddit resume guide ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=222s)).
- Before the app: he sells manual resume reviews. The price goes from a couple hundred dollars to $300, then $400 ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=283s)).
- 2024-08-26: Launch for free. Pricing turns on about a week later ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=343s)).
- Month 2: About $20K in sales ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=374s)).
