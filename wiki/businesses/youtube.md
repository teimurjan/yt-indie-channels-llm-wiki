## What it is

YouTube is the video platform. In this source, [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) reviews a YouTube page about creators as one of six websites for design ideas ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=392s)).

## The creators page

- The page shows creator cards, for example MrBeast, with video backgrounds and a hover glow ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=392s)).
- It has a carousel and zoom transitions into story "worlds" ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=454s)).
- On mobile, a touch triggers the hover effect ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=454s)).
- Tim warns that heavy animated sites like this can fail on older phones and slow internet ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=516s)).
