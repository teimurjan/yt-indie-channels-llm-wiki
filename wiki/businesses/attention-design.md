Attention Design is an inspiration library for YouTube creators, one of two products in Gaurabh Mathure's Starter Story Challenge.

## What it is

- An inspiration library for YouTube creators ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=100s)).

## Founders

- [Gaurabh Mathure](../people/gaurabh-mathure.md), a product designer who built 72 apps in 60 days and made $0 before the challenge (2026-08) ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=65s)).

## Business model

- Attention Design Pro sells at $99 ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s)).

## Metrics

- Days 1 to 30: one sale at $99 ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s)).
- After 60 days: 5 Pro customers at $99. Most of the founder's ~$6,500 came from sister product [VueMagnet](vuemagnet.md) (2026-08) ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=629s)).

## Growth channels

- Daily posts on X, LinkedIn, Reddit and YouTube. LinkedIn gave the signal, and vlog-style video posts got the most impressions ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s)).
- Cold outreach with free mock-ups and personalized demos ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=550s)).

## Timeline

- Day 30: $99. Day 60: 5 customers (2026-08) ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s)).
