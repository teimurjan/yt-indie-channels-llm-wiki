# Gojiberry AI

Gojiberry AI is a B2B tool that finds high-intent leads on LinkedIn and runs AI-personalized outreach to them.

## What it is

- A lead-finding and outreach SaaS. It surfaces high-intent leads on LinkedIn and writes personalized outreach with AI ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md)).

## Founders

- Co-founded by [Roman Czerny](../people/roman-czerny.md). He was a mechanical engineer, then worked in affiliate marketing and freelancing. In 2023 he launched Coco AI, grew it to $50K MRR with outreach and sold it to a US company ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=192s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with three plans. Most users are on the $99/month plan with unlimited high-intent leads (2026-01) ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=133s)).
- Marketing tools per month: Instantly about $800, Sales Navigator $100, Outrank $99, Framer $49, Calendly $20, ChatGPT plus Nano Banana about $40 (2026-01) ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=767s)).

## Metrics

- About $30K MRR about 4 months after $0, with a dashboard that shows about $24K. The video title says $34K/month (2026-01) ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=94s), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=133s)).
- Reddit: over 11M impressions, over 40K website visitors and about the first 100 customers (2026-01) ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=237s)).

## Growth channels

- **Reddit story posts with proof.** "I paid 5 influencers on LinkedIn to promote my SaaS. Here's what $1,250 got me" got 160K views and 10 to 15 clients. A post about failing the Y Combinator interview got over 179K views and about 15 clients ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=276s), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=320s)).
- **Reddit rules.** No product link in the post, only in the bio. Warm up an account for 7 to 14 days. Get 10 upvotes in the first 10 minutes from a group of marketers. Reply to every comment ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=409s), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=477s), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=526s), [9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=587s)).
- **Reddit helps sales.** Leads on demo calls say "you are the Reddit guy" ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=237s)).
- **Cold email** at volume through Instantly ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=767s)).
- Pat Walls's caveat: Reddit gets the first 10 to 100 customers, not the first 1,000 ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=941s)).
