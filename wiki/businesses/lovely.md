Lovely is a couples app by solo founder Jack Friks, with a love-note widget, that got 100K downloads in 10 weeks.

## What it is

- A couples app built around a love-note home screen widget. Couples apps monetize well because the partner pays and shared use drives word of mouth ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [42:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=2571s)).

## Founders

- [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md), solo founder of [Curiosity Quench](curiosity-quench.md), [Post Bridge](post-bridge.md) and [Doof](doof.md) ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md)).

## Business model

- Started with a hard paywall, then switched to freemium after bad reviews ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1730s)).

## Metrics

- 102K users, about $10K proceeds and about $4.2K MRR, against a public goal of $5K MRR in 10 weeks (2025-12) ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [31:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1875s)).
- Twitter and news coverage added about $2K to $3K MRR ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1730s)).

## Growth channels

- **[Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) launch tweet.** A public $0 to $5K MRR goal, quote-tweeted about 6 times with updates ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [26:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1571s)).
- **Luck plus amplification.** Russian news coverage brought 10K downloads in one day. A user tweet that showed the widget used as a grocery list got 16M views, and Jack's quote tweet brought 40K downloads overnight ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [27:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1627s), [28:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1689s)).
- **App-in-use TikTok.** He copied a competitor's whiteboard format, built the feature in a day and posted the next day. An 8 to 10 second clip of the app in use converts about 20x better than meme slideshows ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s), [30:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1813s)).

## Timeline

- Launch with a public goal on Twitter ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [26:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1571s)).
- News and viral tweet spikes. Switch from hard paywall to freemium ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1730s)).
- 2025-12: 102K users and about $4.2K MRR after 10 weeks ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [31:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1875s)).
