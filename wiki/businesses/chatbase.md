Chatbase lets a business build a ChatGPT-style chatbot trained on its own data.

## What it is

- A chatbot builder on a company's own data. The first core feature was "upload a PDF and chat with it" [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=456s).
- People had demoed "ChatGPT for your data", but no one sold it yet [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s).

## Founders

- [Yasser Elsaid](../people/yasser-elsaid.md), a solo founder with no funding. He launched in his final year of university and dropped out at two or three sales a day [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s).

## Business model

- [SaaS](../topics/saas.md) subscriptions from $40 to $500/month, with [product-led](../topics/b2b-saas.md) growth and no sales team (2025-07) [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=431s).

## Metrics

- $0 to $1M ARR in 117 days [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=322s).
- $6.8M ARR, 10,000 paying customers and about 600,000 registered users (2025-07). He says "7 million" in the intro [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=431s), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=43s).

## Growth channels

- **A viral launch tweet** to 16 followers. It had a familiar streaming interface, an aha moment in about 20 seconds, and tags for LangChain and Pinecone, which reposted it [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=193s).
- **[Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) daily** and frame each feature as a new launch [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=210s), [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s).
- **Free chatbots for traffic** on books and famous people, run at a loss for visits and domain authority [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s).
- **Sponsored posts.** One post on a large LinkedIn page brought about $4,000 in one day (2025-07) [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=393s).
- **Affiliates** through Dub [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=570s).

## Timeline

- Built ahead of the ChatGPT API. On the API launch day he tweeted it as new [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=560s).
- About early 2023: launch, about 2.5 years before the interview [source](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=43s).
