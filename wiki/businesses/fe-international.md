# FE International

FE International is an M&A firm that brokers the sale of online businesses.

## What it is

- A broker for websites, SaaS and other online businesses. It has sold over $1B of businesses, with eight-figure deals now routine (2023-04) ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=0s), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=589s)).

## Founders

- [Thomas Smale](../people/thomas-smale.md). He flipped websites in college on his credit card, then wrote a book and course about it. Partner Ismail joined from a large investment bank ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=49s), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=257s)).

## Business model

- Broker fees start around 15% on small deals and fall as deal size grows (2023-04) ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=685s)).
- Small online businesses usually sell at 4x to 10x annual profit (2023-04) ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=717s)).

## Metrics

- Over $1B in businesses sold (2023-04) ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=0s)).
- Early on, the biggest deal was $30K ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=257s)).

## Growth channels

- **Book and course readers** asked Thomas to sell their businesses ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md)).
- **Referrals.** The first $10M+ sale came from a past client's referral ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=641s)).

## Timeline

- About 2008 to 2009: Thomas buys a website for about $100 and sells it for about $500 within a month ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=49s)).
- About 2010: founded as he graduated. The course made about $30K and paid the rent ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=109s)).
- From 2012: growth, after a partner showed him 80% of his time went to the parts that made 20% of the money ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=317s), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=341s)).
- 2023-04: over $1B sold ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=0s)).
