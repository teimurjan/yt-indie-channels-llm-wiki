Hush Blankets is a Toronto weighted and cooling blanket brand that went from $0 to $48M in sales in 48 months and was acquired in 2021.

## What it is

- Weighted blankets, then a cooling blanket, pillows, sheets and a mattress, each from customer calls ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=530s)).

## Founders

- [Aaron Spivak](../people/aaron-spivak.md) and [Leor](../people/leor.md), who started it while both had other jobs ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=68s)).

## Business model

- Direct-to-consumer ecommerce, launched on a pre-order site ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).

## Metrics

- Early revenue $30K, $60K, then $90K/month (2018). In July, sales fell to zero or one a day ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
- Kickstarter: $25K goal, over $1M in 30 days (2019), a top-10 Canadian Kickstarter ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)).
- 3,000 pillows in 72 hours to the email list. $20M sales in 2020, a $40M goal for 2021, $1.5M from the mattress launch ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=467s), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=530s)).
- $0 to $48M in total sales in 48 months ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=597s)).

## Growth channels

- **Search demand plus Google Ads.** About 300,000 people would search "weighted blankets". They bought that expensive keyword for a pre-order site ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=68s), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
- **Kickstarter** seeded by 3,000 customers who said on the phone they would buy ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)).
- **Dragon's Den** for mass reach. The dragons raised the valuation on live TV ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=426s)).
- **Email list and founder stories.** Customers asked the founders to sign their blankets ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=657s)).
- **Pop-up store:** 1,500 opening-day visitors against 700 at a competitor ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=597s)).

## Timeline across sources

- 2018-01: launch. 2018-07: sales collapse because no one planned for summer ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=191s)).
- 2018-08: calls to every customer via Calendly. All said the blanket was too hot ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=297s)).
- The first roll of "ice fabric" cost $100,000 when they had $4,000 left ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=357s)).
- 2019: Kickstarter raises over $1M.
- 2021-10: Canada's largest sleep company acquires Hush ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=597s)).
