Vugola is an AI video editing agent that turns long videos into short clips.

## What it is

- An AI clipping tool for clippers, content creators, agencies and long-form brands ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=91s)).

## Founders

- [Vadim Strizheus](../people/vadim-strizheus.md), 19, with no coding experience. He worked at a marketing startup from age 17 and quit to build ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=179s)).

## Business model

- SaaS subscription. Stack: Codex, GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, Cloudflare, PostHog, DataFast and AWS for rendering ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=331s)).
- He built it by prompting [Lovable](lovable.md) for 4 months, then moved to Claude and Codex with GitHub and Supabase to cut cost ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=259s)).

## Metrics

- About $11.2K MRR over the last 4 weeks, tens of thousands of signups and about 400 paying users (2026-10) ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=91s)).
- Instagram UGC account: 10.8K followers in a few months. Top videos at 132K, 21K and 18K views (2026-10) ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=478s)).

## Growth channels

- **X (Twitter).** Posts tied to viral AI topics, such as an OpenClaw plugin, took it to $2K to $3K MRR ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s)).
- **Instagram UGC.** A new account with ManyChat ($15/month, the only paid growth tool). He copies a competitor's format exactly, posts 3 to 6 times a day and changes only the hook and sound. It takes about 45 minutes a day ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=418s), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=478s), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=554s)).
