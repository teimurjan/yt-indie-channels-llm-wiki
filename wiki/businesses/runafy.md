Runafy is a ranked running app. Its founder sold it at about 5x ARR after only 26 days of revenue.

## What it is

- A running app that ranks users' runs on a leaderboard. GPS makes ranks hard to cheat. It copies the format of Liftoff, a ranked gym app, into an adjacent niche ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [22:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1346s)).

## Founders

- [Caleb Dean](../people/caleb-dean.md), with a CTO. Caleb was earlier CMO at [Blake Anderson](../people/blake-anderson.md)'s 10X, hired within 48 hours from a Notion pitch ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=880s)).

## Business model

- Subscription with a 7-day trial ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=16s)).
- Onboarding sells the outcome. Conversion rose after the pitch moved from "fun ranks" to "become a better runner" ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [33:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2027s)).

## Metrics

- Validation: under 50 copied videos in 2 weeks gave 2,000 waitlist sign-ups and 90+ payments of $5 ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1472s)).
- Pre-orders gave 3,000 downloads in about 2 weeks ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=536s)).
- About 5M Instagram views in the first month. About 90% of traffic organic ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=102s), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=479s)).
- At sale: about $3K MRR estimated, valued at about 4.5x to 5x ARR, a six-figure sale (2026-03) ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [46:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2797s)).

## Growth channels

- **Instagram Reels at volume.** 9 Reels a day. An internal tool made 10,000 variations of a format in about a minute. Instagram did not punish volume. TikTok did ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [28:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1732s), [29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1796s)).
- **App Store pre-orders.** A bare 2-tab v1 got approval and opened pre-orders. On release the app installed itself, and the leaderboard filled to 1,000+ within an hour ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=479s), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=536s)).
- **Launch email** with a 50% referral code ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [41:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2493s)).
- **Build in public.** Tweets from a 200-follower account drew the acquirer ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [44:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2668s)).

## Timeline across sources

- Validation with a $5 Stripe link and copied Liftoff videos, then a full Figma design in 3 days ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1558s), [31:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1878s)).
- Pre-order launch. Once a buggy build went live to 1,000 users because the date was not pushed back ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [39:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2359s)).
- 2026-03: sold after 26 days of revenue. Caleb kept 30% equity with a 6-month earnout and a cash bonus ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2837s)).
