Uber is the ride-hailing company; in this wiki it appears as a design and growth example, not as a founder story.

## What it is

- A ride-hailing app. Sources cite its ride flow, pricing display and design process as patterns to copy ([source](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=359s); [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=525s)).

## Founders

- The sources do not cover its founders. [Dawson Botsford](../people/dawson-botsford.md) was a software engineer there in 2016, before the IPO, and later built [Earnifi](earnifi.md) ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=191s)).

## Business model

- Two-sided marketplace of riders and drivers. The post-ride tip keeps drivers on the platform, which makes supply more reliable ([source](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=420s)).

## Metrics

- Showing one estimated price instead of a price range gave a double-digit lift in rides per user ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=525s)). See [Pricing](../topics/pricing.md).

## Design lessons

- **Peak-end rule.** Each ride ends with haptic feedback, then "rate your driver" and "leave a tip", at the moment the rider feels done ([source](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=359s)). See [Behavioral design](../topics/behavioral-design.md).
- **Problem solving first.** Uber's 2018 e-scooter case study shows the designer learning that e-bike and e-scooter riders have different problems ([source](../sources/lgE0ULApU4E.md), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=196s), [4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=258s)). See [UX design](../topics/ux-design.md).
- **Typography.** Its headings use about 1.2x to 1.5x line height; body text on big sites uses about 1.4x to 1.5x ([source](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=132s)). See [Typography](../topics/typography.md).
