Rabbit Holes AI, now called Canvas Mode, is a desktop canvas for AI chats where users branch a conversation into new nodes.

## What it is

- A canvas for AI chats. Users branch a conversation into nodes instead of one long thread, and can use several models in one place [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=113s).
- A desktop app (Electron) with bring-your-own-key AI [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=225s), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=565s).

## Founders

- [Praneeth](../people/praneeth.md), a builder from India who left a $250,000/year software job (2025-10). He built it from his own friction of switching between ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity tabs [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=47s), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=113s).

## Business model

- A one-time price. Bring-your-own-key moves AI costs to users, and the desktop app moves server costs to their computers [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=225s).
- 60% of buyers hold a lifetime license. 40% bought a one-year license that needs renewal for updates (2025-10) [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [8:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=528s).
- Price went from $29 under 100 users to $49. He plans a higher lifetime price and recurring pricing (2025-10) [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=416s). See [lifetime-deals](../topics/lifetime-deals.md).

## Metrics

- About $80K gross and about $75K profit after refunds in 6 months (2025-10) [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [8:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=528s).
- About 1,200 paying users and about 38,000 unique site visitors (2025-10) [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [8:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=528s).
- Costs about $50/month for Vercel and $200/month for Cursor [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=617s).

## Growth channels

- **A [landing page](../topics/landing-pages.md) that names the customer.** The H1 calls out power AI users [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=468s).
- **Twitter replies that hint, not pitch,** on threads about AI interaction [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=468s).

## Timeline

- First version: a web app with a freemium trial and almost no traction. He had no personal brand, and power AI users already paid for many subscriptions [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=162s).
- Rebuild: desktop app, bring-your-own-key and a one-time price. Buyers gave more serious feedback [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=225s).
- 2025-10: about $80K in 6 months, renamed Canvas Mode [source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=47s).
