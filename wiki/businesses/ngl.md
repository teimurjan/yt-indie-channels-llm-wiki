NGL is an anonymous messaging app built on Instagram's story link feature.

## What it is

- Users post an NGL link in their Instagram story or bio and get anonymous messages ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=128s)).
- It moved an old behavior (Formspring, YOLO, Yik Yak) onto the Instagram graph and added no feeds or tabs ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=712s)).

## Founders

- [Hunter Isaacson](../people/hunter-isaacson.md), who also built Wink and [Bags](bags.md). His earlier apps had over 70M downloads ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=455s)).

## Business model

- Weekly [subscriptions](../topics/subscriptions.md) priced $1 to $7 by country GDP per capita; a small share of users pay. Multi-eight figures a year (2025-07) ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=370s)).
- One funnel, one north star: users who post the link and get at least two replies ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=639s)).

## Metrics

- 150M monthly active users about 3 years after launch (2025-07) ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=79s)).

## Growth channels

- **Viral loop.** Friends keep the link in their bios, so churned users redownload ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=316s)). See [Viral loops](../topics/viral-loops.md).
- **Edgy TikTok hook**: a shocking anonymous message plus the chance to learn who sent it ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=249s)).
- **Sell the result.** "Get your own messages"; onboarding takes 15 to 20 seconds ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=615s)).
- **Seed a tribe.** Private accounts with a school's name and logo followed high-school students, and many followed back before launch ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:39:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2391s)).

## Timeline

- End of October 2021: Instagram opens story links to all users; the team starts NGL on a Halloween Zoom call and builds it in about a month ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=128s)).
- Next 6 months: no users. Rebrand to NGL and ngl.link; influencer tests at about $100 per post ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=155s)).
- A paid influencer activates one school in a lower-income country, so they build Android. 2022-05: one TikTok gets about 1M views and 1,000 users in a day ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=205s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=268s)).
- 2025-07: 150M MAU ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=79s)).
