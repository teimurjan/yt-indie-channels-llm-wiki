Shervin is a tech, gadget and fitness YouTuber who runs the channel [Shervin Shares](../businesses/shervin-shares.md) and earns mostly from affiliate links.

## Who

- He quit a six-figure software engineering job, and later a product manager job, to make videos full time ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md)).
- When he quit, YouTube made $2K to $3K/month. He had $5K to $10K in the bank plus investments, and moved back in with his parents ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=154s), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=213s)).
- He shares a New York apartment with two other creators. Rent is $4,500/month, and his share is $1,600 (2023-04) ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=592s)).

## Businesses

- **[Shervin Shares](../businesses/shervin-shares.md).**
  - About $125K/year by the video title. 80% is affiliate income and 20% AdSense. Two companies make up about 50% of affiliate income (2023-04) ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
  - First year: about $300 in total. 1,000 subscribers took about 2 to 3 years ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=344s), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=380s)).
  - Top video: "I replaced my iPhone with an Apple Watch Ultra", about 1.56M views (2023-04) ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=671s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Search-based reviews with affiliate links.** One fitness-band review led to an affiliate program that paid $50 per referral ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=414s)).
- **Protect watch time.** He held off on sponsors, because a sponsor read can cut views ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
- **Viral formula = timing + topic + past data.** He shipped the Apple Watch Ultra video the day after launch, with rough editing ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=718s)).
- **Frame the idea by search demand.** "Gaining muscle" beats "testosterone supplements" for the same content ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [14:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=843s)).
- **Hire through your network first**, then Upwork. His first editor cost $7 to $10/hour ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=771s)).
- **Build the habit before you quit.** Give one day a week to content ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [13:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=804s)).
