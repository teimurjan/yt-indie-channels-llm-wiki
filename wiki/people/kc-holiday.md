KC Holiday is a former Beverly Hills waiter and bartender who co-founded Qalo, a silicone wedding ring brand.

## Who

- He and his restaurant manager Ted both kept losing or removing metal rings at the gym. His first goal was to replace his $2,000/month restaurant income ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=71s), [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=131s)).
- Handshake deal: KC gave time and energy, Ted gave money ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=131s)).

## Businesses

- [Qalo](../businesses/qalo.md): $30M/year at peak with close to 100 employees and a 20% margin. Over $100M lifetime sales (2024-03) ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=0s)).

## Repeated advice

- Fix the product with what you have. They hand-trimmed about 50,000 rings with eyebrow scissors instead of shutting down ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=390s)).
- A website is not distribution. Their Shopify store launched to zero visitors ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=450s)).
- Seed product through personal connections. A free ring for NFL quarterback Andy Dalton appeared on HBO's Hard Knocks and sales went up 3x to 4x the next day ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=512s), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)). See [influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md).
- Press spikes fade in about 2 days. What you do with the attention matters most ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)).
- As first mover, hire fast before richer competitors arrive ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s)).
- Ship imperfect, then ask customers how to improve it ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=769s)).
