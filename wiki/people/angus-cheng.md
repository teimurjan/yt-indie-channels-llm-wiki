Angus Cheng is a developer in Hong Kong who runs Bank Statement Converter alone.

## Who

- He made indie Xbox Live games from 2010 to 2014, worked at an investment bank in Hong Kong from 2017 to 2019 and at a crypto exchange in 2020. He quit in 2021 ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=477s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Bank Statement Converter](../businesses/bank-statement-converter.md): turns PDF bank statements into spreadsheets. $40K MRR, of which about $39K is profit (2025-07) ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=214s), [8:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=500s)).
- Earlier: Girlfriend Plus, a virtual girlfriend app ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=477s)).

## Repeated advice

- Build first. A 1 to 2 week [MVP](../topics/mvp.md) is a small risk, so pre-launch validation adds little ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=124s)).
- Turn ads off to see organic demand. He still got 2 to 3 signups a day, then fixed every complaint ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=292s)).
- [Google Ads](../topics/google-ads.md) and [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md) failed for him: about $1,000 in ads per $300 in sales, and 1 sale from 3 months of cold email ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=247s)).
- Skip brand pages on social media if your users are not there ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s)).
- Go after markets too small for a VC ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=409s)).
- Save years of runway, not months. It took 2 to 3 years before revenue paid his rent ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=532s), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s)).
