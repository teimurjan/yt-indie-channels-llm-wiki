## Who he is

### Henry, co-founder of Jenni AI

- He co-founded [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md) with [David Park](david-park.md). The two ran 9 or 10 failed startups together before Jenni ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=123s)).
- Henry coded and David sold. Their first target was agencies that write content ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)).

## Businesses

- [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md), an AI writing assistant. It stalled at about $2K/month after GPT-3. A viral Twitter thread took MRR from about $2K to $10K in a month. It passed $3M ARR (2024-01) ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=435s), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=743s)).
- [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md), an AI calorie tracker. It made $30K in month 1 and $1M/month within 8 months. It was on track for about $50M in the year of its sale (2026-08) ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=147s)).

## Repeated advice

The sources do not quote either Henry directly. The team lessons around them:

- **Split the work by skill.** Jenni had one coder and one seller ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)). At Cal AI, each co-founder owns one domain with full trust, so they had no real fights ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=220s)).
- **Recruit from your network.** Cal AI's CEO and CTO knew each other from age 10 ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=298s)).
- **Decide big things together.** The Cal AI team turned down low eight-figure offers at the end of 2024 after constant calls ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=349s)).
