This page collects what the sources say about Mark Vassilevskiy, a young designer from Kazakhstan who runs the agency [Skale](../businesses/skale.md).

## Who he is

- Mark is a 19-year-old designer from Kazakhstan (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=54s)).
- He moved from front-end development to design in Figma more than 5 years ago ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)).
- Every design job he applied for in summer 2024 rejected him ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)).

## Businesses

### Skale

- Skale is a design agency for AI startups. It makes websites, launch videos and billboards ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)).
- Clients include Bolt.new, Krea AI, [Framer](../businesses/framer.md) and [Contra](../businesses/contra.md) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=70s)).
- Revenue is about $80K/month. It doubled about every two months: $10K, $20K, $40K, $80K (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)).
- The team is 20 people (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=54s)).
- It signs 6 to 7 new clients a month. The average retainer is about $20K/month (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)).
- He opened his X account in January 2024 and the agency in November 2024 ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=100s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Turn rejection into free public work**. His first redesign of a famous brand's hero section got about 110K views overnight and 10 to 12 cold bookings ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)).
- **Show the free work to the brand**. He showed a Bolt redesign to the Bolt team at an event. They became a client a week later ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=209s)).
- **Pick brands that people talk about now**. An acquisition.com redesign got a reply from Alex Hormozi and 15 to 16 bookings ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=360s)).
- **Grow on X in five steps**. Comment about 50 times a day. Run giveaways. Study viral posts. Test freely at 3K to 5K followers. Make great work for trending brands ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=248s)), ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=299s)).
- **Volume negates luck**. Do enough DMs or posts that the chance of zero leads is close to zero ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=512s)).
