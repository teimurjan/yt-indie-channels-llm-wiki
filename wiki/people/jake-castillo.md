## Who

- CMO/COO of Cal AI, age 31 (2026-08) [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=86s), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=196s).
- The team hired him as COO after they saw his work at Study Buddy [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=298s).
- He does not speak in these sources. Other people describe his role.

## Businesses

- [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md), with [Zach Yadegari](zach-yadegari.md), [Blake Anderson](blake-anderson.md) and [Henry](henry.md). Revenue reached $1M/month within 8 months. The app sold in 2026 [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=147s).
- Cal AI's marketing ran on influencers. The program grew from 20 to 30 creators to 200+ at once, each posting about four videos a month (2026-08) [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=191s). About 25 assistants sent about 2,000 cold emails a day [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [34:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2075s).
