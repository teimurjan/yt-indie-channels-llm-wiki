Alejandro is the European co-founder of Pushscroll, an app that blocks social media until the user does push-ups.

## Who he is

- He studied computer science. His earlier startups failed because he ignored distribution [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=164s).
- He validated Pushscroll with a TikTok before any product existed, then found [Mario](mario.md) through YC Co-Founder Matching [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=137s), [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s).

## Businesses

- [Pushscroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md): $30K profit in the month before the interview, all from organic marketing (2025-10) [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:01:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=107s). Over $30K/month 4 months after launch, about 300,000 downloads and about 4,000 paying users (2025-12) [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=84s), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=105s).

> Conflict: [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) says the validation-to-launch video had about 175K views and 20K downloads. [ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) says the validation video had 80K views and the free first-week launch brought 20-30K downloads. These may describe different videos.

## Repeated advice

- **Make the video before the app.** Pitch a fake product demo on TikTok and build only after one goes viral [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=481s), [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s).
- **Explain the app in three words** and pick ideas with one strong visual element [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=497s).
- **Free launch, then hard paywall.** Give the MVP free to first-week users to drive shares [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1332s), [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=220s).
- **Hold the early audience in Discord** and let it vote on features [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:15:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=925s), [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=467s).
- **Post organic content yourself until $5-10K MRR**, then scale with UGC, influencers and ads [source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=540s).
- **Watch skip rate.** Their best video had a 19% skip rate. At 30%, change the format [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [1:03:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3792s).
