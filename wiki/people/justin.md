Justin is one of three co-founders of [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md), an AI tool that turns podcasts, videos and other media into written content ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s)). The source gives only his first name.

## Who he is

- He co-founded Castmagic with [Ramon Berrios](ramon-berrios.md) and [Blaine Bolus](blaine-bolus.md) ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s)).
- The founders started their careers at VC-backed startups, then chose to build something for themselves ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=0s)).
- He works from Austin. Ramon is in Fort Lauderdale and Blaine is in Miami ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=691s)).

## Businesses

- **[Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md).** Over $120K MRR about 10 months after the start, growing over 20% a month (2024-06) ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).
- An AppSumo lifetime deal brought about $350K and about 10,000 users ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)).
- The team stays bootstrapped and keeps marketing spend under 20% of revenue ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).

## Repeated advice

The source gives this advice as the team's view. It does not split it by founder.

- **Solve your own workflow problem.** AI apps that sit inside an existing workflow and remove friction win ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=96s)).
- **Borrow distribution early.** Build a personal brand, or find a partner such as AppSumo ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)).
- **Pick co-founders for fit.** Choose partners for alignment and complementary skills, not convenience ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=582s)).
- **Remote cadence.** Async work, 30-minute standups on Monday and Friday, and a week together every quarter ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=691s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=746s)).
