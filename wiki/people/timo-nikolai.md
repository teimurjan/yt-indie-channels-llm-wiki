Timo is a founder who appears in two sources: as Timo Nikolai, co-founder of [Setter AI](../businesses/setter-ai.md), and as Timo, maker of the [Chart Detector AI](../businesses/chart-detector-ai.md) app.

## Who

- In [3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), Timo Nikolai lives in Bangkok. He met [Josef Buettgen](josef-buettgen.md) at a coworking space. Both had spent months on products with no customers (2025-09).
- In [DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), Timo is 23 and a former software engineer at a German car company. He built Chart Detector AI with his brother and works about 20 hours a month while he travels (2026-07).
- The wiki files both under one slug. The sources do not confirm that they are the same person.

## Businesses

- **[Setter AI](../businesses/setter-ai.md)**, with Josef Buettgen. An AI appointment setter that follows up with and qualifies leads.
  - The first $500 payment came in February 2024, before the product existed ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
  - About $10K revenue last month, from 38 paying customers out of about 2,000 signups (2025-09) ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).
  - Pricing starts at about $50/month. The largest customer pays up to $5,000/month (2025-09) ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s)).
- **[Chart Detector AI](../businesses/chart-detector-ai.md)**, with his brother. The app analyzes a photo of a stock or crypto chart with AI.
  - It started as a Telegram bot after ChatGPT added image upload ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=216s)).
  - First 13 months: over 90K downloads and over $260K total revenue. He quit his job after 6 months ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=62s)).
  - $56K/month and 13K to 14K downloads/month (2026-07) ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s)).
  - April: $43.7K revenue, about $20K ad spend, about $11.5K profit, a margin of about 25% (2026-07) ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=358s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Get paid before you build.** Setter AI used a fake landing page on an old, indexed domain, with an AI caller demo and a "book demo" link ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=100s)). Validation means a card swipe, not traffic or calls ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
- **Take a refundable deposit and go high ticket.** For example, $500 for full access at launch ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=288s)).
- **SEO for B2B.** Free tools, "HubSpot alternatives" posts and long-form YouTube. Most closed customers watched a video before the call ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)).
- **Know your ROAS math, then spend.** At $1.50 per download and $3 per user, ROAS is 2x, so spend as much as you can ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=263s)).
- **Prepare the app for ads.** Sell the outcome in onboarding, add social proof, and use a hard paywall with no trial, because ads need cash back fast ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s)).
- **TikTok ads setup.** Start with 6+ videos, Smart+ campaigns, subscription events, $50/day and broad targeting. Leave the 7-day learning phase alone ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=485s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=578s)).
- **Scale slowly.** Raise the budget by no more than 20% every 3 days, and keep a pipeline of new creatives ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=605s)).
