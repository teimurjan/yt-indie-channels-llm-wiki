Kourosh is a side-project founder who built the dictation app Voicy for his father.

## Who he is

- He built Voicy next to a 9-to-5 job ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=0s)).
- His first app, Meet Waves, a community analytics tool, reached $5K MRR ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=209s)).

## Businesses

- [Voicy](../businesses/voicy.md), a speech-to-text app on OpenAI's Whisper API. $16K to $20K a month, with $11K MRR and the rest from lifetime deals (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s)).
  - About 2,500 DAU, 10,000 MAU and 1,400 active subscriptions (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s)).
  - Price: $8.49/month or about $260 lifetime. 20% of users use a disability discount (2026-10) ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=186s)).
- Romeo SEO, his own SEO tool. Local trades want it more than indie hackers ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=746s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Launch as a Chrome extension.** The Chrome Web Store sends free traffic ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)).
- **Repost one proven Reddit angle** in five or six subreddits. This got him 200 to 300 users ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=506s)). See [Reddit marketing](../topics/reddit-marketing.md).
- **Find the painkiller segment in user calls.** Seniors and disabled users were 20% to 30% of users. They loved the MVP ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=602s)). See [customer interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md).
- **Win by being seen first.** An article that ranks above a rival gets the click, even with a weaker product ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=684s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).
- **Let the ICP pick you.** Post everywhere early and chase the easiest market ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=746s)).
- **Do 5x the volume.** Write 10 posts, not one ([source](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=796s)).
