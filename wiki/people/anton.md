Anton is the founder of Letterly, a paid speech-to-text writing app, after 15 years of failed startups.

## Who

- He spent 15 years on six or seven startups that failed, because they tried to be unique and built things people did not need ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=381s)).

## Businesses

- [Letterly](../businesses/letterly.md): turns speech into well-written text.
  - $250K/month, 2 years after launch (2025-11) ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=90s)).
  - 30K monthly active users, 20K paid subscribers, 150,000 downloads in 2 years; paid app with a free trial (2025-11) ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=113s)).
  - Monthly costs: about $30K salaries for 10 people, $5K AI, $200K ads (2025-11) ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=676s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Make simplicity the moat.** Anyone can build the product; few make it easy ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=224s)).
- **UX multiplies every metric**: onboarding, retention, conversion and referrals ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=320s)). See [UX design](../topics/ux-design.md).
- **Simplify an existing feature before you add a new one** ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=381s)).
- **Treat UX as a separate, expensive feature**, even if it delays a promised feature by weeks ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=440s)).
- **Founder checklist.** Co-founders from day one, launch in 1 to 2 months, pick a validated idea, charge from day one, quit your job once revenue shows traction ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=728s)).
