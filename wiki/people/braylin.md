Braylin is a college sprinter who co-founded 3AK, a track and field app, with no coding experience.

## Who

- One of two founders aged 19 and 20 who run track in Texas (Texas Southern and Texas State). Before the app, they lost about $3,000 day trading ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=97s), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)).
- Co-founder: [Christian](christian.md). Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [3AK](../businesses/3ak.md): built in about 2 weeks with Rork, Claude and Claude Code. $10K in month one, about $70K in its first 6 months, about 40,000 users and over $20K/month (2026-06) ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=0s), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=74s), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)).
- A football app, launched after 50 to 60 hours of work over about four days ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=559s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build where you already have influence.** Their track videos had hundreds of thousands of followers before launch ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=299s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=312s)).
- **Seed the brand before launch** with a merch store and "3AK method" in videos and bios ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=318s)).
- **Price for the audience.** Teens will not pay $60 to $70, so the yearly plan is $30 ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)).
- **An app costs under $500** to get into the App Store with AI builders ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=204s)).
- **Monetize what you already do**, because you will stay longer in a niche you love ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=702s)).
