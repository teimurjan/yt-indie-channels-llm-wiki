Yasser Elsaid is a solo, unfunded founder who built Chatbase, a tool for building a ChatGPT-style chatbot on a business's own data.

## Who

- He launched Chatbase in his final year of university to 16 Twitter followers. The launch tweet went viral. Two or three sales a day led him to drop out ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=43s), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Chatbase](../businesses/chatbase.md): $0 to $1M ARR in 117 days. $6.8M ARR, 10,000 paying customers at $40 to $500/month, about 600,000 registered users, no sales team (2025-07) ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=322s), [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=431s)).

## Repeated advice

- Build what demos show but nobody sells ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)).
- Design the launch tweet: a familiar interface, an aha moment in about 20 seconds, and tags for the new tools you used, which repost you ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s)).
- [Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) every day. Expect 2 to 3 months of few views ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=210s)).
- Frame each feature as a new launch for people who do not know you ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s)).
- Give away free bots for traffic and domain authority. One sponsored LinkedIn post brought about $4,000 in a day (2025-07) ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=393s)).
- Build ahead of the model: bet the next model can do what today's cannot ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=499s)).
