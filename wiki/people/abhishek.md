Abhishek is the solo founder of Youform, a cheaper Typeform alternative that he built by "finding the gap" in a popular tool.

## Who he is

- A solo founder. His earlier product, Botflow, was a no-code chatbot builder with about 200 users. Most of them used it as a conversational form after Typeform raised prices. That showed him the gap ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s)).

## Businesses

- [Youform](../businesses/youform.md): a form builder and Typeform alternative, launched in 2023.
  - About $11K MRR after 2 years (2025-08) ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=56s)).
  - About 35,000 registered users, about 500 paying customers, about 35,000 unique visitors a month and over 4M form submissions (2025-08) ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s)).
  - Over 90% of features are free. Free-to-paid conversion is 1.5% to 2% (2025-08) ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s)).
  - MVP in 2 weeks. Monthly expenses under $1,200 (2025-08) ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Do not invent.** An indie founder cannot build the next Uber. Pick a market with big players, because they prove demand ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=163s)).
- **The four-step gap method** ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s)):
  1. Pick a popular tool. Search Twitter and Reddit for "X alternative".
  2. List the pains it does not solve. Price is a valid pain only for a tool that is too expensive.
  3. Reach those users with a basic landing page. The message is the key.
  4. Build a one-click migration tool. On Youform, you paste a Typeform URL and get a Youform form in seconds.
- **Check more than price.** Typeform's price rise was not enough on its own. He searched Twitter, Reddit and Typeform's forum for missing features ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s)).
- **Ship a tiny MVP.** Version one had basic fields and CSV export, with no integrations, no logo and no real landing page ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s)).
- **Use AI against abuse.** A free plan attracts bad actors, so he uses OpenAI for fraud detection ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=472s)).
- **Open gaps he names:** a simple Calendly alternative, because Calendly moves up market. A Forest alternative, because its recent reviews are mostly one star. Avoid social media apps and marketplaces as an indie founder ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=256s)).
