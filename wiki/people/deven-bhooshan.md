Deven Bhooshan is an indie hacker from India and co-founder of SuperGrow, an AI tool for writing LinkedIn posts.

## Who he is

- He started with 600 followers on X and about 500 on LinkedIn [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=232s).
- He used Taplio, ContentIn and AuthoredUp daily and saw that their core writing was weak [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=100s).

## Businesses

- [SuperGrow](../businesses/supergrow.md): a RocketHub lifetime deal made $65K in 3 days, with a 40% platform cut. Now more than $19K/month and 800+ recurring customers at a 60 to 70% margin (2025-09) [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=372s), [14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=840s).

## Repeated advice

- **Enter a validated market and be 1% better** [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=150s).
- **Use a lifetime deal for early cash and feedback.** Cap it (3 days, 300 users). Paid users give blunt feedback; free users ghost you [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=581s), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s).
- **Do tasks by hand, then productize them** from 1 to 2 hours a day in Intercom [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=680s).
- **Turn LTD buyers into launch ambassadors.** 10 to 15 posted on launch day; Product Hunt gave Product of the Week and 50 customers [source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s).
