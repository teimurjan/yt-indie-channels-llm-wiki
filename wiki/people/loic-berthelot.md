Loïc Berthelot is a French founder who builds bootstrapped SaaS tools for ecommerce sellers and grows them through YouTube creators.

## Who

- He spent his first 5 years on a SaaS nobody used, then launched more than 10 SaaS products. Three worked [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=16s), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=64s).
- The turn came when an ecommerce owner brought him a real problem [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=164s).

## Businesses

All three reached at least $35K MRR early and were 100% bootstrapped [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=64s).

- [Infuspy](../businesses/infuspy.md): an Instagram and Snapchat influencer finder for ecommerce. $20K MRR in 2 months, now shut down [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s).
- [Minea](../businesses/minea.md): product research for dropshippers. It peaked at "750K MRR" within 2 years, as he states it, at about 80% margin [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=712s).
- [Drop Magic](../businesses/drop-magic.md): an AI store builder for beginners. Over $45K MRR in its first 4 months (2025-11). A creator co-founder made $10K MRR certain at launch [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=102s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s).
- Across Minea and Drop Magic he works with more than 500 YouTube creators. YouTube brings more than 60% of new customers (2025-11) [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=233s), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s).

## Repeated advice

- **Validate cheapest first.** Newsletter, then a no-code app, then a coded app [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=164s).
- **[YouTube](../topics/youtube.md) over TikTok for conversion.** YouTube has a predictable view floor, long demos and videos that convert 3 years later. "TikTok is really good for brand awareness but it doesn't convert" [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s).
- **A 4-step creator system.** Find creators your competitors use. Qualify on views over 10% of followers, 100+ comments per video and repeat brand deals. Send a 7-email sequence. Deal on a fixed fee plus commission, test one video, then buy a 3 to 4 video package [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=485s).
- **Make a creator a co-founder.** It removes distribution risk and gives fast, free tests of scripts, pricing and angles [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=597s).
- **Kill projects by deadline,** not by emotion [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=755s).
