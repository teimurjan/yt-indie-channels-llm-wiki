Tim Gabe is a product designer and YouTuber who runs the app design agency ZipSnap and teaches UI, UX and app psychology.

## Who he is

- He started his channel in 2022-05 with seven years of product, UX and UI experience at agencies and product companies, most recently Spotify [source](../sources/N0vlBuMggKc.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=0s).
- His path: a physical product idea in his early 20s, a startup incubator, design school, then a small local agency. He says 90% of his early knowledge came from agency work, not school [source](../sources/lgE0ULApU4E.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=0s), [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=122s).
- By 2025 he describes about a decade of design work for Spotify and many startups [source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=0s).
- The channel passed 100K subscribers (2025-01). He took about a year off after building a Framer course, which he found stressful [source](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md) [0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=35s), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=68s).

## Repeated advice

### Design craft (2022-2024)

- **Use a system.** The 8-point grid, at most four font sizes and two weights, and the 60-30-10 color rule [source](../sources/ohF93_k3IMk.md) [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohF93_k3IMk&t=25s), [source](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=181s), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=726s).
- **Copy designs pixel for pixel to learn**, then redesign something with clear flaws [source](../sources/lgE0ULApU4E.md) [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=366s), [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=468s).
- **Put interactions in components** so micro-interactions travel with them. See [micro-interactions](../topics/micro-interactions.md) [source](../sources/2Mvmz34QsLQ.md) [5:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=356s).
- **Framer for websites, Figma for design.** "Figma draws the blueprint, and Framer builds the building." He picks Framer over Webflow except for CMS [source](../sources/XPim-a5R6yw.md) [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=56s), [source](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md) [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s).
- **Landing pages: one clear CTA, outcome copy, scannable text.** The average SaaS site converts under 4%, the best almost 12% (2025-05) [source](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=0s), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=34s).

### App psychology (2025-2026)

- **Compete on feel, not features.** AI makes features and "decent" looks cheap, so emotional design and craft are the moat [source](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=25s), [source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=0s), [source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=62s).
- **AI gets you 80%; human taste does the last 20%.** A bare prompt scored 5/15, too many references 4/15, human art direction 15/15 [source](../sources/bOm9GDv96Tk.md) [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOm9GDv96Tk&t=45s), [source](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md) [1:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=111s), [13:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=784s).
- **Onboarding: value first, as short as possible.** Apps lose about 77% of users in 3 days. Long onboarding is right only if it buys personalization or filters for buyers (Cal AI 20+ steps, Noom 113 screens) [source](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=0s), [source](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=143s).
- **Design the peak and the end.** People remember the most intense moment and the last one; put paywalls, rating and share prompts right after a win [source](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md) [1:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=111s), [source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md) [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=443s).
- **Deliver rewards as gifts, not receipts**: anticipation, reveal, celebration [source](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=62s).
- **Retention beyond streaks.** Tie use to an existing routine and design forgiveness for missed days; put the hero feature on a timer; make competition local and winnable [source](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=203s), [source](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=64s), [source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s), [source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s).
- **Paywalls: structure before price.** Weekly plans grew from 43% to 55.6% of subscription revenue in two years; 82% of subscription starts happen on install day (2026-05). Mojo hid credit packs and lifted new revenue 14% at the same price [source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=83s), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s), [source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s), [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s).
- **Bet against the category's hidden assumption**, ideally the one the leader makes money from (Timeleft, Speak, Evidation) [source](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s), [source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md) [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=250s).
- **Build the social and identity layer before AI agents absorb the function** [source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=0s), [source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=377s).
