Lane Wagner is the founder of [Boot.dev](../businesses/boot-dev.md), a gamified platform for learning back-end development.

## Who he is

- He managed a team of 3 Go engineers for about $200K total comp. Boot.dev made about $2K/month when he went full time ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=181s)).
- In 2020 his Go job posts got only 5 to 7 applicants. That gap became the idea ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [4:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=255s)).

## Businesses

- [Boot.dev](../businesses/boot-dev.md): almost $1M/month and 25,332 active paying members (2025-04) ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=0s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=96s)).
- 2024: $5.7M revenue, almost $2M on marketing and about $2.5M profit ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=772s)).
- $330K angel funding from a former CFO and his partner ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=226s)).

## Repeated advice

- Be a purple cow. Do not copy competitor websites ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=127s)).
- Serve one persona and filter out requests from customers you do not want ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=291s)).
- MVP means minimum quantity, not minimum quality ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=348s)).
- Borrow trust through influencers. Boot.dev runs most sponsorships on gaming channels, and finds creators by asking users who they watch ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=541s), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=575s)).
- Give content away free and charge for interactivity ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=666s)).
- Stay on product. He has about 3 internal meetings a week and spends most days writing courses ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [13:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=814s)).
