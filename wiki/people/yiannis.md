Yiannis is a machine learning scientist who runs organic TikTok for consumer apps. He does not hire creators. He runs "reposters": warmed-up accounts that post content his team makes ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=0s)).

## Who he is

- He started by posting 50 to 100 times by hand on his own French account ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=171s)).
- He calls "the Tabs chocolate guy" his main inspiration for scaling content across many accounts. See [Tabs Chocolate](../businesses/tabs-chocolate.md) ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=252s)).
- His first reposter fleet was for a dating app. A script ran fake chats on a Tinder messaging clone and screen-recorded them ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=252s), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=367s)).

## Businesses

- **Organic growth for client apps.** Last year: about 300M views and, by his estimate, 3M to 5M downloads (2026-05). Clients rarely share hybrid results, so attribution is unclear ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [56:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3415s)).
- He opens 50 to 100 new accounts a week and makes 10M to 50M views a month (2026-05) ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [52:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3120s), [56:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3415s)).
- Joseph Choi says each reposter video costs about $20 to make ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [18:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1082s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Keep the creative in-house.** UGC teams hand the creative to 7 to 10 creators. He makes the content, and reposters only warm up accounts and post ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=315s)).
- **Separate format from content.** A hook-plus-demo structure transfers to any market. A finance-news format or a well-known face does not ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [29:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1742s)).
- **Pick a "gem".** A format that goes viral on 5 to 20 posts across accounts and is easy to mass-produce ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1633s)).
- **Build intent in the words.** A scratch-map video with "post-breakup activity" got millions of views and no downloads. With "procrastinating for my exams", it moved the app up the charts ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [37:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2221s)).
- **Use comment bait to find the audience.** A viral bait post gives later posts from that account a ripple of views ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [12:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=751s)).
- **Watch rank moves outside the US.** Organic hits show first in other English-speaking App Stores ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [47:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2859s)).
- **Validate the format before the app**, and post yourself before you manage others ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [53:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3182s), [54:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3295s)).
- **Brief ambassadors 80/20.** 80% of the post follows a strict structure, and 20% is the creator's own spin ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [24:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1483s)).
