Michael Que is a 20-year-old NYU finance student who built Go Tall, a height prediction app for teens, to about $100K/month.

## Who

- He built the app after rejections from his dream jobs. X: @michaelque22 ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Go Tall](../businesses/go-tall.md): launched summer 2025. About $100K/month on average and about 1M downloads across iOS and Android (2026-07) ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=66s), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=102s)).
- Pricing $35/year or $5/week, plus a freemium tier ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=102s)).
- Revenue path: about $3K/month from his content and a few UGC creators, $10K after paywall work, about $30K in month 5, then past $100K with paid ads ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Mine marketplaces for proven ideas.** A similar app sold on Acquire.com for about $20,000. He built his own ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)).
- **Read the comments and count the DMs.** Teens posted their heights unasked, and 10 to 20 DMs a day asked for predictions ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=341s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=396s)).
- **Do not fear competition.** Calorie trackers still make $30K/month next to [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=396s)).
- **Find one format, then scale it with people.** 10 TikToks a day for a month, proof on 2 more accounts, then DMs to about 50 creators a day. One channel gives clean attribution ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)).
- **Ads only behind proven organic content**, after $30K/month ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=602s)).
- **Drop your ego.** "Last summer, I basically just got rid of my ego." ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=796s)).
