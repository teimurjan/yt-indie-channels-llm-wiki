This page collects what the sources say about Presh, founder of [The Wellness Company](../businesses/the-wellness-company.md), a small studio of health apps.

## Who he is

- Presh dropped out of college after year one and cold-emailed investors ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)).
- He then worked 7 years for an angel investor, where he saw thousands of founder pitches ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=216s)).

## Businesses

### The Wellness Company

- The studio grew from $0 to $120K ARR in about 10 months. Monthly revenue is $10.4K, all subscriptions (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=65s)).
- It has over 20,000 downloads, mostly from App Store search, and over 1,500 paying subscribers (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).
- Free-to-paid conversion is about 8%. Day-30 retention of paid users is 61% (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)).
- Tools cost about $300/month, and margins are 80% to 85% (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=736s)).

### The apps

- [Go Polar](../businesses/go-polar.md) tracks cold plunge and sauna sessions ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s)).
- [SunSeek](../businesses/sunseek.md) tracks morning light and vitamin D ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=126s)).
- [Posture AI](../businesses/posture-ai.md) scans posture for desk workers ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [9:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=594s)).
- [Tempo](../businesses/tempo.md), not yet launched, combines wearable data and blood tests into a health-span score (2025-12) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=655s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build for your own problem**. No good app tracked cold plunges, so he built one. If you feel the pain, you build a better product ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=291s)).
- **Serve one audience with many apps**. Users of one app fit the others, so each launch markets to an existing list ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s)).
- **Five steps**. Pick a passion, find a problem in it, ship an MVP fast, plan distribution from day zero, and put in the reps ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s)).
- **Run a product studio**. The first app rarely wins. SunSeek was better on day one than Go Polar because of earlier reps ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=574s)).
- **Act on energy**. "If you care about it, ship it" ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=750s)).
