Jeremy, known online as "Van Man", runs Vanman, a handmade natural hygiene brand in San Diego.

## Who

- After failed startups he sold his belongings and lived in a $2,000 Craigslist van. He posted on Twitter about animal-based health and his own baking-soda tooth powder [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=0s).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Vanman](../businesses/vanman.md): started in 2021 at about $5,000/month. Later it had its first $1M month and did more than $5M in the last year (2024-03) [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=71s).
  - The first Shopify launch sold out in about 20 minutes, about 100 tooth powders. He expected 5 to 15 [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s).
  - Products are made by hand in one building, 500 to 600 orders a day on good days. Almost 40% of buyers buy again (2024-03) [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s).
  - Best sellers: tallow honey balm and mint bone toothpaste. Most products cost under $20 [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s).

## Repeated advice

- **Document the product in public.** Followers asked to try the tooth powder before it existed [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s).
- **Sell to a community that shares your beliefs.** His first buyers already lived the "animal-based" frame [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s).
- **Post divisive content and current-event memes.** Comments push posts further. A meme takes 5 minutes to make but days to find [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=359s).
- **Raise order value with more products.** Bigger orders lower shipping cost per order and started free cash flow [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s).
- **Stay in the comments yourself** for hours after each post [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s).
- **Start on Etsy.** Find product ideas there, add a twist and sell back on Etsy [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s).
