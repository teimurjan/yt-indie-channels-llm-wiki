Thomas Sanlis is a solo founder who launched over 30 failed projects before Uneed, a Product Hunt alternative, worked.

## Who

- Failed projects include Gum Affiliates (about $500 over 2 years), Frisbee, Gidley, a Twitter feed and a bookmark manager ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=81s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Uneed](../businesses/uneed.md): a launch platform. $8K to $10K/month, about 40,000 users and about 2,000 paying customers (2025-08) ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=495s)).

## Repeated advice

- Five reasons projects fail: quitting after a week or two, a headline that does not say what the product does, silence that kills momentum, "build it and they will come", and bad timing ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=153s)).
- Keep posting. A few weeks of silence means you rebuild from zero ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=198s)). See [build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md).
- Ride a market moment. He pivoted when indie hackers complained about Product Hunt ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=315s)).
- A good idea is one you know how to sell: you know the market, own a channel, or have an angle ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=426s)).
- Competitors are a good sign. Creating a market is "nearly impossible" ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [7:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=470s)).
- Treat the business as a marathon. No 10-hour days ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=607s)).
