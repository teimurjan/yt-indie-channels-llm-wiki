Nico Jeannen is a French indie maker who built 17 apps in about a year and sold two of them for $265K.

## Who he is

- He quit a bank job at 19, tried a tie brand and dropshipping, then locked himself in a hotel room for 2 months to learn to code [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s).
- He sprints, then rests: in a grind phase he works from about 6 a.m. to 7 or 8 p.m. [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=886s).

> Conflict: [miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md) tags a "Nico" who founded Neural Frames as nico-jeannen. That Nico is a former physics PhD and musician, which does not match the bank-job background in [zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md). The two may be different people.

## Businesses

- [MakeLogo.ai](../businesses/makelogo-ai.md): AI logo generator with one-time logo packs. $26K in sales, then sold for $65K cash (about $85K total), about 6 months after he started it (2025-03) [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s).
- [TalkNotes](../businesses/talknotes.md): voice notes to formatted text. $77K in sales and about 10,000 users, sold for $200K in July 2024 [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=124s).
- [Neural Frames](../businesses/neural-frames.md) (if the same Nico): an AI music video generator at about $100K/month with a team of 5 (2025-07). GPUs and APIs cost about $45K/month, almost half of revenue [source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md) [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=52s), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=599s).

## Repeated advice

- **Four idea filters:** value of the problem, market scale, your skill to build it, and a profitable channel [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=266s).
- **Fake the backend.** MakeLogo.ai first sold through a Typeform with no backend [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=422s).
- **Validate organically, then pay for ads.** TalkNotes hit about $1.5K MRR organically, then Facebook ads at $20 to $100/day took his apps to $7K MRR at peak [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=616s).
- **UGC-style ads.** His best ad was a paid person reading a script that led with the problem [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=654s).
- **Sell on Acquire.com** with a listing written like a landing page, and get several buyers bidding [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=752s).
- **Niche down and check keywords.** The Neural Frames founder found niching to musicians "a huge unlock" and checks ideas for 1,000+ monthly searches at low difficulty [source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md) [6:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=395s), [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=304s).
