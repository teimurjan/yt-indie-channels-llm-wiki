Aaron Spivak is the Toronto co-founder of Hush Blankets, which went from $0 to $48M in sales in 48 months.

## Who he is

- He started Hush with co-founder [Leor](leor.md) while both had other jobs ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=68s)).

## Businesses

- [Hush Blankets](../businesses/hush-blankets.md), a weighted and cooling blanket brand.
  - Revenue grew from $30K to $60K to $90K/month in 2018. In July sales fell to zero or one per day ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
  - The first roll of custom "ice fabric" cost $100,000, and they had $4,000 left ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=357s)).
  - The cooling blanket Kickstarter had a $25K goal and raised over $1M in 30 days (2019) ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)).
  - Sales were $20M in 2020 ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=530s)).
  - Canada's largest sleep company acquired Hush in October 2021 ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=597s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Validate with search volume, then pre-sell.** About 300,000 people searched for "weighted blankets". A pre-order site plus Google Ads tested it ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=68s), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)). See [Google Ads](../topics/google-ads.md).
- **Plan for seasonality.** No summer inventory plan almost killed the business ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=191s)).
- **Call every customer.** Every customer said the blanket was too hot, which led to the cooling blanket ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=297s)). See [customer interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md).
- **Crowdfund with pre-validated demand.** 3,000 people had said on the phone that they plan to buy it ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)). See [crowdfunding](../topics/crowdfunding.md).
- **Share the founder story.** "Customers care more about the people behind a brand than they do about the product itself" ([source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=657s)).
