Brett Williams is a self-taught designer who runs DesignJoy, a one-person design subscription.

## Who he is

- He was a 9-to-5 graphic designer. He did landing pages, logos and brand work ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s)).
- He learned design by copying good work and doing fake redesigns ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=435s)).
- He works about 11:30 to 17:00, after mornings with his kids ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=476s)).

## Businesses

- [DesignJoy](../businesses/designjoy.md): unlimited design requests for a monthly fee.
  - About $1.3M/year, about 20 clients at once, no employees or contractors (2023-05) ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=54s), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=85s)).
  - Over $2M a year with about 6 working hours a day (2024-01) ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=0s)).
  - Running costs of $176/month and zero marketing spend (2023-05) ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=292s)).
  - He kept his day job for 3.5 years and quit at $80K/month. Revenue doubled the next month ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=611s)).
  - Price went from $449/month at launch to $5,000/month, with some clients at about $8,000 (2024-01) ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=187s)).
- Scribbles (2019): made in 6 hours, sold for $4.99, over 25,000 downloads ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=568s)).
- Productize Yourself, a course. Info products are 29% of his income (2024-01) ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=629s)).

> Conflict: [dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md) says he built the site in 6 to 7 hours on one Saturday. [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) says 12 hours for $29.

## Repeated advice

- **Productize one skill.** Sell a fixed package with unlimited requests. No hourly rates and no proposals ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s)). See [productized services](../topics/productized-services.md).
- **Set strict boundaries.** All requests go through a Trello board. One active request per client. No calls and no Slack ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s)).
- **Underprice to start, then raise.** A too-good price got the first client within 24 hours ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=127s)).
- **Sell to $5,000 clients.** Cheap clients nag and churn. Rich businesses compare him to a $200K/year hire ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=342s)).
- **Bundle only what you do fastest.** For him: branding, product design and landing pages ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=250s)).
- **Ship the minimum.** He launched DesignJoy in a day and tests whether an idea "bites" before he invests more ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=359s)).
- **Launch, then build in public.** Product Hunt brought the first clients. Twitter later became the main lead source ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)).
