Greg Isenberg is a serial founder who runs Late Checkout, a holding company of community-led businesses.

## Who he is

- He sold Islands, "a Discord for college students", to WeWork and became head of product at WeWork [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s).
- After WeWork ran out of cash, he chose profitable, community-led businesses over venture-backed ones [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s).
- Alex Lieberman names him as an example of a founder who builds around his edge: he is known for community, so he launched a paid community for community builders [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=958s).
- His podcast interview with Joseph Choi taught Jay of NomadTable about multi-account UGC [source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) [51:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3097s).

## Businesses

- [Late Checkout](../businesses/late-checkout.md): eight figures of revenue and mid seven figures of profit (2024-06). Pat Walls says over $10M/year revenue and over $5M/year profit [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s).
- [LCA](../businesses/lca.md), an innovation agency: about $1.5M/year per client on average. Year one made $1.5M from 1.5 clients. Year two made $5M+ (2024-06) [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=387s).
- Dispatch, a design subscription: seven figures in year one with $450K profit (2024-06) [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s).
- Boring Marketing's AI SEO tool: $350K profit in year one, forecast at $2M to $3M profit in year two (2024-06) [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=509s).
- Also You Probably Need a Robot (AI community), Boring Ads and a multipreneur community. Nice Break, built product-first, was shut down [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=448s).

## Repeated advice

- **ACP funnel.** Build an audience first, move part of it into a community, then build the product it wants [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s).
- **Price on outcome.** LCA charges about $1.5M/year where a typical agency charges $1,500/month [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=0s).
- **Multipreneur model.** Several businesses at $25K/month each insure against one platform change. Once a business makes $100K profit, hire an operator and start the next [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=130s), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=645s).
- **Hire from your community.** He recruits the most active members and cold DMs interesting people [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=529s).
- **Mine Reddit and YouTube for ideas** with RedditList, GummySearch and VidIQ [source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=569s).
