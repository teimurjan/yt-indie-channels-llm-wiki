Kelechi is a 22-year-old founder from Nigeria who built two consumer apps with $1.5M in total revenue in 12 months.

## Who he is

- He moved to the US with $100 ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md)).
- His first app, Casp, a group photo app, got a few thousand users and then failed. A dating problem led him to an OpenAI script that wrote texts, and friends at homecoming loved it ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=124s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=168s)).

## Businesses

- [Social Wizard](../businesses/social-wizard.md): an AI texting and social-skills app for young men aged 16 to 24.
  - Launched early 2024. Peak about $60K/month, over 600K downloads (2025-10) ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s)).
  - After 4 weeks of tests he found a viral format. MRR was $3.5K 2 weeks later and over $25K a month after that. Cash collected was about $500K by the end of 2024 ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=184s)).
  - About $10/week, $20/month or $80/year ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=674s)).
- [Clean Eats](../businesses/clean-eats.md): a food barcode scanner for women aged 18 to 28. About $10K in 2 weeks, sold to a UK company about 4 months later ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=95s)).
- Both apps: over 700K downloads. Margins over 90% after Apple's cut, infrastructure $1K to $2K/month (2025-10) ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=21s), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=765s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Show, do not tell.** In the viral format, a streamer puts a girl's Instagram story into Social Wizard and reads the AI reply. Nobody pitches the app ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s)).
- **Find the format yourself, then hand it to creators.** He gave the proven format to small streamers with 100K to 200K views. One $120 collab got 2M views ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=358s)).
- **Creator outreach is a volume game.** Expect about 100 creators to get one that works ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=472s)).
- **Test on TikTok.** Start with 3 videos a day across 2 accounts, then 10, then 20 a day ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=533s)).
- **Use adjacent creators.** Fortnite and reaction streamers reached his audience. A fitness app can use cooking creators ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=596s)).
- **Sell the outcome.** Both apps sell confidence. "This food hurts your skin" beats "healthy alternatives" ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=674s)).
