Caleb Dean is an app founder who sold his ranked running app Runafy after 26 days of revenue.

## Who he is

- His first app, Yuri Brave, helped people with shy bladder. He built it on FlutterFlow. It made $5K to $10K, mostly from ASO, with a 30%+ conversion rate ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=701s), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=761s)).
- He became CMO at [Blake Anderson](blake-anderson.md)'s 10X. He pitched with a Notion page that listed 50 influencers with CPMs and was hired within 48 hours ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=880s)).

## Businesses

- [Runafy](../businesses/runafy.md), a ranked running app. He sold it at about 4.5x to 5x ARR on about $3K MRR, a six-figure sale. He kept 30% equity and got a 6-month earnout plus a cash bonus (2026-03) ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=16s), [46:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2797s), [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2837s)).
- Validation: under 50 copied videos in 2 weeks gave 2,000 waitlist sign-ups and 90+ payments of $5 ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1472s)).
- App Store pre-orders gave 3,000 downloads in about 2 weeks ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=536s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Copy a proven format into an adjacent niche.** Liftoff ranks gym lifts, so Runafy ranks runs ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [22:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1346s)).
- **Charge before you build.** A one-line page and a $5 Stripe link validated demand ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1558s)). See [idea validation](../topics/idea-validation.md).
- **Post 9 Reels a day on Instagram.** Instagram did not punish volume. TikTok views fell to about 5 ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1796s)).
- **Sell the outcome in onboarding.** The pitch moved from "fun ranks" to "become a better runner", and conversion rose ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [33:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2027s)).
- **Pick niches where 1 to 3 apps make $100K+/month,** and discount VC-funded leaders ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [54:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3246s)).
- **Build in public.** Tweets from a 200-follower account drew his acquirer ([source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [44:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2668s)).
