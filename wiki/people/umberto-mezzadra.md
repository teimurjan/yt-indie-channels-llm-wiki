Umberto Mezzadra is the founder of Floga, a yoga app that made over $120K in 24 hours from a lifetime-deal pre-launch.

## Who

- He has an economics degree. His seed-funded startup failed in 2012. He then worked as a ski instructor and fashion photographer ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=166s)).
- From 2016 he worked as an ad and growth strategist ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=166s)).
- He is not a developer. He hired one a few months after he sketched the app ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=223s)).

## Businesses

- **Play Pause Be.** A yoga card deck brand he co-founded in 2020. Its first Kickstarter raised over $200K in its first month ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=166s)).
- **[Floga](../businesses/floga.md).** A mobile app for yoga teachers and practitioners. He pre-launched it on 2025-05-05 with a lifetime deal. The app was not finished. It made $117K that day and over $120K in 24 hours, from about 500 to 600 buyers ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=83s), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s)). It then moved to subscriptions and made $9K to $10K/month with about 4,000 active users (2026-03) ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=116s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Monetize early.** Define the minimum feature set that makes people buy the lifetime deal. Build the product and the revenue engine at the same time ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=298s)).
- **Warm up the list for weeks.** He sent curiosity emails for 5 weeks. They told a story, teased a hidden item, then revealed the app with a demo video ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=516s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=578s)).
- **Hide the price until launch day.** "Never show the price before the actual launch date." People then judge features and vision ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=578s), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=638s)).
- **Use decoy tiers and scarcity.** Tiers of about $109, $199 and $349 anchor the top tier. Limit spots and keep the window to 5 to 7 days ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=838s), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=899s)).
- **Demo honestly, with no refunds.** Show every current feature and the roadmap ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s)).
- **Lifetime buyers are a feedback group.** They paid up front, report bugs and suggest features. Floga put them in a Telegram group ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s)). "Monthly subscribers have optionality. Lifetime buyer has commitment." ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s))
- **People who hate subscriptions pay more.** They pay 3 to 5 times a yearly plan for lifetime access. The founder gets capital with no equity given away ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s)).
- "Perfection is just fear disguised as preparation." ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [19:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1168s))
