Diego is the solo founder of App Alchemy, an AI tool for mobile app design. He grew it with Reddit posts and no audience.

## Who he is

- At his first message to Pat Walls, he had about 70 followers on X. At recording he had fewer than 100 (2025-07) ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=0s), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=266s)).
- The idea came from his own problem. An Upwork designer charged hundreds of dollars and extra for each small change ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=90s)).

## Businesses

- [App Alchemy](../businesses/app-alchemy.md): "like Cursor but for designing mobile apps".
  - About $17K/month, 4 months after launch, with $0 on marketing (2025-07) ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=55s)).
  - More than 1K paying customers and about 20K signups from Reddit (2025-07) ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=248s)).
  - MVP in about 2 weeks. AI API costs about $2.5K/month, margins about 70% (2025-07) ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=116s), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=633s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build with popular tools.** AI writes better code for the most-used libraries ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=143s)).
- **Five-step Reddit playbook** ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=326s), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=372s), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=557s)):
  1. Warm up your account with daily use.
  2. List many subreddits. The Reddit Ads targeting search finds related communities, and you do not have to pay for ads.
  3. Write a value-first post with a headline that does not announce the product. Put the link at the end.
  4. Cross-post at volume. 10 posts at about 10K views each give about 100K views.
  5. Repeat 2 to 3 times a week and vary the format. More risks a ban.
- **Mention a complementary product** such as Cursor, so the post looks less like an ad ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=496s)).
- **Avoid launch-post patterns.** A post with the product in the headline and a feature list got little engagement ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=496s)).
- **Pick a growing market** and spend early time on marketing, not on features no one asked for ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=656s)).
