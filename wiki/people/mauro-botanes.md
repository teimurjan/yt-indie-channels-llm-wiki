Mauro Botanes is a 22-year-old founder who built Symmetry, a gym tracking app for Spanish speakers. UGC and constant A/B tests took it to about $160K/month.

## Who he is

- He and his co-founders were fitness YouTubers before Symmetry. The company started 2 years before the video ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=878s)).

## Businesses

- [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md): a gym tracking app for Spain and Mexico.
  - About $160K/month and about 3M downloads (2026-09) ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=106s)).
  - Launched June 6 of the prior year. Only about 30K downloads that summer, then a spike in January ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=185s)).
  - UGC in the last year: nearly 80,000 videos, more than 500 accounts and nearly 8B views. Creators get nearly half of revenue (2026-09) ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Find the channel that works.** The YouTube community and influencers failed. UGC worked, so they doubled down ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=224s)).
- **Build in three phases.** What we wanted (terrible), what users asked for (mediocre), then a scientific method ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [5:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=332s)).
- **Test high-traffic screens.** A theme toggle touches about 2% of daily users, and a paywall touches most ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=361s)).
- **Pick the metric that leads revenue.** For a gym app, activation is a logged workout ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=453s)).
- **ARPU by country sets creator pay** per download ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=539s)).
- **Experiment loop.** Start from a user-interview problem. Write hypotheses and guardrail metrics. Run each test at least 2 weeks. Drop areas where tests are inconclusive ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=663s)).
- **Most tests do nothing.** Two home-screen redesigns moved nothing. One commitment screen raised retention by 3% ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)).
