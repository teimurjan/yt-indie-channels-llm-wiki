Josh heads M&A at Blue Throne, a firm that buys and scales consumer apps.

## Who he is

- Before Blue Throne he spent 3 years buying game studios for Plarium, maker of Raid: Shadow Legends ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md)).

## Businesses

- [Blue Throne](../businesses/blue-throne.md): buys apps that make about $400K to $15M a year. It has 70 staff (2026-07) ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [41:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2509s), [46:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2799s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Two paths to $100M+.** A fast grower at 30% to 50% EBITDA can get up to 6x run rate, so about $17M ARR is enough. A slow grower needs $30M to $33M ARR (2026-07) ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=373s)). See [acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).
- **Own a narrow category.** [Flo](../businesses/flo.md) narrowed health to period tracking ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=560s)).
- **Build for locals, not tourists.** Resubscribe rate beats DAU. Year one: 50% is amazing, 20% is bad ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s)). See [retention](../topics/retention.md).
- **Earn organic.** 50% organic installs is great. Under 20% is a UA treadmill ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1216s)). He counts TikTok UGC as paid ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [21:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1287s)).
- **Charge premium prices.** ChatGPT reset the anchor to $20/month ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [24:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1487s)).
- **Find the price ceiling.** Below $10/month, test higher prices on 10% to 20% of traffic ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1861s)). See [pricing](../topics/pricing.md).
- **Build the story for one buyer type.** Strategics pay the most. Private equity wants cash flow ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [35:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2118s)).
- **Sell before you need to.** Twelve months of growth reads as a machine ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2591s)).
- **Creator-led apps sell hard.** A buyer needs the creator to stay on ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [28:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1721s)).
