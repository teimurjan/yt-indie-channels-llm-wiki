Max Hirsch is a first-time app founder who built [Maxed AI](../businesses/maxed-ai.md), an AI lifting and health coach, with Claude Code.

## Who he is

A University of Michigan graduate who did finance internships and had never shipped an app. He saw non-coders build apps and bought a Claude Code plan. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=238s)

## Businesses

- **Maxed AI.** An all-in-one lifting and health coach. It makes about $14K to $15K a month with 1,000 active subscribers (2026-10). [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s). He saw the gap: running has Strava and Nike Run Club, but lifters spread across several apps. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=161s)
- **Dish Rate.** An earlier food review app that stalled, because he did not know how to build it. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=238s)

## Repeated advice

- **Hard paywall from the start.** He planned a free app with ads. A mentor he met on Instagram told him to hard paywall it. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s)
- **Charge yearly, upfront.** He charges $24.99 a year upfront. He does not want revenue that depends on people who forget to cancel. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=89s)
- **Film yourself first.** He posted 5 to 10 videos a day and checked hold rate and skip rate. The winners became paid ads. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s)
- **Skip UGC creators early.** Until you know which angles drive downloads, creators burn money. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s)
- **Meta over TikTok for paid ads.** Meta buyers show higher intent and pay more, in his view. He spends about $250 a day (2026-10). [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=435s), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s)
- **Build in public.** About a third of revenue comes from his X followers, after 3 months of tweets. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s)
- "Go all in, film yourself, annoy the crap out of everyone around you." [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=628s)
