Alfie Dickens ran the influencer program at Cal AI and grew it to more than 200 creators posting at once.

## Who

- He fixed chairs at a Minnesota soccer stadium, then got a two-week trial at [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) a month or two after it started ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md): over about two years, creators grew from 20 to 30 to 200+ at once, each posting about four videos a month. Cal AI peaked at about $60M/year and was later acquired (2026-08) ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=191s), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=254s)).
- Outreach: about 25 assistants sent about 2,000 cold emails a day. They contacted about 220,000 creators and signed about 2,000 to 3,000 (2026-08) ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [34:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2075s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build each deal around the golden moment.** The food photo scan shows the app without a hard sell. No script ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=373s)).
- **Show the app in the first 15 seconds** ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1340s)).
- **Minimum view clause**: a paid floor a little below the creator's claim, such as 700K to 800K for 1M ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=575s)).
- **It is a numbers game.** Of 100 contacts, about 10 reply, 2 book a call and 1 signs. Reality is closer to 1 per 1,000 ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [35:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2137s)).
- **Pick the right sub-niche.** Day-in-the-life videos from big creators and moms converted, some at a $10 CPM. Recipe, cooking and mukbang creators did not ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [17:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1048s), [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1171s)).
- **Email beats DMs at scale**, and a call makes a good deal about 10x more likely ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [38:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2298s), [44:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2659s)).
- **Go around agents** with group chats and creator referrals of $100 to $200 per signed friend ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [45:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2733s)).
- Influencer deals at $1 to $2 CPM got much harder in the 6 months before 2026-08, so he now runs UGC next to influencers ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [48:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2926s), [49:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2994s)).
