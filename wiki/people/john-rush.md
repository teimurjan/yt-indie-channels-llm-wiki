John Rush is a bootstrapped founder who runs a portfolio of about 26 tools for founders, with about $3M ARR combined.

## Who

- He spent 10 years in VC-backed startups, then moved to bootstrapping in 2022 ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md). Pat later cites him as the model for [Katie Keith](katie-keith.md)'s multi-product cross-selling at [Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md) ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=713s), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=899s)).

## Businesses

- Portfolio: 26 startups, about 1M B2B users and about $3M ARR, roughly $250K/month (2025-05). Seven products earn most of the revenue ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=58s)).
- [Unicorn Platform](../businesses/unicorn-platform.md): a website builder he bought for almost $1M in 2022. The seller mentored him for a year. 600,000 users (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=103s), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).
- [SEO Bot](../businesses/seo-bot.md): $100K MRR in under 12 months, with $30K/month in AI API costs, about a 70% margin (2025-05). Non-AI tools run at about 90% ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s)).
- [Listing Bot](../businesses/listing-bot.md): submits products to directories ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Validation blueprint.** Start from a pain at work. Post about it. Open a waitlist and aim for 100 signups. Email a 90%-off pre-sale. At 5 sales, deliver by hand. Build only when users are happy ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=215s), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=275s)).
- **Co-maker model.** Pitch a pre-sold idea to a maker who failed at something similar. 50/50 ownership: he runs operations, legal and accounting. They do code and support. Build for 2 to 3 months, then launch on Twitter ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=317s)).
- **Marketing order:** SEO, then the same post every day on X, LinkedIn, Substack and Facebook (at least 30 tweets a month), then directories, then cross-promotion ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s)).
- **Integrate, do not just link.** A "boost my domain rating" button in SEO Bot opens Listing Bot. "Every user that tries one of my product ends up trying at least one more." ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=538s), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=576s)).
- **[Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md).** Before, 90% of his products failed. After, only 10% did ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=906s)).
- **Keep human support.** People build a relationship with humans. With an AI agent they ask and leave ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s)).
- "In a VC world, you pay for growth. In a bootstrap world, you want to have product-led growth." ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=813s)).
