Bohdan "Bo" Drozdov is the marketing and business co-founder of Savvy Nomad, a tax-domicile service for US citizens who live abroad.

## Who he is

- He rebuilt his life after the war in Ukraine. Before Savvy Nomad he worked on a digital-nomad tax startup that he shut down ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=216s)).
- His co-founder met California surfers abroad who still paid California state tax and built the first version. Bo joined for marketing and business ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=810s)).

## Businesses

- [Savvy Nomad](../businesses/savvy-nomad.md): helps US citizens abroad change their state domicile, so they stop paying state taxes.
  - $1.7M ARR, $140K MRR, over 1,400 customers and six employees (2026-05) ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=0s), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s)).
  - Adds 120 to 150 customers a month and charges up front (2026-05) ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s)).
  - About $10M in state taxes saved for customers (2026-05) ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=60s)).
  - The whole product runs on Bubble, so non-engineers can operate it ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Boring niches pay.** Tax and compliance have real demand, high willingness to pay and few capable founders ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=272s)).
- **Show the value next to the price.** Onboarding asks qualifying questions, estimates the savings, then recommends a plan ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=317s), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=713s)).
- **Start where people are stressed and already overpay.** Check whether they DIY from blogs and Reddit or pay a professional thousands. Weigh competition against opportunity, not only market size ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=545s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=596s)).
- **Productize one painful workflow,** with a recurring layer on top. Savvy Nomad does only the state domicile change ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=656s)).
- **Ideas he gives away** ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=387s), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=449s)):
  - Productized immigration: agencies charge $8K to $15K, and a guided product with a lawyer review can charge $1K to $2K.
  - Entry to tax-friendly places (Dubai, Costa Rica, Malaysia) and exit from sticky ones (Canada, UK, Spain).
  - International banking, asset holding and estate planning: one customer with several revenue events.
