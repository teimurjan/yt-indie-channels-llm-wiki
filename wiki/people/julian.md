Julian is a developer from Argentina, living in Australia, who co-founded the AI gym workout app Gravl.

## Who

- Before Gravl, he and his partners ran an influencer marketing startup for mobile games that became an agency. It taught them user acquisition and showed how much fitness apps earn [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=0s).
- In year one he did development, while Matias and Aaron ran growth and ads [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s).

## Businesses

- [Gravl](../businesses/gravl.md): an AI app that builds gym workouts. Over $440K last month and over 70,000 subscribers, about 2 years after launch (2025-11) [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=71s).
  - The MVP took 2 to 3 months. It started as a tracker and became a workout generator [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=241s).
  - Team of 13 to 14 with part-timers. Salaries are $50K to $80K/month. Ad spend is about a third of revenue (2025-11) [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=757s), [13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=803s).

## Repeated advice

- **Clone a good UX and fix the core.** "We need to build Fitbod UI UX with an actual proper workout engine" [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=173s).
- **Launch on [Reddit](../topics/reddit-marketing.md) with a build story.** A technical post got over 300K impressions and the first few thousand users [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=291s).
- **Validate payment before ads,** then buy cheap traffic. The founders translated the app and ran Meta ads in South America at under $50/day [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=424s), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s).
- **Creative volume is the hard part.** Pay creators as little as $50 a piece, use AI video and CapCut, and copy what competitors spend most on in the Meta Ads Library [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=534s).
- **Long onboarding, then a hard paywall,** and show the AI: "Make sure that you are showing the users that this is an AI app" [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=576s).
- **Human support 24/7** through in-app chat [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=700s).
- **Know when to quit.** Keeping the agency for an extra year was a mistake [source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) [13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=837s).
