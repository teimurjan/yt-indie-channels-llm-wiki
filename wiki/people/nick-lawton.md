Nick Lawton is a co-founder of SideShift, a marketplace where apps run UGC creator campaigns.

## Who

- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) on the Superwall podcast ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md): top brands spend $250K to over $700K per month on it. Cursor is a customer (2026-08) ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=0s), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=339s)).

## Repeated advice

- Research competitors on TikTok and Instagram, then remix a winning format by 10% to 20% ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=402s), [11:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=719s)).
- Pay creators $600 to $800 base for 30 to 60 videos plus a $1 to $2 CPM view bonus (2026-08) ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=472s)).
- Content market fit is 5 or 6 videos over 10K views in the first campaign. Then put 90% of creators on the winner and keep 10% testing ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=571s), [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1242s)).
- Judge by comments, not views. AI UGC gets "is this AI?". Real creators get "what is the app?" ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s)). See [UGC](../topics/ugc.md).
- Organic finds winners. Paid scales them. Put spend behind videos with about 5% engagement, starting near $100/day over 14 days. Large spenders put about 80% into performance ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [26:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1568s), [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s), [30:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1815s)).
- Use Meta over TikTok Spark for scale: Meta has an install CTA and attribution ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2000s)).
- Warm up new accounts with CTA-free, controversial posts first ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1129s)).
