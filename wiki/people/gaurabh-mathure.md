Gaurabh Mathure is a product designer who built 72 apps in 60 days for $0, then made about $6,500 in the Starter Story Challenge once he stopped building and started selling.

## Who

- A product designer who built products for Nike and Samsung ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=65s)).
- Coached by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md), who named the problem as an addiction to building, shiny object syndrome and fear of rejection ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)).

## Businesses

- [Attention Design](../businesses/attention-design.md): an inspiration library for YouTube creators. Pro has 5 customers at $99 (2026-08) ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=100s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s)).
- [VueMagnet](../businesses/vuemagnet.md): a QR code and lead-magnet platform for video creators. Its agency plan brought most of the revenue ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=629s)).
- Revenue: $100 (one $99 sale) in the first 30 days. About $6,500 after 60 days (2026-08) ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s)).

## Repeated advice

- Stop building. One 15-minute customer call is worth more than 70 apps ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)).
- Post on every platform, then focus on the one that shows signal. For him it was LinkedIn ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s)).
- Break the pattern: vlog-style video stood out on LinkedIn ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s)).
- Re-target to customers who pay: he moved from YouTubers to experts who want a personal brand ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=729s)).
- Do things that do not scale: free mock-ups and personalized demos in cold outreach ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=550s)).
- Live the problem you solve and start where you have a network ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=669s)).
