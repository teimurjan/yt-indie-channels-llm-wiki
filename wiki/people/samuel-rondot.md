Samuel Rondot is a former optician who runs three SaaS products that copy proven tools.

## Who he is

- He learned to code from one 15-hour YouTube course ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=72s)).

## Businesses

- Combined: about $35K/month (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=58s)).
- [useArtemis](../businesses/useartemis.md): LinkedIn scraping and email finding. About $15K/month and 10,000 customers (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
- [StoryShort](../businesses/storyshort.md): AI faceless video generator. About $20K/month and 4,000 customers (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).
- [Capacity](../businesses/capacity.md): AI coding tool. About $900/month and 50 users (2025-07) ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=454s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Copy what already makes money,** then make it a little better ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=173s)). See [competitor alternatives](../topics/competitor-alternatives.md).
- **Four idea filters.** He uses it. It already works. Demand is real without big marketing. It is simple to maintain ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=173s)).
- **Find proof on X.** MRR screenshots are his best traction signal ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=239s)).
- **Check traffic in Ahrefs.** Prefer competitors that live on paid ads. StoryShort copied a tool with 100% Facebook ad traffic ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)).
- **Ads first, then SEO.** SEO compounds and adds value at sale time ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=695s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).
- **Use affiliates** for a known cost per customer ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=558s)).
- **Ship an MVP in two weeks.** Skip password reset and settings pages ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=668s)).
