Matt Gittleson runs the UGC creator program at [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md), which acqui-hired him from his own student startup.

## Who

- He vibe coded a citation-checker startup for students and grew it with his own TikToks ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md)).
- His first real TikTok (December 2024) got about 245K views. Before it he posted only 5 memes ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1164s)).
- Jenni first DMed him to hire his "creator". The acquisition took about 5 months from February to close ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [53:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3197s)).
- He works 7 days a week, at least 16 hours a day ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=321s)).

## Businesses

- **[Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md).** An AI essay-writing tool. About $775K MRR and 5 to 6M users (2025-11) ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=26s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=163s)).
  - His UGC program in its first 3 months: 0 to 150 creators and 0 to 75M views at under $2 CPM (2025-11) ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=297s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=810s)).
  - The best creator came from beauty with no UGC history. She needed 25 videos to pass 10K views, then made over $5K in one month from bonuses ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=691s)).
- **His citation-checker startup.** Acqui-hired by Jenni ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md)).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick creators for camera charisma, not stats.** Look for emotive delivery, an aesthetic first frame and a "yapper" who tells stories ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=409s)).
- **Hire one exceptional skill, teach the rest.** Expect weeks of coaching ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=691s)).
- **Casual cold DMs with specific proof.** Write in lowercase for college creators ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=871s)).
- **Founder content first.** Without traction you have no proof for creators ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1067s)).
- **Build "viral sense".** Scroll 30 minutes a day inside your niche and 30 minutes outside it ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [22:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1352s)).
- **Iterate, do not clone.** A frame-by-frame copy of a rival's creators flopped ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [25:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1520s)).
- **Engagement bait over rage bait.** A take that splits viewers in half fills the comments. One clip got 30M views on X this way ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [31:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1867s), [28:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1690s)).
- **Name the product.** A viral video that does not name Jenni is useless ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [33:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2020s)).
