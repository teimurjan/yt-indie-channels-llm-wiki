Guillaume Moubeche is the founder of lemlist, a cold email tool that he started with $1,000 and grew to $30M ARR.

## Who

- He grew up in Paris with little money ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=0s)).
- He spent about a year at a friend's lead generation agency and booked meetings for tens of companies ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=346s)).
- "I think deep inside I had a voice that wanted to prove everyone wrong." ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=482s))

## Businesses

- **A t-shirt business** with his father. It sold six shirts ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=0s)).
- **A website personalization startup** built by junior developers in Russia. It cost $4,000 to $5,000, all his savings. LinkedIn changed one line of code, and the startup died ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=443s)).
- **[lemlist](../businesses/lemlist.md).** He started it with $1,000 in the bank. The first MVP took two weeks. The first month made $600 ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=482s), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=643s), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=706s)). ARR reached $250K after year one, $1M in year two and $10M at three and a half years ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=836s)). Growth then stalled near $10M, and two co-founders left ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=932s)). By 2024-12 it made $30M ARR and $10M EBITDA, with about 100 staff and a valuation above $150M ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1092s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick a red ocean.** Competitors prove that product-market fit exists. You only need to be better on one axis. For lemlist, that axis was personalization tied to meetings booked ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=542s)).
- **Do the onboarding for early buyers.** He wrote their first campaigns himself. In return they appeared as success stories in his content ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=643s)).
- **Run a founder-as-user loop.** Use the product daily, publish the best content into a community, collect complaints, improve, repeat ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=706s)).
- **Fix activation even at a cost.** A redesign lifted activation from 15% to 35%. Growth fell to 0% that month, then jumped about 60% the next ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=768s)).
- **Plan the next S-curve.** Growth is a series of S-curves with plateaus, never a smooth exponential ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=899s)).
- **Find the magnet persona.** These users never churn and make others want to join. For lemlist this was sales reps ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1031s)). See [Retention](../topics/retention.md).
- "You got to be patient with the results but you got to be impatient with your action." ([source](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [19:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1152s))
