# George Lampropoulos

George Lampropoulos is an 18-year-old college freshman who cannot code and built the AI wrestling coach app [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md).

## Who

- At 15 he lost his savings on a self-improvement app. Three dev agencies took 1.5 years to ship it, and his 10K-follower waitlist lost interest ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=150s)).
- He builds with Rork, ChatGPT and a $250 Fiverr developer ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=220s)).

## Businesses

- [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md), co-founded with wrestling influencer [Kaden Henshaw](kaden-henshaw.md): about $17K/month (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s)).
- Green, a second app with a 1M-follower influencer. It got about 100 downloads from 1.8M impressions because it was not novel ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=361s), [7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=421s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Viral by nature.** An idea needs uniqueness, helpfulness and a "gotcha moment" that shows value in about 5 seconds ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=361s)).
- **Copy onboarding that converts.** Study Opal and [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md): educate, personalize, create FOMO, then hide the result behind the paywall ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s)).
- **Influencer outreach.** DM 100 people a day, open with "Paid promo?", and close on the phone ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=756s)).
- **Deal terms.** Pay 20% to 50% up front for 4 to 5 videos with a view guarantee at $2 to $5 CPM ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [13:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=812s)).
- **Hire for what you cannot do.** Test a hire on a small task first ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=559s)).
- He takes no money out until $100K MRR ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=614s)).
