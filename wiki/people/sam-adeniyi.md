Sam Adeniyi is a university student with no coding background who vibe coded [Algrow](../businesses/algrow.md), a SaaS for finding viral short-form formats.

## Who he is

- He made about $10K from affiliate marketing with faceless texting-story videos, then moved into YouTube automation ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=195s)).
- He built the first MVP in about a week by pasting ChatGPT code into Notepad, then VS Code, then Cursor ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=243s)).

## Businesses

- [Algrow](../businesses/algrow.md): $25, $45 and $80 per month plans. About $13K to $14K and 480 new customers in the last four weeks, over 10,000 users (2026-01) ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=101s), [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=157s)).
- The first 400 users came from Discord ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=69s)).

## Repeated advice

- Find niche communities with Disboard and watch for repeated questions ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=331s)).
- Screen-share your tool silently in voice chats until people ask about it ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=376s)).
- Help, do not pitch. Most servers ban self-promotion ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=504s)).
- Five steps: find the community, listen (paste chat history into ChatGPT for pain points), DM a short Loom, build with users, give early users free access ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=558s), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=673s)).
- Tell Cursor to plan for 100,000 users ([source](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [12:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=778s)).
