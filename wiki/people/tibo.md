Tibo is a French founder who built and sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio and now runs a portfolio of SaaS products.

## Who

- He built and sold Tweet Hunter and Taplio. Elon Musk's takeover of X almost killed Tweet Hunter when it made $200K/month [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=981s).
- His portfolio makes about $700K/month from 50K paying customers, and grew about 20% a month for over 6 months (2025-12) [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=166s).

## Businesses

All figures are from 2025-12.

- [Revid AI](../businesses/revid-ai.md): turns video or text into engaging videos. About $400K/month, growing about 10% a month [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s).
- [Outrank](../businesses/outrank.md): a blog post generator that is becoming an all-in-one SEO tool. ## Repeated advice

His 12-step playbook: steps 1 to 8 find stickiness with a few real users, steps 9 to 12 find and scale one or two channels [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=377s).

- **Ship an MVP in days or weeks.** He estimates a 90% failure rate, so a year per try means 9 years to a win. Use Bubble or boilerplates [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=377s), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=423s).
- **Talk to 5 to 10 real users every day.** "If your mom is testing your idea... there is absolutely no real knowledge that you can gain from that" [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=423s). Until a product reaches $10K/month, its support link opens his Twitter DMs [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s). See [customer-interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md).
- **Ship requests fast.** A feature shipped within an hour or two turns a user into an advocate [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=593s).
- **Fix [retention](../topics/retention.md) before acquisition.** With low retention, 99% of new users leave [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=593s).
- **Become a media company.** Pick social, SEO or cold email, then scale with SEO, ads and affiliates. Growth usually comes from one or two channels [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=762s), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=871s).
- **Run a portfolio for resilience.** One AI release or platform change can kill one product, but not the company [source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=930s). See [app-portfolios](../topics/app-portfolios.md).
