This page covers "Nic", a name that two sources link to the slug `nic-weber`. Nic Weber co-founded Noise, a paid TikTok posting platform. The Nic of BlogToPin is a Ukrainian developer.

> Conflict: [_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) names Nic Weber, co-founder of Noise and Playbite. [hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) names only "Nic", a Ukrainian developer who built BlogToPin. Neither source links the two, so they are probably different people.

## Who

- **Nic Weber (Noise).** He built Noise as an internal growth hack for his earlier app, Playbite. Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=527s)).

## Repeated advice

### Nic Weber, on short-form growth

- **Volume finds the details.** "Eggs theory": food slideshows with eggs in the hook image got far more views ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=317s)).
- **Copy proven formats** and tune the first line. Nobody likes to be told what they need ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=681s), [15:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=927s)).
- **Two prongs.** Premium UGC with a soft CTA hunts for hits. Cheap slideshows with a hard CTA act like retargeting ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [25:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1538s)).
- **Pay by target install cost.** Noise gets about $0.30 installs on its own app (2026-01) ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=647s)).
- **Pause a playbook after 1 to 3 weeks without a hit** ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [29:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1792s)).

### Nic (BlogToPin), on micro SaaS

- **Seven-step [micro SaaS](../topics/micro-saas.md) playbook:** copy a workflow people already pay for. Read competitor reviews on G2, AppSumo and Capterra. Build a faster, cheaper MVP in 1 to 2 weeks. Talk to your ICP on Reddit, X and cold outreach. Treat the first customer as your employer. Repeat for a few dozen users. Improve churn and onboarding by 1% a day ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=584s), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=644s), [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=704s)).
- **The math.** One new customer a day with 10% monthly churn gives about 200 customers in a year. At $50 that is $10K MRR ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=765s)).
- **Drop the lifetime deal.** The LTD launch made $0. Subscriptions brought real customers within a month ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s)).
- **Asymmetric marketing.** Early articles, outreach, Reddit posts and backlinks now bring customers from SEO and ChatGPT "on autopilot" ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s)).
