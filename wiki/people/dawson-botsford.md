Dawson Botsford is a former Uber engineer who built Earni.fi, an Ethereum airdrop finder, and sold it to Bankless.

## Who

- A software engineer at [Uber](../businesses/uber.md) in 2016, before the IPO. He left, travelled for a year and lives in a Sprinter van in Boulder, Colorado ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=191s)).

## Businesses

- [Earnifi](../businesses/earnifi.md): shows Ethereum users the airdrops they did not claim.
  - Built in 4 to 5 hours at a month-long hackathon, after 2 to 3 weeks of thinking ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=300s)).
  - One launch tweet brought 10,000 signups in 48 hours ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)).
  - Over 250,000 free users, 5,000 paid users and over $1M ARR (2024-04) ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=55s)).
  - After 2 years solo, Bankless bought it from a Twitter DM and he became CTO ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=652s)). See [Acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).

## Repeated advice

- **Solve your own pain**, then build fast with conviction ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=300s)).
- **Put a demo video in the launch tweet** ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)).
- **The "anti-email" strategy.** Email only when an airdrop matches, with the dollar amount; then paywall every result ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=455s)). See [Paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).
- **Public call-outs.** "25 Days of Christmas" tagged a person each day with their unclaimed dollars ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=516s)).
- **Avoid the solo echo chamber** with co-working, communities and conferences ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=579s)).
