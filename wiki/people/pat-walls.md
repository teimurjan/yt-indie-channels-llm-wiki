Pat Walls is the founder and host of Starter Story, a YouTube channel and site of founder case studies, which he sold to HubSpot in 2026.

## Who

- A software developer who moved to New York for a six-figure job at a small startup about 10 years before the sale ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=37s)).
- He started Starter Story in summer 2017 at age 26, with 2 hours of deep work a day at a Starbucks before work ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=0s), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=180s)). He wrote a manifesto on a note card and gave himself 365 days ([xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=109s)).
- He quit with about $12K in savings. His first hire was his sister ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s); [2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=461s)).
- YC rejected him three times ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [14:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=854s)).
- He hosts most Starter Story interviews in this wiki. From 2026 producer Gus co-hosts and joins the closing discussion ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md); [NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md)). Guests include [CJ Zafir](../people/cj-zafir.md), [Blake Anderson](../people/blake-anderson.md), [Tibo](../people/tibo.md), [Yasser Elsaid](../people/yasser-elsaid.md), [Presh](../people/presh.md) and [Matt Robinson](../people/matt-robinson.md).

> Conflict: [T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md) says he started at 26 ([0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=0s)); [xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md) says he was nearly 30 ([0:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=12s)).

## Businesses

- [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md):
  - Month 1: bought starterstory.com for $12/year and launched with a few stories ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)).
  - Reddit self-posts with the full case study inside reached the front page again and again, until Redditors started a petition to ban the site. By then the email list had tens of thousands of subscribers ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=208s), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=420s)).
  - First sponsor: Klaviyo, with a $12,000 check ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=667s)).
  - Day 365: $3,500/month while still employed ([xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=488s)).
  - Over $1M a year and over 4,000 case studies (2024-01) ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=0s), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=307s)).
  - Over 100,000 YouTube subscribers; an email list of 250,000 to 300,000 that makes about $80K to $120K a month (2024-10) ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=544s), [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=667s)).
  - A 5-minute Loom on his Klaviyo flows became a product with about $20,000 in pre-orders in one day (2024-10) ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=31s)).
  - Sold to [HubSpot](../businesses/hubspot.md) for a private, "life-changing" price. He chose the buyer for alignment, and said the business had become his identity and a cage (2026-02) ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=573s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Distribution beats product.** Founders build something nobody cares about, then must learn to reach customers ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=445s)). "First-time founders think about product, second-time founders think about distribution." ([vbEKEWtnndU](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=720s)). "Building it is not the hard part." ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=777s)). See [distribution](../topics/distribution.md).
- **Talk to customers.** One 15-minute call is worth more than 70 apps ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)). Talking to customers is the mechanism to find where to market ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=572s)). AI makes building easy, so founders skip this step ([r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=855s)).
- **Use Reddit for the first customers, not scale.** Study top posts that quietly show off a startup and copy the format ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=373s)). "Reddit is not a platform to get scale." ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=885s)). See [Reddit marketing](../topics/reddit-marketing.md).
- **Treat YouTube and personal brand as distribution.** YouTube is the most important part of Starter Story ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=544s)). YouTube search is underrated ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=963s)). A personal brand is his highest-ROI channel and the part rivals cannot copy ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s); [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=680s)). Content is the moat when AI floods every niche with apps ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=1012s)).
- **Go all in on one channel.** ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=344s)). Intent search still converts even as informational queries move to ChatGPT ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=1070s)).
- **Serve one customer.** Multi-app portfolios work when all apps serve the same customer, so you can cross-sell ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=786s); [47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=540s); [spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=630s)). See [app portfolios](../topics/app-portfolios.md).
- **Build in a niche you know.** Contrast a golfer building for golf courses with founders who build for dentists without knowing dentistry ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=539s)). "Go to small community, solve a really, really niche problem" ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=863s)).
- **Boring B2B beats sexy apps.** "I'd rather make $2 million a year in a boring business than $20,000 a year in a sexy AI app." ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=810s)). Directories rarely work as B2C; sell to businesses with real pain ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)). Ask if a tool makes money, saves money or saves time ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=746s)). Turn repeated client work, such as spreadsheets, into products ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=278s)). See [B2B SaaS](../topics/b2b-saas.md) and [boring businesses](../topics/boring-businesses.md).
- **Market beats execution.** A growing market with good margins forgives mistakes, and even a weak app makes money on a rising trend ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=663s); [vbEKEWtnndU](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=996s)). Old markets with an AI twist grow slowly but last ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [16:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=972s)).
- **Ads test creative, not demand.** ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=669s)). See [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md).
- **Copy an app, but not its niche,** so you do not take the original's customers ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=488s)). Copy proven layouts; apps like Duolingo test onboarding for years ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s)).
- **Simpler wins.** Visualizee grew only after it became close to a ChatGPT wrapper ([TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=730s)). A handcrafted look that does not feel vibe coded helps an app spread ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=815s)).
- **Price a lifetime deal near 24 months of subscription revenue.** ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1046s)). See [lifetime deals](../topics/lifetime-deals.md).
- **Watch the ego.** "Am I building for customers or am I building for what other people who are not my customers will think about me?" ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [14:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=890s)). Demand comes first: "People just need to care." ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [15:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=907s)).
- **Keep the job until the side project is ready.** Daily focused hours compound; growth "never exploded overnight" ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s); [GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=187s)). See [side projects](../topics/side-projects.md).
