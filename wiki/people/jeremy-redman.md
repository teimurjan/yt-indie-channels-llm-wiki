Jeremy Redman is a Los Angeles founder who built and sold TaskMagic, a browser automation SaaS.

## Who he is

- He started non-technical, with a slow no-code app builder ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=260s)).
- He ran TaskMagic with one employee, his CTO. The company made the Inc. 5000 list ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=172s)).

## Businesses

- [TaskMagic](../businesses/taskmagic.md): browser automation for jobs that Zapier cannot reach through APIs.
  - Over 60,000 users, about 8,000 paying customers, about $3M a year (2026-04) ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=134s)).
  - Lifetime deals plus usage-based pricing, because users did not want constant subscriptions ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=228s)).
  - Sold on Acquire.com for a "mid upper seven figures" price. Over 100 buyers messaged him. The sale took 6 to 7 months ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=181s)).
  - During the sale he took on $200K of personal debt to buy out investors ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=696s)).
- [Mail Lead](../businesses/mail-lead.md): a simple cold email tool that brought in "almost seven figures" ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)).
- LeadQuest: a lead tool for Mail Lead users. It is his next core product ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=643s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Tentpole strategy.** Build small side products that rank on their own and funnel paid users to one core product ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=215s)).
- **Sell functionality, not information.** No-code tools make small products cheap to build ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=397s)).
- **Keep side products specific.** Specific products rank faster in search than general ones ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).
- **Build a natural upgrade path.** An "automation" button in Mail Lead opens TaskMagic ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=511s)).
- **Share the lows.** He warns against "toxically positive" founder culture ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=837s)).
