Rasty Turek is a former Google engineer and repeat founder who built Vault Air, an encrypted file app that grows through SEO.

## Who

- Grew up in a Slovak village of 700 people. Worked on Gmail at Google. Sold one company to Amazon, and sold Pex, a VC-backed copyright service, the year before (2026-10) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=295s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md) and Gus.

## Businesses

- [Vault Air](../businesses/vault-air.md): his first iOS app, built in about 3 weeks with coding agents. $66 in month one, $41K in one month about 6 months later, now about $2K to $3K per day (2026-10) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=7s), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=74s)).
- Prices $1.99/week, $19.99/year, $39.99 lifetime. About 60% of purchases are lifetime and bring more than 80% of revenue (2026-10) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)).
- gum, an open-source tool that gives CLI and MCP access to Google services ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=479s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick a proven, simple category.** Vault Air is "Dropbox, but encrypted" ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=342s)).
- **Utility apps convert slowly.** His lowest conversion is on day 1 and his highest near day 60, the opposite of [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s)).
- **Win on the web when the App Store is locked.** Old competitors rank first in the store, so he targets the same keywords with web guides. The web converts to the app at about 10% (2026-10) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=392s), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=452s)).
- **Daily SEO agent, one good guide a day.** Claude reads Keyword Planner and search data, finds keywords with impressions but no clicks, and publishes a guide. No mass AI content ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=479s), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=539s)).

> Conflict: Gus, in [NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), still thinks SEO is mostly dead and says it works here only because the niche is narrow ([14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=842s)).
