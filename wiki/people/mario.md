This page collects what the sources say about Mario, a co-founder of the app [PushScroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md).

## Who he is

- Mario is a software engineer. His earlier app reached about 5M downloads ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=147s)).
- He is one of two European founders of PushScroll. [Alejandro](alejandro.md) found him through YC co-founder matching after a viral TikTok ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=137s)).

## Businesses

### PushScroll

- The app blocks social media until the user does push-ups, squats or a plank ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=625s)).
- On-device pose detection with Google MediaPipe counts the push-ups. The team picked it over an LLM for speed ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=762s)).
- The team shipped a three-screen MVP in about 2 weeks ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=467s)).
- Revenue was over $30K/month 4 months after launch, all from organic content at first (2025-12) ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=84s)).
- The app made $30K profit in the month before an earlier interview (2025-10) ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:01:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=107s)).
- It had about 300,000 downloads and about 4,000 paying users, with a hard paywall at about $30/year (2025-12) ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=105s)).
- Its stack is Compose Multiplatform, Supabase, Superwall, Amplitude, Sentry and Claude Code ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=651s)).

## Repeated advice

The two interviews give the founders' shared advice. They do not split it by founder.

- **Make the video before the app**. A fake product demo got 80K views and about 500 comments that asked them to build it ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s)).
- **Keep the idea simple**. If you cannot explain the app in three words, it is too complex for TikTok ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=497s)).
- **Launch with scarcity**. A free lifetime deal ran for one week on Android and 48 hours on iOS ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1332s)).
- **Watch the skip rate**. Their best video had a 19% skip rate. At 30%, change the format ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:34:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2076s)).
- **Use a ManyChat trigger**. "Comment APP" sends a link by DM. The comment count also lifts reach ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:37:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2250s)).
- **Build onboarding on value**. A Quittr-style flow with goals, pain and research raised conversion from 7% to 16% ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:42:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2543s)).
- **Skip paid influencers early**. Their influencer test lost about $2K ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:29:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1744s)).
- **Post organic content yourself until $5K to $10K MRR**. You then learn which formats sell. Scale with creators and ads after that ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=571s)).
