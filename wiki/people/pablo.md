Pablo is a co-leader of Social, a profitable app studio with 22 apps at $16M annualized revenue.

## Who he is

- He runs Social with [Patrick Stuart-Constant](patrick-stuart-constant.md). [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) interviews them on the Superwall channel. ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=0s))

## Repeated advice

- **Buy apps after the viral peak.** Sellers of declining apps often do not know how to grow them. ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s))
- **Run the LTV/CAC loop.** Tests raise revenue per user, which funds more acquisition. Average revenue per user rose about 3x in 18 months (2026-07). ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=808s))
- **Expect most tests to fail.** Out of 20 A/B tests: 10 neutral, 5 bad, 3 interesting, 2 wins. Retest old losers. ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=898s), [16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=977s))
- **If later placements convert well, removing the onboarding paywall can make more money**, as on Celebs. ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s))
- **Tag each test with its hypothesis** so agents and new hires can learn from it. ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1138s))
- **Humans make ideas, AI makes ads.** About 4,000 AI video ads a month (2026-07). ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s), [41:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2460s))
