Ian Myers is the founder and CEO of Oceans, an offshore staffing company that grew to about $15M in revenue with no outside money.

## Who

- He left finance and a venture-backed app company. He wanted a business that served his life, not a board ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=0s)).
- "You don't have to be a billionaire to do that." ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=235s))

## Businesses

- **[Oceans](../businesses/oceans.md).** It places full-time offshore staff, mostly in Sri Lanka, inside client teams as executive assistants and operators ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- The first contract came in February 2022. Year one revenue was $650K. Last year it was about $15M (2025-03). The video title says $10M/year ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- Clients pay about $3,000 to $4,000/month per full-time person. Oceans pays wages and benefits and keeps the margin (2025-03) ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=155s)).
- It made millions in profit and raised no money ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)). It hires 30 to 40 people a month (2025-03) ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).

> Conflict: the title of [gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md) says $10M/year, while Ian says about $15M in revenue last year.

## Repeated advice

- **Improve a market that already exists.** "Go find something that people already want and do it differently or better." ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=366s))
- **Niche down in a commodity space.** He suggests offshoring for one vertical, such as video editing or CAD for architects. He says such a firm can reach $1M to $2M revenue ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=428s)).
- **Test small.** He trained a first cohort of three people in Sri Lanka. He sold to three friends at a discount, with no profit ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)).
- **Sell first to people you know.** His test: can he call someone tomorrow and get paid within days ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=565s))?
- **Build for referrals.** Make customers look like heroes to their friends. Oceans grew mostly through referrals, with no paid marketing ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=618s)).
- **Over-deliver for the first 20 to 50 clients.** Reply fast, comp months after mistakes and keep the founder on the phone ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=678s)).
- **Write processes down early.** The jump past 5 to 8 people broke everything ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).
- **Hire from unusual backgrounds.** His first US hire ran children's summer camps and is now chief of staff ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=871s)).
