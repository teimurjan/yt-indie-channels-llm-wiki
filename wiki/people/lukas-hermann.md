Lukas Hermann is a software developer in Germany who built [Stagetimer](../businesses/stagetimer.md), a remote-controlled countdown timer for live events.

## Who he is

- He got the idea by watching a friend walk to another room to start a timer on an old Flash app ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=0s)).
- He runs the business with his wife, a former teacher who learned marketing. She runs Google Ads, sales emails and support. He handles product, finance and development ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=189s)).

## Businesses

- [Stagetimer](../businesses/stagetimer.md): about $25K/month, 20K users, 4,400 paying or past paying customers and 86K unique visitors a month (2025-06) ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=46s)).
- Timeline: first commit in November 2020, MVP in 3 days, first dollar after 224 days. He quit his job in 2022 at about $3K/month and reached $10K/month in September 2023 ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=176s), [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=246s), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=307s)).
- Margin is 80% to 90%. Costs are $280 servers, $250 tools and $1,400 ads a month (2025-06) ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=591s)).

## Repeated advice

- Validate in a niche subreddit: post a link with no price and ask for feedback. One post per subreddit. His got 58 upvotes ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=79s)).
- Use the stack you know so you can ship in days ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s)).
- Write integration docs and videos for long-tail searches. Low views, but the searchers buy. About 50% of traffic comes from Google (2025-06) ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=461s)).
- Brand every shared link, and give freelancers a free tier they bring to client events ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)).
- Find ideas by watching people outside tech work in awkward ways ([source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=695s)).
