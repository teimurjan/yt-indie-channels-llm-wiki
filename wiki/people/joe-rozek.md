Joe Rozek is a former Shutterfly engineering manager who built Waitly, a waitlist and reservations app, into a solo business.

## Who

- A former engineering manager at Shutterfly [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=59s).
- The idea came at brunch in 2017, when a Yelp waitlist link forced him to download an app and sign up [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s).

## Businesses

- [Waitly](../businesses/waitly.md): over $40K/month (2025-09) [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=59s).
  - Yearly revenue: $0 in year 1, $14,500 in year 2, about $116K in year 3, $186K, $307K, $354K, and $445K in year 7. On pace for over $500K (2025-09) [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=242s).
  - 700 paying customers in the US and Canada, out of over 15,000 businesses that tried it (2025-09) [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=531s).
  - A flat $100/month for all features [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s).
  - During COVID, retail chains put it in over 700 locations, and the business grew 10x that year [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s).

## Repeated advice

- **Fix a friction you feel.** Waitly texts a link, so the guest needs no app and no account [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=112s).
- **Buy keywords while LTV beats CAC.** [Apple Search Ads](../topics/apple-search-ads.md) CAC is about $130 against $750 to $1,000 LTV (2025-09) [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s). See [unit-economics](../topics/unit-economics.md).
- **Nurture trials** with tip emails, quick-start videos, Zoom onboarding and an end-of-trial promotion [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s).
- **Beat giants on price, simplicity and support.** "Anytime that someone calls our support line, someone answers immediately" [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s).
- **Serve an underserved niche** in a solved problem, such as small restaurants. Build an MVP with AI in a couple of weeks and adapt to feedback [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=436s).
