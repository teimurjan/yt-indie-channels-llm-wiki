Hunter Hammonds is a founder who runs [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md), a studio that launches creator-led productized services.

## Who he is

- He started his first agency at 19. Next, he built a startup to 3.2M active users, but it never made money. Then he built Everest, an agency with eight figures of revenue and clients like Nike and Samsung. He burned out and took a 6-month sabbatical ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=170s)).
- He works 10 to 14 hours a day, 6 days a week (2023-09) ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=613s)).

## Businesses

- [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md): the plan is 12 businesses in 12 months, each aiming for a $1M run rate in its first 30 days. All four public companies were above $1M ARR (2023-09) ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s)).
- Portfolio: Hey Friends (YouTube agency with Ali Abdaal), Off Menu (design agency), Viral Cuts (short-form video agency with [Sam Parr](sam-parr.md) and Codie Sanchez) and Keyframe (animation studio with Sahil Bloom) ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=49s)).
- Team grew from 3 to over 50 people in 4 months (2023-09) ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)).

## Repeated advice

- Enter crowded markets with a strong brand, case studies, premium service and a trusted creator for launch distribution ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s)).
- Match the service to what the creator already teaches, and make creators equity partners, not affiliates ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)).
- Use fixed scope and a simple premium price. Never sell "unlimited" ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=425s)).
- Build a business, not "freelancing in disguise". Over-deliver to earn referrals ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=735s)).
- Plan the week by type: decisions, actions, messages, calls, then writing ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=613s)).
