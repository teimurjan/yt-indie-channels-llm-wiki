Joseph is a self-taught coder in Lakeland, Florida, who built [StealthGPT](../businesses/stealthgpt.md). The app writes AI text that AI detectors do not flag. The source gives only his first name.

## Who he is

He taught himself to code. At 23 he started J Squared Invest, a crypto onboarding firm that collapsed in 2018. The CFTC case took 2 to 3 years, ended in a $400K settlement and cost $50K in legal fees. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=217s), [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=278s)

## Businesses

- **StealthGPT.** A TikTok video about the GPTZero detector gave him the idea in January 2023. He bought the domain for $150 in February and had paying customers in March. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=345s), [6:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=407s)
- StealthGPT reached $190K MRR after about 15 months, with $10K to $30K/month in profit and about $100K/month in marketing spend (2024-07). [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)
- It had 400K lifetime signups and about 8,500 active subscribers (2024-07). [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=136s)
- **J Squared Invest.** The failed crypto firm. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=217s)

## Repeated advice

- **Ride a new behavior.** He expected AI content to flood the web and people to push back. He reverse engineered GPTZero for a few days until every output passed as human. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=345s)
- **List on AI directories.** A TikTok creator who tracks Futurepedia covered StealthGPT and brought the first two customers. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=465s)
- **Pour profit into Google Ads.** Early conversions cost $10 to $20, so revenue went back into ads, "gasoline on a fire". [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)
- **Solve broad problems in one click.** Refuse niche features that cost hundreds of hours for one user. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=579s), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=639s)
- **Hire from Twitter.** All developer hires came from Twitter posts, with no pedigree needed. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=701s)
- **Marketing first.** Without distribution, the product does not matter. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=856s)
