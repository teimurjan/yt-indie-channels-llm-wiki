Mau Baron is a self-taught app developer. He built Prayer Lock, an iOS app that blocks the phone until the user prays.

## Who he is

- A finance graduate who learned to code with AI ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=71s)).
- He spent 3 months on apps nobody wanted before he found his idea ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=170s)).

## Businesses

- [Prayer Lock](../businesses/prayer-lock.md): he coded it in 3 days with Cursor and ChatGPT in June 2025. It has no login and no database ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=236s)).
  - $10K in October, $15K in November and $21K in December 2025 ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s)).
  - About 58K downloads and 6.7K ratings at 4.9 stars (2026-01) ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=71s)).
  - $49.99/year or $9.99/week. Conversion rate 43% (2026-01) ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=92s)).
  - In December he spent about $9K on ads and made about $20K. Cost per trial is $1.88 (2026-01) ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=899s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Copy and improve.** He studied the design, features and distribution of [PrayScreen](../businesses/prayscreen.md) and made a better version ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=170s)).
- **Post in volume.** He posted about 40 times a day across 12 accounts. The first hook that worked came after 40 posts ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=426s)).
- **Make onboarding longer.** He grew it from 5 to 15 minutes, and organic conversion rose more than 3 times ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s)).
- **TikTok over Meta.** Meta gave about $10 per trial. TikTok with his best UGC gave $4 ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s)).
- **Spark Ads on organic winners.** Spark Ads on a teen creator's videos cut cost to $3 per trial ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s)).
- **Do not touch the app. Obsess over distribution** ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=700s)).
- **His 2026 playbook.** Find niches with apps over $50K/month in Sensor Tower. Code a better app in under 2 weeks. Post at least 20 times a day ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=700s)).
- **Onboarding target.** Iterate until download-to-trial conversion reaches at least 10% ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [12:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=748s)).
