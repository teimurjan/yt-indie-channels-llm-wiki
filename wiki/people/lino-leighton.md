Lino Leighton is an 18-year-old non-technical founder who built [AbMaxx](../businesses/abmaxx.md), an abs workout app with an "ab scan".

## Who

- He got the idea at the gym, where his friends kept comparing abs ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md)).
- He dropped out of college after one semester ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=860s)).

## Businesses

- **[AbMaxx](../businesses/abmaxx.md).** A custom abs plan and an ab scan.
  - Built 2 months in Lovable from February, then thrown away and rebuilt in Rork in 1 month ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s)).
  - Just over $7K revenue in the first month ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [4:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=265s)).
  - About $55K this month, on pace for $60K, 16,000 downloads in 28 days and just over 3,000 paying users (2026-10) ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=105s)).
  - Meta ads ran at 2x to 2.5x ROAS. The same creatives on TikTok got 4x to 5x (2026-10) ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Niche down a timeless desire.** "Abs for summer" is an old promise. A niche abs app beats a general workout tracker ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=208s)).
- **Trade equity for distribution.** A large fitness creator got equity, makes the content, and the team runs paid ads behind it ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s)).
- **Cut what loses money.** Early UGC deals ran at 5% to 10% margins. At $10K/month he dropped every UGC and influencer deal for Meta ads ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).
- **Post before you build.** Run a faceless TikTok with 3 to 4 slideshows a day, with "comment app and I will send it". Many comments after 7 days mean demand ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=583s)).
- **Ship in 7 days or less**, then pay micro-creators about $100 for 10 videos to find a winning style ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=627s)).
- **Product last.** Rebuild the product only after marketing is stable at $5K to $10K/month ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=687s)).
- **Avoid his mistake.** Do not pay big influencers thousands per video before you have a proven format ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s)).
