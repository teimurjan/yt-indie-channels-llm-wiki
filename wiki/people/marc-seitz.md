Marc Seitz is the co-founder of Papermark, an open-source alternative to DocSend, which he runs with his wife Iuliia Shnai.

## Who he is

- He and [Iuliia Shnai](iuliia-shnai.md) are a husband-and-wife team ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [0:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=2s)).
- He built the first version of Papermark over a weekend ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=77s)).

## Businesses

- [Papermark](../businesses/papermark.md): secure document sharing, analytics and data rooms on an open-core model ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=267s)).
  - $75K MRR, close to $900K ARR, after about 1.5 years (2025-08) ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [0:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=18s)).
  - $20K MRR after year one ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s)).
  - About 30K users, about 1,000 paying customers, 60 contributors and 7K GitHub stars (2025-08) ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s)).
  - North star metric: 800K views on shared documents (2025-08) ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Test the idea with a tweet.** His idea tweet got about 40K views in a few hours. The launch tweet got about 100K views ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=77s)).
- **Pick a slow incumbent.** DocSend and other data room tools are about 10 years old and focus on enterprise ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=126s)).
- **Open source is a moat.** A free core is hard to undercut. The community adds R&D speed. Banks can audit the code ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=308s)).
- **Out-ship the incumbent.** Become the clear successor, not just another alternative ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=735s)).
- **Build in public.** Share even small, unfinished progress on Twitter and LinkedIn ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=546s)).
- **Go narrow with AI.** Build a targeted CRM for veterinarians or building managers, not a tool for everyone ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=473s)).
