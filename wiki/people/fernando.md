This page collects what the sources say about Fernando, a solo founder who runs two freemium AI tools.

## Who he is

- Fernando is a product designer from Argentina who lives in Sweden ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=0s)).
- He ran an agency for over a decade, then went back to a 9-to-5 job. The company fired him after about 1.5 years, one day before his vacation ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s)).
- He has no employees or co-founders, only a freelancer for content work ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=654s)).

## Businesses

- [Resume Maker Online](../businesses/resume-maker-online.md) and [AI Carousels](../businesses/ai-carousels.md) make about $15K MRR together (2024-12) ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s)).
- Both are freemium. A subscription unlocks the AI and pro features ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=93s)).
- Costs are about $1,000/month, so margins are above 90% (2024-12) ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=654s)).
- He started AI Carousels with a 10-day build-in-public challenge after he lost his job. Its Product Hunt launch got 5 paying users while it was still buggy ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).
- Resume Maker Online won Product of the Week on Product Hunt ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)).
- Resume Maker Online started as a one-time purchase and moved to a subscription in 2024 ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Niche down inside a big market**. Canva must serve everyone. He needs only 0.1% of the market ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=150s)).
- **Remove friction that rivals keep**. His resume tool shows a result after one click, with no sign-up first ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=218s)).
- **Ship a bare MVP under a deadline**. A failure after 10 days costs little ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=385s)).
- **Payment is the real validation**. Praise is weak proof ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).
- **Use free tools for SEO**. He built about 30 free tools that extend the main product ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)).
- **Market one thing, retain with a bundle**. Users who come for carousels find more tools inside, so fewer cancel ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=547s)).
- **Use small size in support**. His emails say "I", not "we", and he often fixes a bug within an hour ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=589s)).
- **Limit work hours**. He does 4 hours of deep work each morning, and the business grew faster ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=741s)).
