Ian is a young Roblox game developer in Austin who made Bathe the Baby over a college winter break and dropped out on its income.

## Who

- Twin brother of [Cole](cole.md), a top-50 Roblox developer. Their friend [Jake](jake.md) runs the agency [Vector 3](../businesses/vector-3.md) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=93s), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=149s)).
- Visited by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Bathe the Baby](../businesses/bathe-the-baby.md) (first called Bake the Baby) on [Roblox](../businesses/roblox.md): about 100M views at release from big Roblox YouTubers, about 150K daily active users, about $1.50 revenue per paying user and about $25K/month, split 50/50. Brand deals add about $5K (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=149s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).

## Repeated advice

- Monetize with daily shops for skins, coins and abilities such as a meteor or invisibility ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=810s)).
- Make games clickable, social and replayable. Expect a few attempts before a hit ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=753s)).
- Work 4 to 5 hours with the phone in another room, then stop at sunset ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1090s)).
