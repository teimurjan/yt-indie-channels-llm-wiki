Dustin Stout is a non-developer founder who built [Magai](../businesses/magai.md), a paid chat app that gives access to many AI models in one subscription.

## Who he is

He moved to California to act, then started a social media and web design agency. In 2014 he co-founded a WordPress plugin company. After he left it, two more products failed. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=79s). His personal blog reached over 100K email subscribers in 10 years. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)

## Businesses

- **Magai.** Almost $100K/month and over $1M total revenue (2025-10). It made $3K in month 1, $10K in month 3 and $30K/month after 1 year. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=55s), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)
- Pricing: no free plan. Solo is $20/month. Team is $40/month for up to 5 users, and each extra seat is $20/month. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)
- A WordPress plugin company, co-founded in 2014. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=79s)

## Repeated advice

- **Fix gaps in the leader's product.** Early ChatGPT had no chat search and no folders, so he built them. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s)
- **Sell against subscription fatigue.** People do not want 5 or 6 AI subscriptions. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s)
- **Build it yourself with no-code.** He took an online course and built the MVP in 8 weeks, mostly on Bubble. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)
- **Launch to an existing audience and build in public.** He ran no ads. "People want to see the journey." [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)
- **Pay affiliates a recurring commission from day one.** A referral that pays 12 times pays the affiliate 12 times. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=327s)
- **Use aggregator APIs.** OpenRouter covers many LLMs and fal.ai covers media models. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=490s)
- "People rejected the products, they didn't reject you." [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=580s)
