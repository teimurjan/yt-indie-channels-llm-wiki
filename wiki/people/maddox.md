# Maddox

Maddox is a college student who built and sold [DuckMath](../businesses/duckmath.md), an "unblocked games" website paid by Google AdSense.

## Who

- He copied a younger TikTok creator who promoted his own unblocked-games site, built his own in a week or two, and out-posted him ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=159s)).

## Businesses

- [DuckMath](../businesses/duckmath.md): from about 5K to 150K daily active users and from about $1K to $15K/month in 4 months (2026-03) ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=49s)).
- He sold it for $120K after a $100K lowball, with escrow (2026-03) ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=519s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Follow the money.** A passive $240 day showed the site deserved real effort ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=204s)).
- **Volume.** 3 TikTok accounts, 3 posts a day, 5 days a week, cross-posted with Repurpose.io ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s)).
- **Lo-fi hook, clear link.** His top video took 30 minutes and got about 5M views across TikTok and Instagram ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=388s)).
- **2026 playbook.** Pick a niche you can market to, build an MVP in 2 to 3 days with AI, study 10 to 20 top competitor videos, post daily on TikTok ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=574s)).
