Dmytro Krasun is a former backend developer who built ScreenshotOne, an API that automates website screenshots.

## Who he is

- A backend developer who quit his job about two years before the interview to build products (2024-11) ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- [ScreenshotOne](../businesses/screenshotone.md): 280 customers and about $12,000 MRR. Plans start around $17/month plus usage (2024-11) ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=98s)).
  - About 2M screenshots a month, $3K to $4K in servers, about $4,500/month in total costs, 40% to 60% margin (2024-11) ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=641s)).
  - Monthly churn fell from about 11% to close to 7% (2024-11) ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)).
- An email validation API that he found boring ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=134s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Filter ideas by your super skill.** He kept only API ideas ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=134s)).
- **Competitors prove demand.** Check the market is big enough for your revenue goal ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=185s)).
- **Validate with 10 paying strangers**, not friends ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=222s)).
- **Launch small.** One feature, no auth or payments. His first build took 5 months. Now he aims for 1 month ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=256s)).
- **Use integration marketplaces and tutorials.** Zapier and Make listings, Product Hunt, and third-party YouTube tutorials that rank in Google ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=357s)).
- **Email every churned customer one yes/no question.** He got close to 100% replies ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=424s)).
- **Raise prices and cut the free plan.** Margins at $5 to $7 were near zero ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)).
- "Don't outsource your decision to anybody. You need to own it." ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=788s)).
