Christian is a college sprinter who co-founded [3AK](../businesses/3ak.md), an all-in-one track and field app, with [Braylin](braylin.md) ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=0s)). The source gives only his first name.

## Who he is

- The founders are 19 and 20 and run track at Texas Southern and Texas State. Christian dropped out to work on apps ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=97s), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=524s)).
- Before the app, the two lost about $3,000 day trading ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)).
- They had no coding experience. They build with Rork and check the code with Claude and Claude Code ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=172s)).

## Businesses

- **[3AK](../businesses/3ak.md).** Built in about 2 weeks and launched on December 16. It made $10K in the first month ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=107s)).
- About $70K in its first 6 months, about 40,000 users and a 4.7 rating (2026-06) ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=74s)). Over $20K a month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=0s)).
- Price: $10/month or $30/year (2026-06) ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)).
- **A football app.** They worked 50 to 60 hours over about four days for its launch ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=559s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build where you already have influence.** Their own track videos had hundreds of thousands of followers before the app ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=299s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=312s)).
- **Seed the brand before launch.** A 3AK Shopify store and "3AK method" in videos and bios meant the audience knew the name ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=318s)).
- **Price for a young audience.** Teens spend $10 to $20 at a time, so the yearly plan is only $30 ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)).
- **Make a demo moment.** The AI analyzer scores a sprint start, which is easy to show on video ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=388s)).
- **Work in shifts.** One studies while the other builds. A student founder needs a partner ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=568s)).
- **Monetize what you already do** ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=702s)).
- **An app can reach the App Store for less than $500** ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=204s)).
