Yahli Hazan is a college student who co-founded Prop GPT, a sports betting analytics app, with [Eyal Hazan](../people/eyal-hazan.md).

## Who

- A college student and co-founder, interviewed by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md)).

## Businesses

- [Prop GPT](../businesses/prop-gpt.md): a machine learning model scores sports picks. About $30K/month, over 40,000 downloads and over 3,000 paying users (2025-11). It peaked at $40K MRR ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=103s), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).

## Repeated advice

- Read the funnel gap. 45% install-to-trial with 13% trial-to-paid meant users wanted the promise but the product did not deliver ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s)).
- Give users answers, not tools. The rebuild showed pre-analyzed bets ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- Pause marketing to fix the product. They stopped for 4 months, then relaunched for the NBA playoffs ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- Track feature clicks in PostHog. Use the most-clicked feature as your main marketing claim ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s)).
- Scale with [influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md). About their 70th video got 600,000 views and took ARR from about $8K to $38K in 3 days ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=436s)).
