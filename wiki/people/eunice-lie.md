Eunice Lie is a 20-year-old college student and startup marketer who went viral by posting her Hinge chats about her startup.

## Who

- She became marketing lead (called CMO) of the AI social network [Series](../businesses/series.md). By 2025-09 she had moved to Sonder, a startup from her Carnegie Mellon friends ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md)).
- She turned down startup offers at 2x to 3x her Sonder pay ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=540s)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses and results

- Over 3M impressions across LinkedIn and X, 3 viral launch videos for Series, over 3,000 RSVPs to her New York Tech Week events, and over 2M views on her Instagram reels (2025-09) ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=34s)).
- An earlier cinematic launch video went viral among VCs and helped that startup raise $3M ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=378s)).
- A Cupidly launch video that featured her got about 1.5M views. Its founder had 2,000 to 3,000 followers ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=886s)).
- Series "race to the Hamptons": about 2,500 applications from one LinkedIn post, 150 racers for 12 spots, over 100 interns posting in 2 days ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1104s), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1225s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Do the opposite of the trend.** Her "[build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md)" tweets went unnoticed because everyone posted them. Real [Hinge](../businesses/hinge.md) chats stood out. She also posts calm horizontal video when everyone posts loud vertical video ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s), [16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=992s)).
- **Founders need a personal brand.** "Your personal brand stays with you forever, but the company will come and go." ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=309s)).
- **Commit fully to a persona.** Play up one true trait. Half-commitment looks fake ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [12:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=721s)).
- **Borrow clout with a real fit**, and accept that outrage raises engagement ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=782s), [13:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=827s)).
- **LinkedIn followers are the highest-value followers** ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1160s)).
- **Hire content people for storytelling, not follower count.** Set qualitative goals first and numeric KPIs once you have data ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [21:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1272s), [21:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1313s)).
