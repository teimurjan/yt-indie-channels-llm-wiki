Steven Cravotta is a mobile app founder. He built the quit-vaping app Puff Count without code or design skills, then sold it to a European app studio.

## Who

- He builds viral mobile apps and outsources design to 99designs and development to Upwork. His apps had over 12M downloads in total, and his earlier word games made money from ads (2024-09) ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s)).
- His Starter Story video on Puff Count inspired Gaurav to start building apps. Gaurav later co-founded [Fastlane](../businesses/fastlane.md) ([source](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=154s)).

## Businesses

- **[Puff Count](../businesses/puff-count.md).** It went live about 4 years before 2024-09 and made no money for its first 4 to 6 months. It made $43K in the 30 days before 2024-09 ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=74s), [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=813s)). It reached $44K MRR before he sold it to a larger mobile app studio in Europe (2025-09) ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=78s)).
- Puff Count's organic TikTok had 120,000 followers and over 50M views. It drove hundreds of thousands of installs for $0 (2025-09) ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=512s)).
- Paid ads: $82K total spend took MRR from $3K to $44K. CAC was $20 to $24 and LTV was $55 to $70+ ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)).
- **[Posted](../businesses/posted.md).** His newer marketplace. Creators submit videos to a brief, and the brand pays only for the videos it approves. Demo prices ran $40 to $110 a video (2025-09) ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=917s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick an idea you can market.** A painful problem, many sufferers and a topic that goes viral make a strong product ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=115s)). Transformation problems such as quitting vaping make marketing easy ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=152s)).
- **Research TikTok before you post.** Log each viral niche video in a spreadsheet: URL, hook, storyline and call to action. Copy formats, not ideas ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=211s), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=257s), [source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=430s)).
- **Volume beats perfection.** Post every day and remake concepts that pop. "Volume negates luck" ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=663s)).
- **Track installs, not views.** One video with 11.6M views drove almost no installs ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=775s)).
- **Turn organic winners into paid ads.** Proven organic videos gave cost per click of 3 to 35 cents from day one ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s), [source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=496s)). Optimize ads for views first, then clicks, installs, trials and subscriptions ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [18:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1088s)).
- **Hard paywall after a long onboarding.** A hard paywall with a free trial "changed my business overnight" and lifted conversion to 20% to 25% (2024-09) ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=547s)). Long onboarding makes users think about their problem before the paywall ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=648s)). See [Paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).
- **LTV is the north star.** Aim for LTV at 3x CAC ([source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1270s)). He tested prices from $4 to $12 and kept the one with the highest LTV ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=617s)).
- **Pay developers per project.** He hires Eastern European developers on Upwork and pays on bug-free completion. A simple MVP costs under $5K (2024-09) ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=344s)).
