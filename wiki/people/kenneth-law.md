Ken (Kenneth Law) is a former Meta engineering manager who left big tech to run Harpagia, a text-based idle RPG.

## Who he is

- He spent 12 years in big tech, last as an engineering manager at Meta ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=0s)).
- After the 2022 layoffs he no longer trusted job security ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=323s)).
- The name Kenneth Law comes from his LinkedIn handle. The video calls him Ken ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- **Big Two Wild.** His first app, a card game he knew from high school. It took about six weeks. He had not written code for over 4 years ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=221s)).
- **[Harpagia](../businesses/harpagia.md).** A free-to-play text-based idle RPG for fans of incremental games such as RuneScape. It earns from optional in-app purchases ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s)).
  - About $13K/month on average and over $150K in the past year (2026-09) ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s)).
  - 3,500 to 4,000 MAU and over 50,000 downloads. About 6% of players pay, against a 2% to 5% norm (2026-09) ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s)).
  - Prices run from $2 to $10. Conversion drops above $9.99 ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s)).
  - Ad spend is $0. Costs are about $500/month (2026-09) ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s)).
  - His first Reddit post came six months after launch. Revenue went from about $1K to $10K/month almost overnight ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=394s)).
  - More Reddit posts took revenue to a peak of about $18K/month ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [8:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=537s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Start with a small scope.** A small card game taught him AI-assisted coding, app review and launch ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=221s)).
- **Set a quit number.** His rule was $10K/month with no paid marketing. He quit Meta the month he hit it ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=394s)).
- **Post on Reddit and welcome criticism.** A harsh thread pushed the game to thousands of players. He posts only major updates every few months ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=478s)).
- **Serve a niche.** A few hundred daily players who love a game can produce over $100K a year ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [9:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=595s)).
- **Launch early, then fix retention before monetization** ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=757s)).
- **Buy assets and outsource art.** That keeps his time on the build ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=706s)).
