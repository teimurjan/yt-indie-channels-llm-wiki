Roman Lakhnyuk is a non-technical founder who runs [Career Hound](../businesses/career-hound.md), a job board that scrapes jobs from company websites.

## Who

- He studied psychology and sold alarm systems door to door for 5 summers. He also managed sales teams, which helps him manage creators ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=177s)).
- He posts on X as @rlakhnyuk ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md)).

## Businesses

- **[Career Hound](../businesses/career-hound.md).** He and his co-founder launched it about 2 years ago and first aimed to make $1,000 each ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=77s), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)).
  - About $25K to $28K last month and $12K MRR (2026-09). Yearly plans push total revenue above MRR ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).
  - Hard paywall with no free plan: $7/week, $20/month or $50/year ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).
  - 8,000+ users, 779 active subscriptions, about $50 LTV and 31% churn. Churn is high because users stop after they find a job (2026-09) ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).
  - 11.7M social views in the last 30 days (2026-09) ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Productize a friend's process.** A friend found a job with a scraper of career pages, and that became the product ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)).
- **Prefer timeless content to trends.** Trends die in a week. Find timeless topics by "content thrifting": scroll Instagram, Reddit and TikTok for hours and save what grabs you ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=364s)).
- **Repeat one converting format.** His format lists companies that hire remotely and worldwide, then sends viewers to the site ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=459s)).
- **Match the creator to the niche, and keep pay low.** Older creators signal credibility for job advice. He DMs people who already post for fun and pays no more than $20 a video in about 90% of cases ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s)).
- **Make the brief stupid simple.** Creators get a sheet of formats and do zero research ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=607s)).
- **Find ideas through creator spend.** Companies that hire creators on SideShift have money ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=657s)).
- **Run a marketing experiment every day.** Each failed test removes one wrong option, like a door in door-to-door sales ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=703s)).
