Asher Hurstman is a co-founder of Clear 30, a science-based app that helps people quit or cut down on weed.

## Who

- He co-founded [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) with [Thatcher Kloff](thatcher-kloff.md) and the clinical psychologist Dr. Fred Munch ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=0s)).
- Some of the app's best ads star his mother, whose calm tone reaches older users ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=380s)).

## Businesses

- **[Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md).** Subscriptions went from $10K to $30K in one month (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=147s)).
- They tried influencers, Reddit, organic content and SEO first. Paid ads on Meta and TikTok started the flywheel ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=178s)).
- The program costs $30 a year, while the average user spends over $55 a week on weed (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [7:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=441s)).
- It has a grant from the National Institute of Health. A partnership with the University of Michigan was pending ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=899s), [30:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1846s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Use hyper-specific call-outs in ads.** For example: "hang out in your basement after school and hit your pen until 3 a.m." ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=307s)).
- **Track from the first screen.** At first they collected no data until phone sign-in, so early drop-off was invisible ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=578s)).
- **Build onboarding on the value equation.** Dream outcome times perceived likelihood, divided by time delay and effort. Each screen serves one term ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=679s)).
- **Add good friction.** Users spend 20 to 30 seconds on goals and triggers questions, with under 5% drop-off ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [12:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=730s)).
- **Say the price early.** One screen says that the program costs money and compares it with the cost of the habit. It raised conversion by about 3% ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1038s)).
- **Ask for a rating after free value.** Reviews grew from about 50 to almost 1,000 after a rating prompt in onboarding ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s)).
- **Show the first 7 days on the paywall.** They replaced a feature carousel with a day-by-day plan. Paywall conversion moved from about 20% to 30% (2025-08) ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s)).
- "85% of users decide if they're going to pay for your app in the first 5 minutes." ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=553s))
