Danny Miranda is the host of the Danny Miranda Podcast, which he started in his parents' basement in 2020.

## Who he is

- He launched in September 2020 with three episodes a week and had done 359 episodes without a missed week (2023-06) [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=64s), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=747s).

## Businesses

- [Danny Miranda Podcast](../businesses/danny-miranda-podcast.md): 30K to 50K downloads/month (2023-06). Gary Vaynerchuk was episode 39 [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=64s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=387s).
- He earns from consulting: he helps new shows book strong guests from day one [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=186s).

## Repeated advice

- **Send 100 messages.** More guests say yes than you think [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=282s).
- **Hack attention on the guest's main platform.** A public tweet and an old blog post about Gary Vaynerchuk landed him as a guest [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=457s), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=517s).
- **Research 5 to 20 hours per guest** [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=342s).
- **Clip heavily.** Daily: 1 YouTube video, 2 Shorts, 3 Reels and 5 Twitter clips (2023-06). Clips that tap a bigger conversation grow the show, not the big guest name [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=583s), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=634s).
- **Reverse-engineer consistency** from Joe Rogan's three episodes a week [source](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md) [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=747s).
