# George

George is a non-technical former wrestler who runs three AI sports apps: [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md), [Brawler](../businesses/brawler.md) and [AbMaxx](../businesses/abmaxx.md).

## Who

- He built his first app, [Fight AI](../businesses/fight-ai.md), with Rork about a year before the video to get feedback on his own training ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md)).
- He is likely the same founder as [George Lampropoulos](george-lampropoulos.md): both built Wrestle AI with Rork and wrestling creator [Kaden Henshaw](kaden-henshaw.md).

## Businesses

- All three apps: almost $41K MRR and $84K revenue in the last 28 days, up from about $25K/month two months earlier (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=191s), [30:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1827s)).
- [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md), 50/50 with Kaden. A pre-order made $8K in week one and reached #19 in Sports ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=503s)).
- [Brawler](../businesses/brawler.md), boxing: about 2,500 active subscriptions; 75% of revenue from ads (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [42:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2578s)).
- [AbMaxx](../businesses/abmaxx.md), fitness: one stitch ad spent over $20K and returned close to $60K over 3 months (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=788s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Influencers first in an empty niche, ads for scale.** Expect to burn about $2K in 2 weeks while the pixel learns ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).
- **Raw creatives win.** Screen recordings, talking heads and stitches beat polished visuals. Put the CTA in the first 15 seconds ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=544s), [14:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=849s)).
- **Structure creator deals** with minimum posts and vesting ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=397s)).
- **Retention.** An in-app feed where partner creators post, and Duolingo-style daily notifications ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [37:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2238s), [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s)).
- **Founder calls** on the third app open show which features users never found ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [42:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2556s)).
