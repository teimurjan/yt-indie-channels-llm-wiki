Nicole is a college senior who co-founded four consumer apps in two years, including Glam Up and Sprout. She grows them with managed UGC creators.

## Who she is

- She handles design and distribution. Her co-founder Aaron codes ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md)).
- She was a college senior at the time of her interviews (2026-05) ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md)).
- About 90% of her learning came from downloading apps and going through their onboarding, not from X ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [53:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3202s)).

## Businesses

- [Glam Up](../businesses/glam-up.md): a glow-up and makeup app for women, "Umax for women". 1M users in 6 months and about 2M downloads. Peak about $150K MRR, over $600K in total revenue (2026-05) ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=525s), [20:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1244s)). It reached number 3 on the App Store for a few days, above Tinder and Hinge ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=525s)).
- [Sprout](../businesses/sprout.md) (formerly PrepearAI): a swipe-to-apply job app for college students. About $250K MRR after 8 months (2025-10). Then steady near $200K MRR (2026-05) ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s)). Team of 10, up to $80/month (2026-05) ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [51:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3080s)).
- Two stealth apps: about $200K MRR combined after about 3 months (2026-05) ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=67s)).
- Reach: 400 to 500M views a month across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts (2026-05) ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Copy a proven app for a new audience.** Glam Up is a female version of [Umax](../businesses/umax.md). Sprout aimed at college students for higher spending power ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s)).
- **Design for virality.** Welcome pages and onboarding are part of distribution ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=357s)).
- **Find the format yourself first.** Nicole and Aaron each warmed up 3 accounts. After a format went viral, they scaled to 30 or 40 creators ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s)). Give each channel 2 to 3 weeks ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=465s)).
- **Lock one viral format.** Glam Up posts show a plain face, then full makeup, then the app. The first slideshow got about 10M views ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=589s)).
- **Run creators like a studio.** Source, interview, train with a course and quizzes, manage, then systematize. Over 50% of creators who finish the course go viral within 2 weeks. At peak they managed about 200 creators ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=542s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=578s), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)).
- **Pay per post plus a falling CPM bonus.** $10 to $30 per post. Bonus example: $40 at 10K views, $800 at 1M ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [48:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2931s)).
- **Refer three friends or pay.** The share text names the app, so TikTok comments rank it in search ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1096s)).
- **Raise prices and use a hard paywall.** Glam Up went from $3 to $9 a week. Conversion rose ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1368s)). A hard paywall right after onboarding lifted conversion by 50% ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2401s)). Every app now has a hard paywall ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=175s)).
- **Pitch the app inside onboarding.** "Fun fact" pages compare the app with rivals ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [38:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2293s)).
