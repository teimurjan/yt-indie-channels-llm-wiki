Nevo is the founder of Postiz, an open-source social media scheduler that he grew from $17K to $143K MRR.

## Who he is

- He worked 2 years at an open-source notification startup that grew from 0 to 32,000 GitHub stars. He then did open-source consulting for about a year. ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=246s))
- After many failures he read Traction and $100M Offers. He now splits time half learning, half building. ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [20:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=1254s))

## Businesses

- [Postiz](../businesses/postiz.md): an open-source scheduler for 25, later 30+, social platforms. The paid cloud product is the same code as the open-source repo. ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=14s))
  - $17K MRR from 472 subscribers, 19% monthly churn, 21% trial-to-paid, over 25K GitHub stars (2026-01). ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=145s))
  - $143K MRR and 4,271 subscribers after a pivot to an "agentic" scheduler. Churn fell to 13.7% (2026-07). ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=82s), [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=148s))

> Conflict: [RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md) gives monthly churn of 19% (2026-01). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) says churn was 26% to 28% before the pivot.

## Repeated advice

- **Open source is a brand and distribution play.** Code has little value now. Developers rarely pay, but they bring word of mouth, bug reports and SEO content. Copies of Postiz gave up within weeks. ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [6:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=406s), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=454s), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=577s))
- **Launch in one week to hit GitHub trending.** Prepare the README as a landing page, write articles, post Show HN with the repo link, post on r/selfhosted, and push all other traffic to GitHub. ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=635s), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=818s), [15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=934s))
- **Ride a trend with the product you have.** He made Postiz agent-ready within days of the OpenClaw launch. ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=199s))
- **Write X articles and buy reposts.** With 200 followers his article still got about 500,000 views. Each article gets about 20 paid reposts at $60 to $350 each (2026-07). ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=328s), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=549s))
- **Agents cut churn.** Users who stop the daily manual work churn. An agent posts every day. ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=491s))
