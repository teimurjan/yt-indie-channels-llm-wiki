Jack Sweeney is a content creator and no-code builder who co-founded the breathwork app Coherence with his brother Nick.

## Who he is

- He grew a YouTube channel to about 20,000 subscribers in university and built no-code apps ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=112s)).
- His brother [Nick Sweeney](nick-sweeney.md) turned to breathwork after a skiing injury and now coaches others ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=72s)).

## Businesses

- [Coherence](../businesses/coherence.md): a breathwork app that helps users regulate the nervous system. They launched the MVP in February 2025. Jack wrote no code by hand ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=315s)).
  - About $85K revenue and over 15,000 downloads in the first 8 months (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=72s)).
  - About $11K/month on average, best month $22K (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=92s)).
  - $40/year with a 3-day trial, or $10/month with no trial (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=611s)).
  - Costs of $5K to $6K a month give about 50% margin (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=662s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Content first, product second.** What went viral showed what the market wanted. They built the app around it ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s)).
- **Use X to test ideas.** A post with traction on X becomes a short-form script. Short-form winners become YouTube videos ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=286s)).
- **Film in bulk.** Shoot 30 to 60 videos at a time, about two months of content ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=417s)).
- **Put ad money behind winners.** Spark Ads and Meta spend test whether people will pay ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=459s)).
- **Use polarizing hooks.** Their 8M-view video opened with what "they" do not want you to know. The first take won ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=519s)).
- **Outmarket everyone.** Post every day, make many reels and hire clippers ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=459s)).
