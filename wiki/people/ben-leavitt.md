Ben Leavitt runs [FollowBuddy](../businesses/followbuddy.md), a subscription app that shows who unfollowed you on Instagram, and grows it only through search videos.

## Who

- He ran a marketing agency for 7 years ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=119s)).
- His "compare lists" video got over 1M views and sent huge traffic to someone else's list tool. He waited about 4 years for build costs to drop, then built FollowBuddy about 2 years ago ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=161s), [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=213s)).

## Businesses

- **[FollowBuddy](../businesses/followbuddy.md).** It uses Instagram's own data export, so it does not break Instagram's terms ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md)).
  - $20K/month by Ben's count, $17K/month in the title (2026-03) ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).
  - 82,000 users, 3,128 paying users and $0 spent on ads (2026-03) ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).
  - Weekly, monthly and yearly plans after a free trial. The $50/year plan gets the most push (2026-03) ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=47s)).
  - One full-time developer ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=833s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Search traffic is high intent.** People who search already want a solution. In low-competition niches, even a weak video can rank #1 ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=252s)).
- **Map the awareness ladder.** List buyer questions at each stage, from aware of the need to ready to buy ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=390s)).
- **Find the exact phrasing.** Start with 10 to 20 keywords ranked by intent and volume. Check volume with Keywords Everywhere or vidIQ ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=444s)).
- **Record for the viewer.** Repeat the search phrase at the start. Show cons honestly, which he says raised conversion ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=506s)).
- **Stack bricks.** Remake variations until they rank. YouTube lets one brand hold several top results ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=672s)).
- **Example.** A 20-minute video, "best Instagram unfollower tracker apps", has 25,000 views after 7 months and ranks #1 for several keywords ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=291s)).
- **Hire the best builder you can.** Give a non-technical founder's builder skin in the game. A weak first partner cost him about a year ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=862s)).
