Matt Giovanisci runs Swim University, a pool and hot tub care education business that made $1M in 2023.

## Who

- A former marketing director at a pool company. He built the site at night, and his boss fired him when he found out ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=139s)).
- He runs a three-person family business with his wife Steph and his brother, all on equal pay ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=568s)).
- Visited by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Swim University](../businesses/swim-university.md): $1M revenue in 2023, about 225K YouTube subscribers and 100K newsletter readers (2024-05) ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=65s), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s)).
- Roasty Coffee: same playbook, about 100K uniques/month in 2 years, then sold ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=642s)).

## Repeated advice

- One script, many formats: a weekly long video becomes clips, SEO posts and emails ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)). See [content marketing](../topics/content-marketing.md).
- Content quality beats production. His top video every year is an unscripted PowerPoint ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)).
- Find price by stepping down until people buy. His first PDF did not sell at $30 but sold well at $24 ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)).
- In a boring niche, sell the outcome fast: from "disgusting" to "clear and safe" ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=434s)).
- Pick a niche you like a little, so you keep going when content gets hard ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=476s)).
- Choose [lifestyle](../topics/lifestyle-business.md) over maximum revenue ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=797s)).
