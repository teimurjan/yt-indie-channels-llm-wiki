Nick is the first name of two founders in this wiki: one runs an automated Instagram comment agency, and one co-founded SideShift. The sources do not say whether they are the same person.

## Who he is

- **Comment agency Nick.** He and [Ivan](ivan.md) worked as a team for 8 years: a Minecraft server that sold, private blockchain work, then an AI flight search app. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1047s))
- **SideShift Nick.** [Drew Levin](drew-levin.md) calls Nick his co-founder at [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md). ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1067s))

## Businesses

- An agency that posts Instagram comments under trending videos from branded accounts on real phones. One campaign for a US health app used about 300,000 comments for about 7,000 link clicks (2026-01). ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2080s))
  - 60 phones, 12 to 24 Instagram accounts per phone per day, up to 60 comments per account per day (2026-01). ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [37:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2222s))
  - Their earlier automated TikTok accounts got about 100M views. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [18:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1103s))
- [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md): a UGC creator marketplace. Nick posted a screen video that cracked a format. After that, 6 or 7 of 15 creator videos got 150K to 300K views each. ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1067s), [19:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1155s))

## Repeated advice

From the comment agency episode:

- **Treat comments like cold email.** Warm up many accounts, spread the volume, and post some comments with no CTA so the account looks human. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [38:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2303s))
- **Be open about the app.** "An app my friends and I co-founded" beat covert ads. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=610s))
- **Comment early on rising videos.** Old videos are not worth a comment. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=375s))
- **Rotate angles.** One angle worked for two weeks, then Instagram filtered it. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1005s))
- **Copywriting is the lever.** Automation scales skill but does not replace it. ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [52:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=3131s))
