Jonathan Fishner is a developer and co-founder of ChartDB, an open-source database diagram tool.

## Who

- A developer who builds for developers. Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md) ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md)).
- His first idea, an AI database client, failed: users had to hand over credentials and install software they did not trust ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=146s)).

## Businesses

- [ChartDB](../businesses/chartdb.md): about $9K MRR, almost $9,400, 16 months after the open-source launch. Over 21K GitHub stars and 250K developers in the year (2026-02) ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=64s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=96s)).
- Launched with a Show HN after 3 weeks of work. It reached the Hacker News front page ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=235s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Remove friction.** ChartDB needs no sign-up, no database access and no install. The user pastes the JSON from one query ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=207s)).
- **Developer tools playbook:** be the user. Design for the persona (self-hosted, no sign-up wall, no sales calls). Start with a wedge, not the full vision. Let usage show what to charge for. Market on GitHub, Hacker News and self-hosted subreddits ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=397s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=458s), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=519s)).
- **[Open source](../topics/open-source.md) as the funnel.** Free self-hosting is the entry point. A small group pays for the hosted cloud version, and team collaboration became the paid feature ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=826s)).
- **Defend one core value.** Growth started once the team made database visualization obvious and ignored the rest ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=717s)).
