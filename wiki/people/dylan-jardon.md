Dylan Jardon is a co-founder of Clipt, an animation and video editing agency, and of Smart Nonsense, an animated YouTube Shorts brand.

## Who he is

- He met [Henry Belcaster](henry-belcaster.md) in 2015. They were college roommates in Chicago. They started a podcast in 2020 and had 14 views after 3 months on unemployment money. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s))
- He is the perfectionist of the pair. Henry ships at 80%, and Dylan sweats the details. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s))

## Businesses

- [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md): an agency that matches animators with companies. About $2M/year in revenue (2023-10). Clients include My First Million and the All-In Podcast. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s))
- [Smart Nonsense](../businesses/smart-nonsense.md): one animated 60-second Short per day. 1 billion views and 1M subscribers in about a year (2023-10). It costs $25K to $30K/month and earns about $5K/month, so the agency funds it. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=125s), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s))

## Repeated advice

- **Blitz and squatter marketing.** Make free, unasked-for videos for famous creators. Jason Calacanis hired them after the first All-In video. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=338s))
- **Story beats abstraction.** A good story in 60 seconds holds people for about 55 seconds. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=428s))
- **Hire people better than you.** Accept only a portfolio that gives a "jaw on the floor" reaction. They hire mostly in the Philippines. ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=491s), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s))
- **Pair a shipper with a perfectionist.** ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s))
