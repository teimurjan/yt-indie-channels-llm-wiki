Nick Buzz is an anonymous designer on X who co-founded Baked, a monthly design subscription agency.

## Who he is

- He grew up in India and moved alone to Canada at 17. His tuition was $16K, about 3x a local student's ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=452s)).
- He entered about 200 logo contests over 6 months before his first win, about $200 ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=209s)).
- In 2016 a client found him on Dribbble. Freelancing then paid $80K to $90K/year ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [8:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=494s)).
- He worked at Meta and left at the end of 2022 with about 2 months of savings ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=614s)).
- He says he makes $1.2M/year (2024-09) ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- A $9 kit of about 50 landing pages, then paid landing page roasts at $50 to $300 each. Roasts peaked at $6K to $7K in a month ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)).
- [Baked](../businesses/baked-design.md): a design subscription agency, co-founded with [Alex](alex.md).
  - The first client paid $4,317/month and came from a "tiny UI" post ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=877s)).
  - MRR went $24K, $48K, $94K, $110K, $120K, $160K, then flattened to $100K to $120K (2024) ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Stay anonymous.** He is an introvert and did not want family to question why he left Meta ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=677s)).
- **Roast work in public.** He quote-tweeted landing pages with "roasted" and showed his fixes ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)).
- **Validate with a cheap product,** then raise prices ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)).
- **Focus after burnout.** He ran 12 projects at 16 to 20 hours a day, then narrowed to Baked ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=810s)).
- **Go back to content.** Leads stalled at first. Posting roasts again brought inbound leads ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=855s)).
- **Be authentic, even faceless.** Tell your story and do not only post about money ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=1012s)).
