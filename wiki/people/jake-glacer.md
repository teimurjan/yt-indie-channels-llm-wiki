Jake Glacer is a solo founder who built [Erly](../businesses/erly.md), an alarm app that turns off only after you do push-ups on camera.

## Who he is

- He is 24, a former Big Four accountant and a self-taught coder (2026-07) ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=11s)).

## Businesses

- [Erly](../businesses/erly.md): a little over $50K in the last 28 days, about 50,000 downloads in the last 30 days, over 200,000 in 2026 (2026-07) ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=98s)).
- Timeline: 19 days from first commit to App Store, first influencer video 7 days after launch, first $30K month in month 3, first $50K month in month 4 ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=368s)).

## Repeated advice

- Building is easy, so put almost all effort into views ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=11s)).
- Pick a problem that is painful, simple and inherently viral. If it is not viral, add a novel twist ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=458s)).
- Watch for new platform APIs. AlarmKit in iOS 26 opened a short window for new alarm apps ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s), [16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=1006s)).
- Pick creators whose day-in-the-life content already fits the app. Pay for guaranteed views at about $2 to $3 CPM and expect a power law (2026-07) ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [9:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=552s), [11:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=674s)).
- Measure spikes, not creators: match view spikes with conversion spikes and treat it as a black box ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=732s)).
