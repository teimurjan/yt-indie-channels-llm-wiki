Matt Robinson is a former senior software engineer on Meta's AI team who built Live Tourney, a golf tournament web app, on nights and weekends and then quit to run it full time.

## Who

- Six years at Meta, through six or more rounds of layoffs. He has two young kids ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=403s)).
- He quit Meta three weeks after [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md) visited ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=705s)).

## Businesses

- [Live Tourney](../businesses/live-tourney.md): about 150 golf courses, about $350K ARR, about $30K/month (2026-07). Players scan a QR code, enter scores, and see a live leaderboard ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=87s), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=214s)).

## Repeated advice

- Find the gap a known tool leaves. His course's pro said Golf Genius was too complex and too expensive ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=174s)).
- Build in your own hobby. He plays in the tournaments he serves ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=539s)).
- Block fixed hours next to a job: he worked from 6:00 to 9:00 each morning ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=523s)).
- Keep the salary until the business needs you. Count layoff risk too ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=344s), [6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=403s)).
