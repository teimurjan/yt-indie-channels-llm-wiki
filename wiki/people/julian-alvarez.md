Julian Alvarez is the technical co-founder of Jungle, an AI study app.

## Who

- A technical founder. His co-founder David handles design and product ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s)).
- Jungle came after 4 to 5 failed pivots, with 3 months of runway left ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=198s)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md): turns PDFs, slides and videos into quizzes and flashcards with a Duolingo-style game layer. About $80K to $100K/month (2025-11) ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=188s)).

## Repeated advice

- Launch, then do only distribution for two weeks. If nothing happens, pivot ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s)).
- Get a floor of 30 to 100 new users a week, or experiments have no signal ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=358s)).
- Use micro-influencers with 5K to 100K followers, multi-video deals and minimum view clauses. He regrets skipping the clauses ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=484s), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=789s)).
- Cut time to magic. Let users upload a document from the landing page and see value before any settings ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=885s)).
- [UGC](../topics/ugc.md) programs are a temporary growth hack. Retention and word of mouth last longer ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1405s)).
- "Your biggest skill set to build as a founder is the ability to figure anything out." ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [24:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=1478s))
