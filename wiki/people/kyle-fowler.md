Kyle Fowler is a self-taught iOS developer who builds card-scanner apps for collectors: CardStock for sports cards and Scanemon for Pokemon cards.

## Who

- He builds around his own hobby, card collecting. He built CardStock while in college and taught two friends to code. The three split revenue for 2 to 3 years ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=175s)).
- He took no VC money. Lifetime revenue was about $700K (2025-11) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1165s)).
- Combined MRR was about $41K (2025-11). Combined revenue was $120K/month with a team of five (2026-06) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=0s), [source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=62s)).

## Businesses

- **[CardStock](../businesses/cardstock.md).** Building started in winter 2019. A Reddit post validated it and then launched it ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=325s), [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=292s)). It launched as a $4.99 paid download. The 2021 move to subscriptions gave the biggest revenue jump ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:08:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=521s), [0:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=685s)). ASO held about $9K to $10K MRR for 2 to 3 years, until he lost the #1 keyword rank in 2024-05 ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=900s), [0:16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=971s)). It reached about 15,000 active subscriptions and up to about $75K MRR (2026-06) ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=119s)).
- **[Scanemon](../businesses/scanemon.md).** The same app for Pokemon cards. Cursor's agent generated the MVP in one day in 2025 ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=325s)). It made about $9K MRR (2025-11), then $17K MRR with 3,400 active subscriptions (2026-06) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:40:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2457s), [source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=119s)).

> Conflict: [xWnqY2Mav4s](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md) gives $120K/month combined, but its two MRR figures add up to about $92K. The video does not explain the gap.

## Repeated advice

- **Solve your own hobby problem.** Keep a notes file of slow, repetitive tasks in your life. Find the most minimal solution, build it, then add a paywall ([source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=400s), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=488s)).
- **Validate and launch on Reddit.** The launch post linked back to the first idea post, so it did not read like an ad ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:06:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=365s)).
- **Put the top keywords in the title and subtitle.** He used Apple's free $100 Search Ads credit and Sensor Tower trials for research ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=771s), [0:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1241s)). See [App Store optimization](../topics/app-store-optimization.md).
- **Show the app in every video.** Silent pack-opening videos got views but no installs. The winning format shows a card, scans it and reveals its value ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:23:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1404s), [0:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1466s)).
- **TikTok slideshows are simple and repeatable.** Pay creators per view through a platform such as [Noise](../businesses/noise.md). It ran about 7,600 posts for $2,600 in a month, at about $1 CPM (2025-11) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:42:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2558s), [source](../sources/xWnqY2Mav4s.md), [9:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWnqY2Mav4s&t=566s)).
- **Learn a channel before you hire for it.** Then you can tell a good agency from a bad one ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:33:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1980s)).
