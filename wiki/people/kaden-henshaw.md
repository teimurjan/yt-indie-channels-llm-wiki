## Businesses

### Wrestle AI

- Wrestle AI is an AI wrestling coach that analyzes match videos ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s)).
- Users of [Fight AI](../businesses/fight-ai.md) kept asking for wrestling. So [George](george.md) partnered 50/50 with Kaden ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=365s)).
- George's Instagram DM assistant reached Kaden through someone who already knew him ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=327s)).
- Kaden hyped a pre-order, which made $8K in the first week. The app reached #19 in Sports ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=503s)).
- [George Lampropoulos](george-lampropoulos.md) says the pre-order with his co-founder's audience put the app at #18 on launch day ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=685s)).
- Kaden's audience was saturated in month two. George then spent about $500 on other influencers ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=685s)).
- Wrestle AI made about $17K/month (2025-12) ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=78s)). It later passed 100,000 downloads on about 10 creators (2026-10) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).

## Lessons from the partnership

Kaden does not speak in either video. These lessons come from George.

- **Launch on a partner's audience**. A creator co-founder can carry the first weeks of a launch ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=685s)).
- **Set terms with creator partners**. Use minimum posts and vesting. George had neither and says he got lucky ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=397s)).
- **Go first in an empty niche**. No other wrestling app existed, so creators had no sponsorship fatigue ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).
