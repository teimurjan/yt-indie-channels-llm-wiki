Alex Pattis runs a startup investing syndicate as a side project next to a full-time startup job. He started with a $1,000 check ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=0s)).

## Who he is

- He worked in medical device sales at J&J, then became the first employee at Market Access Transformation, which later exited ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=214s)).
- He started by backing Jason Calacanis's syndicate for about a year ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=276s)).
- He splits his time about 70% day job and 30% syndicate ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=338s)).

## Businesses

- **The syndicate.** For each deal he sets up a special purpose vehicle (SPV). Limited partners (LPs) can join from $1,000 ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=92s)).
- About $60M deployed into 270 to 275 companies (2023-11) ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=0s)).
- **Model.** 20% carry on profits above principal. LPs keep 80%. He earns money only from returned capital ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=895s)).
- **First deal.** A company raised $2M at a $6M valuation, and his SPV took the final $150K. It later raised at a $500M valuation ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=482s)).
- At the end of 2020 he began to co-syndicate deals with other leads. Then growth took off ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=338s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Give deal flow first.** He shared deals with VCs for years. One VC took 10 to 20 of his deals before it joined one. Now it shares its own lead deals with him ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=788s)).
- **Five sourcing channels.** Other syndicate leads, VC funds, portfolio founders, other angels and his own LPs ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=544s), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=666s)).
- **Back strong founders behind a top fund.** A tier-one fund leads the round and sets terms ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=482s)).
- **Evaluation order.** Founder-market fit first, then market size and growth, early product-market fit, then co-investors ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=666s), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=728s)).
- **Diversify.** One 50x to 250x winner must pay for 10 to 50 losers ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=895s)).
- **Work the edges of the day.** Early calls, evening meetings and weekend coffees with founders ([source](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=338s)).
