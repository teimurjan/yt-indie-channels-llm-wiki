Karthik Shanadi is a co-founder of four custom apparel brands that started in college with $500.

## Who he is

- He started the business in 2013 with his co-founder Luke. He ran it on the side of full-time jobs at Amazon and Salesforce for about two and a half years, six days a week. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=261s))
- He went full time in 2016. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s))

## Businesses

- [Greek House](../businesses/greek-house.md) (fraternities and sororities), [College Thread](../businesses/college-thread.md) (college departments), [Threadly](../businesses/threadly.md) (companies and non-profits) and [Athlete's Thread](../businesses/athletes-thread.md) (licensed athlete merch on Shopify). ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=122s))
  - No funding. The video frames the total as $10M (2023-04). ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=0s))
  - $50K in year one and $100K in year two. Sales were just under $1M in 2016. That year a licensing audit produced a $40K bill. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s))
  - About 300 college licenses (2023-04). ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=61s))

## Repeated advice

- **Use licensing as a moat.** Brands need college licenses plus rights from each athlete. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=683s))
- **Give suppliers software.** A vendor portal makes their account take about one tenth of the time to service, so print partners prioritize them. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=589s))
- **Channel mix.** Outbound email, an ambassador program, Google paid search for high-intent queries, and free marketplaces such as Etsy. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s))
- **Three skills:** clear communication, teaching yourself, and good co-founders and hires. ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [15:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=935s))
