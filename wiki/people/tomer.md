Tomer is an Israeli marketer who built Cal Buddy, a Hebrew AI calorie tracker modeled on Cal AI.

## Who

- He did marketing for other businesses and had no developer background [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=0s).
- He heard [Zach Yadegari](zach-yadegari.md)'s Cal AI podcast in March 2025 and copied the idea for Israel [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=249s).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Cal Buddy](../businesses/cal-buddy.md): about $1K in June 2025, over $81K/month by the month before the interview (2026-08) [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=104s).
  - Built with Cursor. A working version took 2 weeks, and App Store launch took about 2 months [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=188s).
  - Paid ads took it to about $20K/month in 4 months. Influencer revenue share took it from $20K to $80K/month [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=411s), [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=464s).
  - Monthly stack cost (2026-08): Claude $200, PostHog $20, Supabase $35, Expo $100, plus RevenueCat [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=603s).

## Repeated advice

- **Localize a proven winner.** Keep the product and change the language and how people talk about food [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=342s).
- **In a small market, pick a huge niche.** That is why he chose fitness and nutrition [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=249s).
- **Win on acquisition cost.** Half the CAC of competitors gives more cash flow, and that lets you outspend them. Israeli CPMs are low, so ads were profitable fast [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=342s), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=411s).
- **Make influencers partners.** Big creators get a share of all Israeli revenue, with set monthly deliverables. Wait for social proof first: at $20K/month he avoided a 50/50 split [source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=464s), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=556s).
