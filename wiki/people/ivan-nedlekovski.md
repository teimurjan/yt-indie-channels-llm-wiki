Ivan Nedlekovski is a founder from Macedonia who runs a software agency and built Lancer, an AI agent that bids on Upwork jobs.

## Who

- His agency, MB Masters, did over seven figures over 5 years and grew to almost 20 full-time staff, mainly through Upwork ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=153s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Lancer](../businesses/lancer.md): $10K/month by month 3 or 4, with zero paid ads (2026-03) ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=0s), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=78s)).

## Repeated advice

- Build an internal tool first. He hacked Lancer's first version over a weekend for his agency ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=266s)).
- Automate work on a big platform, where customers are public and easy to find ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=206s)).
- Sell to "connectors": people who already have your customers' trust. One connector deal replaces hundreds of sales calls ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s)).
- Pay connectors a lifetime [affiliate](../topics/affiliate-marketing.md) commission: 30% if they sign and onboard, 20% for a plain referral ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s), [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=692s)).
- Pitch connectors personally, for example with a long Loom video. He offered one coach $1,000 for a call ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s), [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=631s)).
