Shaan Arora is the co-founder and CEO of Alia, a bootstrapped SaaS that sells email capture pop-ups to e-commerce brands.

## Who he is

- Co-founder and CEO of [Alia](../businesses/alia.md), with co-founders Cory and Bill. Alia began as a college club project ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=14s), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=582s)).

## Businesses

- [Alia](../businesses/alia.md): $0 to $4M ARR in about one year after a repositioning around pop-ups (2025-08) ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=26s)).
  - Before that, it took about 6 months to grow from 2 to about 20 customers. The first two were a co-founder's cousin and a classmate ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=14s), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=143s)).
  - About 1,500 brands, among them Nike Strength and Toms. 12 people, bootstrapped (2025-08) ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=95s), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=582s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Position around how your best customers use you.** They paid for a pop-up tool, not an education tool. His co-founder found this in April Dunford's "Obviously Awesome" ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=217s)).
- **Say it everywhere.** Website copy ("the next generation of pop-ups"), a pinned post ("Pop-ups equals Alia. Alia equals pop-ups."), the first line of each sales call and internal language ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=270s), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=316s)).
- **Test a new position on sales calls first.** Calls got shorter and closed more often. Content came next, then the website ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=483s)).
- **Set the next small goal.** "We had two customers. My only goal was to get a third." ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=421s)).
- **Use speed against slow incumbents.** Pop-up rivals are 15 to 20 years old. "Do one thing and do that one thing phenomenally." ([source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=608s)).
