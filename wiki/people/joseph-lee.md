Joseph Lee is the founder of [Supademo](../businesses/supademo.md), an AI tool that makes interactive, clickable product demos.

## Who he is

He scaled two products to over $3M ARR. Before Supademo, he ran a venture-funded B2B seafood marketplace. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=59s), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=162s)

## Businesses

- **Supademo.** Over $3M ARR, about $250K MRR and over 150K users, about 2.5 years after launch (2026-03). G2 named it the number 5 fastest-growing product of 2025. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=97s)
- Most Supademo revenue comes from the self-serve Starter and Premium tiers. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=97s)
- **A B2B seafood marketplace.** His earlier, venture-funded company. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=162s)

## Repeated advice

- **Validate with founder calls.** He talked to more than 100 B2B SaaS founders. They hated making demos and hated hearing their own voice. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=229s)
- **Capture existing demand first.** Write comparison pages against every competitor, ship them imperfect, then improve the pages that LLMs cite. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s)
- **Free tools.** Free tools bring about 20% of traffic and convert 15% to 20% of visitors into sign-ups (2026-03). [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=354s)
- **Do things that do not scale.** He asked founders on Reddit and Indie Hackers to post a URL. He made a free demo for each one and replied in public. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=483s)
- **Distribution density.** He says there is no magic channel anymore. In 2026-03, SEO and LLMs bring 30% to 40% of traffic. Word of mouth, watermarks and referrals bring about 30%, and LinkedIn about 20%. [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)
- **Launch early.** "The only advantage you have over an incumbent is urgency and speed." [l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md) [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=618s)
