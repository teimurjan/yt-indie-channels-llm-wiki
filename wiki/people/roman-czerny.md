Roman Czerny is the co-founder of Gojiberry AI, a LinkedIn lead-finding tool he grew with Reddit story posts.

## Who

- A mechanical engineer who moved into affiliate marketing and freelancing ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=192s)).
- He is not a native English speaker. He drafts posts by voice in ChatGPT ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=409s)).

## Businesses

- Coco AI: launched 2023, grew to $50K MRR with outreach and sold to a US company ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=192s)).
- [Gojiberry AI](../businesses/gojiberry-ai.md): finds high-intent LinkedIn leads and runs AI outreach. About $30K MRR 4 months after $0, most users on the $99/month plan (2026-01) ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=94s), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=133s)).
  - Reddit brought over 11M impressions, over 40K visitors and about the first 100 customers (2026-01) ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=237s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Tell a story and show proof.** "I paid 5 influencers on LinkedIn... $1,250" got 160K views and 10 to 15 clients ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=276s)).
- **Keep the product out of the post**; put the link in the bio ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=409s)).
- **Warm up accounts for 7 to 14 days**, one account per browser, profile feed hidden ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=477s)).
- **Get 10 upvotes in the first 10 minutes** from a group of marketers ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=526s)).
- **Reply to every comment** ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [9:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=587s)). See [Reddit marketing](../topics/reddit-marketing.md).
- "Advertise the way you would love to be advertised to" ([source](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=825s)).
