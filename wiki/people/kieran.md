Kieran is a founding team member who runs marketing at StudyFetch, an AI study platform that grew on student TikTok videos.

## Who

- He joined [StudyFetch](../businesses/studyfetch.md) as a founding team member and runs its marketing ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=0s)).
- He filmed his first TikToks in his own college lectures. The first one got about 3M views overnight ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=254s)).

## Businesses

- **[StudyFetch](../businesses/studyfetch.md).** It launched on September 15, 2023, after the founders pivoted from an essay writing tool. The team started at 7 ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=210s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=465s)).
- A December 2023 lecture-hall video got 75M views and hundreds of thousands of signups ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [33:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2031s)).
- It bootstrapped for about 18 months on organic video. Then it raised an $11.5M Series A from Owl Ventures and the College Board and skipped a seed round ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=23s), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=498s)).
- Over 7M users, seven-figure MRR, eight-figure ARR and over 2B views (2026-05) ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=0s), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=182s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Mix three creator types.** Use UGC creators, influencers on new accounts and one-off sponsored posts. Weight them by monthly goals ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=646s)).
- **Give influencers zero-follower accounts.** An influencer opens a new account with "StudyFetch" in the name. Fans follow her there. This hedges against viewers tiring of obvious UGC ads ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=542s), [15:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=949s)). Such a deal costs about $10K/month for a daily video from a creator with about 750K followers (2026-05) ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [13:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=809s)).
- **Vet on comments, not views.** A creator with 10K views and an active comment section beats one with big views and no following ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=853s)).
- **Pay a base plus a view bonus.** UGC base rates run $50 to $2,500 per video, plus $100 per 100K views (2026-05) ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [41:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2480s)).
- **Prefer TikTok for conversion.** Reels can go viral abroad and convert near zero. For US creators, the target is over 80% US viewers ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s), [20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1227s)).
- **Recruit TikTok Shop creators.** They work on affiliate pay, so they know how to sell. Tell them to sell the outcome: better grades ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [23:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1413s)).
- **Build features for the camera.** Live lecture notes that stream onto the screen are the wow moment ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s)). "When the product itself is the reason that the video does well, then you're in the green." ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [31:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1886s))
- **Hire full time, not contractors.** Contractors failed on incentives. The team now has outbound scouts, creative managers and a head of the creator program ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [37:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2254s), [40:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2426s)). The first marketing hire is a growth generalist who is both operational and creative ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2837s)).
