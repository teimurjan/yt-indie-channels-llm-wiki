Katie Keith runs [Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md), a remote team that built 20 WordPress, WooCommerce and Shopify products over 10 years.

## Who

- She leads Barn2's product, marketing and cross-promotion. Nineteen of the products are WordPress and WooCommerce plugins, and one is a new Shopify app ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md)).

## Businesses

- **[Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md).**
  - 2025 plugin revenue just over $1.7M. About $1.8M in total with affiliate income and development services (2026-02) ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
  - About $150K/month on average, higher in November and lower in December (2026-02) ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
  - $9.8M lifetime plugin sales, 90,000+ sites and 17,000 active subscriptions (2026-02) ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
  - Plans for 1, 5 or 20 sites, annual or lifetime. Lifetime deals are about 15% of revenue (2026-02) ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=217s)).
  - The first plugin, WooCommerce Protected Categories, ranked at the top of Google at once and sold within days ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=317s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Mine idea forums.** The first idea had many votes on the WooCommerce Ideas Forum and no existing plugin ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=220s)).
- **Win search with tutorials.** Publish a helpful, unique post for each use case ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=317s)).
- **Cross-sell closely related products.** One companion plugin per product, two-plugin bundles, a 50%-off email three days after purchase, and banners on settings pages ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=461s), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=503s)).
- **Launch one product at a time.** Build the one with the largest audience first and launch it alone. Then launch the second as a standalone product and cross-promote ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=656s)).
- **Work in an area you already know**, and do not spread the team too thin ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [9:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=595s)).
