Alex Lieberman is the co-founder of Morning Brew who now builds a holding company of small bootstrapped businesses.

## Who

- He quit his Wall Street job when Morning Brew had 30,000 subscribers and $0 in revenue [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=201s).
- Morning Brew sold to Axel Springer in October 2020. He stepped down as CEO about 6 months later. It had 200 people and more than $75M/year in revenue (2023-11) [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=86s), [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=263s).
- His plan is a dozen or more small businesses in 5 to 10 years. "I am the CEO of none of but I am the co-founder and chairman of all of them" [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=149s).

## Businesses

- Morning Brew: co-founder.
- [Storyarb](../businesses/storyarb.md): a ghostwriting agency for B2B executives on Twitter and LinkedIn. 12 clients at $7,000/month, about $84K/month and $1M annualized, with 2 staff and 3 freelance writers (2023-11) [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s). The goal is $10M/year from about 110 clients at a 30% to 35% margin [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s).
- A new consumer app, with a co-founder from Consumer Club. Joseph Choi advises him on it (2025-04) [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [1:07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4020s).

## Repeated advice

- **Validate with a tweet.** A tweet to executives willing to pay $5K to $10K/year got 25 DMs. He matched them with 2 ghostwriters in a Slack channel [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s).
- **Step pebble to pebble.** Test in order: tweet, thread, newsletter, video. Interest at each step unlocks the next [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=1000s).
- **Measure product-market fit two ways.** At least 50% of clients would recommend you with their reputation on the line. Average retention targets 10 months, against 3 to 5 for typical ghostwriting agencies [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=609s).
- **Hire a CEO early and pay in equity.** He hires after 2 to 3 months of proof. CEOs get $100K to $200K plus 10% to 50% equity, and profits go to equity holders each quarter [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s), [12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=738s).
- **Hire for "unteachables":** an obsessive brain, critical thinking, aligned values and work ethic [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=676s).
- **Go slow to go fast.** One business must reach product-market fit before the next starts. Four strong businesses beat 12 weak ones [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=836s).
- **Be a magnet for problems,** not a couch brainstormer [source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=775s).
