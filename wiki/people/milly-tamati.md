Milly Tamati is the founder of [Generalist World](../businesses/generalist-world.md), a media and community brand for people who do many different things at work.

## Who

- She runs the business from a 200-year-old cottage on a Scottish island of 191 people. She moved there about 5.5 years ago ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=458s)).
- She has no investors. Her small team are also her friends ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)).

## Businesses

- **[Generalist World](../businesses/generalist-world.md).** A newsletter, a members community, a job board, a summer school and an invented holiday, International Generalist Day ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s)).
  - $30K/month (2026-09) ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s)).
  - Nearly $600K total revenue over 4 years (2026-09). She also calls it "a couple of hundred grand a year" ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)).
  - Hundreds of thousands of people see her content each month (2026-09) ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=289s)).
  - Her largest partnership is a one-year deal with Beehiiv ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).

> Conflict: within [ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), $30K/month implies about $360K a year, but Milly also says "a couple of hundred grand a year" and nearly $600K over 4 years.

## Repeated advice

- **Make calm the main KPI.** Build a business that lasts decades, not one that peaks fast ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s)).
- **Split the day by energy.** Creative work in the morning, a 40-minute lunch walk, meetings in the afternoon, stop at about 3 p.m. ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=248s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=458s)).
- **Fall in love with a big problem, not an idea.** Tens of millions of generalists feel undervalued, so the work never runs out ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=347s)).
- **Choose the life first.** Thousands of support-heavy customers do not fit her life ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)).
- **Pitch partners with proof.** She sent Beehiiv a body of work and its results ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).
- **Be more weird.** Her success grew as she got more honest and more weird ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).
