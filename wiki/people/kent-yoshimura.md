Kent Yoshimura co-founded [NeuroGum](../businesses/neurogum.md), a brand of focus and energy supplements in gum and mints, with his college best friend [Ryan Chen](ryan-chen.md) ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=208s)).

## Who he is

- He kept a day job as a music producer at Sony Music while NeuroGum started ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)).
- The first 4,000 lb of gum sat in his downtown LA apartment. The two ran NeuroGum as a side project from there for about two years ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s)).
- He was part of a Reddit community that helped the Indiegogo campaign hit its $30K goal in 3 to 4 days ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)).

## Businesses

- **[NeuroGum](../businesses/neurogum.md).** Year 1: a little over $100K. Year 2: $750K ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s)). Almost $10M a month (2025-02) ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=852s)).
- The founders took no salary in years 3 and 4, and a good salary only from about year 7 ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s), [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)).
- They walked away from two Shark Tank offers in 2019. The show still gave a "step change" in growth ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s)).

## Repeated advice

The source is told mostly by Ryan. These lessons are the team's.

- **A new format for a known need.** On a scuba trip they saw that pills are hard to share with strangers. So they put the ingredients in gum ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)).
- **Crowdfund the first batch.** Indiegogo paid for inventory and brought press, including Time Magazine and Dr. Oz ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)).
- **Go full-time on repeat-buyer data.** The share of revenue from repeat buyers showed real adoption ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)).
- **Pick a co-founder you trust.** "Who do you want to go into battle with?" ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=628s)).
