Adrian Horning is a solo Node.js developer from Austin who built Scrape Creators, a social media scraping API.

## Who

- App Academy bootcamp, then 3 years as an engineer in Utah. He quit with $30K in savings and tried freelancing, a course and other products first ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=109s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Scrape Creators](../businesses/scrape-creators.md): about $20K/month, 600 paying customers (2025-10) ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=80s)).

## Repeated advice

- Use Acquire.com listings as [idea validation](../topics/idea-validation.md). Filter SaaS by asking price of at least $300K, pick a market you know, find the real site, and reverse engineer how it gets customers. That last step matters most ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=376s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=437s)).
- Copy the concept, not the words. Do something every day to promote or improve it ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=490s)).
- Reply to launch posts in your space with a free-credit offer ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)).
- Win on reliability and access: keep scrapers working, tell customers about outages, be easy to reach ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)).
