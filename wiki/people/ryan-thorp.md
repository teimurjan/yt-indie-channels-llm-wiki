Ryan Thorp ran a portfolio of 30 to 35 acquired consumer apps with over 50M downloads in total. It started from the journaling app [Reflectly](../businesses/reflectly.md) ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=419s)).

## Who he is

- His core team was small: himself, one media buyer and one head of content ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1080s)).
- He sold 80% to 90% of the apps to a buyer from gaming ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [49:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2952s)). He now builds Flo, an AI analytics and agent platform for mobile apps (2026-01) ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [49:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2982s)).

## Businesses

- **[Reflectly](../businesses/reflectly.md).** Launched in 2017 and peaked at about $3M to $4M ARR, but ad spend was about the same as revenue ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=210s), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=286s)).
- **[Done](../businesses/done.md).** Bought around 2020 with 5 or 6 bundled apps. It paid back in 12 to 18 months ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s)). It became the #1 free app in the US App Store on January 2 and 3, two years in a row ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=89s)).
- **The Mindfulness App.** B2B wellness contracts can be six figures over 3 to 5 years, with 6 to 12 months to close ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2574s)).
- **The portfolio.** 30 to 35 apps acquired from 2020 to 2025 ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=419s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Buy apps, not only ads.** An undermonetized app with better paywalls paid back faster than ad spend, and the asset stayed his ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s), [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1216s)).
- **Creators, not influencers.** The best range is 2K to 15K or 20K followers. Filter with a paid test task ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2080s)). Of just over 2,000 creators, about 250 worked regularly and 1% to 2% became viral stars ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [34:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2049s), [38:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2313s)).
- **Pay low, bonus high.** $30 to $40 per video at the low tier, up to $80 to $100 for reliable creators. A flat bonus at 50K to 100K views, and $200 to $1,000 for an ad that takes off ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [40:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2444s)).
- **Strict briefs.** Four types: lifestyle, trend, educational and testimonial. Each sets the sound, hook type, 10 to 15 seconds and about 25 to 27 cuts ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1342s), [30:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1818s)).
- **Three-level TikTok scaling.** Test each video at $10 to $20 and kill it after $5 to $10. Move winners to groups at $100 to $500. Let the top 1 or 2 take 90% of spend ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=971s)).
- **Own January.** For health and productivity apps, acquisition costs about half in early January. The peak is the first Sunday of the year ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [19:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1166s), [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1564s)).
- **Add B2B at the ad ceiling.** Ads stop scaling at some point. Prove ROI to companies with data, such as stress tests before and after ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [44:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2684s)).
