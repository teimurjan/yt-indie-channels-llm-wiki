Adam Lyttle is an Australian indie app maker who went from $200K in debt to a portfolio of small iOS apps making $50K to $60K a month.

## Who

- He ran a web design agency that peaked at about $300K profit a year at $10,000 per site. Wix and Squarespace killed that demand ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=157s), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=219s)).
- He was left with $200K of unsecured debt and mowed lawns for $20 to $30 each ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=341s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=377s)).
- He started a YouTube channel in July 2024 to share app tests ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s)).

## Businesses

- A lawn-mowing route app: 6 months to build, did not sell ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=466s)).
- One app a month: one early app made about $11K/month and sold for $224K, 2x annual revenue ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=650s)). For about two years he sold apps for runway ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=712s)).
- An [app portfolio](../topics/app-portfolios.md) of over 50 apps. After ChatGPT he shipped 9 apps in a month at $2 to $500 each ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=797s)). A new paywall and onboarding took revenue from $10K to $50K/month, then $50K to $60K/month, and cleared his debt (2025-02) ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s)).
- Another founder calls it a portfolio of about 100 apps with ASO history that copycats cannot beat (2026-04) ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [39:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2345s)).

> Conflict: [h9R6ZeNruuI](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md) says he built over 50 apps (2025-02); [7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) says about 100 apps (2026-04). The later count may reflect growth.

## Influence

- A video by Adam changed [Max](max.md)'s approach: he moved from one pet app to 28 small apps in 8 months and grew from $200 to $10K/month (2025-12) ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=30s), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Ship one app per month** and learn by building ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=466s)).
- **Find ideas in daily life**, or in apps that do the job badly ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=437s)).
- **Sell apps for runway, then move to a portfolio** so you no longer have to sell ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=712s), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=797s)). See [Acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).
- **Test [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md) and [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md) in public**; that change gave a 5x revenue jump ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s)).
- **Build from passion**; a project you do not care about feels as bad as debt ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=924s)).
