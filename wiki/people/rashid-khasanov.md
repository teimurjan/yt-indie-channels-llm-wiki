Rashid Khasanov is a bootstrapped founder who runs three database products for startups and PR: [AngelMatch](../businesses/angelmatch.md), [InvestorHunt](../businesses/investorhunt.md) and [JournalistHunt](../businesses/journalisthunt.md).

## Who

- He first built a fintech startup. To fund it, he raised $100,000 from family and friends and maxed out credit cards ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).
- For that raise he built an investor list by hand. This showed him that cold email works and that list-building wastes time ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).

## Businesses

- All three products: about $32K MRR (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=66s)).
- **[AngelMatch](../businesses/angelmatch.md).** A database of about 125,000 angels and VCs. $29K MRR, 360 active subscribers, 33% trial conversion. Plans start at $59/month (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)). Peak MRR was $43K ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s)).
- **[InvestorHunt](../businesses/investorhunt.md).** $2,800 MRR from SEO only, on tiers of $57, $97 and $297 (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s)).
- **[JournalistHunt](../businesses/journalisthunt.md).** A database of 200,000 journalists. $260 MRR on $49 to $99/month tiers (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=188s)).
- The first launch, a 40,000-investor list on Product Hunt, made about $4,000 in month one ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=353s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Productize a painful step you did by hand** ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).
- **Move from a trickle of clicks to SEO.** He hired six content writers and built free tools. After 6 to 8 months, SEO plus Meta ads took MRR from $3K to $20K ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=401s)). Programmatic SEO brings about half of revenue (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- **Three-step database playbook.** Pick a database for a specific problem. Collect the data by hand or with automation. Launch, look for payment signals, then lean on programmatic SEO ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- **Pick an audience you belong to.** He targeted early-stage founders because he was one ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=463s)).
- **Keep costs tiny.** His tools cost about $62/month in total ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)).
