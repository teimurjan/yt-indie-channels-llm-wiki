Marcos Ruiz is a self-described introvert who runs The Birdhouse, a Twitter (X) ghostwriting agency for coaches, consultants and education companies.

## Who

- He started with about $2,000. He bought a $50/month course, signed two $1,000/month clients, then spent all $2,000 on a $2,000/month course ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=136s), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=196s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md) in Miami.

## Businesses

- [The Birdhouse](../businesses/the-birdhouse.md): $0 to $65K/month in one year. About $70K/month at about 60% margins, with a team of 5 to 6 (2024-06) ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s)).

## Repeated advice

- Sell revenue, not followers. If clients make more than they pay, they stay ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=593s)).
- Pick a niche that monetizes views fast: coaches, consultants, education ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s)).
- Stack case studies. His first Upwork job and one client testimonial landed the next clients ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=268s)).
- Send 100 to 200 [cold DMs](../topics/cold-outreach.md) a day, follow up warm leads, and post 2 to 3 tweets a day ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=410s)).
- Be your own case study: grow your own account first ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=634s)).
- Do the same outbound and content daily for 6 to 12 months. Do not hunt for tactics ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s)).
- Hire writers with a paid-style test: one thread prompt for 4 to 5 candidates ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=524s)).
