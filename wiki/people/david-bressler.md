David Bressler built [Formula Bot](../businesses/formula-bot.md), an AI tool for Excel formulas and data analysis, on Bubble with no code.

## Who

- He worked full time from 2011 on (as of 2024-02). He built Formula Bot in about 6 weeks of paternity leave in July 2022, while he still had a senior job ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)).
- Junior analysts kept asking him for Excel help, which gave him the idea ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)).

## Businesses

- **[Formula Bot](../businesses/formula-bot.md)** (first called Excel Formula Bot).
  - About 750K users and about 5,000 paying subscribers. Valued at over $1M (2024-02) ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s)).
  - He spent about $5,000 on OpenAI API costs in days after going viral (2022) ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).
  - The price went from $2.99 to $6.99 to $9 for unlimited use. About 5% to 10% of churn is because of ChatGPT (2024-02) ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=823s)).
  - It was 100% Bubble until a few months before the video, and is now about 5% to 10% no-code ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=315s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Launch on niche subreddits.** A plain post became the top post of the week on r/excel. A post on r/InternetIsBeautiful then got about 10,000 upvotes and made the app go viral for months ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=385s)).
- **Survive the API bill.** He added a donation link and sold an ad, then launched a paywall with logins ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).
- **Trade free help for interviews.** He did users' Excel work for free, but only on a call about what they wanted ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=589s)).
- **Learn no-code from YouTube.** Read the docs, then use free tutorials for specific problems ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=254s)).
- **Build what the platform cannot copy.** About 10 copycats appeared within weeks, and Microsoft announced its own AI. His plan is to build what neither ChatGPT nor Microsoft can copy ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=700s), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=885s)).
