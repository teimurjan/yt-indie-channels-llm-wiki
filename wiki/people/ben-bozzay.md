Ben Bozzay is a software developer who built Tech Lockdown, a website and app blocker for adults. He started it as a side project and went full-time after a layoff.

## Who he is

- Before Tech Lockdown he failed with a lead generation platform for freelance developers and a landing page builder. He also ran a web agency for about 2.5 years ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=147s)).
- In 2020, remote work made him spend too much time online. That gave him the idea ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=203s)).
- In 2023 his employer laid him off. The product then made about $3,000/month ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=249s)).

## Businesses

- [Tech Lockdown](../businesses/tech-lockdown.md): blocks websites and apps through a filtered VPN, with scheduled blocks by category or keyword ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=85s)).
  - 1,300 customers and over $15K MRR, 5x since the layoff. Mailing list of about 20,000 (2025-07) ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s)).
  - Over 2M people found the site in 2 years (2025-07) ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s)).
  - 14-day free trial, then $15/month, or about $10/month on an annual plan (2025-07) ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=104s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Solve acquisition before you quit.** Build an audience that cares about the topic. Then launch day brings qualified buyers. He charged money about a year after his first YouTube video and Reddit post ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=484s), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=203s)).
- **Write deep, free, shareable guides.** A guide to turn an iPhone into a "dumb phone" got hundreds of thousands of reads ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=588s)).
- **Put the value in the Reddit post itself,** then add a short link ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=588s)).
- **Skip branding early.** No logo until a few hundred customers ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=292s)).
- **Split the day by energy.** Code from 5:30 to 8:30 a.m. before the job. Do marketing in the evening ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=343s)).
- **Show your face.** Rivals look corporate, so he writes "I", not "we" ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=671s)).
- **Keep CAC low.** Many side projects sell "a dollar for 90 cents" ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=774s)).
