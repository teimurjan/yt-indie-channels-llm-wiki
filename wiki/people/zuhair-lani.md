Zuhair Lani is the founder of Double Speed, an a16z Speedrun startup that makes AI slideshow content and posts it to TikTok from a phone farm.

## Who

- He moved from sneaker and retail botting to hype marketing for his dad's restaurant. Supreme-style weekly reservation drops built a 6,000-person waitlist and sold the restaurant out for 2 months ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=487s)).
- [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) interviewed him on Consumer Club ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- A TikTok Shop poster store: $100K in sales in the first month with AI content only. His 3PL then failed on 1,000 orders over New Year's and TikTok banned the shop ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [26:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1602s), [26:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1612s)).
- An AI content agency: $40K/month while he was in college ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [0:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=33s)).
- [Double Speed](../businesses/double-speed.md): raised $1M from a16z Speedrun (2025-10). One client got 4.7M views from 15 accounts in under 4 weeks (2025-10) ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [27:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1651s), [19:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1149s)).

## Repeated advice

- **AI does 95%, a human touches up 5%** ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=932s)).
- **Use real phones, not emulators.** TikTok device fingerprinting now blocks emulators ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s)). See [TikTok farming](../topics/tiktok-farming.md).
- **Own creation and posting** so the system learns which prompt, model and format won ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=304s)).
- **Pain-then-solutions slideshows.** Name the pain, show high-effort fixes, then the app as the easy fix ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [16:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=962s)).
- **Clone a recent winner, then vary hooks, images and text** ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [20:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1211s)).
- **Formats decay** within a few months, a year at most ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s)).
- **Test before you scale supply.** After 3 sales he paid a print shop a $100 deposit ([source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=785s)).
