Lots (Lotts Ezeike) is a London founder who builds consumer apps, mostly for Christians. He grows them to $10K to $20K MRR and sells them for cash.

## Who he is

- He worked in banking, then ran a VC-backed startup for about 3.5 years. It failed ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=130s)).
- His goal is an exit in 6 to 12 months for about $250K, not a 10-year VC path ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=229s)).
- He has sold four apps since 2022 for over $500K in total. The apps had over 1M users in total (2025-11) ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s)).
- He has an email list of 1.2M Christians (2025-11) ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=900s)).

## Businesses

- [PrayScreen](../businesses/prayscreen.md): blocks apps until you pray, a copy of one sec for Christians. Launched December 2024. About $120K ARR in 5 to 6 months. Sold for multiple six figures within about a week (2025-09) ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=127s)). Day-30 retention was 60% ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=328s)).
- [Bible Buddy](../businesses/bible-buddy.md): an AI therapist for Christians. It started on WhatsApp. WhatsApp fees pushed costs to about $15K/month, so it moved to iOS and Android ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [2:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=161s)). It had 100,000 monthly active users ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=776s)).
- [Magic Music](../businesses/magic-music.md): a wrapper on Suno, built in 4 days after Suno launched ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [14:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=892s)).
- [Toxic Traits](../businesses/toxic-traits.md): reads personality traits from chat screenshots. It has 330 content pieces ready and was for sale (2025-09) ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [15:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=942s); [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Decide early: legacy or cash flow.** Build cash-flow apps for a clean sale ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md); [NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1242s)).
- **Copy a trend into an underserved niche.** Check the top 20 apps in each App Store category. Then bring the idea to Christians, because faith gets new technology last ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)) ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=272s)).
- **Single-player first. Share an asset, not the app.** Magic Music songs open in a web view with a "Create your own song" button ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=489s)).
- **Rage-bait UGC, then Meta ads.** Hooks that make people argue in the comments. Once a hook wins organically, put it on Meta for low CPIs ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=170s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=387s); [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s)).
- **Monetize non-payers with ads.** With 2.5% install-to-paid, a free tier with four ads a day paid back in 14 to 15 days ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=501s)).
- **Take the fast close, not the top bid.** The top bid takes longest and fails most. Over 90% of deals fall through ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1297s); [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=300s)).
- **Sell on an uptick, and leave levers for the buyer.** Show a rising last three months. Never translate the app, so the buyer sees new markets to open ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s); [NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1050s)).
- **Keep the stack plug-and-play** so a non-technical buyer can run it ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=300s)).
- "Only play games you can win and always have an edge" ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=900s)).
