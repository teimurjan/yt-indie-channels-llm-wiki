Vahe Baghdasaryan runs Tangent Growth, which designs and tests paywalls for subscription apps.

## Who

- He says he has run over 10,000 paywall experiments. He says he helped apps add over $50M in revenue, including Cal AI, Quitter, Claim and Rizz (2025-11) ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- **[Tangent Growth](../businesses/tangent-growth.md).** A paywall growth firm. Some client apps added $100K in revenue in 3 months from paywall tests alone ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=364s)).
- One entertainment app with 40K to 50K organic installs a month went from 1.1% to close to 4% install-to-paid, from April to September. It had no new users and no paid spend ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=397s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Know the benchmarks** (2025-11) ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=291s), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=337s)):
  - A trial start rate above 15% is good.
  - Trial-to-paid above 30% is good.
  - For a no-trial app with mostly US users, install-to-paid of 10%+ is very good, 4% to 10% is OK and under 4% is a problem.
- **Cover every placement first.** Show a paywall at four points: session start, after X actions, low free credits, and after a user cancels ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=510s), [41:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2467s)).
- **Use multi-step paywalls.** A well-made one gives a 20% to 40% uplift over a single screen ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=884s)).
- **Say "free" many times.** Claim's paywall says "free" 5 to 7 times. Its button says "Try for $0" ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=645s)). Do not show the trial length on the plan card. Show a timeline instead ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s)).
- **Test a trial toggle.** High-intent apps such as Cal AI keep one price. Lower-intent apps such as Rizz need a cheaper no-trial price ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [25:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1512s)).
- **Discount only the annual plan.** Discounts on weekly or monthly plans failed in dozens of tests ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s)).
- **Use odd prices.** $3.33/month feels like $3 and feels authentic ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1866s)).
- **Set price from ROAS.** Pick a price that keeps ads profitable, or that pays back in about a month ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [37:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2240s)).
- **Go paid-only only with strong numbers.** Claim had a 34% trial start rate and 50% to 55% trial-to-paid. Then it removed the trial, because paid-only made more money ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=953s), [16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1017s)).
