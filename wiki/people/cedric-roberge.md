Cedric Roberge is a University of Oregon student who built [Pep AI](../businesses/pep-ai.md), a tracker for peptide and GLP-1 users ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)). He grew it with niche creators instead of ads alone.

## Who he is

- His first app, a University of Oregon student marketplace, got 800 users and no revenue ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=142s)).
- A roommate mentioned peptides, TikTok then showed him the trend, and he found no successful app in the niche ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=398s)).
- He built Pep AI in about 2 weeks with Replit, Claude as a "senior developer", RevenueCat, Resend and Firebase ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=205s)). He built from late January and launched at the end of February 2026 ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=237s)).

## Businesses

- **[Pep AI](../businesses/pep-ai.md).** It logs doses, injection sites, vials, nutrition, weight and workouts ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=14s)).
  - $51K total revenue in about 7 weeks, $33K in the last 28 days, $11K MRR, almost 2,000 active subscriptions (2026-05) ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)).
  - $30K in month one, $50K in month two, on pace for $80K in month three (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=14s)).
  - Price: $45/year or $10/month with a 3-day trial (2026-05) ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s)). $40/year with a 3-day trial, or $10/month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [23:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1381s)).

  - Team: 2 developers paid in equity, 2 marketing managers, 4 outreach staff, a CFO and a product lead. An angel investor funded marketing only (2026-06) ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [34:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2099s), [43:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2606s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Distribution beats the idea.** "Marketing and distribution matters way more than your idea" ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [11:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=687s)).
- **Do what rivals skip.** GLP apps only ran ads, so he copied the creator playbooks of [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) and Quitter in an untouched niche ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=331s)).
- **Sign trusted niche creators before launch.** A 300-person waitlist came from influencer posts and Reddit. One feed post got 50K views and about $4,000 that day ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s)).
- **Structure creator deals.** A trial month is 4 videos, 4 stories and a link in bio. A creator with 5,000 views a video gets about $200. Most creators also get a one-time 20% commission ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=913s), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1297s)).
- **Track every creator** with codes and AppsFlyer OneLinks, and lower the rate for weak ones ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=540s)).
- **Read competitor reviews.** Feed likes, dislikes and wishes to Claude or ChatGPT to shape the product ([source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=551s)).
- **Ship and prune weekly.** Two or three updates a week. Users asked for a syringe calculator, which drove traffic. Unused features were cut ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [31:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1897s)).
- **Use Meta for paid in restricted niches.** TikTok rejects peptide Spark ads, but Meta accepts them ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [50:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=3034s)).
