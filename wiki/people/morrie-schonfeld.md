Morrie Schonfeld co-founded [Pingo](../businesses/pingo.md), an AI voice companion for speaking practice in about 20 languages. He started it with his college roommate Michael.

## Who he is

A Northwestern student who launched Pingo from a dorm in late December 2024. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md)

## Businesses

- **Pingo.** About $450K to $500K/month, 14 months after launch (2026-04). [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=135s), [37:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2226s)
- 350M to 400M views in the last 30 days and about 3M users (2026-04). [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=196s), [33:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1988s)
- The founders made all videos themselves until $20K MRR, for about 3 months. The team stayed at two people until mid-October 2025. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1105s), [40:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2404s)
- After Y Combinator, they booked about 100 investor meetings and closed a round in about 3 days. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [55:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3301s)

## Repeated advice

- **Fluency over lessons.** Apps like [Duolingo](../businesses/duolingo.md) do not make people conversationally fluent, and that is what most learners want. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=302s)
- **Show the product in every video.** Viral videos without Pingo do not convert. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=90s), [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1000s)
- **Let creators find formats.** The top format, Pingo roasting a user, came from the custom scenario feature. The top roast got 52M views. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=44s), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=737s)
- **Run creators like a VC portfolio.** About 100 creators after cutting 30 to 50. Cut the bottom, keep the top, coach the middle. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1484s), [41:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2497s)
- **Pay base plus non-stacking bonuses.** A base per video plus one tier bonus at 50K, 100K, 200K, 500K or 1M views. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [34:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2040s)
- **Watch which country goes viral.** About 200K Russian users brought $0, because app stores block payments there. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=98s), [28:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1696s)
- **Show the trial timeline on the paywall.** A page with "reminded 2 days before trial ends" won the tests. Users who abandon a 3-day trial convert higher on a 7-day offer. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2716s), [50:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3048s)
