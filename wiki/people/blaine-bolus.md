Blaine Bolus is a co-founder of Castmagic, an AI tool that turns podcasts and video into written content, and a co-host of DTC Pod.

## Who

- He found the Castmagic idea in DTC Pod's show notes: an assistant did not know enough context to write them ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=96s)).
- Co-founders: [Ramon Berrios](ramon-berrios.md) and [Justin](justin.md). The three work remotely from Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Austin ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md): over $120K MRR about 10 months after start, growing over 20% month over month, bootstrapped (2024-06) ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).

## Repeated advice

The founders give this advice together in one interview.

- Build an AI app that sits inside a workflow you already have. Sell a painkiller, not a vitamin ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=120s)).
- Borrow distribution early. Their AppSumo [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) made about $350K and brought about 10,000 users ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)).
- Keep marketing spend under 20% of revenue ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s)).
- Price with a usage cap and track LLM and transcription cost to protect margin ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=408s)).
- Pick co-founders for alignment and complementary skills ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=582s)).
