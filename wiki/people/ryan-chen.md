Ryan Chen is a co-founder of NeuroGum, a brand of focus and energy supplements in gum and mints. He started it after a snowboard accident left him paralyzed at 19.

## Who

- He founded NeuroGum with his college best friend [Kent Yoshimura](kent-yoshimura.md). They funded R&D with savings and Ryan's cashed-out 401(k) ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)).
- He kept his job as a financial analyst at Hulu at first ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=274s)).
- No salary in years 3 and 4. A good salary only from about year 7 ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s), [13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=792s)).

## Businesses

- [NeuroGum](../businesses/neurogum.md): a little over $100K in year 1, $750K in year 2, almost $10M/month (2025-02). Ryan calls it a top 10 TikTok Shop brand ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=518s), [14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=852s)).

## Repeated advice

- Put a known need in a new format. Pills are hard to share, so they put nootropics in gum ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=208s)).
- Validate cheaply. A Hulu manager who tried a sample stayed awake until 3 a.m. ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=346s)).
- [Crowdfund](../topics/crowdfunding.md) the first batch. Indiegogo hit the $30K goal in 3 to 4 days and brought press ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)).
- Go full time when repeat-buyer revenue proves adoption ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)).
- Use TV for exposure. Shark Tank (2019) gave a "step change" even though they turned down both offers ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s)).
- Go into battle with your best friend as [co-founder](../topics/co-founders.md) ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)).
