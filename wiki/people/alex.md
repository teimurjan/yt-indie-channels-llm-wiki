Alex is a co-founder that three sources name by first name only. The sources give no surname, and the three businesses differ, so they are probably three different people.

## Who he is

- **Alex of Boost App Social.** Co-founder of [Boost App Social](../businesses/boost-app-social.md), an iOS app for Instagram growth. He now runs [Screens Design](../businesses/screens-design.md), a library of app onboarding flows and paywalls ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=26s), [41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2476s)).
- **Alex of Baked.** A designer who co-founded [Baked](../businesses/baked-design.md), a monthly design subscription agency, with [Nick Buzz](../people/nick-buzz.md) ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=877s)).
- **Alex of Faceless Video.** Co-founder of [Faceless Video](../businesses/faceless-video.md) with [Jacob Seeger](../people/jacob-seeger.md). He has a film background ([source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=761s)).

## Businesses

- **Boost App Social.** About $100K MRR, almost only from Meta ads, then sold for seven figures (2026-04) ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=26s)). Total Meta spend was $1.5M to $1.8M over about 2.5 years, and about $800K of it was wasted ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [20:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1254s)). The sale closed after 4 to 5 months of talks ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [32:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1924s)).
- **Screens Design.** Over 2,200 apps with full onboarding flows and paywalls. It grows only through organic channels (2026-04) ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [41:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2476s)).
- **Baked.** First money at the end of July, then MRR of $24K, $48K, $94K, $110K, $120K and $160K. It then flattened at $100K to $120K (2024). Nick and Alex ran it alone until December ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).
- **Faceless Video.** Alex saw viral faceless videos in his TikTok feed and wanted an internal tool. Jacob turned it into SaaS ([source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=192s)). It passed $1M ARR in about 10 months, 100% bootstrapped (2025-06) ([source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=60s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Volume in ad creative.** Only 1 creative in 85 got the spend and worked, so you need many ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=38s)). Use captions, a green screen over the app and wild hooks in the first 5 seconds ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1367s)).
- **Buy perpetual rights to creator videos.** With a one-year license, a creator can demand $100K for a winning ad ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=673s)).
- **Build business apps, not clones.** Business users pay $200 to $300 a year because they earn it back ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [37:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2233s)).
- **Market all the time.** The Baked team went back to posting landing-page roasts, and inbound leads came ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=855s)).
- **Use ads to start the story.** For Faceless Video, Alex's film background shaped the story in the ads. Influencers who saw the ads then promoted it with no outreach ([source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=761s)).
