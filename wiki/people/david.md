# David

David is a non-technical founder who, with his brother Daniel, built the AI app builder [Shipper](../businesses/shipper.md).

## Who

- He and Daniel learned marketing instead of code. They have built, exited and acquired several companies since 2019 ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=177s)).

## Businesses

- [Shipper](../businesses/shipper.md): $25.6K MRR, about $71K gross volume in the month, about 690 paid users (2026-05) ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=99s)).
- Legit Check, a luxury goods authentication service. Its success funded later projects ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=177s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Copy a big market and niche down.** 1% of the AI app-builder market was enough for them ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=258s)).
- **Mine competitor complaints** on Trustpilot, public roadmaps and Discord servers ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=485s)).
- **Remove jargon** so non-technical users understand the promise ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=529s)).
- **Channel sequence with no paid ads:** Product Hunt, then Reddit, then competitor SEO, then building in public on X ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s)).
- **Idea formula.** Take a big product and imagine a version for people like you, such as Duolingo for cooking ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=561s)).
