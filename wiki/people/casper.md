Casper is a finance and side-hustle creator with about 9M followers and the 50/50 distribution co-founder of Payout.

## Who he is

- A short-form creator. About 70% of his views come from non-followers, and he averages about 1.4M views per post across platforms (2026-03). ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [24:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1459s), [30:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1808s))
- He partners with [Connor Burd](connor-burd.md), who builds the apps. ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s))

## Businesses

- [Payout](../businesses/payout.md): an app that finds class action settlements.
  - About $45K/month from influencers alone. Casper had driven about 35,000+ clicks (2025-12). ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=197s), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s))
  - $217K in 5 months. Casper calls it a 100K/month app (2026-03). ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=209s))
  - The idea came from a competitor that offered him only $500 for a sponsored video. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=350s))
- For a third app he gave the developer only 15%. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [50:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=3037s))

## Repeated advice

- **Brand deals are free experiments.** When a sponsored format wins, reuse its hook for your own app. Keep the structure and change the number, time and background. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=953s))
- **Never say the app's name.** "Go to this app" sounds like a friend's tip, not an ad. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1229s))
- **Sell the money, not the news.** Videos about why a company was sued got views but no sign-ups. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [22:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1338s))
- **Use a comment keyword.** ManyChat "comment CLAIM" on Instagram. On one video about 13,000 commenters asked for the link, about 50% clicked, and download-to-paid was about 6.7% (2026-03). ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [14:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=897s), [18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1129s))
- **The pitch that wins him:** an app built for his audience from an ad that already worked for him, plus 50% equity. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=681s))
- **Coordinate pushes.** Five creators posting on one day made $9,700 and put Payout at about #70 to #80 in Finance. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [46:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2771s))
