Sebastian Röhl is a solo developer in Germany who built HabitKit, a habit tracker for iOS and Android.

## Who he is

- A 32-year-old developer (2025-06). He quit his job with 12 months of runway, had few downloads after 6 months, and went back to his old job. He kept building on the side and quit again at the start of 2024. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=0s), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=713s))

## Businesses

- [HabitKit](../businesses/habitkit.md): a simple habit tracker with a tile grid chart, local-only data and no sign-in. The MVP took about 2 months in Flutter. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=198s))
  - $3K MRR at the end of 2023, about $15K MRR at the end of 2024. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=65s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=268s))
  - About 300K downloads and over 2,000 reviews (2025-06). ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=110s))

## Repeated advice

- **Build in public early.** He posted progress on X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads. It led to podcasts and developer contacts. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s))
- **Put the main keyword first in the app name.** "Habit tracker" put him in the top 5 in several countries. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s))
- **Ask for a review at the first success moment,** right after the first habit check-off. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s))
- **Spend a little on Apple Search Ads** (about $100/month, 2025-06) in the hope that Apple favors advertisers. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s))
- **Solve your own problem,** so you are always the first customer. ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=520s))
