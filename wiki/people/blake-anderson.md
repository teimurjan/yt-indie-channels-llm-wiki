# Blake Anderson

Blake Anderson is a self-taught iOS app founder who built or co-founded [Rizz GPT](../businesses/rizz-gpt.md), [Umax](../businesses/umax.md) and [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md).

## Who

- He started building apps at Tulane. His first apps mostly failed before he moved to AI apps ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=86s)).
- After college his goal was to make $50,000 in 12 months ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=202s)).
- He learned to code with ChatGPT in mid-2023 ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s)). Joseph Choi calls him "probably one of the best in the world" at growing apps (2025-04) ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [1:19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4764s)).
- Other founders name him as the benchmark. [Lotts Ezeike](lotts-ezeike.md) tells new founders who "want to become like Blake Anderson" to get a first $5K to $10K MRR win first ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [18:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1109s)). [Benjamin Chen](benjamin-chen.md) credits Blake for much of what he learned ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=732s)).

## Businesses

It peaked a little over $250K MRR and settled at $150K to $200K MRR, about $2.5M/year (2024-12) ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=277s), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=338s)). He later left it ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md)).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick niches where people already spend money.** Skin care and hair care spend led to Umax ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=459s)).
- **Validate by immersion.** Make a new social account and watch only the niche's content ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=533s)).
- **Copycats cap out.** A copy with strong distribution can reach $10K or $100K a month. He never answers a DM about a copycat app ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=518s)). He tweeted that Cal AI would be the most copied app on the App Store ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [1:15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=4524s)).
- **Influencers and in-house UGC over paid ads.** His margins by channel: in-house UGC 50 to 80%, influencers 25 to 70%, paid ads 0 to 30% ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- **Persistent creator outreach.** He once messaged a creator every 10 minutes until he replied ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=698s)).
- **Low prices and paywall tests.** Low prices bring more word of mouth. Superwall tests can lift revenue per download 1.5 to 2x ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)).
- **Hire on Upwork with a trial.** Hire 5 to 10 people for 1 to 2 hours of work and keep the best one ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=932s)).
- **One domain per co-founder, full trust** ([sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=220s)).
