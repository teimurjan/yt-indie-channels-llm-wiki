Leandro Zubrezki is a developer in Buenos Aires who built the one-feature add-on Sync2Sheets.

## Who he is

- He freelanced for 8 years and quit during COVID to build his own products ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=45s)).

## Businesses

- [Sync2Sheets](../businesses/sync2sheets.md), a Google Sheets add-on that syncs Notion databases to Sheets. He built it in 2 weeks with Google Apps Script ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=269s)).
- About $9K/month at 4 years old, with over 70K users and over 450 paying customers (2025-06) ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=45s), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=69s)).
- Margin about 90% (2025-06) ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=722s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Find ideas in API gaps, Zapier and Upwork.** Notion's new API opened the gap. Popular Zapier app pairs and Upwork jobs show what people pay for ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=163s), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=310s)).
- **Validate on Reddit.** He searched the Notion subreddit for "Google Sheets", "Excel" and "CSV" ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)).
- **Use platform marketplaces and SEO.** Marketplace search brings steady installs. Blog posts are a main channel ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=542s), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)). See [platform marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
- **Reply on Reddit with F5Bot alerts,** and say you are the founder ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)). See [Reddit marketing](../topics/reddit-marketing.md).
- **Launch sites give traffic, not customers.** Product Hunt and the Hacker News front page gave spikes and backlinks but few payers ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=651s)).
- **Charge from day one.** Removing the free plan took revenue from about $5K to $8K/month in a few months ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=836s)).
