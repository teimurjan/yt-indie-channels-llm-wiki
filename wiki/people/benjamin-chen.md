Benjamin "Benji" Chen is a 20-year-old NYU student who built more than 45 apps in about a year, then focused on Snag, an iOS app that shows free items nearby.

## Who

- He sold a media company for six figures, then worked in Congress, at investment firms and in media ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=152s)).
- He builds with his team at 10X Studio and credits [Blake Anderson](blake-anderson.md) for much of what he learned ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=732s)).

## Businesses

- [Snag](../businesses/snag.md): about $30K MRR about 4 months after launch, over 100K users, over $80K total proceeds (2026-05) ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s)).
- Earlier: Pillar (11,000 users at almost zero CAC), Height GPT and 43 more products ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=152s)).

## Repeated advice

- Start from the marketing hook, then design in Figma, then build with Claude Code. An app takes 4 to 5 hours without the backend ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=226s)).
- Find top earners on Sensor Tower and build the same app 10% better ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=345s)).
- Interview UGC creators. About 10% are good. Every 100K views makes about $1K to $2K in subscription profit (2026-05) ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=506s)).
- Turn videos over 50K views into Meta ad tests at about $50/day. Scale to $100, $200, then $300 when ROAS is above 1, and keep making new creatives ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=539s)). See [ad creative](../topics/ad-creative.md).
- With no budget, film the videos yourself and hire an editor on a package deal ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=673s)).
