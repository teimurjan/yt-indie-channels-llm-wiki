This page covers two founders named Jack. The sources give no surname for either, and the two are most likely different people.

> Note: [d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md) features Jack, the founder of Profit AI. [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) names Jack as a co-founder of Stronger. Nothing in the sources links them. If a later source gives surnames, split this page.

## Jack of Profit AI

### Who

- He ran a high-margin e-commerce brand that sold music production software. It launched in 2016, and a $1,000 Facebook ads test "just worked" ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=215s)).
- He then consulted for agencies and Shopify brands. He built them profit spreadsheets: P&L, LTV, ROAS calculators and scenario planners ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)).
- He did not know how to code. He says he spent hundreds of hours in Codex to learn ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=275s)).

### Businesses

- **[Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md).** A Shopify app for profit analytics and AI marketing automation. Shopify approved it on Christmas Day 2025 ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)).
- About 6 months later it had made $147,000 in total. It ran at $30K MRR for the app and just under $40K MRR for services (2026-06) ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=54s)).
- It had 122 users and 38 or 39 paying customers. Installs and uninstalls were about equal: 47 against 43 (2026-06) ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)).

### Repeated advice

- **Productize the spreadsheet you already sell.** Export it as CSV, upload it to an AI coding tool and ask it to build the app. Then fix each wrong calculation ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=335s), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=390s)).
- **Only insiders buy? Lower the price.** At $5,000/month only existing clients paid. At $800/month the app sells ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s)).
- **Let free users see everything.** The free plan includes a 30-day trial of the paid plan ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)).
- "Just ask Claude. Just do it and stick at it." ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=589s))

## Jack of Stronger

- Jack co-founded [Stronger](../businesses/stronger.md), a gamified workout tracker from Northern Ireland, with [Peter](peter.md) ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=86s)).
- While Peter worked in management consulting, Jack kept growing the app, and it took off on TikTok ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=86s)).
- After the first viral video, Jack stitched comments such as "I benched 220 lb". He showed the object equivalent on his phone, and each reply became new content ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [34:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2083s), [35:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2153s)).
- Stronger had about 1.2M users and about $600K ARR (2025-08) ([source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s)).
