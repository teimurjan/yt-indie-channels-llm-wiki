Ayush is the marketing co-founder of Elephas, a Mac AI assistant that he grew with Reddit and then SEO.

## Who he is

- He joined Elephas as a consultant after 11 years in a corporate job. His partner codes and he does marketing. The two have over 30 failed projects between them. ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=175s), [18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=1134s))

## Businesses

- [Elephas](../businesses/elephas.md): a Mac AI assistant that indexes local files, PDFs and Apple Notes into "super brains" that users can chat with.
  - About $150K ARR, about $12K/month (2026-01). ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=78s), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=274s))
  - Reddit took it from $0 to $3K MRR in 6 months. SEO brought about $70K from Google alone in 2023. ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=228s), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=274s))
  - Monthly, annual and lifetime plans, because Mac users expect a one-time payment. ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=97s))

## Repeated advice

- **Reddit posts as a demo inside a story.** "My friend had this problem, so I built this. Try it free for 30 days. I want feedback." ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=399s))
- **Reddit playbook.** List at least 15 subreddits, pick one pain point, and post in one subreddit per day with a UTM link. Expect bans and keep backup accounts. ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=499s), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=611s))
- **Measure Reddit by feedback, not upvotes.** A normal post gets 30 to 50 upvotes. The honest comments are the value. ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=639s))
- **SEO playbook.** In Ahrefs, filter for keyword difficulty under 20 and volume over 500. Write listicles such as "18 best ChatGPT Mac apps". AI answer engines now cite them. ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=762s), [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=826s))
