Erikas is a Lithuanian designer in Paris who co-founded the Kaching Shopify apps with a developer.

## Who he is

- He started at a web design agency for $2 per hour and began freelancing around 2015 or 2016 ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=198s)).
- The Shopify Partners page said the top 25% of app developers make almost $300K per year. That made him enter the ecosystem ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=261s)).

## Businesses

- [Kaching Bundles](../businesses/kaching-bundles.md) and four later upsell apps: $4.5M ARR at about 90% profit (2025-04). Kaching Bundles alone is at about $400K MRR (2025-04). Launched in August 2022 ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=62s), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).

## Repeated advice

- Find the gap between indie apps with poor UX and VC apps that ship slowly. A designer-led team can beat both ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=136s)).
- Validate with a design mockup. His screenshot of an unbuilt app got over 100 likes in dropshipper groups ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=341s)).
- Rank in the app store with five-star reviews. About 95% of reviews come from support agents who earn a bonus per review ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s), [12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=733s)).
- Launch free. Charge only after reviews and keyword rank arrive, and grandfather early users ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s)).
- A designer and a developer are enough to start. Do support yourself until tickets grow ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=974s)).
