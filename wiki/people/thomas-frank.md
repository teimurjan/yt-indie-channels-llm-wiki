Thomas Frank is a productivity YouTuber who sells premium Notion templates through a small niche channel and an email list.

## Who he is

- He runs a productivity channel with close to 3M subscribers and about 180M lifetime views (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).
- He burned out on the main channel and started [Thomas Frank Explains](../businesses/thomas-frank-explains.md), a niche channel that only teaches Notion. It had about 230K subscribers (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).

## Businesses

- Notion templates: about $2.5M in lifetime sales to about 30,000 customers (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).
  - [Ultimate Brain](../businesses/ultimate-brain.md): about $90K in 30 days from one email to a waitlist of about 3,200. After a 45-minute walkthrough video, revenue rose to $100K to $120K/month (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=377s)).
  - [Creator's Companion](../businesses/creators-companion.md): his team's own video system from 2018 to 2021, polished. It made about $3K/month at first and $15K/month within 6 months (2021) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=408s)).
- [Thomas Frank Explains](../businesses/thomas-frank-explains.md): the Notion channel that feeds the email list. The list had 60K to 70K Notion subscribers and 200K in total (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).
- Pricing: Creator's Companion launched at $99 and $149. The full bundle is $229. A price rise cut volume a little but raised total revenue (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Niche channel over big channel.** People already search for Notion help. Cast a slightly wider net around the tool, as Zapier does, then show the subset what your product does ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=678s)).
- **Every video pitches something.** A paid template, a free template, or a resource page behind an email opt-in ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)).
- **A long demo video works as an ad.** The 45-minute walkthrough stayed educational and drove the jump to $100K/month ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- **Sell support, not files.** Templates are easy to pirate, so the paid value is gated support in Circle. He did support 8 hours a day for at least a month to learn pain points ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=377s), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s)).
- **Copy the model on any growing software niche.** Monetize with consulting, courses, templates or SaaS ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=739s)).
- **"Fan first" networking.** Share and comment on the work of people you admire. Over time it turns into friendships ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [14:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=842s)).
- "Try to get just a little bit better at something interesting every single time you publish" ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=627s)).
