Florin Pop is a developer and content creator. He made over $500K in 6 years from about 8 small income streams, not one big hit (2026-02) ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=77s)).

## Who he is

- He learned to code in high school and freelanced through college. He then worked 2 years as a developer and quit in 2019 to build his own things ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=168s)).
- His stack: Next.js, Supabase, Lemon Squeezy, Vercel, beehiiv, an analytics tool and ChatGPT ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=698s)).

## Businesses

All figures are lifetime totals (2026-02) ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=108s)).

- **Coding course:** over $180K. It still earns passive revenue ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=633s)).
- **YouTube ads and sponsorships:** over $100K.
- **First successful SaaS:** $68K in revenue, then sold for $50K.
- **Ebook:** over $30K.
- **Consulting:** $14K.
- **Freelancing:** tens of thousands.
- **Small products:** $10K to $15K combined.

## Repeated advice

- **Treat projects like an index fund.** Most projects fail because of timing, distribution or the market. Many small bets spread the risk ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s)).
- **One project at a time.** Grow each until it runs on its own, then start the next ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=399s)).
- **Stay in one niche.** He made content, then products, then a learning platform, all for developers. A course buyer also signs up for the SaaS ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=448s), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=540s)).
- **Five-step loop.** Build small, ship in public, get feedback, add a paid tier early, then double down or move on with no guilt ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=573s), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=633s)).
- **Most projects fail because nobody hears of them** ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=573s)).
- **Content feeds products.** Content gets attention, and attention makes products easier to sell ([source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s)).
