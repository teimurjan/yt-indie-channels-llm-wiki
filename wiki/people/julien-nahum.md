Julien Nahum is a former software engineer who built NotionForms, a form builder for Notion users.

## Who he is

- A former software engineer, 28 at the time of the video (2024-11) ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=0s)).
- He built the first MVP in 6 days next to a 9-to-5 job with a 3-hour daily commute ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s)).

## Businesses

- [NotionForms](../businesses/notionforms.md): a form builder for Notion. He built it after Notion released its public API and copied Airtable's forms feature ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s)).
  - Close to 100K registered users and close to $37K MRR (2024-11) ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=70s)).
  - First sale: a $90 yearly plan on the launch night of the paid tier ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s)).
  - Product Hunt brought traffic and a backlink but, by his account, no sales ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=463s)).
  - Monthly costs: $5K for a team of 3.5, $1K servers, $3K Google Ads, $2K freelancers (2024-11) ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=811s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build on a platform.** Notion already had a big subreddit and many Facebook groups. Solve a sub-problem and borrow the community ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=135s)).
- **Find ideas three ways.** Solve your own problem, ask non-technical friends about hours of manual work, and watch Twitter for new tech ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=170s)).
- **Test ideas by building.** Ship an MVP in about a week with a stack you already know ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=239s)).
- **Put a link in every shared output.** Form respondents see the branding and a link back ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=511s)).
- **Tag beta features "Pro", then turn on billing.** He did it at about 1,000 users. Early users got a lifetime discount ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=638s)).
- **Price below incumbents, then raise.** He saw no visible drop in conversion ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=706s)).
- **Build only what users ask for.** He did all support himself for a year ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=579s)).
