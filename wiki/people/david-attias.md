David Attias is a former quant trader from France who cloned a proven app with Cursor and built Stoppr, a sugar-craving app.

## Who he is

- He worked 8 years as a quant trader. He has no coding background ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=142s)).
- He heard the teen founders of Quitter made $200K/month after 3 months. "I was envious, I was jealous, and that's what got me started." ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=142s))

## Businesses

- [Stoppr](../businesses/stoppr.md): helps Gen Z women stop sugar cravings. Users are women aged about 13 to 25 ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=85s)).
  - $5K in the first month and about $12K/month 5 months later (2025-10) ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=58s)).
  - 60,000 downloads and about 900 paying customers in 5 months (2025-10) ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s)).
  - Profit margin of 35% after 5 months (2025-10) ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=483s)).
  - The full copy of Quitter took 2.5 weeks ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=314s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Copy a proven app and change the niche.** He copied Quitter's onboarding and swapped the content for sugar ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=237s)).
- **Localize an English app.** No one in France offered this kind of app, so he worked with French influencers ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s)).
- **Test demand with trends and content.** "Stop sugar" had risen for 5 years on Google Trends ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=278s)).
- **Clone from screenshots.** Pull every screen from Mobbin or screens.design into Figma. Connect the Figma MCP to Cursor ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=348s)).
- **Find influencers who already promote apps.** Top Yappers finds creators who worked with other apps ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s)).
- [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md) adds a caveat: copy the app but not the niche ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=488s)).
