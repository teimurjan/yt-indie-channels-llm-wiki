This page collects what the sources say about Frank Smith, a sports creator who built the web game [GeoSports](../businesses/geosports.md).

## Who he is

- Frank is a sports short-form creator with over 4M followers. He is not technical ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=47s)).
- He made YouTube videos from 2007 but only broke out in 2020 ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=766s)).

## Businesses

### GeoSports and the Geo Games

- GeoSports is a daily sports geography web game. Players read a clue and tap the place on a map, five questions a day ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=146s)).
- He built it with Claude in about 8 hours over one weekend ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=585s)).
- The Geo Games family (GeoSports, GeoHistory, GeoFooty) makes about $40K/month, three months after launch (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=47s)).
- Programmatic ads bring $30K to $35K/month. About 1,800 Pro subscribers pay through Stripe (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- The games have about 700K monthly active users (2026-09) ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- About 53% of daily players share their score ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)).
- A reply to his own NBA tweet brought about 40K players, then 150K the next day. The game was 5 days old ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=636s)).

### Five Card Draw

An earlier game, Five Card Draw, had about 110K players ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=247s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Remix a game you love**. His brother showed him MapTap. Frank added a sports trivia twist ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=413s)).
- **Spec first, then build with Claude**. He had Claude research MapTap and write a spec. When he got stuck, he sent screenshots ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=456s)).
- **Build for the web first**. People played inside X without leaving the app ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)).
- **Make the result shareable**. The score share is the growth engine, and most players come from group chats ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=357s)).
- **Use guest hosts**. Athletes and sports media people write the daily questions and retweet the game ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=714s)).
- **Block 8 hours to start**. When stuck, ask the LLM ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=585s)).
- **Be patient**. His games took off in 2026, years after he started to post ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=766s)).
