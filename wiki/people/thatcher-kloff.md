Thatcher Kloff is a co-founder of [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md), a science-based app to quit or cut down on weed.

## Who

- He co-founded Clear 30 with [Asher Hurstman](asher-hurstman.md) and the clinical psychologist Dr. Fred Munch ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md)).

## Businesses

- **[Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md).**
  - Subscriptions went from $10K to $30K in one month (2025-08) ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=147s)).
  - The program costs $30 a year. The average user spends over $55 a week on weed (2025-08) ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [7:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=441s)).
  - Paywall conversion went from about 20% to 30% after one change to the first paywall screen (2025-08) ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s)).
  - App Store reviews grew from about 50 to almost 1,000 after a rating prompt in onboarding (2025-08) ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s)).
  - It has a grant from the National Institute of Health ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=899s)).

## Repeated advice

The source presents these as the team's shared tactics.

- **Organic stalled, so they moved to paid ads.** Influencers, Reddit, organic content and SEO were uphill. Meta and TikTok ads started the flywheel ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=178s)).
- **Hyper-specific call-outs in ads.** Comedy and detail grab attention ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=307s)).
- **Track from the first screen.** Early drop-off was invisible before sign-in ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=578s)).
- **Build onboarding on the value equation.** Each screen serves dream outcome, likelihood, time delay or effort ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [11:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=679s)).
- **Say the price early.** A screen that says the program costs money raised conversion by about 3% ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1038s)).
- **Show a day-by-day plan on the paywall**, not a feature carousel ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s)).
