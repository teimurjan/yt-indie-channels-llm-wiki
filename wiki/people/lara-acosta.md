Lara Acosta is a LinkedIn creator who co-founded [Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md), an AI ghostwriter for founders who post on LinkedIn ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=0s)).

## Who she is

- She started posting on LinkedIn about 3 years before the video and built two businesses from it before Kleo ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s)).
- Her background is in agencies and info products. She brought their launch tactics to SaaS ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s)).
- Her co-founders are [Jake](jake.md) and Rob ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s), [15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=905s)).

## Businesses

- **[Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md).** It launched in October 2025 to a waitlist only. It hit $30K MRR in 4 days and about $60K MRR in under 2 months (2026-01) ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)).
- The Polar dashboard showed $62K MRR and 932 active subscriptions (2026-01) ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)).
- The product was still not on public sale. Visitors join a waitlist ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=124s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Content, waitlist, webinars.** Build demand for a product nobody has seen, then launch ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=228s)).
- **Teach, do not pitch.** Posts teach LinkedIn tips or name founder problems, with no call to action ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=291s)).
- **Nurture the list.** More than 10 emails went out in the 4 weeks before launch. The first email answered the main objection: ChatGPT is free, so why pay? The CTA appeared only on launch day, in the first line ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=451s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=511s)).
- **Email beats viral posts.** "It's actually emails where the customers are" ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=572s)).
- **The 4-3-2-1 framework.** Post 4 times a week, on 3 pillars (education, story, sales), for 2 personas (client and follower), with 1 lead magnet ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=651s)).
- **Webinar format.** 20 minutes of teaching, 20 minutes of demo, then the pitch and the link ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=744s)).
- **Scarcity.** 500 spots only and 50% off for life. Scarcity beat curiosity and FOMO as the first push to buy ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=856s)).
- **White-glove onboarding calls.** If users do not understand the software, they churn ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=807s)).
