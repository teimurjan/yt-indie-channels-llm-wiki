# Saeed Ezzati

Saeed Ezzati is a software engineer who runs [Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md), a browser extension that adds features to ChatGPT, on his own.

## Who

- He spent almost a decade at startups as an engineer ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md)).
- He shipped the first version two or three days after ChatGPT launched, in plain JavaScript, HTML and CSS, with no prior extension experience ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s)).

## Businesses

- [Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md): $20K to $30K MRR, about 150K weekly active users (2024-08) ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=76s)).
- A newsletter for the extension's users. Its first sponsor paid a few hundred dollars within a week or two ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build on a big platform.** If 2,000 of hundreds of millions of users want a feature, that pays a living. A small marketplace like Zoom or Salesforce has less competition ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=120s)).
- **Extensions validate fast** before you build a full back end ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=180s)).
- **Listen where users hang out**, build what they ask for, then tell them it exists. He calls this his biggest growth hack ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=228s)).
- **Charge only for new features** and keep existing ones free ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=583s)).
- **Accept platform risk.** OpenAI can kill the product any day. His fallback is extensions for other platforms ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=676s)).
- In hindsight he waited too long to monetize ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s)).
