Jacob Seeger is a former musician who built Faceless Video, a no-code SaaS on Bubble that runs faceless social media channels on autopilot.

## Who

- He took his own music to almost 1M streams on a small budget, then ran Domino, a music marketing agency ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=114s)).
- Faceless Video was his seventh SaaS idea after six failures ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=0s)).
- Co-founder: [Alex](alex.md), who found the idea in his TikTok feed. Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md) ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=192s)).

## Businesses

- [Faceless Video](../businesses/faceless-video.md): over $1M ARR in about 10 months, 100% bootstrapped, over 1.1M signups (2025-06) ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=60s), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=80s)).

## Repeated advice

- Prove the technical core first. The first MVP only turned text into a video. Accounts and payments came later ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s)).
- Stay on [no code](../topics/no-code.md) until it breaks ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s)).
- Use ads to start the story. A $200 Twitter thread ad got hundreds of thousands of views, and influencers who saw it promoted the product unasked ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s)).
- B2C needs viral potential, because low prices make heavy ad spend hard ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s)).
- Plan for the best case: can the app and pricing survive 100,000 signups in a day? ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=700s)).
