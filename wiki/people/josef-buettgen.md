Josef Buettgen is a former freelance software engineer and co-founder of Setter AI, an AI appointment setter.

## Who

- He met co-founder [Timo Nikolai](timo-nikolai.md) at a coworking space. Both had spent months on products with no customers ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md)).
- Applied to YC five times, with one interview (2025-09) ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Setter AI](../businesses/setter-ai.md): follows up with and qualifies leads for solar, coaching and financial services. About $10K revenue last month and 38 paying customers out of about 2,000 signups (2025-09) ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=592s)).
- Pricing from about $50/month up to $5,000/month for the largest customer. Margins 90%+ without ads (2025-09) ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=73s), [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=692s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Sell before you build.** A fake landing page with an ElevenLabs demo, one H1 keyword and a Calendly link earned a $500 Stripe payment in February 2024, before any code ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=100s), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
- **Validation means a card swipe.** Traffic and booked calls do not count ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=350s)).
- **Five-step validation:** have a job to find B2B pain. Build a keyword landing page on a warm domain. Add a calendar link. Take a refundable high-ticket deposit such as $500. Let [SEO](../topics/seo.md) incubate ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=288s)).
- **Double down on the validation channel.** Free tools, "HubSpot alternatives" comparison posts and long-form YouTube. Most closed customers watched at least one of their videos ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)).
- "Charge early on. Go high ticket." ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=725s)).
- He suggests a [Lovable](../businesses/lovable.md)-style builder for native mobile apps as an open idea ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=461s)).
