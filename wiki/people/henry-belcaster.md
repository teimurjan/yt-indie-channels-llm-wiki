Henry Belcaster co-founded [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md), an animation and video editing agency, and [Smart Nonsense](../businesses/smart-nonsense.md), an animated YouTube Shorts brand. His partner is his college roommate [Dylan Jardon](dylan-jardon.md).

## Who he is

Henry met Dylan in 2015. In 2020 they started a podcast on unemployment money and had 14 views after 3 months. They are based in Chicago. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=164s)

## Businesses

- **Clipt.** It matches top animators with companies. It makes about $2M/year in revenue (2023-10). Clients include My First Million, the All-In Podcast, Ali Abdaal, Will Smith and Naval. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s)
- **Smart Nonsense.** One animated 60-second Short per day. It reached 1 billion combined views and 1M YouTube subscribers in about a year (2023-10). The content team costs $25K to $30K/month, and the Shorts earn about $5K/month, so it runs at a loss. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=125s), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)
- The team has about 50 animators and editors, mostly in the Philippines (2023-10). [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)

## Repeated advice

- **Blitz strategy.** [Sam Parr](sam-parr.md) and Shaan Puri asked on Twitter for video help. Henry and Dylan offered to do it free and showed up the next day. For All-In, they sent three videos, and Jason Calacanis hired them. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=284s)
- **Squatter marketing.** Act as though a big creator hired you and make their videos unpaid for a year. Their network then assumes you work together. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=338s)
- **Story beats abstraction.** A good 60-second story holds people for about 55 seconds. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=428s)
- **Pair a shipper with a perfectionist.** Henry ships at 80%, and Dylan sweats details. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s)
- **Stay above the clouds.** Henry spends about 1 hour a day on the agency. The rest goes to research and content. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=690s)
- **Keep the core simple.** One animator matched with one client for 2 years matters more than pricing models. [e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md) [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=810s)
