Ania Wysocka is a non-technical founder who built Rootd, an iPhone app for panic attack and anxiety relief, after her own panic attack at university.

## Who

- She had no technical background. She sketched the app in a notebook, made wireframes in Photoshop and Illustrator, and hired a student developer with her savings ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=208s), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=259s)).
- For several years she worked 4 days a week at a job and 3 days on Rootd. She went full time about 2.5 to 3 years in ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=343s), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=366s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Rootd](../businesses/rootd.md): launched 2019. Over 4M downloads and over $1M revenue, rated 4.8 (2025-08). About 10K downloads in year 1, 100K in year 2, 1M in year 3 ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=59s), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=318s), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=643s)).

## Repeated advice

- Validate through competitor reviews. Users said no app explained a panic attack or how to get through one ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=127s)).
- Be helpful in comments: answer with content from the app, and do not lead with "download my app" ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=430s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=533s)).
- Pitch journalists with a personal story. Cold emails landed Cosmopolitan, Women's Health and Time ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=491s)).
- Run the [ASO](../topics/app-store-optimization.md) loop: the page promises what the product delivers, and reviews repeat the keywords ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=491s)).
- Ship weekly and read every review yourself ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=571s), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=643s)).
