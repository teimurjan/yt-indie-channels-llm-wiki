Peter is a co-founder of Stronger, a gamified workout tracker from Northern Ireland built by three engineers.

## Who

- One of three engineers who bootstrapped Stronger with government grants from Northern Ireland. It began as a hardware idea [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=86s), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=311s).

## Businesses

- [Stronger](../businesses/stronger.md): about 1.2M users and about $600K ARR, 2.5 to 3 years in (2025-08) [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s).
  - A 6-second "fade-in" Reels format brought an estimated 150M to 300M views. One month reached 100M views (2025-08) [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=412s), [8:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=502s).
  - Peak rank: number 3 in Health and Fitness [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [33:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2018s).
- A second app, a gamified focus app described as "Forest meets FarmVille" [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2902s).

## Repeated advice

- **Make the product the marketing asset.** Design features that look good as a screenshot, such as a strength heat map or "you lifted a Boeing 747" [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=269s), [26:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1606s).
- **Scale a winning format across accounts** with micro edits, so each copy is a new video [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=530s).
- **Every video is a funnel:** hook, body, download. Borrow formats from other niches and reskin them [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [41:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2506s).
- **Look a little rough.** "By looking less professional on Tik Tok, you actually perform much better" [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [39:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2353s).
- **Test macro paywall changes first.** A hard [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md), found by a bug, gave about a 25% boost overnight. "If you can give people an excuse to not pay, they won't" [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [24:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1452s).
- **Gamify lightly.** Heavy ranks appeal to ages 15 to 23, but the money is in 30 to 40 year olds [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [28:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1709s). See [gamification](../topics/gamification.md).
- **Keep growth in-house.** Agencies add about a 20% margin and weaker incentives [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [43:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2614s).
- **Moats after vibe coding:** hidden content tooling, technical difficulty and network effects [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=2902s).
