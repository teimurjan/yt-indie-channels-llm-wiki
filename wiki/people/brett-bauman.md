# Brett Bauman

Brett Bauman is a former Loom employee who co-founded [Coconote](../businesses/coconote.md), an AI note-taker for college students that sold to Quizlet.

## Who

- He and [Zack Hargett](zack-hargett.md) both worked at Loom before Coconote ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md)).

## Businesses

- [Coconote](../businesses/coconote.md): bootstrapped to $6.7M ARR with no VC money, 2M+ registered users and over 50% EBITDA. Quizlet bought it about 18 months after launch (2026-04) ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=0s), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=139s)).
- Ramp: $100K ARR in 45 days, $1M ARR in 4 months, $5M ARR after a year ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [32:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1967s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Target intent, not views.** A 10M-view video that reaches students with a real problem beats a 40M-view novelty video. "PDF to brain rot" got 100M to 200M views but made only about $25K ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s), [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=453s)).
- **Small creator team.** 10 to 12 part-time creators at peak, with one coach for 12 ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s)).
- **One big onboarding jump.** Going from about 5 to about 13 screens raised trial conversion 16% ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s)).
- **Login after the paywall**, and a 7-day trial extension in the cancel flow that saves 27% of cancels ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [30:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1801s), [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1906s)).
- **Use a banker for M&A** so founders keep building ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [37:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2247s)).
