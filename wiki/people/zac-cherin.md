Zac Cherin is the co-founder of [Hiro Analytics](../businesses/hiro-analytics.md), a reporting platform for email and SMS marketing agencies.

## Who he is

- His path took about 10 years: a data visualization startup that Amazon bought, work at Amazon, a failed newsletter, an email agency, then Hiro ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=571s)).
- He and his partner Brendan ran an email marketing agency for about 6 years with about 30 clients ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=187s)).

## Businesses

- [Hiro Analytics](../businesses/hiro-analytics.md): launched October 2024, over $1M ARR in about 19 months. About $96.2K MRR, growing close to $10K MRR a month (2026-06) ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=58s), [1:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=90s)).
- Pricing scales with client count: about $500/month for 10 clients, plus about $10 per seat (2026-06) ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=149s), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s)).

## Repeated advice

- Sell to the agency layer. One agency brings 10 to 30 brands and many seats ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s)).
- Use platform partner directories, like Klaviyo's and Shopify's, as a ranked lead list ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)).
- Pitch on time saved and growth without staff. Manual reports are the real pain ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=490s), [11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=698s)).
- Defend against the platform by connecting its competitors ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=445s)).
