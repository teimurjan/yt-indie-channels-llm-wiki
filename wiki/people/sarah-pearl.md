Sarah Pearl ("Hot High Priestess") is a creator with millions of followers who vibe-coded Stella, an AI manifestation app.

## Who

- 1.2M Instagram followers and 2.8M TikTok followers, with over 1B combined TikTok views (2026-07) ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=150s)).
- She built the MVP in about 2 days and the full app in 2 to 3 months with Claude, Claude Code and Google AI Studio ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=399s)).

## Businesses

- [Stella](../businesses/stella.md): over $300K MRR within 2 months of launch, about $350K to $360K MRR at recording, 12,000 paying customers and over 200,000 downloads (2026-07) ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s)).
- A TikTok Shop brand that makes "seven figures a month" (2026-07) ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=220s)).

## Repeated advice

- Distribution beats product. Virality is a skill: she claims she can grow any new account to 50,000 followers in 3 to 4 months. A post-launch account passed 40,000 in a couple of months ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=169s)).
- Build for women. Many apps ignore female problems because men designed them ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=220s)).
- Pick a niche, study its top content, and recreate winning formats "to infinity". Work backward from a viral video to a product that fits it ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=559s)).
- Demo the use case. A skit where she answers "How can I remember all these?" with Stella's lock-screen affirmations converts. A demo video with only 300K views converted very well ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=633s), [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=717s)).
- Make savable content and use ManyChat: "Comment DOWNLOAD" sends a DM with the link and a review request ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=656s)).
- Treat hate as an algorithm boost ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=489s)).
- Content first, product second: build what comments say "I wish this existed" ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=777s)).
