Rob Hallam is a solo founder who builds SuperX and grows it almost entirely through his own X account.

## Who he is

- A laid-off software engineer. He started to post on X in early 2023. His first five products made $0. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=225s))
- He started as a backpacker who built startups and made no money for about a year. He lived in more than 13 countries in the last few years. ([NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=465s))
- A post about his failures got about 150,000 views and brought his first $3,000 dev agency client. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=288s))
- Over 34,000 X followers in just over a year, and about 100,000 impressions a day (2025-12). ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s))

## Businesses

- [SuperX](../businesses/superx.md): a tool that finds viral X posts and helps users write their own. He built it with [Tibo](tibo.md), a former Tweet Hunter co-founder. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s))
  - MRR went from $1K to over $13K in the four months after launch, about 450 subscribers at $29/month (2025-12). X brings 95% of traffic. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=126s))
  - $22K/month, run alone while he travels (2026-08). ([NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=0s))

## Repeated advice

- **Build an audience with a fixed loop.** Set a clear profile and one public goal. Post something entertaining or vulnerable, then education or a pitch, then the result. Study top posts in your niche and copy the concept, not the words. Reply with real value to the same people every day. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=709s), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=796s))
- **Use the format the platform pushes.** Video gave him 10x the reach of text on X. A selfie post beat his launch demo and added $1,000 MRR in 24 hours. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=895s), [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=507s))
- **Put the product link in the second tweet of a thread.** The [Shipper](../businesses/shipper.md) founders credit this tip to him. ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s))
- **Distribution first, charge from day one, build painkillers.** These are his lessons from five failed products. ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=225s))
- **Let customers set priority, and do the boring work.** A critical bug brings five emails. A one-off request was not important. ([NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=283s))
- **Stay in one base for 2 to 3 months.** Moving every few days made deep work almost impossible. He works one long night block of up to 8 hours and protects 9 hours of sleep. ([NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=212s), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=409s))
