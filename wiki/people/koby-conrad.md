Koby Conrad is the founder of Sunflower, an app that helps people quit addictions, and a former growth lead at Rupa Health.

## Who

- He led growth at Rupa Health from $5M to $75M in 3.5 years. Rupa later sold for nine figures ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [37:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2223s)).
- He has spent $10M to $20M on influencer marketing over 10 years ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=279s)).
- He taught himself to code at a bootcamp in 2020 and 2021 to build V1 ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [21:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1268s)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- [Sunflower](../businesses/sunflower.md): about $1M annualized run rate in about 10 months, over 10K paying subscribers (2026-03). About $6.5M raised from YC, a16z Speedrun, Flybridge and angels ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=170s), [38:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2309s)).

## Repeated advice

- Hire the top influencer as an employee with salary and equity instead of paying per post. Sunflower hired Eric ("Mr. Impulsive"), who drives 50K to 100K clicks a month (2026-03) ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=353s), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=212s)).
- Without money, partner 50/50 with a creator and make them the hero ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=654s)).
- Be the biggest sponsor, not the smallest ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [31:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1877s)).
- Put the option you want as the default on the [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md). Trial reminders raised revenue 48% and trial conversions 46% ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s), [46:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2785s)).
- Make the simple core feature great. For Sunflower that is the sobriety timer, not the AI ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1055s)).
- Avoid TikTok account farms. A $100M business needs real audiences ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [29:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1774s)).
- Cheap software will turn apps into lead magnets for services, goods and ads ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [49:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2962s)).
