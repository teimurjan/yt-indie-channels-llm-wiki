Scott Millard is a former freelance video editor who runs a productized service that makes short video ads for B2B SaaS.

## Who he is

- He left college after 2 months, at age 19 (2019). He has made videos since 2016 ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=157s)).
- As a freelancer he earned at most $40-50K/year before tax ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=199s)).

## Businesses

- An unnamed video ad service. It sells short animated "scroll stopper" ads to VC-backed SaaS companies at seed or Series A ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [0:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=28s)).
  - About $50K/month on average, with a range of $35-55K/month (2023-03) ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=282s)).
  - Monthly revenue went $7K, $5K, $9K, $12K, $23K, $33K. It stalled at $12K until he added packages ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=825s)).
  - First video sold for $500. Average order is about $3K. Packages sell for $6-12K (2023-03) ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=677s)).
  - About 65% gross margin and 40-50% net. Tools cost about $1,200/month (2023-03) ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=581s)).
  - About 4 people, all international contractors, including a sales closer ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=505s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Productize the skill.** Sell a fixed package, with a down-sell and an upsell around it. Then hire people to deliver it ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=397s)). See [productized services](../topics/productized-services.md).
- **Niche down in steps.** Videos, then short videos, then online brands, then B2B SaaS. Each step let him raise prices ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=648s)).
- **Sell the outcome.** The package includes videos, reformats for each ad platform and retargeting setup ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=768s)).
- **Two-sentence cold email.** The email points out a missing video above the fold. Reply rate is about 4% (2023-03) ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=877s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- **Do sales first, then hand it off.** He took about 150 calls, wrote down the structure and gave it to a closer ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [16:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=988s)).
- **Post daily for buyers.** One hour a day of educational posts on Twitter and LinkedIn ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=336s)).
- **Hire from the audience.** All his contractors found him through this content ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=557s)).
