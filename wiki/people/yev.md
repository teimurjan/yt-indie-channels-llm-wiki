Yev is head of growth at Sizzle AI, a former data scientist, and runs over 10 faceless YouTube Shorts channels.

## Who

- A former data scientist; head of growth at an edtech learning app. [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) interviewed him ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- [Sizzle AI](../businesses/sizzle-ai.md): churn is 3 to 7 months by audience; each purchase is worth about $30 to $70 LTV, higher on iOS (2025-06) ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [18:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1120s)).
  - One TikTok with 79K likes, picked from 20 to 30 organic videos, drove hundreds of purchases as a paid ad (2025-06) ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=520s), [19:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1167s)).
- Over 10 faceless YouTube channels; one did 26M views in 28 days (2025-06) ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [30:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1844s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Tipping points.** Fix the funnel step with the biggest drop-off for 2x to 3x gains; feature work gave about 10% ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=156s), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=210s)).
- **Creative is the 10x lever**; targeting gives about 10% ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=785s)). See [Ad creative](../topics/ad-creative.md).
- **Amplify organic with paid.** Put a few dollars behind every organic video. The most viral videos often convert worse than middle-virality ones ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=328s), [13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=834s)).
- **Attribution undercounts.** Real purchases are often about double what ads report; measure lift by turning ads off and on ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1336s)).
- **Scale a winner in layers**: headlines, then countries and devices, then in-app flows ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [25:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1500s)).
- **$5K launch plan.** Make up to 100 creatives, spread small budgets, judge on clicks within hours, and take the 2x to 10x outlier to purchase ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [32:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1939s), [36:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=2168s)).
