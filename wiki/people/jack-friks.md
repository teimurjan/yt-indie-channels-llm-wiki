Jack Friks is a bootstrapped solo founder who builds consumer apps and the social scheduler Post Bridge, and grows them with organic short-form video.

## Who

- He quit McDonald's and dropped out of college to build online. He taught himself to code with ChatGPT and worked from his mom's basement ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=0s)).
- Before coding, he tried a book, t-shirts and a crypto YouTube channel over 3 years ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=156s)).
- He had headaches and anxiety for about 2 years. They eased once MRR passed a few thousand a month and he had a year of savings ([source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=218s)).
- Income: $10K/month across two products (2025-03), then $40K/month (2026-08) ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=0s), [source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- **[Curiosity Quench](../businesses/curiosity-quench.md).** A hobby app against doomscrolling. It took 4 months to build while he learned to code. For 8 months it got 50 to 60 downloads a day and little revenue. Then it peaked at $15K in September 2024 ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s), [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=196s), [9:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=579s)). It made $3K MRR and over 100K downloads (2025-03) ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s)).
- **[Post Bridge](../businesses/post-bridge.md).** He built it because he spent 30 minutes a day cross-posting. It made $7K MRR after 4.5 months (2025-03) and about $20K MRR (2025-12) ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=79s), [source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [13:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=824s)). Sahil of Gumroad offered $1M for it on Twitter, and Jack turned it down ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [11:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=718s)). It grew 10x to 100x bigger than his app ([source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=284s)).
- **[Doof](../businesses/doof.md).** A food diary that made $1K in its first 30 days. One meme slideshow got 5M views ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1162s)).
- **[Lovely](../businesses/lovely.md).** A couples app with 102K users, about $10K proceeds and about $4.2K MRR after 10 weeks (2025-12). A user tweet about its widget got 16M views, and his quote tweet brought 40K downloads overnight ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [28:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1689s), [31:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1875s)).

> Conflict: [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) says Post Bridge made about $12K/month around April 2024, when the $1M offer came. [qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) says Post Bridge had $7K MRR after only 4.5 months in 2025-03. The April 2024 date is probably an error.

## Repeated advice

- **Solve your own problem.** His test: does he pay money to solve this problem? If not, he does not build for it ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=225s)). Copying a hit in someone else's niche kills the fun, and that shows in results ([source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=333s)).
- **Let the market judge the idea.** Put out a landing page or a TikTok. Suppose 1,000 people visit and nobody buys. Then the idea or its framing is weak ([source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=333s)). TikTok comments such as "I need this" validated Post Bridge ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=301s)).
- **Scroll first, then copy a winning format.** He scrolls his niche for 15 minutes a day and saves candidates ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=2142s)). One 2x2 meme template, posted about 300 times, drove 60K to 70K signups ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=530s)).
- **Show the app in use.** Meme slideshows are "silver": many views, few downloads. An 8 to 10 second clip of the app in use is "gold" and converts about 20x better ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s)).
- **Use paywall standards.** A template paywall with a free trial took Curiosity Quench from $500 to $1K/month to $2K to $4K/month ([source](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=946s)). On the web, a 7-day card-required trial with no free plan worked better ([source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=795s)).
- **Support first, every day.** Fast replies and fixes kept paying users ([source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s)). See [Retention](../topics/retention.md).
- **Work in sprints and stop at dinner.** Launch weeks get 10-hour days. Quiet months get 1 to 2 hours a day. Dinner is the hard stop ([source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=107s), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=420s)).
