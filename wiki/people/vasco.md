Vasco is the co-founder of [Arvow](../businesses/arvow.md), an AI SEO writer that grew mostly through YouTube.

## Who he is

- He began in 2015 or 2016 with rank-and-rent SEO after an Alex Becker video. He then sold SEO services on Fiverr and Upwork, and built and sold an SEO marketplace called Vetted ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=104s)).

## Businesses

- [Arvow](../businesses/arvow.md), with co-founder Afonso: about $70K MRR and about 600 paying clients, from about $99/month to almost $700/month (2025-10) ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=58s), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=71s)).

## Repeated advice

- Use YouTube because long videos of your face and voice build trust ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=159s)).
- Make three kinds of videos: evergreen, news and viral-style ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=216s)).
- Multiply yourself with owned niche channels run by Upwork creators paid per video and performance ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=277s)).
- Boost videos that already sold with YouTube in-feed ads. Track each video with a UTM and a coupon ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s)).
- Put the call to action at the start and middle, not the end ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=381s)).
- Beginners: post one video every day for 45 days. He recorded 20 to 30 minute videos daily for almost a year ([source](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=548s), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=634s)).
