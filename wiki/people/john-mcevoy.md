John McEvoy is a self-taught graphic designer from Scotland who runs Momego, a live bus and train tracking app, on his own.

## Who

- He worked for a bus company in Edinburgh and wanted to see his bus on a map, like an Uber ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=133s)).
- He designs in Illustrator. He built the first versions in Xamarin. Later he rebuilt the app in native Swift and Flutter in 2 to 3 months ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [2:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=172s), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=235s)).
- "I had a problem I needed to solve, so I built it." ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=10s))

## Businesses

- **[Momego](../businesses/momego.md).** Live transit tracking for over 160 cities. It has about 5.2M downloads since 2017, about 400K monthly active users and about 75K ratings (2025-10) ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=52s), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=77s)).
- Banner ads paid about $8K/month until the pandemic killed ad revenue. A subscription update shipped in August 2020 ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s)).
- In 2021 he ran about 10 paywall experiments over 2 to 3 months. Conversion rose from 0.5% to 8%, and revenue rose from about $8K to over $30K/month ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=573s)).
- About $30K MRR, almost all from subscriptions (2025-10) ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=77s)).
- Biggest costs: about 20 dedicated servers at about $2,500/month and map APIs at about $1,000/month (2025-10) ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [12:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=763s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Use local keywords for ASO.** Put each city's own words in the title and metadata, for example "MTA subway" in New York and "CTA L train" in Chicago. Make location-specific screenshots ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=292s)).
- **Use extra localizations.** Keywords in the Mexican Spanish localization also index in the US App Store ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)). See [Localization](../topics/localization.md).
- **Find long-tail keywords.** Test a few at a time with Apple Search Ads, or read App Store autocomplete ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)).
- **Ask for ratings at a golden moment.** For Momego, that moment is the first view of a live bus moving on the map ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=414s)).
- **Keep onboarding short for impatient users.** People install at a bus stop. So the app asks for location first, shows a few value screens, then the paywall ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=592s)).
- **Offer a reverse trial on close.** A user who closes the paywall gets 7 days of Pro free with no payment details. He calls this "a real unlock" ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=646s)).
- **Track events, not vanity metrics.** Monthly active users did not move the needle. Event analytics and paywall A/B tests did ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=789s)).
