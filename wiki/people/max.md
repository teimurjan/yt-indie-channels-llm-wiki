This page covers two different people named Max: the creator behind [Fitted](../businesses/fitted.md), and an iOS engineer who runs 28 small apps.

The sources give neither person a last name. Both source pages link to this one slug.

## Max of Fitted

### Who he is

- Max built an outfit generator in class, and a TikTok of it went viral. That is how [Reid Moncada](reid-moncada.md) found him ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=38s)).
- He became Reid's co-founder at Fitted, an AI closet app ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=669s)).

### His role

- Reid first paid him $20 a video on a sub-account called "Fitted by Max". Later he added vested equity and a revenue share ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1624s)).
- About 95% of distribution came from Max, cross-posted to the main account and Instagram Reels ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=574s)).
- Fitted had over 500M lifetime views and 600K downloads (2025-11) ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=572s)).
- Max reads comments and DMs, joins design meetings and shapes the roadmap ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=669s)).
- His breakout video was a plain "how to use the Fitted closet" ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=408s)).

## Max with 28 apps

### Who he is

- Max is an iOS engineer with a full-time job and two kids. He worked for years on one pet app with no success ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=30s)).
- A video by [Adam Lyttle](adam-lyttle.md) changed his approach ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=30s)).

### Businesses

- He shipped 28 apps in 8 months next to his job (2025-12) ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- Revenue grew from $200 to $10K per month (2025-12) ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=30s)).
- Four apps make about $1,500 per month each. The rest make very little (2025-12) ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- Tools cost about $300 a month, most of it OpenAI (2025-12) ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=699s)).

### Repeated advice

- **Start from a keyword**. Look for a popularity of at least 20 and a difficulty of about 60 to 70 or lower ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)).
- **Check for money**. Top competitors must make at least $100 to $200 per month ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=249s)).
- **Ship one feature, then move on**. The App Store data shows which apps sink and which float ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=553s)).
- **Reuse everything**. Some apps reuse 90% of an older app's code ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [6:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=389s)).
