Charlie Clark is a former Squarespace engineer who built Liinks, a link-in-bio and simple website tool, into a solo micro-SaaS.

## Who

- He quit Squarespace in 2019 for a print-on-demand marketplace, Visual, built Liinks near the end of that year, went back to Squarespace during COVID, and quit again in summer 2024 ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=318s), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=597s)).
- Interviewed by Gus of [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md).

## Businesses

- [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md): about $31,500 MRR (2026-10) ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=69s)).

## Repeated advice

- Enter a crowded market with a better look. Linktree's pages looked plain ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=23s), [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=351s)).
- Undercut the leader and keep prices low. A rise from $4 to $5/month in late June 2026 added nearly $5K MRR. A $12/month Pro tier balances it out ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)). See [pricing](../topics/pricing.md).
- Use [Google Search Ads](../topics/google-ads.md) for intent. After an Instagram algorithm change he moved to Google, which was "money in, money out" for about 4 years. He spends about $100/day (2026-10) ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=485s), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=788s)).
- Quit at salary parity, not by burning the boats. Tell your employer about side projects up front ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=597s), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=685s)).
