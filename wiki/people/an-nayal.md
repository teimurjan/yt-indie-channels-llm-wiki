An Nayal is the founder of Flame, a couples app that grew on organic TikTok from a farm of phones.

## Who he is

- He moved from India to China in 2013 and opened the first 24-hour gym in Beijing, with 7 locations. Then he worked at a Berlin scale-up from Series B to Series C ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:04:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=273s)).
- The German scale-up went from a $40M Series B to a $200M Series C ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=169s)).
> Conflict: [-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md) says the fitness chain reached $50M ARR. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) gives 15M RMB in revenue.
- He built Flame in New York with his partner, now his wife ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=70s)).

## Businesses

- [Flame](../businesses/flame.md), a daily ritual app for couples. It gives one question a day and an AI relationship coach ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)).
  - Launched as Sparks in 2022-10. A trademark cease-and-desist forced the rename to Flame in 2022-11 ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:07:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=472s)).
  - Day-30 retention went from 0.8% to 50% over about 8 months ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=13s), [0:13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=799s)).
  - He was almost out of money by the end of 2023. VCs gave no term sheet without distribution ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=845s)).
  - 50M views and 150K users, all organic (2026-02) ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=123s)).
  - 250,000 downloads, 50M organic views, and $3K to $10K MRR in under 8 months (2026-06). The app was being sold at the time ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=70s), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=113s)).

## Repeated advice

- **The VSC framework.** Both sources repeat it ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s), [source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:47:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2821s)):
  - Viral: over 100K views on several posts from accounts under 5K followers.
  - Scalable: you can make it 100 times with AI video or cheap UGC.
  - Convertible: the comments ask "what's the app?"
- **Run a phone farm.** 20 to 30 accounts on about 10 iPhones (2026-02), later 15 to 20 accounts (2026-06). Use a new Apple ID and a proxy per phone ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:24:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1490s), [0:26:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1591s), [source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=715s)). See [TikTok farming](../topics/tiktok-farming.md).
- **Track every post in a sheet.** Log account status, views and hooks daily. Feed winners back into the AI prompt ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [9:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=543s), [source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2501s)).
- **The 300-view rule.** Under 300 views is an account problem. 300 to 1K is a content problem ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s)).
- **Seed the comments.** Reply from your other accounts to steer viewers to the app ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=492s)).
- **Build retention around a daily habit.** Use "carrot and stick" notifications. Hide the partner's answer until you answer ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=711s), [0:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=974s)). See [retention](../topics/retention.md).
