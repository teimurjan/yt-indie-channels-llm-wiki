Florian "Flo" Vates is a German iOS developer and former mobile UX lead. He built [MonAI](../businesses/monai.md), a minimal expense tracker with voice entry and Apple Pay automation ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=63s)).

## Who he is

- His previous app took 2 years to build, and nobody cared. He built MonAI v1 in 1 to 2 months ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=177s)).
- Alone for 1.5 years, he reached only about $300 MRR ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=63s)).
- A large Colombian tech and lifestyle creator found his socials in the app and became a profit-share partner ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=749s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=810s)).

## Businesses

- **[MonAI](../businesses/monai.md).** A subscription app with a hard paywall and a 7-day trial. A/B tests showed this beat freemium ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)).
- The first creator video went out at the end of 2024. MRR grew 10x within a week and reached almost $8K within a month ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=271s)).
- A WWDC invite story got 1.7 million views and added almost $5K MRR. A feature story pushed MRR over $35K ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=418s), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=479s)).
- Over $35K MRR about a year after the partnership began (2026-04) ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s)).
- Monthly tools (2026-04): RevenueCat about $400, OpenAI plus Anthropic about $200, Claude Code Max $100, Appwrite $25 ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=930s)).

## Repeated advice

- **One creator, not twenty.** Three story-driven videos a month convert well, even without millions of views. The creator owns the ideas ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=333s)).
- **Give the creator skin in the game.** The deal is now a profit share plus a fixed monthly retainer. A revenue share did not scale well ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=281s)).
- **Five-step creator outreach.** Find an aligned partner and warm up for weeks. Name a specific video and show the fit. Signal pay early with Sensor Tower data ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=567s), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=627s), [11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=688s)).
- **Put your socials in the app.** That is how his creator found him. Inbound creators also give better terms ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=749s)).
- **Reuse creator videos as Meta ads.** He regrets that he started late ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=990s)).
- **Crowded categories are fine.** Budget tracking has no clear winner. "What matters is the distribution" ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=1174s)).
