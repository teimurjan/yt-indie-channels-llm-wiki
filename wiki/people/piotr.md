Piotr is a Polish tech lead who built Visualizee, an AI rendering tool for architects, on nights and weekends.

## Who

- Over 20 years as a developer. He still works full time as a tech lead at a Polish software house (2026-09) [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=63s).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Visualizee](../businesses/visualizee.md): turns sketches and models into renders and videos for architects and interior designers [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=0s).
  - For two years it made about $100 to $150 a month. After a pivot to a chat interface, it reached close to $10K/month in about 6 months (2026-09) [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=21s).
  - $8.2K MRR, about 300 subscribers, and 25% of trials convert (2026-09) [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s).
  - 7-day free trial, then $35/month or $80/month by render volume (2026-09) [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=146s).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick a niche, not a generic AI toy** [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=224s).
- **Replace complexity with chat.** The node-based interface was too hard. A chat assistant, "Vizi", now writes the prompt [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=249s), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=374s).
- **Pivot the presentation, not the audience.** Only the interface changed [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=352s).
- **Remove friction before you blame marketing.** For 2 years he blamed marketing. The real problem was the path to the first render [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s).
- **Charge subscriptions from day one.** One-time payments gave no repeat revenue [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=450s).
- **Competitors are a reason to keep going.** They had customers, so he looked for what was wrong with his product [source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=521s).
