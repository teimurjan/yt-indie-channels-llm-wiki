Justin Welsh runs a one-person knowledge business that sells online courses, newsletter sponsorships and a paid email. It made about $1.7M in 2022 with no employees ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=116s)).

## Who he is

- He spent ten years in tech sales. He worked 5 years at Zocdoc. At 33 he became VP of Sales at a company that grew from no revenue to about $50M recurring in 4.5 years ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=199s)).
- He burned out, and a panic attack in December 2018 ended that career. He then built an audience on LinkedIn and Twitter ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=199s)).
- He works about five hours a day: 45 to 60 minutes of morning engagement, then 3 to 4 hours of creative work after noon ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=0s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s)).

## Businesses

- **Three revenue lines.** He sells two courses: LinkedIn for business, and building a content system. He also sells sponsorships in a newsletter near 100,000 subscribers, and a monthly paid email on social copywriting (2023-04) ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=59s)).
- **Revenue.** About $1.7M last year and about $2.5M forecast for this year (2023-04) ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=116s)).
- **Costs.** About $620 a month. Kajabi is the biggest line at $399 a month. He has never run a paid ad (2023-04) ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=307s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Discovery, trust, offer.** Social posts reach new people. The weekly newsletter builds trust and authority. The offer comes last and is not aggressive ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=426s)).
- **Post daily on two platforms.** Two to three posts a day on LinkedIn and Twitter. At scale, 1% to 2% of a million followers buying at a few hundred dollars adds up ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=125s), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s)).
- **A fixed publishing slot.** Content goes out at 8:15 a.m. Eastern every day ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s)).
- **Themed weekdays.** Monday is the newsletter. Tuesday to Thursday is content. Friday is strategic work ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=584s)).
- **Ideas from proven topics.** He reads aggregator newsletters and the most popular videos on relevant YouTube channels, then adds his own spin ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [7:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=474s)).
- **Hypothesis-driven creation.** Test an idea. If it works, do it 2x to 5x more. If not, drop it ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=621s)).
- "All advice is contextual, mine included. Start, learn, try" ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [11:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=675s)).
