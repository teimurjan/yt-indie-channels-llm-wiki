Jure Sotošek is a 26-year-old developer who left Microsoft and runs four businesses that make about $60K/month together (2025-04).

## Who he is

- He built apps from age 13 or 14 but made no money until about 3 years before 2025-04. He worked at Microsoft for about 1.5 years ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=203s)).

## Businesses

- [Wrumer](../businesses/wrumer.md): a car device that plays fake engine sounds through the OBD2 port. 6,000 units and about $300K last year from about 200M organic short-form views (2025-04) ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s)).
- [Parakeet AI](../businesses/parakeet-ai.md): a real-time AI job interview assistant. Over $35K in the last 30 days (2025-04) ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=73s)).
- [Optibase](../businesses/optibase.md): A/B testing for [Webflow](../businesses/webflow.md). About $6.5K MRR (2025-04) ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)).
- Parakeet AI Apply Agent: applies to jobs for the user. About $1,000 in its first two weeks (2025-04) ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)).

## Repeated advice

- Marketing is as hard as building. Bet on organic short-form video, because most other channels are saturated ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=355s)).
- Pick products that sell in 10 seconds: cheap, funny, slightly controversial ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=333s)).
- Viable means it can make a sale. Ship in about two weeks ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=427s)).
- List about 10 channels, pick one and give it two months ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s)).
- Film combinations of 3 hooks, 5 main parts and 3 endings, then remake winners ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=631s)).
- Find a creator co-founder by cold email. He emailed hundreds of TikTok creators ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=671s)).
