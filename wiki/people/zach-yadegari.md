Zach Yadegari is the CEO and co-founder of Cal AI, an AI calorie-tracking app that sold in 2026.

## Who

- He started coding at 7 and shipped his first app at 12. His first company reached 5M users in 2 years and made $60K a year. He sold it at 16 [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=112s).
- CEO of Cal AI at 19 (2026-08). He met co-founder [Henry](henry.md) at a coding camp at age 10 and cold-DMed [Blake Anderson](blake-anderson.md) [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=86s), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=298s).
- His younger brother is [Evan Yadegari](evan-yadegari.md), who built Locked [source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=0s).
- He does not speak in most of these sources. Other people describe his work.

## Businesses

- [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md). Four co-founders: Zach (CEO), Blake Anderson, [Jake Castillo](jake-castillo.md) (CMO/COO) and Henry (CTO) (2026-08) [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=196s).
  - Revenue: $30K in month 1, over $100K in month 2 and $1M/month within 8 months [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=147s).
  - About $2M/month, per Joseph Choi (2025-04) [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:42:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2546s).

MyFitnessPal was the buyer, and the deal closed around December [source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) [49:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2949s).

## Repeated advice

- **One domain per founder, full trust.** Each co-founder owned one area, so they had no real fights [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=220s).
- **Decide big things together.** During the early buyout offers, they called each other constantly and reached a shared "no" [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=349s).
- **Solve your own problem.** You market to yourself, so you know the design and the pain points [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=556s).
- **The App Store is still early.** The team sees a $100B market growing over 10% a year, with Cal AI at about 1/2000 of it (2026-08) [source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=460s).
- **Build the viral moment into the product.** The photo scan is the feature that every creator shows [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1363s), [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=373s).
