Dawson Gibbs is the founder of Evo Marketing, a UGC agency, and was an early face of Cal AI's content.

## Who he is

- He posted daily for 5 to 6 years: about 1,500 to 2,000 videos and only 2,000 followers. Then a fitness page grew to hundreds of thousands of followers in about 4 months ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=0s), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=207s)).
- He drove about 200M views for [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) in its first ~5 months. He also referred about 100 creators to Cal AI for a fee per creator ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=241s), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=271s)).

## Businesses

- [Evo Marketing](../businesses/evo-marketing.md): a UGC agency founded about two years before the video. It has driven billions of views and posts hundreds of thousands of videos a month (2026-10) ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=170s), [10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=637s)).
  - SMB Market campaign: about 40M views and about $1.2M for the client. Churn hit about 45% from young get-rich-quick signups ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1000s)).
  - A Dupe creator video got 6.9M views and 170K likes ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [44:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2656s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Credible or relatable.** High-trust purchases, such as flights, need an expert hook. Impulse buys need "I am just like you" messaging ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=313s), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=558s)).
- **Vanity domains for attribution.** flightcheatcode.com redirects to Jetpack. It separates Evo's traffic from paid ads and says the value better than an unknown brand name ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=706s)).
- **Do not demo a boring app.** If the UI is dull, sell the outcome ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=833s)).
- **Match creator age to the buyer.** For SMB Market, Evo moved to creators aged 35 to 40, with green screens of the founder ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1056s)). Facebook and Instagram reach 30 to 40-year-olds. TikTok and Snapchat reach 18 to 25-year-olds ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [22:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1363s)).
- **Hands always busy.** Creators must move on camera, and more faces keep viewers past 5 seconds ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1608s)).
- **Repost early winners.** A video at 10K to 20K views with 3%+ engagement gets reposted or reshot with a new hook ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [43:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2596s)).
- **Two-phase campaigns.** Weeks 1 to 2 chase views. Weeks 3 to 4 move winning scripts into talking heads with a credible source ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [48:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2923s)).
- **Moms and older creators convert best** for family products ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [46:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2774s)).
