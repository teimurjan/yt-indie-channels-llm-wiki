Praneeth is a builder from India who quit a $250K/year software job and built Rabbit Holes AI, a desktop canvas for branching AI chats.

## Who

- He left a $250,000/year job to build apps (2025-10) ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=47s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Rabbit Holes AI](../businesses/rabbit-holes-ai.md) (now Canvas Mode): about $80K gross and $75K profit in 6 months, about 1,200 paying users, 60% on lifetime licenses (2025-10) ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [8:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=528s)).

## Repeated advice

- Know why freemium failed. Without a big brand, and with customers who already pay for many AI tools, his web freemium trial got almost no traction ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=162s)).
- Remove variable costs, then sell once. Bring-your-own-key and a desktop app move AI and server costs to users ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=225s)).
- Add a [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) to any bootstrapped product, even next to a subscription ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=288s)).
- Start low and raise the price: $29 under 100 users, then $49 ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=416s)).
- Name the customer in the H1, and hint at the product in Twitter replies instead of pitching ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=468s)).
