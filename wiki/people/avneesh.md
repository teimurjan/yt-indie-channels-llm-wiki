Avneesh is a former big-tech engineer who built Savewise, a tool that helps people stack credit card points, miles and cashback, and grew it through Reddit and Facebook groups.

## Who

- He worked at Microsoft, Instagram, Foursquare and Dropbox before he started [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=94s).

## Businesses

- [Savewise](../businesses/savewise.md): about $25,000 a month after 15 months, with $0 spent on marketing (2025-08) [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=4s), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=64s).
  - Tens of thousands of users and about 1,500 paying customers. About 97% of revenue comes from a [lifetime plan](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) (2025-08) [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=678s).

## Repeated advice

- **Go where users are, not where builders gather.** Launch sites gave a 95% to 96% bounce rate. 300 to 400 influencer emails got one reply [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=146s), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=222s).
- **A five-step [Reddit](../topics/reddit-marketing.md) playbook.** Brainstorm keywords and find subreddits with Map of Reddit. Lurk first. Set one goal per community. Set F5Bot keyword alerts. Be helpful before you mention the product [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=257s), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=306s), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=367s), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=430s).
- **Give away a data asset.** A free sheet of Rakuten and Amex offers in a Facebook group sent over 1,500 visitors [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=531s).
- **Earn a top-level post.** He posted in a weekly Q&A thread for about 3.5 months, then asked the moderator for a top-level post, and it blew up [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=599s).
- **Offer lifetime pricing.** "What's most fascinating is that I learned consumers hate subscriptions" [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=678s).
