Jordan Rejaud is a solo, bootstrapped founder who built Parakeet Chat, an AI chatbot and messaging service for people in US prisons.

## Who he is

- He has a master's degree, worked on a moon rover and as a Fortune 100 researcher, then became a self-taught coder and freelancer in San Francisco. His first business, robotics work for self-driving car and drone companies, passed $1M. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=54s), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=89s))
- A freelance client went to prison and told him that prison services are overpriced and poor. That gave him the idea. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=271s))

## Businesses

- [Parakeet Chat](../businesses/parakeet-chat.md): inmates email a fixed address through the prison email system, and a bot replies. Families pay $15 or $20/month (2026-04). ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=524s), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=590s))
  - $1.5M lifetime revenue and a little over $300K in 2025 (2026-04). ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=612s))
  - 200 paying users in the first month. Profitable from the second month. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=24s), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=127s))

## Repeated advice

- **Build for a customer nobody builds for.** Prison is a closed market with old, overpriced tools. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=271s))
- **If a landing page cannot reach the market, validate with the MVP itself.** Be willing to let the idea die. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=331s), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=386s))
- **Word of mouth plus a referral credit.** A customer who recruits a paying customer gets about a month of free credits. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=456s))
- **Speed over stack.** He has not opened a code editor in 3 to 6 months because AI writes his code. ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=198s))
