Gaurav is an Australian founder who built Fastlane, an AI short-form content tool, on about 2,000 customer calls.

## Who

- He started building apps after the Starter Story video on [Puff Count](../businesses/puff-count.md) ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=154s)).
- Co-founder: Joe. Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Fastlane](../businesses/fastlane.md): AI short-form content for solo app and SaaS founders. $69K MRR from software and over 1,000 paid users about two months after public launch. Over $1M ARR across the business (2026-08) ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s)).
- It began as Cast AI, a horizontal tool for SEO, LLM SEO, Reddit and short-form, then narrowed to short-form only ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=214s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Talk to customers at volume.** About 2,000 calls at about 20 a week ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s)).
- **Pay for calls with access.** An in-app button offers 7 days of extra access for a call ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=400s)).
- **Three call phases.** Discovery with The Mom Test. Usability tests where you watch and stay quiet. Customer success interviews with power users ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=623s), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=683s), [12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=729s)).
- **Index the calls.** Calls go into Notion and Claude indexes them. Segmenting by signup reason showed "curious" users retained worse than "I need marketing now" ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=481s)).
