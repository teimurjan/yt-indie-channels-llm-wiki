Assia Poloni is the Chief Product Officer of [Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md), an Apple Watch app that shows people what drains their energy.

## Who

- She is a former copywriter. She now leads product and onboarding at Welltory ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [24:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1489s)).

## Businesses

- **[Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md).**
  - About $18M ARR and about 17M to 18M downloads after 10 years (2026-08) ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=280s)).
  - Over 90% of users connect health data on the first CTA screen (2026-08) ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [27:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1640s)).
  - Paywall: $99.99/year, shown as $8.83/month. It is a "hardish" paywall with no trial that users can close (2026-08) ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [39:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2363s)).
  - Over 60% of conversions happen after day one (2026-08) ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [40:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2427s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Six-part pitch for onboarding.** Human problem, bold promise, a "key" only you know, the mechanism, proof, then FOMO ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1074s), [19:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1172s)).
- **Hook cold Meta traffic with an inside view.** "This Apple Watch app catches high cortisol" beats a long-term promise ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=386s), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=609s)).
- **Build from the ad's promise, not its features** ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=531s)).
- **Put the CTA on screen one.** Every intro screen she tested before it did worse ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1608s)).
- **Use the user's own data as proof**, then turn to hope ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [21:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1296s), [31:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1898s)).
- **Close with loss, not benefits.** "Stress is building right now" beat an energy-bank chart ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [35:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2122s)).
- **Match the close to the trigger.** Apps with no obvious trigger, such as health, fitness and language, need FOMO ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [37:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2248s)).
- **Story beats copy.** The full story arc matters more than the exact words ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [24:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1489s)).
- **Less data, more meaning.** Use AI to tell the day as a story of drain and recovery ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=929s)).
