Steph France is a marketer who teaches the "gold mining framework", a way to go from no idea to a landing page with AI and Reddit research.

## Who he is

- He showed the framework on [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md) with [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md). It uses 6 tools, 5 prompts and about 45 minutes, with no code (2025-06) ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=0s)).
- His sample co-parenting landing page took about an hour. He knew nothing about the niche before ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=58s)).

## Businesses

- The source features his framework, not a business he runs. The worked example produced "Transition Garden", a tool for child custody handoffs ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=105s)).

## Repeated advice

- Start in health, wealth or relationships, because people already pay there. Expand a market into deeper niches with a prompt ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=129s), [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=246s)).
- Check demand with Keywords Everywhere and Google Trends topics. "Co-parenting" had about 40,000 searches and a steady, growing trend (2025-06) ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=315s), [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=429s)).
- Mine Reddit with an advanced Google query. About 20 threads is enough ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=547s), [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=723s)).
- Run three Claude prompts in a row: pain point extractor, market gap generator, landing page prompt for [Lovable](../businesses/lovable.md) ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=777s), [17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1074s)).
- Use the customer's own words on the landing page, then test demand again with a quiz waitlist and a threshold such as 100 people ([source](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=105s), [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1232s)).
