Ramon Berrios is a co-founder of Castmagic, an AI tool that turns podcasts and videos into written content, where he runs marketing.

## Who

- He co-founded Castmagic with [Blaine Bolus](blaine-bolus.md) and [Justin](justin.md). The three work remotely from Fort Lauderdale, Miami and Austin [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s).
- He tried to raise money for an earlier startup, Trend.io, for almost 4 years [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=629s).
- He is the one-person marketing team, with contractors [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s).

## Businesses

- [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md): over $120K MRR about 10 months after start, growing over 20% a month, with single-digit net churn (2024-06) [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=64s), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s).
  - The first version took two weeks, for an AppSumo [lifetime deal](../topics/lifetime-deals.md) that brought about $350K and 10,000 users [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s).

## Repeated advice

- **Borrow distribution early.** Build a personal brand or find a distribution partner. AppSumo gave brand, users and feedback, at the cost of recurring revenue from those users [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s).
- **Run a [creator program](../topics/creator-programs.md) at volume.** Scrape creators in adjacent niches by hashtag, reach out at volume and negotiate CPM rates from the replies. Keep the ones who perform. Some are paid, others join as affiliates [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=264s).
- **Systems for a small team.** He replaced creator ideation calls with a Notion doc of content concepts [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=283s).
- **Cap marketing at 20% of revenue.** "Marketing always has to be less than 20%, and that is because we want to make money" [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=521s).
- **Do not chase fundraising.** "If you choose to get great at fundraising, you're just going to have the whole thing backwards" [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=691s). See [bootstrapping](../topics/bootstrapping.md).
- **Pick co-founders for alignment** and complementary skills, not convenience [source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=582s).
