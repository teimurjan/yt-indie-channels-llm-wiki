Jay is the CEO of Bloom, a Gen Z "learn to invest" app. The source gives only his first name.

## Who

- CEO of Bloom, interviewed by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- [Bloom](../businesses/bloom.md): Robinhood-style investing with Duolingo-style lessons. "Millions of dollars in ARR" and over 1M active users. The title says $2M/year (2025-07) ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=0s)).

## Repeated advice

- When [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md) stop paying back, move to organic. Bloom's TikTok CAC got worse from mid-2023 ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)).
- Use a broad hook that leads to a deep product, such as "how billionaires raise their kids" ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=159s)).
- Keep the CTA indirect and let a comment team name the app ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s)).
- Pay creators in three phases: a small retainer, bonuses at 10K to 50K views, then conversion-based bonuses over 100K ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=650s)).
- Turn organic winners into paid ads after they fade ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [14:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=876s)).
- [Onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md) order: urgency, then features, then trust ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [19:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1145s)).
- Give organic video at least 90 days ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2574s)).
