Sam Parr is a serial entrepreneur and podcast host who started the newsletter The Hustle. His companies make over $10M a year (2026-07) ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=0s)).

## Who he is

- He records podcasts every Monday and Wednesday ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=307s)).
- In year one of The Hustle, people asked him what his real job was ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=532s)).
- He wakes at 6:00 to 6:30 and trains about 6 days a week. In the evening he uses eBay and Claude from 8:30 to 10:00, then reads until 10:45 ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=34s), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=565s)).
- He uses the Brick phone blocker, after he tried a timed kitchen container and the Opal app ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=74s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Ceaseless action from $0 to $10M.** Book back-to-back 15-minute customer calls with 5-minute breaks, his "zebra calendar". Write the website copy and do the design yourself ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=377s)).
- **Delegate at $5M to $10M.** Hire, then leave the compounding business alone. Keep your hands busy with a side "sandbox" project ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=437s)).
- **Time-box everything.** He thinks he can finish only 1 or 2 real tasks a day, so each task goes on the calendar ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=224s)).
- **Build systems, not intentions.** His 5-year journal shows the same complaint on the same date across years ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=153s)).
- **Quantity beats perfection.** He cites a pottery-class study: the group that made many pots made better pots ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=377s)).
- **Focus beats starting over.** In 9 of 10 cases, focus on your one thing beats starting something new ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=110s)).
- **Pick ideas by track record.** A beginner needs to act now and pivot later. A proven operator can take time to pick an idea in the middle of their ikigai ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=490s)).
- **Use AI for prep.** A Claude skill reads his calendar and researches the day's podcast guest ([source](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=307s)).
