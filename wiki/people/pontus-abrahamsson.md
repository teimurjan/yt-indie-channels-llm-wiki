Pontus Abrahamsson co-founded the bootstrapped startup Midday with his friend Victor. Together they built [Cursor Directory](../businesses/cursor-directory.md) in about 3 hours.

## Who he is

A developer and co-founder of Midday. On a 3-hour flight to France, he saw that no site collected Cursor rules in one place. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [3:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=201s)

## Businesses

- **Cursor Directory.** About $34K to $35K/month with a 99.8% gross margin (2025-11). It had 49,000 registered users and 2.2 million unique visitors since launch. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=21s), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=159s)
- Running costs are about $525/month, and upkeep takes about 3 hours a month, because the community merges rules on GitHub. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=159s), [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=715s)
- Revenue comes from paid job listings and paid featured MCP listings. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=616s), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=665s)
- **Midday.** His bootstrapped startup with Victor. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md)

## Repeated advice

- **Ship in hours.** Victor designed in Figma while Pontus started a Next.js project with a hard-coded JSON file. It was on Vercel in about 30 minutes. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=230s)
- **Act on side ideas.** They usually hold off on the fast ideas they find once or twice a week. This time they did not. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=294s)
- **Launch where your audience is.** They built in public on X with open-source code, then posted to Hacker News. One launch post got about 1 million impressions. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=343s)
- **Design is the moat.** "It's easy to create a website today, but all of them usually look the same." [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=418s)
- **Directories work on a new wave.** They solve a timeless discovery problem. Extend them with each new wave, such as MCPs. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=544s), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=665s)
- **Use the stack you know.** Next.js, TypeScript, shadcn, Resend, OpenPanel, Vercel and Polar. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=715s)
- "You create your own luck by constantly finding ways to move forward, shipping, sharing, and iterate." [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [13:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=784s)
