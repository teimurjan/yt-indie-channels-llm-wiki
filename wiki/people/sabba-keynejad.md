Sabba Keynejad is a co-founder of VEED, a browser video editor that grew to $40M in revenue after Y Combinator rejected it twice.

## Who he is

- He met his co-founder Tim online through a global hackathon a year after university. They founded VEED in London in 2018. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=0s), [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=145s))
- For two years VEED made no money. Both founders took jobs and put half of each salary into the company to pay 2 engineers. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=346s))

## Businesses

- [VEED](../businesses/veed.md): browser video editing, first inspired by Giphy's GIF editor. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [3:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=205s))
  - 0 to 30,000 monthly users in about 9 months, all free. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=346s))
  - YC rejected them after the interview, then again after they added payments over a weekend and got 20 paid users. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=519s), [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=631s))
  - $0 to $1M in the first year of growth, then $2M, $3M, $6M, and later $10M, $20M, $30M and $40M in revenue (2025-05). The video title values it at $200M. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s))

## Repeated advice

- **Cut every cost.** They asked Crisp to cut its price from $100/month to $20/month, and Crisp agreed. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [4:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=285s))
- **Charge early.** ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=581s))
- **Use one goal metric.** His only job was to make traffic and revenue higher than the day or week before. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [10:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=647s))
- **Programmatic SEO by brute force.** He found about 500 search terms such as "trim video" and built a landing page and a YouTube video for each one. ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s))
