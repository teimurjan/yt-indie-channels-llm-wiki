Steve Young runs App Masters, buys apps with organic App Store traffic and weak monetization, and teaches ASO.

## Who

- In apps since 2010. Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md)).

## Businesses

- [App Masters](../businesses/app-masters.md).
- An app bought in 2024 has made over $1M lifetime and about $500K last year, mostly from App Store search (2026-07) ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=517s)).
- Indie App Santa, bought in 2024 and paid back in 3 to 4 months. An $18,000 purchase flopped ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [41:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2469s), [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s)).

## Repeated advice

- Founders with no edge in creatives can start with [ASO](../topics/app-store-optimization.md) and [Apple Ads](../topics/apple-search-ads.md), which only need keywords ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=282s)).
- Find the keyword first, then build the app. Two keywords with volume of about 30 each, ranked first, bring about 100 to 150 downloads a day ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s)).
- Judge keyword difficulty from the results page: titles that use the keyword and review counts at ranks two and three ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s)).
- Use a hard [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) to test product fit. A $60/year hard paywall took one app from $1K to $2K a month to $8K to $10K on the same downloads ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [18:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1131s), [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1716s)).
- Run [Meta ads](../topics/meta-ads.md) only above about $3 revenue per install ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=990s)).
- Buy apps with organic distribution; paid-only apps are "a money eating machine". Fund them with loans ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s), [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2591s)).
