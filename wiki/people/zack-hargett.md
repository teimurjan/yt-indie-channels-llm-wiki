Zack Hargett is a former Loom employee who co-founded [Coconote](../businesses/coconote.md) with [Brett Bauman](brett-bauman.md) and ran its creator team.

## Who he is

- He and Brett Bauman left Loom and bootstrapped Coconote, an AI note-taker for college students, to $6.7M ARR. Quizlet bought it about 18 months after launch (2026-04) ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=0s), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=139s)).

## Businesses

- [Coconote](../businesses/coconote.md): $100K ARR in 45 days, $1M ARR in 4 months, $5M ARR after a year, then $6.7M ARR. 2M+ users and over 50% EBITDA (2026-04) ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=139s), [33:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=2030s)).
- The content team peaked at 10 to 12 part-time creators, one coach per 12 creators ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s), [18:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1139s)).

## Repeated advice

- Target intent, not views. A 10M-view video to students with a real problem beats a 40M-view novelty video ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=453s)).
- Keep a small team of part-time creators ("Navy SEALs, not the Navy"). Hire marketers, not influencers ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s)).
- Scout talent anywhere. He recruited his online Spanish tutor, who became a top creator for Latin America ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [16:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=967s)).
- Judge creators on a pyramid: wants to win, can study viral content, can create trends. He decides in 15 to 30 minutes ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [17:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1029s), [20:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1200s)).
- Make one big onboarding jump. Going from 5 to 13 screens lifted trial conversion 16% ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s)).
- A 7-day trial extension in the cancel flow saves 27% of cancellers ([source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1906s)).
