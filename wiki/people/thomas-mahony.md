Thomas Mahony is a former IT admin who built [Pckgr](../businesses/pckgr.md), a bootstrapped tool that deploys apps to Microsoft Intune in one click.

## Who

- As an IT admin he worked with Microsoft Intune every day. Packaging one app for Intune sometimes took an hour, and forum posts showed many admins had the same problem ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s)).
- He built Pckgr before Cursor and Claude Code existed, so he wrote most of it by hand ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s)).

## Businesses

- **[Pckgr](../businesses/pckgr.md).** A browser-based tool for Intune app packaging.
  - About $60K/month (2026-03). He shows revenue going from $447K to $910K, which looks like year over year ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=96s)).
  - 1,000+ customers by the description, and 100% bootstrapped ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=277s)).
  - First price: $25/month, set after a free beta ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s)).
  - Built on Bubble, GitHub Actions and Azure serverless functions ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=571s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build where you already have credibility.** Look for pain points, not ideas ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s)).
- **Avoid the mass market.** Competitors chased companies with millions of devices. Pckgr targets smaller businesses ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=396s)).
- **Launch free on Reddit, then charge early.** The free MVP got fast traction and harsh feedback. Paying beta users proved demand ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s)).
- **Partner with niche experts.** Microsoft MVPs made demo videos. A YouTube video keeps bringing customers for years ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=254s)).
- **Optimize for freedom, not scale at all costs.** Keep the team and costs small ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=396s)).
- **Plain design is fine.** Pckgr does one useful job and has no fancy design ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=548s)).
