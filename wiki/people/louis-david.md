Louis David is a Canadian developer who built GlowUp, an AI makeup try-on app that made $800K in its first year.

## Who he is

- He did 2 years of software engineering school, then dropped out. About 10 earlier apps failed because he marketed them only after he built them. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=142s), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s))

## Businesses

- [GlowUp](../businesses/glowup.md), built with a co-founder.
  - 0 to 100K users in about 3 days. About 2M users and about 70,000 paying customers (2025-12). ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=72s))
  - $800K in about one year, now about $15K/month, about 90% margin (2025-12). ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=93s), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=644s))
  - $9.99/week or $39.99/year. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s))

## Repeated advice

- **Content first, app second.** Mine comments under big niche TikToks for what people ask for. Build only after the content goes viral. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s))
- **Warm up a US account.** New iPhone, US SIM, always-on US VPN, and 15 to 30 minutes of niche activity a day for about 3 days. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s))
- **Add the anchor link late.** Below about 1,000 views it kills reach. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s))
- **Referral codes that feed the comments.** One video got close to 48M views and about $12.5K in sales in a day. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=488s))
- **Long, personal onboarding** with a rating prompt before the result. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s))
