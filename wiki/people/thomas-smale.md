Thomas Smale is the founder of [FE International](../businesses/fe-international.md), an M&A firm that sells online businesses.

## Who he is

- He flipped websites in college on his credit card. His first deal: bought a site for about $100 and sold it for about $500 within a month, around 2008 to 2009 ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=49s)).
- He wrote a book and course about flipping. The course made about $30K. Readers asked him to sell their businesses, so he fell into M&A ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=109s), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=257s)).

## Businesses

- [FE International](../businesses/fe-international.md): founded around 2010. Over $1B in businesses sold, with eight-figure deals now routine (2023-04) ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=0s), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=589s)).
- Broker fees start around 15% on small deals and fall as deals grow (2023-04) ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=685s)).

## Repeated advice

- Small online businesses sell at 4x to 10x annual profit, not a revenue multiple (2023-04) ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=717s)).
- A good business is evergreen, has recurring customers and is growing ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=482s)).
- Find deals by direct outreach. Set a budget first, then say plainly that you want to acquire ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=396s), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=457s)).
- Buy where your skill adds value, for example a marketer buying a tool from a founder who cannot market it ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=776s)).
- Negotiate on priorities: cash, transition time or the team. Pick battles ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=866s)).
- Start with $100, not $0. Small flips build a track record ([source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [16:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=994s)).
