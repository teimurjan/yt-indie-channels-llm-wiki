Vikash Prajapat is a former Photoshop freelancer from India who built BulkMockup, a Photoshop plugin for bulk print-on-demand mockups.

## Who he is

- A Photoshop freelancer on Upwork. A client expected 3 to 4 days for 1,800 mockups. He did it in about 30 minutes with a script and sold the script for $300. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=136s))

## Businesses

- [BulkMockup](../businesses/bulkmockup.md): about $12K to $13K/month on Gumroad for the past year (2026-02). ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=103s))
  - Sold as a lifetime deal for a couple of years, then turned into a monthly product. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=224s))

## Repeated advice

- **Mine freelance platforms for ideas.** Upwork and Fiverr jobs show repeated work that people pay to automate. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=193s))
- **Run a content flywheel.** Harvest pain from communities, onboarding emails, recorded support and YouTube comments. Turn each pain into a tutorial. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=283s), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=410s))
- **Support is research.** He has over 1,500 recorded support videos and calls in 3 years. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s))
- **Ignore views.** A video with 370 views in 4 months brought $45 MRR. 22% of channel views come from Google search (2026-02). ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s))
