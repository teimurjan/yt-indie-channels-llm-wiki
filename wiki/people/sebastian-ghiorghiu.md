Sebastian Ghiorghiu is a former money YouTuber with nearly 1M subscribers who built the SaaS app Atlas without promoting it to his audience.

## Who

- Background: dropshipping and e-commerce, then YouTube, then SaaS ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s)).
- His viral "Lamborghini" clip got about 100M impressions on Twitter alone, plus press coverage. He felt out of line with that persona and stopped posting ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=212s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Atlas](../businesses/atlas.md) (helloatlas.io): $250K/month in recurring revenue in June 2025, about 9 months from zero. Team of 10+ (2025-09) ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=82s), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s)).
- He bought out his non-technical co-founder on January 6, 2025, at about $13K to $14K MRR ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)).

> Conflict: the video description of [laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md) says Atlas "makes $250K/year". In the transcript he says $250K a month in recurring revenue ([5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=339s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build in the wild, not for fans.** Influencer SaaS products "come and go". He wanted users to adopt and share it on their own ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=284s)).
- **Grow in stages.** Discord communities and user feedback to about $10K/month. Then paid YouTube creator videos. Then copied short-form formats. Then paid ads. YouTube, short-form and paid ads bring 60% to 65% of traffic (2025-09) ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)).
- **Content flywheel.** Cut each creator video into shorts and ads ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)).
- **Hire for quality.** Each good developer hire lifted the whole team ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)).
- **Choose the business over the audience.** A face-tied business leaves you "cuffed to the camera" ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=516s), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=639s)).
