Louis Pereira is a part-time indie maker in India who built AudioPen in about 12 hours.

## Who he is

- He works full-time in his family business in India and builds apps part-time ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [0:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=20s)).
- He tried no-code in 2015, came back in 2021 with Bubble, and built 15 to 20 tools before AudioPen ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=171s)).
- In 2022 he started Half Day Build, a Twitter hackathon. Builders go from idea to at least $1 of revenue between noon and midnight on a Sunday ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=206s)).

## Businesses

- [AudioPen](../businesses/audiopen.md) turns a voice note into clean text in a chosen style. About $15K/month, about 200,000 users and over 5,000 paying customers (2025-12) ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=104s), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=120s)).
- Price: $99/year or $159 for 2 years, as plans that do not renew by themselves (2025-12) ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=120s)).
- Beta testers paid through Stripe before the 12-hour build ended ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build many small things.** Tests cost little. Double down on the one that gets a signal ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=254s)). See [side projects](../topics/side-projects.md).
- **Design before you build.** Design now sets products apart and takes longer than you expect ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=466s)).
- **Build in public.** Months to years on Twitter gave him the credibility to sell on day one ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=426s)). See [build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md).
- **Launch plain, raise the price later.** A Google Form works as a first email list ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=466s)).
- **Keep one pointy feature.** Competitors that do too much get distracted ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=628s)).
- **Sound like an indie hacker, not a big company.** Users like being part of his story ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=526s)).
