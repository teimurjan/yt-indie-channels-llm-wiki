Karl Hughes is the Chicago founder of Draft.dev, a technical content service for developer-focused companies.

## Who he is

- He tried at least 10 side projects before Draft.dev worked ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=575s)).
- His 10-year goal is $100M in total revenue across businesses ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=655s)).

## Businesses

- [Draft.dev](../businesses/draft-dev.md), a [productized service](../topics/productized-services.md). It sells packages of 12, 24 or 48 technical articles written by practicing engineers ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)).
  - Over $2.5M in revenue in about 3 years (2023-10). The intro says 2 years ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)).
  - 6 to 7 full-time staff and hundreds of contractors in 54 countries (2023-10) ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=335s)).
  - Leads come in thirds: referrals, organic search plus social, and cold outreach ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=396s)).
- A second business bought with debt through entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA). He and a friend talked to 50 to 100 founders first ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=655s)). See [acquisitions](../topics/acquisitions.md).

## Repeated advice

- **Start with no website.** A single page of writing samples, sent to his network, got the first clients ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=156s)).
- **Keep a network spreadsheet.** About 50 people and a weekly reminder to reach a few. The first 5 to 10 customers came from it ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s)).
- **Position as premium.** No discounts, no one-off trials, quarterly commitments ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=427s)).
- **Validate part-time.** He kept his job until the business was viable ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=575s)).
- **Interview unhappy customers of existing agencies.** Talk to 10 to 12 of them and design the service around their complaints ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=734s)).
