Ethan is a 19-year-old former national wrestling champion who built CutCoach, a weight-cut app for combat sport athletes.

## Who

- He left a computer engineering degree after AI tools built in a day what took him a week ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [0:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=9s)).
- His first app took 6 months in summer 2024. In early 2025 he built several apps and marketed none, which he calls a mistake ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=154s)).

## Businesses

- [CutCoach](../businesses/cutcoach.md): an iOS weight-cut plan app, launched September 2025.
  - Over $60K total revenue in 6 months, about 38K to 39K downloads, best month over $20K (2026-04) ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=71s), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=101s)).
  - MVP built in about a month with Cursor and ChatGPT. Version 1 was coach-first; his club did not use it, so he made it athlete-first ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Solve a problem inside your hobby** ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s)).
- **Copy the layout of a proven app** in Figma, then have Cursor match it, front end first ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=457s)).
- **Small views can convert.** Posts with 200 to 500 views brought 10 to 15 downloads a day ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=508s)).
- **DM micro-creators** at 1K to 10K views per video, then run their videos as paid ads ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=585s)). See [Influencer marketing](../topics/influencer-marketing.md).
- **Pick niches where failure is costly.** Athletes pay about $30 because missing weight costs more ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=780s)).
