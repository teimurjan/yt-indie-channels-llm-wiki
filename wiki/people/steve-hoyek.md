Steve Hoyek is the co-founder of Journable, an AI calorie counter that makes most of its revenue on Android.

## Who he is

- He went from 125 kg to 80 kg by tracking calories, which led to the app [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=166s).

## Businesses

- [Journable](../businesses/journable.md): bootstrapped from under $1K to $125K in the last 28 days, about $80K MRR and over 30K active subscribers (2026-03) [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=85s).
- About 80% of users and revenue come from Android, with nearly 1M Android downloads (2026-03) [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=146s).
- Google Ads all-time: just under $500K spent, about break-even on attributed revenue (2026-03) [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=417s).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick Android by data.** iOS users cost about 4x more to reach but convert only about 20% better [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=235s).
- **Play Store ads are the core.** Feed Google App campaigns with many assets, start at $10 to $15/day, then move to target CPA at 10x the target cost per day [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=558s), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=739s).
- **Take big creative swings**, not font or color tweaks [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=678s).
- **Find Android gaps**: apps that win on iOS with no Android equivalent [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=913s).
- **Document the journey.** His regret is not posting content from the start [source](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md) [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=1148s).
