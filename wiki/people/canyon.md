Canyon is a co-founder of Sideshift, a marketplace where college students and creators take UGC jobs for apps.

## Who

- He co-founded [Sideshift](../businesses/sideshift.md) and runs its creator marketplace ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=40s)).
- He sees creator programs that reach 7 to 10 figures of views a month ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- **[Sideshift](../businesses/sideshift.md).** In late February 2025 it had about 10K job seekers. 3 to 4 months later it had over 150K, on about $10K of spend (2025-07) ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=40s)).
- Growth broke the product. With one engineer, 50K new creators joined in a week or two, and they paused funnels ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=101s)).
- The team visited 25 college campuses to onboard the first 10K to 20K users ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [40:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=2445s)).
- Sideshift onboarded about 30 agencies in summer 2026. Sideshift V3 runs organic winners as paid ads ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [46:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2772s), [51:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3075s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Expect many posts before a winner.** "Sometimes it takes 300 posts before you find that winning format." One campaign got 16M to 20M views from about 300 posts in 30 days ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=176s), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=271s)).
- **Keep the brief short.** Give the company's value in plain words, one clear persona, links to viral formats and a few hooks. Do not write word-for-word scripts ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=603s), [source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=917s)).
- **Warm up accounts like a human.** Act as the target user in the niche for 3 to 5 days, then post ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [16:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=998s)).
- **Judge creators by content, not past views.** Look for storytelling to camera and fast replies. More than 5 active campaigns is a red flag ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=666s), [13:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=822s)).
- **Hire a manager first.** Once a program has more than 10 creators, hire a generalist manager, ideally an ex-viral creator. Pay a share of creator bonuses so incentives match ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=223s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=317s)).
- **Own a tiny niche, then expand.** Narrow niches such as knitting or ballet can usually reach about $10K/month. Adjacent markets can take them to $100K or $1M/month ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1324s), [23:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1420s)).
- **Test formats abroad.** A video costs about $25 in the US and about $15 in LATAM (2026-09) ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [32:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1975s)).
- **Move organic winners into paid.** A video that flopped organically can still win as an ad ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [49:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2955s), [source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [26:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1616s)).
- **Test an app in two weeks.** Hire a few creators and post 30 to 50 videos. Check retention and payment, not only downloads ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [31:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1894s)).

## Changing views

- In 2025-07, Canyon said the algorithms reward quality content, so follower count no longer matters much ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [35:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=2130s)).
- In 2026-09, he said Instagram drives about 10% to 20% more views than TikTok in aggregate. TikTok shadowbans "monetization niche" apps ([source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1171s), [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1232s)).
