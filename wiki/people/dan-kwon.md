Dan Kwon is a former social media creator who grows consumer apps with organic short-form video.

## Who

- He was a social media creator before he marketed apps. [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) interviewed him on the Superwall channel [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s).
- He ran TikTok Shop marketing for about 8 months, backed by Science Inc. Final Boss Sour ranked first in food and beverage on TikTok Shop [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=683s).

## Businesses

- [Conch AI](../businesses/conch-ai.md): an AI writing tool that bypassed AI detection. It hit $1M ARR in about 30 days and did $500K in one month. Lifetime: about 220M views and about $2.2M revenue, mostly organic. Sold in early 2025 [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=0s), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=625s).
- [Arise](../businesses/arise.md): a Solo Leveling fitness app. About $50K/month at 70% to 80% margins (2025-10) [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s).
- [Shepherd](../businesses/shepherd.md): a Duolingo-style Bible app. A two-week build and a two-week growth sprint gave 20M views, over 100,000 downloads and $75K MRR (2025-10) [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [21:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1319s).

## Repeated advice

- **Ride a momentum wave into an existing cult.** Arise launched near the Solo Leveling season two finale and spoke to fans who wanted "the system" in real life [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [17:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1058s).
- **Go to an extreme in UGC.** Make it so cringe it reads like a friend's ad, or so real it looks like a Snap story [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=327s).
- **Use us-versus-them positioning.** Conch videos made school busywork the enemy [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=496s).
- **Build on news.** Shepherd launched during the backlash to Duolingo's AI content: "God first lamb over the AI first owl" [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [27:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1666s).
- **Three engines.** Post your product, stitch related viral videos, and turn influencers into full-time UGC accounts. Do not touch paid ads until you have at least five winning formats [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [31:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1900s).
- **Founder-led content first** in a cold start [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1002s).
- **Fix K-factor after the spike.** Shepherd paused creator work to build invite loops for prayer buddies [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [22:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1355s).
