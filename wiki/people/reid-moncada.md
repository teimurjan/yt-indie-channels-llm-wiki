Reid Moncada is the founder of Fitted, an AI closet app that grew on TikTok through an equity-paid creator.

## Who

- Fitted began 8 years earlier as a college laundry service in 18 cities ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md)).
- His web app did not convert. He paid $1,400 for a 15-minute call with Nikita Bier, who told him to go mobile. The mobile app took 6 months ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=275s), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=336s)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- [Fitted](../businesses/fitted.md): over 500M lifetime views, 600K downloads and about $150K in subscription revenue (2025-11) ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=572s)).
- Co-founder [Max](max.md) drove about 95% of distribution ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=574s)).
- FitCoin, a token, traded close to $250K in volume a day (2025-11) ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [18:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1127s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Crawl, walk, run with a creator.** Find a creator with 1K to 50K followers. Pay $20 a video on a sub-account, then add vested equity and a revenue share once it works ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=38s), [27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1662s)).
- **Embed the creator in the product.** Max reads comments and DMs and joins design meetings ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=669s)).
- **Boost organic winners with ads.** Ad spend behind videos that already went viral bought downloads at 1 to 15 cents (2025-11) ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)).
- **Test features on TikTok first** before they go into the roadmap ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [15:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=911s)).
- **Distribution is not the hard part.** He says reaching a million users is easy. Retention is hard. He plans to drop the hard paywall ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s), [23:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1433s)).
