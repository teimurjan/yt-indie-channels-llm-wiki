This page covers two founders named Chris, each from one Starter Story video. The sources give no surname, and nothing shows that they are the same person.

## Who they are

- **Chris, the cooking creator.** He left a 9-to-5 job and makes about $30K/month from food and cooking content (2023-03). That source has no transcript, so the facts come from the video description and chapters ([hCvy-Rt7Naw](../sources/hCvy-Rt7Naw.md), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCvy-Rt7Naw&t=217s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCvy-Rt7Naw&t=124s)).
- **Chris, the app developer.** He built Wishlists over about 6 years as a side project, first at university and then next to a full-time job. He quit his job in July 2025 ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=49s), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=413s)).

- The video tags name online nutrition coaching and cookbook sales as the likely revenue streams. No transcript supports this ([hCvy-Rt7Naw](../sources/hCvy-Rt7Naw.md), [0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCvy-Rt7Naw&t=35s)).

### Wishlists (w3zxMrwWrt0)

- [Wishlists](../businesses/wishlists.md) stores wishes and shares them with family. It makes about $150K/year from in-app purchases and Amazon affiliate links (2025-09) ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=49s), [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=661s)).
- 1.1M registered users, about 4K paying customers and 110K monthly active users in the low season (2025-09). Revenue in the Christmas season is about 5 times the low season ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [9:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=598s)).
- Margin is about 99%. Servers are the only real cost (2025-09) ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=628s)).
- Timeline: he started in 2019 and released in 2020. Wishlists 2.0 came in October 2023 with 100K registered users. He went full time in July 2025 ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=413s), [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=211s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Turn a hobby into a product.** The cooking video frames a passion that became a side hustle and then a full-time income ([hCvy-Rt7Naw](../sources/hCvy-Rt7Naw.md), [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCvy-Rt7Naw&t=351s)).
- **Make a prettier version of a crowded category.** The app developer kept his own wish list in Excel. He built a nicer app for it ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=121s)).
- **Users first, monetization later.** His first goal was one stranger who downloads the app ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=306s)).
- **Ask for reviews at success moments.** He shows the review prompt after a user adds or fulfills a wish. He emails users after he ships their fix and asks for a review only after a positive reply ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=498s), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=559s)).
