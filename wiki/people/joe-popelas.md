Joe Popelas is a Toronto computer science student who built an AI-written ebook brand sold through Pinterest ads.

## Who

- Age 20 (2023-08). Before the business, he stocked shelves for $11/hour [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=0s).
- He worked on GPT-3 beta use cases, which led to paid AI advice for small businesses in 2022 [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=48s).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- An ebook brand written with GPT-3 and ChatGPT, sold to middle-aged moms through Pinterest ads. The source does not name the brand [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=0s).
  - $390K total profit at a 70% to 80% margin, and a $930K valuation (2023-08) [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=362s).
  - Costs: about $200/month in extensions, about $3 of GPT-3 per book, a proofreader on retainer, and ads (2023-08) [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=362s).
  - Timeline: 10 to 20 books in August 2022, 40 by the end of September, first profitable day on October 3 ($20), first $1K profit day on December 28 [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=108s), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=171s).
  - The first marketplace offer was $930K. He was selling 40% of the brand to an investor (2023-08) [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=545s).
- He also teaches others to build book brands in the afternoons [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=605s).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick an ambitious niche** with traffic and buyers who want a clear outcome [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=220s).
- **Study competitor books** to learn the structure [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=220s).
- **Generate from an outline**, one topic at a time, then do one careful proofreading pass [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=281s).
- **Pinterest ads in two stages.** Consideration campaigns test niches. Then install the Pinterest tag and run conversion campaigns [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=423s).
- **Always use a landing page.** A bounce rate above 70% to 80% means the page looks unprofessional [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=423s).
- **Test the exit market.** List the brand only to see offers. Build, sell part, build another [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=545s).
- "Prioritize learning over earning" [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=666s).
