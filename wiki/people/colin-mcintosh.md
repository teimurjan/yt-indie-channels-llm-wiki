Colin McIntosh is a former recruiter who founded the bedding brand Sheets & Giggles and the AI resume builder Sheets Resume.

## Who

- He was fired twice and laid off once before he started Sheets & Giggles [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=0s).
- His Reddit resume guide from February 2018 drew about 20,000 questions over 6 years and became the top Google result for "resume advice Reddit" [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=222s).
- For years he bought three mentors lunch every week [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=193s).

## Businesses

- [Sheets & Giggles](../businesses/sheets-and-giggles.md): direct-to-consumer bedding. Incorporated October 2017, first box shipped October 2018, first $1M month in November 2020 at about $1.2M [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=72s).
  - The pre-launch waitlist captured 11,000 emails at a 46% rate. The [crowdfunding](../topics/crowdfunding.md) launch made $45K on day one and $284K in total [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=360s), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s).
  - It sells about 80% to 90% on Shopify and the rest on Amazon, and ships about 100 mattresses a day (2024-05) [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=72s), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=132s).
  - A [SiteGPT](../businesses/sitegpt.md) chatbot costs about $50/month and cut support tickets by about 30% (2024-05) [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=644s).
- [Sheets Resume](../businesses/sheets-resume.md): an AI resume builder trained on his rules, built by his former recruiting partner Nate. About $20K in its second month at about 90% net margin (2025-01) [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=374s), [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=767s).
  - Pricing: $99 lifetime or $29 for one week, with no monthly plan [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=68s).
  - He spends at most 5 to 10 hours a week on it [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=482s).

## Repeated advice

- **Sell before you build.** "You can sell a wireframe, you can sell an idea, you can sell a signup form." A locked-in launch price built the Sheets & Giggles list [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=300s). Sheets Resume started as manual reviews, priced up from a couple hundred dollars to $400 [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=283s).
- **Plan on paper first.** He writes a brand identity map, a financial model and a business model. He says not to use AI for this [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=300s), [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=436s).
- **Business model first, product second.** Pick the business that fits your skills, which he calls founder-market fit [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [3:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=213s).
- **Borrow trust from an audience.** He sponsors podcasts and YouTube channels he watches, with a landing page in each audience's voice [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=488s). He launched Sheets Resume free inside his Reddit post. Keep the product link low in the post [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=551s), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=624s). See [reddit-marketing](../topics/reddit-marketing.md).
- **Polarize.** "Be something for someone" [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=549s).
- **No subscriptions for one-off needs.** He calls monthly plans for job seekers predatory [source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=551s).
- **Brand name rules.** Easy to spell, names what you sell, clean SEO and a .com [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=590s).
