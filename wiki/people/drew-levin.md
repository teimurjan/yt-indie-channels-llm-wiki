Drew Levin is a co-founder of SideShift, a marketplace that supplies UGC creators and campaign managers to consumer apps.

## Who

- Co-founder of [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md). It has 850,000 creators, about 150,000 monthly active, and about 100 vetted campaign managers (2026-06) [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [49:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2959s).
- SideShift trains its creators with a 6-hour, Duolingo-style course. Trained creators earn $10K to $15K a month after 1 to 2 months (2026-06) [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [25:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1545s).

## Businesses

- [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md): supplied Suno, which reached #1 in App Store music with about 8 creator managers [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=614s). One long-running program passed 10,000 posts and 500M views and took an app to #1 [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1740s).

## Repeated advice

- **One manager per about 10 creators,** with a range of 5 to 15. Managers run output and firing. Coaches give delivery feedback [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=569s).
- **Founders start hands-on.** Review videos in the first 3 days and hold weekly calls. Step back once CPMs stay under $4 [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=716s).
- **Engagement rate predicts virality.** (Likes + comments + saves + shares) / views: above 7.5% to 8% is good, above 12% often goes viral [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1104s).
- **Spark rules.** Rank by comment sentiment, then engagement, then views. Test at $20, or $50 to $100 a day for a week. Kill below 5% engagement [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s), [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2145s). "Spark ads are incredibly cracked" [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1740s). Meta partnership ads on small UGC accounts convert better than Spark [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2251s).
- **CPM targets by month.** $5 to $8 in month one, under $3 by month three. Top programs reach $0.80 to $0.90 (2026-06) [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [46:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2777s).
- **Payment structure is "the alpha".** Pay $800/month for 60 videos with non-stacked view bonuses, and pay only for the best of 4 cross-posted platforms. Cap bonuses by views, not time [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [39:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2391s), [44:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2647s). Do not cap a breakout creator: one brand capped a creator at $4K instead of about $75K and lost him [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [44:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2698s).
- **UGC tests positioning.** "UGC is the fastest testing engine for positioning in general" [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [34:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2074s).
