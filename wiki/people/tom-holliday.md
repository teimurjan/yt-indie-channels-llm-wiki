Tom Holliday is an Australian founder who built Avenue, a booking and inquiry widget for trades, with his brother.

## Who

- No coding background. He ran a marketing agency for 10 years, and its 70+ hosting clients paid for Avenue's first hires ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=350s)).

## Businesses

- [Avenue](../businesses/avenue.md): A$85K/month, about US$55K/month, bootstrapped, about a year after launch (2026-09). Stripe went from A$597 in August 2025 to A$63,000 in August 2026 ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=69s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=155s)).
- A marketing agency, see [agencies](../topics/agencies.md).

## Repeated advice

- Solve one old friction: a customer who wants to buy should not need to call ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=130s)).
- Sell door to door with iPads and printed brochures. An iPhone demo made owners think it was an app ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=391s)).
- Win many small clients first. Bigger clients then say "I've seen you guys everywhere" ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=391s)).
- Let the niche choose you. They pitched every business, and trades responded best ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=457s)).
- Ask rejections for referrals ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=517s)).
- Sell before you build. A prospect who asks the price is the signal ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=646s)).
