Phoenix Do is a consumer app founder in the manifesting and self-help niche who built Copia.

## Who

- He builds consumer apps for self-help and manifesting. [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) interviewed him ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=0s)).
- The core audience of his app is women aged 18 to 25 ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s)).

## Businesses

- [Copia](../businesses/copia.md): turns a viral ChatGPT "day in my dream life" prompt into an affirmations app. About $25K MRR and 100K downloads (2025-10) ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=0s), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=132s)).
  - Co-founder Chloe's hook-and-demo video reached about 10M views on Instagram ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s)).
  - A longer onboarding raised install-to-paywall from 50-55% to 85-90%, which doubled conversion (2025-10) ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=696s), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s)).
  - He hired 10 to 20 UGC creators through [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md) ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Hook and demo.** Show the magic moment but do not make it feel like an ad. A 6.6M-view video without the demo got an estimated 20-30% fewer downloads ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=376s), [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=398s)).
- **Put the magic moment after a commitment.** Users get their dream-life story only after they enter their goals ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=226s)).
- **Start with mid-size creators** of 50K to 250K followers at about $1 CPM ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=556s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=616s)).
- **Keep UGC demos fixed; test only the first 10 seconds** ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1192s)).
- **Steal from e-commerce.** Dominate one channel to a 2-3x ROAS before you expand ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [20:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1253s)).
- **Hard paywalls hide weak products.** He praises [Strava](../businesses/strava.md)'s trial-led model and says [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) lacks an investment step ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=855s), [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=903s)). See [Paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).
- **Pick a brandable name** so people can find the app after a viral video ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [23:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1386s)).
