Kevin Espiritu is the founder of Epic Gardening, a gardening media brand that grew into a product business.

## Who he is

- He started gardening with his brother around 2013 as a hobby and registered a blog for it ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s)).
- The business runs from his homestead in suburban San Diego. The team films content, tests products and grows seeds there ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=61s)).

## Businesses

- [Epic Gardening](../businesses/epic-gardening.md), which he calls the world's most followed gardening brand ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=1s)).
  - Blog income: about $400/month at first, then $4K to $5K/month in 2016 ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s)).
  - Revenue: $75K in 2017, $225K in 2018, $540K in 2019, $2.8M in 2020, $7.3M in 2021 ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s)).
  - "Tens of millions a year" in revenue (2024-03). The video description says over $30M/year ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=1s)).
  - 2019 split: about $250K media and $250K product revenue ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)).
  - First product: a metal raised bed, sold through a basic Shopify store and one Instagram story. It sold out in 2 weeks ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Write a lot, early.** He wrote researched articles about 12 hours a day and dropped links in forums ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s)).
- **Be your own sponsor.** Sell your own product in place of brand deals ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s)).
- **Let the audience pick the product.** Viewers kept asking about the raised bed in his garden ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)).
- **Distribute before you develop.** He imported a product that already existed but was not sold in the US ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)).
- **Protect the hobby.** He blocks gardening time with no agenda and no phone ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=461s)).
- **Act right after you learn.** Do not wait for a full business plan ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=534s)).
