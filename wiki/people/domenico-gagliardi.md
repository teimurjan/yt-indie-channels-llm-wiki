This page collects what the sources say about Domenico Gagliardi ("Dom"), a non-technical founder who builds and sells small AI products.

## Who he is

- Dom studied economics. He ran a micromobility business from 2019, then worked as an analyst ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=87s)).
- He is non-technical. His first projects used Bubble ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=486s)).
- In January 2022, while he still had a full-time job, he set a challenge: launch, validate and sell a micro-SaaS every 30 days ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s)).

## Businesses

### Six small AI tools

He sold six tools for about $85K in total ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s)), ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=198s)).

| Tool | Earned | Sale |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch deck generator | $300 | $2,500 |
| Excel formula generator | About $7K | About $25K after 3 months |
| Cover letter generator | Not given | $7K after 40 days |
| AI summarizer | Not given | $34K after 6 months |
| Quiz generator | $1K | $5K after 3 months |
| Prompt-to-landing-page tool | $1.2K | $12K after 3 months |

### Softgen

- [Softgen](../businesses/softgen.md) is an early AI coding app. Dom grew it from $20K to $500K ARR in 3 months ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=60s)).
- He sold it for close to 3x ARR, which was over $1M (2025-10) ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=241s)).
- In 2024 a new job offer was withdrawn after he signed. He used that moment to go full-time on Softgen ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=565s)).

### Kortix

He now works on Kortix, which helps companies replace manual work with AI agents (2025-10) ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=60s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Set the exit goal first**. A 3-month or 6-month goal sets the roadmap and the speed ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=261s)).
- **Validate with a one-page site**. Share it on X and collect emails. A 15% email conversion rate is a good result ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=261s)).
- **Build one key feature, then loop**. Send it to the waitlist, get feedback, fix it and relaunch ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=321s)).
- **Package the business as an asset**. Buyers look at ARR, gross margin, ARPU, LTV, CAC and churn ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=321s)).
- **Ride trends from X**. Timing made the difference between his winners and losers ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=400s)).
- **Build around a day job**. He planned his calendar and worked in 25-minute Pomodoro blocks ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=430s)).
