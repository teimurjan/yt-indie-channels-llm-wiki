This page collects what the sources say about Nick Sweeney, a breathwork coach and co-founder of the app [Coherence](../businesses/coherence.md).

## Who he is

- Nick turned to breathwork after a skiing injury. He now coaches others ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=112s)).
- He built Coherence with his brother, [Jack Sweeney](jack-sweeney.md). Jack had grown a YouTube channel to about 20,000 subscribers in university ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=112s)).

## Businesses

### Coherence

- Coherence is a breathwork app that helps users regulate the nervous system ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=565s)).
- It made about $85K in its first 8 months, with over 15,000 downloads (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=72s)).
- It averages about $11K/month, and the best month was $22K (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=92s)).
- Pricing is $40/year with a 3-day trial, or $10/month with no trial (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=611s)).
- Monthly costs are $5K to $6K, most of it editors and influencers, for about 50% margin (2025-11) ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [11:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=662s)).
- A viral "vortex breath" post gave them the app idea. Their top video got about 8M views across platforms ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=519s)).

## Repeated advice

The source gives the brothers' shared advice. It does not split it by person.

- **Content first, product second**. What went viral showed what the market wanted ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s)).
- **Test ideas on X**. A post that worked on X became a short-form script, and winners there became YouTube videos ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=286s)).
- **Film in bulk**. Shoot 30 to 60 videos at a time, about two months of content ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=417s)).
- **Amplify winners with ads**. Spark Ads and Meta spend test whether people will pay ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=459s)).
- **Use polarizing hooks**. The 8M-view video opened with what "they" do not want you to know ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=519s)).
- **Outmarket everyone**. Post every day, make many reels and hire clippers ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=459s)).
