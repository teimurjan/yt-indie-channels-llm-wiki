This page collects what the sources say about Matt Espinoza, who runs the organic distribution company [Clover Labs](../businesses/clover-labs.md).

## Who he is

- Matt runs Clover Labs, an organic distribution company in Canada ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s)).
- His main claim is that about 90% of a winning campaign is cloned content and 10% is new experiments ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=32s)).

## Businesses

### Clover Labs

- It went from $0 to $8M a year in six months. It drives about 250M views a month (2026-09) ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s)).
- Clients include OpusClip, Superwall and Typing.com ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s)).
- The company was a year and three weeks old at the interview (2026-09) ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [39:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=2342s)).

### Echos

- [Echos](../businesses/echos.md) is Clover's tool. It clones competitor slideshows and videos and posts variants across many accounts ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=545s)).
- It scrapes a database of about 4M TikTok and Instagram accounts. A full clone set takes 6 to 7 minutes ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=606s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Clone with small changes**. Change the hook, CTA, avatar, color or background, so the platform does not flag a duplicate ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=346s)).
- **Expect about 20% of the original's views**. Five to ten clones match the original, and some do 2x to 6x better ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s)).
- **Hook and stitch**. Keep a proven 2-second hook and attach your own demo ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [16:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1009s)).
- **Run evergreen campaigns**. Take the top 10 of 60 posts and make 3 variants of each ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [20:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1221s)).
- **Expand by country**. Each new country adds about 20% more views. Spain, Germany and Australia are the best markets ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s)), ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=933s)).
- **Pay creators by stage**. Pay a high CPM while you search for formats. Pay mostly a base fee for proven clone content ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [28:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1687s)).
- **Rank in AI search**. Test about 2,000 prompts, find the URLs that LLMs cite, then seed posts and comments there ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [29:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1746s)), ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [32:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1930s)).
