Oliver Brocato is the founder of [Tabs Chocolate](../businesses/tabs-chocolate.md), a "sex chocolate" brand that he grew with organic TikTok and no outside money.

## Who

- He started Tabs Chocolate in his college apartment with $30,000, nearly his whole bank account ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=564s)).
- He had no employees and no outside investment (2023-06) ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).
- Other founders cite him as a model for organic TikTok at scale. [Yiannis](yiannis.md) calls "the Tabs Chocolate guy" his main inspiration for running many accounts ([eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=252s)).

## Businesses

- **[Tabs Chocolate](../businesses/tabs-chocolate.md).** Idea to launch took one year. The first inventory sold out in three weeks. Month one did over $280K at about 50% net margin ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=485s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=547s)).
  - Year one: about $4M in sales, despite being out of stock for more months than in stock ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=254s)).
  - About $11M expected revenue for 2023 (2023-06) ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).
  - [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) describes it as $0 to $10M in 1 year with organic TikTok only, posted by teenagers on new zero-follower accounts ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=357s)).
- **Study Buddy.** He started it to use the same playbook in software ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=357s)).
- **Bustem.** A July 2025 update to the video description says he exited Tabs and now builds Bustem, brand protection software ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md)).

> Conflict: [5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md) gives about $4M in Tabs' first year and about $11M expected in 2023. [_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) says Tabs went from $0 to $10M in 1 year.

## Repeated advice

- **Copy a validated viral concept.** A TikTok review of a similar product had 8M views and 2M likes, but the seller had no brand. He built the polished brand version ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=91s)).
- **Pick a product that triggers emotion.** A novel product makes viewers watch to the end and share ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=136s)).
- **Make content that does not look like an ad.** Over 60 creators make videos. Thousands of accounts post them on trending sounds (2023-06) ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=642s), [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=701s)).
- **Pay meme pages for the best creatives.** He adds a little Google Ads, email and SMS at the bottom of the funnel ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=727s)).
- **Find manufacturers by brute force.** He went through 20 to 25 Google pages of contract manufacturers and called each one ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=616s)).
- **Build a brand, not a dropshipping store.** A brand brings repeat customers and equity for an exit ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=366s), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=427s)).
- **One viral post lifts the next ones.** Joseph recalls Oliver's point: after a 1M-view video, TikTok shows the account's next videos to the people who watched ([eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=801s)).
