Ramsri Goutham is an app builder in India who runs two AI SaaS apps next to a full-time job.

## Who

- He keeps his job by choice: no VC money, no team, and time for family [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=0s).
- Path: online courses 2015 to 2018, experiments 2018 to 2020, a Udemy course in 2021 and 2022 that made $10K, then a no-code Questgen in 2022 and 2023 [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=202s).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Supermeme](../businesses/supermeme.md), an AI meme generator: about $130K lifetime revenue, 1.5M+ users and about 400 paying customers (2025-06). Three co-founders: Ramsri (AI), a full-stack developer and a marketer [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=252s).
- [Questgen](../businesses/questgen.md), an AI quiz generator: about $100K lifetime revenue, 150K+ users and about 180 to 200 paying customers (2025-06) [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=73s).
- Together: $8,500/month, a little over $100K ARR, with about 2 hours of work a week on Saturday afternoons (2025-06). Margin is 70% to 75%, mostly AI API costs [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=51s), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=577s).

## Repeated advice

- **Find manual tasks people pay for**, then ask which ones generative AI can improve or replace now or soon [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s).
- **Bet on where AI is going.** In 2020 models were not able to make good quizzes. He bet that they were about to catch up [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s).
- **Build distribution before the product.** Blogs, YouTube and daily LinkedIn posts, with both apps built in public. He spent $0 on marketing [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=451s).
- **Posting finds co-founders too.** His Supermeme co-founders found him through his posts [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s).
- **Learn one hour a day.** Payments, auth, databases, marketing and SEO took years of 1 to 2 hours a day [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=108s).
- **Stitch tools together** with Lovable, Bolt, Make, n8n or Zapier instead of months of R&D [source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) [11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=664s).
