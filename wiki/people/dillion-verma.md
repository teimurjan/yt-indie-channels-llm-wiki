Dillion Verma co-built Halo AI, an AI photo editor that reached $300K MRR in 45 days on mass UGC.

## Who he is

- A founder whose products failed for 18 months before Halo AI, his first consumer app. He built it with a partner on the Gemini API ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=0s)).

## Businesses

- [Halo AI](../businesses/halo-ai.md): live in mid to late October 2025 after 2 to 3 weeks of building. It reached $300K MRR in 45 days (2026-03) ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=0s), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=203s)).
  - 600M views in 60 days and 1.2B in 120 days, from 85 creators making about 300 posts a day (2026-03) ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=27s), [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1502s)).
  - Funded with a $100K personal loan at break-even for 4 to 5 weeks. Early UGC ROAS of 5:1 to 6:1. Margin about 50% (2026-03) ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [50:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3039s), [51:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3062s)).
  - $8.99/week trial paywall. A skippable onboarding paywall and a gate at first action both convert at 16.5% ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [40:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2440s), [41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2501s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Revenue = views x conversion.** A format converts at a steady 0.3% to 0.5% of viewers ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [6:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=383s), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=427s)).
- **Model views as a product:** creators x videos per creator x platforms x views per post ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=591s)).
- **One format only**, copied across all accounts the same day it hits ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1212s), [25:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1551s)).
- **Milestone pay.** $20 base plus $60 to $800 bonuses at 20K to 1M views, under $1 effective CPM. 29 of 30 creators chose milestones over CPM ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [33:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1990s), [35:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2156s)).
- **Screen with a paid trial.** $100 for 5 videos over 5 days ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=743s)).
- **AI coach and Discord community** for creators ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [27:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1663s), [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1798s)).
- **Cut bad fits fast.** "Make the hard decision as early as possible." ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [55:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3353s)).
