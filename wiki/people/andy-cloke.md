Andy Cloke is a solo founder in London who built Data Fetcher, an Airtable extension that pulls data from any API.

## Who

- A solo founder in London [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s).
- His first paid product was a TikTok influencer directory built on scraped data. It reached a few thousand dollars MRR and he sold it [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=63s).

## Businesses

- [Data Fetcher](../businesses/data-fetcher.md): $23K MRR from 600 paying customers at 85% margin (2025-08) [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=520s).
  - He copied API Connector, a Google Sheets add-on with about 100,000 users, and brought it to Airtable's new marketplace [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s).
  - Growth: $1K MRR after a few months, $3K after a year, $10K after no-code integrations, $20K after 3 years [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s).

## Repeated advice

- **Port a proven add-on to a growing platform.** His six steps: find a growing platform, find a pain in its forums, borrow a proven add-on and its UX, check for an API or marketplace, do napkin math, and judge whether the platform will build it [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [4:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=285s), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=346s). See [platform-marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
- **Platforms give leads and trust.** The market is big enough for a solo founder but too small for VC-funded rivals. The risk is a native clone [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s).
- **Where to build now (2025-08).** Notion and Figma. Not ChatGPT or Claude, because competition is too high [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=374s).
- **Focus over side projects.** Each slowdown pushed him into a side business, which cost about 6 months. He asks Claude to talk him out of new projects [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=548s).
- **Talk to users.** After almost a year without user contact, one afternoon of user tests found UX issues. The fixes raised revenue almost overnight [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=596s).
