Dickie Bush is a former BlackRock trader who built a portfolio of four online writing businesses.

## Who he is

- He traded at BlackRock for about $180K at age 22. He started a newsletter in January 2020 and ran a 30-day writing challenge before quitting. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=200s))
- On day 27, Naval and others retweeted a post. His newsletter grew from 250 to 1,000 subscribers. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s))
- His first ghostwriting client named the price. He hoped for $250 and got $5,000. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=437s))

## Businesses

- The portfolio was on pace for $10M in 2024 (2024-07). ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s))
- [Ship 30 for 30](../businesses/ship-30-for-30.md): a cohort writing course for beginners, about $3M/year (2024-07). The first cohort cost $50 with a refund for finishers. After 50+ interviews it relaunched at $99. It made $1M in 2021 at about 90% margin. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s))
- [Premium Ghostwriting Academy](../businesses/premium-ghostwriting-academy.md): group coaching for freelance ghostwriters, about $5M/year (2024-07). ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s))
- [Write With AI](../businesses/write-with-ai.md): a paid newsletter, on pace for $500K (2024-07). [Typeshare](../businesses/typeshare.md): a SaaS for online writers. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=139s))

## Repeated advice

- **Do not quit first.** Quitting first makes you act from scarcity. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=200s))
- **Productize the input, not the output.** Ghostwriting capped out, so he sold the 30-day challenge that produced the attention. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s))
- **Charge, then interview.** ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s))
- **Ghostwrite before you build an audience.** Borrow someone else's audience and earn $5K to $10K/month first. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=677s))
- **Monk mode sprints.** Three or four fully scheduled sprints a year, with rest between them. ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=823s))
