Brian Shin is the co-founder of [Once](../businesses/once.md), a disposable camera app for weddings and parties, which he built with his girlfriend ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=56s)).

## Who he is

- He earlier co-founded a VC-backed, sales-driven B2B startup with 50 people. Once is fully bootstrapped ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [3:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=202s)).
- He and his girlfriend carried disposable cameras across 7 or 8 countries. Once copies that experience in an app ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=266s)).

## Businesses

- **[Once](../businesses/once.md).** Launched in December 2025. It reached $20K monthly revenue in 83 days (2026-05) ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=56s)).
- About $22K in revenue that month, with 10,000 to 12,000 weekly active users (2026-05) ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)).
- Over 300 events in February and about 700 booked for March ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=141s)).
- Price depends on guest count: $2 for a 10-person party, $50 for a 150-guest wedding ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Set a commitment metric before you build.** Pick a deadline and a target number of real usage signals. For Once it was 10 events with real dates before any app code ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=510s), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s)).
- **Exhaust your network, then go cold.** Four friends said yes. Instagram hashtag prospecting reached 250 to 300 people, got about 15 replies and booked about 12 events in a month ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=480s)).
- **Push outreach hard.** "If you haven't been banned in these platforms at least two times, you haven't tried enough" ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s)).
- **Prototype, then throw it away.** He built a web app in 1 to 2 weeks for a Halloween party. It proved that guests love a shared album ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=266s)).
- **Design by hand.** He uses AI for code and finance, not design. Consumer apps need taste ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=349s)).
- **Stop overthinking and just launch** ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=750s)).
