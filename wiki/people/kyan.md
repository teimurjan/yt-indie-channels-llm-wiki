Kyan is a 21-year-old computer science student at San Diego State University who built ToneAdapt, a guitar tone app.

## Who

- A student and guitarist. He learned face-on short-form video by doing UGC for brands ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [ToneAdapt](../businesses/toneadapt.md): tells guitarists how to set up their guitar, amp and pedals to sound like a song. Over 100K users in under 5 months and over $25K in the past 4 weeks: about $11.5K web and about $14K mobile (2026-06) ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=69s), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=108s)).
- Pricing: $10/week or about $60/year ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=108s)).
- He [vibe coded](../topics/vibe-coding.md) V1 in about a week over Thanksgiving break ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=328s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Solve your own tiny problem.** ChatGPT gave him inaccurate guitar tones, and Reddit showed others had the same problem ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=226s)).
- **Pick a hobby you already practice.** You are the customer, so you need no market research ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=516s)).
- **Ship V1 on the web and charge from day one.** No polish, no logo. Build mobile later from feedback ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=611s)).
- **Post 3 times a day for at least a month,** face on camera, reposted to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=416s), [10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=632s)).
- **Ride a winning format until it dies.** Remake it 50 times, pay UGC creators to copy it 60 times a month, and put ad spend behind it ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=670s)).
