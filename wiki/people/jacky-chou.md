Jacky Chou is an SEO founder who runs about 13 small internet businesses, led by the agency Indexsy and the SaaS Local Rank.

## Who he is

- He started the SEO agency Indexsy in 2015 and built content sites ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=54s)).
- In late 2023, Google's helpful content update and AI Overviews wiped out his content site income ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=187s)).
- He uploads to YouTube daily, for 2.5 years so far (2026-03) ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=337s)).

## Businesses

- Portfolio: about $250K/month (2026-03). The video description says $500K/month before the crash ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=54s)).
- [Indexsy](../businesses/indexsy.md): SEO agency, $85K/month via Stripe alone (2026-03) ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
- [Local Rank](../businesses/local-rank.md): local SEO SaaS. $454K total since launch, about $50K/month (2026-03) ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
- Trackings.ai: $25K to $30K/month (2026-03) ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
- Seven to eight other projects make $170 to $5K/month each ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Diversify revenue.** Content sites died, and the agency kept him afloat ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)).
- **Treat YouTube as insurance.** 80% of Local Rank's $20K MRR launch came from a video with 1.8K views. The launch tweet brought 20% ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)). See [YouTube](../topics/youtube.md).
- **Own a narrow niche,** for example SEO for plumbers ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s)).
- **Post daily for six months.** One long and one short video a day. Thirty bad videos teach more than two perfect ones ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s)).
- **Be first on trends.** Cover a new AI model first and you rank, even as a small channel ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=433s)).
- **Pitch by email, not in the video.** Each video links to a newsletter lead magnet ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=495s)).
