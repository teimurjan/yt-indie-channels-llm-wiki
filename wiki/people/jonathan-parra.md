Jonathan Parra is the former lead paywall designer at Superwall, where he designed about 4,000 to 4,500 paywalls.

## Who he is

- He joined Superwall pre-revenue. Paywalls first ran on Webflow, then Svelte, then Superwall's editor [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=225s).
- 2,000 paywalls in 2 years (2024-09); about 4,000 to 4,500 by 2026-02 [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=292s), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=311s).

## Businesses

- Superwall (paywall design). He cites average paywall conversion at about 8%, often lifted to 15% to 20% after a few iterations (2026-02) [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [54:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3254s), [55:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3316s).

## Repeated advice

- **There is no perfect paywall.** The same design wins in one app and fails in another [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [31:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1901s).
- **Test design and packaging before price.** Price tests need cohort management [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=65s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2171s).
- **Cut copy and choice.** A "Continue" button plus "No commitment, cancel anytime" beat control by 10%. Show 2 plans and preselect annual [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=344s), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=641s).
- **Exit drawer, then a one-time offer** of 25% to 33% off [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s), [18:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1115s).
- **Simple beats complex.** A "try for 50% off" paywall with a big image beat a comparison table by 111% [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1564s).
- **Users pay for prompts.** Niche AI apps with tuned prompts can charge more [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1332s).
