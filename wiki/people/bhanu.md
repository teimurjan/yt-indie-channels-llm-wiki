Bhanu is a solo developer from India who built SiteGPT, an AI website chatbot, and grows it with free tools that rank on Google.

## Who

- He says he hates marketing, so he uses "engineering as marketing" ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=107s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [SiteGPT](../businesses/sitegpt.md): about $10K MRR in its first month (2023). $13K MRR and about $500K total revenue, $0 paid marketing (2026-01) ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=63s), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s)).
- [Feather](../businesses/feather.md): reached $5K to $6K MRR. He sold it for $250,000 ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=290s)).

## Repeated advice

- Build for an existing audience first. SiteGPT started as a feature for 100 Feather customers ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=229s)).
- Make free tools related to the main product. Almost 50 tools take about 90% of his Google traffic ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=107s), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).
- Pick tool keywords in Ahrefs with KD under 10 and volume of 1,000 or more, then score on effort and relevance ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=525s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=648s)).
- Clone a tool pattern with Cursor. A new tool takes under 5 minutes ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s)).
- Launch the core feature fast and let feedback set direction ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=755s)).
