Jay is a former Amazon software engineer who built NomadTable, a meetup app for solo travelers, as a bootstrapped solo founder.

## Who he is

- He worked 3 years in big tech. He got the idea on a 3-week solo trip and quit about a week after he came back ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2107s), [50:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3004s)).
- He started the idea during his job as a software engineer at Amazon ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)).
- He works from new cities. He spent 4 months in Bangkok, a hotspot for the app ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s)).

## Businesses

- [NomadTable](../businesses/nomadtable.md): solo travelers create activities and meet people nearby.
  - Over 1M downloads and about $65K/month (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=0s), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=364s)).
  - $140K MRR, 1.8M users and about 100K meetups, about 15 months after launch (2026-09) ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s)).
  - Annual plan $69.99, plus weekly and monthly plans (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=430s)).
  - 60 to 70 creators get 30M to 44M organic views a month. 10% to 15% of new users come from word of mouth, the rest from UGC (2026-02) ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=181s), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=508s)).
  - A waitlist of 12K to 15K brought about 6K installs on day one ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [47:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2836s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Go viral yourself first, then hire creators.** His own content brought the first 50K to 100K downloads. Creators only remake hooks he already proved ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=464s), [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s), [szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [52:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=3177s)).
- **Watch 3-second retention.** A good format holds 75% to 80% of viewers past 3 seconds and gets 4K to 5K views a post. Under about 50 views, drop it ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1624s), [29:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1751s)).
- **Pay creators $1 to $2 CPM, with no cap.** Pay monthly and count views on old posts for 3 months ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [40:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2435s), [44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2687s)).
- **No paid ads for a social app.** ARPU is too low to pay back ad costs ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2172s)).
- **Use your own app on the ground.** Meetups in Bangkok gave him feature ideas and showed new user segments ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s)).
- **Build what you care about, and start now.** Passion is hard to compete with. Software is fast to build next to a job ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=405s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=465s)).
