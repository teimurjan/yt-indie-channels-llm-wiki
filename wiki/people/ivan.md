Ivan is a co-founder, with Nick, of an agency that markets apps through automated Instagram comments posted from branded accounts.

## Who

- Ivan and [Nick](nick.md) worked as a team for 8 years before 2026-01. They went from a Minecraft server that sold, to private blockchain work, to an AI flight search app ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1047s), [18:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1103s)).
- Their automated TikTok accounts got about 100M views. One static image got about 2M views ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [19:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1196s)).

## Businesses

- **The comment agency.** It posts comments under trending videos from branded accounts. The accounts run on real phones, which their own Android build controls ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=0s), [25:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1543s)).
- One campaign for a US health app used about 300,000 comments to get about 7,000 link clicks. They projected about a 4x return at the halfway point (2026-01) ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2080s), [35:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2112s)).
- They ran 60 phones. Each phone runs 12 to 24 Instagram accounts a day. Each account posts up to 60 comments a day (2026-01) ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [37:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2222s), [40:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2458s)).
- Their plan: 1,000 devices and about 60,000 accounts, for about 1.5M videos and 21M comments a month ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [39:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2394s)).
- At recording they were building a self-serve platform for "social agents" ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=0s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Treat comments like cold email.** Warm up many accounts, spread the volume and test angles. Some comments have no CTA at all. Then the account looks human ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [38:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2303s)). "Comments and replies are basically UGC in text form." ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=375s))
- **Be open about the app.** The "app co-founded by my friends and me" angle beat covert ads. One such comment got about 800 likes ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=610s)).
- **Comment early on rising videos.** An early comment sits at the top. Old videos are not worth a comment ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=375s)).
- **Rotate angles.** One angle worked for two weeks, then Instagram began to filter it. Varied prompts fixed it ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1005s)).
- **Expect a power law.** A small share of comments brings almost all results ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [35:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2127s)).
- **Copywriting is the lever.** Automation scales skill but does not replace it ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [52:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=3131s)).
- **Benchmark the profile funnel.** About 15% of profile visits becoming link clicks is good. The range runs from 3% to 50% or 60% ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [47:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2863s)).

> Note: [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md) points out that platforms prefer ad spend over mass UGC. Nick and Ivan argue that content which performs fills a gap that Instagram wants filled ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [29:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1757s), [29:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=1777s)).
