Jake is a first name that two sources use for two different founders: the owner of a Roblox marketing agency, and a co-founder of the LinkedIn ghostwriting app Kleo. Neither source gives a surname.

## Who he is

- **Jake of Vector 3.** A friend of Roblox developers [Cole](cole.md) and [Ian](ian.md) in Austin, Texas. He runs [Vector 3](../businesses/vector-3.md), an agency that makes trailers and TikTok campaigns for brands that enter [Roblox](../businesses/roblox.md) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=195s)).
- **Jake of Kleo.** [Lara Acosta](lara-acosta.md) met him through LinkedIn content, where he was in a similar situation. He co-founded [Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md), an AI ghostwriter for founders on LinkedIn ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s)).

## Businesses

- **Vector 3.** Worked on Roblox launches for Nicki Minaj and SpongeBob. About 450 videos and over 100M views. $36,150 so far in the month, of which $20K to $25K is retainers. Costs are $10K to $15K a month, including two full-timers (2025-04) ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=195s), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)).
- **Kleo.** $30K MRR in 4 days and about $60K MRR in under 2 months after an October 2025 launch (2026-01) ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=0s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Get agency clients from LinkedIn content.** About 95% of Vector 3 clients come from LinkedIn posts of education and past work. Jake does no cold outreach ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)).
- **Get paid for the lead.** Paid consulting calls often turn into execution work ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)).
- **Systemize delivery.** A brand trailer follows a 45-step template, with an SOP and a recorded video for each step ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)).
- **Build skills before a business.** Try many interests, get a bit better than average, freelance, then scale what you like ([source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1049s)).
- **"Edu-selling".** The Kleo Jake named the style: teach first, then show the tool only at the end. His own posts state a common founder problem ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=291s), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=342s)).
