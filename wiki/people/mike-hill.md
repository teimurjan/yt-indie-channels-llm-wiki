Mike Hill is an Australian founder who runs a bootstrapped portfolio of B2B SaaS apps built on lifetime deals and SEO.

## Who

- A former Flash developer who ran a digital ad agency and sold it ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=138s)).
- Pat Walls's team later held him up as the model for a simple, content-led approach, in contrast to TikTok app founders ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [12:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=772s)).

## Businesses

- A portfolio at just over $200K MRR (2025-11). Mike says three businesses make the money and two more are coming ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=0s), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s)):
  - [Curator.io](../businesses/curator-io.md), a social media aggregator for websites and events.
  - [Frill](../businesses/frill.md), a feedback and roadmap tool. Its private lifetime deal raised about $30K ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s)).
  - [Juno](../businesses/juno.md), digital signage; [Fluke](../businesses/fluke.md), no-code product tours; Smile, group e-cards ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=81s), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=106s)).
- Each company has four [co-founders](../topics/co-founders.md) at 25%. It grows to about $10K MRR to cover costs, then profit goes to the founders ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s)).
- Goal: $1M MRR in 5 years, small team, no raised capital (2025-11) ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s)). See [App portfolios](../topics/app-portfolios.md).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick an idea that exists**, ideally with competitors that do well despite bad UX ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)).
- **Fund with [lifetime deals](../topics/lifetime-deals.md)**: private LTD at $59 to $100, then AppSumo, then a last higher-priced private LTD; close with about $100K in the bank. Never give accounts away free ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s)).
- **Write content early**: landing, blog, competitor and "alternative to" pages. "It is never... too early to start writing content" ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s)).
- **Collect reviews on Trustpilot and G2 and answer competitor questions on Reddit** ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s)).
- **Optimize for salaries, not exits** ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s)).
- **Avoid AI-dependent businesses**, because an API you do not control is platform risk ([source](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=621s)).
