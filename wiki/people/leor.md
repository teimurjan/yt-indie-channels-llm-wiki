Leor co-founded Hush Blankets, a Toronto weighted blanket brand, with Aaron Spivak.

## Who he is

- He started Hush with [Aaron Spivak](aaron-spivak.md) while both had other jobs ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md)).

## Businesses

- [Hush Blankets](../businesses/hush-blankets.md): weighted and cooling blankets, then pillows, sheets and a mattress.
  - Early revenue grew from $30K to $60K to $90K/month. In July sales fell to zero or one a day (2018) ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
  - The cooling blanket Kickstarter had a $25K goal and raised over $1M in 30 days (2019) ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)).
  - $20M in sales in 2020. The mattress launch did $1.5M ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=530s)).
  - Canada's largest sleep company bought it in October 2021, after $48M in 48 months ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=597s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Validate with search volume, then pre-sell.** About 300,000 people were set to search "weighted blankets". A pre-order site ran on Google Ads ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
- **Plan for seasons.** No summer inventory plan nearly killed the business ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=191s)).
- **Call every customer.** Every customer said the blanket was too hot ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=297s)). See [customer interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md).
- **Crowdfund pre-validated demand.** 3,000 people had said on the phone that they planned to buy ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)). See [crowdfunding](../topics/crowdfunding.md).
- **Build the product line from calls.** Pillows, sheets and the mattress all came from customer interviews ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=530s)).
- **Share the ups and downs.** Customers asked the founders to sign their blankets ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=657s)).
