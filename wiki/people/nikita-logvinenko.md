Nikita Logvinenko co-founded [Natural Write](../businesses/natural-write.md), an AI "humanizer" that rewrites AI text so it passes AI detectors. He grew it with self-filmed TikTok videos.

## Who he is

A marketer based in Serbia. His co-founder [Evgeniy Mikholap](evgeniy-mikholap.md) is in Poland, and a third member, Alexander, does the tech. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s)

## Businesses

- **Natural Write.** $41K in month 1, $32K in month 2 and $27K in month 3, about $100K in total (2025-09). Students use it most, and marketers too. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=10s), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)
- The funnel: about 9M TikTok views, about 300K site visits, 250K signups and $100K in revenue (2025-09). [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=544s)
- They built the product in two weeks. The server costs $35/month. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s)

## Repeated advice

- **Pick an idea by its growth channel.** Choose a category that already goes viral on TikTok. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s)
- **Film it yourself.** Paid influencers gave views and almost no sales. Self-filmed daily posts made a sale from the first video. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)
- **Copy, then innovate.** "Skip inventing your own TikTok format and just replicate what's already going viral." Invent your own formats only after videos go viral. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=328s), [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=642s)
- **Warm up accounts.** Use a VPN, an iPhone, a local number and a new Apple ID. Spend 15 to 30 minutes a day for 3 to 5 days on niche content. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s)
- **Show the problem, then the fix.** Both top videos show "100% AI" text turning into "100% human" text. One copy of a Jim Carrey format got 4M views. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s)
- **Do it yourself first.** Learn what works before you delegate. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=627s)
