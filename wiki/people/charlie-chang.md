Charlie Chang is a finance YouTuber who runs six YouTube channels and [Paired](../businesses/paired.md), an overseas staffing company.

## Who

- He is 31 and based in Southern California (2024-02). He tried many side hustles, then started finance videos in 2018 ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md)).
- In 2020 he earned $750 in AdSense in one day, and the channel took off. He gained 500 to 1,000 subscribers a day and made $10K to $20K/month in AdSense, all solo ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=240s)).

## Businesses

- **[Charlie Chang Media](../businesses/charlie-chang-media.md).** Six YouTube channels.
  - $200K to $250K in a typical month, close to $300K in the best month, over $2.4M a year (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
  - Revenue mix: affiliate 50% to 55%, sponsorships 20% to 25%, AdSense 20% to 25% (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)).
  - The main channel has about 1.1M subscribers (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
  - A 26-person team costs about $20K to $25K/month (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)).
- **[Paired](../businesses/paired.md).** Overseas staffing, started in 2023, low six figures (2024-02). It charges a one-time placement fee. A full-time hire costs about $600 to $1,000/month ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Make videos people search for**, such as "how to invest in stocks", not only viral attempts ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)).
- **Title formula.** A relatable age, a big number and a dollar amount ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)).
- **Ride trends within your niche.** He moved to crypto, stocks and side hustles as each topic boomed ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=349s)).
- **Cut long videos into shorts** with Submagic, Descript and Opus ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=477s)).
- **Hire overseas.** He used Upwork, Fiverr and OnlineJobs, and now mostly Paired ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)).
- **Use the audience to build equity** in a traditional business ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)).
- **Start with a service.** Learn a high-value skill and start an agency, which needs little capital ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [9:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=586s)).
