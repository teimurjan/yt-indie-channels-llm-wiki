Alex Garcia runs Marketing Examined and four sister newsletters, all paid for by ad sponsors.

## Who

- He worked at The Hustle for about a year before he started his own newsletter ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=94s)).
- Based in Austin, visited by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md)).

## Businesses

- [Marketing Examined](../businesses/marketing-examined.md) plus Content, Social, Landing Page and DTC Examined: 150K to 160K subscribers and about $65K to $70K/month from sponsors (2023-07) ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)).
- A services line at $15K to $20K/month that he is turning into a product (2023-07) ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)).

## Repeated advice

- A [newsletter](../topics/newsletters.md) is the top of the funnel for any other business ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md)).
- Start with "hand-to-hand combat" (DMs, friends, small giveaways), then "buffet marketing" to find the one or two channels with 10x potential ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [4:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=273s)).
- 50 Twitter threads in 50 days took him from 500 to 40,000 followers and the list from about 2,000 to 8,000 or 9,000 ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)).
- With 10,000 subscribers you can sell an ad slot for more than $1,000. Pitch advertisers who already buy ads in other newsletters in your niche ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)).
- Find writers on pages 2 to 4 of Google results ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=482s)).
- Build small products and try to get each to $1,000 in sales ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=609s)).
