Hunter Isaacson is a consumer app builder behind [NGL](../businesses/ngl.md), Wink and the crypto app [Bags](../businesses/bags.md).

## Who

- Before NGL, his apps had over 70 million downloads ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=455s)).
- His first app took 2.5 years with a development agency and died in TestFlight ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=810s)).

## Businesses

- **[NGL](../businesses/ngl.md).** Anonymous messaging built on Instagram's story link feature.
  - Instagram opened story links to all users at the end of October 2021. The team started NGL on a Halloween Zoom call and built it in about a month ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=128s)).
  - Nobody used it for 6 months. One TikTok in 2022-05 got about 1M views and brought the first 1,000 users in a day ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=155s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=268s)).
  - 150M monthly active users (2025-07) ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=79s)).
  - Multi-eight figures a year from weekly subscriptions priced $1 to $7 by country GDP per capita (2025-07) ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=370s)).
- **Wink.** A swipe app to add people on Snapchat, with 70M downloads ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=975s)).
- **Zoom University.** A double-dating app with a 40,000-person waitlist and a 7:1 female-to-male ratio. It reached the top 10 in social ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=871s)).
- **PopStream.** A paid meet-and-greet video app. It failed because a top influencer post cost about $20K ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1235s)).
- **[Bags](../businesses/bags.md).** A 1% fee on trades, most of it passed to the inviter. Its token launchpad pays creators a 1% royalty on every trade ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1367s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Old behavior, new distribution.** Anonymous Q&A existed before. NGL won by riding the Instagram graph and by not adding feeds and tabs ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=712s)).
- **One funnel, one north star.** For NGL: users who post the link and get at least two replies ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=639s)).
- **Sell the result.** "Get your own messages" converts better than "download this app" ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=615s)).
- **Know group psychology.** Predict what a person feels on each screen ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=94s)).
- **Add invites to every product.** A shared link works as an endorsement ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1633s)).
- **Avoid games as a small team.** Paid ads dominate, and retention must reach an hour or more ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1128s)).
- **At $10K MRR with $20K/month to spend.** Run about a dozen small UGC and ad tests and track each cohort. Pair them with invites to lower the real CPI ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1670s)).
