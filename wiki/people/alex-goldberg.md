This page collects what the sources say about Alex Goldberg, who runs the product review site [FinvsFin](../businesses/finvsfin.md).

## Who he is

- Alex started FinvsFin in 2018 as a side project with a business partner, while he had a full-time job ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s)).
- He wrote the first 60 articles himself and learned WordPress to build the site ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- He kept his job until the site replaced his $150K salary, about one year in. He went full-time after about 2 years and bought out his partner ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=242s)), ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=454s)).

## Businesses

### FinvsFin

- FinvsFin compares health and wellness products, mostly telehealth brands. It earns a commission on each referred sale ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s)).
- The first idea was to compare fintech companies, which gave the name "fin vs fin" ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=120s)).
- It makes over $1M a year (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=0s)).
- It took about 6 months to get significant organic traffic ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- He grew it into a portfolio of about six affiliate sites, each in a narrower niche (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)).
- The team is outsourced writers, a VA, a full-time editor, a designer and a web developer (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=689s)).
- He was working on a sale of the business (2024-03) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=750s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Take a true 9-to-5 job**. He moved to a larger company. There he closed the laptop at 5 p.m. and built on the side ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=120s)).
- **Pick a new niche with no content**. A new site cannot win crowded queries, but it can win queries that nobody has written for ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=337s)).
- **Write the best page on each brand**. Use high-intent patterns such as "best X for Y" ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=398s)).
- **Partner with brands directly**. After about 6 months of traffic, he pitched brands, and they named the price per customer ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- **Reuse partners across sites**. One partner can go on five sites ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)).
- **Expect rankings to erode**. Big publishers such as Forbes and Healthline compete in every niche ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=577s)).
- **Ask partners to fund paid media**. He pitched a test spend of a few thousand dollars ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=577s)).
