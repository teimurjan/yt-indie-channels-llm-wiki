CJ Zafir is the founder of CodeGuide, a tool that writes project docs for AI coding tools.

## Who he is

- He ran an MVP agency and did a "12 startups in 12 months" challenge: 11 products, 10 failed [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=229s).
- He does the front end and marketing; a friend does the back end and support [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=438s).

## Businesses

- [CodeGuide](../businesses/codeguide.md): launched 2024-12-24 with 100 users on day one. $17K/month after 49 days, then $42K MRR and over 4,800 users about 90 days in (2025-05), with no paid marketing [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=498s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=96s).
- Pricing: $29/month with no free trial, because AI models cost too much. Costs are about $3,500/month, mostly the OpenAI API (2025-05) [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=761s), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=807s).

## Repeated advice

- **Solve your own repeated problem.** Docs for each client took him 9-10 hours of prompting; a make.com chain saved 7 [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=306s).
- **Validate before you build.** A screen-recorded demo on X plus a Bolt landing page got 1,800 waitlist signups in two weeks [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=352s), [7:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=438s).
- **Ride an existing audience** such as the users of AI coding tools [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=132s).
- **Tutorial marketing.** 4 threads and 3 long posts a week; the product is one step of the shown solution. "If your content is bookmarked, algorithm is in your side." [source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=624s), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=676s)
