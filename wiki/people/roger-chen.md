Roger Chen is a consumer social founder who built two apps that hit #1 on the App Store: Lobby and Bro.

## Who

- He loves social products and calls founder-market fit the key to picking an idea ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [38:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2312s)).
- He works with co-founder Leo, who builds tappable prototypes in ProtoPie ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=348s)).

## Businesses

- **[Lobby](../businesses/lobby.md).** A drop-in video hangout app. It did not grow for about a year. In 2022 it went viral in Israel and hit #1 there ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)). It reached 500K daily active users and #1 in five countries with no paid marketing ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=0s)). Day-90 retention in Israel was 25% to 30%, about twice other markets ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s)).
- **Super Movie.** An AI interactive story app he tried in 2023. He lacked passion for it ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [38:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2312s)).
- **[Bro](../businesses/bro.md).** An AI companion that floats over your texts and lock screen. It hit #1 in the US off TikTok. A brand-account mockup video got 7.9M views ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=0s), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=335s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Prototype before code.** Carry a fake app all day to feel the flows. Many designs look good in Figma but fail in the hand ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=449s)).
- **Use ads for validation, not scale.** Before launch, buy TikTok ads on mockup videos. A few thousand dollars buys fast feedback, while UGC programs take 2 to 3 months ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=531s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=596s)).
- **Find a concrete wedge.** "Always with you" is too vague. Bro started with texting, then dating apps ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=212s)).
- **Hook with a human, then demo.** A raw app screen gets swiped. The best video hid the demo inside a story about texting a crush ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=271s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=2171s)).
- **Network density beats content.** Content brings strangers with no friends on the app. Lobby's key metric was the share of users with 5 friends on day one: about 60% in Israel and 20% elsewhere ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s)).
- **Build a share loop into the product.** Lobby's group-picture button made a watermarked boomerang for Snapchat and Instagram stories. About 3 months later it hit #1 in the UK, Italy and Germany ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s)).
- **Create temporal density.** A one-minute "roll call" call pops up at a random minute each night, like BeReal ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [26:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1597s)).
- "Instead of using AI to create content, we want to use AI to create context." ([source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [30:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1846s))
