Daniel Heintzman is a product designer who co-founded Sway AI and became Head of Product at Cal AI.

## Who he is

- At Sway AI he did design and product. Co-founders Luis ran marketing and operations, and Charles was the iOS engineer ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
- He joined [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) as Head of Product. Cal AI then made a couple million dollars a month (2026-05) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [25:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1529s)).

## Businesses

- [Sway AI](../businesses/sway-ai.md), a dating-profile analyzer. It went from $0 to $2M ARR in 6 months after a summer 2024 launch ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=118s)).
  - Before the app, followers paid about $15 for a manual profile review over DM ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=340s)).
  - Organic slideshows plus one influencer reached $30K to $40K a month before paid ads ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=645s)).
  - A Christmas Day post reached about 4M views and made $5K to $6K in one day (2024-12) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=578s)).
- Cal AI: he worked on retention with redesigned logging screens, groups, leaderboards, streaks and milestone badges ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1621s), [29:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1767s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Build distribution before code.** Sway started as an Instagram account about Hinge prompts ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=340s)).
- **Batch slideshows.** Copy the top posts of a niche account. Fill Figma backgrounds from a spreadsheet in Canva. One or two hours makes a month of posts ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=390s)).
- **Onboarding is a sales funnel.** Explain the problem, ask easy questions, show the dream result, then the [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [15:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=938s), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1297s)).
- **Default to annual.** Annual plans collect cash up front to fund growth ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [19:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1177s)).
- **Design three screens an influencer can show.** For Cal AI these are the camera, the loading screen and the food detail page ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2163s)).
- **Idea filter.** Easy to explain in a video, novel, and in a big proven market with many cheap influencers ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [37:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2230s), [43:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2586s)).
- **Test with influencers first.** They guarantee views, so a test checks one variable ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [47:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2826s)).
