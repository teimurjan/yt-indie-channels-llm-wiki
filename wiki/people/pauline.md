Pauline is a former IBM data scientist who built [AI Crea](../businesses/ai-crea.md), an AI home-staging tool.

## Who she is

- She learned to code in engineering school and worked in data science at IBM. She built AI Crea on nights and weekends and left IBM in June 2024 ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=134s)).

## Businesses

- [AI Crea](../businesses/ai-crea.md): a user uploads a room photo, picks a style and gets a redesigned room. More than 8,000 users and about 8,000 per month (2024-10) ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=73s)).
- Next.js Directory: a side project for developers that she markets on Twitter ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=348s)).

## Repeated advice

- Interview friends about their problems. Then ask: can I do better, can I build it, can I monetize it, can I find clients? ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=162s))
- Validate with payment and ship early. Her first MVP took about a month and had bad image quality ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=204s), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=243s)).
- Go where customers are. For AI Crea, that is Facebook groups and in-person real estate events ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=348s)).
- Offer both a subscription and a one-time pack. Cut churn with a yearly plan, a free trial and better output quality ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s)).
- Launch on Product Hunt mostly for the SEO backlink ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=404s)).
