Cardinal Mason is a self-taught copywriter who went from freelance copy to an email agency and the Copy MBA program.

## Who

- A college dropout who packed boxes for $10/hour, taught himself copywriting and made $2.3M by age 21 (2023-08) ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=0s)).
- First gig: a music blog at 1.5 cents per word. First real client: $2,500/month for emails, ads and blogs ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)).

## Businesses

- Freelance copywriting: peak $48K/month, which he says was not sustainable (2023-08) ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)).
- An email marketing agency: "multiple six figures a year" (2023-08) ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s)). See [Agencies](../topics/agencies.md).
- [Copy MBA](../businesses/copy-mba.md), an education program: $1M in 96 days (2023-08). He sells it with TikTok videos and livestreams ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=74s), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=691s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Find leads from Instagram ads.** A company that pays for ads has a budget for copy. Find the founder's email and send a [cold email](../topics/cold-outreach.md) ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)).
- **Sell retainers, not one-off gigs**: $2K to $4K/month ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=630s)).
- **Write a lot.** He wrote 8 hours a day to get fast ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=412s)).
- **Freelancing caps at about $30K to $40K/month.** Past that, hire and build an agency ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=522s)).
- **Network with a memorable gift.** A custom onesie for an influencer's newborn got him an early gig ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=461s)).
- **AI is a tool, not a replacement**, because owners hire a copywriter to think for them ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=163s)).
