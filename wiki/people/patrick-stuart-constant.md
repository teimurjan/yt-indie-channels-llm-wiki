Patrick Stuart-Constant runs [Social](../businesses/social-app-studio.md), a profitable app studio, with [Pablo](pablo.md). The studio buys consumer apps after their viral peak and grows them with AI ads and constant A/B tests.

## Who he is

A co-leader of Social, a three-year-old studio that raised only a $1M pre-seed (2026-07). [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)

## Businesses

- **Social.** 22 apps at $16M annualized revenue and over 40 million downloads (2026-07). It once hit $11M with 11 people. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=0s), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)
- **[Celebs](../businesses/celebs.md).** A six-year-old celebrity lookalike app, bigger than ever. Weekly plans run about $6.99 to $14.99. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s), [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=866s)
- **Font Maker** makes about $200K/month and **LitStick** just over $300K/month (2026-07). [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=514s), [8:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=535s)
- **[WiseSam](../businesses/wisesam.md).** A consumer OpenClaw agent at $1 for a promo week, then $10/week, with Stripe checkout on the web. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [27:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1675s)

## Repeated advice

- **Buy apps after the viral peak.** Sellers of declining apps often do not know how to grow them. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s)
- **Screen deals two ways.** Top-down: market size, willingness to pay and growth. Bottom-up: run ads for an app before you buy it and compare against your benchmark. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=404s)
- **Run the LTV/CAC loop.** Dozens of tests per app raise revenue per user, which funds more ads. ARPU rose about 3x in 18 months, and portfolio LTV:CAC is about 3x (2026-07). [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=808s)
- **Expect few wins.** Out of 20 A/B tests, 10 are neutral, 5 bad, 3 interesting and 2 wins. Retest old losers, because the rest of the app changes. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=898s), [16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=977s)
- **Question the onboarding paywall.** Celebs made more money without it, because about 10 later placements convert well. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s), [17:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1059s)
- **Tag each test with its hypothesis.** Agents and new hires can then apply principles across apps. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1138s)
- **Humans make ideas, AI makes ads.** The studio makes about 4,000 AI video ads a month (2026-07). [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s), [41:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2460s)
