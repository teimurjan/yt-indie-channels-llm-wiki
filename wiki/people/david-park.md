David Park is the co-founder and CEO of Jenni AI, an AI research and writing tool for students and researchers.

## Who

- He started a clothing brand at 16, dropped out of college, and ran 9 or 10 failed startups with co-founder [Henry](../people/henry.md) before Jenni ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=59s), [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=123s)).
- Henry codes and David sells ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)).
- A thyroid cancer diagnosis almost made him sell Jenni for "a quick few million" ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=578s)).

## Businesses

- [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md):
  - Stuck at about $2K/month after GPT-3 came out in 2020 ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)).
  - Past $3M ARR, valued by David at $10M to $30M (2024-01) ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=743s)).
  - Over $10M/year, profitable, over 5M users (2025-03) ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)).
  - About $775K MRR and 5 to 6M users (2025-11) ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=26s)).
  - Jenni acqui-hired [Matt Gittleson](../people/matt-gittleson.md) to run its UGC program: 150 creators and 75M views at under $2 CPM in 3 months (2025-11) ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=297s)).

## Repeated advice

- Cold-call to the first dollar. He called agencies 8 hours a day, and about 99% said no ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- Ask users what they dislike and what they love about rivals, and watch them work ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)). See [customer interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md).
- Say yes to small podcasts. One led to a $100K check from Jason Calacanis ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)).
- Turn luck into a channel. After a viral thread took MRR from about $2K to $10K, he hired a friend to run TikTok and Reels ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=435s)).
- Explore, then exploit: test many paths up to $1M, then put money into the winners ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=682s); [KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=92s)).
- Judge creators by their ceiling: at least one or two videos with 1M to 10M views ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=288s)).
- Pay half up front at most and tie the rest to conversions or views ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=670s)).
- Turn a winning video into a series. "POV: you have a paper due" ran twice a week for about 6 months and probably made over $500K (2025-03) ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [21:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1276s)). See [short-form video](../topics/short-form-video.md).
- Multiply accounts by country and language. A 48-follower account sometimes beat the 55K main account ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1446s)).
