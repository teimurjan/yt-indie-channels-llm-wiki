Ryan Yao is a former product designer who built the Pomodoro app Cat on Chair with his girlfriend Stephanie.

## Who he is

- He left a $200K product design job to build apps, and wrote the code with AI tools. The surname comes from his LinkedIn handle in the video description [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=84s).

## Businesses

- [Cat on Chair](../businesses/cat-on-chair.md): a Pomodoro timer where a cat sits on your chair while you focus. Over $18K in 30 days, close to $80K in 8 to 9 months, and 127K downloads (2026-04) [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=84s).
- Pricing: lifetime $29.99 or annual $19.99; most sales are lifetime (2026-04) [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=129s).
- Growth: RedNote beta users, then Instagram "app collection" posts (one passed 2M views) and a Threads share that doubled downloads overnight [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=300s).

## Repeated advice

- **Pick an app you already use**, study competitor reviews, and remix mechanics from games, which are built to motivate and retain [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=629s).
- **Win a crowded category with theme.** Ask how to copy a physical experience in the app [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=505s).
- **Use a distinct art style**, from an illustrator or AI [source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=553s).
