Andre Haykal Jr. is the founder of ListKit, a B2B lead-list SaaS for cold email.

## Who he is

- He first ran a lead generation agency that reached $100K/month. Then he built Client Ascension, a coaching program, with Daniel Fazio ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=178s)).

## Businesses

- [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md). $1M ARR within 87 days of launch, near $200K MRR (2024-06) ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s)).
  - 1,000 paying customers in the first 6 months, over 1,500 after about a year (2024-06) ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s)).
  - 5 co-founders and about 40 people in total. Costs are $150K to $175K/month with ads (2024-06) ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)).
- ListKit began as a [productized service](../topics/productized-services.md) that made $105K to $110K/month. All of it went to developers who never shipped. A coaching student, Oliver, then joined as co-founder and shipped the app in six months ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=357s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Sell a manual service behind a SaaS-like front end first.** It needs no development budget and builds cash flow ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=227s)).
- **Fix one flaw of a proven competitor.** Apollo leads were not verified, so ListKit sold verified leads ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=702s)).
- **Cold email with a lead magnet.** He offered 50 free leads to anyone who replied ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=498s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- **No free trial.** A money-back guarantee replaces it, because trials attract "freebie seekers" ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=619s)).
- **Hire offshore.** Developers in Ukraine and support in Lebanon cost $1,000 to $2,000/month each. A US hire costs $5,000 to $8,000 ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=653s)). See [offshoring](../topics/offshoring.md).
- **Run an onboarding call for every customer** to learn why they signed up and cut churn ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=872s)).
- **Pick complementary co-founders** with aligned values ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=750s)). See [co-founders](../topics/co-founders.md).
