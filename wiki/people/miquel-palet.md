Miquel Palet ("Mickey") is a Spanish founder who built Late, a social media API for developers. Google is its only channel.

## Who he is

- Before Late he spent 5 years on startups. He co-founded a VC-backed Spanish edtech company with more than 100 employees ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=176s)).

## Businesses

- [Late](../businesses/late.md): one API that wraps the official Facebook, Instagram, Twitter and LinkedIn APIs, with no limits ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md)).
  - $40K MRR about 7 months after launch (2026-03). Profitable from day one ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=96s)). In the same video he also says $30K MRR in 7 months ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=72s)).
  - 50K total signups, 700+ paying users and churn under 10% (2026-03) ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=156s)).
  - Organic SEO brings $8K+ MRR. Google Search Ads bring about $15K MRR on about $8K/month spend. That is $2.50 revenue per $1, at a $120 target CPA (2026-03) ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=472s), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=548s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Capture intent, do not create it.** Searchers for "social media scheduling API" have about 10x the purchase intent of social media viewers ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s)).
- **One page, two jobs.** Pages that rank get free traffic. Run ads on pages that do not rank ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=381s)).
- **Organic recipe.** Find bottom-of-funnel keywords with 300 to 800 monthly searches and low competition in Ahrefs. Write in-depth guides ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=411s)).
- **Paid recipe.** Bid on the same keywords. Give each ad group its own landing page. Use target-CPA bidding and test copy all the time ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s)).
- **Five-step Google playbook.** List high-intent keywords. Publish 10 to 15 pieces in week one. Launch ad groups with 3 to 5 copy variants. Cut weak keywords and raise budget while ROAS holds. Keep CAC at 20% to 40% of LTV ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=607s), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=668s), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=728s), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=789s)).
- **One channel only.** Go all in on the first channel that works ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=344s)).
- **Docs are the product** for a developer API ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [14:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=882s)).
