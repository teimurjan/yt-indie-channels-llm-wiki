Tim Stoddart is the founder of the SEO agency Stuzzi and a holding company that owns Recovery Local and Copyblogger.

## Who he is

- He got sober and started a blog on Seth Godin's advice. He found SEO by accident. The blog became Sober Nation, and rehab centers asked how he built the audience. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=194s), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s))
- He hired early staff from halfway houses. The team has grown together over 10 years. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=670s))

## Businesses

- [Stuzzi](../businesses/stuzzi.md): an SEO lead generation agency. About $2.5M expected for 2024. The first client paid $2,000/month. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s))
- [Recovery Local](../businesses/recovery-local.md): owns sober.com, detox.com and other addiction-treatment domains. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s))
- [Copyblogger](../businesses/copyblogger.md): he read it daily as a beginner and later bought it. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=194s))
- [Sober Nation](../businesses/sober-nation.md): the original blog. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s))
- The holding company expects $5.2M to $5.5M in total for 2024 (2024-04). ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s))

## Repeated advice

- **One keyword at a time.** Find what people search, then write the article that best solves it. His agency started from "drug rehab marketing". ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s))
- **Start with an agency.** It needs no capital and profits from the first client, but every 2 to 4 clients need another hire. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s))
- **Close in three steps.** Get the prospect to state the pain, raise price and commitment early, then ask "Are you ready to get started?" and wait. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=419s))
- **Three investment filters:** he likes the founder, the business makes cash, and it is evergreen. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=732s))
- **Delegate with Loom and Todoist** in the morning, and do deals in the afternoon. ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=608s))
