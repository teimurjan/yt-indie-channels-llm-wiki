Alex Finn is a former MongoDB consultant who built Creator Buddy, an AI content coach for X, alone with AI coding tools.

## Who

- He started posting on Twitter in December 2021. A thread on the open-sourced X algorithm (March 2023) went viral and got a retweet from Elon Musk ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=107s), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=155s)).
- He found Cursor in August 2024, then moved to Windsurf in October 2024 ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s)).

## Businesses

- [Creator Buddy](../businesses/creator-buddy.md): pulls a user's X posts and coaches them with AI.
  - Launch on 2025-01-24: $100K ARR in 15 minutes, $200K in 2 hours, $300K in 2 weeks ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=0s), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s)).
  - Almost 500 paid subscribers and about $25K/month (2025-06) ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=608s)).
  - Costs about $5,300/month, mostly $5,000 for the X API; about 80% margin (2025-06) ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=668s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Distribution is the moat.** "Knowledge and product is no longer the moat" ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)). See [Personal brand](../topics/personal-brand.md).
- **Build the audience first.** He posted about 5 minutes a day for 3 years before a product ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=556s)).
- **Break features into micro steps** and let ChatGPT plan them for Cursor ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=244s), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=291s)). See [Vibe coding](../topics/vibe-coding.md).
- **Run a long public beta.** 150 testers, each walked through the app ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)).
- **Announce a launch date** and host X Spaces all day ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=473s)). See [Launch strategy](../topics/launch-strategy.md).
- **Act instead of overthinking**, even 5 to 10 minutes a night ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=744s)).
