Connor Burd is a young indie app developer who builds consumer apps with AI and grows them through creator partners.

## Who he is

- He is 23 and has no computer science background ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=58s)).
- His first app, Hotspot Events, was a social app. It took 6 to 8 months to build and failed. He then moved to utility apps, which one person can scale more easily ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=119s)).
- In another telling, his first app was a map app for pickup games that made nothing. His second app reached $10K to $20K/month. A posture app made about $4K to $5K in a few months ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [40:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2422s), [41:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2498s)).

> Conflict: [CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md) names Hotspot Events, a social app, as his first app. [zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md) describes the first app as a map app for pickup games. The sources do not say whether these are the same app.

## Businesses

- [Payout](../businesses/payout.md): an app that finds class action settlements a user can claim. He built it in under 2 weeks with AI and launched it in early August 2025 ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
  - $20K/month, over 12,000 downloads and over 1,300 new subscriptions (2025-10). It won the "Build and Grow" prize at the RevenueCat Shipaton, which had over 55,000 entrants ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)).
  - About $45K/month, almost all from influencer posts (2025-12) ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=197s)).
  - $217K in 5 months. His creator co-founder [Casper](casper.md) calls it a 100K/month app (2026-03) ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=209s)).
- Portfolio: about 6 apps and over $1M in total revenue (2025-10) ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=58s)). Later: 4 or 5 earning apps and over $1M/year, most kept private because of partners (2025-12) ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=259s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Onboarding carries the app.** With a paywall, about 90% of users only see the onboarding. Screenshot the onboarding of about 20 apps in the niche, pick the best screens and restyle them ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s)).
- **Write data structures before vibe coding.** A text file with JSON examples stops the AI from guessing ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=239s)).
- **Remix what already works.** A modified copy of an existing app is already validated. He skips waitlists. He scans top charts and TikTok trends, and asks creators which apps pitch them brand deals ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=434s), [zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [22:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1333s)).
- **Give creators equity and build for their audience.** He showed Casper Figma mock-ups of about 5 ideas and let him pick. Equity gives creators something they own ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=677s), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=737s)).
- **Email big creators. Do not DM them.** They turn notifications off ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=863s)).
- **Creators first, then paid ads.** Winning creator videos become a library of tested Facebook ads ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=492s)).
- **Target core desires:** health, money and looks ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=683s)).
- **iOS first.** Android brings at most 15% of revenue ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [26:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1561s)).
