Evan Yadegari is a 14-year-old founder who built Locked, a gamified habit and focus app, and the younger brother of Cal AI founder Zach Yadegari.

## Who

- A ninth grader. His older brother [Zach Yadegari](../people/zach-yadegari.md) pushed him into apps ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md); [dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=159s)).
- Before apps: Roblox games at 9, then drop shipping, reselling, clipping and vending machines, which all failed ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=159s)). TikTok content for Totally Science grew that account from about 30K to almost 100K followers ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=171s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) and by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- [Locked](../businesses/locked.md): $14K/month about 4 months after launch (2026-04), over $14K/month (2026-06) ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=0s); [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=147s)).
- Problem Pal: peaked at about $2K/month and was sold. Clear AI went nowhere ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=159s)).
- 10X, a "Shopify for apps" builder: over $30K ARR one month after launch (2026-06) ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [22:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1324s)).

## Repeated advice

- Know your RPM before you buy views. His RPM is about $2 to $4 per 1,000 views, and he pays $1 to $2 CPM ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=245s); [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=578s)).
- Use four [influencer](../topics/influencer-marketing.md) deal types: flat rate, capped CPM, minimum view clause and bonus. The minimum view clause is his default ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=486s); [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [13:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=819s)).
- DM at scale with one opener, "paid promo?", and push for a call. Over 500 DMs a day at peak, with 2 to 4% reply rates ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=439s); [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=720s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- The app must show in the first 15 seconds of a creator video ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=283s); [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1018s)).
- Judge engagement and comment quality, not followers ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=604s)).
- [Gamify](../topics/gamification.md) a crowded category ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=207s)).
