Evgeniy Mikholap is a co-founder of Natural Write, an AI text humanizer that made $100K in 90 days from self-filmed TikToks.

## Who he is

- A marketer based in Poland. He co-founded Natural Write with [Nikita Logvinenko](nikita-logvinenko.md), who is in Serbia. A third member, Alexander, does the tech. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [0:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=10s), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=575s))

## Businesses

- [Natural Write](../businesses/natural-write.md): rewrites AI-generated text so it passes AI detectors. Students use it most. Built in two weeks. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s))
  - $41K in month 1, $32K in month 2, $27K in month 3, about $100K in total (2025-09). ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s))
  - About 9M TikTok views, about 300K site visits, 250K signups (2025-09). ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=544s))

## Repeated advice

- **Pick a category that already goes viral on TikTok.** ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s))
- **Film yourself before you pay influencers.** Paid influencers got views and almost no sales. Self-filmed daily posts made a sale from the first video. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s))
- **Seven-step TikTok playbook.** Research the niche, set up a local account, warm it up for 3 to 5 days, recreate viral videos 1:1, post daily, innovate only after virality, then scale accounts. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=328s))
- **Show the problem, then the fix.** The top video, a Jim Carrey format, got 4M views. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s))
- **Do it yourself before you delegate.** ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [10:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=627s))
