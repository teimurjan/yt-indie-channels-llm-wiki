Dan is the founder of Massive, a "Tinder for jobs" app where AI fills out job applications.

## Who he is

- His earlier product raised $3M from VCs and failed. Massive came from the pivot ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)).

## Businesses

- [Massive](../businesses/massive.md). About $200K/month, about $2M ARR (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)).
- Over 10M applications in about 2 years. The team is Dan, one full-time engineer and a few VAs and ops staff (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=78s), [2:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=168s)).
- A test LinkedIn post from an account with about 500 connections got about 8M impressions and 5,000 comments ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)).
- About 3M to 4M views a week, with 3 to 5 viral videos a day (2025-08) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=437s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Skip waitlists and charge.** Massive's waitlist reached 40K to 50K, but only about 0.5% signed up after launch ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=268s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s)).
- **Use a clear analogy.** "Tinder for jobs" lands at once. "Job search on autopilot" does not ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=181s)).
- **Call out the paying audience in the first 3 seconds.** One 11M-view video brought only about $20K MRR. Views are easy, but views from people who pay are hard ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=489s), [17:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1025s)).
- **80/20 for organic.** About 80% of videos copy formats that already went viral, and 20% are new ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [13:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=801s)).
- **Validate with influencers, scale with paid ads.** Spend $1K to $2K max on a few influencers to test. Paid ads give better conversion data than [UGC](../topics/ugc.md) ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s), [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1178s)).
- **Do not fake testimonials.** He calls fake "I landed a job" UGC illegal ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1279s)).
- **Execute instead of researching.** About 80% of experiments fail ([source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [30:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1803s)).
