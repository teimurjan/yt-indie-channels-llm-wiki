Will Cannon is a bootstrapped founder who built UpLead and Signaturely by copying markets that already had product-market fit.

## Who

- He started a mortgage company at 19, which the 2008 crash wiped out. He then sold lead lists as an agency from about 2007 to 2017 ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=84s)).
- He failed at 15 to 20+ businesses before these two ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=294s)).

## Businesses

- [UpLead](../businesses/uplead.md): B2B contact data. Over 4,000 customers, over $30M total sales, over 100K visits a month (2025-10) ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)). It came from his agency's pain: vendor data was only 50% to 60% accurate ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=154s)).
- [Signaturely](../businesses/signaturely.md): a simple DocuSign alternative started in 2020. Over 1.6M users, over $1.5M total sales, over 800K visits a month (2025-10) ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)).
- No outside funding for either ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=59s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Four steps.** Pick a market with product-market fit; find a twist in 1- and 2-star reviews (lower price alone is not one); acquire customers profitably with one skill; execute and expect failure the first time ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=294s)).
- **The $1M cold email.** Subject "Hi {first name}", a neutral opener, "like ZoomInfo but with real-time email verification", and "Are you open to trying it out at no cost?" UpLead's first $1M ARR came from cold email alone ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s)). See [Cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- **SEO with free tools and templates.** A free signature tool and contract templates brought Signaturely's first 1,000+ customers ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=570s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).
- "The playbooks are already out there. Most people just don't study them" ([source](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=40s)).
