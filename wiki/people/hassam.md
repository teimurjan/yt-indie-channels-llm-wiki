Hassam is a non-technical founder and Amazon seller who built Launch Fast, an AI product research tool, and launched it through a partner.

## Who

- He ran two Amazon private-label brands beside a corporate job ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=200s)).
- He failed with 10 to 12 AI side projects, such as AI video and job-application tools, because he did not know the users ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=450s)).

## Businesses

- [Launch Fast](../businesses/launchfast.md): AI Amazon product research, built with Cursor in 48 hours ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=284s)).
  - About $10K MRR at day 30, $17K to $18K at day 60 and about $21.8K at day 90 (2026-01) ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s)).
  - [Legacy X](../businesses/legacy-x.md), an Amazon coaching company, took equity and launched it to its students at $50/month. Gus estimates about 50% equity and about $10K/month net for Hassam (2026-01) ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=82s), [13:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=805s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Trade equity for [distribution](../topics/distribution.md).** "50% of 20K MRR is better than 50% of zero MRR" ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=342s)).
- **Build in your domain** ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=450s)).
- **Six-step playbook**: list 3 to 5 domains you know, find niches with paid tools, mine pain points, build the highest-ROI feature, pitch 3 distribution partners with a demo, and ship one improvement a day for 30 days ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=529s), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=590s)).
- **Ship to 10 people** this weekend and iterate on their feedback ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=753s)).
