Paulius Masalskas is a non-technical solo founder who built the influencer database Creator Hunter with AI tools.

## Who he is

- He did door-to-door sales at 18, then web design, copywriting and sales funnels. He studied game development but "completely sucked at code" ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=85s)).
- He quit his job on 24 December 2024 to work on Creator Hunter full time ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [11:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=715s)).

## Businesses

- [Creator Hunter](../businesses/creator-hunter.md), a [SaaS](../topics/saas.md) database that matches startups with influencers. He built it on his daily commute with Perplexity, Bolt and Cursor ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=223s)).
- Over 1,000 users, over 350 paid users and $30K in total revenue about 7 months after launch (2025-05) ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=59s)).
- Margins about 90%, with Vercel and Supabase on free tiers (2025-05) ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=657s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Bolt for the MVP, Cursor for production.** Bolt gets you about 80% of the way ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=286s)). See [vibe coding](../topics/vibe-coding.md).
- **Design from a free Framer template,** and spend 80% of design time above the fold ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=440s), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=475s)). See [landing pages](../topics/landing-pages.md).
- **Join the bigger conversation.** His top post got close to 500K impressions on X. It tapped the debate over whether AI coding can build a full SaaS ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=554s)). See [build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md).
- **Borrow formats, keep an edge.** He adapted a post format from [Marc Lou](marc-lou.md). Exact clones do not take off ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=607s)).
- **Use domain knowledge.** People who know the problems in their field can "prompt their way" to a product ([source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=685s)).
