Vadim Strizheus is a 19-year-old solo founder with no coding background who built the AI video clipping tool Vugola.

## Who he is

- He worked at a marketing startup from age 17. He quit to build with no coding skills, to force himself to commit ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=179s)).

## Businesses

- [Vugola](../businesses/vugola.md), an AI video editing agent that turns long videos into short clips. About $11.2K MRR, tens of thousands of signups and about 400 paying users (2026-10) ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=91s)).
- He had the idea in October, built for 4 months and started marketing in March. He prompted [Lovable](../businesses/lovable.md) for 4 months, then moved to Claude and Codex with GitHub and Supabase ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=259s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=312s)).
- Twitter alone took the app to $2K to $3K MRR. An Instagram UGC account then took it past $10K. That account had 10.8K followers in a few months, and its top video got 132K views (2026-10) ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=478s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Enter a proven market.** A small share of the clipping market is enough ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=208s)).
- **Ride viral AI topics on X.** He built a [personal brand](../topics/personal-brand.md) and tied Vugola to trending tools such as an OpenClaw plugin ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s)).
- **Copy proven UGC formats exactly.** Copy the text, audio, transitions, hook length, demo and CTA. Post 3 to 6 times a day and change only the hook and the sound ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=418s), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=478s)). See [UGC](../topics/ugc.md).
- **Spend time, not money.** His only paid growth tool is ManyChat at $15/month. He batches content in 30 to 45 minutes a day ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=554s)).
- **Simplify everything.** "As a solo founder in the age of AI, your biggest asset is your time" ([source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=657s)).
