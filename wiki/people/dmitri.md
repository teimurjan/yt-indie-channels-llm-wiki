Dmitri built and sold YourMove AI, an AI dating assistant, as a side project while he was a data science manager at Opendoor.

## Who he is

- A data science manager at Opendoor who built YourMove AI on the side. He quit at $17K to $18K/month, the same time as Opendoor's November layoff gave him severance ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [51:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3065s), [53:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3183s)).
- His first dollar came about 4 years after he started trying. After the sale he joined a16z Speedrun with a new venture-backed company ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [34:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2071s), [50:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3003s)).

## Businesses

- [YourMove AI](../businesses/yourmove-ai.md): writes replies, writes and reviews dating profiles, and makes AI photos. It started in 2022 as a party joke on OpenAI's Davinci model ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=189s)).
  - Peaked at about $30K/month with about 80% margin. It made $28K/month when he stopped work on it. Sold 4 to 5 months before the interview, price undisclosed (2026-01) ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=420s)).
  - 6 months before charging, about a year to $1K/month, a year to $10K/month, a year more to $28K/month ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=448s)).
  - Press (Fox, CNBC, Wired, WSJ and others) gave domain authority for an SEO machine that drove nearly half of traffic ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=510s), [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1105s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Charge early.** Waiting 6 months was a mistake ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=392s)).
- **Turn PR into SEO.** "The PR itself didn't generate any money but allowed us a really good SEO strategy." ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=66s)).
- **Capture demand versus create it.** Google and SEO capture demand but are capped by search volume. Meta and TikTok create demand with more upside ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [20:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1206s)).
- **Give each channel a real test.** SEO needs about 3 months of focused work before any signal ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [24:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1450s)).
- **Sell painkillers.** Dating has a large value gap. A 10% better calendar does not ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [26:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1599s), [29:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1795s)).
- **Solve problems that big apps create.** Tools that help people use a bigger app suit bootstrappers ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [31:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1891s)).
- **Hire coaches, not only doers.** For each new channel he hired a contractor who did the work and trained him. He had 10 to 12 Upwork contractors at one point ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [43:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2594s)).
- **Use the day job as funding.** Contractors at $50/hour beat his own $150/hour time ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [51:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3114s)).
