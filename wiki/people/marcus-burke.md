Marcus Burke is a freelance Meta ads consultant for subscription apps.

## Who

- He has run Meta ads for 14 years. He scaled one app to $2M per month, and his best ad passed $1M in spend (2026-06) ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=0s)).
- Career: InnoGames (mobile games, 2012 to 2019), [Blinkist](../businesses/blinkist.md) from 2019 for about 1.5 years, then StudySmarter and Tandem. He went freelance in 2022 ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:02:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=140s), [0:05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=323s)).
- At Blinkist, his team was among the first to scale ads that sent users to a blog instead of the App Store ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:05:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=323s)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- Client work: the Moonate green-noise ad concept had about 400 iterations and well over $1M in lifetime spend ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:09:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=548s), [0:10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=634s)).
- His freelancers make 99% of client ads with AI (2026-06) ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1300s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Fix the funnel first.** He also works on [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md), [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md) and activation, because a purchase event alone is no longer enough ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:06:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=380s)).
- **Tell viewers it is an app.** Say "app" or show a demo, or the App Store jump hurts install rate ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=491s)).
- **Use the audience's words.** Feed 10 to 20 high-engagement Reddit threads to Claude and ask for sub-audiences, pains and vivid language. Hyper-specific copy lets Meta find the right people with broad targeting ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=733s), [0:24:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1465s)).
- **Five-step [ad creative](../topics/ad-creative.md) process:** copy research, visual research, copywriting, generative AI, editing. Make the scene with an image model first, then animate it, to save credits ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:25:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1524s), [0:34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2080s)).
- **AI is for control, not cost.** It makes creative variables testable ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1345s)).
- **Watch age mix.** Users aged 18 to 24 rarely pay about $70/year. Ask age in onboarding, keep broad targeting, and use value rules that tell Meta users 45+ are worth 30 to 40% more ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:40:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2452s), [0:43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2622s)).
- **Structure ad sets as buckets.** Group by media type, format and message, with budgets per ad set. Pull outlier ads into their own ad sets, since Meta spends about 80% on one ad. With small budgets, use one ad set ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:47:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2849s), [0:50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3015s), [0:54:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3269s)).
- "A trial isn't a subscription." Trial-to-paid comes about 7 days too late for Meta to learn from ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2574s), [0:45:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2733s)).
