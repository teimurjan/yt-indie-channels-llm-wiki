Gil Hildebrand is a repeat founder who built [Subscribr](../businesses/subscribr.md), a bootstrapped AI scriptwriting tool for YouTube creators.

## Who

- He started coding at 15, dropped out of college and freelanced ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=113s)).
- After Hurricane Katrina he moved to New York and co-founded Squidoo, which grew past $10M revenue and was acquired. He later raised millions for a crypto accounting startup and sold it ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=113s)).
- With Subscribr he chose to bootstrap instead of raising venture money ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md)).

## Businesses

- **[Subscribr](../businesses/subscribr.md).**
  - Pre-sold 50 lifetime licenses for $20,000 in 2 to 3 days, before he built the product ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
  - About $30K/month, over $700K in sales in the past year and over 4,000 customers, 18 months after launch (2025-12) ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=75s)).
  - Plans cost $49 to $300/month (2025-12) ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=50s)).
  - Claude Code writes about 90% of the code. Costs are about $3,500 AI compute, $2,000 ads and $1,500 for scraping, hosting and email a month (2025-12) ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=796s)).
- **Squidoo** and a **crypto accounting startup**, both earlier and both sold ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=113s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Find the bottleneck in your own work.** He ran faceless YouTube channels and found that scripts were the hardest part ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=180s)).
- **Enter a new niche on X.** He opened a new account and made free tools and content. Giveaways grew his email list past 1,000 ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s)).
- **Money is the only validation.** "How much will you pay?" gives false comfort ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=505s)).
- **Tiered pre-sale.** The first 10 licenses were very cheap, and the price rose for each next 10 ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- **Refund until delivery.** He promised delivery in 60 days plus a 2-week trial, with a refund at any time ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- **Do the math.** He needed $20,000 for 3 months, so 50 buyers, which set the list size he needed ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=649s)).
- **Profit over growth.** Avoid expensive agencies and side directions ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=850s)).
