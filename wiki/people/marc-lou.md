Marc Lou is a French solo founder who ships many small products and is best known for ShipFast, a code boilerplate for launching startups.

## Who

- He went from a $10/hour waiter job to about $1.5M/year (2024-08) [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=184s).
- He spent about 5 years failing: a "Tinder for sports" app, a VC-funded AI startup in South Korea with zero customers, and an escape-room tool that fell from $4K MRR to $0 in 24 hours when COVID hit [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=246s), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=491s).
- At 28 he took a $9K/month engineering job and was later laid off [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=615s), [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s).
- He makes $77K/month as a solo founder. He shipped about 35 startups at about a 5% hit rate (2026-04) [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=1s), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=163s).
- His day is the same all year: gym, 4 to 6 hours of offline deep work, online work from about 4 p.m., bed at 9 p.m. [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=72s), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=475s).

## Businesses

- [ShipFast](../businesses/shipfast.md): a reusable boilerplate with landing page, payments, emails and accounts, built in under a week [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s).
  - $500 in its first 2 hours and about $40K in month one. It held near $50K/month, with $65K in a Black Friday month [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=981s).
  - Peaked at about $135K/month, then slowed to about $80K/month (2024-08) [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s).
  - Other founders build on it. Jack Friks used it for [Curiosity Quench](../businesses/curiosity-quench.md) [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [14:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=883s).
- TrustMRR: a leaderboard of verified startup revenue that became a marketplace, $35K+/month (2026-04) [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=267s).
- A macOS posture app that warns about slouching made about $1,000 on launch day (2026-04) [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=222s).
- Earlier small bets: 6 projects in 7 months, including Habits Garden (about 10,000 users), still at about $1K/month. Painkiller apps with paywalls reached about $4K/month by mid-2023 [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s), [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s).

## Repeated advice

- **Make many small bets.** Never spend months on a product, never raise money, never hire [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s). With a 5% hit rate, many launches beat one pet project [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=558s).
- **Ship with a buy button.** "The only way to validate an idea is to ship it with a buy button" [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s). Early on, a marketer told him to sell before building. A cold email led to a paying escape-room owner [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=369s).
- **[Build in public](../topics/build-in-public.md) as insurance.** If an app fails, the following stays for the next launch [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s).
- **Painkillers over vitamins.** He moved from fun apps to apps that solve a known pain and have a paywall [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s).
- **Make launches loud.** Comedy skits put him on a Joe Rogan podcast or next to Leonardo DiCaprio [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s). He posts each launch on X, LinkedIn, Threads and Reddit [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=222s). Paulius Masalskas adapted one of his post formats [source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md) [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=607s).
- **Free tools as ads.** A free logo generator carries a ShipFast banner [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [17:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1042s).
- **Protect deep work.** No phone, email or support tickets at the start of the day [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=72s).
- **Count shipped things.** He rejects multi-agent setups. The one KPI is the number of things you ship [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s).
- **Do not quit.** "Each failure is not a failure if you don't give up" [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s).
