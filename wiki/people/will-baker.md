Will Baker is a UK designer who clones proven iOS app categories with better design, grows them with AI-made TikTok ads, and sells them.

## Who

- He tried dropshipping and selling likes at 16, then NFT projects after university. Freelance web design funded his startup tests ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=126s)).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Glowly](../businesses/glowly.md), a looksmaxing face-scan app: about $10K/month (2026-08) ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s)).
- [Overlow](../businesses/overlow.md), a fitness app with a body scan: about $800/month passive (2026-08) ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=92s)).

## Repeated advice

- Pick paid ads over UGC armies. Ads give clear numbers for little ongoing work ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s)). A TikTok subscription campaign costs about $16 per subscription against a $30 to $35 LTV (2026-08) ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s)).
- Make 10 to 20 AI variants of one format and test them like a lottery. Show the app only after the first 3 to 4 seconds. Raise spend on a winner 20% every 3 days ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s), [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s)). See [ad creative](../topics/ad-creative.md).
- Build only apps with a viral feature that explains itself in one screenshot ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=380s)).
- Personalize before the paywall with a 12 to 15 step onboarding ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=405s)).
- Build to sell to an operator who can scale with UGC, then repeat ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=220s)).
- A personal brand is a moat AI cannot copy ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=520s)).
> Conflict: Pat Walls says ads test creative, not demand ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=669s)). Will avoids the issue by picking categories with known demand ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=714s)).
