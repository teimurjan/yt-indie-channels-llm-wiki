Paul Millerd is a former strategy consultant who quit a $250K job. He self-published *The Pathless Path*, which now earns most of his income.

## Who he is

- He quit a New York consulting job in 2017 at age 32, with no plan. His employer made him repay a $24,000 signing bonus and relocation costs. He had $50K to $60K in savings ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=0s), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=149s), [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=174s)).
- He turned down Penguin Random House to keep creative control and higher royalties ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md)).

## Businesses

- [The Pathless Path](../businesses/the-pathless-path.md), a self-published book.
  - Year one: about 10,000 copies and about $50,000 in royalties. First five months of year two: about 15,000 copies and about $75,000 (2023-12) ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)).
  - Royalties to date of $125K to $130K, almost all profit, on about $6K of costs (2023-12) ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=577s)).
  - Amazon royalty per unit: $8.24 hardcover, $7.45 paperback, $4.28 Kindle, $3.98 audiobook. Sales mix: 50% ebook, 29% print, 21% audio (2023-12) ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=551s)).
- Other income: a course (Strategy U), workshops at a fixed price and minimal coaching ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=604s)).
- Total top-line revenue grew from $100K to $249K in one year (2023-12) ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Write daily before you quit.** He wrote on Quora every day for 100 days. Then his blog went from every 3 months to weekly ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=149s)).
- **Self-publish for control and margin.** A traditional publisher pays about $2 a hardcover. Self-published, he makes about $7 to $8 a print book ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=240s)).
- **Spend on a developmental editor.** Get the cover on 99designs, format with Reedsy or Amazon's tools, and proofread at the end ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=480s)).
- **Market through what you enjoy.** Podcasts, and gifted books at $4.50 each including US shipping ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=540s)).
- **Take a 1 to 3 month sabbatical** and drop work that does not light you up ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=420s)).
- **Get reps to beat fear.** Early on nobody reads, so nobody judges ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=654s)).
