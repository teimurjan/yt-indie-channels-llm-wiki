Rafael Kramer is a teenage founder who started with no-code meme games at 13 and now builds consumer apps.

## Who

- He has 50+ apps, about 100M social views and three exits (2026-02) ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md)).
- He built games in Buildbox, a no-code builder, and later hired Unity developers ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=507s)).
- Interviewed by [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md).

## Businesses

- [DaBaby game](../businesses/dababy-game.md): about 1.5M to 2M downloads and more than $100K in one month from AdMob, at age 14 ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=378s)).
- An influencer game company started at 15 and sold at 17 for at least six figures. Its [Skibidi Toilet game](../businesses/escape-the-toilet.md) got 2M to 3M downloads in about 2 weeks ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=218s)).
- [Debloat AI](../businesses/debloat-ai.md): about 40,000 downloads, grown with [TikTok ads](../topics/tiktok-ads.md), then sold ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s)).
- Amanda AI, an AI relationship coach, sold as a small exit after TikTok restricted its ads ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [26:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1618s)).

## Repeated advice

- Find a meme early and make it playable, so people can be the meme and share it ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=465s)).
- Post dev logs and A/B test hooks ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [10:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=659s)).
- Read comment sections as demand signals ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [17:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1061s)).
- Run the scan before the [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) to create sunk cost ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1498s)).
- Pick [influencers](../topics/influencer-marketing.md) by audience fit, not size, and give them equity, such as 50/50 splits ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [33:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1992s), [39:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2389s)).
- Look outside your age group for under-served niches ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [25:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1548s)).
