Eyal Hazan is a college student who co-founded Prop GPT, a sports betting analytics app, with Yahli Hazan.

## Who he is

- A college student. He builds with [Yahli Hazan](yahli-hazan.md) ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md)).

## Businesses

- [Prop GPT](../businesses/prop-gpt.md): a machine learning model scores sports betting picks.
  - The first version took about 5 months and launched mid NFL season (2024) ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=120s)).
  - Before the rebuild: 45% install to trial, 13% trial to paid ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s)).
  - Relaunch on 2025-04-15 at $1,700 MRR. Trial-to-paid then rose above 50%, and MRR peaked at $40K ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
  - About $30K/month, over 40,000 downloads and over 3,000 paying users (2025-11) ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [1:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=103s)).
  - Marketing costs about $10,000/month. Margin is about 50% (2025-11) ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=522s)).

## Repeated advice

- **Read the funnel gap.** High install-to-trial with low trial-to-paid means the product does not deliver its promise ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=337s)).
- **Give answers, not tools.** Users wanted picks handed to them, not a tool to check their own bets ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- **Pause marketing to fix the product.** They spent 4 months on the rebuild only ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s)).
- **Market the most-clicked feature.** Track feature clicks in PostHog and use the top feature as the value claim ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s)).
- **Scale with influencers.** About their 70th video got 600,000 views and took ARR from $8K to $38K in 3 days ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=436s)).
- **Launch with the sports calendar.** Both launches matched big seasons ([rCV-TVCnF6Q](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=120s)).
