Iuliia Shnai is the co-founder of Papermark, an open-source DocSend alternative she runs with her husband Marc Seitz.

## Who she is

- She did not know how to code at the start. She built her first project on top of existing open-source code [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [12:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=779s).

## Businesses

- [Papermark](../businesses/papermark.md), with [Marc Seitz](marc-seitz.md): $75K MRR, close to $900K ARR, after about 1.5 years (2025-08) [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [0:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=18s).
- $20K MRR after year one. About 30K users, about 1,000 paying customers and 7K GitHub stars (2025-08) [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=608s).

## Repeated advice

- **Open core.** Free self-hosting, a paid hosted version and paid enterprise licenses [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=267s).
- **Pick a slow incumbent** and out-ship it until customers switch [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=126s), [12:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=735s).
- **Build in public**, even small unfinished progress, on Twitter and LinkedIn [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=546s).
