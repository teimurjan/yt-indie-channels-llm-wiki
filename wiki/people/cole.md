Cole is a Roblox game developer in Austin, Texas, and one of the top 50 Roblox developers.

## Who

- He started playing Roblox in 2013 and moved to Austin at 18 instead of college. His games reach about 1M daily active users, mostly US and Canadian kids (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=93s).
- He lost about $500K over 3 years on two failed projects [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=676s).
- His twin brother [Ian](ian.md) made [Bathe the Baby](../businesses/bathe-the-baby.md). Their friend [Jake](jake.md) runs [Vector 3](../businesses/vector-3.md), a Roblox marketing agency [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=149s), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=195s).

## Businesses

- [Hide or Die](../businesses/hide-or-die.md): launched about 6 months before the video, about $45K/month (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).
  - With revenue shares from helping other games, he expected about $70K that month, split 50/50 with his programmer partner. 1M Robux pays out about $3.5K (2025-04) [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=676s).
  - He spends about $5K/month on modelers and graphics contractors and runs no ads [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s).
- [Roblox](../businesses/roblox.md) is the platform: about 400M monthly active users. "Roblox is basically just YouTube for video games" [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=312s), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=245s).

## Repeated advice

- **Test first, then build.** After a 3-year, $300K game failed, he and his programmer made Hide or Die in 2 weeks [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [12:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=720s).
- **Clickable, social, replayable.** A dramatic title gets the click, friends play together, and levels bring players back. Expect a few attempts before a hit [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=753s), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=797s).
- **Work or rest, nothing between.** 4 to 5 hours of work with phones in another room, then stop at sunset [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1090s).
- **Build skills before a business.** Get a bit better than average, freelance, then scale what you like [source](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md) [17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=1049s).
