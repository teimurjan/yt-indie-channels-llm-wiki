Denis Yurchak is a solo founder who built Yadaphone, a web dialer for cheap international calls, after the Skype shutdown news.

## Who

- He learned to code in 2 months from books and courses. He had about 60 Twitter followers at launch [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=198s), [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=391s).
- Interviewed by [Pat Walls](pat-walls.md).

## Businesses

- [Yadaphone](../businesses/yadaphone.md): $14K/month and 10,000 registered users, 7 months after launch (2025-10) [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=58s).
  - Revenue: $4,000 in month one, $10,800 by month three, $13,300 in month six [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=80s).
  - 4,500 paying users, 20 enterprise clients and 27,000 calls in September 2025 [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=80s).
  - Pay-as-you-go credits instead of a $30/month subscription. Twilio costs about 35% of revenue (2025-10) [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s), [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=723s).

## Repeated advice

- **Ride a shutdown.** The Skype news made posts about alternatives go viral [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=249s).
- **Pick markets with dinosaur competitors.** Telecom players are 20 to 30 years old and slow. Better design wins in a validated market [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=520s).
- **Launch on Reddit with screenshots and a story** of a lone engineer against a giant [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=391s), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s).
- **Take the departing giant's SEO.** He asked authors of "Skype alternative" articles to add Yadaphone [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s).
- **Text every paying customer** for feedback. He did this for 6 months [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=644s).
- **Add B2B early.** Enterprise clients pay more and churn less [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=644s).
- **Automate support to stay solo** [source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) [12:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=723s).
