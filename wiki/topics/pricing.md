This page collects how founders set, structure and test prices for apps, SaaS, digital products and services.

## Definition

Pricing is what a product charges and how: the amount, the billing period (weekly, monthly, yearly, lifetime), the unit (seat, credit, outcome) and how plans are shown on the paywall.

## Repeated patterns

### Structure before price

- **Test packaging first.** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) argues "structure before price": which plans show, which comes first, and how the trial works. [Mojo](../businesses/mojo.md) hid AI credit packs on its first paywall and new subscription revenue rose 14% (2026-01) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)). Plan-count tests give 63% more conversion uplift than any price test ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s)). [Jonathan Parra](../people/jonathan-parra.md) tests design and packaging before price, because price tests need cohort management ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2171s)).
- **Fewer plans, a clear default.** Show 2 plans and hide the rest behind "View all plans" ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=641s)). [Built With Science](../businesses/built-with-science.md) put annual first and annual picks went from about 60% to 75%-80% at the same price ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).
- **Give the best plan a perk.** [Moonly](../businesses/moonly.md)'s trial on annual only gave +47% revenue per 100 installs; [Headspace](../businesses/headspace.md) gave annual a longer trial (2025-07) ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s)).
- **Anchors and decoys.** [Floga](../businesses/floga.md)'s two lower lifetime tiers anchor the top tier ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=777s)). An undiscounted weekly or monthly plan anchors a discounted annual ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s)). $3.33/month ($39.96/year) feels like $3 ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1866s)).
- **Be explicit and specific.** [Uber](../businesses/uber.md)'s single estimate beat a price range ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=525s)). A line saying when users will be charged raised [Blinkist](../businesses/blinkist.md)'s conversion 23% and cut complaints 55% ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=462s)).

### Raising prices

- **Small rises compound.** [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md) went from $4 to $5/month and added nearly $5K MRR (2026-06) ([source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)). [Glam Up](../businesses/glam-up.md) went from $3 to $9 a week and conversion rose ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1368s)). A price rise cut [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md)'s volume a little but raised revenue ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s)).
- **Start low, then raise.** [Praneeth](../people/praneeth.md) went from $29 to $49 ([source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=416s)). [Brett Williams](../people/brett-williams.md) took [DesignJoy](../businesses/designjoy.md) from $449 to $5,000/month ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=127s)). [Elephas](../businesses/elephas.md) sells monthly, annual and lifetime plans with gradual rises ([source](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=97s)). [NotionForms](../businesses/notionforms.md) started below every form builder and raised with no visible drop ([source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=706s)).
- **Find the ceiling.** Below $10/month, test higher prices on 10% to 20% of traffic ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1861s)). ChatGPT reset the consumer anchor from about $60/year to $20/month ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [24:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1487s)). [Puff Count](../businesses/puff-count.md) tested $4 to $12 and kept the price with the highest LTV ([source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=617s)).

### How to set the number

- **From cost, competitors and ROAS.** Pick a price that keeps ads ROAS-positive or pays back in about a month ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [37:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2240s)). [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md) starts from cost, then checks rivals ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=462s)). AI wrappers often charge $30 to $40/year (2026-02) ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s)).
- **By audience.** [3AK](../businesses/3ak.md) charges $30/year because teens spend $10 to $20 at a time ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)). [NGL](../businesses/ngl.md) prices weekly plans from $1 to $7 by country GDP per capita (2025-07) ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=370s)). In [Harpagia](../businesses/harpagia.md), conversion drops above $9.99 ([source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=151s)).
- **Let buyers name it.** [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md) let his first ghostwriting client set the price: he hoped for $250 and got $5,000 ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s)). [Matt Giovanisci](../people/matt-giovanisci.md) stepped the price down until people bought ([source](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)).
- **Price sets positioning.** [Pauline](../people/pauline.md) of [AI Crea](../businesses/ai-crea.md) says a low price brings a low-cost image and more support work. She offers both a subscription and a one-time pack ([source](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=437s), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s)).
- **If only insiders buy, lower it.** [Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md) at $5,000/month sold only to existing clients; at $800 it sells ([source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=102s)).

### Units and models

- **Usage and credits.** [Scrape Creators](../businesses/scrape-creators.md) sells credit packs from $10 to $500 ([source](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=544s)). [Parakeet AI](../businesses/parakeet-ai.md) advertises "no subscription, one-time payment" ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=756s)). [Once](../businesses/once.md) charges by guest count ([source](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=87s)). [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md) caps usage to protect margin ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=408s)).
- **Shared credits for teams.** [Yadaphone](../businesses/yadaphone.md) sells pay-as-you-go credits that a whole business shares, against rivals' $30/month subscriptions or per-seat fees ([source](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s)).
- **Seats and clients.** [Hiro Analytics](../businesses/hiro-analytics.md) scales with client count (about $500/month for 10) plus about $10 per seat ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=149s), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s)). [Magai](../businesses/magai.md): $20 solo, $40 team, $20 per extra seat ([source](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)).
- **Outcomes.** Intercom's [Fin](../businesses/fin.md) charges per resolved ticket ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=303s)).
- **Credits hide the total.** [ReelShort](../businesses/reelshort.md)'s coins and variable episode prices stop users from adding up about $40 per show; Tim Gabe's ethical version shows a running total ([source](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=257s), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=380s)).
- **Productized fixed fees.** [Scott Millard](../people/scott-millard.md) sells $6K to $12K packages; average order is about $3K (2023-03) ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=677s)). [DesignJoy's flat unlimited plan works out to $300 to $5,000 an hour depending on the client ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s)). [Draft.dev](../businesses/draft-dev.md) gives no discounts and asks for quarterly commitments ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=427s)).

### Free plans and trials

- **Cutting the free plan.** [Sync2Sheets](../businesses/sync2sheets.md) went from about $5K to $8K/month after it removed its free plan ([source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s)). [ScreenshotOne](../businesses/screenshotone.md) raised prices and cut the free plan because $5 to $7 plans had near-zero margin ([source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)).
- **Trial copy.** [Claim](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md)'s paywall says "free" 5 to 7 times and "Try for $0"; do not show "3-day trial" on the plan card ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=645s), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s)). Trial reminders raised [Sunflower](../businesses/sunflower.md)'s revenue 48% ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [46:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2785s)).
- **Discounts on exit.** An exit drawer restates the annual price per week, then a 25% to 33% one-time offer ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s)). Discount only the annual plan ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s)). Sprout shows 50% off when the paywall closes ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [24:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1496s)).

### Lifetime deals

- [Vault Air](../businesses/vault-air.md): about 60% of purchases are lifetime and bring over 80% of revenue at $39.99 (2026-10) ([source](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=236s)). [Savewise](../businesses/savewise.md): about 97% of revenue from lifetime (2025-08) ([source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=678s)).
- [SuperGrow](../businesses/supergrow.md) made $65K in 3 days with $79 to $299 lifetime tiers on RocketHub ([source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=288s)). Pat Walls prices a lifetime deal near 24 months of subscription revenue ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1046s)). See [lifetime deals](lifetime-deals.md).

## Disagreements

- **Lead with weekly or annual?** Tim Gabe says weekly converts 1.7 to 7.4x better than annual and grew to 55.6% of subscription revenue (2026-05) ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=83s), [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=130s)). His Built With Science case leads with annual, and Jonathan Parra preselects annual for its LTV ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s), [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=862s)). [Josh](../people/josh.md) says weekly plans rarely make a sticky acquisition target ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [33:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2010s)).
> Conflict: [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) says lead with weekly. [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) and [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) lead with annual.
- **Raise or cut the price?** Glam Up raised from $3 to $9 a week and conversion rose ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1368s)). Sunflower's users were price-sensitive, so lowering the price made more money ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s)). [Youform](../businesses/youform.md) and Liinks undercut the leader on purpose ([source](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=68s), [source](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)).
> Conflict: [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) raised prices and won. [zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) lowered prices and won.
- **Trial or no trial?** A free trial with nothing up front made 140% more revenue than a discounted intro price at [Pregnancy+](../businesses/pregnancy-plus.md) ([source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)). A weekly plan's 12-month LTV is $54.50 with a trial and $7.40 without ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s)). ListKit uses a money-back guarantee instead, because trials draw "freebie seekers" ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=619s)). [Timo Nikolai](../people/timo-nikolai.md) uses no trial because ads need cash back fast ([source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s)).
> Conflict: [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) and [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) favor trials. [WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md) and [DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) drop them.
- **Lifetime or subscription?** Lifetime deals brought cash fast for SuperGrow and Floga, whose founder says buyers pay 3 to 5 times a yearly plan ([source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=606s), [source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s)). [Julien Nahum](../people/julien-nahum.md) prefers subscriptions: $50/month is easier to sell than $500 once ([source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=743s)). Castmagic's lifetime deal cost it recurring revenue from those users ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)).
> Conflict: [BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) favors lifetime deals. [WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) favors subscriptions.
