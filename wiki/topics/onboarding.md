Onboarding is the flow between install and regular use: the first screens, questions, permissions and, in most subscription apps here, the paywall. Sources split into two schools: short flows that reach value fast, and long quiz flows that sell before the paywall.

## Definition

Onboarding takes a new user from download to the first moment of value, or to the purchase. With a paywall, about 90% of users only ever see the onboarding, so founders treat it as the main sales page ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s)).

## Repeated patterns

### School 1: short, value first

- Apps lose about 77% of users in 3 days. Users who feel core value in the first 60 seconds stay more ([55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=0s), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=50s)).
- Let users taste the product in onboarding: Breathwrk runs a real breathing session, a Sudoku app gives a guided first puzzle ([55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=113s), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=254s)).
- [Tinder](../businesses/tinder.md): Facebook login pulled photos, so users swiped in under a minute ([4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yKtlOWClhA&t=306s)).
- Granola has two screens (Google sign-in, mic access). The next step is your next meeting ([Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=85s)).
- [NGL](../businesses/ngl.md)-style apps onboard in 15 to 20 seconds and sell the result ("get your own messages") ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=615s)).
- [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md) lets users upload a document from the landing page. A 7-question review hides all settings ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=885s)).
- Remove the 1 to 3 biggest frictions between sign-up and first success ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=254s)). Give new users one clear action, as Apple Fitness does ([D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=187s)).
- [Raycast](../businesses/raycast.md) had no formal onboarding early. It teaches itself through use, so "onboarding never ends" ([eWGNjTLK84c](../sources/eWGNjTLK84c.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWGNjTLK84c&t=223s), [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWGNjTLK84c&t=434s)).

### School 2: long quiz, then paywall

- The common formula: educate, personalize with questions, create FOMO, let the user reach the "gotcha" moment, hide the result behind the paywall. Length adds sunk cost ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=559s)).
- Onboarding as a sales funnel: short videos on the problem, easy multiple-choice questions, the dream result ([ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [15:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=938s), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1297s)).
- Hormozi's value equation: dream outcome, pain, failed past methods with cited research, social proof between screens, micro-commitments, trust before permissions ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:42:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2543s), [0:46:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2761s)).
- A generated plan before the paywall: [Gravl](../businesses/gravl.md) asks goals, one-rep maxes and equipment, shows "generating your plan", then a summary and a hard paywall ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=576s)). [Savvy Nomad](../businesses/savvy-nomad.md) shows estimated savings ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=713s)). [Glowly](../businesses/glowly.md) ends 12 to 15 steps with a personal insight ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=405s)).
- Effort makes a result feel earned: "the more users invest before the payoff, the more they value it" ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=243s)).
- "Happy friction": a long survey that reflects answers back. Direct web visitors get a shorter path ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [25:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1537s)).
- Pitch inside the flow: "fun fact" pages compare the app with rivals ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [17:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1047s)). Sell the outcome and add lots of social proof for ad traffic ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s)).

### When long works (the reconciliation)

- Default to short. Go long only if it buys personalization (Cal AI's home screen) or filters for buyers in deep-pain niches ([Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=188s), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=239s)).
- Each quiz answer must change what the app shows. Personal questions with a generic result lose trust ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=400s)).
- Segment by user: [Pep AI](../businesses/pep-ai.md) gives under-25s a short flow and older users a long one with a nutrition plan ([mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1716s)).
- Focus users on the problem, not a feature tour. [Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md) centers stress. One screen asks about tiredness after a low-activity day to bring the feeling back ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=367s), [tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [30:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1838s)).
- Keep one promise from ad to store page to onboarding to paywall ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=462s)).

### Screen-level tactics

- Show progress ("step 2 of 5"), but hide long step counts ([55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=374s), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=430s)).
- Split wordy screens into story taps. [Bloom](../businesses/bloom.md) turned one screen into ~5 and removed "get started" ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [24:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1488s)). Duolingo-style screens with about five words, tap answers instead of free text ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [12:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=758s)).
- Order: urgency, features, trust. Bloom opens with a compound-growth chart and ends with press logos and SIPC ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [19:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1145s)).
- Answer each question with social proof ("81% of users improve mental clarity") ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=782s)).
- Rating prompt after a free personalized result: [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) reviews grew from ~50 to almost 1,000 ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [20:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1225s), [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s)). [GlowUp](../businesses/glowup.md) does the same ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=561s)).
- A commitment screen at the end raised [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md) retention by 3% ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)).
- The paywall at the end is often several screens too. A well-made multi-step paywall beats a single screen by 20% to 40% ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [14:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=884s)).
- Scan before the paywall to create sunk cost, as [Debloat AI](../businesses/debloat-ai.md) did with its face scan ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [24:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1498s)).
- Personalize setup like a car seat (Speechify) ([55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=314s)).
- Move needed friction to where users are motivated: [Undertone](../businesses/undertone.md) moved its mic test into onboarding ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=562s)). Setup-heavy apps (share menus, Safari extensions) need 4 to 6 steps after the sale ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1484s)).
- Emotion at the front builds trust in high-stakes apps ([AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=727s), [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=589s)). Design welcome screens to look good on social media ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=357s)).

### How founders build and test it

- Copy proven flows: screenshot ~20 apps' onboarding into Figma and restyle the best ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s)), or into Canva ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=294s)). [Stoppr](../businesses/stoppr.md) copied Quittr's flow exactly and swapped the niche ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=237s)). About 90% of one founder's learning came from going through other apps' onboarding ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [53:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=3202s)).
- Track from the first screen. Clear 30 could not see early drop-off until it did ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=578s)). Instrument every screen, then ask why in interviews ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=593s)).
- Make one big jump, not one-screen tweaks ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [28:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1714s)). Target at least 10% download-to-trial ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s)).
- A high completion rate can mislead: urgent users finish long flows anyway. About half of Undertone users who finished a 10+ minute flow never recorded a session ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=56s), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=95s)).
- Sell the outcome: [Runafy](../businesses/runafy.md) converted better once onboarding said "become a better runner", not "fun ranks" ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [33:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2027s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: short or long. [55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md) and [DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md) push value within seconds. [E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md) and [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) found longer flows convert better. [Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) reconciles: long only when it buys personalization or filters buyers.

> Conflict: hard paywall at the end of onboarding. [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) (+50%), [2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md) and [DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) use one. [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) says a hard paywall signals a weak product. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) removed Celebs' onboarding paywall because later placements converted, and [qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) makes it skippable.

> Conflict: value before or after the paywall. [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) puts the aha moment after the paywall, and [8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md) hides the result behind it. [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) shows the paywall after the user felt value, and [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) lets users try the core feature first.

> Conflict: what completion means. [EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md) treats a ~91% paywall show rate after a long flow as success. [YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md) warns that high completion measures need, not later use.
