This page covers mobile app design: the interface, flows and feel of iOS and Android apps, and how design choices drive conversion and retention.

## Definition

Mobile app design shapes an app's screens, flows, motion and feedback. The goal is that users reach value fast and want to come back. Most sources here come from [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md), whose agency [ZipSnap](../businesses/zipsnap.md) designs consumer apps.

## Repeated patterns

- **Design the experience, not the frame.** AI will flood the stores with apps. Treat the UI like a movie and join screens into one flow. [86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=830s). Review the app as one product. Use it for an hour like a real user and note each place where it stops feeling like one product. [8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=250s)
- **Design the states AI leaves out.** Show what the system is doing, let users undo, and help them recover. Turn off Wi-Fi mid-flow, fail a payment and empty every list. Then design those states as carefully as success screens. [8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=97s), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=512s). A neobank card client got 15 status screens, such as processing, on hold and declined. Money loaded onto cards rose 79% and successful deposits rose 71% (2026-10). [0GMQRCnOQmo](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md) [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s). Settle states and edge cases before visuals. [wmTkiF23GRQ](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=325s)
- **One clear action, close to the thumb.** More choices mean slower decisions (Hick's law). Apple Fitness gives a new user one action. Waze puts large buttons where a thumb reaches (Fitts's law). [D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md) [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=187s), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=245s). Keep key actions within about three taps. [doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md) [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=569s). Map every decision point in the core flow. Each tap or confirm prompt is an exit ramp. ReelShort plays content at once, while Netflix has at least 6 decision points first. [7dCZrtH15zc](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md) [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=476s), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=599s)
- **Feedback and motion confirm actions.** Use physics-based bounce, haptics and sound to make software feel real. [doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md) [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=47s), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=378s). Apps with repeat actions gain from a small bounce, glow or sparkle after each one. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s). Design the waiting moments, as Perplexity does with its live loader. [D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=123s). See [Micro-interactions](micro-interactions.md).
- **Turn the win into a gift moment.** Every app delivers something: a match, a report, a score. A plain "Congratulations" modal wastes it. [uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=62s). Tinder's full-screen match celebration was screenshotted and shared, which gave free marketing. [4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md) [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yKtlOWClhA&t=506s). Design the peak and the end of the core flow. [11h4H3TAGkM](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=264s), [YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md) [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s)
- **Put personality in the core screen.** A mascot on a loading screen is decoration. Carrot Weather made the forecast itself a character. In Forest, the focus session is a tree. If you leave the app, the tree dies. [MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=91s), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=257s). Cat on Chair beat a crowded Pomodoro category with a cat theme and game-style customization. [V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=505s)
- **Rebuild around the niche or the new tech.** Do not reskin the mass-market UI. Tiimo uses colored blocks sized by duration. Cal AI made the camera the first action. [HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md) [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=115s), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=313s). Bet against the assumption that the category leader makes money from. [zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md) [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)
- **Visual basics.** Use the 60-30-10 color rule and save red for meaning. Cut font sizes and weights, for example from 6 sizes and 4 weights to 4 and 2. Use an 8-point grid. [86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [2:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=122s), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=181s), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=240s). Keep key actions in the same place and break a pattern only on purpose. [doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md) [12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=722s). See [Color](color.md), [Typography](typography.md) and [Design systems](design-systems.md).
- **Taste needs direction.** Score an app on point of view, consistency and execution. Better AI models raise the floor, but human art direction raises the ceiling. [YS7uOXjmJTA](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=0s), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=207s)
- **Prototype motion in Figma.** Smart Animate only tweens layers that exist in both frames, with the same names and nesting. Use "After delay" of 1 ms to chain steps, and tune a custom spring. [RqxI6aODugE](../sources/RqxI6aODugE.md) [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RqxI6aODugE&t=517s), [22:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RqxI6aODugE&t=1334s), [dL-U6bkm0a0](../sources/dL-U6bkm0a0.md) [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL-U6bkm0a0&t=240s), [s19_n6rcXIY](../sources/s19_n6rcXIY.md) [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19_n6rcXIY&t=322s). See [Prototyping](prototyping.md) and [Motion design](motion-design.md).

## Concrete examples with numbers

- **Error states pay.** A crypto card redesign with a clear declined-payment state grew new accounts by 60% and money loaded onto cards by 79% (2026-10). About a third of people leave a product after one bad experience. [8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=512s)
- **Value in the first minute.** When users feel the core value in the first 60 seconds, retention goes up a lot. Breathwrk puts users into a real breathing session during onboarding. [55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md) [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=50s)
- **Onboarding length.** Granola has two onboarding screens. Cal AI runs a quiz of over 20 steps, and Noom runs 113 screens. Keep it short by default. If the length buys personalization or filters for buyers, make it long. [Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=85s), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=143s), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=239s). See [Onboarding](onboarding.md).
- **Paywall design.** A simple "try for 50% off" paywall with a big product image beat a comparison-table paywall by 111%. A plain Apple-style SwiftUI paywall beat custom designs twice. Average paywall conversion is about 8%, and iteration often lifts it to 15% to 20% (2026-02). [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1564s), [46:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2800s), [54:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3254s). See [Paywalls](paywalls.md).
- **Animation and engagement.** Duolingo DAU grew from 14.2M to over 34M within 2 years of its animation rollout. Other changes also drove that growth. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=185s)
- **Rings close loops.** Apple Watch rings produced a 49.5% behavior change across 160,000 people. A 90% ring is an open loop the brain wants to close. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=556s)
- **Craft over commodity data.** Day One holds a 4.8 rating from over 116,000 reviews (2026-06). It adds weather, location, photos and a timeline around plain journal text. [MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md) [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=390s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=437s)
- **Themed indie app.** [Cat on Chair](../businesses/cat-on-chair.md) made over $18K in 30 days and had 127K downloads (2026-04). [V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=84s)
- **Design and price.** Senior app designers close deals of $20K to over $100K, because clients trust them to move business metrics (2025-04). [86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=615s)
- **Motion values.** A Figma splash screen uses a custom spring with stiffness 400 and damping 430. A Dynamic Island demo uses a 48 px island and about a 500 ms click transition. [s19_n6rcXIY](../sources/s19_n6rcXIY.md) [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s19_n6rcXIY&t=322s), [dL-U6bkm0a0](../sources/dL-U6bkm0a0.md) [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL-U6bkm0a0&t=240s)

## Process

ZipSnap runs six stages: onboarding, discovery, UX, UI, emotional integration and delivery. It pins emotional moments during UX. Before the handoff, it runs every screen through a QA list. [wmTkiF23GRQ](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md) [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=113s), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=387s), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=529s). Map the whole journey from sign-up to task completion first, and mark where stress peaks. [11h4H3TAGkM](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md) [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=448s)

## Disagreements

- **Custom design or native look.** Tim Gabe argues that care for every state and a distinct point of view set an app apart, since AI gives everyone the same floor. [8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=187s), [YS7uOXjmJTA](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md) [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=207s). On paywalls, a plain native SwiftUI design beat custom designs twice. [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [46:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2800s)

> Conflict: [YS7uOXjmJTA](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md) treats distinct custom art direction as the moat. [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) found that a plain native look converted better on the paywall.

- **Long or short onboarding.** One rule says to keep onboarding short and show value in the next natural action, as Granola does. [Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=85s). The same source says a long quiz can work, as in Cal AI. There, the quiz buys personalization and filters for buyers. [Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=188s)
