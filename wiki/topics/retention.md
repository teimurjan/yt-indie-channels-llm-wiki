This page covers retention: how apps and SaaS products keep users coming back and paying after the first visit.

## Definition

Retention is the share of users who return or keep paying over time. Churn is its opposite. The average app keeps 7% of users after 30 days, while the best apps in one study reach up to 90% annual retention (2026-04). [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=60s). Apps lose about 77% of users within 3 days on average. [Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=0s)

## Repeated patterns

- **Fix retention before you scale acquisition.** Do not go broad on acquisition with low retention, or 99% of new users leave. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=593s). Harpagia forced a launch at six months, then fixed retention before monetization. [axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=757s). Fitted held back celebrity promotion until retention was solid. [9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md) [24:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1493s). Retention comes before virality, because people share only what they feel attached to. [Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md) [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=606s)
- **Deliver core value fast.** When users feel the core value in the first 60 seconds, retention goes up a lot. [55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md) [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=50s). Remove the 1 to 3 biggest frictions between sign-up and first success. [hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md) [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=254s). Pick the metric that leads revenue. For a gym app, that is a logged workout. For a calorie app, it is one photo. [OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=453s)
- **Remove decision points in the core loop.** ReelShort holds users longer per day than the biggest streaming services. It plays content as soon as the app opens, and a swipe opens the next episode. [7dCZrtH15zc](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=0s), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=538s). Show fees before payment. A fee found at the end feels like a trick and kills the second deposit. [0GMQRCnOQmo](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md) [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=219s)
- **Find the feature people repeat daily.** [Flame](../businesses/flame.md) users came back for the daily question, not the AI coach, so it moved to the home screen. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) [0:10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=650s). Look in analytics for the one moment users return on their own. The trigger often sits outside the app. [YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md) [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=276s). Put the product where users already work, such as a Chrome extension or a Slack command. [eWGNjTLK84c](../sources/eWGNjTLK84c.md) [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWGNjTLK84c&t=66s)
- **Give users a reason to return that they do not control.** A product that changes only on user action gives no impulse to return. A clock does, as with FarmVille crops and Hinge Standouts. [VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md) [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=242s). A fresh Spotify playlist every Monday is predictable delight that builds a habit. [Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md) [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=154s). A daily game also works. [GeoSports](../businesses/geosports.md) relies on players who return every day, and about 53% of its 100K daily players share their score (2026-09). [s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s), [11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=714s)
- **Streaks, designed for the miss.** Plan what the app does the day after a user skips. Duolingo's streak freeze, Alma's vacation mode and Noom's "pause and preserve" let a streak survive a bad day. [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=264s), [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=382s). Streaks and collections turn into identity and stored value, which raises the cost of leaving. [uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md) [8:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=535s). Keep the next threshold close, so the loop never fully closes (Zeigarnik effect). [D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md) [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=445s). See [Gamification](gamification.md).
- **Small, social competition.** A global leaderboard feels unwinnable, so people stop. Put the comparison at completion, shrink the cohort and recognize effort like a human. [BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=268s). Ladder's quiz funnel converted users, but its cohort calendar kept them. [HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md) [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=628s). Turn lonely tasks into community, because loneliness drives churn. [ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=592s)
- **Identity as retention.** Give users a label they are proud to claim (a type, a status, a role). People rarely churn from their own identity. [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=401s). For B2B, find the "magnet persona" that never churns and makes others join. For [lemlist](../businesses/lemlist.md) this was sales reps. [ZEIPj7QbZeI](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md) [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=1031s)
- **Emotional feedback and endings.** Design the peak and the end of the core flow (peak-end rule). [11h4H3TAGkM](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md) [1:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=111s), [YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md) [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s). Without designed emotion, users complete tasks but churn. [AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md) [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=605s). A small bounce or glow after each repeat action helps. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s). See [Behavioral design](behavioral-design.md).
- **Talk to users who leave or complain.** Cancel popups gave weak answers. A yes/no email to each churned company got close to 100% replies. [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=424s). Customer support first thing every morning kept paying users around. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s). A fix in 5 to 10 minutes can win a customer for life. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s)
- **Solve the cold start for social features.** [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md) built an in-app TikTok-style feed and requires partner creators to post there. It also sends daily notifications in Duolingo's style. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) [37:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2238s), [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s). Phoenix Do says Cal AI has a trigger, an action and a variable reward, but no investment step that brings users back. [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) [15:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=903s)
- **Pricing and plan choices change churn.** Add a yearly plan and a free trial, and make cancellation easy. [mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md) [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=471s). Market one thing, then retain with a bundle of extra tools. [G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=547s). Do not show paywalls early or interrupt the core loop. Offer upgrades once users are engaged. [4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md) [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yKtlOWClhA&t=610s)

## Concrete examples with numbers

- **Flame:** day-30 retention went 0.8% (Sparks), 4.2% (ChatGPT wrapper), about 30%, then 50% after the daily-question pivot, over about 8 months (2026-02). He compares this with about 30% for X, 35% for Instagram and 40% for TikTok. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) [0:00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=13s), [0:13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=799s)
- **[PrayScreen](../businesses/prayscreen.md):** 60% day-30 retention, with users praying four times a day (2025-09). Strong retention made a free tier with four ads a day pay back in 14 to 15 days. [NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=328s), [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=501s), [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s)
- **[Lobby](../businesses/lobby.md):** day-90 retention in Israel was 25% to 30%, about twice other markets. The share of users with 5 friends on day one was about 60% in Israel and 20% elsewhere. [jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md) [20:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1208s), [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s)
- **[Postiz](../businesses/postiz.md):** monthly churn fell from 26% to 28% to 13.7% after a pivot to agents that post every day without a person (2026-07). [n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md) [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=148s), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=491s)
- **[ScreenshotOne](../businesses/screenshotone.md):** monthly churn fell from about 11% to close to 7%, partly by stating clearly that the product is for developers (2024-11). [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)
- **[Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md):** one commitment screen at the end of onboarding raised retention by 3%. Two home-screen redesigns moved nothing (2026-09). [OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)
- **[Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md):** a growing tree raised core engagement (questions answered) by 70% (2025-11). [zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md) [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s)
- **[Duolingo](../businesses/duolingo.md):** after its animation rollout, daily active users grew from 14.2M to over 34M in 2 years. New courses and marketing also helped. [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=185s). Doubling the streak freezes a user can hold from 1 to 2 lifted daily active learners by 0.38%. [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=326s)
- **Cancel-flow save:** a 7-day trial extension in the cancel flow saves 27% of users who start to cancel (2026-04). [E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1906s)
- **Hardware subscriptions:** [Whoop](../businesses/whoop.md) says over 50% of members still use it daily 18 months after purchase (2026-04). [ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md) [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=482s). [Oura](../businesses/oura.md) retention is in the high 80s percent behind a $5.99/month plan (2026-03). [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s). [Peloton](../businesses/peloton.md) has 90% annual subscriber retention (2026-04). [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=501s)
- **Resubscribe benchmarks:** year-one resubscribe rate of 50% is amazing, 40% good, 30% okay, 20% bad (2026-07). Dating apps run lower. Resubscribe rate beats DAU as a signal. [u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) [17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s), [19:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1156s)
- **Repeat buyers in e-commerce:** almost 40% of [Vanman](../businesses/vanman.md) buyers buy again (2024-03). [6iqo7lNR_m0](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=463s)
- **Wrong audience, high churn:** an SMB Market campaign got about 40M views and about $1.2M, but churn hit about 45% because signups were get-rich-quick viewers. [VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1000s)

## Disagreements

- **Reminders and notifications.** Tim Gabe cites a 2015 Bristol study: reminders worked only while they kept firing and got in the way of habit forming. Anchor to an existing routine instead. [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=53s), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=85s). Flame credits a morning "carrot" and an evening "stick" notification, tied to the partner, for part of its jump to 50% day-30 retention. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) [0:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=711s)

> Conflict: [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) says reminders do not build lasting habits. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) uses streak-risk notifications as a core retention lever.

- **How much gamification to stack.** A 2025 study found an S-curve: engagement rises with features up to a point, then falls, so do not stack streaks, points, badges, challenges and leaderboards. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=266s), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=320s). The Cal AI team added groups, leaderboards, streaks, progress photos and badges together for retention. [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) [27:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=1621s). Symmetry also stacks ranks, a feed, shared streaks and levels. [OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=821s)

> Conflict: [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) warns against stacking game mechanics. [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) and [OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) stack them as their retention plan.

- **Streaks as a lever or a liability.** Streaks that build toward a reward and become identity raise the cost of leaving. [uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md) [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=392s), [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=451s). Another view: Snapchat streaks are linked to FOMO and problematic phone use, and regulators target streaks with no pause or exit. MacroFactor drops shame mechanics such as broken streaks. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=362s), [HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md) [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=443s)

> Conflict: [uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md) treats loss-based streaks as a retention tool. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) flags the same mechanic as a regulatory and wellbeing risk.

- **Subscriptions vs one-time payments.** Resume Maker Online moved to subscriptions: churn is high, but revenue is more predictable. [G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=503s). Fitted's founder says its hard paywall limits growth and churn is high, so he plans a free tier. [9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md) [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s)
