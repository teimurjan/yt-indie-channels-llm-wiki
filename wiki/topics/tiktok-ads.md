TikTok ads are paid campaigns on TikTok, either standard in-feed ads or Spark Ads that boost existing organic posts, used mostly by consumer apps.

## Definition

Apps pay TikTok to show videos, usually to drive installs, trials or subscriptions. Spark Ads push an organic post from a brand or creator account ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1740s); [F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2000s)).

## Repeated patterns

- **Organic finds, paid scales.** Post organic [UGC](../topics/ugc.md) in volume, then put spend behind videos that already won. This is the most repeated pattern ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s); [AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [14:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=876s); [lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=328s); [orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s); [SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [37:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=2257s)).
- **A trigger to boost.** Prayer Lock sparks organic videos over 10K views ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s)). SideShift's founders use about 5% engagement, or 5 to 10x normal views with 8 to 10%+ engagement ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s); [SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3015s)). Ryan Thorp boosts videos that break from 0 to 300 views into 5K to 10K ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=693s)).
- **Set up an MMP first.** AppsFlyer or Adjust tell TikTok who paid ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=454s); [hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s)).
- **Know the ROAS math.** Pay less per download than a user earns ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=263s)). Aim for LTV at 3x CAC ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1270s)). See [unit economics](../topics/unit-economics.md).
- **Feed fresh creatives.** Creative fatigue is the main limit. Raise budgets by no more than 20% every 3 days ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=605s)). One batch of winning ads can carry an app for 4 to 5 months ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=182s)).
- **Look native.** Copy memes and talking-to-camera formats, and do not show the app in the first 3 to 4 seconds ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [7:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=442s)). A plain demo of someone using the app was Chart Detector AI's best ad ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=331s)). See [ad creative](../topics/ad-creative.md).
- **Hard paywall for paid traffic.** Ads need cash back fast, so Chart Detector AI uses no trial ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s)). See [paywalls](../topics/paywalls.md).

## Concrete examples

- [Chart Detector AI](../businesses/chart-detector-ai.md): $56K/month, about $18K/month on TikTok ads, about 25% margin (2026-07). Smart+ campaigns, at least 6 videos, $50/day minimum, optimized for subscriptions, broad targeting by country ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=98s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=485s)).
- [PuffCount](../businesses/puff-count.md): $82K spend took MRR from $3K to $44K. CAC $20 to $24, LTV $55 to $70+. Clicks cost 3 to 35 cents because the ads reused proven organic videos ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)).
- [Prayer Lock](../businesses/prayer-lock.md): about $360/day, $9K spend for about $20K in December, $1.88 per trial (2026-01). Spark Ads on one creator cut cost to $3 per trial ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=899s), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s)).
- [AbMaxx](../businesses/abmaxx.md): the same creatives earned 2x to 2.5x ROAS on Meta and 4x to 5x on TikTok, because TikTok skews younger (2026-10) ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).
- [Glowly](../businesses/glowly.md) and [Overlow](../businesses/overlow.md): about $16 per subscription on TikTok, LTV about $30 to $35 (2026-08) ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s)).
- [Done](../businesses/done.md) and [Reflectly](../businesses/reflectly.md): a three-level system. Level 1: each video alone at $10 to $20, killed after $5 to $10. Level 2: winners in sets of 4 or 5 at $100 to $500. Level 3: 1 or 2 evergreen ads take 90% of spend. About 75% of the yearly budget goes into January, when acquisition costs about half ([DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=971s), [19:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1166s)).
- [Debloat AI](../businesses/debloat-ai.md): a few cents per download for a few days, then about 30% profit on spend for weeks ([FIgn-1kbzZw](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [15:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=910s)).
- [Sizzle AI](../businesses/sizzle-ai.md): a winning TikTok with 79K likes came from 20 to 30 organic tries and drove hundreds of purchases as an ad. TikTok works better for US targeting, Facebook for global ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=520s), [16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=964s)).
- [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md): paid ads on Meta and TikTok took revenue from $10K to $30K a month in about two months, after organic channels stalled ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=178s)).
- [Stoppr](../businesses/stoppr.md): about $100/month on Spark Ads to boost reels that already work (2025-10) ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s)).
- GoWish reached #1 on the App Store almost entirely through Spark Ads. Top programs reach $0.80 to $0.90 CPMs (2026-06) ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [29:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1784s), [46:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2777s)).
- [Bloom](../businesses/bloom.md): TikTok paid ads scaled in 2023, then CAC worsened from mid-2023 and fell off by year end ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [Chart Detector AI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) optimizes for subscription events from the start ([8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=485s)). [PuffCount](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md) says do not start with a purchase campaign; go from views to clicks, installs, trials, then subscriptions ([18:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1088s)).

> Conflict: [jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md) uses TikTok ads on mockup videos to validate an idea before launch ([8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=531s)). In [zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), Pat Walls says ads test creative, not demand ([11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=669s)).

> Conflict: [qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) calls Spark Ads "a cheat code" ([29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1740s)). [F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) says Spark Ads lack an install button, so Meta ads scale better ([33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2000s)). Both speakers co-founded SideShift.

> Conflict: [Bloom](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md) says TikTok paid ads stopped paying back in late 2023 ([1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=70s)). [RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) reports TikTok ads at twice Meta's ROAS in 2026-10 ([7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).
