This page collects what the sources say about a personal brand: a founder's own audience on X, LinkedIn, YouTube or a newsletter, used as a distribution channel.

## Definition

A personal brand is an audience that follows a founder as a person, not a product. Founders use it to launch products, find clients, hire and validate ideas.

## Repeated patterns

### Build the audience before the product

- [Alex Finn](../people/alex-finn.md) posted on X for 3 years before he had a product, about 5 minutes a day ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=556s)). He announced a launch date 12 days ahead and hosted X Spaces all launch day ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)).
- [Dustin Stout](../people/dustin-stout.md) launched to a blog list of over 100K subscribers built over 10 years. He ran no ads ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)).
- [3AK](../businesses/3ak.md) had track videos with hundreds of thousands of followers. It put "3AK method" in videos and bios before the app existed ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=312s), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=318s)).
- [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md)'s "ACP funnel": audience, then community, then product ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- [Ramsri Goutham](../people/ramsri-goutham.md) wrote blogs, made YouTube videos and posted daily on LinkedIn before launch ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=451s)).
- [Lara Acosta](../people/lara-acosta.md) posted on LinkedIn for about 3 years and built two businesses from it before [Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md) ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=166s)).

### The brand is the moat that AI cannot copy

- Alex Finn: a clone with 10 followers loses to the founder with an audience ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)).
- [Will Baker](../people/will-baker.md) sees a personal brand as a moat that AI cannot copy ([zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=520s)).
- [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) argues that AI coding floods every niche with apps, so content decides who wins ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=1012s)). In a crowded lifting-app market, the brand is the part rivals cannot copy ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=680s)).
- [Eunice Lie](../people/eunice-lie.md): the brand stays with you, and companies come and go ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=309s)).
- [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md) calls building in public insurance. If an app fails, the following stays for the next launch ([WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s)).

### Document one goal in public

- [Rob Hallam](../people/rob-hallam.md)'s steps: set a friendly face photo and a one-line bio, pin your goal, and document it daily ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s)).
- His content loop: an entertaining or vulnerable post, then education or a pitch, then the result with what it means to you ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=709s)).
- [Gaurabh Mathure](../people/gaurabh-mathure.md) posts once a day and documents the journey. Pat Walls calls it his highest-ROI channel ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)).
- [Max Hirsch](../people/max-hirsch.md) shares what works, what fails and his weak points ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s)).

### Show your work on other people's brands

- [Nick Buzz](../people/nick-buzz.md) quote-tweeted founders' landing pages with "roasted" and his fixes ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)).
- [Mark Vassilevskiy](../people/mark-vassilevskiy.md) redesigned the landing pages of brands people talk about. One post got about 110K views overnight and 10 to 12 bookings ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)).
- [Danny Miranda](../people/danny-miranda.md) tweeted that Gary Vaynerchuk was a dream guest. Gary's followers pushed him in the replies, and Gary came on ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=457s)).

### Post on a fixed rhythm, at volume

- [Justin Welsh](../people/justin-welsh.md) posts on LinkedIn and X at 8:15 a.m. Eastern every day, then replies for 45 to 60 minutes ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s)).
- [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md)'s viral post came on day 27 of a daily challenge, after zero engagement the day before ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s)).
- [Marcos Ruiz](../people/marcos-ruiz.md) pairs 100 to 200 cold DMs a day with 2 to 3 tweets a day ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s)).
- Mark Vassilevskiy: "volume negates luck". He left about 50 comments a day on other designers' posts ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=248s), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=512s)).
- [Kyan](../people/kyan.md) posts 3 times a day for at least a month across four platforms ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=416s)).

### Personal posts can beat product posts

- Rob Hallam's launch post got 32,000 views and 26 sign-ups. A selfie post after it got 46,000 views and added $1,000 MRR in 24 hours ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [7:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=447s), [8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=507s)).
- Eunice Lie's "build in public" tweets went unnoticed. Real Hinge chats about her startup stood out ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s)).
- [Nico Jeannen](../people/nico-jeannen.md)'s site said "no VC money, just a tiny company", with his photo everywhere. People buy from people ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=448s)).

### Let the audience pick the product

- [Kevin Espiritu](../people/kevin-espiritu.md)'s viewers kept asking about his raised bed. It sold out in 2 weeks from one Instagram story ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)).
- [Alex Lieberman](../people/alex-lieberman.md) tweeted an offer to executives and got 25 DMs before he built Storyarb ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)).
- [Sarah Pearl](../people/sarah-pearl.md) reads comments for "I wish this existed" and builds that ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=777s)).
- [Hunter Hammonds](../people/hunter-hammonds.md) matches a service to what a creator already teaches, and makes creators equity partners ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)).

## Platforms

- **LinkedIn.** Eunice Lie calls LinkedIn followers the highest-value followers ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1160s)). Gaurabh Mathure found LinkedIn was the only platform with a signal ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s)). Lara Acosta's 4-3-2-1 plan: 4 posts a week, 3 pillars, 2 audiences, 1 lead magnet ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [10:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=651s)).
- **X.** Rob Hallam gets 95% of traffic from X. Video gave him 10x the reach of text ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=895s)). [Vadim Strizheus](../people/vadim-strizheus.md) rode viral AI topics on X to $2K to $3K MRR ([QXxESN94Y6s](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=363s)).
- **Newsletter.** Justin Welsh: social posts bring discovery, the newsletter builds trust, then the offer comes ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s)). [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) puts research in a newsletter first and turns some issues into videos ([dg_LmOjapRA](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=313s)).
- **Pick your medium.** Marc Lou: text on X, video on TikTok, images on Instagram ([WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [18:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=1102s)).

## Examples with numbers

- [Alex Garcia](../people/alex-garcia.md) wrote 50 threads in 50 days. Followers went from 500 to 40,000, and the newsletter from about 2,000 to 8,000 or 9,000 ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)).
- Rob Hallam: over 34,000 X followers in just over a year, about 100,000 impressions a day (2025-12) ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s)).
- Mark Vassilevskiy: three giveaways took him from about 500 to 5K followers. 25K followers and over 25M views in 2025 (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=299s), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)).
- Max Hirsch: about a third of revenue comes from X followers, 3 months after he started to tweet (2026-10) ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s)).
- Nick Buzz: $1.2M/year as an anonymous designer on X (2024-09) ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=0s)).
- Greg Isenberg started LCA with about 10,000 X followers. LCA made $1.5M in year one ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- Marcos Ruiz: about 10K X followers after eight months in business ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=328s)).
- Sarah Pearl: 1.2M Instagram and 2.8M TikTok followers (2026-07) ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=106s)).
- [Frank Smith](../people/frank-smith.md): a reply to his own NBA tweet brought about 40K players, then 150K the next day ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=636s)).
- [Shervin](../people/shervin.md): 1,000 YouTube subscribers took about 2 to 3 years ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=344s)).
- Pat Walls: a viral Reddit post in month 2, Twitter in month 5, $3,500/month at day 365 ([xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=427s), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=488s)).

## Other uses of a brand

- **Founder-led content at a cold start.** [Dan Kwon](../people/dan-kwon.md): in a cold start, the founder makes the most content ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1002s)). [Jay](../people/jay.md) went viral himself before he paid creators ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [38:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=2328s)).
- **Hiring and co-founders.** Ramsri Goutham's co-founders found him through his posts ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)). [Sebastian Ghiorghiu](../people/sebastian-ghiorghiu.md)'s name made outreach and hiring easier ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=456s)).
- **Inbound leads for services.** [Brett Williams](../people/brett-williams.md)'s main lead source became Twitter ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)). [Cardinal Mason](../people/cardinal-mason.md)'s day is mostly TikTok videos and livestreams that sell his program ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=691s)). [Chris](../people/chris.md) built a Twitter audience before coaching ([hCvy-Rt7Naw](../sources/hCvy-Rt7Naw.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCvy-Rt7Naw&t=79s)).
- **Write before you quit.** [Paul Millerd](../people/paul-millerd.md) wrote on Quora daily for 100 days before he quit ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)). Dickie Bush says quitting first makes you act from scarcity ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=200s)).

## Disagreements

- **Face or faceless.**
  - Kyan puts his face on the brand so users feel they join the journey ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=416s)). Rob Hallam keeps a friendly face photo ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s)).
  - Nick Buzz stays anonymous and still makes $1.2M/year. He says to be authentic with a faceless account ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=677s), [16:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=1012s)).
> Conflict: [Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md) and [VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md) want the founder's face on the brand. [TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md) built a large business while anonymous.
- **Audience or skill.**
  - Alex Finn treats the audience as the moat ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)).
  - Sarah Pearl says virality is a skill, not an audience. She grows any new account to 50,000 followers in 3 to 4 months ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=169s)).
  - Sebastian Ghiorghiu built a $1M app without promoting it to his audience. He prefers business income to audience income ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=456s), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=639s)).
> Conflict: [4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md) treats an existing audience as the moat. [Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) and [laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md) say new accounts, skill or paid channels can replace it.
- **Posts or email.** Lara Acosta says email converts better than viral posts, because email has no algorithm ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=572s)).
