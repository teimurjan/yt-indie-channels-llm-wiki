This page covers AI wrappers: products that put a focused interface, prompt or workflow on top of a model that another company owns.

## Definition

An AI wrapper is a product built on a third-party model API, such as OpenAI's. It sells a specific use case, interface or bundle, not the model itself.

## Repeated patterns

- **Sell one use case, not a general chatbot.** Build a single-use-case API wrapper, and include account creation so App Review approves it. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=593s). Users pay for niche apps with tuned prompts and end-to-end flows. [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [22:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1332s). Lawyers and doctors who do not use AI yet are open niches. [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=466s)
- **Fill gaps in the leader's product.** Early ChatGPT had no chat search and no folders. [Magai](../businesses/magai.md) and [Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md) built those features. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s), [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s). Build what demos show but nobody sells yet. [E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)
- **Simplify the interface.** [Visualizee](../businesses/visualizee.md) replaced a node-based UI with a chat assistant that writes the prompt for the user. Revenue went from about $150/month to close to $10K/month in about 6 months (2026-09). [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=21s), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=249s)
- **Timing beats validation.** Ship within days of a new model or API. Superpower ChatGPT shipped 2 to 3 days after ChatGPT launched. [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s). [Chatbase](../businesses/chatbase.md) was ready before the ChatGPT API and launched it as new on API day. [E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=499s). [Domenico Gagliardi](../people/domenico-gagliardi.md) started every project from a trend on X. [plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md) [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=400s)
- **Pick a category that already goes viral.** [Natural Write](../businesses/natural-write.md) chose AI humanizers because TikTok already had many viral humanizer videos. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s). Copy what tops the App Store charts and add a niche. [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)
- **Platform risk is real.** OpenAI can kill an extension on any day. [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=676s). About 5% to 10% of [Formula Bot](../businesses/formula-bot.md) churn is because of ChatGPT (2024-02). Microsoft then announced its own OpenAI tools for Excel. [IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md) [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=760s), [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=823s)
- **Copycats arrive fast.** About 10 copycat Excel formula sites appeared within weeks of Formula Bot. [IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md) [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=700s). Height GPT had about 20 copycats, and some made hundreds of thousands with better UI and funnels. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=345s)
- **Use aggregator APIs.** OpenRouter gives one API format for many LLMs, and fal.ai does the same for media models. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=490s), [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=557s)
- **Wrappers flip well.** Small AI tools sell on marketplaces for a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars. [plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md) [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s), [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=73s). See [Acquisitions](acquisitions.md).

## Concrete examples with numbers

- **[Chatbase](../businesses/chatbase.md):** $0 to $1M ARR in 117 days, then $6.8M ARR with 10,000 paying customers at $40 to $500/month (2025-07). [E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=322s), [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=431s)
- **[Magai](../businesses/magai.md):** almost $100K/month and over $1M total (2025-10). No free plan. Solo is $20/month. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=55s), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)
- **[Formula Bot](../businesses/formula-bot.md):** about 750K users and 5,000 paying subscribers (2024-02). About $5,000 in OpenAI costs in the days after it went viral (2022). [IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md) [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)
- **[Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md):** $20K to $30K MRR and about 150K weekly active users, with no ad spend (2024-08). [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=76s)
- **[Natural Write](../businesses/natural-write.md):** about $100K in 3 months from about 9M TikTok views and 250K signups (2025-09). [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=544s)
- **[Snag](../businesses/snag.md):** about $30K MRR about 4 months after launch (2026-05). [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=95s)
- **[Softgen](../businesses/softgen.md) and six micro tools:** an AI summarizer sold for $34K and a cover letter generator for $7K. Softgen grew from $20K to $500K ARR in 3 months and sold for nearly 3x ARR (2025-10). [plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md) [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=241s)
- **Magic Music**, a Suno wrapper, was one of four apps that [Lotts Ezeike](../people/lotts-ezeike.md) sold for over $500K in total (2025-11). [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=91s)
- **Price benchmark:** text-inference wrapper apps often charge $30 to $40/year, and $29.99/year is common. Apps that target an insecurity can charge more, such as $20 to $22/month (2026-02). [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s), [23:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1417s)

## Disagreements

- **Broad bundle or narrow niche.** Magai sells all top models in one subscription, against subscription fatigue. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s). Benjamin Chen says to build a single-use-case wrapper. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=638s). Piotr says to pick a niche, not a generic AI toy. [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=224s)

> Conflict: [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) wins with a broad multi-model bundle. [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) and [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) say a narrow single use case wins.

- **Free first or charge from day one.** Superpower ChatGPT stayed 100% free for 9 months and earned first from a newsletter. [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s). Piotr says to charge from day one, and Magai has no free plan. [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s), [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)

> Conflict: [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) grew the user base free before it charged. [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) says to charge from day one.

- **Paid influencers.** Natural Write got views but almost no sales from paid influencers, so the founders filmed themselves. [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s). Snag pays vetted UGC creators a retainer plus CPM, and about 100K views make $1K to $2K in profit (2026-05). [P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s)
