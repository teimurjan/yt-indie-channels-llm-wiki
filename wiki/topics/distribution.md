This page collects what founders say about distribution: how a product reaches its buyers, and why many of them now treat it as the moat.

## Definition

Distribution is the set of channels that put a product in front of people who will pay: an audience, short-form video, search, partners, communities, integrations or ads.

## Repeated patterns

### Distribution before product

- **Build the audience first.** [Alex Finn](../people/alex-finn.md) posted on X for 3 years before [Creator Buddy](../businesses/creator-buddy.md). A clone with 10 followers loses to a founder with an audience ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=556s), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)). [Ramsri Goutham](../people/ramsri-goutham.md) wrote blogs, made videos and posted daily on LinkedIn before his apps ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=451s)). [Sarah Pearl](../people/sarah-pearl.md) says distribution beats product ([source](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [2:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=169s)).
- **Make the video before the app.** [Pushscroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md)'s founders validated with one video (80K views, about 500 comments) before building ([source](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s)). [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md): know what the content looks like before you build ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:45:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2753s)). [Yiannis](../people/yiannis.md) goes viral with one account a few times, then builds ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [53:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3182s)).
- **Design the product for the feed.** Treat onboarding and welcome pages as distribution ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=357s)). Cal AI's photo scan is the viral feature. Tracking keeps users ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1363s)). [Jake Glacer](../people/jake-glacer.md) added an alarm to [Erly](../businesses/erly.md) because the first version was not viral enough for influencers ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s)).

### Short-form video

- **Copy proven formats.** Find what went viral and slot your app in ([source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=681s)). [Clover Labs](../businesses/clover-labs.md) clones posts with small changes. A clone gets a median 20% of the original's views ([source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s)). Joseph pays creators to copy a second-by-second brief, not to create ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:29:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1766s)).
- **Find the format yourself, then hand it off.** [Kelechi](../people/kelechi.md) made content until one format worked, then gave it to small streamers ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s)). A founder must post before managing others ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [54:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3295s)).
- **Scale with many accounts.** [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md) ran 5 accounts on 3 platforms, 15 videos a day ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [24:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1446s)). Three posts a day per account avoids shadowbans ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2171s)). [An Nayal](../people/an-nayal.md)'s 300-view rule: under 300 is an account problem, 300 to 1K a content problem ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s)).
- **Intent in the words.** The same scratch-map video got no downloads with "post-breakup activity" and charted with "procrastinating for my exams" ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [37:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2221s)).
- **Soft calls to action.** Viewers search the App Store by name or ask "what's the app?" in comments ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=966s), [source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=611s)).
- **Timeless over timely.** [Roman Lakhnyuk](../people/roman-lakhnyuk.md) repeats one list format of remote-hiring companies and pays creators no more than $20 a video in about 90% of cases. [Career Hound](../businesses/career-hound.md) got 11.7M views in 30 days (2026-09) ([source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=364s), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s)).
- **Go abroad.** Each new country adds about a 20% view lift ([source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s)).

### Creators and partners

- **Creator systems.** Source, train, manage, systematize. Over 50% of Sprout creators who finish the course go viral within 2 weeks ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=542s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=578s)). Expect about 100 creators to get one that works ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=472s)). Pay half up front at most ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=670s)).
- **Creators as co-founders.** [Drop Magic](../businesses/drop-magic.md)'s creator co-founder made $10K MRR certain at launch ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s)). [MonAI](../businesses/monai.md) gives one creator a profit share ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=333s)). Game influencers got 50/50 splits ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [39:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2389s)).
- **Borrow a partner's customers.** [Ivan Nedlekovski](../people/ivan-nedlekovski.md) sells to "connectors" who already have his buyers' trust ([source](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s)). [Hassam](../people/hassam.md) traded equity to a coaching company for distribution ([source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=251s)).

### Intent channels and communities

- **Capture intent.** [Late](../businesses/late.md) grew on SEO and Google Search Ads only: people who search "social media scheduling API" have about 10x the purchase intent ([source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s)). [Steve Young](../people/steve-young.md) says founders without a creative edge can start with ASO and Apple Ads ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=282s)).
- **Reddit.** [Diego](../people/diego.md) writes value-first posts and cross-posts to many subreddits, 2 to 3 times a week ([source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=372s), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=557s)). [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) turned Reddit traffic into an email list ([source](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)).
- **Open source and Hacker News.** [ChartDB](../businesses/chartdb.md) hit the front page with a Show HN ([source](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=235s)). [Postiz](../businesses/postiz.md)'s five steps (repo as landing page, articles, Show HN, r/selfhosted, push all traffic to GitHub) aim for GitHub trending ([source](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=635s), [15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=934s)).
- **Integrations.** [Raycast](../businesses/raycast.md)'s GitHub and Slack extensions show its value inside tools whole teams use ([source](../sources/eWGNjTLK84c.md), [1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWGNjTLK84c&t=66s)).
- **Comments at scale.** One team ran 60 phones posting Instagram comments. About 300,000 comments gave about 7,000 clicks (2026-01) ([source](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2080s)).
- **AI search.** Clover Labs seeds Reddit posts and comments that ChatGPT and Gemini cite ([source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [32:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1930s)).

### Personal brand

- Post daily and document one goal ([source](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s)). [Gaurabh Mathure](../people/gaurabh-mathure.md) posted everywhere, then focused on LinkedIn ([source](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=309s)). [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md) treats YouTube as insurance against Google changes ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)). [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md) says 90% of his products failed before he built in public, and 10% after ([source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=906s)).

### Sequencing

- [Tibo](../people/tibo.md): free channels (Product Hunt, posts) until $10K/month, then SEO, ads and affiliates. Growth comes from one or two channels ([source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=701s), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=871s)). Late's founder and Pat agree: go all in on the first channel that works ([source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [15:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=935s)).
- Get 30 to 100 new users a week, or experiments have no signal ([source](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=358s)).

## Disagreements

- **TikTok or YouTube.** [Loic Berthelot](../people/loic-berthelot.md) says TikTok builds awareness but does not convert. YouTube has the best ROI and old videos still convert ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)). Kelechi tests on TikTok first because new accounts go viral more easily ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=533s)).
> Conflict: [-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) says TikTok does not convert. [9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) builds a $1.5M app business on TikTok-first testing.
- **Measure each creator or the whole program.** Loic tests one video per creator and looks for break-even before a bigger package ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s)). Jake Glacer treats creators as a black box: put in $5K, get out $10K, and accept losses on some ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=732s)).
> Conflict: [-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) measures each creator. [uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md) measures only the total.
- **Who makes the content.** Yiannis keeps creative in-house and uses reposters only to post ([source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=315s)). [Nicole](../people/nicole.md) trains about 200 creators to make it ([source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=400s)).
- **Do low-view posts matter?** [Nic Weber](../people/nic-weber.md) says TikTok sends even 500 views to the right 500 people ([source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [31:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1887s)). An Nayal reads under 300 views as a broken account ([source](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s)).
