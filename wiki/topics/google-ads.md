Google Ads is Google's paid channel: search ads on high-intent keywords, App campaigns that run in the Play Store, and YouTube ads.

## Definition

Founders buy clicks on keywords people already search for, or app installs across Google's surfaces. Sources treat it as a way to capture existing demand, not create it ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s); [CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [20:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1206s)).

## Repeated patterns

- **Intent beats noise.** Someone who searches "social media scheduling API" has about 10x the purchase intent of a social viewer ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s)). Liinks found search intent made ads "money in, money out" for about 4 years ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=485s)).
- **Pair ads with SEO on the same keywords.** Rank where you can; run ads on pages that do not rank ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=381s)). Samuel Rondot starts with Google or Meta ads to test fast, then adds [SEO](../topics/seo.md) because it compounds ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s)).
- **Use ads to validate demand.** Hush Blankets bought the "weighted blankets" keyword for a pre-order site ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)). Angus Cheng launched an MVP in a week and bought Google ads; people uploaded files at once ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=95s)). See [idea validation](../topics/idea-validation.md).
- **Feed the algorithm conversion events and use target CPA.** Send installs, paywall views, trials and purchases ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=558s)). Late uses automated bidding with a $120 target CPA (2026-03) ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s)).
- **Reinvest profit when CPA is low.** StealthGPT poured revenue into Google Ads once conversions cost $10 to $20, "gasoline on a fire" ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)).

## Concrete examples

- [Late](../businesses/late.md): about $8K/month spend, $2.50 revenue per $1, about $15K MRR from search ads (2026-03). Five steps: list high-intent keywords, publish 10 to 15 pieces in week one, launch ad groups with 3 to 5 copy variants, cut weak keywords and raise budget while ROAS holds, keep CAC at 20% to 40% of LTV ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=548s), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=607s), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=789s)).
- [Journable](../businesses/journable.md): just under $500K spent on Google App campaigns, about the same in attributed revenue (2026-03). The Play Store placement works best. Budget 10x the target cost per event per day: a $15 trial needs $150/day ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=417s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=458s), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=739s)).
- [StealthGPT](../businesses/stealthgpt.md): $190K MRR after about 15 months, mostly from Google Ads, with about $100K/month marketing spend (2024-07) ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)).
- [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md): about $100/day on Google Ads, about $31,500 MRR (2026-10). It moved from Instagram ads after an algorithm change ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=788s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=485s)).
- [Hush Blankets](../businesses/hush-blankets.md): search ads took a pre-order site to $30K, $60K, then $90K/month in 2018, then sales fell to near zero in July ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
- [Athlete's Thread](../businesses/athletes-thread.md) and sister brands use paid search for high-intent queries such as a player's T-shirt, plus Google Shopping ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)).
- [Arvow](../businesses/arvow.md) pays for YouTube in-feed ads on videos that already sold ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s)).
- [YourMove AI](../businesses/yourmove-ai.md): Dmitri hired a contractor for Google Ads who did the work and trained him ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [43:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2594s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [Bank Statement Converter](../businesses/bank-statement-converter.md) spent about $1,000 on Google search ads per $300 in sales and cut them after about 6 months ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=247s)). Late earns $2.50 per $1 on search ads (2026-03) ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s)).

> Conflict: [E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) calls search the channel with predictable unit economics ([4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s)). [CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md) says Google and SEO are capped by search volume, while Meta and TikTok create demand with more upside ([20:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1206s)).
