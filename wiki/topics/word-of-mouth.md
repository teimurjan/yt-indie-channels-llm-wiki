Word of mouth is growth from users who tell other people about a product. They talk about it or share something the product made.

## Definition

Word of mouth is acquisition that the founder does not pay for per user. In these sources it comes from two things: a share mechanism built into the product, and care that makes customers want to recommend it.

## Repeated patterns

### Build the share into the product

- **Brand every shared output.** [Stagetimer](../businesses/stagetimer.md) copied Dropbox. Every shared timer shows the "stagetimer.io" logo, so people at events see it and try it ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=484s)).
- **Watermarks as a viral loop.** [Supademo](../businesses/supademo.md) puts watermarks on shared demos ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)).
- **A score worth sharing.** [GeoSports](../businesses/geosports.md) grows on score shares, mostly in group chats ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=357s)).
- **Remove friction.** GeoSports is web-first, so people played inside X without leaving the app ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=523s)).

### Earn it with care

- **Fix problems fast.** After a user hits a problem, [Nic Weber](../people/nic-weber.md) watches the screen recordings. He ships requested features in hours or days ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s)).
- **Make customers look like heroes.** [Ian Myers](../people/ian-myers.md) puts service first. Business owners talk about hiring all the time, so [Oceans](../businesses/oceans.md) has built-in word of mouth ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=618s), [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=678s)).
- **Help, do not pitch.** In Discord servers, [Sam Adeniyi](../people/sam-adeniyi.md) used [Algrow](../businesses/algrow.md) to find channels for people who asked. Word of mouth followed ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=504s)).

### Seed it with early users

- Give early users free access, so they demo the tool to friends who pay ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=673s)).
- A Discord server owner made an unpaid YouTube video about Algrow after Sam silently used it on screen ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=331s)).
- Free users of [Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md) get 30 days of the paid plan, so everyone sees the whole app ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s)).

### It is one channel, not the only one

- B2C needs viral potential, because low prices make heavy ad spend hard ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=473s)).
- Nic Weber: directories and Product Hunt do not replace talking to your ICP ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=644s)).
