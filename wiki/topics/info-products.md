This page covers info products: courses, ebooks, books, templates, cohorts and paid newsletters that sell knowledge instead of software or a service.

## Definition

An info product packages what a creator knows into a digital or printed product that sells many times with no extra work per sale. In these sources it usually sits on top of an audience (YouTube, X, LinkedIn, a newsletter) and next to sponsorships, services or SaaS.

## Repeated patterns

- **Audience first, product second.** Thomas Frank's niche Notion channel feeds an email list, and the list sells the templates [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [11:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=678s). Justin Welsh runs discovery (social posts), trust (newsletter) and then a soft offer [source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md) [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s). Florin Pop: content gets attention, and attention makes products easier to sell [source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s).
- **Productize your own system.** Creator's Companion is the system Thomas Frank's team used from 2018 to 2021 [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=408s). Pat Walls turned a 5-minute Loom of Starter Story's Klaviyo flows into a product with about $20K in one-day pre-orders (2024-10) [source](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) [0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=31s). Dickie Bush sold the 30-day writing challenge that built his audience [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s).
- **Validate cheap, then raise the price.** Nick Buzz sold a $9 kit of 50 landing pages before he raised prices on roasts [source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md) [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s). Ship 30 for 30 started at $50 with a refund for finishers, then relaunched at $99 after 50+ interviews [source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s).
- **Email does the selling.** Ultimate Brain made about $90K in 30 days from one email to a 3,200-person waitlist [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=0s). Starter Story's list of 250K to 300K makes about $80K to $120K a month (2024-10) [source](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) [11:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=667s).
- **Long demo content works as an ad.** A 45-minute template walkthrough lifted Thomas Frank to about $100K/month [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s). Tim Gabe built a demo Framer site to fill a waitlist for his Framer course (2023-10) [source](../sources/R33gaV3EV1I.md) [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=144s).
- **Climb a monetization ladder.** Matt Giovanisci went from AdSense to affiliate links to his own courses and a print book [source](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s). Paul Millerd funded writing with freelancing, then a course, then workshops [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=604s).
- **Sell the outcome.** Pool owners want "clear and safe", not mastery [source](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=434s). Joe Popelas picks niches where buyers want a clear outcome [source](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=220s).
- **Info products scale past services.** Brett Williams turns DesignJoy know-how into templates and a course, 29% of his income (2024-01) [source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=629s). Cardinal Mason moved from freelance copy (capped near $30K to $40K/month) to an education program [source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md) [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=522s).
- **Gate support, not content.** Templates are easy to pirate, so Thomas Frank sells gated support in Circle [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=560s).
- **Self-publish for margin.** Paul Millerd makes about $7 to $8 per print book against about $2 from a traditional publisher [source](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md) [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=240s).

## Disagreements

- **Are courses passive?** Florin Pop's course still earns passive revenue after he automated it [source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=633s). Brett Williams calls info products "build once, sell forever" [source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=568s). Tim Gabe left the course business because building a software course was "taxing" and "stressful" (2025-01) [source](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md) [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=68s).

> Conflict: [47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) and [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) describe courses as passive income. [dg_LmOjapRA](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md) found a software course too draining and went back to client work.

- **Price up or down.** Matt Giovanisci's first PDF did not sell at $50, $40 or $30, and sold well only at $24 [source](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s). Thomas Frank raised prices, lost a little volume and made more in total [source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=523s).

> Conflict: [7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) found the price by stepping down. [OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) gained revenue by stepping up. The audiences differ: a cold pool niche against a warm Notion list.
