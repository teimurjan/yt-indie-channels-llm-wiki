This page covers founders and studios that run many small apps or SaaS products at once instead of one big product.

## Definition

An app portfolio is a set of apps or SaaS products under one owner or studio, built or bought so that wins on a few pay for the rest and no single failure kills the business.

## Repeated patterns

- **Power law inside the portfolio.** Most apps make little; a few carry it. Max: four of 28 apps make about $1,500/month each, an 80/20 split (2025-12) ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)). Adam Lyttle's 9 post-ChatGPT apps made $2 to $500 each but paid the bills together ([source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=797s)).
- **Ship, leave, let data pick winners.** Max ships one-feature apps, rides the App Store new-app boost, and returns only to apps with organic traction ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [9:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=553s), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=614s)).
- **One audience, many products.** Presh's apps serve one wellness audience, so each launch markets to existing users. Pat Walls calls the shared customer "the secret" of a multi-app portfolio ([source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s), [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=786s)). Barn2 cross-sells one companion plugin per product and emails 50% off the next plugin three days after purchase ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=461s)).
- **Resilience.** Tibo runs five products because one OpenAI release or platform change can kill a single product. Musk's X takeover nearly killed Tweet Hunter at $200K/month ([source](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [15:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=930s), [16:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=981s)).
- **Buy instead of build.** Ryan Thorp found undermonetized apps paid back faster than ads ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s)). Social buys apps after the viral peak and screens targets by running ads for apps it does not own yet ([source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=404s), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s)). See [Acquisitions](acquisitions.md).
- **Reuse everything.** Max reuses up to 90% of an older app's code plus ready settings, onboarding and paywall screens ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [6:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=389s)). Social tags each A/B test with its hypothesis so agents and hires can apply it across apps ([source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1138s)).
- **Shared growth machine.** Ryan Thorp's team trained about 250 regular creators across 30 to 35 apps ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [34:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2049s)). Studio Vert Ventures tests many creators and turns organic winners into ads ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [26:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1616s)).
- **Partner equity per app.** Connor Burd gives creators equity and builds each app for their audience; he would give 60 to 70% of a neglected app to a creator rather than let it sit ([source](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=737s), [43:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=2626s)).

## Disagreements

- **Same customer or any category.** Presh and Pat Walls say a portfolio works when every app serves one customer ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [13:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=786s)). Social runs unrelated apps (fonts, stickers, celebrity lookalikes) on a shared ad and test machine ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=514s)).
> Conflict: [SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) ties portfolio success to one shared audience; [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) ties it to a shared acquisition and monetization engine across unrelated categories.
- **Ship and leave, or stay close to users.** Max says "once the app is live, just let it go" ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=38s)). Tibo talks to users daily and iterates until they cannot live without the product before any broad acquisition ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=593s)).
> Conflict: [vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md) lets App Store data choose winners; [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) requires daily user contact to find stickiness.
