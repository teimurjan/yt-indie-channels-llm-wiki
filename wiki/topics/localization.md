This page collects what the sources say about localization: adapting an app, its content or its ads to another language or country.

## Definition

Localization means translating and adapting a product or its marketing for a new language or market. In these sources it covers three moves: copy a proven English app for a new country, translate ads to buy cheaper traffic, and repost winning content in more languages.

## Repeated patterns

### Clone a proven app for an unserved country

- [Cal Buddy](../businesses/cal-buddy.md) copied [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) for Israel, where Cal AI was not known. Being the first local version was the simplest zero-to-one move ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=143s)).
- Keep the product, change the brand. Cal Buddy's core is the same. The changes are language and how Israelis eat and talk about food ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=342s)).
- A small market needs a huge niche, so [Tomer](../people/tomer.md) picked fitness and nutrition ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=249s)).
- [Stoppr](../businesses/stoppr.md) cloned Quittr for Gen Z women in France. No one in France offered this kind of app, so [David Attias](../people/david-attias.md) used French influencers ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=118s)).
- Step one of his cloning playbook: find a very successful app that targets a niche or a region ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md)).
- [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md) is a gym app built for Spanish speakers in Spain and Mexico ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md)).

### Translate to buy cheaper traffic

- The [Gravl](../businesses/gravl.md) founders speak Spanish. They translated the app and ran ads in South America, which costs less than the US ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=424s)).
- Pat Walls says markets in many countries are still open. Founders test ads in cheaper countries such as Argentina first ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=756s)).

### Repost winning content in more languages

- [Clover Labs](../businesses/clover-labs.md) says each new country adds about a 20% view lift ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s)).
- Best markets: Spain, Germany and Australia, then Italy and Brazil. Adding Canada to US content is an easy 20% ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=933s)).
- AI dubs the speech, matches dialect, swaps text overlays and syncs lips. [Matt Espinoza](../people/matt-espinoza.md) rates it 98% of the way there ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=815s)).

### Price and paywalls by country

- [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md) uses ARPU by country to set how much it pays creators per download ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=539s)).
- [Pingo](../businesses/pingo.md) splits paywalls between the US and other countries, because it moves US billing to Stripe ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [47:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2866s)).
- Outside the US, Pingo's 7-day trial converts about 2 points higher than a 3-day trial ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [52:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3164s)). See [free trials](free-trials.md).

### Design for languages

- In Figma, keep copy as variables, with a second language as a mode. Keep language, theme and size in separate collections so each switches on its own ([XnI-WbMCDyw](../sources/XnI-WbMCDyw.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnI-WbMCDyw&t=129s)).
- Modes need the Figma Pro plan (2023-06) ([XnI-WbMCDyw](../sources/XnI-WbMCDyw.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XnI-WbMCDyw&t=96s)). See [design systems](design-systems.md).

## Concrete examples

- [Cal Buddy](../businesses/cal-buddy.md): about $1K in its first month (2025-06), over $81K/month (2026-08) ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=104s)).
- [Gravl](../businesses/gravl.md): early ads under $50/day in South America. The first ad got a subscription in 10 minutes ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=424s)). Over $440K/month later (2025-11) ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=71s)).
- [Stoppr](../businesses/stoppr.md): $5K in the first month, about $12K/month after 5 months (2025-10) ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=58s)).
- [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md): about $160K/month and 3M downloads (2026-09) ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=106s)).
- Clover redid a 37.4M-view Rhyme video in German, Rhyme's best market outside North America ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=815s)).

## Disagreements

- Cheap countries are not always worth it. Viral views can land where nobody pays. Pingo got about 200K Russian users in February and $0, because app stores block payments there ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=98s)).

> Conflict: [2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md) treats cheap non-US traffic as a way in. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) warns to track viewer country and payability, because some countries bring users but no revenue.
