This page collects what the sources say about paid ads on Facebook and Instagram, which Meta runs.

## Definition

Meta ads are paid ads on Facebook, Instagram and Reels. In these sources, founders use them to scale apps and SaaS tools after they find content that converts.

## Repeated patterns

### Organic content finds the winner, Meta scales it

- Run viral organic videos as paid ads. Or hire the organic creators to record paid UGC ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=218s)).
- [Snag](../businesses/snag.md) turns creator videos with over 50K views into Meta ad tests ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s)).
- SideShift moves a video to paid at about 5% engagement. It starts at about $100/day and judges over 14 days ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s)).
- Canyon looks for 5 to 10x the creator's normal views and 8 to 10% engagement ([SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3015s)).
- One boosted [Payout](../businesses/payout.md) video stayed profitable for a few weeks ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [25:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1504s)).
- The [MonAI](../businesses/monai.md) founder regrets that he waited to reuse creator videos as Meta ads ([rGLXc1GmsaI](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=990s)).
- [TalkNotes](../businesses/talknotes.md) reached about $1.5K MRR organically before ads ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=556s)).

### Creative is the main lever

- Yev says targeting tweaks give about 10%, and the right creative gives about 10x ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=785s)).
- At [Boost App Social](../businesses/boost-app-social.md), 1 video in 85 got the spend. About 97% of creatives failed ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=38s)).
- Above $300 to $400/day, creatives fatigue. [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md) tests about 20 a week ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=561s)).
- Keep making new creatives, because each one is "more tickets to the lottery" ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=539s)).
- [Gravl](../businesses/gravl.md) pays creators as little as $50 a piece. It copies what competitors spend most on in the Meta Ads Library ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=534s)).
- Marcus Burke's Moonate concept had about 400 iterations and over $1M in spend ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=603s)). His freelancers make 99% of client ads with AI (2026-06) ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1300s)). See [Ad creative](ad-creative.md).

### What the creative says

- Raw creatives win. Polished visuals got near-zero revenue for [Brawler](../businesses/brawler.md) ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=544s)).
- The best TalkNotes ad was a paid person who read a script in an office ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=654s)).
- Add captions. About 80% of users watch Instagram without sound ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s)).
- Say that it is an app, or show a demo. A surprise at the App Store hurts install rate ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=491s)).
- Use hyper-specific call-outs in the audience's words. [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) writes lines such as "hit your pen until 3 a.m." ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=307s)).
- Specific copy lets Meta find the right people with broad targeting ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=733s)).
- [Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md) hooks cold Meta traffic with a scary inside view of the body, not a long-term promise ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=386s)).

### Account setup and structure

- Set up an MMP first. Optimize for views, then clicks, installs, trials and subscriptions as data builds ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s)).
- Send early pixel events to Meta, such as registration and paywall view. Still optimize for purchase ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [34:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2090s)).
- With a small budget, put all ads in one ad set. Split only near break-even ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:54:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3269s)).
- Meta spends about 80% of an ad set on one ad. Move outlier ads into their own ad sets ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:47:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2849s)).
- A trial converts about 7 days after it starts, which is too late for Meta to learn from ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:45:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2733s)).
- Value rules can tell Meta that users aged 45+ are worth 30 to 40% more ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2622s)).
- Expect to burn about $2K in the first 2 weeks while the pixel learns ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).
- Start a test at about $50/day. If ROAS is above 1, raise it to $100, $200, then $300 ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s)).
- Yev's $5K launch plan puts 30 to 40 creatives on Facebook at low amounts. He picks the outlier on clicks within hours ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [32:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1939s)).
- Run Meta only above a floor of about $3 revenue per install. One app at $1 failed ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=990s)).

### Measurement

- Real purchases are often about double what ads report ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [22:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1336s)).
- Clear 30 adds a "where did you hear about us?" question next to its MMP ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=488s)).
- Young users give cheap trials but convert worst ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2501s)). At Clear 30, users aged 26 to 30 convert at almost double the rate of those aged 18 to 20 ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=380s)).
- A viral moment lowers paid CAC. After a 10M-view launch on X, Meta CAC fell by about 75% for a month ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [33:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2027s)).

### Platform risk

- Meta banned the Boost App Social accounts 6 to 8 times. The team won every account back ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=514s)).
- Ban risk and rising CPMs were a reason to sell the app ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2145s)).
- Meta and TikTok are auctions, so you must monetize as well as your rivals ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [20:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1206s)).

## Disagreements

- **Meta or TikTok for paid**. The [Maxed AI](../businesses/maxed-ai.md) founder prefers Meta because its buyers show higher intent ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s)). The AbMaxx founder got 2x to 2.5x ROAS on Meta and 4x to 5x on TikTok with the same creatives ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).
  > Conflict: [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) prefers Meta. [RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) got better ROAS on TikTok.
- Steven Cravotta found TikTok cheaper but lower quality, and Meta pricier but higher quality ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [19:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1148s)). Yev says TikTok works better for US targeting and Facebook for global targeting ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=964s)).
- Meta has an install button and attribution, but TikTok Spark ads do not ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2000s)). Meta partnership ads on small UGC accounts convert better than Spark ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2251s)). TikTok rejects peptide Spark ads, and Meta accepts them ([mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [50:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=3034s)).
- **Paid first or organic first**. Samuel Rondot starts with ads to test a market fast ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [8:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=496s)). Dan of [Massive](../businesses/massive.md) says paid ads give faster conversion data than UGC ([r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1178s)). Dan Kwon says not to touch paid until you have at least five winning formats ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [31:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1900s)). [Go Tall](../businesses/go-tall.md) waited until $30K/month ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)).
  > Conflict: [8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md) and [r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md) use paid ads early. [2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) and [EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md) wait for proven organic content.
- **Statics**. Marcus Burke says app promotion campaigns deliver statics poorly, so start with video ([GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:19:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1163s)). The best Pep AI ad is a static made in ChatGPT at under $2 per download ([mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=436s)).
  > Conflict: [GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md) says statics deliver poorly in app campaigns. [mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md) found a static ad was its best.
