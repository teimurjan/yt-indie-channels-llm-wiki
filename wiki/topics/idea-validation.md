Idea validation means proof that people want a product and will pay for it. Founders seek it before or soon after they build.

## Repeated patterns

### 1. Payment is the only real proof

- Traffic and booked calls do not count. A card swipe does ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
- "How much will you pay?" gives false comfort. Sell a painkiller, not a vitamin ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=443s)).
- Praise is weak proof. Five people paid for a buggy product, so [Fernando](../people/fernando.md) doubled down ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).
- The only proof of an idea is a launch with a way to pay ([D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s)).
- Friends pay for products that do not exist. Ten paying customers from outside your network are the real test ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=222s)).
- Add a paid tier early ([47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=448s)). Charge early, even at a low price ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s)).
- A prospect who asks the price and is ready to pay shows an idea worth pursuing ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=646s)).
- Prove that the product works and that people pay before you buy ads ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [7:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=473s)).

### 2. Sell before you build

- **[John Rush](../people/john-rush.md)'s blueprint.** Post about the pain, open a waitlist for 100 signups, then email a 90%-off pre-sale. At 5 sales, deliver by hand ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=215s)).
- **Tiered pre-sale.** Subscribr sold 50 lifetime licenses for $20,000 in 2 to 3 days. The price rose with every 10 licenses ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- **A $5 Stripe link.** Under 50 copied videos in 2 weeks gave Runafy 2,000 waitlist signups and 90+ payments of $5. Caleb Dean copied Liftoff's Reels format ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1472s)).
- **Locked launch price.** A $100 set offered at $69 captured 11,000 emails at a 46% capture rate ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=360s)).
- **Pre-order site plus ads.** Hush Blankets put up a pre-order page and bought the keyword "weighted blankets" on Google Ads ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s)).
  - About 300,000 people searched for the term. A later Kickstarter rode 3,000 people who said on the phone that they intended to buy ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)).
- **Cold email a sale.** [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md) sold to an escape-room owner after a 42-minute call, before he built ([WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=369s)).
- **Fake the backend.** For MakeLogo.ai, a Typeform collected the brief and the payment. First sales proved demand ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=422s)).
- **Do it by hand first.** Run an unscalable service, then automate it. Raise the price until demand stops being the problem ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=436s)).
- **A page of samples.** Karl Hughes sent a one-page writing portfolio to his network and asked people to pay him ([i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=156s)).
- **Charge, then interview.** Ship 30 for 30 began at $50 with a refund. After 50+ interviews it relaunched at $99 and made $30K in a month ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s)).

### 3. Copy a market that already pays

- **Competitors are a good sign.** No competitors means no market. Creating a market is "nearly impossible" ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [7:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=470s), [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=185s)).
- **Do not invent.** Big players prove demand. Find the gap they leave ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=163s)).
- **A modified copy is already proven.** [Connor Burd](../people/connor-burd.md) skips the waitlist for such apps and just builds ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [7:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=434s)).
- **Copy the app, change the niche.** [David Attias](../people/david-attias.md) cloned Quitter for sugar cravings and French users ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=237s)).
- **Size the market in Sensor Tower.** Thresholds vary by founder:
  - Top competitors make at least $100 to $200 a month ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)).
  - Many apps make over $10K a month ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=208s)).
  - One to three apps make at least $100K a month ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [54:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=3246s)).
  - Top-20 apps make $100K to $500K MRR ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)).
- **Use marketplaces as proof.** Acquire.com listings show real revenue ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=150s), [EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)).
- **Read competitor reviews.** Panic-attack app users said no app explained an attack in the moment ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=127s)).
  - Others mine Trustpilot, G2, Capterra and app reviews for gaps ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=485s), [hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=584s), [vbEKEWtnndU](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=600s)).
- **Follow the money.** Stripe screenshots on X and competitors that live on paid ads signal real demand ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=239s)).
  - Companies that hire creators on SideShift have money ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=657s)).
- **Improve a proven idea.** Pick an idea another company already proved, and charge from day one ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=728s)).
  - Improve a solved problem for an underserved niche, such as small restaurants ([a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=436s)).
  - Be 1% better in a proven market. Deven Bhooshan used Taplio daily and found its core weak ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=100s)).
  - Build the same app 10% better than a top earner on Sensor Tower ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=345s)).
  - Port a proven add-on to a new platform, as Data Fetcher did for Airtable ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s)).
- **Ask two questions.** Can you make a better product, or market it better ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=355s))?

### 4. Test the content before the product

- **A fake product video.** Pushscroll pitched the concept on TikTok as a real app. The test video got 80K views and about 500 comments ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=137s), [ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s)).
  - A bad video tells you nothing, because you cannot tell which part failed ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s)).
- **Count the "I need this" comments.** One talking-head video drew 15 such comments. [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) kept posting until hundreds asked ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=301s)).
- **Comment "app" test.** Run a faceless TikTok with 3 to 4 slideshows a day. Many comments after 7 days mean demand ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=208s)).
- **Content first, app second.** GlowUp's founder posted first and built once the content went viral ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s)).
  - Coherence tested ideas on X, then short-form, then YouTube ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s)).
- **No video can sell it? Kill the idea** ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s)).
- **Go viral with one account first, then build** ([eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [53:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3182s)).
- **The VSC test.** A pain point with over 100,000 views on several posts from accounts under 5,000 followers ([-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s)).
- **Paid tests on mockups.** [Roger Chen](../people/roger-chen.md) bought TikTok ads on mockup videos before launch ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=531s)).
- **Two-week app test.** Hire a few creators and post 30 to 50 videos. Look at retention and payment, not only downloads ([_a4ch3pk_U0](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [31:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=1894s)).
- **One video that sells.** One influencer video that produced purchases was Jake Glacer's signal ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=368s)).

### 5. Read search and keyword data

- **Gold mining.** Search data, Reddit threads and Claude prompts give an idea and a landing page in about 45 minutes ([L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=0s)).
  - "Co-parenting" has about 40,000 searches and a steady trend (2025-06) ([L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=429s)).
- **A fake landing page on a warm domain.** One keyword from Ahrefs, a fake AI caller demo and a "book demo" link ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=100s)).
- **App Store keywords.** Popularity of at least 20 and difficulty of 60 to 70 or lower ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)).
- **Triangulate.** Puff Count used Sensor Tower, Google Trends and viral TikTok videos ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=245s)).
- "Stop sugar" had risen for 5 years on Google Trends ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=278s)).

### 6. Listen in communities

- **Reddit.** Search a subreddit for your keywords ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)). Post the idea and count replies: 10 to 15 in r/baseballcards ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:04:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=292s)).
  - A no-price feedback post got 58 upvotes and lists of features ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=79s)).
  - People post Hinge profiles on Reddit for feedback. Sway made that private ([ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=207s)).
  - F5Bot emails you each Reddit mention of your keywords ([3X4VneEHvig](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=367s)).
- **Discord and Facebook groups.** A mockup of an unbuilt Shopify app got over 100 likes and download requests ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=341s)).
- Repeated questions in a Discord server were the demand signal for Algrow ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=331s)).
- **X and LinkedIn.** One tweet got about 400 retweets and 300 signups. The waitlist reached 1,800 emails in two weeks ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=352s)).
  - A tweet to executives brought 25 DMs for Storyarb ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)). A LinkedIn post proved demand for an idea Dan disliked ([r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=107s)).
  - A Skype shutdown made posts about alternatives go viral ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=249s)).
- **DMs as a signal.** 10 to 20 DMs a day asking for a height prediction showed real pain ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=396s)).
- **Comments under viral videos.** Ask whether people want to solve the problem or only laugh ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=78s)).

### 7. Talk to customers the right way

- **The Mom Test.** Ask how people solve the problem now and what it costs. Never ask whether they like the idea ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=623s)).
  - Validate with 5 to 10 target users before any lead generation ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=716s)).
- **A commitment metric with a target.** For Once, the bar was 10 events with real dates before any app code ([r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=510s)).
- **Audience, then community, then product.** [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md) learns pain points in a community before he builds ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- **Ask the hard questions.** Ask what users dislike and what they love about rivals ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)).
- **Interview friends about frequency.** Then ask four questions: can I do better, build it, monetize it and find clients ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=162s)).
- **Interview unhappy customers of incumbents.** 10 to 12 of them shaped Draft.dev's service ([i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [12:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=734s)).
- **Book many short calls.** [Sam Parr](../people/sam-parr.md) runs back-to-back 15-minute customer calls ([jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=377s)).
- **Ship imperfect, then ask.** Get even a poor product to customers and ask how to improve it ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [12:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=769s)).

### 8. Start from a pain you know

### 10. Filters that founders repeat

- **Painkiller, many sufferers, viral topic** ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=115s), [uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=458s)).
- **Pick a category that already goes viral on TikTok** ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s)).
- **Big value gap.** Dating is a painkiller. A 10% better calendar is a hard sell ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [26:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1599s)).
- **People already overpay.** Look where people pay a professional thousands ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=545s)).
- **Failure is costly for the user.** Athletes pay about $30 because missing weight costs more ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=780s)).
- **Monetized niches.** High spend on physical products, such as skin care, means software can sell there ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=459s)).
- **Maslow levels.** Height hits 2 of the 5 levels. More is better ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=456s)).
- **Manual tasks people pay for today** that AI can improve ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s)).
- **Four filters.** Value to others, market scale, your skill to build it and a profitable channel ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=266s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md) calls waitlists useless after a 40K to 50K list converted at about 0.5%. [0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) turned a 1,800-email waitlist into the first 100 customers, and [L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md) sets a 100-person waitlist bar.

> Conflict: [jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md) uses paid ads on mockups to test demand. [zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) quotes Pat Walls: ads test creative, not demand.

> Conflict: [3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md) says only a payment counts. [r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md) says the commitment metric need not be payment, only a signal of real usage.

> Conflict: [CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md) and [8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md) treat a copy of a working app as already proven. [sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) says copycats cap out at $10K to $100K a month, and [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) says copying another niche kills the fun.

> Conflict: [r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md) spends $1K to $2K on influencers to test a new app. [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) lost about $2K on an influencer test, and [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) got views but almost no conversions from paid influencers.
