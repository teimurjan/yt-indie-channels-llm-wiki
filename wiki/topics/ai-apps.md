AI apps are consumer and B2B products whose core feature runs on a large language model or another generative model, usually through a third-party API.

## Definition

An AI app takes a task people already pay for, such as calorie counting, writing, design or sales outreach, and lets a model do most of the work. Most apps in the wiki call OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or fal.ai rather than train their own models.

## Repeated patterns

- **Ride a new model or API release.** ChatGPT image upload created [Chart Detector AI](../businesses/chart-detector-ai.md) ([source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=233s)). [Ramsri Goutham](../people/ramsri-goutham.md) bet in 2020 that models would soon write good quizzes ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s)). Dillion Verma applies each new model type (text, voice, image, video) to existing demand ([source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [53:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3238s)). [Supergrow](../businesses/supergrow.md) started when GPT-3 could copy a user's writing style ([source](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=150s)).
- **Encode a manual task or expert rule.** Colin McIntosh turned a 3-hour resume review into an AI that runs hundreds of times a day ([source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=124s)). [CodeGuide](../businesses/codeguide.md) chained prompts that saved 7 of 9 to 10 hours per client project ([source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=306s)). Ramsri looks for manual tasks people pay for today that AI can replace ([source](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=387s)).
- **Show the AI moment on video.** Social Wizard streamers paste a story into the app and read the AI reply ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s)). 3AK's sprint analyzer gives an instant score ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [6:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=388s)). Natural Write videos turn "100% AI" text into "100% human" ([source](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=466s)). Gravl shows the AI in onboarding, "because AI sells" ([source](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=576s)).
- **Rebuild the workflow, do not bolt AI on.** Cal AI made the camera the home page ([source](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=313s)). [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) says to replace interfaces with outcomes, as [Intercom](../businesses/intercom.md)'s [Fin](../businesses/fin.md) did ([source](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=98s), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=303s)). He also suggests a "Trojan horse": put AI inside a familiar pattern, such as the existing search bar ([source](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=279s)).
- **The AI alone is not the product.** At [Sunflower](../businesses/sunflower.md) the sobriety timer matters most. Its AI companion alone had bad retention until it gained memory, follow-ups and clinician escalation ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [15:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=933s), [17:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1055s)). [Shepherd](../businesses/shepherd.md) removed "AI" from its copy after users pushed back ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [28:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1727s)).
- **Software is easy to copy, so the moat is elsewhere.** Distribution ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)), a personal brand ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=520s)), simplicity ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=224s)), full workflow coverage ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=439s)), outcome data the model labs lack ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [26:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=1567s)) and creative direction ([source](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=207s)). Tim Gabe says AI gains become the new floor for every competitor ([source](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=187s)).
- **Platform risk is real.** Microsoft offered to build Formula Bot's add-on for free, then announced its OpenAI partnership ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=760s)). About 5 to 10% of Formula Bot churn is because of ChatGPT (2024-02) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=823s)).
- **AI builds the app.** Founders ship with Cursor, Claude Code, Rork or Lovable. 3AK says an app can reach the App Store for under $500 ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=204s)). Subscribr's Claude Code writes about 90% of its code ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=796s)). Alex Finn asks the AI for one micro step at a time ([source](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=244s)).

## Costs and pricing

- **Model costs eat margin.** [Neural Frames](../businesses/neural-frames.md) paid about $45K/month for GPUs and video APIs, almost half of revenue (2025-07) ([source](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=599s)). [SEO Bot](../businesses/seo-bot.md) pays $30K/month in AI APIs on $100K/month, about 70% margin, against about 90% for non-AI tools (2025-05) ([source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s)). CodeGuide's OpenAI bill was $2,800 of $3,500 in monthly costs (2025-05) ([source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=807s)).
- **Light apps keep high margins.** GlowUp spends about $500/month on AI at about 90% margin (2025-12) ([source](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=644s)). Letterly spends about $5K on AI against about $200K on ads a month (2025-11) ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=676s)).
- **Price around compute.** CodeGuide skips a free trial because models are expensive ([source](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=761s)). YourMove AI puts a hard paywall on AI photos only ([source](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=392s)). Castmagic caps usage ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=408s)). Rabbit Holes AI moves costs to users with bring-your-own-key and sells once ([source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=225s)). Formula Bot spent about $5,000 on OpenAI in days after going viral (2022) ([source](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=465s)).

## Disagreements

- **Copy a winner or build something new?** Blake Anderson says copycats cap out at $10K to $100K a month ([source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=518s)). Cal Buddy copied Cal AI for Israel and passed $81K/month (2026-08) ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=104s)). Letterly's founder took a tested model and made it simpler ([source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=381s)).
> Conflict: [sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) says copies have a low ceiling; [GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) and [wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md) built large apps from copied ideas.
- **AI-made marketing content.** Flame's founder scales formats with AI video ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s)), and Will makes 10 to 20 AI variants per format ([source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=249s)). SideShift says AI UGC gets "is this AI?" comments, while real creators get "what is the app?" ([source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s)).
> Conflict: [-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md) and [zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) use AI-generated content to scale; [F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) says AI UGC does not convert.
- **AI tool directories.** A Futurepedia listing brought StealthGPT's first customers ([source](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=465s)). John Rush says directories work best for "clickbaity" AI products ([source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s)). Julian of [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md) says directories are saturated now ([source](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=298s)).
> Conflict: [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) and [En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) recommend AI directories; [zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md) calls them saturated.
- **Subscription or one-time payment.** Most apps here sell subscriptions. Parakeet AI sells one-time credits because rivals charge monthly ([source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=756s)). Rabbit Holes AI moved to a one-time price after freemium failed ([source](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=162s)). AudioPen sells non-recurring plans that users choose to renew (2025-12) ([source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=120s)).
