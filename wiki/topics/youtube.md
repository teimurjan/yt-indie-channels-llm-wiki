# YouTube

YouTube shows up in the sources two ways: as a long-form search and trust channel that sells software and products, and as a media business that earns from ads, affiliates and sponsors.

## Definition

Using YouTube videos, your own or paid creators', to get customers, or running channels as the business itself.

## Repeated patterns

### Search beats virality for selling

- **Search traffic is high intent.** People who search already want a solution. In low-competition niches a weak video can rank #1 ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=252s)).
- **Low views still convert.** A BulkMockup video with 370 views brought 3 customers; "ignore views, each video is an asset" ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s)). A 1.8K-view video drove 80% of Local Rank's $20K MRR launch, while a 30K-view tweet drove 20% ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)).
- **YouTube videos rank on Google.** 22% of BulkMockup's channel views come from Google search (2026-02) ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)). A two-year-old Arvow video gets 20% of its traffic from Google (2025-10) ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=486s)).
- **Put the keyword in title, description and the first seconds** of speech ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=506s); [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)).
- **One brand can hold several top results.** Unlike Google, YouTube lets you stack videos and channels on one keyword ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=672s)).
- **A video per landing page.** VEED built about 500 landing pages for terms like "trim video", each with its own YouTube video ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)).

### Long video builds trust

- Long videos of your face and voice build the trust that makes people buy ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=159s)). Pat Walls calls YouTube the most important part of Starter Story's business for that reason ([GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=544s)).
- Old videos keep converting 3 years later, and long videos can be cut into shorts and ads ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)).
- Most Setter AI customers watched at least one video before a sales call ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)).
- A 45-minute template walkthrough lifted Ultimate Brain to about $100K/month ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).

### Pay creators

- Loïc Berthelot works with more than 500 YouTube creators; YouTube brings over 60% of new customers (2025-11). He qualifies creators at views/followers over 10% and 100+ comments per video ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s)).
- Boot.dev scaled past $30K/month with paid integrations, mostly on gaming channels ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [9:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=541s)).
- Atlas paid creators for videos, cut them into shorts and ads, then added paid ads ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)).
- Arvow runs owned niche channels fronted by hired Upwork creators and boosts proven videos with in-feed ads ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=216s), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=321s)).

### Repurpose

- One long video a week becomes shorts, blog posts and emails ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)). Charlie Chang cuts shorts with Submagic, Descript and Opus ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=430s)).

### Polish does not matter much

- Matt Giovanisci's top video each year is an unscripted PowerPoint ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)). Vasco recorded whiteboard videos in his basement ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=548s)). Jacky Chou: thirty bad videos teach more than two perfect ones ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s)).

### Channels as a business

- Revenue mix varies. Charlie Chang: affiliate 50% to 55%, sponsors 20% to 25%, AdSense 20% to 25% (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)). Shervin: 80% affiliate, 20% AdSense, no sponsors (2023-04) ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)). Swim University: courses and a printed book bring most of the money ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s)).
- Content as a funnel for your own products: Epic Gardening sold the raised bed its audience asked about ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)). Florin Pop: content gets attention, attention sells products ([47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s)).
- Shorts can reach huge numbers but earn little. Smart Nonsense hit 1B views and 1M subscribers in about a year but earns about $5K/month against a $25K to $30K/month team (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=125s), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)).

## Beginner plans

- Post one video every day for 45 days ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=548s)).
- Post one long and one short video daily for six months in a narrow niche ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s)).
- Map buyer questions on an awareness ladder and make a video per keyword ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=390s)).

## Disagreements

- **Cadence.** Shervin sees weekly, twice weekly or twice monthly work ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=322s)). Jacky Chou and Vasco post daily ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s); [VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=634s)).

> Conflict: [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) says weekly or less works for a creator channel; [spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md) and [VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md) push daily uploads for a product channel.

- **Sponsors.** Shervin holds off on sponsors to protect watch time ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)). Charlie Chang earns 20% to 25% from sponsors ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)).

> Conflict: [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) avoids sponsor reads; [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) treats sponsors as a core revenue line.

- **Sell to your audience or not.** Thomas Frank and Kevin Espiritu sell products to their own viewers ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md); [uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)). Sebastian Ghiorghiu, with nearly 1M subscribers, built Atlas without promoting it to his fans, because influencer SaaS products "come and go" ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=284s)).

> Conflict: [OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) and [uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) monetize their own audience; [laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md) keeps the product apart from the audience.

- **YouTube versus TikTok.** Loïc says TikTok builds awareness but does not convert, and YouTube has the best ROI ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)). See [Short-form video](short-form-video.md) for founders who grow mostly on TikTok.
