A landing page is a single web page built to make a visitor take one action: sign up, book a call, join a waitlist or buy. Sources split into design craft (mostly [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md)) and founders who use pages to validate ideas and capture search traffic.

## Definition

A landing page states the offer, proves it and asks for one action. Tim calls the hero "your first sales pitch": it must catch attention, say the message and convert ([m3NjcBDqFnA](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=0s)).

## Repeated patterns

### One message, one action

- **One call to action.** In an Unbounce study of 18,000+ pages, one CTA converted at 13.5%, two at 11.9% and three at 10.5% ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=34s)).
- **Pick the desired action first:** a call, a purchase, browsing or a signup ([m3NjcBDqFnA](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md), [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=276s)). In the Midjourney redesign, "Join the beta" is the only filled button ([_n39rxieClg](../sources/_n39rxieClg.md), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=194s)).
- **Sell the outcome, not the tool.** "Close tickets three times faster without switching tabs" beats a feature list. Jobs sold "1,000 songs in your pocket" ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=189s), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=250s)).
- **Call out the customer in the H1.** [Rabbit Holes AI](../businesses/rabbit-holes-ai.md) names power AI users in the headline ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [7:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=468s)).
- **Spend 80% of design time above the fold.** The hero needs the full message in seconds: a headline on time, money or pain saved, a subheading and a big CTA ([jpSY4MlWX50](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=475s)).
- **Lead with the product's output.** A generative tool's hero should show a grid of results ([_n39rxieClg](../sources/_n39rxieClg.md), [0:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n39rxieClg&t=27s)). Sketch lets visitors drag real interface parts on its page ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s)).

### Copy that fits

- Heading 40 to 60 characters, body at most 140 to 170 ([m3NjcBDqFnA](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=85s), [2:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=145s)).
- "Goldilocks" copy: enough to answer objections, no more ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=300s)). Short, sweet messages get read ([cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=112s)).
- Use the customer's own words from Reddit threads ([L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=105s)).
- Section structure: pain or outcome headline, a subheading with a new angle, a product visual, proof points, one CTA. Then a scan test: does a fast scroller get what, why and what next? ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=363s), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=414s)).

### Build from references and systems

- Split research into "look and feel" and "structure". Stack borrowed sections as a "Frankenstein wireframe" ([RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=140s), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=327s)). Sources: Lapa Ninja, Land-book, Dribbble, Awwwards ([RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=78s), [m3NjcBDqFnA](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=307s)).
- Trace a locked reference at low opacity and match its text styles and copy length ([vBuyR8uF380](../sources/vBuyR8uF380.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=94s)).
- 8-point spacing and a 12-column grid ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=580s), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=747s), [vBuyR8uF380](../sources/vBuyR8uF380.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=215s)). Container max width 960px, 160px section padding, text blocks under 600px ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=299s), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=400s)).
- Stick to the grid, then break it on purpose: arced text, edge-bleeding carousels, off-grid type ([vBuyR8uF380](../sources/vBuyR8uF380.md), [13:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBuyR8uF380&t=821s), [PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=472s), [evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=15s)).
- Assets make up more than 50% of a layout ([RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=509s)). Strong color only on the main CTA ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=327s)). See [Typography](typography.md).

### Motion and Framer

- Hero tricks in Framer: zoom on load (scale 0.85, 2s), particles, big text between a cut-out character and the background, a Lottie scroll icon, staggered springs ([m2NXQjKPUrg](../sources/m2NXQjKPUrg.md), [4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2NXQjKPUrg&t=253s), [7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2NXQjKPUrg&t=433s), [12:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2NXQjKPUrg&t=721s)). The same two-layer depth trick drives a cinematic movie site ([R33gaV3EV1I](../sources/R33gaV3EV1I.md), [1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R33gaV3EV1I&t=67s)).
- Figma prototypes of award-winning animations: text masks, widening image masks, product reveals. 800 ms fade-in and 300 ms ease-out steps ([NfqdWTfNYKI](../sources/NfqdWTfNYKI.md), [2:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NfqdWTfNYKI&t=160s), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NfqdWTfNYKI&t=344s)).
- Framer basics: think in boxes, use stacks, set breakpoints (960px and 560px in his course), make the page a stack so sections reorder ([wgMgje60Ovw](../sources/wgMgje60Ovw.md), [0:01:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wgMgje60Ovw&t=97s), [1:05:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wgMgje60Ovw&t=3942s), [1:42:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wgMgje60Ovw&t=6144s)).
- Test on mobile and slow devices. Heavy animated sites fail on old phones ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=139s), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=516s)).

### Speed with templates and AI

- Framer AI generates a base page in under 2 minutes. Layout, assets and motion still need manual work ([U2EVNlDzAJQ](../sources/U2EVNlDzAJQ.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=0s)).
- A free Framer template, Coolors, Midjourney and Canva gave a "$5K website" in 5 minutes 22 seconds. Sortlist puts such sites at up to $10K freelance and $20K to $28K agency (2024-07) ([hCKdIwbhS-s](../sources/hCKdIwbhS-s.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=0s), [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCKdIwbhS-s&t=382s)).
- A browser extension copies live layouts into Framer: 6 sites in about 2 minutes. Use it only on your own sites or free templates ([bBamB0yE12c](../sources/bBamB0yE12c.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=125s), [3:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=189s)).
- AI does 80% of a landing page. High-end results still need a designer ([bOm9GDv96Tk](../sources/bOm9GDv96Tk.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOm9GDv96Tk&t=357s)).
- A founder who cannot code shipped [Creator Hunter](../businesses/creator-hunter.md) from a free Framer template ([jpSY4MlWX50](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=440s)).

### Pages as validation and acquisition tools

- **Fake page on a warm domain.** [Setter AI](../businesses/setter-ai.md) put one H1 keyword ("cold call automation"), a fake AI caller demo and a "book demo" link on an old indexed domain. The first $500 deposit came before the product existed (2024-02) ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=100s), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)).
- **AI-built page plus quiz waitlist.** Mine Reddit pain points, have Claude write a Lovable prompt, then set a threshold such as 100 sign-ups before building ([L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md), [17:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1074s), [20:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L_FY6aW9cJ4&t=1232s)).
- **One page per keyword.** [Late](../businesses/late.md) writes a page for each high-intent keyword. Pages that rank get free traffic, and the rest get Google Ads. Each ad group gets its own page ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=381s), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s)).
- **Always send ads to a landing page.** [Joe Popelas](../people/joe-popelas.md) uses it for a value stack and chapter previews. Bounce above 70% to 80% means it looks unprofessional ([irEi7DVBHdA](../sources/irEi7DVBHdA.md), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=423s), [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irEi7DVBHdA&t=484s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: one CTA or two. [TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md) cites Unbounce data for a single CTA, and [_n39rxieClg](../sources/_n39rxieClg.md) keeps one filled button. The same [TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md) later pairs a primary "try for free" with a secondary "watch a demo" for complex SaaS, plus chat "escape valves" ([7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=472s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=533s)).

> Conflict: experience or conversion. [R33gaV3EV1I](../sources/R33gaV3EV1I.md) and [m2NXQjKPUrg](../sources/m2NXQjKPUrg.md) build cinematic, animation-heavy heroes. [evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md) warns that experience-first sites like Strides can convert less and favors Jitter's minimal page.

> Conflict: what validates a page. [L_FY6aW9cJ4](../sources/L_FY6aW9cJ4.md) treats ~100 waitlist sign-ups as the go signal. [3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md) says traffic and booked calls do not count. Only a card payment does.

> Conflict: copy length. [cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md) and [m3NjcBDqFnA](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md) push short copy. [TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md) says too little copy leaves objections open.
