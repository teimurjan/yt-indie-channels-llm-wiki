Micro-interactions are the small animated responses to a user action, such as a hover glow, a button press, a bounce or a celebration after a task.

## Definition

A micro-interaction is one trigger, one state change and the motion between the states. All eight sources come from [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md): five are Figma and Framer tutorials, three argue why the details matter for growth.

## Repeated patterns

- **One component, two states.** Make the element a component, add a hover, pressed or variant state, and let the tool animate the change [source](../sources/inkIfPtmE9s.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inkIfPtmE9s&t=0s), [source](../sources/2Mvmz34QsLQ.md) [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=293s).
- **Nest components so interactions travel.** A hover button keeps working inside a card or dropdown when the interaction lives on its variants [source](../sources/2Mvmz34QsLQ.md) [5:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=356s), [source](../sources/Z-Jic1TrMZQ.md) [0:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z-Jic1TrMZQ&t=17s).
- **Animate with clipping.** Most effects move or resize layers inside a clipped frame: card reveals, slide stacks, the Dynamic Island player, the "shine" sweep [source](../sources/2Mvmz34QsLQ.md) [6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=417s), [source](../sources/dL-U6bkm0a0.md) [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL-U6bkm0a0&t=100s), [source](../sources/inkIfPtmE9s.md) [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inkIfPtmE9s&t=208s).
- **Tune the spring.** Stiffness, damping and mass set the feel; physical bounce makes software feel like an object with weight [source](../sources/inkIfPtmE9s.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inkIfPtmE9s&t=300s), [source](../sources/dL-U6bkm0a0.md) [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dL-U6bkm0a0&t=240s), [source](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md) [4:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=244s).
- **Feedback after every repeat action.** A small bounce, glow or sparkle after a check-in or habit log; animate streaks and progress [source](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=245s).
- **Polish builds trust in high-stakes apps.** In finance, crypto and health, every micro-interaction is a trust signal [source](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=471s).
- **Teach with cues, not tutorials.** Apple's home indicator line shows users to swipe [source](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=203s).
- **Emotional design as a moat.** Purely functional apps lose users to a competitor's sale; motion makes the app hard to replace [source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md) [10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=612s).

## Concrete examples with numbers

- Duolingo added a character animation system in 2022. Daily active users then grew from 14.2M to over 34M within 2 years, and paid subscribers more than doubled. Tim notes curriculum and marketing also helped [source](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=123s), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=185s).
- Phantom animated its ghost mascot in mid-2023 and became the #2 US utilities app, above WhatsApp and Instagram (2025-05) [source](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=335s), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=390s).
- First impressions form in about 50 ms and trust decisions in under 3 seconds; a bad first impression can lose 20 to 30% of new users [source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md) [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=98s).
- Figma timings: button hover 300 ms ease in; page swap 800 ms; dropdown 150 ms dissolve; loading dots loop with a 1 ms "after delay" [source](../sources/2Mvmz34QsLQ.md) [2:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=123s), [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=293s), [13:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=802s), [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Mvmz34QsLQ&t=1112s).
- Framer 3D press button: bottom padding 6 at rest, 2 when pressed, shadow 15% to 5% [source](../sources/inkIfPtmE9s.md) [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inkIfPtmE9s&t=365s), [8:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inkIfPtmE9s&t=480s).
- Framer image hover: transition damping of 140 for a smoother shine [source](../sources/Z-Jic1TrMZQ.md) [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z-Jic1TrMZQ&t=349s).
- Sketch lets visitors drag real interface parts on its landing page; small hover effects on tabs make a page "a pleasure to scroll" [source](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md) [3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=199s).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md) and [doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md) treat motion and transitions as core features. [evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md) warns that heavy animated sites fail on old phones and slow internet, and that experience-first sites can convert less than plain ones.
