# Offshoring

Offshoring is hiring full-time staff in lower-cost countries, either directly or through a staffing firm, to run work that a US hire would cost several times more to do.

## Definition

Founders hire remote, full-time team members abroad for editing, development, support or operations, or build a business that places such staff with clients.

## Repeated patterns

- **Cost gap.** ListKit's developers in Ukraine and support in Lebanon cost $1,000 to $2,000/month, against $5,000 to $8,000/month for a US hire (2024-06) ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=653s)). A Paired full-time hire costs about $600 to $1,000/month (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)).
- **Offshore staff as real team members.** Oceans staff work 40 hours a week, long term, and join the client's Slack, calendar and all-hands ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- **Heavy screening.** Paired recruiters screen 300 to 500 people to present one or two ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)). Oceans hires smart operators, not former EAs, and trains them ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=871s)).
- **Covid opened the market.** Remote work became normal for small firms, so doctors, accountants, law firms and agencies now offshore ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=306s)).
- **Hiring channels.** Upwork, Fiverr and OnlineJobs ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)).

## Concrete examples

- [Charlie Chang](../people/charlie-chang.md): a 26-person media team, mostly overseas, at about $20K to $25K/month for channels making $200K to $250K/month (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s)).
- [Paired](../businesses/paired.md): Charlie's staffing firm, started 2023, low six figures, with a one-time placement fee (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s)).
- [Oceans](../businesses/oceans.md): staff mostly in Sri Lanka. $650K in year one (2022), about $15M last year, no outside money, hiring 30 to 40 people a month (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)). It charges about $3,000 to $4,000/month per headcount and pays wages and benefits from that ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=155s)).
- [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md): about 40 people, 20 in support and sales, with offshore developers and support (2024-06) ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)).

## Building an offshoring business

- **Niche down.** [Ian Myers](../people/ian-myers.md) gets 10 sales emails a day from rivals. He suggests a vertical such as video editing or CAD for architects, worth $1M to $2M revenue ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=366s)).
- **Start with three hires and three friends,** at no profit, then build for referrals ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s), [10:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=618s)).
- **Use an audience to build equity.** Charlie started Paired to own a traditional business he can grow with his channels ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=570s)).

## Disagreements

- Price per offshore hire differs by model: about $600 to $1,000/month with Paired's one-time placement fee (2024-02) ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)), against $3,000 to $4,000/month for Oceans' managed staff (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=155s)). These are different services, not a direct contradiction.
