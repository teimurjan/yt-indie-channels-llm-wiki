This page collects what the sources say about boring businesses: plain, unglamorous products that solve a painful, often B2B or admin problem.

## Definition

A boring business sells a dull product, such as a database, a job board or a tax workflow, into real demand with few capable competitors. The sources argue that people pay well to make a stressful problem go away. With strong marketing, the product can stay simple.

## Repeated patterns

### Start from pain that people already pay for

- [Bohdan Drozdov](../people/bohdan-drozdov.md)'s playbook: start where people are stressed and already overpay. Check whether they DIY from blog posts and Reddit or pay a professional thousands ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [9:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=545s)).
- Pat Walls argues that directories rarely work as B2C. A founder who cannot find investors cannot make payroll, so they pay ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)).
- In taxes, the value is concrete: show the likely savings next to the price ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=317s)).

### Productize one manual workflow

- [Rashid Khasanov](../people/rashid-khasanov.md) built an investor list by hand for his own raise. He turned that step into [AngelMatch](../businesses/angelmatch.md) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).
- [Savvy Nomad](../businesses/savvy-nomad.md) did only one workflow: the state domicile change. Add a recurring layer on top ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=656s)).
- A friend of [Roman Lakhnyuk](../people/roman-lakhnyuk.md) found a job with a scraper of company career pages. That process became [Career Hound](../businesses/career-hound.md) ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=215s)).
- Rashid's database playbook: pick a database for a specific problem, collect the data by hand or with automation, then launch and lean on programmatic SEO ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).

### A plain product, won by marketing

- Career Hound's product is plain, so marketing decides it. Roman wins on content that spikes emotion, built on hours of niche research ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=310s)).
- Programmatic SEO brings about half of Rashid's revenue (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- Low-tech stacks keep costs down. Rashid's tools cost about $62/month ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [10:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=620s)). Savvy Nomad runs the whole product on Bubble, so non-engineers can operate it ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s)).

### Pick a niche you belong to

- Rashid targeted early-stage founders because he was one ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=463s)).
- Savvy Nomad's market fit the team's strengths. Bo had worked on a previous digital-nomad tax startup ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=272s), [3:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=216s)).

Idea lists from the sources:

- Productize immigration workflows. Agencies charge $8K to $15K, and a guided product with a lawyer review can charge $1K to $2K ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=387s)).
- Productize entry to tax-friendly places and exit from sticky ones. Or offer international banking and estate planning, which gives one customer several revenue events ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [7:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=449s)).
- An influencer database sorted by location, audience size and niche, or a newsletter sponsorship database ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [8:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=503s)).

Pat Walls: "I'd rather make $2 million a year in a boring business than $20,000 a year in a sexy AI app" ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=810s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: B2B or consumer. [5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md) says directories rarely work as B2C, so AngelMatch sells to founders. [xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md) sells Career Hound to individual job seekers at $7/week, though churn is 31% because users stop after they find a job.
