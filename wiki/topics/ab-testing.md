A/B testing shows two or more versions of a paywall, flow, ad or page to different users. You keep the version that earns more.

## Definition

An A/B test splits traffic between a control and one or more variants and compares a target metric. In these sources the metric is usually paywall conversion, revenue per user (ARPU) or retention. Most tests run on mobile app paywalls, often through Superwall.

## Repeated patterns

### Test where the traffic and money are

- **Paywalls first.** A paywall test touches most users, and a theme toggle touches about 2% ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=361s)).
- **Fix the worst drop-off.** Fixing a funnel step with over 50% drop-off gave 2x to 3x growth. Feature work gave about 10% ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=156s)).
- **Macro changes before micro changes.** How often the paywall shows and how hard it is matter more than copy. Micro tests need months of volume ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1357s)).

### Test structure before price

- Test localization, trial setup and plan count before $9.99 against $12.99. In Adapty data, localization gave +62.3% LTV and trial structure +59.6% ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s)).
- Start from a plain bullet-list paywall as the baseline. Price tests need cohort management, so test design and packaging first ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [32:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1962s), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2171s)).
- Homework from Tim Gabe: keep the price and change only the structure, such as which plan comes first ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)).

### Speed and volume win

- Apps that run 50+ paywall tests a year make 18.7x the revenue of apps that run one ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=638s)).
- [Pregnancy+](../businesses/pregnancy-plus.md) went from 1 test every 3 months to 3+ a month once tests became fast to ship ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s)).
- [Flo](../businesses/flo.md) ran over 100 experiments a month ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [48:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2902s)).
- Buy cheap traffic to feed tests. Part of the ad budget buys tens of thousands of installs as data for onboarding and paywall tests ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1024s)).

### Most tests lose

- Out of 20 tests at [Social](../businesses/social-app-studio.md): 10 neutral, 5 bad, 3 interesting, 2 wins ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [14:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=898s)).
- At [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md), two home-screen redesigns moved nothing. One commitment screen raised retention by 3% ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)).
- Retest old losers. A test that failed twice won on the third try, because the rest of the app had changed ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [16:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=977s)).
- Expectations are often wrong. Pregnancy+ expected a discounted intro price to win, but a free trial made 140% more revenue ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)).

### Run the test well

- **Write the hypothesis and guardrails.** Start from a problem in user interviews. Run control against variants for at least 2 weeks ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=663s)).
- **Log each test with its hypothesis**, so agents and new hires can reuse the principle ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [18:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=1138s)).
- **One variable at a time.** Headlines first, then countries and devices, then in-app flows per country ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [25:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1500s)).
- **Split cohorts.** [PushScroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md) tests US, under-18 and 18+ users apart, with ARPU as the north star ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:52:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3166s)).
- **Pick the metric that pays.** [DuckMath](../businesses/duckmath.md) earns from ads, so it tests for time on site and pages per visit ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=252s)).
- **Use event analytics, not vanity metrics** ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=789s)).

### Beyond paywalls

- Ads: test Google Ads headlines, descriptions and landing pages all the time. Use 3 to 5 copy variants per ad group ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=668s)). Meta tests start at about $50/day and scale on ROAS above 1 ([P4QodeA_lQ0](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md), [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s)).
- Organic video: educational content is a pure A/B test on hook, saves and shares ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=760s)). Give organic tests at least 90 days ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [42:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=2574s)).
- UX details: test timing, emojis against icons, and animated against static feedback ([11h4H3TAGkM](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md), [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=613s)). Test onboarding paths per emotional persona ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=321s)).
- Pricing ceiling: below $10/month, test higher prices on 10% to 20% of traffic ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1861s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: trial length. [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) found a 7-day trial beat 3 days (2025-10). [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) found 3-day and 7-day trials within 0.3 to 0.7 points in the US, and 7 days about 2 points higher only abroad (2026-04).

> Conflict: trial or no trial. [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) found a free trial made 140% more revenue than a discounted intro price, and [rGLXc1GmsaI](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md) kept a 7-day trial. [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) shows Claim removing its trial and going paid-only because that made more money.

> Conflict: test length. [OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md) runs each test for at least 2 weeks, and [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) says micro tests need months of volume. [lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md) moves through each test layer within a week on bought traffic.

> Conflict: when to test price. [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) and [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) test structure before price. [u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) tells apps under $10/month to test higher prices early to find the ceiling.
