This page collects what sources say about the lifestyle business: a company built to fit the founder's life, not to maximize growth or raise money.

## Definition

A lifestyle business is designed around the life the founder wants. Calm, control and sustainable hours come before scale, investors or an exit.

## Repeated patterns

- **Choose the life first, then the business.** [Milly Tamati](../people/milly-tamati.md) avoids products with thousands of support-heavy customers because they do not fit her walks in the forest. ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)) [Ian Myers](../people/ian-myers.md) left a venture-backed app company because he wanted a business that served his life, not a board. ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=235s))
- **No investors, full control.** Milly has no investors, and her small team are her friends. ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)) Oceans raised no money, and Ian owned 100% at the start. ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)) [Paul Millerd](../people/paul-millerd.md) turned down Penguin Random House to keep creative control and higher royalties. ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=240s))
- **Sustainable hours.** Milly does creative work in the morning, takes a 40-minute lunch walk and stops at about 3 p.m. ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=248s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=458s)) [Thomas Sanlis](../people/thomas-sanlis.md) does not work 10-hour days and treats the business as a marathon. ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=607s))
- **Calm as a metric.** Milly makes calm the main KPI, because she wants the business to last decades. ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s))
- **Rest to find the right work.** Paul recommends a 1 to 3 month sabbatical, and quitting or delegating work that does not light you up. ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=420s))

## Disagreements

- **How big a lifestyle business can be.** Milly keeps Generalist World small on purpose with a very small team. ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=392s)) Oceans also started as a life-first business, but it hires 30 to 40 people a month (2025-03). ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s))

> Conflict: [ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md) treats staying small as part of the lifestyle choice, while [gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md) shows a business that rejected investors for lifestyle reasons yet grew to about $15M and a large team.
