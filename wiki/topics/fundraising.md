This page collects what the sources say about fundraising: raising outside money from family, angels, accelerators or venture funds.

## Definition

Fundraising means selling equity in a company for cash. In these sources it ranges from a family-and-friends round to a Series A. Most founders here bootstrap first, and several raise only after they have traction or distribution.

## Repeated patterns

### Traction and distribution come before the check

- [An Nayal](../people/an-nayal.md) raised [Flame](../businesses/flame.md)'s day-30 retention to 50%. He still got no term sheet from top VCs or a16z Speedrun, because he had no distribution (2023) ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=845s), [0:21:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1279s)).
- Y Combinator rejected [VEED](../businesses/veed.md) after the interview and asked why it did not charge. The founders added payments and got 20 paid users in a weekend. YC rejected them again ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=519s), [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=581s)). VEED later grew to $40M in revenue without that check (2025-05) ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)).
- [Koby Conrad](../people/koby-conrad.md)'s raise deck showed revenue growth, proof of outcomes and pilot LOIs, not usage ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [41:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2499s)).

### Bootstrap first, raise later

- [StudyFetch](../businesses/studyfetch.md) bootstrapped for about 18 months on organic video. It then skipped seed. It raised an $11.5M Series A from Owl Ventures and the College Board ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=498s)). The money went to scaling creators and brand work such as cafe pop-ups ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [36:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2166s)).
- [Sunflower](../businesses/sunflower.md)'s founder put in $100K of his own money first and raised about $350K. Total funding later reached about $6.5M from YC, a16z Speedrun, Flybridge and angels ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [38:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2309s), [40:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2406s)).
- [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md) stays bootstrapped at over $120K MRR (2024-06). One co-founder's past startups raised over $80M, and [Ramon Berrios](../people/ramon-berrios.md) tried to raise for an earlier startup for almost 4 years ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=629s)).

### Make the money last

- [VEED](../businesses/veed.md) had about one year of runway. The founders asked Crisp to cut its price from $100 to $20/month. Crisp agreed ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=241s)). Later both founders took jobs and put half of each salary into the company ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=346s)).
- After a $100K check from Jason Calacanis, the [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md) founders moved to Malaysia to cut burn ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)).
- Jenni tested many paths up to $1M ARR. From $1M to $3M it put money only into what worked ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [11:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=682s)).

### Checks come through people

- A scout for Jason Calacanis heard [David Park](../people/david-park.md) on a small podcast. That led to Jenni's first $100K check ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=355s)).
- After YC, [Pingo](../businesses/pingo.md) booked about 100 investor meetings and closed a round in about 3 days ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [55:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3301s)).
- [Zuhair Lani](../people/zuhair-lani.md) raised $1M from the a16z Speedrun program for [Double Speed](../businesses/double-speed.md) (2025-10) ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [27:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1651s)).

### The investor side

- [Alex Pattis](../people/alex-pattis.md) runs a syndicate. Each deal gets its own SPV, and LPs join from $1,000. He deployed about $60M into 270+ companies as a side project (2023-11) ([p3xa6mpxvOY](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=0s), [1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=92s)).
- He judges founder-market fit first, then market size, early product-market fit, then who co-invests ([p3xa6mpxvOY](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=666s)).
- He earns 20% carry on profits. One 50x to 250x winner must pay for 10 to 50 losers ([p3xa6mpxvOY](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=895s)).

### Fundraising pain as a business

- [Rashid Khasanov](../people/rashid-khasanov.md) built an investor list by hand for his own raise. He turned that pain into [AngelMatch](../businesses/angelmatch.md), a database of about 125,000 angels and VCs at $29K MRR (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=98s)).
- Pat Walls says founders pay for it because a founder who cannot find investors cannot make payroll ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [12:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=755s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: raise or stay bootstrapped. [b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) (Castmagic) and [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) (VEED) grew large without outside money. [zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) (Sunflower) and [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) (StudyFetch) raised millions to scale creator programs.

> Conflict: what investors want to see. [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) says strong retention was not enough without distribution. [PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md) had 30,000 monthly users and still lacked revenue. [zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) pitched revenue growth and outcomes, not usage.
