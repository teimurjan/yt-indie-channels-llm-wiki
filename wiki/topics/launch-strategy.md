# Launch strategy

Launch strategy is how founders put a new product in front of buyers on day one: who they warm up first, where they post, and what offer makes people pay right away.

## Definition

The plan for the first days of sales: the audience or waitlist built before launch, the launch channels (X, Hacker News, Product Hunt, Reddit, lifetime-deal platforms, App Store pre-orders), and the offer that turns attention into payments.

## Repeated patterns

### Warm an audience or list before launch day

- **Announce a date and be live all day.** Alex Finn posted for 3 years, about 5 minutes a day, then announced Creator Buddy 12 days ahead and hosted X Spaces on launch day. It hit $100K ARR in 15 minutes and $300K ARR in 2 weeks (2025-01) ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=556s)).
- **Nurture a waitlist by email, no CTA until launch.** Kleo sent more than 10 emails in 4 weeks, then put the CTA in the first line on launch day. $30K MRR in 4 days, about $60K/month within 2 months (2026-01) ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=0s), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=451s)).
- **Curiosity emails.** Floga ran a 5-week sequence that revealed the app "behind the red curtain" and hid the price until launch day. $117K by end of day one, over $120K in 24 hours (2025-05) ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=83s), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=516s), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=578s)).
- **Locked launch price for sign-ups.** Sheets & Giggles offered a $100 set at $69 and captured 11,000 emails at a 46% rate; 450 customers bought on day one ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=360s), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s)). Its rule: plan 30% of a crowdfunding goal on day one, and expect about 3% of a list to convert ([dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s)).
- **One email to a waitlist.** Ultimate Brain made about $90K in 30 days from one email to about 3,200 people ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- **App Store pre-orders.** Runafy shipped a bare 2-tab v1 to pass review, then listed it for pre-order: 3,000 downloads in about 2 weeks, and the leaderboard filled within an hour of launch ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=536s)).

### Use scarcity

- Kleo: 500 spots at 50% off for life; scarcity beat curiosity and FOMO ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=856s)).
- Floga: limited spots and a 5 to 7 day window, with two decoy tiers below the top tier ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=777s), [14:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=899s)).
- SuperGrow capped its lifetime deal at 300 users over 3 days ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s)).

### Lifetime deals as a launch

- SuperGrow made $65K in 3 days on RocketHub, which took a 40% cut but ran the emails, ads and assets (2025-09) ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=325s)).
- LTD buyers pay 3 to 5 times a yearly plan, and early on you do not know LTV or churn anyway ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s)). See [Lifetime deals](lifetime-deals.md).

### Launch tweet with a demo

- Chatbase launched to 16 followers. The tweet had a familiar ChatGPT-style interface, an aha moment in about 20 seconds, and tagged tools (LangChain, Pinecone) that reposted it. $0 to $1M ARR in 117 days ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=322s)).
- Earni.fi's demo video searched an address with many airdrops: 10,000 signups in 48 hours from one tweet ([u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [6:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=386s)).
- Cursor Directory's launch post got about 1M impressions on X and reached the Hacker News front page ([LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=343s)).
- Marc Lou launched ShipFast with comedy skits; it made $500 in its first 2 hours and about $40K in month one ([WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [14:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=859s), [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s)).
- Frame each new feature as a new launch for people who do not know you ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [5:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=347s)).

### Launch platforms

- **Hacker News.** ChartDB's Show HN reached the front page after 3 weeks of work ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=235s)). Neural Frames posted a rough product a week after going live and reached the top 6 ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=448s)). For open source, link the GitHub repo, not the website; a front-page post brings about 10,000 views ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=818s)).
- **Product Hunt.** Supermeme was number one on launch day ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)). Resume Maker Online won Product of the Week; AI Carousels got 5 paying users while still buggy ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)). DesignJoy's first clients came from Product Hunt ([dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)).
- **Reddit.** Formula Bot's plain post became the week's top post on r/excel, then about 10,000 upvotes on r/InternetIsBeautiful ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=385s)). Pat Walls put full case studies inside Reddit self-posts with a small link at the end ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=208s)). Pckgr launched free on Reddit, then added a $25/month plan ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s)).
- **Stack them in one week.** Postiz runs articles, Show HN, r/selfhosted and social posts in the same week to hit GitHub trending ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=934s)). Marc Lou posts on X, LinkedIn, Threads and Reddit at once ([D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=222s)).

### Test the message before launch

- Post before launch to find the angle people care about, and kill the idea if no video can sell it ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=223s)).
- Runafy: under 50 copied Reels in 2 weeks gave 2,000 waitlist sign-ups and 90+ payments of $5 ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [24:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1472s)).
- Dris's Instinct signed about $1.2M in annualized contracts in its first 7 days through sales calls, with no product (2025-09) ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=207s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=268s)).
- Framing decides pickup. Methods got few sign-ups as "the UGC marketplace app" and grew fast as "make money online" ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=542s)).

### Many launches, fast

- Marc Lou has shipped about 35 startups with about a 5% hit rate; one launch removes about 80% of the fear of showing work ([D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=163s), [6:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=364s)).
- Florin Pop: build small, ship in public, add a paid tier early, then double down or move on ([47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=573s)).
- Fernando ships a bare MVP in a 10-day public challenge, because founders get attached after 6 to 12 months ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s)).
- Momentum dies when you stop posting after launch ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=198s)).

### After launch day

- Turn LTD buyers into ambassadors for a later launch: 10 to 15 SuperGrow buyers posted on its Product Hunt day ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s)).
- White-glove onboarding calls with early users ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=807s)); a long public beta with 150 testers ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)).
- Convert rented attention into an email list ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)).

## Disagreements

- **Is Product Hunt worth it?** Pauline launches there mostly for the SEO backlink, because its visibility is lower than before ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=404s)). SuperGrow's Product of the Week brought 50 customers ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s)). Thomas Sanlis built Uneed because indie hackers felt Product Hunt only featured big products ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=315s)).

> Conflict: [mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md) treats Product Hunt as a backlink source; [BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) and [CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md) got real customers and a #1 day from it.

- **Launch free or charge from day one.** Pckgr and ChartDB launched free and charged later ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s); [7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=397s)). Marc Lou says the only proof is a launch with a buy button ([D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s)), and Runafy charged $5 before it built anything ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [25:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1558s)).

> Conflict: [LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md) and [7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md) launch free for traction; [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) and [yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md) say launch with payment.

- **Validate first or just ship.** Yasser Elsaid did no validation for Chatbase ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)). Joseph Choi says to test videos and kill ideas no video can sell before you build ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s)).

> Conflict: [E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) launched with no validation; [TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md) puts message testing before the build.

- **Cinematic launch videos.** Eunice Lie's cinematic launch video went viral among VCs and helped a startup raise $3M ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=378s)). Dris says launch videos are top of funnel for tech Twitter, while UGC converts about 4x better at 10 to 20x lower cost for his clients ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=462s), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=489s)).

> Conflict: [MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md) shows launch videos winning attention and funding; [szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md) says they rarely drive users compared with UGC.
