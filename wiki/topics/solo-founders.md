This page collects what the sources say about building and running a business alone, with no co-founder and few or no employees.

## Definition

A solo founder (or solopreneur) owns and runs a business alone. Most sources here keep costs low, use contractors or tools in place of staff, and pick markets that are too small for funded teams.

## Repeated patterns

### Ideas

- **Solve your own problem.** Bank Statement Converter came from Angus Cheng's own runway spreadsheet. Momego came from John McEvoy's bus job, and Go Polar from Presh's cold plunges. Jack Friks builds only what he pays money to solve himself [source](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=62s), [source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md) [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=133s), [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=291s), [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=225s).
- **Build in a hobby or job you know.** Matt Robinson plays the golf events Live Tourney serves. Pat Walls contrasts this with founders who build for dentists and quit after 3 months [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [8:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=539s). John Rush calls it founder-idea fit [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=959s).
- **Pick markets too small for VCs.** Bank Statement Converter is too small for a VC but enough for one person. Data Fetcher sits in a market "too small for VC-funded rivals" [source](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=409s), [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s). A few hundred daily players can produce over $100K a year for a niche game [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [9:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=595s).
- **Copy proven demand.** Adrian Horning picks validated ideas from Acquire.com listings [source](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md) [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=376s). Others mine Upwork jobs and Zapier app pairs [source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=163s), [source](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=193s). Competitors prove people pay [source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=185s).
- **Build on a platform.** Notion, Airtable, Google Sheets and Chrome bring users and a community. The cost is platform risk [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=135s), [source](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s), [source](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=120s). New platform APIs (Notion API, iOS AlarmKit) open short windows [source](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s), [source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md) [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s).
- **A good idea is one you know how to sell.** Thomas Sanlis's Gum Affiliates failed because he knew neither Gumroad nor affiliates. Uneed worked because he knew launch platforms and had a Twitter audience [source](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md) [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=426s).

### Building

- **Ship an MVP in days, not months.** NotionForms in 6 days, Stagetimer in 3 days, Superpower ChatGPT in 2 to 3 days, Earni.fi in 4 to 5 hours, AudioPen in 12 hours [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s), [source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md) [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s), [source](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s), [source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=300s), [source](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s).
- **Use a hard deadline.** A 10-day public challenge, a 12-hour hackathon, or a fixed date forces only essential features [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s), [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=316s).
- **Use the stack you know, and AI for the rest.** Known tools shipped Stagetimer in 3 days. Julien Nahum still uses his 10-year-old stack [source](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md) [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s), [source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) [13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=811s). Non-coders ship with Bolt, Cursor or Lovable [source](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md) [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=223s), [source](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md) [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=259s).
- **Payment is the only real validation.** Five people paid for a buggy AI Carousels. Marc Lou says the only proof is a launch with a buy button. Dmytro Krasun wants 10 paying strangers [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s), [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s), [source](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=222s).

### Many small bets

- **Treat projects like an index fund.** Florin Pop spreads risk over many small projects [source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s). Marc Lou shipped about 35 startups at a 5% hit rate [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=163s). Nico Jeannen built 17 apps in a year and sold 2 [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=63s). Max built 28 apps in 8 months. Four make most of the money [source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md) [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s).
- **One audience, many products.** Presh's health apps share one user base. Florin Pop stays with developers. Jeremy Redman builds side tools that feed TaskMagic [source](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md) [2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=175s), [source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=448s), [source](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md) [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=215s).
- **Automate the winner, then start the next one** [source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) [10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=633s).

### Distribution

- **Marketing first.** Joseph of StealthGPT: without distribution the product does not matter [source](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) [14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=856s). "Build it and they will come" is one of Thomas Sanlis's five reasons projects fail [source](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md) [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=153s).
- **Reddit, done as a helper.** Lurk, give value, link at the end, post 2 to 3 times a week [source](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md) [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=372s), [source](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md) [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=306s), [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=430s).
- **Free tools for SEO.** SiteGPT gets about 90% of Google traffic on free tools. Fernando built about 30 [source](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md) [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=107s), [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s).
- **Build in public as insurance.** If an app fails, the following stays for the next launch [source](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s). John Rush says 90% of his products failed before he built in public, and 10% after [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=906s).
- **Organic short-form at volume.** One meme template posted about 300 times drove 60K to 70K signups for Curiosity Quench. DuckMath ran 3 TikTok accounts at 3 posts a day [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=530s), [source](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md) [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s).
- **Paid ads once LTV beats CAC.** Waitly: Apple Search Ads CAC about $130 against $750 to $1,000 LTV (2025-09) [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s).

### Staying solo

- **Productize services.** DesignJoy and Scott Millard's video studio sell fixed packages, not hours [source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md) [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s), [source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md) [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=397s). Brett Williams uses async Trello boards, one active request per client and no calls [source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s).
- **Use small size as a feature in support.** Fernando writes "I", not "we", and fixes bugs within an hour [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=589s). Jack Friks does support first every day [source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s). Joe Rozek beats giants on price, simplicity and support [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=380s).
- **Keep costs tiny.** DesignJoy ran on $176/month (2023-05). Justin Welsh on about $620/month (2023-04) [source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md) [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=292s), [source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md) [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=307s).

### Leaving the job and daily routine

- **Build next to the job until revenue is real.** Matt Robinson worked 6:00 to 9:00 each morning. Brett Williams quit at $80K/month after 3.5 years. Ania Wysocka worked 4 days at a job and 3 on Rootd [source](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) [8:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=523s), [source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md) [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=179s), [source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=343s). Kenneth Law set a quit number of $10K/month with no paid marketing [source](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md) [6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=394s).
- **A fixed deep work block.** Pat Walls did 2 hours at a Starbucks at 6 a.m.. Marc Lou does 4 to 6 hours with no phone. Fernando caps deep work at 4 hours [source](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md) [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=180s), [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=72s), [source](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) [12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=741s).
- **Save long runway.** Angus Cheng needed 2 to 3 years before revenue paid rent [source](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s). Jack Friks's anxiety eased only with a year of savings [source](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=218s).

## Disagreements

- **Validate before or after building.** Angus Cheng says a 1 to 2 week MVP is a small risk, so pre-launch validation adds little [source](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=124s). John Rush wants 100 waitlist signups and 5 pre-sales before he builds [source](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=215s).

> Conflict: [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) says build first. [En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) and [WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) say sell before you build.

- **Social media.** Angus Cheng skips social media because his users are not there [source](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s). Justin Welsh posts 2 to 3 times a day on two platforms [source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md) [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=125s).

> Conflict: [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) advises against social media. [v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md) and [47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) make it the main channel.

- **Many bets or one focus.** Marc Lou and Florin Pop launch many projects [source](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=558s), [source](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md) [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=302s). Nick Buzz burned out on 12 projects and narrowed to one [source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md) [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=810s).

> Conflict: [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) favors many launches. [TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md) favors one business.

- **Design and branding early.** Ben Bozzay had no logo until a few hundred customers [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=292s).
