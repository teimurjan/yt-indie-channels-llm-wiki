This page collects what the sources say about SaaS: software sold as a subscription, mostly built by solo founders and small teams.

## Definition

SaaS is software that customers use online and pay for on a recurring plan. In these sources it is mostly small, bootstrapped web tools, many of them AI tools, built by one to three people.

## Repeated patterns

### Copy a proven product, then niche down or fix a gap

- [Abhishek](../people/abhishek.md) built [Youform](../businesses/youform.md) as a Typeform alternative. His method: search "X alternative" on Twitter and Reddit, list the pains, then build a one-click migration tool ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s)).
- [Shipper](../businesses/shipper.md) took one pain point from a huge market led by Lovable and Base44. It removed jargon for non-technical users ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [4:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=258s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=485s)).
- [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md)'s rule: never build something that does not already exist and already make money ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=173s)).
- [Dustin Stout](../people/dustin-stout.md) built the chat search and folders that early ChatGPT lacked ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [2:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=139s)).
- [Charlie Clark](../people/charlie-clark.md) built a nicer Linktree alternative and priced it below Linktree ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=351s), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)).
- [Matt Robinson](../people/matt-robinson.md) built a simpler golf scoring tool because Golf Genius was too complex and expensive ([2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=174s)).
- [Fernando](../people/fernando.md): Canva serves everyone, so one narrow task is open for a solo founder ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=150s)).

### Solve your own problem

- [CJ Zafir](../people/cj-zafir.md) chained his own ChatGPT prompts for client docs and saved 7 of 9 to 10 hours per project ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=306s)).
- [Angus Cheng](../people/angus-cheng.md) wanted his bank PDFs in Excel and built [Bank Statement Converter](../businesses/bank-statement-converter.md) ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=62s)).
- [David Bressler](../people/david-bressler.md) built [Formula Bot](../businesses/formula-bot.md) because junior analysts kept asking him for Excel help ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s)).
- [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md) calls it founder-idea fit: solve the problems you see in your own job ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=959s)).

### Ship an MVP in days or weeks

- [Tibo](../people/tibo.md) estimates a 90% failure rate. A year per try means 9 years to a win, so take shortcuts ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=377s)).
- [Julien Nahum](../people/julien-nahum.md) built the [NotionForms](../businesses/notionforms.md) MVP in 6 days while he commuted 3 hours a day ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s)).
- Samuel Rondot ships in two weeks and skips password reset and settings pages ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=668s)).
- [Dmytro Krasun](../people/dmytro-krasun.md)'s first build took 5 months. Now he aims for 1 month or less ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=256s)).
- [Faceless Video](../businesses/faceless-video.md)'s first MVP only turned text into a video. Accounts, payments and pricing came later ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s)).

### Charge early, because payment is the validation

- YC asked [VEED](../businesses/veed.md) why it did not charge. The team added payments over a weekend and got 20 paid users ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [9:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=581s)).
- Fernando: five people paid for a buggy product, so he doubled down ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)).
- Dmytro Krasun: 10 paying customers from outside your network is the real test ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=222s)).
- [Florin Pop](../people/florin-pop.md): add a paid tier early, because payment is the real validation ([47QXbPGyzBI](../sources/47QXbPGyzBI.md), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47QXbPGyzBI&t=573s)).

### Talk to users all the time

- [Fastlane](../businesses/fastlane.md) ran about 2,000 customer calls, about 20 a week. A button offers 7 days of extra access for a call ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=400s)).
- Tibo's support link opens his Twitter DMs until a product reaches $10K/month ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s)).
- Dmytro Krasun emails each churned customer one yes/no question and gets close to 100% replies ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=424s)).
- [David Park](../people/david-park.md) asks users what they dislike and what they love about rivals ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=295s)).

## Pricing and margins

- **No free plan for AI tools.** [CodeGuide](../businesses/codeguide.md) skips a free trial because AI models are expensive. It charges $29/month (2025-05) ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [12:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=761s)). [Magai](../businesses/magai.md) has no free plan. Solo is $20/month (2025-10) ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [8:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=536s)).
- **Small price rises compound.** [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md) went from $4 to $5/month and added nearly $5K MRR (2026) ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)).
- **Start below incumbents, then raise.** NotionForms raised prices with no visible conversion drop ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=706s)).
- **Lifetime deals as a launch.** [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md)'s AppSumo deal made about $350K and about 10,000 users ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)). [TaskMagic](../businesses/taskmagic.md) used lifetime deals plus usage pricing ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=228s)).
- **AI costs eat margin.** [Neural Frames](../businesses/neural-frames.md) spends about $45K/month on GPUs and video APIs, almost half of revenue (2025-07) ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=599s)). SEO Bot pays $30K of $100K/month for AI APIs. Non-AI tools have about a 90% margin (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=764s)).
- **Non-AI tools can be almost all profit.** Bank Statement Converter keeps about $39K of $40K/month (2025-07) ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [8:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=500s)).
- See [pricing](pricing.md), [lifetime deals](lifetime-deals.md) and [unit economics](unit-economics.md).

Its agent pivot cut churn from 26-28% to 13.7% (2026-07) ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=148s)).
- [SuperX](../businesses/superx.md): churn close to 30% (2025-12) ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=155s)).
- [ScreenshotOne](../businesses/screenshotone.md): from about 11% to close to 7%, with a target under 5% (2024-11). Clear copy that it is for developers cut churn ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)).

## Disagreements

- **Validate first or build first.**
  - Angus Cheng says a 1 to 2 week MVP is a small risk, so pre-launch validation adds little ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=124s)).
  - John Rush gets 100 waitlist signups and 5 pre-sales before he builds ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=215s)). CJ Zafir posts a demo and collects a waitlist first ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=352s)).
> Conflict: [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) says build first. [En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md) and [0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) validate with a waitlist or pre-sales before they build.
- **Free plan or not.**
  - Youform gives over 90% of features free and converts 1.5% to 2% (2025-08) ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=425s)).
  - ScreenshotOne cut the free plan because margins at $5 to $7 were near zero ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)).
> Conflict: [_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) grows on a generous free plan. [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md) and [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) skip free plans or trials.
- **Google Ads.**
  - Angus Cheng spent about $1,000 per $300 in sales and cut the ads after 6 months ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=247s)).
  - StealthGPT and Liinks made Google Ads profitable ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=521s)) ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=485s)).
> Conflict: [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) found Google Ads never profitable. [8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md) and [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) scaled on them.
- **Social media.**
  - Angus Cheng advises against brand pages on social media, because his users are not there ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=568s)).
  - Alex Finn and Rob Hallam built their SaaS on X ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)) ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s)).
- **Product Hunt.**
  - NotionForms got traffic and a backlink but no sales ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=463s)).
  - Shipper got its first $50 MRR there, and Supermeme was #1 on launch day ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s)) ([CoqAxV6b6j4](../sources/CoqAxV6b6j4.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoqAxV6b6j4&t=499s)).
- **Audience or no audience.**
  - Alex Finn treats the audience as the moat ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)).
  - [Diego](../people/diego.md) reached $17K/month with fewer than 100 X followers ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=266s)). [Sebastian Ghiorghiu](../people/sebastian-ghiorghiu.md) kept his app away from his audience on purpose ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=284s)).
