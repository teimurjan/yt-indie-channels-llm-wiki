This page covers side projects: businesses that founders build next to a full-time job or a main company, and how some of them grow into the main income.

## Definition

A side project is a business that a founder runs in spare hours, usually next to a job. Some grow until the founder can quit. Others stay small on purpose.

## Repeated patterns

- **Fix your hours around the job.** Chris did 1 hour of [Wishlists](../businesses/wishlists.md) tasks at 8:00. He worked his job from 9 to 6. Then he built from 8 to 11 p.m. [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [2:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=150s). [Karthik Shanadi](../people/karthik-shanadi.md) worked night shifts at Amazon and built by day, six days a week for 2 to 2.5 years. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=261s). [Alex Pattis](../people/alex-pattis.md) splits about 70% day job and 30% syndicate and uses mornings, evenings and weekends. [p3xa6mpxvOY](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md) [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=338s)
- **Quit at salary parity, not before.** Charlie Clark quit for good in August 2024, once [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md) MRR matched his salary. He told his employer about side projects up front. [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=597s). Chris first negotiated a 4-day week, then quit in July 2025. [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=211s), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=413s)
- **Ship small and fast.** [Cursor Directory](../businesses/cursor-directory.md) went from idea to Vercel in about 3 hours after a flight. Its founders usually hold off on fast ideas, but this time they acted. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=230s), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=294s). Louis Pereira built the [AudioPen](../businesses/audiopen.md) MVP in about 12 hours during a Sunday hackathon. [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=206s). Liinks took 2 weeks. [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=23s)
- **Build a nicer version of a crowded category.** Wishlists is a prettier wish list app. Liinks is a nicer Linktree. [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=121s), [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=100s). AI sites all look the same, so design is the moat. [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=418s)
- **Set small first goals.** Chris's first goal was one stranger downloading the app. [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [5:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=306s). Half Day Build asks for at least $1 of revenue between noon and midnight. [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) [3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=206s)
- **Fix the product before you blame marketing.** Piotr blamed marketing for 2 years while [Visualizee](../businesses/visualizee.md) made $100 to $150/month. The real problem was the path from visitor to first success. [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s)
- **Build in public as insurance.** The audience stays for the next launch, even after an app fails. [WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s). Months of public posts gave AudioPen credibility, so it sold on day one. [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s)

## Concrete examples with numbers

- **[Cursor Directory](../businesses/cursor-directory.md):** about $34K to $35K/month at 99.8% gross margin, with about 3 hours of upkeep a month (2025-11). [LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md) [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=21s), [2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=159s)
- **[Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md):** about $31,500 MRR (2026-10). A $1 price rise from $4 to $5/month added nearly $5K MRR. [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=69s), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)
- **[Wishlists](../businesses/wishlists.md):** about $150K/year at about 99% margin, with 1.1M registered users, after about 6 years as a side project (2025-09). [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=49s), [9:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=598s)
- **[AudioPen](../businesses/audiopen.md):** about $15K/month and over 5,000 paying customers. Its founder still works full time in his family business (2025-12). [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=104s)
- **[Visualizee](../businesses/visualizee.md):** close to $10K/month about 6 months after a UI pivot, built on nights and weekends next to a tech lead job (2026-09). [TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md) [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=21s), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=63s)
- **[Greek House](../businesses/greek-house.md):** started with $500 in 2013. Year one did $50K and year two $100K, part time. In 2016 the founders went full time. Sales were just under $1M at that point. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=191s), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=481s)
- **Syndicate:** Alex Pattis deployed about $60M into 270 to 275 startups as a side project, starting from a $1,000 check (2023-11). [p3xa6mpxvOY](../sources/p3xa6mpxvOY.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p3xa6mpxvOY&t=0s)
- **[ShipFast](../businesses/shipfast.md):** after 6 side apps in 7 months at about $1K/month, Marc Lou built a boilerplate in under a week. It made about $40K in month one. [WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s), [15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=921s)
- **Too many projects:** Gaurabh Mathure built 72 apps in 60 days and made $0. After a ban on new building, he reached about $6,500 in 60 days (2026-08). [AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=65s), [9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=583s)

## Disagreements

- **Many small bets or one focus.** Marc Lou set rules: never spend months on a product, ship many small bets. [WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) [12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=736s). Louis Pereira also ships many small tools and doubles down on the one with a signal. [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=254s). Pat Walls calls building 72 apps an addiction and shiny object syndrome. He banned new building and asked for two customer conversations a day. [AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=143s)

> Conflict: [WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md) and [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) favor shipping many small projects. [AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md) treats nonstop building as the problem and says to stop and sell.
