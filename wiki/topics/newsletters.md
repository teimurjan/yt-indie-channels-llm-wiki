A newsletter is an email list that a founder or creator sends on a fixed schedule. In these sources it is either the business itself, paid for by sponsors, or the trust layer that sells another product.

## Definition

A newsletter owns a direct line to readers. [Alex Garcia](../people/alex-garcia.md) calls it the top of the funnel for anything else you want to build ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=133s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Two roles

- **The newsletter is the business.** [Marketing Examined](../businesses/marketing-examined.md) and its four sister lists earn all their revenue from ad sponsors ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=52s)). [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md) made its first money from newsletter sponsors and affiliates ([source](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)).
- **The newsletter builds trust for another product.** [Justin Welsh](../people/justin-welsh.md) uses social posts for discovery, a long newsletter for trust, then a low-pressure offer ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=426s)). [Generalist World](../businesses/generalist-world.md) wraps a newsletter with a community, job board and summer school ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s)).
- **A newsletter for a product's users.** [Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md) stayed free for 9 months and earned first from a newsletter for its users ([source](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s)).
- **A content companion.** [Smart Nonsense](../businesses/smart-nonsense.md) turns each animated Short into an illustrated email ([source](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=149s)). [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) uses his newsletter as a research journal that feeds future videos ([source](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=313s)).

### Getting the first readers

- **Do things that do not scale.** DM people, ask friends to share, and run small giveaways that add 10 or 15 subscribers each ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [4:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=273s)).
- **Publish on social every day.** Alex wrote 50 Twitter threads in 50 days ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)). [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md) got a viral post on day 27 of a 30-day writing challenge ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=316s)). Justin posts two to three times a day on LinkedIn and Twitter ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=125s)).
- **One viral Reddit post.** It gave Starter Story thousands of subscribers and a validated model ([source](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s), [source](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=427s)).
- **Then paid growth.** Reuse winning Twitter CTAs in paid ads, and buy subscribers through cross-promotion networks such as SparkLoop, Beehiiv and ConvertKit ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=508s)).
- **A signup page with one action.** The hero needs an email field and one button ([source](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md), [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=276s)).

### Making money

- **Sponsors first.** With 10,000 subscribers you can sell an ad slot for more than $1,000 ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)). Pitch the advertisers who already buy ads in other newsletters in your niche ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)).
- **Make buying easy.** Superpower ChatGPT gets sponsor inbound through Passionfroot and grows through Beehiiv Boosts ([source](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=615s)).
- **Paid tiers.** Lenny's Newsletter charges about $8.99 to $10.99 ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=396s)). [Write With AI](../businesses/write-with-ai.md) is a paid newsletter ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)). Justin sells a monthly paid copywriting email ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=59s)).
- **Platform partnerships.** Milly Tamati's largest deal is a one-year partnership with Beehiiv. She pitched it with a body of work and results ([source](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).

### Running it

- **Fixed schedule.** Justin sends the newsletter on Monday and publishes social posts at 8:15 a.m. Eastern every day ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s), [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=584s)).
- **Small stack.** Zapier, Notion, Slack and an email service provider ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=453s)). Justin's whole business costs about $620/month (2023-04) ([source](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=307s)).
- **Hire writers per niche.** Alex pays one writer per newsletter and finds them on pages 2 to 4 of Google results ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=482s)).
- **Time cost drops.** Saeed Ezzati wrote for 6 to 7 hours a day at first and now about 2 hours ([source](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s)).

## Disagreements

- **Starter Story's first months.** One telling puts the viral Reddit post in month 4 and the first money in month 6 ([source](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)). Another puts the Reddit post in month 2 and the first sponsor dollar in month 4 ([source](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=427s)).
> Conflict: [T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md) says Reddit in month 4 and first money in month 6. [xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md) says Reddit in month 2 and first money in month 4.
- **The Klaviyo sponsorship amount.** The captions give both $12,000 and $112,000 ([source](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=373s), [source](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZDofUHbZHA&t=427s)).
> Conflict: [T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md) closes on $12,000. [xZDofUHbZHA](../sources/xZDofUHbZHA.md) captions say $112,000, which looks too high next to $3,500/month at year end.
- **When to monetize.** Starter Story built an audience for months before it sold anything ([source](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)). Saeed Ezzati says he waited too long to charge, though the free period grew the user base ([source](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s)).
