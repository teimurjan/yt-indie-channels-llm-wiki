Bootstrapping means building a company with your own money and its revenue, without outside investors.

## Definition

A bootstrapped company funds itself from savings, a day job, customer pre-payments or its own profit. It keeps 100% of the equity and must be profitable early. Sources contrast it with VC: "In a VC world, you pay for growth. In a bootstrap world, you want to have product-led growth." ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=813s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Fund the start cheaply

- **Keep the day job.** [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) built [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md) in 2 hours of deep work each morning before work. Stripe went from zero to a few hundred dollars in about a year. He quit with about $12,000 in savings ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=138s), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s), [T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [3:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=180s)).
- **Use the job as the funding engine.** [Dmitri](../people/dmitri.md) ran [YourMove AI](../businesses/yourmove-ai.md) at a loss in his final year and paid $50/hour contractors instead of using his own $150/hour time. His quit target was $20K/month revenue. He left at $17K to $18K ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [51:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3065s), [51:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3114s)).
- **Half a salary each.** After 2 years with no revenue and no funding, the [VEED](../businesses/veed.md) founders took jobs and put half of each salary into the company to hire 2 engineers. They also talked Crisp down from $100 to $20/month ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [4:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=285s), [5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=346s)).
- **Use another business.** [Tom Holliday](../people/tom-holliday.md)'s agency still had 70+ hosting clients, and that income paid [Avenue](../businesses/avenue.md)'s first hires ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=350s)).
- **Start with $100.** [Thomas Smale](../people/thomas-smale.md) flipped a $100 website for $500, then founded [FE International](../businesses/fe-international.md). A $30K course paid the rent ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=49s), [16:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=994s)).
- **Bet the savings.** The [Qalo](../businesses/qalo.md) founders emptied their savings for the first inventory, and one moved in with his mother ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=267s)). [TABS Chocolate](../businesses/tabs-chocolate.md) started with $30,000, nearly its founder's whole bank account ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=564s)).

### Let customers fund the product

- **Pre-sales and lifetime deals.** [Floga](../businesses/floga.md) made over $120K in 24 hours from a lifetime deal on an unfinished app (2025-05) ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=83s)). LTD buyers pay 3 to 5 times a yearly plan, which is capital with no equity given away ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s)). [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md)'s AppSumo deal brought about $350K and 10,000 users ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s)).
- **Add a lifetime deal to any bootstrapped product**, because VC money lets a company delay product-market fit and a bootstrapper cannot ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=288s)).
- **Crowdfunding.** [Hush Blankets](../businesses/hush-blankets.md) raised over $1M on Kickstarter in 30 days against a $25K goal (2019) with $4,000 left in the bank ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=357s), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s)). [Neurogum](../businesses/neurogum.md) hit its $30K Indiegogo goal in 3 to 4 days ([BeRKQkU5Ceo](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s)). See [Crowdfunding](crowdfunding.md).
- **A personal loan at break-even.** [Halo AI](../businesses/halo-ai.md) funded growth with a $100K personal loan while early UGC ran at 5:1 to 6:1 ROAS ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [50:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3039s)).

### Pick markets VCs ignore

- A bootstrapped company can target smaller markets than a VC company that needs a billion-dollar outcome ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=629s), [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=409s)).
- Build tools that help people use a bigger app. A new dating app needs network effects and VC money. A dating helper does not ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [31:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1891s)).
- Boring niches work: [Pckgr](../businesses/pckgr.md) makes about $60K/month from Intune packaging (2026-03) and targets smaller businesses ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=96s), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s)).
- Improve a market that already exists, like offshoring at [Oceans](../businesses/oceans.md) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=366s)).

### Profit over growth

- Optimize for salaries, not exits: spend little on ads and staff so profit goes to founders. [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md)'s portfolio aims for $1M MRR in 5 years with no raised capital (2025-11) ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=52s), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=232s)).
- Avoid expensive agencies and side directions that eat profit ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [14:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=850s)).
- Margins are high: [Wishlists](../businesses/wishlists.md) about 99% (2025-09), [Minea](../businesses/minea.md) about 80% (2025-11) ([w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [10:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=628s), [-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=712s)). AI products can be thin: [Neural Frames](../businesses/neural-frames.md) spent about $45K of $100K monthly revenue on GPUs and APIs (2025-07) ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=599s)).
- Reinvest everything: TABS expected about $11M in 2023 with no outside investment ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=62s)).

### Cheap, owned distribution

- Organic first, paid later. [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md) runs SEO, daily posts, directories, then cross-promotion ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=476s)). [Oceans](../businesses/oceans.md) grew to about $15M with referrals and no paid marketing (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s)).
- One acquisition skill is enough: cold email, cold calling, SEO or ads ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)).
- "Play the indie card": a "no VC money, just a tiny company" footer and the founder's face everywhere ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=448s)).
- Learn a channel yourself before you hire for it ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:33:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1980s)).

### Solo and tiny teams

- Solo founders at scale: [NomadTable](../businesses/nomadtable.md) at $140K MRR (2026-09) and [Bank Statement Converter](../businesses/bank-statement-converter.md) at $40K MRR (2025-07) ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s), [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=214s)). See [Solo founders](solo-founders.md).
- [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) works in sprints: 1 to 2 hours a day in calm weeks, 8 to 10 around a launch ([k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=107s)).
- Write processes down before you pass 5 to 8 people ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: how much runway to quit with. [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) and [2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md) show Pat Walls quitting with about $12K in savings. [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) says a few months of runway is not enough and to save years, and [CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md) and [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) kept the job until the product nearly replaced the salary.

> Conflict: lifetime deals. [hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) and [DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md) say every bootstrapped product should use one for cash. [67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) used a private LTD for about $30K but plans to close all lifetime deals once it has about $100K in the bank.

> Conflict: bootstrapping as the end state. [miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md) and [67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) treat it as freedom to stay small and profitable. [CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md) shows Dmitri moving on to a venture-backed company in a16z Speedrun after his bootstrapped app, and [b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) notes a Castmagic founder spent almost 4 years trying to raise for an earlier startup.

> Conflict: debt. [PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) funded growth with a $100K personal loan. [Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md) advises starting with about $100 and aiming only for a profit.
