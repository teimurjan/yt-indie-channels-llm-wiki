Building in public means sharing a product's progress, numbers, wins and failures openly, usually on X, so the audience follows the journey and becomes the first customers.

## Definition

The founder posts the work as it happens: ideas, screenshots, revenue, failures. In these sources the main platform is X (Twitter), with LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky and Indie Hackers as extras. The goal is distribution the founder owns. "You need to be building in public. People want to see the journey." ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Distribution is the moat

- Anyone can copy software now. A clone with 10 followers loses to the founder with an audience ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=677s)).
- The audience is insurance: if an app fails, the following stays for the next launch ([WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=797s)).
- [John Rush](../people/john-rush.md) says 90% of his products failed before he built in public, and only 10% after ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=906s)).
- A good idea is one you know how to sell, and a Twitter following counts ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=426s)).

### Test ideas in public before building

- [Papermark](../businesses/papermark.md)'s idea tweet got about 40K views in a few hours. The launch tweet the next Monday got about 100K ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=77s)).
- [Vanman](../businesses/vanman.md)'s founder tweeted each tooth powder experiment. Followers asked to buy before it existed. The first drop sold out in about 20 minutes ([6iqo7lNR_m0](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=237s)).
- [Subscribr](../businesses/subscribr.md)'s founder built an X audience and email list in a new niche, then pre-sold 50 lifetime licenses for $20,000 in 2 to 3 days ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- [HabitKit](../businesses/habitkit.md): positive replies to early screenshots told Sebastian Röhl to ship fast ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=135s)).

### Launch to the audience

- Announce a date. [Creator Buddy](../businesses/creator-buddy.md) announced on January 12 for January 24 and hosted X Spaces all day: $100K ARR in 15 minutes and $300K in 2 weeks (2025-01) ([4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=53s), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4hMg_CZauJs&t=411s)).
- Set a public goal. [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) announced $0 to $5K MRR for [Lovely](../businesses/lovely.md) and quote-tweeted it about 6 times with updates ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [26:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1571s)).
- Design the launch tweet: a familiar interface, an aha moment in about 20 seconds, and the tools used ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s)).
- Put the product link in the second tweet of a thread ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s)).
- Launch on many channels at once: X, LinkedIn, Threads and Reddit ([D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=222s)). Open source plus Hacker News helped [Cursor Directory](../businesses/cursor-directory.md) get about 1M impressions from one post ([LWPN-PAhtLA](../sources/LWPN-PAhtLA.md), [5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWPN-PAhtLA&t=343s)).

### How to post

- **Share real work, not quotes.** "The easiest way to grow a personal brand is to not grow a personal brand at all." ([qeY283nBhIA](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md), [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=699s)).
- **Share weaknesses too.** Post what works, what fails and your weak points ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=514s)). Share small, unfinished progress ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=546s)).
- **Sound like an indie hacker**, not a big company. Users like being part of the story ([k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=526s)).
- **Rob Hallam's seven steps:** a friendly photo and pinned goal. One goal documented daily. A loop of attention post, education or pitch, then result. Copy concepts from top posts. Double down and kill. Real replies to the same people daily. Use the format the platform pushes. Video gave him 10x the reach of text ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=648s), [11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=709s), [14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=895s)).
- **Join the bigger conversation.** Build-in-public posts rarely go viral alone. Paulius Masalskas's top post, near 500K impressions, tapped the debate over whether AI coding can build a full SaaS ([jpSY4MlWX50](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=554s)).
- **Free public work and giveaways.** [Mark Vassilevskiy](../people/mark-vassilevskiy.md) posted a redesign of a famous brand's hero section: about 110K views overnight and 10 to 12 bookings. Three giveaways took him from about 500 to 5K followers ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s), [4:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=248s)).
- **Expect a slow start.** Plan for 2 to 3 months of practice with few views ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=210s)). 3K to 5K followers is a good range to test freely ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=299s)).
- **Stay in the comments** yourself for hours after each post ([6iqo7lNR_m0](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s)).

### It is one channel among several

- [Supademo](../businesses/supademo.md) gets about 20% of traffic from building in public on LinkedIn, beside SEO, LLMs and word of mouth (2026-03) ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=548s)).
- [Shipper](../businesses/shipper.md) went Product Hunt, Reddit, SEO, then X. Around day 50 of building in public it went parabolic ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s)).
- [Outrank](../businesses/outrank.md) grew from $0 to $20K/month with building in public only. Ads, an SEO hire and affiliates took it to $200K ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=810s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: audience first or product first. [4hMg_CZauJs](../sources/4hMg_CZauJs.md) posted for 3 years before having a product, and [7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md) built the audience before the build. [E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md) launched Chatbase to 16 followers and still went viral, and [VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md) says "I went viral first and then got the followers second."

> Conflict: does it stand out. [DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) says every founder needs to build in public. [MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md) says her build-in-public tweets went unnoticed because everyone posted them, and [jpSY4MlWX50](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md) says such posts rarely go viral alone without a bigger debate to join.
