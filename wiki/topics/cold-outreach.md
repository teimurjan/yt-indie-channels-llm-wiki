Cold outreach covers unsolicited emails, DMs and calls to people who do not know you yet: customers, creators, journalists, partners or sellers.

## Definition

Cold outreach is a direct message to a stranger with one clear ask. In these sources it finds first customers, signs UGC creators, gets press and finds businesses to buy.

## Repeated patterns

### Short and human beats long and polished

- **Two or three sentences.** [Scott Millard](../people/scott-millard.md): "We noticed your landing page has no video above the fold. That's something we do. Have you looked into it?" Long paragraphs do not get read ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=877s)). [Brian Shin](../people/brian-shin.md) also sent 2 to 3 sentence pitches ([r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s)).
- **Look like a person.** [UpLead](../businesses/uplead.md)'s $1M email had the subject "Hi {first name}" and a neutral opener. It compared itself to a known tool: "like ZoomInfo but with real-time email verification". It ended with an easy ask: "Are you open to trying it out at no cost?" ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s)).
- **Match the reader's voice.** [Matt Gittleson](../people/matt-gittleson.md) wrote to college creators in lowercase, with specific proof, not vague claims ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=871s)).
- **Copywriting is the lever, not automation.** Automation scales skill but does not replace it ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [52:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=3131s)).

### Give something first

- **A lead magnet in the email.** [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md) offered 50 free leads to anyone who replied. Competitors now copy this ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=498s)).
- **Free work or mock-ups.** [Gaurabh Mathure](../people/gaurabh-mathure.md) sent free mock-ups and personal demos ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=550s)). [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md) made free videos for Sam Parr and All-In until Jason Calacanis hired them ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s)).
- **A memorable gift.** [Cardinal Mason](../people/cardinal-mason.md) sent an email influencer a baby onesie: "email marketing is dead dad". It got him one of his first gigs ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=461s)).
- **A personal pitch for big targets.** [Ivan Nedlekovski](../people/ivan-nedlekovski.md) sent long Loom videos to coaches. Once he paid $1,000 for a call ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=571s)).

### Find the leads where intent shows

- **Companies that already pay for ads** have budget. Mason found founders' emails from Instagram ads, using Hunter.io and Mailtrack ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s), [6:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=365s)).
- **Hashtags.** Brian Shin searched #wedding and #birthdayparty for event hosts ([r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=428s)).
- **Creators that competitors already hire.** Qualify by views over 10% of followers and 100+ comments. Prefer creators a brand hired at least 3 times ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s)).
- **Your own network first.** [Karl Hughes](../people/karl-hughes.md) kept a spreadsheet of about 50 contacts. His first 5 to 10 customers came from it ([i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s)). Brian Shin's playbook also starts by going through every contact ([r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [8:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=510s)).

### Volume and persistence

- **Send more than feels normal.** [Danny Miranda](../people/danny-miranda.md): send 100 DMs and stop rejecting yourself first ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=282s)). Brian Shin's effort test is to get banned on a platform at least twice ([r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md), [10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4R_Hlw7sbo&t=615s)).
- **Cold calls for the first dollar.** [David Park](../people/david-park.md) called agencies 8 hours a day for weeks. About 99% hung up or said no ([Gv2fzC96Z40](../sources/Gv2fzC96Z40.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv2fzC96Z40&t=179s)).
- **Sequences and multiple channels.** Send a 7-email sequence with a new angle in each, plus Instagram and X messages ([-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s)).
- **Spread volume over many accounts.** Instagram allows about 100 DMs a day per account ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=743s)). [Boost App Social](../businesses/boost-app-social.md) ran 1,000 Gmail accounts at 10 emails a day each ([7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=796s)). Comment agencies warm up accounts like cold email inboxes ([SyVVLbMgAXc](../sources/SyVVLbMgAXc.md), [38:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyVVLbMgAXc&t=2303s)).

### Get to a call

- **Push for a call, not DM haggling.** [Locked](../businesses/locked.md)'s DM opens with "paid promo?". Then it asks for a call ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=780s)). [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) found a call makes a good creator deal about 10x more likely ([l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [37:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2259s)).
- **Do the sales calls yourself first.** Scott Millard took about 150 calls, wrote down the structure and handed it to a closer ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [16:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=988s)).
- **Sell before you build.** [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md)'s cold emails found an escape-room owner who agreed to pay after a 42-minute call ([WGVxAmmJUmY](../sources/WGVxAmmJUmY.md), [6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WGVxAmmJUmY&t=369s)).

## Other uses

- **Buying a business.** Email owners with a stated budget and model. Say plainly that you want to acquire, because a vague "can we call?" gets a no ([Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=396s)).
- **Acquihires.** [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md)'s DM to hire Matt's "creator" became an acquisition ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [53:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3197s)).
- **Sales to the layer above the customer.** One connector deal replaces hundreds of calls for a $200/month product ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s)).
- **Physical goods.** [Athletes Thread](../businesses/athletes-thread.md) used one-to-one outbound email, then outbound sales ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md) got UpLead's first $1M ARR from cold email. [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) ran cold email for [Bank Statement Converter](../businesses/bank-statement-converter.md) for 3 months and got 1 sale and many angry replies. [WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md) says cold email works for high-ticket offers, and a low-ticket tool needs a lead magnet.

> Conflict: [l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) says email beats DMs at scale, because Instagram limits DMs per day. [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) worked around the limit with many Instagram accounts and sent 500+ DMs a day.

> Conflict: reply rates vary widely. Creators replied at 10% to 20% for [7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) (2026-04) and about 10% for [l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) (2026-08), but 2% to 4% for [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) (2026-06). Software buyers replied at about 4% for [BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md) (2023-03).
