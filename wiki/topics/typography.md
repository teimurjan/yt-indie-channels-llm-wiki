Typography is the choice and setting of fonts: family, size, weight, line height, letter spacing, alignment and line length. All sources here are [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) design tutorials from 2022 to 2025.

## Definition

Typography decides how text looks and reads in an interface. In these sources it is the first step of a landing page or app screen: "Without good type, the rest of the page fails" ([cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md), [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=23s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Few fonts, few sizes

- **One font is enough**, for beginners and seasoned designers ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [0:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=33s), [PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=21s)).
- **Cut sizes and weights.** A beginner screen used 6 sizes and 4 weights. The senior version uses 4 and 2 ([86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=181s), [12:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=726s)).
- **Show hierarchy with weight and color, not size.** Too many sizes look messy ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=476s)).
- **Set a type scale first**, by hand or with the Type Scales plugin, from a base size (for example 18px) and a ratio (1.25 or 1.3) ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=95s)).
- **Map styles to page roles.** H1 for the hero and final CTA, H2 for section headings, H3 for subheadings. Drop one or two sizes in dense sections such as social proof ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=82s)).

### Copy settings from proven sites

- The same font (Inter) can look balanced or broken, depending on line height, weight and letter spacing. Copy the settings from a reference ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=215s)).
- In Chrome or Brave, inspect text, open Computed, and copy family, size, weight, letter spacing and line height ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=82s)).
- In Figma, trace a locked screenshot at 20% opacity and tune type until it matches. Reset line height to "auto" after a size change ([blDSmD5I61s](../sources/blDSmD5I61s.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blDSmD5I61s&t=390s), [11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blDSmD5I61s&t=711s)).
- Play safe as a beginner. Sans: Inter, DM Sans, Montserrat, Poppins. Serif: Merriweather, Lora, Source Serif Pro ([RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md), [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=692s)).

### Readability rules

- **Line height:** 1.1x to 1.3x for headings, 1.3x to 1.5x for body. Larger text needs a smaller ratio ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=132s), [3:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=194s)).
- **Letter spacing:** about -1% to -2% on headings. Default on body text ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [3:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=217s)).
- **Alignment:** left-align text longer than three lines, and never mix a centered heading with left-aligned body ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=301s), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=361s)).
- **Line length:** 50 to 75 characters, about 600px at 18px on desktop ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=437s)). Text blocks no wider than 600px ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=299s)).
- **Legibility over awards.** For content-heavy pages, readable text beats award-site style. 12px is not legible for long paragraphs ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=277s)).
- **Contrast:** body text must pass at least WCAG AA ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=511s)). Text opacity about 80% for the largest sizes and 60% to 70% for smaller ones ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=624s)).

### Spacing around text

- Space by relationship: a heading sits 1x (16px) from its body and 2x (32px) from the text above ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=518s)). 24px inside a text group and 48px to buttons ([PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGQnGEH7c-E&t=179s)).
- Each section down the page reduces size and spacing ([cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=149s)).
- Copy length must fit the layout. A layout can look great with two words and weak with four ([RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=753s), [cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=112s)).

## Tools

- Google Fonts (free), Adobe Fonts (paid), Typewolf for pairs, WhatTheFont to identify fonts ([RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md), [10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RdHXcv1k_Dg&t=631s)).
- Fontshare's "Pairs" tab for free font pairings ([cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md), [0:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cocnZubvfFE&t=23s)).
- Figma plugins: Type Scales and Contrast ([cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=95s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cmSBbnrFGPg&t=511s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: one font or a pair. [cmSBbnrFGPg](../sources/cmSBbnrFGPg.md) and [PGQnGEH7c-E](../sources/PGQnGEH7c-E.md) say one font is enough. [cocnZubvfFE](../sources/cocnZubvfFE.md) starts from a downloaded font pair, and [RdHXcv1k_Dg](../sources/RdHXcv1k_Dg.md) points to Typewolf for pairs.

> Conflict: how many sizes. [88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md) tries to use only two font sizes. [86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md) treats 4 sizes and 2 weights as senior-level.
