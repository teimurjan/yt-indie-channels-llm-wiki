This page collects what the sources say about referral programs: rewards and gates that get users, affiliates or creators to bring in more people.

## Definition

A referral program rewards an existing user, affiliate or creator for each new user or customer they bring, with free access, credits, cash or a share of revenue.

## Repeated patterns

- **Gate a wanted result behind invites.** [Glam Up](../businesses/glam-up.md) blurs scan results: users pay or invite 3 friends. The share text says "download Glam Up", so the app name ranks in TikTok search. Sharers still converted well [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1096s), [19:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1140s), [21:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1266s). [Airbuds](../businesses/airbuds.md) unlocks the full weekly recap only after you invite friends [source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s).
- **Referral codes can feed the algorithm.** [GlowUp](../businesses/glowup.md) gave a free makeup look for 3 invites. Users posted their codes in the video comments, which raised engagement [source](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=488s).
- **Make sharing a flex.** Tim Gabe: find something true the app knows that users want to brag about, put it on a clean card, and make sharing the same action as inviting [source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s). A referral program attached to something nobody wants to show off does nothing [source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=578s).
- **Invites in every product.** Hunter Isaacson says a shared link works as an endorsement [source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md) [27:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1633s). At $10K MRR he pairs paid tests with invites to lower the real CPI [source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md) [27:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1670s). See [viral-loops](viral-loops.md).
- **Pay the inviter from revenue.** [Bags](../businesses/bags.md) charges a 1% trade fee and passes most of it to the inviter [source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md) [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1367s). [Magai](../businesses/magai.md) built a recurring affiliate program from day one: a referral that pays 12 times pays the affiliate 12 times [source](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s). See [affiliate-marketing](affiliate-marketing.md).
- **Credit for a paying referral.** [Parakeet Chat](../businesses/parakeet-chat.md) gives about a month of free credits to a customer who recruits a paying customer. Users showed it to other inmates [source](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md) [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=456s).
- **Creator referrals.** [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) paid creators about $100 to $200 for each friend it signed. Referrals also let it reach creators without agents [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [45:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2733s).

## Disagreements

- **Forced invites or earned shares.** Glam Up and GlowUp gate results or rewards behind 3 invites and report good results [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [21:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1266s), [source](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md) [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=488s). Tim Gabe says a referral only works when the shared thing is worth bragging about [source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=578s).

> Conflict: [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) gets results from a plain "invite 3 or pay" gate. [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) says bolted-on referrals fail without a flex.
