A community is a group of users or peers in a shared space, such as Discord, Reddit or Facebook groups.

## Repeated patterns

- **Borrow communities where users already gather.** Founders get first users in subreddits, Facebook groups and Discord servers of their niche ([3X4VneEHvig](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=222s)).
  - Skip builder spaces such as SaaS and Indie Hackers subreddits. Go where users use products ([3X4VneEHvig](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=222s)).
  - Build on a platform with a big community, such as Notion, and solve a sub-problem ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=135s)).
- **Listen before you pitch.** Paste days of Discord chat into ChatGPT and ask for recurring pain points ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=558s)).
- **Own a community as a research lab.** Users vote on designs and features, and frequent requests get built ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:15:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=925s)).
- **Audience, then community, then product.** [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md) calls this the ACP funnel. The community shows which product people want most ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- **Community as the product.** Paid cohorts and member groups sell access to peers ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- **Community as a retention moat.** Social comparison and belonging keep users, even against easy copies of features ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s), [ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=592s)).

## Examples

### Borrowed communities as distribution

- **[Savewise](../businesses/savewise.md).** One spreadsheet post in a Facebook group sent over 1,500 visitors. The sheet linked to the site as the data source ([3X4VneEHvig](../sources/3X4VneEHvig.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3X4VneEHvig&t=531s)).
- **[Algrow](../businesses/algrow.md).** [Sam Adeniyi](../people/sam-adeniyi.md) shared his screen in Discord voice chats while he used the tool. The first 400 of over 10,000 users came from Discord (2026-01) ([9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9r0qPeSJGog&t=69s)).
- **[NotionForms](../businesses/notionforms.md).** Notion subreddits and Facebook groups tolerated links because the product was new and free ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=400s)).
- **[Postiz](../businesses/postiz.md).** Open-source projects can self-promote in r/selfhosted. Nevo reposts each new version every month ([RPM4ImzcIFc](../sources/RPM4ImzcIFc.md), [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPM4ImzcIFc&t=872s)).

### Owned communities

- **[Pushscroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md).** A Discord of 3K to 4K members (2025-10). A request seen 20 to 50 times gets built. Members also send UGC ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:16:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1015s), [0:19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1174s)).
- **[Halo AI](../businesses/halo-ai.md).** A creator Discord with channels for wins, ideas and feedback keeps creators motivated ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1798s)).
  - An AI agent in the Discord writes scripts from every video with 1M+ views ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1502s)).
- **[Framer](../businesses/framer.md).** Its Discord gives several answers to a question fast. Framer uses community feedback to choose what to build ([NT8w5ZS13ao](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=149s), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=220s)).
- **[lemlist](../businesses/lemlist.md).** The founder uses the product daily, publishes the best content and pushes it into a community. Complaints then improve the product ([ZEIPj7QbZeI](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=706s)).
- **[Hush Blankets](../businesses/hush-blankets.md).** The founders shared ups and downs in public. Customers asked them to sign their blankets ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [10:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=657s)).
  - They sold 3,000 pillows in 72 hours to their email list ([IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=467s)).

### Community as the product

- **[Ship 30 for 30](../businesses/ship-30-for-30.md).** A community writing course for beginners, about $3M/year (2024-07) ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
  - It began as a Slack channel. [Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md) and Nicolas Cole turned it into a full course ([OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=556s)).
- **[Generalist World](../businesses/generalist-world.md).** A newsletter, members community, job board and summer school. It makes $30K/month with a very small team (2026-09) ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=149s)).
- **[Consumer Club](../businesses/consumer-club.md).** [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md)'s Discord where app founders share distribution tactics. Members range from solo devs with millions in ARR to funded founders (2025-04) ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:02:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=171s)).
  - Members trade skills. An offered retention lessons and got TikTok lessons back ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:56:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=3397s)).
- **[Late Checkout](../businesses/late-checkout.md).** Greg Isenberg's holding company includes an AI community and a multipreneur community. His lane: community-based products beat non-community products ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=68s), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=645s)).
  - He sold Islands, "a Discord for college students", to WeWork ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=179s)).
  - He hires the most active members ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=529s)).

### Community inside the product

- **[Strava](../businesses/strava.md).** Over 100 million athletes. Segments rank you against your neighborhood or friends, not only the world ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)).
- **[Peloton](../businesses/peloton.md).** People kept paying $44/month for years. [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) credits instructors who call out riders by name (2026-06) ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=197s)).
- **[Robinhood](../businesses/robinhood.md).** Its social feature centers on learning and discussion, not copy trading ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [9:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=592s)).
- Tim's advice: make achievements visible to others and design metrics as a mirror against peers ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [12:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=764s)).
- Tim also says to build the community layer early. Late founders will compete with products that already own user identity ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=708s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) says to move an audience into a community where you own emails and SMS. [9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md) says Discord is 10x better than email for a relationship with young users.

- The sources split on the role of a community. Some use it for growth and feedback. Others sell it as the product itself.
