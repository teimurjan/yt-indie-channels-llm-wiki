App Store optimization (ASO) is the work of ranking an app in app store search and charts through keywords, metadata, ratings and store page design.

## Definition

ASO covers the app name, subtitle, keyword field, screenshots and ratings, so that people who search for a problem find and install the app ([source](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)).

## Repeated patterns

- **Title and subtitle carry most of the weight.** Steve Young says title and subtitle are most of ASO, and copies App Store autofill phrases into the subtitle and keyword field ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [21:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1269s)). Sebastian Röhl starts the HabitKit name with "habit tracker" ([source](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)). Cardstock's title is "Cardstock: Sports Card Scanner" ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=900s)).
- **Pick the keyword first, then build.** Max picks a keyword in Astro with popularity of at least 20, then builds one core feature for it ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)). Steve Young names apps after found keywords ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=929s)).
- **Ask for ratings at the first win.** Momego asks at the first view of a live bus on the map ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=414s)). HabitKit asks after the first habit check-off ([source](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=460s)). Wishlists asks after a wish is added or fulfilled ([source](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=498s)). Tim Gabe calls this "win mapping" based on the peak-end rule ([source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=443s)).
- **Support creates reviews.** Kaching gets about 95% of its Shopify App Store reviews from support agents who earn a bonus per review ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s)). Chris emails users when he ships their fix and asks for a review only after a positive reply ([source](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=559s)).
- **Match store copy to user words.** Rootd's page promises what the app delivers, so users repeat the same keywords in reviews ([source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=491s)). Joseph Choi says the first screenshot should repeat the winning video angle ([source](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=370s)).
- **Use localizations for more keywords.** Keywords in the Mexican Spanish localization also index in the US App Store ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)).
- **Social pushes lift chart rank.** Five creators posting Payout on one day lifted it to about #70 to #80 in Finance. Top-20 category rank brings organic store traffic ([source](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [46:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2771s), [48:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2880s)). Glam Up reached number 3 on the App Store for a few days ([source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=525s)).

## Concrete examples

- **[Cardstock](../businesses/cardstock.md):** #1 for "baseball card scanner" for 2 to 3 years, worth about $9K to $10K MRR with no marketing. It lost the rank in 2024-05 ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=838s), [0:16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=971s)).
- **[Momego](../businesses/momego.md):** hyper-local keywords per city ("MTA subway", "CTA L train") and city screenshots. About 5.2M downloads and about $30K MRR (2025-10) ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [0:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=52s), [4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=292s)).
- **[App Masters](../businesses/app-masters.md):** two keywords with volume of about 30 each, ranked number one, bring about 100 to 150 downloads a day. One health app made about $4M of about $6M last year from organic search (2026-07) ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=517s), [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s)).
- **[HabitKit](../businesses/habitkit.md):** top 5 for "habit tracker" in several countries. $3K MRR at end of 2023, about $15K MRR at end of 2024 ([source](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [1:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=65s), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=268s)).
- **Max's portfolio:** 28 keyword-first apps in 8 months, from $200 to $10K per month. Four apps make about $1,500 per month each (2025-12) ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=30s), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- **[Rootd](../businesses/rootd.md):** over 4M downloads and a 4.8 rating (2025-08) ([source](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=59s), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=643s)).
- **[Wishlists](../businesses/wishlists.md):** no paid marketing. Reviews from friends and family at the start, then well-timed prompts. About $150K/year (2025-09) ([source](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=49s), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=498s)).
- **[Kaching Bundles](../businesses/kaching-bundles.md):** Shopify App Store ranking drives $4.5M ARR with almost no ad spend (2025-04) ([source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=62s)).

## Tools

- Astro for keyword tracking ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1241s), [source](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=562s)), Sensor Tower for competitor revenue ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=249s)), ASOMobile to see every keyword an outlier app indexes for ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [23:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1404s)), Appfigures for research ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=711s)), Apple Search Ads to test keywords ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=354s)), and appstoretracker.com for rank and metadata changes ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [13:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=813s)).
- Lotts Ezeike finds ideas in the charts: check who is number one in each category and which top-20 apps do the same thing for other niches ([source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)).

## Disagreements

- Max uses tool difficulty scores, aiming for about 60 to 70 or lower ([source](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=187s)). Steve Young says to judge difficulty by the results page, not tool scores: count titles that use the keyword and check review counts at ranks two and three ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=713s)).
> Conflict: [vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md) relies on Astro difficulty scores; [Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) says to ignore tool scores and read the search results.
- Steve Young says ASO plus Apple Ads or Meta can reach $5M ARR ([source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=360s)). Kyle Fowler's Cardstock lost its #1 rank in 2024-05 and needed TikTok to grow again ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=971s)).
> Conflict: [Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) treats ASO as a channel that can scale alone; [CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) shows a single-keyword rank can vanish.
