This page collects what the sources say about gamification: game mechanics such as streaks, points, levels, leaderboards and rewards in non-game apps.

## Definition

Gamification adds game mechanics to an app to raise engagement, retention or sharing. Most sources here are [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) design breakdowns, plus founder interviews about consumer apps that use game layers.

## Repeated patterns

### Points, badges and leaderboards alone fail

- Points, badges and leaderboards (PBL) are the most common mechanics and the best-documented failures ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=126s)).
- LinkedIn retired its Top Voice badges in 2024, because badge chasers posted quantity over quality. Foursquare dropped badges in 2014 ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=77s)).
- A leaderboard without social comparison underneath is "a glorified list" ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md)).
- Too many mechanics hurt. A 2025 study found an S-curve: engagement rises with features up to a point, then falls ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=266s)).
- Track one progress measure users obsess over, not 20 scattered badges ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=335s)).

### Make competition small and winnable

- Shrink the cohort until winning feels possible. A global board feels unwinnable, so people stop trying ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)).
- [Strava](../businesses/strava.md) segments are thousands of local leaderboards, sorted by age and gender ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s)).
- Put the comparison at completion: the end of a workout, a transaction or a listen ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=77s)).
- Social comparison makes quitting a public act ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=717s)).

### Put the hero feature on a clock

- Some products change only on user action. They give no reason to come back. A clock does ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s)).
- [FarmVille](../businesses/farmville.md) crops rotted after a window. [Hinge](../businesses/hinge.md) Standouts reset every 24 hours. [Life Reset](../businesses/life-reset.md) runs a boss fight every two days ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=413s)).
- Show a stake, not a metric. In [Forest](../businesses/forest.md), switching to Instagram kills the tree ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=257s)).
- Use the Zeigarnik effect: unfinished tasks nag. Keep the next threshold close ([D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=445s)).

### Design rewards as gifts, not receipts

- A reward has three stages: anticipation, reveal and celebration ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=223s)).
- Reveal items one at a time. Each one resets anticipation ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=274s)).
- Keep most rewards predictable, and add controlled surprises ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=335s)).
- Copy the ceremony, not the data. Spotify Wrapped gates its data and reveals it once a year ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [7:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=474s)).

### Forgive the missed day

- Plan what happens the day after a user skips. Make the streak survive their worst day ([IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=264s)).
- [Duolingo](../businesses/duolingo.md) doubled the streak freezes a user can hold from 1 to 2. Daily active learners rose 0.38% ([IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=326s)).
- Alma pauses a streak for up to 14 days. Noom lets users skip a day on purpose ([IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [6:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=382s)).
- A streak with no pause or exit is the version regulators watch ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=410s)).

### A game layer differentiates a crowded category

- [Locked](../businesses/locked.md) gives a habit tracker XP, levels, badges and leaderboards ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=300s)).
- [Cat on Chair](../businesses/cat-on-chair.md) makes a Pomodoro timer feel like a pet. Its customization is inspired by Animal Crossing ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=433s)).
- [Ryan Yao](../people/ryan-yao.md) remixes mechanics from games first, because games are built to retain ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=629s)).
- [Bloom](../businesses/bloom.md) pays its "up to $75" stock offer mostly through games and loot boxes ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [22:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1331s)).
- Visible game elements spread an app. Students see [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md)'s tree on other screens in libraries ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s)).

### Identity and emotion beat mechanics

- Emotional design belongs at chosen moments. Duolingo celebrates progress and makes you feel guilty for missed days ([AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=504s)).
- As AI agents absorb the functional layer, identity and community become the product ([ZXWUg1e1PAs](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=187s)).
- Engineer mastery. Gamification does little for competence, so show real progress, as Peloton personal records do ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=646s)).

## Concrete examples

- [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md): a growing tree raised questions answered by 70% ([zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=963s)).
- [Locked](../businesses/locked.md): over $14K/month (2026-06) ([qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=147s)).
- [Cat on Chair](../businesses/cat-on-chair.md): over $18K in 30 days and 127K downloads (2026-04) ([V_4kiR3R4QU](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=84s)).
- [Stronger](../businesses/stronger.md): a gamified workout tracker at about $600K ARR and 1.2M users (2025-08) ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=163s)).
- Apple Watch rings produced a 49.5% behavior change across 160,000 people ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=556s)).
- [Peloton](../businesses/peloton.md): 90% annual subscriber retention (2026-04) ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=501s)). The average app keeps 7% of users after 30 days ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=60s)).
- Strava deleted 3.9M activities in 2025 and early 2026 because users gamed segment leaderboards ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=593s)).
- [Robinhood](../businesses/robinhood.md) paid a $7.5M fine in 2024. The order bans celebration tied to trading frequency ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=331s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) calls points, badges and leaderboards the best-documented failures. [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) and [dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md) credit XP, badges and leaderboards for making Locked stand out in a crowded habit category.

> Conflict: [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) says to gamify lightly, because heavy ranks appeal to ages 15 to 23 and the money is in 30 to 40 year olds. [qYYeu0E-1SI](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) builds a heavy game layer for Gen Z and makes over $14K/month (2026-06).

> Conflict: [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) recommends streaks that compound and put earned value at risk. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) warns that streaks turn into obligation and links Snapchat streaks to problematic phone use.
