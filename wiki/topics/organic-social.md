This page collects what sources say about organic social: unpaid posts on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, X, LinkedIn and Reddit that bring users without ad spend.

## Definition

Organic social is growth from posts, comments and creator accounts that the platform distributes for free. Founders, hired creators or account farms make the posts. Paid ads are not organic, but many sources use organic posts to find the creatives that later run as ads.

## Repeated patterns

### Content before product

- **Post the idea before you build it.** [Louis David](../people/louis-david.md) failed with about 10 apps that he marketed after building. For GlowUp he posted first and built once a video went viral. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s)) PushScroll's validation video got 80K views and about 500 comments before the app existed. ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s)) [Sarah Pearl](../people/sarah-pearl.md) reads comments for "I wish this existed" and builds that. ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=777s))
- **If no video can sell the idea, kill it.** [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) says finding a market does not get easier after launch. ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s)) [Lino Leighton](../people/lino-leighton.md) runs a faceless TikTok for 7 days. If the comments ask for the app, he builds it. ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=525s))
- **Pick a category that already goes viral.** Natural Write chose AI humanizers because TikTok was full of viral humanizer videos. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [1:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=119s)) Tabs Chocolate copied a review with 8M views from a seller with no brand. ([5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=91s))

### Copy formats, then iterate

- **Recreate what already works.** Natural Write made 1:1 copies of viral niche videos and innovated only after a hit. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s)) [Steven Cravotta](../people/steven-cravotta.md) logged every viral vape video for 7 days before his first post. ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=211s)) [Vadim Strizheus](../people/vadim-strizheus.md) copies a format exactly and changes only the hook and sound. ([QXxESN94Y6s](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=418s))
- **Change one variable at a time.** Joseph's three layers: copy, iterate one variable, then move a whole concept from another niche. ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=508s), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=617s)) A clone gets a median of about 20% of the original's views, so make 5 to 10. ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s))
- **Small details decide reach.** Eggs in a food hook image and dishwashing in a money video beat other visuals. ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=317s), [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=865s)) "Procrastinating for my exams" sold a scratch map that "post-breakup activity" did not. ([eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [37:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2221s))

### Founder first, creators second

- **Find the format yourself before you pay anyone.** [Jay](../people/jay.md) tests every hook on his own accounts and gives creators only the hits. ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s)) [Kelechi](../people/kelechi.md) made content until a format worked, then gave it to small streamers. ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s)) [Dan Kwon](../people/dan-kwon.md) says the founder makes the most content in a cold start. ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1002s)) [Yiannis](../people/yiannis.md) says a founder must do the research and posting before managing others. ([eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [54:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3295s))
- **Learn the channel before you hire it.** [Kyle Fowler](../people/kyle-fowler.md) says doing it yourself lets you tell a good agency from a bad one. ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:33:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1980s))

### Warm up accounts

- **Act like the target user first.** Use a new phone with a local SIM and VPN. Watch, like and comment in the niche for 15 to 30 minutes a day for about 3 days. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s)), ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s)), ([_a4ch3pk_U0](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [16:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=998s)) Post CTA-free videos first so the algorithm sees a real person. ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1129s))
- **Diagnose a dead account.** Under 300 views is an account problem, 300 to 1K a content problem, and 1K to 5K means vary the copy. ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s))

### Show the product, measure conversion

- **Views are not sales.** A PuffCount video got 11.6M views and almost no installs. ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=775s)) [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) calls meme slideshows "silver" and an 8 to 10 second clip of the app "gold", about 20x better. ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s)) A clickbait hook got 300K views but 8 comments. ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1051s))
- **Hook, then demo.** Copia's hook-and-demo video reached about 10M views, and a video without the demo lost an estimated 20-30% of downloads. ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [5:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=305s), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=376s)) Cardstock shows a card, scans it and reveals the value. ([CAi50V_eIRA](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:24:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1466s)) Social Wizard's streamers used the app on screen and never pitched it. ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s))
- **Read the comments.** "What is the app?" means the format converts. "lol" means it does not. ([-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s)), ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s)) StudyFetch vets creators on comment sections, not views. ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=853s))
- **The VSC test.** Viral (100K+ views on accounts under 5K followers), scalable (you can make it daily), convertible (comments ask for the app). ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:47:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=2821s)), ([-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s))

### Volume and many accounts

- **Post a lot, across accounts.** [Mau Baron](../people/mau-baron.md) posted about 40 times a day across 12 accounts, and the first hook worked after 40 posts. ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=426s)) Halo's team makes about 300 posts a day, and about 5% pass 100K views. ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [24:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1471s)) [Maddox](../people/maddox.md) ran 3 TikTok accounts at 3 posts a day and grew DuckMath from 5K to 150K daily users. ([qIlX7cQ2UdU](../sources/qIlX7cQ2UdU.md), [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=49s), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIlX7cQ2UdU&t=310s))
- **Turn a winner into a series.** Jack Friks made one format about 400 times. ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=304s)) Jenni AI's "POV: you have a paper due" ran twice a week for about 6 months. ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [21:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1276s)) Stronger posts the same format from many accounts with micro edits. ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=530s))
- **Cross-post everywhere.** Repost to Reels, TikTok and Shorts, rewriting the hook text per platform. ([KcCnqLBhvXY](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=1018s)), ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=393s))
- **Expand by country.** Each new country adds about a 20% view lift. ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=766s))

### Calls to action

- **Soft CTAs and comment seeding.** [Casper](../people/casper.md) never says the app's name. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1229s)) Bloom's comment team names the app because "download Bloom" reads as an ad. ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s)) Viewers often close TikTok and search the App Store by name. ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:16:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=966s))
- **Comment keywords.** "Comment APP" with ManyChat sent almost 90,000 DMs for PushScroll, with about 33% DM click-through. The comment count also lifts reach. ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:39:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2367s), [0:37:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2250s))
- **Add the anchor link late.** Below about 1,000 views a TikTok "download" link kills reach. ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s))

### Controversy and engagement

- **Divisive posts get comments.** Sarah Pearl's most controversial videos perform best. ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=489s)) Vanman posts divisive Instagram content and current-event memes. ([6iqo7lNR_m0](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=359s)) PrayScreen's hooks made people argue in the comments. ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=170s)) [Matt Gittleson](../people/matt-gittleson.md) prefers engagement bait that splits viewers to rage bait. ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [31:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1867s))

### Build virality into the product

- **A shareable result.** [GeoSports](../businesses/geosports.md) has a 53% share rate: of 100K daily players, about 53K share their score. ([s9aLA4IOSZE](../sources/s9aLA4IOSZE.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9aLA4IOSZE&t=85s)) Stronger designs features to look good as screenshots. ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=269s))
- **Features for the camera.** StudyFetch's live lecture notes are the wow moment on video. ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s)) Cal AI's camera, loading and food detail screens must read at a glance in influencer videos. ([ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2163s))

### Text platforms: X, LinkedIn and Reddit

- **X.** [Rob Hallam](../people/rob-hallam.md) grew SuperX almost entirely from his own account. X brings 95% of its traffic (2025-12). ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [2:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=155s)) [Nevo](../people/nevo.md) says X is now an interest platform: his article reached about 500,000 views from an account with 200 followers. ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=328s)) [Mark Vassilevskiy](../people/mark-vassilevskiy.md) redesigned famous brands' pages in public. One post brought 15 to 16 cold bookings overnight. ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=209s)) [Nick Buzz](../people/nick-buzz.md) roasted founders' landing pages to win design clients. ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s))
- **LinkedIn.** [Justin Welsh](../people/justin-welsh.md) posts 2 to 3 times a day on LinkedIn and X and has never run a paid ad. ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=307s)) [Eunice Lie](../people/eunice-lie.md) calls LinkedIn followers the highest-value followers. ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1160s))
- **Reddit.** Value-first posts with the link at the end, cross-posted to many subreddits, 2 to 3 times a week. ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=372s), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=557s)) Warm up for 7 to 14 days and keep the product out of the post. ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=477s)) Put the best content in the post itself. ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=588s)) Rootd's founder wrote helpful comments on anxiety posts. ([VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VfNRd5Rk0cM&t=430s))

### Organic finds, paid scales

- **Run organic winners as ads.** Fitted put ad money behind viral videos for downloads at 1 to 15 cents (2025-11). ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)) PuffCount's ads reused proven organic videos, for 3 to 35 cent clicks from day one. ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)) Mau Baron put TikTok Spark Ads on organic videos with more than 10K views. ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=700s)) Organic only shows correlation spikes. Paid gives attribution. ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s))

### Creator pay

- **Retainer plus performance.** Bloom pays a small retainer plus $2 to $4 CPM bonuses, because pure CPM made new creators quit (2025-07). ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [7:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=470s), [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=650s)) Halo pays $20 a video plus milestone bonuses, under $1 CPM in effect. 29 of 30 creators chose milestones over CPM. ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [33:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1990s), [35:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2156s)) Jay pays $1 to $2 CPM with no cap once he has proven the format (2026-02). ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [39:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2390s), [44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2687s)) [Roman Lakhnyuk](../people/roman-lakhnyuk.md) pays no more than $20 a video in about 90% of cases. ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s))
- **Equity for creators.** [Connor Burd](../people/connor-burd.md) gives creators equity and builds each app for their audience. ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=737s)) [Reid Moncada](../people/reid-moncada.md) started a creator at $20 a video, then added vested equity. ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=38s))

## Disagreements

- **Is there a ceiling on organic?** Joseph Choi says organic TikTok and Reels cap out at about $1M to $3M a year per app. ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=192s)) StudyFetch reports eight-figure ARR from a creator program, and Clover Labs $8M a year. ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [3:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=182s)), ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=163s))

> Conflict: [0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md) puts a $1M to $3M/year ceiling on organic per app, while [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) reports eight-figure ARR driven by creators.

- **Is UGC organic at all?** Josh counts TikTok UGC as paid, because you pay for every video. He says buyers want organic growth that costs nothing per post. ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [21:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1287s)) Most other sources treat creator UGC as organic.

- **TikTok or Instagram.** [Canyon](../people/canyon.md) sees Instagram drive 10 to 20% more views than TikTok (2026-09). ([SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1171s)) Connor Burd says Instagram beats TikTok for his posts. ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=527s)) [Kieran](../people/kieran.md) prefers TikTok for conversion, because Reels can go viral abroad and convert near zero. ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s)) Kelechi tests on TikTok, not Instagram, because new TikTok accounts go viral more easily. ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=533s))

> Conflict: [SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md) and [zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md) favor Instagram, while [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) and [9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) favor TikTok.

- **How much volume.** [Caleb Dean](../people/caleb-dean.md) posted 9 Reels a day. Instagram did not punish it, but TikTok views fell to about 5. ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1796s)) [Dillion Verma](../people/dillion-verma.md) sets no posting cap. ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1092s)) [Nic Weber](../people/nic-weber.md) says each niche has a fixed daily attention budget. Flooding it spreads the views across more posts. It does not make more hits. ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [32:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1941s))

> Conflict: [PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) treats posting volume as uncapped upside, while [_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) says a niche's daily views are fixed and extra posts split them.

- **Copy or invent.** Most sources copy proven formats. Sarah Pearl says Stella worked because nothing like it existed. ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=830s)) Matt Gittleson's frame-by-frame copy flopped, so he iterates instead. ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [25:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1520s))

- **Soft or hard CTA.** Casper never names the app, and Bloom leaves it to comments. ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1229s)), ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s)) Matt Gittleson says a viral video that does not name Jenni is useless. ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [33:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2020s))

> Conflict: [I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md) hides the app name to avoid looking like an ad, while [Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) says to plug the product clearly even at the cost of views.

- **Paid influencers.** Natural Write's paid influencers got views and almost no sales. ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)) PushScroll lost about $2K on influencers. ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:25:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1553s)) Payout makes about $45K/month from influencer posts. ([zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=197s))

> Conflict: [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) and [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) found paid influencers unprofitable, while [zpTXi8WxeM0](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md) built a $45K/month app on them, using equity instead of per-post fees.

- **When to switch to paid.** Lino Leighton dropped every UGC and influencer deal at $10K/month and moved to Meta ads. ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)) Jay says paid ads do not pay back for low-ARPU social apps. ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2172s))
