Competitor alternatives covers a cheaper, simpler or better version of a product that already sells. The founder markets it as "the alternative to X".

## Definition

A competitor alternative enters a market that a leader has already proven. It wins on one gap: price, UX, a missing feature, a niche audience or a faster ship rate. It then captures search traffic for "X alternative".

## Repeated patterns

### Competitors prove demand

- No competitors means no market, and creating a market is "nearly impossible" ([rPguT9z6kRQ](../sources/rPguT9z6kRQ.md), [7:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rPguT9z6kRQ&t=470s)).
- An indie founder cannot build the next Uber. Big players prove demand, and you find the gap ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=163s)).
- Look for competitors that do well in spite of bad UX ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)).

### Find the gap in complaints

- **Search "X alternative"** on Twitter and Reddit, then list the pains the leader does not solve ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s)).
- **Read reviews, roadmaps and Discords.** [Shipper](../businesses/shipper.md) mined competitor Trustpilot pages and support chats. Users asked to turn websites into mobile apps, so Shipper added that ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=485s)).
- **Use the product yourself.** [Deven Bhooshan](../people/deven-bhooshan.md) used Taplio every day and saw its core content creation was weak. He built [Supergrow](../businesses/supergrow.md) to be 1% better ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=100s)).
- **Remove jargon.** Competitors felt technical, so Shipper promises a live business with zero skills ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=529s)).

### Pick slow or departing incumbents

- **Dinosaurs.** Telecom players are 20 to 30 years old and slow. Better UX wins ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=520s)). DocSend is about 10 years old and enterprise-focused ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=126s)).
- **Leaders that move up market.** Calendly moves up market, which leaves room for a simple alternative ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=256s)).
- **Shutdowns.** The Skype shutdown news made "Skype alternative" posts go viral ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=249s)).
- **Out-ship them.** Customers switch at feature parity ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=546s)). Shipper caught up with competitors by month 4 and led on features by months 5 and 6 ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=300s)).

### Copy the format, change the niche

- [PrayScreen](../businesses/prayscreen.md) took one sec's "breathe before Instagram" idea and built it for Christians. About 70 clones followed in 2 to 3 months ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=272s)).
- [Stoppr](../businesses/stoppr.md) copied Quittr's onboarding screen by screen in 2.5 weeks and pointed it at sugar ([orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=314s), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=237s)).
- Idea formula: a big product made for people like you, such as Duolingo for cooking or a smaller Calendly ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [9:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=561s)).

### Copy the traffic source too

- Check the competitor's traffic in Ahrefs. Ads plus SEO means strong demand and an easy copy ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=239s)).
- Prefer competitors that live on paid ads. [StoryShort](../businesses/storyshort.md) copied a tool with 100% Facebook-ad traffic and matched that channel in about a week ([8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=320s)).

### Make switching easy

- [Youform](../businesses/youform.md) built a one-click migration: paste a Typeform URL and get a Youform form in seconds ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=193s)).
- [Yadaphone](../businesses/yadaphone.md) sells pay-as-you-go credits where competitors charge about $30/month or per seat ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=127s)).
- [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md) anchors its paid plan below Linktree ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)).

### Win the "alternative" search

- **Comparison pages against every competitor** piggyback on their traffic and get cited by LLMs ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s)).
- **High-intent keywords:** "<competitor> alternatives" and "how much does <competitor> cost" ([Nnpz1wsTjBI](../sources/Nnpz1wsTjBI.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnpz1wsTjBI&t=390s)).
- **"Alternative to" pages early**, paid for with lifetime deal cash, so Google and ChatGPT index them over time ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=340s)).
- **Take the departing giant's SEO.** [Denis Yurchak](../people/denis-yurchak.md) messaged authors of "Skype alternative" articles. Some added Yadaphone ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s)).
- **Announce the alternative publicly.** Marc Seitz tweeted his plan to build an open-source DocSend alternative. It got about 40K views in a few hours ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=77s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [orYTlk_BhW8](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md) has Pat Walls advise copying the app but not the niche, so you do not take the original's customers. [_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) and [88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) go straight at the leader's own users.

> Conflict: [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) undercuts Linktree on price. [_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md) says Typeform's price rise was not enough on its own. For him, price is a valid pain only for a tool that is too expensive.

> Conflict: [8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md) says useArtemis broke Samuel Rondot's own rule, because competing with Apollo and lemlist is hard to maintain. Yet it was his biggest app at ~$15K/month (2025-07).
