Micro SaaS is a small, focused software product that does one job for a narrow market, usually run by one founder or a tiny team without outside money.

## Definition

A micro SaaS solves one specific pain, often for businesses, and earns from a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars a month. In these sources it is bootstrapped, high margin (40% to 98%) and too small for venture capital but enough for one person ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=409s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Find the idea in work you already do

- **Your own job.** [Thomas Mahony](../people/thomas-mahony.md) built [Pckgr](../businesses/pckgr.md) because packaging one app for Microsoft Intune took an hour ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s)). [Angus Cheng](../people/angus-cheng.md) wanted his bank PDFs in Excel ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=62s)).
- **Client work and freelance platforms.** [Vikash Prajapat](../people/vikash-prajapat.md) made 1,800 mockups in 30 minutes with a script and sold the script for $300 ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=136s)). Upwork and Fiverr jobs show repeated work people pay to automate ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=193s), [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [5:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=310s)).
- **The spreadsheet you already sell.** [Jack](../people/jack.md) uploaded his client profit spreadsheets to Cursor as CSV and built [Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md) ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=390s)).
- **Proven listings and app pairs.** Filter Acquire.com SaaS listings by asking price of at least $300K and copy the concept ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=376s)). Popular Zapier app pairs show messy integrations one tool can replace ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=163s)).
- **Use your super skill.** [Dmytro Krasun](../people/dmytro-krasun.md) kept only API ideas because APIs were his strongest skill ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=134s)).

### Competitors are proof, not a threat

- Competitors prove people pay ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=185s)). Read their reviews on G2, AppSumo and Capterra and check how little they use AI ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=584s)).
- Enter a crowded market with a better look or a lower price. [Liinks](../businesses/liinks.md) undercut Linktree ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=526s)). Pckgr skipped enterprises and targeted smaller businesses ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s)).

### Build small and fast

- Build times: Sync2Sheets in 2 weeks, Liinks in 2 weeks, Bank Statement Converter in about a week, Scrape Creators in a couple of weeks ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=269s), [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=95s), [4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=230s)). See [MVP](mvp.md).
- Launch one feature without auth or payments, then add them ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=256s)).
- Plain design is fine if the tool does one useful job ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [9:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=548s)).

### Distribution where the buyer already searches

- **Marketplaces and integrations:** Google Workspace Marketplace for Sync2Sheets, Zapier and Make for ScreenshotOne ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=542s), [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=357s)).
- **Long-tail YouTube tutorials** rank on YouTube and Google without backlinks. A video with 370 views brought $45 MRR ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)). Niche experts' demo videos keep bringing Pckgr customers years later ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=254s)).
- **Reddit:** launch free there, and use F5Bot keyword alerts to reply as the founder ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=193s), [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=590s)).
- **Comment on launches** with a free-credit offer ([4BsxnGRbF4k](../sources/4BsxnGRbF4k.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BsxnGRbF4k&t=283s)).
- **[SEO](seo.md) and LLM answers** bring customers "on autopilot" after early grind ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s)).

### Charge early, price up

- Charge from day one: free usage disappears when you start to charge ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=836s)). Paying beta users prove demand ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=396s)).
- Removing the free plan took Sync2Sheets from about $5K to $8K/month ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s)). ScreenshotOne raised prices and cut its free plan because margins at $5 to $7 were near zero ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=540s)).
- Small rises compound: $4 to $5/month added nearly $5K MRR to Liinks (2026) ([RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md), [3:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSlhgyAXsBI&t=231s)).

### Churn and support

- Email each cancelled customer one yes/no question. Replies near 100%. ScreenshotOne cut churn from about 11% to close to 7% (2024-11) ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=424s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=485s)).
- Support as marketing: record each fix as a video. Ship requests the same day. Treat the first customer as your employer ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s), [hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=704s)).
- The math: one new customer a day at 10% monthly churn gives about 200 customers in a year. At $50 that is $10K MRR ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=765s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: validate before you build. [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) says 10 paying strangers are the real test. [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) says a 1 to 2 week MVP is so cheap that pre-launch validation adds little.

> Conflict: free plans. [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md) and [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) removed free plans and grew revenue. [d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md) keeps a free plan with a 30-day full trial, and [LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md) launched free on Reddit before charging.

> Conflict: paid ads. [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) spent about $1,000 on Google ads per $300 in sales and cut them. [RSlhgyAXsBI](../sources/RSlhgyAXsBI.md) calls Google Search Ads "money in, money out" for about 4 years.

> Conflict: lifetime deals. [hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) made $0 with a lifetime deal and switched to subscriptions. [W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md) sold BulkMockup as a lifetime deal for a couple of years before moving to monthly.

> Conflict: launch sites. [TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md) says Product Hunt helped awareness and SEO. [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md) got traffic spikes from Product Hunt and Hacker News but few paying users.
