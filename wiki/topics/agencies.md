This page collects what the sources say about agencies: service businesses that sell skilled work such as design, video, SEO, ghostwriting or UGC to clients.

## Definition

An agency sells done-for-you work to clients, usually on a monthly retainer. Many sources run a productized agency with a fixed offer and price. Others use agency cash to fund products, or sell software to agencies.

## Repeated patterns

### Agencies make cash fast and fund the next thing

- An agency needs no capital and is profitable from the first client. The downside: every 2 to 4 new clients need another hire ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)).
- [Tim Stoddart](../people/tim-stoddart.md) reinvests agency cash in cash-flowing assets such as [Copyblogger](../businesses/copyblogger.md) and [Recovery Local](../businesses/recovery-local.md) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=134s)).
- [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md) uses agency cash to fund its own Shorts brand, [Smart Nonsense](../businesses/smart-nonsense.md), at a loss ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=563s)).
- [Hunter Hammonds](../people/hunter-hammonds.md) treats productized services as a cash engine that will fund products and SaaS later ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md)).
- [Avenue](../businesses/avenue.md)'s founder paid his first hires from a 10-year-old agency with 70+ hosting clients ([mr4YV-9MY0Q](../sources/mr4YV-9MY0Q.md), [5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mr4YV-9MY0Q&t=350s)).
- An agency diversifies. [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md)'s SEO agency kept him afloat after Google wiped out his content sites ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=280s)).

### Win clients with free public work

- Blitz: [Henry Belcaster](../people/henry-belcaster.md) and [Dylan Jardon](../people/dylan-jardon.md) made free videos for All-In until Jason Calacanis hired them ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=224s)).
- Squatter marketing: make videos for a big creator for a year unpaid. Their network assumes you work together ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [5:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=338s)).
- [Mark Vassilevskiy](../people/mark-vassilevskiy.md) posted free redesigns of famous brands on X. One post got 110K views and 10 to 12 cold bookings overnight ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=153s)).
- [Nick Buzz](../people/nick-buzz.md) "roasted" founders' landing pages in public and showed his fixes ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s)).

### Content brings inbound clients

- About 95% of [Vector 3](../businesses/vector-3.md) clients come from LinkedIn posts. Jake does no cold outreach ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)).
- [Baked](../businesses/baked-design.md) leads stalled until the team went back to posting roasts ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=855s)).
- [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md)'s ACP funnel: audience, then community, then product. He met his first LCA clients at parties he promoted to his audience ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- Be your own case study. Prospects with more followers than you will ignore you ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=634s)).
- [Alex Lieberman](../people/alex-lieberman.md) validated [Storyarb](../businesses/storyarb.md) with one tweet that got 25 DMs ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)).

### Outbound still works

See [cold outreach](cold-outreach.md).

- [Marcos Ruiz](../people/marcos-ruiz.md) sends 100 to 200 cold DMs a day, plus warm DMs and 2 to 3 tweets a day ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s)).
- [Cardinal Mason](../people/cardinal-mason.md) listed every company that ran Instagram ads and cold-emailed the founder. A company that pays for ads has a budget for copy ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=250s)).
- [Karl Hughes](../people/karl-hughes.md) keeps a list of about 50 people and contacts a few each week. His first 5 to 10 customers came from it ([i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s)).

### Sell retainers and outcomes, not hours

- Sell retainers of $2K to $4K/month, not one-off gigs ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=630s)).
- Sell revenue, not followers. If the client makes more than they pay, they stay ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=593s)).
- Price on outcome. LCA charges about $1.5M/year, against a typical agency fee of $1,500/month ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- Target $5,000 clients, not $500 clients. Cheap clients nag and churn ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [5:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=342s)). See [productized services](productized-services.md).
- A freelancer tops out at about $30K to $40K/month. Past that you must hire and build an agency ([9pP4qSrt5GU](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=522s)).

### Systemize delivery and step out

- A Vector 3 trailer follows a 45-step template, each step with an SOP and a video ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)).
- [Zipsnap](../businesses/zipsnap.md) gives each client a Notion portal from day one, then runs six stages from discovery to handoff ([wmTkiF23GRQ](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md), [2:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=147s)).
- Henry spends about 1 hour a day on Clipt. The rest goes to content ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=690s)).
- Alex Lieberman hires a CEO for each business early and aims for 5 to 10 owner hours a week ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
- Hire editors offshore. A US editor at $5K to $6K was too expensive, so Clipt hired in the Philippines ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)). See [offshoring](offshoring.md).

### Creator-led agencies

- [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md) co-owns each agency with a creator who brings distribution. Creators are equity partners, not affiliates ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)).
- Match the service to what the creator teaches. Ali Abdaal fronts a YouTube agency ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s)).

### Sell software to the agency layer

- One agency brings 10 to 30 brands and many seats. [Hiro Analytics](../businesses/hiro-analytics.md) passed $1M ARR in about 19 months (2026-06) ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=58s)).
- Shopify and Klaviyo partner directories rank agencies, so they work as a lead list ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)).
- [Lancer](../businesses/lancer.md) began as an internal tool at Ivan's Upwork agency and reached $10K MRR by month 3 or 4 ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=266s)).
- [Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md) turned the spreadsheets Jack sold to agency clients into a Shopify app ([d8PdJJcEtE0](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=335s)).

### Hiring a growth agency

- One founder lost $30K/month on an agency with good case studies but poor incentive fit ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=695s)).
- [Evo Marketing](../businesses/evo-marketing.md) separates its organic traffic from other agencies with vanity domains ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=706s)).
- Founders can borrow design taste from a freelancer or agency ([YS7uOXjmJTA](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=435s)).
- Some agencies built on Lovable make over $200K/month from internal tools (2025-04) ([qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=99s)).

## Concrete examples

- [DesignJoy](../businesses/designjoy.md): over $2M a year with no staff (2024-01) ([vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=0s)).
- [Baked](../businesses/baked-design.md): $24K to $160K MRR within months, then $100K to $120K (2024) ([TNZmNIYv050](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=918s)).
- [Skale](../businesses/skale.md): $10K, $20K, $40K, $80K/month in 10 months. Average retainer about $20K/month (2025-10) ([YtyJ4reSQY0](../sources/YtyJ4reSQY0.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YtyJ4reSQY0&t=427s)).
- [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md): about $2M/year with about 50 animators (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=64s)).
- [Draft.dev](../businesses/draft-dev.md): over $2.5M with 6 to 7 staff and hundreds of contractors (2023-10) ([i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=63s)).
- [The Birdhouse](../businesses/the-birdhouse.md): $0 to $65K/month in a year, about 60% margins (2024-06) ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s)).
- [Storyarb](../businesses/storyarb.md): 12 clients at $7,000/month, about $1M annualized (2023-11) ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
- [Stuzzi](../businesses/stuzzi.md): about $2.5M expected for 2024 (2024-04) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=72s)).
- [Indexsy](../businesses/indexsy.md): $85K/month via Stripe alone (2026-03) ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=106s)).
- [Vector 3](../businesses/vector-3.md): $36,150 so far in the month, $20K to $25K of it retainers (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=999s)).
- [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md): four public agencies above $1M ARR in 4 months. Team from 3 to over 50 (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=109s), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)).
- Tim Gabe's Web3 design agency peaked at $40K/month (2025-01) ([dg_LmOjapRA](../sources/dg_LmOjapRA.md), [1:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dg_LmOjapRA&t=68s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) sells unlimited requests for one monthly fee (DesignJoy). [wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md) says fixed scope, no "unlimited": tell customers exactly what volume they get.

> Conflict: [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) says underprice to win the first client, then raise prices. [i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md) positions Draft.dev as premium from the start: no discounts, no trials, quarterly commitments.

> Conflict: [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) says keep growth in-house, because agencies add about a 20% margin and weaker incentives. [0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md) says an agency is worth considering at $50K to $100K/month.
