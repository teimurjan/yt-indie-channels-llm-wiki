Behavioral design is the use of psychology findings in product design to change what users do: sign up, pay, return and share. All sources on this page are videos by designer [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md), who runs the studio [Zipsnap](../businesses/zipsnap.md).

## Definition

Behavioral design ties each screen, timer or reward to a named effect, such as loss aversion, the Zeigarnik effect or the peak-end rule. Tim's claim is that the best apps win as "psychological engines", not on features or looks ([source](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=0s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Trust in the first seconds

- People form a first impression in about 50 milliseconds and decide on trust in under 3 seconds ([source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=98s), [source](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=297s)).
- A bad first impression can lose 20 to 30% of new users at once ([source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=98s)).
- A money app must answer three worries. Is this company real? What if something goes wrong? Am I still in control? ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=48s)).

### Fewer choices

- **Hick's law.** More choices mean slower decisions. Apple Fitness gives a new user one action, not 30 workouts ([source](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=187s)).
- **One call to action.** An Unbounce study of 18,000+ pages found 13.5% conversion with one CTA, 11.9% with two and 10.5% with three ([source](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=34s)).
- **Remove friction, then add back on purpose.** Netflix's Skip Intro had 136 million presses a day by 2022 ([source](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [3:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=193s)). The goal is the right level of friction, not zero ([source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=262s)).

### Open loops and clocks

- **Zeigarnik effect.** People want to finish what is unfinished. Keep the next threshold close ([source](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=445s)). A 90% Apple Watch ring is an open loop the brain wants to close ([source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=556s)).
- **Put the hero feature on a clock.** [FarmVille](../businesses/farmville.md) crops rot. [Hinge](../businesses/hinge.md) Standouts reset every 24 hours. [Life Reset](../businesses/life-reset.md) runs a boss fight every two days ([source](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=112s), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=242s), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=413s)).
- **Show a stake, not a metric.** In [Forest](../businesses/forest.md), a switch to Instagram kills the tree. The timer is secondary ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=257s)).

### Loss aversion

- People feel a loss about twice as strongly as an equal gain ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=396s)). A missed Standouts window is lost for good ([source](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=303s)).
- **Never let users finish.** Audit the product for "done" states. Peloton's lifetime totals never cap ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=501s)).

### Forgiveness

- **Design for the miss.** Plan what the app does the day after a user skips ([source](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=264s)).
- [Duolingo](../businesses/duolingo.md) raised the streak freezes a user can hold from 1 to 2, and daily active learners rose 0.38% ([source](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=326s)). A streak with no pause or exit is the version regulators watch ([source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=410s)).
- **Anchor to an existing routine** instead of reminders. Habits took 18 to 254 days to form in one study ([source](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=85s), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=146s)).

### Social comparison

- **Shrink the cohort until winning feels possible.** [Strava](../businesses/strava.md) segments are thousands of local leaderboards ([source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s), [source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=154s)).
- **Show the comparison at completion**, and recognize effort as a person does ([source](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [4:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=268s)).
- **Live social proof.** Discord shows lit avatars and "2,312 people online" ([source](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=381s)).
- **The social layer as a moat against AI agents.** Agents can do the task but cannot replace belonging ([source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [2:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=128s)).

### Identity

- **Celebrate who users are, not what they did.** [Spotify](../businesses/spotify.md) Wrapped tells users who they are ([source](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=546s)).
- **Labels retain.** [16Personalities](../businesses/16personalities.md) types go into dating bios. People rarely churn from their own identity ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=401s)).
- **The flex.** Make the share card the invite. [Airbuds](../businesses/airbuds.md) gates the full weekly recap behind invites ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s)).
- **One identity number.** [Whoop](../businesses/whoop.md)'s weekly Pace of Aging score changes what Tim does ([source](../sources/ZXWUg1e1PAs.md), [6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZXWUg1e1PAs&t=419s)).

### Timing of asks

- **Win mapping.** By the peak-end rule, put paywalls, rating prompts and share prompts right after a win, not at moments of confusion ([source](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=443s)).
- **Give before you ask.** The endowment effect: [SolCard](../businesses/solcard.md) shows the user's own card from sign-up onward ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=170s)).
- **Value first.** Breathwrk puts users in a breathing session during onboarding ([source](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [0:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=50s)).

### Emotional design

- Most apps stop at usability. Emotion is how a product stands out ([source](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md), [10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=600s)).
- Add emotion only where it pays, as Duolingo does with celebration and guilt ([source](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [8:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=504s)).
- [Carrot Weather](../businesses/carrot-weather.md) used the same data as Apple Weather but made the delivery the product ([source](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s)).
- Physical feedback, such as [Apple](../businesses/apple.md)'s rubber-band scroll and haptic clicks, makes software feel real ([source](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=47s)).
- An explained wait feels shorter. SolCard designed 15 status screens ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s)).

## Disagreements

- **Onboarding length.** One video says do less: keep steps few and hide long step counts ([source](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=134s), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=430s)). A later one says a long quiz is not friction but an amplifier, because effort makes the result feel earned ([source](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=243s)).
> Conflict: [55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md) favors short onboarding. [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) favors long, effortful onboarding.
- **Streaks.** Streaks turn into obligation and draw regulators ([source](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [6:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=362s)). Zipsnap still built a diamond streak system for Freecash as an "infinite game" ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=451s)).
> Conflict: [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) warns against streaks. [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) recommends streaks that compound.
- **Rewards and reminders.** A 2015 Bristol study found neither built lasting habits ([source](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=53s)). Variable rewards are the "craving machine" ([source](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=114s)), and Zynga stacked reminders on FarmVille's clock ([source](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s)).
> Conflict: [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) says rewards and reminders do not build habits. [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) and [VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md) use them as core mechanics.
- **Fees.** StubHub's hidden fees raised purchases 14%, but Tim says a fee found at the end kills the second deposit ([source](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=219s)). Both claims come from one source.
