Affiliate marketing is a commission paid per referred sale. Founders here use it from two sides: they earn commissions as publishers, and they pay commissions to grow their own products.

## Definition

A publisher sends buyers to a brand through a tracked link and gets a commission on each sale. [7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=60s)

## Repeated patterns: earning commissions

- **Search content carries the links.** [FinvsFin](../businesses/finvsfin.md) targets "best X for Y" queries. [7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md) [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=398s). [Shervin](../people/shervin.md) makes search-based product reviews on YouTube. [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) [6:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=414s). [Charlie Chang](../people/charlie-chang.md) makes evergreen search videos such as "how to invest in stocks". [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) [6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=371s)
- **Affiliate is often the biggest stream.** Shervin earns 80% from affiliate and 20% from AdSense (2023-04). [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s). Charlie earns 50% to 55% from affiliate (2024-02). [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s). [Wishlists](../businesses/wishlists.md) makes most of its money from affiliate links (2025-09). [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=661s)
- **Go direct once you send sales.** After about 6 months of traffic, FinvsFin pitched brands directly, and the brands named their price per customer. [7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md) [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s). A fitness-band company moved Shervin from free months to $50 per referral. [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) [7:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=475s)
- **Concentration risk.** Two companies make up about 50% of Shervin's affiliate income (2023-04). [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s). Big publishers such as Forbes and Healthline erode a site's rankings over time. [7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md) [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)

## Repeated patterns: paying affiliates

- **A known cost per customer.** [StoryShort](../businesses/storyshort.md) pays affiliates who make YouTube videos and articles about it. [Samuel Rondot](../people/samuel-rondot.md) gets reach at a fixed cost per customer. [8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md) [9:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8BtHk-oNlN0&t=558s)
- **Recruit connectors, not single users.** [Lancer](../businesses/lancer.md) pays Upwork coaches a lifetime commission. One coach deal replaces hundreds of sales calls for a $200/month product. [LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s)
- **Growth that runs without the founder.** [Sheets Resume](../businesses/sheets-resume.md) uses an affiliate marketing agency next to Google Ads and Reddit. [yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=624s)
- **Pay in product.** [Threadly](../businesses/threadly.md) and its sister brands run an ambassador or affiliate program that gives free product for content and promotion. [CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=799s)

## Concrete examples with numbers

- **FinvsFin.** Over $1M/year from about six affiliate sites (2024-03). Organic traffic took about 6 months. One brand partner can go on five sites. [7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=0s), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s), [8:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=515s)
- **Swim University.** $1M in revenue in 2023. It went from AdSense to affiliate only (mostly Amazon), because the ads looked irrelevant. Then it moved to its own courses and a book, which now bring most of the money. [7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s), [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s). See [Swim University](../businesses/swim-university.md).
- **Lancer terms.** A coach who signs and onboards a client gets 30% for life. A plain referral gets 20%. Upwork coaches bring 5 to 20 new clients a month. [LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [7:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=463s), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=524s)
- **Lancer playbook.** Define the ICP, find connectors, send a very personal pitch, agree on 20% to 30% lifetime commission, then track and pay monthly. A big network can also get an up-front payment. [LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md) [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=571s), [11:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=692s)
- **Wishlists.** About $150K/year with 1.1M registered users (2025-09). Product links that users import, for example from Amazon, become affiliate links. [w3zxMrwWrt0](../sources/w3zxMrwWrt0.md) [0:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=49s), [9:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=598s), [11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w3zxMrwWrt0&t=661s)
- **Charlie Chang.** Over $2.4M a year from YouTube (2024-02). The mix is 50% to 55% affiliate, 20% to 25% sponsors and 20% to 25% AdSense. [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=60s), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)

## Disagreements

- **Sponsors next to affiliate links.** Shervin held off sponsors, because a sponsor read can cut views. [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s). Charlie takes 20% to 25% of revenue from sponsors. [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=115s)

> Conflict: [DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md) avoids sponsors to protect watch time. [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) treats sponsors as a core stream.

- **Affiliate as the end state.** For Shervin, Charlie and Wishlists, affiliate is the largest stream. Swim University treats affiliate as a middle step before its own products. [7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) [4:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=296s)

> Conflict: [G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md) earns most from affiliate. [7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) earns most from its own products.
