B2B SaaS is subscription or usage-priced software sold to businesses, from solo-built niche tools to venture-scale platforms.

## Definition

Software that a business pays for, usually monthly, to save time or make money. In this wiki most examples are bootstrapped and sold self-serve, with a few that use sales calls ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=97s); [WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)). See also [SaaS](../topics/saas.md) and [micro-SaaS](../topics/micro-saas.md).

## Repeated patterns

- **Enter a proven market and fix one flaw.** Competitors prove demand; be better on one axis ([ZEIPj7QbZeI](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=542s)). Find the twist in the leader's 1-star and 2-star reviews ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=232s)). ListKit copied Apollo but verified the leads ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [11:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=702s)). Old, slow incumbents are the best targets ([F8i0kkrQ8_o](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=126s); [88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=520s); [MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [10:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=608s)). See [competitor alternatives](../topics/competitor-alternatives.md).
- **Find the pain at work.** B2B pains come from inside businesses, not Reddit ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=288s)). Pckgr, Lancer and AngelMatch all began as a problem the founder hit in his own job ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=136s); [LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=266s); [5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=281s)).
- **Validation means payment.** Setter AI took a refundable $500 deposit before the product existed ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)). ListKit ran a manual productized service first ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=227s)). Charge early, even at a low price ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s)). See [idea validation](../topics/idea-validation.md).
- **Sell to the layer above the customer.** One agency or coach brings many end customers ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=302s); [LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=401s)). Or trade equity for a partner's distribution ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=251s)).
- **Capture search intent.** Comparison pages, free tools and programmatic pages are the most cited channel ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s); [Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s); [w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=570s); [5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s); [E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=411s)). See [SEO](../topics/seo.md).
- **Build on a platform's marketplace.** It sends qualified leads and users trust approved apps ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s); [vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=580s); [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [2:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=135s)). See [platform marketplaces](../topics/platform-marketplaces.md).
- **Talk to every customer early.** Onboarding calls, texts to every payer, founder phone numbers ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [14:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=872s); [88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=644s); [XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [13:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=807s)). Andy Cloke went a year without talking to users; one afternoon of tests raised revenue almost overnight ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=596s)). See [customer interviews](../topics/customer-interviews.md).
- **Cut friction to first value.** lemlist's redesign took activation from 15% to 35% ([ZEIPj7QbZeI](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=768s)). ChartDB needs no sign-up or install ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=207s)). Visualizee replaced a node editor with chat ([TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=249s)).
- **Pick a niche too small for big companies.** It suits a solo founder ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s); [LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=335s); [2_lpZ503ZGg](../sources/2_lpZ503ZGg.md), [2:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_lpZ503ZGg&t=174s)).

## Concrete examples

- [UpLead](../businesses/uplead.md): first $1M ARR from cold email alone; over $30M total sales (2025-10) ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [8:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=509s), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=457s)). See [cold outreach](../topics/cold-outreach.md).
- [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md): $1M ARR within 87 days, about 40 staff, costs $150K to $175K/month (2024-06) ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=77s), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: ListKit gives no free trial, only a money-back guarantee, because trials attract "freebie seekers" ([WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=619s)). Hiro gives every agency a 30-day free trial ([NPpky92ZfjA](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=149s)), and SiteGPT converts 25% to 40% of trials ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=156s)). See [free trials](../topics/free-trials.md).

> Conflict: Mike Hill says never give accounts away free ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s)). ChartDB stayed free until usage showed what to charge for ([7vz6b_Ohdl0](../sources/7vz6b_Ohdl0.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7vz6b_Ohdl0&t=397s)), and Kaching launched free until reviews arrived ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s)).

> Conflict: Setter AI says validation means a card swipe before you build ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=238s)). Chatbase did no validation and launched to 16 followers ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=62s)).

> Conflict: Mike Hill avoids AI-dependent businesses because of platform risk ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=621s)). Chatbase advises building ahead of the next model ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=499s)).
