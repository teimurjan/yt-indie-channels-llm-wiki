This page covers how founders in the sources pick partners, split roles and equity, and handle partner exits.

## Definition

A co-founder is a partner who shares ownership of a company from the start, usually with equity in place of salary. In these sources the shape ranges from best friends to spouses, a team of four equal partners, and creators who join for distribution.

## Repeated patterns

- **One domain per founder.** Cal AI's four co-founders each owned one area and had no real fights ([source](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sAr9FWEQZAk&t=220s)). ListKit's five co-founders split CEO, CTO, sales and two marketing roles ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=136s)).
- **Complementary skills, shared values.** Marketing, sales, technology and a leader, with aligned values and vision ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=750s)). Clipt pairs a shipper who ships at 80% with a perfectionist ([source](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [9:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=597s)).
- **Trust over convenience.** NeuroGum's Ryan Chen: "you go into battle with your best friend" through years of inventory and freight grind ([source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md), [9:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=567s)).
- **Find technical partners through relationships.** Colin McIntosh says you cannot convince a random engineer to build your idea without paying a lot; his partners came from years of work and from Reddit ([source](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=482s)).
- **The distribution co-founder.** A creator joins for equity and brings the audience. Casper is the 50/50 distribution co-founder of [Payout](../businesses/payout.md) ([source](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [3:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=209s)).
- **Bought out when it no longer fits.** Sebastian Ghiorghiu bought out his non-technical co-founder at Atlas, then wrote the target and steps to $250K/month ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)).

## Disagreements

- **How much equity a builder gets.** Colin McIntosh splits equity three ways with his engineer and says you cannot get a random engineer without paying a lot ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=482s)). Casper gave the developer of his third app only 15% and notes he could pay $10K to $15K for a build instead ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [50:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=3037s)).
> Conflict: [yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md) treats the technical builder as an equal partner; [I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md) treats the build as a small-equity or cash job once distribution exists.
- **More partners or fewer.** Mike Hill says four co-founders reduce founder fallout ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=171s)). Sebastian Ghiorghiu grew Atlas fastest after he bought out his only co-founder ([laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=363s)).
> Conflict: [67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) argues for more equal partners; [laRliIVNZgU](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md) shows growth after reducing to one owner.
