Productivity here means how founders plan their day, protect focus and decide what to work on. Most sources are "How I Work" interviews from [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md).

## Definition

Productivity is the output a founder ships per unit of time and energy. In [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s), the one KPI is the number of things you ship, not hours.

## Repeated patterns

- **One protected deep-work block.** [Marc Lou](../people/marc-lou.md) keeps 4 to 6 hours of deep work at the start of the day. He allows no phone, email or support in that block. [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=72s). [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) did two hours every morning before his day job for about a year. [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=138s), [GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=187s). [Rob Hallam](../people/rob-hallam.md) works one block of up to 8 hours at night with no messages. [NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md) [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=212s)
- **Few tasks per day.** [Sam Parr](../people/sam-parr.md) thinks he can finish only 1 or 2 real tasks a day. So he writes each task down and puts it on the calendar. [jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md) [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=224s). [Jack Friks](../people/jack-friks.md) works on one project a day besides support. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=166s)
- **A rolling list, ranked by users.** Marc keeps a ship list and picks the item with the most user impact. [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=317s). Rob keeps a short list of features, improvements and bugs. A bug that brings five emails is critical, and a one-off request is not. [NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md) [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=264s), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=283s)
- **Quantity beats perfection.** Marc shipped about 35 startups with a hit rate of about 5%, so he plays the numbers. [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=163s). Sam cites a pottery-class study: the group that made many pots made better pots than the group that made one perfect pot. [jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=377s)
- **Sleep and a hard stop.** Rob sleeps 9 hours and turns devices off an hour before bed. [NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md) [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=409s). Jack sleeps 7 to 8 hours and stops at dinner, after laptop dinners hurt his relationship. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=420s), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=481s). Marc eats dinner at 5:30 and goes to bed at 9 p.m. with no alarm. [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=514s)
- **Systems over willpower.** Sam's 5-year journal shows the same complaint on the same date across years. So he uses systems such as a hired coach and the Brick phone blocker. [jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md) [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=74s), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=153s)
- **Work in a state of play.** Rob shipped most in the places where he was happiest. [NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md) [7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=465s). Pat plays tennis one to two hours a day to get away from metrics. [GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=462s)

## Concrete examples with numbers

- Marc Lou: $77K/month alone, about 300 marketplace features in 3 months with AI, and six new apps in 2026 so far (2026-04). [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=1s), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=415s)
- Jack Friks: $40K/month alone (2026-08). Some weeks have 4 hours of work and others 40. Launch weeks get 10-hour days. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=0s), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=107s)
- Rob Hallam: $22K/month while he travels (2026-08). Work on [SuperX](../businesses/superx.md) only sped up once he stayed 3 months in one place. He now keeps a base for 2 to 3 months. [NFWV9wxRuv4](../sources/NFWV9wxRuv4.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=0s), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NFWV9wxRuv4&t=105s)
- Sam Parr: companies over $10M/year (2026-07). From $0 to $10M he used a "zebra calendar" of back-to-back 15-minute customer calls. At $5M to $10M he delegates. [jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=0s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=377s), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=437s)
- Pat Walls: a 5-minute Loom made for Pieter Levels became a product with about $20,000 in pre-orders in one day (2024-10). [GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) [0:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=31s)
- Tools: keyboard shortcuts save an average of eight days a year, per Brainscape. [zFGG0tyLJtI](../sources/zFGG0tyLJtI.md) [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFGG0tyLJtI&t=0s). An AI email assistant such as [Serif](../businesses/serif.md) drafts replies so that creative time stays creative. [qmJVN2158Mk](../sources/qmJVN2158Mk.md) [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qmJVN2158Mk&t=390s)
- Founder burnout: Pat says the business he built for freedom took over every minute of his life, and he sold it to [HubSpot](../businesses/hubspot.md). [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=295s), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s)

## Disagreements

- **Support first or last.** Marc reads no support tickets at the start of the day, because a bug report pulls him away from new work. [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D4fkiQfzw_I&t=100s). Jack does support first every day, because it is his least favorite task and fast fixes keep users. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=32s)

> Conflict: [D4fkiQfzw_I](../sources/D4fkiQfzw_I.md) puts support after deep work. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) puts support first.

- **Where to work.** Pat goes to a coffee shop for deep work. [GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=187s). Jack says cafes do not work for him and uses one fixed desk. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) [1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-aEdS28AH0&t=82s)

> Conflict: [GFnBp8lMIf4](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md) works from coffee shops. [k-aEdS28AH0](../sources/k-aEdS28AH0.md) rejects cafes.

- **Build next to a job or take a break.** Pat built [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md) in early mornings next to a full-time job. [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=138s). [Sebastian Röhl](../people/sebastian-rohl.md) had no energy after 8 hours of coding at work, so he saved a 12-month runway for a sabbatical. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) [11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=713s)

> Conflict: [v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) builds around the day job. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) needed a sabbatical to start.
