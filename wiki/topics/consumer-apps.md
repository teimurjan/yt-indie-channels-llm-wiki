This page collects what the sources say about how to build and grow consumer apps.

## Definition

A consumer app sells to individuals, not to companies. In these sources it is usually a small iOS or Android app with a subscription paywall. Short-form video, creators and paid ads grow it.

## Repeated patterns

### Ideas: copy proven demand, then niche down or localize

- **Clone a winner for a new audience**. Glam Up is "Umax for women" ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=299s)). [Cal Buddy](../businesses/cal-buddy.md) is a Hebrew [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=143s)).
- **Take a trend to a late niche**. [PrayScreen](../businesses/prayscreen.md) took the one sec idea to Christians. Faith gets new technology last ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=427s)).
- **Mine marketplaces and charts**. [Go Tall](../businesses/go-tall.md) started from an app listed on Acquire.com for about $20,000 ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)). Others search Sensor Tower for niches where many apps make over $10K/month ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=525s)).
- **Pick a niche you live in**. [3AK](../businesses/3ak.md) serves track athletes ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=299s)). [CutCoach](../businesses/cutcoach.md) serves wrestlers ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=373s)). [ToneAdapt](../businesses/toneadapt.md) serves guitarists ([Q4k8JNYKJT0](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=516s)). [Go Polar](../businesses/go-polar.md) tracks cold plunges ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=291s)).
- **Sell a core desire**. Health, money and looks sell ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=683s)). Niches with high spend on physical products, such as skin care, convert ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=459s)).
- **Single-player first**. Social features double the marketing work ([PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=489s)). Small teams do better to avoid games, where paid ads dominate ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1128s)).

### Build: fast, cheap and AI-assisted

- Builds take days to weeks with Rork, Cursor or Claude Code. [Payout](../businesses/payout.md) took 14 days ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=76s)). A prayer app took 3 days to code ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=236s)).
- One founder built 28 apps in 8 months next to a day job ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=84s)).
- A common stack is React Native or Swift, Supabase or Firebase, RevenueCat, Superwall and PostHog ([32vqaJa90kw](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md), [12:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=773s)).
- App Review is often the slowest step. [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md) spent two weeks on rejections ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=220s)). Cal Buddy took about 2 months to go live ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=188s)).

### Distribution: content first, then creators, then paid ads

- **Find a format yourself, then give it to creators**. Kelechi did this for [Social Wizard](../businesses/social-wizard.md) ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s)). Nicole did it for Glam Up ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s)). Jay tests every hook on his own accounts first ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s)). See [UGC](ugc.md) and [Short-form video](short-form-video.md).
- **Make the video before the app**. [PushScroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md) validated with a video that got 80K views and about 500 comments ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s)). Stella's founder reads comments for "I wish this existed" ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=777s)).
- **Show the product, not a pitch**. Make the product the main character ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=38s)). A short clip of the app in use converts about 20x better than a meme slideshow ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s)).
- **Judge by comments, not views**. "What is the app?" means that the format converts ([-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s)). SideShift and StudyFetch use the same test ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s)), ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=853s)).
- **Put paid spend behind proven organic content**. [Go Tall](../businesses/go-tall.md) started ads only after $30K/month ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)). [Puff Count](../businesses/puff-count.md) reused organic winners and got 3 to 35 cent clicks ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)). See [Meta ads](meta-ads.md).
- **Partner with creators for equity or a revenue share**. One creator, [Max](../people/max.md), gave [Fitted](../businesses/fitted.md) about 95% of its distribution ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [9:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=574s)). An influencer revenue share took Cal Buddy from $20K to $80K/month ([GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=464s)). See [Creator programs](creator-programs.md).
- **Build sharing into the product**. The NGL link in a user's bio is the viral loop ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=316s)). Glam Up blurs results until the user pays or invites 3 friends ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1096s)).
- **Other channels work in some niches**. App Store search carries [Go Polar](../businesses/go-polar.md) ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=88s)). Web SEO carries [Vault Air](../businesses/vault-air.md) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [6:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=392s)). Google Play ads carry [Journable](../businesses/journable.md) ([0pp4X58q_0s](../sources/0pp4X58q_0s.md), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pp4X58q_0s&t=458s)).

### Onboarding and paywall

- **Long onboarding, then the paywall**. A quiz personalizes the app and builds sunk cost. The result then sits behind the paywall ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s)). With a paywall, about 90% of users only see the onboarding ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s)). See [Onboarding](onboarding.md).
- **Tim's rule**. Keep onboarding short by default. If a long quiz buys personalization or filters for buyers, make it long ([Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Aa89MC8jX2c&t=239s)).
- **Paywall work moves revenue more than features**. [Momego](../businesses/momego.md) ran about 10 paywall tests in 2021. Conversion went from 0.5% to 8%, and revenue went from about $8K to over $30K/month ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s)).
- [Curiosity Quench](../businesses/curiosity-quench.md) went from $500 to $1K/month to $2K to $4K/month after a template paywall with a trial ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=946s)). See [Paywalls](paywalls.md).
- **Weekly plans are common for young users**. Glam Up charges $8.99 to $12.99 a week. When a user closes the paywall, it offers 50% off ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [24:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1496s)).
- Teen users will not pay $60 to $70 a year, so 3AK charges $30 a year ([DiRxTfnHTaY](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)).

### Retention and design

- Design for the missed day, as Duolingo's streak freeze does ([IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=264s)). See [Retention](retention.md).
- Put personality in the core screen, as [Carrot Weather](../businesses/carrot-weather.md) does ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=140s)).
- Rebuild the main action around new tech. Cal AI made the camera the first action ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=313s)).
- Give value first and sell upgrades in context, as [Tinder](../businesses/tinder.md) does ([4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md), [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yKtlOWClhA&t=610s)).
- One commitment screen raised [Symmetry](../businesses/symmetry.md) retention by 3% ([OV09yPLf8BI](../sources/OV09yPLf8BI.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OV09yPLf8BI&t=706s)).

### Benchmarks

- Margin by channel is 50 to 80% for in-house UGC and 0 to 30% for paid ads ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- Joseph Choi puts paid ads at 10 to 30% margin and mass UGC at about 50% ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=63s)).
- Organic TikTok and Reels cap out at about $1M to $3M a year per app ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=192s)).
- A year-one resubscribe rate of 50% is amazing, and 20% is bad ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s)).
- An organic install share under 20% means a UA treadmill ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [20:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1216s)).
- The average app keeps 7% of users after 30 days (2026-04) ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=60s)).
- Apple takes 15% up to $1M a year, closer to 20% in practice ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- iOS app releases rose 80% year over year in Q1 2026 ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=0s)).
- Apps sell for 2x to 6x ARR, and over 90% of deals fall through ([NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md), [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1242s)). See [Acquisitions](acquisitions.md).

## Disagreements

- **Hard paywall or freemium**. A hard paywall lifted Glam Up conversion by 50% ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [40:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=2401s)). It gave [Stronger](../businesses/stronger.md) about 25% overnight ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [24:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1452s)). The Fitted founder says it limits growth ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1066s)). [Lovely](../businesses/lovely.md) moved to freemium after bad reviews ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [28:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1730s)).
  > Conflict: [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) and [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) say a hard paywall raises revenue. [9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md) and [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) moved away from it.
- **Low or high prices**. Blake Anderson says low prices bring more users and word of mouth ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)). Glam Up went from $3 to $9 a week, and conversion rose ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [22:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1368s)).
  > Conflict: [5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) favors low prices. [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) found that higher prices converted better.
- **Organic creators or paid ads**. Conch AI dropped paid ads for low margins ([2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [10:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=625s)). Jay says social apps cannot pay back paid ads ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2172s)). The [AbMaxx](../businesses/abmaxx.md) founder cut all creator deals and doubled down on Meta ([RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).
  > Conflict: [2cDIFx4WWD0](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) and [RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) favor organic. [RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) found paid ads more profitable than creators.
- **TikTok or Instagram**. StudyFetch says TikTok converts better, and Reels can convert near zero abroad ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s)). Canyon says Instagram now drives 10 to 20% more views (2026-09) ([SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1171s)).
