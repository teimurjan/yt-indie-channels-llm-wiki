Acquisitions covers buying and selling small online businesses (apps, SaaS, content sites and brands), from a $100 site flip to nine-figure app exits.

## Definition

An acquisition is a sale of a whole business, or a controlling part of it, to another owner. In these sources the buyers are indie flippers, app studios and aggregators, private equity, strategic companies and acquihirers.

## Repeated patterns

### Selling

- **Build to sell, then repeat.** Several founders plan the exit from day one: Lotts Ezeike aims to exit in 6 to 12 months for about $250K; Domenico Gagliardi sets a 3- or 6-month exit goal that drives the roadmap; Will Baker sells each app to a buyer who can scale it with UGC [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=229s), [source](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md) [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=138s), [source](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrsGDDLP7WM&t=220s).
- **Sell for runway.** Adam Lyttle sold apps at about 2x annual revenue to fund the next launch, then moved to a portfolio so he no longer had to sell [source](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md) [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=712s), [13:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=797s).
- **Take the fast close, not the top bid.** The top bid takes longest and fails most. Prefer the third-highest offer that closes in 3 to 5 days [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=300s), [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1242s). Over 90% of deals fall through [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1297s).
- **Sell on an uptick and before you need to.** Show a rising last three months; let the buyer pay for the coming peak. Twelve months of growth reads as a machine; three months can be three viral videos [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=611s), [source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2591s), [45:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2757s). Boost App Social sold to "take chips off the table" before bans or CPM spikes [source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2145s).
- **Package an asset someone else can run.** A plug-and-play stack, clean operations, and metrics buyers check: ARR, gross margin, ARPU, LTV, CAC and churn [source](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PmyXmpkTIdc&t=550s), [source](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md) [3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=198s). Boost App Social's sale included the warm ad account and about 300 perpetual-rights creator contracts [source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) [39:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=2345s).
- **Leave growth levers for the buyer**, such as untranslated markets [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=1050s).
- **Pick the buyer type.** Aggregators want growth they can 10x; strategics pay most for fit; PE wants cash flow; acquihires are rare in consumer apps. MyFitnessPal bought Cal AI for its funnel [source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) [35:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2118s), [39:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2375s). Pat Walls chose HubSpot for alignment, not only price [source](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md) [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=512s).
- **Where sales come from:** Acquire.com listings written like a landing page, Twitter, brokers, and inbound DMs. Bullish tweets from a 200-follower account drew Runafy's acquirer; a DM started Jenni AI's acquihire of Matt Gittleson [source](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md) [12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=752s), [source](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md) [44:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=2668s), [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [53:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=3197s).

### Buying

- **Buy where your skill adds value.** A marketer buys a proven tool from a founder who cannot market it [source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md) [12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=776s).
- **Buy organic distribution with weak monetization.** Steve Young adds a hard paywall and raises prices; paid-only apps are "a money eating machine" [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s), [44:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2652s).
- **Buying apps can beat buying ads.** Ryan Thorp found undermonetized apps paid back faster than ad spend, and the asset stayed his [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=360s).
- **Buy after the viral peak.** Social buys declining apps whose sellers cannot grow them, then screens new targets by running ads for apps it does not yet own [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=404s), [9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=577s).
- **Find deals by direct outreach** with a stated budget and intent; skip brokers on $10K deals [source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md) [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=396s), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=457s). Karl Hughes talked to 50 to 100 founders and bought one with debt [source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md) [10:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=655s).
- **Finance with debt.** A $10K/month-profit app bought for $350K on a 10-year loan nets about $5K/month from day one [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [43:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2591s).
- **Negotiate on priorities**: cash, transition time or team. Do not fight all 10 points [source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md) [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=866s).
- **Good targets** are evergreen, have recurring customers and are growing [source](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md) [8:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Km0FNcBI9CM&t=482s).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: valuation multiples differ by source. [Km0FNcBI9CM](../sources/Km0FNcBI9CM.md) says 4x to 10x annual profit (2023-04). [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) says 2x to 4x EBITDA (2025-11). [NmIHgCRd04E](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) says 2x to 6x ARR (2025-09). [h9R6ZeNruuI](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md) sold at 2x revenue. [u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) says fast-growing apps with 30-50% EBITDA get up to 6x run-rate revenue (2026-07).

> Conflict: [PmyXmpkTIdc](../sources/PmyXmpkTIdc.md) and [plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md) advise building to sell within months. [sAr9FWEQZAk](../sources/sAr9FWEQZAk.md) shows Cal AI turning down low eight-figure offers in late 2024 and growing toward about $50M a year, and [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) shows Jack Friks turning down $1M for Post Bridge.

> Conflict: [Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) says buy only apps with organic distribution, because paid-only apps eat money. [oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) and [DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) buy apps and grow them mainly with paid ads.

> Conflict: [u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) says creator-led apps sell hard because the buyer needs the creator to stay. [zrsGDDLP7WM](../sources/zrsGDDLP7WM.md) builds apps specifically to sell to buyers who scale them with UGC creators.
