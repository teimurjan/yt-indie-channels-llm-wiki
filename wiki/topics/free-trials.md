This page collects what the sources say about free trials: how long they run, where they sit on the paywall, and when to drop them.

## Definition

A free trial gives the user the paid product for a fixed time, usually 3 to 30 days, before the first charge. In mobile apps it usually sits on a [paywall](paywalls.md) right after [onboarding](onboarding.md).

## Repeated patterns

### Put the trial on the annual plan

- **The trial is an exclusive perk of the plan you want to sell.** Moonly put the trial on annual only and got +39% conversion and +47% revenue per 100 installs [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s). Headspace gave a 7-day trial on monthly and 14 days on annual, for a double-digit lift [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s).
- **Common setup.** Annual with a 3 or 7-day trial, and weekly or monthly with no trial. Examples: [Coherence](../businesses/coherence.md) at $40/year with a 3-day trial or $10/month without (2025-11) [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=611s). [Locked](../businesses/locked.md) at $40/year with a 3-day trial or $7/week [source](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md) [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s). [Wrestle AI](../businesses/wrestle-ai.md) at $59.99/year with the trial on yearly [source](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md) [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=96s). [Pep AI](../businesses/pep-ai.md) at $45/year with a 3-day trial (2026-05) [source](../sources/vbEKEWtnndU.md) [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbEKEWtnndU&t=85s).
- **Hide the no-trial plan.** [Pingo](../businesses/pingo.md) shows annual with the trial and a weekly price. Monthly has no trial and sits behind "view all plans" [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2716s). [Built With Science](../businesses/built-with-science.md) moved annual first and monthly one tap away. Annual picks went from about 60% to 75% to 80% at the same price (2026-10) [source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s).

### Remove trial anxiety

- **Show the trial as a timeline.** Blinkist's day-by-day timeline gave +23% trial signups [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s). It still performs after years [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [40:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2417s). [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) copies it with "No payment due now" and a reminder 2 days before the end [source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md) [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1405s).
- **Promise a reminder.** Sunflower's trial reminders raised revenue 48%, trial conversions 46% and trial starts 30% [source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) [46:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2785s). Pingo's winning page said "reminded 2 days before trial ends" [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2716s). Cal AI used a 3-screen explainer headed "No payment due now" [source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=256s).
- **Say "free", not "trial".** Claim's paywall says "free" 5 to 7 times, calls annual "Try it free" and has a "Try for $0" button. Do not print "3-day trial" on the plan card, because it makes people think about cancelling [source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=645s), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s). Headspace renamed "trial" to "30-day guest pass" for +7% signups [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=106s).
- **Cut steps.** A one-tap trial start gave Slopes +25% trial starts [source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md) [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=259s).

### Trial plus hard paywall

- **A trial required before any use.** [Puff Count](../businesses/puff-count.md) moved to a hard paywall with a free trial and conversion went to 20% to 25% (2024-09) [source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md) [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=547s). [Stronger](../businesses/stronger.md) got about a 25% boost overnight [source](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) [24:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1452s). [Monai](../businesses/monai.md) A/B tested a 7-day trial behind a hard paywall against freemium, and the trial won [source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md) [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=89s).
- **It doubles the trial start rate.** On one app the hard paywall raised trial starts from 6% to 13%, trial-to-paid held, and revenue doubled [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1174s).
- **Long onboarding feeds the trial.** It adds sunk cost, so more users start the trial [source](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md) [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s). Coconote went from about 5 to about 13 onboarding screens and trial conversion rose 16% [source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) [29:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1761s).

### Save the user who leaves

- **Reverse trial on close.** [Momego](../businesses/momego.md) gives users who close the paywall 7 days of Pro, with no card [source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md) [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=646s).
- **Trial toggle.** Rizz offers $9.99/week with a 7-day trial or $6.99/week to skip it. About 10% of users pick no trial on an abandonment paywall with a toggle [source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) [22:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1328s), [34:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2085s). High-intent apps such as Cal AI keep one price. Younger audiences need a cheaper no-trial option [source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) [25:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1512s).
- **Layer longer offers.** Pingo users who abandon a 3-day trial and then get a 7-day offer convert higher [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [50:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3048s).
- **Extend in the cancel flow.** A 7-day extension saves 27% of Coconote users who start to cancel [source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) [31:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=1906s).

### Read the funnel

- **Install day decides.** 82% (RevenueCat) to 89.4% (Adapty) of subscription starts happen on install day. 55% of 3-day trial cancellations happen on day 0 [source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s). About 50% of trial users cancel right after they subscribe [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=703s).
- **High trial starts with low trial-to-paid means the product fails the promise.** [PropGPT](../businesses/prop-gpt.md) had 45% install-to-trial but 13% trial-to-paid. After a rebuild, trial-to-paid passed 50% [source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md) [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=410s), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=235s). If trial-to-paid is under the 30% to 40% norm, fix the product, not the channel [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1716s).
- **Viral spikes give low-quality trials.** Coconote's "PDF to brain rot" feature drove 3,000 trial starts in a few days. At full conversion that was $360K, but it made about $25K [source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=251s).
- **Young users trial cheaply and convert worst.** Ask age in onboarding to map trial-to-paid by age. Trial-to-paid comes about 7 days late for Meta's optimizer [source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md) [0:41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2501s), [0:45:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2733s).
- **Test trial structure before price.** In Adapty data, trial-structure tests lift LTV +59.6%. A weekly plan has $7.40 12-month LTV without a trial and $54.50 with one [source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s).

### SaaS and web trials

- **Longer trials.** [Hiro Analytics](../businesses/hiro-analytics.md) gives every agency 30 days free [source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md) [2:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=149s). [Tech Lockdown](../businesses/tech-lockdown.md) gives 14 days, then $15/month (2025-07) [source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md) [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=104s). [Profit AI](../businesses/profit-ai.md) gives free users a 30-day trial of the paid plan so they see the whole app [source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md) [2:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=162s).
- **Card up front on the web.** For [Curiosity Quench](../businesses/curiosity-quench.md), a 7-day card-required trial with no free plan beat pushing yearly plans [source](../sources/qeY283nBhIA.md) [13:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qeY283nBhIA&t=795s).
- **Free content as a long trial.** [Boot.dev](../businesses/boot-dev.md) gives its content free and charges for interactivity [source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md) [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=666s).

## Disagreements

- **Trial or no trial.** Claim removed its trial because paid-only made more money. Vahe Baghdasaryan says that needs high volume, strong trial rates and a pressing problem [source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=999s), [16:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1017s). [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md) uses a money-back guarantee instead, because trials attract "freebie seekers" [source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md) [10:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=619s). Tim Gabe's data says trials raise LTV and beat discounts [source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s), [source](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s).

> Conflict: [WctFUP4w0sc](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md) and [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) drop the trial. [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) and [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) find trials earn more.

- **Trial length.** Pushscroll found a 7-day trial beat 3 days [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:52:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3166s). In Pingo's US test the two were nearly equal, and the 3-day trial had a 5% to 7% higher cancel rate [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [52:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3164s). Many apps still pick 3 days on annual [source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md) [23:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1439s), [source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md) [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=611s).

> Conflict: [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) says 7 days wins. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) found no clear US difference.

- **Hard paywall with a trial.** Many sources gate the app behind a trial and see higher revenue [source](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md) [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=547s), [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [19:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1174s). Phoenix Do thinks a hard paywall signals a weak product and praises Strava's trial-led model [source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) [14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=855s).

> Conflict: [loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md) and [rGLXc1GmsaI](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md) favor a trial behind a hard paywall. [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) is skeptical of hard paywalls.

- **Trial start benchmark.** Tangent Growth calls 15%+ good [source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=291s). Steve Young aims for 5% to 10% [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1716s).

> Conflict: [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) sets the bar at 15%. [Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) sets it at 5% to 10%.

## Other notes

- Erikas launches Shopify apps free, adds a trial later, and offers full refunds, because a one-star review costs more [source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md) [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s).
- Koby Conrad's paywall sequence for Sunflower: monthly only, add annual, make annual default, add a trial toggle, then offer weekly, monthly and annual [source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) [43:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2622s).
- [Mojo](../businesses/mojo.md)-style transformation apps use a hero's-journey paywall with progress steps [source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) [14:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=896s).
