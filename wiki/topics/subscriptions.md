This page covers recurring billing for apps and SaaS: plan mix, prices, trials, and the numbers founders report.

## Definition

A subscription charges a user again each week, month or year until they cancel. In these sources it is the default model for consumer apps and most SaaS, sold through a [paywall](paywalls.md), often after a [free trial](free-trials.md).

## Repeated patterns

- **Switching to subscriptions is often the biggest jump.** Kyle Fowler's move to subscriptions in 2021 was his largest revenue jump ($5/month, $50/year, $99 lifetime) ([source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md), [0:11:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=685s), [0:19:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1180s)). Momego went from about $8K/month in banner ads to over $30K/month after a subscription update and about 10 paywall tests ([source](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s), [9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=573s)). Visualizee was stuck on one-time payments until monthly plans ([source](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=450s)). Julien Nahum: "$50/month is easier than $500 once" ([source](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=743s)).
- **Most subscriptions start on install day.** 82% (RevenueCat) to 89.4% (Adapty) of starts happen on day 0, and 55% of 3-day trial cancellations happen on day 0 ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s)).
- **Few plans, annual preselected.** Show 2 plans, hide the rest behind "View all plans", preselect annual for highest LTV ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=641s)). Plan-count tests give 63% more conversion uplift than price tests ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s)).
- **Give annual the exclusive perk.** Moonly put the trial on annual only: +39% conversion, +47% revenue per 100 installs. Headspace gave annual a 14-day trial and monthly 7 days ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=319s)). Coherence: $40/year with a 3-day trial or $10/month with none (2025-11) ([source](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [10:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=611s)).
- **Kill trial anxiety.** Trial timelines (Blinkist +23% signups), "guest pass" wording (Headspace +7%) ([source](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=106s)). Trial reminders lifted Sunflower's revenue 48% ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [46:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2785s)). About 50% of trial users cancel right after they subscribe ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [11:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=703s)).
- **Price for the audience.** Teens who spend $10 to $20 at a time get a $30/year plan at 3AK ([source](../sources/DiRxTfnHTaY.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DiRxTfnHTaY&t=341s)). Most 18 to 24 year olds cannot pay about $70/year ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:40:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2452s)). Odd prices like $3.33/month feel authentic and add about $4/year per user ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [31:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1866s)).
- **Discount only the annual plan on exit.** Weekly and monthly discounts failed in dozens of tests ([source](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s)). On close, restate annual in weekly terms ($39/year = 76 cents/week), then offer 25% to 33% off ([source](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s)).
- **Test a lot.** Apps with 50+ paywall experiments a year make 18.7x the revenue of apps with one ([source](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=638s)). Flo ran over 100 a month ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [49:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2946s)). Blake Anderson's tests lift revenue per download 1.5 to 2x ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)).
- **Retention is the real metric.** Year-one resubscribe: 50% amazing, 40% good, 30% okay, 20% bad ([source](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [17:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1028s)). Product hooks such as hidden partner answers keep couples app Flame sticky ([source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=227s)). See [Retention](retention.md).
- **Push annual in SaaS but keep monthly.** Tech Lockdown: $15/month or about $10/month annual, because many users quit after a month ([source](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=104s)). Savvy Nomad charges up front, so cash runs above MRR ([source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=84s)).

## Disagreements

- **Weekly or annual.** Weekly grew from 43% to 55.6% of subscription app revenue in two years and converts 1.7 to 7.4x better than annual; "ask for a coffee" ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=83s), [2:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=130s)). Others preselect annual and read many weekly buyers as an onboarding failure ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1006s)), and say weekly apps rarely make sticky acquisition targets ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [33:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=2010s)).
> Conflict: [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) says lead with weekly; [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) and [u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) favor annual.
- **Low or premium prices.** Blake Anderson keeps prices low for more users and word of mouth ([5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s)); Sunflower made more money after lowering its price ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [44:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2675s)). Josh says anything under $10/month should test higher prices ([u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md), [31:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u4tpE9AvBpc&t=1861s)).
> Conflict: [5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) and [zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md) favor low prices; [u4tpE9AvBpc](../sources/u4tpE9AvBpc.md) favors premium prices.
- **Trial or no trial.** A weekly plan has $7.40 12-month LTV without a trial and $54.50 with one ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s)). Claim dropped its trial and went paid-only because it made more ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=999s)); Welltory runs no trial ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [39:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=2363s)).
> Conflict: [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) shows trials multiply LTV; [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) shows a paid-only switch winning when trial numbers are strong.
- **Do subscriptions last?** Koby Conrad predicts cheap software turns apps into lead magnets for services, goods and ads ([zFfBuyKoaDM](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [49:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=2962s)). Most other sources treat subscriptions as the default.
