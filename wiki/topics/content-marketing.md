This page collects what the sources say about content marketing: free posts, videos, articles, newsletters and podcasts that bring buyers to a product.

## Definition

Content marketing means publishing useful or entertaining content so the right people find you, trust you and then buy. In these sources it covers founder posts on X and LinkedIn, YouTube tutorials, blog articles, newsletters, Reddit posts and short-form video.

## Repeated patterns

### Teach the solution, and make the product one part of it

- [CJ Zafir](../people/cj-zafir.md)'s "tutorial marketing": the hook names the problem, the body shows the full solution, and the product is one part of it. He writes 4 threads and 3 long posts a week ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=624s)).
- [Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md)'s "edu-selling" posts teach LinkedIn tips and mention the tool only at the end ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=291s)).
- [Data Fetcher](../businesses/data-fetcher.md) wrote posts and videos for its most common integrations ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s)).
- [Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md) publishes a tutorial for each use case. Most customers come from organic search ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=317s)).
- [BulkMockup](../businesses/bulkmockup.md) turns customer pain into low-view tutorials. A 370-view video brought 3 customers ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=334s)).

### Discovery, trust, then the offer

- [Justin Welsh](../people/justin-welsh.md): social posts reach new people. The newsletter builds trust. The offer comes last and is not aggressive ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=366s)).
- Convert rented attention into owned attention. Pat Walls optimized a signup box so he no longer depended on Reddit ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)).
- Email converts better than viral posts, because you do not compete with an algorithm ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=572s)). See [email marketing](email-marketing.md).
- [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md)'s videos pitch a paid template, a free template or an email opt-in. The first email offers the paid product with a discount ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)).
- [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md) links each video to a newsletter lead magnet and pitches later by email ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=495s)).

### Build the audience before the product

- [Starter Story](../businesses/starter-story.md) published stories for months and added a newsletter before it tried to make money ([T_wEmB5MapE](../sources/T_wEmB5MapE.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wEmB5MapE&t=249s)).
- [Tech Lockdown](../businesses/tech-lockdown.md) built an audience with free guides, YouTube and Reddit for about a year before charging ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=203s)).
- [Coherence](../businesses/coherence.md) posted first. What went viral showed what to build ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s)).
- [Epic Gardening](../businesses/epic-gardening.md) saw its audience ask about a metal raised bed and sold it. It sold out in 2 weeks ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=387s)).
- Post for 14 days to warm up an audience, then build for its main pain ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=624s)).

### One idea, many formats

- [Swim University](../businesses/swim-university.md) turns one long video a week into short clips, SEO posts and emails ([7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)).
- Coherence tests ideas on X, turns winners into short-form scripts, then into YouTube videos ([nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=286s)).
- [Danny Miranda](../people/danny-miranda.md) cut each podcast into daily clips: 1 YouTube video, 2 Shorts, 3 Reels and 5 tweets (2023-06) ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=634s)).

### Native posts on Reddit and LinkedIn

- Put the whole story inside the Reddit post, with a small link at the end ([ptT0tfzeu8I](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [3:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=208s)). See [Reddit marketing](reddit-marketing.md).
- [Roman Czerny](../people/roman-czerny.md) tells a story with proof and keeps the product out of the post. His Reddit posts got over 11M impressions and about 100 customers (2026-01) ([pvjalHFNM9Q](../sources/pvjalHFNM9Q.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvjalHFNM9Q&t=237s)).
- About 95% of [Vector 3](../businesses/vector-3.md) clients come from LinkedIn posts ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=937s)).
- [Eunice Lie](../people/eunice-lie.md) says LinkedIn followers are the highest-value followers ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [19:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1160s)).
- [Gaurabh Mathure](../people/gaurabh-mathure.md) posted everywhere for 30 days. LinkedIn was the only signal, and vlog-style video stood out there ([AoE1yX9ogCM](../sources/AoE1yX9ogCM.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AoE1yX9ogCM&t=418s)).

### Stand out with a pattern break

- Common formats go unnoticed. Real Hinge chats about her startup stood out for Eunice Lie ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=109s)).
- Everyone posts loud vertical video, so try calm, horizontal video ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [16:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=992s)).
- [Milly Tamati](../people/milly-tamati.md)'s success grew as she got more honest and more weird ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=524s)).
- [Boot.dev](../businesses/boot-dev.md) follows "purple cow": do not copy competitor websites ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=127s)).

### Volume and consistency

- Quantity beats perfection. Sam Parr cites a pottery-class study ([jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=377s)).
- Thirty bad videos teach more than two perfect ones ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s)).
- Justin Welsh posts at 8:15 a.m. every day, then engages for 45 to 60 minutes ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [8:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=538s)).
- Danny Miranda did 359 episodes in 2.5 years without missing a week (2023-06) ([Fk2J2U7669o](../sources/Fk2J2U7669o.md), [12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fk2J2U7669o&t=747s)).
- Test, then do winners 2x to 5x more ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [10:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=621s)).

### Customers and users as content

- [Guillaume Moubeche](../people/guillaume-moubeche.md) wrote early users' campaigns himself. In return they appeared as success stories in his content ([ZEIPj7QbZeI](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [10:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=643s)).
- A long demo can work as an ad. Thomas Frank's 45-minute walkthrough took sales to about $100K/month ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)).
- [Alia](../businesses/alia.md) tested a new position on sales calls first, then in content ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=483s)). See [positioning](positioning.md).

### AI content at scale

- [Double Speed](../businesses/double-speed.md) runs AI slideshows on phone farms. One client got 4.7M views from 15 accounts in under 4 weeks (2025-10) ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [19:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1149s)). See [TikTok farming](tiktok-farming.md).
- Formats decay. One strategy works for a few months, a year at most ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s)).
- [Career Hound](../businesses/career-hound.md) pays creators about $20 a video and gives them a simple format sheet. It got 11.7M views in 30 days (2026-09) ([xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=106s), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s)).

## Concrete examples

- [Marketing Examined](../businesses/marketing-examined.md): 50 Twitter threads in 50 days took followers from 500 to 40,000 ([5MaPEK-yVVY](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=336s)).
- [Ultimate Brain](../businesses/ultimate-brain.md): about $90K in 30 days from one email to a 3,200-person waitlist ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=317s)). About $2.5M lifetime template sales (2024-04) ([OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=81s)).
- [Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md): $30K MRR in 4 days, $62K MRR in under 2 months, from LinkedIn content, a waitlist and webinars (2026-01) ([XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=63s)).
- [SuperX](../businesses/superx.md): X brings 95% of traffic. MRR went from $1K to over $13K in four months (2025-12) ([VYT-ip21P08](../sources/VYT-ip21P08.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYT-ip21P08&t=80s)).
- Justin Welsh: about $1.7M in a year, no employees, no paid ads (2023-04) ([v6IF-lVuvbs](../sources/v6IF-lVuvbs.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=116s), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs&t=307s)).
- [CodeGuide](../businesses/codeguide.md): one tweet got about 300 signups. $42K MRR after about 3 months (2025-05) ([0T55DRYqeeA](../sources/0T55DRYqeeA.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=352s), [1:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T55DRYqeeA&t=96s)).
- [Boot.dev](../businesses/boot-dev.md): $0 to $2K/month came from Lane's blog. Free courses on freeCodeCamp took it from $10K to $30K/month ([tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=481s)).
- [Draft.dev](../businesses/draft-dev.md) sells technical content itself: over $2.5M (2023-10). A third of its clients come from search and social ([i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=396s)).
- [Shervin Shares](../businesses/shervin-shares.md): about $125K/year, 80% from affiliate links in search-based reviews (2023-04) ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [8:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=499s)).
- [The Pathless Path](../businesses/the-pathless-path.md): about 10,000 copies and $50K in royalties in year one. The author markets through podcasts and gifted books (2023-12) ([lWwZ-fsD8gM](../sources/lWwZ-fsD8gM.md), [1:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWwZ-fsD8gM&t=89s)).
- [Generalist World](../businesses/generalist-world.md): $30K/month from a newsletter, community and job board (2026-09) ([ICTRNFIdZvc](../sources/ICTRNFIdZvc.md), [0:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ICTRNFIdZvc&t=4s)).

Other sources apply content to search: [FinVsFin](../businesses/finvsfin.md) ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md)), [FollowBuddy](../businesses/followbuddy.md) ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md)), [Elephas](../businesses/elephas.md) ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md)), [VEED](../businesses/veed.md) ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md)), [Arvow](../businesses/arvow.md) ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md)), [Vault Air](../businesses/vault-air.md) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md)), [Signaturely](../businesses/signaturely.md) ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md)) and [Stuzzi](../businesses/stuzzi.md) ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md)). ## Disagreements

> Conflict: [xEoLsAQFnOY](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md) prefers timeless content, because trends die in a week. [spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md) and [VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md) ride trends and news, because being first on a new topic ranks even a small channel.

> Conflict: [MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md) says "build in public" tweets went unnoticed because everyone posts them. [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) says Outrank grew from $0 to $20K/month with building in public alone.

> Conflict: [jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md) has Sam Parr warn against topics picked only for views. [kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) optimizes content for views with automated accounts and cloned formats.
