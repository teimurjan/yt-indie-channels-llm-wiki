# Short-form video

Short-form video is vertical video of roughly 6 to 60 seconds on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat, and in these sources it is the main organic growth channel for consumer apps.

## Definition

Founders, hired UGC creators, influencers or automated "reposter" accounts post short videos and slideshows that show a product or a problem, and turn views into downloads through comments, links and App Store search.

## Repeated patterns

### 1. The founder finds the format first, then creators scale it

- Kelechi made Social Wizard content himself until a format worked, then gave that exact format to small streamers ([9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=243s)).
- Glam Up's founders each warmed up 3 accounts. After a format went viral, they scaled to 30 or 40 creators ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s)).
- Pingo's two founders made every video until $20K MRR, about 3 months ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1105s)).
- Jay tests every Nomadtable hook himself; all flops happen on his own accounts ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s)).
- Michael Que posted 10 TikToks a day for a month to find Go Tall's format, then DMed about 50 creators a day to copy it ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s)).
- Without traction you have no proof for creators, so start with founder content ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1067s); [eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [54:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=3295s)).

### 2. Copy a proven format, then vary it

- Make high-quality 1:1 copies of viral niche videos; innovate only after videos go viral ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s)).
- Three layers: copy, iterate one variable at a time, then move a whole concept from another niche into yours ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=508s), [10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=617s)).
- A clone gets a median of about 20% of the original's views; 5 to 10 clones match it ([ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=291s)).
- Copy formats, not ideas: Puff Count dunked a vape in water because viral videos did it first ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=257s)).
- Change only the hook and sound; keep demo and CTA ([QXxESN94Y6s](../sources/QXxESN94Y6s.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXxESN94Y6s&t=418s)).

### 3. Views are not conversions: show the product and target intent

- Jack Friks calls meme slideshows "silver" and an 8 to 10 second clip of the app in use "gold", converting about 20x better ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=1362s)).
- Puff Count's 11.6M-view video drove almost no installs ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=775s)). Coconote's "PDF to brain rot" got 100M to 200M views but made about $25K ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=308s)).
- Massive's 11M-view video brought only about $20K MRR; call out the paying audience in the first 3 seconds ([r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=489s), [17:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1025s)).
- The same scratch-map video got no downloads as "post-breakup activity" and climbed the App Store as "procrastinating for my exams" ([eh1SDHX84vQ](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md), [37:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=2221s)).
- Make the product the main character; can the viewer picture themselves using it? ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=38s)).
- Revenue = views x conversion rate ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=134s)).

### 4. Comments are the signal

- "What is the app?" means a format converts; "lol" or arguments mean it does not ([-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s)). AI UGC draws "is this AI?" instead ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s)).
- Vet creators on active comments, not views ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=853s)).
- Engagement rate (likes + comments + saves + shares) / views above 12% often goes viral ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=1104s)).
- Comments also show ideas: Go Tall started from teens posting their heights under videos ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=290s)).

### 5. Volume across many accounts

- Halo AI's creators make about 300 posts a day; about 5% pass 100K views and 1% pass 1M ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [24:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1471s)).
- Noise sends out at least 100,000 posts a day, most at 500 to 2,000 views ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [3:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=212s)).
- Prayer Lock: about 40 posts a day across 12 accounts; the first working hook came after 40 posts ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=426s)).
- Three posts a day per account avoids shadowbans ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2171s)).
- Warm up new accounts for 3 to 5 days on niche content before posting ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=267s); [LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [4:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=272s); [_a4ch3pk_U0](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [16:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=998s)).
- The "300-view rule": under 300 views is an account problem, 300 to 1K a content problem ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:29:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=1741s)).

### 6. Build the viral moment into the product

- Cal AI's photo scan is the viral feature ([_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:22:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1363s)); influencer videos show three screens, so those must read at a glance ([ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2163s)).
- StudyFetch builds features for the camera, such as live notes streaming onto the screen ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s)). Coconote built creator-only screens ([E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md), [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=660s)).
- Stronger designs features to look good as screenshots, like "you lifted a Boeing 747" ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [26:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1606s)).

### 7. Calls to action

- ManyChat "comment a word" DMs the link and lifts comment counts ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [10:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=656s); [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:37:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=2250s)).
- Comment seeding from other accounts steers viewers to the app ([-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=492s); [AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s)).
- Add an anchor link only after a video passes a few thousand views ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=423s)).
- Vanity domains like flightcheatcode.com give attribution and say the value ([VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=706s)).
- Many TikTok viewers later search the App Store by name, so App Store search downloads are partly TikTok ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=220s)).

### 8. Organic winners become paid ads

- Boost proven organic videos: Fitted paid 1 to 15 cents per download ([9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=153s)). Puff Count spent $82K and went from $3K to $44K MRR at 3 to 35 cent clicks ([hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md), [16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=979s), [17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1026s)).
- Spark Ads on organic videos over 10K views ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=700s)); kill a Spark if engagement drops below 5% ([qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md), [35:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2145s)).
- Organic finds, paid scales and gives attribution ([F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md), [28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1718s)).

### 9. Test the idea with video before building

- Pushscroll posted the video before the app: 80K views and about 500 comments ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s)).
- GlowUp, Coherence and Stella all posted content first and built what went viral ([LYdJ3dfNCVs](../sources/LYdJ3dfNCVs.md), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYdJ3dfNCVs&t=186s); [nsn94Ad47GY](../sources/nsn94Ad47GY.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsn94Ad47GY&t=224s); [Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=777s)).
- Kill the idea if no video can sell it ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s)).

### 10. Organic has a ceiling and formats decay

- Organic TikTok and Reels cap at about $1M to $3M/year per app; mass UGC organic margin is about 50% against 10 to 30% for paid ads ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=63s), [3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=192s)).
- Formats last about 3 to 6 months ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [27:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1648s)), a few months to a year at most ([kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md), [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s)).
- Each niche has a fixed daily views budget; flooding it spreads views thinner ([_aNVPMvd8WU](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md), [32:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1941s)).

## Disagreements

- **Show the app or hide it.** Casper never says the app's name ([I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=1229s)); Bloom uses an indirect CTA and a comment team ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=295s)). Joseph Choi says do not hide the app ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=38s)), and Pingo shows it in every video ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [16:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1000s)).

> Conflict: [I10xtUiXfeY](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md) and [AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md) keep the app implicit; [Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md) and [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) make the product the center of the video.

- **Paid influencers for a new app.** Dan of Massive validates new apps with $1K to $2K of influencers because UGC iterates too slowly ([r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s)). Natural Write's paid influencers got views and almost no conversions ([1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)), and Pushscroll's test lost about $2K ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:29:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=1744s)).

> Conflict: [r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md) uses influencers to validate; [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) and [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) found influencers did not convert and self-made posts did.

- **Paid ads.** Jay says social apps should skip paid ads because ARPU is too low ([RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [36:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2172s)). Go Tall passed $100K/month only with paid ads ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=593s)).

> Conflict: [RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) says paid does not pay back for a social app; [EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md) and [hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md) scaled utility apps with ads on top of organic.

- **TikTok versus Instagram.** StudyFetch and Kelechi prefer TikTok: higher intent, and new accounts break out more easily ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s); [9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=533s)). Caleb Dean found Instagram did not punish 9 Reels a day, while his TikTok views fell to about 5 ([yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md), [29:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yw5iIgO4PbY&t=1796s)).

> Conflict: [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) and [9WWvLj-NqEE](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md) favor TikTok; [yw5iIgO4PbY](../sources/yw5iIgO4PbY.md) got better volume results on Instagram.

- **Paid platform.** Max Hirsch prefers Meta for buyer intent ([aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s)). Mau Baron got $4 per trial on TikTok against about $10 on Meta ([yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md), [9:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=564s)).

> Conflict: [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) favors Meta ads; [yBjcmMhXSDk](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) found TikTok cheaper per trial.

- **Copy or invent.** Most sources copy proven formats. Sarah Pearl says Stella worked because nothing like it existed ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=830s)), and a frame-by-frame clone flopped for Jenni AI ([Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [25:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1520s)).

> Conflict: [1nfPvJKrYYQ](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md) and [ka3gqzmUuvc](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) build on 1:1 copies; [Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) and [Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) warn that pure copies underperform.

- **How long formats last.** Sarah Pearl reuses the same viral formats year after year ([Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eg93po_DAYo&t=559s)); others see 3 to 6 months ([AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [27:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=1648s)).

> Conflict: [Eg93po_DAYo](../sources/Eg93po_DAYo.md) says formats keep working for years; [AtYlYzw40vo](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md) and [kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) say they decay within months.

- **Launch videos versus UGC.** Eunice Lie's cinematic launch videos win attention ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=378s)); Dris says UGC converts about 4x better at 10 to 20x lower cost ([szgT-8gXd3s](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md), [8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=szgT-8gXd3s&t=489s)). See [Launch strategy](launch-strategy.md).
