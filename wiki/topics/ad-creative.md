This page collects what app founders and ad buyers say about the videos and statics they run as paid ads: hooks, formats, volume and how to find a winner.

## Definition

Ad creative is the asset itself (video, static, text wall) that runs on [Meta](meta-ads.md) or [TikTok](tiktok-ads.md). These sources treat it as the main lever in paid growth, ahead of targeting and bids.

## Repeated patterns

- **Creative is the 10x lever.** Targeting and small tweaks give about 10%. The right creative gives about 10x ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [13:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=785s)).
- **Most creatives fail.** At [Boost App Social](../businesses/boost-app-social.md), only 1 video in 85 got the spend, and about 97% of creatives failed ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=38s), [12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=746s)). [Social](../businesses/social-app-studio.md) makes about 4,000 AI video ads, hundreds of playables and thousands of statics a month (2026-07) ([source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=221s)).
- **Raw beats polished.** Local micro-influencers reading scripts beat big brands' polished ads from 2018 to 2020 ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [8:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=518s)). For [Brawler](../businesses/brawler.md), raw screen recordings, talking heads and stitches worked. Polished visuals got near-zero revenue ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=544s), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=648s)).
- **The first seconds decide.** The first 5 seconds matter most, in copy and image. Name a specific pain ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [22:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1367s)). Yev's structure: a shocking facial-expression hook, a demo that calls out the audience, a pattern-interrupt ending ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=540s)). Creators should keep their hands busy to hold viewers past 5 seconds ([source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [26:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1608s)).
- **Hyper-specific call-outs.** [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) wrote "hang out in your basement after school and hit your pen until 3 a.m." ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [5:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=307s)). [Marcus Burke](../people/marcus-burke.md) uses the audience's words, such as "rewire toxic attachments", and lets broad targeting find them ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=733s)).
- **Match the messenger to the buyer.** High-trust purchases need an expert hook. Impulse buys need "I am just like you". [Evo Marketing](../businesses/evo-marketing.md) swapped 20-year-old creators for 35 to 40-year-olds to raise intent ([source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [5:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=313s), [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1056s)). Clear 30's best ads star a co-founder's mother for older users ([source](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [6:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=380s)).
- **Find winners organically, then pay.** Put a few dollars behind every organic video ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [5:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=328s)). [Ryan Thorp](../people/ryan-thorp.md)'s team boosted videos that broke out of 0 to 300 views, then ran them as Spark Ads ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=693s)).
- **Viral is not converting.** The most viral videos often convert worse than top-25% videos ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=834s)). A "best wrestling apps" ranking fails organically but works as a paid ad ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [11:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=699s)).
- **Formats that recur:** green screen in front of an app demo ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s)). Green screen reactions with an extreme spoken hook ([source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [35:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2107s)). Text walls and statics for older Facebook users ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:38:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2291s)). Captions, because about 80% watch Instagram without sound ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s)).
- **AI makes the variants.** Marcus's freelancers make 99% of client ads with AI (2026-06). His process: copy research from Reddit threads with Claude, visual research, copywriting, image-then-video generation in Higgsfield, then scale in Weavy ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:21:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1300s), [0:25:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1524s)). AI is not cheaper yet. It makes variables testable ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:22:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=1345s)). At Social, humans make ideas and AI makes ads ([source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [41:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2460s)).
- **Own the rights.** Boost App Social bought perpetual rights. With a one-year license a creator can demand $100K for a winner ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=673s)).

## Testing and scaling systems

- **Three levels on TikTok** (Ryan Thorp): each video in its own $10 to $20 ad set, killed after $5 to $10. Winners in ad sets of 4 or 5 at $100 to $500. The top 1 or 2 become evergreen ads that take 90% of spend ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=971s), [17:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1031s)).
- **$5K launch plan** (Yev): up to 100 variations. On TikTok 20 creatives at $20 each, off after about $5. Judge clicks within hours. Pick the 2x to 10x outlier ([source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [32:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=1939s)).
- **Ad set buckets** (Marcus): group by media type, format and message, with own budgets and cost targets. Meta spends about 80% on one ad per ad set, so pull outliers into their own sets ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:47:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=2849s), [0:50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=3015s)).
- **Creator briefs** (Ryan Thorp): lifestyle, trend, educational or testimonial. Trending sound, 10 to 15 seconds, about 25 to 27 cuts, POV shots of the app ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1342s), [30:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1818s)).
- **Repost early winners** (Dawson Gibbs): 10K to 20K views with 3%+ engagement gets reposted or reshot with a new hook ([source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [43:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=2596s)).

## Disagreements

- **One concept, many cuts?** Boost App Social turns about 5 creator videos into about 15 cuts and warns against spinning one video 2,000 ways ([source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md), [29:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1753s)). Marcus Burke ran about 400 iterations of one Moonate concept ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=603s)).
> Conflict: [7JtttjbTRAQ](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) limits remixes of a winner. [GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md) scaled one concept to hundreds of versions.
- **Show the app or sell the outcome?** Marcus says do not hide that it is an app. Say "app" or show a demo ([source](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md), [0:08:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUkXzJ2S6Z8&t=491s)). Dawson Gibbs says do not demo a boring app. Sell the outcome ([source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md), [13:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=833s)).
> Conflict: [GUkXzJ2S6Z8](../sources/GUkXzJ2S6Z8.md) wants the app shown in the ad. [VGJWRjz6nq8](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) hides a dull UI and sells the result.
- **Copying a winner.** Ryan Thorp turns a winner into a brief for all creators ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [13:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=788s)), yet his biggest hit could not be copied by 20 to 40 creators ([source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md), [20:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1253s)). Social says explore new concepts before making variants ([source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [47:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=2878s)).
