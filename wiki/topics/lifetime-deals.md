A lifetime deal (LTD) sells permanent access to a product for one payment instead of a subscription. Founders in these sources use it to fund a launch, validate demand and recruit engaged early users.

## Definition

An LTD is a one-time price for lifetime access. It runs as a private pre-sale to an audience, through a marketplace such as AppSumo or RocketHub, or as a permanent plan beside a subscription.

## Repeated patterns

### Fund the product with customer money

- [Gil Hildebrand](../people/gil-hildebrand.md) pre-sold 50 lifetime licenses of [Subscribr](../businesses/subscribr.md) for $20,000 in 2 to 3 days, before he built it ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=312s)).
- [Umberto Mezzadra](../people/umberto-mezzadra.md) made over $120K in 24 hours from 500 to 600 LTD buyers of an unfinished [Floga](../businesses/floga.md) (2025-05). He calls it capital from customers, with no equity given away ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=359s), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s)).
- [Mike Hill](../people/mike-hill.md) funds each new SaaS with LTDs: private, then AppSumo. [Frill](../businesses/frill.md) raised about $30K from a private LTD. The cash pays for early SEO content ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=400s), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s)).
- [Praneeth](../people/praneeth.md) argues every bootstrapped product should add an LTD, even as a limited offer: cash, morale, proof of demand and engaged users ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=288s), [5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=351s)).

### Borrow a marketplace's distribution

- [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md) built its first version in two weeks for an AppSumo slot. The LTD brought about $350K and about 10,000 users, at the cost of recurring revenue from them ([b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [2:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=163s), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s)).
- [SuperGrow](../businesses/supergrow.md) made $65K in 3 days on RocketHub, which took 40%. RocketHub set prices, emailed its buyer list and ran ads ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [1:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=80s), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=325s)).
- [TaskMagic](../businesses/taskmagic.md) paired LTDs with usage-based pricing and won AppSumo awards ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=228s)).

### LTD buyers are a feedback group

- Paid users give blunt feedback. Free users ghost you. "LTD exposes bad products really faster" ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=562s)).
- SuperGrow spent 1 to 2 hours a day in Intercom with LTD buyers and turned their workflows into features. 10 to 15 of them became launch-day ambassadors ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=680s), [12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=742s)).
- Floga put LTD buyers in a Telegram group. Many later features came from them ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [15:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=958s)).
- Mike Hill collects Trustpilot and G2 reviews from LTD buyers to lift domain ranking ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s)).

### Limit the downside

- Cap the deal by time and count: SuperGrow ran 3 days and capped at 300 users ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=646s)).
- Tier the offer. SuperGrow: $79, $199, $299 ([BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BNr1JOQdSN0&t=288s)). Floga: about $109, $199 and $349 for the full roadmap ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=838s)).
- Close the LTD forever after one last, higher-priced private round, with about $100K in the bank ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s)).
- Remove variable costs first. Bring-your-own-key AI and a desktop app let [Rabbit Holes AI](../businesses/rabbit-holes-ai.md) sell once with no running costs ([hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md), [3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYW9YDwHE20&t=225s)).

### Pricing the deal

- Pat Walls prices an LTD near 24 months of subscription revenue ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1046s)).
- People who hate subscriptions pay 3 to 5 times a yearly plan for lifetime access ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=1018s)).
- Mike Hill's single payment is about $59 to $100 ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [7:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=460s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: does an LTD launch work? [BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) and [DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md) made $65K and $120K+ in days. [hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md) says the [BlogToPin](../businesses/blog-to-pin.md) LTD launch made $0 at first, and a switch to subscriptions brought the first real customers within a month ([4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=282s)).

> Conflict: keep or close the LTD. [67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) closes the LTD forever once the bank holds about $100K. [hYW9YDwHE20](../sources/hYW9YDwHE20.md) and [NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md) keep lifetime as a main, permanent plan.

> Conflict: price level. [DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md) prices near 24 months of subscription, or 3 to 5 times a yearly plan. [67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md) sells at about $59 to $100, and [BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) gave buyers 4 to 5 years of value at $79 to $299.

> Conflict: the cost. [b5JhfGHafZA](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md) names lost recurring revenue as the price of an AppSumo LTD. [BNr1JOQdSN0](../sources/BNr1JOQdSN0.md) says a capped deal keeps support load and lost revenue small, and upsells LTD buyers later.
