Influencer marketing is the practice of paying, or partnering with, creators who already have an audience so that they show a product in their content. This page collects how app and SaaS founders in these sources find, pay, brief and measure creators.

## Definition

The creator shows the product to their audience. In return, the brand pays a flat fee, a CPM (cost per 1,000 views), a revenue or profit share, or equity. It differs from [UGC](ugc.md), where hired creators post on new or brand-owned accounts, but most sources run both side by side ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=646s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Know your numbers first

- **Keep CPM under RPM.** Find your revenue per 1,000 views, then close creators only below it. [Locked](../businesses/locked.md) has an RPM of about $2 to $4 and targets a creator CPM of $1 to $2 (2026-04, 2026-06) ([source](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=245s), [source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=578s)). [Arise](../businesses/arise.md) paid $1 to $2 CPM against a $6 to $7 RPM (2025-10) ([source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md), [18:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1112s)).
- **Margins by channel.** [Blake Anderson](../people/blake-anderson.md) gives these profit margins (2024-12): in-house UGC 50 to 80%, influencers 25 to 70%, paid ads 0 to 30% ([source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md), [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s)).
- **Find content-market fit before you spend.** Study what the niche already watches and asks in comments ([source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=197s)). Pick an app idea that is easy to understand in a video and plugs into a large content niche with many cheap creators ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [37:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2230s)). [Erly](../businesses/erly.md) added an alarm feature because the first MVP was not viral enough for influencers ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s)).

### Finding creators

- **Volume outreach with a short opener.** Start the DM with "Paid promo?" and send 100 a day ([source](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=756s), [source](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=439s)). [Pep AI](../businesses/pep-ai.md) sends over 1,000 DMs a week ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [41:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2516s)). Expect about 100 creators to find one that works ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=472s)) and more than 50% to never reply ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=614s)).
- **Ask customers who they watch.** [Boot.dev](../businesses/boot-dev.md) and [Jenni AI](../businesses/jenni-ai.md) ask users which creators they follow, then let the algorithm suggest similar ones ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=575s), [source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=171s)).
- **Copy competitors' creator lists.** Search YouTube by keyword, see which creators competitors use, and prefer creators a brand hired at least 3 times ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s)). Top Yappers finds creators who already promoted apps ([source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s)).
- **Use adjacent niches.** Boot.dev sponsors gaming channels, not coding channels ([source](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMkpiFIW8Xg&t=481s)). A fitness app can use cooking creators ([source](../sources/9WWvLj-NqEE.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WWvLj-NqEE&t=596s)).
- **Email big creators.** They turn DM notifications off. Self-managed Gmail addresses answer best ([source](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=863s)). Go around agents through group chats and creator referrals ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [31:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1919s)).
- **Look credible.** A strong Instagram profile, mutual followers and a blue check raise reply rates ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=312s)).

### Vetting creators

- **Comments beat follower counts.** Read comment sections for real questions about the creator's life, not emojis ([source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=604s), [source](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [29:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=1763s), [source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [14:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=853s)). A 5M-follower creator with 2M views a video still flopped ([source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [33:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=1992s)).
- **Ratios.** Views over followers above 10% and at least 100 comments per video ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=424s)). Judge the ceiling: at least one or two videos with 1M to 10M views ([source](../sources/KcCnqLBhvXY.md), [4:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KcCnqLBhvXY&t=288s)). A low like-to-view ratio on a sponsored post means it was boosted ([source](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [9:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=568s)).
- **Format fit.** Day-in-the-life creators convert best, because they use the app on camera ([source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=411s), [source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [9:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=552s), [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [20:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1247s)). Recipe, cooking and mukbang creators did not convert for [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1171s)).
- **Creators who sell their own product rarely promote yours.** Dating coaches sell coaching, so few promote [Sway](../businesses/sway-ai.md) ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=757s)).

### Deal structures

- **Four standard deals:** flat rate, CPM with a cap, minimum view clause (MVC), and base plus bonus ([source](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=511s), [source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [13:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=819s)).
- **Minimum view clause.** Set a paid floor a little below the creator's claim, for example 700K to 800K views for a 1M claim ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [9:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=575s)). The creator posts until views add up ([source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [15:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=951s)). [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md) regrets not using MVCs ([source](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md), [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=789s)).
- **Test one, then buy a package.** Open about 30% low, test one video, then buy 3 to 4 videos at a lower rate ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=485s)). Pep AI runs a trial month of 4 videos, 4 stories and a link in bio, then a 3-month deal ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=913s)).
- **Retainer plus bonus for new creators.** Pure CPM made new creators quit. A small retainer keeps them going ([source](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [10:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=650s)).
- **Affiliate cuts.** Pep AI gives a one-time 20% commission on subscriptions ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1297s)). [Castmagic](../businesses/castmagic.md) pays some creators and signs others as affiliates ([source](../sources/b5JhfGHafZA.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5JhfGHafZA&t=203s)).

### Creators as partners

- **Equity or revenue share instead of fees.** Several founders build an app for a creator's audience and split it 50/50 ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [10:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=654s), [source](../sources/FIgn-1kbzZw.md), [39:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FIgn-1kbzZw&t=2389s), [source](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=681s)). Equity appeals because brand-deal income is unstable ([source](../sources/zpTXi8WxeM0.md), [12:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpTXi8WxeM0&t=737s)). [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md) makes creators equity partners in each agency ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)).
- **Crawl, walk, run.** [Fitted](../businesses/fitted.md) paid a small creator $20 a video, then added vested equity and a revenue share ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=1624s)).
- **Hire the top influencer.** [Sunflower](../businesses/sunflower.md) gave its top influencer salary, equity and a mission. Rivals cannot easily take him ([source](../sources/zFfBuyKoaDM.md), [5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFfBuyKoaDM&t=353s)).
- **Use structure.** Set minimum posts and vesting with creator partners ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=397s)).
- **Embed the creator in the product.** The creator reads DMs, joins design meetings and shapes the roadmap ([source](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md), [11:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9655E10fxsk&t=669s), [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s)).

### Briefs and content

- **Show the app early.** Show it in the first 15 seconds and seed a "what's this app?" comment ([source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md), [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=1018s), [source](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=283s)).
- **The golden moment.** Cal AI gives no script. Creators only fit the food scan into a normal video ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=373s)). The screens a creator shows must be clear at a glance ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [36:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2163s)).
- **Match the CTA to the creator.** Demo videos get a URL overlay at the end. Known influencers weave the product into a vlog with no hard CTA ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [27:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1658s)).
- **Convert in the comments.** Creators answer "where do you find jobs?" style comments with the app name ([source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=508s), [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:17:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1027s)).
- **Rent the link in bio.** One Pep AI creator kept the link up for 2 weeks with no post and still drove many downloads ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=836s)).

### Measuring and scaling

- **Track per creator** with creator codes and AppsFlyer OneLinks ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=540s)). Or treat it as a black box and match view spikes to conversion spikes ([source](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=732s)).
- **Repeat exposure works.** Many "App Store search" downloads come from people who saw the app from several creators ([source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=220s)).
- **Turn winning videos into ads.** Run creator videos as Meta ads or Spark Ads ([source](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [9:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=585s), [source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=990s), [source](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [8:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=492s), [source](../sources/orYTlk_BhW8.md), [7:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=orYTlk_BhW8&t=425s)). Cut long YouTube integrations into shorts and ads ([source](../sources/laRliIVNZgU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laRliIVNZgU&t=696s)).
- **Coordinate a push.** Five creators posting on one day made $9,700 and pushed the app into the Finance charts ([source](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [46:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2771s)).
- **Build a team at scale.** [StudyFetch](../businesses/studyfetch.md) moved from contractors to full-time scouts, creative managers and a head of the creator program ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [37:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2254s)). Cal AI gave monthly prizes to its outreach assistants ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [42:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2553s)).

### Beyond short-form

- **YouTube integrations.** [Minea](../businesses/minea.md) and [Drop Magic](../businesses/drop-magic.md) work with 500+ YouTube creators. Old videos still convert 3 years later ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)). Microsoft MVPs made demo videos for [PCKGR](../businesses/pckgr.md) that keep bringing customers ([source](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=254s)).
- **Podcast and YouTube sponsorships.** [Sheets & Giggles](../businesses/sheets-and-giggles.md) sponsors shows its founder watches, each with a landing page in that audience's voice ([source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=488s)).
- **Paid reposts on X.** [Postiz](../businesses/postiz.md) buys about 20 paid reposts per article at $60 to $350 each (2026-07) ([source](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=390s)).
- **Seeding.** [Qalo](../businesses/qalo.md) gave a ring to Andy Dalton as a wedding gift. A TV moment later tripled sales the next day ([source](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s)).
- **Organic pickup.** Influencers who saw [Faceless Video](../businesses/faceless-video.md) ads promoted it with no outreach ([source](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=533s)). App-collection posts drove [Cat on Chair](../businesses/cat-on-chair.md)'s jump ([source](../sources/V_4kiR3R4QU.md), [5:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_4kiR3R4QU&t=300s)).

### Failures

- Paid influencers got views and almost no conversions for [Natural Write](../businesses/natural-write.md). Self-filmed posts sold from the first video ([source](../sources/1nfPvJKrYYQ.md), [3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nfPvJKrYYQ&t=184s)).
- An app made with a 1M-follower influencer got about 100 downloads from 1.8M impressions because it was not novel ([source](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=361s)).
- A campaign with millions of views made barely $500 ([source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=483s)). A $1,500 post can still lose money ([source](../sources/I10xtUiXfeY.md), [48:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I10xtUiXfeY&t=2880s)).
- PopStream failed because a top influencer post cost about $20K ([source](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=1235s)).
- [AbMax](../businesses/abmaxx.md)'s early big UGC deals ran at 5 to 10% margins. At $10K/month the founder dropped all influencer deals for Meta ads ([source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md), [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s)).

## Disagreements

- **Creator size.** Start with mid-size creators, because million-follower creators protect their brand ([source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=556s)). Day-in-the-life videos from big creators converted best for Cal AI ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [20:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1247s)).
> Conflict: [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) and [zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md) favor small and mid-size creators. [l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) finds big creators convert best.
- **Platform.** TikTok's audience has higher intent than Reels ([source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1150s)). YouTube has the best ROI, and TikTok builds awareness but does not convert ([source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md), [4:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=289s)).
> Conflict: [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) says TikTok converts best. [-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) says TikTok does not convert and YouTube does.
- **Briefs.** Pay creators to copy a proven video second by second ([source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md), [0:29:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1766s)). Give full creative freedom ([source](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [20:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=1204s)), or let the creator own the ideas ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=333s)).
> Conflict: [_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) and [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) want strict briefs. [mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md) and [rGLXc1GmsaI](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md) give creators freedom.
- **Revenue share.** A share of revenue took Cal Buddy from $20K to $80K/month ([source](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md), [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GQ27QVp3SzQ&t=464s)). MonAI found revenue share did not scale and moved to profit share plus retainer ([source](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rGLXc1GmsaI&t=281s)).
> Conflict: [GQ27QVp3SzQ](../sources/GQ27QVp3SzQ.md) credits revenue share for growth. [rGLXc1GmsaI](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md) says it did not scale.
- **Saturation.** Later Cal AI deals converted better because viewers already knew the app ([source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md), [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=739s)). Another founder says Cal AI wore out subtle fitness integrations for everyone else ([source](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [28:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=1735s)).
> Conflict: [l0vswWcGmMs](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) says saturation raised ROI. [7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md) says it wore out the fitness niche.
- **Payout caps.** Always cap CPM deals ([source](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=511s), [source](../sources/AtYlYzw40vo.md), [7:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AtYlYzw40vo&t=470s)). Do not cap payouts ([source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [40:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2435s)).
> Conflict: [dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md) caps CPM payouts. [RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) does not cap them.
- **Influencers vs paid ads for testing.** Influencers give guaranteed views, so they test one variable ([source](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md), [47:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukGtpnfBpvU&t=2826s), [source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s)). Others test formats on their own accounts first and pay creators only after proof ([source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md), [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s), [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md), [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1067s)).
> Conflict: [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) tests with paid influencers first. [RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) tests on founder accounts before paying creators.
