This page collects how founders turn a skill or service into a fixed package with a set price, scope and delivery process.

## Definition

A productized service sells a defined deliverable at a fixed price, often as a monthly subscription, instead of billing hours or quoting projects ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=397s)).

## Repeated patterns

- **Fixed packages, not hours.** [Scott Millard](../people/scott-millard.md) sells a main package with a down-sell and an upsell around it, then hires people to deliver ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [6:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=397s), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=445s)). [Brett Williams](../people/brett-williams.md) sells unlimited requests with no proposals. His effective rate runs from $300 to $5,000 an hour ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s)).
- **Niche down.** Scott went from "videos" to "short videos for B2B SaaS", which let him raise prices ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=648s)). [Ian Myers](../people/ian-myers.md) says a niche offshoring firm (video editing, CAD) can reach $1M to $2M ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=366s)). [Karl Hughes](../people/karl-hughes.md) picks the overlap of what he likes and what customers pay for ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s)). [Marcos Ruiz](../people/marcos-ruiz.md) picks clients who monetize views fast: coaches, consultants, education ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s)).
- **Sell the outcome.** Scott's package covers videos, ad-platform reformats and retargeting setup. A single video is the down-sell ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=768s)). Marcos sells revenue, not followers ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=593s)). [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md)'s LCA prices on the client's outcome ([source](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=326s)).
- **Start with people you know.** Karl began with one page of samples sent to his network. His first 5 to 10 customers came from a 50-person spreadsheet ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=156s), [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=251s)). Ian sold the first three seats to friends at no profit ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=505s)).
- **Content brings leads.** Brett's main lead source is Twitter. He spends $0 on marketing ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [5:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=319s)). [Nick Buzz](../people/nick-buzz.md) got [Baked Design](../businesses/baked-design.md)'s first $4,317/month client from a "tiny UI" post and grew by roasting landing pages in public ([source](../sources/TNZmNIYv050.md), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=716s), [14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNZmNIYv050&t=877s)). Scott posts educational content daily ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [5:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=336s)).
- **Borrow a creator's audience.** [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md) makes creators equity partners, such as Ali Abdaal for a YouTube agency ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=363s), [8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=487s)).
- **Async, low-touch delivery.** Brett runs every client through a Trello queue with one active request at a time and no calls ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=237s)).
- **Service first, then software.** [ListKit](../businesses/listkit.md) filled lead orders by hand within 24 hours, which made $105K to $110K/month before the SaaS ([source](../sources/WctFUP4w0sc.md), [3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=227s), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WctFUP4w0sc&t=357s)). [Savvy Nomad](../businesses/savvy-nomad.md) productized one painful workflow, a state domicile change. [Bohdan Drozdov](../people/bohdan-drozdov.md) suggests immigration at $1K to $2K against agency fees of $8K to $15K ([source](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=387s), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=596s)).
- **Write processes down.** Ian says growing past 5 to 8 people broke everything ([source](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).
- **Sell the knowledge too.** Info products are 29% of Brett's income (2024-01) ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=629s)).

## Disagreements

- **Unlimited or fixed scope?** Brett sells unlimited requests ([source](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md), [3:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXKzST0FE-A&t=203s)). [Hunter Hammonds](../people/hunter-hammonds.md) says no "unlimited": tell customers exactly what volume they get ([source](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=170s)).
> Conflict: [dXKzST0FE-A](../sources/dXKzST0FE-A.md) sells unlimited requests. [wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md) rejects "unlimited" for fixed scope.
- **Sales calls.** Brett takes no sales calls. Clients buy on the website ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=53s)). Scott took about 150 calls, then handed sales to a closer ([source](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [16:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=988s)).
> Conflict: [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) sells with no calls. [BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md) sells through a closer on calls.
- **Start cheap or premium?** Brett underpriced at launch to get reps, then raised prices ([source](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md), [2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vedkm3ZmHIE&t=127s)). Karl positions as premium: no discounts, no trials, quarterly commitments ([source](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i6kCkGmJc9M&t=427s)).
> Conflict: [vedkm3ZmHIE](../sources/vedkm3ZmHIE.md) starts underpriced. [i6kCkGmJc9M](../sources/i6kCkGmJc9M.md) starts premium with no discounts.
