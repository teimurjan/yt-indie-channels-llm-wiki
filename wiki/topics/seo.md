This page collects what the sources say about SEO: getting buyers from Google, YouTube search and AI answer engines without paying per click.

## Definition

SEO (search engine optimization) means making pages and videos rank for the words people search. In these sources it is mostly a bottom-of-funnel channel for SaaS and apps: pick keywords with buyer intent, publish the best page or video for each, and let it compound.

## Repeated patterns

### Capture intent, do not create it

- People who search "social media scheduling API" have about 10x the purchase intent of social media viewers ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [4:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=240s)).
- Search captures existing demand but is capped by search volume. Meta and TikTok create demand with more upside ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [20:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1206s)).
- Search traffic is high intent. In low-competition niches even a weak video can rank #1 ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [4:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=252s)).
- Long-tail integration searches get few views, but those searchers have a concrete problem and buy ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s)).

### Pick low-difficulty keywords with volume

- [Bhanu](../people/bhanu.md) keeps keyword difficulty under 10 in Ahrefs. "AI image generator" is KD 87 ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=525s)).
- [Ayush](../people/ayush.md) filters for KD under 20 and volume over 500. He writes 2 to 3 posts a week, then 6 to 7 ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=826s)).
- [Miquel Palet](../people/miquel-palet.md) targets bottom-of-funnel keywords with 300 to 800 monthly searches ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=411s)).
- Learn query patterns such as "best X for Y" and write the best page online for each brand ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [6:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=398s)).
- Go one keyword at a time. [Stuzzi](../businesses/stuzzi.md) started from one keyword, "drug rehab marketing" ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=298s)).
- Keyword data also validates ideas. Look for over about 1,000 monthly searches and low difficulty ([miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md), [5:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=miEUhwSR2-0&t=304s)).

### Free tools and programmatic pages

- About 90% of [SiteGPT](../businesses/sitegpt.md)'s Google traffic lands on free tools. A new tool takes under 5 minutes with Cursor ([Adl5_lJfkEE](../sources/Adl5_lJfkEE.md), [1:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=107s), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Adl5_lJfkEE&t=339s)).
- [Signaturely](../businesses/signaturely.md) got its first 1,000+ customers from a free signature tool and contract templates ([w8oUlfgvcTI](../sources/w8oUlfgvcTI.md), [9:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8oUlfgvcTI&t=570s)).
- [Fernando](../people/fernando.md) built about 30 free tools around his main product ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=450s)).
- [VEED](../businesses/veed.md) built a landing page for each of about 500 terms such as "trim video", plus a YouTube video for each ([PSx0vcUY-sk](../sources/PSx0vcUY-sk.md), [11:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PSx0vcUY-sk&t=693s)).
- [Supademo](../businesses/supademo.md) free tools bring about 20% of traffic and convert 15% to 20% to sign-ups (2026-03) ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=354s)).
- Side products that rank on their own can feed a core product. Specific products rank faster than general ones ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [7:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=444s)).

### Comparison and "alternative" pages

- Write comparison pages against every competitor, to piggyback on their traffic ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s)). See [competitor alternatives](competitor-alternatives.md).
- [Yadaphone](../businesses/yadaphone.md) asked authors of "Skype alternative" articles to add it. Some replaced Skype with it ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [9:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=582s)).
- Use lifetime-deal money to write "alternative to" pages early. Google and ChatGPT index them over time ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md)).

### YouTube is search too

- YouTube lets one brand hold several top results. [FollowBuddy](../businesses/followbuddy.md)'s 20-minute video ranks #1 for several keywords ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=291s), [11:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=672s)).
- YouTube videos rank on Google without the backlinks a blog post needs ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=596s)).
- A rough [Arvow](../businesses/arvow.md) video from 2 years earlier still gets about 300 views a day. 65% comes from YouTube search and 20% from Google (2025-10) ([VZ1XspToV1E](../sources/VZ1XspToV1E.md), [8:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VZ1XspToV1E&t=486s)).
- Pat Walls calls YouTube search underrated, because most people prefer AI-written blog SEO ([4KfFB-dh71Y](../sources/4KfFB-dh71Y.md), [16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4KfFB-dh71Y&t=963s)). See [YouTube](youtube.md).

### Pair SEO with search ads

- One page, two jobs. Pages that rank get free traffic. Run ads on pages that do not rank ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=381s)).
- [Late](../businesses/late.md): organic SEO brings over $8K MRR. Google Search Ads bring about $15K MRR at $2.50 revenue per $1 (2026-03) ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [7:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=472s), [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s)). See [Google Ads](google-ads.md).

### Use AI, but add something new

- [Rasty Turek](../people/rasty-turek.md) runs a daily Claude agent. It finds keywords with impressions but no clicks, then writes one guide a day. He avoids mass AI content ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=479s)).
- Use AI for research and outlines, and add new information of your own ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [15:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=931s)).
- Ship an imperfect page fast to get ranked and cited in LLMs. Then improve the pages that LLMs cite ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=293s)).

### Backlinks and technical basics

- Press links gave [YourMove AI](../businesses/yourmove-ai.md) high domain authority with no paid backlinks. SEO drove nearly half of traffic ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [13:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=839s)).
- Reviews on Trustpilot and G2 help domain ranking ([67zh8_yiPh4](../sources/67zh8_yiPh4.md), [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67zh8_yiPh4&t=522s)).
- Reddit posts can rank on Google. Keep the product link low in the post ([yue9in5oh64](../sources/yue9in5oh64.md), [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yue9in5oh64&t=624s)).
- A sitemap without the "www" prefix made Google ignore Yadaphone's pages ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=767s)).
- Request indexing in Google Search Console, and a page can be live within 24 hours ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=207s)).

### Expect a slow start

- SEO needs about 3 months of focused work before any signal ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [24:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=1450s)).
- [FinVsFin](../businesses/finvsfin.md) took about 6 months to get real organic traffic ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=181s)).
- Early grind on articles and backlinks now brings [BlogToPin](../businesses/blog-to-pin.md) customers "on autopilot" from SEO and ChatGPT ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=450s)).

## Concrete examples

- [InvestorHunt](../businesses/investorhunt.md): $2,800 MRR from SEO alone. Programmatic SEO brings about half of [Rashid Khasanov](../people/rashid-khasanov.md)'s revenue (2026-08) ([5-G9WHwQMwQ](../sources/5-G9WHwQMwQ.md), [2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=138s), [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5-G9WHwQMwQ&t=547s)).
- [Subscribr](../businesses/subscribr.md): programmatic SEO brings over 30,000 Google views a month (2025-12) ([7KqlwjBM_tM](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=75s)).
- [Elephas](../businesses/elephas.md): about $70K from Google alone in 2023 ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=274s)).
- [Stagetimer](../businesses/stagetimer.md): about 50% of traffic from Google (2025-06) ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=400s)).
- [Tech Lockdown](../businesses/tech-lockdown.md): over 2 million people found the site in 2 years (2025-07) ([ZGwQoRw7mh8](../sources/ZGwQoRw7mh8.md), [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGwQoRw7mh8&t=560s)).
- [Setter AI](../businesses/setter-ai.md): ranked on page one within a week, then number one worldwide for its keyword ([3eY71OS4MFw](../sources/3eY71OS4MFw.md), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3eY71OS4MFw&t=530s)).
- [Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md): most customers come from tutorials for each WooCommerce use case ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=317s)).
- [Vault Air](../businesses/vault-air.md): web traffic converts to the app at about 10% (2026-10) ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [7:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=452s)).
- [SEO Bot](../businesses/seo-bot.md) reached $100K MRR in under 12 months (2025-05) ([En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=En34iY-rQc0&t=179s)). [Outrank](../businesses/outrank.md) passed $200K/month (2025-12) ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=104s)).
- [Epic Gardening](../businesses/epic-gardening.md): about 12 hours a day of writing took the blog from $400 to $4K-5K/month in 2016 ([uBGbvKScavk](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md), [3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=233s)).

## Disagreements

- AI answers hurt informational content. Google's AI Overviews made [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md)'s content sites zero-click ([spiC5m6AJNs](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=187s)). YourMove AI's blog traffic fell for the same reason ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [15:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=908s)).
- Intent pages still work. Pat Walls says informational queries move to ChatGPT. Queries like "social media scheduler" still convert on Google ([E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md), [17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=1070s)).
- AI answer engines now cite Elephas listicles and send ready-to-buy traffic ([R4BS_UiTBPw](../sources/R4BS_UiTBPw.md), [11:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R4BS_UiTBPw&t=691s)).

> Conflict: [NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md) has Gus say SEO is mostly dead and only works in narrow niches. [E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) says SEO is not dead for buyer-intent queries.

> Conflict: [8BtHk-oNlN0](../sources/8BtHk-oNlN0.md) says run ads first to test the market, then add SEO after traction. [En34iY-rQc0](../sources/En34iY-rQc0.md), [88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md) and [miEUhwSR2-0](../sources/miEUhwSR2-0.md) start SEO on day one.
