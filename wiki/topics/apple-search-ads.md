Apple Search Ads (also called Apple Ads) are paid placements at the top of App Store search results, bought by keyword.

## Repeated patterns

- **A keyword channel, not a creative channel.** Founders with no edge in ad creatives can start with ASO and Apple Ads. Both only need keywords ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [4:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=282s)).
- **One of the big three app channels.** [Steve Young](../people/steve-young.md) names ASO, Apple Ads and Meta. He says one or two channels can reach $5M ARR ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=360s)).
- **Spend while LTV beats CAC.** With LTV at 6x to 8x CAC, every extra dollar returns profit ([a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)).
- **A side channel.** Founders run a small budget next to ASO, creators or Meta ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s), [mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [47:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2833s)).

## Examples

- **[Waitly](../businesses/waitly.md).** Apple Search Ads is the main paid channel ([a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=59s)).
  - CAC is about $130 and LTV is $750 to $1,000 (2025-09) ([a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)).
  - Spend is about $500 a month (2025-09) ([a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=578s)).
  - Trial users then get tip emails, quick-start videos and Zoom onboarding ([a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)).
- **[HabitKit](../businesses/habitkit.md).** About $100 a month on Apple Search Ads (2025-06). [Sebastian Röhl](../people/sebastian-rohl.md) runs it in the hope that Apple favors advertisers in organic rank ([gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A&t=396s)).
- **[Pep AI](../businesses/pep-ai.md).** About $150 a day on Apple Search Ads next to about $400 a day on Meta (2026-06) ([mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [47:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2833s)).
- **App acquisitions.** Steve Young buys apps with organic App Store reach. He then widens keyword coverage and adds a hard paywall ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [42:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=2530s)).

## Disagreements

- The sources use Apple Search Ads for different jobs. Waitly treats it as a profit engine with clear unit economics. HabitKit treats it as a cheap hedge for organic rank.

> Conflict: [a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) measures Apple Search Ads by CAC against LTV. [gpn1jEoWT4A](../sources/gpn1jEoWT4A.md) spends on it mainly in the hope of a better organic rank, which no source proves.
