This page collects what the sources say about unit economics: what a customer costs, what a customer earns, and what is left after fees and costs.

## Definition

Unit economics compares the cost to get one customer (CAC, cost per install, cost per trial) with the revenue that customer brings (LTV, revenue per install, ARPU), after platform fees and running costs.

## Repeated patterns

- **Spend while LTV beats CAC.** [Waitly](../businesses/waitly.md) pays about $130 CAC on Apple Search Ads against $750 to $1,000 LTV (2025-09). At 6x to 8x, every extra dollar returns profit [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s). Timo Nikolai: pay less per download than a user earns. $1.50 per download against $3 per user is 2x ROAS, so spend as much as you can [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=263s).
- **LTV is the north star.** Steven Cravotta tracks cost per install, trial conversion, subscription rate, retention and LTV, and aims for LTV at 3x CAC [source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md) [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1270s). [Late](../businesses/late.md) keeps CAC at 20% to 40% of LTV and under 30% of first-year revenue [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=789s).
- **Set a revenue-per-install floor before paid ads.** Steve Young runs Meta only at about $3 revenue per install, or a $10 to $15 cost per trial. One app at $1 RPI failed on Meta [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [16:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=990s). He hunts niches with high revenue per install [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=653s).
- **Raise revenue per user to fund acquisition.** [Social App Studio](../businesses/social-app-studio.md) runs dozens of tests per app. ARPU rose about 3x in 18 months, and portfolio LTV:CAC is about 3x (2026-07) [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=808s). Paywall tests can lift revenue per download 1.5x to 2x [source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) [12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=749s). A hard paywall at $60/year took one app from $1K to $2K a month to $8K to $10K on the same downloads [source](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md) [17:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1069s).
- **Margin depends on the channel.** Blake Anderson's ranges: in-house [UGC](ugc.md) 50% to 80%, influencers 25% to 70%, paid ads 0% to 30% [source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s). Joseph Choi sees 10% to 30% on paid ads ($10 returns $11 to $13) and about 50% on mass UGC [source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md) [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=63s).
- **Revenue per view.** [Locked](../businesses/locked.md) earns about $3 to $4 per 1,000 views and pays $1 to $2 CPM [source](../sources/qYYeu0E-1SI.md) [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYYeu0E-1SI&t=578s). Halo AI's early UGC ran at 5:1 to 6:1 ROAS [source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) [50:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3039s).
- **Saturation can raise ROI.** Cal AI later paid more per creator deal because viewers already knew the app and converted better. Some mom influencers were profitable at a $10 CPM (2026-08) [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=739s), [17:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1048s).

### Platform fees and running costs

- **Apple takes 15% up to $1M**, closer to 20% in practice. Above $1M it takes 30%, closer to 33% [source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s).
- **AI costs are small.** Under 3% of revenue for Blake Anderson's apps, more for image and voice [source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s). [Letterly](../businesses/letterly.md) spends about $5K a month on AI (2025-11) [source](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md) [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=676s).
- **Tooling fees scale with revenue.** RevenueCat takes 1% and Stripe 3%. Text messages at about $2,500 are Waitly's biggest cost (2025-09) [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=578s). PropGPT pays Superwall $0.20 per conversion [source](../sources/rCV-TVCnF6Q.md) [8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCV-TVCnF6Q&t=522s).
- **People cost most in SaaS.** [Papermark](../businesses/papermark.md) spends about 80% on freelancers and founder salaries, 15% on marketing tests and 5% on tools [source](../sources/F8i0kkrQ8_o.md) [11:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F8i0kkrQ8_o&t=706s).

## Disagreements

- **Target LTV:CAC.** Steven Cravotta and Social App Studio aim for about 3x [source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md) [21:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=1270s), [source](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md) [14:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=866s). Timo Nikolai scales hard at 2x ROAS [source](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) [4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=263s).

> Conflict: [hKqLceTPLWM](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md) wants LTV at 3x CAC. [DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) says spend as much as you can at 2x.

- **Paid ads margin.** Blake Anderson puts paid ads at 0% to 30% margin [source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s). Late gets $2.50 back per $1 on Google Search Ads [source](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) [8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_rX4JJrYkY&t=490s), and Waitly sees 6x to 8x on Apple Search Ads [source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s).

> Conflict: [5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) and [0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md) call paid ads low-margin. [E_rX4JJrYkY](../sources/E_rX4JJrYkY.md) and [a1EXyJlSx9g](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md) show high returns on search ads.
