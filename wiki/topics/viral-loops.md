A viral loop is a product mechanic. Normal use by one user shows the product to new users, who join and repeat it.

## Repeated patterns

- **Put the product in the output that users share.** Shared forms, links and cards carry the brand back to new people ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=511s)).
- **Share the user, not the app.** People share Spotify Wrapped to say something about themselves. The app is only the vehicle ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=488s)).
- **Make sharing the same action as inviting.** Package a true, flattering fact as a clean card ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s)).
- **Design "screenshot moments" on purpose.** Celebratory animations, funny errors and human labels like "97% match" get shared ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=498s), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=609s)).
- **Ride an existing graph.** NGL moved an old behavior, anonymous Q&A, onto the Instagram graph ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [11:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=712s)).
- **Retention first.** Sharing works only after users form an emotional attachment ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [10:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=606s)).

## Examples

- **[NGL](../businesses/ngl.md).** Friends keep the NGL link in their bio, so churned users come back. NGL has 150 million MAU about 3 years after launch (2025-07) ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [1:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=79s), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=316s)).
  - Its north star: users who post the link and get at least two replies ([DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTree0unT_0&t=639s)).
- **[Lobby](../businesses/lobby.md).** A group-picture button makes a watermarked boomerang that users post to Snapchat and Instagram stories ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s)).
  - About 3 months after the feature, Lobby hit #1 in the UK, Italy and Germany ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [23:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1382s)).
  - The key metric was the share of users with 5 friends on day one: about 60% in Israel against about 20% elsewhere ([jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md), [21:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=1286s)).
- **[NotionForms](../businesses/notionforms.md).** Form respondents see the branding and a link back. Close to $37K MRR (2024-11) ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=70s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=511s)).
- **[Chatbase](../businesses/chatbase.md).** The launch tweet tagged LangChain and Pinecone. They reposted the demo, which spread it further ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=132s)).
  - Chatbase reached $1M ARR in 117 days ([E_sPvPHwpuk](../sources/E_sPvPHwpuk.md), [5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_sPvPHwpuk&t=322s)).
- **[Spotify](../businesses/spotify.md) Wrapped.** Tens of millions of social shares each year (2026-01) ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=546s)).
- **[Airbuds](../businesses/airbuds.md).** The full weekly recap unlocks only after the user invites friends ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s)).
- **[Netflix](../businesses/netflix.md).** "Are you still watching?", the "ta-dum" sound and match scores work as shareable moments ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [8:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=498s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [DTree0unT_0](../sources/DTree0unT_0.md) says to add invites to every product, because a shared link works as an endorsement. [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) says a referral program bolted onto something nobody wants to show off does nothing.

> Conflict: [Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md) says retention comes before virality. [jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md) shows the reverse order at Lobby: friend density from the loop drove the higher retention.
