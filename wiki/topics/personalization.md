Personalization is the practice of making a product feel built for each user, through tailored content, onboarding, labels or a model that learns the user.

## Definition

Products adapt what they show to each user, or make the user feel seen. Sources split this into real personalization (a model trained on the user) and perceived personalization (generic output that feels personal) ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s); [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md)).

## Repeated patterns

- **Hide the tech in a familiar pattern.** Spotify's Discover Weekly is a normal playlist; early Pandora made users train it with ratings, which felt like homework ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=117s), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=279s)).
- **Personalize early in onboarding.** Speechify asks for voice, highlight style and speed at the start ([55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md), [5:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=314s)). See [onboarding](../topics/onboarding.md).
- **Personas by emotion, not demographics.** Build "emotional pathways" for your 3 main personas and A/B test them ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [7:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=431s)).
- **Use the user's own data as proof.** Welltory shows each user their own stress minutes before the [paywall](../topics/paywalls.md) ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [31:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1898s)).
- **Celebrate identity, not activity.** "A hardworking night owl" beats "you completed 10 tasks" ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=440s)). Labels like an INFJ type are hard to churn from ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [6:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=401s)).
- **Make personal results shareable.** Spotify Wrapped, Airbuds recaps, Strava maps and the Wordle grid turn a personal result into a [viral loop](../topics/viral-loops.md) ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [8:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=532s), [10:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=639s); [Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [8:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=488s)).
- **Personalization as a moat.** A model trained on the user cannot be exported. Measure it: a 6-month user should get better results than a 10-day user ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s)). See [retention](../topics/retention.md).

## Concrete examples

- [Netflix](../businesses/netflix.md): every homepage is unique for 230M users, and about 80% of viewing comes from recommendations ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=321s)).
- [Spotify](../businesses/spotify.md): Discover Weekly (2015) gives 30 songs every Monday. Tim cites 100B tracks streamed through it and 56M+ new artist discoveries a week. Wrapped gets tens of millions of shares a year (2026-01) ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=154s), [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=546s)).
- [Midjourney](../businesses/midjourney.md): V7 turns personalization on by default, and over 70% of active users adopted it within 3 months ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=222s)).
- [Oura](../businesses/oura.md): retention in the high 80s (percent) on a $5.99/month subscription (2026-03). It shows the investment, such as 1,095 nights tracked ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=416s), [7:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=478s)).
- [Ramp](../businesses/ramp.md) learns company spend rules across 50,000+ companies (2026-03) ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=540s)).
- [Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md): over 90% of users connect health data on the first screen. About $18M ARR (2026-08) ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [27:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=1640s), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=0s)).
- [Starcrossed](../businesses/starcrossed.md) assembles a "soulmate" from preset traits after a long quiz, then paywalls the reveal ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=121s)). [16Personalities](../businesses/16personalities.md) tests have been taken over a billion times ([nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md), [5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=339s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) says a long, personal quiz is not friction; effort makes the result feel earned ([4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQKWYmrixCU&t=243s)). [55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md) says keep steps few and hide long step counts, because users who see 12 steps before value leave ([2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=134s), [7:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55hDj88zKa8&t=430s)). Both are by [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md).

> Conflict: [nQKWYmrixCU](../sources/nQKWYmrixCU.md) shows apps that make users "feel seen" without real personalization, via the Barnum effect. [yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md) says the moat is real, compounding personalization a user cannot export ([4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s)).
