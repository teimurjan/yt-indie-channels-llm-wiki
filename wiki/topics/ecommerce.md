E-commerce here means selling physical products online, mostly through Shopify, Amazon or TikTok Shop, and building software tools for the sellers who do.

## Definition

The sources cover two kinds of business: direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands that sell their own products, and SaaS tools sold to e-commerce merchants. The two share one audience, so the patterns overlap.

## Repeated patterns

### DTC brands

- **Content first, product second.** Epic Gardening and Vanman built an audience, then sold what it asked for. Kevin Espiritu chose to "be the brand that sponsors his own content" [source](../sources/uBGbvKScavk.md) [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBGbvKScavk&t=326s), [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=132s).
- **Pre-sell before inventory.** Hush ran Google Ads to a pre-order page with one sample; Sheets & Giggles built an 11,000-email waitlist with a locked-in price [source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md) [2:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=129s), [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=360s).
- **Crowdfund the first batch.** NeuroGum raised $30K on Indiegogo; Hush raised over $1M on Kickstarter; Sheets & Giggles made $284K [source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md) [6:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=408s), [source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md) [6:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=366s), [source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s).
- **Native short-form at volume.** Tabs had 60+ creators and thousands of reposting accounts; Wrumer got 200M organic views in a year; Double Speed runs phone farms of AI content [source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md) [10:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=642s), [source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [9:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=565s), [source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=219s).
- **Products that sell in 10 seconds.** Novel, cheap, emotional or slightly controversial products go viral; long-consideration products do not [source](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) [5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=333s), [source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md) [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=136s).
- **Talk to customers to pick the next product.** Hush called every customer, heard "too hot", and built the cooling blanket. Every later product came from calls [source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md) [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=297s), [8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=530s).
- **TV and press spikes.** Qalo sales went up 3x to 4x after Hard Knocks; NeuroGum and Hush used Shark Tank and Dragon's Den for reach. Spikes fade in about 2 days [source](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md) [9:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=562s), [source](../sources/BeRKQkU5Ceo.md) [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BeRKQkU5Ceo&t=648s), [source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md) [7:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=426s).
- **Inventory is the main risk.** Tabs was out of stock more months than in stock in year one; Hush did not plan for summer and nearly died [source](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md) [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5C3YCTL-36c&t=254s), [source](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md) [3:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IuoscQiQQLg&t=191s).
- **Raise order value with a product line.** Vanman's five or six products under $20 made bigger orders and started free cash flow [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [6:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=402s).
- **Free marketplaces.** Etsy and Google Shopping are "free marketing" for Athlete's Thread; Jeremy suggests starting on Etsy [source](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md) [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s), [source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md) [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=524s).
- **TikTok Shop.** Founder-shot "small business" content and flash sales; "we made too much candy" was copied by every creator [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [14:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=846s).

### Tools for sellers

- **Build in a domain you know.** Hassam sold on Amazon and built Launch Fast; Jack productized the profit spreadsheets he sold to Shopify clients [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=450s), [source](../sources/d8PdJJcEtE0.md) [5:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d8PdJJcEtE0&t=335s).
- **Borrow distribution.** Launch Fast traded equity to the coaching company Legacy X; Loïc Berthelot pays YouTube creators or makes them co-founders [source](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md) [4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=251s), [source](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) [9:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-OPKa8DIzKU&t=547s).
- **Shopify App Store reviews are the channel.** Kaching gets about 95% of reviews from support agents who earn a bonus per review [source](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md) [11:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=671s), [12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=733s).
- **Position on one job.** Alia went from "customer education" to "pop-ups" only and grew to $4M ARR [source](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md) [0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=26s), [4:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=270s).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: [KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md) says Facebook and Google ads did not work for Wrumer and organic short-form is the best bet. [dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) says Facebook, Instagram, Google and Amazon ads all work for Sheets & Giggles, and [IuoscQiQQLg](../sources/IuoscQiQQLg.md) launched Hush on Google Ads.

> Conflict: [5C3YCTL-36c](../sources/5C3YCTL-36c.md) argues dropshipping has no edge and a brand is the only path to equity. [-OPKa8DIzKU](../sources/-OPKa8DIzKU.md) built Minea and Drop Magic into large businesses by selling tools to dropshippers.
