This page collects what sources say about conversion rate optimization (CRO): changes to landing pages, onboarding and paywalls that turn more visitors into paying users.

## Definition

CRO is the practice of raising the share of visitors or installs that take the target action, such as a signup, trial or purchase, through design, copy, offer structure and testing.

## Repeated patterns

- **Fix the leak before you buy traffic.** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) says a leaky product funnel wastes revenue even with positive ad ROI. ([v1f9iYZ2GhQ](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=56s)) [Yev](../people/yev.md) points the whole team at the funnel step with the biggest drop-off. ([lXGmWdFLsuM](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md), [3:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=210s))
- **Keep one promise from ad to paywall.** [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) says the ad, App Store page and onboarding must sell the same result. Fix that before you test price. ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=462s)) [Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md) builds onboarding from the promise its Meta ads prove. ([tv5hEdmbUTM](../sources/tv5hEdmbUTM.md), [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tv5hEdmbUTM&t=531s))
- **Sell the outcome, not features.** Replace "real-time notifications with Slack and Jira" with "close tickets three times faster". ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [4:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=250s)) [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) replaced a feature carousel with a 7-day plan of early wins. ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [24:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1481s))
- **Fewer choices convert better.** One CTA beats two or three on landing pages. ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=34s)) [Jonathan Parra](../people/jonathan-parra.md) shows 2 plans and hides the rest. ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=641s)) [Mojo](../businesses/mojo.md) hid AI credit packs and raised new subscription revenue 14% (2026-01). ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s))
- **Structure before price.** Test plan count, plan order and trial setup before price points. ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=0s)), ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s)), ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2171s))
- **Remove trial anxiety.** Show the trial day by day and promise a reminder. Blinkist's timeline gave +23% trial signups. ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s)) Claim says "free" 5 to 7 times and shows a timeline, not "3-day trial". ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=645s), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s)) "No commitment, cancel anytime" plus "Continue" beat control by 10%. ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=344s))
- **Ask at the win, not at frustration.** Put paywalls, rating and share prompts at peak moments (peak-end rule). ([v1f9iYZ2GhQ](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=443s)) Clear 30's rating prompt after free feedback took reviews from about 50 to almost 1,000. ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [21:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1265s))
- **Design is a trust signal.** People judge trust in about 50 milliseconds, and a bad first impression can lose 20 to 30% of new users. ([v1f9iYZ2GhQ](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=98s)), ([AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=297s)) For money apps, answer "is this real, what if it goes wrong, am I in control". ([0GMQRCnOQmo](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [0:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=48s))
- **Readable, scannable copy.** Keep body text at 50 to 75 characters per line, because long lines make users skip benefits. ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=375s)) A hero heading of 40 to 60 characters and one desired action. ([m3NjcBDqFnA](../sources/m3NjcBDqFnA.md), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=85s), [4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3NjcBDqFnA&t=276s)) Jitter puts a one-line value proposition next to a product GIF. ([evhZQg2NHfo](../sources/evhZQg2NHfo.md), [12:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=evhZQg2NHfo&t=759s))
- **Positioning drives conversion.** [Alia](../businesses/alia.md) repositioned around pop-ups only. Sales calls got shorter and closed more often. ([MLAH5OZ5FO8](../sources/MLAH5OZ5FO8.md), [8:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLAH5OZ5FO8&t=483s)) [Massive](../businesses/massive.md)'s "Tinder for jobs" lands at once, while "job search on autopilot" does not. ([r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md), [3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=181s))
- **Test a lot.** Apps with 50+ paywall experiments a year make 18.7x the revenue of apps with one. ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [10:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=638s)) Pregnancy+ went from 1 test every 3 months to 3+ a month. ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s))

- **Benchmarks.** Vahe Baghdasaryan: trial start above 15% and trial-to-paid above 30% are good. Without a trial, install-to-paid of 10%+ is very good and under 4% is a problem (2025-11). ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [4:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=291s)) Jonathan Parra: average paywall conversion is about 8%, often 15% to 20% after iterations (2026-02). ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [54:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3254s)) RevenueCat 2026 download-to-paid medians are 2.8% for high-priced and 2% for mid-priced apps. ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [9:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=556s)) Average SaaS sites convert under 4% and the best almost 12% (2025-05). ([TIfiy1fodDs](../sources/TIfiy1fodDs.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIfiy1fodDs&t=0s))
- **Day-1 vs slow conversion.** 82% to 89.4% of subscription starts happen on install day. ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=200s)) [Vault Air](../businesses/vault-air.md) has its lowest conversion on day 1 and its highest near day 60. ([NuYO7DWzlaM](../sources/NuYO7DWzlaM.md), [1:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NuYO7DWzlaM&t=108s))

## Disagreements

- **Trial or no trial.** Claim removed its trial and went paid-only because it made more money. ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=953s)) Pregnancy+ found a free trial made 140% more revenue than a discounted intro price, and a weekly plan with a trial has $54.50 LTV against $7.40 without. ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)), ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [6:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=404s))

> Conflict: [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) shows paid-only beating a trial for Claim, while [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) and [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) report trials winning. Vahe adds that paid-only needs high volume and strong trial numbers first.

- **Show the price early or late.** Clear 30 tells users early that the program costs money, which raised conversion about 3%. ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [17:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1038s)) Quitter hides prices until the user taps "Start my journey today". ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [27:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=1624s))

> Conflict: [cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md) says to state the price early, while [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) shows hiding it until the user commits.

- **Lead with weekly or annual.** Tim Gabe says weekly is the new default and converts 1.7 to 7.4x better than annual. ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=83s)) Jonathan Parra preselects annual, and Built With Science gained by showing annual first. ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=862s)), ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=117s))

> Conflict: [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) says lead with weekly, while [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) and [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) say lead with annual.

- **How deep a discount.** Vahe's abandonment offers go to 80% off with a trial or 90% without. ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s)) Jonathan offers 25% to 33% off and saves bigger discounts for Black Friday to keep the brand premium. ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [19:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1186s))

> Conflict: [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) uses 80% to 90% off, while [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md) caps routine offers at 25% to 33%.
