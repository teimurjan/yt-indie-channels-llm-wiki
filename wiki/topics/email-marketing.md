This page collects how founders build an email list and use it to launch, sell and keep customers.

## Definition

Email marketing is selling to people who gave you their address: launch sequences, welcome and abandoned-checkout flows, trial nurture and cross-sells. Sources call the list "owned attention", unlike social reach ([source](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s)).

## Repeated patterns

- **Turn rented attention into a list.** [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md) optimized a signup box on Reddit-driven traffic, so he no longer depended on Reddit ([source](../sources/ptT0tfzeu8I.md), [5:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=357s), [7:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptT0tfzeu8I&t=445s)). One Reddit post with about 400 upvotes brought about 1,000 signups ([source](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=373s)).
- **Lead magnets from content.** [Thomas Frank](../people/thomas-frank.md)'s videos pitch free templates behind an opt-in. The first autoresponder delivers them and offers [Ultimate Brain](../businesses/ultimate-brain.md) with a discount ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)). [Jacky Chou](../people/jacky-chou.md) links each video to a newsletter lead magnet and pitches later by email ([source](../sources/spiC5m6AJNs.md), [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=spiC5m6AJNs&t=373s)). [Matt Giovanisci](../people/matt-giovanisci.md) turns video scripts into blog posts whose visitors join the list ([source](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=367s)).
- **Launch to a warm list.** [Dustin Stout](../people/dustin-stout.md) launched [Magai](../businesses/magai.md) to his 100K-subscriber blog list with no ads ([source](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=264s)). [Kleo](../businesses/kleo.md) sold only to its waitlist ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=228s)).
- **Nurture before you sell.** Kleo sent more than 10 emails in 4 weeks with no CTA until launch day. The first email answered the main objection ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=451s), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=511s)). [Umberto Mezzadra](../people/umberto-mezzadra.md) ran a 5-week curiosity sequence that revealed the [Floga](../businesses/floga.md) app "behind the red curtain" ([source](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [8:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=516s)). [Gil Hildebrand](../people/gil-hildebrand.md) emailed weekly, then ran a 7-day benefits sequence, a date, and launch-day reminders ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=249s), [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=649s)).
- **Size the list from the math.** Gil needed 50 buyers for $20,000, which set the list size ([source](../sources/7KqlwjBM_tM.md), [10:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7KqlwjBM_tM&t=649s)). [Colin McIntosh](../people/colin-mcintosh.md) plans on about 3% list conversion, so a $30K first day needs about 10,000 emails ([source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s)).
- **Email beats the algorithm.** Lara Acosta says email converts better than viral posts, because there is no algorithm in the way ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [9:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=572s)). Vanman's founder found email worked better than he expected ([source](../sources/6iqo7lNR_m0.md), [4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6iqo7lNR_m0&t=297s)).
- **Core flows.** A 5-email welcome sequence that tells a story and an abandoned-checkout sequence with an optional discount ([source](../sources/GFnBp8lMIf4.md), [12:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GFnBp8lMIf4&t=727s)). [Waitly](../businesses/waitly.md) sends trial users tips and videos, then a promotion near the trial's end ([source](../sources/a1EXyJlSx9g.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1EXyJlSx9g&t=277s)). [Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md) emails 50% off the next plugin three days after a purchase ([source](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=461s)).
- **Newsletters as a business.** [Alex Garcia](../people/alex-garcia.md) treats the list as an ad platform: with 10,000 subscribers an ad slot sells for more than $1,000. He pitches advertisers who already buy ads in rival newsletters ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=569s)). Growth comes from Twitter threads, paid ads and cross-promotion networks such as SparkLoop ([source](../sources/5MaPEK-yVVY.md), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MaPEK-yVVY&t=508s)). See [newsletters](newsletters.md).
- **Email as a service.** [Cardinal Mason](../people/cardinal-mason.md) sells email retainers of $2K to $4K/month. One email a week for $800/month is "probably fair" ([source](../sources/9pP4qSrt5GU.md), [10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pP4qSrt5GU&t=630s)). [Hiro Analytics](../businesses/hiro-analytics.md) came out of an email agency and finds leads in the Klaviyo partner directory ([source](../sources/NPpky92ZfjA.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=187s), [6:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NPpky92ZfjA&t=363s)).

## Disagreements

- **How often to email.** [Earnifi](../businesses/earnifi.md) sent only one kind of email, a matched airdrop with its dollar value, and got very high open rates ([source](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md), [7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u8sW-NhGfXw&t=455s)). Swim University mails 2 to 3 times a week ([source](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md), [1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7pCgbhUWW54&t=93s)).
> Conflict: [u8sW-NhGfXw](../sources/u8sW-NhGfXw.md) sends almost nothing. [7pCgbhUWW54](../sources/7pCgbhUWW54.md) mails 2 to 3 times a week.
- **When to pitch.** Kleo kept a CTA out of every email until launch day ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=511s)). Thomas Frank's first autoresponder already offers a discount code ([source](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OKl0C3zg0LU&t=457s)).
> Conflict: [XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md) waits to pitch. [OKl0C3zg0LU](../sources/OKl0C3zg0LU.md) pitches in the first email.
- **Expected list conversion.** Colin McIntosh plans on about 3% ([source](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md), [6:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dphK9rh2F5U&t=399s)). The Kleo episode's host math uses 1% ([source](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md), [17:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XifgHi9R5Rc&t=1046s)).
> Conflict: [dphK9rh2F5U](../sources/dphK9rh2F5U.md) assumes 3% list conversion. [XifgHi9R5Rc](../sources/XifgHi9R5Rc.md) assumes 1%.
