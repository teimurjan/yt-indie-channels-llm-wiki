Ghostwriting is writing social posts, threads and articles that clients publish under their own name.

## Definition

A ghostwriter or ghostwriting agency writes for a client's account, usually on X (Twitter) or LinkedIn, for a monthly fee.

## Repeated patterns

- **A fast first income from a writing skill.** Ghostwriting lets you earn before you have an audience, by borrowing a client's audience ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=677s)). Marcos Ruiz started with about $2,000 and signed two $1,000/month clients ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=136s)).
- **Pick clients who can monetize attention.** The Birdhouse serves coaches, consultants and education companies ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=62s)). Storyarb serves B2B executives ([source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)).
- **Retention is the hard part.** Alex Lieberman says getting customers is easy and keeping them is hard. Storyarb targets 10 months average retention against 3 to 5 months for typical agencies ([source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=609s)). Marcos sells monetization, not followers: a client who earns more than they pay stays ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=593s)).
- **Your own account is the case study.** Marcos grew his own account first, because prospects with more followers ignore you ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [10:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=634s)). Dickie Bush's first client came from a viral tweet ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=376s)).
- **Validate demand with a public post.** Alex Lieberman tweeted for executives willing to pay $5K to $10K/year and got 25 DMs ([source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [7:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=466s)). Marcos hired writers with a public tweet and a shared thread test ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=524s)).

## Concrete examples

- **[Dickie Bush](../people/dickie-bush.md):** let his first client name the price. He hoped for $250 and got $5,000 ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=437s)). He later built [Premium Ghostwriting Academy](../businesses/premium-ghostwriting-academy.md), a coaching program for freelance ghostwriters, at about $5M/year (2024-07) ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=77s)).
- **[Storyarb](../businesses/storyarb.md):** 12 clients at $7,000/month, $84K/month top line (2023-11). One interview per month becomes 12 pieces of content per week per client. Margin 30 to 35%. Goal: $10M/year with about 110 clients ([source](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s), [9:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=588s)).
- **[The Birdhouse](../businesses/the-birdhouse.md):** $0 to $65K/month in one year, then about $70K/month at about 60% margins with a team of 5 to 6 (2024-06) ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=0s), [7:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=458s)). Outbound was 100 to 200 cold DMs a day plus 2 to 3 tweets a day ([source](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [5:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=349s)).

## Disagreements

- Dickie Bush says ghostwriting caps out at $5K to $10K/month as a side hustle, so he productized the input (a writing course) instead ([source](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md), [8:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoFqTMPW_3E&t=497s)). Marcos Ruiz and Alex Lieberman scaled ghostwriting itself as an agency with hired writers.
> Conflict: [OoFqTMPW_3E](../sources/OoFqTMPW_3E.md) treats ghostwriting as capped and not scalable; [trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) and [nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) scale it as an agency.
- Margins differ: Storyarb reports 30 to 35% (2023-11), The Birdhouse about 60% (2024-06).
> Conflict: [nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) gives 30 to 35% ghostwriting agency margins; [trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md) gives about 60%.
