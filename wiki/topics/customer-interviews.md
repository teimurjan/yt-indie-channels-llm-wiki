This page collects what sources say about customer interviews: direct calls and conversations with users to decide what to build and for whom.

## Definition

Customer interviews are one-on-one conversations with prospects or users, by call, DM or in person, used to find the real pain, the best segment and the next feature.

## Repeated patterns

- **Talk to users all the time, not once.** [Tibo](../people/tibo.md) says builders skip the hard part, which is to talk to users every day. Until a product reaches $10K/month, its support link opens his Twitter DMs. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=485s)) [Gaurav](../people/gaurav.md) ran about 2,000 calls at about 20 a week. ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s))
- **Calls reveal the segment that loves the product.** [Kourosh](../people/kourosh.md) found that 20% to 30% of Voicy users were seniors or disabled users who needed it and were happy with the MVP, while most users only wanted 10% more speed. ([LrLDsvbdPr0](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [10:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=602s)) Gaurav found that "curious" sign-ups retained worse than "I need marketing now" sign-ups. ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=481s)) [Jay](../people/jay.md) went to meetups from his own app in Bangkok and found new sub-segments. ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [4:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=262s))
- **Trade value for the call.** Fastlane gives 7 days of extra access for a booked call. ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=400s)) [David Bressler](../people/david-bressler.md) did users' Excel work for free, but only on a call where they told him what they wanted. ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=589s)) [Vikash Prajapat](../people/vikash-prajapat.md) offers each new customer a custom tutorial by email. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [6:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=410s))
- **Support is interview time.** Vikash recorded over 1,500 support videos and calls in 3 years and mines them for content. ([W48emwbUlUE](../sources/W48emwbUlUE.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W48emwbUlUE&t=471s)) Tibo says a fix shipped within an hour or two turns a user into an advocate. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=542s))
- **Ask about past behavior, not opinions.** Gaurav uses The Mom Test: ask how they solved the problem last time and what doing nothing costs. Never ask whether they like the idea. ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=623s))
- **Learn the user's end goal.** Tibo says help with the ultimate goal gives 10x to 100x more value. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=542s))

## Examples with numbers

- **Fastlane:** about 2,000 calls in three phases (discovery, usability tests, customer success). $69K MRR about two months after public launch (2026-08). Calls go into Notion, and Claude indexes them into a "customer love score". ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [1:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=63s), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=542s), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=683s))
- **Supademo:** [Joseph Lee](../people/joseph-lee.md) talked to more than 100 B2B SaaS founders before building. They hated making demos and hated their own voice. Supademo passed $3M ARR (2026-03). ([l4WEqPX52Cg](../sources/l4WEqPX52Cg.md), [1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=74s), [3:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4WEqPX52Cg&t=229s))
- **Formula Bot:** weekly free-help calls turned a formula generator into a data-analysis platform with about 5,000 paying subscribers (2024-02). ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=69s), [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=589s))
- **Voicy:** calls led to a senior and disability focus. 20% of users use the 20% disability discount, and the app makes $16K to $20K a month (2026-10). ([LrLDsvbdPr0](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=125s), [3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=186s))
- **NomadTable:** a solo founder at $140K MRR and 1.8M users who still meets users in person (2026-09). ([0sO2OSN3jZw](../sources/0sO2OSN3jZw.md), [1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0sO2OSN3jZw&t=99s))

## Disagreements

- **Who to listen to.** Tibo says feedback from people outside the target audience is worthless, so reach 5 to 10 relevant people first. ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [7:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=423s)) Kourosh posts everywhere early and lets the calls show which customer picks him. ([LrLDsvbdPr0](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrLDsvbdPr0&t=684s))

> Conflict: [xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md) says to define the target audience before collecting feedback, while [LrLDsvbdPr0](../sources/LrLDsvbdPr0.md) says to stay broad and let interviews reveal the ICP.
