# Push notifications

Push notifications are messages an app sends to bring a user back, and the sources treat them as one part of a retention system rather than a fix on their own.

## Definition

A reminder sent outside the app (push, widget, Live Activity or a feed post) that prompts the user to return to a feature.

## Repeated patterns

- **Tie the notification to stakes.** Flame sends a morning "carrot" reminder to answer the daily question, then a later "stick" that warns the streak and the partner are at risk. The partner's answer stays hidden until you answer ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=711s), [0:15:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=924s)).
- **Copy Duolingo's daily style.** [George](../people/george.md)'s apps send daily notifications in Duolingo's style next to an in-app feed ([7yLgHeYeQAE](../sources/7yLgHeYeQAE.md), [41:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7yLgHeYeQAE&t=2468s)).
- **Reminders serve a clock, they do not replace it.** Zynga flooded Facebook feeds with requests, but the urge to return came from crops that rot on a timer ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=173s)).
- **Notify at the moment users already return.** Undertone users opened the recap a median of 10 minutes after a session. The redesign holds the thread in the Dynamic Island, then sends a "recap ready" notification ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=344s), [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s)).
- **Use surfaces beyond push.** Home and lock-screen widgets ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:16:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=974s)) and Live Activities ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [11:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=665s)).
- **Do not depend on one channel.** FarmVille's reminder engine broke when Facebook limited game posts ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=235s)).

## Concrete examples

- [Flame](../businesses/flame.md): day-30 retention rose from 0.8% to 50% with the habit model, carrot-and-stick notifications and widgets, over about 8 months ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:00:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=13s), [0:13:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=799s)).
- [Duolingo](../businesses/duolingo.md): doubling the streak freezes a user can hold from 1 to 2 lifted daily active learners by 0.38% ([IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [5:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=326s)).
- [Hinge](../businesses/hinge.md) Standouts reset every 24 hours, so a missed window is lost ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=242s)).

## Disagreements

- [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) cites a 2015 University of Bristol study: reminders worked only while they kept firing and got in the way of habit forming. He says to anchor the app to an existing routine instead ([IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md), [0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=53s), [1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKrLPhaFcp4&t=85s)).
- [An Nayal](../people/an-nayal.md) credits notifications, with widgets and the habit model, for Flame's 50% day-30 retention ([pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md), [0:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pb4JIH8FubE&t=711s)).

> Conflict: [IKrLPhaFcp4](../sources/IKrLPhaFcp4.md) says reminders do not build lasting habits; [pb4JIH8FubE](../sources/pb4JIH8FubE.md) treats carrot-and-stick notifications as a core retention lever.
