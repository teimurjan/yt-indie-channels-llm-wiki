# Platform marketplaces

Platform marketplaces are app stores and add-on directories inside a bigger product (Shopify, Airtable, Notion, Google Workspace, WordPress, Chrome, Roblox, Upwork) where a small tool can borrow the host's users.

## Definition

A founder builds an extension, add-on, plugin or game that lives inside a host platform, and gets customers from that platform's marketplace search, communities and trust.

## Repeated patterns

- **Build on a growing platform with a new API.** NotionForms and Sync2Sheets both started right after Notion released its public API ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s); [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)). [Data Fetcher](../businesses/data-fetcher.md) launched as Airtable opened its marketplace ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s)). [Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md) shipped two or three days after ChatGPT ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [5:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=352s)).
- **Port a proven add-on from an older platform.** Data Fetcher copied API Connector for Google Sheets, which had about 100,000 users ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=146s)). NotionForms copied Airtable's forms ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=91s)).
- **The marketplace is a lead stream.** It sends qualified leads, users trust approved apps, and the market is too small for VC-funded rivals ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=228s)). Google Workspace Marketplace search brings Sync2Sheets steady installs ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [9:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=542s)).
- **Reviews drive rank.** On the Shopify App Store, a new app starts at the bottom. Installs become reviews, and reviews become rank and revenue ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [9:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=580s)).
- **Borrow the platform's communities.** Notion subreddits and Facebook groups tolerated links to new free tools ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [6:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=400s)). Search the platform's subreddit for the pain ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=104s)).
- **Mine the platform's idea forums.** Barn2's first plugin had many votes on the WooCommerce Ideas Forum and no existing plugin ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=220s)).
- **Accept platform risk.** OpenAI can kill an extension any day ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=676s)). Judge whether the platform will build your feature by reading its roadmap ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [4:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=285s)).

## Concrete examples

- [Kaching Bundles](../businesses/kaching-bundles.md) (Shopify): about $400K MRR; five apps at $4.5M ARR and about 90% margin, almost no ads (2025-04) ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=62s), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=94s)).
- [Barn2](../businesses/barn2.md) (WordPress and WooCommerce): 20 products, about $1.8M/year, 17,000 active subscriptions (2026-02) ([9sJ2R0rM3CA](../sources/9sJ2R0rM3CA.md), [2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9sJ2R0rM3CA&t=156s)).
- [NotionForms](../businesses/notionforms.md) (Notion): close to $37K MRR, close to 100K users; MVP in 6 days (2024-11) ([WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=70s)).
- [Data Fetcher](../businesses/data-fetcher.md) (Airtable): $23K MRR from 600 customers, solo, 85% margin (2025-08) ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=45s)).
- [Superpower ChatGPT](../businesses/superpower-chatgpt.md) (Chrome on ChatGPT): $20K to $30K MRR, about 150K weekly active users, no ad spend (2024-08) ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=76s)).
- [Sync2Sheets](../businesses/sync2sheets.md) (Google Workspace): about $9K/month, 70K users, built in 2 weeks (2025-06) ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=45s), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=269s)).
- [Lancer](../businesses/lancer.md) (Upwork): an AI agent that bids on Upwork's about 200K monthly job posts. $10K MRR by month 3 or 4 (2026-03) ([LKARRA0MvY4](../sources/LKARRA0MvY4.md), [1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=78s), [2:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LKARRA0MvY4&t=153s)).
- [Hide or Die](../businesses/hide-or-die.md) (Roblox): about $45K/month. Roblox has about 400M monthly active users (2025-04) ([8xgnm6SynH4](../sources/8xgnm6SynH4.md), [5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=312s), [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8xgnm6SynH4&t=616s)).
- [Athlete's Thread](../businesses/athletes-thread.md) and its sister brands use Etsy and Google Shopping as free marketplaces ([CnE_fLDqa7M](../sources/CnE_fLDqa7M.md), [14:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CnE_fLDqa7M&t=860s)).

## Where to build

- [Andy Cloke](../people/andy-cloke.md) names Notion (new API, automation gaps) and Figma (export to site builders) ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=374s)).
- [Saeed Ezzati](../people/saeed-ezzati.md): big platforms (Gmail, YouTube, Roblox) bring users; small ones (Zoom, Salesforce) bring less competition ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=120s)).

## Disagreements

- Free first or charge first. Saeed kept his extension 100% free for 9 months and earned first from a newsletter ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=519s)). Kaching launched free and charged only after reviews ([vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md), [13:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLLBzUZr6-s&t=782s)). Leandro says to charge from day one; removing his free plan took revenue from about $5K to $8K/month ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [12:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=754s), [13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=836s)).

> Conflict: [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) and [vLLBzUZr6-s](../sources/vLLBzUZr6-s.md) launch free to build users and reviews; [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md) says free usage disappears when you charge, so charge from day one.

- Building on ChatGPT. Andy says not to build for ChatGPT or Claude because competition is too high (2025-08) ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=374s)). Saeed's ChatGPT extension makes $20K to $30K MRR (2024-08) ([ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md), [1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ty9ZcimL6VE&t=76s)).

> Conflict: [NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md) advises against building on ChatGPT; [ty9ZcimL6VE](../sources/ty9ZcimL6VE.md) shows a profitable ChatGPT extension from the launch window.
