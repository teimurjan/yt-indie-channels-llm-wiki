This page collects what sources say about no-code: building and running real products with visual tools such as Bubble, Framer, Webflow and Lovable instead of hand-written code.

## Definition

No-code means building an app or website in a visual builder, with little or no hand-written code. In these sources it covers two uses: founders who run whole SaaS products on Bubble, and designers who ship websites in Framer or Webflow.

## Repeated patterns

- **Non-technical founders ship real businesses.** [David Bressler](../people/david-bressler.md) built Formula Bot on Bubble in about 6 weeks of paternity leave, learning from the Bubble docs and YouTube. ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=136s), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=254s)) [Dustin Stout](../people/dustin-stout.md) had no money for developers and built the [Magai](../businesses/magai.md) MVP on Bubble in 8 weeks. ([DV7bBCAABg4](../sources/DV7bBCAABg4.md), [3:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DV7bBCAABg4&t=221s)) [Jeremy Redman](../people/jeremy-redman.md) started [TaskMagic](../businesses/taskmagic.md) non-technical with a slow no-code builder. ([iVy5J7iE-3Q](../sources/iVy5J7iE-3Q.md), [4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVy5J7iE-3Q&t=260s))
- **Prove the core, then add the rest.** Faceless Video's first-month MVP only turned text into a video with Bubble and APIs. Accounts, payments and pricing came later. ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s)) [Louis Pereira](../people/louis-pereira.md) built AudioPen in a 12-hour hackathon and opened a waitlist about 10 hours in. ([k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s))
- **Build many small things.** Louis built 15 to 20 Bubble tools before AudioPen, and says to ship tools you can shut down without harm. ([k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=171s), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=254s))
- **No-code lets non-engineers run the company.** [Savvy Nomad](../businesses/savvy-nomad.md) runs the whole product on Bubble and the site on Framer so non-technical operators can work on it. ([BHhg-l9AZpM](../sources/BHhg-l9AZpM.md), [12:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BHhg-l9AZpM&t=765s)) [Pckgr](../businesses/pckgr.md) also runs its app on Bubble. ([LRX8TWC2hTM](../sources/LRX8TWC2hTM.md), [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRX8TWC2hTM&t=571s))
- **No-code users are also a market.** [Andy Cloke](../people/andy-cloke.md) says ready-made no-code integrations let less technical users succeed, which took [Data Fetcher](../businesses/data-fetcher.md) to $10K MRR. ([NvtsM8Nk72c](../sources/NvtsM8Nk72c.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvtsM8Nk72c&t=418s))
- **AI builders join the toolset.** [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) built an app with login, search, upload and comments in [Lovable](../businesses/lovable.md). He plans pages and data first, builds the UI before the database, and keeps each prompt small. ([CfwNxDEXe6I](../sources/CfwNxDEXe6I.md), [0:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CfwNxDEXe6I&t=59s), [2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CfwNxDEXe6I&t=121s), [13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CfwNxDEXe6I&t=837s)) Framer AI made a site from one prompt in under 2 minutes, but layout, assets and motion still needed manual work (2023-06). ([U2EVNlDzAJQ](../sources/U2EVNlDzAJQ.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U2EVNlDzAJQ&t=0s))

### Website builders for designers

- **Framer designs and publishes in one place, but Figma still needs a developer.** Framer only makes web output, so keep Figma for apps, graphics and dashboards. ([XPim-a5R6yw](../sources/XPim-a5R6yw.md), [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=16s), [0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPim-a5R6yw&t=56s))
- **Framer vs Webflow.** Webflow needs HTML and CSS knowledge. A Figma designer feels at home in Framer in a couple of hours (2023-05). ([NT8w5ZS13ao](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [0:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=43s)) Webflow wins on CMS and suits developer-minded teams. Framer's Mini plan was $5 against Webflow Basic at $14 (2023-09). ([t8ThPNdc_9w](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s))
- **Figma to Framer.** Build clean, nested auto layout in Figma, export one desktop frame, then fix fonts, fill widths and breakpoints in Framer. ([AJBaBScKdwI](../sources/AJBaBScKdwI.md), [0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJBaBScKdwI&t=24s), [5:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJBaBScKdwI&t=318s)), ([0SzJOMS0RPA](../sources/0SzJOMS0RPA.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0SzJOMS0RPA&t=125s)), ([7drmXMS8Jj8](../sources/7drmXMS8Jj8.md), [0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7drmXMS8Jj8&t=30s)) A browser extension copied 4 Webflow template pages into Framer in about 57 seconds. ([bBamB0yE12c](../sources/bBamB0yE12c.md), [2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBamB0yE12c&t=125s))
- **Stacks are the core skill.** Framer stacks work like Figma auto layout and CSS flexbox. Use them for every responsive layout, and start from a style guide of colors, text styles and components. ([PYskozIWh-E](../sources/PYskozIWh-E.md), [0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PYskozIWh-E&t=35s), [9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PYskozIWh-E&t=550s)), ([rEjd7QRxtDY](../sources/rEjd7QRxtDY.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rEjd7QRxtDY&t=0s)), ([wgMgje60Ovw](../sources/wgMgje60Ovw.md), [0:18:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wgMgje60Ovw&t=1122s))
- **Built-in features replace code.** Framer has a CMS, SEO settings, analytics, code components for custom behavior, carousels, tickers and scroll effects. ([XLWXztyA80Q](../sources/XLWXztyA80Q.md), [2:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=140s), [12:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=766s), [13:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XLWXztyA80Q&t=809s)), ([ADrNwS19i5I](../sources/ADrNwS19i5I.md), [2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ADrNwS19i5I&t=154s)), ([nS_9yAABLwc](../sources/nS_9yAABLwc.md), [0:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nS_9yAABLwc&t=17s)) Components use states, variants and variables, and can nest. ([13OKVxg6ecI](../sources/13OKVxg6ecI.md), [0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=13OKVxg6ecI&t=7s)), ([Z-Jic1TrMZQ](../sources/Z-Jic1TrMZQ.md), [0:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z-Jic1TrMZQ&t=17s))
- **Motion without code.** Sticky scroll, scroll transforms, scroll speed and loop effects are panel settings. ([DRvge30wQFM](../sources/DRvge30wQFM.md), [0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRvge30wQFM&t=16s)), ([zsxcr7aP2yo](../sources/zsxcr7aP2yo.md), [3:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zsxcr7aP2yo&t=214s)), ([tBOazoVzBNk](../sources/tBOazoVzBNk.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBOazoVzBNk&t=100s))

## Disagreements

- **How long to stay on no-code.** Faceless Video says stay on no-code until it breaks: Bubble handles security, scale and backend work, and Bubble support fixed their one scale issue. ([x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md), [4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9TUDb4sLE0&t=259s)) Formula Bot was 100% Bubble until a few months before its video, then moved to about 5 to 10% no-code. ([IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md), [5:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZsQqarWXtY&t=315s))

> Conflict: [x9TUDb4sLE0](../sources/x9TUDb4sLE0.md) says Bubble scales past $1M ARR with no need to leave, while [IZsQqarWXtY](../sources/IZsQqarWXtY.md) shows a product that moved most of its code off Bubble as it grew.

- **Which builder.** Tim Gabe's 2023 video says designers are leaving Webflow for Framer. ([NT8w5ZS13ao](../sources/NT8w5ZS13ao.md), [1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NT8w5ZS13ao&t=100s)) His later comparison still gives Webflow the win on CMS and calls pricing a tie. ([t8ThPNdc_9w](../sources/t8ThPNdc_9w.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=0s), [1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8ThPNdc_9w&t=61s))
