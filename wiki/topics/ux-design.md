UX design is the design of how a product works and feels across a whole flow: what users see, do, wait for and feel at each step. Most sources here are [Tim Gabe](../people/tim-gabe.md) breakdowns from 2022 to 2026, plus a few founder interviews.

## Definition

UX design decides the steps from first impression to repeat use: structure, friction, feedback, states and emotion. Tim's frame: features are cheap now, so "how the product left them feeling" is what users remember ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=25s), [1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=86s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Process: strategy before pixels

- [ZipSnap](../businesses/zipsnap.md)'s six stages: onboarding, discovery, UX, UI, emotional integration, delivery. Wire flows settle states and edge cases before visuals ([wmTkiF23GRQ](../sources/wmTkiF23GRQ.md), [1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=113s), [5:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmTkiF23GRQ&t=325s)).
- A written strategy doc, then screen maps, then wireframes. The doc doubles as prompt material for AI prototyping ([AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=157s), [3:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=219s)).
- Fix the core job first: a beautiful pizza app that takes 30 minutes to order still fails ([AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [1:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=95s)).
- Wireframe from real sites: screenshot at 1440px and 375px, crop by section, stack in auto layout, then take structure, not style ([40UAK-LKheA](../sources/40UAK-LKheA.md), [1:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=40UAK-LKheA&t=62s), [4:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=40UAK-LKheA&t=294s)).
- Customer calls drive the roadmap. [Fastlane](../businesses/fastlane.md) ran about 2,000 calls in three phases: Mom Test discovery, silent usability tests, power-user interviews ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s), [10:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=623s), [11:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=683s)).

### Remove friction and decisions

- Map the journey from sign-up to first success, then remove the 1 to 3 biggest frictions ([hIm3d3JAsPw](../sources/hIm3d3JAsPw.md), [4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIm3d3JAsPw&t=254s)).
- Map every decision point. Each tap or confirm prompt is an exit ramp. Netflix has at least 6 decision points before you watch. [ReelShort](../businesses/reelshort.md) plays on open ([7dCZrtH15zc](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=476s), [9:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=599s)).
- Hick's law (Apple Fitness gives one "Let's go") and Fitts's law (Waze's big, thumb-reachable buttons) ([D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [3:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=187s), [4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=245s)).
- Keep key actions within about three taps. Remove everything, then add back with progressive disclosure ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=569s)). [Raycast](../businesses/raycast.md) keeps zero clutter and reveals power as users go deeper ([eWGNjTLK84c](../sources/eWGNjTLK84c.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWGNjTLK84c&t=223s)).
- Simplicity as the moat. [Letterly](../businesses/letterly.md) asks "can we simplify an existing feature?" before adding one, and puts the most used action one tap away ([wvYej-2KWDg](../sources/wvYej-2KWDg.md), [3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=224s), [6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=381s), [8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvYej-2KWDg&t=533s)).
- Remove friction before blaming marketing. [Visualizee](../businesses/visualizee.md) replaced a node editor with a chat assistant and went from about $150/month to near $10K/month in about 6 months (2026-09) ([TUN8Y8__QGU](../sources/TUN8Y8__QGU.md), [0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=21s), [6:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=374s), [8:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TUN8Y8__QGU&t=485s)).

### Design every state, not only the happy path

- AI tools leave out states: about a quarter of the design reasoning that 5 AI builders stated never reached the interface ("design theater") ([8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md), [1:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=97s)).
- Break the product on purpose: kill Wi-Fi mid-flow, fail a payment, empty every list ([8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=512s)).
- Status screens are the product. [SolCard](../businesses/solcard.md) got 15 status screens and one-tap controls (freeze card, limits). Money loaded onto cards rose 79% and deposits 71% (2026-10) ([0GMQRCnOQmo](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md), [5:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=334s), [6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0GMQRCnOQmo&t=396s)).
- One pattern for one state. [Undertone](../businesses/undertone.md) had two different "sessions used" screens. The fix is one badge with a count and refresh date ([8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md), [5:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8EAYZe6y3I8&t=311s)).
- "Vacuum the lows": wait screens, errors and long forms are remembered too ([11h4H3TAGkM](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md), [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=569s)).

### Peaks, ends and emotion

- Peak-end rule: pick one "holy dang" peak and design the end. About 70% of Disney visitors return. Uber ends with a haptic, a rating and a tip ([11h4H3TAGkM](../sources/11h4H3TAGkM.md), [1:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=111s), [5:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=359s), [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11h4H3TAGkM&t=508s)).
- Win mapping: put paywalls, rating prompts and share prompts at the biggest wins, never at moments of confusion ([v1f9iYZ2GhQ](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md), [7:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1f9iYZ2GhQ&t=443s)).
- Gift, not receipt: anticipation, ceremony, afterglow. Reveal items in sequence for many dopamine events ([uoLpH_20qKw](../sources/uoLpH_20qKw.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=223s), [4:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw&t=274s)). Undertone's recap now arrives wrapped, with tape to peel, confetti and a booking prompt inside the peak ([YA3QpYIKqNI](../sources/YA3QpYIKqNI.md), [14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YA3QpYIKqNI&t=865s)).
- Emotion where it pays. In apps, the missing human signal (tone, body language) must be designed ([AnL49w4yIOw](../sources/AnL49w4yIOw.md), [9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=563s), [10:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnL49w4yIOw&t=605s)). Headspace sets mood with color, motion and sound ([D9SXaj6qQIQ](../sources/D9SXaj6qQIQ.md), [5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9SXaj6qQIQ&t=302s)).
- Personality in the core screen, not the edges: [Carrot Weather](../businesses/carrot-weather.md) made the data's delivery the product ([MAvKHToGiVY](../sources/MAvKHToGiVY.md), [1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=91s), [3:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MAvKHToGiVY&t=200s)).
- Physics-based micro feedback: rubber-band bounce, haptics, sound cues ([doiaMtD80p8](../sources/doiaMtD80p8.md), [0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=47s), [6:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=doiaMtD80p8&t=378s)). In finance and health, every micro-interaction is a trust signal ([Du2lkZ_cux8](../sources/Du2lkZ_cux8.md), [7:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Du2lkZ_cux8&t=471s)).

### Habit loops and retention

- Something should change without the user: FarmVille's rotting crops, Hinge's 24-hour Standouts ([VkFDdGGGA-s](../sources/VkFDdGGGA-s.md), [1:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=112s), [4:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=242s), [7:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VkFDdGGGA-s&t=457s)).
- Shrink leaderboard cohorts until winning feels possible, and put comparison at completion ([BxhsCu9hNpY](../sources/BxhsCu9hNpY.md), [1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=77s), [2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxhsCu9hNpY&t=124s)).
- Build the game, not the scoreboard. Stop before the peak of the S-curve of features ([LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md), [2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=126s), [5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXX_qOA5D8E&t=320s)).
- Craving machine, infinite game, invisible scoreboard: stacked, they turn engagement into identity ([yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md), [1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=114s), [11:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBpv5rZoBjA&t=717s)).
- Cumulative personalization: the product should get better for this user. Compare a 6-month user with a 10-day user. No gap means no lock-in ([yYs7iv81Ppk](../sources/yYs7iv81Ppk.md), [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=222s), [4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYs7iv81Ppk&t=283s)).
- Vanity mirror: celebrate who users are, not what they did, as Spotify Wrapped does ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [7:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=440s)).

### Rethink the category, not the screen

- First-principles design: [Tiimo](../businesses/tiimo.md)'s visual blocks, [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md)'s camera home, [MacroFactor](../businesses/macrofactor.md)'s shame-free tracking, [Ladder](../businesses/ladder.md)'s team workouts ([HA4yB_sd62Q](../sources/HA4yB_sd62Q.md), [1:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=115s), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HA4yB_sd62Q&t=375s)).
- Bet against the assumption the leader makes money from: [Timeleft](../businesses/timeleft.md) (no swiping), [Speak](../businesses/speak.md) (talk, not tap), [Evidation](../businesses/evidation.md) (no new device) ([zH0SVLzLBpU](../sources/zH0SVLzLBpU.md), [1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=60s), [2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zH0SVLzLBpU&t=178s)).
- Trojan horse: put the most advanced feature inside the most familiar pattern ([Tpg0pxKHrCA](../sources/Tpg0pxKHrCA.md), [4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tpg0pxKHrCA&t=279s)).
- AI and invisible UI: repetitive utility flows are the danger zone. Experience products stay. [Fin](../businesses/fin.md) prices per resolved ticket ([zRUoPIwCxfw](../sources/zRUoPIwCxfw.md), [5:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=303s), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRUoPIwCxfw&t=377s)).

### The designer's role

- Problem solving, not pixel pushing ([lgE0ULApU4E](../sources/lgE0ULApU4E.md), [3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lgE0ULApU4E&t=196s), [IaFrUED28mo](../sources/IaFrUED28mo.md), [4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaFrUED28mo&t=247s)). Ask "why" for every choice ([bOm9GDv96Tk](../sources/bOm9GDv96Tk.md), [1:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOm9GDv96Tk&t=116s)).
- Design the experience, not the frame. Seniors cut copy and reuse one visual pattern ([86PGRyQjdzQ](../sources/86PGRyQjdzQ.md), [11:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=677s), [13:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86PGRyQjdzQ&t=830s)).
- Portfolios: show problems and fixes, keep the site simple, publish ([j0KT9yaa-NI](../sources/j0KT9yaa-NI.md), [1:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=69s), [2:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j0KT9yaa-NI&t=144s)). Tim started his channel with seven years of product, UX and UI work (2022-05) ([N0vlBuMggKc](../sources/N0vlBuMggKc.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0vlBuMggKc&t=0s)).
- Readability basics still apply: 50 to 75 characters per line, left-aligned long text ([88XxC0_zs74](../sources/88XxC0_zs74.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88XxC0_zs74&t=437s)). See [Typography](typography.md).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: streaks. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) says streaks need agency (goal levels, a freeze) because a streak with no pause is what regulators target. [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) praises ZipSnap's Freecash diamond streak, where a missed day risks the diamonds and freezes must be earned.

> Conflict: more game layers or fewer. [yBpv5rZoBjA](../sources/yBpv5rZoBjA.md) stacks variable rewards, infinite progress and social scoreboards. [LXX_qOA5D8E](../sources/LXX_qOA5D8E.md) cites an S-curve where stacking streaks, points, badges and leaderboards lowers engagement.

> Conflict: friction. [55hDj88zKa8](../sources/55hDj88zKa8.md) and [4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md) cut steps to reach value fast. [Aa89MC8jX2c](../sources/Aa89MC8jX2c.md) and [v1f9iYZ2GhQ](../sources/v1f9iYZ2GhQ.md) say too little friction leaves users lost, and long flows can earn personalization or filter buyers. See [Onboarding](onboarding.md).

> Conflict: hidden costs. [0GMQRCnOQmo](../sources/0GMQRCnOQmo.md) notes StubHub's hidden fees raised purchases 14% but says a late fee kills the second deposit. [7dCZrtH15zc](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md) shows ReelShort's "anti-calculator" pricing working, and calls it a dark pattern.

> Conflict: what AI does to design. [IaFrUED28mo](../sources/IaFrUED28mo.md) (2022-11) says context and tradeoffs are beyond AI. [bOm9GDv96Tk](../sources/bOm9GDv96Tk.md) (2025-06) says AI gets 80% of most design tasks, and [8EAYZe6y3I8](../sources/8EAYZe6y3I8.md) (2026-10) says AI gains are the new floor, so care for states sets you apart.
