This page collects what the sources say about UGC: paying many small creators to post short videos and slideshows that sell an app or product.

## Definition

UGC (user-generated content) is native-looking short-form content, made by paid everyday creators or by the founder, and posted at volume on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts. It is cheaper and more numerous than [influencer marketing](influencer-marketing.md).

## Repeated patterns

### Find the format yourself first

- **The founder goes viral before hiring anyone.** Jay of [NomadTable](../businesses/nomadtable.md) got the first 50K to 100K downloads from his own videos and still tests every hook himself. Creators only remake hooks that hit [source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) [7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=464s), [26:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1560s). His own viral videos also prove to creators that a CPM deal is easy money [source](../sources/szgT-8gXd3s.md) [38:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2328s).
- **Post at high volume to find it.** Michael Que posted 10 TikToks a day for a month, proved the format on 2 more accounts, then DMed about 50 creators a day [source](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md) [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=531s). Max Hirsch posted 5 to 10 videos a day and says creators burn money until you know which angles work [source](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=377s), [7:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLidq3_FwW0&t=467s). Nicole and Aaron each warmed up 3 accounts for [Glam Up](../businesses/glam-up.md), then scaled to 30 or 40 creators [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [15:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=937s).
- **Cold start is founder-led.** Dan Kwon and Matt Gittleson both started with founder talking-head videos [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1002s), [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [17:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1067s). With no budget, a small team can post UGC daily on its own accounts [source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) [7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=448s).
- **Know why a format wins before scaling.** Vary hook, tone and when the app appears, to rule out a random hit [source](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md) [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=409s). An-Nayal's test: viral (100K+ views on several posts from accounts under 5K followers), scalable (makeable 100 times) and convertible (comments ask "what is the app?") [source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md) [5:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=324s), [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s).

### Formats that convert

- **Hook, then demo.** A strong first 5 seconds, then the app on a phone or a green screen [source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) [21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=1306s), [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=728s). Jay borrowed a hook from another niche ("I have done X for N years, and now I find out about this"). A creator version got 74K likes [source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) [13:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=783s), [22:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1340s).
- **A "golden moment" in the product.** [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) built deals around the food scan, with no script [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [6:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=373s). [StudyFetch](../businesses/studyfetch.md) built live lecture notes for the camera [source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) [26:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1582s). Coconote built creator-only screens [source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) [11:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=660s). [Cardstock](../businesses/cardstock.md) has a hidden setting that shows the app name in the scanner [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:39:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2361s).
- **Slideshows.** Cheap "story time" carousels and pain-then-solution slides with the app as the easy fix [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:12:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=728s), [source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) [16:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=962s). Small visual details decide reach: eggs in a food hook image got far more views [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=317s).
- **Engineer the comments.** "What is the app?" means it converts. "lol" or arguments do not [source](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md) [6:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VlJIgj6gM4&t=393s). Creators reply to comments to plug the app [source](../sources/_a4ch3pk_U0.md) [8:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_a4ch3pk_U0&t=508s). Lots used shock hooks so viewers argued [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [2:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=170s). Matt Gittleson prefers engagement bait that splits viewers in half [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [31:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1867s).
- **Name the product.** A viral video that does not name the app is useless [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [33:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2020s). Silent pack-opening videos got views but no installs [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:23:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=1404s).
- **Match creator to buyer.** Evo switched to 35 to 40-year-old creators for a business app [source](../sources/VGJWRjz6nq8.md) [17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGJWRjz6nq8&t=1056s). Older creators signal credibility for job advice [source](../sources/xEoLsAQFnOY.md) [9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xEoLsAQFnOY&t=546s). Mom creators converted but cost more [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [17:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=1048s).
- **Formats decay.** Joseph Choi's timeline: influencers (2021), mass UGC (2022), slideshows (2023), HeyGen avatars (2024), Flux avatars (2025) [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:49:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2973s). One strategy lasts a few months, a year at most [source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) [18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1084s).

### Recruiting creators

- **Small creators, picked on content, not follower count.** Best range 2K to 15K or 20K followers [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2080s). 5K to 100K [source](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md) [8:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdbimhZjm7k&t=484s). Under 30K with one breakout video [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [25:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1528s). A 768-follower creator outperformed a 552K-follower one (2025-09) [source](../sources/hKqLceTPLWM.md) [15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hKqLceTPLWM&t=917s).
- **Screen for charisma and reliability.** Emotive delivery, an aesthetic first frame, a storyteller [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [6:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=409s). A "face card" and fast replies. More than 5 active campaigns is a red flag [source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md) [11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=666s). Use a paid trial: [Halo AI](../businesses/halo-ai.md) paid $100 for 5 videos [source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) [12:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=743s).
- **Outreach at scale.** Cal AI ran about 25 assistants sending about 2,000 cold emails a day, contacted about 220,000 creators and signed 2,000 to 3,000 (2026-08) [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [34:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=2075s). [Boost App Social](../businesses/boost-app-social.md) used 1,000 Gmail accounts at 10 emails a day each, with 10% to 20% replies [source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) [13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=796s). Marketplaces such as [SideShift](../businesses/sideshift.md) and JoinBrands also supply creators [source](../sources/lXGmWdFLsuM.md) [7:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXGmWdFLsuM&t=429s), [source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) [17:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1050s).
- **TikTok Shop creators know how to sell** [source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) [23:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1413s), [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [36:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=2217s).

### Managing creators

- **Train them like a studio.** Sprout's creators take a course with quizzes and a content bank. Over 50% go viral within 2 weeks [source](../sources/32vqaJa90kw.md) [9:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32vqaJa90kw&t=578s), [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [31:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1902s).
- **Strict briefs.** [Reflectly](../businesses/reflectly.md) briefs set the sound, hook type, 10 to 15 seconds and 25 to 27 cuts [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [22:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1342s). Keep the demo the same and test only the first 10 seconds [source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) [19:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=1192s). Yiannis uses 80% fixed structure and 20% creator spin [source](../sources/eh1SDHX84vQ.md) [24:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh1SDHX84vQ&t=1483s).
- **One manager per about 10 creators.** SideShift keeps a range of 5 to 15 [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [9:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=569s). Coconote has one coach per 12 [source](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) [13:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E_4gRy4O03w&t=800s). Past 10 creators, hire a manager paid a share of creator bonuses [source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md) [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=223s). StudyFetch moved from contractors to full-time scouts and creative managers [source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) [37:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2254s).
- **Community and copying hits.** Group chats with wins and leaderboards [source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) [9:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=571s). Halo AI sends each viral video to all creators to remake the same day, and runs an AI coach in Discord [source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) [25:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1551s), [25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1502s).
- **Prune like a portfolio.** Cut the bottom, coach the middle, keep the top [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [24:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1484s). Dillion Verma kept weak creators 2 to 3 weeks too long [source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) [55:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=3353s). Keep 90% on the winner and 10% testing new formats [source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) [20:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=1242s).

### Volume and accounts

- **Views are a product of factors:** creators x videos per creator x platforms x views per post [source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) [9:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=591s).
- **About 3 posts a day per account** avoids shadowbans. One founder runs 5 accounts per phone [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=2171s). On TikTok, ramp from 3 to 5 to 10 a day [source](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md) [18:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=1092s).
- **Volume spreads luck.** Jay gets about one viral video a month from daily posting. 60+ creators give steady baseline downloads [source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) [31:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=1882s). Noise slideshows mostly get hundreds of views, but thousands of posts add up [source](../sources/CAi50V_eIRA.md) [0:45:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAi50V_eIRA&t=2745s).
- **US reach needs US creators.** VPN and SIM tricks got Pushscroll shadowbanned. US creators cost about $1,000/month instead of $400 [source](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) [0:58:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3539s). StudyFetch targets over 80% US viewers [source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) [20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1227s).
- **Repeat exposure helps.** Many "App Store search" downloads come from people who saw the app on TikTok several times [source](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md) [3:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=220s). Cal AI found saturation raised conversion per video [source](../sources/l0vswWcGmMs.md) [12:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l0vswWcGmMs&t=739s).

### Organic to paid

- **Organic winners become ads.** Run viral videos as ads, attach winning hooks to ad creative, and hire top organic creators for paid UGC [source](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md) [3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=218s). Lots got Meta installs at $0.39 to $0.80 from organic winners (2025-09) [source](../sources/NmIHgCRd04E.md) [5:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmIHgCRd04E&t=355s). See [meta-ads](meta-ads.md).
- **Thresholds.** Videos over 50K views become Meta tests [source](../sources/P4QodeA_lQ0.md) [6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4QodeA_lQ0&t=405s). A video at 5 to 10x the creator's normal views with 8%+ engagement is an ad candidate [source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md) [50:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=3015s). Do not touch paid until you have five winning formats [source](../sources/2cDIFx4WWD0.md) [31:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2cDIFx4WWD0&t=1900s).
- **Spark vs Meta.** Spark ads are cheap but have no install button. Meta has a CTA and attribution [source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) [33:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=2000s). Meta partnership ads on small UGC accounts beat Spark [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [37:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2251s). Mau Baron's Spark Ads on a 15-year-old creator cut cost to $3 per trial [source](../sources/yBjcmMhXSDk.md) [10:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yBjcmMhXSDk&t=624s).
- **Buy perpetual rights.** With a one-year license, a creator can demand $100K for a winning ad [source](../sources/7JtttjbTRAQ.md) [11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JtttjbTRAQ&t=673s).
- **Scale one winner hard.** Remake a converting video 50 times and pay creators to copy it 60 times a month [source](../sources/Q4k8JNYKJT0.md) [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4k8JNYKJT0&t=670s). Take the top 10 of 60 posts, make 3 variants each [source](../sources/ka3gqzmUuvc.md) [18:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ka3gqzmUuvc&t=1119s).

## Disagreements

- **UGC margins.** Blake Anderson puts in-house UGC at 50% to 80% margin [source](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) [13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u9u8yzPEpA&t=815s). Lino Leighton's early UGC and influencer deals ran at 5% to 10% margins, and at $10K/month he dropped them all for Meta ads [source](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) [6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=370s), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RypUD_Fuaqw&t=450s).

> Conflict: [5u9u8yzPEpA](../sources/5u9u8yzPEpA.md) says UGC is the highest-margin channel. [RypUD_Fuaqw](../sources/RypUD_Fuaqw.md) found it lost money next to Meta ads.

- **UGC or paid ads for early validation.** Most sources test formats organically first. Dan says UGC takes too long and gives weak conversion signals, so he validates with a few influencers and paid ads [source](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md) [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=377s), [19:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r_JUxa3RZgM&t=1178s). Roger Chen buys TikTok ads on mockups because UGC programs take 2 to 3 months [source](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md) [8:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ1W85VdIBk&t=531s).

> Conflict: [RVnnqTrSKZY](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) and [EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md) validate with organic posts. [r_JUxa3RZgM](../sources/r_JUxa3RZgM.md) and [jZ1W85VdIBk](../sources/jZ1W85VdIBk.md) validate with paid ads.

- **Copy exactly or iterate.** Joseph Choi pays creators to copy a proven video second by second [source](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) [0:29:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9agAuKOOPg&t=1766s). A frame-by-frame copy flopped for Matt Gittleson [source](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) [25:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ca_VnMTosRo&t=1520s), and 20 to 40 copies of a 66.9M-view video failed for Ryan Thorp [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [20:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=1253s).

> Conflict: [_9agAuKOOPg](../sources/_9agAuKOOPg.md) favors exact copies. [Ca_VnMTosRo](../sources/Ca_VnMTosRo.md) and [DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) saw exact copies fail.

- **AI UGC.** Nick Lawton says AI UGC gets views but "is this AI?" comments, not "what is the app?" [source](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) [13:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F2dVvzvwtBY&t=818s). Zuhair Lani builds slideshows with AI faces and skips creators for hook-and-demo [source](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) [16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1018s), [22:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMO0VYTBC7U&t=1377s).

> Conflict: [F2dVvzvwtBY](../sources/F2dVvzvwtBY.md) says AI UGC does not convert. [kMO0VYTBC7U](../sources/kMO0VYTBC7U.md) and [-VlJIgj6gM4](../sources/-VlJIgj6gM4.md) use AI content at scale.

- **Slideshows.** Glam Up's first slideshow got about 10M views [source](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) [9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=589s). Pingo's slideshows burned out and converted far worse [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [20:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=1256s). On Noise's own dashboard, slideshows gave only 2M of 54M views (2026-01) [source](../sources/_aNVPMvd8WU.md) [23:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_aNVPMvd8WU&t=1425s).

> Conflict: [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) built on slideshows. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) found they converted poorly.

- **Creator size.** Ryan Thorp prefers 2K to 20K followers [source](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) [34:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcDOgWGh4bc&t=2080s). Phoenix Do targets 50K to 250K [source](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) [10:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=616s).

> Conflict: [DcDOgWGh4bc](../sources/DcDOgWGh4bc.md) says under 20K followers. [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) says 50K to 250K.

- **Caps on creator pay.** Jay and SideShift say never cap a breakout creator. One brand capped a creator at $4K instead of about $75K and lost him [source](../sources/RVnnqTrSKZY.md) [44:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RVnnqTrSKZY&t=2687s), [source](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) [44:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qnDdabj8Fok&t=2698s). Pingo runs Noise creators on a capped CPM model [source](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) [35:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2103s).

> Conflict: [qnDdabj8Fok](../sources/qnDdabj8Fok.md) says do not cap. [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) uses capped CPM.

- **TikTok or Reels.** StudyFetch says Reels can go viral abroad and convert near zero, so non-US creators post on TikTok [source](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) [20:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=1227s). Canyon tells creators to cross-post to Facebook Reels for a small bonus [source](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md) [19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=1171s).
