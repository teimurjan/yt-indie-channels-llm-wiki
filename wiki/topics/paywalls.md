This page collects what the sources say about paywalls: the screen where an app asks the user to pay, start a trial or subscribe.

## Definition

A paywall is the screen that gates the product behind a payment. A hard paywall blocks all use until the user pays or starts a trial. A soft paywall can be closed. Freemium keeps a free tier and gates features. Most sources are subscription mobile apps, and many run their paywalls through Superwall.

## Repeated patterns

### The paywall is the biggest lever

- Paywall changes alone moved revenue in many apps. [Momego](../businesses/momego.md) ran about 10 paywall tests in 2 to 3 months (2021). Conversion went from 0.5% to 8%, and revenue from about $8K to over $30K/month ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [8:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=512s)).
- [Adam Lyttle](../people/adam-lyttle.md) changed his paywall and onboarding and went from $10K/month to $50K/month (2025-02) ([h9R6ZeNruuI](../sources/h9R6ZeNruuI.md), [14:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9R6ZeNruuI&t=862s)).
- [Curiosity Quench](../businesses/curiosity-quench.md) made $500 to $1K/month with a confusing credit shop. A Superwall template paywall with a free trial took it to $2K to $4K/month ([CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md), [15:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CtqEbgutSb4&t=946s)).
- With a paywall, about 90% of users only ever see the onboarding. So the onboarding is the product that sells ([CwHD6Fg-Mjs](../sources/CwHD6Fg-Mjs.md), [2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CwHD6Fg-Mjs&t=179s)).
- Superwall data: average paywall conversion is about 8%. It often reaches 15% to 20% after a few iterations (2026-02) ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [54:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=3254s)).

### Hard paywall after a long onboarding

- The common consumer-app pattern: a quiz of goals and pain points, a "generating your plan" screen, a plan summary, then a hard paywall ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=576s)). [Gravl](../businesses/gravl.md) made over $440K in one month this way (2025-11) ([2dfMUtHlQik](../sources/2dfMUtHlQik.md), [1:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dfMUtHlQik&t=71s)).
- A long quiz makes the user think about their problem and adds sunk cost ([loXc0Tyi4R4](../sources/loXc0Tyi4R4.md), [10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loXc0Tyi4R4&t=648s), [8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [8:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=514s)). [Go Tall](../businesses/go-tall.md) keeps a 91% paywall show rate despite a long onboarding ([EQfZCe3MkTU](../sources/EQfZCe3MkTU.md), [11:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EQfZCe3MkTU&t=681s)).
- Let the user do the "gotcha" moment, then hide the result behind the paywall ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=559s)).
- Ads need cash back fast, so apps that run Meta ads use a hard paywall with no trial ([DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md), [6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPb-M0Vt4uI&t=415s)).
- A hard paywall also tests product fit. Aim for 5% to 10% trial starts and 30% to 40% trial-to-paid ([Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [28:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6RstwPUWdU&t=1716s)).
- Get the user to the paywall. [Copia](../businesses/copia.md) raised the share of installs that reach the paywall from 50-55% to 85-90%, and conversion doubled (2025-10) ([D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md), [11:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7eaHcznLZU&t=696s)).

### Show the trial as a timeline, and repeat "free"

- [Blinkist](../businesses/blinkist.md) showed what happens each day of a 7-day trial and got +23% trial signups ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=40s)). The format still performs years later ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [40:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=2417s)).
- "No payment due now" plus a reminder before the charge removes trial anxiety. [Cal AI](../businesses/cal-ai.md) uses a 3-screen trial explainer ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=256s)). [Clear 30](../businesses/clear-30.md) and [Pingo](../businesses/pingo.md) promise a reminder 2 days before the trial ends ([cYcU4_HQdIk](../sources/cYcU4_HQdIk.md), [23:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cYcU4_HQdIk&t=1405s), [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [45:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=2716s)).
- Claim's paywall says "free" 5 to 7 times and calls the annual plan "Try it free". Do not print "3-day trial" on the plan card, because it makes people think about cancelling ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=645s), [13:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=825s)).
- Word choice matters. [Headspace](../businesses/headspace.md) renamed "trial" to "30-day guest pass" and got +7% new signups ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [1:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=106s)).

### Lead with one plan, hide the rest

- Show 2 plans, such as annual and weekly. Hide the rest behind "View all plans" and preselect annual ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [10:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=641s)).
- [Built With Science](../businesses/built-with-science.md) put annual first and moved monthly one tap away. Annual picks went from about 60% to 75%-80% at the same price (2026-10) ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=55s)).
- Put the trial only on the plan you want sold. [Moonly](../businesses/moonly.md) put it on annual only and got +39% conversion and +47% revenue per 100 installs ([puldme__Ckk](../sources/puldme__Ckk.md), [3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=puldme__Ckk&t=195s)).
- [Mojo](../businesses/mojo.md) hid AI credit packs on its first paywall. New subscription revenue rose 14% (2026-01) ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=0s)).
- Cut copy. Users do not read. Add "No commitment, cancel anytime" ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=456s), [7:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=477s)). A plain "try for 50% off" paywall beat a comparison table by 111% ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1564s)).

### Second-chance offers

- When a user closes the paywall, show a discounted annual plan. Discounts on weekly or monthly plans failed in dozens of tests ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [33:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2024s)).
- Examples: [Locked](../businesses/locked.md) offers $20/year after a $40/year paywall ([dWeoSKLt_fc](../sources/dWeoSKLt_fc.md), [1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWeoSKLt_fc&t=105s)). [Glam Up](../businesses/glam-up.md) shows 50% off on close ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [24:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1496s)). [PushScroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md) offers about 80% off ([PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md), [0:53:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdudtXwEsOQ&t=3218s)).
- Softer version: first restate the annual price in weekly terms ("$39/year is 76 cents/week"), then offer 25% to 33% off on a second "no" ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=956s)).
- [Momego](../businesses/momego.md) gives 7 days of Pro free to users who close the paywall, with no card ([T5zMsTw8GWQ](../sources/T5zMsTw8GWQ.md), [10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T5zMsTw8GWQ&t=646s)). Pingo offers a 7-day trial to users who abandon a 3-day one ([s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md), [50:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9qQPM92JNs&t=3048s)).

### Cover every placement

- Place paywalls at four points: session start, after X actions, low free credits, and after a cancel ([24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md), [41:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24QMRDXck70&t=2467s)).
- [Halo AI](../businesses/halo-ai.md) lets users skip the onboarding paywall, then gates the first edit. Both placements convert at 16.5% ([PFfo4JySptw](../sources/PFfo4JySptw.md), [41:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PFfo4JySptw&t=2501s)).
- [Celebs](../businesses/celebs.md) converts in about 10 places. Removing the onboarding paywall let users reach the reveal faster and pay later ([oxr_Dz1vhdU](../sources/oxr_Dz1vhdU.md), [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxr_Dz1vhdU&t=960s)).
- [Tinder](../businesses/tinder.md) never interrupts the core loop. It sells Boost, Gold and Super Likes in context once users are engaged ([4yKtlOWClhA](../sources/4yKtlOWClhA.md), [10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yKtlOWClhA&t=610s)). [ReelShort](../businesses/reelshort.md) asks for attention, then effort, then money, and puts the coin paywall at the cliffhanger ([7dCZrtH15zc](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md), [1:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dCZrtH15zc&t=88s)).
- Glam Up blurs scan results. The user pays or invites 3 friends ([Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md), [18:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bf12MNPO-Js&t=1096s)). [Undertone](../businesses/undertone.md) removed the blur on users' own therapy recaps, because it felt like a loss ([qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md), [2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJ4qYhTqAz0&t=164s)).

### Test structure before price

- Test localization, trial setup and plan count before $9.99 against $12.99. Adapty data: localization +62.3% LTV, trial structure +59.6% ([sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md), [5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sYRhXB_ZcLI&t=341s)).
- Macro changes first: how often the paywall shows and how hard it is matter more than copy ([si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=si3HzkBTPl4&t=1357s)).
- Fix the promise first. Ad, App Store page and onboarding must sell the same result. [Welltory](../businesses/welltory.md)'s paywall has no feature list and repeats the pain point ([Qh314MypshM](../sources/Qh314MypshM.md), [6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=367s), [7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qh314MypshM&t=462s)).
- Read the plan mix. Many monthly or weekly buyers suggest the onboarding did not sell well enough ([lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [16:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkwX_kc0NS8&t=1006s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: hard paywall or freemium. [Y6RstwPUWdU](../sources/Y6RstwPUWdU.md), [si3HzkBTPl4](../sources/si3HzkBTPl4.md), [Bf12MNPO-Js](../sources/Bf12MNPO-Js.md) and [rGLXc1GmsaI](../sources/rGLXc1GmsaI.md) found that a hard paywall made more money. [9655E10fxsk](../sources/9655E10fxsk.md) plans to drop [Fitted](../businesses/fitted.md)'s hard paywall because it limits growth and churn is high. [CtqEbgutSb4](../sources/CtqEbgutSb4.md) moved [Lovely](../businesses/lovely.md) to freemium after bad reviews. [zdbimhZjm7k](../sources/zdbimhZjm7k.md) keeps [Jungle](../businesses/jungle.md) freemium. [D7eaHcznLZU](../sources/D7eaHcznLZU.md) says a hard paywall signals a weak product.

> Conflict: paywall before or after value. [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) puts the aha moment after the paywall to keep the path simple. [E_4gRy4O03w](../sources/E_4gRy4O03w.md) lets Coconote users record a full lecture first, and [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) shows the paywall after the user has felt value.

> Conflict: weekly or annual first. [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) says lead with weekly, which converts 1.7x to 7.4x better than annual. [lkwX_kc0NS8](../sources/lkwX_kc0NS8.md), [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) and [ukGtpnfBpvU](../sources/ukGtpnfBpvU.md) default to annual for LTV and up-front cash. [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) found weekly beat monthly by a small margin.

> Conflict: trial or no trial. [24QMRDXck70](../sources/24QMRDXck70.md) shows Claim dropping its trial because paid-only made more money, and [DPb-M0Vt4uI](../sources/DPb-M0Vt4uI.md) runs no trial for ad cash flow. [qJ4qYhTqAz0](../sources/qJ4qYhTqAz0.md) found a trial made 140% more revenue than a discount, and [sYRhXB_ZcLI](../sources/sYRhXB_ZcLI.md) shows a trial raises weekly-plan LTV about 7x.

> Conflict: trial length. [PdudtXwEsOQ](../sources/PdudtXwEsOQ.md) found 7 days beat 3 days (2025-10). [s9qQPM92JNs](../sources/s9qQPM92JNs.md) found them within 0.3 to 0.7 points in the US, with 7 days about 2 points higher only abroad (2026-04).

> Conflict: renewals. [aLidq3_FwW0](../sources/aLidq3_FwW0.md) charges yearly up front so revenue does not depend on people who forget to cancel. [7dCZrtH15zc](../sources/7dCZrtH15zc.md) describes ReelShort's hidden coin math as a deliberate dark pattern.
