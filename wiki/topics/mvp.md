An MVP (minimum viable product) is the smallest version of a product that real users can try and pay for, shipped fast to learn whether the idea works.

## Definition

In these sources an MVP is one core feature. Founders build it in hours to about a month. Real users see it before polish, auth or extra features. "Viable means it can make a sale" ([KpVPST_P4W8](../sources/KpVPST_P4W8.md), [7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpVPST_P4W8&t=427s)).

## Repeated patterns

### Ship in days or weeks

- Speed limits the cost of failure. With a 90% failure rate, a year per try means 9 years to a win, so use no-code tools or boilerplates ([xeUhKuJbeWQ](../sources/xeUhKuJbeWQ.md), [6:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeUhKuJbeWQ&t=377s)).
- A failure after 10 days costs little. After 6 to 12 months founders get attached and ignore bad feedback ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=323s)).
- A hard deadline forces only the essential features ([zPijWd9uk-I](../sources/zPijWd9uk-I.md), [5:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPijWd9uk-I&t=316s)).
- Use the stack you know, or reuse your last codebase, so you only write the core logic ([HBCwzfFbopE](../sources/HBCwzfFbopE.md), [2:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HBCwzfFbopE&t=133s), [WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md), [3:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuzxmeUP6ro&t=239s)).
- AI builders shorten the path. [Lovable](../businesses/lovable.md), Replit or Bolt serve for MVP tests ([SJOFUBNHYqU](../sources/SJOFUBNHYqU.md), [8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJOFUBNHYqU&t=513s)). Bolt gets about 80% of the way, then Cursor for production ([jpSY4MlWX50](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpSY4MlWX50&t=223s)). AI writes better code for popular libraries ([BaWUPamqWlA](../sources/BaWUPamqWlA.md), [2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BaWUPamqWlA&t=143s)). See [Vibe coding](vibe-coding.md).

### One core feature

- Ship only the core loop. Big apps "earned the right" to social feeds and AI coaches ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s)).
- [Yadaphone](../businesses/yadaphone.md): one outbound call, done well ([88BbTpbWVpY](../sources/88BbTpbWVpY.md), [8:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=88BbTpbWVpY&t=520s)). [Youform](../businesses/youform.md) V1: name, email, star rating and CSV export, no integrations or logo ([_KaFS4Dxs5k](../sources/_KaFS4Dxs5k.md), [6:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KaFS4Dxs5k&t=368s)). [Sync2Sheets](../businesses/sync2sheets.md) first only moved the data ([npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md), [6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npcL7oRZQlI&t=413s)).
- Launch one feature without auth or payments. Add them later ([TCGXT7ySco8](../sources/TCGXT7ySco8.md), [4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCGXT7ySco8&t=256s)).
- Plan with AI: list all screens and flows, then build only the feature tied to the keyword ([vw2Yc4Y1PsU](../sources/vw2Yc4Y1PsU.md), [7:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vw2Yc4Y1PsU&t=451s)).

### Real users and money are the test

- Payment beats praise. Five people paid for a buggy [AI Carousels](../businesses/ai-carousels.md). Its founder then doubled down ([G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md), [6:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G4nsGvL4Fo0&t=418s)). [AI Crea](../businesses/ai-crea.md)'s real estate agents paid for a month-old MVP with bad image quality ([mjCa8IFjM2E](../sources/mjCa8IFjM2E.md), [3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=204s), [4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjCa8IFjM2E&t=243s)).
- Test with a real group: [CutCoach](../businesses/cutcoach.md)'s wrestling club did not use the coach-first MVP, which showed too much friction ([PIXXEAfo6MY](../sources/PIXXEAfo6MY.md), [4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIXXEAfo6MY&t=277s)).
- In a closed ecosystem, the MVP itself is the validation tool ([bq3-qH-CpYQ](../sources/bq3-qH-CpYQ.md), [5:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bq3-qH-CpYQ&t=331s)).
- Show it to the target audience, not friends and family. Build from support emails and drop-offs ([EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md), [3:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EF3uyvHHBfM&t=188s)).
- Ship to at least 10 people, then iterate ([FCGpgPZqmkY](../sources/FCGpgPZqmkY.md), [12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FCGpgPZqmkY&t=753s)).

### Marketability before code

- Make the video before the app. [PushScroll](../businesses/pushscroll.md)'s validation video got 80K views and about 500 comments ([ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md), [0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=11s), [4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ST2ROUFbdvU&t=281s)).
- If no video can sell the idea, kill it ([TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md), [2:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCRUvJ-Fq9U&t=141s)).
- [Erly](../businesses/erly.md)'s first MVP, an accountability log, was not viral enough for influencers, so its founder added a push-up alarm in a day ([uvIcGuN2iO8](../sources/uvIcGuN2iO8.md), [4:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8&t=257s)).
- A website is not distribution: [Qalo](../businesses/qalo.md)'s store launched to zero visitors ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=450s)).

### Pre-sell before the product exists

- [Floga](../businesses/floga.md) sold a lifetime deal on an unfinished app: over $120K in 24 hours (2025-05). Define the "minimum launchable product" ([DkmStHS8NP0](../sources/DkmStHS8NP0.md), [1:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=83s), [11:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DkmStHS8NP0&t=716s)).
- [AudioPen](../businesses/audiopen.md) opened a waitlist 10 hours into a 12-hour build. Testers paid before the hackathon ended ([k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md), [6:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2jecxFu2as&t=375s)).
- A one-page site to collect emails, aiming for 15% conversion, then one key feature for the waitlist ([plrni3IBEyI](../sources/plrni3IBEyI.md), [4:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=261s), [5:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=plrni3IBEyI&t=321s)).

### After the MVP

- Talk to users. [Fastlane](../businesses/fastlane.md) ran about 2,000 calls, which narrowed the product ([BA_fC95p43c](../sources/BA_fC95p43c.md), [0:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BA_fC95p43c&t=8s)).
- Treat the first customer as your employer and ship their requests the same day ([hYF4fQYlrso](../sources/hYF4fQYlrso.md), [11:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYF4fQYlrso&t=704s)).
- Fix activation even at a cost: [lemlist](../businesses/lemlist.md) went from 15% to 35% activation after a redesign ([ZEIPj7QbZeI](../sources/ZEIPj7QbZeI.md), [12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZEIPj7QbZeI&t=768s)).
- Fix retention before monetization ([axH1e2cZSrw](../sources/axH1e2cZSrw.md), [12:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axH1e2cZSrw&t=757s)).

## Disagreements

> Conflict: MVP quality. [tMkpiFIW8Xg](../sources/tMkpiFIW8Xg.md) says an MVP means minimum quantity, not minimum quality, and a poor one will flop. [ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) tells founders to ship one "so simple that it is embarrassing", and [VfNRd5Rk0cM](../sources/VfNRd5Rk0cM.md) and [9r0qPeSJGog](../sources/9r0qPeSJGog.md) kept users despite bugs and errors.

> Conflict: design up front. [k2jecxFu2as](../sources/k2jecxFu2as.md) designed in Figma before building, and [r4R_Hlw7sbo](../sources/r4R_Hlw7sbo.md) threw out the prototype to design by hand. [jpSY4MlWX50](../sources/jpSY4MlWX50.md) ships from a free Framer template, and [GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md) regrets agonizing over a store theme.

> Conflict: validate first or build first. [TCRUvJ-Fq9U](../sources/TCRUvJ-Fq9U.md) and [ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) test demand with a video before building. [EF3uyvHHBfM](../sources/EF3uyvHHBfM.md) and [WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) say a one-week MVP is the validation.

> Conflict: Product Hunt for an MVP. [G4nsGvL4Fo0](../sources/G4nsGvL4Fo0.md) got 5 paying users from it. [WuzxmeUP6ro](../sources/WuzxmeUP6ro.md) got traffic and a backlink but no sale.

> Conflict: free or paid at launch. [ST2ROUFbdvU](../sources/ST2ROUFbdvU.md) gave the MVP free for the first week. [npcL7oRZQlI](../sources/npcL7oRZQlI.md) says to charge from day one. Free usage disappears when you start to charge.
