This page collects what the sources say about hiring: when small founders add people, where they find them, how they test them and what they pay.

## Definition

Hiring here means the first people a small or solo founder adds: contractors, offshore staff, VAs, creator managers and, later, operators who run a business for the owner.

## Repeated patterns

### Hire from your audience or network first

- [Scott Millard](../people/scott-millard.md): all of his contractors reached out after they saw his content ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=557s)).
- [Greg Isenberg](../people/greg-isenberg.md) recruits the most active members of his community. He also cold DMs interesting people, even employed ones ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [8:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=529s)).
- [Joseph](../people/joseph.md) of StealthGPT hired all his developers from Twitter posts, with no pedigree needed ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=701s)).
- [Shervin](../people/shervin.md) asks creator friends and people at events first, then uses Upwork ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=771s)).
- [Assembly](../businesses/assembly.md) gets senior designers from the network. Video editors at volume come from referral bonuses ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [10:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=613s)).
- [Pat Walls](../people/pat-walls.md)'s first hire at Starter Story was his sister ([v-uhjlMg9L0](../sources/v-uhjlMg9L0.md), [3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-uhjlMg9L0&t=226s)).

### Test with a small paid task

- [Marcos Ruiz](../people/marcos-ruiz.md) tweeted for a writer and gave 4 to 5 candidates the same thread prompt ([trNGj1BN1GU](../sources/trNGj1BN1GU.md), [8:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trNGj1BN1GU&t=524s)).
- [George Lampropoulos](../people/george-lampropoulos.md) tests a hire on a small task first. His best return was a developer from Pakistan ([8kM-JcKpcDs](../sources/8kM-JcKpcDs.md), [9:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kM-JcKpcDs&t=559s)).
- [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md) accepts only a portfolio that gives a "jaw on the floor" reaction. A hesitant gut means no ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=491s)).

### Hire for traits, not résumés

- [Alex Lieberman](../people/alex-lieberman.md) hires for "unteachables": an obsessive brain, critical thinking, aligned values and work ethic ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [11:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=676s)).
- [Ian Myers](../people/ian-myers.md)'s first US hire ran children's summer camps and is now chief of staff. [Oceans](../businesses/oceans.md) hires smart operators and trains them ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=871s)).
- [Tim Stoddart](../people/tim-stoddart.md) hired early staff from halfway houses. The team has grown together over 10 years ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=670s)).
- [Eunice Lie](../people/eunice-lie.md) judges content hires on storytelling, not follower count ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [21:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1272s)).
- Assembly hires people who are comfortable with ambiguity ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)).

### Hire offshore to stretch the budget

- [Clipt](../businesses/clipt.md): a US editor at $5K to $6K was too expensive. They hired about 50 animators and editors, mostly in the Philippines (2023-10) ([e6COsh5wfNA](../sources/e6COsh5wfNA.md), [1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=87s), [8:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6COsh5wfNA&t=525s)).
- [Charlie Chang](../people/charlie-chang.md) runs a 26-person media team for about $20K to $25K/month (2024-02). His placement firm [Paired](../businesses/paired.md) places full-time hires at about $600 to $1,000/month ([G5cbMnFXcj0](../sources/G5cbMnFXcj0.md), [8:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=491s), [8:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G5cbMnFXcj0&t=534s)).
- Oceans places staff in Sri Lanka and South Africa who join the client's Slack and all-hands. It hires 30 to 40 people a month (2025-03) ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=94s), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).
- Shervin's first editor came from Upwork at $7 to $10/hour ([DZTApvWzMuY](../sources/DZTApvWzMuY.md), [12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZTApvWzMuY&t=771s)).
- See [offshoring](offshoring.md).

### Hire in a set order

- [Alex Goldberg](../people/alex-goldberg.md): writers first, then a VA for publishing, then a full-time editor, then design and dev. This freed him for partnerships ([7LZ0MTkNr34](../sources/7LZ0MTkNr34.md), [11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LZ0MTkNr34&t=689s)).
- [Dmitri](../people/dmitri.md) hired a contractor for each new channel who did the work and trained him ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [43:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2594s)).
- Ian Myers says to write processes down before you grow past 5 to 8 people ([gU9FtZIfb_M](../sources/gU9FtZIfb_M.md), [13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gU9FtZIfb_M&t=810s)).

### Creator programs need their own team

- [Canyon](../people/canyon.md): past 10 creators, hire a generalist manager first, ideally an ex-viral creator. Pay the manager a share of creator bonuses ([SuUJgmbQrVc](../sources/SuUJgmbQrVc.md), [3:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=223s), [5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SuUJgmbQrVc&t=317s)).
- [Joseph Choi](../people/joseph-choi.md) names three roles: head of social growth, creator manager or coach, and talent scout ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=695s)).
- [StudyFetch](../businesses/studyfetch.md)'s first marketing hire is a growth generalist who is operational and creative ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [47:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2837s)).
- [Cedric Roberge](../people/cedric-roberge.md)'s 4 outreach staff send over 100 DMs a day ([mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [41:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2516s)).
- See [creator programs](creator-programs.md).

### Pay with equity when cash is short

- Cedric's 2 developers are paid in equity (2026-06) ([mOpYezjqU9c](../sources/mOpYezjqU9c.md), [34:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOpYezjqU9c&t=2099s)).
- Alex Lieberman pays a CEO $100K to $200K plus 10% to 50% equity ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=738s)).
- A senior founding designer is expensive and often needs equity ([YS7uOXjmJTA](../sources/YS7uOXjmJTA.md), [7:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YS7uOXjmJTA&t=435s)).

## Examples with numbers

- StealthGPT: a team of 6 to 7, salaries $50K to $60K/month (2024-07) ([8yCXyCN_NR0](../sources/8yCXyCN_NR0.md), [1:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8yCXyCN_NR0&t=75s)).
- [Qalo](../businesses/qalo.md) hired 2 to 3 people a month and reached close to 100 employees at $30M/year (2024-03) ([GGxUH6EXF9I](../sources/GGxUH6EXF9I.md), [11:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GGxUH6EXF9I&t=684s)).
- Assembly grew from 3 to over 50 people in 4 months (2023-09) ([wqN-gjnpns8](../sources/wqN-gjnpns8.md), [9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqN-gjnpns8&t=551s)).
- Dmitri had 10 to 12 Upwork contractors. A support person cost $5/hour. Contractors at $50/hour beat his own $150/hour time ([CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md), [43:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=2594s), [51:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyaWU1qxQg4&t=3114s)).
- At $17K MRR, [Postiz](../businesses/postiz.md) did not have the money to hire. After growth it hired one support and one developer ([n7Obxh9-Rc8](../sources/n7Obxh9-Rc8.md), [9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n7Obxh9-Rc8&t=549s)).
- An agency needs another hire for every 2 to 4 new clients ([zTKYJWAEl78](../sources/zTKYJWAEl78.md), [5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zTKYJWAEl78&t=358s)).

## Disagreements

- **Contractors or full-time staff.**
  - StudyFetch says contractors failed on incentives, so creator roles are now full-time ([CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md), [37:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CdBuMyrqWCs&t=2254s)).
  - Scott Millard runs his business with only contractors ([BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md), [8:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BskeAo66er0&t=505s)).
> Conflict: [CdBuMyrqWCs](../sources/CdBuMyrqWCs.md) says contractors fail on incentives. [BskeAo66er0](../sources/BskeAo66er0.md) and [CyaWU1qxQg4](../sources/CyaWU1qxQg4.md) built their businesses on contractors.
- **When to hand off the business.**
  - Greg Isenberg hires an operator once a business makes at least $100K profit ([akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md), [10:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akM6P97_0B8&t=645s)).
  - Alex Lieberman hired a CEO after 2 to 3 months of proof ([nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md), [8:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzVcbrLl9VU&t=527s)).
  - [Sam Parr](../people/sam-parr.md) delegates at $5M to $10M ([jjUf7lrb_xQ](../sources/jjUf7lrb_xQ.md), [7:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjUf7lrb_xQ&t=437s)).
> Conflict: [akM6P97_0B8](../sources/akM6P97_0B8.md) waits for $100K profit before an operator. [nzVcbrLl9VU](../sources/nzVcbrLl9VU.md) hires a CEO after 2 to 3 months.
- **Young hires for social.**
  - Joseph Choi lists Gen Z hires who know trends but not conversion as a sign of a flop ([0mOLs315i04](../sources/0mOLs315i04.md), [4:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mOLs315i04&t=269s)).
  - Eunice Lie, 20, ran a campaign with about 2,500 applications from one LinkedIn post ([MsJNJfFeOCg](../sources/MsJNJfFeOCg.md), [18:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MsJNJfFeOCg&t=1104s)).
